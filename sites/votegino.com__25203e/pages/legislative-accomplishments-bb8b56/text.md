HB0038 – 10th Amendment HB0039 – Civil Justice HB0187 – Insurance HB0239 – Sex Defined HB0257 – Disability HB0306 – Girls’ Athletics HB0335 – Special Election HB1631 – Pre-K-12 Firearms Policies HB1634 – Discrimination HB1828 – State Books HB1907 – Illegal Alien HB1908 – BOLO Act HB1909 – Laken Riley Act HB2435 – Baby Olivia Act HB2546 – Stalking; Harassment HB2612 – Local Government HB0038 – 10th Amendment This is the 10th Amendment bill that was a principal basis for Gino’s campaign in 2022.
The bill allows the Speaker of the House and the Speaker of the Senate, acting jointly, to retain counsel at no cost to the state to vindicate Tennessee’s rights in federal court.
It is the mechanism Gino plans to use to rein in out-of-control federal spending, which is a primary cause of inflation.
This law went into effect on July 1, 2023.
HB0306 – Girls’ Athletics This bill changed the rules for student participation in sports at private schools in Tennessee.
Students may only participate in athletic activities organized by the Tennessee Secondary School Athletic Association in accordance with their biological sex at birth.
Biological boys are neither allowed to enter a girls’ locker room nor compete in girls’ athletic programs.
These new rules went into effect on April 28, 2023.
HB1907 – Illegal Alien This bill tightens up the definition of “illegal alien” in Title 50 of the Tennessee Code to ensure that taxpayer dollars may not be used to provide entitlement benefits to illegal aliens.
The new law went into effect starting July 1, 2024.
