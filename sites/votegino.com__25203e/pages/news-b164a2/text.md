Tennessee Right to Life Endorses State Representative Gino Bulso
Brentwood, TN—District 61 Tennessee State Representative Gino Bulso has proudly accepted the Tennessee Right to Life PAC’s recent endorsement of his re-election campaign.
Tennessee Right to Life also endorsed Gino Bulso in 2022.
The official endorsement can be viewed here: https://www.vote4life.org/candidate_endorsements
“This endorsement is significant because there is nothing more precious than life.
Our most solemn obligation as legislators is to protect life and liberty.
And protecting life begins with protecting the unborn,” stated TN State Representative Gino Bulso.
Republican Bulso is a dedicated pro-life constitutional conservative who has and will continue to support the most innocent and defenseless.
Gino’s commitment to defending Tennessee’s unborn children is the reason Tennessee Right to Life, the oldest and largest pro-life organization in Tennessee, has extended its support once again for his campaign.
State Representative Gino Bulso recently stated,” I have and will always vote to protect an unborn child’s right to life.”
Gino Bulso is an accomplished attorney, state legislator, and Republican Party member, representing Williamson County’s 61st District of Tennessee in the House of Representatives.
Gino and his wife, Kathy, have been married since 1986 and together have five children and eight grandchildren.
They attend St.
Edward Church, where their son is the pastor.
In 2022, Gino was elected to the Tennessee House of Representatives.

Town Hall Scheduled for District 61 State Representative Gino Bulso at Brentwood Library
(Brentwood, TN) District 61 State Representative, Gino Bulso, will hold a town hall meeting on Friday, March 15, 2024, from 4:00pm to 5:30pm at the Brentwood Library.
Representative Bulso will discuss important topics, recent legislative activities and bills, and the progress of bills he has sponsored and co-sponsored this legislative session.
“I’m looking forward to discussing these topics with my fellow residents of District 61.
We’ve had a very productive session in the General Assembly and I’m eager to get feedback from the voters”, stated State Representative, Gino Bulso
Bills reviewed and discussed will be:
HB0257 Improving communications between disabled drivers and law enforcement.
HB1828 Designates George Washington’s Farewell Address to the American People, Alexis de Tocqueville’s Democracy in America, the Aitken Bible, and a collection of Pulitzer Prize winning works of fiction and non-fiction written in Tennessee as official Tennessee state books.
HB1605 Known as the “flag bill” that ensures parents remain in control of what values their children are exposed to in public school across the state.
HB1631 Extends private pre-K through 12 schools the ability to develop their own firearms policies to protect students, teachers and staff.
For those unable to make the in-person meeting at the Brentwood Library, there will be a live stream that can be accessed on the “Gino Bulso TN State Representative” Facebook page.
Everyone will be able to watch the meeting and present any questions.
Town Hall Meeting Location: Brentwood Library, 8109 Concord Rd, Brentwood, TN 37027.
