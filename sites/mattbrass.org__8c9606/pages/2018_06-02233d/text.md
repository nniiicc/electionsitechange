As reported by the Newnan Times-Herald: Just a few hours after a ceremonial ribbon cutting for the new “adventure cabins” at Chattahoochee Bend State Park Friday, the first cabin guests arrived.
The three cabins are booked every weekend through July 4, and there are several reservations for weekdays as well, according to Erin Kenner, park manager.
“We’re thrilled about that,” Kenner said.
