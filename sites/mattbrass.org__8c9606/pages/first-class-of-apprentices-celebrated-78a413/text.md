First class of apprentices celebrated
September 30, 2019
The first group of apprentices in Georgia’s German-style apprenticeship program – piloted in Coweta County – graduated last spring, and the students and businesses involved in the initial effort were honored by the German American Chamber of Commerce of the Southern United States, Inc.
(GACC South) at the Wunderbar Together PopUp Tour in Midtown Atlanta Wednesday.
Pictured from left are Mark Whitlock, CEO of the Central Educational Center; Stefanie Ziska, president and CEO of GACC South; David Keller, president of E.G.O.
North America; Irene Munn, skills advisor to the GACC South; Josiah Henderson, apprenticeship program graduate; former Lt.
Gov.
Casey Cagle; State Rep.
Matt Brass; and Steve Cromer, West Georgia Technical College senior director of advanced manufacturing.
