Mission
I’m running for State Delegate to make life better for someone else.
That starts by restoring common sense to Annapolis—putting families, students, small businesses, and taxpayers first.
My mission is simple: build safer schools, strengthen our workforce, and ensure every resident of District 44B has the opportunity to rise without unnecessary government barriers.
Vision
I believe in a Maryland where communities are safe, education opens real doors, and hard work actually pays off.
District 44B should be a place where families thrive, young people are prepared for success, and government respects your freedom and your paycheck.
Together, we can build a future grounded in responsibility, opportunity, and the shared belief that our district deserves better—and can be better.
