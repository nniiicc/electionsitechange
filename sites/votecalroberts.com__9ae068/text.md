WHY I’M RUNNING
HD 46 faces dynamic challenges—rapid population growth, congested infrastructure and government mandates.
Yet, we are also positioned to seize generational opportunities, including The Point development and the expanding horizons of Silicon Slopes.
Over multiple campaigns, I’ve knocked on the doors of thousands of HD 46 residents.
The prominent issues that came up—the prison site development, local control and land use issues, affordable housing, economic development, infrastructure investment—are issues that demand common-sense, conservative solutions at the Capitol.
Draper and the East side of Bluffdale deserve a State Representative fighting for these issues and advocating on behalf of our residents.
I’m committed to solving real problems for Draper and Bluffdale.
As a parent with three young children who will experience life in our community—playing at our parks, attending our public schools, depending on our natural resources—I’m dedicated to building a future where they can flourish, contribute to our local economy, and one day, start their own families.
I care about their future and the shared future of all residents of HD 46.
I will fight to protect and defend our incredible quality of life and the things that make our corner of Utah special.
