As Chairman of the South Carolina House Judiciary Committee, Weston Newton plays a leading role in shaping laws that protect families, strengthen communities, and uphold South Carolina’s values.
In the General Assembly, Weston has worked to pass meaningful legislation that protects children online, strengthens public safety, defends the unborn, and keeps government accountable to the people it serves.
As a real estate and land attorney, Weston brings a practical, solutions-oriented approach to the State House — focused on clear laws, responsible governance, and delivering real results for South Carolina families.
