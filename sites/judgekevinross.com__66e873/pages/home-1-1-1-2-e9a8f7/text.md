Ross Minnesota Court of Appeals ​ ​ A Note from Judge Ross A Message to you from Judge Ross ​ Fellow Minnesotan, ​ It continues to be my overwhelming honor to serve you on the Court of Appeals.
My mission remains what it's been since I accepted the office two decades ago: to wear the robe with humility, to serve with integrity, and to do justice with uncompromising impartiality and fidelity to the rule of law.
I hope you can see that commitment in each of the 4,000 appeals I've judged and in the 1,300 judicial opinions I've authored. ​ If I've again earned your vote of confidence, I will be humbled to remain in your service for a new term on the Minnesota Court of Appeals.
