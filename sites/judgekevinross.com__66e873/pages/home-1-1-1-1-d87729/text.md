Ross Minnesota Court of Appeals ​ ​ Family and Faith Judge Ross is a man of faith and family. ​ His faith inspires his civic mission to help maintain order and justice in a troubled world.
Consistent with this, he has also been active in all Minnesota churches of which he was a member.
He is currently a board member of Eden Prairie Assembly of God and helps to facilitate its men’s ministry.
He previously served on the board of directors of Christ Church in Minneapolis and as president of Park Avenue United Methodist Men.
Judge Ross finds support for this mission from his loving family.
He is married, and he has five adult children and nine grandchildren. ​ Aside from his many civic, faith, and family commitments, you might find Kevin enjoying a game of chess or pickleball, cycling one of Minnesota’s many scenic trails, or camping in the Boundary Waters Canoe Area.
