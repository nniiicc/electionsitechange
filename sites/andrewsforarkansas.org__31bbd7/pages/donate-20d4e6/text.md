Donations
Help the campaign by contributing.
Under Arkansas law financial contributions are capped at $3,500 for the primary election and $3,500 for the general election.
Financial contributions can only be made by an individual or by an Arkansas registered political action committee (PAC).
Cash contributions cannot exceed $100.
Corporate and business contributions are prohibited.
