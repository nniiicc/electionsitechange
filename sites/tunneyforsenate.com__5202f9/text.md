MEET JASON TUNNEY Download Headshot Saginaw Roots Jason was born and raised in Saginaw.
He attended Saginaw's Heritage High School where he played varsity football and was a member of the debate team.
He graduated with a B.A. in Political Science from the University of Michigan in 1994, followed by a law degree from Case Western Reserve University in 1997.
He was admitted to the Michigan Bar in 1997 and began his legal career as an Assistant Prosecuting Attorney in Saginaw County.
In 2002, Jason joined the Arter & Hadden law firm in Cleveland, Ohio, where he was later admitted to the Ohio Bar.
Family is a cornerstone of Jason's life.
He permanently returned to Saginaw with his young family in 2004, in order to join the company started by his grandfather, the late John R.
Burt.
Jason put his legal career on hold while he learned the essentials of the family business, working in the Quality Assurance Department.
Jason eventually became the company's General Counsel, Executive Vice-President and a member of the Board of Directors.
At Duro-Last, Jason earned several accolades, including a six sigma greenbelt certification from the Michigan Manufacturing Technology Center, and a certification in crisis management from the Kellog School of Management at Northwestern University.
In 2023, Jason worked to successfully complete the acquisition of Duro-Last by Holcim, a worldwide leader in commercial building products.
Jason is committed to his family and his community.
He and his wife Pamela were married in Saginaw in 2001, and together they have three sons.
Jason serves as the President of the John R. & Mildred B.
Burt Charitable Trust Foundation, which supports students attending Christian schools.
Jason is also a member of the St.
Gerard Society, which works to make Catholic education affordable for students in the Saginaw area.
He is a trustee on the Nouvel Catholic Central School Board and a trustee for the Harvey Randall Wickes Charitable Foundation which benefits causes in Saginaw.
Jason and his wife Pamela are members of St.
Thomas Aquinas Catholic Church in Saginaw.
In July 2025, Jason joined Tunney Law, PLLC, marking a new chapter in his professional journey.
With a commitment to both his profession and his community, Jason Tunney continues to make a meaningful contribution in Saginaw and beyond.
