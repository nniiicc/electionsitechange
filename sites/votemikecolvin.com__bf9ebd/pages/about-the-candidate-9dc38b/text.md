As a practicing licensed funeral service professional, Mike brings years of experience by serving families throughout the community for over 20 years.
Mike serves in the community daily in his leadership roles at Colvin Funeral Homes and Paye Memorial Funeral Establishment.
Additionally, Mike maintains active roles as a political advisor, political strategist, and policy advisor to various elected officials, professional organizations, and community leaders.
Mike is a proud Duke University graduate and a faithful member of the Simon Temple AME Zion Church, where he serves as a devoted member of the Steward Board and the Lay Council Ministries.
Local Roots, Community Commitment A native of Cumberland County and House District 42, Colvin's roots run deep, fostering genuine connections with local officials and community leaders.
Endorsed by his predecessor, Retired NC House Representative Marvin Lucas and a coalition of elected officials, Colvin embodies the trust of North Carolina.
He is a community leader, and his passion for the district fuels a comprehensive approach to address pressing issues and elevate the community's well-being.
The Mike Colvin Promise Mike Colvin's extensive experience as an advisor to five-term incumbent mayor (Mitch Colvin) of year Fayetteville, NC, positions him as a seasoned navigator of local governance.
Beyond party lines, Colvin's platform prioritizes crucial issues such as education, healthcare, vocational training, economic and community development.
