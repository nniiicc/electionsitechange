State Representative Mike Colvin has been engaged in political activity and community service throughout Cumberland County and North Carolina for many years.
State Representative Mike Colvin has demonstrated his leadership across diverse roles in business, the church, the NAACP, and the North Carolina Democratic Party.
He has served as a political action committee coordinator within the North Carolina NAACP, as a past Minority Affairs Chairman for a congressional district, and former Chairman of the North Carolina Democratic Party's 8th Congressional District.
Mike currently serves as the State Representative for North Carolina House District 42 and remains an active member of Simon Temple AME Zion Church, where he serves on the Lay Council ministries.
Representative Mike Colvin's Commitment to Community Service: A Fayetteville native, Representative Mike Colvin was born to the late Henry Mitchell Colvin Sr. and Elva “Tina” Colvin, the founders of Colvin Funeral Homes of Fayetteville and Lumberton, North Carolina.
As a practicing, licensed funeral service professional, Mike has professionally served families throughout the community for over 20 years.
Mike serves in the community daily in his leadership roles at Colvin Funeral Homes and Paye Memorial Funeral Establishment.
Additionally, Mike maintains active roles as a political advisor, political strategist, and policy advisor to various elected officials, professional organizations, and community leaders.
Mike is a proud Duke University graduate and a faithful member of the Simon Temple AME Zion Church, where he serves on the Lay Council Ministries.
Experience That Matters Representative Mike Colvin's leadership experience as a senior campaign advisor and political consultant to elected officials and his multifaceted involvement underscore a deep understanding of the political landscape.
As the 2026 State House Election approaches, Representative Mike Colvin, maintains the full support, endorsement, and blessings of his predecessor, Retired North Carolina State Representative Marvin Lucas, as well as current colleagues, former legislators, and elected officials.
