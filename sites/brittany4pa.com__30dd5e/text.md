She is running for State Representative because she believes government should be a force to support opportunity, fairness, and dignity for every family.
Brittany grew up in a small Pennsylvania town in a working-class union family, valuing hard work, responsibility, and community.
With the help of her supportive parents, she put herself through Gettysburg College and Pitt Law with scholarships, student loans, and part-time jobs, and built a successful legal career focused on problem-solving and helping people navigate complex systems.
Across roles and sectors, Brittany has built a reputation for careful preparation, sound judgment, and achieving results.
Her professional career reflects a deep respect for the rule of law, public institutions, and the real-world consequences of policy decisions.
She will bring that same practical, steady approach to serve the people of the West Hills.
As a mother raising her daughters in the 45th District with her husband Steve, Brittany sees firsthand what families need to thrive: safe and healthy neighborhoods, excellent schools, good job opportunities, and affordable, quality childcare.
She is deeply invested in making our communities a place where our kids can thrive, and eventually build careers and raise their own families.
Brittany is committed to responsible governance, defending civil rights, expanding access to healthcare, protecting women’s rights, and making thoughtful investments in education, families, small businesses, and our local communities.
