86th House District
Pennsylvania’s 86th House District includes all of Perry County and parts of Juniata County.
Alongside all of Perry County, the 86th District includes the following Juniata County municipalities:
- Beale Township
- Delaware Township
- Fermanagh Township
- Greenwood Township
- Lack Township
- Mifflin Borough
- Mifflintown Borough
- Milford Township
- Port Royal Borough
- Spruce Hill Township
- Susquehanna Township
- Thompsontown Borough
- Turbett Township
- Tuscarora Township
- Walker Township
