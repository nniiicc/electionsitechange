SHARON
Sharon Cintolo is running to be your State Representative in the 2nd Norfolk District.
She was born and raised in the City of Presidents.
Growing up in a large family she understands how changes in the economy can affect the well-being of the family.
Working as a paramedic for the past 30 years she has come to the aid of those in need.
She has witnessed firsthand the results of policies that are soft on crime, having treated victims of violent crimes.
She has also served as a union steward and Vice President of the Boston Police Paramedm's Association EMS Division Union, where she fought for workers' rights.
