Proven, Effective Leadership
Joan Fitzgerald is a proven leader with deep connections to the communities of the 46th Assembly District.
A retired public school teacher and former County Supervisor, Joan has made positive change happen and made the lives of the people she serves healthier and safer.
Connected to our Communities
Joan knows that the first job of a legislator is to listen and serve the people she represents.
She has prioritized building relationships with community members and local elected officials and has focused on engaging with voters throughout the district.
Focused on our Shared Values
Joan believes that state financial support for our public schools and local municipalities is an important step to reduce the burden on property taxpayers.
She understands that housing and healthcare should be both accessible and affordable.
She will continue to bring her commonsense perspective and ability to work with others to the State Assembly.
