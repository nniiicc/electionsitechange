About Me
I’m running for State Representative to make sure every family in our community still has a real shot at building a good life here at home.
- My Mission: Fighting to ensure every Lower Bucks family has a real shot at building a good life right here at home.
- My Perspective: As a lifelong Lower Bucks resident and father of three public school kids, I’m running to deliver real results for our schools and local families.
- My Economy: Working hard should pay off—I will fight to lower everyday costs and keep our community affordable.
- My Experience: Ready on day one: Proven local leadership with the hands-on experience to cut red tape and deliver for you.
