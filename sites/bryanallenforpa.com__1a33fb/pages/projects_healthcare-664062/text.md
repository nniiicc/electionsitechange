Healthcare costs are rising faster than wages, and too many Pennsylvania families are being forced to choose between paying medical bills, filling prescriptions, or covering everyday expenses .
Bryan’s priorities include: Lowering healthcare and prescription drug costs for working families and seniors by increasing transparency and accountability across the healthcare system.
Protecting access to quality local healthcare , including hospitals, urgent care centers, and community-based providers.
Defending reproductive healthcare access and personal medical freedom , ensuring patients and doctors—not politicians—make healthcare decisions.
Expanding mental health and addiction treatment services , including stronger support for recovery and community-based care programs.
Supporting healthcare workers and caregivers by strengthening workforce recruitment and retention efforts in hospitals, nursing homes, and care facilities.
Protecting seniors from skyrocketing medical and prescription costs , while preserving access to Medicare and long-term care services.
Improving access to preventative and primary care , helping families address health issues before they become emergencies.
Fighting insurance company practices that drive up costs or deny necessary care for patients and families.
