Bryan Allen believes no homeowner or renter should be forced to absorb repeated rate hikes while corporate utilities and outdated systems drive up costs.
Bryan’s priorities include:
- Opposing utility rate hikes that unfairly burden families and seniors and pushing for stronger oversight of utility pricing decisions.
- Lowering energy costs through expanded renewable energy development like solar and wind, which reduce long-term dependence on expensive fossil fuel markets.
- Investing in clean energy jobs that create good-paying jobs, while modernizing Pennsylvania’s energy economy.
- Upgrading the electric grid to improve reliability and reduce long-term costs for consumers.
- Expanding energy efficiency programs that help homeowners and small businesses permanently lower their monthly bills.
- Protecting clean air and clean water
- Promoting a balanced energy strategy that prioritizes affordability, reliability, and long-term sustainability.
