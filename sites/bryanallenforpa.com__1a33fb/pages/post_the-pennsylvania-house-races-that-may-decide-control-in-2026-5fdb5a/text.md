USA Today: The Pennsylvania House races that may decide control in 2026
Aug 26
- Pennsylvania's divided state government is at stake in the upcoming midterm elections as Democrats aim to win the state Senate.
- Control of the state legislature will determine the fate of key policy issues like minimum wage, marijuana legalization, and abortion access.
- A party trifecta, where one party controls the governor's office and both legislative chambers, has been rare in recent Pennsylvania history.
