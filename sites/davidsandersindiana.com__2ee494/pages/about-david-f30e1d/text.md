Dr.
David A.
Sanders is running for State Senate District 23 with a clear mission: restoring trust, integrity, and accountability to Indiana’s leadership.
A scientist, educator, and veteran local official, David believes that state government must be open, ethical, and fiercely protective of the people it serves.
He has been a resident of West Lafayette for 30 years.
David’s platform is built on defending local communities and protecting individual freedoms.
Local Sovereignty: David successfully built a bipartisan coalition to protect the Wabash River from the LEAP-Lebanon project.
He will continue to fight against state preemption of local self-rule.
Personal Freedom: He opposes government interference in marriage and childbearing decisions, advocating for the privacy and autonomy of Indiana families.
He has been a vocal opponent of government surveillance.
If you are a registered voter in District 23, there’s a good chance that you have met me.
Perhaps you would recognize me by the hats I wear.
My three sons attended West Lafayette public schools and I am a firm supporter of public education.
Having served as West Lafayette City Councilor as a Democrat since 2016, I am recognized for safeguarding taxpayer money and opposing government invasion of privacy and have championed local control of government functions.
I personally answer every message sent by constituents and have held monthly Meet Your Councilor meetings.
I also will continue to focus on ensuring that veterans receive the benefits that they have earned.
