Capital Outlay
As your state legislator, I help secure funding for one-time, long-term public projects like infrastructure, facilities, and equipment—primarily through state bonds and public funds.
In the 2026 Legislative Session, each Representative was allocated $2.5 million to invest in projects across their district.
I prioritized public safety, infrastructure, education, and affordable housing.
Many of these projects are also supported by additional funding from other legislators and agencies to ensure they are fully completed.
