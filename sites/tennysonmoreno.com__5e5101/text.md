Meet Tennyson Moreno
A Sixth-Generation Texan and Proud Conservative Republican
A sixth-generation Texan, Tennyson cares deeply about our community.
He’ll help residents flourish by adhering to his conservative principles.
Tennyson grew up in a single-parent, lower-middle class home.
Being raised by his single mother, Tennyson overcame hardship with faith and hard work.
Now, Tennyson is president of two conservative organizations as well as an experienced campaign manager, licensed pilot, and dedicated local volunteer.
I am running for the Texas House of Representatives to protect families, neighborhoods, and our conservative principles in Hays County.
High cost of living, and high crime rates are major issues, and we need a Representative who will address them.
We need smart, safe, and sustainable solutions to our problems.
