Paul Bonzani.
After a career that took me to six different states, I chose Kensington, New Hampshire, as my final destination #ago and quickly knew I had found my home.
I am running for State Representative in Rockingham District 36 because I want to protect the New Hampshire I love from misguided policies that have driven up property taxes while weakening our public schools and healthcare system.
For the past 15 years, Republican policies have delivered a very different version of “freedom”: The freedom to be taxed out of your home because you can no longer afford your property taxes.
The freedom to fall into medical debt because healthcare costs continue to rise.
The freedom to pay more and get less as public education is undermined and families are forced to consider costly alternatives.
The freedom to watch your children leave New Hampshire because they cannot find affordable housing or good-paying jobs here.
It's time to restore true freedom and fairness to New Hampshire.
