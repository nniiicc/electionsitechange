Meet Dr Paul Bonzani
Dr.
Paul Bonzani is a Doctor of Occupational Therapy, a nationally recognized expert in hand and arm rehabilitation, and an Associate Clinical Professor of Occupational Therapy at the University of New Hampshire.
Throughout his career, he has combined clinical practice, teaching, and service to improve the lives of others.
Paul has volunteered as an international educator, helping develop occupational therapy programs in Haiti and Malawi.
Closer to home, he is an avid gardener and a supporter of the University of New Hampshire Student Food Pantry, reflecting his commitment to strengthening communities and helping neighbors thrive.
