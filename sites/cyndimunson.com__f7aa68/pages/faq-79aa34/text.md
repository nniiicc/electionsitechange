Voters should know that Cyndi Munson is the daughter of a Vietnam War Veteran and a South Korean immigrant.
Raised in Lawton by a single father who worked multiple jobs while serving in the Army, Munson became the first person in her family to graduate from college.
She has served as a leader in the nonprofit community for decades and was elected to the Oklahoma House of Representatives in 2015.
In the State House, she championed bipartisan legislation to lower sales tax on groceries, fought for increased funding for public schools, and stood up to insurance companies.
As Governor, Munson will focus on rebuilding the Oklahoma working class, lowering costs for everyday Oklahomans, and bringing reason back to our government.
What are the top 4 items that voters should know that Cyndi will prioritize as Governor?
Voters should know that Cyndi Munson will: Lower home insurance costs by holding insurance companies accountable for price gouging owners Increase funding for public schools and increase teacher and support staff pay Lower healthcare costs by reducing prescription drug prices and health insurance premiums Reprioritize existing tax dollars to fund programs that benefit everyday Oklahomans rather than providing tax breaks to big corporations What should voters know about Mike Mazzei's stance on public schools?
Voters should know that as State Senator Mike Mazzei voted for a budget that cut education funding, and says as Governor he will vote against increasing funding to public schools.
He has also consistently supported the voucher program that allows wealthy families to use public school tax dollars for private schools and supports using taxpayer funding for religious charter schools, which has been previously blocked by the Supreme Court.
What should voters know about Mike Mazzei's stance on women's healthcare?
Voters should know that Mike Mazzei believes that the government should make health care decisions for Oklahomans.
Mazzei is against abortion in all cases, even in the cases of rape or incest.
He voted to ban safe and legal medical abortion and supports criminalizing doctors who perform the procedure and women who receive it - even if their lives are at risk.
What should voters know about Mike Mazzei's stance on corruption and the Inola Smelter?
Voters should know that Mike Mazzei originally opposed an aluminum facility near Tulsa owned by a United Arab Emirates company that cost taxpayers billions of dollars.
But he changed his position and publicly supported the foreign-owned project.
His campaign also paid nearly $70,000 to a consulting firm owned by Roger Stone, one of Trump’s closest confidants.
Mazzei then received Trump’s endorsement.
Mazzei is running the typical corrupt “pay to play” campaign that sells out Oklahomans.
What should voters know about Mike Mazzei's stance on affordability?
Voters should know that Mike Mazzei wants to cut programs and services that keep daily life affordable for regular Oklahomans.
He supports abolishing the minimum wage and privatizing Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid.
This may put more money in the government’s pockets, but means lower wages and fewer benefits for hardworking Oklahoma families.
