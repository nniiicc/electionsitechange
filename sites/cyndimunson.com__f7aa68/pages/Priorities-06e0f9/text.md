Jobs and Workforce Development
Cyndi believes the key to attracting and growing businesses in Oklahoma is an educated and healthy workforce.
She wants to focus on policies that will help everyday Oklahomans take care of their families, and afford housing.
She will work to institute mandatory paid family leave for parental and family caregiving.
As a legislator, she supported fully funding childcare subsidies for families to get the childcare they need and will continue to fight for that as Governor.
Cyndi supports incentives for the building of affordable housing in urban and rural Oklahoma and will stand up to insurance companies to lower home insurance costs for Oklahomans.
Cyndi has continually authored legislation to raise the minimum wage and supported the state question earlier this year.
She will make it a top priority as Governor to raise the minimum wage for all Oklahomans and provide pay raises for state employees.
