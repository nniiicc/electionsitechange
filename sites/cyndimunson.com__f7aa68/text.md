Cyndi Munson has spent her life helping others.
She carries their stories with her today.
“When I look at my life I know that Oklahoma's leaders can help people.
And I believe that a campaign should be about people.
During this campaign I want to fill box after box with your stories, your photos, and your letters.
Anything that speaks to why you love Oklahoma.
It's what I will carry with me on this new journey.
And when times are hard it is what will carry me and guide my way as your next Governor."
