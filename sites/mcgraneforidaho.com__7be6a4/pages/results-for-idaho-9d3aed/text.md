WE'RE JUST GETTING STARTED Delivering Results for Idaho Phil McGrane ran for Secretary of State with a simple promise: strengthen Idaho elections, bring more transparency to government, and stand up for Idaho values.
That is exactly what he has done.
Since taking office, Phil has brought a steady, conservative, common-sense approach to the Secretary of State’s Office.
He has worked side by side with Idaho’s county clerks, modernized outdated systems, expanded access to trusted election information, and pushed back when Washington, D.C. tried to overreach.
The results are clear.
Idaho conducted the largest election in state history, crossed the one-million registered voter mark, strengthened election security, improved transparency, and earned national recognition for the way Idaho runs elections.
And Phil is just getting started.
Strengthening Idaho Elections Phil McGrane believes Idaho elections should be easy to vote in and hard to cheat — and that starts with doing the basics right.
As Secretary of State, Phil has worked hand in hand with Idaho’s forty-four county clerks to run secure, accurate, and transparent elections.
Together, they have conducted statewide elections, strengthened election official training, improved election procedures, expanded voter registration outreach, and confirmed election accuracy through audits and recounts.
Phil has also worked with the Idaho Legislature to strengthen Idaho election law, including stronger voter ID protections, using new technologies for improved voter roll maintenance to remove ineligible voters, and other reforms that improve election security, protect Idaho voters, and give the public greater confidence in the process.
All while protecting Idahoans access to the polls either by absentee voting, early voting or on Election Day.
Phil also helped implement Governor Brad Little’s “Only Citizens Will Vote” Act , strengthening Idaho’s work to verify voter eligibility and ensure that only U.S. citizens vote in Idaho elections.
He partnered with county clerks to adopt the award-winning “12 Principles of Idaho Elections” which is a shared commitment to doing things the Idaho way: elections that arelocally run, transparent, accountable, and secure. ​ At the same time, Phil has worked to meet voters where they are, registering Idahoans at university football games, air shows and community events throughout the state.
More Transparency, Less Red Tape Phil McGrane knows Idahoans deserve a government that is open, accountable, and easy to understand.
That is why his office launched a redesigned, award-winning VoteIdaho.gov website, modernized campaign finance and lobbyist reporting systems, and expanded public access to election results and transparency tools.
These improvements make it easier for Idahoans to find trusted information, follow the process, and hold government accountable.
Phil has also worked with the Idaho Controller’s Office and Idahoans for Openness in Government to promote transparency and make government work better for the people it serves.
Standing Up for Idaho ​ Phil McGrane has never been afraid to stand up for Idaho.
He testified before Congress on election integrity, pushed back against the federal Corporate Transparency Act, and as a member of the Executive Committee for the Republican Secretaries of State Committee defended small business owners from unnecessary federal mandates.
He has also served in national leadership roles with other Secretaries of State and helped bring national election security partners to Idaho — making sure Idaho has a seat at the table while keeping Idaho values front and center.
As a member of the Idaho Land Board, Phil has supported responsible stewardship of Idaho’s endowment lands and natural resources.
He also completed wildland firefighter training to better understand the challenges facing Idaho’s forests, firefighters, and rural communities.
The Work Continues Phil McGrane has delivered real results for Idaho: secure elections, more transparency, stronger partnerships with local officials, and a Secretary of State’s Office that reflects Idaho common sense.
His approach is simple: Make it easy to vote. ​ Make it hard to cheat.
Keep government transparent.
And always put Idaho first.
That is what Phil promised.
That is what he is delivering.
