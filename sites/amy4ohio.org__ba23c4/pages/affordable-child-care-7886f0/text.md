Child Care needs to be more affordable and easier for families to access.
Affordable Child Care My ideas on how to solve this problem are as follows: Reduce the Cost of Child Care by Offering a Tax Credit or a Tax Refund for all.
Reduce the Cost for some families by income, if a family makes below a certain amount, they will be eligible for Child Care at NO Cost to the Family.
Provide Child Care Centers with government subsidies or incentives so that they can secure better wages and benefits for current employees, actually employ more professionals, expand their care centers, and allow more children to be cared for, allowing more parents to go back to work.
Build more workplace child care centers, so that a person working in a large corporate office workplace has childcare available to them, in the same building in which they work, so parents can visit their children on lunch breaks or on their 15 minute breaks, and children can be closer to their parents.
In order to pay for this, we would request Federal Grants and Funding for Early Education and Development.
We would also need to look at our own system, the Ohio Dept. of Children and Youth, to see where monies might be able to be shifted or re-allocated towards.
Ultimately, we might need to put it on the ballot and have Ohioans vote and potentially have a small amount of tax dollars be allocated to making Child Care Affordable.
