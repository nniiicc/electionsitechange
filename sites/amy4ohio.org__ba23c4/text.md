About: Amy is a 3rd Grade Teacher, proud mother of 4, wife, and pet parent.
Her main reason for running for the Ohio House of Representatives, is to make the world a better place for all of us, especially our children.
Causes she supports: Amy is a champion for children; supporting parents, schools, and teachers.
She is also a strong supporter of Universal Health Care for all, especially Free Health Care for all Children, Disabled People, the Elderly, Pregnant People, and Veterans.
Goals: Amy is a strong believer in valuing Community over viewing everyone as Competition.
She believes that there are always topics that everyone can agree upon.
Amy also understands that compromise is key in order to achieve anything.
Her main goal will be to reach across the aisle and work on legislation that builds a better, stronger, and safer future for all Ohioan Children.
Campaign Promises: Amy wants to enact legislation that puts our kids first.
Her 1st pieces of legislation that she hopes to introduce are Free (to the Student) School Breakfast & Lunch to ALL of Ohio’s K-12 Schools.
Over 500,000 (1/2 a Million) children in Ohio suffer from Food Insecurity and Hunger.
That’s 1 in 5 Children who don’t know where their next meal is coming from.
Providing Free School Breakfast & Lunch for ALL K-12 students is a good way to start putting and end to Childhood Hunger.

Elder Care Ideas about how to help our Elders & Senior Citizens stay in their homes, with their families for longer like expanding programs that pay for family members to care for their elderly &/or disabled loved ones.

Expanding and building more programs that help Veterans transition to Civilian life.
