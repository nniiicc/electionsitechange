ISSUES
COMMON-SENSE, CONSERVATIVE FAMILY VALUES
Cameron believes in common-sense, conservative Arkansas values.
He believes government should be smaller, less bureaucratic, and should continue to cut red tape to decrease burdens on small business owners.
He will work to ensure that the legislature is a wise steward of the taxpayer’s dollar.
HELPING RURAL COMMUNITIES
Cameron is an advocate for small town and rural Arkansas.
He has helped our local rural communities meet their needs and preserve their identities by working to get them new community highway signs and increased grant funding for local libraries, community ballparks, volunteer fire departments, and community centers.
Cameron understands the importance of agriculture to our district and state is a staunchsupporter of farmers and ranchers and their right to use their private property as they see fit, with no overrreaching government regualtions on their industry.
Cameron has worked to secure additional grant funding for statewide rural broadband program to improve internet access throughout the state.
There is an increased need for individuals who wish to work from home and students and families who wish to receive their education virtually.
ASSISTING SENIOR CITIZENS
Cameron has assisted our senior citizens by helping the ensure senior centers and senior meal deliveries are adequately funded.
HEALTH CARE
Cameron has advocated expanding the use of telemedicine in Arkansas and voted for bills in the legislature to make this a reality.
Cameron is an advocate for those struggling with mental health and addiction issues and has worked professionally for many years with individuals with intellectual and mental health disabilities.
EDUCATION
Cameron supports students and teachers and believes that every child should have access to the best possible education, including the freedom to choose public school, charter school, private school, or home school.
He supports policies to ensure each student receives the best education, tailored to their specific needs.
PROTECTING OUR RIGHTS
Cameron believes in free speech, religious freedom, and educational freedom.
He will work tirelessly to protect our Second Amendment.
Cameron recognizes our rights do not come from the government; our rights come from God.
He stands for individual liberty and personal responsibility.
Cameron will represent District 57 not as a politician, but as a public servant.
