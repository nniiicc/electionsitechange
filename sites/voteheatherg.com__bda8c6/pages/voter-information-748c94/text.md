Primary Election Day is June 9, 2026
General Election Day is November 3, 2026
On Election Day, you can either vote in person or drop off your mail-in ballot at any Vote Center.
You no longer have to go to one specific “assigned” Election Day polling place.
Instead, you may choose the most convenient Vote Center for you.
You do not need to have your ballot with you to vote at a Vote Center (polling place)

Mail-In Ballot Tips
If you need help with Election Day voting information, you can call our campaign at 317-701-3768 or 775-430-9216.
For more information, visit Washoe County Registrar of Voters.
Anyone can deliver a completed ballot on behalf of any voter: Completed ballots may be returned by mail, dropped off at a drop-off location or Vote Centers on Election Day, or in person at the Registrar of Voters Office located at the Washoe County Administrative Complex, 1001 E 9th Street, Bldg A, Rm 135.
The ballot cannot be faxed or emailed.
If you give your ballot to someone else to turn in, make sure they are someone you can trust.
