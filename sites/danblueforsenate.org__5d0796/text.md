Meet Dan
Growing up in his parent’s four-room farmhouse in Robeson County, Dan quickly learned that the values of hard work, faith, and education were the keys to a successful future – lessons learned from his parents, his teachers and his community.
Never losing sight of those values, Dan has worked hard his entire life to help provide North Carolinians with the same opportunities to succeed.
Throughout his tenure in the General Assembly, Dan has consistently been rated one of the most effective members of the General Assembly.
In addition to serving as Speaker of the House, Dan has served as Chairman of the Judiciary and Appropriations committees.
What We’ve Achieved
How Dan’s Working for You:
✓ Medicaid Expansion
In March, 2023, North Carolina became the 40th state to approve Medicaid Expansion.
The path to expansion is the result of years of negotiations with Republican lawmakers.
Once enacted, this will provide affordable healthcare for over 500,000 working North Carolinians, and create more than 80,000 jobs across our state.
✓ Clean Energy Initiative
Under Governor Roy Cooper’s leadership, the General Assembly passed one of the strongest clean energy plans in the country.
The plan provides a path for a 70 percent reduction in carbon emissions for 2030, and carbon neutrality by 2050.
This was a bipartisan effort to put North Carolina on the path to a cleaner future.
✓ Re-Open Schools Safely
Following the COVID-19 pandemic, state and local officials struggled with creating a plan to get students back in the classroom, while maintaining safety protocols.
Senator Blue’s bill to reopen schools safely passed with bipartisan support, getting students and families back into the classroom.
