In a resounding victory, North Carolina Senate Leader Dan Blue won reelection to the N.C.
State Senate’s 14th District.
Sen.
Blue captured a decisive 73.44% of the vote, defeating Republican candidate Angela McCarty, who received 23.69%, and Libertarian Sammy Brooks, who trailed with 2.87%.
Since joining the state Senate in 2009, Sen.
Blue has been a consistent advocate for North Carolina’s communities and has worked tirelessly to promote solutions to the challenges facing families statewide.
As the Senate’s Democratic leader, he has led the charge on key issues like affordable health care and education.
Senator Blue’s continued leadership in the Senate will ensure that Democrats remain committed to advocating for the needs of all North Carolinians, and ensuring that their voices are heard.
