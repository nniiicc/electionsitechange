ABOUT THE 44th
Get to know our district.
With over 160,000 people, the 44th is melting pot of cultures, workers, families, students and more.
We are located north of Seattle, and our district is known for its diverse geography — including the Cities of Snohomish and Mill Creek, Maltby, Clearview and parts of Bothell and Everett.
