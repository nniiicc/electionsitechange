Donate ABOUT THE 44TH Located in Snohomish County — the third most populous county in Washington State — WA’s 44th District represents more than 163,109 people, according to 2020 data collected by the US Census Bureau .
We are located north of Seattle, and our district is known for its diverse geography — including the Cities of Snohomish and Mill Creek, Maltby, Clearview and parts of Bothell and Everett.
We humbly reside on the native land of the Coast Salish, Stillaguamish, Snohomish, and Tulalip tribes.
This year, the redistricting based on the 2020 census has shifted our district northeast to include North Creek, More of Snohomish, a small portion of Bothell, and Lord Hill Park.
The district no longer includes Ebey Island and parts of Lake Stevens.
