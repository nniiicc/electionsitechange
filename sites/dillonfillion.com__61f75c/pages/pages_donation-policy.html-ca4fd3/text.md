Donation Policy
Last updated: May 2026
Contributions to Fillion for Iowa House are subject to Iowa campaign finance law and applicable federal regulations.
Please read the following policy before making a contribution.
Payment Processing
All online contributions are processed securely through WinRed, a federally registered payment processor for Republican campaigns.
By making a contribution, you agree to WinRed's terms of service.
Fillion for Iowa House does not store credit card or payment information on this website.
Eligibility to Contribute
By submitting a contribution, you affirm that:
- You are a United States citizen or a lawfully admitted permanent resident alien.
- You are making this contribution from your own personal funds, not those of another person or entity.
- You are not a foreign national, foreign government, or agent of a foreign principal.
- You are at least 18 years of age (or the contribution is made with parental/guardian approval on behalf of a minor).
Foreign National Contributions Prohibited
Federal law strictly prohibits contributions, donations, or expenditures by foreign nationals in connection with any U.S. election.
This prohibition applies to all levels of government, including state legislative races.
The Campaign will not accept or retain contributions from foreign nationals.
Corporate Contributions Prohibited
Contributions from corporations, labor organizations, national banks, federally chartered corporations, or any other prohibited entity may not be accepted.
All contributions must come from individuals using personal funds.
Contribution Limits
Contributions to Iowa state legislative campaigns are subject to limits established by Iowa law.
Current limits for Iowa House candidates are set by the Iowa Ethics and Campaign Disclosure Board.
Please consult ethics.iowa.gov for the most current limits applicable to your contribution.
The Campaign is responsible for returning any contribution that exceeds applicable limits.
If your contribution exceeds the legal limit, we will contact you to arrange a refund of the excess amount.
Refund Policy
Contributions are generally non-refundable once made.
However, the Campaign will consider refund requests submitted in writing within 30 days of the original contribution date.
To request a refund, email contact@dillonfillion.com with your full name, contribution amount, contribution date, and reason for your request.
Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method within a reasonable time.
Contributions that are found to be from prohibited sources will be refunded regardless of the 30-day window.
Disclosure
Contributions are reported to the Iowa Ethics and Campaign Disclosure Board as required by Iowa law.
This may include your name, address, employer, occupation, and contribution amount if your contribution meets the applicable reporting threshold.
Questions
If you have questions about this policy or your contribution, please contact us at contact@dillonfillion.com or through the Contact Us page.
