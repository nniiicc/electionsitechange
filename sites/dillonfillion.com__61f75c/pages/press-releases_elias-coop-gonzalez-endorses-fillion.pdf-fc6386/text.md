FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE For media inquiries, please contact Dillon Fillion at fillionforus@gmail.com Dillon Fillion Earns Endorsement from West Virginia State Delegate Elias Coop-Gonzalez in Campaign for HD 81 Davenport, IA - July 10, 2025 - Dillon Fillion, candidate for State Representative in House District 81, proudly announces the endorsement of West Virginia State Delegate Elias Coop-Gonzalez, a rising conservative leader representing District 67 in the West Virginia House of Delegates.
Coop-Gonzalez’s support strengthens Fillion’s campaign to lower costs, enhance public safety, and promote family-first policies for Iowa.
“I’m proud to endorse Dillon Fillion for the Iowa State House,” said Coop-Gonzalez.
“Dillon brings fresh energy, common-sense values, and a deep commitment to serving his community.
He understands the challenges everyday Iowans face and is ready to fight for strong schools, economic opportunity, and responsible government.
Dillon’s integrity and work ethic make him the kind of leader Iowa needs.” “I am very grateful for Delegate Coop-Gonzalez’s endorsement,” said Fillion.
“His steadfast leadership in West Virginia is fantastic.
HD 81 families need a voice to address soaring costs and defend their core values.
With Elias’s support, I will continue my mission to secure lower taxes, slash unnecessary spending, and empower Iowans to keep more of their earnings.” Coop-Gonzalez, born in Guatemala and a resident of West Virginia since age 12, serves as one of the youngest state legislators in the United States, having taken office at age 20 in 2022.
A graduate of Liberty University and a former intern for Congressman Alex Mooney, he has championed conservative initiatives such as tax cuts, Second Amendment protections, and pro-life policies.
His endorsement underscores a shared commitment with Fillion to limited government and community-focused leadership.
Fillion is a strong advocate for law and order, pledging to support law enforcement, push for tougher penalties for repeat offenders, and oppose policies that compromise community safety.
He is also dedicated to supporting veterans and military families, ensuring they receive the resources and respect they deserve.
“Homeownership should be within reach for all Iowans,” Fillion stated.
“I will fight for property tax relief and protect families from excessive government overreach.” With Coop-Gonzalez’s endorsement, Fillion is intensifying his campaign to bring principled, family-first leadership to Des Moines.
His focus remains on economic relief, safer communities, and policies that prioritize Iowa families over political insiders.
