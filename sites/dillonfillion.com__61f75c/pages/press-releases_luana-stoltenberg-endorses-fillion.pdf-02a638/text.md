Former State Representative Luana Stoltenberg Endorses Dillon Fillion for HD 81 Davenport, IA – May, 28 – Dillon Fillion, candidate for State Representative in Iowa House District 81, proudly announces the endorsement of former House District 81 State Representative Luana Stoltenberg.
A respected author, public speaker, and community leader, Stoltenberg’s support strengthens Fillion’s campaign to deliver lower costs, safer communities, and stronger protections for homeowners in Scott County.
“I support Dillon Fillion in his run for Iowa House District 81,” Stoltenberg said.
“Dillon is working very hard to listen to the people in the 81st District.
He cares deeply for people and for the State of Iowa as well as our Nation.
Dillon will fight hard for what is right for the people in his district rather than for the lobbyist or other special interest groups.
He has already been involved in writing legislation, working on campaigns, and educating himself on how the process works.
Please vote for Dillon Fillion for Iowa House.” Stoltenberg, a board member of the Pregnancy Resource Center, state director for Operation Outcry, and member of the Iowa Firearms Coalition, brings significant conservative credentials to her endorsement.
Her advocacy for family values and Second Amendment rights resonates with Fillion’s commitment to representing the people of House District 81 over special interests.
Fillion’s campaign focuses on reducing property taxes, supporting law enforcement to ensure public safety, and advocating for veterans and military families.
“I’m greatly honored by former Representative Stoltenberg’s endorsement,” Fillion said.
“Luana’s steadfast commitment to Iowa’s values reinforces my pledge to deliver lower taxes, safer streets, and affordable homeownership for every family in House District 81.” To learn more about Dillon Fillion’s campaign for House District 81, visit dillonfillion.com.
