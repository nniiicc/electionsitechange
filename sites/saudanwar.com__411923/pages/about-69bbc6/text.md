Dr.
Saud Anwar is an American physician, public health leader, and elected statesman whose career represents the intersection of medicine, public service, and humanitarian leadership.
For more than three decades, he has dedicated his life to advancing the health and well-being of individuals and communities through patient care, healthcare leadership, legislative innovation, and civic engagement.
His work reflects a rare combination of clinical excellence, strategic vision, and an unwavering commitment to improving lives through evidence-based policy and compassionate leadership.
As a Yale-trained pulmonary and critical care physician with a Master of Public Health, Dr.
Anwar has devoted his medical career to caring for patients with complex pulmonary disease, respiratory failure, and critical illness.
For nearly twenty years, he served as Chair of the Department of Internal Medicine at Manchester Memorial and Rockville General Hospitals, providing steady physician leadership through one of the most transformative periods in modern healthcare.
During his tenure, he helped guide the medical staff through evolving healthcare systems, workforce shortages, the COVID-19 pandemic, and significant structural changes within healthcare delivery, including the growing influence of private equity and healthcare consolidation.
Throughout these challenges, he remained focused on strengthening quality of care, advancing patient safety, promoting physician collaboration, and ensuring that organizational decisions remained centered on the well-being of patients and the communities the hospitals serve.
Under his leadership, the Department of Internal Medicine fostered a culture of professionalism, accountability, continuous quality improvement, and multidisciplinary collaboration that benefited patients throughout the Greater Manchester region.
In public office, Dr.
Anwar has established himself as one of Connecticut's leading voices on healthcare, public health, fiscal responsibility, and social policy.
Representing Connecticut's Third Senate District, he serves as Senate Co-Chair of the Public Health Committee, Vice Chair of the Insurance and Real Estate Committee, and a member of the Appropriations and Human Services Committees.
His legislative work has contributed to expanding healthcare access, reducing prescription drug costs, strengthening behavioral health services, advancing maternal and child health, protecting reproductive freedom, improving environmental health, supporting working families through paid family and medical leave, expanding telehealth, addressing the opioid crisis, protecting consumers, and increasing affordability for Connecticut residents.
Throughout his legislative career, he has emphasized practical, bipartisan solutions rooted in science, compassion, and long-term public benefit.
Long before his service in the State Senate, Dr.
Anwar demonstrated his commitment to community leadership as Mayor of South Windsor and a member of the Town Council.
He helped lead initiatives that strengthened economic development, expanded civic engagement, promoted environmental sustainability, enhanced educational opportunities, and fostered inclusion across diverse communities.
He has also founded and supported numerous nonprofit organizations and community initiatives focused on hunger relief, educational opportunity, environmental stewardship, humanitarian service, and community partnership.
His approach to leadership has consistently emphasized bringing people together across disciplines, perspectives, and backgrounds to solve complex challenges.
Dr.
Anwar's leadership has extended beyond state and local government into national and international arenas.
He has coordinated medical missions for disaster relief, participated in humanitarian and peace-building initiatives, advised governmental and civic organizations on public health, emergency preparedness, healthcare systems, and community resilience, and has spoken before audiences across the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.
His expertise has been sought by federal agencies, international organizations, and professional institutions, and he has testified before the United States Congress while serving as a consultant and speaker on issues involving healthcare, homeland security, public health, and intercultural engagement.
Through these efforts, he has consistently advocated for dialogue, evidence-based decision-making, and collaborative solutions to complex global challenges.
Throughout his career, Dr.
Anwar has remained guided by the belief that leadership is measured not by titles but by service.
Whether caring for critically ill patients in the intensive care unit, leading physicians through periods of unprecedented healthcare transformation, crafting legislation that affects millions of residents, or contributing to humanitarian and public health initiatives beyond Connecticut, he has pursued a singular mission: to improve lives while strengthening the institutions that serve society.
His ability to unite medicine, public policy, and community leadership has earned widespread respect across professional, governmental, and civic communities.
His contributions have been recognized through numerous honors, including the American College of Physicians Legislator of the Year Award, the Federal Bureau of Investigation Director's Community Leadership Award, the Anti-Defamation League's Torch of Liberty Award, the Connecticut Coalition to End Homelessness Social Justice Award, the Eastern Connecticut Health Network Professionalism and Leadership Award, and recognitions from governors, attorneys general, community organizations, and professional societies.
Together, these distinctions reflect a career devoted to advancing health, strengthening democracy, building resilient communities, and demonstrating that principled leadership can create lasting and meaningful change.
Sought out, at home and abroad.
Dr.
Anwar is a trusted voice on public health and international affairs, invited onto national and international television and radio - including CNN (Wolf Blitzer, Deborah Feyerick, Susan Candiotti), NBC-30, Fox-61, NPR, Vectone TV (UK), Geo TV, and Voice of America.
His public service has drawn citations from Governor Rell, Attorney General Blumenthal, Secretary of the State Bysiewicz, and members of the Connecticut General Assembly.
He and his wife, Dr.
Yusra Anis-Anwar, who maintains a private practice in Internal Medicine, live in South Windsor with their sons, who attended the local elementary and middle schools.
Senator Anwar's mother also lives in South Windsor.
