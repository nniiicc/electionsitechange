Requirements
If you are passionate about our campaign’s mission and want to be part of our dedicated team, we encourage you to join us as a volunteer.
Here’s how you can get involved:
- Fill out the volunteer application form on our website.
- Attend an orientation session: Once you have submitted your application, we will provide you with the details of our volunteer orientation session.
This session will give you a comprehensive overview of our campaign's goals, values, and the different volunteer roles available.
- Select your volunteer role: During the orientation session, we will discuss the different volunteer roles available.
Based on your interests and skills, you can choose the role that suits you best.
Whether it's door-to-door canvassing, phone banking, event planning, social media management, or administrative support, there is a place for you in our campaign.
- Attend training sessions: To ensure that you are well-prepared for your volunteer role, we will provide training sessions and resources.
These sessions will equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to effectively contribute to our campaign.
