Aaron Zimmer has earned the endorsement of CSEA and the Connecticut workers the union represents.

In the State Senate, Zimmer will stand with working people in the fight for fair pay, strong benefits, safe workplaces and the freedom to organize.
Zimmer is grateful to have CSEA standing with his campaign—and he will always stand with Connecticut’s workers.
