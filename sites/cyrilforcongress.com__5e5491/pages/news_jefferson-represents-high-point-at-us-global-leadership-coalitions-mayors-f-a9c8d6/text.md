Jefferson Represents High Point at U.S.
Global Leadership Coalition’s Mayors Foreign Policy Summit
The Triad of North Carolina has taken a significant step onto the international stage as Mayor Cyril Jefferson of High Point participates in the prestigious Mayors Foreign Policy Summit, hosted by the U.S.
Global Leadership Coalition (USGLC) in Washington, DC, on November 18 and 19, 2025.
This invite-only summit brings together city leaders from across the country to shape America’s global diplomatic and development strategies.
Mayor Jefferson’s Leadership in Global Discussions
Mayor Jefferson’s presence at the summit highlights his commitment to advancing High Point and the broader Triad region’s interests in global conversations.
His leadership will be instrumental in discussions focused on international trade and tariffs, the role of Artificial Intelligence in national security, and innovations in global public health.
By engaging with national and international policymakers, Mayor Jefferson is ensuring that the voices and concerns of High Point, North Carolina’s 6th Congressional District, and the state are heard at the highest levels.
“Having a seat at the table means our community’s perspectives aren’t just heard, they help shape the decisions that impact us all,” said Mayor Cyril Jefferson.
“It is critical that High Point and the Triad are part of the conversation on issues that resonate close to home but also have global significance.”
“In today’s connected world, it’s not enough to simply react to challenges.
We must proactively engage, collaborate, and innovate with partners across the globe,” Jefferson added.
“This summit provides the opportunity to ensure our region’s voice is included as we tackle pressing matters like trade, technology, and public health.”
Summit Details
The U.S.
Global Leadership Coalition’s Mayors Foreign Policy Summit is an exclusive gathering taking place in Washington, DC.
The event convenes mayors from across the nation to collaborate on strategies that strengthen America’s role in global affairs.
Mayor Jefferson’s participation underscores the Triad’s growing influence in shaping policies that impact both local communities and the world.
About the U.S.
Global Leadership Coalition (USGLC)
The USGLC is a nonprofit coalition comprised of businesses, nonprofits, and faith-based organizations dedicated to promoting U.S. diplomatic and development efforts worldwide.
By bringing together diverse leaders and stakeholders, the USGLC advocates for effective international engagement that supports American security, economic growth, and humanitarian values.
Local Impact and Relevance
Mayor Jefferson’s active engagement in global policy discussions equips him to address issues that directly affect High Point and the region.
Whether it’s navigating trade and tariff policies that influence local businesses, harnessing cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence for public safety, or preparing public health responses to global challenges, his leadership at the summit positions High Point and North Carolina to benefit from informed, forward-thinking strategies.
