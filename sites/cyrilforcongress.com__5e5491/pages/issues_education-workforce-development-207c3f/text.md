Education & Workforce Development
Great schools and strong job training fuel economic growth.
As a former teacher and current mayor, Cyril will:
- Raise teacher pay and reduce class sizes.
- Expand affordable childcare so parents can work.
- Create debt-free career pathways after high school, including apprenticeships that let students earn while they learn.
- Invest in underused spaces for childcare and job training—saving taxpayer dollars.
- Tie all funding to real outcomes like job placement and wage growth.
Proven record: In addition to being a former educator, Cyril's experience as an Education Policy Fellow, Cohort Member of ElevateNC, and Director for Higher Education at Stratagon gives him the expertise to drive meaningful change.
