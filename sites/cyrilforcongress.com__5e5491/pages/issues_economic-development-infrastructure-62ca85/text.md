Economic Development & Infrastructure
Our district once powered North Carolina’s economy.
Cyril’s plan will bring that strength back by:
- Bringing tax dollars home to fix roads, expand broadband, and improve rail service without raising local taxes.

- Revitalizing manufacturing by ending hidden-tariff taxes and supporting job training.
- Building more affordable housing through smart zoning and incentives—while protecting neighborhood character.
