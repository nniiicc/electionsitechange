Preserving democracy requires respect for the Constitution, separation of powers, and leaders who answer to the people—not to special interests or partisan extremes.
In Congress, Cyril will: Defend the separation of powers and push back against executive overreach from either party.
Protect free and fair elections and uphold the peaceful transfer of power.
Reject political violence and ensure the voices of the people—not intimidation or extremism—guide our democracy.
Advance meaningful structural reforms, including congressional term limits, overriding Citizens United, banning stock trading by members of Congress, establishing a moratorium on lobbying after leaving office, and implementing a binding code of judicial ethics for the U.S.
Supreme Court.
Increase transparency and accountability standards for federally funded programs so taxpayers know their money is being used effectively.
Cyril’s approach to public safety reflects these same principles.
As Mayor of High Point, he implemented a focused, data-driven crime reduction strategy that helped deliver a 67% drop in homicides and an overall reduction in violent crime—proving that effective government is measured by results, not rhetoric.
He supports investing in modern policing tools, mental health crisis response teams, and proven prevention programs—while maintaining clear accountability metrics for outcomes.
Why it matters: Democracy depends on limits to power, transparency in leadership, and a government that works for the people.
North Carolinians deserve constitutional leadership, accountable institutions, and safe communities built on trust and results—not chaos or overreach.
