Healthcare & Hunger Relief
No family should have to choose between a doctor visit and putting food on the table.
Cyril will:
- Protect Medicaid and Medicare for North Carolina families and seniors.
- Lower prescription drug prices by expanding Medicare’s negotiating power.
- Hold big insurers accountable—ending surprise billing and capping out-of-pocket costs.
- Treat mental health like health by funding local clinics and telehealth solutions.
- Fight hunger as a health issue by defending SNAP, expanding farm-to-school programs, and making fresh food affordable.
Proven results: Under Cyril’s leadership, High Point transformed from one of the worst-ranked areas for food hardship to a 2024 All-America City Award winner for tackling hunger.
