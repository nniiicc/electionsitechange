Take BAC Congress Take Back Congress is a grassroots reform organization dedicated to restoring accountability, integrity, and trust in the U.S.
Congress.

Supreme Court.
Take Back Congress backs leaders who believe government should work for people, n ot special interests, and who are willing to challenge a system that too often rewards corruption and insider privilege.
Their endorsement reflects confidence in Cyril’s reform-minded leadership, his record of public service grounded in accountability, and his commitment to making Congress ethical, transparent, and responsive to the people it serves.

George C.
Simkins Jr.
Memorial PAC The George C.
Simkins Jr.
Memorial PAC is a longstanding Black political action committee based in Greensboro and Guilford County, named for civil rights leader Dr.
George C.
Simkins Jr., whose legal fights helped desegregate hospitals and public facilities in North Carolina in the 1950s.
For decades, the Simkins PAC has played a significant role in supporting candidates and civic engagement efforts that advance justice, equity, and opportunity for Black North Carolinians and all communities that have been historically overlooked.
Their leadership is rooted in community advocacy, voter education, and grassroots mobilization.
In recent election cycles, the Simkins PAC has joined with other Black political action committees to endorse and organize around Democratic candidates at the statewide and local level, engage voters, and drive turnout through voter registration drives, canvassing, and poll transportation efforts.
Their endorsement of Cyril reflects confidence in his commitment to expanding opportunity, strengthening communities, and standing up for equitable representation and accountability in government service.

Mental Health Now Mental Health Now is a national, nonpartisan campaign powered by Inseparable that encourages elected officials and candidates to prioritize mental health policy as a core part of their public service.
Leaders who sign the Mental Health Now Statement of Support commit to advancing meaningful, people-centered solutions that address the mental health challenges facing Americans today.
Signers promise to support policies that increase access to quality mental health care, making care easier to find and afford; strengthen crisis response systems so anyone experiencing a mental health emergency receives compassionate help; expand wellness, prevention, and early intervention services, especially for youth; and build a stronger mental health workforce so more people in all communities can get the care they need.
Cyril’s commitment to the Mental Health Now Statement of Support reflects his belief that mental health is fundamental to overall health and that government must act boldly to make quality mental health care accessible, responsive, and equitable for all.

D.C.
Statehood Pledge The D.C.
Statehood Pledge represents a commitment to granting Washington, D.C. full statehood and equal representation in Congress for its residents.
