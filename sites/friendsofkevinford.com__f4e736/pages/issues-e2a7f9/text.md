- I will work to direct school resources to the classroom, not a growing bureaucracy.

Agriculture
District 42 contains some of the most productive farmland in Maryland.
We have a rich history in farming and it is a dedicated way of life which must be protected.
Maryland agriculture contributes more than $8 billion a year to our economy.
Maryland agriculture involves more than 15,400 farms across our state.
It is important to protect farmlands from being taken through eminent domain and other assaults like the Maryland Piedmont Reliability Project.
Public Safety
Recently, Maryland as well as our country have experienced the problems associated with illegal immigration.
We have seen the problems violent illegals have caused in the murders of Rachel Morin, a Harford County mother of 5 and Kayla Hamilton, a 20-year-old autistic woman.
Both of these senseless murders were committed by people who should not have been in our country.
I support allowing our local law enforcement offices to work with ICE and oppose all forms of sanctuary counties.
Education
I believe that parents have a right to be involved in the education of their children.
I think our schools should focus on the fundamental aspects of a good education by focusing on math, science, language arts, and history.
Our children need to be prepared to go to college or go into the workforce with a good, solid education.
Our school systems must have accountability, measurable achievements, and a safe learning environment free from distractions.
As a father of four, I believe Maryland needs more choice when it comes to education.
