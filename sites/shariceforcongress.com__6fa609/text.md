MEET SHARICE
A Life of Service
Sharice was raised by a single mom who spent more than 20 years serving in the US Army.
A proud graduate of Leavenworth High School, Sharice was the first person in her family to attend college.
She worked her way from Johnson County Community College to Cornell Law School, juggling multiple jobs to put herself through school.
