About Priorities Media In the News Our Media Store Vote Volunteer About Priorities Media In the News Our Media Store Vote Volunteer Facebook Instagram Youtube Donate Donate Donate Donate October 13, 2026 News Davids Hosts Nonpartisan Voter Registration Drive, Encourages All Kansans to Vote in General Election OVERLAND PARK, KS — Today, on the final day to register to vote in Kansas before the November election, Representative Sharice Davids joined local leaders and community volunteers for a nonpartisan voter registration drive aimed at helping every eligible Kansan make their voice heard this fall.
“Our democracy is at its strongest when every eligible voter can participate and make their voice heard,” said Davids .
“Whether you’re a Republican, Democrat, or Independent, your vote is your power, and making sure our community has easy access to clear, reliable voting information is what today was all about.
I want to thank everyone who registered today and hope all Kansans will make a plan to vote early or on Election Day in November.” At the Overland Park Central Resource Library, Davids and volunteers provided one-on-one assistance to Kansans, helping them check their voter registration status, update their addresses, request mail-in ballots, and access official state election resources.
The nonpartisan event focused on ensuring voters across Kansas’ Third District have the tools they need to participate in the upcoming election, regardless of party affiliation.
WATCH: Davids urges Kansans to register and make a plan to vote How to Register or Update Your Voter Status Today Today, October 13, is the official voter registration deadline for the upcoming general election in Kansas: Online: Kansans with a valid Kansas driver’s license or state ID card can register online or update their voter registration until 11:59 pm tonight at ksvotes.org or through the Kansas Secretary of State’s website .
By Mail or In Person: Paper registration forms must be postmarked by today, October 13, or turned in to your local county election office before the close of business today.
Important Upcoming Kansas Voting Deadlines As the November election approaches, Kansans should keep the following key dates in mind: Wednesday, October 14, 2026: Advance voting in person begins, and county election offices begin mailing advance ballots to voters who requested them.
Tuesday, October 27, 2026: Deadline to apply for an advance ballot by mail (applications must be received by your local election office by 5:00 PM).
Monday, November 2, 2026: Deadline for advance voting in person (ends at 12:00 PM noon).
Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (Election Day): Polls are open from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
Mail-in advance ballots must be postmarked by Election Day and received by Friday, November 6.
To check your polling place, view a sample ballot, or track your mail-in ballot, visit the Kansas Secretary of State website or contact your local county election office.
