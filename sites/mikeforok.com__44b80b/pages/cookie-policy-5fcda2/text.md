This Cookie Policy explains how Mazzei For Governor (“we,” “us,” “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on MikeforOK.com (the “Site”).
1) What cookies are
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
Similar technologies include pixels, tags, local storage, and SDKs.
These help websites function, improve performance, and understand how visitors use the site.
2) Why we use cookies
We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Operate the Site (security, load balancing, preventing fraud)
- Remember preferences (language, form progress, and user settings if applicable)
- Measure performance and usage (what pages people visit, what works, what does not)
- Support campaign communications and advertising (where enabled), such as measuring effectiveness of ads or showing relevant messages
3) Types of cookies we may use
Essential cookies
Required for core Site functionality and security.
These cannot be switched off in many systems.
Performance and analytics cookies
Help us understand how visitors use the Site (for example, page views, clicks, time on page).
We use this to improve the Site.
Functional cookies
Remember choices you make (for example, region or preferences).
Advertising and targeting cookies (if enabled)
May be used to measure ad performance or show relevant campaign messages on other platforms.
These cookies may be set by us or by third-party providers.
4) Third-party cookies and tools
We may use third-party services that set cookies or similar technologies.
These may include:
- Analytics providers (for example, Google Analytics)
- Email and CRM tools
- Payment processors (if donations are accepted)
- Social media pixels (for example, Meta and YouTube) if enabled for measurement
These third parties may collect information about your device and browsing activity.
Their use of that data is governed by their own privacy policies.
5) What data cookies can collect
Depending on configuration, cookies may collect:
- Browser type and settings
- Device identifiers
- IP address (often used for rough location like city or region)
- Pages visited and actions taken on the Site
- Referring website or campaign source (how you arrived at the Site)
6) Your choices and controls
You can control cookies in several ways:
Browser controls
Most browsers let you block or delete cookies.
If you block some cookies, parts of the Site may not work correctly.
Consent banner or cookie settings (if implemented)
If the Site shows a cookie banner or settings panel, you can adjust your preferences there.
Ad platform controls
If the Site uses advertising cookies, many ad platforms provide their own opt-out controls for targeted ads.
7) Do Not Track
Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal.
Because there is no consistent industry standard for interpreting it, we do not respond to DNT signals in a uniform way.
