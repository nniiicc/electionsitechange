WHEN IS EARLY VOTING and where can i vote?
2
Early voting begins Oct. 21 and ends Oct. 31.
You can vote everyday, except for Sunday, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
In Monongalia County, there are two early voting locations.
- Mountaineer Mall: 500 Greenbag Road, Morgantown, WV - between Anybody Fitness and the former Mylan site
- The Red Barn at Mason Dixon Park: 79 Buckeye Rd, Core, WV
