PROUD TO BE ENDORSED BY
DELEGATE JOHN WILLIAMS, D-80, MONONGALIA COUNTY
“I am proud to endorse Olivia Miller to be my Delegate.
I spent about a half hour with Olivia and ended up telling her, ‘you have to run.’ Not just for the sake of filling the ballot, but because West Virginia needs her experience, energy and ideas.
Olivia will represent us well.”
DELEGATE EVAN HANSEN, D-79, MONONGALIA COUNTY
“I’m proud to endorse Olivia Miller for House of Delegates in the 80th District.
Olivia has the experience, judgment, and work ethic to be an effective legislator from day one She’s also not afraid to speak out.
In fact, Olivia has already been standing up to secure clean drinking water and protect West Virginia’s precious natural resources.
I need more allies in the Legislature, and I know that Olivia will be ready to work.”
