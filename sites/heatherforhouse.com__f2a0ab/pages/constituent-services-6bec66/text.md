Constituent Services My top priority is serving the people of South Carolina House District 68.
Whether you need help with a state agency, have questions about legislation, or want to get involved, my office is here to help.
Below are some of the most common requests and resources.
Please reach out anytime with questions, comments, or concerns — I’m honored to serve you. ~ Heather Request a South Carolina Flag You can request a South Carolina flag (3×5 nylon) flown over the State Capitol.
For prices and more details, please call my Columbia office at (803) 212-6933.
Flag orders are processed once payment and request are received.
Please allow 2–4 weeks for delivery.

South Carolina House Page Program ​ Each year, students with outstanding academic achievement can apply to serve as a House Page, gaining hands-on experience at the State House and learning about state government.
To apply: Send your resume and cover letter describing your goals and interest in the program to: Rep.
Heather Crawford PO Box 31385 Myrtle Beach, SC 29588 Requirements: - At least 18 years old on or before the first day of work - Enrolled in or recently graduated from college - A U.S. citizen For questions, contact Rep.
Crawford’s office or the Office of the Clerk of the SC House of Representatives.
South Carolina Legislative Manual The South Carolina Legislative Manual, published annually, includes details on all branches of state government, contact information for local and federal offices, and highlights of our state’s history and symbols.
You can purchase it online at www.scstatehouse.gov.
For questions, contact Rep.
