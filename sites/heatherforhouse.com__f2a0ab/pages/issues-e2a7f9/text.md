Strengthening Infrastructure. ​ Chair of the House DOT Modernization Committee, cutting red tape and improving accountability.
Helped secure $1B in the last two years for roads and bridges in the state budget — including key Horry County and coastal drainage projects.
Leading efforts to reduce traffic congestion, prevent flooding, and support Horry County’s tourism-driven economy.
Focused on safe, reliable infrastructure that protects property, families, and jobs.
Keeping Our Communities Safe.
Secured funding for a domestic violence shelter right here in Horry County.
Championed the expansion of South Carolina’s Safe Haven law to protect vulnerable infants.

Supports legislation that protects the integrity of women’s sports and preserves sex-specific spaces for women and girls.

Promoting responsible, sustainable growth that keeps the Grand Strand’s quality of life.
