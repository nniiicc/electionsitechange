Leading With Compassion and Courage
Throughout my political career, I led with a rare combination of empathy and conviction.
My advocacy didn’t stop at speeches—it translated into tangible results.
From opposing the expansion of private school funding in favor of strengthening public education to co-sponsoring legislation that protected pregnant employees from discrimination, my work reflected my unwavering principles.
Even when faced with political pressure, I never backed down from what I believed in.
In the Florida House, I was the only Democrat to support a 24-hour abortion waiting period, driven by my personal values, despite criticism from my own party.
My courage to stand alone showed my commitment to personal integrity over political convenience.
My leadership was never performative.
It was hands-on, deeply personal, and always focused on the people.
Whether it was helping local families avoid foreclosure or securing life-saving resources for health programs, I consistently put the needs of my constituents first.
