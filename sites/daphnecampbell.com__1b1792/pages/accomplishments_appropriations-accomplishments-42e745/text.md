Home Initiatives Petition & Yard Sign Support Form Bio Accomplishments Comment Accomplishments Press Releases Blog Contact Stand With Daphne – Give Today 0 Appropriations Accomplishments Senator Daphne Campbell has a proven track record of securing vital appropriations to support the needs of her constituents and communities across Florida.
Through her diligent advocacy and strategic leadership, she has successfully secured over $4 billion in appropriations from 2011 to 2018, both as a State Representative and as a State Senator.
2011 : As a State Representative, Senator Campbell secured $176,016,100 in appropriations to address critical needs in her district and throughout the state.
2012 : Senator Campbell continued her commitment to securing funding by securing $269,018,488 in appropriations during her tenure as a State Representative.
2014 : In 2014, Senator Campbell’s advocacy efforts resulted in $201,293,765 in appropriations to support important initiatives and projects across Florida.
2017 : Transitioning to the role of State Senator, Senator Campbell continued to deliver for her constituents, securing $149,270,712 in appropriations to address pressing needs in her district and beyond.
2018 : In her final year as a State Senator, Senator Campbell secured $133,400,300 in appropriations, further demonstrating her dedication to delivering results for the people of Florida.
Senator Campbell’s appropriations accomplishments reflect her unwavering commitment to serving her constituents and improving the quality of life for all Floridians.
Through her effective leadership and advocacy, she has made a lasting impact on communities across the state.
