Stacy Zinn
Representative, HD 52
Stacy is a retired DEA agent who spent her career tackling cartels & terrorists.
She served four tours in Afghanistan, nearly 6 years in South America & 3 years on the South West Border.
She was appointed by the Yellowstone County Commission earlier this year to fill in for Judge Bill Mercer, who was appointed by President Trump to serve as a US District Court Judge in Missoula.
Stacy is running for House District 52 in Billings to continue serving her constituents and bring principled, conservative change to Montana.
