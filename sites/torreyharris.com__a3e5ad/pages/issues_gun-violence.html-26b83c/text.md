It is a public health crisis, and we have to treat it like one by investing in prevention, especially the community-based approaches that actually save lives.
In Nashville, I stand up to the gun lobby and fight for common sense reforms, even in a chamber that keeps moving in the wrong direction.
What I'm Fighting For Support universal background checks for all gun sales, including private sales and gun shows.
Pass order-of-protection (“red flag”) laws to keep guns away from people who are a clear danger to themselves or others.
Repeal permitless carry and keep guns out of schools, hospitals, and government buildings.
Oppose the reckless push to arm teachers in our classrooms.
Fund community violence intervention programs that meet young people where they are and stop the shooting before it starts.
Require common sense safety measures: waiting periods, safety training, and safe storage.
