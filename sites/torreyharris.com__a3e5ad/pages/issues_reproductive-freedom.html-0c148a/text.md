Every person deserves the right to make their own healthcare decisions without politicians standing in the way.
Here in Tennessee, that freedom is under attack.
Our near-total abortion ban has forced patients, families, and doctors into impossible situations and turned private medical decisions into a public battle.
What I'm Fighting For
- Repeal Tennessee’s abortion ban and restore real access to care.
- Fix the narrow, dangerous medical exceptions that leave doctors afraid to act and patients in crisis.
- Protect access to fertility care, including IVF.
- Protect the right to contraception for everyone.
- Protect doctors and patients from criminalization.
No provider or patient should face punishment for seeking or offering medically necessary care.
- Confront the maternal mortality crisis head-on, especially the outcomes for Black women in Shelby County, by expanding maternal health programs and requiring transparency on pregnancy-related deaths so no family’s pain goes unseen.
