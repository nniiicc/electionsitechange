Elected officials answer to the people, not to billionaires and corporate donors.
We have to restore trust in our democracy, and that starts right here in Tennessee.
That means protecting the freedom to vote, ending suppression in all its forms, and getting big money out of politics.
What I'm Fighting For
- Restore voting rights for Tennesseans who have completed their sentences.
Our state has one of the harshest, most broken rights-restoration systems in the country, and it silences too many of our neighbors.
- Oppose voter suppression, expand early voting and access, and make it easier, not harder, for eligible people to register and cast a ballot.
- End partisan gerrymandering, including the maps that cracked our communities apart to dilute our voices.
- Strengthen ethics laws and ban legislators from trading on the decisions they make in office.
- Get dark money out of Tennessee politics so your voice isn’t drowned out by the highest bidder.
