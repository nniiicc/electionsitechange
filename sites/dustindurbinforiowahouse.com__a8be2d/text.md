“I want to be your representative because Western Iowa has something to say, and I will be your voice” DISTRICT 12 I am campaigning to represent District 12 in the Iowa House of Representatives.
The district encompasses Crawford County, Ida County, and parts of Shelby County including Harlan, Earling, Westphalia, Defiance, Panama, Portsmouth, and Irwin.
MY STORY I was raised in Ashland, Nebraska, a small town between Omaha and Lincoln.
My parents along with my brother and his family still reside there.
My sister and her son live outside of Des Moines.
As for myself, I live with my partner on an acreage outside of Dow City.
I enjoy the serenity and sunsets over the western Iowa fields.
My hobbies include grilling with friends, hiking around the acreage, and if there is time, catching up on suduko.
