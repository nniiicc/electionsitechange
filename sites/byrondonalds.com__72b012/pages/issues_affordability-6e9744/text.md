Affordability
“Florida cannot become too expensive for working families and seniors on fixed incomes.
The status quo has a cost.
Progress doesn't pay the bill sitting on your kitchen counter today—it's time to bring down costs.”
Issues Agenda
01
Protect reforms and stop lawsuit abuse that jacks up rates for everyone, saving homeowners hundreds of dollars a year.
02
Lower costs by trimming down state fees when reserves are full so those extra dollars stay in your wallet.
Pursue regulatory reforms that lower insurance premiums.
03
Reform the My Safe Florida Home program and other grants programs to fund real, storm-hardening upgrades that lower your annual premium the most.
04
Keep Citizens Insurance small so everyday drivers and homeowners never get hit with a surprise "hurricane tax" bill after a big storm.
05
Cut the red tape and slow permitting that make rebuilding a home after a storm needlessly expensive and drive up your insurance premiums.
Create a simple online Scorecard where you can compare insurance companies side by side on price, how fast they pay claims, and how often they deny coverage.
Require insurers to report accurate data or pay real fines, keeping the tool free for taxpayers by having the insurance industry cover the cost.
Keep property taxes in check so seniors and working families aren't taxed out of their homes.
Stop giant industrial data centers from passing their massive electric and water bills onto everyday households.
