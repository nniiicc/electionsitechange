Healthcare
“You deserve to know what healthcare costs before you get treated.
Your Doctor, Your Price puts you back in charge, cuts out the red tape, and lets doctors focus on their patients.”
Issues Agenda
01
Require doctors and hospitals to post plain, guaranteed cash prices for the 50 most common medical tests and procedures.
02
Ensure everyday patients paying cash get the lowest discounted rate that the hospital gives to big insurance companies.
03
Let what you pay to an out-of-network doctor count directly toward your yearly insurance deductible.
04
Require insurance companies to approve common treatments within three days instead of letting paperwork delay your care.
05
Expose pharmacy middleman markups so prescription discounts get passed straight to you at the checkout counter.
