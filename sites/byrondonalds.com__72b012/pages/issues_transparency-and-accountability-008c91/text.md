← All Issues Issues Agenda Transparency and Accountability “It should be as easy to see government spending as it is to order off Amazon: One click, one website, and all the information you have a right to know.
It's your hard earned money—we are going to put Florida's entire checkbook in the clear light of day.” Regular Independent Audits: 01 Run annual audits across all levels of government—from Tallahassee agencies down to local utility and special districts.
02 Expand state watchdog audits to track down waste, cut bloated programs, and save taxpayers money.
Every Dime Online: 01 Put every state, county, and city contract onto one searchable, public website that anyone can look up from their phone.
02 Require every city hall and county commission to post a simple pie chart showing where your tax money actually goes.
Open Public Salaries: 01 Put local city managers and high-ranking municipal bureaucrats on the same public salary tracker that already lists state worker pay.
Honest Taxes and Fees: 01 Clean up annual property tax mailings so you see a clear, simple chart of exactly which local offices are taking a cut of your money.
02 Review all local permits and municipal charges to stop local governments from slipping hidden mystery fees onto your monthly utility bills.
