Join the Coalition Read to Succeed Reading by third grade 4 Paths College, Trades, Military, Business Every Setting Public, Charter, Private, Homeschool THE COALITION Parents and teachers at the head of the table The coalition is open to families and educators across every setting — public schools, charter schools, private schools, and homeschool.
The goal is the same in all of them: children who can read, parents who have a real say, and teachers who have the tools to do the job.
Byron Donalds’ education agenda is built around a simple idea: every child is unique, and the adults closest to that child — parents and teachers — know a one-size-fits-all system isn’t the answer.
Read to Succeed puts proven reading instruction, early screening, and tutoring behind students so more children are reading at grade level by third grade, and it recognizes teachers who get results.
Student Success Plans give every middle and high school student a personalized roadmap — college, the trades, the military, or entrepreneurship — written with parents and educators in the room.
Families who need a different setting keep the freedom to choose it.
Education dollars follow the child.
Parents and Teachers for Byron exists to take that agenda to the people who live it.
The coalition will raise awareness of what Florida already offers, share real stories from classrooms and kitchen tables, and make the case for a governor who expands opportunity instead of narrowing it.
The coalition will work statewide to give busy parents and working teachers a way to get informed and plug in without giving up a Tuesday night — through shareable content, local and virtual conversations, and the understanding that meeting the educational needs of our children does not end on Election Day.
Members will share their stories, invite the people already in their circles, and help elect a governor who keeps parents and teachers at the head of the table.
