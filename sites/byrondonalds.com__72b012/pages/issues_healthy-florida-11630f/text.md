← All Issues Issues Agenda Healthy Florida “When your child sits down in the school cafeteria, you should know what's on their tray.
When they take a drink from the fountain, you should know that water is clean and safe.” Clean School Food: 01 Clear ultra-processed junk food, chemical additives, and artificial dyes off school breakfast and lunch menus.
02 Stop schools from selling candy, sugary sodas, and energy drinks at after school events.
03 Require school districts to post all cafeteria menus and ingredient lists online so parents can easily see what their children are eating.
04 Support our state's farmers by connecting local Florida farms straight to school lunchrooms.
Clean Water in Every School: 01 Require every Florida school to test its tap water for lead and harmful chemicals, and post every result online.
02 Fix old pipes and install certified water filters immediately whenever unsafe water is detected.
03 Test for modern contaminants like microplastics, pharmaceuticals, and "forever chemicals".
04 Provide free or low-cost water testing for Florida families with young children who rely on private well water.
