Space and Tech
“Every American who walked on the Moon took off from Florida's coast.
It's time to bring NASA home, build out our launchpads, and create thousands of high-paying jobs right here in Florida.”
Issues Agenda
01
Work to move NASA headquarters and the Space Force Academy right to our Space Coast where the rockets actually launch.
02
Pre-approve the top 10 industrial space manufacturing sites across the state so aerospace companies set up shop in Florida without delay.
03
Train Florida high schoolers and college students to work as skilled aerospace welders, machinists, and launch technicians.
04
Bring leading private aerospace innovators and engineers together to ensure Florida stays the world's top launchpad.
