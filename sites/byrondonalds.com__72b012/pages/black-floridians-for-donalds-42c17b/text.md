Join the Coalition 25 Leaders Statewide Earn, Learn, Own The Plan Tallahassee To Miami THE COALITION Earn, Learn, Own: from Tallahassee to Miami Twenty-five Black leaders from across Florida launched the Florida Coalition of Black Leaders for Donalds, a statewide coalition supporting Republican nominee Byron Donalds for governor and his running mate, Bryan Ávila, for lieutenant governor.
The coalition includes pastors, sheriffs, state lawmakers, business owners, educators and civic leaders from Tallahassee to Miami.
Donalds and Ávila have spent time in Black communities across the state, in churches, barbershops, small businesses and on HBCU campuses, hearing from families about the cost of living, public safety, education and economic opportunity.
Donalds began his college education at Florida A&M University and finished at Florida State University.
If elected, he would be Florida’s first Black governor.
Coalition members will carry Earn, Learn, Own, the campaign’s plan for jobs, education and ownership, into churches, barbershops, campuses and small businesses across the state.
