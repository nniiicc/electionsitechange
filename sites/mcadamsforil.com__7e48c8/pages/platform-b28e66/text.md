- Everyone deserves good healthcare As someone who lives with a chronic illness, I know just how important it is to have access to good healthcare.
With the Republican Party in Congress cutting billions of dollars from the Illinois’s healthcare system, it's more important than ever that we invest in public healthcare at the state level.
Illinois will be one of the states most impacted by cuts to Medicaid from the Republican “big, beautiful budget bill” passed last summer.
Estimates predict as much as $57 billion will be taken out of Illinois’s healthcare system due to these cuts.
In District 24, hospitals bring in over $200 million annually from Medicaid.
When those funds go away, hospitals will have to make up that lost revenue by raising prices or cutting vital services.
To keep hospitals open, and to prevent costs from further skyrocketing for our residents, we need to make substantial investments in our local healthcare systems.
That includes access to mental health and reproductive healthcare.
Currently Illinois uses Care Management Agencies (CMAs) to administer Medicaid throughout the state.
In the past, Medicaid was administered in-house by the state.
Eliminating CMAs as a middleman would save our state hundreds of millions in overhead costs, which can offset Medicaid cuts set to take effect next year.
It would also allow us to take a patient-centric approach to provide the best care possible, as opposed to CMAs which focus on profits first.

- For too long Illinois has overtaxed working and middle-class families Illinois relies heavily on property taxes to fund government services.
Property taxes are a regressive tax which takes a higher percentage of income from you if you earn less.
It's time that we ease the tax burden on the vast majority of Illinois households which are not in the top 1% and instead ask the wealthiest to pay their fair share.
I would proudly support House Joint Resolution 18, introduced in 2025, which calls for raising new revenue from the ultrawealthy to help fund our communities.
By closing corporate tax loopholes and taxing billionaires, we ease the tax burden on everyday people and raise the revenue we desperately need to fund our communities.

- There has been an explosion of data centers all throughout Illinois over the past year.
Residents all throughout Illinois and the 24th district are worried about the massive increase in data centers being built in our communities and the coming of AI that is associated with them.
These are valid concerns.
Large data centers use up lots of water to cool down their equipment, consuming lots of energy and raising local utility prices.
They also release greenhouse gases, and there and there have been issues of noise pollution near data centers.
We need to make sure that communities have their voices heard as to whether they want a data center built nearby, and that any data center that is built is done in an environmentally sustainable way.
Passing legislation like the POWER Act will make sure that any data centers which are built are done so in an environmentally friendly manner.
Climate change is here, and Illinois needs to prepare by building a green energy system and economy for the future.
Not only will this help combat the impact of climate change, but it will also revitalize the local economies in communities across the state.
It will also protect us from “oil shocks” like we’re seeing as a result of Donald Trump’s illegal war with Iran.
By investing in more green energy, we can diversify our energy sources and be better equipped to handle shocks in the fossil fuel industry.
