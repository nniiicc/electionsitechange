Tarah Probst, a lifelong resident of northeast Pennsylvania, serves as the Representative for House District 189 in the Pennsylvania General Assembly.
Tarah was re-elected to her second term in 2024 with a margin of 15%.
Before her election in 2022, she made history as the first female Mayor of Stroudsburg, a position she held since 2015.
Born and raised in East Stroudsburg, Tarah earned her Law degree from Widener University School of Law.
She has a background in marketing and co-owned a downtown restaurant, showing her commitment to community development.
As mayor, Tarah focused on fostering positive change and economic growth in Stroudsburg.
She also worked on regional issues and was recognized nationally for her efforts in community improvement.
With a passion for public service, Tarah is dedicated to serving the people of her district and advocating for real change.
Her extensive involvement in various committees and boards demonstrates her commitment to community engagement and collaboration.
Tarah's experience, leadership, and proven track record make her the right candidate to represent the 189th district in the State House of Representatives.
