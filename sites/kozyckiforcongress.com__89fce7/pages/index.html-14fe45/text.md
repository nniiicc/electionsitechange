A LEADER FORGED BY SERVICE
Tested Leadership When It Matters Most
Tony Kozycki has spent his life stepping into the moments that matter.
Raised in LaGrange, Georgia, he learned early the values of discipline, accountability, and hard work as a student-athlete.
Those lessons carried with him to the United States Naval Academy, where he was nominated by Senator Saxby Chambliss and trained to lead with integrity under pressure.
After earning his commission, Tony became a Naval Aviator, flying the F/A-18 Super Hornet.
He completed three combat deployments, leading missions in complex environments where decisions carried real consequences.
He worked closely with allied forces, building trust and coordination across nations, and served with the discipline and accountability that only comes from knowing others are counting on you.
After returning home, Tony continued his life of service as an attorney, fighting for families across Georgia and standing up to systems that too often fail the people they are supposed to protect.
He is also a father and a coach, grounded in the same responsibilities and pressures that working families face every day.
Tony is running to bring that same standard of leadership to Congress, grounded in service, tested under pressure, and focused on delivering for the people who depend on it.

Service Didn't End with the Uniform
Tony brought that same mindset home to Georgia.
Not to sit on the sidelines, but to keep fighting.
After leaving active duty, Tony returned home and enrolled at Georgia State University College of Law, graduating summa cum laude.
As an attorney, he has represented families navigating some of the most difficult moments of their lives, fighting for due process, fairness, and accountability in systems that too often fall short.
He has seen firsthand how broken policies impact real people, not in theory, but in courtrooms, in homes, and in communities across Georgia.

Tony is also continuing his leadership development through the Harvard Kennedy School Public Leadership Credential, working toward a mid-career Master in Public Administration.

Our Solutions
AFFORDABILITY
Working families are doing everything right and still falling behind.
Costs keep rising while stability feels further out of reach.
It is time to put working families back at the center of our economy.
RESTORING TRUST IN GOVERNMENT
Trust in government is low because people feel like it is not working for them.
That is not a perception problem, it is a leadership problem.
It is time to earn that trust back.
CHILDCARE & WORKING FAMILIES
Childcare has become one of the biggest bills families face every month.
Parents are being forced to choose between working and staying afloat.
That is not a choice families should have to make.
HEALTHCARE
Healthcare costs keep rising while families are left to deal with the consequences.
Recent decisions have made coverage more expensive instead of more affordable.
That is hitting working families where it hurts most.
SENIORS & RETIREMENT SECURITY
Social Security and Medicare are promises Americans earned over a lifetime of work.
Tony will protect those benefits, fight rising prescription drug costs, and crack down on scams that target seniors and their savings.
HOUSING
Housing costs are climbing and working families are getting squeezed out.
Rent is rising, homeownership is slipping away, and stability is getting harder to find.
This is one of the biggest pressures families face today.
IMMIGRATION
We need a secure border and a system that actually works.
Right now we have neither.
Serious leadership means fixing both at the same time.
NATIONAL SECURITY & DIPLOMACY
America is strongest when we lead with strength and stand by our allies.
National security is not about politics, it is about trust and credibility.
That is how we keep this country safe.
VETERANS AFFAIRS
Veterans have already done their part for this country.
They should not have to fight for the care and benefits they earned.
We owe them better than that.
BIPARTISAN LEADERSHIP
People are tired of politics that leads nowhere.
Real leadership means working with others to actually solve problems.
That is how things get done.
