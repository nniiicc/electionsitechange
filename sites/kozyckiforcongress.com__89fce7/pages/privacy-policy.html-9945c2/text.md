Cookies & Online Tracking
Like most websites, kozyckiforcongress.com uses cookies and similar technologies, including third-party tracking pixels, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our outreach.
These tools may automatically collect information such as your device and browser type, the pages you visit, and actions you take on the site, including signing up, volunteering, or clicking through to donate.
We use this information to measure website activity and to build audiences so that we can show campaign-related advertising to people who have visited our site.
This may involve third-party advertising and analytics partners that process the data on our behalf.
You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of many interest-based advertising cookies through your browser or device privacy controls.
Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the core features of this site.
