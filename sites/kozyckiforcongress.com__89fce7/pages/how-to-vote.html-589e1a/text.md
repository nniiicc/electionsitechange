Here's a quick checklist.
Check your voter registration The registration deadline for the general election is October 5, 2026.
Verify your status at Georgia My Voter Page .
Have a valid photo ID ready Georgia requires a valid photo ID to vote in person.
See the accepted ID list below.
Know your polling location For Election Day voting, you must go to your assigned precinct.
For early voting, you can visit any early voting site in your county.
Look it up at mvp.sos.ga.gov .
Confirm you're in GA-7 Not sure if you're in the 7th District?
Use our interactive district map to check your address.
Accepted Photo ID You'll need one of these to vote in person in Georgia.
Georgia driver's license (even if expired) Valid state or federal government photo ID Valid U.S. passport Valid U.S. military photo ID Valid employee photo ID (government entity) Valid tribal photo ID Free voter ID card (from county registrar or DDS) For absentee voting, you'll need to provide your Georgia driver's license number or state ID number on your application.
No photo ID is mailed in.
