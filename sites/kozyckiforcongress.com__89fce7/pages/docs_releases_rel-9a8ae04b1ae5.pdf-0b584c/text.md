House: A Statistical Dead Heat in an R+11 District On the ballot test, likely voters in GA-07 split Tony Kozycki (D) 43% to Rich McCormick (R), Incumbent, 46%, with 11% not sure — a 3-point margin that falls well within this survey's ±4.7% margin of error.
That makes GA-07's U.S.

U.S.
House Share Tony Kozycki (D) Rich McCormick (R), Incumbent Not Sure 43% 46% 11% Context matters here.
Georgia's 7th Congressional District has been one of the more reliably Republican seats in the state — in a typical cycle, this is an R+12 district.
Senator Ossoff does not need to carry GA-07 to win statewide — he can build a winning coalition without it.
But the fact that voters here have this race tied within the margin of error tells a bigger story about the political environment this cycle.
20/20 Insight, LLC — GA CD-07 Poll Memo Page 1 For Tony Kozycki to be running even with a sitting Republican incumbent in a district this red is a significant sign of erosion in GOP support and a real opportunity in a seat national handicappers would not otherwise consider competitive.
Part of what's driving that strength is the candidate himself.
Tony Kozycki is a Navy veteran — a Naval Academy graduate who completed three combat deployments flying in the F/A-18 and earned three Air Medals before going on to earn his law degree summa cum laude and build a career representing Georgia families.
When surveyed voters heard this biography, 56% said they would consider voting for him, including 35% who said they would greatly consider it — a strong result for a Democrat introducing himself in a district this red, and a sign that his background as a combat veteran and attorney is resonating with voters across the political spectrum.
