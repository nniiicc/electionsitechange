mail-in ballots
Pennsylvania provides “No-Excuse Mail-In Voting.” Act 77, passed with bipartisan support in 2019, gives a voice to every legally registered voter who can now cast a ballot by mail without needing to provide an excuse or reason.
WHAT HAS REP MOUL DONE?
Moul first voted for Act 77, then, as a 2020 election denier, he joined a lawsuit against it.
The reason he gave when asked about his change of heart?
“So my bad.
I should’ve checked the constitutionality of that big bill.”
Note: The Pennsylvania Supreme Court ultimately upheld Act 77 as constitutional in August 2022
