Meet Chris
Air Force Veteran — Chris served our country honorably, joining the Air Force immediately after high school.
Family man — Chris and his wife, Katy, love their six children.
When he isn’t keeping up with his six children, he enjoys hunting, fishing, driving his tractor and welding on his farm north of Okmulgee.
Business Owner — Chris Banning has started multiple, successful companies from scratch, creating jobs in our community.
Leading Employer of Veterans — Chris leads a team of over 50-employees and has been nationally recognized for his work with veterans, serving on non-profit boards dedicated to improving the lives of those who served.
