Over the past few legislative sessions, Republicans in the Arizona state legislature have introduced countless pieces of legislation to restrict access to abortion.
Now that Roe v.
Wade has been overturned, their next steps are to ban access to birth control and in-vitro fertilization – necessary healthcare for so many Americans.
We must respect each person's right to make the best choice for their unique circumstances.
It means supporting policies that reduce the need for abortion by expanding access to contraception, healthcare, and education.
Reproductive freedom is also about acknowledging the significant disparities in access to healthcare that exist in our state.
Far too many Arizonans, especially those in marginalized communities, face barriers to accessing the reproductive healthcare they need.
As your representative, Stephanie will work tirelessly to address these disparities and ensure that every person, regardless of their background or income, has equal access to reproductive healthcare services.
Stephanie understands that this issue is deeply personal and often evokes strong emotions on both sides.
However, it's essential that we approach it with empathy and a commitment to protecting individual rights and healthcare access.
She is committed to fostering a respectful and open dialogue with all voices in our community.
Stephanie’s support for reproductive freedom is unwavering.
She will be a strong advocate for the rights and health of all Arizonans, and she will work tirelessly to protect and expand reproductive freedom in our great state.
Together, we can ensure that every individual in Arizona can make their own reproductive healthcare decisions, free from interference, and with the respect and dignity they deserve.
