Building stronger families and brighter futures for Arizona
"Hello, I'm Stephanie.
As a mother, state representative, teacher, and active member of our vibrant Arizona community, I've experienced firsthand the challenges and triumphs our families face.
From the classrooms of Deer Valley to the dinner tables at home, I understand the importance of quality education, sustainable living, affordable housing, and safeguarding our rights.
Together, we can make Arizona not just a place to live, but a place to thrive.
Let's shape the future, side by side."
Education
Our public schools have been in crisis mode for over a decade, and it's thanks to historic divestments in funding.
Stephanie sees that we have a responsibility as a community to come together, to make changes so our students and staff can flourish in their learning environments.
Affordability
The costs of healthcare, housing, childcare, food, transportation, and utilities are rising, while purchasing power is falling.
Dignity and security in Arizona cannot be reserved for those who can afford to pay more than ever for everyday needs.
Reproductive Freedom
Far-right Republicans have been waging war against reproductive freedom for decades all around the country and right here at home.
Now that Roe v.
Wade has been overturned, their next steps are to ban access to birth control and in-vitro fertilization – necessary healthcare for so many Americans.
Housing
The housing crisis in Arizona is not just a statistic; it's a real problem that is impacting the lives of countless families.
Whether you're a young professional trying to find your first home, a working-class family struggling to make rent, or a senior citizen on a fixed income, the rising cost of housing has become an unbearable burden for too many.
