GET UPDATES Economy Education Keeping Idaho Home About James James is a conservative with proven leadership ability who has lived in Idaho since he was 2 years old.
He has spent the last ten years building businesses in the Treasure Valley.
They include Upland Optics, a sporting optics company for hunters.
He is a graduate of both Boise State and Harvard Universities, and he is a passionate outdoorsman who spends much of his time on Idaho’s public lands.
He began his first term in December of 2022.
He sits on the Appropriations, Resources, and Transportation committees.
