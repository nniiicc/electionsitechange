Privacy Policy
Last updated: February 2026
Martinez For the People is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard any personal information you provide when you contact us, sign up for updates, donate, or otherwise interact with our organization.
1.
Information We Collect
We only collect the personal information you voluntarily provide to us, such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)
- Mailing address (if provided)
- Any other details you include in messages or forms when reaching out to us
We do not collect information automatically through tracking technologies (e.g., cookies, pixels, or analytics scripts) unless explicitly stated otherwise on a specific page.
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use your personal information solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry or request.
Specifically, we may contact you directly via email, phone, or mail to:
- Answer questions
- Provide information you requested
- Follow up on conversations or commitments
Your information is never used for marketing purposes unless you explicitly opt in to receive such communications.
3.
We Do Not Sell or Share Your Information
Martinez For the People will never sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own commercial purposes.
We do not share your information with data brokers, advertisers, political action committees, or any other entities outside our direct operations.
4.
Limited Sharing
Your information may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:
- With trusted service providers (e.g., email service provider, payment processor) who assist us in operating our communications and are contractually bound to keep your data confidential and secure.
- If required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or court order.
5.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
6.
Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Request access to the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
To exercise these rights, please contact us at campaign@martinezforthepeople.com.
7.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
