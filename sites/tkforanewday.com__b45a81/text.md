For far too long, we have leadership that does not represent us, but rather uses accusatory, fear-based rhetoric that escalates a policy disagreement into a moral and personal threat .
I’m Tara Killen – citizen, coach, and advocate for a more inspired kind of politics.
This campaign is about renewal – restoring integrity, compassion, and common sense to public service.
It’s about helping our communities build the kind of future they’d love to live in, together.
Our founders believed in government by and for the people.
I believe in that too.
Not as a slogan, but as a sacred trust.
It’s time for a new day in District 10 – one guided by purpose, not partisanship; vision, not ambition.
Donate Volunteer Your Current Senator in Action Meet Tara Killen: Advocating for Minnesota As the saying goes, those who don’t know history are doomed to repeat its mistakes.
Our nation’s founders knew this well – they built a government of checks and balances, designed to be run by the people and for the people .
Somewhere along the way, we lost that balance.
The voices of everyday Minnesotans have been drowned out by politics, power, and noise.
It’s time to bring the spirit of true representation back.
TK for a New Day is about learning from both our distant and recent past – remembering what worked, healing what hasn’t, and building a future rooted in liberty, justice, and shared purpose.
As your State Senator for District 10, I will approach every decision through both lenses – the big picture and the fine details – to restore balance, accountability, and hope.
Together, we can build a more perfect union where every person has the freedom to pursue happiness and live life in liberty and fairness.
