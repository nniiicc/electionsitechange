Grounded in Truth.
Focused on the Future.
Our founders understood that lasting freedom depends on balance – between government and the governed, between liberty and responsibility, between faith and reason.
That balance has been lost in modern politics.
My vision for District 10 is to restore it – not through rhetoric, but through service, listening, and thoughtful action.
Each of us wants the same things at heart: safety, opportunity, fairness, and the freedom to live life on our own terms.
It’s time to stop fighting over labels and start building together again.
This campaign is not about left or right – it’s about right and wrong, about restoring balance where imbalance has taken hold.
Together, we can renew the promise of self-governance: where faith informs compassion, history guides wisdom, and the people lead their government once again.
TK, For a New Day – because Minnesota’s future depends on balance, love of neighbor, and the courage to begin again.
Commitment to Change Empowering Families and Communities Strong communities begin with strong families.
As your senator, I’ll advocate for policies that keep families stable, safe, and supported – from affordable housing and childcare to accessible healthcare and mental health resources.
Every Minnesotan deserves to feel secure in their home and hopeful about their future.
Strengthening Education for All Ages Education is the foundation of a free society.
I support empowering parents, teachers, and local districts to shape learning that fits their students’ real needs – not one-size-fits-all mandates.
And education doesn’t stop at graduation: career retraining, technical programs, and lifelong learning opportunities are essential for a changing economy.
Supporting Local Business and Rural Prosperity Our small businesses and farms are the heartbeat of District 10.
They deserve a government that works with them, not against them.
I’ll fight for fair taxation, responsible regulation, and infrastructure investments that help local economies thrive – from broadband to clean energy to safe roads.
Protecting Natural Resources with Stewardship and Common Sense Minnesota’s lakes, forests, and farmland are sacred trusts.
Conservation shouldn’t be a political issue – it’s a moral one.
We can balance economic growth with environmental responsibility, ensuring our children inherit a state as beautiful and bountiful as the one we were blessed with.
Restoring Trust and Accountability in Government Government exists to serve the people, not to serve itself.
I will work for greater transparency, limit wasteful spending, and hold myself and others to the highest ethical standards.
Representation means remembering who sent you there – and who you work for.
Protecting Freedom, Faith, and Fairness Our Constitution guarantees liberty and justice for all – not just for some.
I will defend every citizen’s right to have due process, live, speak, and worship according to their conscience while promoting mutual respect and compassion.
Freedom and love of neighbor are not opposites; they are partners.
