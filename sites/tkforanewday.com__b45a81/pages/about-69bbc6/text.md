“Be the change you want to see in the world”
That idea, often attributed to Gandhi, is more than a saying – it’s a way of living.
It means that transformation begins with the individual who’s willing to stand up and live their values.
That’s why I’m running for the Minnesota State Senate, District 10.
We don’t need more politicians chasing power.
We need citizens who understand service.
Our government was designed to be balanced, accountable, and guided by the people – not by career interests or partisan agendas.
Somewhere along the way, we’ve drifted from that vision.
It’s time to bring it back.
As a coach, I’ve spent years helping people align their lives with their values – to live intentionally, with clarity and courage.
Now, I want to bring that same spirit of empowerment to public service.
My faith is simple: Love thy neighbor as thyself.
It’s one of Christ’s most direct teachings, yet it’s often the one we forget.
To love one’s neighbor means to listen, to serve, and to make decisions that honor everyone’s dignity – even those who disagree with us.
The people of District 10 deserve representation that reflects those values.
A senator who doesn’t just talk about faith or freedom, but lives them.
A representative who approaches each issue – large or small – with balance, humility, and heart.
This campaign isn’t about division.
It’s about restoration – restoring truth, balance, and compassion to the place where decisions are made on behalf of the people.
Together, we can make government what it was meant to be: a partnership between citizens, built on service, guided by wisdom, and dedicated to liberty and justice for all.
I will work in service of the people, and am applying to you, the people, to be your MN Senator for District 10.
