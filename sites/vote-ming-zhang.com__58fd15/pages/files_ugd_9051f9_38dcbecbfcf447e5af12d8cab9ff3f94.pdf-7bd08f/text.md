Local Leadership: Lessons from Leominster During my conversation with Leominster Mayor Dean Mazzarella, I was reminded that effective leadership begins with understanding the community you serve.
We discussed challenges facing cities and towns across our district: supporting seniors facing rising costs, creating housing opportunities, maintaining financial responsibility, and investing in the future.
What I Heard This Month Three conversations that stayed with me: • Housing: Young families want to stay in the communities they grew up in, but affordability is becoming harder. • Cost of living: Seniors and working families are feeling pressure from rising expenses. • Local decision-making: Residents want state government to respect local knowledge while helping communities address shared challenges.
