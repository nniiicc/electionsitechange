Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA Dom Belza Earns Key Endorsement from California Rifle & Pistol Association and Earns NRA’s Top Grade Marysville, Calif. – Today, Dom Belza’s campaign announced that he has earned a key endorsement from the California Rifle & Pistol Association.
The California Rifle & Pistol Association (CRPA), founded in 1875, is a nonprofit membership and donor supported organization with tens of thousands of members throughout California.
The endorsement is key in the 3rd Assembly District which is home to thousands of sportsmen and gun owners.
The endorsement reflects Belza’s strong commitment to protecting the Second Amendment, supporting responsible gun ownership, and defending the constitutional rights of law-abiding citizens.
“I’m honored to have the support of CRPA and look forward to being an advocate for them,” said Belza.
“The California Legislature likes to test the boundaries of legality when it comes to stomping on the constitutional rights of law-abiding citizens.
When I’m elected I will be a voice for responsible gun ownership and protecting the rights of gun owners.
It’s as simple as that.” In related news, Belza received an “A” rating from the National Rifle Association, an organization of which he is also a member.
