Marysville, Calif. – Dom Belza’s campaign is running on all cylinders today as ballots are scheduled to begin arriving for the June 3 election.
Today, Belza announced that he is the first candidate in the 3rd Assembly District race to launch a television ad.
This comes after Belza announced last week that he was also the first candidate in the race with a radio buy.
Today’s announcement confirms Belza is the only candidate in the race to be on TV, Radio and in Mailboxes at a critical time as ballots are arriving in voters’ hands.

The TV AD will air across the district on local cable, broadcast tv and streaming services.
Like Belza’s radio, it features his broad support in the election, including the official endorsements of public safety, as well as the California Republican Assembly and longtime incumbent Republican Assemblyman James Gallagher.
