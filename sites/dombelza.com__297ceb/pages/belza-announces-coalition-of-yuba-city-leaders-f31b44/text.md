Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA Belza Announces Coalition of Yuba City Leaders Yuba City, Calif. – Significant support from the community continues to come in for Dom Belza for the State Assembly – this week Belza announced a coalition of Yuba City endorsements, including the new endorsement of former Mayor Kash Gill .
Mayor Gill was first elected to the City Council in 2006 and served two terms as Mayor – the first Sikh Mayor in the history of the United States.
The newly announced endorsements join the previously announced current and former City Council Members, who include: Marc Boomgaarden, Mayor Mike Pasquale, Vice Mayor Toni Cole, Councilmember Wade Kirchner, Councilmember Dave Shaw, Councilmember Shon Harris, Councilmember (Ret.) John Dukes, Councilmember (Ret.) Rory Ramirez, Councilmember (Ret.) Former Mayor Kash Gill added, “I’m proud to support Dom Belza for the State Assembly.
Dom and his family have called the North State home for generations.
He understands our unique needs and our values and that’s exactly why he is the right man to represent us.
He has my full support.” Wade Kirchner, former Yuba City Mayor and current City Councilmember said, “During our time as City Councilmembers for neighboring jurisdictions, I was able to work closely with Dom Belza.
In that time I was able to appreciate his work ethic, his broad knowledge base and his passion to serve.
It is for these reasons, and more, that I am endorsing him for the position of State Assembly District 3.” Dom Belza placed first in a three-way field of Republicans with 45.2% of the vote in the California Statewide Primary, winning all six of the district’s counties.
The nearest challenger finished nearly 16% behind Dom Belza at 29%.
About Dom Belza: Dom has been married to his wife, Julia, for 12 years, and together they have four children: three daughters—Rylee (11), Raegan (9), and Ruby (6)—and one son, Johnny (3).
Outside of his professional life, Dom is passionate about serving and giving back to his local community.
This dedication inspired him to run for City Council.
He also volunteers at his church and with many local service organizations and industry groups.
Dom’s family has been involved in agriculture for four generations in the North State.
He understands the challenges our farming and ranching community faces on issues like labor, water, and costs.
The 3rd Assembly District encompasses all or portions of six counties: Butte, Sutter, Yuba, Tehama, Glenn, and Placer.
