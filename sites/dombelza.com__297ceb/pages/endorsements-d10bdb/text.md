Dom’s common-sense approach to public policy was refreshing and his commitment and energy relentless.
Todd Retzloff Former Sutter County Assessor I’ve known Dom Belza for a long time and he is the right candidate to be our next Assemblyman.
Our local community deserves someone who understands the complexity of the State’s tax code and how it impacts our local community at every level.
Dom will be a common-sense voice at the state level to help local taxpayers, property owners and small businesses thrive.
We need that sort of representation in Sacramento.
