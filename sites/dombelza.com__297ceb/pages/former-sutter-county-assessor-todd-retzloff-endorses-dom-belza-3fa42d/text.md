Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements What They Are Saying Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements What They Are Saying Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA Former Sutter County Assessor Todd Retzloff Endorses Dom Belza Belza Earns More Local Support After Big Primary Win Sutter County, Calif. – Belza continues to pick up key endorsements in the wake of his major election victory on June 2nd.
Today Belza announced the endorsement of former community leader Todd Retzloff.
In addition to being a local realtor, and small business owner, Todd formerly served as Sutter County’s Appraiser and then Assessor “I’ve known Dom Belza for a long time and he is the right candidate to be our next Assemblyman,” said Retzloff.
“Our local community deserves someone who understands the complexity of the State’s tax code and how it impacts our local community at every level.
Dom will be a common-sense voice at the state level to help local taxpayers, property owners and small businesses thrive.
We need that sort of representation in Sacramento.” Belza has been adding local supporters daily in his already impressive campaign supporter list since the Statewide Primary Election.
Just last week Belza announce strong endorsements from the City of Chico with Councilmembers Tom van Overbeek, Mike O’Brien and former Councilmember Sean Morgan announcing their support for Belza.
A full endorsement list can be viewed at www.DomBelza.com.
Dom Belza placed first in a three-way field of Republicans with 45.2% of the vote in the California Statewide Primary, winning all six of the district’s counties.
The nearest challenger finished nearly 16% behind Belza at 29%.
The 3rd Assembly District encompasses all or portions of six counties: Butte, Sutter, Yuba, Tehama, Glenn, and Placer.
