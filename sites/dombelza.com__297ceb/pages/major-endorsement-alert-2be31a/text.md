Making an early statement in the campaign to replace Assembly Republican Leader James Gallagher who is serving his final term, Belza announced Chico Mayor Kasey Reynolds is endorsing his campaign.
Chico is the largest city in the 3 rd Assembly District.
Mayor Kasey Reynolds is one of the most recognizable leaders in Butte County.
Reynolds was first elected to the Chico City Council in 2018 and re-elected in 2022 and most recently held the position as Vice-Mayor.
In addition to her role as the Mayor, Reynolds is the 4th generation co-owner of Shubert’s Ice Cream & Candy, an iconic business located in the heart of Downtown Chico.
Today’s announcement demonstrates Belza’s appeal and support throughout the 3rd Assembly District.
Just last week, Belza announced uniting a majority of Yuba-Sutter local elected officials behind his candidacy.
In his announcement Dom Belza stated, “I’m honored to have Mayor Kasey Reynolds as an early supporter to my campaign.
The Mayor is someone who has shown great leadership in Chico and I appreciate her willingness to tackle issues head on.
I look forward to working side by side with her in the coming months as we engage voters at a grassroots level about our vision to protect the North State’s values and improve the quality of life for our community.” About Dom Belza Dom Belza is an agricultural business owner, from a legacy farming family, and up until recently served as a local City Councilman.
For the past 19 years, he has worked as a local realtor and businessman, investing in and selling agricultural and commercial properties.
Dom Belza has been married to his wife, Julia, for 11 years, and together they have four children: three daughters—Rylee (9), Raegan (7), and Ruby (5)—and one son, Johnny (2).
