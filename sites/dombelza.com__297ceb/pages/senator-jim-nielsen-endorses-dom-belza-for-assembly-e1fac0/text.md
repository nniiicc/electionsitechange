Senator Nielsen represented North State communities in the California State Legislature as a member of both the State Assembly and State Senate during a career that spanned 19 years, including serving as the Senate Republican Leader.
“I know Dom Belza to be a family man with an honest passion for service.
He has a strong history of putting service over self and has a deep understanding of issues most important to the North State,” said Senator Nielsen.
“As Governor Gavin Newsom continues to drag California down, we need someone with the courage and moxie to stand up for our values, our agricultural-based economy, and our residents — and I am confident that’s Dom Belza.
He has my full support and endorsement.” Dom Belza expressed his gratitude for the Senator’s endorsement, saying, “Senator Jim Nielsen is the definition of a statesman and a leader.
I’ll work hard to emulate his leadership style as a conservative consensus builder and I am honored to have his support for my campaign.
His friendship and knowledge are invaluable as I work to be a member of the next generation of conservative leaders to represent the North State.” Belza, who was encouraged to run by incumbent Assemblyman James Gallagher, has quickly become the clear frontrunner in the 3rd Assembly District.
He has amassed an impressively long list of endorsements from across the district and continues to gain momentum by the hour.
For more information, please visit www.DomBelza.com.
About Dom Belza Dom has been married to his wife, Julia, for 11 years, and together they have four children: three daughters—Rylee (9), Raegan (7), and Ruby (5)—and one son, Johnny (2).
Outside of his professional life, Dom is passionate about serving and giving back to his local community.
This dedication inspired him to run for City Council and he continues to drive his volunteer work through his church and local service groups, including various service organizations and industry groups.
