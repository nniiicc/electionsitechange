Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA Dom Belza Earns Endorsement from the California Republican Assembly Marysville, Calif. – Dom Belza, candidate for State Assembly, today announced the endorsement of the California Republican Assembly (CRA) and he previously announced the endorsement of the Placer County Republican Assembly in his campaign to represent the 3rd District in the State Assembly.
The California Republican Assembly, one of the oldest and most influential conservative grassroots organizations in the state, has long been known for supporting candidates who champion constitutional principles, fiscal responsibility, and limited government.
Former President Ronald Reagan once famously described the CRA as “the conscience of the Republican Party,” a testament to its long-standing role in promoting principled conservative leadership.
The Placer County Republican Assembly plays a leading role in mobilizing conservative voters and activists throughout the county.
Belza said he is honored to receive the support of both organizations.
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the California Republican Assembly and the Placer County Republican Assembly,” said Belza.
“The CRA has a proud legacy of supporting leaders who stand firmly for the Constitution, individual liberty, and responsible government.
I look forward to working with their members and grassroots activists across the district to restore common sense in Sacramento, protect taxpayers, and ensure our communities remain safe and prosperous.” Belza’s campaign continues to gain momentum as community leaders, grassroots activists, and public safety advocates rally behind his vision for stronger communities, economic opportunity, and accountable leadership in the State Assembly.
