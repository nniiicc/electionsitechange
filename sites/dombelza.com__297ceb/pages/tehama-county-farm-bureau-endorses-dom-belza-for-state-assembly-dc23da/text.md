Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements What They Are Saying Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements What They Are Saying Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA Tehama County Farm Bureau Endorses Dom Belza for State Assembly Tehama County – Today, Dom Belza announced a major endorsement in his campaign for California’s 3rd Assembly District, earning the support of the Tehama County Farm Bureau.
“Agriculture needs leaders who understand what it takes to keep family farms and ranches viable for the next generation,” said Janet Rabo, President of the Tehama County Farm Bureau .
“Dom Belza has spent his career working alongside farmers, protecting agricultural land, and helping rural communities solve real problems.
We trust that he’ll be a strong voice for agriculture in Sacramento.
The Tehama County Farm Bureau is proud to endorse Dom Belza for State Assembly.” Agriculture is one of the largest economic drivers in Tehama County.
The Tehama County Farm Bureau is the voice of agriculture in the county, which includes roughly 3,000 square miles of ag land and 1,700 farming operations.
“I want to thank the Tehama County Farm Bureau for their support,” said Belza .
“Too often in Sacramento, our farmers and ranchers are ignored by politicians who don’t care about our rural way of life.
In the State Assembly, I will be a fearless advocate for North State agriculture.
I won’t back down.” In the June Primary, Dom Belza placed first in a three-way field of Republicans with 45.2% of the vote.
Belza finished first in all six counties of Assembly District 3 and outperformed the second-place finisher by nearly 16%.
