Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us Home About Issues Media News Volunteer Endorsements Contact Us DONATE ENDORSE DOM BELZA California Charter Schools Association Advocates Endorse Dom Belza MARYSVILLE, CA – Today Dom Belza, candidate for California’s 3rd Assembly District, announced an endorsement from California Charter Schools Association (CCSA) Advocates.
CCSA Advocates works to expand access to high-quality public education and advance the interests of California’s charter public schools, students, educators and families.
“Dom Belza understands that a quality education can change the trajectory of a child’s life and that California families deserve access to public schools, including charter public schools, that meet the unique needs of their children.
Dom will be a strong voice in Sacramento for students, parents and educators, and we are proud to support his campaign for State Assembly,” said Gregory McGinity, Executive Director of CCSA Advocates.
The endorsement carries particular significance for Belza, whose own children attend a charter public school in the district.
“I’m honored to earn the support of CCSA Advocates,” said Belza.
“Every family should have the opportunity to choose the public school that best meets the needs of their child, regardless of their ZIP code or income.
In the State Assembly, I will work to ensure parents have a meaningful voice in their children’s education.” Belza emerged from the three-way Republican Primary as the clear frontrunner finishing in first place with 45.2% of the vote.
His nearest challenger received approximately 29% of the vote, 16-points behind Belza.
Julia and Dom have been married for 12 years and have four children: their late daughter, Rylee (11); daughters Raegan (9) and Ruby (6); and son, Johnny (3).
