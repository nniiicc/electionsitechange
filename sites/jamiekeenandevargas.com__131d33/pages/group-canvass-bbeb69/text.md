Canvassers will always be assigned a partner if they don't bring their own.
Once you're signed up, check your email.
You will also be sent text reminders leading up to the canvass!
Canvassing shifts will begin with a brief training and introductions meeting, and will end with a recap/review meeting accompanied by a meal.
Select canvassing shifts with the checkboxes below the form!
Supplies you'll need to bring:
- Comfortable walking shoes
- A tote/purse for canvassing supplies
- A water bottle
- Your phone (well-charged!)
Everything else will be provided.
