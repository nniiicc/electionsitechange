Moran Stands with Harrison County
From manufacturing and trucking along I-20 to healthcare and education in Marshall, Harrison County is full of opportunity.
Nathaniel fights for policies that cut taxes, reduce red tape, and let small businesses and family farms grow.
With I-20, US-59, and Loop 390 running through Harrison County, safe roads are critical for freight, families, and everyday travel.
Nathaniel has pushed for federal support for projects like the Marshall Relief Route to make sure local communities benefit from infrastructure dollars.
Marshall’s historic train depot connects the county to Dallas, Longview, and beyond.
Moran supports the I-20 Passenger Rail Corridor because it means more travel options, stronger tourism, and new life for downtowns across East Texas.
With East Texas Baptist University, Wiley College, and Texas State Technical College all in Marshall, Harrison County is preparing the next generation of leaders and workers.
Moran supports initiatives that connect students directly to good-paying jobs.
