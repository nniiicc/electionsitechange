Congressman Nathaniel Moran knows what makes Harrison County strong: a respect for faith, a love for family, and a commitment to hard work.
In Washington, he carries those same values forward.
Whether it’s defending the unborn, supporting law enforcement, or protecting local jobs, Nathaniel is standing up for Harrison County every single day.
And when he’s back home, you’ll find him with his family and connected to the people he serves.

Nathaniel fights for policies that cut taxes, reduce red tape, and let small businesses and family farms grow.
Roads With I-20, US-59, and Loop 390 running through Harrison County, safe roads are critical for freight, families, and everyday travel.
Nathaniel has pushed for federal support for projects like the Marshall Relief Route to make sure local communities benefit from infrastructure dollars.
Rail and Downtown Revitalization Marshall’s historic train depot connects the county to Dallas, Longview, and beyond.
Moran supports the I-20 Passenger Rail Corridor because it means more travel options, stronger tourism, and new life for downtowns across East Texas.
Education With East Texas Baptist University, Wiley College, and Texas State Technical College all in Marshall, Harrison County is preparing the next generation of leaders and workers.
Moran supports initiatives that connect students directly to good-paying jobs.
