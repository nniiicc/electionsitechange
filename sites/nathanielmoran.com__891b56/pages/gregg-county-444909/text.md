Moran sits on House Ways & Means (and its Work & Welfare subcommittee), shaping tax, trade, and workforce policy that affect Gregg County employers.
His Working Families Tax Cut work highlights his focus on lowering taxes for families and small businesses.
He backed the I-20 passenger rail corridor, specifically calling out how it can revitalize historic downtowns in East Texas communities like Longview and Kilgore.
Through the Work & Welfare subcommittee, he pushes skills-first pathways and apprenticeships.
He’s highlighted local programs in East Texas and advanced district requests like commercial driver training funding.
Moran advanced the Rural Weather Monitoring Systems Act in the House to improve storm tracking and resilience for rural areas.
This is important for lake communities, parks, and outdoor events.
