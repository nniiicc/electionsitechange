From small shops in town to timber and ranching across the county, San Augustine’s employers keep families working.
In Congress, Rep.
Moran serves on the House Ways & Means Committee, where he works on tax and trade policies that help family businesses and rural producers grow.
San Augustine County borders Sam Rayburn Reservoir and includes part of the Sabine National Forest where families enjoy fishing and camping.
Rep.
Moran backs conservation and infrastructure that keep these spaces safe and open to the community.
San Augustine sits at the crossroads of US-96, SH-21, and SH-147.
These routes are essential for schools, freight, and everyday life.
Rep.
Moran pushes for federal investments that improve safety and reliability on rural roads.
