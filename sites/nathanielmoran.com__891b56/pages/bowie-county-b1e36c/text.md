In Washington, Congressman Moran is a strong voice for those values, working to protect local jobs, support veterans, and ensure that rural communities like Bowie County have the resources they need to thrive.
He serves on the Judiciary, Foreign Affairs, and Education & Workforce Committees, carrying the concerns of Bowie County with him to the nation’s capital.

The Red River Army Depot, local hospitals, and small businesses are central employers.
Congressman Moran advocates for policies that attract new investments and support local jobs.
Quality of Life & Natural Beauty Bowie County offers a high quality of life.
From boating and fishing at Wright Patman Lake to family-friendly events in Texarkana and New Boston, this is a place where community still matters.
Moran is committed to protecting that way of life for future generations.
Education & Local Growth With strong schools, higher education campuses, and vocational training options, Bowie County is preparing the next generation.
Congressman Moran supports opportunities for young people to build their future right here in East Texas.
Addressing Local Priorities Issues like border security, infrastructure (roads, broadband), veterans’ services, and access to affordable healthcare are front and center.
Congressman Moran is working for federal resources and legislation that respond specifically to Bowie County’s needs.
