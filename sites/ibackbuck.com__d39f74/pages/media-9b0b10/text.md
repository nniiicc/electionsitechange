About Senator Buck Newton
Senator Buck Newton is a conservative leader, attorney, and State Senator who is fighting to protect North Carolina values, strengthen our justice system, and support small businesses.
A lifelong advocate for the Second Amendment and pro-life policies, he is committed to opposing government overreach and standing with law enforcement.
Born and raised in Wilson, Buck runs a successful law practice and is dedicated to serving his community alongside his wife, Hope, and their three children.
