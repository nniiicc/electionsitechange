Join Team Elmquist and the SD40 DFL for the Slice of Shoreview Parade.
We will have free t-shirts for everyone that wants to walk with us (we even have some kid sized shirts!).
We are asking volunteers to arrive to the staging area (near the intersection of Arbogast and Victoria Street) no later than 9:30 am.
We will have someone on-site after 9:00 am, so just look for the friendly folks with the Elmquist shirts on!
There is a shuttle that runs from the end of the parade route (Seven Lakes properties parking lot, across the street from Island Lake County Park) that will take you to the staging area.
