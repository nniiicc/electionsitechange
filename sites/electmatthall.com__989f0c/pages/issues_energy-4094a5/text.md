When Matt Hall ran for state representative, he faced strong opposition from the utility industry.
Organizations linked to Consumers Energy spent hundreds of thousands of dollars trying to defeat him.
Matt Hall prevailed—and since then, he has remained a strong advocate for affordable, reliable energy, local control over wind and solar projects, and lower energy bills for Michigan families.
What Matt Hall Has Accomplished
Opposed 2023 Utility-Influenced Energy Laws
In 2023, Matt Hall voted against energy laws passed by the Democrat House majority.
These laws took decision-making authority away from local governments over large-scale wind, solar and energy storage projects and placed that authority with the state Public Service Commission, whose members are appointed by the governor.
The laws ban affordable, reliable energy sources, including natural gas, by 2040 and replaces them primarily with large-scale wind and solar projects.
The law also allows utility companies to pass along the enormous costs of closing existing power plants and constructing large-scale wind and solar farms to consumers while continuing to make record profits for their shareholders.
A recent Mackinac Center study projects that these laws will increase the average family’s annual household energy costs by $2,700 once fully implemented.
Matt Hall stood against these policies because Michigan families deserve energy that is affordable, reliable and secure.
Voted to Restore Local Control
In 2025, Matt Hall voted to repeal PA 233 of 2023, the law that took authority over wind, solar and energy storage siting decisions away from local communities.
He also voted to repeal the 2023 energy law and replace it with a modern energy plan focused on improving the security, reliability and affordability of Michigan’s energy supply.
These bills passed the House but have not received a vote in the Democrat-controlled State Senate.
What Matt Hall is Working to do
Restore Local Control Over Energy Projects
Matt Hall believes local communities—not Lansing bureaucrats—should decide whether large-scale wind, solar and energy storage projects are built in their communities.
State officials should not be able to override the decisions of local governments and residents who understand the needs of their communities on wind, solar, and energy storage projects.
Prioritize Affordable, Reliable and Secure Energy
Michigan’s current wind and solar mandates are driving up the cost of your energy bill.
Rates have already increased by $1 billion since the 2023 law was enacted.
Matt Hall believes Michigan should repeal the wind and solar mandates before they drive household energy costs even higher and direct regulators to prioritize affordable, reliable and secure energy production.
This means building more clean natural gas plants and investing more in modern nuclear energy technology.
Force Utilities to Lower Rates by $1 Billion
Matt Hall has proposed a plan to force utilities to lower their rates by $1 billion, wiping out the last three years of rate increases and freezing rates for an additional two years.
His plan would save the average Michigan family $250 per year on their energy bill.
Michigan families deserve energy that is affordable, reliable and secure.
Matt Hall will continue working to lower energy costs, restore local control and hold utility companies accountable.
