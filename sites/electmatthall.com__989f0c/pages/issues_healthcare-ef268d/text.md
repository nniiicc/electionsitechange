What Matt Hall Has Accomplished Lowering Prescription Drug Costs Matt Hall helped lower prescription drug costs by ending practices middlemen used to gouge patients , including billing a patient more for a prescription than it was sold for.
Protected Federal Medicaid Funding Medicaid is intended to provide a critical health care lifeline for low-income people, pregnant women, people with disabilities and those living with long-term illnesses.
Matt Hall worked across the aisle to protect 100% of Michigan’s federal Medicaid funding , while identifying $479 million in efficiencies to improve the program’s long-term sustainability.
He also ensured that Michigan is implementing work, education and volunteer requirements for able-bodied adults receiving Medicaid.
Young adults who are able to work, pursue education or volunteer should be encouraged to engage in these opportunities and take steps toward greater independence while receiving taxpayer-funded health care.
Kept 8,000 Doctors Practicing Across Michigan Matt Hall brokered a bipartisan agreement to renew Michigan’s participation in the medical licensure compact, protecting the ability of 8,000 doctors to continue practicing in Michigan.
The agreement makes it easier for Michigan to attract doctors and allows physicians licensed in other states with comparable licensing standards to practice in Michigan without going through years of duplicative state licensing requirements.
Without Matt Hall’s bipartisan agreement, an estimated 100,000 medical appointments would have been disrupted across Michigan each day.
Investing in Rural Hospitals Matt Hall secured $47.4 million directly for rural hospitals across Michigan.
This funding has been a lifeline for rural hospitals, helping ensure they remain open to provide quality care and emergency services to rural communities.
What Matt Hall is Working to Do Health care costs in Michigan continue to rise at an unsustainable rate.
Matt Hall believes Michigan must do more to make healthcare affordable, increase access to care, and protect families from unfair and predatory practices.
Create a Health Care Cost Review Board Matt Hall has introduced legislation to create an independent Healthcare Cost Review Board to lower Michigan’s rising health care costs.
The board would: Require healthcare systems to reduce rates, lowering insurance premiums and out-of-pocket costs.
Break up healthcare monopolies that reduce competition and drive-up prices Stop large hospital systems from requiring doctors to sign non-compete agreements that limit access to care and increase hospital profits.
End Facility Fees and Require Site-Neutral Pricing All over Michigan, big hospital systems are buying up local independent doctors’ offices, raising prices, and then charging patients a surprise “facility fee ” every time they visit their own doctor.
Matt Hall is working to put an end to these predatory fees and protect patients from unexpected charges.
As big hospital systems acquire more independent doctors’ practices, some are charging different prices for the same services depending on which office performs the service.
Matt Hall is working to require site-neutral pricing , meaning healthcare systems would charge the same price for the same medical service regardless of which location in the system provides that service.
Help Michigan Families Facing Medical Debt If you or a loved one experiences an unexpected medical emergency, you should not lose your home or have your credit ruined.
Too many Michigan families facing unexpected medical bills can find themselves buried under tens of thousands of dollars in medical debt.
Big hospital systems will hire debt collectors to garnish wages without a court order, place liens on homes, pursue foreclosure, and take other aggressive actions that can damage a family’s financial future.
Matt Hall is working across the aisle to stop these predatory practices.
Michigan should prohibit aggressive and predatory collection practices for medical debt and require hospitals to offer reasonable payment plans and more flexible timelines for families facing financial hardship.
Make Medical Prices Transparent Matt Hall is working to require clear, understandable price transparency for medical procedures, medications and healthcare services.
You deserve to see and understand the potential costs before receiving medical care.
Healthcare providers that fail to provide the required transparency should face meaningful consequences, including being unable to collect any medical debt.
