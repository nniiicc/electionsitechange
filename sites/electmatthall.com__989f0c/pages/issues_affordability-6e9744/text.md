Michigan families are facing higher costs for the things they need every day.
Matt Hall is working to lower those costs, help families afford homes and childcare, and connect more Michiganders to good-paying jobs.
What Matt Hall Has Accomplished
Fixing Roads and Saving Drivers Money
Michigan drivers spend an estimated $750 a year on car repairs caused by bad roads.
When Matt Hall first ran for office, he promised to make sure the money drivers pay at the pump went toward the roads they drive on.
Matt Hall kept working to make that promise a reality.
He negotiated a bipartisan agreement with Governor Gretchen Whitmer that puts all state taxes collected at the pump toward roads and provides $2 billion more annually for state and local roads—the largest road repair funding deal in Michigan history.
This historic investment will fix roads and bridges across Michigan, reduce wear and tear on vehicles, and help drivers keep more of their hard-earned money.
The deal will also create 22,000 jobs across the state.
Matt Hall brought Republicans and Democrats together, solved a problem that politicians failed to resolve for decades, and delivered on a promise he made to Michigan voters.
Giving Working Families a Fair Shot at Homeownership
Too many Michigan families are being squeezed by the rising cost of buying a home.
Large corporations, Wall Street investment firms and private equity funds have been buying up single-family homes, making it harder for Michigan families to compete for homes in our own communities.
Matt Hall negotiated a bipartisan agreement to put Michigan families first.
The new law stops large institutional investors from buying single-family homes, helping level the playing field for Michigan families.
It also creates a new tax credit to help build more homes that working families can afford, while cutting red tape and unnecessary regulations that drive up costs.
Matt Hall is working to protect homeownership, lower the cost of housing and give Michigan families a fair shot at owning a home.
Saving Families Thousands on Pre-K
The early years of a child’s education can make a lasting difference.
Access to quality Pre-K helps children build the early reading, math and social skills they need when they enter kindergarten.
Matt Hall supported making Michigan’s free Pre-K program available to all 4-year-olds, saving Michigan families an estimated $14,000 a year in childcare costs.
Free Pre-K gives Michigan children a better start in school while helping working families keep more of their hard-earned money.
Keeping Parents Working with Affordable Childcare
The high cost of reliable childcare makes it difficult for many parents to continue working.
Matt Hall believes parents should not have to quit their jobs because quality, affordable healthcare is out or reach, which is why he helped create a program to lower the cost for working families.
Matt Hall supported creating the MI Tri-Share program and making it permanent in law.
MI Tri-Share helps working parents by splitting their child-care costs equally among the employee, employer and State of Michigan, reducing the employee’s child-care costs by 67%.
Today, more than 300 employers participate in MI Tri-Share, and working families have saved more than $14 million in childcare costs.
Connecting Michiganders to Good-Paying Jobs
Michigan needs skilled workers, and too many adults who never earned a college degree face barriers to getting the training they need for a good-paying career.
Michigan Reconnect gives these adults the opportunity to earn a tuition-free associate degree or skilled-trades certificate.
Matt Hall helped create Michigan Reconnect and supported expanding the program to adults 21 and older, giving more Michiganders the opportunity to build a career and improve their earning potential.
Nearly 20,000 Michiganders have graduated with a degree or skills certificate through Michigan Reconnect.
What Matt Hall is Working to Do
Stop Price Gouging on Gas and Groceries
Families should pay the same price for the same product—not a higher price because a company has collected information about them.
New technology allows companies to track what you buy, where you shop, and what is happening in your life, then use the information to charge you a higher price.
Matt Hall supports banning surveillance pricing so companies cannot use your personal information to take advantage of you when you’re in a situation where you have little choice but to buy.
A company should not be able to see that you're in a bind and then charge you more for gas, groceries or other goods and services simply because it knows you have fewer options.
Michigan families deserve fair prices—not prices based on how much a company thinks it can get away with charging them.
Stop Insurance Companies from Charging You More for Being Loyal
Michigan drivers should not be punished for being loyal customers.
Some insurance companies use sophisticated algorithms and personal data to identify customers who are less likely to shop around and then use that information to charge higher premiums.
Matt Hall supports banning price optimization by auto insurance companies because using a customer's loyalty to charge them more is another form of price gouging.
Insurance companies should compete for your business with competitive rates—not use your personal information to figure out how much more they can get away with charging you.
Cut Red Tape and Lower Costs
Government regulations should not make everyday life more expensive for Michigan families.
Matt Hall has helped advance more than 50 bills through his Red Tape Reduction Initiative to eliminate unnecessary regulations that increase costs for working and middle-class families.
Housing is a clear example.
Nearly $94,000 of the cost of a new home is caused by regulations, building codes, land-use rules, fees and other government requirements.
These costs get passed directly on to families through higher home prices.
Matt Hall is working to cut the red tape that drives up the cost of living and make Michigan more affordable for working families.
