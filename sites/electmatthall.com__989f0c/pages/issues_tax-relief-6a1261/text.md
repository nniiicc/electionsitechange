He has worked across the aisle to lower taxes on working families and seniors while pursuing broader property tax relief.
What Matt Hall Has Accomplished No Tax on Tips, Overtime and Social Security Matt Hall negotiated a bipartisan deal to eliminate state taxes on tips, overtime and Social Security.
This builds on the relief Michigan workers and seniors will receive from the federal Working Families Tax Cut.
Under the new law: 300,000 Michigan tipped workers will save $1,500.
500,000 Michigan overtime workers will save $2,000.
Michigan seniors will save $500.
That means more of your hard-earned money stays in your pocket.
What Matt Hall is Working to Do Michigan families still need more relief.
That is why Matt Hall proposed the largest bipartisan tax reform in Michigan’s history.
Real Property Tax Relief Matt Hall proposed eliminating the 6-mil State Property Tax, the personal property tax and the real estate transfer tax, saving the average Michigan homeowner about $1,000 a year.
End the Pop-Up Tax Matt Hall also proposed ending the pop-up tax , which causes property taxes to uncap when a family sells their home causing the next family to pay significantly higher property taxes.
Because of the pop-up tax, seniors cannot afford to downsize into a smaller home, and many families cannot afford to move into a new home because the taxes will be significantly higher —even for a comparable home in the same neighborhood.
Protect Funding for Essential Services Matt Hall is working to secure a bipartisan compromise that provides real property tax relief while ensuring the state continues to fully invest in education, local communities and public safety.
