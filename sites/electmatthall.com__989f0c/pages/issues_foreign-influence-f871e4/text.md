The Chinese Communist Party should not benefit from your tax dollars, own Michigan farmland, access your healthcare information or influence Michigan’s public education system.
Matt Hall is working to ensure Michigan’s resources and institutions remain in the hands of Michiganders—not foreign adversaries.
What Matt Hall Is Working to Do
Protect Michigan Tax Dollars
Matt Hall has a plan to prohibit China and other countries of concern from receiving state grants and other taxpayer-funded benefits.
Protect Michigan Farmland
Matt Hall is working to prevent China and other countries of concern from purchasing Michigan farmland.
Protect Sensitive Health Information
Matt Hall is working to prevent adversarial foreign governments and entities from storing sensitive medical records.
Protect Michigan Educational Institutions
Matt Hall is working to protect Michigan’s public schools and universities by prohibiting them from accepting gifts or entering into agreements with adversarial foreign governments or entities that could influence, direct or control educational programs, curriculum or content.
