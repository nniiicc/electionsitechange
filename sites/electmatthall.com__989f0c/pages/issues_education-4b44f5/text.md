Matt Hall delivered for our students, parents, and teachers with a bipartisan education budget that makes a record $19.8 billion investment in Michigan schools.
What Matt Hall Has Accomplished
Free School Meals
Matt Hall delivered the Hall Universal Breakfast and Lunch in Schools Program, providing free breakfast and lunch to all Michigan students and saving families around $1,000 per child each year.
Record Funding for Local Classrooms
Matt Hall made sure local schools receive the resources they need with record per-pupil funding of $10,300 per student.
This support gives local schools more resources to invest in updated textbooks, classroom technology, smaller class sizes and the tools teachers need to help students succeed.
Teaching Kids to Read
Matt Hall has made teaching kids to read a priority.
He negotiated a bipartisan agreement that brings phonics back to the forefront of reading instruction, strengthens reading standards, and expands literacy coaches and high-impact tutoring to give students more help learning to read.
No Cell Phones in The Classroom
Matt Hall also supported legislation to keep cell phones out of classrooms.
The goal is simple: students should be paying attention to their teachers instead of their phones.
The new law requires students to put away their cell phones during class, except in emergencies.
Bonuses for Teachers
Matt Hall advocated for and secured $150 million for bonuses for teachers across Michigan.
The bonuses are a way to thank teachers for the hard work they do every day to help Michigan kids learn, grow and succeed.
School Safety and Mental Health Funding Restored
Matt Hall restored the full $325 million in school safety and mental health grants after the funding had previously been cut.
The funding helps local schools make their buildings safer, hire school resource officers and provide students with critical mental health resources.
What Matt Hall is Working to Do
Finish the Job on Reading
The work does not stop with increased funding and new programs.
Michigan must make sure those investments actually translate into better reading outcomes for students.
Matt Hall believes Michigan should finish the job by pairing investments in the science of reading with strong accountability, making sure students are reading at grade level and parents have clear, honest information about how their schools are performing.
Prepare Students for Life After High School
Matt Hall believes education should prepare students for more than the next test.
His Pathways to Success plan would give students more flexibility to pursue courses that match their talents and future plans, including career and technical education, skilled trades, dual enrollment, construction mathematics, accounting, computer science and coding.
The plan also emphasizes personal finance, giving students practical skills they can use throughout their lives.
Build on the School Safety Investment
Matt Hall is building on the restoration of school safety and mental health funding to make Michigan schools safer and better prepared.
His plan would require schools to keep their safety plans up to date, provide better emergency training for teachers and staff, conduct regular safety drills, and improve coordination between schools and local law enforcement.
It would also strengthen OK2SAY, giving students and families a trusted way to report threats and concerns.
These steps will help schools respond quickly and keep students and staff safe.
