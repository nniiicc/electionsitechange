Volunteer fire companies, EMS agencies, and rescue squads play a vital role in keeping Pennsylvania communities safe.
These non-profit organizations depend on fundraising to maintain equipment, train personnel, and continue providing lifesaving services.
Allowing online raffles under Pennsylvania’s Small Games of Chance Act would give these organizations a modern, convenient way to raise critical funds and connect with supporters.
As a member of the Pennsylvania State House of Representatives, I will prioritize a commonsense amendment to modernize charitable fundraising and strengthen the emergency services that our communities rely on every day.
-Missy Barnes, Candidate for PA House District 93
