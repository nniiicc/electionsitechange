Penn Ag Democrats was founded more than #ago to foster discussions between the political party, which is primarily associated with urban Pennsylvania, and rural residents.
The annual picnic is held in conjunction with Penn State's Ag Progress Days, Pennsylvania's largest outdoor agricultural exposition.
This three-day event in August features over 400 exhibitors from the United States and Canada, offering something for everyone to enjoy with free admission and parking.
The show is hosted by Penn State's College of Agricultural Sciences and highlights educational programs, current research, and the latest innovations in agricultural equipment and technology.
The cost is $30.00.
