Sydney Batch is a trailblazer, a fighter for families, and a proven leader in the North Carolina Senate.
A family law attorney, child welfare advocate, and social worker, Sydney has spent over two decades helping families through their most challenging moments.
Her deep commitment to justice and opportunity led her to public service—first as a member of the North Carolina House of Representatives, and now as the Leader of the Senate Democratic Caucus, making history as the first woman and first Black woman to hold the role.
