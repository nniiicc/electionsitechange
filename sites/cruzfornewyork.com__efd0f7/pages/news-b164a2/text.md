- AM Cruz Pushes for Right to Counsel and Legal Services for Immigrants “Everyone comes here looking for freedom,” said Cruz, a Queens Democrat, former immigration lawyer and the first Dreamer to be elected to the state Legislature.
“Whether you came five minutes ago, five years ago or 15 years ago, you are a New Yorker, and we are going to fight for you.”
In 2019, Cruz first introduced the Access to Representation Act, legislation intended to guarantee state funding covering legal services for every migrant statewide.
