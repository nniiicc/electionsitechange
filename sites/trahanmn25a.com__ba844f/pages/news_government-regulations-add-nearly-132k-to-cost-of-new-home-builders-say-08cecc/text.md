Friends - Government rules, fees, and delays now add about $130,000 to the cost of a new home.
That’s not just a number; it’s the difference between renting and owning for many families.
The average age of first-time homebuyers is now 35-40.
That is unacceptable.
A home is not just a purchase; it’s a long-term investment and the place where people build their lives, raise families, and create lasting memories.
Minnesota families are being priced out of homeownership.
Not because we can’t build homes, but because it takes too long and costs too much to get them approved.
If we want to make home-ownership attainable again for working families, seniors, and first-time buyers in District 25A, we must make it easier, not harder, to build safe, quality homes.
Let’s streamline permitting and reduce unnecessary barriers to housing.
We can do better.
Once elected, I am eager to tackle this never-ending, talked-about issue in St.
Paul.
We now need to put talk into action, and fix this long-standing issue!
Brad Trahan
Olmsted County GOP Endorsed Candidate - MN House District 25A
