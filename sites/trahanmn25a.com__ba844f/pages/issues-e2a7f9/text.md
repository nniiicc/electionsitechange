Public Safety - Safe Communities & Safe Neighborhoods
Every Minnesotan deserves to feel safe at home and in their community.
I am committed to strengthening public safety and supporting the dedicated men and women in law enforcement who work every day to protect us.
- I will prioritize accountability for violent offenders and ensure victims and their families are treated with fairness, respect, and support.
- By reinforcing our public safety laws and making sure police have the tools and resources they need, we can help build a safer Minnesota for today and for the future.
Stop the Fraud
Our state government should serve hardworking Minnesotans, not those who take advantage of the system.
I will be committed to strengthening accountability and restoring trust in how tax dollars are spent.
- We will work to stop waste, fraud, and abuse so that resources go where they are truly needed.
- It’s time for a government that is transparent, responsible, and worthy of the public’s trust.
- As your State Representative, I will call out any fraud committed by our state government.
Common Sense Leadership
In the most recent election, voters signaled a desire for balance in state government, resulting in a tied Minnesota House.
- With that tie, House Republicans helped elect a Republican Speaker and established the Fraud Prevention Committee to strengthen oversight and accountability in state spending.
- Efforts were also made to reduce the projected budget deficit by addressing excessive spending.
- I will focus on priorities that reflect many Minnesotans’ concerns, improving public safety, making the state more affordable, and restoring practical, common-sense leadership in St.
Paul.
Cost of Living - Affordability - Taxes
Minnesota is a great place to live, but for many families the cost of living has been rising faster than wages, making it harder to stay ahead.
Minnesotans deserve more control over their own paychecks, not more growth in government spending or unnecessary projects.
- I will support permanent, meaningful tax relief for families and small businesses, rather than short-term fixes that don’t last.
We should be working to make life more affordable for Minnesotans, and that starts with cutting taxes and easing the burden on families and small businesses.
- Minnesotan’s are extremely concerned about the cost of living.
Voters in Minnesota want to see tax cuts, not more tax increases!
- When people keep more of what they earn, they have more freedom to pay bills, save for the future, and invest back into their communities.
Responsible tax relief isn’t about government, it’s about trusting hardworking Minnesotans to make the best decisions for their own lives.
- Let’s focus on a tax system that is fair, simple, and built to help people succeed.
