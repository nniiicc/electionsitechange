Brad Trahan vs Kim Hicks - League of Women Voters & Rochester Area Chamber of Commerce Debate Forum
About this event
Brad Trahan (GOP) debate forum vs Opponent Kim Hicks (DFL)
Thank you for filing and for committing the time, effort, and dedication required to run for office.
To educate our local voters, the LWV-Rochester--in conjunction with the Rochester Post-Bulletin, the Rochester Area Chamber of Commerce, and the Rochester Public Library--will conduct Candidate Forums for local, nonpartisan contested positions and for State Senate Seats Districts 20, 24 & 25 along with House Seats 20B, 24A & 24B, 25A & 25B.
Candidate Forums are free and open to the public via in-person attendance and livestream.
Livestream links will be available on both the library's and LWV Rochester's websites.
Media outlets may be present.
Forums will be recorded, making the events available for viewing afterwards.
Recording will be available on both the library's YouTube page and LWV Rochester's website.
You may direct followers to these sites.
