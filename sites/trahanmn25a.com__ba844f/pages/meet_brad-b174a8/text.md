Meet Brad Issues News Volunteer Contribute Meet Brad For more than four decades, Rochester has been my home.
It is where my wife Joanie and I raised our family with Brenden, Reece, and Payton, and where we built lifelong friendships, and where I have dedicated my career to serving people through workforce development, community leadership, disability advocacy, and volunteer service.
I am running for Minnesota House District 25A, as a Republican, because I believe our district deserves practical leadership focused on people, not politics.
Throughout my life, I have worked alongside families, workers, veterans, business leaders, first responders, caregivers, and individuals with disabilities.
As the father of a son with severe autism, I understand firsthand the challenges many Minnesota families face navigating healthcare systems, disability services, rising costs, and uncertainty about the future.
My priorities will focus on: Affordable Living Safe Communities Competent, Accountable Government Thank you for your support and confidence in our campaign.
Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more affordable future for District 25A and for all Minnesotans.
