AARM Disability Support - Round Table
This event is an opportunity to meet with constituents and local leaders from the disability services field to learn about the current landscape of disability supports, the challenges facing providers and families, answer questions you may have, and talk about how you—as a policymaker—can help ensure people with disabilities have access to quality, stable services in the years ahead.
Why Your Presence Matters The disability services sector supports tens of thousands of Minnesotans with disabilities and employs more than 50,000 workers.
Much of the funding for these services comes from Medicaid and is subject to complex and evolving federal and state policy decisions.
Today, our industry is navigating: • A historic and ongoing workforce crisis, driven by demographics, wages, and competition across sectors • Rising costs and a lack of sustainable funding to meet the needs of people supported and the professionals who care for them • Increased expectations for person-centered, community-integrated services—rightly driven by the disability community itself, but often under-supported by a lack of sufficient resources • Policies aimed at combatting Medicaid fraud with the unintended consequence of hurting and, at times, forcing out of business long-standing, legitimate providers.
Thank you for your service and for considering this opportunity to engage with the disability community in your district.
