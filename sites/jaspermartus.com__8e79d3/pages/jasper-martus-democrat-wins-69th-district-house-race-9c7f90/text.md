The 69th District covers northwest Genesee County and includes 45 precincts.
Final tally is: Martus 56.31% Couch 41.04% Childress 2.66 Martus will be one of the youngest members of the state house at age 22.
He is a graduate of Powers Catholic High School and Michigan State University’s James Madison College.
Martus has worked for Democrats at all levels of government including US Congressman Dan Kildee.
Most recently he was a communications advisor in Lansing for the Michigan House Democrats.
“The votes are in and we are headed to Lansing!
Even on the hardest days of the campaign, we stayed focused and driven to deliver a win for our community.
Thank you for your support, prayers, and words of encouragement at every step of the way.
Now the real work begins,” said Martus.
