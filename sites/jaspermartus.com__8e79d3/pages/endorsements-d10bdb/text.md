The Genesee County Young Democrats are proud to endorse Jasper Martus for State Representative in the new 69th House District.
We believe Jasper’s platform focusing on economic development, educational growth and rebuilding our infrastructure is critical for the future of Genesee County.
As Young Democrats, ensuring that our community is a viable place to live and start a family is paramount, and we believe Jasper will be a strong voice for us in Lansing.
We strongly encourage voters in the area to join us in supporting Jasper Martus for State Representative!
