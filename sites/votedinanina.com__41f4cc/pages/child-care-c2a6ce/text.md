The cost of child care is out of control.
Families should never have to choose between earning a paycheck and making sure their children are safe and supported during the workday.
I know how essential child care is for families and our economy, and as a member of the State Assembly I will fight for universal childcare that is safe, affordable, and accessible for everyone.
I will fully fund Child Care Counts so that child care centers can stay open and incentivize Child Care Cooperatives to provide more affordable options for working families .
In the legislature, I will work to pass paid family and parental leave for all workers in Wisconsin.
I will also invest in grants that help providers expand into child care deserts, especially in rural and low-income communities where families often have no options at all.
When we take care of our kids, everyone does better.
