Fully Fund Public Education
- Fully fund public schools so all of our kids can thrive and school districts do not have to rely on referendums.
- End the state funded voucher program and stop private interests from siphoning money away from our public school classrooms.
- Raise special education reimbursement rates to at least ninety percent.
- Give teachers the raise they deserve and ensure they have the tools they need to be successful.
- Continue the fight for Free Healthy School Meals for All so no child goes to school hungry and can focus on learning.
- Offer free community college to all Wisconsin residents and expand opportunities for technical and vocational training.
- Prioritize and fund student mental health services.
Wisconsin has a constitutional obligation to fund public education, and it’s time we finally meet it.
Our kids deserve schools that are fully funded without the need for referendums just to keep the lights on.
Public dollars should stay in public schools which are required to serve every student and can be held accountable.
That means ending the state-funded voucher program and stopping private interests from siphoning resources out of classrooms.
Our teachers deserve competitive pay for the heroic work they do, and students deserve to be staffed by educators who are valued and supported.
We must also raise the special education reimbursement rate to at least 90% so districts can support students with disabilities without cutting other essential programs.
No child should go to school hungry, which is why I will continue the fight for Free Healthy School Meals for All.
We must also prioritize funding student mental health services so young people can learn in a safe and supportive environment.
Finally, education doesn't stop at high school.
We will make community college tuition-free for all Wisconsin residents and expand technical and vocational training so every student has a pathway to good-paying jobs and a great future.
When we invest in public education, we invest in Wisconsin’s future, and as your State Representative I will invest in our future first.
