I’m a food service worker and small business owner who made history as the first transgender woman elected to the Madison Common Council.

I don't have the typical background of someone who runs for the State Assembly.
I've worked for $2.33 an hour plus tips for most of my career in the service industry.
I’ve experienced homelessness and gone without healthcare for much of my life.
And as a transgender woman in America today, I know what it feels like to see your rights and freedoms stripped away by an authoritarian government.
And I know that I’m not alone.
Every day, our neighbors face those same struggles.
The cost of living is out of control, our democracy is under attack, and people are being priced out of the neighborhoods they love.
District 76 deserves a legislator who will fight like hell for the bold, progressive change we need.
That's why I’m running for the Wisconsin State Assembly.
As an alder, I've delivered for our communities – and my record proves it.
I removed barriers to accessing housing by eliminating credit score and carceral history from subsidized housing applications.
I expanded mental health services and the CARES program, secured historic investments in affordable housing, stood up for organized labor and disability rights, and reformed zoning codes to build more homes in Madison.
Now I'm ready to take that fight to the state.
In the Assembly, I'll fight for a living wage tied to inflation, BadgerCare for All so every Wisconsinite has quality healthcare coverage, fully funded public schools that meet the needs of every kid, truly affordable housing so families aren't priced out of their communities, and our democracy – because none of this matters if we don't protect our basic rights and freedoms from an authoritarian federal government.
The 76th District has a long history of progressive leadership.
We stand up for our neighbors and help those in need.
I'm running to bring those same values to the State Capitol and build a government that works for working people – not billionaires.
Will you join me?
