Everyone deserves a safe and stable place to call home.
But Wisconsin is facing a housing crisis, and our government needs to act with urgency to ensure that people can actually afford to live and stay here.
As an alder, I have seen how cities are restricted by the state from providing truly affordable housing to their residents.
We must begin with restoring local control and allowing municipalities to build the housing that best meets the needs of their communities.
We must increase and diversify our housing supply to drive down costs and meet demand while protecting tenants and renters from predatory landlords.
In the legislature, I will increase funding for WHEDA so we can support more workforce and affordable housing projects and champion new programs that help first-time homebuyers overcome the barriers that make ownership feel out of reach.
Our state also needs to step up its commitment to caring for our unhoused neighbors , joining municipalities in investing in services, shelters, and transitional housing programs.
As someone who has experienced homelessness, I will never forget what that uncertainty feels like.
I will fight every single day to make sure all our neighbors have the stability and dignity that they deserve.
