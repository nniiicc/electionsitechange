Strengthen Local Food Systems
- Support family farms & market farmers in Wisconsin.
- Enforce antitrust laws against large agribusinesses.
- Protect access to nutrition programs in the face of federal attacks.
- Expand Buy Local, Buy Wisconsin programs.
- Invest in climate resilience for farms across the state.
- Continue the fight for Free Healthy School Meals for All, partnering with local farmers and food providers to ensure no child goes to school hungry.
- Eliminate food deserts by supporting local grocers, food producers, and community gardens.
Wisconsin would not be what it is today without the hard work of our farmers.
But Republican lawmakers have turned their back on our farmers and agricultural workers.
In the Assembly, I will expand Buy Local Buy Wisconsin programs so farmers can reach more customers and keep local food dollars in our communities.
I will invest in climate resilience projects to help farms adapt to extreme weather, protect soil and water, and remain productive for generations to come.
We must also enforce antitrust laws to prevent large agribusinesses from squeezing out family farms and eliminate food deserts so that all of our kids and families have access to high-quality, nutritious food.
This isn’t something we can afford to get wrong.
As your State Representative, I will make it a priority to combat hunger, strengthen our farms, and keep Wisconsinites fed.
