Advance Labor Rights & Dignity for All Workers
- Repeal Act 10 and fully restore protections for public sector workers.
- Expand apprenticeships and workforce training programs.
- Raise the minimum wage to at least $20 an hour and tie it to inflation.
- Strengthen workplace safety standards.
- Crack down on wage theft and the exploitation of workers.
- Pass legislation to protect Wisconsin workers from the weakening of labor standards by the federal government.
- Prohibit employment discrimination against formerly incarcerated individuals.
- Fight tirelessly for unions and collective bargaining rights.
Our workers move our state forward.
As a former union member and someone who has worked in the food service industry for most of my career, I know firsthand how life-changing it can be to have a union fighting for your rights in the workplace.
In the State Assembly, I will proudly fight to repeal Act 10 and restore collective bargaining rights to our public sector workers.
I will also expand apprenticeships and workforce training programs to ensure people can build careers with good pay and growth opportunities.
I will fight to strengthen workplace safety standards that will protect every worker from harm and create a culture where safety and dignity on the job are non-negotiable.
As an alder, I have always stood with our local unions and fought to ensure that working people have a seat at the table.
I look forward to doing the same as a member of the State Assembly.
