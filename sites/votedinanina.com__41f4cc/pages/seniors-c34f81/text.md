Care for Wisconsin’s Seniors
- Secure greater funding for senior care facilities and invest in affordable and low-cost housing projects that meet the needs of our aging populations.
- Cap the cost of prescription drugs and expand access to generic alternatives.
- Expand coverage for hearing aids, vision, and dental insurance under BadgerCare.
- Work to protect seniors from elder abuse and financial exploitation.
- Fully fund tax assistance and in-home care programs for those who wish to age in place to ensure all people can retire with dignity.
- Lower housing costs across the board to prevent our family members and neighbors living on a fixed income from being priced out of their homes.
Our seniors built Wisconsin.
They deserve to retire with dignity, security, and the freedom to live where and how they choose.
Right now, our family members are being forced to choose between their medications and their groceries.
This is unacceptable.
I will work to cap prescription drug costs and expand access to more affordable generic alternatives, because no one should have to ration life-saving medication just to make ends meet.
I will also fight to expand BadgerCare coverage to include hearing aids, vision care, and dental services, as well as fully fund property tax assistance programs and in-home care to allow those who wish to age in place to do so.
Supporting our seniors should be a priority for the state – not an afterthought.
