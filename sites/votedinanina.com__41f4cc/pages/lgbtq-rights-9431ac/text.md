In 2023, I became the first transgender woman elected to the Madison Common Council.
Representation matters, but it’s not enough just to have a seat at the table.
We need bold, transformative change to protect our rights, safeguard our freedoms, and fight back against fascism in all its forms.
In the State Assembly, I will be a strong advocate for expanding Wisconsin’s non-discrimination laws to include both gender identity and expression , securing protections for gender-affirming care, enshrining a conversion therapy ban into state law, and classifying violence against transgender and gender non-conforming people as a hate crime.
I would also fight for a Transgender Bill of Rights in Wisconsin that includes access to healthcare, housing, public accommodations, and more.
As an alder, I was proud to introduce a Transgender Sanctuary Resolution in our city and will work to do the same at the state level.
All issues are LGBTQ+ issues.
Lifting people out of poverty, making housing more affordable, guaranteeing healthcare as a human right, addressing the epidemic of gun violence, raising the minimum wage, fully funding our public schools, and so much more are all intertwined with queer and trans liberation.
When elected, I will use my position not only to fight back against anti-LGBTQ+ bills in the legislature, but to finally advance a positive, proactive agenda for full equality in our state.
