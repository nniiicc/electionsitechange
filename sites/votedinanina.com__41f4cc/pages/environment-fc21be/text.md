Combat Climate Change & Protect Our Environment
- Invest in clean energy projects across the state to accelerate our transition to 100% renewable energy and create good-paying, union jobs in the process.
- Crack down on corporate polluters and secure funding for PFAS research, remediation, and clean up.
- Improve access to green spaces and protect our public parks and lands from privatization.
- Fully restore funding to the Knowles Nelson Stewardship Program.
- Allow cities to expand public transit options to reduce congestion and emissions.
- Support state service commissions, including Serve Wisconsin, and establish a Wisconsin Climate Corps that funds climate resilience and mitigation jobs.
- Ensure we have commonsense regulations on data centers to safeguard our natural resources, protect local jobs, and prevent corporations from passing off costs to working families.
We are running out of time to tackle the climate crisis.
In the Assembly, I will accelerate our transition to 100% renewable energy and make real our commitment to a carbon-free future.
Fighting climate change means creating good-paying, union jobs in resilience, mitigation, and the clean energy economy.
These can and must go hand-in-hand.
Wisconsin is blessed to have beautiful natural resources.
Our state government should be invested in protecting them – for Wisconsinites today and for generations after us.
We must restore funding to the Knowles Nelson Stewardship Program to preserve our natural areas and wildlife habitat, protect water quality and fisheries, and expand opportunities for outdoor recreation.
As your State Representative, I will also crack down on corporate polluters and protect our public lands from privatization.
All Wisconsinites deserve clean air, clean water, and a livable climate.
These are human rights.
Lastly, we need transparency and commonsense regulations on AI data centers before they cause irreparable harm.
As an alder on the Madison Common Council, I co-sponsored first-in-the-state legislation to impose a one-year moratorium on the construction of large-scale data centers so that we can review the impacts on our natural resources and public utilities.
As a legislator, I’ll fight to ensure that Wisconsinites are not subsidizing energy costs for big corporations that need to pay their fair share.
Because the demands of corporate shareholders should never come before the health and wellbeing of our communities.
