Reimagine Criminal Justice & Public Safety
- Create a statewide mental health response program that diverts mental-health related emergencies from law enforcement, similar to the successful CARES program in Madison.
- Work to address the root causes of crime in our communities by combating hunger, housing insecurity, and unstable access to healthcare and employment.
- Legalize marijuana and expunge the records of those formerly convicted of possession.
- Repeal mandatory minimums and eliminate cash bail.
- Increase support for substance addiction recovery and rehabilitation programs.
- Establish and invest in programs that reduce recidivism and prepare incarcerated individuals for reintegration into society.
Our criminal justice system should not be about locking people up and hoping that it decreases crime.
It should be about care, rehabilitation, and addressing the root causes of violence.
In the Assembly, I will work to end mandatory minimums, eliminate cash bail, and invest in programs that actually reduce recidivism.
I will secure funding so local governments can institute programs like Madison’s CARES – which allows trained mental health professionals to respond to behavioral health-related calls instead of uniformed police officers.
I will also vote to finally legalize marijuana and expunge the records of those convicted of possession.
We need to better address mental health crises, substance abuse, hunger, and housing instability across Wisconsin.
Because when people's basic needs are met, it contributes to the safety of our neighborhoods.
As a legislator, I will work to ensure all of our communities have the resources they need to thrive.
