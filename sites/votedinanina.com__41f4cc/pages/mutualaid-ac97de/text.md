Now more than ever, we need to take care of each other.
September is Hunger Action Month!
Our campaign will be collecting non-perishable food items to help those who are struggling with hunger and food insecurity.
We’ll have donation bins all across District 76 where you can drop off items until September 30th.
Everything will be donated to the River Food Pantry and the Fritz Food Pantry at Goodman Community Center.
