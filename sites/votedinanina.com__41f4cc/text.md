ABOUT DINA NINA My name is Dina Nina Martinez-Rutherford.
I’m a food service worker, small business owner, and a proud transgender woman.
I am also a member of the Madison Common Council, representing neighborhoods on the Isthmus and East Side.
I don't have the typical background of someone who runs for the State Assembly.
I've gone without healthcare and worked for $2.33 an hour for most of my career.
I don't just see the challenges our neighbors face – I live them.
Our perspective is missing in the State Capitol, and it's exactly what I'll bring.
I'm running for the 76th Assembly District because I believe in a Wisconsin where we take care of each other.
I'll fight for the bold, progressive change we need: a living wage, truly affordable housing, universal healthcare and childcare, fully funded public schools, and a democracy that protects our rights and freedoms.

OUR FIGHT.
Politics as usual isn’t working.
No flashy slogans or empty promises are going to get us out of this mess.
We need leaders who will fight like hell for working people and deliver bold, progressive change to lower costs and protect our rights.
Read Dina's Full Platform Lower Costs to Make Life Affordable Too many people are working hard and still can’t get ahead.
The cost of living has risen faster than our wages, all while billionaires and big corporations rake in record profits.
This isn’t a coincidence – it’s by design.
We need bold, progressive change to unrig our economy and undo decades of harmful Republican policies.
That means raising wages, capping healthcare and utility costs, investing in affordable housing, and fixing our school funding formula so we can lower property taxes.
Healthcare as a Human Right For most of my life, I have gone without health insurance because I simply couldn't afford it.
Too many Wisconsinites know exactly what this feels like.
We need a BadgerCare public option so that every Wisconsinite can access high-quality, affordable care.
Healthcare should relieve stress, not add. to it.
I will fight every day to ensure that our state treats it as a human right.
Fully Fund Public Education Wisconsin has a constitutional obligation to fund public education, and it’s time we finally meet it.
Public dollars should stay in public schools which are required to serve every student and can be held accountable.
That means ending the state-funded voucher program and stopping private interests from siphoning resources out of classrooms.
Fight Fascism and Safeguard Our Democracy Our democracy is being torn apart by Donald Trump and all who enable him.
We cannot continue to normalize a federal government that is attacking our neighbors, our rights, and our basic institutions.
In the Assembly, I’ll continue to be a strong voice against authoritarianism and fight to restore Wisconsin as a beacon of our democracy.
