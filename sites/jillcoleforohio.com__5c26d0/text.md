MEET JILL COLE
Jill Cole serves as Mayor of Blue Ash and has spent much of her life serving her community.
She is a U.S.
Army veteran, small business owner, and longtime local leader.
In 2006, Jill and her husband, Steve, founded Cole Realty Associates, which they continue to own and operate today.
Nearly two decades in business have given Jill firsthand experience making tough decisions, managing budgets, and planning for the future.
Jill’s public service began on the Sycamore Community Schools Board of Education, where she served for eight years, including as president and vice president, before later serving the City of Blue Ash.
Along the way, Jill has earned a reputation as a practical, non-partisan problem solver who is willing to listen, work with people, and find common ground.
That pragmatic approach has guided her throughout her time in public service.
