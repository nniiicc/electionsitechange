Mission
My mission is to restore accountability in Washington and fight for the people of California’s 51st District.
I believe public office should be a responsibility, not a career, which is why I support congressional term limits and stronger anti-corruption measures.
I want to make life more affordable for working families, help small businesses grow, and remove unnecessary barriers that keep young entrepreneurs from getting access to capital.
Our government should serve the people with honesty, transparency, and results, not special interests.
I’m running for Congress to bring principled leadership, fresh energy, and real accountability back to government.
