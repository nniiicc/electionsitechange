Cabrera For Congress 2026
I’m Ricardo Cabrera, I am running for Congress in CA-51 to fight for term limits, affordable living, anti-corruption reform, and more opportunity for young entrepreneurs.
I believe government should serve the people, not political insiders.
It’s time for honest leadership that lowers costs, restores trust, and helps the next generation build and succeed.
Our Purpose
My purpose is to serve the people of California’s 51st District with honesty, courage, and accountability.
I am running to fight for congressional term limits, lower the cost of living, confront corruption, and create more opportunity for young entrepreneurs.
Public office should belong to the people, not political insiders.
