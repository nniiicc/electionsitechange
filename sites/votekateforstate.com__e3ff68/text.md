My Priorities
In Indiana, we look out for each other.
Our community values are shaped by the bonds we build and the support we give one another.

These shared conversations fuel my commitment to addressing the issues that matter most to us all.
- Affordable Housing That Families Can Actually Afford

- Strong Public Schools Our Kids Deserve
- Infrastructure That Actually Works
- Supporting Our Farmers and Rural Communities
Where is District 6?
Indiana’s 6th State Senate District is rooted in the hardworking, diverse counties of Lake, Newton, Jasper and Benton, communities built on a proud industrial heritage and strengthened by generations of working families.
