FOR TEXAS
MEET ODUS
A lifelong advocate for working families
Odus Evbagharu is running for the Texas House because he believes in building a future where every family has the chance to succeed.
As the son of immigrants, Odus grew up in the Cy-Fair community, attended Cy-Fair public schools, and graduated from the University of Houston.
Today, he is also a small business owner.
His life experience taught him when people get involved, they can shape the future they want for themselves and the next generation.
Odus has already made history in Harris County.
In 2021, he became the youngest and first Black leader elected Chair of the Harris County Democratic Party.
During his time as Chair, Odus led one of the most inclusive voter contact programs in county history.
His work helped deliver the first Democratic supermajority on the Harris County Commissioners Court since the 1970s.
That record shows his ability to organize, build coalitions, and win tough fights — skills he will bring to the Texas Legislature.
DELIVERING REAL RESULTS
Odus also knows how the state legislature works, serving as a Chief of Staff in House District 135.
In that role, he worked daily on issues that matter to Texas families: defending public schools, protecting women’s autonomy, and pushing for an economy working for everyone.
He’s partnered with progressive organizations and Democratic campaigns across the state, helping them grow their voices and make real change in their communities.
His work has made him a trusted leader who can navigate both grassroots activism and legislative policy.
Odus is willing to stand up to anyone – work with anyone — if it means delivering real results for the people he serves.
His campaign platform, called A Plan for US, centers on growth, opportunity, and practical progress.
He believes when working families thrive, Texas thrives.
Committed to Community
Education is at the heart of Odus’s vision.
He knows from personal experience schools can change lives.
He will fight to fully fund public schools, invest in technology and career pathways, and give teachers the respect and equitable pay they deserve.
As a small business owner, Odus understands the challenges of starting and growing a business.
He will work to cut red tape, expand access to capital, and help small business owners succeed.
He believes in an economy that pays workers a living wage, respects unions, and ensures safe and equitable workplaces.
Odus’s plan includes fighting for affordable healthcare by expanding Medicaid, protecting reproductive freedom so women have autonomy over their bodies, and ensuring every family has access to quality care.
He will work for safer neighborhoods, better roads and broadband, flood control projects, and strong investments in infrastructure that create good-paying jobs.
At the core of everything, Odus believes in equity ensuring working-class communities are never left behind.
His record proves he is a fighter who gets results, and now ready to bring that fight to Austin.
Odus Evbagharu is not just running to win a seat.
He is running to lift up the voices of his neighbors, protect working families, and build a Texas working for everyone.
