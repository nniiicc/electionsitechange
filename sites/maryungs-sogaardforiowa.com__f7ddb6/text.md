Why I'm running
I plan to bring a practical, common-sense focus to Des Moines.
My campaign is centered on three priorities that affect families across the district every day:
Health Care & Brain Health
Health care is too expensive and difficult to access.
People are waiting too long for mental and brain health services.
We can do better and make care more available when it’s needed most.
Iowa's Land, Water & Future
Our land and water need serious attention.
Protecting Iowa’s natural resources is essential to our health, economy and future.
We can do better and leave a stronger Iowa for the next generation.
