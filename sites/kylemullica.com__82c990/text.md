Learn More About Kyle On The Issues Kyle Mullica currently represents House District 34 in the Colorado House of Representatives, which encompasses Thornton and Northglenn- he will begin his term as State Senator for Senate District 24 in January of 2023.
During his two terms in the legislature, Kyle has passed bills that have focused on lowering the cost of health care and prescription drugs, investing in affordable housing, protecting our environment and water, finding ways to lower the cost of living, and promoting economic development.
He has also earned recognition as the House’s leader on Health Care with his real-world expertise as an ER Nurse, during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Kyle knows that the work isn’t done and that’s why he ran for the State Senate.
He is committed to providing a strong, effective voice at the Capitol, always focusing on putting his neighbors in Adams County’s interests first.
