Voice of the Voiceless Making a Difference Karen Spalding is devoted to the people of Kansas City.
That is why she has chosen to become involved in politics.
She would like to offer something that has been missing for years in her Missouri House District--the conservative choice and voice.
After years of experience working in education and her community, her goal is to become the "Voice of the Voiceless," bringing the true concerns of the people in Kansas City's District #19 to Jefferson City.
It's this intense dedication, passion, and devotion which fuels the desire for positive changes in the lives of families, seniors and young people alike.
Mrs.
Spalding also wants to advocate for businesses striving to succeed in this inflated economy.

Spalding and the opportunites you have to join her in acheiving those goals.
