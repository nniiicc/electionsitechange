Home About Positions Why I'm Running Endorsements Events Contact Contribute About Ted Nordblum A California Story: From Union Worker to Small Business Owner About Us A California Story: From Union Worker to Small Business Owner My Journey to Service My story isn't one of political ambition.
It's a story of hard work, family, and a deep love for the community I call home.
I was born in Buffalo, New York, into a working-class family where the values of self-reliance and determination were instilled in me from a young age.
We weren't given much, but we were taught that with hard work, anything was possible.
My journey brought me to California, where I started my career as a union heavy equipment operator.
I was proud to be part of the labor force that built our state, and I learned firsthand the importance of a good-paying job and the dignity of hard work.
But I also saw how government regulations and red tape could make it harder for working families to get by.
Building a Business, Creating Jobs Driven by an entrepreneurial spirit, I decided to take a risk and start my own business.
For over 20 years, I've been the owner of Titan Medical, a medical device company right here in our community.
We've provided good jobs, contributed to our local economy, and helped countless patients with innovative medical solutions.
Running a small business in California has taught me what it takes to balance a budget, navigate complex regulations, and make payroll every week.
It's a perspective that's sorely missing in Sacramento.
Join the Movement to Save California A Personal Fight for Our Future My decision to run for State Assembly wasn't just about politics; it was personal.
In November 2021, my family was shattered when I lost my brother to a fentanyl overdose.
It was a devastating loss that opened my eyes to the silent epidemic that is destroying families across our state.
I knew then that I couldn't sit on the sidelines any longer.
I had to fight for a California where our children are safe, our communities are strong, and our government is accountable.
