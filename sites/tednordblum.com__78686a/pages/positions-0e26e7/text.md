Home About Positions Why I'm Running Endorsements Events Contact Contribute My Positions Common-Sense Solutions for a Better California Positions Common-Sense Solutions for a Better California Introduction For too long, Sacramento has been dominated by one-party rule, leading to policies that have made our state unaffordable, unsafe, and unresponsive to the needs of working families.
I believe in a different approach—one that's based on common sense, fiscal responsibility, and a commitment to our local communities.
Here's where I stand on the issues that matter most to you.
Restoring Economic Opportunity Sacramento's tax-and-spend policies have made California one of the most expensive states in the nation to live and do business.
Working families are being squeezed out.
I'll fight to cut wasteful spending, lower the tax burden on individuals and job creators, and restore the kind of fiscal discipline that lets people keep more of what they earn.
Cut Taxes and Control Spending I will work to cut the gas tax, defend the taxpayer protections of Proposition 13, and demand a balanced state budget that doesn't rely on raising your taxes.
Government needs to live within its means, just like you do.
Support Small Businesses Sacramento's crushing regulations and sky-high taxes are forcing small businesses to close or leave the state.
I've built a business from the ground up.
I know what it takes.
I'll cut red tape and create an environment where small businesses can hire, grow, and thrive right here in California.
Develop Our Resources California sits on enormous natural resources, yet we import energy and pay some of the highest utility costs in the country.
I'll push to responsibly develop our energy and water resources, lower costs for families and businesses, and build the infrastructure our region needs.
Ensuring Safe Neighborhoods Public safety is the first and most important responsibility of government.
I'll work to ensure that our law enforcement officers have the resources they need to keep our communities safe and that criminals are held accountable for their actions.
Tackle the Fentanyl Crisis The fentanyl epidemic is a public health crisis that has devastated families across our state, including my own.
I'll fight for tougher penalties for fentanyl traffickers, increased funding for addiction treatment, and greater public awareness of the dangers of this deadly drug.
Support Law Enforcement I will always back the blue.
I'll work to ensure that our police and sheriff's departments have the funding, training, and support they need to protect our communities and keep our streets safe.
Hold Criminals Accountable I'll fight to repeal the soft-on-crime policies that have emboldened criminals and made our communities less safe.
We need to restore truth-in-sentencing and ensure that violent offenders serve their full terms.
