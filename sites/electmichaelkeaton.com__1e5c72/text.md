Inspired by America's 250th Anniversary "A s our nation reaches its 250th anniversary, I am reminded of the courage, sacrifice, and determination of those who built the greatest experiment in self-government the world has ever known.
The Semiquincentennial is more than a celebration of our history — it is an opportunity to reflect on the principles that have sustained our nation for two and a half centuries: freedom, personal responsibility, faith, and the belief that government exists to serve the people.
The story of America inspires me to keep fighting for those same values here in Washington State.
Just as previous generations worked to leave a stronger nation for their children, we have a responsibility to preserve opportunity, protect liberty, and ensure that future generations inherit a government that is accountable to the people.
As we commemorate 250 years of American independence, I remain committed to standing up for taxpayers, defending our constitutional freedoms, and working to build a brighter future for every family in our community.
The challenges we face today are different, but the spirit that built America continues to inspire us to meet them with courage, optimism, and determination." Fix Washington.
Save Washington.
Washington families are feeling the impact of rising costs, growing government spending, and policies that too often make life less affordable.
Michael is committed to advancing solutions that improve public safety, strengthen our economy, and make government more accountable to the people it serves.
Fix Washington highlights Republican-led solutions focused on addressing many of the challenges facing our state, including affordability, education, housing, public safety, transportation, and government accountability.
Save Washington is the Washington Senate Republican budget plan that demonstrates how the state can fund essential priorities, live within its means, and avoid new taxes and fee increases on Washington families.
Michael believes Washington can move forward through responsible budgeting, practical solutions, and policies that put working families first.
