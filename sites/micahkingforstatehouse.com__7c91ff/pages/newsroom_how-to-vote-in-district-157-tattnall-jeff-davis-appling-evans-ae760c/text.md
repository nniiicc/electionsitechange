How to Vote in Georgia House District 157: Tattnall, Jeff Davis, Appling & Evans County Guide (2026 General Election)
The November 3, 2026 General Election is one of the most consequential elections in recent history for Southeast Georgia.
Voters across Georgia House District 157, encompassing Tattnall County, Jeff Davis County, Appling County, and Evans County, will choose their representative for the Georgia State House of Representatives.
Whether you plan to vote early in-person, vote by absentee ballot, or head to your precinct on Election Day, this official guide provides all the key dates, polling resources, and candidate information you need to make your voice heard.
1.
Key Election Dates & Deadlines
Mark your calendar with these critical election deadlines for Georgia voters:
- Voter Registration Deadline: October 5, 2026
- Advance In-Person Early Voting Begins: October 13, 2026
- Mandatory Saturday Voting Days: October 17 & October 24, 2026
- Absentee Ballot Request Deadline: October 23, 2026
- Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (Polls open 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM)

4.
How to Check Your Registration Status & Sample Ballot
Before heading to the polls, verify your voter registration and view your personalized sample ballot online:
- Visit the official Georgia Secretary of State My Voter Page: mvp.sos.ga.gov
- Enter your First Initial, Last Name, County, and Date of Birth.
- Check your specific Election Day polling place location, view your sample ballot, and track absentee ballot requests.
5.
What to Bring to the Polls
Georgia law requires photo identification when voting in person.
Acceptable forms of ID include:
- Any valid state or federal government-issued photo ID (Georgia Driver’s License, GA Voter ID card)
- Valid U.S.
Passport
- Valid U.S.
Military photo ID
- Valid employee photo ID from any branch, department, or agency of the U.S.
Government, Georgia, or any county or city in GA.
