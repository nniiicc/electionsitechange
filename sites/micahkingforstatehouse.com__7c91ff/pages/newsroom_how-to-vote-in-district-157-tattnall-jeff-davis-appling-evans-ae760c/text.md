Why Your Vote for Micah King Matters in District 157 The November 3, 2026 General Election is one of the most consequential elections in recent history for Southeast Georgia.
Voters across Georgia House District 157 , encompassing Tattnall County, Jeff Davis County, Appling County, and Evans County , will choose their representative for the Georgia State House of Representatives.
Whether you plan to vote early in-person, vote by absentee ballot, or head to your precinct on Election Day, this official guide provides all the key dates, polling resources, and candidate information you need to make your voice heard.
1.
Key Election Dates & Deadlines Mark your calendar with these critical election deadlines for Georgia voters: Voter Registration Deadline: October 5, 2026 Advance In-Person Early Voting Begins: October 13, 2026 Mandatory Saturday Voting Days: October 17 & October 24, 2026 Absentee Ballot Request Deadline: October 23, 2026 Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (Polls open 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM) 2.
Who Is Running for Georgia House District 157?
In the November 3, 2026 General Election, voters in District 157 will choose between two candidates on the ballot: Micah King (Democrat): Local leader, community advocate, and veteran champion.
Focused on lowering the cost of living, protecting rural healthcare, supporting local farmers, and improving emergency infrastructure across District 157.
Bradley B.
Anderson (Republican): Businessman and former police chief/coroner from Reidsville, GA.

How to Check Your Registration Status & Sample Ballot Before heading to the polls, verify your voter registration and view your personalized sample ballot online: Visit the official Georgia Secretary of State My Voter Page : mvp.sos.ga.gov Enter your First Initial, Last Name, County, and Date of Birth.
Check your specific Election Day polling place location, view your sample ballot, and track absentee ballot requests.
5.
What to Bring to the Polls Georgia law requires photo identification when voting in person.
Acceptable forms of ID include: Any valid state or federal government-issued photo ID (Georgia Driver’s License, GA Voter ID card) Valid U.S.
Passport Valid U.S.
Military photo ID Valid employee photo ID from any branch, department, or agency of the U.S.
Government, Georgia, or any county or city in GA.
