Read the 2026 Candidate Guide View County Early Voting Hours & Locations Frequently Asked Questions About District 157 What counties are in Georgia House District 157?
Georgia House District 157 represents communities across Southeast Georgia, including Tattnall County, Jeff Davis County, Appling County, Evans County, and Candler County.
What cities and towns are in District 157?
Major communities include Glennville, Reidsville, Collins, Cobbtown, and Manassas in Tattnall County; Hazlehurst and Denton in Jeff Davis County; Baxley and Surrency in Appling County; Claxton, Hagan, Bellville, and Daisy in Evans County; and Metter and Pulaski in Candler County.
How do I check if my address is in District 157?
You can verify your exact state legislative districts by entering your voter registration information into the official Georgia Secretary of State My Voter Page (mvp.sos.ga.gov) or by downloading our official district map.
What does a Georgia State Representative do?
Members of the Georgia House of Representatives serve two-year terms in the Georgia General Assembly in Atlanta.
Representatives author, debate, and vote on the annual state budget, state tax policy, healthcare appropriations, infrastructure funding, and rural economic policies directly affecting local communities.
