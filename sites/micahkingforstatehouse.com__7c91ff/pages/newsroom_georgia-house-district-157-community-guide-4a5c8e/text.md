A County-by-County Breakdown July 1, 2026 5 min read By Micah King Table of Contents • Tattnall County: The Heart of the District • Jeff Davis County: Where Family Farms Still Thrive • Appling County: Southern Georgia’s Quiet Strength • Evans County: Deep Roots, Strong Community • Candler County: Small Town, Big Heart • The District as a Whole Georgia House District 157 is one of the most geographically sprawling and culturally rich districts in Southeast Georgia.
It stretches from the piney woods and farm roads of Jeff Davis County in the west, through the agricultural heart of Tattnall County, and east into Evans and Candler Counties.
These are communities that share deep rural roots, a strong faith tradition, and a belief that hard work still means something.
If you’ve ever wondered whether you live in District 157, or simply want to understand the community Micah King is fighting to represent, this breakdown is for you. 📥 Download the Official District 157 Map (PDF) Tattnall County: The Heart of the District Tattnall County is the geographic and political center of District 157.
Home to the county seat of Reidsville and the agricultural hub of Glennville , Tattnall has long been one of Georgia’s premier agricultural counties, famous statewide for its sweet Vidalia-style onions, timber production, and row crop farming.
The county also hosts Smith State Prison , one of Georgia’s largest correctional facilities.
That creates a complex relationship with local emergency services that Micah has spoken about directly: rural EMS units stretched thin, law enforcement resources strained, and local taxpayers absorbing costs that should be shouldered by the state.
Tattnall is a community where people know their neighbors, where Friday night football matters, and where a handshake still means something.
It deserves a representative who shows up in the off-years too, not just when there’s an election on the calendar.
Key communities: Reidsville, Glennville, Collins, Manassas, Cobbtown Jeff Davis County: Where Family Farms Still Thrive Jeff Davis County sits in the western portion of District 157, anchored by its county seat of Hazlehurst .
It is agricultural land through and through.
Timber, livestock, and row crops are the economic backbone of a county that has retained its rural identity despite pressure from larger regional economies.
Like Tattnall, Jeff Davis families feel the daily pressures of rising input costs, insurance premiums that keep climbing, and a healthcare system that requires long drives for routine care.
The county has strong ties to technical and vocational education, and Micah’s commitment to expanding workforce training is deeply relevant here.
Key communities: Hazlehurst, Denton, Lumber City (shared with Telfair County) Appling County: Southern Georgia’s Quiet Strength Appling County anchors the southern reach of District 157, with Baxley serving as its county seat.
Baxley is a working town.
It has the character of a community that built itself through timber, turpentine, and agriculture, and it has weathered the economic shifts of the last several decades without losing its identity.
Appling County families face similar challenges to the rest of the district: rural healthcare access, childcare affordability, and the need for economic development that doesn’t require leaving home to find opportunity.
The county’s school system and its connection to community institutions are a source of pride.
Key communities: Baxley, Graham, Surrency, Odum, Empire Evans County: Deep Roots, Strong Community Evans County is one of Georgia’s smaller counties by land area, but it punches above its weight in community pride.
The county seat of Claxton is known nationally for one thing: the Claxton Fruitcake , a holiday institution that has shipped millions of cakes across the country since the early 1900s.
Evans County is a community of farmers, veterans, teachers, and tradespeople who deserve representation that takes their concerns seriously.
The county has seen economic pressures similar to the rest of the district, and its residents are looking for leadership that puts people over politics.
Key communities: Claxton, Hagan, Daisy, Bellville Candler County: Small Town, Big Heart Candler County rounds out the northeastern reach of District 157.
With Metter as its county seat, Candler is a tightly-knit rural community with deep agricultural traditions and a strong sense of local identity.
Candler County has faced some of the same rural healthcare access challenges that define the district as a whole.
The drive to the nearest major medical facility is long enough that preventive care gets skipped and emergencies become crises.
Micah’s commitment to reviewing Georgia’s Certificate of Need laws and expanding telehealth options has direct relevance here.
Key communities: Metter, Cobbtown (shared with Tattnall), Pulaski The District as a Whole What unites these five counties is more than lines on a map.
It’s the sense of being overlooked by a state government that too often makes decisions in Atlanta without considering what those decisions mean when they land in Reidsville, Hazlehurst, Baxley, Claxton, or Metter.
The neighbors of District 157 don’t overlook each other; they take care of one another.
That’s the part that never shows up in the county data.
Micah King is running to be a representative who actually shows up: for town halls, for conversations, for votes that matter to the people who sent him there.
