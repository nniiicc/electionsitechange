Privacy Policy
Updated October 5, 2026
1.
Information We Collect
The Committee to Elect Micah King ("we," "us," or "our") collects information that you provide directly to us when you visit our website, sign up for our newsletter, make a donation, or contact us.
This may include your name, email address, physical address, and employer information (as required by campaign finance laws).
We may upload your information into VoteBuilder and update your records to keep your information current for future election cycles (much of this information is already publicly viewable via voter registration files).
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to communicate with you about campaign updates, processing donations, and complying with Georgia state law.
We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.
3.
Cookies and Tracking
We use privacy-first tracking technologies to analyze site traffic and improve your experience.
We utilize **Umami Analytics**, which is a cookieless, anonymized service that does not collect personal data or track you across other websites.
4.
Third-Party Services
Our site may contain links to third-party services like ActBlue or Stripe for donation processing.
These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing your information.
5.
Campaign Finance Compliance
Please be aware that under Georgia law, political campaigns are required to report certain donor information (including name, address, occupation, and employer) to the Georgia Government Transparency and Campaign Finance Commission.
This includes any unitemized donation over $100, which will be publicly viewable and associated with your name and address in accordance with Georgia state compliance law.
6.
Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.
7.
Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@votega157.com.
