Why You Should Use Ballotpedia When it comes to non-partisan, unbiased election research, Ballotpedia is the gold standard.
Often referred to as the “digital encyclopedia of American politics,” Ballotpedia is a nonprofit organization that doesn’t care about campaign slogans.
They care about data.
They track voting records, campaign finance reports, and official candidate filings.
More importantly, they offer the Candidate Connection Survey .
This survey forces candidates to answer tough, specific questions about their political philosophy, their priorities, and their vision for the office they are seeking.
There is no PR team to spin the answers.
It is the raw, unedited truth directly from the candidate.
