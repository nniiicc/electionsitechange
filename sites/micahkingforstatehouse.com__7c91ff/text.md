Raised Here.
Working for Us.
Micah King grew up in Glennville watching his dad go straight from the pulpit to city council meetings.
He’s spent his life helping neighbors solve real problems, and he’s running because District 157 needs a representative who actually knows rural life and will show up to get things done.
As an Accredited Financial Counselor and Army Transition Assistance counselor, Micah has worked directly with veterans and families facing financial pressure and major transitions.
He has also served the community as a city councilman, civic leader, and youth minister, giving him real experience in both service and leadership.
