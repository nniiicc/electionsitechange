The Alabama Supreme Court is the highest court in our state.
It interprets our Constitution and determines how laws passed by the legislature actually work in everyday life.
Its rulings decide: What rights we have in practice, not just on paper What the government can and cannot do How laws are enforced in courtrooms across Alabama When difficult legal questions arise, this court is the final authority.
Its decisions guide every lower court in the state and can even shape cases that reach the Supreme Court of the United States.
Alabama is often a testing ground for major legal questions—on voting rights, personal freedom, and constitutional protections.
What happens here doesn’t stay here.
In short, this court shapes how justice works in Alabama.
The Court doesn’t make laws — but it decides what those laws mean in real life.
That affects: Voting rights and access to the ballot Free speech and the right to protest Criminal justice and due process Child custody and family law Healthcare and education policy Property rights and economic fairness From deeply personal family decisions to fundamental constitutional protections, the Court’s rulings shape the rights and responsibilities of every Alabamian.
