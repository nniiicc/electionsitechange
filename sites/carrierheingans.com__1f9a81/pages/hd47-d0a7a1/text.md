MI HD-47 is an exciting district to live in.
Our median age is 42 years old. 8% of residents speak another language other than English at home.
Over 3% of residents moved from another state last year. 5% of residents are veterans.
Our top industries are education, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, hospitality, and professional (scientific & technical services).
