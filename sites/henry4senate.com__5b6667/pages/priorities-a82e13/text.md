Jackie's number-one priority is accountability: ending the fraud and waste that have drained hundreds of millions from Minnesota taxpayers.

After a lifetime here and a career starting and running businesses, she knows how to talk to people, and she knows who to ask.
- Root out fraud and waste in state spending
- Demand real oversight, accountability, and consequences

- Hold every agency answerable to the taxpayers who fund it
Affordability & Property-Tax Relief
Property taxes keep climbing while paychecks stay flat, and after years of one-party control in St.
Paul, families across southern Minnesota are feeling the squeeze.
This isn't about left or right — it's about your property-tax bill and your grocery receipt.
Jackie will fight for property-tax relief and a lower cost of living — starting with a solid, honest review of the regulations that have driven prices up.
She's pro-paycheck and pro-business: cut the red tape that makes everyday life more expensive, because no family should have to choose between their home and their groceries.
- Push back on rising property taxes and levies
- Review and cut the regulations driving prices up
- Lower the everyday cost of living for working families
- Relief for homeowners and seniors on fixed incomes
Jobs & Trade Skills
College has its place, but it isn't the only path — and not everyone needs a four-year degree to build a good life here.
