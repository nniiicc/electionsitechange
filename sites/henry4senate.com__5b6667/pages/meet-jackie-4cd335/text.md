- The farm A farm kid from Nicollet County Jackie was born and raised on her family's farm — dairy cattle, hogs, and crops.
She grew up in the barn and in the field, helping with every part of the fieldwork, with chores that came before school and after it.
- Age 14 Dairy, hogs & crops When Jackie was 14, the family sold the dairy herd and expanded the hog operation.
She stayed hands-on in all of it — the fieldwork and especially the hogs — and was just as active in her brother's hog operation.
She learned young what every farm family knows: you earn every dollar, and you make it stretch.
- 1968 A southern Minnesota kid Jackie graduates high school with a farm kid's work ethic and a love for the place she still calls home.
- 1976 She put family first She worked to put her husband through school, then earned her own business degree — economics and accounting on the side.
- 36 years Helping families plan Running her own financial-planning practice as an independent contractor, Jackie learned how working families really earn, spend, and save — and how to help them reach real financial freedom and manage their own economy.
- 2003 A family business She stood beside her husband at AJ Henry Fence Company, handling the day-to-day operations of a small southern Minnesota business from 2003 until he retired in 2020.
Meeting payroll and serving neighbors comes as naturally to her as breathing.
- 2017 A new chapter She sold her practice and went right back to work as an independent Medicare broker.
Today she helps neighbors — especially seniors — cut through a complicated system, understand what health care really costs, and choose the right plan so they can manage on their own.
- Today Ready to serve Jackie is excited to go to work for you — because this was never about one person, it's about all of us.
From dairy, hogs, and crops on the family farm to her own financial and Medicare practices to her husband's fence business, she's helped run four small businesses.
That's real experience with agriculture, taxes, and a budget that has to balance — exactly how southern Minnesota lives, and how the state ought to be run.
