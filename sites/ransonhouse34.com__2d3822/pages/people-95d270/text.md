Donate People, Politics & Pints People, Politics & Pints was born from the need to push back against political misinformation in an easy, personable way.
Since we began, our initiative has grown to actively engaging the community, fostering informative discussions and civic education—all in a nonpartisan safe space.
Each session’s topic is lead by a civics educator trained in fostering small group discussion, as well as a question and answer segment, and a closing toast.
Participants have found themselves continuing conversations long after the program is completed.
Come out and see for yourself just how fun civics education can be!
