Throughout my life I’ve experienced what it’s like to raise a family during a wide spectrum of economic conditions.
One thing that has remained constant is that when the cost of living is too high, families suffer.
PA’s 40th District is made up of hard-working people who simply want to make sure their bills are paid, they can afford basic necessities, and they are not held hostage by a system that is inherently flawed.
By focusing on affordability, accessibility and accountability, I will represent the people with whom I’ve shared so many life experiences both directly and indirectly.
I can’t wait to meet you on the road to the 2026 election, and I hope you’ll consider joining me to make a positive impact on our region.
Let's Bring Trust Back to Harrisburg I believe Pennsylvanians deserve a government that answers to them, not to wealthy donors, corporations, lobbyists, or special interests.
I’ll support meaningful anti-corruption reforms, such as a ban on gifts to public officials, limits on campaign contributions, more transparency around political spending and lobbying, and better disclosure requirements so voters know who’s funding campaigns and political advertising.
I’ll also support efforts to reduce the influence of corporate and dark money in our elections, make ethics and conflict-of-interest rules stronger, and stop public officials from using their office for private financial benefit.
I’ll also continue to support independent redistricting reform, so voters choose their representatives instead of politicians choosing their voters.
These shouldn’t be partisan issues.
I believe PA residents of every political affiliation deserve a government that’s transparent and accountable to the people it serves.
