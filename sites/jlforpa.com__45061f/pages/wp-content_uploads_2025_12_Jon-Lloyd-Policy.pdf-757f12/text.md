Jon Lloyd’s Policy Platform Jon Lloyd is running for the Pennsylvania House because families in Allegheny and Washington Counties deserve a community that is affordable to live in, accessible to navigate, and accountable to the people—not special interests.
His vision is simple: government should make life easier, not harder.
This is a campaign for everyone—every family, every neighbor, every person who calls District 40 home.
Jon is committed to building a community where all residents, including our marginalized neighbors, feel safe, valued, and protected.
AFFORDABILITY Building a future where families can stay, grow, and thrive.
Why it matters: Across District 40, rising costs are stretching family budgets and making it harder for young people to stay and seniors to age in place.
Jon understands this firsthand—his own family has experienced hard financial times in the past, and he knows how expensive it is to struggle in this state.
No one should be priced out of the community they love.
Pennsylvania’s stagnant minimum wage is a clear sign of a flawed system—costs continue to rise, yet people’s ability to pay for those necessities does not.
Every family deserves a dignified life with the ability to meet basic needs without constant struggle.
Jon will: • Support the effort to raise the minimum wage. • Address skyrocketing childcare costs by supporting direct investments and stabilizing programs that help parents return to work. • Increase availability of workforce and starter homes through partnerships with local governments, nonprofits, and responsible developers. • Fight for fair school funding so homeowners aren’t carrying an unfair share of the burden, with direct funding prioritized for the most accessible and effective systems—always choosing the path of least resistance for families and districts. • Bring state and federal dollars home to invest in roads, bridges, and community facilities— reducing pressure on local budgets.
What this means for you: More stable housing costs, stronger neighborhoods, and a future where families can afford to stay.
ACCESSIBILITY Ensuring every resident can fully participate in community life.
Why it matters: From reliable transportation to digital access, too many barriers keep people from getting where they need to go or receiving the services they deserve.
Jon believes communities function best when everyone can participate.
Jon will: • Improve transportation options by championing investments in transit, safe streets, and regional mobility. • Expand ADA-compliant infrastructure in public spaces, parks, sidewalks, and municipal buildings. • Modernize online government services so residents can access information, applications, and assistance without bureaucratic hurdles. • Support mental and physical health accessibility by strengthening funding for local providers and community resources. • Provide robust assistance for parents facing the mental health and technological challenges of parenting in today’s world. • Expand senior scam prevention initiatives to protect older residents from fraud and predatory practices.
What this means for you: Easier movement, smoother access to services, and a community built for all ages and abilities.
ACCOUNTABILITY & AVAILABILITY Government that listens, is transparent, and earns your trust.
Why it matters: People are tired of political games and secretive decision-making.
Jon believes elected officials should answer to their neighbors—not party bosses or special interests.
Jon will: • Increase transparency by requiring plain-language summaries of budgets, legislation, and votes. • Strengthen ethics rules to ensure officials disclose financial interests and avoid conflicts and support the gift ban for lawmakers. • Create clear channels for resident input through regular town halls, open office hours, and accessible digital feedback tools. • Offer in-demand town hall options with Jon—including a booking link for constituents to easily schedule conversations and share their concerns directly.
What this means for you: A representative who shows up, listens, and reports back—because government only works when people can see it and shape it.
COMMUNITY SAFETY Protecting our neighborhoods with common-sense solutions.
Why it matters: Every family deserves to feel safe in their home, their school, and their community.
Jon supports practical, bipartisan measures that keep guns out of dangerous hands while respecting responsible gun ownership.
Jon will: • Support red flag laws that allow temporary firearm removal from individuals who pose a danger to themselves or others. • Crack down on ghost guns by requiring serialization and closing loopholes that let untraceable weapons circulate in our communities. • Strengthen background checks to prevent violent offenders from accessing firearms.
What this means for you: Safer neighborhoods, fewer tragedies, and a community where public safety is built on responsibility and common sense.
Jon’s Commitment Jon Lloyd is running for the same reason he raised his family here: District 40 deserves a future where every resident feels supported, valued, and heard.
These policies reflect Jon’s core belief that when we invest in affordability, accessibility, accountability, and common-sense safety measures, we build stronger communities—together.
This campaign is for everyone, and Jon will always stand with and fight for all who call this district home.
