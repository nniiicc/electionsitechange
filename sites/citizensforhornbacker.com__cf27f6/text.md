Tim Hornbacker: Leadership and Our Vision for Ohio Our Vision Protecting Ohio's Independent Spirit Restoring Public Trust Future-Ready Neighborhoods We champion the independent spirit of Ohio's farmers and small business owners - protecting family farms from corporate land grabs, breaking down the barriers that lock small businesses out of apprenticeships and fair markets, and making sure District 40's economy works for the people who live here.
We restore public trust by putting people over politics - closing corporate tax loopholes, keeping tax dollars in our communities, demanding absolute transparency in every deal made with Ohio's money, and making sure no backroom agreement ever shields a corporation from the accountability it owes to District 40.
We invest in infrastructure, education, and healthcare that prepares our next generation to lead and innovate in a changing world - not to hand it over to corporations that consume our land, drain our water, and drive up our energy bills while paying next to nothing in taxes.
