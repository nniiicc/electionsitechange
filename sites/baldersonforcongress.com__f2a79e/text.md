Troy Balderson About Troy Balderson Growing up in rural Ohio, I learned the true meaning of hard work, responsibility, and the importance of community.
These values were instilled in me by my family and the close-knit community that surrounded us.
It’s this upbringing that has shaped my principles and guided me throughout my life. ‍ I ran for Congress because I saw the challenges plaguing our country, challenges that hit close to home.
Out of control spending, skyrocketing cost of living, parents worried about what their kids were seeing both in and out of the classroom, and many Americans feeling left behind - these are issues that I couldn’t ignore. ‍ I knew that if we didn’t take action, the America I grew up in would be at risk, and the next generation would face even greater challenges.
I remember a time when hard work could put food on the table, fill up the gas tank, and provide a sense of security for families.
Just like a classic car, America is worth fixing.
That starts with relying on our tried and true values again, like hard work, personal responsibility, and family. ‍ In Congress, I am fighting alongside the Trump Administration to ratchet back Biden Administration’s job-killing policies, pump the brakes on inflation, and shine a light on common sense.
Getting the job done takes more than me or one congressional seat.
