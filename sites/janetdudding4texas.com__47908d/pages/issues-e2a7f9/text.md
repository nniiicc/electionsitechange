Academic Freedom: Protecting the Integrity of the Degree
Academic freedom—to teach, research, publish, and discuss without political interference—is foundational to higher education.
SB 37, backed by Gov.
Abbott, Lt.
Gov.
Patrick, and Rep.
Dyson, threatens First Amendment protections and invites political interference that chills scholarship.
It also creates compliance risk for research universities,
34 CFR 75.500(b)(1) recognizes academic freedom as part of First Amendment compliance and as a material condition of covered federal grants.
Don’t let politics devalue—and defund—our top-tier research.
