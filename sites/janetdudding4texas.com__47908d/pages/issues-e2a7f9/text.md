More info Academic Freedom: Protecting the Integrity of the Degree Academic freedom—to teach, research, publish, and discuss without political interference —is foundational to higher education.
SB 37, backed by Gov.
Abbott, Lt.
Gov.
Patrick, and Rep.
Dyson, threatens First Amendment protections and invites political interference that chills scholarship.
It also creates compliance risk for research universities, 34 CFR 75.500(b)(1) recognizes academic freedom as part of First Amendment compliance and as a material condition of covered federal grants .
Don’t let politics devalue—and defund—our top-tier research.
More info Data Centers: Solving the Water Paradox & Defending Local Control For the past 100 years, oil & gas’s the engine that runs our economy here in Texas, but now we've got 2 water crises here in Texas: too much "produced water" contaminating our groundwater - and - not enough water to drink .
F rom produced water to data centers, we need guardrails in place before state politicians squander what we need to live – in exchange for their campaign war-chest.
Let's use drinkable water for drinking & require data centers to use recycled produced water - or greywater - for their cooling & energy generation needs.

More info Healthcare There's truth in the adage: "When you have your health, you have everything." So why does Greg Abbott refuse to allow the money we've already paid to the federal government to come home to Texas to pay for healthcare?
IRS doesn't discount our income tax.
Before Trump's Big Bad Bill's cuts, Texas only covered pregnant women, children, the patiently disabled, & very elderly-low-income people.
Expanding the pool lowers the cost for all of us . - and is a lifeline for keeping our rural hospitals open.
More info Property Tax Reform Too many families are being taxed as if their homes were investment properties.
In Bryan and College Station, investor-driven rental pricing can distort homeowner appraisals and drive up tax burdens.
I’m standing with the families who live in these neighborhoods, not the market forces that turn homes into yield calculations.
When investor pricing distorts homeowner tax values, government should fix the appraisal model — not expect families to absorb the cost.
But first, we need to close the loopholes that let corporations skate on their fair share.

Decriminalize, Legalize, Regulate and TAX adult-use THC.

More info Fix state Corruption As a governmental CPA, fixing corruption is something I've been doing for over 40 years.
Get politicians out of billionaires' back pockets.

For forward action to happen, we need majority and ethical statewide leadership.
We all need to work together .
More info ICE & Public Safety Texans believe in law and order.
We also believe government should show its face, follow the law, and answer for its actions.
Families deserve to feel safe in their neighborhoods.
They should not have to wonder whether masked, armed agents in unmarked vehicles are going to tear through their streets, stop people without clear identification, or leave families scrambling for answers.
That is not the kind of public safety Texans believe in.
More info Justice for Hannah Rapp Hannah Rapp ’s death should move Texas to act.
Texas has considered Safe Passing legislation before, but it has still not become statewide law.
I support passing a strong Safe Passing law with clear, enforceable standards to protect cyclists and other vulnerable road users, including stronger protections around large and commercial vehicles.
I also support reviewing Texas law to strengthen repeat offender protections when a defendant has a documented pattern of dangerous conduct.
Courts should have better tools to protect the public and take repeated dangerous behavior seriously.
This is a matter of public safety, accountability, and basic respect for human life.
Texans should not have to accept preventable tragedy as the cost of using our roads.
