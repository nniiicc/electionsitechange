Hi, I'm Omari.
I was born and raised in DeKalb County in the State of Georgia, and now serve as a Representative in the Georgia General Assembly for State House District 89.
I represent 60,000 Georgians who live in South DeKalb, the City of Decatur, the City of Atlanta neighborhoods of Kirkwood and East Lake, and the Druid Hills area around Emory University.

I run... a lot.
I used to race the 800-meter in college, and now I race to reduce the cost of living for Georgians, improve our criminal justice system, and give our kids the quality education they’re entitled to.
Policy Rundown I also run for real!
I’m collaborating with the Metro Atlanta YMCA to run a half-marathon in all 50 states from 2025 to 2029.
I’m visiting YMCA’s along the way and learning the to-do’s (and not-to-do’s!) from lawmakers in each state.
