Micah Kalama
District 51 House State REPRESENTATIVE candidate
Micah Kalama is deeply rooted in Waimānalo and Kailua, raised in a multi-generational family on a local farm where he learned the value of culture and the land.
Inspired by parents who dedicated their lives to archaeological preservation and traditional craftsmanship, Micah carries forward a legacy of hard work and community stewardship.
A lifelong waterman and competitive athlete, Micah's professional journey spans hospitality, media, and community leadership.
From his role at Hawaiian Airlines to serving as a youth coach and race director, he has consistently worked to strengthen Hawai'i's ties to its heritage while creating new opportunities for the next generation.
Micah is running for State Representative to ensure our homes remain affordable, our traditions are honored, and our district has a leader who listens.
He is committed to protecting the unique character of District 51 while building a sustainable future for all our families.
Micah believes strong communities are built by listening to people, supporting local families, and protecting Hawai'i's natural resources for the next generation.
Micah serves as the Hawaiian Affairs Caucus Secretary
As HAC Secretary, Micah urges all Hawaiʻi voters to prepare for the upcoming election.
Verify your voter registration status, stay informed about key deadlines, and finalize your voting plan today.
Your participation is vital for our community and the future of District 51.
How to Register to Vote in Hawaiʻi
Exercising your right to vote is easy and essential.
You can register online through the State of Hawaii: Office of Elections Online Voter Registration system in just a few minutes.
Ensure you have your Hawaii Driver License or State ID and Social Security Number ready to complete the online form.

Visit Site

2.
