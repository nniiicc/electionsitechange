Voter Registration FAQ
Who is eligible to register to vote in Hawaii?
To register, you must be a U.S.
Citizen, a resident of Hawaii, and at least 18 years of age.
Hawaii law also allows 16-year-olds to pre-register, and they will be automatically registered upon turning 18.
What information do I need to register online?
You will need a valid Hawaii Driver License or State ID card and your Social Security Number.
The system uses these to verify your identity with the Department of Customer Services.
What are the key deadlines for the General Election?
Voter registration for the General Election must be completed at least 10 days before the election to receive a mail-in ballot.
However, Hawaii offers same-day registration at Voter Service Centers during the early voting period and on Election Day.
How do I update my registration if I moved within District 51?
You can use the same Online Voter Registration system to update your address.
Keeping your information current ensures your ballot is mailed to the correct location and you are voting in the proper contests for Kailua and Waimānalo.
