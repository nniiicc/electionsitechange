Join Amy Brooks and Team 13 as we march in the annual River Hill Independence Day Parade.
This is a fun experience for your whole family, groups of friends, or solo walkers- no parade experience needed!
We will line up at 8:30AM (Pointers Run ES/Clarksville MS) and the parade will run from 9am-12noon.
There will be a reception for parade participants inside the fire station at the end of the parade route.
