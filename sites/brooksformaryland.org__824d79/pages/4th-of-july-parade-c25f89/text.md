Thank You for an Incredible July 4th!
We’re so grateful to everyone who came out to support Amy Brooks at the River Hill July 4th Parade!
The energy was electric, the weather was perfect, and the community spirit was in full swing.
Amy and a fantastic group of supporters joined the celebration with music, smiles, and high energy.
Amy made it a point to personally greet as many neighbors as possible.
She was running up to shake hands, share a laugh, and connect with the people she hopes to represent.
It was a wonderful day of community, conversation, and celebration.
