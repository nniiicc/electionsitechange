Senator Rick Holdcroft is a conservative fighter – delivering tax relief for hardworking Nebraskans, supporting our law enforcement officers, securing our elections, defending our constitutional rights, and cutting government waste.
As a former business executive, Rick brings a business mindset to government by working to balance the budget, slash unnecessary spending, and reduce the size of government.
And as a Navy veteran, Rick continues his service by defending and protecting the Constitution of Nebraska and the United States.
Lowering Taxes
Senator Holdcroft introduced legislation to provide tax relief on electrical costs and sales taxes, helping lower the cost of living for Nebraska families.
He backed property tax relief to make the American Dream of homeownership more achievable.
Rick is fighting to keep more of your hard-earned money in your pocket.
Backing the Blue
As a Navy veteran, Rick understands the importance of keeping our communities and families safe.
He has consistently supported law enforcement and worked to ensure officers have the tools, resources, and backing they need to protect Nebraska communities.
Securing Our Elections
Senator Holdcroft led efforts to strengthen election integrity and protect confidence in Nebraska’s voting system.
He fought to ensure our elections remain fair, secure, and free from outside influence.
Protecting Our Children
Children are our most precious gift, and Rick has fought to keep them safe.
He supported changes to Nebraska’s Safe Haven Law to protect newborns and ensure vulnerable babies receive safe care and medical attention.
Defending Parental Rights
Senator Holdcroft believes parents, not bureaucrats, know what’s best for their children.
He has fought to give parents a stronger voice in education and to ensure schools focus on academics, not political agendas.
Protecting Children Online
Rick supported legislation to rein in Big Tech and put parents back in control of their children’s social media use.
He voted to require age verification, parental consent, and accountability for social media companies that target minors, helping protect kids from harmful content and online exploitation.
Supporting Veterans
After serving nearly three decades in the United States Navy, Rick understands the sacrifices made by our veterans and their families.
He has been a strong advocate for honoring service, expanding opportunities for veterans, and ensuring Nebraska remains a state that respects those who wore the uniform.
Reducing Government
Rick has worked to rein in government overreach by supporting reforms that cut red tape, improve efficiency, and hold state agencies accountable because the government should work for taxpayers, not the other way around.
