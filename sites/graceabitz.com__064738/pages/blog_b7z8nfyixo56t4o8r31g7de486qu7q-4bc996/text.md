Fox Cities Youth Rugby
On a typical Tuesday night at the ever-manicured Lions Park in Greenville, WI, you’ll see youth from the greater surrounding communities practicing rugby.
That evening, running up the hill to the pavilion was the conditioning assignment.
Andi from the Green Bay Kelpies, formerly Fox Valley Chaos, and myself ran alongside them, cheering them on!
Three options for participation include:
Co-ed Flag for 2nd-8th graders playing in summer.
Middle School Tackle is played as Rugby 7s for the boys’ team and girls’ team in the spring.
High School Tackle Boys 15s in the spring and 7s in the summer.
High School Tackle Girls 15s have a fall season and 7s in the spring.
For more info contact foxcitiesyouthrugby@gmail.com
