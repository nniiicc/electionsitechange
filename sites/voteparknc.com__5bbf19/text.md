Support the Campaign Read the Platform The Platform Fighting For NC House District 96 Across our community, voters consistently raise concerns about critical issues — infrastructure, water systems, healthcare access, and schools.
These issues require strong state leadership.
01 Protect The first responsibility of leadership is to protect people, communities, and the institutions they rely on.
Government has an obligation to safeguard public safety, public education, voting rights, and access to healthcare, while protecting taxpayer dollars through accountable governance.
02 Provide Public service should genuinely serve the public.
We need leadership that shows up, listens, and delivers tangible results for communities—investing in infrastructure, mental health, and transparent, reliable public services that people can truly count on.
03 Prosper A hopeful and practical vision for what our communities can become with strong leadership.
We must focus on economic opportunity, workforce development, support for small businesses, and affordable housing so individuals and families can build stable, fulfilling lives and succeed here at home.

About Park A Fourth-Generation North Carolinian I am a proud fourth-generation North Carolinian.
I grew up playing in creeks, running through pastures, and enjoying the natural beauty of the North Carolina foothills.
Today, I serve as the North Carolina State Director for Young People's Alliance , a bipartisan nonprofit that equips the next generation of leaders with the tools to advocate for positive change.
In this role, I manage and organize young people across party lines on critical AI regulation policy.
My work in our community has changed my life.
The conversations I have at doorsteps across our district continue to deepen my understanding of what our neighbors truly need.
I was born in Statesville and raised in Hickory.
My faith was nurtured at Northminster Presbyterian Church.
I attended Hickory Public Schools, graduated from Colorado Rocky Mountain School, and studied political science at Northeastern University in Boston, graduating with honors. ★ Now, I'm ready to take those voices — your voices — to the State House.
