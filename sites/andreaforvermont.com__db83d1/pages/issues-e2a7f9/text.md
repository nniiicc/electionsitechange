Making Vermont affordable for all Vermonters
"My approach is simple: listen first, understand the real cost of every proposal, and work with people from both parties to find practical solutions.
And when a proposal does not work for our rural towns, I will speak up and bring Windsor District’s priorities to the table." ~ Andrea Murray Windsor County, Thetford, and Pittsfield deserve a senator who will bring people together, protect what makes our communities special, and remain focused on affordability and economic growth.
Andrea will fight for property tax relief, support for small businesses, and policies that make Vermont more affordable without sacrificing the services our communities depend on.
- Tax relief and social security income tax exemption
- Incentivize farming, working lands, and small businesses
- Shift from legislation with unrealistic mandates to goals
- Open health ins. markets for lower costs and greater access
