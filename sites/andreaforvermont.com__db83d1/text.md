To the voters of Windsor District: Your vote is your voice and you have a historic opportunity to turn that voice into a movement for a brighter, more prosperous future for our district.
Today, we are at a crossroads, and it is time to introduce a new voice to Montpelier—a leader who understands that people are more important than politics and that common sense must prevail over ideological extremes.
It's time for a Senator who puts affordability first, fights for our rural communities, and brings fiscal restraint to our district's Senate team.
Why is Andrea the right choice for voters looking for balance?
A Real Affordability Agenda: Andrea shares a steadfast commitment to making Vermont a place where families and young people can afford to stay and thrive.
She advocates for reducing government spending and lowering taxes to incentivize local businesses rather than forcing our workforce to flee to more affordable states.
Restoring Educational Excellence: Andrea believes it is unacceptable that Vermonters are paying some of the highest per-pupil costs in the nation while seeing declining outcomes.
Andrea will fight to restore educational excellence by putting students and teachers first, strengthening local control, and making sure education dollars reach the classroom and deliver better results.
