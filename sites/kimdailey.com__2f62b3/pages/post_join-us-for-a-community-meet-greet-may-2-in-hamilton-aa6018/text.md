Join Us for a Community Meet & Greet – May 2 in Hamilton
We are excited to invite you to a special Meet & Greet with local candidates who are committed to serving and strengthening our Montana communities.
This is a great opportunity to connect, ask questions, and engage in meaningful conversation with the people working to represent you.
Join:
- Kim Dailey
- Kathy Love
- Kenneth Allen
The event will be held at Silver Coin Bar & Casino, located at 109 W.
Main St. in Hamilton, Montana, on Saturday, May 2 from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
This Meet & Greet is designed to be a relaxed and welcoming environment where community members can speak directly with candidates, share their perspectives, and learn more about the priorities and vision each brings to the table.
Whether you are actively engaged in local issues or simply looking to become more informed, your participation is valuable.
Following the Meet & Greet, the evening will continue with live music from Mix Tape Band, performing hits from the 70s, 80s, 90s, and 2000s.
It’s a great opportunity to enjoy time with friends and neighbors while celebrating the spirit of community.
Local leadership plays a critical role in shaping the future of our region.
Events like this provide an important space for open dialogue, connection, and engagement.
We hope you will join us for an evening focused on community, conversation, and shared commitment to Montana’s future.
