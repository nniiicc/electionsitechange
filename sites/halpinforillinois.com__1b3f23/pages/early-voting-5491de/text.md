I want to make sure everyone has the opportunity to vote and have their voice heard.
The 36th Senate District has seven election authorities, so make sure you get information from the proper office.
Below, you will find information on each election authority, along with webpages for additional information on voting by mail and early voting.
You can request a vote-by-mail ballot through Nov. 3.
Early voting begins on Sept. 29.
Please don’t hesitate to reach out to 309-553-1429 if you need assistance checking your registration status, applying for a vote-by-mail ballot, finding an early voting location or answering any other questions you may have.
