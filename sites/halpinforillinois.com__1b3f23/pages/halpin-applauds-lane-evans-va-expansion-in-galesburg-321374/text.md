Toggle navigation Skip to content Take Our Survey News Get Involved Voting Resources About Mike Issues Donate Events Contact Halpin applauds Lane Evans VA expansion in Galesburg Posted on June 27, 2022 June 27, 2022 by Mike Halpin GAL ESBURG – The following statement on the city of Galesburg’s approved agreement to expand the Lane A.
Evans VA Community Based Outpatient Clinic in Galesburg can be attributed to Rep.
Mike Halpin.
“When it comes to healthcare access, our veterans who bravely served our country deserve to be first in line.
Our veterans made the sacrifice, we owe them our gratitude and we must continue to provide them services when they return home.
As a former aide to congressman Lane Evans, I’ve always known the importance of putting our veterans first.
My sincere thanks go to the City of Galesburg and the Department of Veterans Affairs for expanding this important clinic and for keeping Lane’s legacy alive in western Illinois.” Read Full Story This entry was tagged Lane Evans , Mike Halpin .
