DONATE Greene Launches First TV and Radio Ads of Re-Election Campaign October 13, 2026 Categories: Campaign Update The spots highlight Greene’s Senate work to help everyday Michiganders save money, and his leadership as a Marine and Fire Captain SAGINAW, MI : Today, State Senate candidate Chedrick Greene launched the first television and radio ads of his re-election campaign for Michigan’s 35th Senate District.
The 30-second TV spot, titled “ Neighbors ,” gives a nod to Greene’s working-class experiences and efforts to improve the bottom lines of everyday Michiganders.
The ad will run on mid-Michigan broadcast stations in advance of the November 3rd rematch against the Republican opponent he resoundingly defeated in the MI-SD-35 special election earlier this year.
The radio spot, also 30 seconds, is on the air in the Saginaw media market, and features veteran Shane Peltier attesting to Greene’s decades of leadership while serving his country and community as a U.S.
Marine and firefighter.

Keep up the good work!
As Mid-Michigan’s newest state Senator, I work for them —making insurance companies cover the cost of inhalers and insulin, securing funding for the Lafayette Bridge, … Heads up, Chedrick! [Chedrick throws football back to neighbor] Thank you, sir! … still pushing to lower taxes for working families. [neighbors walk by] Thanks Chedrick.

Serving is the greatest honor of all. [Greene] I’m Chedrick Greene, and I’m asking for your vote for State Senate.
Greene launches both ads on the heels of consolidating highly sought-after support from Michigan’s business, farming, and labor communities.
The Small Business Association of Michigan and Detroit Regional Chamber PAC s endorsed the Marine veteran and retired fire captain as the first absentee votes were being cast.
Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPac endorsed Sen.
Greene this summer, the first time that organization has endorsed a Democrat for MI-SD-35.
And when the Michigan Fraternal Order of Police named him as their favored candidate last month, it marked Greene’s 25th labor organization endorsement .
Sworn in as Senator for Michigan’s 35th district on May 21, 2026, Greene is completing the final months of the term vacated by Kristen McDonald Rivet after she was elected to Congress.
MI-SD-35 has been trending red at the presidential level for over a decade.
After McDonald Rivet won the district by nearly seven points in 2022, Donald Trump only lost to Kamala Harris here by less than one percentage point in 2024.
Absentee voting in Michigan for the November 3, 2026 general election began September 24, 2026.
Michiganders also have nine days of early, in-person voting: Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1, 2026.
