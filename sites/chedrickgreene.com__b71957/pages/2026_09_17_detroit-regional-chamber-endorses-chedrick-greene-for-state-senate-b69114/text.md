One week before absentee voting begins, retired Marine and firefighter Greene earns coveted business group’s endorsement in re-election bid to represent Michigan’s 35th Senate District
SAGINAW, MI – Today, the Detroit Regional Chamber PAC announced their endorsement of State Senator Chedrick Greene in his re-election bid to represent Michigan’s 35th Senate District.
The announcement comes one week before citizens in Bay, Midland, and Saginaw can vote via absentee ballot for who they want representing them in Lansing for the four-year Senate term that begins January 2027.
Greene received the endorsement along with 15 Republicans and 8 other Democrats.
Detroit Regional Chamber’s Vice President of Public Policy and Business Advocacy, Brian J.
Shoaf, says the bipartisan slate of endorsees “represents pragmatic public servants from both sides of the aisle who are committed to working with the Chamber to expand the talent pipeline, eliminate regulatory hurdles, and ensure Michigan is a top destination for investment.”
“I am honored to carry the endorsement of this longtime, respected leader for Michigan’s business community,” said Sen.
Chedrick Greene.
“Tariffs, inflation, and chaotic economic decisions at the federal level have severely hurt Michigan businesses of all sizes as well as their customers.
The Detroit Regional Chamber knows I’ll work with anyone to create better-paying jobs, strengthen our workforce, and attract investments that make life better for residents and businesses here in mid-Michigan and across our state.”
With today’s endorsement, Chedrick Greene is the only candidate in this pivotal race who has the public backing of Michigan’s business, farming, and labor communities.
Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPac endorsed Greene earlier this summer — the first time that organization has endorsed a Democrat for MI-SD-35.
And earlier this week, the retired Marine and Fire Captain notched his 25th labor organization endorsement.
Greene continues to grow the broad coalition that reflects the Great Lakes Bay Region residents he serves, and positions him to win re-election.
After his 20-point special election victory, Chedrick Greene was sworn in as Senator for Michigan’s 35th district on May 21, 2026, to complete the final months of the term vacated by Kristen McDonald Rivet who was elected to Congress.
A purple seat in a purple state, MI-SD-35 has been trending red at the presidential level for over a decade.
After McDonald Rivet won the most recent full Senate term election by nearly seven points in 2022, Donald Trump only lost to Kamala Harris in SD-35 by less than one percentage point in 2024.
Absentee voting for the November 3, 2026 general election gets underway September 24, 2026 in Michigan.
Michiganders also have nine days of early, in-person voting: Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1, 2026.
