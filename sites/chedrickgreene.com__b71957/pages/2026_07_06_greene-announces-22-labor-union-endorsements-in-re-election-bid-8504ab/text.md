DONATE Greene Announces 22 Labor Union Endorsements in Re-Election Bid July 6, 2026 Categories: Campaign Update As early votes are cast for the 35th Senate District’s four-year term, Michigan’s newest State Senator retains robust support from union and working families like his own SAGINAW, Mich. – State Senator Chedrick Greene announced today that he has secured endorsements from 22 labor unions in his re-election bid to represent Michigan’s 35th Senate District.
The announcement comes as citizens in Bay, Midland, and Saginaw counties are casting votes via absentee ballot for who they want representing them in Lansing for the four-year Senate term beginning in January 2027.
“My union roots are deep, and my experiences with the challenges facing working families are real and personal,” said Senator Chedrick Greene .
“Just as my union family has had my back for the past 31 years, I’m always going to have theirs.
I’m very grateful to have such strong support from Michigan’s labor movement as we fight to keep a pro-worker majority in the Michigan Senate.” Leaders of labor unions re-endorsing Sen.
Greene for this pivotal district — the decisive, majority-making seat for Michigan Senate Democrats in 2022 — shared statements underscoring their support: Ron Bieber, President, MI AFL-CIO: “Michigan’s labor movement knows the stakes haven’t changed.
As the ultra-wealthy and special interests try to buy more power and influence, it’s never been more important to have a trusted public servant and working-class leader like Chedrick fighting for all Michiganders to have a fair shot at a decent life.
We are proud to stand with one of our own, and we are all-in to re-elect Chedrick.” Jeff Pilarski, Business Manager/Secretary-Treasurer, LiUNA Laborers Local 1098: “LIUNA Local 1098 was a proud part of Chedrick Greene’s victory in May, and we are committed to helping this pro-labor champion win again in November.
As both a lifelong union member and military public servant, we know Chedrick will always bring the voices of workers and their families to the table in Lansing.” Ed Clements, President of MEA local Bay City Education Association: “Chedrick Greene is the fighter our students and teachers need in Lansing.
Fiercely committed to strengthening opportunities and outcomes for all, Chedrick will prioritize literacy programs, reduced class sizes, better teacher pay, and more.
The MEA was a proud supporter of his special election campaign, and we are excited to help Chedrick win the full, four-year term on the ballot this fall.” The 22 labor organizations backing Greene for re-election include unions representing public safety servants, teachers, nurses, skilled trades workers, and more.
Today’s endorsements for the November election build on public backing that Greene announced last week from the Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPac , the first time that organization has endorsed a Democrat for MI-SD-35, as well as Everytown for Gun Safety and Moms Demand Action.
Sen.
Greene is running unopposed in the August 4th Democratic primary for MI-SD-35 following dominant performances in the recent special primary and general elections.
The retired Marine and Fire Captain’s working-class leadership helped power his 33-point win in a 6-way primary last February and his 20-point victory over Republican Jason Tunney in May.
Chedrick Greene was sworn in as Senator for Michigan’s 35th district on May 21, 2026 to complete the final months of the term vacated by Kristen McDonald Rivet who was elected to Congress.
A purple seat in a purple state, MI-SD-35 has been trending red at the presidential level for over a decade.
After McDonald Rivet won the most recent full Senate term election by nearly seven points in 2022, Donald Trump only lost to Kamala Harris in SD-35 by less than one percentage point in 2024.
Absentee voting for the August 4 primary is underway as of June 25, 2026.
Winners will compete in the November 3, 2026 general election.
