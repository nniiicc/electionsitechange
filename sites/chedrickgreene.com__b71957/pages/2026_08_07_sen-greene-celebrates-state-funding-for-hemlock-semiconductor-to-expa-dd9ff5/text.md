LANSING, Mich.
(Aug. 5, 2026) — Sen.
Chedrick Greene (D-Saginaw) applauded today’s announcement that Hemlock Semiconductor will receive $40 million in state funding from the Make It in Michigan Competitiveness Fund.
This money will be used to leverage $407 million in private investment and $325 million from the federal CHIPS Incentives Program approved under the Biden administration.
This investment will enable the company to construct a new manufacturing facility to increase their production capacity.
The project is expected to create 180 new jobs.
“There is no better place to invest in the future than the Great Lakes Bay Region,” said state Sen.
Chedrick Greene.
“Hemlock Semiconductor’s expansion adds up to 180 new, good-paying manufacturing jobs and over a thousand construction opportunities for hardworking families.
I am proud to support investments that strengthen Michigan’s leadership in the global semiconductor supply chain, grow our economy, and help Michiganders build rewarding careers and a strong future for their families.”
Hemlock Semiconductor is based in Saginaw County and was founded in 1961.
They are one of the largest producers of hyper-pure semiconductor-grade polysilicon in the country.
