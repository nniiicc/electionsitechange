VOTER INFORMATION
Tuesday, November 3, 2026 is Election Day in Michigan.
*Absentee ballots will be available to voters September 24, 2026 — 40 days in advance of Election Day.
Mail or hand-deliver to your local clerk or drop box, which you may look up here.
*9 days of early, in-person voting will take place Saturday, Oct. 24 – Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026.
Make sure you ARE registered and can vote early by mail or in person.
👉 Visit Michigan.gov/vote for all your voting needs.
You may register online if you have a valid Michigan driver’s license or state ID and start the process early (at least 15 days before Election Day).
Oct. 19, 2026 is the last day to register to vote by mail or online.
You may also register to vote in person at your local clerk’s office now through 8PM on Election Day.
