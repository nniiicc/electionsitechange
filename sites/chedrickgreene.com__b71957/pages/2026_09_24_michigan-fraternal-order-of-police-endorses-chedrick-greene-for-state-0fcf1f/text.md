DONATE Michigan Fraternal Order of Police Endorses Chedrick Greene for State Senate September 24, 2026 Categories: Campaign Update Retired Marine and firefighter Greene earns coveted police union’s support in re-election bid to represent Michigan’s pivotal 35th Senate District SAGINAW, MI – Today, the Michigan State Lodge Fraternal Order of Police announced their endorsement of State Senator Chedrick Greene in his re-election bid to represent Michigan’s 35th Senate District.
The Michigan F.O.P. represents more than 10,000 members and is one of the state’s oldest law enforcement unions.
Endorsing Sen.
Greene, the union’s president praised the recently retired fire captain’s public safety service and commitment to work with law enforcement.
“As a fellow public safety servant for decades, Chedrick respects our work and understands what we need to stay safe while doing our jobs and protecting Michiganders,’ said Michael Sauger, President of the Michigan Fraternal Order of Police.
“The men and women of the Fraternal Order of Police appreciate the support that he has shown to the law enforcement community.” “I’ve always worked to keep my neighbors and community safe, so having the Michigan FOP in my corner means a great deal,” said Sen.
Chedrick Greene .
“I will always stand with my brothers and sisters in public safety to advance pro-safety and pro-law enforcement funding and policy in Lansing.” Today’s announcement adds momentum to Greene’s re-election campaign and demonstrates the breadth and depth of his coalition.
The Detroit Regional Chamber endorsed the retired Marine veteran and fire captain last week, following Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPac ’s public support announced for Greene earlier this summer — the first time that organization has endorsed a Democrat for MI-SD-35.
It marks his third endorsement from a public safety union — joining the Michigan Professional Fire Fighters Union and Michigan Association of Police Organizations — and his 25th labor organization endorsement overall.
After his 20-point special election victory , Chedrick Greene was sworn in as Senator for Michigan’s 35th district on May 21, 2026, to complete the final months of the term vacated by Kristen McDonald Rivet who was elected to Congress.
A purple seat in a purple state, MI-SD-35 has been trending red at the presidential level for over a decade.
After McDonald Rivet won the most recent full Senate term election by nearly seven points in 2022, Donald Trump only lost to Kamala Harris in SD-35 by less than one percentage point in 2024.
Absentee voting for the November 3, 2026 general election is underway as of September 24, 2026 in Michigan.
Michiganders also have nine days of early, in-person voting: Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1, 2026.
