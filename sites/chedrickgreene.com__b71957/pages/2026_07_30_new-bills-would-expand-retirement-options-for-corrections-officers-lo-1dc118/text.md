WNEM TV5 — Michigan House and Senate Democrats are announcing the reintroduction of nine bills that would lower costs and provide long-term relief for thousands across Michigan.
Sen.
Chedrick Greene reintroduced Senate Bill 1134, which would expand pension options for Michigan’s corrections officers.
“Michigan workers deserve fair wages, competitive benefits, strong retirement options and my bill will help deliver on that promise,” Sen.
Chedrick Greene said.
“Our corrections officers work hard to keep our communities safe and by improving their retirement options, we can make a tough job a lot more secure and a lot more attractive.”
Senate Bill 1134 would allow corrections and other state law enforcement officers to become members of the Michigan State Police hybrid retirement system if they wish.

The other bills reintroduced would lower health care costs for teachers, firefighters and other public sector workers.
“Our corrections officers, firefighters, police officers, teachers and other public employees have dedicated their lives to serving and protecting us, and this and the other bills introduced today show that my Senate Democratic colleagues and I continue to have their backs,” Sen.
Greene said.
