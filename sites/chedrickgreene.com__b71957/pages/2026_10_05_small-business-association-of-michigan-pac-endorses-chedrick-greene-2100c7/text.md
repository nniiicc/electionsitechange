DONATE Small Business Association of Michigan PAC Endorses Chedrick Greene October 5, 2026 Categories: Campaign Update Greene is one of only four Democratic state Senate candidates to earn the coveted business group’s endorsement for the November election SAGINAW, MI – Today, the Small Business Association of Michigan ’s (SBAM) Small Biz PAC announced their endorsement of State Senator Chedrick Greene in his re-election bid to represent Michigan’s 35th Senate District.
“Small businesses in mid-Michigan face countless challenges and deserve strong representation in Lansing so their concerns are voiced and addressed,” said SBAM President and CEO Brian Calley .
“We’re proud to endorse Chedrick Greene for the Michigan Senate and look forward to working with him at the Capitol next term to ensure there are less obstacles on that path to success.” “I am proud to be endorsed by the Small Business Association of Michigan’s Small Biz PAC — a leading voice for an enormously important segment of our state’s economy,” said Sen.
Chedrick Greene .
“Together, we’re going to pursue every practical step to make it easier on the small business owners, employees, and aspiring entrepreneurs who have been facing extraordinary financial pressures from Republicans’ trade wars and skyrocketing costs.” The Small Biz PAC is the political action committee of the Small Business Association of Michigan, which endorses candidates who have demonstrated support for Michigan’s small business community, regardless of political party.
For the November 3rd election, Greene received the endorsement along with 18 Republicans and 3 other Democrats running for the Michigan Senate.
SBAM is the latest prominent business group to publicly back the retired Marine and fire Captain, joining the Detroit Regional Chamber, Michigan Restaurant & Lodging Association, and Michigan Credit Union League.
Chedrick Greene is the only candidate in this pivotal race who has the public backing of Michigan’s business, farming, and labor communities.
Michigan Farm Bureau AgriPac endorsed Greene this summer — the first time that organization has endorsed a Democrat for MI-SD-35.
When the Michigan Fraternal Order of Police named Greene as their favored candidate last month, it marked his 25th labor organization endorsement .
Greene continues to grow the broad coalition that reflects the Great Lakes Bay Region residents he serves, and positions him to win re-election.
After his 20-point special election victory , Chedrick Greene was sworn in as Senator for Michigan’s 35th district on May 21, 2026, to complete the final months of the term vacated by Kristen McDonald Rivet who was elected to Congress.
A purple seat in a purple state, MI-SD-35 has been trending red at the presidential level for over a decade.
After McDonald Rivet won the most recent full Senate term election by nearly seven points in 2022, Donald Trump only lost to Kamala Harris in SD-35 by less than one percentage point in 2024.
Absentee voting in Michigan for the November 3, 2026 general election began September 24, 2026.
Michiganders also have nine days of early, in-person voting: Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1, 2026.
