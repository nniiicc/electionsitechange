Get Involved
Winning this race will take more than one person — it will take a community.
Whether you have an hour a week or can jump in whenever you’re available, there is a role for everyone on this campaign.
Volunteers are the heart of this movement.
Every phone call, door knocked, event hosted, and conversation helps reach voters, share Katie Crosby’s message, and build a district that works for everyone.
How You Can Help
Make Phone Calls
Help connect with voters from home or at campaign events.
Phone banking is one of the most effective ways to share information, answer questions, and turn support into action.
Knock on Doors
Face-to-face conversations matter.
Door knocking helps ensure voters know where Katie stands on the issues that impact their families and community.
Host an Event
From house parties to meet-and-greets, hosting an event helps grow the campaign and bring neighbors together around shared values.
Donate
Financial support helps fund voter outreach, printed materials, digital ads, and organizing efforts that keep the campaign moving forward.
Other
Have a special skill or a different way you’d like to help?
Whether it’s graphic design, data entry, event setup, or something else — the campaign would love to hear from you.
