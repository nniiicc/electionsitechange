Pennsylvania State House · District 15
Fighting for Working Families in PA’s 15th House District
I’m Sydney Speicher, a lifelong resident of Washington County and a first-generation college graduate from a working-class family.
I am running to be a representative who listens, acts, and stands up for our community every day.
I want to ensure that every child, every worker, and every family in District 15 can thrive.
Together, we can build a stronger, healthier, and more prosperous district where people feel supported and heard.
Forward for District 15
“Everyday essentials are getting more expensive—Sydney will fight to make life more affordable for working families.”
- Back programs to assist with housing, including initiatives for first-time homebuyers, so families can have a stable place to call home.
- Support local tax relief measures that help working families and seniors manage the rising cost of living.
- Partner with local businesses to create career training programs and living-wage jobs, ensuring that residents can build sustainable careers close to home.
“Big corporations are rushing data centers into Pennsylvania—Sydney will stand up for our communities.”
- Work to strengthen zoning and environmental review standards to prevent projects that could strain local resources or harm our neighborhoods.
- Carefully assess the energy and water impact before approving industrial development to protect residents and the environment.
- Promote community-focused, environmentally responsible technology projects that provide local benefits without negative impacts on our homes and resources.
“Our schools are being shortchanged while costs rise—Sydney will fight for fully funded public education.”
- Advocate for increased state funding to ensure every school in our district has the resources it needs to give children a fair start.
I want to make sure teachers have the support they deserve and that every classroom has enough aid, so students get individualized attention.
- Work to keep class sizes manageable so no student is overlooked and every child has the space to learn and grow.
- Expand mental health and counseling services in schools because I know that emotional well-being is essential for academic success.
- Partner with local businesses and universities to create mentorships, internships, and extracurricular programs that prepare students for the future and strengthen our community.
“Healthcare costs are rising while access shrinks—Sydney will stand up for affordable, accessible care.”
- Push to expand Medicaid and other programs to make healthcare and prescriptions affordable for all families and seniors.
No one should have to choose between paying rent and accessing medical care.
- Advocate for stronger funding for community health centers, especially in rural or underserved areas, to make sure residents have care nearby.
- Promote policies that reduce surprise medical bills and improve transparency, so families can plan and avoid financial hardship.
“Decisions about healthcare are being taken out of women’s hands—Sydney will fight to protect reproductive freedom.”
- Defend access to safe abortion services and reproductive health clinics.
Every woman should have the ability to make decisions about her own body.
- Increase availability of reproductive health education in schools and communities to ensure young people have accurate information.
- Work with local nonprofits to provide support and resources for women and families, because healthcare should be both accessible and compassionate.
“Working families are being left behind while wages lag—Sydney will stand up for workers and labor rights.”
- Fight for fair wages, including increasing the minimum wage and enforcing labor protections, because hardworking people deserve to be paid fairly for their work.
- Advocate for paid family and sick leave programs so families do not have to sacrifice their health or income to care for loved ones.
- Support affordable childcare initiatives that help parents stay in the workforce and build stable, secure futures for their children.
“Short-term profits are being prioritized over our environment—Sydney will fight for responsible, lasting protections.”
- Enforce clean water and air standards to protect the health of our families, especially children and seniors.
- Support renewable energy projects and initiatives to reduce industrial pollution while creating jobs for residents.
- Collaborate with local organizations to monitor environmental hazards and ensure residents are informed and safe.
Pennsylvania’s 15th House District covers 32 municipalities across western Beaver County and western Washington County.
