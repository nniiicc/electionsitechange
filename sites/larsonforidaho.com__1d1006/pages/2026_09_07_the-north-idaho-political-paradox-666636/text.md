During my business career, I discovered that strongly-held beliefs often drive behavior that leads to the opposite outcomes of what was intended.
I see this happening in Idaho today.
People are moving here from liberal states, drawn by Idaho’s conservative reputation.
Their fear that Idaho is headed toward socialism drives them to vote the party line to “keep things conservative.” What they don’t understand is that outside interests have set up shop here, too.
They just use camouflage words like freedom, liberty, conservatism and republican.
They use smokescreen labels to interrupt critical thinking.
Dark money pours into our primary elections to get their loyalist elected.
In the 2026 primary, $3.99M was spent in Independent Expenditures (IEs) to influence the outcome of Idaho primaries.
Until 2008, Idaho was run by Idahoans.
It was a conservative state.
Then outside interests, committed to a national agenda, established themselves in Boise.
In 2011, party leaders successfully sued the state to close the Republican primary.
That made it more cost-effective to buy an election.
Every year, they flood the statehouse with bills built outside of Idaho to further their agenda.
They rate legislation against their own definition of freedom, then grade legislators according to whether they voted their way.
If you look below the surface, the Freedom Foundation’s definition of freedom doesn’t include North Idaho.
The party line says, they cut your taxes.
Look a little deeper.
Their tax cuts cost you more than they return.
The median Idaho family got $127 back from “the biggest tax cut in the history of Idaho” in 2025.
This tax cut left Idaho critically underfunded.
Successive tax cuts have reduced Idaho’s annual state revenue by about a billion dollars each year.
We: Drained the firefighting fund. ( My opponent voted against all funding for fire and was rewarded by the scorecard. ) Took $275M from road projects. ( The average Idaho motorist spends $546 a year on additional vehicle operating costs due to rough roads. ) Dropped healthcare reimbursement rates. ( Bonner General estimates that it will lose $400,000 this year. ) Reduced school funding even more.
(Idaho was already dead last in the country for funding and our rural schools are hit hardest.) We now have $2.7B in unfunded transportation projects .
Do you think Hwy 95 will be a priority?
Those in power stoke false fears of liberalism while pushing us towards insolvency.
It puts us in jeopardy of having to sell off our public lands just to balance the budgets.
If you moved here to escape liberal politics, don’t rush into the arms of its mirror image.
Voting R, without understanding the game, will accelerate our progress toward becoming exactly the thing you fled.
I’m an independent.
Nobody is asking me to pledge my loyalty to party or a platform or a scorecard.
My agenda is to keep our communities from being hollowed out by special interests.
