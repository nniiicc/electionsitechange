As of 7/31, no funds had been released for rural healthcare improvements, only nearly half a million for administration.
All funds must be obligated by 10/30/26 or they go to other states.
States were to start reporting on progress by August.
Several states obligated all of their money prior to 7/31, so they could actually show results.
Idaho had barely started.
Remember that Idaho Freedom Foundation (IFF) scorecard?
In order to release the funds, legislators had to vote to release it.
The IFF score was a punishing -3.
My opponent voted no and kept his near perfect score.
The legislature approved funding on the last day of the legislative session.
Anyone who voted yes, took a hit on the scorecard.
The delay has cost us the ability to show the results.
Results determine future funding.
Now one of our own hospitals is struggling to stay open.
Boundary County will vote on a levy just to keep the Boundary Community Hospital delivering the same level of service.
Think about it.
Your federal tax dollars, that were intended to improve rural health care, still haven’t reached Idaho’s rural providers.
Now you are being asked to fund the same effort through an increase to your property taxes.
The IFF, who backs the property tax elimination scheme, is also penalized every legislator who voted to release the BBB federal funding for your local healthcare.
Makes you go hmmmm.
Other states got busy.
Idaho dragged its feet.
The funds are part of a five year plan to help rural hospitals develop sustainable ways to operate.
The funds are designed to help create systemic change for rural healthcare.
To help them achieve greater sustainability.
That takes time and thought.
Funding for the coming year depends upon results.
OUR STATE LEGISLATURE DIDN’T EVEN RELEASE THE FUNDS until the final day of the legislative session.
Guess how much Idaho will get next year?
Any funds not obligated within the next 19 days will go to other states.
Is that okay with you?
Are you okay with your tax dollars supporting OTHER STATES’ RURAL HEALTHCARE.
This was not some Blue State plan.
This was Donald Trump’s Big Beautiful Bill.
Sometimes you have to invest to keep the community healthy.
Let me say that again.
If Idaho misses the deadline, our tax dollars will subsidize healthcare in other states.
If our rural healthcare providers are unable to demonstrate legitimate results, after starting well behind other states, we will lose funding in the coming years.
The scorecard hurts us more than it helps us.
Where is the accountability?
We need legislative representation that is not beholden to scorecards that won’t disclose their donors.
We need legislative representation that answers only to the people who live here.
