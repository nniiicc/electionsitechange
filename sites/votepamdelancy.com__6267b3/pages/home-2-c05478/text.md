Home DONATIONS Meet Pam The Veteran Nurse News Issues Volunteer Contact Voter Information Colonel Pamela Y.
DeLancy (U.S.
Army, Retired) is a distinguished military veteran, registered nurse, and community leader representing Georgia Public Health District 4 as a Board of Health member , and a 2026 Democratic candidate for Georgia’s 10th Congressional District .
With more than 35 years of military service , Colonel DeLancy’s career reflects a deep commitment to national security, public health, and principled leadership.
Military Service and Leadership Colonel DeLancy began her military career in September 1980, enlisting in the United States Army before earning a commission through the University of Houston ROTC program in 1987.
Colonel DeLancy holds a Master of Science in Strategic Studies from the U.S.
Army War College.
Over more than three decades, she served in senior leadership roles across the U.S.
Army, including Deputy Surgeon for Clinical Operations with the Office of the Chief Army Reserve, Director of Reserve Medical Programs at the Department of Defense, and Brigade Commander of the AMEDD Professional Medical Command at Fort Gillem, Georgia.
Her service earned honors including the Legion of Merit , Defense Meritorious Service Medal , and membership in the Order of Military Medical Merit .
