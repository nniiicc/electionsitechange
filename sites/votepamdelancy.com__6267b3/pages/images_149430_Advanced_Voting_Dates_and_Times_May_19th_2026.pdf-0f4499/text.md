Henry County Elections and Voter Registration Office Notice of Early Voting Henry County, GA – Notice of Advanced Voting for the May 19, 2026, General Primary/Nonpartisan Election.
Voting will be conducted at the following locations and times listed below.
The last day to register to be eligible to vote in the May 19, 2026, General Primary/Nonpartisan Election is April 20, 2026.
Voters may check their voter registration at mvp.sos.ga.gov.
For further information or to request an absentee ballot call 770-288-6448.
Set three weeks before Election Day, Early Voting gives Georgia voters the option of voting in person or by voting absentee by mail. *** EARLY VOTING IN GEORGIA BEGINS ON MONDAY, APRIL 27, 2026 – FRIDAY, MAY 15, 2026.
Any voter registered in Georgia can vote absentee by mail.
To vote absentee by mail, one must complete an application found on the Georgia Secretary of State’s website then mail or drop off the application to the Henry County Registrar’s Office.
The office is located at 1550 South Zack Hinton Pkwy in McDonough.
The application can also be emailed as an attachment to henryabsentee@henrycountyga.gov The deadline to request and submit a Georgia Official Absentee Ballot Application is Friday, May 8, 2026.
Absentee ballots must be received by the Elections Office by Tuesday, May 19, 2026, at 7:00PM.
To vote early in person, one may visit one of the Early Voting precincts in Henry County (listed below).
Remember to bring an acceptable form of identification to include any valid state or federal government issued ID, a student ID from an acceptable Georgia public college or university, a valid U.S.
Passport ID or a valid U.S. military photo ID.
