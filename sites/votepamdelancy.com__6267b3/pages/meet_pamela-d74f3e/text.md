Meet Pam
I was born in Louisiana and raised in Houston, Texas, the oldest of six children.
When my parents divorced during my teenage years, I stepped into responsibility early—helping care for my siblings while my mother worked long hours as a licensed nurse.
By 17, I was working summers in a local restaurant, often performing assistant manager duties.
Those experiences shaped my strong work ethic and taught me accountability, budgeting, and leadership.
For more than 20 years, McDonough and Henry County, Georgia have been home.
My husband and I raised our daughters here; both were cheerleaders at Henry County High School and proudly graduated.
Our oldest child, our son, now lives in Kentucky.
Throughout my military service, I remained deeply engaged in the community—volunteering with Hands of Hope and A Friend’s House alongside my daughters.
More recently, I have volunteered with Reaping the Harvest Outreach Ministries Food Bank, continuing my commitment to serving those in need.
I sit on District 4 Board of Public Health in Henry County.
I am also a proud member of Shiloh Baptist Church and American Legion Post 516.
