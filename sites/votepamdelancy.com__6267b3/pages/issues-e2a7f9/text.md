Healthcare
Healthcare should be accessible and affordable for all families.
I will work to protect and expand access to quality healthcare across Georgia’s 10th District, ensuring that families, seniors, and veterans are not left without coverage.
My focus is on policies that support both mental and physical well-being, because strong communities begin with healthy people.
I will address the threat of veterans earned healthcare benefits being diminished or abolished.
I will address the need for more robust mental health policies with an increased focus on the needs of veterans, youth, senior citizens and individual residents in underserved areas.
Economy
The rising cost of housing and food continues to place a heavy burden on working families and underserved communities.
Too many people are spending a growing share of their income just to meet basic needs like food and shelter.
I will support policies that provide real economic relief for the people of Georgia’s 10th District and help families afford the essentials they need to live and thrive.
Clean water is essential to human life and to the health of our communities.
We must protect this vital resource.
The rapid expansion of data centers in Georgia cannot come at the expense of our water supply, our environment, or the health of our residents.
Economic development should never compromise public health, and we must demand responsible, sustainable growth that puts people first.
Safety
Our community has experienced the tragic loss of life due to gaps in safety, and that reality cannot be ignored.
Every resident deserves to feel safe—whether they are spending time in their neighborhood, attending school, or pursuing their education.
I am committed to working collaboratively with community leaders, educators, law enforcement, and local organizations who share the goal of creating safe, supportive environments.
Public safety is about prevention, accountability, and partnership, and together we can build communities where everyone feels secure and protected.
Immigration
We need a secure border and a functional immigration system with legal policies and procedures that eliminates cruelty, inhumane and sometimes murder of citizens facing apprehension by security personnel.
An oversight committee is needed to train and review that processes and procedures are aligned with the judicial law system.
We can enforce the law and eliminate chaos in the systems but mandating accountability and transparency for the safety and security of all citizens.
I support comprehensive reform that strengthens border security, repair the asylum process, and create a clear, legal pathway for work and maintain family unity.
We can and must build an immigration system that is secure, lawful, and rooted in American values.
