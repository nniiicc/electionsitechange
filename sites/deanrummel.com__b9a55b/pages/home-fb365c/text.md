DEAN RUMMEL
NORTH DAKOTA SENATE
A lifelong Dickinson resident, business leader, and State Senator, Dean Rummel delivers results for District 37.
With decades of experience balancing budgets and a record of conservative leadership in the Legislature, Dean is focused on protecting taxpayers, strengthening our economy, and keeping North Dakota strong for the next generation.
DEAN'S PRIORITIES
LEADERSHIP & IMPACT
Dean has a proven track record of leading District 37 with experience, common sense, and fiscal discipline.
He has earned respect for his leadership in the Senate, holding key roles, including:
- Vice Chair of Senate Finance and Taxation Committee
- Chairman of the Tax Reform and Relief Advisory Interim Committee
- Member of the Standing Transportation Committee and Interim Judiciary Committee
As Vice Chair of the Senate Finance and Taxation Committee, he was instrumental in passing the largest property tax relief and reform package in North Dakota history, allowing eligibility for all primary residents to access a $1,600 property tax credit.
The package also capped local political subdivisions to a 3% increase on their property tax budgets, giving families and seniors real, lasting property tax relief.
As a business leader, Dean Rummel spent nearly 40 years at TMI Systems Design, rising from staff accountant to President and Chief Operating Officer before retiring in 2017.
His career was built on responsible growth, balanced budgets, and long-term planning — the same principles he brings to state government today.
ROOTED IN FAMLY & COMMUNITY
Dean and his wife Danita have raised their family in Dickinson, where they remain deeply involved in the community.
Dean has served on the Dickinson School Board, as School Board President, on regional healthcare and human service boards, and with numerous civic, education, and public safety organizations over several decades.
He understands District 37 because he’s lived it — as a parent, employer, taxpayer, public servant and volunteer.
