Priorities
Lower Costs for All Residents
As State Senator, David will focus on practical solutions that put money back in people's pockets.
That means supporting policies that lower everyday costs, expanding access to affordable housing, strengthening public transportation, and ensuring state government works for taxpayers—not special interests.
Connecticut should be a place where families can afford to live, work, and build a future.
David will fight to make that a reality.
Hold Eversource Accountable
It's time for stronger oversight and real accountability.
David will fight for greater transparency in how utility rates are set, push for reforms that put consumers first, oppose efforts that shift unnecessary costs onto ratepayers, and fight against Eversource's influence over policymakers—starting with his opponent.
Connecticut families deserve reliable energy service at a fair price, and utility companies must be held accountable when they put profits ahead of the people they serve.
Lower Property Taxes
We need a smarter partnership between state and local government.
David will work to increase support for municipalities, reduce pressure on local budgets, and pursue policies that help communities fund essential services without relying so heavily on property tax increases.
By tackling the root causes of high property taxes, we can provide relief to families while maintaining the quality schools, public safety services, and community resources residents depend on.
Deliver More for Seniors
David will fight to protect programs that help seniors stay in their communities, expand access to affordable healthcare and prescriptions, and strengthen services that support independent living.
Whether it's protecting retirement security, improving transportation options, or expanding tax relief, David believes Connecticut must do more to support the seniors who have contributed so much to our state.
Fight for Education Funding
David will advocate for fair and reliable education funding so schools have the resources they need to help students thrive.
That means supporting teachers, investing in modern classrooms and technology, expanding career and technical education opportunities, and ensuring students receive the academic and mental health support they need.
Education is one of the smartest investments we can make, and David will work to ensure every community gets its fair share of state support.
