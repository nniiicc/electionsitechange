Mission Statement
Our communities in California’s 32nd District represent the very best of the American story-hardworking families, diverse cultures, innovative businesses, and people who care deeply about the place they call home.
From Riverside, Orange, San Bernardino, and San Diego Counties, we deserve leadership that listens, leads with integrity, and delivers real results.
I am running for State Senate because I believe government should work for everyone-not just the well-connected or the powerful.
As a physician and community advocate, I have spent my career helping people navigate complex systems and find solutions that improve their quality of life.
Now I am ready to bring that same level of compassion, expertise, and problem-solving spirit to Sacramento.
My mission is simple but powerful: people, progress, and purpose.
Building a future where everyone can thrive, not just survive.
Together, we can make the 32nd District a model of what’s possible when we lead with compassion, stand up for fairness, and put people first.
