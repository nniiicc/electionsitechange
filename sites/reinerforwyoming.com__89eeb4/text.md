Contact Donate Keeping Wyoming Strong, Innovative, and a Great Place to Raise a Family I’m Luke Reiner, retired Commanding General of the Wyoming Military, Director of WYDOT, husband, father, and grandfather, and I’m running for Wyoming House District 8 because I believe our community deserves steady, thoughtful leadership grounded in service, responsibility, and respect for others.
Why I’m Running Our communities deserve leaders who listen carefully, work collaboratively, and make informed decisions with integrity and common sense.
I want to help ensure future generations can continue to live, work, and raise their families here while preserving the opportunities and traditions that make Wyoming special.
Priorities Wyoming is a place where families put down roots, where hard work is valued, and where our communities are shaped by the people, industries, and landscapes that make this state unlike anywhere else.
I’m committed to protecting that way of life while helping ensure Wyoming remains strong, resilient, and prepared for the opportunities and challenges ahead.
