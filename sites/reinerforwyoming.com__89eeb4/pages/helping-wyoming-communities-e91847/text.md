| Supporting Wyoming Communities |
| |
| |
| I believe every family deserves to live in a community that offers opportunity, security, and a bright future for the next generation.
I will work to ensure our communities continue to thrive by focusing on the priorities that matter most to Wyoming families.
Creating and supporting thriving communities means supporting jobs, expanding access to quality healthcare, increasing the availability of affordable housing, and ensuring every student has access to a quality education.
We must build communities that meet the needs and expectations of our children and grandchildren.
Throughout my career as an accountant, military, and community leader, I have been able to see that lasting solutions come from listening and working together to solve challenges.
I will continue to advocate for practical, common-sense policies that strengthen our district while preserving the Wyoming values that make our communities such special places to live, work, and raise a family. |
