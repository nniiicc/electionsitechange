Contact Donate Luke’s Priorities As a longtime Wyoming resident, I know the issues people truly care about, and I understand how to find solutions that work in the real world.
Pro-Second Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is a fundamental constitutional liberty that has long been part of Wyoming's way of life.
As a lifelong hunter and outdoorsman, I understand the importance of responsible firearm ownership and the traditions that accompany it.
In my family, firearms have always been used safely and responsibly for hunting, protecting livestock and property, and personal defense.
Passing those values on through education and safe handling is just as important as protecting the right itself.
I will continue to defend the Second Amendment and oppose efforts to weaken the rights of responsible, law-abiding citizens.
Pro-Life I believe every human life has inherent worth and deserves to be protected.
That conviction extends to the unborn and shapes my support for Wyoming's pro-life laws and policies.
At the same time, I believe we should provide meaningful support to mothers, children, and families by strengthening adoption opportunities, improving the foster care system, and investing in education and community resources that help children succeed.
Building a culture that values life means offering both legal protections and practical support, and I will continue working toward policies that reflect those principles.
Pro-Family Values Strong families have always been the foundation of Wyoming communities.
For generations, our neighbors have built their lives through hard work, personal responsibility, respect for others, and a willingness to serve.
Those values continue to make Wyoming a place where families want to live, work, and raise their children.
As our state grows and changes, we must preserve the principles that have made our communities strong.
That means defending parents' rights, promoting safe schools and neighborhoods, and supporting policies that keep Wyoming an affordable place where families can build a future for generations to come.
Pro-Individual Property Rights The right of an individual to acquire, keep and enjoy his own property is foundational.
Every individual has the right to develop his or her own potential, to use and enjoy his own property, tangible or intellectual, and pursue his own interests, free from the restrictions of arbitrary force.
Individuals are always presumed to be the best stewards of their property.
I support responsible property tax relief that helps keep homeownership within reach while ensuring local governments remain accountable.
I have backed efforts to create a more stable, predictable tax system.
Government should protect private property rights and allow families to keep more of what they have worked hard to earn.
Balanced Budget Responsible budgeting is one of the most important duties of government.
Wyoming has a long tradition of living within its means, and I believe that principle should guide every spending decision.
With a degree in Business and Accounting, and years of experience as a Certified Public Accountant, I understand the value of careful financial management.
As Director of WYDOT, I oversaw a budget of more than $850 million while maintaining fiscal discipline and accountability.
These experiences have given me a deep understanding of the flow of money within large organizations.
In the Legislature, I will continue to support balanced budgets, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, and policies that keep government efficient, transparent, and focused on its core responsibilities.
Business Development Wyoming must remain open for business and continue creating an environment where businesses can grow and succeed.
By attracting and retaining a vast array of industries, we can strengthen our economy and create lasting opportunities for future generations.
I want to ensure our children and grandchildren have the opportunity to build successful careers in Wyoming.
Energy Wyoming has an opportunity to lead in both traditional and emerging energy sectors that create long-term jobs and strengthen our state’s economy.
I believe we must continue to support our core oil, coal, and gas industries while also ensuring Wyoming remains competitive in an evolving energy landscape, including the safe development of nuclear energy and the expansion of uranium mining and processing.
Thriving Communities We must have communities that thrive in Wyoming, communities that meet the needs and expectations of our next generations.
We must aggressively work to build an environment that helps keep our kids and grandkids here in the state.
I see those minimum requirements as excellent jobs, available healthcare, affordable housing, and a quality education.
Military As a military man myself, I know firsthand the sacrifices that our brave soldiers make to ensure our freedoms.
Supporting the men and women who serve our nation and the families who stand beside them is crucial for maintaining our strength and our values.
We must continue honoring their sacrifice and strengthen opportunities for veterans, active-duty personnel, and military families across Wyoming.
Agriculture Agriculture has long been a cornerstone of Wyoming’s economy, culture, and way of life.
Growing up around my family’s farming operations taught me the value of hard work, stewardship, and the importance of rural communities.
I am committed to supporting policies that keep agriculture strong, sustainable, and viable for future generations.
Infrastructure Our roads, power grids, water systems, and other critical infrastructure must remain reliable and resilient to meet the needs of our communities and businesses.
Having served as Director of WYDOT, I understand firsthand the importance of maintaining and improving the systems that keep Wyoming connected.
We must continue making smart investments that prepare our state for the future while responsibly managing taxpayer dollars.
Public Lands For generations, Wyoming's public lands have shaped our economy, our traditions, and the outdoor lifestyle that makes our state unique.
From the wilderness and rivers where families hunt and fish to the ranches and small businesses that depend on healthy landscapes, public lands play a vital role in the lives of Wyoming residents.
Protecting these lands is essential to the well-being of local families, businesses, and future generations.
As a lifelong hunter and outdoorsman, I understand the importance of public lands and open spaces to Wyoming's way of life.
I do not support selling federal or state public lands but would support land swaps that increase public access.
While public land policy is addressed at the state level, ranchers, sportsmen, local businesses, and nearby communities deserve a meaningful voice in shaping Wyoming's approach.
I will work to ensure state leaders listen to local experience, protect public access, and preserve these lands for future generations.
