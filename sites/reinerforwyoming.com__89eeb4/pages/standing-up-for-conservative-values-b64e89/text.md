Contact Donate Luke Reiner for House District 8 This is Luke Reiner, candidate for House District 8.
Wyoming has always been a place where freedom, faith, and personal responsibility matter.
Those values have shaped our communities for generations, and they're the values I'll continue fighting for in the Legislature.
I will stand firm in defending our constitutional rights, protecting Wyoming families, and keeping government accountable to the people it serves.
Supporting Conservative Values Protecting the Second Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is a fundamental constitutional liberty that has long been part of Wyoming's way of life.
Instilled during my upbringing and solidified during my years in uniform, I understand the importance of responsible firearm ownership and the traditions that accompany it.
I'm proud to have received an AQ (pro-gun candidate) rating from the NRA.
This is the highest rating a candidate can receive without a voting record.
As I've been my whole life, I am committed to defending the Second Amendment.
Standing for Life I am pro-life and believe every human life has inherent worth and deserves to be protected.
I will continue to stand for policies that protect life and support Wyoming families.
Individual Property Rights The right of an individual to acquire, keep, and enjoy his/her own property is foundational.
Individuals are always presumed to be the best stewards of their property.
I support a responsible property tax structure that keeps homeownership within reach while ensuring government remains funded and operational.
Government should protect private property rights and allow families to keep more of what they have worked hard to earn.
Keeping Government Responsible Taxpayers deserve leaders who spend their money wisely.
As a Certified Public Accountant and former Director of WYDOT, I understand the importance of balanced budgets, fiscal discipline, and transparency.
In the Legislature, I will vote to support balanced budgets, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, and policies that keep government efficient, transparent, and focused on its core responsibilities.
Wyoming's conservative values are worth protecting, and I'll continue working to ensure our state remains a place where families, businesses, and future generations can thrive.
If you have questions about my legislative priorities, please respond to this email.
I ask for your vote on August 18th.
