Meet Steven
Representative Steven Sainz, a proud Camden County High School and Valdosta State University graduate, earned a Bachelor of Arts in Public Administration and a Master of Business Administration from Georgia Tech.
Currently Managing Principal of Camden Connection, a community development corporation, Steven played a key role in securing $18 million for community projects in infrastructure, health, and education.
Before his legislative role, he was a deputy sheriff for the Camden County Sheriff’s Office, teaching the C.H.A.M.P.S. curriculum to elementary and middle school students.
Actively engaged in the community, Steven chaired the planning commission for Woodbine, served on the Camden County Chamber of Commerce board, and was the executive committee secretary for the Woodbine Lions Club.
Steven also serves on the Saint Marys Salvation Army community advisory board.
With a diverse background in education, law enforcement, and community development, Steven Sainz brings extensive experience and dedication to House District 180.

on The Issues
Representative Steven Sainz is deeply committed to the values of House District 180, prioritizing safe communities, family prosperity, student success, and funding for our district.
He ardently supports conservative policies, including boosting the local economy, backing law enforcement and first responders, and safeguarding Constitutional rights.

Steven has worked with the House Study Committee on State & Local Law Enforcement Salaries to look at the possibility of a statewide law enforcement retirement plan.
