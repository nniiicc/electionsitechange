Affordable Healthcare
No family should have to choose between paying for medicine and putting food on the table.
Rural Iowans are also seeing decreasing access to hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies.
Healthcare costs are crushing working families in our district.
Polly will work to expand Medicaid coverage, lower prescription drug costs, and ensure that mental health services are accessible to everyone.
Polly’s Plan
- Expand Medicaid to cover more working families
- Lower prescription costs by enabling state-level drug price negotiation
- Invest in mental health with more community clinics and school counselors
- Protect coverage for pre-existing conditions
- Reverse the privatization of Medicaid, which has resulted in higher out-of-pocket costs for families.
