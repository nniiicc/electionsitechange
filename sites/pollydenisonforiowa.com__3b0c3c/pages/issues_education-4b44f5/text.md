Polly believes that education is the cornerstone of a thriving community.
As your State House representative, she will fight for increased per-pupil funding in our public schools, competitive teacher salaries, and expanded access to early childhood education programs.
Polly believes the school voucher experiment in the State of Iowa has failed our children, and should be repealed.
Polly’s Plan Increase public school funding to ensure every classroom has the resources it needs Support teachers with competitive pay and professional development Expand pre-K access so every family can give their child a strong start Modernize facilities to create safe, inspiring learning environments Support Polly's fight for investing in our public schools.
