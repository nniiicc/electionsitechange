Donate Now Learn More ↓ Meet Your Candidate Meet Polly Denison Polly Denison is running for the Iowa House of Representatives, serving District 84.
“I’m running because Iowans—and the people of the 84th District—deserve better from our government,” Denison said.
“We deserve representatives who listen and reflect our common values.
I promise to be that representative.
I will take the time to learn the issues that matter most to you.” Polly Denison was born in Iowa City into a family deeply rooted in community service.
She graduated from the University of Iowa with a double major in Political Science and Psychology.
Her father was a small business owner who provided fair wages and benefits to his employees, while her mother served as a nurse at the University of Iowa Hospitals & Clinics.
Together, they raised four children and instilled in them the values of hard work, volunteerism, and community.
After college, Polly worked as a paralegal at the Arizona Department of Health Services, the Johnson County Attorney’s Office, and later at a Cedar Rapids law firm.
In 2013, she shifted careers, earning her insurance licenses and building a practice dedicated to helping families make informed financial decisions.
Polly is married to Neal Denison, a proud AFSCME Local 620 member.
The couple moved to Van Horne to enjoy the quiet of rural life, quickly forming strong community ties in Benton and Linn Counties.
“As your Representative, I will be a strong voice for children, families, and working Iowans across District 84 and beyond,” Denison said.
“I look forward to listening to you and working together to make Iowa stronger.” For more information, please contact Polly Denison at 319-530-9184 or pollydenisonforiowa@gmail.com .
