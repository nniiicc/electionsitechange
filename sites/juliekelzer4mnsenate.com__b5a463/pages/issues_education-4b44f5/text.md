As of March 2026, 50 school districts across the state have seclusion rooms.
These need to be outlawed.
The 2023 law banning use of these rooms for for students in 3rd grade and below did not go far enough.
No one deserves to be put into a padded room that is locked from the outside.
Data indicates that 100% of the children placed into these rooms have disabilities.
Every child deserves a high-quality education no matter where they live.
The current funding calculation method between districts needs updating.
Each district is not funded equally per student.
Our rural school districts receive less funding per student than the schools located closer to the Twin Cities.
Money should not be a factor for students to be in youth programs and sports.
Every child deserves to be able to participate in extra curricular activities.
