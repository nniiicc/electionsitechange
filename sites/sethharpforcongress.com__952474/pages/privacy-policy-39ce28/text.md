This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our website, make a contribution, sign up for updates, or otherwise interact with the Campaign. ​ Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including but not limited to: Name Email address Phone number ZIP code and other location information Contribution information (when making a donation) Online contributions are processed securely through ActBlue, a third-party payment processor.
The Campaign does not store your full credit card information.
We may also collect limited non-personal information automatically, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, to help improve our website and communications. ​ How We Use Your Information The Campaign may use your information to: Process donations and comply with federal campaign finance laws Communicate with you about the campaign, events, volunteer opportunities, and updates Respond to your inquiries or requests Improve our website, outreach, and voter engagement efforts Comply with applicable legal and reporting requirements Text Messages and Email Communications By providing your phone number or email address, you consent to receive campaign-related communications from Seth Harp for Congress.
Message and data rates may apply for SMS messages.
Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may unsubscribe from emails using the link provided in those communications.
Sharing of Information The Campaign does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share information only with: Trusted service providers who assist with campaign operations (such as email, texting, or compliance services) ActBlue, for the purpose of processing contributions Government agencies, as required by law, including the Federal Election Commission Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
Third-Party Websites Our website may include links to third-party websites, including ActBlue.
This Privacy Policy applies only to Seth Harp for Congress.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.
Children’s Information This website is not intended for children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
