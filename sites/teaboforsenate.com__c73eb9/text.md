SHANNON TEABO
FOR STATE SENATE, WORCESTER & HAMPSHIRE DISTRICT
As a mother with experience in the service industry, I have felt the impacts firsthand of rising costs vastly outpacing increases in wages and opportunities.
I believe that our elected officials need to do more to ensure their constituents can afford to live.
If elected, I will fight for policies that allow families and individuals across Massachusetts to live without fearing they won't have enough money to cover for basic necessities.
