Need a bag?
Your choices may soon be paper or thick plastic
Washington is on the cusp of outlawing thin plastic carry-out bags and promoting paper bags as an alternative.
A bill imposing a statewide ban on single-use plastic bags is on its way to Gov.
Jay Inslee.
The measure, if signed, would preempt similar prohibitions in force in four cities in Snohomish County.
