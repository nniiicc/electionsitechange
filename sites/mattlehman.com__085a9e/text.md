Matt Lehman State Representative, House District 79 It is truly an honor and a privilege to serve in the Indiana General Assembly representing House District 79.
It is a responsibility that I take very seriously.
Northeast Indiana is a wonderful place to live, work and raise a family.
By working hard for the values that we share, I am convinced that we can make Indiana even better.
We will strengthen Indiana’s economy by creating both a taxpayer-friendly and pro-business environment.
We will continue to offer future generations of Hoosiers an excellent education system.
And we will work to protect the values that have made Indiana the place where more people want to live than to leave.
As a lifelong Hoosier I want my children and grandchildren to experience the greatness of Indiana as I have and I will work to keep the American dream alive.
Please use this website and the provided links to assist you and feel free to contact me with any issues, concerns or comments.
