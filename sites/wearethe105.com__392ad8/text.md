We are the 105.
Across the 105th, there's plenty of work to do — but four problems can't wait.
Four issues, one squeeze.
These aren't separate problems — they're the same problem, hitting from four sides.
We'll take on the cost of food , housing , and healthcare , because nobody should have to prove their worthiness of a meal, shelter, or care.
We'll raise wages and protect skilled trades .
We'll back small businesses and demand universal healthcare.
Because when families finally get ahead of one bill, they shouldn't be drowning in the next.
