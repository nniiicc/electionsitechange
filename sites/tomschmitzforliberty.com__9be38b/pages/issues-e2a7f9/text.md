ISSUES
A Foreign Policy of Peace, Due Process, and Non-Interventionism
The war in Iran is an immoral war of aggression and a strategic disaster for the United States.
Tragically, American troops have now died in this needless, senseless, illegal war.
We had no right to attack Iran, a nation that did not attack us and where there was no imminent attack coming.
Nobody wants Iran to have a nuclear weapon, but only through peaceful negotiation, diplomacy, and communication could we ever hope to prevent Iran from having the ability to eventually procure a nuclear weapon if they chose to do so.
We must end the war with Iran as soon as possible and urgently pursue diplomacy.
The bombing of alleged drug boats in the Caribbean is a betrayal of our sacred American values of due process and the presumption of innocence.
These cruel extrajudicial killings shock the conscience and are overthrowing the traditional American criminal justice system.
The invasion of Venezuela and kidnapping of head of state Nicholas Maduro was an unjustified and immoral attack on a sovereign nation that did not attack us.
The sovereign people of Venezuela should be left alone.
We need decisive change to a foreign policy of peace and non-interventionism.
The United States’ current interventionist foreign policy is causing unnecessary harm and suffering here at home in the United States and around the world.
We need to stop policing the world, interfering in other nation's affairs, and engaging in endless wars.
Our current interventionist foreign policy is not in our national security interest, it makes us less safe, it unnecessarily places our brave young men and women in harm's way, and it results in catastrophic loss of life for everyone involved.
In addition, we can't afford these expansive military operations, as we are now nearly 40 trillion dollars in debt.
Spending on the military industrial complex is now completely out of control.
It is outrageous to spend over 1 trillion dollars on national defense.
We also must end all foreign aid, across the board.
It shocks the conscience to take money from the American people through direct taxation or inflation, and then to give that money, which rightfully belongs to the American people, to foreign governments and citizens of other countries.
Uniparty's Endless Money Printing and Forced Collectivism Are Causes of Affordability Crisis, Free Markets Are the Solution
The number one problem with affordability in the United States is rampant inflation.
Inflation is caused primarily by the uniparty government printing up trillions and trillions of dollars out of thin air.
This dilutes the money supply and causes the dollar to rapidly lose its spending power.
This uniparty inflation is an insidious regressive taxation that crushes the poor and the middle class, it hurts them the most.
The uniparty government politicians will never stop printing up money, and that is the main cause of inflation.
Politicians cannot be trusted to stop spending.
As long as they control the printing presses, they will never stop printing money.
The only solution is to take this power to manage currency away from the state and return it to the people.
We must transition away from our current fiat currency system and embrace a free market, voluntary monetary system.
Separating currency and state is the only way to end inflation.
The next part of affordability is the state's constant destruction, interference, and stifling of the free market.
This government's extensive involvement in healthcare and education industries in particular is especially destructive, as the costs of both healthcare and education have now spiraled completely out of control in accordance with the increasing level of tyrannical government control over these industries.
The more the government is involved in any industry, the more prices go up and spiral out of control.
We must embrace free market competition in all areas of our economy.
The free market always drives prices down as far as possible, often times far exceeding expectations, and gets the most goods and services to the most people in the most efficient manner possible.
Separation of Healthcare and State, Especially for Patients with Pre-Existing Conditions and the Indigent
The federal government has continued to strengthen its iron grip on the healthcare industry, stifling patient freedom of choice, eliminating free market competition, and taking us down a dark path toward full authoritarian government control of our healthcare system.
With government controlled healthcare, everyone will be at the mercy of a massive government bureaucracy to provide for themselves and their family.
When this monolithic, government controlled system proves unsatisfactory and unacceptable, fundamentally, there will be no recourse, and no escape from it.
We need separation of healthcare and state, a liberated healthcare system free from government coercion and control, a free-market system which will drive down prices, inspire innovation, and increase quality through competition.
We need a healthcare system where patients and healthcare providers interact with each other voluntarily, and where patients are free to pursue the medications and treatments they want without any interference from government.
Only the patient has the right to decide what medication they take or what treatment they pursue, because it is their body, and it is their choice.
The FDA has absolutely no right to forcibly prevent a patient from taking a medication or pursuing a medical treatment.
Regarding pre-existing conditions, a free market system is the only ethical answer to this problem, and it is also the most practical.
Free market competition and innovation will provide a myriad of options and solutions for those with pre-existing conditions who don't have health insurance.
Only through free market innovation and competition can we solve this problem.
The free market always rises to meet customer demand, often in unpredictable ways, and it always drives prices down, often in unforeseeable ways.
Patients with pre-existing conditions have the right to a free market healthcare system and the incredible benefits that system will produce.
The government has taken this right away from them.
Artificial Intelligence
A.I. is a true double-edge sword.
While I am hopeful about this new technology and remain an A.I. optimist, I recognize the potential serious and significant harm that could also result from this technology falling into the wrong hands or escaping human control.
I appreciate that the A.I. industry is taking steps to self-regulate, but I also believe that there is a role for government in this space, for the same reason there is a reason for government to prohibit drunk driving, or on a larger scale, to stop the proliferation of nuclear weapons.
Put simply, after spending a significant amount of time listening to experts on all sides and researching this issue myself, I believe A.I will very soon present a substantial risk of involuntary harm, possibly at a catastrophic scale.
Addressing these risks is one of the very few things the federal government should be doing to protect the American people.
End the Tyrannical Drug War
We need to end the war on drugs, because people voluntarily taking a substance into their body is their right, and should be their choice.
Voluntary drug use in and of itself is a victimless crime.
Currently controlled substances should be legalized and treated the same under the law as alcohol.
Most drug overdose deaths are from street drugs where the user didn't know exactly what they were ingesting.
Freedom would allow for drug users to know exactly how much of a drug they were taking, and would ensure the substances were not contaminated.
Further, ending drug prohibition would put violent black market drug cartels out of business immediately.
Address Climate Change Crisis by Removing Government Barriers to Nuclear Power which is the Feasible, Available Solution with Virtually Zero Carbon Emissions
The federal government has failed to protect our environment.
Because the federal government is so big, bloated, and involved in so many areas it should not be involved in, the federal government has neglected one of its only legitimate responsibilities: protecting our environment.
It is important to note that the federal government is by far the biggest polluter in the United States, no other entity comes close.
This EPA failed the American people when in 2010 it allowed BP to dump millions of gallons of the hazardous chemical "Corexit" into the gulf of Mexico as an impulsive response to the Deepwater Horizon oil spill.
This Corexit dumping, approved by the EPA, only exacerbated the problem and added to the long-term ecological damage and human suffering caused by this disaster.
We need a revamped environmental protection agency that is one main sector of a limited government, where government can focus dramatically increased effort and attention on protecting our environment.
In addition, this government has failed to effectively fight global climate change by severely blunting the development of nuclear power.
Nuclear power, in terms of carbon emissions, is by far the cleanest mass produced source of energy we have today.
Nuclear power plants produce virtually zero carbon emissions.
Nuclear power, here and now, is exactly what we need to fight global climate change.
The government needs to stop restricting the development and production of nuclear power immediately.
While I'm optimistic about green energy sources being primary generators of electric power in the future, we have clean nuclear technology right now with the potential to power the entire country.
Experts and scientists are telling us we are in an urgent situation with global climate change.
We need to heed their admonitions.
The government needs to immediately cease forcibly preventing nuclear power development and production.
Freedom of Conscience and Voluntary Taxation
We need to implement a system of voluntary taxation.
Nobody should be forced to provide the fruits of their labor to further policy and government actions they don't agree with.
Having your money taken from you and then having that money spent on things you believe to be morally reprehensible is a violation of your freedom of conscience.
Further, no person should be forced to be responsible for another, no person should be forced to labor for the benefit of another, or to have the fruits of their labor forcibly taken from them for the benefit of another.
A free society requires that the government's broken, fragile, and unsustainable safety net rooted in coercion be phased out and replaced with sustainable, voluntary charity.
Eliminate All Bailouts, Handouts, Giveaways, and Subsidies
Taxpayers should never have their money, which has been taken from them against their will, used to bailout failing corporations, banks, other business entities, or individuals, for any reason.
There is no such thing as "too big to fail".
In a free market, failing entities must be allowed to fail with no exceptions.
Further, government should not play favorites by awarding subsidies to certain companies while not awarding subsidies to others.
Subsidies are not only rooted in coercive taxation, but also unjustly provide one entity a competitive advantage over another in the marketplace.
The Right to Keep and Bear Arms
We need to vigilantly protect our right to keep an bear arms; the American people should never be helpless, defenseless victims.
The right to bear arms is a human right.
The 2nd Amendment is important in that it recognizes this human right, but our right to keep and bear arms does not come from government.
We must resist the expansion of "red flag" laws, which are a dangerous slippery slope toward government obtaining the power to disarm American Citizens at will and without any semblance of due process.
Criminal Justice Reform
We need real, substantive policy change in our law enforcement and criminal system.
In addition to criminal prosecution, it is also essential that police officers who violate the rights of others be held personally liable in civil court.
I stand for ending qualified immunity, which will allow for civil action against individual police officers who have harmed others and violated their individual rights.
While this is only one of many issues that needs to be addressed, ending qualified immunity would be a small but important step in the right direction toward fighting injustice and addressing racial disparities in our justice system.
Unions are Essential
Unions have the right to organize, collectively bargain, and strike.
Organized labor is fundamental to workers being able to negotiate for a wage that reflects the value of their labor.
Unions are a critical and essential part of the free market and must always be allowed to form and flourish freely without any interference from government.
