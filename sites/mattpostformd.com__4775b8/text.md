By and For District 14
Matt Post has called District 14 home his entire life.
A graduate of Sherwood High School & Yale Law, he’s never stopped fighting for us: strengthening our schools on the Board of Education, making our community safer in the movement to end gun violence, and defending public education in the labor movement.
Now, Matt’s running to bring new energy to the once-in-a-generation policy challenges facing our state.
Former School Board Member
On the Montgomery County school board, Matt championed career pathways education, expanded mental health resources, secured hundreds of thousands in funding for immigrant students, and got the votes to close school for Eid al-Fitr.
Leader in the Gun Safety Movement
After leading March For Our Lives’s 2018 new voter turnout program, Matt started its legal team, defending the constitutionality of hard fought, common sense gun safety measures all the way up to the Supreme Court.
Labor Attorney in Public Education
In the labor movement, Matt fights for teachers, bus drivers, secretaries and support professionals across Maryland, working to lift wages and ensure our schools have the resources and support they need to uplift every student.

The Strongest Pushback to Trump
- 🧢Stop ICE agents from hiding behind masks through real penalties, including drivers’ license suspensions
- 📚Strengthen protections against book bans by requiring officials to certify they have read the books they seek to remove and making the decisions of librarian-and-parent review committees final
- 🛑Prevent ICE detention facility expansion by requiring state approval before any utility hookup may serve a large secure detention facility
- ⚖️Empower prosecutors to criminally charge ICE agents who use excessive force
The Fairest
Economy
- 💡Lower energy bills by requiring data centers to cover the cost of their power needs
- ⚡Ban utilities from charging us for speculative costs by prohibiting the use of forecast test years
- 💰Prohibit the utility companies from donating to state political candidates responsible for regulating them
- 📄Reduce paperwork & permitting/licensing delays for small businesses
- ⚖️Fund a Worker Protection Unit in the Attorney General’s office to prosecute wage theft
- 🔓Ban the use of anticompetitive non-compete agreements for all employees
The Best
Public Schools
- 🧑🔧Expand career education & mandate financial literacy class for high schoolers
- 🍎Provide free breakfast and lunch to every student in every school
- 👩🏫Require central office staff to substitute teach in classrooms
- 📚Regularly audit state-imposed administrative tasks on teachers to free up time for teaching and learning
- 💵Create a minimum-wage for paraeducators, custodians, and other education support professionals
- ☎️Fund the special education support hotline to help parents navigating the IEP/504 process
The Safest
Communities
- 🕊️Tax gun companies for every shooting their products enable in Maryland to fund violence intervention programs that address the root causes of gun violence
- 🛡️Require owners of the most commonly stolen or misused firearms to carry liability insurance
- 🚫Strengthen Maryland’s protective order system to block domestic abusers from owning firearms from the start of the process
- 🔇Ban the sale of suppressors statewide
- 🏥Fund hospital-based support services for victims and survivors of gun violence
The Most Accountable Government
- 💰Ban corporate donations to state-level political candidates
- 📈Require Marylanders running for Congress to disclose whether they intend to trade individual stocks and include that information on the ballot
- 📺Require campaign ads run by PACs and Ballot Issue Committees to list their top 3 funders so voters know who is really behind the message
- 🚪Prohibit former legislators from lobbying for at least 5 years after leaving office to close the revolving door between government and special interests
The Most Responsive Government
- ⏱️Guarantee all MVA appointments start within 15 minutes of the scheduled time, or any service fee is waived
- 🪪Implement a money-back guarantee for all delayed state permits and licenses
- 🏗️Build up state capacity to build big projects faster by limiting the use of consultants and hiring more in-house engineers, planners, and project managers, with pay scales pegged to the private-sector
- ⚖️Transition to “Best Value” procurement for state construction contracts
- 📨Require state agencies to respond to complaints within 30 business days of filing
The Most Abundant Affordable Housing
- 🏘️Convert underutilized public land into affordable housing dedicated to school staff and first responders
- 🚉Eliminate mandatory parking minimums for new housing developments located near public transit
- 🏠Enact just cause eviction protections
- 🏢Legalize single-stairway apartment buildings
- 🚇Allow multi-family housing by-right within walking distance to MARC and Metro stations
- ⚖️Set fair housing targets based on jobs-to-homes balance and require faster approvals in counties with inadequate housing
The Healthiest Environment
- 🌍Require large transportation projects to measure and offset their greenhouse gas emissions by investing in green infrastructure and public transit
- ⚖️Empower the Attorney General to more aggressively prosecute corporations for polluting our waterways and air
- ⚡Establish “shot clock” permitting for green energy & public transportation projects to speed up construction
- 🥩Expand state green bank investment eligibility to support next-generation cultivated and alternative proteins that reduce agricultural emissions
- 🥚Ban the use of cruel battery cages in egg production
