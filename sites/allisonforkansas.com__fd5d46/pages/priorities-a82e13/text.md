Allison knows that struggle—because she’s lived it herself.
As a single mom and working professional, she understands how rising costs for housing, groceries, utilities, and childcare can stretch a paycheck to the breaking point.
In office, Allison will fight for practical solutions that actually ease the burden on working families.
That means supporting affordable housing, lowering utility costs, and ensuring wages keep up with the real cost of living.
Kansans deserve the chance not just to get by—but to get ahead.
Healthcare Healthcare costs shouldn’t force families to choose between their well-being and their budgets.
Allison has seen firsthand how hard it is for working families to access affordable care, especially when unexpected medical bills pile up.
She supports expanding Medicaid to ensure more Kansans can get the care they need—whether it’s for routine checkups, emergency care, or managing chronic conditions.
Healthcare is a basic necessity, and Allison is committed to making it more accessible and affordable for every family in our community.
Public Education Strong schools are the foundation of a thriving community—and every child deserves a quality education.
Allison believes investing in public education means investing in our future.
She’s committed to fully funding schools so teachers have the resources they need, classrooms aren’t overcrowded, and students get the support to succeed.
Education should open doors, not create barriers.
Allison will stand up for policies that prepare our kids for real opportunities and build a brighter future for all families.
