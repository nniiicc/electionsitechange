Meet Andre
Leadership Rooted in Service and Community
Andre Stafford is a lifelong Georgian, restaurant manager, and community leader running for State House District 42.
Guided by faith and a commitment to service, Andre believes leadership should be practical, compassionate, and focused on real solutions for working families.
He’s running to bring common sense, accountability, and respect back to the Georgia General Assembly, while representing every neighbor with dignity and care.
"Georgia deserves leaders who are grounded in reality, respectful of differences, and focused on practical solutions—not partisan talking points."
Affordability
Bring Affordability Back to Georgia
Lower everyday costs, eliminate state income tax, and bring high-paying jobs to the state.
- Cut wasteful spending and bring relief to groceries, utilities, and essentials.
- Put more money back in your pocket so you keep what you earn.
- Attract employers with smart incentives for good wages and opportunity.
EDUCATION
Education Isn’t a Privilege, It’s a Promise!
Empower families with educational options, increase funding for lower-income schools, and raise teacher pay to inspire the next generation.
- Empower families with options that fit their child's needs, not one-size-fits-all.
- Increase funding to lower-income school areas – Level the playing field so every kid gets a world-class education.
- Reward and retain the best educators to inspire our next generation.
Housing
Unlock Housing for All
Cut barriers for builders to increase housing supply and lower costs for buyers.
Prevent institutional investors from buying single-family homes to keep homeownership accessible.
- Cut red tape, build more homes.
- Remove barriers so builders can meet demand and increase supply fast.
- Cut cost for builders, cut cost for buyers.
- Incentives and reforms that lower development expenses and pass savings on.
- Stop Wall Street from buying up single-family homes.
