From frontline crisis response to building programs for youth and families, and now in restorative justice, my work has always been centered on listening to those most impacted by inequity and making sure their voices shape the decisions that affect them.

WHY I’M RUNNING
I care deeply about this community, and I feel increasingly concerned about the challenges we’re up against.
While living amidst crisis and conflict isn’t new, the severity and frequency that we’re experiencing today does feel unique.
The burden of our broken systems is only getting heavier on those already most impacted, while so many of the root causes creating these problems remain unaddressed.
We are at a critical moment as a society, and I think it is a moment more important than ever to have leadership that reflects our communities more fully and who understand these challenges firsthand.
Better solutions for our communities stems from having better representation of that community in politics.
I’m running for the voices too often silenced, for the generations who will be living with the impact of our decisions today, and the planet that deserves to thrive again.
