Platform Fighting for What Matters Real solutions for real problems facing New Hampshire families ⚡ Energy Freedom I will fight to eliminate regulatory barriers that prevent New Hampshire from developing our own energy resources and attracting energy companies that can lower costs for families already struggling with high property taxes.
I will vote against any carbon tax or mandates for intermittent energy sources that drive up electricity rates and force more businesses to leave our state.
New Hampshire needs energy sources that prioritizes affordability and reliability over political correctness, ensuring our families and businesses have the most economical, dependable power in New England. $ Taxes and Economy As your representative, I will vote against any attempt to implement a state income or sales tax and work to reduce the regulatory burden that drives up costs for New Hampshire businesses.
I will push for targeted property tax relief by eliminating wasteful state spending and returning those savings directly to towns and cities to reduce their reliance on property taxes.
My focus will be on keeping New Hampshire's economic advantages intact while finding concrete ways to lower the tax burden on hardworking families in Merrimack. 📚 Committed to Education Excellence Every child deserves an education that prepares them for success.
I support empowering parents with school choice, maintaining high academic standards, and ensuring our tax dollars are spent efficiently in the classroom.
Our students are our future, and they deserve an education system that puts their needs first, respects parental rights, and focuses on academic achievement over political agendas.
My Commitment to Merrimack I will always vote to protect the New Hampshire Advantage and fight for the conservative principles that make our state great.
