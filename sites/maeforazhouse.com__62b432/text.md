History in the Making: Trailblazing Leaders for Legislative District 6.
For the first time, two traditional Navajo women are running for the Arizona Legislature.
Each bring essential experience and has deep roots as longtime residents of Legislative District 6.
Together, they have lived in and traveled throughout the largest legislative district in Arizona.
Jamescita Peshlakai will be serving as your State Senator and Mae Peshlakai as your State Representative.
Elect these 2 highly qualified women to the 2 Open Seats!
Jamescita Peshlakai has a distinguished record of public service, including one term as a State House Representative and two terms in the State Senate.
She served as the tribal liaison for the U.S.
Department of the Interior under President Biden and, in 2025, was appointed by Governor Hobbs to the Arizona State Transportation Board.
Representative Mae Peshlakai is seeking a third term in the State House.
Overcoming her experience as a federal boarding school survivor, she has become an internationally celebrated artist and a revered elder dedicated to preserving and sharing Indigenous knowledge.
