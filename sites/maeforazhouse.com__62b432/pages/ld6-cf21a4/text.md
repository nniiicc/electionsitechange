Arizona's Legislative District 6
DISTRICT 6 is truly unique and diverse
LD 6, the largest state legislative district in Arizona and the United States.
LD 6 includes some of the most beautiful parks and natural wonders in the world, including the Grand Canyon, the San Francisco Peaks, Monument Valley and the White Mountains, and the Colorado and Little Colorado Rivers.
Eight tribes make up the majority of district’s constituents; Hualapai, Havasupai, Kaibab Paiute, San Juan Southern Paiute, Navajo, Hopi, White Mountain Apache, San Carlos Apache and some Zuni Pueblo.
The district spans 7 counties and includes Flagstaff, Winslow, Page, Holbrook, Pinetop, Fredonia, and more!
