Last Updated: 2/12/2026 “Mae Peshlakai for AZ House” respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our campaign, including through our website, mobile programs, and other communication channels.
A) Information We Collect We may collect the following types of information: Personal contact information: such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
Demographic information: such as your city, ZIP code, and voting district.
Engagement information: including your preferences for campaign updates, volunteering, or event participation.
Mobile information: including your mobile phone number when you opt in to receive SMS updates or alerts.
B) How We Collect Information We collect information in the following ways: Through website forms when you sign up to volunteer, donate, or receive updates.
Through online petitions or event registrations hosted directly by Mae Peshlakai for AZ House.
When you opt-in to receive SMS text messages or marketing communications via online or offline sign-up forms.
Through interactions on social media platforms or other digital communications where you voluntarily provide information.
C) How We Use Your Information The information you provide may be used to: Send you updates, news, and information about Mae Peshlakai for AZ House.
Respond to inquiries, questions, or requests for information.
Contact you regarding campaign events, volunteer opportunities, or voter engagement.
Process donations or confirm participation in campaign activities.
We use your information solely for communications and support related to the Mae Peshlakai for AZ House campaign.
D) Opt-Out Instructions You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time.
You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by texting STOP to any text you receive from us.
Mobile opt-ins are excluded from third party sharing for marketing purposes.
To stop receiving campaign text messages, simply text STOP to unsubscribe.
For email communications, you may click the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any campaign email; or reply “unsubscribe” if the link is unavailable.
E) Information Sharing and Sale Policy Mae Peshlakai for AZ House does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
All personal data is used only by the campaign and authorized staff or vendors who assist in campaign operations (e.g., communications or donation processing) and who are bound by this Privacy Policy.
We will not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal data to third parties for promotional/marketing purposes.
However, we may share your information with service providers for business operations and/or legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights.
F) Mobile Opt-In Information Mae Peshlakai for AZ House confirms that mobile opt-in information will not be shared with third parties, except as necessary to send you campaign-related communications using secure messaging platforms.
Your mobile information will never be sold, rented, or distributed for any external use.
G) Data Security We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
While no system is fully secure, we strive to ensure your personal information remains private and safeguarded.
