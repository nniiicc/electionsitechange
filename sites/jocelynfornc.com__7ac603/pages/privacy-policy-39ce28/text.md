Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 1/23/2026
At Jocelyn Torres's Campaign, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share when visiting the website.
This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you interact with our website.
When you visit our site, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, donation details, volunteer sign-ups, or messages submitted through forms.
We may also collect limited technical information automatically, including IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on the site, and general usage patterns.
This data helps us understand how visitors use the site and improve the overall experience.
We use your information to communicate campaign updates, respond to inquiries, manage volunteer and supporter outreach, process donations, send newsletters or political messages if you opt in, and enhance website functionality.
We may also use data to comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
We may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to analyze website traffic, improve performance, and personalize your experience.
You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies, though some features of the site may not function fully as intended.
Our website may include third-party services such as analytics tools, email platforms, or social media integrations.
These providers operate under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them if you have concerns about how your data is handled externally.
We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal information, including technical safeguards and access controls designed to prevent unauthorized use or disclosure.
While we strive to protect your data, no digital system can guarantee complete security.
You have the right to opt out of campaign communications at any time and may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
We will make reasonable efforts to honor such requests in accordance with applicable laws.
This website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
If you have questions about this policy or how your information is used, please contact us.
