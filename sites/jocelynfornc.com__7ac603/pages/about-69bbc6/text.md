ABOUT JOCELYN TORRES
Meet Jocelyn
BACKGROUND
Jocelyn Torres was born and raised in Stanly County and is a proud product of its public school system.
From an early age, her family instilled in her the core values of hard work, honesty, and community uplift.
Growing up, Jocelyn saw firsthand the realities of working families, often balancing school and extracurriculars while helping her mother clean houses and buildings on weekends.
This early exposure to the economic challenges facing working-class families sparked her lifelong passion for civic engagement.
A dedicated student and leader, Jocelyn graduated from Albemarle High School before earning her undergraduate degrees in Political Science and Psychology from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro.
While at UNCG, she participated in the Model United Nations Club, competing in numerous conferences and winning the "Best Position Paper" award multiple years in a row.
Deeply committed to service, she also volunteered with local non-profit organizations, including Real World English and New World Institute, helping teach English and literacy skills to adults and their children.
In 2023, Jocelyn made history in her family by becoming a proud first-generation college graduate.
VISION FOR THE FUTURE
Making history once again as the youngest woman to run for North Carolina House District 67, Jocelyn is dedicated to bringing authentic, hard-working leadership to Raleigh.
As your representative, Jocelyn is committed to building a stronger community.
Key priorities include:
- Economic Opportunity and Fair Wages: Advocating for livable wages and affordable housing so that no family has to work multiple jobs just to make ends meet.
- Investing in Public Education: Jocelyn understands first-hand, from working within the local school system to advocate for public school educators, staff, and students, ensuring every child receives a high-quality education.
- Supporting Farmers and Local Economies: Protecting our agricultural heritage, backing our farmers, and providing the resources necessary to help small businesses thrive.
- Senior Advocacy and Resources: Ensuring District 67’s older residents have access to the care, resources, and support they need to thrive and enjoy life throughout their retirement.
MISSION STATEMENT
Empowering Communities, Driving Change
Jocelyn Torres' mission is to build a society where every person has equal access to opportunities and resources.
She aims to foster a culture of collaboration, transparency, and accountability in governance.
With a focus on inclusivity and sustainable development, she is dedicated to shaping a future where all individuals can thrive and contribute to the betterment of society.
