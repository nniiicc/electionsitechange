Accessibility Statement Last Updated: 01/23/2026 The Jocelyn Torres Campaign is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.
We believe all individuals deserve equal access to information, resources, and opportunities to engage in the democratic process.
We strive to make our website easy to navigate and usable for people with a wide range of abilities.
Our goal is to follow recognized accessibility standards, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), by improving readability, supporting screen readers, providing alternative text for images when possible, and maintaining clear, user-friendly design.
We understand that accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we are continually working to improve the experience for all visitors.
If you encounter any barriers while using our website or have suggestions on how we can improve accessibility, we welcome your feedback.
If you need assistance accessing any part of this site, or would like information in an alternative format, please contact us.
