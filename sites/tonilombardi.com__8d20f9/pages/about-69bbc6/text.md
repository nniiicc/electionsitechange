Meet Toni Lombardi,
Toni is a dedicated public servant running for State Representative in the 118th District
As a current Milford Alderman, Toni has worked firsthand to address the issues that matter most to the community — from protecting the coastline to ensuring strong public schools and making life more affordable for families and seniors.
Toni brings experience from her work in Milford Public Schools, where she saw the challenges facing students and educators every day.
She also gained valuable insight into state government through her work in the Connecticut State Capitol, giving her a deep understanding of how to advocate effectively for Milford in Hartford.
Her commitment to this community is personal.
As a proud mother of two, Toni understands the pressures families face and is ready to fight for solutions that make a real difference.
