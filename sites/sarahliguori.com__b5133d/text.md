Get to know Sarah!
Sarah is a passionate voice for Democratic values that make Arizona a great place to live.
She’d be honored to continue serving the people of Legislative District 5.
Sarah is focused on delivering results for LD5.
Public Education Funding Education is the foundation for everything.
Funding our schools is the best return on investment for our children’s future.
Homelessness & Housing Affordability Arizona is undergoing a massive transformation with home values and rental prices soaring.
Wages and supply need to increase proportionally to keep people in their homes.
Climate Change & Water Water is the most precious resource in our desert state, and conservation is key to our sustainability.
We must work to provide shade, water, and clean air to everyone who lives here.
Voting Rights Through decades of intentional planning and hard work, Arizona has one of the most reliable and efficient voting processes.
Whether by mail or in-person voting, we must defend against the anti-democratic attacks to suppress the voices of our people.
Income Inequality & Equity Our society flourishes when everyone has an equitable opportunity to succeed in life.
Addressing systemic racism and paying people livable wages are two areas Sarah is fighting for progress.
Reproductive Rights People should be allowed to make health care and family planning decisions privately and with their doctor.
The government has no place in these spaces.
Period.
