Water: Too Big To Fail The Water Infrastructure Finance Authority was established between the AZ legislature and the Governor's office.
It will set aside over $1B to put to use to conserve projects and water augmentation.
This effort towards solutions to our water crisis could not have been presented any sooner.

This finance authority will allocate funds to immediate conservation and long term augmentation.
