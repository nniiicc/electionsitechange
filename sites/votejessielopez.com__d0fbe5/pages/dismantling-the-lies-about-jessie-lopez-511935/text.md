THE TRUTH MISLEADING In 2024, Santa Ana voters voted on Measure FF, which would increase Santa Ana City Councilmember salaries to 33% of that of an Orange County Superior Court judge.
This issue was voted on by the residents of Santa Ana, not a closed-door City Council vote, and was introduced by Councilmembers Phil Bacerra and Thai Viet Phan.
Councilmember Jessie Lopez’s stance was that the voters should be the ones to democratically decide whether they wanted full-time City Councilmembers, which would be a “yes” vote on the measure, as such a salary increase would require full-time dedication to City Council duties to be justified.
She encouraged voters to vote “no” if they wanted salaries and part-time Council duties to remain as they were.
The voters ultimately decided to keep City Council salaries as they were and Measure FF did not pass , with “no” votes accounting for 65.66% of those cast.
