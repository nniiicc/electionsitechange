Jessie Lopez has been a tireless advocate for working families, tenants, and marginalized communities in Santa Ana.
First elected in 2020 and re-elected in 2024, her leadership on the City Council has resulted in transformative policies that prioritize equity, accountability, and community investment.
- Passed the Rent Stabilization and Just Cause Eviction Ordinance in 2021, later solidified by voters in 2024.
- Allocated $24 million for affordable housing, including Wise Place Transitional Housing.
- Brought forth an item: “Ordinance Prohibiting the Use of Algorithmic Pricing Tools”
- Secured direct payments for families struggling during the pandemic.
- Invested in parks, libraries, and childcare services to support community resilience.
- Championed hazard pay for frontline and essential workers
- Led efforts in 2021 to align city code with state law, allowing non-citizens to serve as commissioners for better representation.
- Secured $300,000 for immigrant legal representation through the Immigrant Defenders Law Center.
- Established the Police Oversight Commission in 2022 to enhance transparency and accountability in law enforcement.
- 3-Year contract for School Crossing Guard Prigram
- Reduced 911 response times by voting to expand the police force in 2023.
- Invested over $1 million in improved lighting for safety in Riverview, Saddleback, and other neighborhoods (124 in fiscal year 2025/2026).
- Advocated for street repairs, sidewalk improvements, and upgraded water infrastructure.
- Lighted crosswalk in Fisher Park neighborhood
- Traffic and traffic light improvements at Bristol St. and Santa Clara
- Flower St./Memory Ln Traffic improvements and beautification in Fisher Park and Morrison Park neighborhoods
- Landscaped medians in Cabrillo Park neighborhood
- Secured $2.7 million in grants for vocational training programs benefiting low-income residents.
- Brought forward item: Prohibiting the Award of Agreements, Contracts, or Grants to Immediate Family Members of the Mayor, City Council Members, or City Officials
- Approved a Program of Financial Assistance for Families Impacted by Federal Immigration Enforcement Actions called Ayuda sin Fronteras
- Secured funding for the city’s first Climate Action Plan, general plan updates, and lead remediation programs.
- Approved $3.5 million for Santiago Park renovations, including ADA walkways, an amphitheater, and playground improvements.
- Achieved “Clean California Community” designation for Santa Ana
- Organized and participates in monthly Santiago Creek cleanups in Santiago Park
- Pickleball courts and repaved basketball courts in Morrison Park
- Shade structures being added to baseball dugouts city-wide
- Playground shade structures in Cabrillo Park
- Mabury Park Dog Park
- City’s first splash pad in Portola Park
