Before serving on the Council, I fought to protect public land and expand affordable housing.
As a City Councilwoman, I have taken on special interests trying to buy local elections and delivered real results for working families.
I worked with residents to make rent increases above 3 percent per year illegal and expanded eviction protections for seniors being forced out of their homes.
I passed a strict lobbyist registration law to stop pay to play politics and increase transparency at City Hall.
I also supported small businesses by removing outdated laws that blocked them from advertising.
I worked with our police department to improve support and response for victims of sexual assault and crime survivors.
I am running for State Assembly because California needs leaders who will fight for affordable housing, support Medicare for All, stop corporations from buying single family homes, increase investment in public education, and defend our communities from federal overreach that threatens immigrant families.
I show up, do the work, and stand up to powerful interests when our community is at stake.
I respectfully ask for your vote so we can build a more affordable, transparent, and just California for every family.
Endorsed by: National Union of Healthcare Workers, California Environmental Voters, California Federation of Teachers, United Domestic Workers, OC Supervisor Vicente Sarmiento, the Progressive Caucus, Civil Rights Leader Dolores Huerta, Yes We Can Democratic Club, and the California Working Families Party.

Santa Ana, Orange, and Anaheim About California's 68th Assembly District California Assembly District 68 is a vibrant and diverse community that includes Santa Ana, Anaheim, and Orange.
With a rich cultural heritage and a strong working-class population, the district is home to many immigrants, families, and small business owners striving for a better future.
Why is Jessie the best fit for District 68?
A lifelong Santa Ana resident and daughter of immigrants, Jessie knows firsthand the struggles working families face – affording rent, securing good-paying jobs, and accessing quality public services.
From a young age, she dedicated herself to serving her community, tutoring students, volunteering at food banks, and later fighting for tenant protections, affordable housing, and environmental justice.
Her deep roots and lived experience make her uniquely qualified to represent the district in Sacramento.
