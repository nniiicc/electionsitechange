Why I’m running.
I’m running for one final term because I don’t believe in walking away when the job isn’t done.
I’ve spent my career focused on making sure families can afford food, housing, health care, education, and a decent living.
Right now, too many North Dakotans are being squeezed, and those fundamentals are under real pressure.
This term is about finishing the work and helping the next generation of leaders be ready to carry it forward.
North Dakota deserves steady leadership, especially in uncertain times.
