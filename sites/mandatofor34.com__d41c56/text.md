Brandi Mandato is a working mom and wife raising her family in North Haven.
She's the granddaughter of Italian American immigrants, and the oldest of eight children raised in a working-class family.
With more than 20 years of experience in economic and workforce development, she currently serves as an Economic Development Commissioner and Constable in North Haven.
Brandi has spent her career fighting for human rights, quality education, and economic opportunity and she’s running for State Senate to make Connecticut work better for all of us.
