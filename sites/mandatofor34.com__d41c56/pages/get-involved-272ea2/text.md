Ways to Get Involved
Here are some ideas of how to get involved.
Fill out the contact form below and let us know what you're interested in doing.
Feel free to get creative!
We are open to new ideas, too!
01.
Write a letter to the editor in support of Brandi or host a meet and greet.
02.
Join us and canvas neighbors to encourage folks to vote.
03.
Sign up to phone or text bank for Brandi as we get closer to the election.
04.
Write postcards to friends and neighbors about why they should vote.
05.
Help us with social media and the website so we can grow our reach.
CONTACT US
Reach out with questions or to help us organize and elect Brandi.
