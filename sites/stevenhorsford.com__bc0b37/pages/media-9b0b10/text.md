Updated October 5th
Overview
Voters in Nevada’s Fourth Congressional district need to know Congressman Steven Horsford gets things done for Nevada.
He’s always put working families ahead of politics and corporate interests, and at a time when politics divide us, Horsford is focused on serving all Nevadans, no matter who they vote for.
Summary
Voters, including those on the go, need to see, read and hear in the Las Vegas media market that:
- Congressman Steven Horsford says corruption has a cost and Nevada families shouldn’t be left paying the bill.
Democrats and Republicans use insider information to make millions in the stock market, while corporations are never held accountable for higher prices.
Horsford does not buy or sell stocks.
He will ban Congress from trading stocks, while holding corporations accountable for price gouging their Nevada families.
- Congressman Steven Horsford worked multiple jobs to support his single mother and younger siblings, before going on to lead Nevada’s largest job training program.
He knows what it means to work hard and still struggle to get ahead.
Because of that, Horsford is focused on tackling rising prices, working with Democrats and Republicans alike to bring health care, groceries, housing, and electrical costs under control.
Horsford will fight the big corporations and special interests responsible for higher prices, and hold them accountable for ripping us off and fight to make Nevada more affordable.
- Congressman Steven Horsford believes no one should be forced into bankruptcy or lose their home because an insurance company refuses to pay a claim.
Horsford says it is wrong to pay for expensive insurance policies, while at every turn, insurance companies do everything to avoid paying health care claims.
Horsford wants to change that by banning insurance companies from denying health care prescribed by a doctor and limiting how much they can raise rates on customers.
