WHY I AM RUNNING FOR HOUSE DISTRICT 30
I am honored to represent the residents of Hillsboro, Helvetia, and Rock Creek in the Oregon House of Representatives.
I want to continue my service because I’ve experienced what so many working families are going through now.
I know the frustration and the fear of struggling to make ends meet while the cost of education, food, housing, transportation, and everything else is increasing faster than your income.
It’s easy to become overwhelmed and cynical.
Yet, I’ve also seen the impact that people can have when they work together to build a better future.
We can give voice to those who too often have been overlooked or forgotten.
We can foster collaboration between the public, private, and non-profit sectors to solve our most pressing problems.
We can create a more equitable and prosperous society for everyone.
That is what I will continue to do as the representative for HD 30 and I hope you will join me!
