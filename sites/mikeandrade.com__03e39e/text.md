MEET MIKE
A Public Servant Working For Us
Mike is a public servant working for all of us.
He represents House District 12 in the Indiana House of Representatives, covering the Town of Munster, Town of Highland, Town of Griffith, and a portion of Schererville.
He serves on three committees: House Financial Institutions, Ways and Means Committee and Roads & Transportation.
Mike is the Ranking Democrat on the Financial Institutions Committee.
