Community
UNIONS
Unions are the backbone of Northwest Indiana’s economy, and Mike wholeheartedly supports the hardworking men and women in our unions.
His district is home to one of the largest concentrations of union families in the region.
These unions play a crucial role in our community, offering upward mobility and stability for thousands of families.
FIRST RESPONDERS
First responders are essential to the safety and well-being of our communities.
Mike has tirelessly advocated at the Statehouse to ensure they have the resources they need to protect us effectively.
Beyond their role in public safety, first responders are also pillars of our community, exemplifying dedication and service.
EDUCATION &
TEACHERS
Teachers are fundamental to our community, shaping the education of our future generations.
From the very beginning, Mike has fought to secure the pay raises our teachers deserve.
Education is one of the most powerful tools for achieving upward mobility and giving back to our community.
Mike remains steadfast in his commitment to advocate for education and our teachers at the Statehouse.
VETERAN’S FOOD DRIVE
Veterans deserve to be honored as heroes when they return home.
Mike has tirelessly dedicated himself to ensuring our veterans receive the recognition and resources they deserve.
Each year, he organizes a veterans’ food and turkey drive in our community, giving back to those who have served our nation.
DIVERSITY OF
COMMUNITY
Diversity is a cornerstone of our community’s strength.
As a Latino, Mike understands the importance of representation and has actively engaged with various underrepresented communities.
He has organized listening sessions with the Indian-American community, the Hispanic community, and many other ethnic groups, fostering collaboration for the betterment of our community.
