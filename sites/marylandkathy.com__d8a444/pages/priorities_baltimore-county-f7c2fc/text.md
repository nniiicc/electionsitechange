District 7A is home.
Kathy’s roots in this part of Maryland go back generations on her mother’s side, and she has represented these communities since 2011 — first as part of District 7 alongside Harford County, and as District 7A since the 2023 redistricting.
The work that matters most here rarely makes the news.
In the 2026 session she secured $165,000 for an electronic message sign at Loch Raven High School and $100,000 for Kingsville Elementary School, and filed requests for Fraternal Order of Police Lodge No. 34 and a sign at Pine Grove Elementary.
The rest is constituent service: a permit stuck in an agency, an unemployment claim nobody will answer, a road that never got fixed.
It is the least glamorous part of the job and the part she takes most seriously.
“When an Apple Store — a flagship retailer that was always busy — announces it is leaving because of rampant shoplifting, link-up robberies, assaults, and slow police response, the message is unmistakable: radical policies have consequences.”
