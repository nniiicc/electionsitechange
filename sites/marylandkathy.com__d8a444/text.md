Faith keeps it.
Kathy Szeliga grew up in a military family with a career Army father.
Her family moved about every two years, but Baltimore was always home base — where generations of her mother’s family have lived and where her Maryland roots run deep.
She met her husband, Mark, in Ocean City, and they eloped in Colorado more than #ago.
In the early years Kathy worked a string of jobs to make ends meet — dishwasher, parking lot attendant, maid, drywall worker, waitress — while Mark learned the carpentry trade.
In 1986 they moved back to Maryland, took a leap of faith, and started their own small construction business.
