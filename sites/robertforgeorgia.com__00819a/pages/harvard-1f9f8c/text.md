Dawson elected to Harvard Extension Alumni Association Board of Directors
I’m grateful for the opportunity to serve on the Board of Directors of the Harvard Extension Alumni Association.
Being chosen by my fellow alumni is both humbling and inspiring, and I thank you for the trust you’ve placed in me.
To everyone who encouraged me, helped my campaign and stood with me throughout this election, your support has been invaluable.
I carry it with me into this next chapter of service.
I also want to recognize the other candidates who stepped forward.
Their commitment to strengthening our alumni community and Harvard University speaks to the values we all share.
It’s a true privilege to represent, and I look forward to working hard to uphold our legacy and advance the mission of our alma mater for years to come.
