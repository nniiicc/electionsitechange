Jillian England’s core message
1.
Break the addiction–homelessness–crime cycle
A path out — of addiction. of homelessness. of hopelessness.
- Accountability with a path: a roof with rules, treatment that is not optional, and a real way back.
- Fund the exit: treatment capacity and recovery housing that move people out of crisis — not blank checks that just manage it.
- Name the market: open-air dealing along corridors like Aurora is a market to shut, not scenery to manage.

2.
Energy you can count on
Our energy infrastructure is more fragile than it should be.
- Energy abundance: build new nuclear, including small modular reactors (SMRs).
- Protect hydro: keep the dams that already deliver clean, firm power.
- Reliable and affordable: power that stays on in a cold snap and bills families can afford.

Why your vote here matters
In several Seattle-area districts this year (for example, LD 32, 34, 37, and 43), the November ballot has no Republican — the top-two primary left voters with two Democrats or a single unopposed choice.
LD 36 voters do have a choice.
That happened because a candidate filed, organized, and finished in the top two with about 5,876 votes.
November is about building on that: a bigger vote for a path out of addiction and homelessness, reliable energy, and lower taxes.
Primary result
Primary certified Aug 19, 2026: Frame 87.69% (42,548), England 12.11% (5,876); both advance under top-two.
