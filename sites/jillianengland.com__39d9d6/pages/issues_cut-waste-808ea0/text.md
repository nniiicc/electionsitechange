We need accountability, not more blank checks for bureaucracy.
My Plan to Cut Waste
End CCA Fund Diversions
Stop the wasteful spending and redirect resources to real priorities like roads, public safety, and practical energy solutions.
Audit and Cut Failed Programs
Review every major spending line for fraud, waste, and duplication.

Focus resources on outcomes, not endless bureaucracy.
Protect vulnerable residents while cutting red tape that drives up costs.
Restore Fiscal Accountability
Require transparency in how every tax dollar is spent and tie funding to measurable results.
Self-reliance beats dependence.
We choose practical reality over political fantasy.
