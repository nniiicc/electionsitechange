This lack of fair representation meant many voices went unheard, especially in the most impoverished areas.
The 1990 redistricting effort aimed to change that.
A group of brave heroes and she-roes stepped forward to demand change.
Lonnie Middlebrook Jr., Hattie Middlebrook, Reginald Robinson, Vicky Robinson, Albert Veasley, and Ollie Neal led the charge.
They understood that without fair districts, the community would remain powerless in shaping policies that affected their daily lives.
Their work was not easy.
They faced resistance and uncertainty, yet they stood firm.
Their goal was clear: to allow Mississippi County citizens to elect a state representative who truly represented their interests and needs.
Why Representation Matters for Mississippi County Representation is more than just a seat at the table.
It is about having someone who understands the unique challenges of the community and fights for solutions that improve lives.
Mississippi County has areas that struggle with poverty, limited access to healthcare, education gaps, and economic challenges.
Without a representative who listens and acts, these issues remain unaddressed.
The heroes of 1990 knew this well.
They fought so that the community could elect leaders who would speak boldly for their needs.
Their efforts gave Mississippi County a voice in the Arkansas state legislature, a voice that could push for better schools, healthcare access, and economic opportunities.
Standing on the Shoulders of Giants Today, Mike Middlebrook carries the torch passed down by those heroes.
As a proud Democrat and a native of Mississippi County, he understands the importance of community representation.
His parents, who were part of the fight for redistricting, taught him that standing up for the community means standing up for justice and equality.
Mike Middlebrook promises to continue this legacy.
He pledges to speak for those who have not had a seat at the table and to ensure that the most needed areas of Mississippi County have a strong voice in the state legislature.

Voting for Mike Middlebrook means voting for a representative who understands the community’s struggles and will fight for policies that bring real change.
It means supporting someone who values every voice and works to ensure no one is left behind.
