Young Democrats Mississippi County 34
Young Democrats prioritize economic stability (cost of living, housing, inflation), reproductive rights, climate change action, and strengthening democracy.
Key concerns also include student loan debt, healthcare affordability, gun violence, and ensuring a just transition to clean energy.
Their focus centers on equity, social justice, and long-term sustainability.
- Economic Issues & Cost of Living: High inflation and the rising cost of living are dominant concerns, with particular focus on affordable housing, increasing the minimum wage, and reducing the wealth gap.
- Reproductive Rights & Healthcare: Following the overturning of Roe v.
Wade, abortion access remains a major galvanizing issue.
Access to affordable health insurance is also prioritized.
- Climate Change & Environment: Young Democrats advocate for a "just transition" to green energy, prioritizing sustainable policies, environmental justice, and protecting natural resources like the Amazon.
- Education & Student Debt:Addressing the high costs of education and alleviating student loan debt are key priorities for young voters.
- Democracy & Social Justice:Protecting voting rights, combating authoritarianism, and ensuring fair elections are major focuses, often framed as protecting individual rights.
- Gun Violence Prevention: Gun control and safety are frequently cited as top issues driving voter engagement.
- Job Security & Rights: The future of work, including rights for workers and transitioning to cleaner energy jobs, is a priority.
- Foreign Policy: Specific concerns include opposing authoritarian regimes (such as in Cuba, Nicaragua, and Venezuela) and opposing the U.S. embargo on Cuba.
