Registering to Vote in Arkansas
Before you can cast your ballot, you must be registered to vote.
Arkansas has specific requirements and deadlines that you should know to ensure your registration is valid.
- Eligibility: You must be a U.S. citizen, a resident of Arkansas, and at least 18 years old on or before Election Day.
- How to Register: You can register online, by mail, or in person at your county clerk’s office or other designated locations.
- Deadline: Registration must be completed at least 30 days before the election.
- Updating Your Registration: If you move within Arkansas or change your name, update your registration promptly to avoid issues on Election Day.
Registering early is a smart step.
It gives you time to resolve any issues and ensures you receive your voter information card.
You can find more detailed instructions and register online through the official Arkansas Secretary of State website.
Voting in Arkansas: Methods and Options
Arkansas offers several ways to vote, making it easier for everyone to participate regardless of their circumstances.
In-Person Voting
On Election Day, you can vote at your assigned polling place.
Polls are open from 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM.
Bring a valid photo ID, such as a driver’s license or state ID card.
If you forget your ID, you can still vote by provisional ballot, which will be counted once your eligibility is confirmed.
Early Voting
Early voting is available for those who want to avoid the Election Day rush.
It usually starts about two weeks before the election and ends a few days prior.
Early voting locations and hours vary by county, so check with your local election office.
Absentee Voting
If you cannot vote in person due to illness, disability, travel, or work commitments, you can request an absentee ballot.
You must apply for an absentee ballot by mail or in person before the deadline, which is typically a week before Election Day.
Once you receive your ballot, fill it out carefully and return it by mail or in person.
Using these options can make voting more convenient and accessible.
Planning ahead ensures your vote counts.

Important Dates and Deadlines to Remember
Marking key dates on your calendar is essential to ensure you don’t miss any voting opportunities.
- Voter Registration Deadline: 30 days before Election Day.
- Early Voting Period: Usually starts 15 days before and ends 4 days before Election Day.
- Absentee Ballot Request Deadline: Typically 7 days before Election Day.
- Election Day: Polls open from 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM on the designated date.
Staying aware of these deadlines helps you plan your voting process smoothly.
Check your county’s election website for any updates or changes.
