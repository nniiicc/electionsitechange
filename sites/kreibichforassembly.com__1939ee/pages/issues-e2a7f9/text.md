On The Issues With Rob Kreibich
Tax Relief
- Eliminate state tax on pensions
- Freeze the gas tax
- Require 2/3 majority to raise taxes
- Hold down property tax increases
- Tax cuts for small businesses
Government Spending
- Implement zero based budgeting
- Cut all state agency budgets by 10 percent
- Close all two year UW institutions
- Freeze state hiring
- Don’t fill vacant state positions
Education
- Freeze University of Wisconsin tuition and fees
- Expand school-to-work initiatives
- Create “Boots to Business” program for veterans
- College credit for teenage work
- Expand Youth Apprenticeship Program
- Protect parental rights
