KANSAS STATE HOUSE Brandon Whipple is a candidate for Kansas House District 83 and a former Mayor of Wichita.
He previously served in the Kansas House of Representatives, where he worked to improve public schools, expand economic opportunity, and strengthen services for Kansas families.
A Wichita resident, husband, and father, Brandon is running to deliver practical solutions that lower costs, support neighborhood schools, protect individual freedoms, and improve quality of life for the people of Wichita.
