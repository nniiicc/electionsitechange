Supporters and volunteers may choose to receive SMS messages from Whipple For Kansas, the campaign committee supporting Brandon Whipple for Kansas House District 83.
Users opt in by completing the campaign volunteer form and selecting "Yes" in response to the question "Can we text you?" Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not required to volunteer with the campaign.
Users who select "Yes" and provide a phone number consent to receive campaign text messages.
Messages may include campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, fundraising requests, event invitations, and reminders to vote.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Recipients may reply STOP to unsubscribe and HELP for assistance.
