Caroline for Congress — Privacy Policy Effective Date: August 1, 2025 Contact: caroline@carolinefornewyork.com 1.
Introduction Caroline for Congress (“We,” “Us,” “Our,” or the “Campaign”) respects your privacy and values the trust you place in us.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information when you engage with our campaign — whether online, by email, through mobile messaging, social media, or in person.
By visiting our website, signing up for updates, donating, volunteering, or otherwise providing information, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our collection and use of information as described below.
2.
Information We Collect We collect both personal and technical information to operate an effective campaign: Information You Provide Directly: Name, address, email address, and phone number Payment or donation details (processed securely by trusted third parties) Volunteer preferences, event registrations, and form submissions Communication preferences and consent history Automatically Collected Information: Device type, browser, IP address, and operating system Pages visited, referral sources, and time spent on our website Mobile Messaging Data: If you opt in to our SMS or MMS messaging program, we collect your mobile number, message activity, and engagement data (such as responses or delivery confirmations).
3.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to support, grow, and communicate the goals of the campaign, including to: Send political messages, news, and issue updates Solicit contributions and conduct fundraising activities Contact supporters, volunteers, and donors about campaign events and priorities Process donations and comply with election and campaign-finance laws Improve outreach, digital engagement, and website functionality Deliver targeted advertising and personalized content Ensure compliance with applicable laws and FEC/State reporting requirements All campaign communications comply with federal and New York state election laws.
Recognize returning visitors and tailor messages accordingly Measure ad effectiveness and supporter engagement Deliver political and fundraising messages more effectively through digital targeting We may work with third-party analytics and advertising platforms (such as Google Analytics, Facebook, and other ad networks) that collect or receive information from our website and use it to provide ad measurement, audience insights, and targeting services.
5.
Information Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for commercial marketing purposes.
We may share limited data with: Vendors and Service Providers assisting in campaign operations such as email delivery, text messaging, payment processing, data analytics, and digital advertising — all bound by confidentiality obligations.
Digital Platforms and Advertising Partners for targeted political and fundraising communications.
Regulatory Authorities to comply with campaign-finance disclosure laws.
Legal Entities when required by law, subpoena, or court order.
All third-party partners are required to use supporter data only for authorized campaign purposes and protect it consistent with this Policy.
6.
Mobile Messaging Program By opting into our mobile messaging program, you consent to receive recurring text messages regarding campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, and fundraising appeals.
We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Message and data rates may apply.
Frequency varies based on engagement.
Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
We are not responsible for delays or undelivered messages.
T-Mobile and other carriers are not liable for delayed or failed delivery.
7.
Data Security We use reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.
While no system is completely secure, we work only with reputable vendors and employ encryption and secure technologies for contributions and supporter data.

Data Retention We retain personal information as long as necessary for campaign operations, legal compliance, and reporting.
When no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize your data.
10.
Children’s Privacy We do not knowingly collect or store personal data from individuals under the age of 13.
If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, it will be deleted promptly.
11.
Dispute Resolution Any dispute or claim arising out of this Privacy Policy or your interactions with the campaign shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York, administered by JAMS under applicable rules.
Arbitration will be conducted on an individual basis, not as part of a class or collective action.

Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
Any revisions will be posted with a new effective date.
Continued interaction with the campaign after updates means you accept the revised terms.
