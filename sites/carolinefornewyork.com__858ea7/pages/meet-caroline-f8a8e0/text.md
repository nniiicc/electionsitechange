Federal Reserve Board of Governors Bank of Israel Bank for International Settlements European Central Bank Studied at Sciences Po Corporate Attorney SAG-AFTRA Member About Caroline Shinkle Your Next Congresswoman Caroline Shinkle lives in Midtown Manhattan and has always valued education, hard work, and pushing the limits of what’s possible.
This foundation helped shape Caroline’s path from academic achievement, to working at the center of the U.S. financial system and legal industry, to her civic and community involvement.
Caroline Shinkle graduated from MIT, where she majored in economics and became deeply interested in central banking and global financial regulation.
She completed stints at the European Central Bank in Frankfurt, Germany, and the Bank of Israel in Jerusalem, Israel, before beginning her career at the Federal Reserve Board of Governors in Washington, D.C.
Caroline Shinkle also spent a semester studying in Paris at l’Institut d’Etudes Politiques de Paris (Sciences Po) where she took all her classes in French.
She went on to earn her J.D. from Harvard Law School.
Upon graduation, Caroline Shinkle put her law degree to work at a leading international law firm in New York City, where she advised businesses and financial institutions on mergers and acquisitions, capital markets, and financial regulatory matters.
During law school, Caroline continued to hone her understanding of global financial regulation, spending time at the Bank for International Settlements in Basel, Switzerland.
Caroline Shinkle is admitted to the United States Supreme Court, New York, Washington, D.C., Ohio, and Massachusetts bars. ‍ In these times of economic uncertainty, Caroline Shinkle’s experiences working at central banks around the world have provided her with a strategic understanding of the importance of the U.S. dollar remaining the global reserve currency.
Furthermore, Caroline Shinkle’s legal, economic, and central banking background, both domestic and international, provide her with a unique understanding of the economy and the conditions needed to turbocharge widespread economic growth and prosperity. ‍ Caroline Shinkle has served in leadership roles with legal, educational, and community organizations, and founded a nonprofit focused on encouraging and inspiring informed civic engagement among the rising generation.
She is also a long-time member of the Screen Actors Guild–American Federation of Television & Radio Artists (SAG-AFTRA).
