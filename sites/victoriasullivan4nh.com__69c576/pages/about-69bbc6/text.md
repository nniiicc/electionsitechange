Senator Victoria Sullivan represents New Hampshire Senate District 18, which includes the town of Litchfield and Manchester Wards 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9.
Victoria has spent nearly a decade advocating for children, families, and people on the road to recovery.
Her work isn't theoretical — it's hands-on, in her own community, with the people who reach out to her every day looking for help.
That lived experience is what she carries into every committee hearing, every floor vote, and every conversation with constituents.
She believes good government is honest, accountable, and rooted in the values that make New Hampshire unique: independence, hard work, and looking out for one another.
In the legislature, Victoria has fought consistently for education freedom, parental voice, and supports for those struggling with addiction and mental illness.
Her voting record reflects a clear commitment to fiscal responsibility and to protecting the New Hampshire Advantage for the next generation.
