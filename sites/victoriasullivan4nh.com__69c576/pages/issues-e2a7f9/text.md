Honest positions on the questions that matter most to Litchfield and Manchester families.
Fiscal Responsibility Protecting the New Hampshire Advantage means living within our means.
Victoria supports disciplined state spending, low taxes, and a state government that respects the dollars taxpayers send to Concord.
Sanctuary Cities & Public Safety We are a nation of immigrants and a nation of laws.
New Hampshire cannot allow sanctuary cities to override our immigration laws.
Fentanyl trafficking has taken a devastating toll on Manchester and surrounding communities — we must put New Hampshire families first.
Education Freedom According to the NH Journal, 75% of New Hampshire voters support education freedom.
Every child and every family deserves a choice and a voice in their education.
Victoria has fought for parental voice for nearly a decade and will continue to defend the programs that benefit children.
Mental Health New Hampshire is facing a mental health crisis.
Resources are scarce, and families — especially since COVID — are desperate for help.
We must redirect resources away from arresting our way out of addiction and toward genuine care and treatment.
Supporting Recovery Victoria works directly with people in recovery and those who have been homeless or incarcerated.
She brings that perspective to Concord, advocating for real, on-the-ground supports for the recovery community.
Strong Communities Strong communities are built by neighbors who show up — for their schools, their churches, their first responders, and each other.
Victoria's work in the Senate reflects the same commitment she brings to her neighborhood every day.
