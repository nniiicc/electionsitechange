PEOPLE OVER POWER
Our Vision is Clear
Hawai’i will become a place where communities and her people live in Aloha, and abundance.
It is where cultural values will be experienced and shared.
Our ‘Ohana will become established by equal justice and liberty for all.
We will build a government of virtue, honor, and integrity, where elected officials listen and serve the people.
Each decision will be made in transparency, discipline, and righteousness.
We will Turn the Tide from corruption and financial hardship, where local families can thrive rather than survive.
It is where homeownership, the cost of living and remaining in the Islands for generations is not a dream but a reality.
Our Mission is Tangible
Our mission is to institute public policy based on the will of the people of Hawai’i.
We will implement accountability and transparency in public policies and provide for the wellbeing of all the people of Hawai’i.
We will etablish a Government Transparency Office to make spending, contracts, and decisions visible to the public.
We will end entitlement, nepotism, and back room deal making in government and restore the honor of merit, hard work, inspiration, and self-motivation.
We will reduce the cost of living by eliminating GET taxes on food and medicine, and freezing property taxes at the age of retirement on primary homes.
