Freeze property taxes for Kūpuna at retirement and on their primary residence.
Create tax exemptions for Native Hawaiian homeowners.
Ensuring nutritious school lunches for the keiki of Hawai'i while reducing the cost for families. ​ Why it matters : It allows families to stay in Hawai‘i, contribute to the local economy, and live with security.
This is more than relief — it’s empowerment.
END THE CORRUPTION.
REBUILD ALOHA. ​ Trust in leadership begins with transparency.
Gary will: Establish a Government Transparency Office to make spending, contracts, and decisions visible to the public.
Require annual audits of all public-private partnerships and government departments.
Forward all corruption cases directly to the Attorney General for swift action.
Why it matters : Taxpayers deserve to know exactly how their money is used and to see swift consequences for misconduct.
AFFORDABLE HOUSING​: FEE-SIMPLE HOUSING AT SCALE Through the Housing Master Plan, Gary will deliver 15,000 fee-simple homes priced between $400K–$600K, targeted for working families, teachers, nurses, and Kūpuna.
Execution includes: Land reform and permitting streamlining to cut years off project timelines.
Elimination of redundant regulations that stall housing supply.
End no-bid contracts to ensure fairness and cost-efficiency.
Housing Enterprise Zones to encourage community-led development.
Why it matters : This plan creates generational stability and keeps our workforce here — not priced out to the mainland.
NATIVE HAWAIIAN JUSTICE & PRESERVATION ​ Moving forward with aloha means honoring our past and empowering our future: Form a Hawaiian-led, deadline-oriented action group for justice and transparency.
Renegotiate Hawaiian Homes Act terms — addressing blood quantum, land rights, and fairness in access.
Advance reconciliation through actionable, measurable policies.
Integrate Hawaiian language & cultural education into all schools statewide.
Why it matters : Our cultural heritage is not an accessory — it is the foundation of Hawai‘i’s identity and unity.
EDUCATION REFORM THAT WORKS FOR HAWAI‘I ​ Our keiki deserve more than standardized testing; they deserve education that prepares them for life. ​ Gary’s plan includes: Financial literacy & problem-solving in every classroom.
Hawaiian language instruction beginning in 3rd grade.
Merit pay for teachers to reward excellence.
Proficiency-based graduation, supported by tutoring — replacing the “test-and-graduate” model.
Local budget autonomy for each island to meet unique community needs.
Why it matters : Education must serve our local culture, economy, and future — not just standardized metrics.
