Biography
Congresswoman Betty McCollum is running for re-election to continue representing Minnesota’s Fourth Congressional District in Congress.
Over her career representing the East Metro, Betty has been a strong voice for the environment, universal health care, quality education, economic opportunity, organized labor, human rights, a smart national security policy, and more.
Betty is the Dean of the Minnesota Congressional Delegation and as Minnesota’s sole member of the Appropriations Committee in Congress, she is uniquely-positioned to secure billions in federal funding each year for projects, programs, and services that save lives, create jobs, boost economic security, improve our infrastructure, and grow our local economy.
Betty is a mother to two adult children and a proud grandmother to two grandsons.
She lives in Saint Paul, MN.
