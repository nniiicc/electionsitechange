2022 ENDORSEMENTS & RATINGS National Federation of Independent Business Pennsylvania's largest small business organization endorse Milou because she has a proven record of supporting policies that help create jobs and support the small businesses who are the backbone of our economy.
Our Police - The Fraternal Order of Police The men and women of law enforcement support Milou Mackenzie.
Both the Le-Hampton Lodge 35 and Montgomery County Lodge 14 Fraternal Orders of Police endorse Milou because stands with our police and local families in the fight against crime.
Pennsylvania Chamber of Commerce The PA Chamber of Commerce - ChamberPAC - endorses Milou because she stands with the job creators of Pennsylvania in fighting for the things that will help grow our economy.
Pennsylvania Association of Realtors The PA Association of Realtors - representing tens of thousands of Realtors across the state - endorse Milou for her hard work to reduce and eliminate property taxes and make it easier for people to achieve the American Dream of home ownership.
