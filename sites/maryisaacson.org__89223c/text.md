I have represented the 175th District as state representative since 2019, and prior to that, for 12 years as staff.
I am humbled by the work required to ensure that every Pennsylvanian is able to work and live a quality day-to-day life with access to the resources they need – especially in these economically trying times.
I am dedicated to bringing home state dollars to invest in our communities and neighborhoods and I have been ranked among the top legislators in the state when it comes to investing in their district.
I am proud of many of the projects I have worked on over the years turning into reality – like the I95 cap and revitalizing the Waterfront.
I am an education advocate (supporting our public schools, community colleges, and access to affordable higher education) and environmentalist.
I will never stop fighting for constitutional equality, the protection of Roe in our state, and workers’ rights to unionize.
I believe in good government, affordable healthcare, paid family and medical leave, and the humane treatment of animals.
