Kimberly Pomerleau
For Senate District 22
Kim Pomerleau is currently a two-term State Representative from Standish Representing House District 85 Representative Pomerleau serves on the State and Local Government Joint Committee where Kim focuses on local government policy, operations and firsthand solutions.
Kim also serves on the House Elections Committee.
With over 40 years as a small family business owner, you can trust Kim to always advocate for small businesses and their survival.
Kim is the former owner of Maine-ly Heating Oil.
Representative Pomerleau currently owns Roadway Utility Trailers, Standish Auto and Trailer Sales and Standish Towing and Recovery.
Kim's experience and entrepreneurship gives her a first-hand understanding of the challenges facing Maine businesses.
Kim and Phil have five children, 9 grandchildren and five great grandchildren.
Kim enjoys attending sporting events, our outdoor heritage, volunteering and is an avid cyclist who has participated in the Trek Across Maine, the Dempsey Challenge and the Tri for a Cure.
