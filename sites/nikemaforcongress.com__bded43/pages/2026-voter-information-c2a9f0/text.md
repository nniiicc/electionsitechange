IF YOU WANT TO VOTE BY MAIL: The first day you can request your absentee ballot is March 2, 2026.
The last day to request an absentee ballot is May 8, 2026 .
Remember, your absentee ballot must arrive at your County Registrar’s office by election day.
IF YOU WANT TO VOTE EARLY IN-PERSON: Early in-person voting begins April 27, 2026 and ends the Friday before the General Primary Election.
IF YOU WANT TO VOTE ON ELECTION DAY: Election Day voting means voting at your designated polling location based on where you are registered to vote.
Polls are open 7:00 am - 7:00 pm.
