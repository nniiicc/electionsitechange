PRIVACY POLICY
Nikema for Congress has created this privacy policy to explain how we use information that you may provide while visiting our website and to demonstrate our firm commitment to internet privacy.
Nikema for Congress may modify this policy from time to time, we encourage you to check this page when revisiting this website.
HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
When you sign up to volunteer, contribute, or take any other action on this site, we may ask you to give us contact information, including your first and last name, e-mail address, physical address and phone number.
We may obtain information about you from outside sources and add it to or combine it with the information we collect through this site.
We use this information to operate this site, send you news and information to you about Nikema for Congress & to solicit your participation in Nikema for Congress programs, events and activities, and obtain and confirm RSVP’s to events and programs.
We use your e-mail address to send such information by e-mail and may use your telephone number to call you for these purposes.
We will never provide your personal information or e-mail address to any other person or organization, for any purpose.
If you make a contribution online, we may also ask for your credit card number and expiration date.
That information is used solely for processing your contribution; is not maintained by our organization; and is never disclosed to anyone, for any other purpose than processing your contribution, under any circumstance.
Nikema for Congress does not share or sell collected information without consent or unless necessary to comply with the law.
TO UNSUBSCRIBE:
People who subscribe via this website will receive periodic updates from Nikema for Congress by e-mail or text.
You may opt out of receiving future communications by using the unsubscribe procedure specified in the message.
HOW TO CONTACT US
Questions regarding this privacy policy should be emailed to info@nikemawilliams.com
