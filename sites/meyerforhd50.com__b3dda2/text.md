Keep Me Posted Why I'm Running Families are paying more while roads, schools, water systems, and essential services struggle to keep up.
Growth should improve our quality of life, not leave working families with a bigger bill.
Roads & Infrastructure Our Roads Need to Keep Up With Our Growth Families sit in traffic every day because our roads were not built for the community we have become.
New developments keep getting approved while road projects lag years behind.
I'll fight for our fair share of state transportation funding and make sure infrastructure comes before density.
See the growth and infrastructure data Water Security Protecting Our Water, Planning Our Future Water is the defining issue of Eagle Mountain's future.
Large water-using developments keep getting approved without honest public accounting of the long-term impact on our supply.
I'll push for independent water impact assessments before major projects move forward, and for real transparency so families see actual data instead of optimistic projections.
Schools & Education Every Eagle Mountain Kid Deserves Great Schools Nearly half our population is under 18.
Our kids deserve classrooms that are not overcrowded and teachers who are supported and want to stay.
Families should not have to wonder whether Lake Mountain School District can keep pace with enrollment.
I'll fight for funding that matches our growth and protect the school mental health resources our kids depend on.
Recommended by UEA-PAC I'm grateful to have earned the Utah Education Association Political Action Committee's recommendation for House District 50.
Educators know what our students and schools need.
I'll listen to them and fight for public schools that can keep up with our growing community.
See UEA-PAC's recommended candidates Family & Disability Support Real Support for Families, Not Just Promises I'm raising a son on the autism spectrum, and my sister lives with a permanent disability in a full-time care facility.
Our family knows how exhausting it is to navigate waitlists and piece together support from systems that do not talk to each other.
I'll push for adequate funding for disability providers, shorter waitlists, and real coordination between schools, Medicaid programs, and community services.
