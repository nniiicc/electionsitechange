EASY VOLUNTEER GUIDE
For Supporters who care, but life may be busy, overwhelming, or you might be a little bit shy.
Not everyone is ready to knock on doors or speak at events—and that’s absolutely okay.
This guide is for those who want to support in small but meaningful ways, all of which make a big impact.
Support on Social Media
Set an alarm each evening to check posts of your supporting political candidates!
It will help more than you know
- Like posts
- Leave a supportive comment (even a heart emoji helps!)
- Share posts to your story or timeline
Why it matters: Every share expands our passive reach and tells the algorithm “this matters.”
Connect with People You Already Know
You don’t have to talk to strangers.
Start small.
- Mention the campaign in group chats
- Tell a friend or family member why you’re supporting
- Text a link to someone you trust
Why it matters: Word of mouth from people you trust is still the #1 way people make decisions.
Donate When You Can
Even $1 makes a difference in a grassroots campaign.
- Give when you feel comfortable
- Set a small monthly donation if you're able
- Attach a donation promise to a habit you are trying to kick
Why it matters: Every dollar helps fund yard signs, flyers, and outreach—no corporate money here.
Support Someone Who Volunteers
Behind every active volunteer is someone who’s cheering them on.
- Watch the kids while they canvass
- Help cook dinner when they’re at an event
- Encourage them and let them know their time matters
Why it matters: Campaigns are powered by networks of care, not just individuals.
Show Up for Your Community
You don’t have to show up for us, just show up.
- Attend festivals, parades, and farmers’ markets
- Say hi to neighbors
- Be present in your local space
Why it matters: Healthy, vibrant communities are the foundation of strong democracy—and that’s what we’re fighting for.
