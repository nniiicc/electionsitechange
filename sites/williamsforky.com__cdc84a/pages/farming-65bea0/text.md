AMERICAN FARM RESILIENCE
AND RENEWAL PLAN
Farmers and farmworkers sustain our country, and their work should provide a dependable path to health, security, and opportunity.
This plan expands access to healthcare, strengthens protections for agricultural workers, and helps family farms withstand financial hardship and unexpected disruptions.
Through training, mentorship, succession assistance, and support for stable employment, it creates opportunities for new farmers while helping established families pass viable operations to the next generation.
- Expands healthcare access for farmers and farmhands while strengthening rural medical services and protecting agricultural families from sudden losses of affordable coverage.
- Extends Medicaid eligibility to full-time farmers and farmhands earning under $50,000 annually.
- Gradually phases down healthcare assistance between $50,000 and $75,000 in annual income, providing a transition in coverage as earnings increase.
- Provides grants to rural clinics for mobile care units, preventative services, and mental health treatment tailored to agricultural communities.
- Creates accessible paths into farming by rebuilding local knowledge networks, strengthening producer cooperatives, and connecting new farmers with practical skills, mentorship, and markets.
- Funds agricultural training through universities, extension offices, and FFA chapters, with tax credits for experienced farmers who provide structured mentorship to new entrants.
- Supports cooperative grain elevators, shared equipment leasing, and cold-storage facilities that reduce startup and operating costs for beginning and small farmers.
- Expands farmers’ markets, farm-to-school purchasing, and local food distribution networks to help new growers reach buyers and keep agricultural spending within their communities.
- Strengthens small and family farms against economic shocks, weather disasters, and market volatility while helping producers build more dependable sources of income.
- Creates rapid-response relief payments for small and family farms harmed by droughts, floods, severe storms, or major trade disruptions.
- Allows qualifying small farms to refinance high-interest agricultural loans through USDA at reduced rates, with flexible repayment options during documented hardship.
- Expands insurance options for diversified and specialty crop farms while supporting mixed-crop production, on-farm renewable energy, and agritourism as additional income sources.
- Helps families transfer working farms across generations by supporting succession planning, affordable ownership transfers, and the resolution of inherited property disputes.
- Provides affordable legal, financial, and succession-planning assistance to help families prepare for changes in farm ownership and management before retirement or death.
- Offers low-interest USDA financing for family buyouts, allowing heirs who actively farm to compensate other heirs without selling productive acreage.
- Funds mediation and title-resolution assistance for inherited farmland with multiple owners, helping families resolve disputes and avoid preventable forced sales.
- Makes agricultural employment more dependable for farms and workers through predictable hiring, practical workforce pathways, and stronger protections against exploitation and unsafe conditions.
- Establishes clear application timelines and transparent processing for agricultural hiring programs, with dedicated assistance for small farms navigating workforce requirements.
- Creates workable visa options for year-round agricultural jobs, with fair wage requirements, domestic worker recruitment, and protections allowing workers to change participating employers.
- Strengthens enforcement against wage theft, unsafe working conditions and housing, and abusive recruitment fees, with protection from retaliation for workers who report violations.
