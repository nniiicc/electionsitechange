Get to know Ed Soliday: Married to Mary, father and grandfather Decorated Army veteran and pilot Community leader From serving our country to serving our community, Ed Soliday is dedicated to improving the lives of others.
As a helicopter pilot, Ed earned the Distinguished Flying Cross, two Bronze Stars and the Purple Heart for his service.
After leaving the Army, he had a distinguished career at United Airlines and is nationally recognized as an aviation safety expert.
While at United, he also led a nonprofit managing orphanages around the world.
He and his wife, Mary, have lived in Porter County for over 50 years and have two children and two grandsons.
Ed Soliday has Delivered Results for Porter County!
Voted for back-to-back record increases in K-12 funding and expanded work-based learning in our high schools.
Coauthored the largest tax cut in state history and returned over $1.5 billion to Hoosiers via taxpayer refunds.
Working to ensure the reliability and security of our electric grid, while protecting affordability.
Increasing competition and transparency in healthcare pricing to lower patient costs.
