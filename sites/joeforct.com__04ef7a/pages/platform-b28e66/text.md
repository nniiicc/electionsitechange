JOE'S PRIORITIES
Joe de la Cruz is a proven leader with decades of experience serving our community.
As a former State Representative, Town Councilor, and lifelong union member, Joe knows how to bring different people together and get things done for working families.
Joe wants to return to the Capitol to continue the important work of making lives better for our community.
As a State Representative, he built strong bipartisan relationships in Hartford that he'll leverage to deliver results for the 18th Senate District.
Joe has solutions that make sense.
Joe is One of Us for All of Us.
HEALTHCARE FOR OUR COMMUNITIES
Joe is fighting for universal health coverage so no family has to go without critical care, even when life circumstances change.
SUPPORT MENTAL HEALTH & ADDICTION RESOURCES

LOWER COSTS

SAFE NEIGHBORHOODS
