David Valadao to Represent CA-22 testing May 15 2026 PRESS RELEASE CA-22 Candidate Randy Villegas Endorsed By Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez AOC’s endorsement comes following the DCCC’s decision to meddle in CA-22’s primary Kern County, CA – Today, Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (NY-14) announced her endorsement of Randy Villegas in California’s 22nd district.
The endorsement comes just a week after the Democratic Congressional Campaign’s decision to put their thumb on the scale in the Central Valley by endorsing a Latino in this Latino majority district.
Earlier this week, a new poll showed Randy Villegas leading the DCCC’s handpicked candidate, Jasmeet Bains, 25 percent to 21 percent.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of AOC as we take on corporate greed and help working families in our districts,” said Randy Villegas, Congressional candidate in CA-22.
While D.C. insiders who haven’t stepped foot in the Central Valley are propping up my opponent who has taken hundreds of thousands of dollars from Big Pharma, I’m dedicated to tackling Trump’s healthcare cuts to ensure everyone can afford to go to the doctor.” Additional Context: AOC joins Senator Bernie Sanders, Congresswoman Nanette Barragán, Congressman Ro Khanna, Dolores Huerta, the Congressional Progressive Caucus, BOLD PAC , and the California School Employees Association (CSEA).
Following the DCCC’s decision to undercut Latino representation in a district that is #% Latino, Randy Villegas has seen an outpouring of contributions from individual donors and gained key endorsements.
Last week all four County Democratic Party Chairs in CA-22 pushed back against the DCCC’s endorsement of Bains.
#### Randy Villegas is a Central Valley native, auto repair shop owner, and a community college professor.
Growing up in a working-class immigrant family, Randy worked hard to help his parents make ends meet.
Randy’s family relied on the crucial federal programs like WIC and Medicaid that are now in danger of being cut.
Randy worked his way out of poverty through education and has dedicated his life to public service.
As a local and school board trustee Randy has delivered for working families by standing up for the best interests of our children.
As the only Latino and only Democrat on the school board, Randy has worked across the aisle to get things done.
Now, Randy is running for Congress to stand up for other hard-working families who he sees falling through the cracks on David Valadao’s watch.
