🏠 Housing
Stop corporations buying up our homes
We used to talk about the Central Valley as one of the only places in our state where an ordinary family could still afford to buy a home and have a good quality of life.
Now that dream is slipping further away as corporate landlords and Wall Street are robbing the American people of their dream of home ownership by buying up entire neighborhoods and pricing us out of the market.
I will fight to end corporate ownership of single-family homes.
Build affordable housing
We need an ambitious federal program to build an abundance of affordable and safe housing across the country, creating hundreds of thousands of well-paid union jobs in the process.
