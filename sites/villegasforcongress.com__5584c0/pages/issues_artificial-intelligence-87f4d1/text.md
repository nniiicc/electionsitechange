🧠 Artificial Intelligence
Artificial intelligence is changing our businesses, our schools, and our way of life, but Congress is doing little to protect workers and families against its potential dangers.
We need leaders who understand its risks and are willing to fight for a future that works for everyone.
Defend Your Right to Data Privacy: Your personal data should belong to you.
Billion-dollar tech corporations should not be able to sell personal data to advertisers or use it to train their AI models without your consent.
In Congress, Randy will work to enact legislation that protects your data from landing in the wrong hands.
Regulate AI Safety for All: Congress has been too slow to protect against the potential dangers of new AI technologies.
Randy will fight to protect workers, families, and children from its risks.
Protecting Families: In Congress, Randy will pass legislation mandating age verification and parental consent for high-risk AI tools, requiring bots to screen for content related to unsafe behavior, and cracking down on the creation and distribution of AI-generated child sexual abuse material with the full force of the law.
Protecting Workers: Technology should be a tool for workers, not a threat of replacement.
For workers across all industries, Randy will ensure large AI companies pay their fair share in taxes and will fight to protect working people’s job security against the threat of replacement by AI.
