Why I'm Running
I’m running as Democrat to represent District 149 because I believe our district deserves someone who listens first, works hard, and stays grounded in real life—not political noise.
For the past eight years, I’ve served on the South Berwick Town Council, working directly on the issues that shape people’s daily lives.
That experience taught me something important: good government isn’t about ideology—it’s about showing up, listening carefully, and making thoughtful decisions with limited resources.
I’m running because I think we can do better, and because I’ve already seen what works at the local level.
Listening Comes First
Serving on the Town Council put me face-to-face with neighbors, parents, retirees, business owners, and town staff.
I’ve listened to concerns about taxes, schools, infrastructure, and growth.
I’ve learned that most people want the same things: fairness, transparency, and practical solutions that actually move things forward.
People don’t want government to perform.
They want it to function.
Why Now
After eight years in local government, it’s clear to me that many of the challenges towns face are shaped—or constrained—by decisions made at the state level.
Communities like South Berwick need state representatives who understand how those policies land on the ground.
This feels like the right moment to bring that local perspective to Augusta.
I’m running now because I’m ready to step up, do the work, and advocate for solutions that respect both our towns and the people who live in them.
What I Bring to the Table
My experience on the Town Council taught me how to:
- Weigh competing priorities
- Work collaboratively across differences
- Make tough decisions in public, not behind closed doors
- Stay accountable to the people affected by those decisions
I bring a steady, thoughtful approach to problem-solving.
I value collaboration, even when it’s uncomfortable.
I don’t pretend to have all the answers—but I do commit to asking the right questions and listening before acting.
What Success Looks Like
Success isn’t measured by headlines or political wins.
It looks like:
- Constituents who feel heard and respected
- Clear communication about what’s happening in Augusta
- State decisions that support, not strain, local communities
- A representative who remains accessible and accountable
I’ve spent eight years doing this work locally.
I’m ready to continue it at the state level.
