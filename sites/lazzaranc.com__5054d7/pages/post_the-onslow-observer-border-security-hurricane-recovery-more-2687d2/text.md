MORE DISASTER RECOVERY FUNDING
This week, the NC Senate delivered $528 million in additional relief to help western NC communities rebuild after Hurricane Helene — supporting families, farmers, small businesses, and the rebuilding of local infrastructure.
Plus, House Bill 47 sets aside $217 million for eastern NC's continued recovery from Hurricanes Matthew & Florence.
This is a crucial investment in our state’s future, and I’ll keep pushing for the resources our communities deserve.

NC BORDER PROTECTION ACT
Illegal immigration is a public safety crisis.
That’s why I’m co-sponsoring Senate Bill 153 – the NC Border Protection Act – which we passed in the Senate earlier this month.
State law enforcement WILL work with ICE
No more sanctuary cities shielding criminals
Taxpayer benefits go to citizens, not illegal aliens
By passing this important legislation, we are strengthening law & order, supporting law enforcement officers, and protecting North Carolina families.
