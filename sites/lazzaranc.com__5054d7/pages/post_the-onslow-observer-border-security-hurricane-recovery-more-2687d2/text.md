The Onslow Observer: Border Security, Hurricane Recovery, & More...
Mar 20, 2025 2 min read Dear Neighbor, From securing more disaster recovery funding to strengthening public safety, I’m working hard this session to deliver results for Onslow County and all of North Carolina.
Whether it’s ensuring our communities get the resources they need to rebuild, supporting our state’s hospitality industry, or taking action to stop illegal immigration, I’m proud to stand up for the people of our state.
In this update, you’ll find highlights of key legislation we’ve passed to protect families, grow our economy, and uphold law & order.
As always, my office is here to serve you — please don’t hesitate to reach out if we can be of assistance.
At your service, Michael Lazzara North Carolina Senator MORE DISASTER RECOVERY FUNDING This week, the NC Senate delivered $528 million in additional relief to help western NC communities rebuild after Hurricane Helene — supporting families, farmers, small businesses, and the rebuilding of local infrastructure.
Plus, House Bill 47 sets aside $217 million for eastern NC's continued recovery from Hurricanes Matthew & Florence.
This is a crucial investment in our state’s future, and I’ll keep pushing for the resources our communities deserve.
LEGISLATIVE CHAMPION FOR NC RESTAURANT & LODGING ASSOCIATION It was an honor to be named Legislative Champion of the Year by the NC Restaurant & Lodging Association.
Our hospitality industry is a vital part of North Carolina’s economy, and I’m proud to support the businesses and workers who make it thrive.
Thank you to NCRLA for this recognition!
NC BORDER PROTECTION ACT Illegal immigration is a public safety crisis.
That’s why I’m co-sponsoring Senate Bill 153 – the NC Border Protection Act – which we passed in the Senate earlier this month.
State law enforcement WILL work with ICE No more sanctuary cities shielding criminals Taxpayer benefits go to citizens, not illegal aliens By passing this important legislation, we are strengthening law & order, supporting law enforcement officers, and protecting North Carolina families.
