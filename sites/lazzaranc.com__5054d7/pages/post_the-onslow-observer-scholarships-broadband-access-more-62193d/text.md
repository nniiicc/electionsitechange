GOLDEN LEAF SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM
High school seniors and community college transfer students from Onslow County and other rural areas of North Carolina may qualify for the Golden LEAF Scholarship.
This program offers up to $3,500 per year to help ease the cost of attending participating North Carolina colleges and universities.
📅 Application Deadline: March 1, 2026
💻 Learn More & Apply: scholars.goldenleaf.org

NEW BROADBAND ACCESS IN ONSLOW COUNTY
ICYMI: Spectrum has launched new gigabit broadband service in Onslow County for more than 950 homes and small businesses through its RDOF investment.
Reliable high-speed internet is essential for education, healthcare, small business, and economic opportunity — and this expansion will help close the digital divide in our communities.
ONSLOW COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE CITIZENS ACADEMY
Want a behind-the-scenes look at what it takes to serve and protect Onslow County?
The Onslow County Sheriff’s Office Citizens Academy is now accepting applications for its Spring 2026 session — and spots are filling fast!
This 10-week program gives residents a hands-on look at the daily work of deputies, including:
✅ Ride-alongs with deputies
✅ Tours of the Detention Center and 911 Center
✅ Meetings with the local District Attorney and a judge
There are only 12 seats left, and enrollment is first-come, first-served.
