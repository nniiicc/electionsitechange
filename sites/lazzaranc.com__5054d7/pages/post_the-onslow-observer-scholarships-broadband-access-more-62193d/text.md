At your service, Michael Lazzara North Carolina Senator HONORING OUR LAW ENFORCEMENT Earlier this month, we celebrated National Law Enforcement Appreciation Day — a time to pause and thank the dedicated law enforcement officers who serve Onslow County each day.
Their courage and commitment to protecting our communities make a real difference.
We are grateful for the work they do to keep our families and neighbors safe.
GOLDEN LEAF SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM High school seniors and community college transfer students from Onslow County and other rural areas of North Carolina may qualify for the Golden LEAF Scholarship.
This program offers up to $3,500 per year to help ease the cost of attending participating North Carolina colleges and universities. 📅 Application Deadline: March 1, 2026 💻 Learn More & Apply: scholars.goldenleaf.org SAVE THE DATE: TOWN HALL Join me for a coffee & conversation Town Hall on Saturday, February 21st from 9:00-10:30 AM at the Rotary Conference Center in Jacksonville.

NEW BROADBAND ACCESS IN ONSLOW COUNTY ICYMI: Spectrum has launched new gigabit broadband service in Onslow County for more than 950 homes and small businesses through its RDOF investment.
Reliable high-speed internet is essential for education, healthcare, small business, and economic opportunity — and this expansion will help close the digital divide in our communities.
