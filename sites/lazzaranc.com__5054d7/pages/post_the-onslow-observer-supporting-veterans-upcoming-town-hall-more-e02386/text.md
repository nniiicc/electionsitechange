DELIVERING RESULTS FOR VETERANS & MILITARY FAMILIES
As a Marine and your State Senator, I am honored to represent nearly 50,000 veterans and two active-duty military bases here in Onslow County.
Service runs deep in our community, and I remain committed to fighting in the NC General Assembly for our troops’ mission readiness — and for the support their families deserve at home.
Join our efforts at www.LazzaraNC.com!
