2025 PUBLIC SAFETY ACT
Earlier this year, my colleagues on the Senate Judiciary Committee and I passed Senate Bill 429, revising North Carolina law to increase penalties for secretly peeping into a room occupied by another person.
Starting December 1, 2025, tougher penalties went into effect for these crimes — especially when a camera or recording device is used.
These cases can now be charged as felonies, giving prosecutors the tools they need to pursue justice for victims and hold offenders accountable.
This law closes dangerous loopholes and makes it clear: we will NOT tolerate this kind of predatory behavior in our schools or communities.
Protecting our citizens and restoring peace of mind to victims is non-negotiable.
Stronger laws like SB 429 reflect that commitment.
As of December 1, another important provision of the NC Senate’s 2025 Public Safety Act is now law in North Carolina...
It is now a felony to knowingly allow a child under 16 to be exposed to illegal drugs.
This law is about one thing: protecting vulnerable children.
If you suspect a child is in danger, report it to local law enforcement immediately.
Your voice could save a life.
