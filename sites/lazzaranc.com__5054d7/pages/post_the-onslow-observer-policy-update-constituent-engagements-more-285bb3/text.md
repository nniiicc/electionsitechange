NC SENATE CROSSOVER HIGHLIGHTS
Crossover Week is behind us, and I’m proud to report the NC Senate moved forward practical, common-sense legislation, including:
The SCRIPT Act (SB 479): Strengthens community pharmacies and boosts transparency by giving patients more freedom to choose where they get their prescriptions, especially in underserved areas.
Title Fraud Prevention (SB 423): Helps combat real estate title fraud by requiring ID verification when recording property documents, protecting North Carolinians from scams and unlawful transfers.
The Farmland and Military Protection Act (SB 394): Protects North Carolina land and military bases by banning ownership of agricultural land and property near bases by adversarial foreign governments.
The Farmers Protection Act (SB 554): Prohibits banks from discriminating against farmers based on their use of traditional practices like fossil fuels and fertilizers.
HOA Reform (SB 378): Provides homeowners more rights and transparency, requiring fair procedures from homeowners’ associations and limiting predatory fines and contract terms.
The Parents Protection Act (SB 442): This bill reinforces parental rights by ensuring parents can raise their children consistent with their biological sex without fear of state interference.
Repeal of Certificate of Need Laws (SB 370): Cuts red tape in healthcare by repealing burdensome regulations that limited competition and access to care, particularly in underserved areas.
From protecting parental rights to securing farmland and cutting red tape in healthcare — we’re delivering results that matter to North Carolina families.
