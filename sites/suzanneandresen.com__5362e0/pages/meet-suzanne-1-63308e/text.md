She is dedicated to transparency, integrity, and long-term community well-being.
Trusted Voice for District 49 Suzanne is committed to representing District 49 with integrity.
She listens to residents’ concerns and works diligently to find practical solutions, honoring Maine’s values of community, stewardship, and resilience.
Committed to Community & Progress Suzanne is dedicated to serving District 49 through active community engagement and effective legislation.
Her priorities include affordable healthcare, fair energy policies, property tax fairness, aging-in-place support, and bolstering Maine’s working waterfront.
She believes strong policies build resilient communities and vibrant local economies.
Approach to Municipal Revaluation Suzanne is committed to supporting fair and efficient municipal revaluations.
With her experience in West Bath's revaluation process, she understands the challenges communities face, including the need for qualified firms, timely processes, and taxpayer fairness.
As your representative, she will advocate for legislative solutions that promote transparency, data-informed decisions, and balanced perspectives that benefit all residents.
Listening, Leading, Delivering Suzanne Andresen is dedicated to serving District 49 with honesty, persistence, and community focus.
She believes in steady advocacy, working together, and revisiting policies to ensure lasting stability and growth for Maine families.
