Welcome to Byron’s platform — where helping people isn’t just a promise, it’s the bedrock foundation of our campaign.
- Agriculture
- Oppose the development of new data center facilities on Indiana farmland and call on the State of Indiana to eliminate tax subsidies for data center projects.
- Protect the property rights of family farmers through policies that respect private landownership.
- Oppose legislation and zoning ordinances that fail to accommodate the needs of modern agriculture.
- Fully fund County Extension offices, support agricultural research, and invest in the innovation that keeps Indiana agriculture strong and competitive.
- Support lasting farmland property tax relief by promoting a fair, stable, and predictable assessment system that protects family farms and preserves Indiana's agricultural heritage for future generations.
- Public Health
- A strong Hoosier State is a healthy Hoosier State.
As State Senator, Byron will:
- Support policies that expand access to emergency and critical care for rural communities throughout the district.
- Expand addiction treatment and recovery resources that give every Hoosier a second chance to reach their full potential.
- Fight to improve maternal health outcomes and expand access to healthcare providers that serve families, parents, and older Hoosiers.
- Support increasing Medicaid income eligibility guidelines so working Hoosiers can maintain access to the healthcare coverage they need.
- Affordability
- Utility companies should be held accountable for transparent rate-setting processes and ensure Hoosiers have a clear understanding of proposed increases.
- Expanding reliable broadband access is essential to economic development, education, healthcare access, and connecting Hoosiers to the resources and support they need.
- Oppose unnecessary expansion of toll fees that create additional financial burdens for working families, farmers, and businesses.
