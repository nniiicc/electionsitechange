Skip to content ← Back to Newsletters Newsletter • August 24, 2026 Building Momentum Across Suffolk It has been a busy stretch on the campaign trail.
Trish Kopp has been traveling throughout the district and across Suffolk County, talking with people in our communities, listening to what matters to them, supporting fellow Democrats, and continuing to build this campaign.
From community events and fundraisers to exciting campaign news, there is a lot to catch up on.
Our Coalition Is Growing We are so excited and honored to announce the endorsements of New York State United Teachers (NYSUT) and Congressman Jamie Raskin!
Proud to have the support of leaders who stand up for public education, working families, and our democracy.
We’re building something special in Assembly District 7 and we’re just getting started!
New York State United Teachers endorses Trish Kopp Trish Kopp with Congressman Jamie Raskin Out Across Suffolk Team Trish has been spending time throughout the district and across Suffolk County, talking with people, listening to what matters to them, and supporting fellow Democrats who are running.
Here are some of the places the campaign has been lately.
Lynn Trupiano-O’Keefe Fundraiser Wonderful fundraiser with Lynn Trupiano-O’Keefe, candidate for New York State Assembly District 8, a dedicated community advocate working to bring strong, accountable leadership to Long Island.
Josh Slaughter Fundraiser It was great supporting Josh Slaughter, candidate for New York State Assembly District 3.
He is a strong union leader who understands the importance of protecting workers, strengthening organized labor, and making sure working families always have a voice in Albany.
Left on LI Youth Town Hall Our team loved tabling at Left on Long Island’s Youth Town Hall with our amazing interns and talking about the future of Long Island!
Monica Martinez Fundraiser Last week, we had the pleasure of celebrating Senator Monica Martinez’s birthday alongside friends and supporters, honoring her continued leadership and dedication to our Long Island communities!
Chris Gallant’s Fundraiser Had a great time at Chris Gallant’s fundraiser in the Hamptons, celebrating the work to flip Suffolk blue and the momentum to take back Congress!
Fire Island Craft Fair Had a great day tabling at the Fire Island Craft Fair, talking with residents and connecting with the community!
A huge shoutout to our East Islip team for putting it all together and making it such a great day.
