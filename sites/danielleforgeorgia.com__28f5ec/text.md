Meet Danielle Bell-Candidate for GA State House, District 44 We deserve better I am ready to serve YOU on day one!
Fighting to make everyday life more affordable for Georgians Danielle is a community-focused leader running for the Georgia State House to serve District 44.
Her vision is simple: every Georgian should have a chance to succeed, every voice should be heard, and state government should be built on trust, faith, service, and accountability.
Top Priorities Affordability Access to Healthcare Access to Healthcare Lowering housing costs, childcare expenses, and utility bills so hard working families can get ahead and build a future they an count on.

Danielle will listen first, tell the truth, and put people before politics.
Learn more Frequently Asked Questions Please reach us at info@danielleforgeorgia.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.
What office is Danielle Bell running for?
Danielle is running for Georgia State House of Representatives, District 44.
What qualities will make Danielle an effective representative in the Georgia State House?
Danielle's superpower is her ability to listen, and empathize.
She has done this for over two decades as a clinical social worker and therapist.
Danielle focuses on finding middle ground and developing solutions for issues everyone agrees on.
What is Danielle’s position on data centers and electricity rates?
Danielle would favor a Data Center Moratorium to better understand this techonology and its impacts.
As a law maker, Danielle would also ensure that the legislature works with the PSC to provide actual oversight of Georgia Power.
What ideas does Danielle have that can lower energy costs?
Expanding renewables such as solar in the state of Georgia, and providing legislative oversight to the onslaught of Data Centers in the state.
Why is healthcare access so important to Danielle?
How does she want to help?
As a licensed clinical social worker, Danielle has seen firsthand how her patients and family fell through the cracks of the healthcare system.
To address this, Danielle supports fully expanding Medicaid, which will fully insure hundreds of thousands of Georgians.
How does Danielle’s work as a therapist shape her views?
Danielle's work as a therapist has given her a firsthand look at the failures of the medical system.
Being a therapist also deepened her understanding of substance abuse and effective ways to treat it.
Does Danielle support raising the minimum wage in Georgia?
Danielle supports raising the minimum wage to $15, and she is also in favor of having it increase alongside the cost of living.
What is Danielle doing to understand what the people of her district care most about?
Every day Danielle engages with members of the district, whether it be phone calls or door knocking; Danielle always looks for feedback from the people who know best.
The constituents.
Through that outreach she has identified the key pillars of her campaign, and once elected, she will deliver on those promises.
Who funds Danielle's campaign?
Grassroots donors.
Since launching this election in 2025 I have received almost 2,000 individual donations!
How can I meet Danielle or get involved?
I have an open door policy, and love being easily accessible to constituents.
You can reach out to me at info@danielleforgeorgia.com with any questions!
