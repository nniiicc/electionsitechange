Issues & Platform
As your representative, I will fight tirelessly for the issues that matter most to the families of District 18.
My platform is built on listening to our communities and taking action on their behalf.
Affordable Healthcare
Healthcare is a right, not a privilege
Healthcare is a fundamental right, not a privilege.
I will fight to ensure every family in District 18 has access to quality, affordable healthcare.
- Lowering prescription drug costs for seniors
- Protecting and expanding the Affordable Care Act
- Increasing access to mental health services
- Supporting community health centers
- Addressing the healthcare worker shortage
“Healthcare is a right, not a privilege”
— Deva Simmons

Economic Stability

- Protecting workers' rights to organize
- Lowering the cost of living for families
“An economy that works for all”
