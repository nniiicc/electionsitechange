Registration is closed See other events Time & Location Oct 04, 2026, 3:30 PM – 6:30 PM Monett City Park Casino, 101 S Lincoln Ave, Monett, MO 65708, USA About the event Allied Democrats of the Ozarks is hosting the Fourth Quarter Fundraiser on Sunday, October 4, from 3:30 PM to 6:30 PM at The Casino in Monett Park.
Tickets are $50 per person, with all proceeds directly supporting participating candidates in Missouri's 7th Congressional District.
The afternoon schedule features doors opening at 3:30 PM, candidate speeches at 4:00 PM, and a full dinner served at 4:45 PM.
An after-dinner speaker will present at 5:30 PM, followed by a live pie auction, silent auction winner announcements, and the 50/50 raffle drawing at 6:00 PM.
The dinner menu includes spaghetti with sauce, meatballs (with beef and Alpha-Gal friendly options available), fresh bread, salad, and dessert.
