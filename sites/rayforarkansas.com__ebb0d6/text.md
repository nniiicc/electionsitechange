A Message from David Ray
As your Representative in the Arkansas House, I have been a leader in the fight for lower taxes, limited government, and defending Arkansas family values from attacks by the radical Left.
My goal is to protect our God-given individual liberties and to make Arkansas the best possible place to live, work, raise a family, and start a business.
I am a Christian, a husband, a father, and an Arkansas conservative, and it is truly an honor to represent District 69 (northern Pulaski and southern Faulkner Counties) in the Arkansas House of Representatives.
If I can ever help or answer a question, please call or text me on my cell phone at (501) 916-0286.
