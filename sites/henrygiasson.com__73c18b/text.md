Meet Your Candidate
Henry Giasson spent 24 years in the Army, serving in war zones, peacekeeping missions, and disaster relief operations.
He's seen firsthand what duty means — putting service before self, standing firm under pressure, and always completing the mission.
Now retired from military service, Henry is running for the New Hampshire House to bring that same unwavering sense of duty to Goffstown.
He believes government has lost its way, growing too big, spending too much, and forgetting its basic responsibilities to the people.
His approach is as straightforward as his military service: follow the Constitution, protect individual liberty, and keep government small.
Henry knows that less government spending means lower taxes, and lower taxes mean Goffstown families keep more of their hard-earned money.
