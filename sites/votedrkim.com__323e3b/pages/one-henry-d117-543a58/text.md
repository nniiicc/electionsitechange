OUR MESSAGE: ONE HENRY
One Voice.
One Vote.
One Henry.
Every person has a story.
Every vote matters.
Henry County is one community.
For Dr.
Kim Thomas Smith, this slogan isn’t just a phrase—it’s how she has lived and served for decades.
She believes leadership starts with listening and is strengthened when everyone feels represented, not just the loudest voices or the most powerful ones.
What “One Voice” Means
“One Voice” means every resident deserves to be heard—seniors, families, students, small business owners, caregivers, and neighbors who often feel overlooked.
Kim knows people don’t need politicians who talk at them; they need leaders who listen to them.
That’s why she is committed to creating real ways for the community to be part of the legislative process, including regular town hall meetings, community listening sessions, and resident surveys.
Because when people are heard, the laws and policies she champions will reflect real needs not just politics.
How “One Voice” Turns Into Action
This is how “One Voice” turns into action:
- Listening leads to understanding.
- Understanding leads to informed decisions.
- Informed decisions lead to better laws and stronger representation.
Kim’s leadership is rooted in respect, collaboration, and service.
She is committed to being a State Representative who reflects the people she serves—where every voice matters, every vote counts, and every resident knows they have a seat at the table.
What “One Vote” Means
“One Vote” means every voice counts, no matter who you are or where you live in Henry County.
Kim believes participation is power.
Voting is how residents shape their future, hold leaders accountable, and make sure their interests are represented at the State Capitol, not just the interests of lobbyists or special interests.
She will work to expand civic engagement, keep residents informed, and make sure state government is accessible, transparent, and responsive.
What “One Henry” Means
“One Henry” means we are stronger together.
Henry County isn’t made up of separate neighborhoods or competing interest, it’s one community with shared hopes and shared challenges.
Kim believes that listening leads to informed decisions, and informed decisions lead to better legislation and stronger advocacy for Henry County at the state level.
What You Can Expect From Dr.
Kim
If elected, Kim will lead with a clear, consistent approach:
- A listening-first office that stays connected to residents year-round
- Regular community touchpoints (town halls, listening sessions, surveys)
- Transparent updates so residents know what’s happening at the Capitol and why
- Partnership-driven problem solving with local leaders, community organizations, and everyday neighbors
This isn’t politics for show.
It’s service brought to the policy level.
