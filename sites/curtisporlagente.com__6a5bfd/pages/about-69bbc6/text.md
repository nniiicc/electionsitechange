As a 4th generation Idahoan and a graduate of Columbia High School, I’ve watched our community grow and change from every angle.
Today, I live and work in the heart of District 12, serving our neighbors at the Dutch Bros window and finishing my degree in Religious Studies at LSUA Online.
My perspective isn't shaped by an inherited political legacy; it’s shaped by the same hard work and daily life that defines our district.
A Legacy of Service and Hard Work This commitment to service is a value passed down through generations of my family.
My grandfather dedicated his life to public service, first in the U.S.
Air Force and later serving as a city mechanic in South Gate for over 35 years.
My mother has spent her career as a public servant in the medical profession, caring for people in their most vulnerable moments, while my father has been a working-class man his entire life, building our communities through construction and the trades.
Their examples of dedication to others are what lead me to advocate for our neighbors today.
An Inclusive Vision for District 12 My own path of service has taken many forms, from competing as a National Qualifier in Congressional Debate across Idaho to serving as a Brazilian Children’s Ministry Pastor.
My faith and my background as an openly gay man have taught me that every person deserves dignity and a seat at the table, regardless of their story.
I am not in this race to run against a name; I am running to ensure the people of District 12 have a representative who truly understands their lives.
Inspired by the concept of Dannelse—the holistic growth of every individual—I am dedicated to protecting our intellectual freedom and building a foundation where every neighbor is empowered to thrive.
