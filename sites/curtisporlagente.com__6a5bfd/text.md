WHO IS cURTIS
A 4th generation Idahoan and Nampa local, Curtis James is a working-class candidate running to represent the families and workers of District 12.
From serving neighbors at the Dutch Bros window to studying the diverse perspectives of Religious Studies at LSUA Online, Curtis understands that true representation isn't inherited—it’s earned through hard work and a commitment to service.
Guided by the values of Transparency, Availability, and Accountability, he is dedicated to the principle of Dannelse—fostering a community where intellectual freedom and personal growth are protected.
Curtis isn't running to represent a political legacy; he’s running to be a shield for your self-determination and a voice for the working class.
