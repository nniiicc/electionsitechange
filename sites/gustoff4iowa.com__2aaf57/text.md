ABOUT BILL
Bill Gustoff is a native Iowan with a long history of fighting for individual liberties, constitutional principles, limited government, religious freedom, and making Iowa a friendly place to do business and raise a family.
First elected to the Iowa House in 2022, Bill now takes that energy and passion to the Iowa House of Representatives to secure the blessings of liberty for future generations of Iowans.
