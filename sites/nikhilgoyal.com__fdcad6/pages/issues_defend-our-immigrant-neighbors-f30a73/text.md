Defend Our Immigrant Neighbors
Nikhil is the proud son of Indian immigrants who came to this country to pursue the American Dream.
He loves that Vermont is home to so many immigrants and refugees who contribute to our economy and way of life.
Abolishing ICE is the conservative position.
It is time to prosecute ICE.
When Mohsen and Rümeysa and immigrant farmworkers and construction workers were kidnapped by ICE, he spoke up at rallies in their defense.
When the Trump administration announced a new ICE surveillance center in Williston, he joined fellow Vermonters to protest.
He has fought against deportations, family separation, and human rights abuses throughout his career.
As your State Senator, Nikhil will use his platform and voice to stand up for the safety and dignity of immigrants and accompany them to their court hearings and check-ins with ICE.
