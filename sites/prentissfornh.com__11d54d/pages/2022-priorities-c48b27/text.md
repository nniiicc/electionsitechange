Sue’s Seven
Plans to create real change.
1
An Affordable N.H. for All
Livable wages, affordable housing, lower energy costs, and small businesses that drive New Hampshire's economy.
2
Healthcare and Public Health
Invest in accessible healthcare, strengthen systems of care, and build a modern public health infrastructure.
3
Childcare and Family Support
Expand access to affordable childcare so every family can work, thrive, and give their children a strong start.
4
Supporting New Hampshire's Rural Hospitals
Protect rural hospitals to ensure every community has access to high-quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
5
Supporting New Hampshire's Infrastructure
Repair roads and bridges, modernize critical infrastructure, and expand affordable, reliable high-speed internet to every New Hampshire community.
6
Protecting Public Education
Support fair school funding, strengthen public education, and provide property tax relief for New Hampshire families.
7
Reproductive Rights
Protect safe, legal access to reproductive healthcare by enshrining those rights in New Hampshire law.
