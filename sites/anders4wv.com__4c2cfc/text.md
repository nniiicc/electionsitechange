Legislative Policy Chris is dedicated to restoring and defending the Constitution and your individual liberty, both personal and economic.
Moving into the 2027 Legislative Session , Chris will be championing legislation to: Ban FLOCK Cameras and Mass Warrantless Surveillance — Stop government from tracking the movements of innocent West Virginians without a warrant and restore the protections of the 4th Amendment .
End Backdoor Red Flag Gun Confiscation — Protect due process and ensure government cannot strip a law-abiding citizen of their 2nd Amendment rights without being charged with or convicted of a crime.
Defend and Expand the 2nd Amendment — oppose unconstitutional firearms registries, and defend the fundamental right of West Virginians to keep and bear arms.
Restore the 4th Amendment and End the Open Fields Doctrine — Protect West Virginians against warrantless government searches and surveillance on their own private property.
Private property means private property.
Get a warrant.
Restore Medical Freedom — End government mandates and protect the right of parents and individuals to make their own medical decisions without government coercion.
Stop the Trafficking of Abortion Pills into West Virginia — Strengthen protections for unborn life and hold those who illegally traffic abortion drugs into our state accountable.
Defend Private Property Against Eminent Domain Abuse — Stop government from taking a West Virginian's land simply to hand it over for the profit of another private corporation.
Economic development does not override private property rights.
Overhaul West Virginia's Education System — Return power to parents, taxpayers, teachers, and local communities; expand educational freedom; demand accountability; and reduce the power of unelected bureaucracies that continue to spend more while producing unacceptable results.
Restore Economic Liberty — Cut taxes, eliminate unnecessary regulations, reduce government spending, and get government out of the way of West Virginia families, farmers, workers, and small businesses.
Reduce the Size and Scope of Government — Eliminate wasteful programs and unnecessary bureaucracy while returning power from Charleston to the people.
Protect Personal and Economic Liberty — Whether it is your property, your paycheck, your medical decisions, your firearms, or your privacy, government should respect the rights of the individual.
