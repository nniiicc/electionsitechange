About Chris
Meet Delegate Chris Anders
West Virginia House of Delegates – District 97
Constitutional Conservative | Defender of Liberty | Voice for the People
Chris Anders serves in the West Virginia House of Delegates with a clear and unwavering mission: to defend liberty, restrain government, and protect the God-given rights of West Virginians.
Representing the 97th District, Chris brings more than two decades of experience in grassroots politics and the private sector to Charleston—not to blend in, but to stand up and fight back.
Chris lives near Martinsburg with his wife Laura, where they raise animals and live quietly on a small farm—far from the halls of power, and exactly where he belongs: among the people he serves.
Freedom Caucus Chair
S.
Chris Anders was elected by his fellow State Senators and Delegates to serve as Chair of the West Virginia Freedom Caucus, a recognition of his consistent leadership and unwavering commitment to constitutional principles.
As Chair, he works alongside a coalition of like-minded legislators to advance policies that defend individual liberty, protect private property, and rein in the size and scope of government.
His election reflects the trust of his peers and his proven record of standing firm on principle, even in the face of political pressure.
A Philosophy Rooted in Principle
Chris Anders is a constitutional conservative whose worldview is shaped by natural rights, Biblical values, and Founding-era principles.
He believes:
- Government’s only legitimate role is to protect life, liberty, and property.
- Our rights come from God—not government, and must never be surrendered for convenience or compliance.
- The Tenth Amendment must be enforced—the states are sovereign, and the federal government must be shoved back into its constitutional box.
- Bureaucrats—whether in Washington or Charleston—have no right to rule over free people.
Chris draws inspiration from the boldness and wisdom of Thomas Jefferson, Patrick Henry, Samuel Adams, Frederic Bastiat, and Ron Paul—men who understood that liberty must be defended with action, not just words.

Legislative Leadership & Sponsored Bills
Chris doesn’t just talk—he leads.
Below are key bills he has sponsored or co-sponsored to restore constitutional government:
HB 2469 – Abolish the “Rain Tax”
Blocks cities and counties from charging stormwater fees that tax residents for simply owning property.
“You shouldn’t be taxed for rain falling on your land.”
HB 5313 – The Fourth Amendment Protection Act
Bans state and local agencies from aiding warrantless federal surveillance programs.
“If they want to spy on West Virginians, they’d better bring a warrant—or pack their bags.”
HB 5105 – Medical Freedom & Big Pharma Accountability
Strips pharmaceutical companies of immunity and lets citizens sue for vaccine injuries—restoring accountability.
Universal Ban on Vaccine Mandates(Legislative push across multiple bills)
Chris supports and has backed legislation to **ban all vaccine mandates—**not just COVID—for both children and adults.
“Your body, your medical decisions.
Period.”
HB 4386 – Repeal Certificate of Need (CON)
Breaks up government-protected monopolies in healthcare, allowing competition and expanding access.
HB 4299 – Ban Abortion Pill Trafficking
Criminalizes the illegal distribution of chemical abortion drugs in West Virginia.
HB 4298 – Eminent Domain Reform
Protects property owners from seizure by out-of-state corporations or government cronies.
HB 4313 – Constitutional Carry in the Capitol
Ensures law-abiding citizens can carry firearms with a concealed carry permit inside the West Virginia Capitol—restoring the right to self-defense where it matters most.
HB 4808 – Ban COVID Vaccine Mandates in Schools
Prohibits COVID shots from being mandated in West Virginia’s K-12 public education system.
State-Level Criminalization of Illegal Immigration
Chris supports making illegal immigration a state-level crime, empowering local law enforcement to act where the federal government fails.
