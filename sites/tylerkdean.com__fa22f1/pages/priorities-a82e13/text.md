Priorities
Unity-Driven Progress for Kentucky Families
SAFER COMMUNITIES
Kentucky families deserve to feel safe in their homes, schools, and neighborhoods — no exceptions.
Real safety isn’t about political talking points; it’s about practical investments that work.
I support strengthening local law enforcement partnerships, expanding mental health and addiction services, improving emergency response, and giving communities the tools they need to prevent crime before it happens.
Safety comes from stability, opportunity, and support — and I’ll work with anyone, Republican or Democrat, to make sure every Kentucky family feels protected and respected.
AFFORDABLE LIVING
Kentuckians are being squeezed by rising costs while politicians argue instead of act.
Families need relief now — not someday and not after another round of partisan games.
I’ll fight to lower healthcare costs, protect Medicaid, defend SNAP, reduce prescription prices, and make sure working families can afford groceries, utilities, and childcare.
I believe in budgeting responsibly, protecting essential services, and ensuring that Kentucky’s economy works for the people who make it run.
No extremes — just common-sense solutions that help families breathe easier.
THRIVING SMALL BUSINESSES
Small businesses are the backbone of Kentucky’s economy — from Lexington to Danville to every town in between.
After 25 years in the Kentucky workforce, I know firsthand how hard it is to keep a business running and a team supported.
I’ll work to cut red tape, expand access to capital, protect local entrepreneurs, and make sure small businesses get their fair share of state resources.
When local businesses thrive, communities thrive — and I’ll always fight to keep Kentucky’s economy strong, competitive, and open for growth.
STRONG SCHOOLS
Strong public schools build strong communities.
Period.
Kentucky children deserve classrooms with qualified teachers, safe buildings, modern tools, and the support they need to succeed — no matter their ZIP code.
I oppose out-of-state voucher schemes that drain money away from public schools and threaten rural districts.
I support fully funding public education, raising teacher pay, and keeping decisions close to parents, teachers, and local communities.
Our kids deserve better than political battles; they deserve opportunity.
PRACTICAL SOLUTIONS THAT HELP FAMILIES
Kentucky families don’t need more noise — they need problem-solvers.
I’ll always approach issues with common sense, not partisan angles.
That means protecting healthcare, supporting caregivers, strengthening working families, and giving parents and seniors the tools they need to thrive.
My priorities come from real life: raising kids, caring for aging parents, working full-time jobs, and dealing with the everyday pressures Kentucky families face.
I’ll focus on what actually helps people, not what makes headlines, and I’ll work with anyone to deliver real results.
Health
Protecting Kentucky Families, Strengthening Care, and Fixing What Frankfort Broke
Health shouldn’t be a political football.
It’s the foundation of strong families, strong communities, and a strong Kentucky.
But recent cuts to Medicaid, SNAP, and veterans’ services, along with harmful changes to how nurses are classified, have created a crisis that working families are paying for every single day.
Tyler believes in practical, people-first solutions that protect care, invest in prevention, and keep our communities healthy and strong.
Cuts to Medicaid and the Impact on Kentuckians
Medicaid is a lifeline for seniors, children, people with disabilities, and working families — especially in rural counties where access to doctors and hospitals is already limited.
But recent cuts and redeterminations have pushed tens of thousands of Kentuckians off their coverage, driving up costs, closing rural clinics, and increasing pressure on emergency rooms.
- Fully fund Medicaid and stabilize it long-term
- Ensure no Kentuckian loses coverage due to paperwork or politics
- Increase reimbursement rates so hospitals — especially rural ones — can stay open
- Modernize the system so people keep coverage while they work, care for family, or face temporary setbacks
Medicaid cuts don’t save money — they shift costs onto families and force hospitals to the brink.
Tyler will fight to stop the bleeding and strengthen care across the Commonwealth.
Cuts to SNAP Benefits and the Impact on Kentuckians
SNAP is one of the most efficient, effective programs for reducing hunger — especially for children, seniors, and low-wage workers.
The recent federal cuts and new work requirements mean tens of thousands of Kentuckians are now at risk of losing food assistance, even as grocery prices continue to climb.
- Oppose harmful SNAP restrictions that punish families
- Ensure the state steps up when federal cuts threaten hunger
- Support food banks, senior nutrition, and school meal programs
- Protect Kentucky’s working poor, who rely on SNAP to bridge the gap
No Kentuckian should have to choose between feeding their family and paying their bills.
Cuts to Veterans Services and the Impact on Kentuckians
Kentucky has one of the highest rates of military service in the nation.
Yet veterans are facing shorter clinic hours, reduced mental health access, longer wait times, and cuts to community-based care.
Many of these federal reductions shift costs to the state — and Frankfort has yet to offer a plan to support veterans who fall through the cracks.
- Fully fund veteran services, especially mental health and disability support
- Protect community clinics that serve rural veterans
- Expand state support for housing, jobs, and medical care
- Demand transparency and accountability for how veteran funds are spent
Kentucky’s veterans kept their promises to us.
It’s time for us to keep ours to them.
Reclassification of Nurses as Employees
Kentucky’s nursing workforce is already stretched thin.
Reclassifying nurses in ways that limit flexibility, reduce pay, or force burdensome employment structures risks driving more nurses away from the profession — worsening shortages in hospitals, long-term care facilities, and rural health systems.
- Oppose any reclassification that harms nurse flexibility or wages
- Protect the nursing workforce from unnecessary, politically driven regulation
- Ensure nurses can choose the employment structures that work for their families
- Support incentives to recruit and retain nurses across Kentucky
If we want healthy communities, we must respect and support the people who provide care.
State Government Investment in Preventive Health
Kentucky ranks among the highest in chronic illnesses — including heart disease, diabetes, cancer, and addiction.
Yet our state invests far too little in prevention.
Prevention saves lives and money by catching problems early, reducing hospitalizations, and strengthening families and workplaces.
- Increase investment in preventative screening, maternal health, and behavioral health
- Support school-based health programs and rural access to early intervention
- Expand addiction prevention and recovery services
- Ensure state health dollars focus on long-term outcomes, not short-term politics
Preventative health isn’t just good policy — it’s common-sense economics.
