Safer Communities Affordable Living Thriving Small Businesses Strong Schools Practical solutions that help families Learn More What Makes District 12 Unique A Blend of Urban, Suburban & Rural Kentucky District 12 covers Boyle County, Kentucky, Mercer County, Kentucky, Woodford County, Kentucky, and part of Fayette County, Kentucky.
The Fayette-County portion brings in parts of Lexington, Kentucky, with its more urban/suburban dynamics, universities, and cultural economy.
Woodford, Mercer, and Boyle Counties — along with smaller towns in the Fayette portion — represent Kentucky’s small towns, rural areas, horse country, bourbon country, and traditional working-class communities.
That mix gives the district a diverse demographic and economic identity: from young families and professionals in Lexington to farmers, small-business owners, bourbon and hospitality workers, and long-time rural residents.
