FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: July 1, 2020 CONTACT: (920) 850-4521 RACHAEL CABRAL-GUEVARA ENDORSED BY WRTL Wisconsin Right to Life Makes Endorsement in Assembly District 55 [NEENAH, WI…] Rachael Cabral-Guevara today announced that she has been endorsed for the 55th Assembly District by Wisconsin Right to Life (WRTL).
Kristen Nupson, WRTL Political Action Committee Director, issued a letter of endorsement stating, ​“We deeply appreciate your strong advocacy for the right to life.
Over the course of your time on the campaign trail, we have been privileged to witness your vocal support of legislative measures that protect the sanctity of life and opposition to measures that would demean human life.
We believe your election would greatly contribute to the building of a culture of life in your community and in our state.” Cabral-Guevara said she’s honored to have the endorsement of Wisconsin Right to Life.
“I’m proudly pro-life and I believe a society should advocate for a culture of life, not death,” Cabral-Guevara said.
“As a medical professional, my goal is to care for all human life and with the scientific gains in neonatology we know that unborn babies smile, laugh and suck their thumbs.
We must do all we can to protect human life and the unborn.” Cabral-Guevara is also endorsed by the Wisconsin Nurses Association, State Representatives and a Neenah alderwoman.
Rachael Cabral-Guevara is the owner of Nurse Practitioner Health Services, LLC (NPHS), which provides direct access healthcare to families with high deductibles or no insurance.
NPHS provides house calls, nursing services, labs, X-ray, and a full services clinic in Appleton.
She earned a Masters in Science Nursing from UW-Milwaukee and a Bachelors of Science in Nursing from UW-Oshkosh.
In addition, Cabral-Guevara has served as a senior lecturer at UW-Oshkosh for more than a decade.
Her role at UW-Oshkosh includes instructing more than 90 students in both classroom and clinical nursing.
Cabral-Guevara lives in Neenah and is mother of four.
She is a Neenah Rotary member and volunteer at Alliance Elementary School. —30—
