​FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: April 11, 2022 CONTACT: (920) 850-4521 RACHAEL CABRAL-GUEVARA ENDORSED BY REPRESENTATIVE DAVE MURPHY [Fox Crossing, WI] - Today, State Representative Dave Murphy (R-Greenville) announced his endorsement of Rachael Cabral-Guevara in her run for the 19th State Senate District.
“I’m honored to stand and fight for the Fox Valley with my friend Rachael.
She’s a rising star with tremendous skills.
Whether it’s healthcare, education, or the economy, her experience as a nurse practitioner, teacher and business owner will serve our families well,” said Representative Murphy.
Murphy currently represents the 56th Assembly District, one of three assembly districts within the 19th Senate District.
He has served the district since 2013.
“I am honored to receive Dave’s support in this race.
He has been a true advocate for folks here in the Valley and I hope to continue working with him in the legislature to improve the lives of hardworking families across the area,” said Rep.
Cabral-Guevara.
