Rachael for Assembly 55th Assembly District RachaelForAssembly.com FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: July 13, 2020 CONTACT: (920) 850-4521 FVI’s ED PERKINS ENDORSES RACHAEL CABRAL GUEVARA FOR 55th AD County GOP Exec Member and Fox Valley Initiative’s Leader Endorses in GOP Primary [NEENAH, WI…] The Assembly campaign of Rachael Cabral Guevara today announced the endorsement of Ed Perkins, a member of the Outagamie Republican Party Executive Committee and leader of the conservative Fox Valley Initiative.
Perkins, a Outagamie County Republican Party Executive Committee Member, said he knows “Rachael has the support of many Republicans along with many local Republican leaders.” “I know Rachael would make an excellent 55th representative.
One of the reasons for me is her medical and business background,” said Perkins.
“We need someone like Rachael in the Legislature when the Assembly deals with health and medical issues.” Cabral Guevara, a small business owner, Nurse Practitioner and nursing instructor at UW-Oshkosh, is running for the 55th Assembly District to replace outgoing Rep.
Mike Rohrkaste (R-Neenah).
The Fox Valley Initiative is a conservative group that seeks to inform Appleton and Fox Valley area voters of state, local and federal issues that impact the everyday lives of citizens and families.
Cabral Guevara said she was thankful for the endorsement from the local conservative and Republican leader.
“Fox Valley voters deserve a strong voice in Madison who’ll fight to protect our tax dollars, make commonsense decisions regarding health care, and support our main street businesses,” said Cabral Guevara.
“I’m honored to have Ed Perkins endorsement.
I’ll work hard to protect our freedoms and liberties, and ensure state government is held accountable to area citizens.
Cabral-Guevara is also endorsed by the Wisconsin Nurses Association, Wisconsin Right to Life and current state representatives.
Rachael Cabral Guevara is the owner of Nurse Practitioner Health Services, LLC (NPHS), which provides direct access healthcare to families with high deductibles or no insurance.
NPHS provides house calls, nursing services, labs, X-ray, and a full services clinic in Appleton and Green Bay.
She earned a Masters in Science Nursing from UW-Milwaukee and a Bachelors of Science in Nursing from UW-Oshkosh.
In addition, Cabral Guevara has served as a senior lecturer at UW-Oshkosh for more than a decade.
Her role at UW-Oshkosh includes instructing more than 80 students in both classroom, clinical, and labs on clinical nursing.
Cabral Guevara lives in Neenah and is mother of four.
She is a Neenah Rotary member, volunteer at Alliance Elementary School and Boy Scout volunteer.
