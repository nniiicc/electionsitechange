Accessibility
Haskins for Kansas | Effective Date: August 2026
Haskins for Kansas is committed to ensuring that haskinsforkansas.com is accessible to all visitors, including those with disabilities.
We believe every Kansan deserves equal access to information about their representative and this campaign.
Our commitment
We strive to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.
These guidelines help make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.
Accesibility features
We work to ensure this site includes clear heading structures that support screen reader navigation, sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds, descriptive alt text for images, keyboard-navigable menus and links, and readable font sizes throughout.
Known limitations
We are actively working to improve the accessibility of this site.
If you encounter any content that is not accessible to you, we want to hear about it.
Feedback and contact
If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this site or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at: Haskins for Kansas, 1430 SW Woodhull St., Unit 3911, Topeka, KS 66604, or visit haskinsforkansas.com to find current contact information.
We will make every effort to respond promptly and provide the information you need in an accessible format.
Alternative access
If you are unable to access any content or functionality on this site due to a disability, please contact us and we will work to provide that information through an alternative method.
