At the Statehouse, Kirk Haskins has stood up for voting access, public schools, and fair policies that put people over politics.
As Ranking Minority Member on the House Elections Committee, he’s led the fight against voter suppression and worked to ensure every Kansan’s voice is heard.
01.
AFFORDABILITY Making Kansas More Affordable Kansas should be a place where working families can keep more of their hard-earned money and easily build a future.
Kirk is fighting to lower living costs for Kansas families by cutting taxes on everyday essentials, reducing utility and healthcare expenses, and delivering direct property tax relief for homeowners and renters.
02.
VOTING RIGHTS Securing Your Right to Vote Every eligible Kansan deserves a voice — and Kirk has spent his time in Topeka making sure that voice isn't silenced.
As Ranking Member of the House Elections Committee, he has been on the front lines opposing every bill designed to make it harder for Kansans to cast their ballot, register to vote, or have their vote counted.
03.
EDUCATION Protecting Our Public Schools ​ Strong schools don't happen by accident — they happen when legislators show up, fight for funding, and refuse to let public education become a political punching bag.
Kirk has done exactly that.
From fully funding K-12 classrooms and fighting for teacher due process rights, to supporting the behavioral health resources that students increasingly need just to get through the school day, Kirk believes that investing in Kansas kids is the single best investment the state can make.
04.
HEALTHCARE Healthcare That Works for Every Kansan Too many Kansans are one diagnosis away from financial disaster.
Kirk believes access to quality, affordable healthcare is not a privilege reserved for those with the right job or the right zip code — it is something every Kansas family deserves.
