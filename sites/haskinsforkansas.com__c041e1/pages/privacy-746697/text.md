Privacy Policy
Haskins for Kansas | Effective Date: August 2026
Haskins for Kansas ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information gathered through haskinsforkansas.com.
Information we collect
We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number when you sign up for updates, contact us through the site, or make a contribution.
We also collect standard website usage data such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site through analytics tools.
How we use your information
Information you provide may be used to send campaign updates and newsletters, respond to inquiries, process contributions, and contact you about volunteer opportunities or events.
We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.
Contributions
Online contributions are processed through a secure third-party platform.
We do not store your payment information on our servers.
Contributions to Haskins for Kansas are not tax-deductible.
Cookies
This site may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and analyze site traffic.
You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function properly as a result.
Third-party links
Our site may contain links to external websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.
Contact
