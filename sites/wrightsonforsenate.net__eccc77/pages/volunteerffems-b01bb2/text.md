These men and women sacrifice nights, weekends, and family time to answer the call.
We cannot continue taking them for granted.
My plan begins with retention because we cannot out-recruit poor retention.
Departments must offer structured mentorship, clear training pathways, flexible opportunities, and welcoming cultures.
We should also recognize that volunteers can serve through communications, fundraising, administration, technology, and other support roles.
There should be a place for everyone who wants to help.
Pennsylvania should establish recruitment programs and promote volunteer service as a pathway to careers in emergency services, healthcare, and public safety.
To reward and retain volunteers, I would: Expand earned-income tax benefits, including a state earned-income tax credit based on qualifying service.
Make Property Tax Credits Mandatory in all Municipalities.
Help volunteers purchase required protective equipment when departments cannot cover the cost.
Offer tax credits to employers that allow volunteers to answer emergencies during working hours.
Create a statewide workers’ compensation coverage for volunteers injured while serving.
Similar to State Park Volunteer Program Expand PA SERVPA with secure firefighter and EMS credentialing to strengthen mutual aid during major emergencies.
Allowing for more available personnel.
Pennsylvania has asked volunteers to do more with fewer people and resources for too long.
They are our neighbors, relatives, friends, and coworkers.
They risk life and limb while the rest of folks are asleep at home.
Pennsylvania must do more than thank them.
We must invest in them.
