Press For Immediate Release July 25, 2026 — Ingham & Eaton Counties, MI Josh Burns Endorses Dan Ewart for Michigan State Representative, District 73 Josh Burns , Republican candidate for Michigan State Senate District 21, today announced his endorsement of Dan Ewart for State Representative in Michigan’s 73rd House District.
“I’ve door-knocked with Dan.
I’ve campaigned alongside him.
We’ve spoken at the same events.
We’ve rubbed shoulders a lot on this trail — and I don’t have a bad thing to say about him,” Burns said.
“Dan shows up.
He listens.
He fights for families, for kids stuck in a failing school system, and for the character of the communities we love.” Burns praised Ewart’s commitment to pro-life and pro-family values, his willingness to take a stand when local leadership fails the people it is supposed to serve, and his tireless ground game across District 73.
“In Lansing, District 73 needs a voice that works hard, tells the truth, and puts constituents first,” Burns said.
“That’s Dan.
If you live in the 73rd, I’m asking you: vote Dan Ewart for State Representative.” Ewart, a lifelong Mid-Michigan resident, professional opera singer, educator, and stay-at-home father, is running in the Republican primary for the 73rd House District.
His campaign has emphasized family, education reform, defending life, and restoring accountable representation in Lansing.
Burns is the Republican candidate for Michigan State Senate District 21, covering communities in Eaton and Ingham Counties.
His campaign is built on the pillars of Life.
Land.
Liberty. — protecting Michigan families from overreach, waste, and the loss of their fundamental rights.
