Contact your Town Clerk to confirm the polling location in your town. clerks by town To Vote Using An Absentee Ballot: Any Connecticut voter who wishes to vote using an absentee ballot is permitted to do so.
Obtaining an absentee ballot is a multi-step process, however, so you need to plan ahead.
This process is designed to be sure your vote is secure.
Your Town Clerk can explain the details.
APPLY FOR ABSENTEE BALLOT Go to your Town Clerk’s office.
The Clerk has applications and instructions to help you fill it out.
You can also download an absentee ballot application here .
If you apply to your Town Clerk in person, you can receive your absentee ballot and fill it out on the spot.
No need to use the mail if you don’t want to.
Return the application to your Town Clerk.
You can do that by: Giving the application back to the Town Clerk after you have filled it out.
Putting it in the secure Official Ballot Drop Box at your Town Hall.
Mailing it back.
Be sure to return the application to YOUR Town Hall.
You cannot send your application to a Town Hall different from the town you vote in.
RECEIVE YOUR BALLOT, VOTE, AND RETURN YOUR BALLOT Follow the instructions included with your absentee ballot to vote for the candidates of your choice.
After voting, put the ballot in the optional “privacy sleeve” and insert into the return envelope.
BE SURE TO SIGN THE BACK OF THE OUTSIDE OF THE RETURN ENVELOPE.
Your ballot won’t be counted if you don’t sign the return envelope.
If you’re at Town Hall, you can give your completed ballot, sealed inside the signed return envelope, back to your Town Clerk.
It will be placed in a locked box and counted on Election Day.
You could also place the ballot, sealed inside the signed return envelope, into the secure Official Ballot Drop Box outside of your polling place.
Do not put your ballot in the Drop Box of another town.
If you decide to mail it, please allow extra time for the Post Office to deliver it.
All ballots must be received by the close of the polls at 8 p.m. on Election Day, Nov.
3.
