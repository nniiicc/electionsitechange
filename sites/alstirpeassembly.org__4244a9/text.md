Public Financing Match
Any contributions from an individual who lives in my District (towns of Clay, Cicero, Manlius & Cazenovia), between $5-$250 for this election cycle, will be matched between 8-1 & 12-1.
Married couples can contribute separately and will be eligible for the match.
That means a $50 contribution will provide a $600 match
• $100 brings a $1,050 match
• $250 provides a $2,300 match.
If you’ve given since the last election, you must take that into consideration.
Remember, the contribution limit is $250.
