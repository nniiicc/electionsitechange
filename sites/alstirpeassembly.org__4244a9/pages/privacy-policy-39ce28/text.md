Personally identifying information is collected when submitted through one of the following web forms: Donation, Email Sign-up, and Contact Us.

We also work closely with Democratic candidates, parties and allied organizations and may share some basic contact information with them, and they may contact you.
Text messaging opt-in is excluded from information sharing with third parties.
Personal and financial information is required when making a contribution to Al Stirpe.org This information is used to process your contribution or merchandise request.
All contributions made through Al Stirpe.org are regulated by New York State Election Law.
Campaign finance law requires us to collect contributor information, including employment.
SMS OPT-IN Consent and Data will not be shared with third parties Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
