GEORGIA HOUSE DISTRICT 90 REPRESENTATIVE
MEET SAM
As a Millennial, devoted wife, and proud bonus mom, Samantha Boston understands the real-life challenges families face while building their futures in today’s rapidly changing world.
A graduate of Tuskegee University and a dedicated community advocate, she brings both heart and experience to the issues that matter most.
Now running for Georgia House District 90, Samantha is committed to delivering thoughtful, results-driven leadership focused on education freedom, economic affordability for families and small businesses, safer neighborhoods, and compassionate, practical solutions to homelessness.
With more than 20 years of community advocacy, Samantha Boston is a proven problem-solver who listens, leads, and takes action.
She is driven by a clear vision—to make East Atlanta the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
Samantha Boston isn’t just talking about change—she’s ready to lead it.
