Rick Crosson for Congress is making constant efforts to improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use for those of us with disabilities.
We aim to make all pages and content on rickcrosson.com accessible, but some content may not yet fully meet the highest accessibility standards.

We may revise this Statement periodically to reflect improvements or changes to our accessibility practices.
