Priorities
Accountability
No one is above the law, including the president, the wealthy, and the powerful.
Congress must do the oversight it has walked away from.
Affordability
Lower costs for groceries, gas, housing, child care, health care.
A fair and living wage no one should fall below.
Data Center Regulation & Accountability
No data centers without strict state and federal regulation to protect the environment, quality of life, and avoid harmful consequences; prepayment to ensure taxpayers don’t bear the costs, and local communities ultimately determining the outcome.
State support to local communities to negotiate any interest in building.
Health Care
Affordable, Accessible, Guaranteed Healthcare for Everyone.
Transparency in pricing.
Lower prescription costs, Freedom to choose your doctor, Life-saving procedures without the red tape, critical care available nearby.
Immigration
Pass critical immigration reform with a focus on laws that reward hard work and contribution.
The “right way” should mean something.
Preserving Family Farms
Keeping our farms profitable and in the family, and standing up for them against tariffs, and corporate squeezing.
Public Education
From Pre-K through trade school or a degree, public education should be free.
Quality and access to education should not depend on what a family can afford.
Reproductive Freedom
A woman’s right to choose her reproductive healthcare, and to access it, belongs with her and her doctors, not the government.
Support for Military Veterans
A living wage, affordable housing, protecting earned benefits, fixing VA wait times and standing up for veterans’ mental health.
Recognition of every veteran who served including Hmong and Lao Americans still waiting for what they earned.
Supreme Court Reform
Expand the court.
Pass term limits and a code of ethics that includes an enforcement mechanism.
We deserve a Supreme Court that serves the people and the Constitution, not the party that appointed it.
