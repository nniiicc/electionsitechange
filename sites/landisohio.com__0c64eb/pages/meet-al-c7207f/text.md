He is a native of Tuscarawas County having been born and raised in Dover.
His career has included 33 years with Greer Steel Company serving as an Inside Sales Rep, a District Representative and in Sales and Marketing Management.
He retired in 2009 to run for the Ohio House of Representatives in 2010.
Having been successful with his run, Al went on to serve 4 terms, 8 years in the Ohio House.
During his time representing the 96th District and later the 98th district he served on a number of key committees including Agriculture and Natural Resources, Commerce and Labor, and Economic Development.
Al served as Vice Chairman of Armed Forces and Veteran’s Affairs for 4 years and as Chair of Energy and Natural Resources for 4 years.
He currently serves as the Chair of the Senate Addiction and Community Revitalization Committee and as Vice Chair of the Energy Committee.
He had the honor of serving on the State of Ohio’s Controlling Board for his last 13 months in the House and served as Co-Chair of Energy and Environmental Policy for the National organization, Counsel of State Government for 2 years.
Following his 8 years in the Ohio House, Al served as a Tuscarawas County Commissioner for 4 years.
Al is a life member of the NRA, a member of the Ohio Farm Bureau, the Tuscarawas County Chamber of Commerce, and serves on Dover Chemical’s CAP team.
Al and Debby, his wife have 2 adult children and 5 grandchildren.
