LaVanna believes safer neighborhoods begin with strong support for law enforcement, firefighters, and first responders who put their lives on the line to protect our communities.
She knows that while progress has been made in parts of our region, too many families still worry about violent crime, property crime, and the loss of safety in the places they live, shop, work, and socialize.
LaVanna will ensure public safety professionals have the resources, training, and backing they need to prevent crime, respond quickly, and keep neighborhoods secure.
By standing with those who serve and focusing on practical solutions, LaVanna will work to restore confidence, strengthen communities, and make St.
Louis County a place where families can feel safe again.
