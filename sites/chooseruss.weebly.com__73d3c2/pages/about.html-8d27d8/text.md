Utah House District #66 CHOOSE RUSS HATCH Home About Platform Constitution Party About Me My name is Russell (Russ) Hatch and my wife and I are residents of Manti, where we have lived for the past five years.
We have two children with a number of grandchildren.
We are both retired, but are involved in various community and church activities.
I graduated from the University of Utah with a B.S. in Political Science and spent five years active duty in the United States Army.
I have been involved in political functions at the local, county, state, and national levels.
I am committed to the platform of the Constitution Party and find that these principles are most representative of traditional American values.
The measure of effectiveness of legislation is in not how much is passed but it’s result and if it contributes to the pursuit of liberty according to Constitutional principles.
I will use the Constitution and moral values and limits on government as my guide.
I am committed to the people of the communities of House District 66.
I will be focused on my District and its relationship to the state of Utah and ensure that the citizens in our District are fully represented in the Legislature.
