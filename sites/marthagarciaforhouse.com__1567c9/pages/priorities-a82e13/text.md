PRIORITIES
I am focused on delivering practical solutions that support rural and tribal communities across District 6.
Lowering costs for families
- Lowering healthcare costs and expanding care in rural and tribal communities
- Building more affordable housing so families can stay in their communities
- Expanding child care, education, and workforce training—from cradle to career
- Investing in infrastructure—water, acequias, roads, and broadband in rural and tribal areas
- Keeping food, housing, and energy costs affordable for working families
Infrastructure & Water
- Investing in roads, water systems, and utilities to ensure safe, reliable services and long-term sustainability.
Public Safety
- Supporting law enforcement and emergency services with the resources needed to keep our communities safe.
Education
- Strengthening schools, supporting teachers, and expanding opportunities for students and workforce training.
Healthcare Access
- Protecting and expanding access to healthcare, especially in rural and underserved areas.
Economic Opportunity
- Creating good-paying jobs by supporting small businesses, workforce development, and responsible economic growth.
