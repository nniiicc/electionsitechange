Dear Neighbor,
I’m Martha Garcia, and I am running to continue representing New Mexico House District 6 in the State Legislature.
I was appointed by Governor Michelle Lujan Grisham following the passing of Representative Eliseo Lee Alcon in 2024—a leader I deeply respect and whose commitment to our communities I strive to carry forward.
It has been an honor to serve.
District 6 is not only my home—it is home to rural communities, tribal nations, working families, ranchers, small business owners, veterans, educators, and public servants.
I work every day to carry your voices into committee rooms and onto the House floor.
This past legislative session, I focused on the priorities I hear most from our community: healthcare, education, public safety, infrastructure, water, and good-paying jobs.
I remain committed to delivering practical solutions that make life better and more affordable for families across District 6.
I respectfully ask for your support on June 2, 2026.
—Martha Garcia, State Representative
