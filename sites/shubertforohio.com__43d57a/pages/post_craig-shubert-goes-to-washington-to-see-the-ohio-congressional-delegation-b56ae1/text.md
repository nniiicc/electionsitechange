Mayor Craig Shubert goes to Washington to see the Ohio Congressional Delegation
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Former Hudson Mayor Craig Shubert was on Capitol Hill Tuesday to see members of the Ohio congressional delegation and their staffs.
Shubert, the GOP’s nominee for the Ohio House’s 34th District, visited the offices of U.S.
Senators Jon Husted and Bernie Moreno, as well as Representatives Max Miller (R-OH 7) and Michael Rulli (R-OH 6), among others..
Craig Shubert’s first political work was on Capitol Hill 50 years ago.
He later went on to become a wire service correspondent and television news reporter, covering numerous candidates, campaigns, policies, and legislation, from city hall to the halls of Congress, and from the courthouse to the White House, prior to a successful business career.
