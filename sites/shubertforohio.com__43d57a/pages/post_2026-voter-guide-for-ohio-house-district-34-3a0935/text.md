Craig Shubert — Candidate for Ohio House District 34 I am Craig Shubert, a candidate for State Representative in Ohio House District 34 .
As the former Mayor of Hudson and a longtime business leader, I am running to represent you, your family, and your community because Ohio needs bold, principled leadership focused on delivering results.
That means fighting for the resources our communities need for roads, bridges, schools, public safety, and other critical services while ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
My priorities include growing Ohio's economy and creating good-paying jobs, making everyday life more affordable for Ohio families, providing meaningful income and property tax relief, improving access to affordable, quality healthcare, strengthening education, investing in critical infrastructure, supporting safe communities, and bringing greater transparency and accountability to state government.
Throughout this campaign, I have had the opportunity to meet with voters across the 34th House District, which includes North Akron, Hudson, Munroe Falls, Silver Lake, Stow, and Tallmadge.
I am grateful to everyone who has taken the time to share their concerns, priorities, and ideas for Ohio's future.
