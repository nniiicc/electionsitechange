Here is the 2026 Republican Slate Card : Craig Shubert — Candidate for Ohio House District 34 HUDSON – The former Mayor of Hudson and a longtime business leader, Craig Shubert, is running to represent you, your family, and your community because Ohio needs bold, principled leadership focused on delivering results.
That means fighting for the resources our communities need for roads, bridges, schools, public safety, and other critical services while ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
His priorities include growing Ohio's economy and creating good-paying jobs, making everyday life more affordable for Ohio families, providing meaningful income and property tax relief, improving access to affordable, quality healthcare, strengthening education, investing in critical infrastructure, supporting safe communities, and bringing greater transparency and accountability to state government.
"Throughout this campaign, I have had the opportunity to meet with voters across the 34th House District, which includes North Akron, Hudson, Munroe Falls, Silver Lake, Stow, and Tallmadge.
