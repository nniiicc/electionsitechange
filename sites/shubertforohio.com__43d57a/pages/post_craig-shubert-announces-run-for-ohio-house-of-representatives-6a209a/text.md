The first official campaign event was held April 9 at Lake Forest Country Club in Hudson, where community leaders and residents gathered to show their support for the former mayor, and to hear directly from Shubert about his vision for the district.
Craig Shubert addresses community members at Lake Forest Country Club in Hudson A Strong Start in Hudson The fundraiser brought together members of the community, reflecting early momentum for the campaign.
Attendees had the opportunity to meet Craig Shubert, to connect with others in their district, and learn more about the priorities shaping his run for office, including how he will address out-of-control property taxes.
In his remarks, Shubert emphasized the importance of bold, principled leadership and the need to focus on job growth, bigger paychecks, lower taxes, world-class education, and infrastructure improvements.
Building on a Record of Leadership Craig Shubert enters the race with a successful track record of addressing local challenges.
His time in office was focused on infrastructure improvements, public safety, and responsible fiscal management.
That experience now serves as the foundation for his campaign for the Ohio House, representing residents of Akron, Hudson, Munroe Falls, Silver Lake, Stow, and Tallmadge, where he is positioning himself as a candidate focused on practical solutions and long-term results.
Looking Ahead The April 9th event marks the beginning of a broader campaign effort across Ohio’s 34th District.
In the coming months, Shubert will meet with thousands of voters to hear their perspective on important issues, attend a wide-range of community events, and will share why he is the best qualified candidate to serve voters in the general assembly.
Supporters can expect additional opportunities to get involved as the campaign builds momentum.
