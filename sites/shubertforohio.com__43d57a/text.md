Craig Shubert for State Representative From an Eagle Scout in Iowa to the mayor’s office in Hudson, Ohio, Craig Shubert has dedicated his life to service, leadership, and defending the values that make communities strong.
He believes Ohio’s future demands a strong economy, good paying jobs, lower taxes, world-class education, reliable infrastructure, and leaders willing to stand for what is right.
LEARN MORE Job Growth and Bigger Paychecks To drive job growth and bigger paychecks in Ohio, we must lower operating costs, cut burdensome red tape, and make it easier than ever for businesses to succeed.
Data Centers Data centers can deliver billions in private investment, boost local tax revenue, create high-quality jobs, and support Ohio's technology economy, but not at the expense of taxpayers and utility customers.
Full transparency of energy and water use, utility rate impacts, and emerging technologies is essential.
A vision for Ohio built on transparency, accountability, and common sense governance to ensure the strength of our local communities and families.
Lower Property Taxes Ohioans face some of the highest property taxes in the nation, and while recent action by the General Assembly is a step forward, it’s not enough.
My plan delivers real relief for seniors while protecting the strength of our state budget.
Key Issues Affordability Ohio families deserve a secure financial future.
Government cannot control every price, but it should never make life more expensive.
Decreasing income taxes, reducing mandated fees, and incenting development of affordable housing and lower cost energy will make a difference.
Education A strong economy starts with strong schools, grounded in reading, writing, and math excellence.
We must refocus Ohio schools on core academic achievement, empower parents with a meaningful voice, and ensure they have the freedom to choose the best educational path for their children.
Stopping Waste, Fraud, and Abuse Taxpayers deserve confidence that every public dollar is spent wisely.
We must strengthen efforts to detect, investigate, and prosecute Medicaid fraud by providing law enforcement with the tools, resources, and authority needed to recover stolen funds and hold bad actors accountable.
Infrastructure Continued investment in roads, bridges, utilities, energy, and modern technology is essential to keeping Ohio competitive.
I will secure these investments for 34th House District to strengthen public safety and expand job opportunities.
