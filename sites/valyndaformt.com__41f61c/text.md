Leadership That Listens I’m Valynda Holland, the Democratic nominee for Montana Senate District 11.
I am a proud Montanan, a graduate with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, a community advocate, and a member of the Advisory Board for the Mansfield Center for the Performing Arts.
I entered this race because too many working Montanans feel ignored by the people making decisions on their behalf.
Our families are facing rising housing costs, unaffordable healthcare, increasing utility bills, threats to reproductive freedom, and a justice system that too often prioritizes punishment and profit over accountability and rehabilitation.
In the Democratic primary, the people of Senate District 11 chose a campaign grounded in lived experience, community engagement, and leadership that listens.
Now, we are carrying that momentum into the general election on November 3, 2026.
Together, we can build a Montana that works for everyday people—not corporations, political insiders, or the wealthy few.
