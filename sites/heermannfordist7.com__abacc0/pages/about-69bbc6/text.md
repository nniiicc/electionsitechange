Community Service Serving the Community I am Mellissa Heermann, a District 7 Representative in the South Dakota House.
From an early age, I learned the importance of community service and the responsibility we have to serve those around us and contribute to our communities in meaningful ways.
Public Service Experience I developed a love and passion for policy work during college while working for a Senator at the Capitol in Lincoln.
I was able to see firsthand how impactful this role could be when done right.
This experience in policy work set me up to be successful in my role on the Brookings School Board, where I also serve as a District 7 Representative in South Dakota.
I have had the wonderful opportunity to serve on the Lake Preston City Council and briefly as Mayor of Lake Preston.
This was such an amazing experience for me, and the people of Lake Preston were incredibly warm and welcoming to me during our time there.
In July of 2022, I completed my 6th year on the Brookings School Board.
There are many things I am proud of from that time; however, implementing a new strategy for updating and maintaining our School Board policies is at the top of the list.
Ensuring that the policies of the school district comply with state regulations and staff and student needs is critical.
This new process will set the district up for continued success for many years to come.
During my two terms on the Brookings School Board, there have been a number of challenges and obstacles to overcome, which required working collaboratively as a board and within the community.
Whether it be district construction projects, safety issues, budgets, strategic planning, or the creation of the Brookings School District Foundation, I have focused on listening to community feedback with respect for others and incorporating it into strategic decision making.
Proven Leadership Six years on the Brookings School Board, four of those years in a leadership role which was voted on by the full board.
Work on multiple committees including construction and facilities, performance overview and policy.
Experience in public service on the Lake Preston City Council and served as Mayor of Lake Preston.
Real Experience I have successfully completed four years representing District 7 in Pierre.
I have built strong relationships, learned the logistics of creating successful legislation, and made a difference for the people of South Dakota through thoughtful leadership and strong decision making.
Areas of Focus I believe in the American people and the dream this country was built on.
I enter into conflict with a level head, extending respect for others and their right to be heard.
As a leader during a time of conflict, I have worked through many different points of contention and have learned from those experiences along the way.
I want to use all that I have learned in the last four years in the House of Representatives to continue to advocate well for District 7 and South Dakota.
If given this opportunity I will work hard for District 7 and continue the history of strong leadership in Pierre.
