THE PROVEN CHOICE FOR CONGRESS Ryan Mackenzie has worked every day to deliver results for everyone in our community since joining Congress in 2025.
That means saying no to politics, putting more money in our pockets, keeping our streets safe, and bringing resources home to support our neighborhoods.
As a former State Representative, current U.S.
Congressman, and lifelong resident of our community, it’s what Ryan has always done – and what he will always do.
While politicians often talk a big game about what they’ll do if elected but let us down when they get there, Ryan Mackenzie is different.
He has proven that he will fight tooth-and-nail – and work together with both sides – to follow through on his promises and deliver results.
Whether it’s standing up for seniors on Medicare, reaching across the aisle to lower costs for working families, or leading the charge to crack down on congressional stock trading, Ryan has earned our trust.
