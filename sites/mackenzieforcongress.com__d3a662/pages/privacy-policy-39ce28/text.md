Is my information secure?
We make every effort to protect and secure all user data and information.
Any portion of our site that collects user credit card information is served over a secure socket layer (SSL), and encrypted by a signed and valid certificate.
No credit card information is stored in our database.
Cookie information is used to customize your interaction with our site, and to provide you with the best possible experience.
A cookie is a tiny text file that is placed on your hard drive, and does not contain any personal information about you.
These text files cannot be used by us, or anyone else, to access information that you have not voluntarily provided to us.
As you browse our website, advertising cookies may be placed on your computer, so that we can understand your interests, and your interactions with us.
Our advertising partner then enables us to present retargeted advertising on other sites, based on your pervious interactions with our site.
These techniques do not collect personal information about you.
