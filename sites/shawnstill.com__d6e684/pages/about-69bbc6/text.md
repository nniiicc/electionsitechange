Meet Senator Shawn Still
Shawn Still built something real before he ever ran for office.
As owner and president of Olympic Pool Plastering & Shotcrete — the largest swimming pool subcontractor in the Southeast — he’s met a payroll, created more than 100 Georgia jobs, and learned firsthand that results come from showing up and doing the work.
He brought that same standard to the State Senate.
Shawn is a graduate of the University of Alabama, where he competed as a multi-sport athlete.
He has lived in the district before Johns Creek was a city, and now calls South Forsyth home.
He’s raising three daughters here, and it’s their future, and yours, that drives his work under the Gold Dome.
“I treat being your senator as a full-time job, and I think the results show it.
There’s more work to do and I’m just getting started.”
