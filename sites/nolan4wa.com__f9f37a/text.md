Alan Nolan is your leader for the 6th Legislative District.
Eastern Washington needs a leader who will stand firm, push back against government overreach, and fight for policies that restore opportunity and accountability.
With proven leadership as a military officer, small business owner, and school board member, Alan understands how to deliver results — not rhetoric.
As your State Representative, Alan will fight for you.

What’s missing is leadership willing to stand up and fight for them.
