DONATE All Articles Search Misprinted Ballots Alan Nolan Jul 22 3 min read Monday moring, August 21st, I identifed errors in the ballots mailed out by Spokane County.
Below is a short summary of the issue, stories on it, as well as maps to identify if you have been affected my the mistake.
This is a breaking story and I will post updates on my Facebook account first.
Spokane County Elections department head Ryan Doss confirmed over the phone yesterday an error in printing some ballots.
The error, coincidentally enough they left STA Prop 1 off for some areas and added it on other ballots.
Monday morning I identified the issue and notified Spokane County Elections office.
An office supervisor initially told me the STA Board and set the boundaries and ballots were correct.
I showed here the map below from the Spokane County Ele ctions Office, which is a source document, to explain what the error was.
She then told me her supervisor would contact me with more information.
It wasn't until yesterday afternoon and several more calls to the elections off that Mr.
Doss called and confirmed the error.
Specifically, he acknowledged the west half of precinct 6038, which is in Colbert off the Hwy 395 Hatch Road exit, north of Midway Rd and east of Hatch Road (see close up map) as well as a handful of voters in precinct 9005 in south Spokane County who incorrectly received the ballot measure.
Ryan claims they have reviewed all the other precincts and there are no other errors.
My suggestion?
Trust but verify.
Details on how to do it below.
Ryan Doss stated ballots are being reprinted and mailed to affected voters.
He also said a press release will be published.
I asked for him to send it to me, but as of yet, I have not received anything nor is there information on the Spokane County Elections website.
I did just find a Spokesman-Review story that I've listed below.
