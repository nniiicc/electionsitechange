Servant Leadership
State Representative Scott Conklin continues to deliver for the people of the 77th district here at home and in Harrisburg.
His servant leadership is centered around the needs of working families, safeguarding women’s rights, increasing voter accessibility, and equitable treatment of all Pennsylvanians under the law.
Scott’s office never fails to meet the needs of our community.
Scott Conklin has been proudly serving the 77th District in Harrisburg since 2007.
He hails from the Philipsburg area, where he has raised a family with his wife, Terri, and their son, Spencer.
Before his election to the State Legislature, Scott served for seven years as a Centre County Commissioner, where his financial stewardship of Centre County led to the county earning the highest financial rating in its history.
In Harrisburg, Representative Conklin currently serves as the Majority Chairman of the House Commerce Committee and the Treasurer of the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee.
Scott stands ready to fight for you, as he always has.
