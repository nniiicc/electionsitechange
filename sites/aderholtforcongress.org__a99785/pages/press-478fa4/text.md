HALEYVILLE, AL — Former President Donald J.
Trump has endorsed Congressman Robert Aderholt (AL-04) for reelection.
President Trump released this statement regarding his endorsement of Congressman Aderholt:
“Congressman Robert Aderholt is doing a fantastic job representing the people of Alabama’s 4th Congressional District.
“Robert is an America First Conservative who is working hard to Secure our Borders, Strengthen our Military, Stop the Weaponization of Justice, Expand Rural Broadband, and Bring Manufacturing Back to the Great State of Alabama.
“Robert Aderholt has my Complete and Total Endorsement!” – President Donald J.
Trump
Congressman Aderholt issued this statement in response to Trump’s endorsement:
“I am honored to have the endorsement of President Donald J.
Trump.
During his Presidency, Donald Trump and I worked together to put the American people, and their interests, first in everything we did.
He saw firsthand that I am always fighting for the people of the 4thDistrict and the people of Alabama.
Trump’s words send a clear message that he knows I will continue to fight for the things we both believe in and the things the people of the 4th District believe in.”
Aderholt had earlier this year endorsed President Trump for re-election.

Haleyville, Ala. — Today, Congressman Robert Aderholt, (Republican AL-04) released the following statement to announce the hiring of Alex Vanderford as his new campaign manager to organize his 2020 re-election campaign.
“I am excited to announce that Alex Vanderford will be joining our team.
Alex has a wealth of campaign experience and he shares my vision of reigniting the economy after this pandemic, bringing jobs back from China to America, expanding broadband to all rural areas, and protecting the unborn.
I know Alex will work tirelessly to show the voters how much progress we’ve made, but also how we can make even more progress in the future.
This is a pivotal election.
Speaker Pelosi wants to consolidate her control of Congress and defeat President Trump.
I will be working closely with the President to make sure that doesn’t happen.
I take pride in the fact that President Trump received a higher percentage of the vote in the 4th Congressional District than any congressional district in the nation.
During the past four years, I have formed a personal relationship with President Trump, and we have fought side by side for rural America.
“
Alex Vanderford most recently worked as the North Alabama field director for the Bradley Byrne Senate Campaign.
He also worked as the Grassroots Coordinator/Coalitions Director for Marsha Blackburn’s senate campaign in Tennessee.
Alex is a native of Muscle Shoals in Colbert County in the 4th District and is a graduate of Martin Methodist College in Pulaski, Tennessee.
