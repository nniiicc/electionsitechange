Read with Me is an initiative to showcase civically-minded children’s books and foster a lifelong love of learning.
Inspired by contemporary discussions surrounding explicit materials, this series provides an age-appropriate alternative for families.
Book Selection Criteria
- Age-Appropriate for grade school readers
- Teaches civics, government, and/or history in a non-partisan way
- Preference given to books with a New Hampshire connection
- Amazing Americans Series: Showcases the lives of Americans who have positively impacted history.
