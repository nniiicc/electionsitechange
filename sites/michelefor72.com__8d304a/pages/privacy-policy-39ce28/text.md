Privacy Policy
Effective Date: February 24, 2026
Michele Henry for Indiana State House (“Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide through michelefor72.com.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, volunteer, or make a contribution:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- ZIP code
- Employer and occupation (for campaign finance compliance, if donating)
- Any information you voluntarily submit through forms
We may also automatically collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited for analytics purposes.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities
- Process donations through our secure fundraising platform (including ActBlue or other payment processors)
- Comply with state and federal campaign finance laws
- Respond to inquiries
- Improve website performance and outreach efforts
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
Donations
Online donations are processed through secure third-party platforms such as ActBlue.
When you donate, you may provide payment information directly to that processor.
We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
Donation information may be publicly disclosed as required by Indiana campaign finance law.
Text Messaging (SMS) & 10DLC Compliance
If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, you agree to receive campaign and donation-related SMS communications from Michele Henry for Indiana State House.
- Message frequency may vary
- Message and data rates may apply
- Reply STOP to opt out
- Reply HELP for assistance
- Consent is not a condition of purchase
We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties for marketing purposes.
Email Communications
You may opt out of campaign emails at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link included in campaign emails.
Cookies & Analytics
Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools to improve performance and understand visitor engagement.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings.
Data Security
We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal information.
However, no online transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.
Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including donation processors.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not intended for children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
