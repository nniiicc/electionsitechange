ABOUT MICHELE
Michele Henry is a local civil rights lawyer who raised her family in Georgetown, Indiana - in the heart of District 72.
Michele has practiced law since 2002, and now, Michele represents victims of discrimination and labor unions seeking fair and equitable working conditions for their members.
Michele is well respected among her peers, having been selected as a Best Lawyer in Kentucky annually since 2015 and as a Super Lawyer annually since 2016 in the plaintiff employment practice area.
While not practicing law, Michele is a committed community servant, serving on the Board of Directors for the Waterfront Botanical Gardens and Women Lawyers Association.
She’s also an active volunteer with Access Justice, providing free, quality legal help to all of our neighbors across Kentuckiana.
Michele lives in Georgetown with her husband, Gordon Henry, and whichever foster or senior care dog most recently needed a loving home.
She’s a triathlete and completed the 140.7 mile Ironman triathlon in 2015.
Michele has two children, Sarah and Sean Henry, who are both proud graduates of Floyd Central High School.
