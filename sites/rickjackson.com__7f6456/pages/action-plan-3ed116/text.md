Make Georgia More Affordable
Georgia families are getting squeezed.
Groceries cost more.
Rent costs more.
College costs more.
Property taxes are out of control.
Politicians talk about affordability, but talk never paid a bill.
Rick Jackson will treat affordability like a crisis, because for too many families it already is.
As Governor, Rick Jackson will:
- Freeze property taxes immediately to stop runaway assessments that price families out of their homes
- Cut the state income tax in half within 4 years
- Use technology and AI to eliminate wasteful spending and improve services
- Freeze tuition, room, and board at public colleges and technical schools
Create More Jobs Than Any Governor In Georgia History
Georgia is the best place in America to do business, and as a business leader who built a company from the ground up, Rick Jackson will keep it that way.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Recruit jobs to every corner of the state
- Support small businesses and entrepreneurs
- Make Georgia the #1 state for entrepreneurship in America
- Make rural Georgia a center for 21st Century jobs
- Stand with our farmers so Georgia Grown products can compete and win
Support Georgia Agriculture
Georgia’s farmers and agribusinesses need affordable capital and relief from rising input costs.
Rick Jackson will make sure they have the tools to grow and compete.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Provide efficient, cost-effective capital options for farmers
- Help farmers negotiate better prices for key materials and equipment
Dignity Through Work — Not Dependency
Rick Jackson believes work brings dignity, purpose, and independence.
Creating dependency is not compassion.
Forcing people to work is not a punishment; it is a means to lift people up out of poverty.
Government assistance should be a bridge, not a lifestyle.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Tie public assistance to work or job training for able-bodied adults
- End endless dependency programs
- Expand job training and vocational pathways
- Reward effort, responsibility, and self-reliance
An Outsider Who Gets Results — Not a Career Politician
Georgia doesn’t need another politician who knows how to give speeches.
It needs a leader who knows how to deliver results.
Rick Jackson built businesses by identifying problems, demanding accountability, and getting outcomes.
That’s how he’ll run Georgia.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Enact term limits
- Audit every government program for results and effectiveness
- Eliminate programs that don’t work
- Raise expectations in schools, agencies, and public spending
- Make clear what success looks like — and enforce it
Pass The Strongest Ethics Laws in America
Public service should never be used to pad your pockets, prop up insiders, or as patronage for the professional political class.
Rick knows that taxpayer money should be protected like it belongs to someone else.
Because it does: It belongs to YOU.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Enact the strongest ethics laws in America
Transform Healthcare
Rick has spent his life in healthcare.
He knows where the costs are, the waste is, how the system helps families, and also how it hurts them.
Too many Georgians are getting less and paying more.
That’s going to change.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Use better technology in healthcare and cut out the middlemen who drive up costs
- Increase access to rural healthcare
- Make Georgia a national model for high-quality, accessible, and affordable healthcare
- Negotiate better pharmacy rates
Opportunity for All Georgians
Being the number one state for business means nothing if it does not apply to all of our businesses, in all of our neighborhoods.
Rick knows what it means to be written off, and he knows what one opportunity can do.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Create a new state Legacy Corridor Initiative, consisting of six of Georgia’s historic commercial districts
- Connect entrepreneurs to capital, training, talent, and ownership
- Create capital programs for start-up, early-stage, and growth companies
Strengthen Education
Rick knows firsthand that education is the doorway to opportunity.
If that doorway is closed to a child, the American Dream gets a lot farther away.
Georgia parents and children may not have a lobbyist or political connections, but they will have a governor in their corner.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Raise teacher pay
- Make Georgia a national leader in childhood literacy
- Provide career pathways for EVERY type of student
Invest In Georgia Children
A $100 check, given anonymously one Christmas morning, changed Rick’s life when he was in a children’s home.
He knows firsthand that a little extra money can change everything for a child’s future.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Push Georgia to match the $1,000 Trump Accounts contribution for every child in Georgia to help support their education
- Create educational support for children in poverty
Protect Children
Rick Jackson is clear and unapologetic.
Children deserve protection — not political experiments.
And he believes it’s about time foster kids went from the back of the line towards the front.
He’s fought to give foster kids a better chance to succeed, all at no extra cost to taxpayers.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Strengthen Georgia’s Parents’ Bill of Rights
- Ban ideological indoctrination in schools
- Ensure boys do not compete in girls’ sports or use girls’ bathrooms or locker rooms
Support Children in Foster Care
Rick Jackson grew up in 5 foster homes and knows what it’s like to have to fight your way to success.
That’s why he’s made supporting foster children a major cause of his life — and will continue to do so as governor.
He believes it’s about time foster kids went from the back of the line toward the front.
He’s fought to give foster kids a better chance to succeed, all at no extra cost to taxpayers.
Rick Jackson:
- Led the charge to pass the Fostering Success Act
- The law allows foster youth to attend public colleges and universities tuition-free and at no additional cost to taxpayers
- Believes kids should not be punished for circumstances beyond their control
As Governor, Rick will:
- Streamline the process to speed up permanency
- Improve access to mental health
Keep Every Georgia Community Safe
Public safety isn’t a left or right issue.
It’s a public trust issue.
Rick will work to make every neighborhood and every street safe.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Support law enforcement
- Make sure families feel secure in their homes, schools, and neighborhoods
- Combat the mental health crisis in the criminal justice system
- Ensure every city upholds the law
Support Law Enforcement
Rick Jackson grew up in places where law enforcement was the difference between safety and chaos, and where law enforcement was treated with respect.
He knows if you listen to and respect the police, you won’t get hurt.
He will always stand with those who protect our communities.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Fully support law enforcement with resources, training, and respect
- Ban revolving-door justice
- Reject soft-on-crime policies
- Launch a PSA promoting respect for police, starting in schools
Support Georgia Veterans
Rick Jackson will stand with the men and women who served our country and with the families who sacrificed alongside them.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Remove barriers to work by waiving professional licensing fees for military families and veterans
- Deliver real healthcare solutions that will bring access to high-quality health care for our veterans
- Establish a Governor’s Office of Military and Veterans Affairs
- Establish a 24/7 Veterans Support Hotline
Fight Criminal Illegal Immigration
Rick Jackson will work to protect Georgians from the consequences of Washington’s failures.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Make Georgia number one in the nation for deporting all CRIMINAL illegal immigrants
- End policies that put Georgia families at risk
- Enforce the law without apology
Stop Woke Ideology and Ban Reverse Discrimination
Schools exist to educate — not indoctrinate.
Rick Jackson will restore common sense and parental authority in Georgia classrooms.
As Governor, Rick will:
- Prohibit DEI programs in state government, public universities, and classrooms
