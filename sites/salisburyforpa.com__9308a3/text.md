First elected in a special election last February 2023, State Rep.
Abigail Salisbury accomplished quite a lot in a little over a year for the 34th District and the Commonwealth overall.
In this short period of time, she:
- Introduced and shepherded the passage of bills to address blighted and abandoned properties and establish a municipal grant assistance program,
- Forwarded an additional bill to ban lobbyist involvement in political campaigns,
- Helped launch and currently co-chairs Pennsylvania’s bipartisan Charitable Nonprofit Caucus to support and promote the commonwealth’s thousands of charitable nonprofit organizations, and
- Aided in the cultivation of almost $10,000,000 in funding, grants, and tax credits to the district for a host of projects including blight remediation, community programs, stormwater management, traffic safety, water & wastewater infrastructure, and more.
I am your representative in the truest sense of the word, advocating for our district in the same way I advocate to get the best I can for my law clients.
Abigail Salisbury
