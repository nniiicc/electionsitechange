Spearheading Initiatives & Leading Colleagues
Nonprofit Support
State Representative Abigail Salisbury is a tireless advocate for nonprofit organizations within Pennsylvania’s State House District 34 and across the entire Commonwealth.
As a not-for-profit attorney, she recognizes the crucial role these organizations play in our community.
Salisbury has spearheaded initiatives to support and empower nonprofits, ensuring they have the tools and resources needed to thrive.
Grant-Writing Scholarship Program
Just this February 2024, Rep.
Salisbury launched a groundbreaking grant-writing scholarship program in collaboration CCAC Homewood-Brushton with community nonprofit partners.
The scholarship program offers 10 scholarships to residents living in low-income census tracts in District 34, providing them access to valuable state grant dollars.
Rep.
Salisbury, co-founder and chair of the Pennsylvania Charitable Nonprofit Caucus, believes we must empower changemakers within our community who may lack formal training in grant-writing.
Pennsylvania Charitable Nonprofit Caucus
Last summer, Rep.
Salisbury launched the Pennsylvania Charitable Nonprofit Caucus.
This bipartisan group was formed to champion the thousands of charitable nonprofit organizations across the Commonwealth.
Recognizing the diverse and vital roles these nonprofits play—from combating food insecurity to supporting local libraries and providing disaster relief—Rep.
Salisbury knows ongoing legislative support is needed.
As the Democratic House Chair of the caucus, Rep.
Salisbury aims to create a legislative environment that fosters the growth and success of charitable nonprofits.
This includes advocating for funding, support, and initiatives that directly benefit these organizations and the communities they serve.
Continuing Support for Community Nonprofits
Rep.
Salisbury’s dedication to supporting community nonprofits extends beyond these initiatives.
Her ongoing commitment to being a voice for those who serve the most vulnerable members of our society underscores her deep-rooted belief in the power of community action.
With a track record of bipartisan collaboration and a clear vision for the future, Rep.
Abigail Salisbury stands as a steadfast advocate for Pennsylvania’s charitable nonprofits.
Her work ensures that these organizations remain pillars of strength within District 34, enriching lives, creating jobs, and fostering vibrant, resilient communities.
