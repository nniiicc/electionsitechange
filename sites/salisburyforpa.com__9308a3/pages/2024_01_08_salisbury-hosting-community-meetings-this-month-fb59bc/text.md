Abigail Salisbury is hosting three community meetings in January to get residents’ feedback about which issues they consider most important to their legislative district.
“I want to hear from residents about their priorities,” said Salisbury, D-Allegheny.
“I want folks to tell me what’s on their minds and what are the No.
1 issues they want to see me tackle.
I’m hoping these meetings will be the perfect forum for productive dialogue, and I’m urging everyone to stop by, speak their mind and tell me how I can best work for our community.” The meetings will take place at the following times and locations.
No registration is necessary.
Churchill Borough January 10 7:30 PM – 9 PM Churchill Borough Building 2300 William Penn Highway Pittsburgh, 15235 Forest Hills Borough January 16 6 PM – 8 PM Forest Hills Municipal Complex 4400 Greensburg Pike Pittsburgh, 15221 Braddock Borough January 18 6 PM – 8 PM Greater Valley Community Services 300 Holland Ave.
