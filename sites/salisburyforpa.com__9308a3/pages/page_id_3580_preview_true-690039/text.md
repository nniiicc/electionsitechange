As your State Representative, Abigail Salisbury champions the cause of economic justice, recognizing that the foundation of a strong community lies in the well-being of every individual.
The time is now to address one of the most critical issues facing our families and our future: raising the minimum wage in Pennsylvania.
The Urgent Need for Change Despite the hard work and dedication of Pennsylvanians, too many in our community struggle to make ends meet.
The current minimum wage does not reflect the reality of living costs, from housing to healthcare, education, and beyond.
This discrepancy not only challenges individual dignity but also hampers our collective potential.
Raising the minimum wage is more than an economic policy; it is a commitment to the principle that anyone who works full time should not live in poverty.
It’s about affording every citizen the opportunity to not just survive but thrive.
The Salisbury Commitment Abigail Salisbury has been at the forefront of advocating for policies that uplift working families and ensure economic fairness.
By pushing for a significant increase in the minimum wage, Salisbury aims to: Boost Economic Growth: Higher wages mean more disposable income, driving demand for local businesses and stimulating our economy.
Enhance Family Well-being: Ensuring parents can provide for their children’s needs without constant financial strain promotes healthier, happier, and more stable family units.
Reduce Income Inequality: A fair wage policy narrows the gap between the highest and lowest earners, fostering a more equitable society.
Looking Forward: A Plan of Action The journey to a higher minimum wage requires dedication, collaboration, and innovative thinking.
Abigail Salisbury is committed to working alongside community leaders, businesses, and policymakers to craft legislation that benefits all Pennsylvanians.
This approach is not about imposing burdens but about creating opportunities—for workers, for businesses, and for our community.
Your Voice Matters As we stand at this pivotal moment, your support is crucial.
Together, we can make a difference in the lives of countless Pennsylvanians.
Join Abigail Salisbury in advocating for a minimum wage that reflects our values and our vision for a prosperous future for everyone in District 34 and beyond.
