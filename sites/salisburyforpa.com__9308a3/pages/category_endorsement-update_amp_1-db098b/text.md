Abigail Salisbury received the official endorsement of the Allegheny County Democratic Committee (ACDC) for the 2024 Pennsylvania Democratic Primary.
by Abigail Salisbury
LPAC, the only organization dedicated to electing LGBTQ women and nonbinary people to local, state, and federal office, announced endorsements of 12 more incredible LGBTQ women and nonbinary candidates today which included Pennsylvania State Rep.
Abigail Salisbury.
