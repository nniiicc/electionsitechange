Pennsylvania State Representative Abigail Salisbury, deeply committed to preserving this beauty, understands that the essence of our community’s health and prosperity lies in our ability to protect and enhance our environmental resources.
This understanding drives her relentless advocacy for sustainable environmental policies, including significant strides in stormwater management—an essential step toward guaranteeing clean air and water for every Pennsylvanian.
Championing Stormwater Management in District 34 Recognizing the critical challenge that stormwater runoff and wastewater poses to our waterways and communities, Rep.
Salisbury has been instrumental in securing vital funding for District 34.
With awards of almost $3 million funding 9 projects to bolster stormwater management and wastewater infrastructure, these initiatives mark significant advancements in our environmental stewardship efforts.
The projects not only aim to reduce flooding and pollution but also signify a broader commitment to enhancing our community’s resilience against climate change.
Our Vision: A Green and Thriving Pennsylvania The securing of these awards reflects our collective vision for a Pennsylvania where clean air and water are not mere aspirations but realities for all its citizens.
It embodies our commitment to: Building Sustainable Communities: Through improving stormwater management and wastewater infrastructure, we are laying the groundwork for more sustainable urban development, ensuring our communities can thrive without compromising our environmental integrity.
Promoting Public Health: Clean water is fundamental to public health.
By mitigating pollution and preventing flooding, we are taking proactive steps to protect our community’s well-being.
Ensuring Environmental Justice: Access to clean air and water is a right for all, not a privilege for some.
Our efforts are rooted in the belief that environmental benefits must be equitably distributed, regardless of socioeconomic status.
Moving Forward: Your Role in Our Environmental Journey This moment marks a pivotal point in our environmental journey—a journey that demands the engagement and commitment of every community member.
Rep.
Salisbury invites you to be an integral part of this mission.
Whether by supporting local sustainability projects, participating in community clean-ups, or advocating for environmental justice, your actions can contribute to a healthier, greener future for Pennsylvania.
Together, We Make a Difference The strides we have made in stormwater management are just the beginning.
Our dedication to protecting Pennsylvania’s natural resources goes beyond individual projects; it’s about fostering a culture of environmental responsibility that will be passed down to future generations.
Join Rep.
Salisbury in this critical campaign for environmental justice.
Together, we can protect our natural legacy and ensure that clean air and water are guaranteed rights for all citizens in the Commonwealth.
Your voice, your vote, and your volunteerism are crucial in building a sustainable future for Pennsylvania.
