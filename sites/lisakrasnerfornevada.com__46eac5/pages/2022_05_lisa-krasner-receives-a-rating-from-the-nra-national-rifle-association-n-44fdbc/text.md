Lisa Krasner Receives “A” Rating from the NRA, National Rifle Association; NRAILA for State Senate District 16.
Monday, May 23 rd, 2022
Lisa Krasner has received an “A” rating from the NRA, National Rifle Association, NRAILA, for NV State Senate District 16.
An “A” rating signifies a Pro-2nd Amendment Legislator.
