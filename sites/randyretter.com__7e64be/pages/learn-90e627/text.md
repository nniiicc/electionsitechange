The Retter Story Randy Retter isn’t just running to represent district 56; he’s part of its history.
With family roots stretching back over a century, Randy grew up working the soil on his family’s farm—an experience that instilled the values of hard work and personal responsibility he still carries today.
A Career of Service Randy has dedicated his life to protecting Hoosiers.
From his early days as a patrol officer to his two terms as Wayne County Sheriff, he has seen firsthand the challenges our communities face.
35+ Years in Law Enforcement Two-Term Wayne County Sheriff Graduate of the Indiana Law Enforcement Academy & Ball State University Rooted in Community Randy and his wife, Jacqueline, raised their daughter right here in Wayne County.
Whether it’s serving on the board of the Boys & Girls Club or attending Community Bible Church in Milton, Randy believes that a representative should be an active part of the community they serve.
