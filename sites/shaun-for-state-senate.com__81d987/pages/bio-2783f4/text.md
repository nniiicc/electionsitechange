Why Shaun is running for State Senate
My decision to run for State Senate is deeply rooted in my personal journey.
I've faced significant challenges, including major health issues and losing my home in a fire.
These experiences have given me a unique perspective, allowing me to see the struggles that many politicians never encounter.
After regaining my health, I felt compelled to step up because I grew tired of watching politicians make our lives harder.
It's time for leadership that understands real-world problems and is committed to finding real solutions.
I am also a retired hockey player. played for 17 years in the minors.
Core beliefs and guiding principles
I believe in honesty, accountability, and showing up for the people I serve.
My decisions are guided by facts, fairness, and what truly strengthens our community.
I don't engage in party games; my focus is always on real problems, real solutions, and treating everyone with respect.
These are the foundations upon which I will build my service to Kalamazoo and Michigan.
A vision for Kalamazoo and Michigan
My primary goal is to bring honesty, accountability, and real problem-solving back to Lansing.
If elected, I aim to strengthen public safety, improve economic opportunity for all, and ensure that Kalamazoo families are heard and respected.
I envision a government that shows up, listens, and delivers tangible results for the people, not for party insiders.
Let's work together to create a brighter future for our state.
