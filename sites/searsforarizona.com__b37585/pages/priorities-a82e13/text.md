Home Priorities Contribute Contact Endorsements Voting Resources More Home Priorities Contribute Contact Endorsements Voting Resources Home Priorities Contribute Contact Endorsements Voting Resources Priorities Healthcare Every Arizonan deserves access to affordable, high-quality healthcare.
Kiana will fight to lower costs, expand access, and ensure families can get the care they need without fear of medical debt.
That means protecting reproductive freedom, lowering prescription drug prices, and ensuring rural and underserved communities aren’t left behind.
Public Safety Every family deserves to feel safe in their home, school, and neighborhood.
Kiana believes in a balanced, community-focused approach to public safety, one that supports first responders while investing in prevention, accountability, and trust.
That includes expanding mental health crisis response, strengthening community policing partnerships, and ensuring departments have the training and tools they need to keep people safe.
Safe communities start with strong schools, stable housing, and accessible services.
Kiana will prioritize solutions that reduce crime at its roots, protect victims, and build safer, healthier neighborhoods for everyone.
Jobs & Skills Arizona’s economy should work for everyone, not just those at the top.
Kiana will champion policies that expand access to high-wage jobs, strengthen workforce training, and support small businesses that keep our communities thriving.
That means partnering with local employers, community colleges, and trades programs to prepare workers for the jobs of the future.
From advanced manufacturing to healthcare, clean energy, and tech, Arizona’s economy is growing but too many families still struggle to get ahead.
Kiana is committed to creating pathways to good-paying careers, supporting working families, and building a more resilient, equitable economy.
