Senator Kiana Maria Sears has served Mesa County for over twenty years, advocating for children in the foster care system, fighting for quality public education for every child on the board of Mesa Public Schools, and shaping critical water policy for the state on the Arizona Corporation Commission.
Kiana Maria is running because while hardworking Arizonan families and small businesses owners struggle to make ends meet, corporations and billionaires are reporting record profits.
Grocery prices, medication prices, gas prices, utility bills, and housing costs keep climbing — and Republicans in the legislature have refused to act.
Kiana Maria is determined to change that.
As a state senator, Kiana Maria funded nutrition programs to help families put food on the table, fought for housing Mesa families could afford, and has been a champion for our children.
As a State Senator, Kiana Maria is committed to making Arizona more affordable, protecting public education, expanding access to quality healthcare, and creating pathways to good-paying jobs.
