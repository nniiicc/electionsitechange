About I’m Mark Fisher—and I’m running for State Delegate because too many people feel like the system isn’t built for them anymore.
Costs are rising.
Services feel inconsistent.
And too many voices go unheard.
I’ve spent my career helping people grow, lead, and take control of their future.
Now I’m bringing that same mindset to public service—because leadership isn’t about talking, it’s about delivering.
This campaign is about real people, real solutions, and real accountability.

I’m committed to opening doors and removing barriers.
