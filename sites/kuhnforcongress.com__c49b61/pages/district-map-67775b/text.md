With the recent changes to the Tennessee Congressional District Map, we felt it important that you be able to quickly check if you’re in the “Kuhn for Congress” district.
Simply type in the address on your voter registration card to identify the voting district you reside in.
If you’re within the District 8 boundaries, you’ll see a “✅”.
If you’re in District 1 through 7 or 9, you’ll see a “❌”.
