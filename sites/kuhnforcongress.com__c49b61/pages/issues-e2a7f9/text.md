Task Forces Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) I believe in enforcing our laws, but enforcement should never come at the expense of constitutional rights, human dignity, or basic accountability.
What we are seeing from ICE raises serious concerns about racial profiling and people being targeted because of how they look, the language they speak, or assumptions about where they come from.
And here in Tennessee’s 8th Congressional District, we have a CoreCivic detention facility operating with far too little transparency or communication about what is happening, how people are being treated, or how these operations are being carried out.
Law enforcement should be based on facts and due process, not fear, appearance, or ethnicity.
When federal agencies and private contractors operate in our communities, we deserve transparency, oversight, and accountability.
National Guard I believe every American deserves to feel safe.
When it comes to deploying the National Guard, that should never be a political decision.
The Guard should be used when there is a clear need to protect lives, restore order during emergencies, or respond to natural disasters, and when state and local resources are overwhelmed.
Any deployment should have a clear mission, respect constitutional rights, and be coordinated with state and local leaders whenever possible.
I don’t believe we have to choose between public safety and civil liberties.
We can support law enforcement, enforce the law, protect peaceful protesters’ constitutional rights, and hold anyone who commits violence or destroys property accountable.
My approach is simple: keep our communities safe, uphold the Constitution, enforce the law fairly, and ensure every action taken by the government is guided by accountability, transparency, and respect for the rights of the American people.

Mandate that developers are fully transparent and covering the cost of infrastructure and grid upgrades.
Require that developers create good-paying jobs for West TN workers.
