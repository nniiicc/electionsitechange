Meet Will
Will Worsham is a husband, father, and seventh-generation Webster Countian raised on a century farm.
He earned his degrees from Drury University and the University of Missouri and went on to build a successful law practice in Rogersville — representing individuals, families, and businesses across the region.
As a business builder and employer, Will knows what it takes to meet a payroll, navigate regulation, and create opportunity for working Missourians.
His legal career spans prosecution, courtroom advocacy, business and estate planning, and service as a judge — a breadth of experience that means he'll be ready to work on Day One in Jefferson City.
