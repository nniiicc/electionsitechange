Here's your guide to updating your registration and how to vote in 2026.
You MUST request a new mail-in ballot every election This is the most important change to Mail-in voting.
Florida Statutes now require all voters to request a new ballot yearly .
To ensure your ballot arrives on schedule, apply for a mail-in/absentee ballot as soon as possible!
Remember, Election Day is Tuesday, March 10, 2026!
Important legislative changes to Vote-by-Mail were made by Senate Bill 90 ​ Pursuant to Florida Statutes §101.62(a), the maximum amount of time your request can be honored is "[f]or all elections through the end of the calendar year of the next regularly." Therefore, you now have to request a vote by mail ballot for every election year.
What you need to apply/reapply To request a Vote-by-Mail ballot you will need either the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number, or the your Florida Driver's License / Florida ID# on file with the Supervisor of Elections office.
If you have received a new driver's license, you will not be able to make a vote-by-mail request over the phone or online.
You will have to complete a new voter registration with your updated information.
