Meet Ramon Issues News Events Volunteer Contribute News / Juneteenth At the African American Heritage Museum 20 Jun Saturday, 5:20 PM · 2026 Juneteenth At the African American Heritage Museum There was incredible exhibits of African America History and presenters that showcased the life of Sandy Couch, the Clayton twelve, Rita Mack, Sara Spencer Washington, with speakers Reverend David Mallory, Kaleem Shabazz and other prominent local leaders giving powerful remarks!
It was a special honor to have met Rev.
David Mallory who was apart of the sit ins in Richmond Virginia and listen as he answered questions I asked about his experience and memories of that very specific moment in history.
My takeaways is that it is important to not whitewash and sanitize our history in America no matter how sinister so that we may learn from these mistakes and prevent repeating them; and that If we want change in the world we need to be willing to fight for it.
Change will not happen on it's own.
Much like this campaign, we need to be the change we want to see in this world.
