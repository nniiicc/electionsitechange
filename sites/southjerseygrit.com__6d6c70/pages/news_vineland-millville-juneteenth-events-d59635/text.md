Meet Ramon Issues News Events Volunteer Contribute News / Vineland & Millville Juneteenth Events 19 Jun Friday, 10:07 PM · 2026 Vineland & Millville Juneteenth Events Today on Juneteenth we celebrate a day overcoming a dark chapter in our country's history.
In 1865, 2 years after the declaration that set the slaves free in America, the final slaves in Texas were actually set free.
Now make no mistake, while the physical and visible shackles are removed we still have victims shackled by the likes of systemic oppression in this country.
From the injustice of over policing black and brown communities to the financial disparities black and brown people face.
In this country we still allow slavery as a punishment in Prisons and it is no coincidence that minorities are disproportionately sentenced to jail time.
We must fight back against Injustices of today.
Whether it is ICE operating like old slave catchers, or police over policing minority communities, we must stand tall.
Because until we are ALL free from the shackles of poverty and injustice....no one is truly free.
