Meet Ramon Issues News Events Volunteer Contribute Meet Ramon I am South Jersey Native, born and raised.
I am just a regular guy.
No political ties or connections and I do not come from big money.
I have lived in South Jersey all my life and have seen our area neglected by elected officials who put their pockets and private interests in front of our people and I am tired of it.
I'm running because I truly believe and understand that the number one role of this job is to represent THE PEOPLE, not myself, and definitely not Big Corporations.
I am running because as a proud Jerseyan, we do not bend or cower to bully's, liars, thugs, and conmen...we challenge them.
We are one of the original 13 states who fought back against the King of England because we were not scared of a fight.
And a fight is what I am willing to bring to Congress.
When I was 8 years old, I made a "vote for me" poster with my core values at the time(check out the photo below).
Funny thing is, those core values are still going strong today and have only expanded.
My values and ethics have been unmoving unlike Van Drew.
I graduated college and suffered a back injury that unfortunately took me out for a few years.
After meeting all the candidates, and being on the tail-end of my healing journey, I decided I could no longer wait to try to make a difference and decided to jump into the race.
Check out my Instagram or Tiktok where I expand more on what I would like to deliver for the people.
Ever since I was 8 years old I knew that my path to help the world become a better place was through Politics and that's exactly what I will do with your help!
Join the movement to bring integrity and South Jersey pride to Congress.
If elected, rest assure I will LISTEN to the People of South Jersey, and South Jersey WILL be heard!!!
