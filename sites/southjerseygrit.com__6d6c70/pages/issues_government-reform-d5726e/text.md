15) Senate Reform
*Would take a constitutional amendment*
- A fraction of the US population should not hinder the population of majority of America
- Example:
- 800,000 residents in north Dakota 2 senators
3,200,000 in Puerto Rico 0 Senators
- 935,094 residents in South Dakota 2 senators
- ~800,000 residents in South Jersey
- 9,500,000 residents in All of New Jersey 2 senator
- Bottom 6 states in population make up 0.014% of the population, yet have 12% voting power over the country.
- States Should have no less than 1 senator and no more than 3 senators.
- Bottom 10 states no less than 1, top states no more than 3, rest 2.
- House and Senate would both have population influence; however, senate has a representative Cap, where house does not.
