Housing Affordability
- Remove Private Corporations from buying up homes
- Housing is 1 of 5 fundamental pillars to human stabilization.
Before any other need can be achieved, food air water shelter and connection must be stable and secure.
- Allowing for the privatization at the scale only wall street can do will remove buying power from average citizens trying to get their own place to live and be productive in society.
- Transitional Housing
- All this talk of single-family housing leaves other populations out of options
- We need housing options that encompasses everybody through various life stages.
- The unhoused
- The 18-24 year old who live’s in an unhealthy home situation
- The College student and eventual graduate who does not need single family housing
- All the way to housing options for the elders of America.
- Citizen Owned Units
- are tax-payer funded apartment complexes that have fixed upcharge on the cost to operate the complex (i.e.10-20% above cost is the rent)
- This 10-20% profit would then be reinvested into building more units,
- or into the communities in which they serve to be able to improve the towns that host them.
- VA Home Loan programs that beat commercially available loan offers.
- Life for a life worth living.
- More availability in Forbearance and deference Programs for hardships.
- Hardships happen, we should do our best to keep people in their homes during that time, especially if they have a history of responsibly paying their housing.
- Make it illegal to take someone's house for delinquent property taxes
- Make it harder for government to use Eminent Domain against homeowners.
- Tax Break for longtime homeowners/Renters living in the same principle residence for 7+years.
- All to often we give tax breaks to businesses that stay for a little and leave when they cant benefit from taking advantage of the community that hosted them.
It's time we help out the people who are in their communities for the long haul for a change.
