HealthCare Agenda
We are only as strong as our Weakest link.
1) Medicare/ Gov. quality HealthCare for all
- I want the healthiest population in the world to be Americans
- Government Employees like congress get the best healthcare in the country, and I believe that should be extended to every American.
2) Get rid of Insurance companies
- Our healthcare costs to results are a joke to the world.
Americans spend the most for the least effective care in the world.
We deserve better.
- Insurance companies making billions in profit is code for stealing billions from Americans that could have otherwise gone into actual healthcare services.
3) Allow Medicare to negotiate
- Medicare should not have to be forced to pay whatever the insurance company demands.
4) Reduce/balance subsidizing of corn for high-fructose corn syrup and Subsidize all fruits and vegetables instead
- A salad should not cost more than a burger
5) All Immediately Life-Saving Drugs should be capped at no more than $5-$10
- Goal is these meds can be provided at no cost to patients who suffer the conditions that require the drugs and that others can buy the drugs as a just in case for their loved ones.
6) Better VA access and overall care
- If you are willing to risk your life for our country's goals and defense, we owe it to veterans to take care of that life.
- Take care of both Active and Inactive military members by not frivolously risking their lives on irrational Wars.
- Better improve mental Healthcare for all personnel
7) Government Grants/Investments for municipalities for outdoor workout infrastructure at parks.
- Let's make America Stronger and healthier.
8) Pro-Choice and Pro-Life.....Let me Explain
- I am First and Foremost Pro-Choice.
Every woman, EVERY person, deserves to be able to have full autonomy over their own body full stop.
- We know that banning Abortions does not lead to a reduction of abortions, it leads; to unsafe abortions; expensive out of state procedures; now leads to arrests and legal punishment; and leads to unwanted pregnancies causing concern for the safety of the child.
- The way I am Pro-Life is because I would like to see more people WANT to have children and if they don't that its 100% their right.
One of the biggest obstacles to having children among those who want to but don't is the lack of financial support.
We have seen in the past that when the economy is good and there are strong safety nets in society, the birth rate goes up and abortions go down.
- I would like to create an ideal scenario where partners who want to start a family can do so and be able to focus on raising their kids instead of struggling to survive.
I want kids to be born into loving families, not abusive ones or resentful ones.
That can happen by addressing the biggest root cause....Poverty.
- I believe if you are an individual who is strictly pro-life that you should be with a partner who is as well and that will be 100% your right to have not have an abortion, but that choice should not be forced upon others.
