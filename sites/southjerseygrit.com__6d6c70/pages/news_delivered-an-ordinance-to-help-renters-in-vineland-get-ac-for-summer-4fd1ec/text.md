Today I stood before Vineland City Council to propose an ordinance that will help renters in Vineland against cheap Landlords by lifting our Living standards to include A/C utilities as a necessary appliance for habitability of a rented unit.
In the City of Vineland we have ordinances on the books that require heating appliances be provided by landlords but NOTHING for A/C units to keep cool in the Summer.
Those same ordinances state that a rental unit without heat is uninhabitable yet the same is not said for cooling.
This is unacceptable to me.
I am familiar with how dangerous Heat-induced illnesses can be.
Heat Strokes are life threatening and so I have been working on holding cheap landlords accountable over the past few weeks.
Because left unchecked this is what can happen after paying your hard earned money for rent:
- No A/C.
Renter installs window unit often involving screws.
Windows get damaged, Security deposit gets dipped into.
- No A/C.
Renter's kids and family can experience: heatstroke, death, Heat cramps, Heat exhaustion, ER level Dehydration, poor sleep hygiene, poor work performance for adults, poor academia performance for kids, increased level of agitation, aggression, and hostilities.
As well as avoidable ER visits due to severe heat-induced illnesses that could have burdensome financial costs.
