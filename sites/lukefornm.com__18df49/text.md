Donate About Luke Luke Jungmann is a New Mexico native, born and raised in Albuquerque, now residing in Rio Rancho where he lives with his wife, Dany Alfaro, their cats, and their dog Arie.
After attending the Albuquerque public schools, Luke received a Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering from New Mexico Tech and a Master's in Robotics Engineering from the University of Pennsylvania.
Luke then worked as a Senior Software Engineer for a photogrammetry product company called Mapware for 7 years.
Afterwards, Luke joined a small startup called Undreamt, where he wore many hats, including Project Manager, Team Lead, and high-level Technical Support.
Recently, he has begun working at Intel as a Site Automation Engineer.
Luke Jungmann will bring his extensive knowledge and experience to the public sector, with the intent to benefit New Mexico’s economy, environment, and people.
As a young Millennial, he understands the challenges facing the State and wants to make New Mexico better for his generation and the generations to follow.
