Fye Flight Attendant • Union Leader • Mother of Two Candidate, Florida House District 109 Satin Fye did not come to politics looking for a title.
She came through the same doors most working people do.
She has spent her life serving others, raising a family, representing workers, and helping people navigate systems that too often seem designed to wear them down.
As a flight attendant, she learned how to lead in moments that mattered.
When people were stressed, frustrated, uncertain, or afraid, she learned to listen, stay steady, and solve problems.
As a union leader, she learned something else.
Working people are often told to wait.
Wait for relief.
Wait for opportunity.
Wait for someone to finally pay attention.
She spent years fighting to make sure they did not have to.
As a mother, she understands what every family understands: that the biggest decisions made in government eventually show up at the kitchen table.
In the cost of housing.
In the cost of healthcare.
In the quality of schools.
In whether a paycheck stretches far enough to make it to the next one.
When Satin began spending time across District 109, she heard the same concerns again and again.
People working harder than ever but falling further behind.
Families forced to choose between necessities.
Young people wondering whether they will ever be able to afford a future in the communities that raised them.
The problem was not that people lacked ideas.
The problem was that too many of the people making decisions were disconnected from the people living with the consequences.
Satin has spent enough time in community meetings, workplaces, advocacy spaces, and government buildings to see both sides of that reality.
She knows what happens when working people are left out of the conversation.
And she knows what becomes possible when they are finally heard.
District 109 does not need another politician talking at people.
It needs someone willing to carry their concerns into every room where decisions are being made.
Someone who understands the challenges families face because she has lived them.
Someone who knows that representation is not about holding a seat.
It is about making sure the people who sent you there never lose theirs.
That is why Satin Fye is running for Florida House District 109.
Because enough decisions have been made about us without us.
Mentoring young leaders.
Showing up before there was a campaign.
Building relationships.
Learning the process.
Staying rooted in the people.
