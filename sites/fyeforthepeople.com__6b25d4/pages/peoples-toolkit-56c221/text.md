A State Representative serves in the Florida House of Representatives and is elected to a two year term.
Write, sponsor, and vote on state laws Advocate for funding and resources for the district Bring district concerns directly to Tallahassee Help connect residents to state agencies and services Work on issues like housing, healthcare, education, wages, public safety, the environment, and government accountability The work should not stop when session ends.
District leadership should continue at home.
Why It Matters How Tallahassee Impacts District 109 Decisions made in Tallahassee can affect everyday life here at home. 🏠 Housing State law shapes tenant protections, rent regulations, and affordable housing funding. 🏥 Healthcare Medicaid eligibility, community health funding, and health insurance rules are set in Tallahassee. 📚 Public Schools School funding formulas, curriculum standards, and teacher pay come from the state budget. 💼 Wages and Workers Minimum wage increases, worker protections, and labor rules are decided at the state level. 🥦 Food Access State agencies run SNAP and food assistance programs that families in District 109 depend on. 🚌 Transportation State transportation budgets fund roads, bridges, transit improvements, and safety projects. 🏪 Small Businesses Licensing, permitting, and small business support programs are managed by state agencies. 🌱 Environmental Justice Water quality, flood management, and environmental enforcement are partly controlled by state law. 📋 Insurance Costs Homeowners and auto insurance rates are regulated at the state level and directly affect District 109 residents. 🏘️ Community Funding State appropriations fund parks, libraries, community centers, and local infrastructure projects.
