Full Official Biography Satin S.
Fye is a flight attendant, union leader, mother of two growing young men, and community advocate who has built her life around service, leadership, and standing up for working people.
Throughout her career, Satin has worked directly with people from every walk of life.
As a flight attendant, she learned the importance of listening, staying calm under pressure, and serving others during both routine and challenging moments.
Her work gave her a unique perspective on the everyday struggles and aspirations of working families across diverse communities.
Her commitment to service naturally evolved into leadership.
As a union leader, Satin represented workers, advocated for fair treatment, and worked to ensure that everyday people had a voice in decisions that affected their lives.
Sitting across the table from decision-makers taught her an important lesson: when working people are not represented, their needs are often overlooked.
Beyond her professional life, Satin is a working mother of two who understands firsthand the realities many families face.
Like countless Floridians, she has experienced the pressures of balancing work, family responsibilities, rising costs, and the desire to create greater opportunities for the next generation.
As she spoke with residents throughout District 109, Satin heard stories that echoed her own experiences.
Parents worried about the affordability of housing.
Workers questioned whether their paychecks could keep up with the cost of living.
Seniors wondered whether they could remain secure in the communities they helped build.
Small business owners spoke about the challenges of staying afloat while serving their neighborhoods.
These conversations reinforced Satin's belief that public service should be rooted in listening, accountability, and action.
She believes government works best when it reflects the experiences of the people it serves and when those most affected by decisions have a meaningful voice in shaping them.
Satin is running for Florida House District 109 because she believes working people deserve leadership that understands what life actually costs.
She is committed to strengthening communities, supporting families, expanding opportunity, and ensuring government works for the people doing the work.
She has spent years serving people, representing workers, and listening to families.
Now she is bringing that experience, perspective, and commitment to public service on behalf of District 109.
