#106 SATIN FYE IS #106 ON YOUR BALLOT • FLORIDA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES • DISTRICT 109 SOUND ON WHY I'M RUNNING.
You already know what is wrong.
You have sat in the meetings.
You have heard the promises.
You have watched the session end and waited to see what changed.
So have I.
I am one of those working people.
I know what it costs to do everything right and still wonder if it will be enough.
I know what it means to follow every instruction you were given and still not be able to see solid ground ahead.
And I know what it means to sit in this community and not know what is being done on your behalf.
To wonder whether the person in that seat is still in the fight when the cameras are gone and the session is over.
This district has had people who fought hard on the floor.
People who delivered real resources to real neighbors.
People who showed up with genuine commitment.
That work matters.
And this district deserves more of it.
Because what District 109 has never had is both at the same time.
The values and the delivery.
The fight and the funding.
The promise and the proof.
That is not a criticism of what was built.
That is the next standard.
And it is the one I am running on.
I am running because this district deserves a representative who refuses to choose between what is said on the floor and what is built on the ground.
Enough decisions made about us without us.
