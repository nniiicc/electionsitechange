Ballard: Working Hard for the Citizens of House District 20 Learn more about Melissa's Work Your Representative In District 20 My name is Melissa Garff Ballard and I am proud to have represented you, your family and our community in House District 20 for the last 7 years.
My Vision for Our State: Our state has one of the best economies in the country, having the best economic outlook for the 18th year in a row.
This success has come in large part because of conservative ideals.
Business-friendly policies, a skilled workforce, Utah’s amazing outdoor opportunities and our quality of life have all contributed to the success we now enjoy.
Utah is also one of the fastest-growing states in our nation.
Our population is expected to double by 2050.
As your state Representative, I am working tirelessly to protect Utah values while also helping to plan responsibly for growth in education, infrastructure, air quality and economic development.
Sign Up For Periodic Newsletters Davis County is my home.
I was born here.
I grew up here.
My husband and I have raised six children here.
I love this community and the opportunities here.
While generations before us have worked incredibly hard so that we can enjoy this area, there is still much to do.
Together, we are making that happen.
