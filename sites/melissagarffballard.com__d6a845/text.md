Your Representative In District 20
My name is Melissa Garff Ballard and I am proud to have represented you, your family and our community in House District 20 for the last 7 years.
My Vision for Our State:
Our state has one of the best economies in the country, having the best economic outlook for the 18th year in a row.
This success has come in large part because of conservative ideals.
Business-friendly policies, a skilled workforce, Utah’s amazing outdoor opportunities and our quality of life have all contributed to the success we now enjoy.
Utah is also one of the fastest-growing states in our nation.
Our population is expected to double by 2050.
As your state Representative, I am working tirelessly to protect Utah values while also helping to plan responsibly for growth in education, infrastructure, air quality and economic development.
