Education
Ensuring that every Utahn has access to quality education is central to my legislative efforts.
Education is the foundation of opportunity for families and the key to our community’s future economic strength.
By attracting, training, and retaining dedicated teachers, we can ensure the success of our children and the growth of Utah’s workforce.
Programs that support early childhood literacy, K-12 innovation, and higher education are essential for building lifelong learners and reducing dropout rates, which will lower incarceration and create more opportunities for all.
As Chair of Higher Education Appropriations and and Chair of Education and Mental Health, I championed initiatives that improved education at every level.
Our investment of $16 billion over the past four years has supported teachers, students, and programs that make a difference, including bills like HB192, which expanded parental leave for teachers.
As part of the Legislative Education Standing Committee, I remain committed to keeping Utah’s education system strong and preparing students to excel in today’s competitive economy.
