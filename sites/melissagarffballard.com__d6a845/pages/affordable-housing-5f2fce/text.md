Affordable Housing
Rising housing costs are making it difficult for many Utah families to stay in their communities.
I’m committed to ensuring the American Dream of homeownership remains possible in Utah by pushing back against federal spending that drives inflation and interest rate hikes.
In the 2024 legislative session, I supported six pieces of legislation (HB13, HB465, HB572, SB268) aimed at aligning state resources and policies to make homeownership more affordable.
I also voted for streamlined processes (HB476) to increase the supply of affordable homes and supported a statewide building code (SB168) to help first-time buyers.
While we’ve made progress, there’s more to do.
I will continue to champion efforts that make homeownership a reality for all Utahns, helping our families and communities thrive.
