2nd Amendment & Rural Values
A belief in limited government and the constitutional right to bear arms has always guided my values.
I love the outdoors and grew up hunting, fishing, and riding on cattle drives with my family.
This shaped a commitment in me to reduce federal overreach in rural areas and ensure Utah landowners have the freedom to manage their property while enjoying the state’s unmatched beauty and world-class recreation.
Some of the legislation I have sponsored and co-sponsored aligning with 2nd Amendment & Rural Values includes:
HCR019 2021 - Concurrent Resolution Recognizing Farmers Feeding Utah [PASSED]
HB0244S02 2022 - Geological Carbon Sequestration Amendments [PASSED]
HB0425S04 2023 - Energy Security Amendments [PASSED]
HB0507S02 2023 - Firearm Possession Revisions [PASSED]
HB0406 2024 - Firearms Financial Transaction Amendments [PASSED]
HB0076S01 - Firearm Preemption Amendments
HJR007 - Joint Resolution on Existing Weapons Restrictions
