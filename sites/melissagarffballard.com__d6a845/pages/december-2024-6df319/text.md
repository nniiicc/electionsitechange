Donating this Holiday Season Taking Martha to Washington It was an honor to be part of Taking Martha to Washington December 10-11, 2024.
Her statue was ready in 2020, but like many around the world, her departure was postponed due to the pandemic.
It was a long 10 year journey made possible by many in District 20— former Representative Becky Edwards, Senator Todd Weiler, and many volunteers and organizers with Better Days 2020.
Martha Hughes Cannon was a Welsh born pioneer in Utah, doctor, mother, suffragette, and the first woman to be a State Senator in all the United States.
Each US state is allowed 2 statues in the US Capitol, and Martha is only one of 14 women with that honor.
She is now in the Emancipation Hall of the US Capitol Visitor’s Center.
It was an amazing 2 days with leaders and elected officials from all over Utah, including many elected women from city, county, school board, and state offices continuing Martha’s work of public service to better our state.
In 2019, I sponsored the Utah Resolution marking February 14th as Women Voter Registration Day in honor of Seraph Young, the first woman in all the United States and Territories to cast the first female vote February 14, 1870, with then Senator Deidre Henderson as the Floor Sponsor.
Thanks to the many Utah women who have followed Martha and Seraph’s path in forging trails and bettering our state…we also appreciate the great men around us who clear more paths and cheer us on.

Wednesday, January 15th 7:00 - 8:30 pm Bountiful City Hall Legislative Auditors Release Report on Higher Ed In November, legislative auditors released a major report on higher education efficiency and workforce alignment.
The report urges institutions to better address Utah’s workforce needs, especially as they face projected declines in student enrollment.
Explore the latest legislative audits , including detailed reviews of OpenEd (formerly My Tech High) and emergency medical transportation in Utah.
Findings from the higher education audit: Institutions do not calculate program-level costs or fully understand market demand when making institutional decisions.
Institution presidents should act with the system in mind to reduce program duplication, improve program outcomes, and coordinate programs.
In some areas, institutions are losing student market share to private non-traditional institutions.
