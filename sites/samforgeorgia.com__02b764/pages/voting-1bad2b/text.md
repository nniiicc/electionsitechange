VOTER REGISTRATION
Register to vote and use your power to determine the direction of Georgia and our country.
You can register online with a valid driver’s license or identification card issued by the Georgia Department of Driver Services.
VOTING ADDRESS
If you move within Georgia, provide your updated address in writing to your County Board of Registrar's Office, or submit a new voter registration application.
VOTING STATUS
To check your voter registration status, update your information, or locate your polling place visit the State of Georgia’s My Voter Page.
It’s important to check your registration status ahead of an election to confirm your eligibility to cast a ballot.
UPCOMING ELECTIONS
View a list of upcoming elections and registration deadlines on the Georgia Secretary of State’s election calendar.
