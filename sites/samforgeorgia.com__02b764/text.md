Meet Sam Park Born and raised in Georgia, Sam grew up with his two sisters and a single mother.
Sam’s mother, who immigrated to the United States during the Korean War, worked multiple jobs to provide for the family.
After defeating a six-year incumbent Republican and flipping a Georgia State House seat in 2016, Sam has worked to push for healthcare access for all Georgians, fight for more funding for our public schools, and create economic opportunities so all of our communities can thrive.
LEARN MORE Priorities BUILDING A HEALTHIER, STRONGER GEORGIA To build a healthier, stronger Georgia, we must expand access to healthcare for all who call our state home.
Protecting and conserving our natural lands and waterways is also a key factor in ensuring strong public health in the Peach State.
PROTECTING THE AMERICAN DREAM To protect the American Dream, Sam knows we must build an economy that supports working people, create economic opportunities that empower Georgia’s communities to thrive, ensure all children in the Peach State have access to high-quality education, and defend Georgians’ freedoms to exercise their Constitutional right to vote and choose leaders who will support their priorities.
ACHIEVING LIBERTY & JUSTICE FOR ALL To live up to America’s promise, we must continue working towards achieving liberty and justice for all.
Reforming our justice system is a vital step toward creating a fair and equal country, where everyone is treated with dignity and respect, regardless of their race, religion, or background.
