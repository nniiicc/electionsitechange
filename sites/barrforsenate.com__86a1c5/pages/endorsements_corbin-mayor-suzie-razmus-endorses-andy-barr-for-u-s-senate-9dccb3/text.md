Corbin, KY—Today, Corbin Mayor Suzie Razmus endorsed Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.
“Corbin needs a partner in Washington who will fight for Kentucky, and Andy Barr always will,” said Mayor Razmus.
“Andy is a champion for Kentucky families, jobs, small businesses, law enforcement, and tourism.
I’m behind him all the way and look forward to working with him when he’s our U.S.
Senator.”
“Corbin is a community on the rise, and Mayor Razmus deserves so much credit for that,” said Barr.
“I’m honored to have her support, and I’ll keep fighting for jobs, safety, and the industries that power our region — from manufacturing to energy businesses.”
Mayor Razmus was elected as Corbin’s Mayor in 2018.
She has been a small business owner in Kentucky and Tennessee for over 30 years.
