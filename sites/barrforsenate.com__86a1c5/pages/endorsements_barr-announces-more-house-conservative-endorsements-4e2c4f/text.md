Craig Goldman (R-TX), Rep.
Lloyd Smucker (R-PA), Rep.
Laurel Lee (R-FL), Rep.
Vince Fong (R-CA), Rep.
Ryan Mackenzie (R-PA), Rep.
Russ Fulcher (R-ID), Rep.
Tim Walberg (R-MI), and Rep.
Juan Ciscomani (R-AZ).
“Andy Barr has been with President Trump every step of the way, delivering the agenda that Kentucky voters supported,” the Members said in a joint statement.
“Andy is a tested conservative, a tireless champion of the conservative movement, and a leader you can trust when it counts.
He will be a great U.S.
Senator for Kentucky and we are proud to endorse him.” Barr has over 160 major endorsements, more than all of his Republican primary opponents combined.
