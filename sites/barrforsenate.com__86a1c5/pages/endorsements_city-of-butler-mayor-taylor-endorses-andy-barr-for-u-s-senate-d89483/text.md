Senate Endorsements — May 7, 2026 Butler, Kentucky — Today, Mason Taylor, the Mayor of Butler endorsed Andy Barr to the next U.S.
Senator from Kentucky.
“I am proud to support Andy Barr in the U.S.
Senate,” said Mayor Taylor.
“Andy is a warrior for the people of Kentucky.
His vision for Kentucky’s future puts our families and communities first.
He will be a great Senator for Kentucky.” “I am honored to have the support of Mayor Taylor,” said Barr.
“Mason is a true patriot, visionary, and strong leader in Kentucky.
I am eager to represent Butler and all Kentucky when I am your next U.S.
Senator.” When elected in 2022, Mason Taylor—known as Butler’s “coin flip” mayor—became the youngest mayor in that election cycle.
He is a community servant that serves as a mayor, firefighter, and a paramedic.
He is focused on economic growth, community safety, and making Butler a thriving city.
Mayor Taylor joins President Donald J.
Trump and a multitude of others in endorsing Barr’s campaign.
Barr has over 170 major endorsements, more than all other campaigns combined.
