Salyersville, Kentucky—Today, Kentucky State Representative John Blanton (R-HD-92) endorsed Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.
“Andy Barr is a proven winner who is the best candidate to keep this Senate seat for our conservative values,” said Rep.
Blanton, Chairman of the Transportation Committee in the Kentucky State House. ”Andy has a record of backing the blue, protecting Christians, and delivering key infrastructure projects for communities he represents.
I’m proud to endorse Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.”
“As a state trooper and now State Representative, John Blanton has always put Kentucky first.
John knows how to win tough elections, flipping a seat in the Kentucky State House and helping us grow our Republican Party in Kentucky.
Together, we will fight for conservative values, for our law enforcement, and for Kentucky infrastructure.
I’m honored to have Chairman Blanton behind my campaign,” said Barr.
Chairman Blanton served in the Kentucky State Police for over two decades, retiring as a Major.
He serves as a Baptist minister for several churches in Kentucky.
Chairman Blanton was first elected to serve the 92nd House District in 2016.
The District now includes Knott and Magoffin Counties, plus part of Pike County.
