Support of Former Kentucky Senate Majority Floor Leader Latest Signal of Strength for Barr’s Senate Bid.
Lexington, KY – Today, U.S.
Congressman Andy Barr (R-KY) announced the endorsement of former Majority Floor Leader of the Kentucky State Senate Damon Thayer in his bid for the United States Senate.
Thayer, a resident of Scott County, served in the Kentucky State Senate from 2003 to 2024, and operates several successful businesses including Kentucky Senator Bourbon.
Thayer’s support signals growing support for Barr’s campaign, which launched last week.
“I enthusiastically endorse Andy Barr to be the next U.S.
Senator from Kentucky,” said Damon Thayer of Scott County, former Majority Floor Leader of the Kentucky State Senate.
“He’s been a model Congressman, a solid Reagan conservative, has great constituent services, and loves our country.
Nobody in Kentucky is more battle-tested.”
“I am grateful for the endorsement of my friend Damon Thayer, a great Kentucky leader who knows how to get things done and win,” said Andy Barr.
“Damon’s support will be a tremendous asset as we communicate our America First agenda with voters across the Commonwealth.”
Thayer first announced his endorsement of Barr on X: https://x.com/damon_thayer/status/1916807600244457800.
