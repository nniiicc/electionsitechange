Senator from Kentucky.
Judge Knochelmann praised Congressman Barr for delivering results for Central Kentucky and providing excellent constituent services during his time in Congress.
“I’m proud to support Andy Barr for Senate,” said Judge Kris Knochelmann.
“I have watched Andy for years as a public servant who looks at the big issues facing our country while serving his district with a servant’s mindset.
Andy is humble, intelligent, and understands the needs of Kentucky.
He will be an extraordinary Senator who makes a difference for our region.” “I’m honored to have Judge Kris Knochelmann’s support,” said Congressman Barr.
“Kris is a respected leader who has produced real results for Kenton County, and I look forward to working with him to deliver even more for Kenton County and all of Northern Kentucky.” Judge Knochelmann joins 30 other County Judge/Executives throughout the Commonwealth who are supporting Congressman Barr’s U.S.
Senate campaign.
Barr is also backed by Kentucky State Representative Stephanie Dietz, who represents part of Kenton County in Frankfort.
