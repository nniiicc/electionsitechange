While Booker claims to champion working families and struggling Kentuckians, his own nonprofit’s financial records raise questions about whether the organization prioritized charitable work or Booker’s personal compensation.
According to federal tax filings , Booker paid himself $87,000 in 2020, the organization’s largest reported individual expenditure that year, and another $87,000 in 2021, its second-largest individual expenditure.
In total, Booker received $174,000 from Hood to Holler over those two years.
“The biggest expense at Charles Booker’s so-called “charity” was Charles Booker.
His salary topped every other expense, and not one penny went to food or health care for Kentuckians in need.
Hood to the Holler should be renamed Hood to Holler to Himself.
