Senator from Kentucky.
Judge Mitchell has served as Knox County Judge Executive since 2019.
“I am excited to endorse Congressman Andy Barr for the U.S.
Senate,” said Mike Mitchell, Judge Executive of Knox County.“After meeting with Congressman Barr and discussing the issues affecting Knox County and our region, I believe he is the best choice for Senator.” “Judge Mitchell is doing outstanding work for the people of Knox County,” said Barr.
“Together, we’ll fight to bring more resources for opioid prevention, rehabilitation, and law enforcement to stop the flow of fentanyl into our communities.
We’ll also work side by side to protect and strengthen Kentucky coal, which will deliver critical economic development and energy dominance for the region.
I’m grateful for Judge Mitchell’s partnership—and proud to have his support in this campaign.” Judge Mitchell is a native of Eastern Kentucky who was a businessman before entering public service.
