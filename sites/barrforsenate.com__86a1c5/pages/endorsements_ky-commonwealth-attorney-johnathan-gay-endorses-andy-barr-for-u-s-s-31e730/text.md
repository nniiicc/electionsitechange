Flemingsburg, Kentucky — Today, Kentucky Commonwealth Attorney of the 19th Judicial Circuit, Johnathan Gay, endorses Andy Barr (R-KY) to the United States Senate.
“I am proud to endorse Andy Barr to be Kentucky’s next U.S.
Senator,” said Gay.
“Andy has been a consistent partner to prosecutors and law enforcement.
He understands that backing our justice system means backing safer communities, and I’m confident he will continue that work in the Senate for Kentucky.”
“I’m deeply grateful for Commonwealth’s Attorney Johnathan Gay’s support,” said Barr.
“He has been on the front lines of justice in our Commonwealth, and I appreciate his leadership in standing with law enforcement and victims’ families.
Together, we share a commitment to strengthening public safety and advancing commonsense reforms to better protect Kentucky communities.”
Johnathan Gay is the Commonwealth’s Attorney for Kentucky’s 19th Judicial Circuit, where he serves as the lead prosecutor handling felony cases on behalf of the Commonwealth.
He is known for his advocacy for victims, commitment to fairness in the justice system, and strong collaboration with law enforcement.
Respected as steady and principled counsel, he focuses on holding offenders accountable while working to strengthen public safety across his circuit.
Attorney Johnathan Gay joins a growing coalition of leaders across Kentucky in endorsing Barr’s campaign.
Barr has over 170 major endorsements, more than all other campaigns combined.
