Senate, has earned the endorsement of the Kentucky Motorcycle Association (KMA), a leading voice for motorcycle riders across the Commonwealth.
The Kentucky Motorcycle Association represents riders from across Kentucky and advocates for policies that promote safety, protect rider freedoms, and strengthen the state’s motorcycle culture.
“I’m honored to have the support of the Kentucky Motorcycle Association,” said Barr .
“As our next Senator, I’ll always make sure that Kentucky riders have a voice in Washington.
I’m committed to protecting our veterans, our freedoms, and our future.” “Kentucky’s motorcycling community values freedom and common-sense leadership—and Andy Barr delivers on this,” said Tommy Williams, President of the Kentucky Motorcycle Association .
“He understands the importance of protecting our rights, supporting veterans, and ensuring that federal policies don’t stand in the way of our way of life.
KMA is proud to stand with him in this race.” “Kentucky riders want a Senator who will stand up for them.
Andy Barr has earned our trust by defending individual liberty, supporting veterans, and fighting for Kentucky values.
We’re proud to support him for the U.S.
Senate,” said David Owen, Secretary of the Kentucky Motorcycle Association .
“Andy Barr has consistently listened to Kentucky riders and understands that the freedom to ride goes hand in hand with protecting individual liberties,” said Dave Newman, Vice President of Legislation .
“He has a proven record of standing up for our members, and we know he’ll continue fighting for common-sense policies in the U.S.
Senate.” “Our members care about freedom, responsibility, and preserving the traditions that make Kentucky special.
Andy Barr shares those values and has always been willing to listen to the motorcycle community,” said Glenda Hobbic, Presidential Public Affairs Liaison .
