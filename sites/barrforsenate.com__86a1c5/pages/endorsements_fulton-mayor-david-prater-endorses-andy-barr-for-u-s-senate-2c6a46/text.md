Fulton, KY—Today, Fulton Mayor David Prater endorsed Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.
“West Kentucky needs a leader who will be able to make a difference for us on day one,” said Mayor Prater.
“Andy Barr has proven that he can fight alongside President Trump to deliver for our farmers, families, and rural communities.
Let’s get behind Andy and put him in the U.S.
Senate.”
“Mayor Prater is doing a great job in Fulton.
He’s advanced major infrastructure projects during his time as Mayor.
With our partnership when I’m in the Senate, we can take these projects to the next level,” said Barr.
“I’m grateful for his support and look forward to getting to work for Kentuckians in Fulton.”
A longtime resident of Fulton, Mayor Prater and his wife own Evans Drug Company, an independent pharmacy that underscores his commitment to local business and healthcare access.
Mayor Prater is also active in statewide municipal leadership as a member of the Kentucky League of Cities Board of Directors.
