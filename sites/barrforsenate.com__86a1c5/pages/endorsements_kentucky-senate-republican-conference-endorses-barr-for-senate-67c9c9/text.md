LEXINGTON, KY— Today, Andy Barr, the Republican nominee for U.S.
Senate, was endorsed by the Kentucky State Senate Republican Conference.
Barr was endorsed by 31 Kentucky State Senators, including Senate President Robert Stivers.
“I’m honored to have the support of President Stivers and 30 Republican members of the Kentucky State Senate,” said Andy Barr.
“Together, we’ll work to deliver results for every corner of our Commonwealth, from creating good-paying jobs and growing Kentucky’s economy to investing in infrastructure and advancing conservative, pro-growth policies.”
“As President of the Kentucky State Senate, I’m fully behind Andy Barr.
I believe that most of the Republican Caucus if not all of the Caucus are fully behind Andy Barr,” said Kentucky State Senate President Robert Stivers.
“Andy will help deliver economic development and infrastructure projects.
Andy will fight for pro-growth, conservative policies that will lower taxes, reduce regulations, and create jobs.
Kentucky needs to send Andy Barr to the Senate in November!”
Barr is backed by over 170 major endorsers in Kentucky and nationwide.
He has seven times the cash-on-hand as radical Socialist Charles Booker.
