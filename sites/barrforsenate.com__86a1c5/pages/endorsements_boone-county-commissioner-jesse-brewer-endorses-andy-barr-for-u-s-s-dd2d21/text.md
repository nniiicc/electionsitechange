Senate Endorsements — April 15, 2026 Burlington, Kentucky — Today, Boone County Commissioner, Jesse Brewer, announced his endorsement of Andy Barr (R-KY) for the United States Senate.
“I’ve worked with Andy Barr for years as a Boone County Commissioner, and one thing is clear, he shows up,” said Commissioner Brewer.
“Even when we weren’t in his district, he made the time, built relationships, and helped deliver results for Boone County.
That’s real leadership.
Kentucky does not need more politicians who only show up during election season, we need proven servant leaders.
Andy Barr is exactly that, and he will be a strong voice for Boone County and all of Northern Kentucky in the U.S.
Senate.” “I am grateful to Commissioner Brewer for his support and for the work he does every day on behalf of Boone County,” said Barr.
“Local leaders like Jesse are the backbone of our communities, and his endorsement means a deal deal.
I look forward to strengthening Boone County and the rest of Kentucky together.” Jesse Brewer has served as Boone County Commissioner since 2018.
A former police officer and U.S.
Army Reserve member, he later built a successful real estate investment and property management business.
Brewer also founded community initiatives like Holiday Angels and the Brewer Foundation for Civic Engagement, reflecting his commitment to public service, economic development, and helping Boone County families.
He is a husband, father, and patriot.
Commissioner Brewer joins a multitude of other County officials in endorsing Barr’s campaign.
Barr has over 160 major endorsements, more than all other campaigns combined.
