Buena Vista, Kentucky—Today, Harrison County Judge/Executive Jason Marshall endorsed Andy Barr to be the next Senator from Kentucky.
“Andy Barr has been our champion before and when we put him in the Senate, he will do the same again,” said Judge Marshall.
“Andy and his team are always responsive to the needs of our community.
Andy has a proven record of delivering for our farmers, families, and business owners.
I’m proud to endorse him.”
“I’m looking forward to representing Harrison County once again when I’m elected to the U.S.
Senate,” said Barr.
“Judge Marshall will be an incredible partner for me at the local level, and I admire his many years of service to the community, as a farmer and business owner.”
Judge Marshall is a lifelong native of Harrison County and has farmed in the community all his life.
He owns two small businesses in Harrison County.
