Benton, Kentucky—Today, Kentucky State Representative Chris Freeland (HD-6) endorsed Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.
“Andy Barr has a proven record of fighting for Kentucky jobs, energy, and values,” said Rep.
Freeland.
“As a small business owner and lifelong Western Kentuckian, I’m for Andy Barr because he will hit the ground running as our next Senator and deliver for Western Kentucky.
I’m proud to endorse him.”
“As a small business owner of one Western Kentucky’s oldest and most respected radio stations, and now as a State Representative, Chris Freeland has always put Western Kentucky first,” said Barr.
“I’m honored to have his endorsement and look forward to working alongside him to deliver real results for the region.”
Representative Freeland represents Kentucky’s Sixth District in the State House which now includes Lyon, Marshall, and part of McCracken Counties.
He’s served as a State Representative since 2019.
He is native of Marshall County and is the General Manager of Freeland broadcasting.
