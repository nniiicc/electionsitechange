Senator from Kentucky.
Judge Hall has served as Judge Executive of Greenup County since 2023.
“Stronger Counties mean a stronger Kentucky – and Andy Barr understands that,” said Judge Hall.
“As the Greenup County Judge Executive, I’ve seen Andy Barr’s dedication to not just his district, but to all 120 counties across Kentucky.
His support for rural communities like Greenup County shows his commitment to building a stronger, more united Commonwealth of Kentucky.
I’m proud to fully support Andy Barr for U.S.
Senate.” “Judge Hall understands what it takes to deliver for Greenup County and Eastern Kentucky families,” said Barr.
“He and I will partner together to protect Kentucky coal, farmers, and fight for key economic development projects that will power communities in Eastern Kentucky into the future.
I’m grateful to have his support to be your next U.S.
Senator, and I look forward to partnering together for years to come.” Prior to becoming Greenup County CJE, Bobby Hall served as the Property Value Administrator for almost a decade.
