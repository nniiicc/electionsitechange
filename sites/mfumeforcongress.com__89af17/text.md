FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
On Maryland’s 7th Congressional District Election:
Where is Maryland’s 7th Congressional District?
Maryland’s 7th Congressional District covers most of the City of Baltimore and broad portions of western and eastern Baltimore County.
When does Early Voting open and close?
Early Voting for the Primary Election – Thursday, June 11, 2026 through Thursday, June 18, 2026 from 7 am until 8 pm.
Early voting for the General Election – Thursday, October 22, 2026 through Thursday, October 29, 2026.
