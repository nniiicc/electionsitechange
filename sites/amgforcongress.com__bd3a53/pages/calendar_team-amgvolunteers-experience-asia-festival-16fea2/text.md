Team AMG members and volunteers will be staffing a table at the Tallahassee Experience Asia Festival.
Come say hi, grab a sign, or pick up AMG literature!
The Asian Coalition of Tallahassee (ACT) presets its annual Experience Asia Festival for 2025 on Saturday, September 27th from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. at Tom Brown Park.
Featuring free admission, the family-friendly festival celebrates the diversity of Asian and Asian Pacific cultures through performances, demonstrations, cuisine, crafts, and more.
