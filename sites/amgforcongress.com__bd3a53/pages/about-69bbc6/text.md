Born at San Diego (Balboa) Naval Hospital, Amanda moved to Florida when she was two and was primarily raised in St.
Petersburg until she moved to the Big Bend region to attend Florida State University, where she studied economics, international affairs , emergency management , and public administration .
In her first semester as a graduate student, she was recruited by the largest implementing partner for the U.S.
Agency for International Development (USAID), until it was dismantled by Elon Musk, an unelected billionaire, and DOGE, early last year.
Over the past decade, she has served as an operations director and pricing expert, supporting programs to help stop crises before they reach our borders and keep the American people safe at home.
Amanda specialized in cutting waste while contracting with the U.S. government, working to ensure maximum program efficiency and the most efficient use of taxpayer dollars .
She looks forward to bringing rigor to government spending, passion for serving the American people, and vision for a brighter future to Congress.
Raised by a large, loving family, Amanda understands the sacrifice required for military service as the daughter and granddaughter of veterans, the importance of public service, and the heart and grit required for entrepreneurship.
As the proud member of a family that hunts, fishes, camps, and hikes, Amanda knows we must protect our North Florida way of life from overdevelopment and mismanagement.
The historic devastation from hurricanes over the last decade is a stark reminder that we must prioritize economic growth without compromising our safety, economic responsibility, and resiliency Amanda married her high school sweetheart, and together they’ve built their life here in the Big Bend where they’re growing their family in a community they cherish.
In early 2026, they welcomed their very first child, a beautiful baby boy.
She is lucky to have her entire extended family – parents, bonus-parents, grandparents, sister, and cousins living close to her in Tallahassee to help with the baby while she’s on the campaign trail.
