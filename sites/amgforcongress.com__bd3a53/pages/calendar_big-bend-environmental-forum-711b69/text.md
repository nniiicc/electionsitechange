The Big Bend Environmental Forum (BBEF) is hosting its candidate forum in preparation for this year’s important election.
The BBEF is an alliance of organizations that have come together to conserve the region’s environmental qualities through education, advocacy, research and networking among its members.
You can read more about BBEF and its member organizations on the website, bbef.org.
