Young professionals are helping shape the future of Bay County—and the decisions made by local elected leaders directly affect where we live, work, build businesses, raise families and plan our futures.
Political Pulse gives Bay County's young professionals the opportunity to hear directly from candidates seeking local office about the issues that matter to their generation.
During this nonpartisan candidate forum, participating candidates will be asked questions focused on the challenges and opportunities facing young professionals throughout Bay County.
