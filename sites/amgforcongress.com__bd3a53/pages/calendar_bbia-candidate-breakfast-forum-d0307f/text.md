Join the Bay Building Industries Association Government Affairs Committee for the 2026 Candidate Breakfast Forum.
This informative event provides BBIA members and community stakeholders an opportunity to hear directly from candidates seeking elected office before the upcoming election cycle.
Candidates will introduce themselves, discuss their priorities, and respond to questions submitted in advance.
Attendees will gain valuable insight into the issues, policies, and leadership decisions that may impact our community, economy, and building industry.
Enjoy breakfast, network with fellow members, and become a more informed voter before heading to the polls.
