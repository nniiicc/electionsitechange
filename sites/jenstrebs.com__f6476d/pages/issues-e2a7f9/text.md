- I believe healthcare is a human right, and I’m committed to building a system where no one is forced to choose between medical care and financial survival.

In the State House, I will:
- Advocate for a true single-payer system.

Michigan’s future depends on fully funded classrooms, respected educators, and public colleges that are accessible without lifelong debt.

- Data centers are expanding rapidly across the country, and Michigan is being targeted because of our water, land, and energy resources.
I believe we need to slow this down and take a responsible, community-first approach.

If data centers are going to exist in Michigan, they must meet the highest standards for clean energy, water protection, and community benefits.

In the state house, I will:
