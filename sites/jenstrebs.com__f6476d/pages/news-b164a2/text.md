Kalamazoo County Commission chair announces bid for State House
KALAMAZOO, MI (WKZO AM/FM) – The current chair of the Kalamazoo County Commission is hoping to make a move to Lansing next.Jen Strebs officially announced her bid for the 41st District State House seat Friday.
Current Representative Julie Rogers will be seeking state Senate seat currently held by Sean McCann, who is running for the 4th District Congressional seat next year.Prior to being elected to the county commission in 2021, Strebs served three years as a Kalamazoo Township trustee.
