“So Much More” shows Oregonians that Christine Drazan cannot be trusted to protect abortion rights or reproductive healthcare providers and patients from federal attacks.
“SO MUCH MORE” “Since the overturning of Roe vs.
Wade , Republicans like Christine Drazan have stopped at nothing to attack reproductive freedom and healthcare.
Oregonians deserve a governor who will stand up for our values and fight for their right to make decisions about their own bodies,” said Governor Kotek.
“That’s why I created a first-in-the-nation plan to maintain funding for Planned Parenthood and strengthened Oregon’s ‘Shield Law’ to protect patients and providers.” Christine Drazan’s extreme, anti-abortion record: In May, Drazan touted her fifth consecutive endorsement from Oregon Right to Life — which opposes abortion even in cases of rape, incest, or health of the mother.
In March, Drazan voted against using state dollars to backfill funding to Planned Parenthood .
In March, Drazan also voted against protecting abortion and gender-affirming care providers and seekers from lawsuits and professional discipline.
In 2022, Drazan called herself a “pro-life woman” .
In 2022, Drazan said she would veto a bill if it expanded abortion access in Oregon.
In 2022, Drazan celebrated the Dobbs vs.
Jackson Women’s Health Organization decision .
Drazan has sponsored three anti-choice bills that could punish doctors , including two that would allow prison time.
In 2018, Drazan supported a ballot measure that would prohibit public funds from being spent on abortions in Oregon.

### ABOUT GOVERNOR KOTEK Governor Tina Kotek has spent her career making history and fighting for others.
Over the last three years, Governor Kotek has expanded access to affordable childcare, created thousands of new shelter beds, protected abortion access and vote-by-mail, and funded early literacy and summer learning programs.
She has also been a national leader in pushing back against Donald Trump’s attacks on our communities, our healthcare, and our rights by blocking his illegal attempts to deploy the Oregon National Guard in our communities and working through the night to reverse his decision to strip food assistance from Oregonians by taking away SNAP benefits.
Governor Kotek is running for reelection to continue defending Oregon values and tackling the state’s biggest challenges – lowering costs, reducing homelessness, strengthening schools, expanding mental health and addiction care, and bringing affordable childcare to every corner of the state.
