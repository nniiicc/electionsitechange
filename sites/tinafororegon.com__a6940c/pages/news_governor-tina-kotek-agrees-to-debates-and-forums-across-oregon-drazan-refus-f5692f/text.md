PORTLAND, OR – Today, Governor Tina Kotek announced she’s participating in debates and forums across Portland, Eugene, Medford and Bend to share how she’s continuing to tackle the biggest issues facing Oregon and how she’s fighting back against Donald Trump’s attacks directly with voters.
Meanwhile, extremist Christine Drazan is refusing to face voters at a Portland debate in front of a live audience – preferring to hide behind a TV studio debate so she won’t have to answer directly to Oregonians about her support for Donald Trump’s MAGA agenda that includes eliminating vote by mail, banning abortion, and gutting Oregonians’ health care.
“Governor Kotek accepted invitations to debates and forums in Portland, Eugene, Medford and Bend,” Federico Araujo, communications director for Tina for Oregon.
“Meanwhile, Christine Drazan refused opportunities to debate in front of a live audience in both Portland and Eugene because she’s afraid voters will hold her accountable for siding with Donald Trump as he attacks our state.
With just over 40 days until the election, the choice couldn’t be any clearer: Christine Drazan is ready to open the door to Trump’s MAGA agenda and Governor Kotek is fighting every day to protect Oregonians from federal overreach and harm.”
All debates and forums will be broadcast or livestreamed, meaning Oregonians will have ample opportunity to hear from both candidates.
Governor Kotek will be participating in debates and forums with:
- Portland Forum, September 24th: Oregon Business & Industry and Portland Business Journal
- Eugene Debate, October 1st: Oregon Journalism Project, Lookout Eugene-Springfield, KVAL TV, and the UO School of Journalism and Communication
- Medford Debate, October 7th: KOBI-TV
- Bend Debate, October 13th: KTVZ and The Source

ABOUT GOVERNOR KOTEK
Governor Tina Kotek has spent her career making history and fighting for others.
Over the last three years, Governor Kotek has expanded access to affordable childcare, created thousands of new shelter beds, protected abortion access and vote-by-mail, and funded early literacy and summer learning programs.
She has also been a national leader in pushing back against Donald Trump’s attacks on our communities, our healthcare, and our rights by blocking his illegal attempts to deploy the Oregon National Guard in our communities and working through the night to reverse his decision to strip food assistance from Oregonians by taking away SNAP benefits.
Governor Kotek is running for reelection to continue defending Oregon values and tackling the state’s biggest challenges – lowering costs, reducing homelessness, strengthening schools, expanding mental health and addiction care, and bringing affordable childcare to every corner of the state.
