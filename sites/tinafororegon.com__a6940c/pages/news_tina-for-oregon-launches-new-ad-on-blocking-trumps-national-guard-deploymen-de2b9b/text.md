“Leadership” highlights Governor Kotek’s success in blocking President Trump’s illegal National Guard deployment in Portland
PORTLAND, OR – Today, Governor Tina Kotek’s re-election campaign launched a new ad highlighting Oregon’s success in blocking President Trump’s illegal National Guard deployment in Portland.
Ahead of the one year anniversary of the attempted deployment, “Leadership” shows Oregonians that Governor Kotek will always fight to protect them from federal overreach.
“Last year, President Trump tried to mobilize the National Guard against the very communities they are meant to protect,” said Governor Kotek.
“But I didn’t let that happen.
We sued Trump in court, and we won.
Unlike Christine Drazan, I will always stand up for Oregon values and the rule of law.”
While Governor Kotek quickly defended Oregon, Kotek’s opponent, Republican Christine Drazan, failed to stand up to Trump’s deployment of the National Guard.
She supported the federal military deployment in Portland, and even called anti-ICE protesters “domestic terrorists.” And when it came to protecting Oregon’s future, Drazan voted against preventing further federal overreach with the Oregon National Guard.

ABOUT GOVERNOR KOTEK
Governor Tina Kotek has spent her career making history and fighting for others.
Over the last three years, Governor Kotek has expanded access to affordable childcare, created thousands of new shelter beds, protected abortion access and vote-by-mail, and funded early literacy and summer learning programs.
She has also been a national leader in pushing back against Donald Trump’s attacks on our communities, our healthcare, and our rights by blocking his illegal attempts to deploy the Oregon National Guard in our communities and working through the night to reverse his decision to strip food assistance from Oregonians by taking away SNAP benefits.
Governor Kotek is running for reelection to continue defending Oregon values and tackling the state’s biggest challenges – lowering costs, reducing homelessness, strengthening schools, expanding mental health and addiction care, and bringing affordable childcare to every corner of the state.
