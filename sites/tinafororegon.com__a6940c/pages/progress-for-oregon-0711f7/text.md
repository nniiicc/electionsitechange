From her first day on the job, Governor Kotek has been focused on building a better future for Oregonians.
Now, President Trump and a Republican-led Congress have presented more challenges – chaotic tariffs that are driving up prices, increasing health care premiums, and cutting Medicaid and food assistance for Oregonians.
She’s tackling our biggest challenges head-on: fighting to help Oregonians struggling to make ends meet, increasing affordable housing, reducing homelessness, expanding access to mental health and addiction treatment, improving our schools, and so much more.
Highlights from Governor Kotek’s first term include: Tackling Rising Costs As chaos in the federal government increases the cost of living for Oregon families, Governor Kotek has gone after corporate price gouging, worked to lower housing costs, and fought to put more money back in the pockets of Oregonians.
