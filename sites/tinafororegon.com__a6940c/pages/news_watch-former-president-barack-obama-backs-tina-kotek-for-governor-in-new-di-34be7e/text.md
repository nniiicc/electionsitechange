In the ad, first reported by Julia Shumway in the Oregon Capital Chronicle , President Obama says, “Tina knows things are broken, but she’s a fixer — always has been, always will be.
And she’s the leader we need in this moment.” Watch the new digital ad here .
In his message to Oregonians, President Obama praises Tina’s leadership in raising the minimum wage, protecting reproductive rights, and standing up for workers.
He also emphasizes Tina’s problem-solving agenda, including her plans to make housing and health care more affordable.
Here is the full transcript of President Obama’s video in support of Tina : “Oregon, I want to tell you about my friend, Tina Kotek.
She’s the real deal.
Tina led the effort to raise Oregon’s minimum wage.
She stands up to workers and our environment, and she’s fighting to lower the cost of housing and health care.
“Tina knows things are broken, but she’s a fixer — always has been, always will be.
And she’s the leader we need in this moment.
Tina passed the strongest abortion access law in the country.
And as governor, she’ll bring people together to tackle Oregon’s challenges, fight for the values we share, and build a better future for everyone.
“Oregon, I’m counting on you.
Mail your ballot back by November 8th, and vote Tina Kotek for governor.” Join us!
