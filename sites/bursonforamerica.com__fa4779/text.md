One promise.
Putting Georgia first starts with putting DeKalb County first.
Lower Your Taxes Property and income taxes in DeKalb are too high.
I will vote to raise homestead exemptions, lower millage rates, and cut state income taxes — or eliminate them altogether.
Better Schools DeKalb spends $4,000 more per student than the rest of Georgia, yet only 1 in 3 kids reads at grade level.
I will vote for funding that rewards real proficiency in reading, math and science.
Safer Neighborhoods DeKalb's crime rate is nearly 3× the national average.
I will back our law enforcement officers and support mandatory tougher penalties for repeat offenders so families can feel safe at home, at school and on every street.
My platform also includes: Lower Energy Costs; Separate boys and girls sports - Separate gender dressing and baths; Lower Healthcare Costs; Election Integrity; Less Government Regulation; Lower Government Spending; Second Amendment Protections Meet Tim 37 years fighting for accountability.
After 37 years as a Probate Court trial attorney, Tim retired in 2023 to a quiet home in Redan.
He grew up in Clayton County, lettered on the Forest Park football team, and spent three decades in Cobb County as a Sunday School teacher, mission director and youth sports coach.
With his daughter off to college, Tim started knocking on doors in his new neighborhood for the 2024 election.
What he heard convinced him to run.
"Property taxes higher than anywhere else I'd lived in Georgia.
Schools where only 1/3 of children can read at grade level.
Sirens blaring every night.
It's time for representation that actually fights for you." — I am your choice for your voice.
