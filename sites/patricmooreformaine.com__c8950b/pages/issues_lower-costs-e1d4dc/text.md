Lower Costs
1.
Tackling Everyday Expenses
Focus on the rising costs of essentials like groceries, fuel, and utilities that are putting pressure on working families.
2.
Responsible Tax Relief
Support targeted tax relief that puts more money back into the pockets of Maine residents without compromising essential services.
3.
Energy Affordability
Promote solutions that lower heating and electricity costs, especially during Maine’s long winters.
4.
Increasing Market Competition
Encourage policies that promote competition and transparency to help bring down prices across key industries.
5.
Supporting Local Production
Invest in local agriculture, food systems, and small businesses to reduce reliance on costly imports and keep prices more stable.
6.
Smarter Government Spending
Advocate for efficient, accountable use of taxpayer dollars to reduce waste and avoid unnecessary cost burdens on residents.
