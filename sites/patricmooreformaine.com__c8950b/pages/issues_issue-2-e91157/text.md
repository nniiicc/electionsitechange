Protect Healthcare
1.
Protecting Rural Access
Ensure our rural hospitals and clinics remain open and accessible so no one has to travel hours for basic care.
2.
Supporting Healthcare Workers
Advocate for policies that help recruit and retain nurses, doctors, and frontline staff in our communities.
3.
Lowering Costs
Work to reduce out-of-pocket costs for prescriptions, insurance, and essential services so people can actually afford the care they need.
4.
Expanding Mental Health Services
Increase access to mental health and substance use treatment, especially in underserved rural areas.
5.
Strengthening Preventative Care
Promote early intervention and preventative services to improve long-term health outcomes and reduce overall costs.
6.
Cutting Through Complexity
Push for a simpler, more transparent healthcare system that’s easier for patients to navigate and understand.
