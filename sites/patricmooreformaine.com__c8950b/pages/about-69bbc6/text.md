Maine Senate District 16
Patric Moore
Air Force veteran and a leader in Central Maine’s economic development.
Moore brings a diverse professional background to the race.
He served ten years in the U.S.
Air Force, including work as a Cryptologic Language Analyst in the Intelligence Community and later as the Air Force Recruiter for Central Maine.
He later served as the Director of Entrepreneurship at Main Street Skowhegan, where he managed the local business ecosystem and redevelopment projects aimed at revitalizing the regional economy.
Moore holds a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from Southern New Hampshire University, as well as a Master's in Food Business from the Culinary Institute of America.
He lives in Waterville with his dog, Barkley.
