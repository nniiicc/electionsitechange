Centre County deserves a leader who actively listens to the constituents, fights for our values with integrity, and defends our district in Harrisburg with resolve.
I understand the difficulties facing our families, and the dreams we have for our community.
I will aim to foster a healthy fiscal environment for everyone in our area, protect your constitutional rights and freedoms, and bring Centre County's voice to Harrisburg.
I am committed to transparency, accountability, and serving the people of the 77th District with integrity.
Multiple generations of my family reside here in Centre County.
As a mother, grandmother, and friend to many in our town, I intend to extend the intentionality with which I would care for my own relationships to all of the constituents in our district.
With good planning, grounded conservative wisdom, and faithful endurance, I know that positive change is possible for the 77th District.
The Issues Priorities for the 77th District Fiscal Responsibility Cutting wasteful spending and keeping taxes low so hardworking families keep more of what they earn.
Quality Education Empowering parents with choice and ensuring every student in Centre County has access to an excellent education.
Second Amendment Protecting Second Amendment rights and opposing any legislation that infringes on law-abiding citizens' freedoms.
Public Safety Supporting law enforcement and first responders to keep our neighborhoods and communities safe.
Family Values Defending the values and freedoms that strengthen our families and the fabric of our community.
Community Growth Promoting smart development and local business growth that preserves Centre County's character.
Rural Priorities Advocating for infrastructure, broadband access, and agricultural support for our rural communities.
