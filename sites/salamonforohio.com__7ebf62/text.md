About Samantha
Samantha Salamon is a lifelong Northfield Center resident, a celebrated victims’ rights attorney, and a proud mom.
Growing up in a single-parent household, after watching her father suffer a fatal heart attack, Samantha was instilled with the values of hard work, determination, and empathy.
Samantha attended Cleveland State University on a soccer scholarship and graduated summa cum laude in social work and Middle Eastern studies in 2012.
She continued her education at Cleveland State to receive her Juris Doctor in 2015.
Upon passing the bar exam, Samantha moved to Dubai to work as a real estate and corporate attorney.
This new journey allowed her to work with clients from around the world while juggling the challenges of moving to a new country.
Samantha moved back to where her heart really belonged, Ohio, in 2017.
She opened her own law firm, where she worked as a criminal defense attorney.
While serving as a criminal defense appointed attorney, Samantha began volunteering at Asian Services in Action, Inc.
(ASIA) as an immigration attorney.
Soon after, Samantha joined ASIA full-time and developed a holistic legal program to give a voice to victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, and human trafficking.
Now as ASIA’s Chief Counsel, Samantha has expanded her work with victims to work alongside the Ohio Attorney General's Human Trafficking Commission, the Governor's Human Trafficking Task Force, and multiple other agencies as an expert in various forms of victimization.
Samantha also serves on the Northfield Center Township Zoning Commission, the Nordonia School District Financial Activities Communication Team, the Executive Board of Family Promise - Summit County, the Chair of the Summit County Collaborative Against Human Trafficking, and as the Founder and Chair of the Ohio Victim Services Bar Association.

Fiscal Responsibility
Samantha believes government must be a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars.
Families across District 35 work hard for their income, and they deserve to know their tax dollars are being spent wisely, transparently, and with accountability.
Samantha supports practical budgeting that prioritizes essential services, strengthens communities, and ensures public funds are used efficiently to benefit the people they are meant to serve.
Children Come First
From keeping children safe in their home to setting them up for success with exceptional public schools, children are a top priority for Samantha.
Samantha has represented multiple children, most of whom are victims of sexual assault and/or domestic violence, and she has learned the importance of meeting children where they are and fighting for what they deserve.
Children are the future of this country, and providing them all the resources necessary is a priority Samantha doesn't take lightly.
Protecting Basic Human Rights
Government Transparency & Local Control
The public deserves complete and full transparency in the planning and development of data centers.
Samantha believes in home rule first, meaning communities should have a meaningful role in the decision-making process.
Samantha will support legislation to ensure communities are clearly informed of what is being proposed in their neighborhoods and can ask questions, review information, and provide input before projects move forward.
Residents deserve to know where the funding for any new data center projects is coming from, who would own and operate the facilities, and what potential impacts they may have -- without NDAs standing in the way.
Additionally, there must be transparency in the environmental and economic impact these data centers would have on the communities to make sure we are protecting the valuable resources our communities need and deserve.
If the people of Geauga, Portage, and Summit counties choose to allow data centers in our communities, increased utility costs should be shouldered by the entities driving the increased demand and our residents should absolutely not be on the hook to pay the price.
Additionally, there must be a requirement for all new construction to be completed by skilled union labor.
