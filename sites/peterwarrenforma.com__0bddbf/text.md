Peter Warren is retired from a 37-year career in the commercial fire alarm/life safety industry.
Starting at the bottom, he rose through the ranks to sales and management, building a career on hard work and teamwork.
Peter is a former Selectman and former Finance Committee member and currently serves on the Memorial Hall Renovation Committee.
He and his wife reside in Monson with their two rescue dogs.
They were proud to help renovate the new food pantry in Monson, demonstrating their commitment to neighbors helping neighbors.
