- Better Schools and Safer Neighborhoods
- Supporting our Teachers and Law Enforcement
- Keeping Taxes Low and Government Small
- Defending Conservative Values
- Protecting and Growing Small Business
I have spent my life being a passionate and principled voice for Knox County.
