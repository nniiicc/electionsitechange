Seemingly overnight, and without public consent, major technology companies have embedded artificial intelligence (AI) into the bedrock of daily life.
Writing an email, choosing a TV show, using a search engine — all of these tasks are now mediated by AI.
While the long-term consequences of this technology are still unfolding, the immediate costs are clear: AI places a massive burden on our environmental resources, eliminates jobs, and devalues genuine human skill and creativity.
As long as tech billionaires are permitted to amass unchecked profits from this new technology, AI’s influence will only expand — regardless of the harms it inflicts on working people and the planet.
We cannot stand by while a handful of CEOs reshape our entire economy to further concentrate wealth and power.
As your representative in Congress, Claire will fight to hold major technology corporations accountable, protect our workforce from the harms of AI, and ensure that new technologies benefit communities, not just corporate executives.
In Congress, Claire will support:
- Sen.
Bernie Sanders and Rep.
Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s Artificial Intelligence (AI) Data Center Moratorium Act which places a responsible pause on new data center construction while establishing strict environmental protections, banning corporate subsidies, empowering local community oversight, and mandating strong union labor standards.
- Legislation that requires users to opt-in to generative AI instead of imposing it on users without their consent, and obtain their consent before collecting data to train models or conduct surveillance.
- Reclaiming the economic gains of automation for workers by supporting a 32-hour work week with no reduction in pay.
- Mass education programs across industries and regions that will bring workers together to discuss the local impacts of AI, build solidarity, and equip them with tools to organize.
Higher education should be a public good.
But our current system of universities and colleges are increasingly unaffordable, often elitist, and unfairly reliant on labor from low-paid teaching positions.
Whether you are pursuing an undergraduate or technical degree, Claire supports guaranteed access to tuition-free higher education.
She wants to dismantle the tuition-and-loan business model that leaves students in crushing, often lifelong debt and redirect federal grant funds to support a network of participating public institutions, private colleges, regional schools, and rural universities.
These funds would cover the cost of attendance and necessary living expenses, regardless of means-testing, complex income caps, or restrictive demographic requirements.
She will support legislation that ensures all people delivering education, particularly adjuncts and contingent faculty, have fair compensation, job security, and collective bargaining rights.
For early and developing education, Claire supports a moratorium on generative AI use.
Data suggests that AI has adverse effects on students’ cognitive skills, critical thinking, and mental health.
By eliminating AI in schools, we support healthy brain development, safeguard against inherent technological biases, and protect student privacy.
