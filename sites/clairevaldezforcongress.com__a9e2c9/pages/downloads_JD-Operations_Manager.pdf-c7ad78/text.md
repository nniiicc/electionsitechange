Claire Valdez, Democratic Nominee New York Congressional District 7 Position: Operations Manager Location: New York City or Washington, DC Job Overview Claire Valdez, Democratic nominee for NY-7 and presumptive incoming Congressperson, is seeking an Operations Manager for her congressional office.
The Operations Manager is responsible for the office’s budget, payroll, benefits, human resources, compliance, vendors, intern/fellowship programs, and internal systems.
This is a senior role that reports to the Chief of Staff.
Responsibilities ●​ Oversee the office’s annual Members’ Representational Allowance (MRA) budget in coordination with the Chief of Staff. ●​ Manage district office leases in coordination with the District Director and Chief of Staff. ●​ Work with the Office of Payroll and Benefits to administer payroll and benefits for staff, including hiring paperwork, student loan repayment agreements, and bonuses. ●​ Run the office's hiring program, including the process for managing postings, applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and presenting offers. ●​ Establish and manage the onboarding process for new employees. ●​ Maintain the office’s employee handbook, ensuring policies are consistent with the values of the Member and House administrative rules. ●​ Ensure compliance with the Congressional Accountability Act and House employment rules. ●​ Manage relationships with vendors, including security, custodial, software, media, and IT services. ●​ Manage day-to-day operations, including supplies, assets, technology and accounts, mail, and security procedures. ●​ Run the office’s internship program, including hiring, supervision, and coordination with paid internship programs. ●​ Run the office’s fellowship programs, including coordination with the Legislative Director and/or District Director as necessary. ●​ Serve as the office’s primary contact with the CAO, House Administration Committee, Office of Payroll and Benefits, Office of Finance, and House Information Resources. ●​ Coordinate official travel policy and disclosure requirements with the Scheduler and Ethics Committee. ●​ Perform all other duties as assigned by the Member and Chief of Staff.
Qualifications and Characteristics ●​ Demonstrable experience running operations, finance, or HR for an organization.
Preference if the organization had an operating budget of $1 million or more. ●​ Exceptional attention to detail and a record of producing flawless financial and personnel work. ●​ Demonstrable experience managing a budget of $1 million or more. ●​ Warm, clear, and direct communication style, including the ability to explain complex rules or processes to colleagues in a simple and understandable way. ●​ Discretion with confidential personnel and financial information. ●​ Direct working knowledge of House financial and payroll systems and the Members’ Congressional Handbook is preferred. ●​ Experience running operations or HR for a House or Senate office is preferred. ●​ Familiarity with office systems and software, including Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Slack, Airtable, Fireside, IQ, Asana, or others. ●​ Alignment with the Member's democratic socialist values and platform. ●​ Familiarity with New York City or NY-7 is a plus.
Terms and Compensation This is a full-time, salaried, benefits-eligible position based in Washington, DC or NY-7, with occasional travel between DC and NY-7.
The starting salary is $95,000.
Employment will begin upon the Member's swearing-in in January 2027.
How to Apply Interested applicants should submit a resume and cover letter in PDF form to jobs@clairevaldezforcongress.com with the subject line "Operations Manager Application: [First Name] [Last Name]." Please include the email and phone number for two professional references.
Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis.
