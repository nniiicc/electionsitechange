Claire Valdez, Democratic Nominee New York Congressional District 7 Position: Scheduler Location: Washington, DC Job Overview Claire Valdez, Democratic nominee for NY-7 and presumptive incoming Congressperson, is seeking a Scheduler for her congressional office.
The Scheduler is responsible for managing the Member’s calendar and binder, including incoming requests, travel, and advance.
This is a high-trust role that liaises daily with the Member and reports to the Chief of Staff.
Responsibilities ●​ Oversee the Member’s master calendar, including hearings, votes, meetings, events, press, and travel. ●​ Manage incoming scheduling requests and communicate promptly and professionally with requesters. ●​ Manage the Member’s daily binder in coordination with the Chief of Staff, ensuring it contains all relevant memos and background materials prepared by the team. ●​ Coordinate with the Chief of Staff, District Director, Communications Director, and Legislative Director to ensure that the Member’s schedule is informed by all facets of the organization. ●​ Manage the Member’s travel and lodging, including filing Ethics travel reports where necessary. ●​ Facilitate a weekly or biweekly scheduling meeting with the Member, Chief of Staff, and relevant Directors to review the Member’s upcoming schedule. ●​ Run advance for public appearances, ensuring clear information regarding location, run-of-show, contacts, and security coordination with the Sergeant at Arms, Capitol Police, and/or NYPD. ●​ Support day-to-day operations in Washington, DC. ●​ Perform all other duties as assigned by the Member and Chief of Staff.
Qualifications and Characteristics ●​ Demonstrable experience providing scheduling or operational support for a principal. ●​ Organized and detail-oriented, with an ability to spot logistical details that may go wrong before they do. ●​ Discretion regarding the Member’s movements and meetings. ●​ Commitment to protecting the Member’s time. ●​ Calm and comfortable working around a timeline that shifts frequently and on short notice, with availability at all hours of the day. ●​ High social intelligence, and the ability to communicate warmly with stakeholders and deliver a polite no. ●​ Alignment with the Member's values and platform. ●​ Familiarity with the geography of NYC and NY-7 is a plus. ●​ Familiarity with House rules on travel and ethics is a plus.
Terms and Compensation This is a full-time, salaried, benefits-eligible position based in Washington, DC, with occasional travel.
The role requires on-call availability on weekends and evenings.
The starting salary is $82,000.
Employment will begin upon the Member's swearing-in in January 2027.
