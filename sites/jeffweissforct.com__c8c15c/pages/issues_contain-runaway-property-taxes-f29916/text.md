Stop unfunded mandates that inflate town budgets.
The state legislature has imposed over 1400 unfunded state mandates on our local governments.
These are mandates such as binding arbitration for municipal employee contracts; Minimum Budget Requirement for education forcing towns to spend at least as much as they did the previous year even if that’s not needed; and prevailing wage laws which also serve to undermine competitive local contractors.
Cap property assessment increases which tax unrealized gains.
Property value assessments should be frozen and, going forward, locked in at the sale price of the property.
The assessment is what the property tax rate (mill rate) is applied to in order to calculate the property tax.
When the value of the assessment is increased, the property owner is effectively paying a tax on unrealized gains.
This is especially harmful to lower income households and the elderly living on fixed incomes.
This can result in someone being forced to sell their home because they cannot afford the spiraling taxes.
We need to put an end to this. • Cap yearly property-tax increases at the rate of inflation, unless voters approve a one-year exception by supermajority referendum.
Towns need to operate within their means.
Limiting the increase in property taxes to keep up with inflation will force local governments to maintain a disciplined approach to spending.
