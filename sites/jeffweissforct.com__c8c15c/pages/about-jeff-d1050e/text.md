Menu About Jeff Issues Videos Subscribe About Jeff Here’s a Little About Me I’m a lifelong Connecticut resident, having grown up in Oakville.
I graduated from Holy Cross High School in Waterbury and then earned my Bachelor of Science in Engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI).
Today, my wife and I are proud residents of Cheshire, where we are blessed to care for an elderly parent.
Between us, we have five adult children.
I am a small business owner.
I also worked for five years as Deputy Registrar of Voters.
That role deepened my commitment to public service and inspired me to run for State Representative in the 103rd House District.
In my free time, I enjoy cooking, live music, and travel.
I especially like subjecting my family to new recipes.
I am a creative problem solver who is known for being measured, resourceful, and a trusted sounding board for others.
Above all, I am a man of faith who believes deeply in our Constitution and the essential protections it provides against government overreach.
I look forward to serving our community by representing you in Hartford.
