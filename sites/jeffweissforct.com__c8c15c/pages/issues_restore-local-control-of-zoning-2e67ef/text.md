While intended to create more affordable housing, these laws have failed to make a meaningful impact on housing costs and have instead created litigation that overrides local knowledge of infrastructure, schools, and town history.
Strengthen local authority over zoning so towns can plan according to their own priorities and values.
Members of the local community are far more in tune with the needs of their town than are bureaucrats in Hartford.
Keep development decisions at the local level to create sustainable neighborhoods.
Local Planning and Zoning officials are well aware of the infrastructure of their towns and what is needed to manage growth.
Mandates from the state do more harm than good.
Remove top-down mandates and allow the free market to fill the housing needs.
The free market is a better driver of sustainable development than government mandates.
Every town has different infrastructure, and each community should be able to grow accordingly.
The citizens of Cheshire, Hamden, and Wallingford deserve to be in charge of their own communities.
