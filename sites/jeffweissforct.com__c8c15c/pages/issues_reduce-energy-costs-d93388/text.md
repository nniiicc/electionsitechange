Menu About Jeff Issues Videos Subscribe ← All Issues Reduce Energy Costs Connecticut has the 3rd-highest electricity rates in the U.S.
Reduce or eliminate the Public Benefits Charge that funds unnecessary state government programs.
The Public Benefits Charge deserves closer scrutiny.
While some components provide value, others function as hidden costs that fund programs with limited accountability.
The Systems Benefit Charge shifts costs in ways that invite inefficiency; the Renewable Energy Investment Charge subsidizes sources that should increasingly stand on their own; and while Conservation and Load Management programs have reduced demand, they must be evaluated to ensure they are delivering results efficiently.
Repeal costly mandates on electric utilities that drive up rates for families.
In their zeal for so-called “green energy,” the Connecticut legislature has forced the electric utilities into purchasing some of the most expensive and least reliable sources of electricity and those costs are passed directly to Connecticut residents and businesses.
Enter into sensible market-based power purchase agreements that do not lock ratepayers into multi-year above-market electric rates.
The current power purchase agreement with Dominion Energy for Millstone has locked ratepayers into above-market rates while delivering strong returns to the operator.
Future agreements should be negotiated transparently and grounded in market realities, not political considerations.
