His Story A PRINCIPLED PERSON CALLED TO SERVE Elliott Neff has never wanted political office.
He is busy — running a business, coaching chess, and raising a large family.
But he and his wife believe that if principled and values-driven individuals are not willing to stand up and do their best to preserve freedom, then it becomes their fault when those freedoms are eroded.
Elliott is a National Master chess player and has spent 21+ years running Chess4Life, a mission-driven organization that brings the benefits of chess — problem-solving, critical thinking, and strategic planning — to children across the country.
He understands what it means to think many moves ahead.
Having lived in Washington State for 30 years, Elliott watched sales tax climb from approximately 6% into double digits.
Now in 2026, Washington state has passed a new income tax despite state law prohibiting personal income taxes and voters overwhelmingly reaffirming that prohibition just two years earlier.
He moved to South Dakota five years ago, and he loves this state.
He will do all he can to ensure that does not happen here.
Elliott and his wife have a deep sense of responsibility for their children's future and the future of District 6 — and that is what drives this campaign.
