Fighting for Southwest Georgia's Families Too many politicians talk about what they will do when elected, but abandon their values and the people they represent and fail to get the job done once they get in office.
NOT Mike Cheokas.
Mike understands hard work.
He knows when it is time to roll up his sleeves and get down to work.
As our voice at the Capitol, Mike has been a champion for Southwest Georgia.
Mike puts people above politics and delivers real results for our families.
As our State Representative, Mike has led to keep taxes low and reduce red tape to energize our economy.

Mike never forgets his constituents and works tirelessly every single day to deliver to make our lives better and our future brighter.
