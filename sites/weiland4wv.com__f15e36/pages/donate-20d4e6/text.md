Your contribution helps us reach more voters, share our message, and fight for policies that put West Virginians first — from supporting our first responders to investing in education and families.
Every donation, big or small, makes a difference.
West Virginia law requires all campaign donations to be reported to the Secretary of State.
Contributions over $50 cannot be made in cash, and donations totaling more than $250 must include the contributor’s name, address, occupation, and employer.
State law also limits individual contributions to $2,800 per election (primary and general) to a candidate’s committee.
Our campaign follows these laws carefully because transparency and accountability matter.
Every contribution helps us connect with voters and build a campaign focused on West Virginia families — and every dollar is reported as required by state law.
