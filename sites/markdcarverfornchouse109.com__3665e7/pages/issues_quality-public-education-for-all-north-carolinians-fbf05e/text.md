Quality Public Education for all North Carolinians
Programs such as the voucher system put our public schools in danger of falling to the wayside.
Every single child and young adult in North Carolina should be guaranteed access to a quality education regardless of their family's income.
If elected, I will strive to:
- End the voucher system and the destructive effect it has on our public schools.
- Seek higher pay for North Carolina educators.
- Institute curriculum that rewards reading and critical thinking skills over rote memorization.
