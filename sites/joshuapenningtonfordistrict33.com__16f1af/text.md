Joshua's Main Priorities
Growing up in a family of volunteer firefighters, Joshua has firsthand knowledge of the value that our town's volunteer firefighters and other first responders provide to our communities.
Not only do they protect us and ensure our safety, but they also bring our communities together, provide much needed resources, and are the beating-hearts of the small towns we live in and love.
If elected he will seek to ensure that the needs of our first responders are brought to the attention of the Legislature.
Joshua believes that establishing more funding and allocated resources will be key to ensuring that our first responders have what they need for our communties.
In addition, he will support legislative effort to further recognition and support(s) where also needed.
