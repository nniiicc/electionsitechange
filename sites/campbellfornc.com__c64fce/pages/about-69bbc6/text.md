MEET GRANT CAMPBELL Grant Campbell Representative for N.C.
House District 83 "Conservative Champion for Cabarrus & Rowan County" Born and raised in North Carolina by a single mother, Grant Campbell learned the value of hard work, perseverance, and personal responsibility at an early age.
His family sometimes struggled to make ends meet, and Grant came to understand that education offered the surest path to a better future.
He went on to attend the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where he was a Dean’s List student and earned his undergraduate degree.
While at Carolina, Grant discovered his calling in medicine and met his wife, Anita.
He later earned his medical degree from UNC, receiving multiple awards for academic excellence and leadership and being elected student body president by his classmates.
Grant and Anita have two adult children.
Following the terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001, Grant answered the call to serve his country as a physician in the United States Army.
He completed three tours in the Middle East, including one with U.S.
Special Forces, and ultimately retired from military service as a lieutenant colonel.
After returning home, Grant resumed the private practice of medicine while remaining actively involved in serving fellow veterans and helping low-income patients obtain the medical care they need.
Before his election to the North Carolina House of Representatives, Grant served as vice chairman of the N.C.
State Board of Community Colleges and was frequently consulted by state lawmakers on healthcare and veterans’ policy.
He has also served on the boards of the UNC General Alumni Association, The Independence Fund, and the Interpreting Freedom Foundation and has been an active volunteer in the Republican Party. ​ In 2024, the people of Cabarrus and Rowan counties elected Grant to represent N.C.
House District 83.
Drawing on his experience as a physician, veteran, and community leader, he has worked to strengthen North Carolina’s healthcare workforce, support veterans and first responders, protect taxpayers, improve public safety, and hold government accountable. ​ Grant and his family are once again answering the call to serve.
He is running for re-election to continue providing conservative, common-sense leadership for the people of House District 83.

About Grant Campbell for N.C.
House Born and raised in North Carolina by a single mother, Grant learned early the value of hard work and education.
He earned both his undergraduate and medical degrees from UNC-Chapel Hill before answering the call to serve after 9/11 as a U.S.
Army physician.
Grant completed three tours in the Middle East and retired from the Army as a lieutenant colonel before returning to private medical practice and serving his community and fellow veterans.
Elected to the North Carolina House of Representatives in 2024, Grant proudly represents the people of Cabarrus and Rowan counties in House District 83.
He is running for re-election to continue delivering conservative, common-sense leadership for his community.
