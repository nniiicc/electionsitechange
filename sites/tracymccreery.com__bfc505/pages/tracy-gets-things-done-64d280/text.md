She is committed to addressing the challenges facing our state with members of both parties.
But she’ll never back down from the needs facing seniors, working families and consumers.
Improving Education Tracy McCreery believes every child deserves a world class public education.
Tracy has worked to fully fund our public schools, reduce class sizes, and increase teacher pay.
Tracy has: Fought for and passed an additional $330 million for school transportation to fully fund our school transportation system since 1991.
Fought to fund our schools by fully funding our Foundation Formula when most politicians wanted to strip funding from our public schools.
Increased Teacher pay from $25,000 to $38,000 while fighting for a higher starting salary.
Revitalized a career ladder where teachers can earn additional compensation based upon the number of hours of work that is done outside of contracted time.
Consumer Protection, Advocacy, and Freedom Tracy McCreery has always been an advocate for families and consumers.She believes every Missourian should have the freedom and information we need to make our own decisions without being taken advantage of.
As your Senator, Tracy insisted on utility consumer protections including increasing the Hot or Cold Weather Rule from 24 to 72 hours and created a cap on residential PISA rate charges.
Tracy has: Updated consumer protections for utility service customers, including strengthening protections for Missourians facing utility shutoffs.
Fought against utility rate increases.
Led a bipartisan effort to eliminate tax subsidies for Stan Kroenke.
Sponsored the COOL Act (Country of Origin Labeling Act) so we knew what country our meat is from.
Led the charge for the ‘Right to Repair’ which allows you to fix your smartphones and electronics instead of the big tech companies telling you who you can choose to repair your device.
Sponsored legislation against restricted tickets which prohibits the purchaser of tickets from reselling or transferring tickets that they had bought.
Updated the Circuit Breaker Tax Credit to help seniors and Missourians with disabilities manage rising property taxes and stay in their homes.
Increased funding for Missouri’s Affordable Housing Assistance Tax Credit Program to expand affordable housing opportunities for working families.
Sponsored the Sewer Lateral Fee Fairness Bill which would ensure people in parts of St.
Louis County are not being overcharged for sewer lateral insurance.
Sponsored a tax credit bill that allowed those who choose to purchase an electric vehicle to be eligible for a tax credit.
Sponsored a bill to ban surprise medical billing.
Affordable and Accessible Healthcare Tracy McCreery believes all Missourians deserve access to quality healthcare and is a leading advocate for Medicaid expansion in Missouri.
Tracy stood up to politicians trying to cut funding for health care—money that would expand local care, keep hospitals open, and create good-paying healthcare jobs.
Tracy has: Led the charge and won to fully fund MO HealthNet (Medicaid) in 2022.
Improved health insurance coverage for mammograms and breast health exams, helping ensure earlier detection and better outcomes.
Co-created the “Earned Family and Medical Leave Act” which provides family and medical leave to Missourians.
Passed long-acting reversible contraception transfer reform which reduces government waste and allows for inventory to be held by prescribers for same-day insertion.
Sponsored a bill to ban surprise medical billing.
Led the charge on prescribing contraceptives for 13 months to help patients avoid a lapse before they can see prescriber again.
Sponsored modernization of Missouri’s outdated and stigmatizing HIV laws.
Sponsored the ban on so-called conversion therapy for LGBTQ+ kids.
Serves on the MO HealthNet Oversight Committee as a liaison between the legislature and the Medicaid program.
Reduced barriers for first responders and law enforcement when responding to opioid-related overdoses, helping save lives during emergencies.
Passed legislation that added proceeds from the opioid settlements with pharmacies to the Opioid Addiction Treatment and Recovery Fund.
Designated the first Saturday of October as “Breast Cancer Awareness Day.” Passed legislation to help address Missouri’s shortage of audiologists and speech-language pathologists.
Implemented Medicaid coverage for hearing aids and cochlear implants, expanding access to critical hearing care.
Protecting Our Most Vulnerable From her time as a community volunteer and now a legislator, Tracy McCreery has always fought for those who don’t have powerful lobbyists.
Tracy has: Sponsored a bill that would close the deadly domestic violence loophole created when Missouri became a constitutional carry state.
Sponsored the Missouri Nondiscrimination Act (MONA) which bans employers from firing someone, denying housing, or refusing basic services because they are a part of the LGBTQIA+ community.
Led the charge to limit foreign corporate ownership of agricultural land.
Sponsored a bill to address confidentiality for human trafficking survivors.
Passed $105 MILLION to the FY 2023 budget to provide essential services for Missourians.
Sponsored payday loan reform to reduce interest to 36% from 400+%.
Created the Stop Cyberstalking and Harassment Task Force to help law enforcement prevent cyberstalking and harassment while supporting survivors.
Raised the legal age to marry to 18 for everyone in Missouri, helping protect children from forced marriage.
Increased the Champion for Children Tax Credit to provide additional support for families caring for vulnerable children.
Streamlined public assistance applications for SNAP, TANF, and child care assistance, making it easier for families to access the help they need.
Accomplishments, Recognitions, and Community Volunteer Positions Tracy McCreery has earned the recognition of many community groups throughout the state.
Tracy’s Service and Recognition: Family Care Health Centers Board Member Olivette in Bloom Volunteer (Fundraiser for Community Garden) Missouri Community College Association Distinguished Legislator Award Friend of Corn Award from the Corn Growers Association Safe Connections Volunteer and Board Member (reducing the incidence and impact of sexual assault and relationship violence) Conservation Missouri House Legislator of the Year in 2020 Missouri Public Utilities Alliance Public Service award Big Sister with Big Brothers and Big Sisters Interfaith Legal Services for Immigrants Legislative Award PROMO Equality Advocate Award House Democratic Co-Chair Friends of the United Kingdom Caucus House Democratic Co-Chair Sportsmen’s Caucus Public Safety and Criminal Justice Reform Tracy McCreery works with Republicans and Democrats to improve public safety and pass meaningful criminal justice reform.
Tracy has: Passed an Anti-Shackling Law that bans women from being shackled while pregnant or giving birth.
Advocated and passed a law to assure prison nurseries will allow a mother to have more time with their baby.
Passed funding to allow inmates to have access to menstrual hygiene products.
Sponsored the Geriatric Parole Bill which specifies that any incarcerated offender 70 years of age or older who has no prior felony conviction of a violent nature will receive a parole hearing.
Passed Valentine’s Law, legislation that makes it a felony to flee from police.
Valentine’s Law is named in honor of the late St.
Louis County Police Detective Antonio Valentine, who was killed in a crash while responding to assist other officers.
Secured $50 million to help build the new St.
Louis County Police Center, supporting the officers who keep our communities safe.
Strengthened prosecutors’ ability to investigate and prosecute sex crimes, helping deliver justice for survivors.
