“She’s made a tremendous difference in her first term in the Senate.
I’m all in for Tracy.” Tracy McCreery has always put people over politics.
She was first elected to the House of Representatives as an Independent and has continued her tradition of putting her constituents above partisan politics.
As your State Senator, she focuses on making real change that you can see and feel.
Fighting for Better Education Tracy McCreery believes every child deserves access to a high-quality public education, regardless of their ZIP code.
In the Missouri Senate, she has fought to fully fund public schools, increase teacher pay, support career and technical education, and secure significant investments for institutions like St.
Louis Community College to expand educational opportunities.
Tracy knows that investing in students, supporting educators, and strengthening our public schools is one of the best ways to prepare the next generation for success and build a stronger Missouri.
Supporting our Police and Fire Fighters Tracy McCreery has earned the endorsement of the St.
Louis County Police Officers Association, the Missouri State Council of Fire Fighters, and other first responder organizations because they know she stands with those who put their lives on the line to protect our communities.
In the State Senate, Tracy has worked to strengthen public safety by passing Valentine’s Law to crack down on dangerous high-speed police pursuits, securing millions in funding for the St.
Louis County Law Enforcement Center and law enforcement wellness initiatives, and supporting the tools first responders need to do their jobs safely and effectively.
Tracy believes safe communities start by supporting the men and women who serve, investing in public safety, and working together to keep Missouri families safe.
Affordablity Missouri families are feeling the pressure of rising costs, and Tracy McCreery is focused on lowering everyday expenses and helping people keep more of what they earn.
She has led efforts to expand property tax relief for seniors and people with disabilities, strengthen consumer protections against rising utility costs, increase affordable housing opportunities, and create lower utility rates for qualifying low-income households.
Tracy believes government should make life more affordable for working families by putting people first, protecting consumers, and fighting for practical solutions that lower costs.
Access to Quality and Affordable Healthcare Tracy McCreery believes every Missourian deserves access to affordable, high-quality health care, regardless of where they live or how much they earn.
In the State Senate, she has championed legislation to expand postpartum Medicaid coverage for new mothers, improve access to hearing aids and cochlear implants, strengthen breast cancer screenings, support rural hospitals, reduce prescription drug barriers, and protect health coverage for people with disabilities who want to work.
Tracy will continue fighting to lower health care costs, protect access to care, and ensure Missouri families can get the treatment they need close to home.
Protecting our Seniors Tracy McCreery believes older adults deserve the opportunity to age with dignity, independence, and financial security in the communities they call home.
That’s why she successfully led efforts to modernize Missouri’s Circuit Breaker tax credit by increasing income eligibility, raising the maximum benefit, and tying both to inflation so nearly 300,000 seniors and people with disabilities can receive meaningful property tax relief for years to come.
Tracy has also fought to protect consumers from unfair utility rate increases and lower energy costs because no senior should have to choose between paying their bills and staying safely in their home.
Supporting Common Sense Gun Laws Tracy McCreery believes Missouri families deserve safe neighborhoods, safe schools, and leaders who are willing to take commonsense action to reduce gun violence.
She has worked to strengthen laws that keep firearms out of the hands of dangerous individuals, protect survivors of domestic violence, and support law enforcement’s efforts to remove illegal guns from our communities.
Recognized as a Gun Sense Candidate by Moms Demand Action and endorsed by Missouri’s law enforcement community, Tracy will continue bringing people together to advance practical, bipartisan solutions that protect both public safety and constitutional rights.
Protecting Women’s Healthcare Choices Tracy McCreery believes every person deserves the freedom to make their own health care decisions with their family and doctor, without interference from politicians.
A longtime advocate for reproductive freedom who previously worked for Planned Parenthood, Tracy has fought to protect access to comprehensive reproductive health care, defend the will of Missouri voters following the passage of Amendment 3, and oppose efforts to roll back those rights.
She will continue standing up for women’s health care, protecting access to medically accurate information and essential services, and ensuring personal medical decisions remain where they belong—with patients, not politicians.
Supporting Working Families Tracy McCreery believes Missouri’s economy is strongest when working families have the opportunity to earn a good paycheck, receive quality benefits, and retire with dignity.
As the daughter and granddaughter of Teamsters, she understands the value of hard work and has consistently stood with union workers by fighting against so-called “Right to Work,” protecting prevailing wage laws, defending collective bargaining rights, and supporting policies that create good-paying jobs.
