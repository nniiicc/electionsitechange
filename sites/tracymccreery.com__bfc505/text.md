Fighting for Access to Healthcare
Tracy McCreery believes every Missourian deserves access to affordable, high-quality health care, regardless of where they live or how much they earn.
In the State Senate, she has championed legislation to expand postpartum Medicaid coverage for new mothers, improve access to hearing aids and cochlear implants, strengthen breast cancer screenings, support rural hospitals, reduce prescription drug barriers, and protect health coverage for people with disabilities who want to work.
Tracy will continue fighting to lower health care costs, protect access to care, and ensure Missouri families can get the treatment they need close to home.
