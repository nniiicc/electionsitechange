The Platform
Fully fund Public Schools
Our public schools are under attack - with reduced funding, bad teacher: student ratios, and rising property taxes to fund these schools
Our state isn't paying their fair share.
We need to expand school funding, end school vouchers to keep $ in our public schools and create universal Pre-K for all of our kids.
Lower grocery & utility costs
Our grocery and electric bills keep going up.
The reason?
Grocery monopolies that don't support farmers and data centers being built.
Our State Legislature is bought out by corporate interests.
We must make sure data centers & corporations pay their own bills - instead of putting that cost onto US.
We deserve healthcare
SW Ohio is home to the best medical care facilities in the country.
But hardworking Ohioans are one medical emergency away from bankruptcy.
Insurance companies must publish denial rates and premiums.
Drug prices should be capped by production cost to ensure fair profits and patient affordability.
End Corruption
Ohio's State Legislature just gave a $4,500,000 tax break to a data center that will create only 10 jobs - instead of towards our roads and schools.
Politicans are wasting your hard-earned tax dollars.
We deserve politicans who want to solve OUR problems first.
