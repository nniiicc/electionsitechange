DONATE Press Releases Tricia Nakamatsu Wins Primary Election for Hawaiʻi State Senate District 13 August 8, 2026 Today, Tricia Nakamatsu released the following statement after election results confirmed her victory in the Democratic primary race for Hawaiʻi State Senate District 13: “I am deeply honored and humbled by the trust the people of our district have placed in me.
Mahalo to every voter, volunteer, supporter, and member of our community who believed in this campaign and made tonight possible.
I also want to thank my family for their unwavering love, encouragement, and dedication throughout this campaign.
Your support has meant everything to me.
“This campaign has always been about the people of our communities and the future we want to build together.
As a fourth-generation member of this community, a working mom, a prosecutor, and a public servant for the past 20 years, I understand the challenges our families face—from the rising cost of living to concerns about public safety, housing, and creating opportunities that allow our children to build their futures here in Hawaiʻi.
“My work experience and life experience have reinforced my belief that lasting progress happens when we listen to one another, work collaboratively, and stay focused on practical solutions instead of politics.
I am grateful for the opportunity to move forward as our party's nominee, and I look forward to continuing to earn the trust and support of community members across District 13 as we head toward the general election.
Mahalo!” Senate District 13 encompasses Dowsett Highlands, Pu‘unui, Nu‘uanu, Pacific Heights, Pauoa, Punchbowl, Pālama, Liliha, Iwilei, Chinatown, and Downtown.
The seat was previously held by Karl Rhoads, who decided not to seek reelection.
Tricia Nakamatsu Officially Enters Race for Senate District 13 May 29, 2026 Yesterday, Tricia Nakamatsu officially filed to run for the Hawai‘i State Senate in District 13, launching a campaign rooted in community, public service, and a commitment to local families.
Senate District 13 encompasses Dowsett Highlands, Pu‘unui, Nu‘uanu, Pacific Heights, Pauoa, Punchbowl, Pālama, Liliha, Iwilei, Chinatown, and Downtown.
“As a fourth-generation resident of this district—with my children representing the next generation—my family’s roots here run deep, and my commitment to this community is lifelong.
I am running for State Senate because I believe we deserve government systems that actually work for the people, ” said Tricia.
Proudly representing five generations of Chinese, Japanese, and Okinawan heritage in Hawai‘i, Tricia has dedicated the past 20 years of her career to public service, serving as a Deputy Corporation Counsel, Deputy Prosecuting Attorney, and currently as a Deputy Attorney General.
Throughout these experiences, Tricia tackled complex legal and legislative issues with an emphasis on collaboration, thoughtful policymaking, and strengthening our local communities.
“Over the past two decades, I have served the people of O‘ahu and Hawai‘i with diligence, integrity, and perseverance—as a public servant and a professional,” continued Tricia.
“I’ve spent my career working within our systems to make our communities safer and government more responsive.
I’m running for State Senate to provide that same proactive, professional representation to District 13 and communities across the state.
Whether it’s expanding access to mental health or substance abuse treatment for those who need it, making our government more accountable and accessible to the people, or ensuring our roads are safe for everyone, especially our keiki & kupuna, I believe we need proven leadership that delivers results.” “I’d like to thank Senator Rhoads for his years of dedicated service to the families of District 13.
I’m excited to officially file as a candidate in this race and continue his work as a champion of public safety and advocate for our community.
It would be an honor and a privilege to serve in this capacity, and I invite all those who call District 13 home to join me as we work together to implement effective, compassionate, long-term solutions that allow our families to thrive.” Paid for by Friends of Tricia Nakamatsu P.O.
