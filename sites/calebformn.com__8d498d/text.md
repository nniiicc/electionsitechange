About Me Priorities  Affordability Minnesota has one of the highest tax burdens for individuals and businesses, which forces citizens to make difficult financial decisions daily.
We must create solutions that lower taxes on gas, license tabs, and everyday items.  Safety for All As an officer in the Minnesota National Guard, I understand the importance of rushing to aid those in need.
It is crucial to provide our police officers and first responders with the necessary support and resources to ensure the safety of our community.  Environmental Resources This outdoor enthusiast believes clean water, air, and energy are key to preserving our beautiful Minnesota natural resources.
We need affordable solutions that preserve our beautiful outdoors for future generations.
Investing in new and innovative technology to promote affordable renewable resources is crucial for maintaining the land of 10,000 lakes.  Education As a teacher, father, and graduate of a Minnesota Public School, I know that not all schools in Minnesota have the same needs.
Minnesota’s test scores are plummeting, indicating that our one-size-fits-all approach to funding schools isn’t effective.
We need to fully fund education with fewer mandates which will give educators control over how to achieve results in their schools.  Mental Health As a teacher and a soldier who sees firsthand the effects of mental health on our most vulnerable children and our fighting men and women in uniform, I can tell you we need a stronger solution.
Our school counselors are working tirelessly and they are burning out risking some schools to go without the much-needed resources.
We need to leverage support from our communities.
Hundreds of untapped people could listen, love, and discern in our schools and our ranks.
Let us contract the talent of our community to work for our fellowship.
Together we can learn resilience and overcome.
