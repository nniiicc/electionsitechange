SO DOES HOW WE ENFORCE IT.
The recent ICE activity across the South Coast deserves a clear and measured response.
I believe in the rules of law.
Immigration laws should be enforced, and our communities should be safe.
But enforcement must also be lawful, fair, and humane .
When an immigration violation is a civil matter rather than a criminal offense , we should expect a process that respects due process and treats people with dignity.
Families should not be unnecessarily traumatized, children should not be left wondering where their parents are, and people should not be targeted because of the color of their skin, ethnicity, accent, or perceived national origin.
No one should be above the law.
But no one should be beneath it either .
We can enforce our immigration laws without abandoning compassion.
We can protect public safety without racial profiling.
And we can demand accountability without treating entire communities as suspects.
Massachusetts law already makes clear that racial and other profiling has no place in law enforcement, and state law places limits on state and local participation in federal civil immigration enforcement.
As your State Representative, I will stand for equal treatment under the law, due process, public safety, and basic human dignity .
Law and compassion are not opposites.
