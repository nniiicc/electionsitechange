Contribute
By law, the maximum amount an individual may contribute is $1,000 ($2,000 per couple) per year.
The law requires us to ask for your employer and occupation.
If you don't have an employer or are retired, put N/A, and if you are self-employed, put "self-employed" in the employer and describe your occupation.
By clicking "Donate," I certify that the following statements are true and accurate:
- I am at least 18 years of age and I am a United States citizen or a lawfully admitted permanent resident of the United States ("green card holder").
- This contribution is made from my personal funds and is not made from the funds of a corporation, labor organization, partnership, LLC, out-of-state PAC, or another person or entity.
I will not be reimbursed by anyone for this contribution.
This contribution is made using my personal credit card or debit card for which I have the legal obligation to pay, and is not made using the credit card or debit card of a corporation, labor organization, or another person or entity.
My contributions to the Pacheco Committee will not aggregate in excess of $1,000 ($2,000 per couple) per calendar year ($100 per calendar year for lobbyists).
By providing your phone number, you are consenting to receive calls and texts, including auto-dialed and automated calls and texts, to that number from the Pacheco Committee.
If you would like to contribute via check, please send your contribution to the :
Pacheco Committee
188 Hixville Rd
Dartmouth, MA 02747
Contributions to the Pacheco Committee are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes.
