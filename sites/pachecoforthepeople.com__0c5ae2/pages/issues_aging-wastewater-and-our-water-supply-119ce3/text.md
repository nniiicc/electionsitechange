I will work to support funding on a state/federal level for construction of whatever Dartmouth needs to ensure we have Safe waste water facilities that do not endanger our soil or water tables.
I will also work to secure funds to make sure that Dartmouth ALWAYS has clean, safe drinking water.
Currently our town buys water from our neighbor, New Bedford.
We sadly have 6 of our wells down since 2020 due to contamination.
A proposal from our DPW , would require funds to create a pipeline to Fall River to purchase water from them if needed.
If other reasonable alternatives are not available , I will fully support our DPW's efforts on this matter, to the best of my ability.
