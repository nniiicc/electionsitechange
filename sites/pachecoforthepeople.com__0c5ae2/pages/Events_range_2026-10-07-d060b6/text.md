Home Issues 2026 Ballot Questions Positions Meet Ed Yard Signs Fundraiser Events Contribute Volunteer Professional Endorsement Voter Endorsements News Photos Contact Endorsements Events The Dartmouth Democratic Town Committee will host a Candidates Night for the Massachusetts House 9th Bristol District race on Tuesday, August 11, at 6:00 p.m. in the Select Board Room (Room 304) at Dartmouth Town Hall.
Doors will open at 5:00 p.m.
The event will feature Democratic candidates seeking to represent the 9th Bristol District in the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
This seat will be a vacant seat for the first time in over 14 years!
Ed will participate in the forum and welcomes the opportunity to continue to speak directly with residents about the future of the district, the priorities facing local communities, and the issues affecting families throughout Dartmouth and New Bedford.
The forum is designed to give voters the opportunity to hear directly from the candidates, learn about their positions on key issues, and gain a better understanding of their vision for the 9th Bristol District and the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The evening's program will include: Candidate introductions and opening statements Questions focused on priorities for the communities of the 9th Bristol District Discussion of statewide issues affecting Massachusetts residents Audience questions Candidate-to-candidate questions The event will also be recorded and broadcast by Dartmouth Community Television, giving residents across the district the opportunity to watch and stay informed.
Community members are encouraged to attend, engage in the discussion, and learn more about the candidates seeking to represent the 9th Bristol District .
