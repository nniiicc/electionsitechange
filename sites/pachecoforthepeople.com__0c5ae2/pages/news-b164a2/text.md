Press Release:
Pacheco Committee 9th Bristol District State Representative Candidate.
Regarding Dartmouth Weekly Omission of Facts
It has come to my attention that a recent news article incorrectly listed only two candidates for the open 9th Bristol District State Representative seat.
My name is Edward Pacheco, and I am also a candidate for this seat, running as a moderate Democrat.
I have proudly lived in our community for over 20 years.
I am a small business owner and have worked to mentor and support other local businesses in my role as an ambassador with the One SouthCoast Chamber of Commerce, where I was honored as Ambassador of the Year in 2022.
I have also served our community as an evening security officer at Dartmouth High School.
Many residents have likely seen me walking the halls or attending Friday night football games, helping to ensure a safe environment for students, families, and visitors.
I invite voters to learn more about my campaign by visiting Pacheco for the People on Facebook, with my campaign website launching in the coming weeks.
It is unsettling and disappointing when media outlets rush to publish a story without all of the facts.
This will be a long campaign season, with meaningful discussion and debate about the direction each candidate believes the role of State Representative should take, and all perspectives deserve to be fairly and accurately reported.
This is not, by any measure, a two-person race.
The deadline for residents of the 9th Bristol District to submit nomination papers is April 28, and we are still in February.
Filling the seat that Chris Markey has admirably held for more than a decade deserves thoughtful, complete, and responsible coverage.
There will be a primary.
There will be discussion and an exchange of ideas.
There are multiple candidates.
I respectfully call on the news outlet to retract the incomplete article, conduct proper due diligence, and repost an accurate version.
Thank you,
Ed Pacheco
Democratic Candidate for
9th Bristol District State Representative
