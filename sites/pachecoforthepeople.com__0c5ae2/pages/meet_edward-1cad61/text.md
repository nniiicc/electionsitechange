Meet Edward
Edward “Ed” Pacheco: A Bridge Builder for the 9th Bristol District
For over 20 years, Ed Pacheco has lived in Dartmouth.
As a youth, Ed grew up in New Bedford and remembers his roots.
As a husband, a father of six daughters, a homeowner, and a small business owner, Ed doesn't need a briefing on the struggles of the 9th Bristol District.
He lives them, side by side with you, every day.
A Record of Service and Safety Whether he’s managing complex transportation logistics for South Coast residents with disabilities or supporting local entrepreneurs as the One Southcoast Chamber’s 2022 Ambassador of the Year nominee, Ed’s career is defined by service.
Many of you know him better as the man in the "Green D" cap.
As the Night Security Officer at Dartmouth High, Ed didn't just protect the building; he built bonds with the PTO, the Band, and the Theater families, ensuring our children were safe and our community felt heard.
Common Sense Representation: The 9th Bristol District faces a crossroads: an aging wastewater system, a growing crisis in educator retention and recruiting, and the rising pressure of 40B developments.
Solving these problems requires more than a party-line vote; it requires a bridge builder.
Ed is headed to Beacon Hill to be a moderate, balanced voice who puts results over rhetoric.
He isn't interested in political games—he’s interested in the people.
