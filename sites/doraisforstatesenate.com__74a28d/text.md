Let Families Keep More of What They Earn
Connecticut families work hard for their money.
They should not have to watch more of every paycheck disappear into taxes while state government continues to grow.
Earlier this year, Senate Republicans proposed a major affordability package focused on permanent tax relief and greater fiscal discipline.
The plan included a $1 billion working- and middle-class income tax cut, with relief targeted toward families and individuals who need it most.
It would also effectively eliminate the car tax for many working families through a refundable income-tax credit while protecting municipal revenue.
The plan would also:
- Cap annual municipal property-tax levy increases at the greater of 2% or inflation, while allowing voters to approve exceptions for major projects.
- Eliminate occupational licensing fees for professions including electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, teachers and real estate professionals.
- Reduce Connecticut's Paid Family Leave payroll tax by 20%.
- Rein in the growth of government spending and demand greater accountability for taxpayer dollars.
- Target waste, fraud and abuse before asking taxpayers for more.
Connecticut doesn't need another temporary rebate or election-year gimmick.
Families need lasting relief and a state government that treats every tax dollar as carefully as Connecticut families treat their own money.
