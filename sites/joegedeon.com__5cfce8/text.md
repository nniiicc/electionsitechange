Get Involved Support the Campaign Meet Joe Gedeon Joe Gedeon was appointed in 2025 to represent House District 18, serving the communities of Hawai'i Kai, Kalama Valley, and Portlock.
As co-founder of JPG Hawaii and JPG Media, Joe brings decades of marketing and business leadership experience to the State Legislature.
Born and raised in Hawaiʻi Kai where his family has lived for 45 years, Joe is deeply committed to serving his community and fighting for the issues that matter most to District 18 residents.
