These digital tools are designed to put the oversight of our community back into your hands. ​ Practical Tools for an Informed & Efficient Community ​A smarter government requires an engaged citizenry.
These digital resources allow you to bypass the red tape, hold departments accountable, and take direct action to maintain our infrastructure and safety. ​Direct Oversight: HNL 311 ​The HNL 311 system is your direct line to city accountability.
Instead of calling a general hotline and waiting on hold, this app puts the power of a "work order" in your hands. ​Snap & Pin: Take a photo of the issue (pothole, illegal dumping, broken sign).
The app uses GPS to tag the exact coordinates so crews can’t miss it. ​Direct-to-Dept Routing: Your report bypasses the middleman and goes straight to the specific department responsible (Facility Maintenance, Parks & Rec, etc.). ​Duplicate Prevention: View the live map to see if an issue has already been reported.
This prevents the government from wasting tax dollars on double-entry work. ​Real-Time Tracking: Create a profile to receive notifications when your report is "Assigned" and "Resolved." ​Access the Portal: www.hnl311.com Skyline Tracker: Impact & Accountability ​Whether you support the project or not, the construction of the Honolulu Rail (Skyline) is directly impacting our district’s small businesses, traffic, and daily life.
We provide these links so you can monitor the use of your tax dollars and stay ahead of construction delays. ​Real-Time Traffic Alerts: Don't get stuck in Dillingham gridlock.
Access the latest lane closures and utility relocation schedules before you leave the house. ​Business Support (Shop & Dine on the Line): Our local District 13 businesses are the backbone of the 'Ohana.
Use this tool to find and support shops that are currently impacted by the Segment 3 construction. ​HART Accountability: View the latest fiscal reports and project timelines to ensure the "Quality over Quantity" standard is being met. ​ Access the Rail Portal: www.honolulutransit.org ​ HNL.Info: Real-Time Transparency ​Information is the first line of defense.
The HNL.Info app provides direct alerts from the city and county without the filter of traditional media. ​Emergency Alerts: Get immediate notifications on public safety, weather, and traffic. ​Government Transparency: Access news releases and official announcements directly from the source. ​Stay Informed: Knowledge of current events in our district ensures you aren't caught off guard by bureaucratic changes. ​ Access the Portal: www.hnl.info ​Hawaii State Public Library System ​Individual liberty is built on a foundation of self-education.
The library system is a vital resource that directly supports the quality and growth of the individual. ​Digital Access: Use the app to browse the entire state catalog, reserve books, and access research databases from your phone. ​Resource Management: Manage your account and educational resources without needing to step into a government office. ​Lifelong Learning: Accessing information is the key to staying competitive and informed in a free-market economy. ​ Access the Portal: www.librarieshawaii.org ​ TheBus: HELE ​Infrastructure is a core responsibility of government.
The HELE app ensures that the transportation systems we pay for are functioning with operational excellence. ​GPS Tracking: See exactly where your bus is in real-time. ​Time Efficiency: Don’t waste time waiting at a stop; use the data to manage your schedule with precision. ​System Accountability: Use the data to see if our public transit is meeting the standards of a high-quality, efficient government. ​Access the Portal: www.thebus.org DONATE TO THE PEOPLE'S CAMPAIGN!
