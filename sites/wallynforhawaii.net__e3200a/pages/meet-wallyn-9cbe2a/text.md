I was born and raised on Oʻahu, the youngest of four, and raised by my grandmother, who gave us strength, love, and strong values that still guide me today.
I am a proud mother of four and a grandmother—“Puna”—to eight moʻopuna.
Like many local families, mine is spread between Hawaiʻi and the mainland due to the high cost of living, but we stay closely connected.
I was educated in Hawaiʻi’s public schools and earned degrees from Hawaiʻi Pacific University and Life Christian University in Justice Administration, Business Management, and Theology.
Balancing family responsibilities and a full-time career, I worked hard to pursue higher education and build a strong foundation for service and leadership.
My commitment to public service led me to run for public office in 2022 and 2024.
Those experiences strengthened my understanding of the challenges facing our communities and reinforced my desire to serve the people of Hawaiʻi.
Since 2024, I have proudly served on the Neighborhood Board, where I continue to advocate for residents and work alongside community members to address local concerns.
I am also actively engaged in the legislative process, regularly providing testimony at both the city and state levels in support of the rights, needs, and priorities of our community.
With that same commitment to service, I am now entering the race for State Senate in 2026 to continue being a voice for the people and to help advance practical solutions that strengthen our communities and improve the quality of life for Hawaiʻi's families.
Service has always been a guiding part of my life—whether supporting local families, caring for our kupuna, standing up for our keiki, advocating for animal welfare, or engaging in community advocacy.
I remain committed to standing up for our people and working together to build a stronger future for Hawaiʻi.
