Chaunté Gilmore is a Director of Rehabilitation and community leader rooted in northwest Oklahoma City with deep ties to Bethany and Warr Acres.
For her, service to others is more than a job, it's her passion.
Each day, she works directly with patients and families, helping them recover from injury, hold on to their independence, and navigate systems that are often feel stacked against us.
She knows what happens when systems fail and leave people behind.
But the issues don’t end with healthcare.
Our State systems are also failing us in housing, education, data privacy, job opportunities, and cost-of-living.
Chaunté has seen how all of it connects and impacts those of us just trying to get by.
For Chaunté, leadership starts with listening to voters and representing our interests, not special interests.
