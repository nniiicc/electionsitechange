For her, service to others is more than a job, it's her passion.
Each day, she works directly with patients and families, helping them recover from injury, hold on to their independence, and navigate systems that are often feel stacked against us.
She knows what happens when systems fail and leave people behind.
But the issues don’t end with healthcare.
Our State systems are also failing us in housing, education, data privacy, job opportunities, and cost-of-living.
Chaunté has seen how all of it connects and impacts those of us just trying to get by.
For Chaunté, leadership starts with listening to voters and representing our interests, not special interests.
About Chaunté Issues After years of status quo, northwest Oklahoma City isn’t any healthier, smarter, or safer.
Chaunté listens to us and has a plan to raise our standard of leadership. learn more about Our Campaign's Top Issues Hold Big Pharma Accountable Raise Literacy & Math Skills Property Tax & Home Insurance Relief Protect Your Data Privacy A More Affordable Economy Commited to Public Safety Hold Big Pharma Accountable Chaunté has built her career in healthcare , caring for patients and leading teams across northwest OKC.
She’s seen what happens when profit margins become more important than your provider's recommendations for your health.
Chaunté is focused on expanding access, strengthening our healthcare workforce, and fighting Big Pharma's hold on the industry.
Raise Literacy & Math Skills Chaunté believes every child deserves a high-quality public education, no matter their zip code.
Public education should prepare kids with the skills they need for success, and under status quo leadership, student literacy and math skills have increasingly deteriorated.
Chaunté supports empowering educators by investing in the classroom resources needed to build student skills, as well as increasing teacher pay so that educators can remain focused on their passion instead of worrying about inflation.
Property Tax & Home Insurance Relief Cost-of-living is out of control, and the status quo has done nothing to address it.
Chaunté supports increasing the Homestead Exemption to provide relief for working and retired families in way that doesn't hurt our public school funding.
Protect Your Data Privacy Between the rise in data centers and surveillance pricing, it feels like Big Tech is watching our every move.
Chaunté will work toward accountability for AI, more responsible data center management, and keeping your privacy safe from government and corporate overreach.
A More Affordable Economy As your bills keep inflating, Chaunté is working to increase access to good-paying jobs in a tough economy.
She supports workforce training programs, career pipelines, and economic development strategies that bring new industries and opportunities to the state.
Commited to Public Safety Safe neighborhoods are part of what helps a community and its economy thrive.
I've seen firsthand how much is asked of first responders and how they show up for us in difficult moments.
Chaunté will listen to first responders and support the pensions they've earned.
What you need to know about my opponent's record.

Voted AGAINST banning pedophiles from marrying children.
