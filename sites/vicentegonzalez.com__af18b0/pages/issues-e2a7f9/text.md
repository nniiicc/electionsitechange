Affordability
Families across South Texas are feeling the pressure of rising everyday costs.
From groceries and housing to energy and transportation, increased expenses have made it harder for working families to stay financially secure.
Vicente Gonzalez believes that people who work hard should be able to afford a stable and dignified life.
Addressing affordability means focusing on practical solutions that reduce financial strain and help families keep more of what they earn.
The impact of rising costs goes beyond household budgets.
It affects education, healthcare decisions, and long-term financial planning for families throughout the district.
Vicente is committed to supporting policies that promote economic stability, protect working families, and ensure South Texans are not left behind as costs continue to rise.
