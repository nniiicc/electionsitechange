Congressman Vicente Gonzalez is the only candidate in this race who was born and raised in the district.

His mother told him education would open doors, so after dropping out of high school, he earned his G.E.D., worked his way through community college and university, and went on to earn his law degree.
02.
Vicente believes immigrants play a key role in our economy and that the reckless deportations of workers in key industries, like construction, farming, hospitality, has threatened our economy.

In Congress, Vicente has spent his career fighting for working families and forcing Washington to deliver for South Texas.

Vicente has always fought for a strong South Texas economy.
He’s worked across the aisle to protect our trade relationship with Mexico that supports 301,000 good-paying jobs along the border, and he led the fight to save 2,500 manufacturing jobs in Corpus Christi.

Vicente knows every young person in South Texas deserves the chance to build a life here, whether they go to college or not.
