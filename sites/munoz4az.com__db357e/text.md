I'm focused on expanding opportunity and protecting our community." Meet betsy muñoz Betsy Muñoz is a proud Latina who was born and raised in Arizona, in the cities of Tolleson and West Phoenix.
She is the daughter of immigrants and a product of our local public schools.
Muñoz graduated from Arizona State University with a Bachelor of Science in Public Service and Public Policy where she has worked for education nonprofit organizations to increase college access for all students.
Muñoz coaches school staff from multiple schools across the West Valley on how to better support and prepare our students for their college and career journey, and she assists students with college applications, scholarships, and FAFSA.
Muñoz's experience in education has fueled her passion to run as she has witnessed the gaps in our public education system and its effect on our communities.
Her aim is to ensure our students, families, and community are at the forefront of conversations at the state legislature.
