Five steps to cast your ballot
Your guide to voting in Arizona
Each step below links to official state and county resources.
These tools are provided by the Arizona Secretary of State and the Maricopa County Recorder.
Check Your Voter Registration
Confirm you are registered and that your address, precinct, and party are current before Election Day.
The process takes less than a minute.
Find Your Polling Location
See your assigned polling place and its hours for Election Day, or locate an early voting center near you.
Request an Early Ballot
Vote from home at your own pace.
Request a ballot to be mailed to you and return it by mail or at an official drop-off location.
Join the Active Early Voting List (AEVL)
Automatically receive a ballot by mail for every election — formerly the Permanent Early Voting List (PEVL).
Not sure where to start?
Begin by confirming that your registration is current.
Then make a plan to vote and share it with family and friends.
