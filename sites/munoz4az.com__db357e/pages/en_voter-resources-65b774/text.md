Register to Vote or Update Your Registration New to voting or moved recently?
Register online in a few minutes, or update your name, address, and party.
The registration deadline for the 2026 general election is October 5.
Register or update Check Your Voter Registration Confirm you are registered and that your address, precinct, and party are current before Election Day.
The process takes less than a minute.
