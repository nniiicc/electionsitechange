I believe strong systems prevent crises, thoughtful policy creates opportunities, and informed advocacy can turn complex laws into meaningful change for people.
Caring without action is not enough; to truly care is to listen deeply, connect people and systems, and do the hard work of working towards real solutions.
Connecticut residents pay too much for electricity.
We need to balance our clean energy goals with smart grid investments.
- Find more equitable ways to pay for public benefits
- Make sure that the upcharge on infrastructure projects isn’t too excessive for ratepayers
- Make our grid more efficient to lower the cost of delivery to customers
- Keep affordable energy as an option as we transition to green energy
- Diversify our energy production portfolio to drive down the overall cost of our supply

Senior Affordability
Connecticut seniors are being priced out of the towns they helped build.
We need to protect fixed incomes through targeted tax relief, expanded housing options, and investments in home care.
- Expand eligibility and credit caps for the state’s senior property tax relief "circuit breaker" program
- Preserve and expand state tax exemptions on Social Security, pension, and retirement distributions
- Increase state funding for accessible, downsized senior housing and independent living communities
- Protect and fully fund the Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders (CHCPE) so seniors can age in place safely
- Provide tax credits for family caregivers paying out-of-pocket for home modifications and health support
Education
As an educator endorsed by labor unions, I know firsthand that investing in public education from early childhood through post-secondary learning is the single most powerful tool we have to strengthen Connecticut's workforce, support working families, and build an equitable economy.
- Modernize the Education Cost Sharing (ECS) foundation grant to keep pace with real inflation and deliver funding directly to local school classrooms
- Protect and expand state funding for early childhood education, universal Pre-K, and after-school programs to support working parents
- Fully reimburse municipalities for special education excess costs to ensure every student receives individualized support without squeezing local school budgets
- Elevate the teaching profession by addressing teacher shortages, lowering student-to-educator ratios, and protecting fair collective bargaining rights
- Fully fund higher education pipelines through state universities, community colleges, and debt-free tuition initiatives like PACT to train the next generation of local workers
