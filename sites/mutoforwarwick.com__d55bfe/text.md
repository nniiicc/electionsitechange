A Personal Message from Bill
Dear Neighbor,
My name is Bill Muto and I am running to be your State Representative for District 23.
I grew up right here in Warwick — in Buttonwoods — and this community has shaped everything about who I am and what I believe in.
My parents worked hard to give our family a better life, and they taught me the values I carry into public service every day: family, hard work, integrity, and giving back.
My wife Joanne, a teacher at Pilgrim High School, and I chose to raise our children here because we believe in Warwick’s future.
I want to hear from you.
What are the issues that keep you up at night?
What does a better District 23 look like for your family?
Together, we can build it.
With gratitude,
Bill Muto

Legislative Priorities
Where Bill Stands on the Issues Impacting District 23
Bill Muto believes every family in District 23 deserves a representative who fights for them every day.
Here's where Bill stands — no spin, no political lingo, just action.
Public Education & Schools
Warwick's public schools are the foundation of our community's future.
Bill will fight to give every child — from pre-K through graduation — access to the resources they need.
- Fully fund Warwick public schools and eliminate the funding gap
- Oppose unfunded mandates that pressure local budgets
- Expand universal pre-K access for working families.
- Support teacher recruitment, retention, and fair compensation
Healthcare & Affordability
No one in District 23 should have to choose between paying rent and paying for medicine.
Bill will make healthcare accessible and affordable for every family.
- Protect and strengthen Medicaid coverage
- Cap prescription drug costs and support pharmacy transparency
- Expand community mental health services and reduce wait times
- Support rural and community health clinic funding
Housing & Affordability
Rhode Island's housing crisis is hitting District 23 hard.
Bill will work to make Warwick a place where working families can afford to live and stay.
- Expand affordable housing units in Warwick and across Rhode Island
- Streamline permitting to accelerate housing construction
- Protect renters from unfair eviction and price-gouging
- Create first-time homebuyer programs
Local Economy & Jobs
District 23 is built by small business owners, tradespeople, and working families.
Bill will fight to keep Warwick's economy strong and growing for everyone.
- Support small business development and lending access
- Fight for living wages that reflect the real cost of living
- Invest in job training programs tied to in-demand careers
- Partner with local employers to create good-paying local jobs
Environment & Climate
Warwick's 39 miles of coastline are a treasure.
Bill will protect Narragansett Bay, fight for clean energy, and ensure District 23 is resilient in the face of climate change.
- Protect Narragansett Bay water quality from pollution and runoff
- Invest in clean energy infrastructure and local renewable energy
- Strengthen flood resilience for coastal Warwick neighborhoods
- Oppose industrial development that threatens our shoreline
Public Safety
Safe neighborhoods are the foundation of strong communities.
Bill believes in supporting our first responders while building community trust that makes everyone safer.
- Fund and support Warwick Police, Fire, and EMS departments
- Expand community policing and neighborhood engagement programs
- Support smart, evidence-based criminal justice reform
- Invest in addiction recovery and mental health crisis response
Seniors & Veterans
The people who built this community and defended our country deserve our full support.
Bill will fight to protect and honor District 23's seniors and veterans.
- Protect Social Security and Medicare from cuts
- Eliminate the Rhode Island veterans' pension income tax
- Expand veterans' services, housing assistance, and mental health support
- Invest in senior centers, elder care, and Meals on Wheels
Transportation & Infrastructure
District 23 residents deserve roads that work, transit that runs, and streets safe for everyone.
Bill will prioritize real infrastructure investment.
- Secure state funding to fix Warwick's worst roads and bridges
- Expand public transit options and RIPTA service frequency
- Improve bike lane and pedestrian safety infrastructure
- Advocate for fair distribution of state transportation dollars
