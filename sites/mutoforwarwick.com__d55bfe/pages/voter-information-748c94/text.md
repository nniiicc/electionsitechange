Yes!
Update your voter registration address online at vote.sos.ri.gov.
In Rhode Island, you may also update your address at your polling place on Election Day.
Q: What ID do I need at the polls?
Rhode Island requires a photo ID such as a driver's license, state ID, passport, or student ID.
If you don't have a photo ID, you may cast a provisional ballot.
Q: Can I vote if I have a felony record?
In Rhode Island, you may vote if you are not currently incarcerated for a felony conviction.
Your voting rights are restored upon release from prison.
Q: Can I vote early in Rhode Island?
Yes, Rhode Island offers early voting and mail ballot options.
Check the RI Secretary of State website for the exact schedule and locations.
