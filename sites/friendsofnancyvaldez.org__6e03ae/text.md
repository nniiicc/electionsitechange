Like many local families who call Hawaiʻi home, I know firsthand how challenging it can be to make ends meet.
As a single mother of four wonderful boys, I worked a full-time job and two side jobs to provide for my family.
Trying to balance work, family responsibilities, and being there for every one of my sons’ school and sporting events was not easy.
But like so many parents, I did everything I could to support my children and give them the best opportunities possible.
Sadly, many local families are facing the same struggles today.
The high cost of living and limited opportunities have forced some of our loved ones to move to the mainland in search of more affordable living and a better future.
Two of my own sons have made that difficult decision.
No family should have to choose between staying in Hawaiʻi and being able to afford a decent life.
That is why I am running for office.
I want to fight for our working families, our kūpuna, and everyone who wants to continue calling Hawaiʻi home.
Our people work hard, yet the rising cost of housing, groceries, utilities, and taxes continues to put enormous pressure on household budgets.
I believe our residents deserve more than just getting by.
They deserve the opportunity to live, work, raise their families, and retire with dignity in the place they love.
As your State Representative for District 31, I will work to be a voice for the people, advocate for responsible government, and push for solutions that make Hawaiʻi more affordable for the generations who call these islands home.
Together, we can work toward a Hawaiʻi where our children can afford to stay, our families can thrive, and our kūpuna can enjoy the retirement they deserve.
It doesn't matter what party you are.
What matters is who will be your voice and fight for you.
I understand that if I am elected that you will be the one to ask me the questions that matters to you the most.
I am running because I have a deep passion for the people of Hawaiʻi and believe true leadership begins with listening.
Our community deserves a representative who is accessible, accountable, and committed to hearing directly from the people.
Too often, residents feel heard during election season but forgotten afterward.
My commitment is simple: listen to the community, understand the challenges we face, and work toward real solutions.
THE ISSUES THAT MATTER: AFFORDABILITY Hawaiʻi families are struggling with the rising cost of groceries, utilities, healthcare, and everyday necessities.
We need solutions that help local families continue to live and thrive in Hawaiʻi.
HOUSING Our children and grandchildren deserve the opportunity to live, work, and raise their families here.
We must prioritize practical housing solutions for local residents.
GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY We need term limits for all elected officials to bring fresh perspectives, encourage new leadership, and give more citizens the opportunity to serve their communities.
Our government should represent the people, not allow political power to remain in the same hands indefinitely.
The people deserve transparency and accountability.
You deserve to know how your tax dollars are being spent and have a representative who listens to your concerns.
PUBLIC SAFETY Safe communities matter.
We must work together to protect our families, seniors, children, and neighborhoods.
TRAFFIC & INFRASTRUCTURE Transportation and development decisions directly affect our daily lives.
Our communities deserve responsible planning and a voice in decisions that impact our neighborhoods.
PROTECTING HAWAIʻI'S FUTURE We must protect our communities, resources, and opportunities so future generations can continue to call Hawaiʻi home.
OUR KEIKI We need to provide healthier, more nutritious meals in our schools and teach financial literacy to help our children become financially responsible, informed, and productive citizens who are prepared for the future.
OUR KŪPUNA Our kūpuna deserve dignity, respect, and affordable living.
We must protect their well-being, support their needs, and ensure they can enjoy their golden years here in Hawaiʻi, surrounded by the families and communities they love.
MY PROMISE TO YOU I WILL LISTEN.
Your voice matters.
I WILL BE TRANSPARENT.
You deserve to know what your representative is doing.
I WILL BE ACCESSIBLE.
Your representative should be available year-round, not just during election season.
I WILL WORK FOR YOU.
Public service means putting the people first.
TOGETHER, WE CAN MAKE A DIFFERENCE.
This campaign is about bringing a stronger voice for District 31 to the State Capitol.
I look forward to hearing your concerns, learning from your experiences, and working alongside our community.
I humbly ask for your support and your vote.
VOTE NANCY VALDEZ STATE HOUSE REPRESENTATIVE • DISTRICT 31 Listening to the People.
