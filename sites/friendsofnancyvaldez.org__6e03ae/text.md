It doesn't matter what party you are.
What matters is who will be your voice and fight for you.
I understand that if I am elected that you will be the one to ask me the questions that matters to you the most.
I am running because I have a deep passion for the people of Hawaiʻi and believe true leadership begins with listening.
Our community deserves a representative who is accessible, accountable, and committed to hearing directly from the people.
Too often, residents feel heard during election season but forgotten afterward.
My commitment is simple: listen to the community, understand the challenges we face, and work toward real solutions.
THE ISSUES THAT MATTER: AFFORDABILITY Hawaiʻi families are struggling with the rising cost of groceries, utilities, healthcare, and everyday necessities.
We need solutions that help local families continue to live and thrive in Hawaiʻi.
HOUSING Our children and grandchildren deserve the opportunity to live, work, and raise their families here.
We must prioritize practical housing solutions for local residents.
GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABILITY The people deserve transparency and accountability.
You deserve to know how your tax dollars are being spent and have a representative who listens to your concerns.
PUBLIC SAFETY Safe communities matter.
We must work together to protect our families, seniors, children, and neighborhoods.
TRAFFIC & INFRASTRUCTURE Transportation and development decisions directly affect our daily lives.
Our communities deserve responsible planning and a voice in decisions that impact our neighborhoods.
PROTECTING HAWAIʻI'S FUTURE We must protect our communities, resources, and opportunities so future generations can continue to call Hawaiʻi home.
MY PROMISE TO YOU I WILL LISTEN.
Your voice matters.
I WILL BE TRANSPARENT.
You deserve to know what your representative is doing.
I WILL BE ACCESSIBLE.
Your representative should be available year-round, not just during election season.
I WILL WORK FOR YOU.
Public service means putting the people first.
TOGETHER, WE CAN MAKE A DIFFERENCE.
This campaign is about bringing a stronger voice for District 31 to the State Capitol.
I look forward to hearing your concerns, learning from your experiences, and working alongside our community.
I humbly ask for your support and your vote.
