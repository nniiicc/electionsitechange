Lori has called the 51st District home for over 6 years (Illinois for 30 years) and is currently raising her two daughters.
She knows the struggles families face and why so many are frustrated with Springfield.

Experience that belongs in Springfield Lori did not start in politics.
She started with students.
As a teacher, she worked with children who needed more time, more skill, and more advocacy.
As a director of special education and student services, she ran programs, managed staff, followed the law, and made sure families were not left to fight the system alone.
As a superintendent, she led at the district level — setting direction, protecting students, and answering for how the money was spent.
She also ran a therapeutic day school that helped children with severe disabilities gain the skills they need to thrive.
That work taught her the same lesson she will take to Springfield: if you do not protect kids and you do not watch the dollars, you are failing the public.
