Meet the Candidate Meet Tracy Robinson Tracy Robinson is a small-town girl from Madison, Florida — and proud of it.
She grew up in the heart of North Florida's rural communities, where neighbors knew each other by name, church bells rang on Sunday mornings, and a handshake still meant something.
But her story is far more than small-town roots — it is a life of breaking barriers, serving others, and building things that matter.
A Legacy of Firsts Tracy is the proud Daughter/Niece of the late Marvin "Pete" Mobley — a towering figure in Madison, Florida history and the county's first Black police officer .
His courage, his pioneering spirit, and his love for this community live on in everything Tracy does.
She does not just carry his name — she carries his mission.
Tracy is a proud graduate of Florida A&M University , where she developed a deep, lifelong passion for public service.
During her time in Tallahassee, she also volunteered with the FSU Media Department , gaining hands-on experience in communications and community engagement.
In her younger years, Tracy graced the runway as a model for Marsha Doll Models in Tallahassee, Florida , was a proud runner-up for Miss Madison County , and competed as a preliminary contestant in the Miss America Pageant — a testament to her grace, poise, and the quiet confidence of a woman who has always known how to carry herself with dignity.
Closer to home, Tracy built a remarkable academic foundation at North Florida College in Madison.
She was a proud member of the NFC Sentinel Singers and was among the first graduates to earn a degree in Criminal Justice Technology at the college — a trailblazing milestone that reflects her drive to lead where others had not yet gone.
She went on to complete the Police Academy at North Florida College , earning her law enforcement credentials and bringing both education and experience to her service. 🚨 Featured Service — State of Florida Emergency Management Coordinator Florida Division of Emergency Management (FDEM) Tracy served as an Emergency Management Coordinator for the State of Florida , deployed as an independent contractor through ACDC to the Florida Division of Emergency Management (FDEM) .
She was called to the front lines during two of Florida's most catastrophic back-to-back disasters — Hurricanes Helene and Milton , which struck the state within days of each other in one of the most devastating stretches in Florida's modern history.
Tracy stood in the gap — coordinating relief, supporting impacted communities, and serving Floridians when they needed it most.
Technology & Innovation AI Engineer & AI Engineer in Medicine Tracy is an AI Engineer and AI Engineer in Medicine trailblazer — designing and building software programs, mobile applications, and artificial intelligence solutions that serve real people with real needs.
She has graduate-level advanced training in AI in Medicine through the University of Louisville School of Engineering and School of Medicine — one of the first programs of its kind in the nation — continuing to advance her expertise at the intersection of technology, healthcare, and human impact.
Tracy also built FocusedPrayer.com — a prayer app born from her deep faith, designed to encourage people to embrace intentional, focused prayer in their daily lives.
She has worshipped at churches in Madison, Monticello, and Lamont, Florida — singing in the choir and building bonds of faith and fellowship throughout District 9.
Tracy has watched the weight of neglect hollow out the small towns she loves — crumbling homes, shuttered businesses, struggling schools, and aging neighbors left behind.
She refuses to accept that as the final chapter.
Madison, Jefferson, and Leon Counties are lands where many of America's early leaders lived, worked, and helped shape this nation.
This region carries a proud piece of the foundation of the United States.
As America celebrates her 250th birthday, let us rise together, rebuild our communities, restore our pride, and show the nation that we still have what it takes to lead, work, and thrive.
Our best days do not have to be behind us — they can begin again right here at home.
