Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal Endorses Ed Markey for Senate

BOSTON, Mass. — Today, Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal (WA-07), Chair Emerita of the Congressional Progressive Caucus, endorsed Senator Ed Markey for re-election.
Jayapal and Markey are longtime legislative partners: Markey is the lead Senate author of the Trans Bill of Rights, which Jayapal leads in the House, and the two reintroduced the AI Civil Rights Act together this Congress to root out algorithmic discrimination in housing, job employment, health care, and every aspect of daily life.
“Working families are getting squeezed from every direction – rent, groceries, child care, health care – and they deserve a Senator who wakes up every single day fighting to change that.
Ed Markey is that senator,” said Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal.
“Ed always meets the moment and when human rights are under assault, Ed is always there – he is the lead Senate author of our Trans Bill of Rights, and when we saw algorithms deciding who gets a job, a home, or health care, he took on the harms of artificial intelligence as the Senate author of the AI Civil Rights Act.
Ed is the only progressive in this race, and he is fighting for working families with everything he has.
He is a great partner in the Senate on every one of these fights, and I am proud to endorse him for re-election.”
Jayapal, who represents Washington’s 7th Congressional District, built the Congressional Progressive Caucus into a bloc representing nearly half of the House Democratic caucus.
Her endorsement adds one of the nation’s most prominent progressive voices to the coalition backing Markey’s re-election.
“Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal’s endorsement means so much to me, because she knows what it means to always fight for working families and marginalized communities – she does it every single day,” said Senator Ed Markey.
“Pramila and I wrote the Trans Bill of Rights together, because trans rights are human rights.
We introduced the AI Civil Rights Act together, because no algorithm should ever decide whether a person gets a home, a job, or health care.
I am grateful for Pramila’s friendship, her partnership, her endorsement, and together we are going to keep fighting for the working families of Massachusetts and this country."
