Congresswoman Katherine Clark Endorses Ed Markey for Reelection
September 11th, 2025
BOSTON — Congresswoman Katherine Clark (MA-05), Minority Whip of the U.S.
House of Representatives, today endorsed Senator Ed Markey for re-election in a new video in which she highlights his commitment to the issues that families across the country and in Massachusetts are focused on, including health care, housing, and. making everyday life affordable.
“When Congresswoman Clark speaks, people listen,” said Markey.
“In the toughest fights and at the highest levels of Congress, it is Katherine Clark’s powerful voice that carries the needs, hopes, and dreams of Massachusetts families.
From universal health care and childcare, to parental leave, to reproductive freedom, Congresswoman Clark fights for the policies and programs that create opportunity, equality, and a brighter future for children, students, and families.
I am honored that she represents me in Congress, and I am proud to have her endorsement for reelection.”
