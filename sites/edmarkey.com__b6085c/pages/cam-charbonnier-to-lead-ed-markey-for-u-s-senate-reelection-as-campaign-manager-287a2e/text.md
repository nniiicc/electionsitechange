Cam Charbonnier to Lead Ed Markey for U.S.
Senate Reelection as Campaign Manager
July 22, 2025
BOSTON — Senator Ed Markey today announced that Cam Charbonnier will be managing his reelection campaign for the U.S.
Senate.
Charbonnier is a Boston-based Democratic strategist with more than a decade of experience leading high-profile campaigns and advising elected officials at every level of government.
He began his political career on Marty Walsh’s 2013 mayoral campaign, later serving the Walsh administration at Boston City Hall for seven years.
In 2020, Cam served as the Massachusetts State Director for the Biden for President campaign.
“Cam is the best person to lead our grassroots campaign of big issues, forward-looking ideas, and Massachusetts values,” said Markey.
“His experience managing campaigns, building teams, and understanding the issues that matter most to voters reflect the leadership, know-how, and vision he has to lead this campaign to victory.
I'm thrilled to have Cam join as campaign manager.”
“Massachusetts deserves a U.S. senator who doesn’t just talk about our values—but shows up, every single day, ready to fight for them, and that’s Ed Markey,” said Charbonnier.
“He’s taken on corporate polluters, fought to defend reproductive freedom, and gone head-to-head with Donald Trump and the far-right extremists trying to dismantle our democracy.
He’s never backed down—and he never will.
I’m proud to lead this campaign to keep Ed Markey in the U.S.
Senate where he belongs, fighting for the people of Massachusetts.”
Today’s announcement comes on the heels of endorsements from dozens of federal, state, and local leaders, including Senator Elizabeth Warren, Congressman Richard Neal, Congresswoman Lori Trahan, and more.
