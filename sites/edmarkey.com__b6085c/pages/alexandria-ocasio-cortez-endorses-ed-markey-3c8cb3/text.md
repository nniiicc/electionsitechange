Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez Endorses Ed Markey
AOC backs longtime legislative partner and proven progressive champion
August 20, 2026
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Thursday, August 20, 2026
Media Contact:
Jonathan Ng

BOSTON, Mass. – Today, Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (NY-14) endorsed Senator Ed Markey for re-election to the U.S.
Senate, backing her progressive legislative partner as a proven champion for working families.
“At a time when powerful special interests are making life harder for everyday Americans, Massachusetts working families need a champion in the U.S.
Senate to stand up for them and fight corporate greed,” said Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.
“Ed Markey has spent his career doing exactly that, from our partnership on the Green New Deal to running his campaign without accepting a dime of corporate PAC money.
His work is an inspiration to many of us who are committed to building a better future for all, and that’s why I’m proud to endorse him.”
Senator Markey thanked Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez for her endorsement.
“The toughest fights need the most inspiring leaders, and that’s Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.
She’s got vision, she knows what’s at stake, and she never backs down,” said Senator Ed Markey.
“We introduced the Green New Deal to tackle the climate crisis, lower costs, and build an economy that works for working people, not corporations and billionaires.
I am incredibly proud to have her endorsement and look forward to continuing to fight alongside her for the livable future we deserve.”
Markey and Ocasio-Cortez first introduced the Green New Deal in 2019, laying out a national mobilization to confront the climate crisis while creating millions of good-paying jobs, strengthening economic security, and advancing environmental justice.
