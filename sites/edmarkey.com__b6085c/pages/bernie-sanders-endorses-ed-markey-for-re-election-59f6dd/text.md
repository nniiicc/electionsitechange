Senator Bernie Sanders Endorses Ed Markey For Re-Election
June 29, 2026
BOSTON, Mass. — Today, U.S.
Senator Ed Markey announced he received the endorsement of Senator Bernie Sanders (I-VT) in his re-election campaign to the United States Senate.
For decades, Senators Markey and Sanders have stood shoulder-to-shoulder in the fight to take on corporate power, strengthen workers’ rights, combat climate change, and build an economy that works for working people—not billionaires and special interests.
Together, they have championed Medicare for All, led efforts to raise wages and expand workers' rights, challenged corporate greed in health care and private equity, and advanced bold action to confront the climate crisis through the Green New Deal and other transformative policies.
In announcing his endorsement, Senator Sanders said:
“Ed Markey is one of the strongest progressive voices in the United States Senate.
He has been an ally in taking on the billionaires, Big Tech, Wall Street and Private Equity as they continue to try to take over every aspect of our lives and further corrupt our government.
“Ed comes from the working class, and he understands the needs of working families in Massachusetts and throughout the country who are struggling to afford the basic necessities - health care, housing, education, groceries and utilities.
That's why he is my partner in leading the fight to win Medicare for All, to ensure that every person earns a living wage, and that we transform our rigged economic and political systems so that they work for ordinary Americans.
“Ed has a clear track record.
He knows which side he's on.
He will stand up to powerful corporate interests and fight for working families.
“I am proud to endorse Ed Markey in his reelection campaign for the U.S.
Senate."
Senator Markey thanked Sanders for his support and highlighted the importance of continuing their shared fight for working families:
“Bernie Sanders has spent his career speaking truth to power and standing up for working people against entrenched corporate interests.
I am honored to have his endorsement,” said Senator Ed Markey.
“Bernie and I have fought together for Medicare for All, for workers' rights, for climate action, and for an economy that puts people ahead of corporate profits.
At a time when working families are being squeezed by rising costs and billionaire special interests are exerting more influence than ever, we need leaders who are willing to take on powerful interests and deliver real results.
That's exactly what Bernie has done throughout his career, and it's what I will continue to do for the people of Massachusetts.”
Sanders joins a growing coalition of progressive leaders, labor unions, grassroots activists, and elected officials supporting Markey's re-election campaign.
His endorsement underscores Markey's record as one of the Senate's leading progressive voices and a champion for working families, climate action, health care as a human right, and economic justice.
