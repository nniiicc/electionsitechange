Congressman Jim McGovern Endorses Senator Ed Markey for Reelection
July 31ST, 2025
BOSTON— Highlighting his commitment to the working families of Central Massachusetts, Congressman Jim McGovern (MA-02) today endorsed Ed Markey for reelection to the U.S.
Senate.
The Massachusetts legislators, who served together in the U.S.
House of Representatives for 16 years, have secured millions in funding for housing, infrastructure, environmental protection, and education programs in Worcester and throughout Central Massachusetts and the Pioneer Valley.
Markey and McGovern continue to partner on legislation to protect global human rights and reduce the threat of nuclear war, and, most recently in April, traveled together with Congresswoman Ayanna Pressley (MA-07) to Louisiana to meet with Tufts University student Rümeysa Öztürk at an ICE detention facility where she was being held illegally without charge and in terrible conditions.
“Congressman Jim McGovern is the conscience of the Congress,” said Markey.
“His career-long advocacy for the most vulnerable in Massachusetts, to end hunger and poverty, and for human rights everywhere are an inspiration.
He has a vision for a more just and peaceful world that extends from a single mother in Worcester who relies on SNAP to the nations of the world where basic rights and freedoms must be defended.
He embodies a commitment to social justice, and I am proud to have his endorsement for reelection to the U.S.
Senate.”
