The Sergeants Benevolent Association has endorsed Mike Tannousis for State Assembly in the Republican Primary scheduled for June 23rd.
“As I’ve said before, law enforcement is about integrity.
There is no question that Mike Tannousis has the integrity to do the job and to support law enforcement – two things that are essential for those seeking to serve in Albany.”
– Ed Mullins, Sergeant’s Benevolent Association President
