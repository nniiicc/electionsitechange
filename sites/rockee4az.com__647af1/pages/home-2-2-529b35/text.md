We must make the right investments in Arizona’s students, educators and schools to ensure every student gets an excellent education.
Lowering Costs For Families And Seniors As the cost of housing, healthcare, and utilities continues to rise, Rockee believes we must take stronger action to keep life affordable for working families and seniors.
Rockee will fight to maintain a diverse energy supply to help keep utility rates down, protect healthcare access, and ensure that first-time homebuyers and seniors can afford housing.
Building A Stronger Economy For All Rockee believes a skilled workforce is essential to a thriving economy.
That’s why she supports ongoing retraining and education programs to help Arizonans compete in today’s global economy.
We must continue to make Arizona a great place for businesses to attract and keep jobs.
Public Safety This means collaborating with law enforcement and supporting local municipalities to develop common sense community safety legislation.
Protecting Arizona’s Long-Term Water Supply and Rebuilding Our Infrastructure Rockee believes we must make critical upgrades to our state’s infrastructure and protect our long-term water supply.
That means repairing Arizona’s aging transportation systems to reduce commute times and support the rapid growth in the East Valley.
