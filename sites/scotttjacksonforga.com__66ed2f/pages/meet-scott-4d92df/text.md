Leadership Rooted in Service
Scott T.
Jackson is a business leader and community advocate dedicated to empowering communities and creating opportunities for people to succeed.
With more than 15 years of experience in leadership development, operations management, and workforce advancement, Scott has built a career focused on helping individuals and organizations grow.
Throughout his professional career, Scott has developed leadership programs that support people with disabilities, older workers, and individuals from diverse backgrounds in attaining and maintaining leadership positions within organizations.
His work has centered on strengthening teams, developing talent, and creating pathways for long-term success.
Education
Scott holds a Master of Business Administration in International Business from Southern New Hampshire University, a Bachelor of Science in Technical Management and Entrepreneurship from DeVry University, and an Associate of Science in Business from Gwinnett College.
A Commitment to District 111
Scott believes effective leadership begins with listening to the community.
As a candidate for the Georgia House of Representatives, he is committed to bringing practical, thoughtful solutions to the issues that matter most to District 111 — including economic opportunity, strong schools, safe communities, and support for local families and small businesses.
Scott lives in Georgia with his wife and daughter and believes that strong communities begin with strong families, responsible leadership, and leaders who are willing to listen and serve.
