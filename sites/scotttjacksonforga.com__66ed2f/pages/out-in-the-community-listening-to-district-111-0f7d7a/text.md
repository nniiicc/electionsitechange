Out in the Community: Listening to District 111
One of the most important parts of this campaign has been getting out into the community and listening.
Over the past several weeks, I’ve spent time meeting with residents, visiting neighborhoods, and having real conversations with people across District 111.
These conversations are not scripted.
They are honest, thoughtful, and grounded in everyday experiences.
I’ve heard from parents who want better opportunities for their children.
I’ve spoken with small business owners who are working hard to grow but face real challenges.
I’ve connected with community members who want to feel seen, heard, and supported by their leadership.
These moments matter.
They shape how I think about the work ahead and reinforce why this campaign is so important.
Leadership is not about speaking at people.
It is about listening, understanding, and working together to find solutions.
Every conversation adds to a clearer picture of what our district needs.
And those voices will guide the decisions I make as your State Representative.
This campaign is not built in an office.
It is built in neighborhoods, on sidewalks, and through conversations with the people who call District 111 home.
