Over the past several months, I’ve spoken with families, small business owners, educators, and community members across our district.
While every story is different, the concerns are often the same.
People want opportunity.
They want stability.
They want leadership that understands what they are facing every day.
These are the priorities I will focus on as your State Representative.
Join Team Jackson Strengthening Education and Workforce Opportunities Every student deserves access to a quality education, and every adult deserves the opportunity to build a better future.
I will work to support our public schools, invest in workforce development programs, and expand access to training that prepares people for today’s job market.
Whether someone is entering the workforce for the first time or looking to advance their career, we need systems that support their success.
Supporting Small Businesses and Local Growth Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy.
I will advocate for policies that make it easier for small businesses to start, grow, and succeed.
That means reducing unnecessary barriers, supporting local entrepreneurs, and making sure our communities continue to attract investment and opportunity.
Expanding Access to Healthcare Access to affordable healthcare should not depend on your zip code.
I will work to improve access to care, support community health resources, and advocate for solutions that make healthcare more affordable for families across District 111.
Investing in Stronger Communities Strong communities are built through intentional investment.
I will support initiatives that improve infrastructure, strengthen public safety, and make sure every neighborhood in District 111 has the resources it needs to thrive.
Moving Forward Together These priorities reflect the real conversations I’ve had with people across our district.
This campaign is about building a future that works for everyone.
And that only happens when we work together.
