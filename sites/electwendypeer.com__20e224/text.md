Volunteer Donate Shop Merch Get Updates About Wendy Peer I’ve called Fort Smith home for more than 37 years after moving here from Missouri, and this is where my family has built our life.
I have four children, four grandchildren, and two great-grandchildren who call this area home.
Like so many families in our district, we work here, volunteer here, attend our excellent local public schools, and worship alongside friends and neighbors.
I’ve spent my career serving Arkansas families—20 years in radio and television and 15 years in public education.
I’ve also worked with local organizations advocating for students and educators because I believe strong public schools are the backbone of a strong community.
My experiences allow me to see firsthand the grit, determination, and resilience of Arkansas families—and the real challenges they face when decisions are made by out-of-touch politicians.
I’m running for State Representative to deliver practical, people-focused progress on the issues we talk about every day—at the coffee shop, on the sidelines, and around the kitchen table: good jobs, honest government, and a fair shot for every child in our public schools.

Arkansas House – District 50 District 50 is the heart of Fort Smith.
Extending West to the state line, North to Park Avenue, East to I-540, and generally South of downtown, curving around South Zero Street.
This district is diverse in population and socio-economic status, serving as a vibrant mixed residential and business area.
Our district is home to critical community anchors, including both Mercy and Baptist Hospitals .
It is a center for excellence in education, housing schools such as Southside, Ramsey, Bonneville, Euper Lane, Raymond Orr, Fairview, and Ballman .
From the commerce centers of Central Mall, Target, and Walmart Neighborhood Market to our local financial institutions like Arvest, First National, and Regions Bank , District 50 drives the local economy.
We also take pride in our beautiful community spaces like Creekmore Park and the Hardscrabble Golf Course .
