About Dan
| | Dan grew up in Menomonee Falls and graduated from Menomonee Falls East High School.
While he was in high school he started his first small business, selling ice cream and other concessions out of a mobile vending truck.
Dan’s been a small business man his whole life.
He was most recently the owner and operator of Reef Point Resort on Pike Lake.
He currently lives in Germantown where he raised his four children and enjoys spending time with six grandchildren. |
