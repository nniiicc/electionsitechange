| I'm Dan Knodl, a small business owner, father, and grandfather who has called this community home my entire life.
I raised my kids here, and now I have the joy of watching my grandchildren grow up in the same wonderful place.
When I was 17, I launched my first small business in Menomonee Falls, selling concessions from a mobile vending truck.
That early experience taught me the importance of hard work and the value of a dollar.
These lessons have shaped my approach as your state representative in Madison.
My top priority is to ensure our communities are safe, thriving, and that state government works efficiently for you.
Through commonsense budgeting and cutting government waste, we’ve successfully reduced the tax burden on families and small businesses.
These efforts have allowed us to invest more in education, improve our roads, and lower healthcare premiums, among other achievements.
