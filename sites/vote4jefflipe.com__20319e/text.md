Jeff Lipe
A Different Choice for Southern Utah
If you’re like me, you’re frustrated that the Utah legislature is too preoccupied with their own interests to care about the needs and well-being of their voters.
They refuse to enact law that serves you and me, instead spending our precious tax dollars to pay themselves to create politically motivated messaging and policy that reflects a lack of compassion and common decency, and doing very little to actually make our lives any better.
It was out of that frustration that I decided to run for the Utah House of Representatives for District 74, which covers the Western part of St George, along with Ivins, Santa Clara, Bloomington, and Sun River.
I am not a politician.
But I am strongly motivated by a desire to do what’s right for the people, and to bring a constructive balance to an extremely one-sided legislature.
I think we deserve better from our state lawmakers.
If you agree, please check out my platform and join me to help make the Utah government work for all of us.
