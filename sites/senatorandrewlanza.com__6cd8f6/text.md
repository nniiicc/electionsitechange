SENATOR ANDREW LANZA
It’s been my honor and privilege to serve you in the New York State Senate.
I am proud of my accomplishments and I am truly humbled by the confidence you place in my leadership abilities in the Senate.
As always, my ultimate goal is to address the needs and concerns that matter to you, and to the people of our great community.
Please use this website and my Facebook page to learn more about what I’ve done during my time in the Senate, as well as to learn more about my future plans to continue enhancing the quality of life on Staten Island.
I truly enjoy representing you in Albany, I value the trust you have placed in me, and I look forward to continuing to fight for you and Staten Island.
