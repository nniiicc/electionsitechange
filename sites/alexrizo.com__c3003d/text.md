Rizo’s Platform
Education
- Support Parental rights in education and common-sense approaches to education, not indoctrination.
- Provided the largest expansion of school choice in Florida’s history.
- Supported voucher program that now provides $8,000 annually per child, for parents to take their children to the school that best meets their needs.
- Protects female athletes and the integrity of female sports.
- Repeatedly voted for record increases in public school funding, as well as an additional $2.5 billion for teacher pay raises.
- Reinstitute District Cost Differential in student funding to help our local public schools
- Expand Workforce Development and Technical programs in public schools.
- Passed numerous bills to eliminate bureaucracy, support teachers, protect students and enhance teacher professionalism and training
The Elderly
- Sponsored and brought funding to enhance quality of living for seniors.
- Lower costs of drugs through free-market principles of competition, reduce excess regulations, and pool buyer resources.
- Create health saving plans options.
The Economy
- Fight against tax and hidden fee increases.
- Voted for a record tax cut of $4.2 billion dollars, including permanent tax cuts on baby supplies like cribs, diapers and strollers.
- Voted for laws to eliminate property taxes for low-income seniors.
- Supported a $500 million dollar toll rebate program to reimburse Florida drivers who pay excess highway tolls.
- Sponsored and brought millions of dollars for drainage infrastructure to combat flooding.
- Support policies that keep Florida competitive in an ever-increasing technical world.
Conservative Values
- Co-sponsored Victims of Communism Day law.
- Supported the law that requires high school students to learn about the horrors and failures of communism.
- Sponsored legal protections for our police officers and has worked with local elected officials to ensure public safety in our neighborhoods.
- A champion for religious freedom by supporting additional protections for churches, synagogues and religious organizations to ensure their safety and practice their faiths without intervention from the government.
Insurance & Condo Law Reforms
To lower our property insurance costs, Representative Rizo supports embracing various policies, including:
- Creation of an Insurance Fraud Task Force to help root out corruption and fraudulent claims that drive up the cost of insurance.
- By lowering the cost of homes, we can lower the cost of homeowner’s insurance which can be accomplished by building more homes and lowering the costs of construction.
Cheaper houses = lower costs of insurance.
- Require independent estimates of home damages after a hurricane or storm to ensure insurance companies are paying the true value of the cost to repair.
- Require Big Insurance companies and lawyers to review and respond to homeowner claims faster and more efficiently.
Supports revising the new state condominium law after the Surfside Condo collapse to safeguard condo owners’ excessive assessment fees so that they may keep their homes while ensuring safety.
