A Personal Letter to District 9 My roots in East Springdale and District 9 began in 1978 when my grandfather bought a house just west of Old Missouri Road.
When he passed, my mother moved in, then my sisters, and when I retired from the Army in 2014, I bought the house and now have enjoyed 12 years as a member of the East Springdale community.
My 95 year old mother still lives with me and my wife.
As I have campaigned the last several months across District 9, I have met people of many backgrounds, white and black, hispanic, Marshallese, and Laotian (and I am sure there are some I have not yet met, but hope I will).
I have seen churches, schools and businesses, large and small, along with dozens of non-profit groups, all seeking to make our community a better place and provide resources for our residents.
As your State Representative, I pledge to represent “ALL” the residents and interests of East Springdale and to help coordinate between City, County and state levels of government.
I pledge to share information with you from the State Legislative level and to take your input and concerns with me as I travel to Little Rock.
Every person is important and I will make it my aim to be available to listen and attend to the concerns of District 9 to the best of my ability.
