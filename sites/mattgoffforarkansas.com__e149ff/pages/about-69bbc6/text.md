Home Meet Matt News Volunteer Donate Donate Meet Matt Matt Goff was born into a military family and spent his early years on the move.
He came to Northwest Arkansas in 1972, where he attended Fayetteville High School and graduated from the University of Arkansas in 1980 with a B.A. in Radio and TV Broadcasting.
After completing ROTC, he was commissioned as a 2nd Lieutenant in the United States Army.
Following a 30-year career, first as a Signal Officer and then as a Chaplain, he retired from the Army in 2014 and returned to NWA.
Matt has been married to his wife, Amy, for 44 years and together they have seven children and 22 grandchildren.
Matt runs his own business and performs disaster relief missions with various organizations, including 8 Days of Hope and the Arkansas Southern Baptist Disaster Relief teams.
Matt and his wife reside in Springdale and attend First Baptist Church in Fayetteville.
As a retired Soldier and now empty nester, Matt has the capacity and experience to represent the families and businesses of East Springdale (District 9) with the same dedication and selfless service that he provided to Soldiers and Families throughout his military career.
