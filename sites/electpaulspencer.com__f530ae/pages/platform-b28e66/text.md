New Mexico 1st Platform & Priorities Economic & Job Growth: I plan to stimulate economic growth by creating more job opportunities and supporting small businesses.
My economic policies aim to boost the local economy, providing a solid foundation for community prosperity.
I will support policies that prioritize American workers and businesses. ​ Protecting Our Border: By focusing on safety and securing our borders, we can create a more secure and equitable future for all New Mexicans. ​ Family Values & Parental Rights: I am pro-family and pro-life.
I will always support traditional family values and parental rights, and fight the war on children! ​ Supporting Veterans: I am dedicated to honoring and supporting veterans within the community.
My platform focuses on ensuring that veterans receive the resources and recognition they deserve for their service. ​ Transparency & Accountability: I pledge to maintain transparency and open communication with constituents.
I promise accountability in my actions and decisions, ensuring that the community is always informed and involved. ​ Education : I am committed to improving the quality of education, ensuring that every child has access to high-quality schooling and necessary resources.
My focus includes enhancing educational facilities and curriculums to better serve students.
Crime & Safety: I will focus on reducing carjackings, violent crimes, and auto theft, ensuring our safety and security. ​ 2nd Amendment Rights: The safety of our citizens is of utmost importance.
I am a strong supporter of the 2nd Amendment and will fight to uphold the rights of law-abiding gun owners.
I oppose the governor's "suspension" of the 2nd Amendment, and I will fight for the protection of our constitutional rights.
Election Integrity: Elections belong to citizens, not agencies, and I will work to protect the integrity of the voting process. ​ Transportation Freedom : I will stand against the mandated use of electric vehicles, protecting individual choice and freedom of movement in transportation options. ​ Local Infrastructure: I recognize the importance of a strong local economy; I will support local infrastructure development and business prosperity here in New Mexico.
This includes tracking and analyzing local infrastructure projects to better serve communities with affordable funding. ​ Healthcare: As a candidate for the New Mexico State House Representative, my top priority is the health and well-being of our citizens.
I will work to improve access to quality healthcare, address the lasting effects of the 2020 pandemic, and ensure that every New Mexico family has access to high-quality childhood education.
By prioritizing health, we can create a stronger, more resilient community for all New Mexico residents.
Environmental Concerns: I am an advocate for water and wastewater infrastructure investment to ensure clean and accessible resources for all communities as well as sustainable service practices and maintenance programs addressing water, forest overgrowth issues, and pseudo climate change.
I will fight against the United Nation environmental social government policies and Sustainable Development Goals that create discrimination exclusion through Indoctrination and that ultimately cause more harm to our environment.
This includes fighting against Environmental Social Governance (ESG) policies that threaten individual sovereignty. ​ A Stronger, Better New Mexico: I will tackle the lasting effects of the 2020 pandemic and work to build a resilient and sustainable future for New Mexicans!
PEACE & PROSPERITY: To ensure a prosperous and equitable future for all residents of New Mexico.
A vote for me is a vote for a better New Mexico.
If elected, I will fight to support the success, advancement, health, individual rights, freedom, sustainability, and equity of all New Mexicans.
I will also work to address systemic issues that disproportionately affect certain communities, such as poverty, discrimination, and environmental hazards. ​ Join me in fulfilling a prosperous and equitable future for all residents of New Mexico!
