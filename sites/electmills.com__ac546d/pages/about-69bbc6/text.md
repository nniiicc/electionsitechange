Michael authored the book "Battling Democracy's Decline " based upon his graduate research into the Civil Rights Movement and his civic service.
Audrey, Lewis and Michael enjoying the Finger Lakes Professionally, Michael is sought after for his public affairs, organizational management, executive leadership coaching, and grassroots organizing expertise.
He has owned Involvement, Inc., a communications and coaching company for the past decade, helping nonprofit and business clients engage stakeholders to deliver positive outcomes, and individuals hone their leadership skills.
He developed a deep reservoir of economic development and policy experience as an executive at multiple public relations firms, while also running regional public affairs for one of America’s largest corporations, and leading multiple civic and environmental nonprofits.

He moved to Atlanta for graduate school, earning a master's in communications from Georgia State University before “boomeranging” back to the Finger Lakes.
Upon returning he fell in love with a region that had grown so much during his time away yet retains its small town charm.
And he wants to lend his entrepreneur’s perspective and decades of experience tackling environmental, agriculture, healthcare, business, and community issues to protect our region and state's bright future.
Michael and his wife Audrey moved to the “Chosen Spot” of Canandaigua from Rochester in 2018 because of the community’s welcoming attitude, historic charm, access to the best of the Finger Lakes, and the jewel that is Canandaigua Lake.
The excellent school system became an additional benefit when they welcomed their son Lewis to the world in 2020.
