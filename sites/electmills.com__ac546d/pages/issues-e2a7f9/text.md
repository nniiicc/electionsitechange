I am proposing a Community Stewardship Standard for Infrastructure based on four principles.
1.
Community consent.
The people who live with the consequences should have the deciding voice.
2.
Developer responsibility.
Facilities should run on 100 percent renewable energy paid for by the companies, in addition to the necessary grid improvements, environmental protections and infrastructure their projects require.
Those costs should never be shifted onto taxpayers or ratepayers.
3.
Public transparency.
All decisions should be public before they're made.
4.
Lasting public benefit.
