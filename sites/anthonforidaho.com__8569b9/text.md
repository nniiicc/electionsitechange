What are the three most important issues to you and/or your family?
Working for you.
Working for Idaho. “ I was born and raised in the Burley area and am a seventh-generation Idahoan.
Joelle and I have been blessed to raise our kids on our family farm, and I’m determined to work hard and do my best for our community and the people of Idaho.
I believe in a life of service and in being a faithful steward of the trust and responsibility given to me by our voters.
Agriculture is the lifeblood of the Idaho economy and the foundation upon which we built our great state.
I will make sure we serve Idaho’s businesses, especially our farmers, ranchers, and food producers.
I will continue to be a voice for rural communities and make sure that policies in Boise represent and work for the people of District 27.
While the issues we grapple with can be heady, the first and last thing on my mind is, “is it good for Idaho families?” I believe strongly in protecting citizens’ Constitutional rights, our 2nd Amendment rights, and ensuring we protect our citizens’ freedoms to do what they want to do as they see fit - how we parent, our medical choices — and that small government is the best government.
We need strong voices and steady leadership in the Idaho Legislature.
