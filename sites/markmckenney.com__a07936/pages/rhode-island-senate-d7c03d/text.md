McKenney Committee Appointments Senator McKenney serves in leadership roles on several key Senate committees.
These appointments reflect the confidence of his colleagues and place him at the center of legislative oversight, judicial review, and education policy in Rhode Island.
Committee Appointments Senate Committee on Rules, Government Ethics & Oversight Chair Responsible for Senate procedure, ethics standards, and internal governance.
As Chair, Senator McKenney oversees matters that ensure transparency, accountability, and the integrity of legislative operations. ​ ​Senator McKenney sponsored and secured passage of reforms to strengthen procedural transparency and modernize public access to committee records.
