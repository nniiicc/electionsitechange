Senate unveils 2026 health care legislation
March 12, 2026 at 12:00:00 AM
Rhode Island Senate leaders unveiled a 17-bill healthcare package aimed at improving access to care, supporting individuals experiencing behavioral health crises, and strengthening the state's healthcare workforce.
Among the proposals is legislation introduced by Senator Mark McKenney to establish a commission that will study the impact of medical malpractice claims on healthcare costs, provider recruitment, and the long-term stability of Rhode Island's healthcare system.
Sponsors, advocates call on General Assembly to invest in multilingual education
March 25, 2026 at 12:00:00 AM
Senator Mark McKenney and Representative David Morales joined students, educators, and community advocates to support the Support and Access to Bilingual Education (SABE) Act, legislation designed to expand multilingual education opportunities in Rhode Island schools.
The proposal would create a state fund to support dual-language programs and help more educators obtain bilingual teaching certifications, addressing the growing needs of Rhode Island’s multilingual student population.
Senate passes McKenney resolution to create commission to study effect of medical malpractice on healthcare
May 21, 2026 at 12:00:00 AM
The Rhode Island Senate approved legislation introduced by Senator Mark McKenney to establish a commission studying the impact of medical malpractice claims on healthcare costs, provider recruitment, and access to care.
As part of the Senate’s broader healthcare package, the commission would bring together lawmakers, healthcare professionals, insurers, patient advocates, and other stakeholders to examine the issue and develop recommendations for improving Rhode Island’s healthcare system.

House OKs Rep.
Boylan bill to ban invasive plants
Legislation to prohibit the sale and distribution of invasive plants in Rhode Island cleared the House, advancing efforts to protect the state's biodiversity and natural ecosystems.
Senator Mark McKenney has introduced companion legislation in the Senate, where lawmakers are considering similar measures to prevent the spread of non-native invasive species and reduce the long-term environmental and economic costs associated with their removal.
Senate passes McKenney bill to revive civil claims springing from childhood sex abuse
June 3, 2026 at 12:00:00 AM
The Rhode Island Senate approved legislation introduced by Senator Mark McKenney that would revive certain previously time-barred civil claims involving childhood sexual abuse, expanding opportunities for survivors to seek accountability from individuals and institutions.
The bill was amended during the committee process to address constitutional concerns and clarify provisions related to court judgments, state liability, and the calculation of prejudgment interest before advancing to the House for further consideration.
