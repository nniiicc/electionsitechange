The bill outlines specific firearm features subject to regulation, includes exemptions for certain entities such as law enforcement, and allows continued possession of legally owned firearms under limited conditions.

The criminal justice process should be aimed at rehabilitation, and reintegration under the guidance of the state rather than private entities operating as moneymaking endeavors.
