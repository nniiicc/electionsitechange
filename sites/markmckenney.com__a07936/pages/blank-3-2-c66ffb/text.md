Public Safety
Senator McKenney supports advancing reasonable gun safety laws, strengthening protections for domestic violence survivors, improving DUI enforcement, and supporting law enforcement accountability and training for safer communities.

2025 Session

This legislation establishes restrictions on the manufacture, sale, transfer, and purchase of certain semiautomatic firearms defined as assault weapons under state law.
The bill outlines specific firearm features subject to regulation, includes exemptions for certain entities such as law enforcement, and allows continued possession of legally owned firearms under limited conditions.

This legislation proposed a ban on possession, sale, and transfer of assault weapons.
Possession of assault weapons owned on the effective date of the act would be "grandfathered".
Violations of the law would be punishable by a fine or imprisonment.
The legislation is currently being held in committee for further study.

This legislation would prohibit the operation of privately run, for profit correctional or detention facilities in Rhode Island.
