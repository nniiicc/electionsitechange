About Capitol TV
Rhode Island Capitol TV department, located in the Rhode Island State House, is the media outlet for Rhode Island’s General Assembly.
All of the programming aired on Capitol TV is available “on demand” on the state’s website at www.rilegislature.gov, under WATCH.
Also stream LIVE from your favorite devices: Roku, Apple TV OS & iOS, Amazon Fire Stick, and Android.
Just search for Capitol TV of RI.
Plus, download our app, Capitol TV of RI, from your preferred app store.
