Meet Mark Mark McKenney is a two-term Rhode Island State Senator representing District 30 in Warwick.
An attorney and longtime community advocate, he is running for reelection to continue expanding healthcare access, strengthening public education, and supporting working families.
Learn More Senator McKenney Focused on the Issues That Impact Rhode Island Families Click any category below to learn more.
Healthcare Expanding access to care, lowering prescription and insurance costs, addressing medical debt, strengthening reproductive health protections, and supporting providers so Rhode Islanders receive affordable, reliable healthcare.
Education Investing in strong public schools, protecting academic freedom, expanding early childhood education, modernizing facilities, and ensuring students learn in safe, respectful environments prepared for future success.
Economic Opportunity & Affordability Supporting working families through fair wages, tax relief for small businesses and seniors, reducing the car tax, and strengthening Rhode Island's economy so residents can fed to live and thrive.
Civil Rights Protecting equal pay, reproductive rights, freedom to read, anti-discrimination protections, and ensuring dignity and fairness for women, LGBTQ+ residents, seniors, veterans, and individuals with disabilities.
Housing & Infrastructure Addressing the housing crisis by streamlining development, strengthening renter protections, expanding affordable housing, investing in roads and schools, and improving shoreline access and land use processes.
Public Safety Advancing reasonable gun safety laws, strengthening protections for domestic violence survivors, improving DUI enforcement, and supporting law enforcement accountability and training for safer communities.
The Environment Protecting Narragansett Bay, reducing plastic waste, banning harmful chemicals, supporting clean energy, strengthening climate policy, and preserving public shoreline access for future generations.
Government Accountability & Transparency Strengthening ethics laws, increasing legislative transparency, holding oversight hearings, banning deceptive election practices, and ensuring responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
