Home About Issues News FUND THE FIGHT → Home About Issues News Cheryl Bean’s Blueprint for Texas From rising property taxes to the cost of groceries, Texas families are facing real challenges — and they deserve a champion who has lived those struggles and fought her way through.
Cheryl Bean built everything she has the right way, not the easy way.
Her blueprint for Texas is rooted in conservative values and built for action: lowering costs for families, protecting Texas jobs, keeping our kids safe, and making sure Texas stays strong for generations to come.
Making Texas Affordable Texans work hard, and they deserve to keep more of what they earn.
Cheryl Bean understands what it means to live on a budget when costs are rising uncontrollably.
She worked her way through college, struggled to buy a home as a newlywed, competed in a man’s workforce successfully, shouldered the responsibilities of a single mom, brought together a blended family, and built a company from scratch during all kinds of trials and economic downturns.
Cheryl WILL fight for hardworking families and push real solutions to drive down the cost of groceries and the everyday necessities families can’t go without. ﻿ Providing Tax Relief And Affordable Housing Combined with increased home and building costs and the large influx of “new” citizens, Texas has a major housing problem.
Cheryl Bean, the only candidate with home-building experience, believes owning a home is the American Dream, but that dream is fading for many Texans.
Even worse, Texans are losing their homestead as they enter retirement because they can no longer afford the property taxes.
Cheryl has already built a network of experts and city officials across the state to protect the critical services our local citizens deserve while significantly reducing and ultimately eliminating property taxes for good.
Protecting Texas Jobs Cheryl understands business, and she understands that good-paying jobs keep the Texas Miracle alive.
After working her way up to leadership at a local national defense company, Cheryl built a home-building company from the ground up.
For months, particularly during COVID, she floated paychecks out of her own pocket to take care of her employees and their families.
She’ll bring that same commitment to Austin — fighting to create and protect meaningful, quality jobs right here in the district.
Protecting Our Kids Keeping kids safe today means keeping them safe online.
Cheryl supports real safeguards around adult content, cracking down on the gaming and betting websites designed to get young adults hooked on gambling, and giving law enforcement the tools to stop online sexual predators before they reach our children.
As a mother and grandmother, she knows there's nothing more important than protecting our kids.
Providing Good Education for All Texas Children Education is still the key to opening the doors to success.
As a public charter school board chairman, a past college professor, and a substitute teacher in K-12, AND a parent and grandparent of children in Tarrant County and HD94, Cheryl Bean knows the issues our schools face too well.
She will address funding demands, ensure teachers are supported and paid, keep parents engaged and involved, and support a return to teaching life skills and trades in classrooms for use in the real world.
Keeping Families Safe Safety is one of the essential needs of every person.
When a family calls for help, help should come immediately.
Cheryl Bean will make sure our cities are able to fully fund emergency services, so first responders are there when seconds count.
Eliminating Foreign Threats Within Our Borders The human landscape of our great state has changed over last few years.
Cheryl Bean will use her strong background in national defense to stop Human Trafficking, stop cartels from trafficking people and drugs into our communities, and keep Texas safe from hostile foreign enemies.
Ensuring Every Citizens’ Vote Counts Having been actively involved at the local, state, and national levels, Cheryl Bean will continue to support all methods available to ensure that it is easy for Citizens to vote but hard for anyone to cheat.
Advocating for Reliable Texas Energy With the abundance of reliable energy we have right here in our state, Texas should never go dark.
Cheryl supports an all-of-the-above energy strategy that strengthens our grid, prioritizes natural gas, and ensures Texas families and businesses have affordable, year-round energy.
