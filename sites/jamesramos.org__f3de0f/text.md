James Ramos for Assembly
Fighting for the Inland Empire
In the State Assembly my focus remains on helping the Inland Empire – now more than ever.
I have fought for resources for our firefighters and been a strong voice for victims of crime.
I’m proud to have earned the support of law enforcement, firefighters and Crime Victims United.
I worked across the aisle to get things done in Sacramento – working to stop bullying in our schools, more resources for those suffering from mental illness and pushing for an Office of Suicide Prevention.
I am proud of this, but I know we will have more work to get our businesses back on track.
I worked with small business advocates to try and protect small businesses from frivolous lawsuits.
I opposed cuts to our schools and healthcare system because I believe we need to invest in our future.
I would be honored to have your support.
