“Trickle-down” policies don’t work, so we must focus on “middle out” economic policies.
Right now wealth is concentrated in the hands of a select few families and large corporations, while families across our nation struggle to make ends meet.
This is unacceptable.
We need to ensure our financial systems support the interests of the average American, rather than shareholders of large companies.
