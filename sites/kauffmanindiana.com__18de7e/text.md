My candidacy exists so that Hoosiers who are deeply concerned about the direction of our state and country can register that concern clearly and publicly.

Kelsey Kauffman holds a Doctorate in Education from Harvard University and has spent her career in classrooms, not boardrooms — teaching at DePauw University, founding education programs in Indiana prisons, and directing legislative education programs at the Statehouse.
She created the Greencastle Summer Enrichment Program, now in its 38th year serving low-income children.
A Greencastle resident since 1987, Kelsey is a mother of three, grandmother of three, and at 78, she is training for her first triathlon this June.
