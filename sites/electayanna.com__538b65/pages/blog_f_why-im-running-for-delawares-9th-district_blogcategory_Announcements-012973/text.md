VOTE - November 3rd 2026 Early Voting Begins October 22, 2026 Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog More Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog My Blog All Posts Why I'm Running for Delaware's 9th District July 14, 2026 | Announcements, News Photo taken by Errol Ebanks By Ayanna Khan-Flowers When people ask me why I'm running for Delaware's 9th District, my answer is simple: Because I believe our community deserves leadership that is focused on the future.
Over the years, I have had the privilege of working alongside entrepreneurs, small business owners, nonprofit leaders, educators, and families across Delaware.
Through that work, I have seen firsthand what makes our communities strong.
I have seen people step up to solve problems, support their neighbors, and create opportunities for others.
I have seen the determination and resilience that define Delaware at its best.
I have also seen the challenges that too many families face every day.
In District 9, we are experiencing tremendous growth.
Families continue to move here because they recognize what many of us already know: this is a wonderful place to live, work, and raise a family.
But that growth has also created real concerns.
Traffic is increasing.
Schools are feeling the pressure of growing enrollment.
Housing costs continue to rise.
Families are working harder than ever to keep up with the cost of living.
The question facing our district is not whether we will continue to grow.
The question is whether we will grow in a way that strengthens our communities and creates opportunities for everyone.
That is why I am running.
I am running because I believe economic opportunity should not be reserved for a select few.
Every family deserves the chance to build a stable future.
Every entrepreneur deserves a fair shot at success.
Every student deserves access to a quality education.
Every resident deserves a voice in the decisions that shape our community.
For much of my career, I have worked to help create those opportunities.
As a business leader and community advocate, I have helped entrepreneurs launch and grow businesses, supported organizations that strengthen our communities, and built partnerships that bring people together around solutions.
Those experiences taught me something important: lasting progress happens when we stop focusing on what divides us and start focusing on what we can accomplish together.
The people of District 9 are not looking for political theater.
They are looking for results.
They want safe neighborhoods.
Strong schools.
Reliable infrastructure.
Affordable housing options.
Good jobs.
Thriving local businesses.
They want leaders who listen, who show up, and who are willing to work with anyone to get things done.
That is the kind of leadership I intend to bring to Dover.
I am not running because I have all the answers.
I am running because I know the value of listening.
The best ideas often come from the people who live with challenges every day.
Effective leadership begins with understanding the concerns of residents, business owners, parents, educators, first responders, and community organizations.
It requires collaboration, accountability, and a willingness to put people before politics.
I believe we can support responsible growth while preserving the quality of life that makes District 9 special.
I believe we can strengthen our schools and better prepare students for the future.
I believe we can invest in infrastructure before problems become crises.
I believe we can support small businesses, create jobs, and expand economic opportunity.
And I believe we can do all of this while bringing people together rather than driving them apart.
At a time when politics often feels dominated by division and partisanship, I am offering something different: practical leadership rooted in service, collaboration, and results.
The future of District 9 will be shaped by the choices we make today.
We can accept the status quo, or we can build a future that better reflects the aspirations of the families who call this community home.
I am running because I believe in the promise of District 9.
I believe in the people who live here.
And I believe that with the right leadership, our best days are still ahead.
I would be honored to earn your trust, your support, and the opportunity to serve as your next State Representative.
