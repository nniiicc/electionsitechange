VOTE - November 3rd 2026 Early Voting Begins October 22, 2026 Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog More Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog My Blog All Posts Regarding Comments from School Board Member Elena Brenner October 14, 2026 | News, Official Statement Throughout my years of working with community organizations and supporting minority and underserved populations, I have witnessed firsthand the damage that discriminatory rhetoric can cause to families and entire communities.
The recent statement from School Board member Elena Brenner is atrocious, deeply offensive, and raises serious concerns about the responsibility that comes with educational leadership.
Ms.
Brenner asked, “Why would I want that ideology here?” My response is simple: EVERYONE IS WELCOMED!
Over the past several days, several community organizations asked me to help shape their public responses.
They came to me because I have spent years working with minority communities on the receiving end of exactly this kind of generalization.
I work with business owners and families who know what it means to be judged as a group before they are seen as individuals.
I have watched parents stop raising concerns because they expect to be dismissed, and I have watched children learn to make themselves smaller.
That is the harm a post like this does, and it outlasts the post itself.
Muslim families are a valued part of this district.
They are parents, students, teachers, coaches and neighbors.
This is not up for debate.
I am calling for a formal public apology, entered into the school board’s official meeting record, and a review of appropriate disciplinary measures, including probationary status if permitted under applicable law and policy.
Freedom of speech does not eliminate public accountability.
All children deserve leadership that reflects the principles of fairness, dignity, and inclusion.
EVERYONE IS WELCOMED HERE Diversity is not the threat.
Exclusion is.
