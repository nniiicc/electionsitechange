October 5, 2026 | News I believe every Delaware worker deserves a fair shot.
I’ve stood with Delaware’s minority- and small-business owners when they raised concerns that certain mandates could make it harder for them—and the people they employ—to compete for public projects.
That isn’t about being against unions or union workers.
I respect the contributions union workers make to our communities and our state.
It’s about making sure opportunity extends to everyone—union workers, non-union workers, small businesses, minority-owned businesses, and the families who depend on them.
We can support workers while also making sure Delaware’s public investments create opportunities across our communities.
That’s where I stand: Pro-worker.
Pro-small business.
Pro-opportunity.
