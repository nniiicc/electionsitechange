VOTE - November 3rd 2026 Early Voting Begins October 22, 2026 Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog More Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog Home About Ayanna Volunteer Blog My Blog All Posts My 2026 Legislative and Policy Advocacy July 30, 2026 | News Building a Stronger Delaware: A Vision for Inclusive Economic Growth in District 9 My 2026 Legislative and Policy Advocacy Throughout 2026, I have remained committed to advancing policies and initiatives that create economic opportunity, strengthen Delaware's small business community, and foster inclusive growth.
My approach to advocacy has always been rooted in collaboration—bringing together government, businesses, nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, and community leaders to address the economic and social challenges facing our state.
One of my primary areas of focus has been supporting entrepreneurship and small business development.
I have been proud to help launch and promote initiatives such as the M&T Small Business Accelerator Program and the Pitch Perfect Business Competition, which provide education, mentorship, and greater visibility for emerging entrepreneurs.
I have also worked to advance conversations around financial innovation, fintech accessibility, and artificial intelligence because I believe technology and modernization are essential to ensuring Delaware's businesses remain competitive in a rapidly evolving economy.
Expanding access to capital and advancing supplier diversity have remained central priorities in my work.
By partnering with financial institutions, business leaders, and community organizations, I have advocated for greater economic opportunity for historically underserved entrepreneurs.
Through my continued engagement with the National Black Chamber of Commerce, the Delaware Hispanic Commission, the Office of New Americans, and numerous statewide organizations, I have worked to ensure Delaware's economic development efforts are inclusive and reflective of the diverse communities we serve.
My legislative advocacy has focused on policies that strengthen Delaware's economy while promoting a fair and competitive business climate.
I supported the Housing for Every Delawarean Act (SB 23) because I recognize the direct connection between affordable workforce housing and long-term economic growth.
I also participated in advocacy efforts surrounding SB 272, SB 241, SB 57, SB 58, and SB 214, working alongside coalition partners and legislators on issues affecting small businesses, economic competitiveness, and sound public policy.
At the same time, I opposed House Bill 63 because of concerns that it would place unfair liability burdens on Delaware contractors and create unnecessary challenges for local businesses.
My advocacy has extended beyond Delaware as well.
I have participated in national policy discussions by supporting bipartisan federal permitting reform, endorsing the federal Prove It Act of 2025 to promote greater regulatory transparency, and engaging in U.S.
Chamber of Commerce initiatives designed to strengthen the business climate for employers and entrepreneurs nationwide.
I firmly believe that economic prosperity extends beyond business alone.
Strong economies are built on strong communities, which is why I have championed initiatives supporting workforce development, childcare accessibility, family economic stability, minority health, and community wellness.
These priorities are interconnected, and together they help create the conditions necessary for individuals, families, and businesses to thrive.
I have also worked to expand Delaware's presence in the global economy by participating in discussions surrounding international trade and business partnerships.
Through engagement with the North American Trade Parliament (CUSMEX), representatives from The Gambia, the Go Global Conference, and other international organizations, I have sought to position Delaware businesses for new opportunities in trade, investment, and global collaboration.
Community engagement has remained at the heart of my work.
Throughout the year, I have partnered with organizations including the EPA, DNREC, Visa, Woodlawn Trustees, Automation Xcel, the U.S.
Global Leadership Coalition, and numerous business and civic organizations to promote public education, economic development, innovation, and collaborative problem-solving.
I believe meaningful progress happens when people from different sectors come together around shared goals and practical solutions.
As I reflect on my work throughout 2026, I remain committed to a comprehensive approach to public policy—one that integrates economic development, workforce advancement, equitable access to opportunity, responsible legislation, international engagement, and community well-being.
I believe sustainable economic growth is achieved through thoughtful policymaking, strong partnerships, and a shared commitment to ensuring that every Delawarean has the opportunity to succeed.
This work continues to guide my vision for Delaware's future: an economy that is innovative, inclusive, globally competitive, and built on opportunity for every individual, every business, and every community.
