A STRONGER 12th DISTRICT
Dave Weber’s vision for a stronger 12th Assembly District extends beyond rhetoric; it is built on tangible action and a deep-seated commitment to the families of Huntington, Northport, East Northport, Centerport, and Huntington Bay.
As a seasoned public servant and small business owner, Dave understands that our communities are at their best when we bridge divides and work toward a shared future.
Listening to Every Voice
In Albany, the needs of Long Islanders are often ignored in favor of a one-size-fits-all agenda.
Dave believes that effective governance starts at home.
By hosting town halls, engaging with local civic associations, and maintaining an “open door” policy, Dave will ensure that the residents of the 12th District have a seat at the table.
Whether you live in a village or a suburban hamlet, your concerns about affordability, public safety, and local zoning are Dave’s top priorities.
Rooted in Our Region
With deep roots in this district and years of leadership in local organizations and the Northport Fire Department, Dave is a unifying figure.
He isn’t a career politician, he’s a neighbor who understands our unique heritage.
From protecting our beautiful shoreline on the Long Island Sound to supporting the small businesses that line our Main Streets, Dave is dedicated to fostering a sense of belonging and shared purpose across the entire 12th District.
Transparency and Accountability
Dave’s inclusive approach to leadership resonates across political and social lines.
He believes that transparency is the foundation of trust.
In the State Assembly, Dave will fight to restore accountability to our state government, ensuring that our tax dollars are used efficiently and that state mandates never overrule the will of our local communities.
A Path Forward Together
“A Stronger 12th” isn’t just a slogan, it’s Dave Weber’s guiding principle.
By promoting collaboration and civic engagement, Dave seeks to harness the collective energy and talent of our residents to build a stronger, safer, and more affordable New York for future generations.
