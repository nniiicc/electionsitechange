Carrie Syczylo is committed to restoring accountability, transparency, and strong representation for the people of Indiana House District 60.
Her priorities are rooted in protecting rural communities, strengthening local voices, and ensuring government serves the people it represents.
Fighting for Hoosier Communities
People Over Profit
Communities across Indiana deserve leadership that puts people first.
For too long, decisions have been made that prioritize corporate interests over the needs of local residents and communities.
Carrie believes government should exist to serve the people, protect community resources, and ensure that development and policy decisions consider the long-term impact on Hoosier families.
Her focus is on protecting local communities, ensuring responsible decision-making, and standing up for the people who call Indiana home.
Transparency & Accountability
Government must operate with honesty and openness.
Hoosiers deserve to know how decisions are made and how policies impact their communities.
Carrie believes elected officials must be accountable to the people they represent.
Transparency in government helps build trust, encourages responsible leadership, and ensures communities have a voice in the decisions that affect them.
She is committed to supporting policies that promote open communication, transparency, and accountability at every level of government.
Protecting Rural Communities
Rural communities are the backbone of Indiana and deserve strong representation and protection.
Carrie is committed to supporting policies that protect local resources, maintain community character, and ensure rural residents have access to essential services and opportunities.
From healthcare access to responsible development and protecting quality of life, Carrie believes rural communities should have a strong voice in shaping their future.
Strong communities are built when people come together to protect their shared values and future.
Carrie Syczylo is committed to listening to the concerns of Hoosiers and working toward solutions that strengthen our communities and protect the rights of the people.
