Show your support for Carrie Syczylo and the communities of Morgan, Johnson, and Monroe Counties with this patriotic campaign hat.
Featuring the Carrie for HD 60 design and the message “Rural People Deserve Good Things,” this hat represents a campaign focused on accountability, transparency, and standing up for rural Indiana.
With its bold American flag mesh backing and classic fit, this hat is perfect for campaign events, community gatherings, or everyday wear while supporting Carrie’s vision for stronger rural communities.
Product Details:
- Classic trucker-style hat
- Patriotic American flag mesh back
- Structured front panel with campaign patch
- Adjustable snapback closure for a comfortable fit
- Lightweight and breathable design
Wear it proudly and help spread the message that rural communities matter.
