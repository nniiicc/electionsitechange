The People Behind the Campaign
This campaign is made possible by a dedicated group of community members who believe in transparency, accountability, and strong representation for House District 60.
Each member of the team brings their time, skills, and commitment to supporting Carrie Syczylo and the people of Morgan, Johnson, and Monroe Counties.
Together, we are working to build a campaign that listens to the community and represents the voices of Hoosiers.
Hannah Bailey
Hannah Bailey is a mom of four who enjoys ornithology, regenerative agriculture practices, and highlighting the beauty of small, rural communities.
As a former 10-year 4H member, she recognizes the importance of pouring into our youth and supporting the community a person lives in.
Hannah has fought against multi billion dollar corporations to block projects that would harm prime farmland.
With a history in various styles of volunteering and nonprofits, Hannah will be supporting the campaign as campaign manager by identifying the needs and strengths that are critical to the communities that Carrie Syczylo desires to represent and bring unity to.
