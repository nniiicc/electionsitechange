Accessibility Statement
Accessibility Statement
Lindsay “Rubia” Garcia for Congress is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities.
We strive to continually improve the user experience for everyone and to apply relevant accessibility standards to this website.
Our goal is to make this website as accessible as possible by following best practices and guidelines defined by the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.
Accessibility Features
We have taken the following steps to help ensure accessibility:
Use of clear fonts and readable color contrast
Consistent navigation throughout the site
Alternative text for images when possible
Keyboard navigation support where available
Responsive design for mobile and tablet devices
Ongoing Efforts
We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of this website.
While we strive to ensure that all pages and content are fully accessible, some content may not yet meet the strictest accessibility standards.
We welcome feedback from visitors and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility concerns.
Need Assistance?
If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you need assistance with any content, please contact us and we will do our best to help.
