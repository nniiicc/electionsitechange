From Baton Rouge and Livingston to Ascension, Pointe Coupee, the River Parishes, and communities along Bayou Lafourche, District 6 is home to working families, educators, small-business owners, farmers, industrial workers, students, and seniors.
Rubia is running to lower costs, improve infrastructure, protect our rights, strengthen schools, create good-paying jobs, and make sure every community has a voice in Washington.
What I'll fight for as your Congresswoman:
- Lower Costs & Grow the Economy: Make everyday life more affordable, protect Social Security, Medicare and Medicaid, lower prescription costs, raise wages, expand housing and childcare, and support local small businesses.
- Fix Roads, Traffic & Flooding: Bring federal dollars home to improve I-10 and I-12, bridges, roads, drainage, flood protection, water and sewer systems, and affordable flood insurance.
- Create Jobs & Strengthen Education: Grow manufacturing, energy, construction, technology, healthcare and skilled-trade jobs while expanding apprenticeships, technical training, early education and support for teachers.
- Protect Healthy & Connected Communities: Ensure clean air and water, responsible energy development, strong property-rights protections, rural investment, affordable broadband and better access to healthcare.
- Keep Communities Safe & Protect Our Rights: Fight violent crime, trafficking and fentanyl while supporting fair justice, constitutional policing, voting rights, civil rights, due process and a government accountable to the Constitution.
