My Priorities
No Income or Sales Tax
We have what is called the "New Hampshire Advantage" over other states because we have no income or sales tax.
Democrats have tried to pass one for years but it would dramatically increase the tax burden for everyone Granite Stater and I will fight tooth and nail against one.
Lowering High Prices
Howard Pearl understands that rising prices are squeezing New Hampshire families and small businesses.
He is committed to keeping taxes low, cutting unnecessary regulations, expanding housing opportunities, supporting local agriculture, and creating an environment where businesses can grow, compete, and create good-paying jobs.
Strong fiscal leadership helps keep everyday costs affordable for Granite Staters.
Work to Lower Property Taxes
The best way to stop higher property taxes is to control government spending.
As President Ronald Reagan said, "The problem is not that people are taxed too little, the problem is that government spends too much."
