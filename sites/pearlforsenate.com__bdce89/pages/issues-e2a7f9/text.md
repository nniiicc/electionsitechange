Lowering High Prices Howard Pearl understands that rising prices are squeezing New Hampshire families and small businesses.
He is committed to keeping taxes low, cutting unnecessary regulations, expanding housing opportunities, supporting local agriculture, and creating an environment where businesses can grow, compete, and create good-paying jobs.
Strong fiscal leadership helps keep everyday costs affordable for Granite Staters.
Work to Lower Property Taxes The best way to stop higher property taxes is to control government spending.
As President Ronald Reagan said, "The problem is not that people are taxed too little, the problem is that government spends too much." Protect Our Constitutional Rights There is a full on assault of our Constitutional rights.
Whether it's the freedom of speech, or our right to bear arms, the Constitution is being trampled by radicals.
I am a Constitutional conservative and will fight to protect and preserve our Constitutional rights.
Fight the Status Quo Howard Pearl believes New Hampshire succeeds when leaders challenge outdated ideas and pursue practical solutions.
He isn't afraid to ask tough questions, reform government where needed, and stand up to special interests.
Howard will continue fighting for transparent, accountable government that puts taxpayers, families, and local communities first.
