Expanding Jobs and Opportunity
Inflation has made it harder and harder for everyday families to make ends meet.
Pro-growth policies such as recent tax cuts help small businesses and allow West Virginians to keep more of their hard-earned money.
Tristan is committed to policies that add jobs and attract more people to live, work, and raise a family in our great state of West Virginia.
Protecting West Virginia Values
West Virginians prize faith, family, and freedom.
These values are essential for strong communities, yet increasingly under attack in the world today.
Tristan is a conservative Republican and is pro-faith, pro-family, pro-life, and pro-Second Amendment.
He will work diligently to defend these and other West Virginia values.
Promoting Wellness and Recovery
Most of us have been impacted by the opioid epidemic in one way or another.
Tristan has lost several loved ones to mental illness, which is often at the root of addiction.
He believes firmly in the power of community to encourage wellness and promote recovery.
Making Government work for you
Government agencies exist to serve taxpayers.
From the DMV to emergency medical services to our children’s education, West Virginians want clear results from their precious tax dollars.
Tristan has over a decade of experience improving government, and will make sure the government works for you.
