A Note from the Treasurer Every year, Minnesota residents can get refunded up to $75 per person donated to political campaigns, including Lucy's!
It's a state program called the Political Contribution Refund, or the PCR.
It was started in 1990 to ​encourage grassroots fundraising and public participation in political campaigns.
How it works: Donate $75 per person to Lucy's campaign Lucy's Treasury group will send you a PCR receipt and a simple tax form.
These forms are generally sent out the Thursday following your donation date.
Please check your email junk folder.
Otherwise email TreasurerLucyRehm@outlook.com to get them resent or to request a paper version mailed to you.
Fill out the simple tax form and send it and the receipt to the state Within 3-4 weeks the entire $75 is deposited into your bank account This program allows refunds of up to $75 per person per year for contributions made to any entity using this program.
This means, regardless of how much or how many groups you donate to, you will only receive up to $75 back from the state.
