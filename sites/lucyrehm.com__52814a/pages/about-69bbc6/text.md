DONATE About Me Hi!
I’m Lucy Rehm, your representative at the Minnesota House of Representatives, and I’m running for re-election in District 48B.
Since 2022, I brought my passion for the community of Chanhassen and Chaska to St.
Paul.
And every day, I work hard to improve the lives of the Minnesotans who live in my district.
My husband Phil and I chose to put roots down in Chanhassen #ago because we knew it was a great place to raise a family.
From the exemplary area schools my children attended to the parks and natural spaces we enjoy as a family, this community exceeds our expectations.
It’s why I chose to run and then serve on the Chanhassen City Council in 2020, and why, when the opportunity presented itself in 2022, I ran and was elected to be the Representative for House District 48B.
I was then re-elected in 2024.
I’m honored to represent Chanhassen and most of Chaska.
I’m proud of what we accomplished together.
With your support, I’ll continue to bring my lifelong passion for healthy living, families and the environment to the work I do for our communities in St.
Paul.
“I am running for re-election because I want to preserve the things that make this a great place to live while doing the work to make them even better.” Why I’m running Divisive politics prevent us from solving real problems.
My husband and I love this community.
In 2022 I decided to ran to preserve our natural spaces, improve our parks and trails, grow our thriving business community and support our outstanding schools.
I was honored to be re-elected in 2024.
My commitment to you starts with listening.
I’m open to respectful conversations with all my constituents.
I’ll advocate forward-thinking, innovative solutions to the challenges our communities face.
And I’ll continue to do the necessary work to deliver tangible results for all residents.
Why Lucy?
This district deserves a representative who understands that the job is about serving you, not a political party .
I will continue to lead with honesty and respect while continuing my focus on achieving real results for our community.
