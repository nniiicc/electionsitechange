Policy Priorities
- Healthcare & Mental Health Healthcare is a human right.
No one should choose between rent or life-sustaining medications.
If re-elected, I’ll continue to work toward access to affordable healthcare for every Minnesotan including robust mental health and chemical dependency services to support families and individuals in crisis.
- Public Education Every Minnesotan deserves an exceptional public education.
I’m fighting to close the K-12 education funding gap that has persisted in Minnesota for two decades and will continue to ensure that school funding keeps up with inflation.
I support compensating teachers and student teachers, funding teacher pensions, and making it easier for veteran educators to retire earlier with dignity and without withdrawal penalties.
- Environment Minnesota is rich in natural spaces, lakes, creeks, wetlands, parks, and trails.
As your representative, I’ll continue to fight for policies that preserve our environment.
I’ll work to strengthen and expand mass transit and to convert our energy grid to renewable sources to protect the land, water, and air we all share.
- Pro-Choice As a lifelong feminist, I am 100% pro-choice.
I believe all healthcare decisions should be made between a patient and their doctor.
I’ll continue to oppose all legislation that attempts to limit access to abortion and other reproductive healthcare.
