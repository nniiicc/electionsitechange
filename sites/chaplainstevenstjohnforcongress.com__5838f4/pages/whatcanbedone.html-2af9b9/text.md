Protect Healthcare
Healthcare policy should protect access to care and prevent eligible families from losing coverage because a system is too difficult to navigate.
- Protect Medicare and Medicaid and closely review the Medicaid eligibility changes scheduled for 2027 before Nevada families lose coverage.
- Require clear notice, meaningful opportunities to correct errors, and practical ways for eligible people to verify work, education, caregiving, or an exemption before coverage is terminated.
- Protect affordable individual-market coverage and work to keep premiums and out-of-pocket costs within reach for working families.
- Continue efforts to lower prescription-drug costs, including Medicare drug-price negotiation and assistance for people with high medication costs.
- Protect federal support for home healthcare, hospice, nursing-home care, and home- and community-based services so seniors and people with disabilities can receive care in the most appropriate setting.
- Expand access to mental-health services and strengthen the healthcare workforce through training, recruitment, and retention of nurses, physicians, caregivers, and other professionals.
- Address medical debt and improve price transparency so patients can better understand what care will cost before the bill arrives
