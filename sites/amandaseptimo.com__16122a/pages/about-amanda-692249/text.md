Raised in the Bronx, Amanda understands firsthand the challenges many families face — from housing costs and healthcare access to education and economic opportunity.
That experience shaped her commitment to public service and community advocacy.
Since being elected to the New York State Assembly, Amanda has focused on delivering results for the people of the Bronx by fighting for affordable communities, healthcare access, stronger public schools, safer neighborhoods, and economic justice.
In Albany, Amanda has built a reputation as a thoughtful, community-driven leader who believes government should work for everyday people.
She continues working alongside local organizations, labor unions, advocates, and residents to make sure Bronx communities receive the investment and representation they deserve.
ABOUT THE DISTRICT Serving the 84th Assembly District The 84th Assembly District is home to vibrant Bronx communities filled with hardworking families, small businesses, young people, seniors, and longtime neighborhood advocates.
Amanda is proud to represent a district built on resilience, diversity, and community.
Her focus remains making sure every neighborhood has access to opportunity, quality public services, safe streets, strong schools, affordable housing, and responsive leadership.
Why Amanda Is Running for Reelection Amanda Septimo is running for reelection because there is still more work to do.
Families across the Bronx continue facing rising costs, pressure on healthcare systems, housing instability, and concerns about public safety and quality of life.
Amanda believes the Bronx deserves leadership that listens, works hard, and delivers real results — and she remains committed to fighting for the communities that raised her.
