A Bronx Voice That Shows Up Amanda Septimo was born and raised in the Bronx, and she’s never forgotten who she fights for.
In Albany, Amanda has focused on the issues families talk about around kitchen tables every day: rising costs, healthcare, housing, public safety, good schools, and making sure Bronx communities are not left behind.
She believes government should work for everyday people — not just the well-connected.
That’s why Amanda has fought to expand access to healthcare, support survivors of domestic violence, protect tenants, invest in public education, and bring real resources back to the Bronx.
AMANDA’S PRIORITIES Fighting for Affordable Communities Amanda is working to make New York more affordable for working families by supporting tenant protections, economic opportunity, and investments that help families stay in the communities they built.
Expanding Healthcare Access Amanda has been a strong advocate for affordable healthcare, community health resources, and protecting programs families depend on.
She’s spoken out against cuts that would hurt working people and continues fighting for healthcare access across the Bronx.
Supporting Safer Neighborhoods Every family deserves to feel safe where they live.
Amanda supports investments in violence prevention, mental health services, youth programs, and community-based solutions that strengthen neighborhoods and improve quality of life.
Investing in Schools and Young People Amanda believes every child deserves a strong education and real opportunity.
She continues fighting for better-funded schools, youth programs, childcare access, and pathways for young people to succeed.
Delivering for the Bronx Amanda Septimo has built a record focused on real issues affecting Bronx families.
From healthcare advocacy and tenant protections to environmental justice and infrastructure improvements, Amanda continues working to make sure the Bronx gets the investment and attention it deserves.
