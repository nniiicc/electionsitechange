Why I'm Running RESPONSIBLE GROWTH Due to unprecedented growth, it is time that our area has a comprehensive, cost-effective plan for the next 10-15 years.
This plan should embrace economic opportunity and bring more jobs to our community without overwhelming our home.
I am committed to a responsible plan for growth.
This plan will be one that does not raise taxes, but rather cuts frivolous, unnecessary spending and government waste.
Fiscal conservatism is the key to solving our area's growth problems.
As a parent, coach, and teacher, I know and understand firsthand the challenges that students, parents, and teachers face.
Because every household and every community is different, it would be a disservice to students to use a "one-size-fits-all" approach to education.
I'll advocate for parents to have more choice and involvement in their children's education.
Legislators, parents, and teachers must work together to create a better future for our children.
EDUCATION There are so many modern technological advancements that will aid our first responders, and I am committed to making sure they receive these resources and anything else they need.
The people of District 28 deserve to raise a family and work in a safe environment.
Advancing crime prevention measures and cutting down EMS & Fire response times are possible, and I promise to get the job done.
Additionally, investments in modern technology like this can assist local election officials with voter accountability.
PUBLIC SAFETY CONTACT I’ve proudly called Forsyth County home for decades and have enjoyed raising my family, growing my business, coaching football, and teaching here.
Our community and its people are incredible.
They deserve a strong leader who will fight for them in the legislature.
I look forward to this campaign and sharing my conservative vision for the future."
