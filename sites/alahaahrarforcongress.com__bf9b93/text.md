01 / THE CASE VA-07 · LOCAL FIRST VA-07 DESERVES MORE THAN AN ARGUMENT.
“A Christian family, a Muslim family, a veteran family, an immigrant family, and a young person starting out all want the same basic things: safety, dignity, a good job, a chance to build something, and a home they can afford.” — ALAHA AHRAR That is why this campaign is not built around another national shouting match.
It is built around what makes a local future possible: skills, ownership, housing, and the freedom to build a life in the community you call home.
02 / THE PLAN JOBENOMICS VA-07 · FIELD PLAN BUILD THE FUTURE FROM THE BLOCK UP.
One connected economic agenda: training should lead to work; work should lead to ownership; and ownership should create stability for the next generation.
01 DIGITAL ACADEMY + BUSINESS GENERATOR Jobs, skills & small business Practical digital skills, career certification, entrepreneurship coaching, and local business support—so residents can connect experience to opportunity.
Career-linked training in cybersecurity, cloud work, AI operations, e-commerce, and digital marketing.
Entrepreneur Centers and peer-based business clubs for mentorship and startup support.
A rapid-response pathway for career changers and federal workers navigating transition.
02 SERVICE INTO OPPORTUNITY Veterans & military families Service should be met with more than gratitude.
It should lead to a serious path to civilian prosperity, ownership, and stability.
Startup mentoring and shared-workspace models designed around veteran needs.
Connections to responsible financing, technical assistance, and planning support.
Flexible home-based and remote-work opportunities for military families.
03 SAFETY THROUGH INDEPENDENCE Economic security & dignity No one should have to remain in danger because they lack a job, a credential, or a safe place to live.
Career pathways that help survivors build financial independence.
Trauma-informed mentorship that respects privacy and dignity.
Housing-bridge pathways that move people from crisis toward stability.
04 TALENT THAT BELONGS HERE Immigrant advancement Virginia's immigrant communities are a source of skill, language, entrepreneurship, and leadership.
Multilingual learning and digital-certification pathways.
Support for immigrant-owned startups and community-rooted enterprise.
Dignity-centered access to tools for stability and ownership.
05 ATTAINABLE HOMEOWNERSHIP Homes & sustainable communities Working families deserve a realistic path to own a home in the communities they serve.
Factory-built, energy-smart housing and ADU options.
Community design that lowers long-term household costs.
Ownership structures that help families build equity over time.
06 LOCAL FOOD + FAMILY WEALTH Local opportunity that lasts Regenerative agriculture, indoor farming, and family wealth tools can create jobs while strengthening local resilience.
Indoor and controlled-environment agriculture tied to training and enterprise.
Triple-net-zero community concepts adapted to local needs.
Financial education and ownership pathways for the next generation.
