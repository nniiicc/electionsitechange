On the Issues
Keeping the California Dream in Reach for Working Families
The rising cost of housing, childcare, and transportation is putting too much pressure on families.
Chris is fighting to make life more affordable—because nobody should be priced out of the community they call home.
From launching a first-time homebuyer program to expanding rent protections and access to childcare, Chris is delivering results.
In the Assembly, he’s championing policies that reduce homelessness, build affordable housing, and support working families.
Putting Rural Communities First
Our region isn’t just a smaller version of the big cities—it has its own challenges and strengths.
Chris is making sure rural communities are heard and prioritized in Sacramento.
He’s pushing for better broadband, support for rural schools and clinics, and infrastructure investments that reflect our local needs.
Whether it’s broadband or water systems, he knows that rural communities deserve their fair share.
Protecting What Makes the North Coast Special
Chris believes we have a responsibility to protect the forests, rivers, and coastline that define our home.
He’s working to restore critical salmon habitat, prevent wildfires, and promote climate resilience.
Partnering with Tribes, environmental groups, and local communities, Chris is advancing bold conservation and climate action efforts—while expanding access to open space and outdoor recreation for all.
