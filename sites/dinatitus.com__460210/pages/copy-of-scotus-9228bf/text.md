Dina Titus Statement on the U.S.
Supreme Court’s Possible Decision to Strip Away Our Reproductive Rights 5/3/2022 Las Vegas, NV – Last night a leaked draft of the U.S.
Supreme Court’s initial majority opinion for Dobbs v.
Jackson Women’s Health Organization revealed a decision to strike down the landmark Roe v.
Wade precedent.
Rep.
Dina Titus, candidate for Congress in NV-01, released the following statement in response: ​ “If this report is true, I am saddened but not surprised that after years of Republican efforts, this court plans to strike down Roe v.
Wade and take women’s personal health care decisions away from us.” ​ “This decision would be unprecedented and devastating, but it is not the end of the fight.
I am deeply committed to ensuring that this country does not turn back the clock on women’s equality.
That’s why I proudly co-sponsored and voted to pass the Women’s Health Protection Act.
I have repeatedly called for the Senate to end the filibuster in order to pass this critical bill.” ​ “Every one of my GOP opponents supports the assault on women's right to make our own health care decisions.
