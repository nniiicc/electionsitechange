Effective: March 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Friends of Butch (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website located at fitzsimmonsforassembly.com (the “Site”).
By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
1.
Information We Collect
Personal Information: When you sign up for updates, make a donation, or volunteer, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and payment information.
Voter‑file information: In addition to information supporters provide directly, our campaign works with voter-registration information about registered voters in the district (such as names, addresses, and, where available, phone numbers) that we have lawfully obtained for the purpose of voter contact and outreach.
Usage Information: We automatically collect information about how you interact with our Site, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time and date of your visit.
Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyze Site traffic.
2.
Campaign Field Tools (Voter Viewer App)
Our campaign uses Voter Viewer, a field-organizing application provided by Seamless Communications Group Inc., as an internal tool for our authorized staff and volunteers.
Through this application, authorized campaign workers view voter-registration information we have lawfully obtained — such as names, residential addresses, and, where available, telephone numbers — in order to canvass, phone-bank, and conduct voter outreach, and they record the results of that outreach.
The application is not available to the public and requires campaign‑issued sign‑in credentials.
Information handled in the application is used only for our campaign’s outreach and is not sold.
Any text messages sent through the application are subject to our SMS Terms and the consent and opt‑out practices described in this policy.
For details on how the application itself handles information, see the Voter Viewer Privacy Policy
3.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities
- Process donations and fulfill your requests
- Improve and personalize your experience on our Site
- Analyze Site usage and trends
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights
4.
Sharing Your Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share your information in the following circumstances:
Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who help us operate our Site, process donations, or send communications on our behalf.
Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to legal process.
Campaign Partners: With your consent, we may share your information with allied organizations or campaigns.
This does not include mobile phone numbers or SMS/text-messaging consent data, which are never shared.
5.
SMS / Text Messaging
If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in to receive text messages from the Friends of Butch campaign, we use it only to send you the campaign updates, event notices, and volunteer reminders you signed up for.
We do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in and consent data with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
The only parties who receive your mobile number are the service providers that deliver the messages on our behalf (such as our messaging platform), and only for the purpose of delivering those messages.
The “Campaign Partners” sharing described in this policy does not apply to mobile phone numbers or SMS consent data – those are never shared with allied organizations or other campaigns.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
Opting out of texts will not affect any other way you hear from the campaign.
See our SMS Terms.
