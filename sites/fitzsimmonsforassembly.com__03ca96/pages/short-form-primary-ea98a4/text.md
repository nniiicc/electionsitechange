PC2 – Health Care A Human Right Health care reform is crucial for all Americans.
The current system is failing, leading to bankruptcy, medical bankruptcy, and the destruction of families.
We need a solution that works for everyone, not just a few.
New York’s New Health Act is a single-payer system that would provide affordable, accessible health care to all.
It would eliminate co-pays, uncovered costs, and networks, and ensure that doctors and hospitals are available for your choice.
This would also reduce costs for municipalities and end the yearly hassle that employers and employees face with the health insurance industry.
Carl Fitzsimmons is your progressive candidate who is dedicated to fighting for health care reform.
He has been advocating for this solution for decades and is ready to bring it to the governor’s desk in the first year.
Join Carl Fitzsimmons in supporting the New Health Act and improving health care for all Americans.
Vote for him during early voting and on June 23rd.
PC2 – Education Unfunded School Bus Mandate: Time for Action Attention Albany: the unfunded school bus mandate is a pressing issue that needs immediate attention.
Many districts have already made the transition, benefiting from the increased efficiency and safety it brings.
However, those who have not stepped up must replace them.
Early Childhood Professionals: A Growing Need Early childhood professionals serving children ages 3 to 5 have been drastically cut in numbers over the past seven years.
Despite this, the demand for their services has only increased, with the lack of these professionals being a major contributor to the school-to-prison pipeline.
These professionals, typically women, are essential for the development of our youngest children.
Failure to act now will only create greater costs as these children become adults.
Erosion of Public Schools: The Voucher Program The erosion of public schools through the voucher program to IDAs for AI is another issue that needs to be addressed.
These programs diverge tax revenues from local municipalities and school districts, ultimately harming the education system.
It is time for a change.
Join us for a discussion on these critical issues and how we can work together to improve the education system in Albany.
