Carl Fitzsimmons · NYS Assembly · 130th District Webster & Wayne County Health Care Spend less.
Get more.
Health insurance isn't health care.
It's health access .
Did you know?
3 in 10 adults skipped or put off care they needed because of the cost.
1 in 7 say their health got worse because they waited.
Source: KFF 1 Where is the money going?
They call it a premium.
It's really a TOLL.
Privatized gains.
Socialized losses. ⅓ of every health care dollar goes to billing, insurance and paperwork, not care.
A family plan through work now costs about $27,000 a year .
Insurers posted record profits while Wayne County lost its last birthing center.
2 What is it going to pay for?
The New York Health Act: every New Yorker in one plan.
No premiums.
No deductibles.
No copays.
Any doctor, any hospital.
No network to be out of.
Prescriptions, dental, vision, hearing and long-term care included.
Paid by one graduated payroll tax.
Employers pay 80%, and the first $25,000 of income is exempt.
3 What do I get back for what I pay?
Care before you're that sick.
Higher costs come from waiting.
9 in 10 households would pay less, RAND found: about $2,800 less per person a year, on average.* Your coverage moves with you from job to job.
Your school district's and town's health bill drops, and that comes straight off the property-tax levy.
