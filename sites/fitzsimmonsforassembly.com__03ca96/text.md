I am very proud to announce that our campaign has received the Gun Sense Champion of Distinction recognition from Moms Demand Action for our stance on comprehensive and effective gun safety policy.
I take this issue quite seriously.
As a Vietnam Era Army Veteran who received multiple marksmanship awards as well as a long time Boy Scout leader, I fully understand that the ownership and use of guns requires the utmost in safety, security and respect.
As a member of the NY Assembly I will support legislation that requires certifications, registration and insurance be obtained in conjunction with the ownership of certain specific types of guns.
The ownership of any gun comes with serious responsibilities.
Effective and mindful legislation will allow gun enthusiasts the opportunity to continue to enjoy their recreational use while also protecting our communities from the avoidable dangers that irresponsible gun ownership may cause.
