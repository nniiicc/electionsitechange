Back to All Events Rehumanize Conference 2026 Friday, October 16, 2026 9:00 AM Sunday, October 18, 2026 11:30 PM Casa Iskali 9430 West Golf Road Des Plaines, Illinois, 60016 United States (map) Google Calendar ICS The Rehumanize Conference is an annual event dedicated to education, discourse, and action on a range of issues related to human rights.
People from all walks of life attend to hear from trailblazing activists, academics, and educators with a particular emphasis on the voices of those who have experienced dehumanization and violence first-hand.
With plenty of opportunities for engagement with speakers and other conference attendees, this event is sure to challenge your apathy, increase your knowledge, and expand your horizons.
Along with these paradigm-shifting ideas, you will also be given practical guidance on how to go out and challenge the systems and institutions that perpetuate oppression and build a culture of peace and life in your own communities.
Our campaign will be on site, and Mike Vick will be among the featured panelists.
