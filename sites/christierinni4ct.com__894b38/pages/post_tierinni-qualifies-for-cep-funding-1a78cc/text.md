Tierinni qualifies for CEP funding
East Hartford Gazette, dated 9.24.2026
Endorsed 10th Assembly District Republican candidate Chris Tierinni qualified for funding through the state Citizens’ Election Program (CEP).
Candidates for state representative accomplish this by raising $6,700 with at least 150 coming from individuals in the district.
Tierinni said qualifying for public funding marks a major milestone in his campaign, and credits residents who contributed.
“This campaign has always been about the people of East Hartford,” Tierinni said.
“I’m incredibly grateful to everyone who contributed, encouraged us, and believed in what we are working to accomplish.
Qualifying for the Citizens’ Election Program shows that we have real grassroots support behind this campaign.”
This is the second time Tierinni has sought the 10th District seat held for 21 years by Democrat Henry Genga.
Genga deferred another term endorsing Councilwoman Angie Parkinson, who then defeated Board of Education Chair Tyron Harris August 11 in a party primary.
Tierinni praised Genga for his longtime leadership representing the town, and feels his views align with the needs of East Hartford.
Affordability, education, opportunity and strengthening East Hartford’s voice in Hartford are what his campaign has focused on.
“Families across East Hartford are feeling the pressure of rising property taxes, utility bills, housing costs and everyday necessities,” Tierinni said.
“I’m running because our community deserves a strong voice in Hartford focused on making Connecticut more affordable and making sure East Hartford gets the attention and resources it deserves.”
Born and raised in East Hartford, Tierinni owns his own contracting business and volunteers for a number of community causes as well as serving on the Board of Education.
He is also an MDC commissioner, a member of the Planning and Zoning Commission and Redevelopment Agency.
