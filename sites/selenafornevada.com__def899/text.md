Selena La Rue Hatch Assembly District 25 needs the voice of someone who understands what it means to be a working Nevadan today.
A voice of someone who knows what it means to carry thousands of dollars in student debt and pay almost half your income in childcare expenses.
A voice of someone who knows the struggles of affording housing in a market that has priced out all but the richest Nevadans.
A voice of someone who has never been afraid to question the status quo and advocate for everyday Nevadans.
I have been and will continue to be that voice in the legislature for Assembly District 25.
I have always been a fierce advocate for our community, and will never back down from the fight for working Nevada families.
I will fight to ensure your voices are heard in the halls of power and our children have the supports they need to thrive.
Please join our movement for a brighter Nevada!
