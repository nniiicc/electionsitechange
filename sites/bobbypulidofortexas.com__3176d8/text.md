Meet Bobby
Bobby Pulido is a lifelong son of South Texas, small business owner, father of four, and award-winning Tejano artist now running for Congress in Texas’ 15th District.
Born and raised in Edinburg, he studied political science at St.
Mary’s University before building a genre-defining music career.
Beyond the stage, Bobby has run businesses, created jobs, and faced the same challenges as families across the Valley.
He is a South Texan who understands the struggles families face because they are his own: rising costs, a healthcare system that too often fails, and an immigration debate that ignores the realities of border communities.
He believes in practical solutions, honest leadership, and putting people ahead of party.
