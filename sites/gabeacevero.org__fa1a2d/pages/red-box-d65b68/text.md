Key Messaging Themes Independent groups are encouraged to focus on the following themes in their messaging: Equity and Justice: Highlight Delegate Acevero’s commitment to social, racial, gender, and economic justice .
Community Investment : Emphasize his work to uplift underserved communities and ensure every Marylander has the opportunity to thrive.
Progressive Leadership : Showcase his legislative accomplishments and leadership on issues like criminal justice reform, healthcare access, and environmental justice .
Inclusivity : Reflect Delegate Acevero’s dedication to creating a Maryland that is fair, inclusive, and brimming with opportunity.
Policy Priorities Independent groups can align their messaging with Delegate Acevero’s key policy priorities: Criminal Justice Reform: Sponsored Anton’s Law to increase police accountability and transparency.
Advocates for violence prevention programs and mental health services.
Healthcare Access and Equity : Co-sponsored legislation to improve maternal and infant health.
Voted to codify reproductive healthcare in the Maryland Constitution.
Housing Stability : Supports affordable housing initiatives and tenant protections.
Co-sponsored Just Cause legislation to prevent unjust evictions.

Key Achievements Independent groups can highlight Delegate Acevero’s legislative accomplishments to showcase his impact: Anton’s Law : Increased police accountability and transparency .
Climate Solutions Now Act : Reduced greenhouse gas emissions and promoted sustainability.
Youth Transitioning from Foster Care Program : Provided direct cash assistance to foster youth aging out of the system.
Guaranteed Basic Income : Advocated for direct cash assistance to low-income Marylanders.
Equal Access to Public Services Act : Expanded access to resources for individuals with limited English proficiency and disabilities.
District 39 Overview Messaging should reflect the unique characteristics and priorities of District 39: District 39 is home to a vibrant and diverse community, majority people of color and working-class families, including a large Muslim population and Spanish-speaking residents.
We have large immigrant communities from the Caribbean, Central America, West Africa and South Asia.
It includes the communities of Gaithersburg, Montgomery Village, Germantown, Clarksburg, and the town of Washington Grove.
