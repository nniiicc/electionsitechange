Blair Eddins Files for Re‑Election to Represent North Carolina House District 94
Representative Blair Eddins officially filed for re‑election in North Carolina’s House District 94 on December 1st, 2025, reaffirming his commitment to serving the people of Alexander and Wilkes Counties by protecting conservative values, defending taxpayers, and standing strong for families and our local communities.
Since taking office, Eddins has championed a number of bills that reflect the priorities of voters in District 94, from education and agriculture legislation to laws protecting the rights and well‑being of citizens across the district.
“Serving the people of District 94 has been one of the greatest honors of my life,” Eddins said.
“We’ve passed meaningful legislation, from protecting our children’s learning environment to supporting our farmers and safeguarding elderly and vulnerable citizens.
But there is more work to do.
I’m running for re‑election to continue standing up for our values, our communities, and our future.”
Eddins pledged to continue standing up for rural communities, supporting law enforcement, defending parental rights, and protecting constitutional freedoms.
Blair Eddins resides with his family in the district.
The campaign will release additional details about events, volunteer opportunities, and voter outreach in the coming weeks.
The primary election will be held on March 3, 2026, and the general election will be November 3, 2026.
More information can be found at www.eddinsfornc.com.
