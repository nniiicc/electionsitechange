Eddins Earns Grass Roots North Carolina Recommendation
Blair Eddins, candidate for NC House in District 94, has earned four out of four stars and a recommendation from Grass Roots North Carolina (GRNC), an all-volunteer organization "devoted to educating the public about trends which abridge the freedoms guaranteed by the Bill of Rights, and engaging in grassroots activism to preserve those freedoms."
GRNC's recommendation and four-star rating are based on Blair Eddins' strong support of citizens' Second Amendment protections.
"Our right to keep and bear arms is a fundamental freedom that is non-negotiable, and this is clearly communicated in the US and North Carolina Constitutions," says Eddins.
"We must never allow our elected officials to restrict our right to protect and defend ourselves and our families."
