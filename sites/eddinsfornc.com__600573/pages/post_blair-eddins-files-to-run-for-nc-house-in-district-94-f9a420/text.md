DONATE Blair Eddins Files to Run for NC House in District 94 Blair Eddins Dec 5, 2023 1 min read Blair Eddins, a Constitutional Conservative Republican, filed yesterday as a candidate in the March 5, 2024 primary election to represent District 94 in the North Carolina House of Representatives.
A strong Constitutional Conservative, Blair Eddins will stand up for western North Carolina and against the liberal agendas that are destroying our state and our country.
As your state representative, Blair will advocate for limited government, Biblical family values, quality education, building a strong economy and protecting our God-given freedoms.
Since announcing his campaign in both Wilkes and Alexander County, Blair has been meeting with voters across the district, listening to their concerns and discussing the importance of protecting our state, our children, and our future from the radical agendas of the political elite in Washington, DC.
He is committed to being a strong and active voice for our district and for the values our communities hold dear.
Blair lives with his wife of 27 years, Angie, in western Wilkes County.
An active member of his local church and community, he has served on multiple non-profit and church boards and committees and is well-prepared to represent District 94 in the State House.
North Carolina’s 94th House District includes Alexander County and the greater part of Wilkes County.
The primary election for NC House will be held on March 5, 2024.
