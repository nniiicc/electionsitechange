Student Teacher Promotion Act
I was a guest on "Lawmakers" with host Donna Lowry and Rep.
Imani Barnes to discuss education legislation, including my bill the Student Teacher Promotion Act (HB 310).
The Student Teacher Promotion Act would compensate college students for completing their student teaching.
Currently, student teaching requires an unpaid 40 hour-a-week internship in the classroom for one full semester or 500 total hours.
Student teachers would receive up to $7,500, removing unpaid full-time work as a barrier to aspiring educators.
