Legislative Update: 2/10 - 2/23
Promoting Education Solutions
I spent the past few weeks meeting with stakeholders and promoting two of my education bills: 1) HB 245, the "Georgia Educational Opportunity Act"; and 2) HB 310, the "Student Teacher Promotion Act".
From press conferences to panels, walking meetings with student teachers to co-hosting Afterschool Day at the Capitol, I have enjoyed discussing the significance of solutions to support our children, educators, and college students passionate about entering the teaching profession.
Special thanks to the following groups for your advocacy and support: Georgia Statewide Afterschool Network (GSAN), Professional Association of Georgia Educators (PAGE), and the Georgia Association of Educators (GAE).
