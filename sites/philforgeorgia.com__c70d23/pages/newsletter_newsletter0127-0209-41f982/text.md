I look forward to keeping you updated on their status.
HB 245 , the "Georgia Educational Opportunity Act", would provide additional state funds to local schools to support students living in poverty; HB 305 , the "Protect the Dream Act", would restrict the number of single-family homes a hedge fund or corporation could purchase in a county; HB 310 , the "Student Teacher Promotion Act", would compensate college students who have completed all of their coursework and are teaching full-time in schools; and HR 191 , would create a house committee to study how we can better leverage local fees to address housing affordability Press release and more information on the Protect the Dream Act below.
