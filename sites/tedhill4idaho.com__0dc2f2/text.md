Ted Hill People often ask me, ‘After 38 years of service in the US military, including three combat tours and 78 missions, and currently flying as a contract fighter pilot in the F-5 in support of training Navy fighter pilots, why pursue a role in the Idaho Legislature?’ My answer is simple: because service doesn’t stop.
I’ve dedicated my life to protecting our nation, and I want to continue that service by representing our community and fighting for what’s right in the Idaho Legislature.
Service is all that I know and I have more to give.
A Lifelong Conservative, Protecting and Defending Idaho Now more than ever, Idaho needs strong, decisive leadership.
We need someone unwavering in the defense of our rights, our liberties, and our Constitution.
Born and raised in Idaho, I am proud to be that leader.
When I entered the Navy, I swore an oath to defend the Constitution of the United States against all enemies, foreign and domestic.
That oath did not expire with my active duty service it binds me for life.
It would be my honor to continue serving this Republic as your State Representative, fighting every day for the people of Idaho.
I’ve seen firsthand the consequences of those unable to protect themselves.
It will be my duty to defend and protect your 2nd Amendment Rights.
