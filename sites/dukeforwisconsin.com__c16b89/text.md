Duke is currently serving in the Assembly for the 75th District of Wisconsin.
He is running for re-election to serve a second term.
The election is on November 3, 2026.
This is Duke’s campaign website.
Born and raised in the Trade Lake farming community in Burnett County.
After graduating from Grantsburg High School, Duke enlisted in the United States Air Force, where he proudly served in Desert Storm/Shield as a combat communications specialist.
Duke, with his wife Roxanne, proudly raised their two daughters in Grantsburg, Wis.
Duke has served as the Operations Manager at Grantsburg Telcom for 28 years and has been heavily involved in the community.
He feels strongly that service is essential to maintaining our rights and freedoms, and he is ready to begin a new chapter; he looks forward to serving the people in the 75th District.
