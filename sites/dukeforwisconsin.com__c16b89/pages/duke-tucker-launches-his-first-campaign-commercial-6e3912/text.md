campaign@DukeforWisconsin.com Facebook Facebook Home Biography District 75 Endorsements News The Issues Donate Contact Select Page Duke Tucker Launches His First Campaign Commercial by Admin01 | Aug 5, 2024 | News Duke Tucker | Press Release Monday, August 5, 2024 | 11:00 AM CDT [Grantsburg, Wis.] – Duke Tucker, a Republican from Grantsburg, WI launched his first campaign commercial for his run for State Assembly in the newly redrawn 75 th Assembly District.
In it, Duke highlights his commitment to freedom, family, and our future.
Duke is committed to protecting an individual’s rights and freedoms.
As a staunch supporter of the 2 nd Amendment, he is committed to responsible gun ownership and the right to defend yourself and your family.
Duke believes education should prepare students for success after graduation.
That means different things for different kids.
Whether a student chooses military service, trade school, or college, they should be prepared for success and graduate able to read and write, do math, and think for themselves.
If they choose to go to college or a trade school, they should be able to enter the work force and make a great living.
Duke supports farming in rural Wisconsin.
It is getting harder and harder to own and operate a farm.
Duke wants to work with farmers to help them thrive, not just barely survive.
He will work hard in Madison to make family farming achievable.
Duke is committed to fighting for lower taxes for Wisconsin.
That starts with holding Madison accountable.
We need a fiscally responsible representative that fights for small businesses, local economic growth, and prosperity.
Duke is a pro-life candidate and is committed to protecting the sanctity of life by standing up for the most vulnerable among us.
Duke Tucker is running for Assembly in the 75th Assembly District.
He is a conservative champion for our values and our future.
Together, we can build a brighter tomorrow for Wisconsin.
