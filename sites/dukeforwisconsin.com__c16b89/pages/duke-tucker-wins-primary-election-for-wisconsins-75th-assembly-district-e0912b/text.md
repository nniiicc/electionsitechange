Grantsburg, WI – August 14, 2024 – Duke Tucker has emerged victorious in the Primary Election for Wisconsin’s 75th Assembly District.
Tucker, a dedicated advocate for conservative values in rural Wisconsin, extends his deepest gratitude to the voters and their unwavering support.
Tucker also wishes to acknowledge his two opponents for their efforts and commitment to serving the people of the 75th District.
“I want to thank both Jay Calhoun and Neil Kline for running strong campaigns.
Their dedication to the issues facing our district is commendable, and I wish them both the very best in their future endeavors.”
With the primary behind him, Tucker is now looking ahead with enthusiasm to the November 5th General Election.
“This victory is just the beginning.
I am energized and ready to continue our campaign for positive change in the 75th Assembly District.
Together, we can build a brighter future for our community,” Tucker said.
The campaign will now focus on engaging with voters across the district, addressing the key issues that matter most, and ensuring that every voice is heard.
Tucker remains committed to working hard to represent the people of the 75th Assembly District.
