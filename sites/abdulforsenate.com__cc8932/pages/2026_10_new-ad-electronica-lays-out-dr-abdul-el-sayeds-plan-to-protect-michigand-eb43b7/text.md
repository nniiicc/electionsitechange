Abdul El-Sayed’s plan to take the AI industry on and ensure that the emerging technology serves the interests of Michiganders, not corporations and billionaires.
Abdul: “When the big corporations write their own rules, we all lose.” Michiganders know all too well what happens when career politicians like Mike Rogers let corporations write their own rules.
NAFTA, the disastrous trade policy which Mike Rogers supported , bankrupted Michigan communities and sent our manufacturing jobs overseas.
Now, unregulated AI expansion threatens to do the exact same thing.
Abdul is the only candidate in this race that has introduced a comprehensive plan to bring AI Under Control and ensure that Michiganders never have to face the hardships brought on by catastrophic policies like NAFTA ever again.

Create an AI kill switch to protect Americans from dangerous AI systems gone rogue .
Establish public ownership of frontier AI labs and implement strict AI No-Gos, including a ban on autonomous weapons and warrantless surveillance.

Establish severe criminal penalties for corporations and individuals abusing AI for malicious purposes.
