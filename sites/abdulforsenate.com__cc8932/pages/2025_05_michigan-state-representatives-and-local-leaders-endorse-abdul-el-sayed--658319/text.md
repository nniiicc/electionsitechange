Michigan State Representatives and Local Leaders Endorse Abdul El-Sayed for U.S.
Senate
Momentum builds as key elected officials back Abdul’s campaign
DETROIT, MI – U.S.
Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed is proud to announce endorsements from several Michigan State Representatives and local leaders, reflecting the growing grassroots momentum behind his campaign.
These new endorsements build on recently announced support from Wayne County Chief Executive Warren Evans and Flint Mayor Sheldon Neeley.
Abdul is proud to have earned the endorsements of:
- State Representative Erin Byrnes, House District 15 (D-Dearborn)
- State Representative Emily Dievendorf, House District 77 (D-Lansing)
- State Representative Alabas Farhat, House District 3 (D-Dearborn)
- State Representative Dylan Wegela, House District 26 (D-Garden City)
- State Representative Jimmie Wilson Jr., House District 32 (D-Ypsilanti)
- Former State Representative and Washtenaw County Commissioner Yousef Rabhi
“I’m humbled by the growing wave of support we’re earning from leaders who roll up their sleeves for their communities every single day,” said Abdul.
“These are organizers, bridge-builders, and fighters for working people, and I’m proud to be standing shoulder-to-shoulder with them.
Together, we’re building a people-powered campaign that’s flipping the script on politics as usual and putting Michiganders first.”
