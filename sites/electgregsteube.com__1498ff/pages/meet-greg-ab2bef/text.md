Greg Steube is an Army veteran and proven conservative who has spent his career defending Americans, protecting taxpayers, and cutting through government bureaucracy.
Service Before Politics ARMY VETERAN After September 11, Greg Steube answered America's call.
He joined the United States Army, served as an Airborne Infantry Officer and JAG Corps Officer, and deployed to Iraq.
That service shaped how he leads today.
Steube believes public office demands discipline, courage, and accountability.
He fights for the country he defended and the Southwest Florida community he calls home.
FIFTH GENERATION FLORIDIAN From Iraq to Congress, Greg Steube Never Stopped Serving A fifth-generation Floridian, Greg Steube earned his undergraduate and law degrees from the University of Florida.
After serving in the Florida House and Senate, Greg was elected to Congress in 2018.
A stalwart conservative, he has fought to cut taxes, protect seniors, support veterans, and defend women's sports.
Greg broke with party leadership to force a vote and secure historic hurricane tax relief for Floridians.
He is the only Republican member of Congress in more than four decades to author tax legislation signed into law by presidents of both parties.
Proven Service for Southwest Florida In Congress, Steube has delivered tax relief for hurricane victims, pressed federal agencies to move Southwest Florida recovery projects forward, defended constitutional rights, and fought to make government answer to the people it serves.
Greg Steube will bring that same mission-focused leadership to Fort Myers and every corner of CD 17.
“I didn’t go to Congress to talk about problems.
I went to solve them .” GREG STEUBE Greg Steube served America in uniform.
Now he serves Southwest Florida in Congress.
