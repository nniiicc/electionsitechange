The Issues Priorities for Bangor Real solutions require practical thinking, not partisan bickering.
Here is where I stand.
Lower the Cost of Living Maine families are being squeezed by inflation, high taxes, and rising energy costs.
We must rein in wasteful spending in Augusta and provide real tax relief so hardworking people can afford to live and retire here.
Common-Sense Regulation Our state government creates too much red tape that stifles innovation.
As a retired engineer, I know how to evaluate complex systems.
We need practical rules that protect our state without crushing enterprise.
Support Local Business Small businesses are the backbone of Bangor.
We need to create an economic environment that encourages entrepreneurship, rather than punishing job creators with endless fees and complex mandates.
Fiscal Responsibility It's simple: government shouldn't spend money it doesn't have.
I will scrutinize the budget with the eye of a businessman, cutting waste and ensuring every tax dollar serves a clear, necessary purpose.
Public Safety A thriving community requires safe streets.
We must support our local law enforcement and first responders, giving them the resources they need to tackle crime and the ongoing substance abuse crisis effectively.
Focus on Education Schools should be focused on core academics that prepare our kids for successful careers and trades.
We must empower parents and support teachers in delivering high-quality, practical education.
