Virginia Stapleton’s Campaign for Senate Launches with Powerful Momentum: 100+ Community Supporters; 100+ Dedicated Volunteers; Over $20,000 Raised
Salem, OR – The campaign to elect Virginia Stapleton to the Oregon State Senate, District 11, is off to a fast and energetic start.
In just a few weeks since filing, Virginia has secured 101 major organizational and community endorsements, including Rep.
Mark Gamba, Rep.
Paul Evans, Former Mayor Chris Hoy, and the Ironworkers Union.
Her campaign’s fundraising has topped $20,000 to date.
Virginia’s grassroots energy is strong, with 102 volunteers already signed up to knock doors, make calls, and connect with voters.
Meanwhile, the campaign is already solidifying intense support.
View more about Virginia’s campaign message, which is resonating with folks across the district.
“From day one, this campaign is about bringing people together to solve problems for working families,” said Stapleton.
“I am grateful for the outpouring of support from across Marion County and beyond.
Together, we are building a campaign rooted in trust, hard work, and results.”
With endorsements climbing, fundraising ahead of schedule, volunteers mobilized, and outreach underway, the Stapleton for Senate campaign is in full swing and ready to meet voters across Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Gervais and the surrounding communities.
