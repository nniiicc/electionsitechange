Voter Information
October 19 is the last day to register to vote for the November 3 election.
There are provisions to register at the same time as voting.
Why make it complicated?
If you can, register before October so you can get your sample ballots and actual ballots on the same schedule as everyone else.
Register to vote or check your status by clicking here.
October 5 is when primary ballots start to be mailed to all registered voters - another reason to register early.
Those ballots will have instructions how to return them.
There are several alternative methods.
The important thing is to return them early.
Voter information guides will also be mailed in October.
They are very useful in deciding your vote and cross outs are fine.
They also save a lot of time when actually marking your ballot, whether for the mail or in person at a voting station.
November 3 is the last day to return a ballot and the mail postmark must be November 3 or earlier.
Be early, don't wait.
If you are at the post office on November 3, ask for a manual postmark stamp.
