Thank you for considering this contribution.
This is definitely a grass roots campaign, with no affiliation or funding arrangement with either party.
Note: This campaign website does not collect or have access to your credit card payment information.
When you initiate a contribution, you are transferred to Stripe, a popular payment processing company common in stores and restaurants, both online and physical.
They process your contribution, take their fee, and send the net amount to the campaign checking account in a separate transaction a few days later.
Political campaign rules require the name, address, job, employer, and certain contact information, depending on the cumulative amount across multiple donations.
It is OK to say "retired" for both job and employer.
For reference, this campaign has a limit for individuals of $5900 as set by the State Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC).
Limits for other races and types of contributors such as PAC will vary.
