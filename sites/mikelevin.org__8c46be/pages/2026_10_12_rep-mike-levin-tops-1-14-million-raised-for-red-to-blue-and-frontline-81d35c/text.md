Mike Levin Tops $# Million Raised for Red to Blue and Frontline Candidates CARLSBAD, CA. – Rep.
Mike Levin (CA-49) has now raised more than $# million for Red to Blue and Frontline candidates across the country.
He has also paid his full $# in DCCC dues and raised an additional $# directly for the DCCC.
Levin reached these totals in less than a year, having begun this work only after California voters approved Proposition 50 on November 4, 2025.
Levin first won the seat as part of the 2018 Red to Blue class and went on to serve as a Frontline member in the 2020, 2022, and 2024 cycles, holding one of the most competitive districts in the nation.
“I know what it takes to win a tough race, because I’ve been there myself, more than once,” said Levin.
“Every dollar we raise helps elect candidates who will fight to lower costs, protect our democracy, and stand up to corruption in Washington.
I’m proud to do my part to help Democrats win back the House.” Levin said a Democratic majority would move quickly to undo the damage done by the Trump administration and its allies in Congress.
That starts with stopping the corrupt economic policies driving up prices on gas, diesel, groceries, housing, and health care.
It means ending the cruel, backwards cuts to Medicaid and the Affordable Care Act that have already cost millions of Americans their coverage.
And it means repealing H.R.
1, the Big Ugly Bill, which takes food off the table and health care away from working families to pay for massive tax cuts for billionaires.
Levin also called for ending Trump’s unauthorized war in Iran, holding Trump and his MAGA allies accountable for their corruption, protecting democracy and the right to vote, restoring women’s reproductive freedom, reversing the administration’s destructive attacks on our environment and climate, and bringing basic decency back to the way our government works.
“Families are paying more and getting less, while the people at the top keep getting richer,” said Levin.
“Winning back the House is how we start to fix that, and I’ll keep working every day until we do.” ### Thank you for visiting my campaign website.
