Michele Clark is ready to roll up her sleeves and get to work for the people of the 33rd Senate District.
She’s dedicated to ensuring that all residents, from young families to seniors, have the resources and support they need to thrive.
With her deep roots in the community and her commitment to fairness, Michele will be a voice for all who call this district home.
Michele’s Key Issues
- Government that Works for Taxpayers Michele supports term limits for state House and Senate leaders and ethics laws to ban Springfield politicians from working as lobbyists right after they leave office.
She also will fight to get dark money out of politics, because Illinoisans deserve a government that's working for them, not powerful insiders.
Michele also backs a law to ban elected officials from owning individual stocks, so that politicians can’t get rich off insider information and aren’t profiting from the companies they are supposed to be overseeing.
- Fighting Rising Costs Michele understands that the rising cost of living is a serious burden for Illinois families, particularly in the suburbs.
From grocery bills to childcare prices and property taxes, it feels like families are getting squeezed from every direction.
As your state senator, Michele will push for policies that help lower the cost of living and make Illinois more affordable for everyone.
- Childcare Affordability As a former childcare business owner, Michele Clark has seen firsthand the challenges that families face when trying to access affordable, high-quality childcare.
She knows how difficult it can be for parents to balance their careers and family responsibilities, especially when childcare costs are high and availability is limited.
- Standing Up For Seniors Seniors are facing the dual pressures of rising home insurance premiums and higher living costs.
Michele is committed to standing up for seniors, especially those on fixed incomes.
She supports policies that help freeze property taxes for senior homeowners, providing financial relief to those who have lived in the community for decades.
- Supporting Our Schools As a mom of 2 college aged daughters and an experienced youth development advocate, Michele understands that high quality public education is the foundation of healthy communities.
Michele will support and invest in our schools, stand up for educators, and expand youth programs.
Michele will work hard to protect funding for school breakfast and lunch programs, and stand strong against federal cuts that put children and families at risk.
- Keeping Communities Safe Michele supports more police funding and will block efforts to defund the police.
She will fight gun violence by backing the ban on assault weapons and untraceable ghost guns, get illegal guns off the street, and toughen penalties on criminals who bring in guns from nearby states.
- Investing In The Next Generation Young people are struggling to find their path to lucrative employment.
By expanding apprenticeship programs and strengthening partnerships between local businesses, trade schools and universities, Michele will help ensure that our young adults are equipped with the skills they need to succeed in today’s economy.
