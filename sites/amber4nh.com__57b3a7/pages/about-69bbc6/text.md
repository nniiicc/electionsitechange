I want New Hampshire to be a place where they can build their futures, put down roots, and truly thrive as they step into adulthood.
That vision starts with thoughtful, sustainable economic planning that makes our beautiful state more affordable and accessible.
To me, a strong community is not one where people are merely getting by, but one where they have the opportunity to grow, contribute, and succeed.
The programs and policies we invest in should help our neighbors excel—not just survive.
As a healthcare professional working in the nonprofit sector, I see the challenges Granite Staters face every day.
From limited provider access and gaps in insurance coverage to the need for stronger community supports, too many barriers stand between people and good health.
I believe that affordable, comprehensive healthcare—available across every stage of life—is foundational to a healthy, thriving community.
But healthcare alone is not enough.
People also need access to affordable housing, strong job opportunities, high-quality and affordable education, and a clean, healthy environment.
These are the building blocks of a community where families can flourish.
As State Representative for Belknap County District 1, I am committed to supporting legislation that advances these priorities—for the people who live here today and for those who will choose to stay, contribute, and help shape our shared future.
