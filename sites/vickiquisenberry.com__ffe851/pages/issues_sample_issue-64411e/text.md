A better educated workforce attracts higher-quality businesses.
Pre-K for All PreK for All is vital to increasing educational attainment and workforce development.
More than 50% of our children in Senate District 8 entering Kindergarten are not 'kindergarten ready'.
Funding state-wide Pre-K for all four-year-olds will add another year of learning to that more of our children are kindergarten ready and prepared to become more successful students.
Daycare and Pre-K Quality, affordable daycare is critical to providing working parents options for childcare and children a safe, secure environment.
While moving our children toward being better prepared for Kindergarten, we must also realize that four-year-olds often subsidize the younger children in a daycare center.
(State law requires more adults in child care rooms for infants than for four-year-olds.
If daycares charge the same for infants as older children, the older children subsidize the babies.) After meeting with and discussing daycare center financial struggles with numerous daycare center owners, it is imperative we develop a state-wide safety net to insure the financial viability of existing daycare centers as we create a Pre-K program to better educate our students.
Additionally, there is limited space in existing elementary schools to add Pre-K for all four-year-olds.
We need to explore public-private partnership opportunities to take advantage of daycare space that could be available for Pre-K and the possiblity of certifying current daycare center staff for public Pre-K and/or placing public educators in daycare centers to work exclusively with four-year-olds.
