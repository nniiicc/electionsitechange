Meet Vicki Issues Volunteer Contribute Meet Vicki Quisenberry Vicki and her husband, Tab, are the parents of four adult children and grandparents to three (with one due in May).
She is the daughter of former Superintendent of Owensboro Public Schools, Dr.
Billy Chandler, and Sandra Woodward Chandler.
Education and Work History Vicki graduated from Apollo High School and earned her Bachelor of Science in Computer Systems Management and Masters of Business Administration, both from Murray State University.
Following her undergraduate graduation, she worked as a computer consultant with Green River Area Development District and Lake Cumberland Area Development District before being hired as GRADD's first computer systems manager.
When she started working at GRADD, there were three word processors for the secretarial pool.
She supervised the installation of a personal computer in every office, worked with small towns and county governments to move from paper processing to computerization and trained staff in software navigation.
She worked at GRADD until shortly after the birth of their second child, opting to stay home with their girls due to inability to find adequate child care.
She worked as a part-time computer programmer with Don Moore Automotive Team.
Within a few years, two more children were added to the family.
Vicki was involved with many activities involving her children including classroom literacy and reading volunteer, PTO leadership in OPS and DCPS systems.
She thoroughly enjoyed her years as a recreational soccer coach for all four children.
When their children wanted to swim at the brand new HealthPark, Vicki became treasurer for the Owensboro Marlin swim team.
After realizing a Stroke and Turn officiating wasn't for her, she put her computer background to work and learned to operate the scoreboard and meet management systems, eventually becoming meet manager for USA Swimming meets and high school regional swim meets.
When two of her children went on to swim in college (DePauw University and Transylvania University), Vicki ran scoreboards/meet management systems at collegiate meets and conference meets.
When their children ran cross country and track, Vicki worked with coaches to organize parental support, timed at practices and meets and worked in the finish chute.
Two of their children went on to run D1 cross country (St.
Louis University and Seton Hall University).
She and Tab are fortunate to have been able to travel to many of their collegiate swim, cross country and track meets.
Vicki enjoyed being the unofficial team photographer for her children's sports, focusing on making sure each athlete had action photos to share on their social media and to be used at the year-end banquets.
Foundation for Daviess County Public Schools While her four children were attending DCPS, Vicki was asked to become a parent representative on the Foundation for Daviess County Public Schools (FDCPS) Board of Directors during the superintendency of Stu Silberman.
After serving on the board for several years, Vicki was asked to become Executive Director during the superintendancy of Dr.
Tom Shelton.
During her time with FDCPS, Vicki listened to the needs of teachers, gathered resources from the community and developed several programs to benefit students.
Just a few of these programs include Exploration Station , Kids on Campus ( Murray State , KWC, BU, USI, UofL, WKU, Bellarmine) and First Generation Scholars.
During her tenure, the Foundation funded a variety of needs: hotspots for students during Covid funded by Jagoe Homes and Southern Star; Black In Blue Dinner & a Movie , by Academy Award winning documentary film maker Paul Wagner, honoring Houston Hogg; private buses to transport fifth graders to Frankfort, Sorgho Learning Zone (with First Security Bank), connecting local companies like Southern Star with needs in schools.
She promoted Distinguished Alumni to celebrate the achievements of DCPS alumni like U.
S.
Senator Wendell Ford , and Dr.
Mark Lynn and Rod Kuegel , Loretta Dunn Schmitzer, Chrisopher Payne and more.
Empowerment Academy Through her work with the Foundation, Vicki became aware of high school students living in inadequate situations that keeps them from meeting their educational goals.
She founded the Empowerment Academy (EA), a registered 501(c)3 organization, with the mission to provide long-term safe housing with a focus on educational attainment and life skills development.
She and the EA board of directors have been able to gather materials, supplies and funding to create an 18 bedroom, six bath home that opened in January 2026.
Johnson, Depp and Quisenberry In 2017, Tab and Vicki purchased the iconic Johnson, Depp and Quisenberry on Frederica.
After a year of cleaning out all of the items left by the previous occupants and another year renovating the 1958 Mid-Century Modern building, the commercial office complex was ready to open.
Before long, the back building was occupied by a glass and glazing business while the main building was occupied with an architecture firm, an insurance group and a home healthcare and hospice agency.
Soon mechanical, electrical and plumbing engineers moved in along with a technology company, a physician with a yoga studio, a financial advising firm and a personal trainer/massage therapist.
Then came the commercial cleaning company, the construction business, a vehicle glass replacement business (in the back) and a fitness business.
The conference room and terrace have been occupied by numerous business lunch & learns, strategic planning sessions, regional sales meetings and other business gatherings.
At night and on the weekends, the JDQ Conference Room hosts baby, bridal and wedding showers, rehearsal dinners, engagement parties, adult birthday celebrations, end-of-life family celebrations, photographers and much more!
Vicki and Tab enjoy working with entrepreneurs to create and grow their businesses.
Vicki has a proven history of identifying issues, coordinating resources and addressing real world issues.
