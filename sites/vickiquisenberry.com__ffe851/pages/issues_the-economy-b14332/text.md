As a business owner who specializes in working with entrepreneurs to start their small businesses, Vicki understands the challenges.
Working with international clients, national firms and local entrepreneurs to provide high quality, yet affordable commercial rental space has given her a unique perspective on creating solutions for a variety of business owners.
She was general contractor on a full renovation (roof, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, windows, walls) project of a 12,000 sf 1958 historic landmark in Owensboro and currently working on renovation of an 8,600 sf building to house local entrepreneurs.
As a local non-profit leader, Vicki can testify to the fact that the most successful projects benefitting the community come from people with a variety of backgrounds who believe in the same mission.
While Executive Director of the Foundation for Daviess County Public Schools (FDCPS), Vicki worked diligently to put local resources into classrooms to benefit at-risk students, provide educational enhancements not funded by tax dollars and gathering local investments for unfunded mandates put in place by the Kentucky legislature.
While working with FDCPS, Vicki became aware of the need in Daviess County for long-term housing for insufficiently housed and homeless high school students.
She set up the Empowerment Academy, a 501(c)3 non-profit to address this need not only for students in Daviess County Public Schools, but also for Owensboro Public School students.
