Issues Contribute EARLY VOTING STARTS IN October 19, 2026 at 7:00 AM My name is Ebony Turner and I am running for Texas House District 96.
I am a 25-year practicing attorney.
My 2nd job out of law school was at Texas Legislative Council drafting bills for the Members of the Texas Legislature.
While I have legislative experience drafting hundreds of bills, some of which became laws, the main reason I'm running is my son, Isaiah.
Isaiah was diagnosed with Down Syndrome 9 weeks postpartum.
Since then, he has had heart surgery and faced additional challenges.
One of those challenges has been in the special education system in our public school.
We have asked for certain resources to help him thrive and have been told repeatedly that funding just wasn't there.
Our story isn't unique.
We have heard similar experiences and stories from parents of special needs children all over the State of Texas.
I need you to vote to send me to Austin so I can fight for these and other issues because it's not political, it's P.E.R.S.O.N.A.L.
Vote EB for HD96!
