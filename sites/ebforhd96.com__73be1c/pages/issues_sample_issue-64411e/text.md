E.
Educational Excellence for All Students
- Special emphasis on ensuring children with Special Needs have all of the resources they need to be as successful as possible.
- Fully fund education; Pay raises for Teachers, hiring more paras.
- Stiffer penalties for school districts that don't follow IEPs.
- Mandatory information at all levels for college-bound students with IEPs and 504 plans.
- Require all schools in District to offer same special education services to ensure continuity of services.
- Mandatory special education training for all teachers.
