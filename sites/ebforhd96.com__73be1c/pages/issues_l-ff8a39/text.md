Livable Wages for All Texans
- At my first job, I made $4.25/hr and now 34 years later, minimum wage is only $7.25.
That doesn't make dollars and it surely doesn't make sense!
So I am going to push for increasing the minimum wage so that all Texans can make a living to be able to buy a house, afford healthcare, send their kids to college, take vacations, and live the proverbial American Dream.
