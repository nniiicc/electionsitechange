No Vouchers
- Repeal the Voucher Law.
The rollout of this law has already proven what we predicted-vouchers are no more than a coupon for the rich as more than 75% of the applicants are for students already in private schools.
Vouchers divert public funds from public schools which will ultimately disproportionately negatively affect children with Special needs and the resources available to them.
