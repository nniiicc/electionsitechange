Ebony Turner Speaks Out Against Tarrant County Polling Place Closures 60 Days Before Election
MANSFIELD, TEXAS - With just 60 days until Election Day, Democratic Texas House District 96 candidate Ebony Turner is speaking out against the closure of 92 polling locations in Tarrant County, particularly when those decisions impact Black and Brown communities, and students at the University of Texas at Arlington.
“When polling locations are closed or moved in communities that already face barriers to voting, we have to ask who is being impacted and whether every voter is being given a fair opportunity to participate in our democracy,” Turner said.
Turner is particularly concerned about the impact of polling-place changes in historically underrepresented communities and young voters, including the thousands of students who attend the University of Texas at Arlington.
“UTA students are voters.
People in Black and Brown communities are voters.
Every eligible voter deserves reasonable access to the ballot box,” Turner said.
“We should not be creating additional obstacles for people who are already balancing work, school, family responsibilities, transportation challenges and other demands.”

EBONY TURNER FILES FOR TEXAS HOUSE DISTRICT 96
Mansfield, TX — November 26, 2025 — Attorney and community advocate, Ebony M.
Turner, has officially filed for Texas House District 96.
Turner previously ran for this seat in the last election cycle, and now enters the race as the district prepares for new leadership with Representative David Cook announcing that he will not seek re-election to the seat.
Turner, known for her work expanding access to justice, championing community-based solutions, and fighting for special needs students and parents, said she is running to bring her passion and fierce advocacy for these issues to the forefront in Austin.
“House District 96 deserves a representative who listens, leads with integrity, shows up for the people, and is accountable to the people—not political interests,” Turner said.
Turner’s campaign will focus on the following issues that are personal to the citizens of HD 96:
- Lower, fairer property taxes that keep families, seniors, and homeowners secure;
- Fully funded public schools, including opposition to private school vouchers and full support for teachers and students;
- Greater support for special needs students, ensuring every child receives the education, support services, therapies, and dignity they deserve;
- Affordable health care and expanded access to essential services;
- Expanded economic opportunity for workers, small businesses, and entrepreneurs; and
- Livable wages that allow every family to thrive.
Upcoming Endorsements
Turner’s campaign is building a broad coalition of supporters.
Official endorsements will be announced soon, including: Local elected officials; Community leaders; Education advocates; Business leaders; and Grassroots and Civic organizations.
House District 96 encompasses all of Kennedale and parts of Mansfield, Arlington, Burleson, Crowley, Fort Worth, and Rendon.
For more information or to get involved, visit www.ebforhd96.com.
About Ebony Turner
Ebony M.
Turner is a 24-year practicing attorney, which includes being a former Texas legislative attorney who drafted hundreds of bills.
She is an advocate and community leader with a deep commitment to the residents of Texas House District 96.
She has built her career on ensuring justice for marginalized individuals and more recently, training the next generation of legal advocates through the internship program she founded.
Turner lives in Mansfield, Texas, with her family, including her son, Isaiah, who has Down Syndrome, and who she considers to be her inspiration for seeking this position.
