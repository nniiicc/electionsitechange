Ashburn, VA – Yesterday, the NewDem Action Fund announced the endorsement of Suhas Subramanyam in Virginia’s 10th Congressional District. https://newdemactionfund.com/candidates "Today, I am thrilled to announce our endorsement by the NewDems, a group of over 100 pragmatic members of Congress who collaborate across the aisle to deliver for the American people.
In the Virginia General Assembly, I was proud to be a co-founder of the bipartisan Commonwealth Caucus, where we worked together as moderate Democrats and Republicans on behalf of all Virginians.
At the national level, I look forward to securing our border, building our economy, combating climate change, and many other issues where we can find common ground for all Americans,” said Subramanyam. https://x.com/VoteNewDems/status/1818258017327906888 ### Senator Suhas Subramanyam is the Democratic nominee for Congress in Virginia’s 10th Congressional District to replace outgoing Congresswoman Jennifer Wexton.
Suhas has dedicated his life to public service as a State Senator, Obama White House advisor, Capitol Hill staffer, and as a Loudoun volunteer firefighter/EMT.
In Richmond, he has worked across the aisle to pass gun violence prevention bills, protect democracy, and defend abortion rights.
Suhas resides in Ashburn, Virginia (in VA-10) with his wife, Miranda, and their two daughters.
