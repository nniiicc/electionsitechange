NewDems endorse Suhas Subramanyam for Congress.
Ashburn, VA – Yesterday, the NewDem Action Fund announced the endorsement of Suhas Subramanyam in Virginia’s 10th Congressional District. https://newdemactionfund.com/candidates
"Today, I am thrilled to announce our endorsement by the NewDems, a group of over 100 pragmatic members of Congress who collaborate across the aisle to deliver for the American people.
In the Virginia General Assembly, I was proud to be a co-founder of the bipartisan Commonwealth Caucus, where we worked together as moderate Democrats and Republicans on behalf of all Virginians.
At the national level, I look forward to securing our border, building our economy, combating climate change, and many other issues where we can find common ground for all Americans,” said Subramanyam. https://x.com/VoteNewDems/status/1818258017327906888
