Statement on vandalism of BAPS Temple in Melville, NY
Subramanyam condemns acts of hate, calls for peace.
Ashburn, VA – “I am dismayed by the anti-Hindu vandalism attack on the Bochasanwasi Shri Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS) Temple in Melville, NY yesterday.
Hate has no place in our country.
I join BAPS in condemning this act and calling for peace, respect, and unity,” said Virginia State Senator Suhas Subramanyam.
