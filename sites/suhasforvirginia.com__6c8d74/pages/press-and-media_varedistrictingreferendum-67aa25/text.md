Congressman Suhas Subramanyam issued the following statement: Virginians have spoken, and the message is clear: we support evening the playing field.
Voters approved a temporary measure to respond to what President Trump started in Texas and other red states.
I look forward to running in the redrawn 10th Congressional District and taking back the House majority this fall.
Together, we will bring down costs, expand healthcare access, and hold this corrupt administration accountable.
### Congressman Suhas Subramanyam represents Virginia’s 10th Congressional District in Congress.
He was the first Indian-American elected to represent the East Coast in Congress.
Suhas is dedicated to serving Virginians, supporting working families, and protecting federal workers.
He has worked tirelessly to do so since being sworn into Congress in January, 2025.
Suhas resides in Ashburn, Virginia (in VA-10) with his wife Miranda and their two daughters.
