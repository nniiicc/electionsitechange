Supervisor Tekrony represents about 55,000 people in Loudoun County.
"I’m thrilled to endorse my next Member of Congress, Suhas Subramanyam,” said Supervisor Tekrony.
“He has been a leader fighting to stop gun violence, protect democracy, and conserve our environment in the state legislature - and most importantly, he gets things done for our community.
Suhas is going to make a fantastic Member of Congress for Loudoun County and the surrounding areas.” Supervisor Tekrony has demonstrated her belief in the value of public service through her volunteer work, including being involved with the parent-teacher associations at her children’s schools, serving on the Aldie Heritage Association Board of Directors and being appointed to serve on two Loudoun County advisory boards – the Parks, Recreation and Open Space Board and the Heritage Commission.
Supervisor Tekrony joins Loudoun County School Board Chair Melinda Mansfield, Asian American Action Fund, Indian American Impact Fund and Hindu American PAC in solely supporting Subramanyam’s candidacy.
Senator Subramanyam has represented parts of the Little River district for over 4 years in the state legislature.
### Senator Suhas Subramanyam has dedicated his life to public service as a State Senator, Obama White House advisor, Capitol Hill staffer, and as a Loudoun volunteer firefighter/EMT.
In Richmond, he has worked across the aisle to pass gun violence prevention bills, protect democracy, and defend abortion rights.
Senator Subramanyam is best positioned to keep VA-10 in Democratic hands because he represents more VA-10 constituents than any of the 16 primary election candidates, and he has consistently outperformed the Democratic ticket and delivered for his constituents.
Suhas resides in Ashburn, Virginia (in VA-10) with his wife, Miranda, and their two daughters.
