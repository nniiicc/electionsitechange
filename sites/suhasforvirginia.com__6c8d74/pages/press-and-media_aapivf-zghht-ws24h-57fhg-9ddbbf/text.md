Loudoun Career Fire Fighters Endorse Senator Suhas Subramanyam for Congress
Fire Fighters Unite Behind Sen.
Subramanyam, Citing His “Impeccable Voting Record”
Ashburn, VA – Today, Senator Suhas Subramanyam, Democratic Nominee for Virginia’s 10th Congressional District (VA-10), announced the endorsement of the International Association of Fire Fighters (IAFF) Local 3756, the Loudoun Career Fire Fighters Association.
This endorsement marks the first public safety association that has endorsed one of the candidates for Virginia’s 10th Congressional District.
"The Loudoun Career Firefighters Association IAFF Local 3756 is proud to endorse State Senator Suhas Subramanyam for the 10th US Congressional District race,” said IAFF 3756 PAC Chairman Jeremy Mader.
“Senator Subramanyam, a volunteer firefighter/EMT, has a long track record of supporting Virginia's firefighters and 911 dispatchers at the state level.
Senator Subramanyam has an impeccable voting record on supporting firefighter related bills throughout his time serving Loudoun County while in Richmond.
We look forward to continuing working with Senator Subramanyam while he represents Virginia in the US Congress."
“I am proud to be endorsed by our career firefighters here in Loudoun,” said Virginia State Senator Suhas Subramanyam.
“Nothing is more important than keeping our communities safe, and we owe career firefighters our gratitude for all that they do.
I will continue to be their champion in Congress.”
