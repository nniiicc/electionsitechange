Josh is a proud product of Valley public schools and is incredibly grateful for the great education he received.
Like so many parents in our community, Josh is deeply concerned about what the pandemic has done to our kids.
That’s why he spoke out early and often to get kids back in classrooms once it was safe and he’s working hard to direct more funding for our kids’ mental health.
Josh is also a strong proponent of trade and skills education so every kid can graduate with a career ahead of them whether they plan on going to college or not.
