The event will happen at a real drive-in theater – where the Congressman’s image will be projected onto the screen answering questions, and each attendee will have access to a radio which will transmit audio.
Attendees will have the opportunity to submit questions using their phones.
The event will kick off with a live performance by local musicians.
Interested constituents are asked to RSVP at this link: https://bit.ly/3lKwxJs .

8th 7:30-9:00pm (Gates open at 6:00pm) Members of the media are invited to attend.
Please RSVP to Ian@HarderforCongress.com .
Cars will be spaced out to accommodate social distancing, and guests are asked to wear a mask if they exit their vehicle to purchase concessions or use the restroom.
Congressman Harder has been named one of the most accessible members of Congress by the nonpartisan Town Hall Project, a nonprofit dedicated to increasing accessibility among elected officials.
