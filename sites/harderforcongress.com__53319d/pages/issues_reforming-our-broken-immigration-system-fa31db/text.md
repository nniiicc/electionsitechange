Josh knows our country draws its strength from our diversity.
Here in the Valley over 10,000 Dreamers are stuck in the shadows.
Josh is a proud co-sponsor of the DREAM Act and believes they must have a pathway to citizenship.
He also believes we can’t just stop there – Josh is a vocal advocate for real, comprehensive immigration reform to give immigrants and their families the protections they need to participate fully in our community.
Josh also knows strong border security and enhanced technology are vital for protecting our country from drug smugglers and human traffickers.
He believes both border security and immigration reform are important pieces to our country’s success.
