He and his wife, Stephanie, raised their three children right here in our district. ​ Mike is a former member of both the Florida House and Senate, where he served as President of the Senate.
Now a member of Congress, Mike serves as Chairman of the Space Committee and Whip of the Financial Services Committee. ​ In Congress, he focuses his efforts on supporting our space industry, improving the water quality of our Indian River Lagoon, fighting to protect election integrity, banning insider trading in Congress, and expanding access to affordable housing.
Endorsements Mike is honored to have the endorsement of President Trump, Governor DeSantis, Senator Scott, Senator Rubio, Speaker Mike Johnson, Congressman Jim Jordan, and our local sheriffs, among dozens of others.
These endorsements reflect his commitment to conservative values and effective leadership.
