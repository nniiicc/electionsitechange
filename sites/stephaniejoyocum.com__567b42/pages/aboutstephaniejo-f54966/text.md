Education and Employment A Hoosier through and through - I graduated from Marion High School in Marion, IN (Go Giants!) and attended Hanover College in Hanover, IN.

I serve as the Chair of the Governor-appointed Commission on Service and Volunteerism for the state of Indiana, Serve Indiana, and I actively advocate for AmeriCorps at the federal level.
