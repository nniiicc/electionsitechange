Announcements & Upcoming Events Affordability Everyday costs are putting real pressure on families across our communities.
Basic necessities—like utilities—have become far too expensive, stretching household budgets and making it harder for people to plan for the future.
Healthcare costs are another major burden.
No one should have to worry that getting the care they need will create financial hardship, yet for many families, that is the reality they face today.
We must continue pushing for practical solutions that make healthcare more accessible and affordable.
No family should have to go into bankruptcy because of the medical debt incurred by a sick child or family member.
And of course, we all know that housing remains a serious challenge.
Families are struggling to find homes they can afford, and young people who want to build their lives here often feel they have no place to start.
These issues should not be political—they are everyday realities for all of us.
Addressing the cost of living, improving healthcare affordability, and tackling our housing challenges must remain top priorities.
Suzanne seeks answers and knows how to define the problems clearly so that we can solve them.
