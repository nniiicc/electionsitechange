It is a time of coming together, reflecting on the year, and showing support for those who have dedicated themselves to serving our community.
In that spirit, I am excited to spend time with you at Team Cabral’s first Holiday Fundraiser on Wednesday, December 6th, 6:00 – 8:00 pm, at the Wamsutta Club in New Bedford.
I hope you will join us at this festive “Jingle and Mingle” event, filled with goodwill, community spirit, and support for the work of Team Cabral.
Your support will help my fight for New Bedford and the South Coast.
As your State Representative, I’m in the fight for: supporting New Bedford as the #1 Commercial Fishing Port. expanding universal pre-kindergarten. stimulating housing production in Gateway Cities. developing offshore wind and clean energy. redistributing local aid more equitably to Gateway Cities. modernizing public meetings and the Open Meeting Law. improving consumer protection. securing a municipal minimum wage. seeing South Coast Rail finally launch service in New Bedford.
I look forward to celebrating the spirit of the season and the promise of a better, stronger New Bedford with you.
