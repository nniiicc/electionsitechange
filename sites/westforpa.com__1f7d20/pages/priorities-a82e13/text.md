The governing test Start with the questions that matter.
Thomas approaches each priority by asking what problem a proposal solves, what it costs, who pays, who benefits, and how the public will know whether it worked.
01 Affordable Living ↓ 02 Safe Communities ↓ 03 Responsible Growth ↓ 01 Priority 01 Affordable Living Families should not be priced out of the communities they built.
The principle Thomas will listen first, ask what proposals cost and who pays, and show the work behind the choices that affect local families.
Questions Thomas asks What will this cost local families?
Who pays—and who benefits?
How does the proposal help people stay in the communities they built?
