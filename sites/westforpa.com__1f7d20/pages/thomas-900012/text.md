Thomas West is a Pennsylvania native, small-business owner, and former television news producer running for State Senate in District 38.
A practical approach Public service should begin with careful questions—and stay accountable for the result.
Thomas West is a Pennsylvania native and Highland Park resident.
His approach to leadership is direct: listen to the people affected, verify the facts, explain the tradeoffs, and follow through.
That means asking what a decision costs, who pays, who benefits, what problem it solves, and how the public will know whether it worked.
Experience Work that rewards preparation and follow-through.
01 Small business More than a decade in business.
For more than a decade, Thomas has run TRIM Pittsburgh.
The work keeps practical questions close.
Costs, customers, employees, and rules all matter—and decisions have to work in practice, not only on paper.
02 Local journalism Fifteen years asking better questions.
Before business, Thomas spent 15 years producing local television news.
That work reinforced habits he still brings to public questions: verify the facts, make complicated issues understandable, and explain why a decision matters.
