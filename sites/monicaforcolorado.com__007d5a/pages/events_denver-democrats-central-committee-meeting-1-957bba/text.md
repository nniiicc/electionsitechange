The Democratic Party of Denver Central Committee meets multiple times throughout the year.
This meeting is in-person only.
All members of the Central Committee are required to attend or delegate their vote to a proxy.
Members of the public are welcome to attend, but cannot participate in any of the proceedings.
At this meeting, the Denver Democrats will vote on a number of items including whether to adopt an oppose or support stance on Denver ballot initiatives.
