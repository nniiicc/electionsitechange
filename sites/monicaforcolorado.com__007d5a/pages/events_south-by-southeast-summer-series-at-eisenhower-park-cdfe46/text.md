Eisenhower park within the South by Southeast Summer Series is CANCELLED.
South by Southeast was developed after an extensive visioning series to find out what southeast Denver residents wanted to see in the district.
Community members expressed a strong desire for improved restaurants and retail, walkability and bikeability, and a community gathering place.
While the city continues to work to create this grand vision for the area, planning and development can take a very long time.
So, these festivals showcase the possibilities and opportunities available in southeast Denver.
