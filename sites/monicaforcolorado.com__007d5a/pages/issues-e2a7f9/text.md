When is the Colorado general election, and how do I vote in HD9?
Ballots drop in the mail on October 12th, 2026.
They are due back via mail or in a ballot drop box by 7pm on Tuesday, November 3rd, which is Election Day.
House District 9 covers Virginia Village, Glendale, Four Mile, Goldsmith, Holly Hills, Hampden Heights, Virginia Vale, and Cook Park.
Monica's race to be District 9’s Representative is underneath Emily Sirota’s race for the State Senate, so vote all the way down to Representative.
Check your registration or find your nearest drop box at GoVoteColorado.gov .
