Join Monica to tailgate for dads on Father’s Day during the Denver Onyx tailgate and home game.
The Onyx are Colorado’s only professional rugby team and the reigning national champions.
This week’s tailgate is specially designed to be family-friendly and to celebrate dad.
We’ll have bubbles, sidewalk chalk, poster making, food, juice boxes and someone who’s NOT a dad manning the grill so he can relax and enjoy a sports day.
Tailgate is in the parking lot northeast of the stadium and starts two hours before kickoff at 1pm.
Tickets for the game which kicks off at 3pm are available at https://www.universe.com/events/denver-onyx-vs-ny-exiles-tickets-2YTD5W
