Monica will join the Colorado Consumer Health Initiative (CCHI) Community Voice Conference to listen and learn.
The event is hosted by one of Monica’s favorite advocacy groups: CCHI.
Join CCHI for our annual Community Voices Conference (CVC), a space for community members to learn about and practice storytelling as a means for personal, social and political change.
This year’s theme: Courage to Speak, Power to Stay.
In a moment when the ground feels like it’s shifting beneath us, CVC is about finding the courage to tell your story anyway, and using that story to stay rooted, connected, and ready to act in your community.
The conference will feature a keynote speaker, small‑group workshops on advocacy and storytelling, a panel discussion, and dedicated time for lunch and networking.
While we appreciate the continued support from our partner advocacy organizations, we ask that no more than two members from other advocacy organizations attend to preserve the intention of this conference as a space primarily for community members.
Registration required: https://cohealthinitiative.org/events/community-voices-conference-2026/ stipends available for community members to attend.
