Bringing your voice to the Capitol
I believe that democracy works only when the people's voice is heard.
In this time of polarization it seems politicians have lost sight of this.
Instead of focusing on issues that directly impact the working class, we have become divided on culture war issues, which only distract us from the struggles the average person faces in their daily life.
This is why I want to hear from as many of you as possible.
So that I can take your concerns with me to the Capitol to ensure that working class needs are met and not just the needs of big business.

This world in arms is not spending money alone.
