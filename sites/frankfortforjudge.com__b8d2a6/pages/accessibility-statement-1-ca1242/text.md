Accessibility Statement
This statement was last updated on August 8, 2025.
We at Todd Frankfort for Justice, First Court of Appeals, Place 3, are working to make our site frankfortforjudge.com accessible to people with disabilities.
Our Commitment
The Todd Frankfort for Justice, First Court of Appeals, Place 3 campaign strives to ensure that our website complies with best practices and standards as defined by Section 508 of the U.S.
Rehabilitation Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.
These standards and guidelines help make web content more accessible for all people, including those with disabilities such as:
- Visual impairments
- Hearing impairments
- Mobility impairments
- Cognitive disabilities
- Speech disabilities
Measures Taken
To ensure accessibility, our website implements various measures, including but not limited to:
- Used the Accessibility Wizard to find and fix potential accessibility issues
- Set the language of the site
- Set the content order of the site’s pages
- Defined clear heading structures on all of the site’s pages
- Added alternative text to images
- Implemented color combinations that meet the required color contrast
- Reduced the use of motion on the site
- Ensured all videos, audio, and files on the site are accessible
Conformance Status
The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities.
It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.
The Todd Frankfort for Justice, First Court of Appeals, Place 3 campaign website strives to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.
Level AA conformance means that the website satisfies all the Level A and Level AA Success Criteria, or that an accessible alternative is provided.

Compatibility with Browsers and Assistive Technology
The Todd Frankfort for Justice, First Court of Appeals, Place 3 campaign website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:
- Screen readers (including JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack)
- Speech recognition software
- Screen magnification software
- Keyboard-only navigation
The website is also designed to be compatible with the following browsers:
- Google Chrome (latest 2 versions)
- Mozilla Firefox (latest 2 versions)
- Apple Safari (latest 2 versions)
- Microsoft Edge (latest 2 versions)
Assessment and Ongoing Improvements
The Todd Frankfort for Justice, First Court of Appeals, Place 3 campaign is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and in doing so adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines.
This website is regularly tested using a combination of automated testing tools and manual testing with assistive technologies.
As new content is added to the website, it is reviewed for accessibility compliance.
Texas-Specific Accessibility Compliance
This website aims to comply with the Texas Administrative Code, Title 1, Part 10, Chapter 206, Subchapter C, which establishes accessibility requirements for state websites, and extends these standards to our campaign website as a best practice.
We recognize the importance of making electronic and information resources accessible to all Texans, including those with disabilities.
Limitations
While we strive to ensure that our website is accessible, there may be some limitations.
The following limitations are currently known:
- Some older content may not yet be fully accessible, but we are working to address these issues
- Third-party content that is not under our control may not be fully accessible
We are committed to addressing these limitations and improving the accessibility of our website over time.
