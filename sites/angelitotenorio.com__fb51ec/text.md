ABOUT ME
Putting My Experience to Work
As a former soldier in the Wisconsin Army National Guard, I firsthand understand the importance of servant leadership.
It's that same sense of duty and service that motivates me to run for Wisconsin State Assembly.
As former Engagement and Development Manager with Wisconsin Conservation Voters, I have the knowledge and experience necessary to advocate and fight for the natural world around us.
My servant leadership on the West Allis Common Council has also prepared me to be effective in the legislature.
From leading the campaign to ban LGBTQ+ conversion therapy in West Allis, to passing a resolution supporting our Asian American Pacific Islander community, I have the drive and experience to make West Allis a better place for all to live and thrive.
I believe in fighting to make healthcare more accessible, advocating for reproductive rights, working to make our community safer, and investing in the future of West Allis and our great state.
I believe in the power of people from all backgrounds coming together to make a difference for the better.
