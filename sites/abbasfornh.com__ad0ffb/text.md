STATE SENATOR DARYL ABBAS STATE SENATE DISTRICT 22 Learn More DONATE District 22: Atkinson, Pelham, Plaistow and Salem About ABOUT Daryl Abbas serves as Senate President Pro Tempore in the New Hampshire Senate, representing the communities of Atkinson, Pelham, Plaistow, and Salem.
An attorney and lifelong Salem resident, he is committed to protecting taxpayers, strengthening public safety, and preserving the New Hampshire Advantage. ​ Salem has always his home.
Daryl and his wife, Jessica, chose to build their life together on Canobie Lake, where they are raising their two young children.
Like so many families in the district, they want to ensure their children have the same opportunities and quality of life that made Southern New Hampshire such a wonderful place to grow up.
