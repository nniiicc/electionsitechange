Meet kerry
Kerry Forestal is a lifelong public servant, a proven leader, and a strong Democrat who has spent his career standing up for the people of Indiana.
He currently serves as Marion County Sheriff, where he has led with integrity, accountability, and a clear commitment to public safety.
Before that, Kerry served as U.S.
Marshal for the Southern District of Indiana after being appointed by President Barack Obama, bringing steady, disciplined leadership to one of the nation’s most important law enforcement roles.
Throughout his career, Kerry has built a reputation for doing the job the right way and following through on what he says.
He believes public service is about protecting families, earning trust, and delivering real results for the people you serve.
Now, Kerry is running for State Senate to bring that same record of leadership to the Statehouse as a strong Democratic voice for Hoosier families.
He is ready to stand up to the Republican supermajority and fight for safe schools, lower health care and child care costs, and stronger accountability for utility companies that keep raising costs on working people.
Kerry Forestal knows who he is fighting for, and he is ready to keep fighting for us.
