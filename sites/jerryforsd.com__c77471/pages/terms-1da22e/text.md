Program Description.
Friends of Jerry Jongeling operates an SMS messaging program to keep supporters informed about the campaign.
By opting in, you consent to receive recurring autodialed text messages from Friends of Jerry Jongeling.
Consent is not a condition of any purchase or contribution.
Types of Messages.
Subscribers may receive text messages including, but not limited to: campaign updates and news, volunteer opportunities, event invitations and reminders, voting information (including dates, times, and polling locations for upcoming elections), get-out-the-vote reminders, fundraising appeals, and other communications related to the Friends of Jerry Jongeling campaign.
