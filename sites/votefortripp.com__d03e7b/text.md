Meet Tripp
Tripp Charles Hutchinson is a born and raised Idaho local and City Councilman.
He is fed up with careerist, elitist politicians selling out the working class and families for their own personal gain in our nation’s capital.
It is time for leaders, from all walks of life, with morality and integrity to enter the arena and fight for independent business, rural land and self determination for all citizens.
This is a grassroots campaign that will never accept money from billionaire and corporate PACs.
Where I Stand
Every Idaho family should be able to live a normal, fulfilled and decent life on one household income.
This was once a reality in America.
The mechanic or hard-working waitress was able to own a home and support a family on that income alone.
For decades we have allowed the greed of the few to ruin the livelihoods of the many.
Today, both parents are forced to work, not for a happy life, just to decide if they can pay for daycare or put food on the table.
It does not have to be this way.
We currently have a government of, by, and for the wealthy elites and multinational corporations.
Below are my commitments to getting the United States of America back to a government of, by and for the people.
