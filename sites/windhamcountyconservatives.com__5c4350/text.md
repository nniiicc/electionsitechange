If this photo looks familiar, it’s the same one I used two years ago during my Lynne Wilkins campaign for Vermont State Representative from District 4, which includes Putney and Dummerston.
This election cycle, I am running again for the same seat.
I live in Putney, have raised four children here, and enjoy a happy marriage of over twenty-five years.
As a retired individual with a deep vested interest in the well-being of my community, I am committed to serving the people of Windham County.
As I campaigned door to door, I heard from many residents who feel hesitant to share their traditional conservative perspectives due to concerns about cultural estrangement and potential backlash.
Many do not agree with the current trends affecting school kids, young adults, business owners, property owners, and our traditional ways in Vermont.
Vermont recognizes three major parties: Republican, Democrat, and Progressive.
As a Constitutional conservative, I am proud to run as a Republican.
I believe that conservatism has played a vital role in shaping the Vermont many of us have cherished for generations and the Vermont that attracts new residents.
I truly believe that it is conservative values that can best protect the essence of Vermont for future generations.
