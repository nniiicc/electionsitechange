CITATION REQUEST
Make your request at least two weeks before the print or event deadline.
Requests can be made by this online form.
Please include all necessary information in your request:
- Your contact information (phone, email, mailing address)
- The date of the event
- The date you wish printed on the citation
- The name of the individual(s) or organization being honored
- The reason for recognition (1-2 sentences maximum)
Please note: the Office of State Representative Michael J.
Soter maintains the discretion to determine whether a citation request will be fulfilled.
