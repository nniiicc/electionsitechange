Central Coast Families Deserve Better Than Rhetoric on Healthcare
Independent.com
By Bob Smith
The Santa Barbara Independent published a full-length op-ed today by retired U.S.
Navy Commander and Republican congressional candidate Bob Smith.
The coverage of the op-ed marks the latest in a growing wave of local media attention to his 2026 challenge against five-term incumbent Salud Carbajal in California’s 24th District.
In the piece, titled “Central Coast Families Deserve Better Than Rhetoric on Healthcare,” Smith presents a detailed, bipartisan plan to prevent massive premium spikes when enhanced ACA subsidies expire next month.
He also calls for long-overdue structural reforms.
The op-ed has earned praise for tackling a complex issue with the same results-driven approach he brought to 26 years of Navy service.
The Independent’s decision to run the op-ed follows Smith’s clean sweep of endorsements from all three county Republican committees in the district.
It reflects increasing recognition that his campaign, built on national security experience, middle-class restoration, and common-sense solutions, is resonating across the Central Coast.
