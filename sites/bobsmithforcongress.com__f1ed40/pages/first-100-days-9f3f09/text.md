Here is my first hundred days.
He has represented this district since January 2017.
Engineers publish the spec before they build, so here is mine, in writing, and you can hold me to it.
Days 1 to 10 Open the district Staff constituent services offices in San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara, and Ventura counties, with a published response standard for casework on VA claims, Social Security, and passports.
Days 1 to 30 Town halls in every county Three open, unscreened town halls with published dates.
Spanish interpretation at every one.
Days 15 to 45 Affordability first Cosponsor legislation targeting the cost of housing, energy, and groceries, and vote against spending that adds to inflation without a plan to pay for it.
Days 30 to 60 Water and fire resilience package File a Central Coast water storage, recycling, and wildfire mitigation request with local water districts and county partners at the table.
Days 45 to 75 Defend the coast economy Work committee relationships to protect Vandenberg Space Force Base and Naval Base Ventura County missions and the jobs attached to them.
Days 60 to 90 Veterans casework surge A dedicated veterans caseworker and a claims clinic in each county, because a backlog is a solvable engineering problem.
Day 100 Publish the scorecard A public report on every commitment above: done, in progress, or missed, with the reason.
Then do it again every hundred days.

Then publish the results.” Cmdr.
