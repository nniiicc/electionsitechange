Things almost nobody argues about
A paycheck that covers rent
Housing and energy costs are the top pressure on Central Coast families, whatever their party registration.
Water that does not run out
Storage, recycling, and drought planning are engineering questions, not ideological ones.
Schools that teach the basics
Reading and math proficiency, and real pathways into trades and technical careers.
Safe streets and a secure border
Enforcement against violent crime and fentanyl trafficking, handled with competence and without cruelty.
Care for veterans
Claims answered, appointments kept, promises honored.
This is administration, not politics.
A coast worth inheriting
Clean water, protected beaches, working farms, and wildfire resilience for the next generation.
Talking across the aisle
From Santa Barbara Forum, the programme Bob hosts.
His guest that week was a Democrat and a former Santa Barbara City Council member.
“We need more people that are willing to talk, talk on issues, talk across the aisle, and engage on all the things that are affecting our community.
And right now we have a lack of that going on.
We have a lot of partisan, divisive politics going on in our communities and in our country, and we need to solve those issues.”
Not a slogan
Bob hosts a public affairs programme and invites people who disagree with him onto it.
Common ground is not a line on a website here.
It is a standing invitation, on the record, on camera.
Rules of engagement
- Read the bill.
All of it, including what got added at 2 am.
- Work with anyone who will work on the problem, from either party.
- Never attack a neighbor’s motives to win an argument about policy.
- Say the same thing in Washington that he says in Lompoc.
- Show the math when he votes no.
