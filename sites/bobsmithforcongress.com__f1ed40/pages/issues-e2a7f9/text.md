Common sense
for CD-24.
Five pillars.
One test for every one of them: does it make life on the Central Coast more affordable, safer, and more secure?
Affordability
Cut wasteful spending, lower the cost of housing, energy, and food, and expand homeownership for working families.
Education
Reading, math, and STEM excellence, vocational pathways, and research partnerships with UCSB and Cal Poly.
Energy and Water
A balanced grid with reliable baseload power, coastline protection, and secure water storage and drought resilience.
Immigration
Secure the border, remove violent offenders, provide legal clarity for long term residents, and modernize agricultural and seasonal visas.
Defending America
Stop fentanyl at the source, lead in AI and cyber defense, and strengthen Vandenberg SFB and Naval Base Ventura County.
What that looks like in practice
Affordability
- Audit and cut wasteful federal spending that fuels inflation.
- Expand housing supply and first time homeownership.
- Lower energy and grocery costs for working families.
- Protect Social Security and Medicare for the people who paid in.
Education
- Fundamentals first: reading, math, and STEM.
- Fund vocational and trade pathways, not just four year degrees.
- Partner with UCSB and Cal Poly on applied research.
- Transparency and parental involvement in local schools.
Energy and water
- Reliable baseload power alongside clean generation.
- Protect the coastline and our fisheries.
- Invest in storage, recycling, and drought resilience.
- Support Central Coast agriculture with dependable water.
Immigration
- Secure the border and stop the flow of fentanyl.
- Remove violent offenders without apology.
- Legal clarity for long term residents who work and pay taxes here.
- Modernize agricultural and seasonal visa programs.
Defending America
- Peace through strength and a mission ready force.
- Lead in artificial intelligence, space, and cyber defense.
- Strengthen Vandenberg Space Force Base and Naval Base Ventura County.
- Keep faith with veterans through accountable VA care.
