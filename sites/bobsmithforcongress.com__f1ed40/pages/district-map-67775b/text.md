California’s
24th District.
Three counties, a working coastline, and about three quarters of a million neighbors.
The territory
From the vineyards to the harbor
CD-24 stretches along California’s Central Coast, covering communities in San Luis Obispo County and Santa Barbara County and extending into Ventura County.
It is farmland and fishing, aerospace and universities, small downtowns and military installations.
- San Luis Obispo County, including San Luis Obispo, Paso Robles, and Arroyo Grande.
- Santa Barbara County, including Santa Maria, Lompoc, Goleta, Santa Barbara, and Carpinteria.
- Ventura County communities along the coast, including Ventura.
