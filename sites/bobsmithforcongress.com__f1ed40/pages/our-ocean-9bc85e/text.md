The Numbers This is not an attack on renewables Offshore wind was meant to solve something real.
Cleaner power, less imported fuel, and a coast that stays liveable.
Those are good goals, and the people pushing them mostly want what the rest of us want.
But the numbers have to work.
Three gigawatts of nameplate capacity sounds enormous.
Offshore wind runs at about a third of its nameplate, so Morro Bay would deliver closer to one gigawatt on average.
That is less than half of what Diablo Canyon already produces, from a plant that is the largest private employer in San Luis Obispo County.
