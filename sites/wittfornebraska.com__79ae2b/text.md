SuAnn was born and raised in Sarpy County and is a proud lifelong Nebraskan.
SuAnn began her career in education with Omaha Public Schools right after high school, working in managerial and support roles.
There, she managed a large team and bid for and developed district-wide budgets for telecommunications and internet services totaling over $8 million.
Her passion for public schools and education did not stop there.
After over 35 years with OPS, SuAnn transitioned to the Nebraska Department of Education, where she worked for 13 years as the State E-rate Coordinator and Rule 89 Program Manager, overseeing the application process and management of state funds.
Most District 14 residents will recognize SuAnn as the 2025 Board President of the Papillion La Vista School Board, where she has served as an elected member since 2018.
Throughout her career, SuAnn has voted to lower the property tax levy 7 times and held the line to control local spending.
