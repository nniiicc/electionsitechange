Donate Volunteer About Me The biggest struggles in HD-16 are no mystery to me: I've lived them.
From housing and transportation insecurity to the trauma and mental health struggles that are common in our community, I know what it's like.
I’ve chosen to turn my survival into a life of public service, and use what I've learned to craft public policy that gets people on their feet, and puts a solid foundation under us all so that we can live our best lives.
My Why When I became HD-16's Representative in 2023, I strongly believed that Colorado Springs was ready for an upgrade in our representation in the state Capitol, and that my lived experience and working class grit would make for a valuable contribution.​ ​ I’m not a member of the political class.
I come to this work as a survivor of childhood trauma, mental illness, and disability -- one of the many people left behind by the status quo. ​ ​​​ I lived to fight another day, and finally got my feet under me, only because a few things worked out for me just in time.
This shouldn't be a matter of chance, and we can do better to ensure everyone has a real opportunity to thrive.
The hardworking people of our community deserve a government that supports their interests and ensures the basics of a good life for all. ​​ It was an enormous privilege to serve our district in the people’s house of Colorado and I am eager to get back to work!
I look forward to earning your vote in November 2026 so I can serve our district again.
