Donate Volunteer Privacy Policy ​ Effective Date: January 20, 2026 Thank you for visiting Stephanie Vigil’s campaign website.
Your privacy and the security of your personal information are important to us.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information gathered through our website https://www.stephanievigil.com/ and through our text message (“SMS”) program.
By using our Site or participating in our text messaging program, you agree that your personal information will be handled as described in this policy.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including: Contact information: such as your name, email address, street address, zip code, and phone number.
Communication preferences: including whether you opt in to receive email or text messages.
Usage data: such as pages you visit on the Site, links you click, and other interactions with the Site.
How We Use Your Information We use personal information for purposes including: Campaign communications: sending newsletters, updates, event invitations, and calls to action by email, SMS, or phone.
SMS program: to send text messages you have opted in to receive, including notifications, reminders, and campaign information.
Improving the Site: analyzing trends and user interactions to enhance the Site and tailor content.
Compliance: meeting legal obligations under federal and state laws, including communications disclaimers and political advertising rules.
SMS/Text Messaging Program We may send text messages to you if you choose to opt in to our SMS program.
By opting in: You consent to receive recurring text messages from or on behalf of Stephanie Vigil’s campaign at the phone number you provide.
Message frequency varies depending on campaign activities and announcements.
Message and data rates may apply through your wireless carrier. ​ Opting In: You can opt in through the Site, a campaign form, or by texting a keyword to a campaign number.
Opting Out: To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP to any message we send you.
After you send the STOP request, we will send a one-time confirmation message and you will no longer receive messages from us.
Help: For help regarding SMS, reply HELP to any campaign text or contact us at info@stephanievigil.com .
We adhere to applicable laws governing text messaging, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and industry standards for application-to-person (A2P) messaging.
Messages will clearly identify the campaign as the sender and include opt-out instructions as required.
Sharing Your Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information: With service providers and vendors who support the campaign (e.g., email/SMS delivery partners, analytics providers).
With legal and compliance advisors.
As required by law (e.g., public campaign finance reporting or legal process).
We will not share your information with third parties for their independent marketing use.
Recognize you when you return to the Site.
Measure and improve the Site’s performance.
Serve campaign content more effectively.
Data Security We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information.
However, no security system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.
Children’s Privacy Our Site and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Your Rights and Choices You may: Opt out of marketing communications (email or SMS) at any time.
Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
Request correction or deletion of your personal information to the extent permitted by law.
To exercise these rights, contact us at info@stephanievigil.com .
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
The “Effective Date” at the top will indicate when the policy was last revised.
We encourage you to review this page periodically.
