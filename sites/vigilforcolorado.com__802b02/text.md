The District
HD16 is located in central Colorado Springs and contains historic and diverse neighborhoods like Knob Hill, the Old North End, Patty Jewett, Mid Shooks Run, and Cragmor.
The district is home to the University of Colorado at Colorado Springs, and cherished natural spaces like Palmer Park and Pulpit Rock Open Space.
