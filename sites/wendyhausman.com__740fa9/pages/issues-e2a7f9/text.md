WENDY HAUSMAN - MISSOURI STATE REPRESENTATIVE District 65 Conservative Record Principles Matter.
Leadership Requires Action.
Wendy Hausman believes conservative leadership means putting principles into action.
She stands for limited government, individual liberty, strong families, fiscal responsibility, parental rights, and the Constitution.
Her legislative record shows those principles at work. ​ Protecting Children Digital Exploitation and AI Wendy has worked to strengthen Missouri's laws against the exploitation of children and individuals through digitally created and manipulated images.
HB 1887 — Digital Exploitation Protections Wendy sponsored legislation addressing the disclosure and distribution of intimate digital depictions and worked to strengthen accountability for the misuse of emerging technology. ​ Protecting Children in Foster Care HB 3322 — Foster Care Educational Stability Wendy sponsored legislation to help children in foster care maintain educational stability when placement changes occur.
Protecting Children From Abduction Uniform Child Abduction Prevention Act Wendy worked to strengthen protections for children and families when there are concerns about unlawful removal or abduction. ​ Strengthening Families Supporting Missouri Childcare HB 482 — Childcare Facility Licensing Wendy sponsored legislation that modifies childcare facility licensing requirements, helping improve the regulatory process while maintaining protections for Missouri children.
Protecting Families From Unnecessary Taxes HB 594 — Tax Relief on Diapers and Feminine Hygiene Products Wendy sponsored legislation eliminating sales tax on diapers and feminine hygiene products.
She believes basic necessities should not be treated as luxury purchases.
Protecting Taxpayers Senior Property Tax Relief Wendy has worked to provide greater property tax relief for qualifying Missouri seniors and has advocated for policies that protect homeowners from unreasonable tax burdens. ​ Responsible Government Spending Through her appropriations experience and leadership on government efficiency, Wendy has worked to ensure taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly and that government agencies remain accountable. ​ Government Accountability Chair, Government Efficiency Committee As Chair of the Missouri House Government Efficiency Committee, Wendy is leading efforts to examine state government operations, identify unnecessary duplication and outdated functions, and find ways to improve efficiency and accountability.
She believes government should constantly be challenged to answer one question: Are taxpayers getting value for their money? ​ Conservative Principles in Action Wendy's conservative record is reflected in the issues she chooses to fight for: Limited Government Reducing unnecessary bureaucracy and demanding accountability.
Individual Liberty Protecting constitutional rights and limiting unnecessary government interference.
Strong Families Supporting parents, children, foster families, and communities.
Fiscal Responsibility Respecting taxpayers and demanding responsible government spending.
Parental Rights Recognizing parents as the primary decision makers for their children.
Constitutional Government Respecting the limits placed on government power. ​ A Record You Can Measure Wendy Hausman does not believe a conservative label is enough.
Principles matter.
But leadership is measured by action.
Her record is public, her legislation is measurable, and she will continue fighting for a government that protects freedom, strengthens families, and respects the people who pay the bills. ​​​​​ ​ Rep.
