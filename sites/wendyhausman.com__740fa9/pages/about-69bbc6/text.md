A Commitment to Serve ​ Wendy Hausman believes public service is a responsibility, not a title.
First elected to the Missouri House in 2022, Wendy represents St.
Charles County with a focus on protecting children, strengthening families, respecting taxpayers, and holding government accountable.
As Chair of the Missouri House Government Efficiency Committee, Wendy works to identify waste, improve accountability, and make government work better for taxpayers.
Her experience in appropriations has also given her firsthand insight into how Missouri spends taxpayer dollars. ​ Conservative Leadership Wendy believes in limited government, individual liberty, personal responsibility, strong families, fiscal responsibility, and the Constitution.
She believes government should protect freedom, not control it, and that elected officials are accountable to the people they serve.
Family & Community Wendy has spent years working in and serving the St.
Charles County community she now represents.
She knows the people, understands the challenges facing local families and businesses, and believes effective leadership starts with listening.
A wife, mother, and grandmother, Wendy understands that decisions made in Jefferson City affect real families, businesses, schools, and communities.
Her Christian faith is an important part of her commitment to service, personal responsibility, and doing what is right. ​ Why She Serves Wendy entered public service because she believed she could make a difference.
That commitment has not changed.
She believes Missouri is strongest when families are strong, people are free to pursue opportunity, and government understands its proper role.
