An Economy for Everyone
Written By Carey Hamilton
“People” vs.
“bottom line” is a false choice.
We must provide the climate for businesses to succeed.
But when Hoosiers can't afford groceries, utilities, childcare, or housing — a growing economy doesn't feel like growth at all.
By focusing on workers and their families — from affordable healthcare to livable wages and lower everyday costs — we can build a thriving economy that truly works for everyone.
