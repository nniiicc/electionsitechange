Healthcare
Written By Carey Hamilton
Going to the doctor shouldn’t break the bank.
Every Hoosier deserves affordable preventative and life-saving healthcare.
Indiana must hold insurance companies and corporate middlemen accountable for driving up premium and prescription drug costs.
I also believe we should protect healthcare access for children, seniors, the disabled, and the low-income workers powering our state.
And government should stay out of your family planning decisions and respect patient privacy.
