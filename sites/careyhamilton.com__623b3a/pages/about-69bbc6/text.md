About Carey Hamilton
Carey Hamilton knows what it means to grow up in a working family without enough support.
Raised by a single mother with no affordable child care, she became a latchkey kid — and never forgot it.
She is also a military mom, sister, and daughter.
Those experiences helped shape her life's work.
Elected to the Indiana House of Representatives in 2016 representing Indianapolis's northside District 87, Hamilton has spent her legislative career fighting for working families.
She has stood against the dismantling of Indiana's child care assistance program and the closure of child care centers pushing working parents — especially mothers — out of the workforce.
She has fought to expand Pre-K to all Indiana children, believing every kid deserves a strong start regardless of zip code or family income.
And she has championed fully funded public schools, opposing voucher programs that divert taxpayer dollars from neighborhood schools to subsidize private tuition for Indiana's wealthiest families.
She has fought for clean air and clean water, building on a career spent fighting pollution and conserving resources before she ever reached the Statehouse.
Hamilton is also founder and co-chair of the bipartisan Indiana Legislative Trails Caucus, supporting low-cost infrastructure that improves quality of life, spurs local economic development, and expands recreation opportunities across Indiana.
A graduate of Pike High School and Indiana University-Bloomington, Hamilton lives in Indianapolis with her husband Derek, co-owner of a local small business.
They have two grown children and one sweet rescue pup.
Carey’s qualifications
Carey Hamilton has worked in the environmental field for nearly three decades.
During that time, she has worked in the public, private and non-profit sectors on a range of environmental issues, most recently serving as an executive in the climate tech sector.
Prior to that, Hamilton was the executive director of the Indiana Recycling Coalition (IRC) for nine years.
Under her leadership, the IRC won two national awards - recognizing the IRC as the best of state organizations across the country.
Prior to serving in this role, Hamilton owned an environmental consulting firm.
Earlier in her career, she served as a program manager for the Indiana Department of Environmental Management and as the first leader of Indiana’s Greening the Government Program.
Around that time, she accomplished one of her proudest personal goals that also shaped her later work: hiking the 2,100-mile Appalachian Trail.
In 2020, Hamilton launched the bipartisan, bicameral Indiana Legislative Trails Caucus.
The Trails Caucus has successfully worked to secure $90 million for trails across the state during the last two budget cycles.
These investments represent the first ever legislative designation of funds towards trails infrastructure.
In 2022, Hamilton received the Rails to Trails Conservancy's national Rail Trail Champion Award and in 2021, Hamilton received the Indiana Greenways Award for Outstanding Public Official, honoring her work in the General Assembly to advance trail access for all Hoosiers.
That same year, she also received the Indiana Wildlife Federation's Conservation Champion Award.
Hamilton earned the 2020 Civility in Government Award from the Association of Retired Members of the Indiana General Assembly and in 2019 was named Legislator of the Year by the Indiana Friends Committee on Legislation for supporting key proposals regarding health, the environment and restorative justice during the 2019 General Assembly session.
Additionally, Hamilton recently completed two terms on the Washington Township Schools Foundation Board.
She co-led environmental programs for the 2012 Indianapolis Super Bowl Host Committee, and is a former board member of Keep America Beautiful.
She is a 2011 graduate of the Stanley K.
Lacy Executive Leadership Program, a 2013 graduate of the Institute for Conservation Leadership’s Executive Leadership Program, and was named one of the Indianapolis Business Journal’s “Forty under 40” up-and-coming leaders.
