Our Environment
Written By Carey Hamilton
If we make smart choices, the environment can benefit our health, promote energy independence, and create jobs.
Natural systems like wetlands keep us safe from dirty water and excess flooding, but recent state laws have threatened to destroy them, harming our health and worsening natural disasters.
Investing in all types of clean energy - including individual- and community-based solar - drives down your utility bill and creates high-wage jobs for Hoosiers.
