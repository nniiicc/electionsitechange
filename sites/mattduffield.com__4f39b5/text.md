Matt facts Here’s What We’ve Accomplished So Far… Working for Veterans Co-sponsored policy to exempt disabled veterans spouses from burdensome personal property taxes.
Took the lead on legislation that eliminated the sales tax requirement for the Disabled American Veterans Association.
Matt always fights to help those who have fought for us.
Lowering Taxes Voted twice to lower income taxes for the people of Arkansas and co-sponsored a law increasing the Homestead Property Tax Credit.
He remains steadfast in his efforts to reduce our tax burden, and to allow Arkansans to keep more of their hard-earned money.
Protecting Our Right to Bear Arms Lead house sponsor on SJR11 which will be on the ballot in November 2026 and give the voters the choice to permanently instill the individual right to keep and bear arms and ammo, protecting citizens from the gun hating bias of a liberal judge.
He also co-sponsored legislation that will require public schools to teach firearm safety to help protect our youth and educate them with the knowledge to protect themselves and others.
Protecting You and Your Family Championed legislation that prohibits perverted sex offenders from going within 100 feet of our public water parks and swimming pools where children innocently play.
Co-Sponsored a bill to create the death penalty for soulless child rapists and amended the death penalty options to include nitrogen gas.
Saving our Pets Matt is the first lawmaker in years to successfully champion policy to protect our dogs and cats.
Named after his now deceased rescue dog Daisy, Daisy’s Law passed and will give law enforcement officers the ability to assist animal rescue shelters in saving dogs and cats from the hands of heartless animal abusers.
