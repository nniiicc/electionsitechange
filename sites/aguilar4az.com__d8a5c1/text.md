A Voice From The Community, For The Community.
Cesar Aguilar was born and raised in Phoenix, Arizona by a working class family.
Cesar’s mom was an English Language Learner teacher and his dad was a Union Heat & Frost Insulator in Local #73.
Cesar graduated from Northern Arizona University with a Bachelors in Political Science in 2016.
After graduating, Cesar went off to work for the Arizona Democratic Party.
Cesar today focuses his time working for a nonprofit, the Arizona Students’ Association, where Cesar works on advocating for college accessibility and affordability.
Cesar truly believes that education is the greatest equalizer and our communities are strengthened by working class values.
Cesar has experience serving as a current state representative as well as past service on multiple governing boards advocating for Latino children in K-12 and higher education.
Cesar knows what it takes to advocate for working class people and knows that education and opportunity go a long way.
