John Schlegel Announces Re-Election Bid for PA State House schlegel4statehous Jan 8, 2024 2 min read Lebanon – Today, Republican John Schlegel announced his intention to seek re-election as state representative.
Schlegel is serving his first term representing the 101st district in Lebanon County, which consists of Cornwall Borough, North Cornwall Twp., West Cornwall Twp., Lebanon City, North Lebanon Twp., South Lebanon Twp., and West Lebanon Twp.
Schlegel serves on the House Aging and Older Citizens, Children &amp; Youth, Education, and Human Services Committees.
He has a 100% attendance record in the House chamber and is active in all committee voting sessions and hearings.
During his first year in office, Schlegel co-sponsored several bills, including HB 27, to address a substitute teacher shortage throughout Pennsylvania, which was recently signed into law.
Schlegel also received a perfect 100% on his voting record from The National Federation of Independent Businesses.
He is currently working on legislation to address local issues initiated by constituents.
“Lebanon County is the second fastest-growing county in Pennsylvania.
Along with growth comes a need to work with people to address challenges that arise,” stated Schlegel.
“I plan to serve the people by continuing to be visible and accessible to my constituents.
Listening to their concerns and communicating in multiple ways is crucial to my work on their behalf, Schlegel continues.
