Meet Brad Sherman
COMMITMENT TO SERVICE
Congressman Brad Sherman was born and raised in Southern California and represents the San Fernando Valley and much of the Westside.
In Congress, Brad has developed a reputation as a champion for working families and a strong advocate for consumer protections.
He is a senior member of the House Foreign Affairs Committee and House Financial Services Committee and was among the leaders behind the formation of the Consumer Financial Protection Agency.
