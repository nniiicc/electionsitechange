Preventing Youth Smoking
Illinois shouldn’t spend $2 billion dollars a year in Medicaid dollars on diseases we can prevent if we keep young people from smoking.
That is why, in 2021, Julie passed historic legislation in Springfield to raise the legal age to buy all nicotine and tobacco products to 21.
She has also spearheaded legislation to crack down on youth vaping and ensure vaping companies are not marketing their products to minors.
