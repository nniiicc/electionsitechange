Skip to content Home About Issues Endorsements News Voter Resources Contact Home About Issues Endorsements News Voter Resources Contact Donate The Issues gun control & public safety In 2018, Julie sponsored one of the strongest “red flag” laws in the United States.
The Firearm Restraining Order Act, allows family members or a law enforcement officer to alert the courts that an individual poses a danger of personal injury to themselves or the public and has access to a firearm.
Since then, Julie has remained a staunch proponent of strong gun safety laws.
She has passed legislation promoting gun safe storage tools and giving State Police a greater ability to track stolen firearms or firearms without serial numbers.
When it comes to gun safety and the safety of our communities, Julie doesn’t just talk about solutions , she fights for them every day.
