We encourage those who vote by mail to mail their completed ballot by October 27th, a week before Election Day, to ensure it reaches the election authorities in time.
If you have reservations about mailing your ballot via USPS, you can always drop it off at a drop box, an early voting site, or your voting location on Election Day.
