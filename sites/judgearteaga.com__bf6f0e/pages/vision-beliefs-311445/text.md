“I believe deeply in a judicial system that is fair, impartial, and accessible to everyone.”
My vision for the Texas 4th Court of Appeals is rooted in three core principles.
- Fairness and Equal Treatment
Every person who comes before the court deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
I will continue to apply the law without bias, ensuring decisions are based on facts, not politics or personal beliefs.
- Commitment to the Law
The Constitution and the rule of law must guide our courts.
I believe in the consistent application of legal principles so that outcomes are trusted by the public.
- Service and Accessibility
I’ve dedicated my career to making the justice system more responsive and more connected to the communities it serves.
As Chief Justice, I will work to keep the court efficient and transparent ensuring that the people of South and Central Texas continue to have faith in their appellate courts.
