Nearly 20 Years of Fair, Steady Leadership in the Courts
For nearly two decades, Judge Antonia “Toni” Arteaga has served Texans from the bench – making tough decisions and ensuring the courts function fairly.
Currently serving her fifth term as the longest-serving civil district judge at the courthouse, she has presided over hundreds of jury trials and complex civil matters.
She brings deep experience and steady judgment to every case, proving she is ready to lead on day one.
A Judge Who Understands How Courts Work
In addition to her courtroom responsibilities, Judge Arteaga has served extensively as Presiding Judge of the Bexar County Civil District Courts, one of the largest civil court systems in Texas.
She understands that justice delayed is justice denied, and why she works everyday to ensure the justice system works for everyone.
Key Responsibilities:
🏛️ Operational Management: Manages daily court operations to ensure stability and consistency.
🕗 Real-Time Efficiency: Assigns cases in real time to prevent delays and keep dockets moving.
⚖️ Fair Workloads: Balances workloads fairly among judges to maximize judicial resources.
❗ Urgent Response: Ensures urgent matters are handled promptly so critical issues are de-escalated as quickly as possible.
The “Chief Justice” Connection: This hands-on leadership role is similar to the responsibilities of a Chief Justice.
It requires leadership, fairness, efficiency, and the ability to earn the trust of judges, attorneys, and the public alike.
Judge Arteaga excels in this role and is considered one of the best at performing the duties of Presiding Court Judge.
Statewide Judicial Leadership
Judge Arteaga has also been trusted to lead beyond her courtroom.
She served as President of the Texas Association of District Judges, representing trial judges across the state and working with judicial leaders on improving court operations, safety, and public confidence.
Leadership in Times of Crisis
When courts faced unprecedented challenges, Judge Arteaga stepped up.
She has a proven track record of coordinating complex operations to keep the justice system running safely and responsibly.
Key Initiatives:
- Courthouse Security & Safety: As Chair of the Bexar County Court Security Committee, Judge Arteaga coordinated vital safety efforts directly with law enforcement and emergency services.
Her leadership was instrumental in developing the 1st Active Shooter Training for the courthouse, reinforcing the safety of staff and the public.
- Navigating the COVID-19 Pandemic: During the pandemic, when courts nationwide faced disruption, Judge Arteaga led the effort to implement remote court operations.
These swift actions ensured that access to justice continued without interruption during one of the most challenging moments in recent history.
Service to the Community
Judge Arteaga’s commitment to service extends beyond the courtroom.
She actively uses her experience to mentor others and advocate for safety and education in the community.
Key Roles & Engagement:
- Texas Latinx Judges: She is a founding board member and current President of this organization, fostering diversity and excellence in the judiciary.
- National Campaign to Stop Violence: She has served for a decade as the Bexar County Chair, working to reduce violence and promote safety.
- Civic Education: Judge Arteaga regularly speaks at schools and community events to educate the public about the justice system and inspire the next generation.
