Re-Elect Mike Oliverio for WV State Senate - District 13 Mike Oliverio proudly serves as the State Senator for District 13, which encompasses parts of Monongalia and Marion counties.
This area is home to West Virginia University and Fairmont State University, along with vital sectors such as healthcare, tech, research, manufacturing, and natural resources.
Mike Oliverio is dedicated to representing the great people who live and work in District 13, serving the state of West Virginia and beyond.
