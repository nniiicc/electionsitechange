Our nation was built on the ideas of individual liberty, not government mandates.
Our founders believed that each person has the unalienable right to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness.
These rights are endowed to us not by family lineage, the traditions of men or by government.
These rights are woven into our very fabric by God the Creator.
It is therefore each citizen’s duty to remind government of this fact — that there is a higher power than government — and demand that government fulfill its primary obligation which is to protect our natural rights, not mandate them away.
The government which governs best, governs least.
Your LIBERTY First!
Sadly today this is not the case.
From the Federal, to the State, to the County/Municipal levels, every government desires to do more by telling you how you must live your life, what and when products are acceptable for purchase, what type of energy is best to use, what types of health services are approved, how many people you can meet with and what is good and acceptable for the community.
A major problem with State government today is Executive Order and Administrative Rule — a system where the Governor makes "law" out of thin air, often with no approval from the legislature.
State agencies then write rules & regulations — without legislative authority — execute those rules & regulations, and then arbitrate (judicial function) any disputes about their "laws".
All of these powers reside within the executive branch itself with no direct accountability to the people.
Wrongly, these administrative agencies are loyal and accountable only to government, not the people.
To add salt to the wound, our judicial system continues to rule in favor for such executive mandates.
