This is a fluid document and I will update the guide as needed to properly display my values Healthcare Insurance Zebulon supports a government-run single-payer healthcare system This system would be funded through progressive income taxes.
Employers would be able to pay employees more as they would no longer be responsible for employee healthcare benefits.
Zebulon views this as a net positive, as profit margins would be removed from the equation.
Healthcare is a human right and should not operate as a profit-driven market.
Mental health care would be included Reproductive Freedom Zebulon supports the ability to obtain an abortion up to 24 weeks of pregnancy, with procedures after that time requiring medical approval.
This timeframe is based on guidance from medical professionals and may evolve as medical standards develop.
Zebulon supports monthly complimentary mental health care during and after pregnancy.
Education Funding Zebulon supports a base increase in federal fundin g for public schools.
This funding would prioritize essential student supports first: School meals (ensuring access to quality meals) Teacher training Mental health services (trained professionals on site) Special education Low-income student support Remaining funds would be allocated at the discretion of the school.
Curriculum Zebulon supports local control over curriculum Energy Energy Independence Zebulon believes reducing reliance on fossil fuels is both an economic and national security priority Renewable Energy Zebulon does not support the total elimination of fossil fuels as an energy source at this time.
A diversified energy grid ensures a more reliable and stable system.
Zebulon Strongly supports investing in renewable energy research and development.
Solar generation and battery storage technologies have proven to be strong investments in many other countries.
Energy Grid Zebulon supports research and investment to modernize our energy and data infrastructure.
Politics and Government Political Corruption / Accountability / Transparency Zebulon believes the United States currently lacks sufficient government transparency.
Information should be readily available to the public.
Public trust in government has eroded.
Zebulon aims to lead by example through regular town halls and a public transparency page on his campaign website.
The Department of Defense has repeatedly failed financial audits while its budget continues to grow year after year.
Citizens United Zebulon supports overturning Citizens United .
Every American citizen should have an equal voice in our political system.
Government must serve the people first, not corporate interests.
Insider Trading Zebulon believes that members of Congress should not be allowed to buy, sell, or trade individual stocks while in office.
Allowing stock trading while serving in Congress creates potential conflicts of interest and outside influences that undermine public trust.
Congress should work for the people, not for corporate interests or personal financial gain.
2 Party System Zebulon believes the current two-party system is harmful to the health of our democracy.
When only two parties dominate elections, meaningful debate and representation are limited .
Zebulon supports Ranked Choice Voting Ranked Choice Voting allows voters to support their preferred candidate without wasting their vote.
Immigration and Customs Citizenship Zebulon supports a clear and efficient path to citizenship.
A strong immigration system welcomes people who want to work, contribute, and become part of our communities.
ICE Zebulon supports restructuring federal immigration enforcement by dissolving ICE and redistributing its responsibilities among other agencies.
This would reduce agency overlap and improve oversight, efficiency and accountability.
Foreign Policy Tariffs Zebulon does not support blanket tariffs.
Tariffs are a tool to be used with care, not a threat to be thrown at trade partners and allies.
Israel Zebulon Does not support using American Taxpayer Dollars to fund Israel’s Military Our tax dollars should be spent here in the US, here in Michigan Housing Construction Zebulon supports the expansion of trade schools and programs Zebulon spent 5 years as a traveling Wind Turbine Technician after attending a 6 month trade school NWREI Zebulon supports innovation in housing construction to reduce building costs and increase housing supply.
Emerging construction technologies can help address the housing shortage while lowering costs.
3D-printed housing Manufactured and modular housing Prefabricated building systems that reduce construction time and labor costs Ownership Zebulon supports first-time homebuyer incentives to help more families achieve homeownership.
Programs that assist with down payments and financing can help working families enter the housing market.
Zebulon supports reasonable regulations on large-scale corporate ownership of residential housing.
Economy Extreme Wealth The American Dream depends on a strong and stable middle class.
Extreme concentrations of wealth threaten the stability of that middle class and run counter to the principles behind the American Dream.
Zebulon believes trillion-dollar corporations and soaring billionaire wealth are proof that trickle-down economics was never designed to work for working people This economic approach has failed many Americans.
Zebulon believes trillion-dollar corporations and aggressive mergers undermine small businesses, limit competition, and erode the core principles of capitalism.
Taxation Zebulon supports a simplified, government-run tax filing system .
No more filing taxes This reform has long faced resistance from tax preparation companies that benefit financially from maintaining the current system.
Zebulon supports a targeted wealth tax to address extreme concentrations of wealth.
A 5% annual tax on net wealth exceeding $1 billion A wealth tax would encourage capital to be actively invested in businesses, innovation, and economic growth rather than remaining concentrated and idle.
When wealth circulates through investment, wages, and new enterprises, it strengthens the broader economy and creates opportunity for more people.
Zebulon supports higher income tax brackets for those with incomes above certain thresholds.
Lower taxes on lower incomes, and higher taxes on very high incomes.
The strongest and most stable middle class in American history existed during the #s and #s.
During that time, the highest marginal tax rates ranged from roughly 70–90%.
Higher tax rates on extreme income can encourage companies to reinvest profits into wages, expansion, and innovation.
In many cases, companies choose to invest in employees or growth rather than pay higher taxes.
Minimum Wage Zebulon supports a country wide minimum wage increase Starting between $15–$20 per hour, with adjustments based on regional cost of living.
Local Issues Line 5 Zebulon believes protecting the Great Lakes must take priority over aging fossil fuel infrastructure like Line 5.
Renewable energy investment in technologies such as wind, solar, and grid-scale battery storage will strengthen long-term energy security while protecting the Great Lakes.
Data Centers Zebulon does not support a blanket prohibition on data centers When (not if) outright bans fail, the greatest burden often falls on poorer and more marginalized communities.
Zebulon supports strict zoning standards Data centers should be planned with their physical footprint in mind.
Minimizing environmental impact must be a top priority when approving new facilities.
Zebulon supports strict noise standards and monitoring requirements for data centers to minimize impacts on nearby residents and sensitive wildlife habitats.
Zebulon supports strict water usage standards Closed-loop cooling systems should be prioritized to minimize water consumption.
Zebulon supports strict energy standards Policies must ensure that rising energy demand from data centers does not increase utility costs for families and businesses in our district.
Housing Zebulon supports increased investment in local trade schools and skilled trades programs to help meet the growing demand for builders.
More on housing above Medical Zebulon supports a single-payer healthcare system to reduce financial strain on rural hospitals and communities.
Zebulon supports research and development in remote diagnosis and telemedicine to improve healthcare access in rural areas.
More on medical above MMOP Zebulon supports the Michiganders for Money out of Politics initiative https://miunited.org/en/michiganders-for-money-out-of-politics-mmop/ RCV Zebulon supports bringing Ranked Choice Voting to Michigan https://rankmivote.org/ 2nd Amendment Gun Access Zebulon fully supports the second amendment Zebulon supports addressing the Charleston Loophole Everyone purchasing a firearm should be required to complete a background check, regardless of where the purchase occurs.
Gun Use Zebulon does not support firearms being carried or used in schools.
Schools with safety concerns may implement security measures such as metal detectors when necessary.
Responsible gun ownership includes education, safe storage, and accountability.
Firearm Education Zebulon believes firearm safety education should be required in certain situations.
First-time firearm purchases Applications for concealed carry permits Youth safety programs After negligent use or unsafe storage violations.
Professional or high-responsibility roles involving firearms Zebulon believes firearm education and safety training should be government funded and accessible at no cost to those who want to learn.
Artificial Intelignece (AI) Deep Fakes Zebulon supports making it illegal to knowingly distribute AI-generated media intended to deceive the public about real events, individuals, or elections.
Political advertisements and public communications using AI-generated media should be clearly labeled.
AI and Elections Zebulon supports strict rules preventing the use of deceptive AI-generated media in election campaigns.
AI should not be used to impersonate candidates, fabricate statements, or mislead voters.
Transparency in AI Media Zebulon supports requiring clear labeling or watermarking on AI-generated images, audio, and video used in public communications.
Citizens deserve to know when media has been artificially generated or altered.
There are very valid arguments against this method and I am open to discussion on this subject.
Copyright and Creative Work Zebulon supports protections for artists, writers, and creators whose work may be used to train AI systems.
Creators should have the right to opt out or receive compensation when their work is used in AI training.
Responsible AI Development Zebulon supports reasonable regulations to ensure AI systems are developed and deployed responsibly.
Innovation should continue, but safeguards are necessary to protect privacy, public trust, and democratic institutions.
AI and Workers Zebulon believes the economic benefits created by AI and automation should be shared broadly across society.
Zebulon supports exploring policies such as Universal Basic Income or shared national wealth funds to ensure technological progress strengthens the middle class rather than replacing it.
