Home About Volunteer Endorsements DONATE Home About Volunteer Endorsements DONATE Home About Volunteer Endorsements DONATE Meet Arlis Raised on North Dakota dairy farms, Arlis learned early on that hard work and family are what truly matter.
After working the oil fields and earning an agricultural engineering degree from NDSU, he spent 35 years at Cargill leading teams and later earned an MBA to better support small businesses.
He and his wife, Mary, have been married for over 40 years, and couldn’t be happier to call West Central Minnesota home living near their four children and seven grandkids.
He's running to bring his experience in agriculture and business to work for our neighbors and our future.
