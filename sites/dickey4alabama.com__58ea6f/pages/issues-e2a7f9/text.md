Where I Stand on What Matters to you
Good Wages for Alabama's Workers
- Support Alabama having its own minimum wage and adjusting it to inflation.
- Expand career and technical education in high schools, letting students earn trade certifications before graduation.
- Encourage cooperation between high schools and unions to allow training and apprenticeships as a part of shop/technical/trade classes.
- Repeal SB231 and protect card check.
An Affordable Alabama
- End algorithmic middlemen in the rental market
- Work with non-profits to expand child care availability
- Support Medicaid Expansion
- Seek to reform the certificate of need process
- Support Alabama's public school systems and provide the needed resources for expansion of opportunities to go from school to career.
Put Compassion Back in Public Policy
Life doesn't go as planned for all my friends and neighbors.
Just because life throws you troubles like losing your job or your business failing doesn't mean that the state shouldn't treat you with respect and work with our churches and community partners to get you back on your feet.
Protecting Your Rights
- Ban Flock cameras.
- A moratorium on data center construction.
- Ensure that data centers pay their entire cost of working in Alabama.
- Ensure that the PSC, the Alabama Attorney General, and local governments have the tools they need to regulate data centers, including telling data center companies "no" to building in communities that don't want them.
- Hear from experts and communities to ensure that policies are what North Alabamians want.
- End NDA's between businesses and local officials.
- Repeal SB67 and support a politically independent Alabama Department of Veterans Affairs.
