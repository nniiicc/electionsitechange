Pennsylvania’s 148th District voter resources
November 3rd is General Election Day!
Mark your calendar, and use the resources below to stay informed.
Important Dates
- Last day to register to vote: October 19, 2026
- Last day to request a mail-in ballot: October 27, 2026
- General Election Day: November 3, 2026
- Polls are open from 7am to 8pm
