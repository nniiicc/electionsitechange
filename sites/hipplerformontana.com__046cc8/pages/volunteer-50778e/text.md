Ways to Engage
Want to help get Julie elected?
We have a number of ways you can help, whether you want engage with people out in the neighborhoods or just want to participate from home.
We're currently looking for folks who can:
- Write postcards and/or letters to the editor
- Text or email people you know
- Curate news relevant to Julie's priorities
- Distribute campaign signs
- Knock on doors and tell folks about Julie
- Drop literature at doors
If you're interested, please fill out our volunteer sign up form and we'll get back to you ASAP.
