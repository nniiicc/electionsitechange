Skip to content Home From the Trail Meet Cathy Contact My Vision Donate Welcome from the trail March 19, 2026 • Cathy’s Team By Cathy Thorne Bynum Welcome, friends, Thank you for taking the time to visit and follow along on this journey.
Over the coming weeks and months, I’ll be sharing updates from across District 8—stories from our communities, conversations with families and small business owners, and reflections on the issues that matter most to the people who call this region home.
This campaign is about listening first.
As I travel throughout our towns and neighborhoods, I’m continually reminded of the strength, resilience, and values that define District 8.
From our farmers and small business owners to our teachers, parents, and community leaders, the people here care deeply about their families, their future, and the direction of our state.
Through this blog, I’ll be sharing what I’m hearing and learning along the way—whether it’s concerns about education, economic opportunity, public safety, or the cost of everyday living.
My goal is to provide a clear and honest look at the challenges we face and the ideas being discussed to address them.
I believe that leadership starts with showing up, asking questions, and being willing to have real conversations.
That’s exactly what I intend to do throughout this campaign.
You’ll also find updates on events, community visits, and opportunities to get involved.
I encourage you to stay connected, share your thoughts, and be part of the conversation.
Your voice matters, and it should always be part of shaping the future of our district.
