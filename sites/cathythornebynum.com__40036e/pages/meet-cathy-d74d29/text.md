Cathy Thorne Bynum
Candidate for N.C.
House District Eight
A Life of Faith, Service, and Leadership
Cathy Thorne Bynum’s life is a powerful story of perseverance, faith, and commitment to others.
Born in Elm City, North Carolina, she was the eighth of nine children raised by parents who were former sharecroppers.
Growing up in poverty, Cathy learned early the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and gratitude—lessons that continue to guide her today.
Inspired by her family’s determination, Cathy followed in her older brother’s footsteps and attended North Carolina A&T State University.
She earned a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering, graduating Magna Cum Laude in 1980 through a competitive cooperative program with the U.S.
Navy.
She began her career with Procter & Gamble before making the decision to prioritize her growing family.
Cathy and her husband, Terry, raised three daughters together, instilling in them the same values of faith, discipline, and service.
Cathy later answered a calling to education, earning her teaching credentials from East Carolina University and spending nearly two decades teaching high school math in Pitt County Schools.
Known for her dedication and high standards, she was honored as Teacher of the Year at Farmville Central High School.
“My faith guides me, my family grounds me, and my community inspires me to serve with integrity every single day.”
Her passion for service extends beyond the classroom.
Cathy has led community initiatives, including youth programs and neighborhood revitalization efforts, and remains active in civic and faith-based organizations.
A devoted wife, mother, and grandmother, Cathy Thorne Bynum believes in strengthening families, improving education, and building safer, more connected communities.
Her life’s journey—from humble beginnings to leadership in education and community service—drives her commitment to serving the people of North Carolina with integrity, compassion, and purpose.
