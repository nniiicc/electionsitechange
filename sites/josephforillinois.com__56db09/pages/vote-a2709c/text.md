Life is busy.

With Vote By Mail, you can:
- Vote when it works for you
- Take your time and get it right
- Avoid long lines or last-minute issues
HOW IT WORKS
- Apply online (takes 2 minutes)
- Receive your ballot in the mail
- Ballots begin mailing weeks before the election
- Vote at home
- Return your ballot
- Mail it back (postage paid)
- OR drop it at a secure drop box or early voting site
