Because of the education I received in Iowa’s public schools, I was able to put myself through Drake Law School.
At Drake, I passed my first bill - allowing abandoned teenagers to emancipate from their parents in 2008.
I also clerked and worked for Governor Tom Vilsack.
I’ve served as the president of the Beaverdale Neighborhood Association and chair of Des Moines Neighbors.
As the chair for the Polk County Democrats, I was proud to revive the Steak Fry and build up neighborhood groups countywide.
I live with my wife, Lindsay, and our two sons, Teddy and Franklin, in the Beaverdale neighborhood of Des Moines.
We spend most nights and weekends working on our perennial gardens and restoring our 1935 house.
