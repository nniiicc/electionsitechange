Contact About Me I bring a lifetime of real-world experience, service, and perseverance to my campaign for Congress.
Born and raised in Mount Vernon, New York, I‘ve built a 25-year career supporting legal teams across a wide range of fields before continuing my education as a working adult, ultimately earning a Bachelor’s degree in Behavioral Studies and a Master of Divinity.
My professional path has included roles in social services, education, elder care, and community-based programs—reflecting a deep commitment to helping people navigate life’s challenges with dignity and support.
After moving to Colorado in 2013, I continued that commitment as a paraeducator with Aurora Public Schools, working closely with both special needs and general education students.
As a dedicated community advocate, I have long been involved in housing, shelter, and food access initiatives, helping develop programs and mobilize volunteers to meet urgent needs.
I am also an active voice in international human rights advocacy, particularly regarding Middle East issues.
My life is grounded in service, faith, and a belief in progress through compassion and accountability.
Known for my integrity, resilience, and sense of humor, I am running for Congress to bring thoughtful leadership and a people-first perspective to Washington.
