DONATE WATCH NOW TO LEARN MORE ABOUT NICK ABOUT MEET NICK Elected in 2024 to represent District 14, Nick is a sixth generation Arkansan and was born and raised in Northwest Arkansas.
Nick is a graduate of Baylor University and received a master's degree in accounting from Rice University.
His passion for conservative policy led him to work in Washington, D.C. for Senator Tom Cotton where he learned first-hand the need for conservatives to stand up for what they believe in.
As an accountant, Nick has a unique perspective on the economic challenges facing all Arkansans.
He is well versed in finance and budgeting, and he is eager to put that knowledge to work finding common sense solutions to reduce the size and scope of government.
He will always promote limited government, free markets and individual liberty. ​ ABOUT THE ISSUES PROMOTE SMALL BUSINESSES Government should stay out of the way of the innovators, entrepreneurs, and hard-working taxpayers who drive our economy and create new jobs.
You don’t have to look far to see the impact of small business in Bentonville.
Whether you are talking about the homegrown #1 Fortune 500 company or your favorite local food truck, small businesses have built Northwest Arkansas into the economic engine of our state.
I will promote job growth policies such as lowering taxes, cutting red tape, and fighting over-regulation of small businesses.
CONSERVATIVE ARKANSAS VALUES I am committed to protecting our shared conservative Arkansas values.
As your state representative, I will support policies that protect the unborn, safeguard the 2nd Amendment, guarantee parental rights in education, and promote religious freedom.
As your voice in Little Rock, I will not shy away from battles with the radical left on these issues.
I will always defend our constitutional rights and conservative principles.
EDUCATION It is critically important that we have a strong, well-funded public education system in Arkansas.
Northwest Arkansas is blessed with great schools and great teachers, and we need to support them.
I also believe it’s important for all families to have choices.
Not every child learns the same way or has the same needs, and parents – not government – should always be in the driver’s seat when deciding what is best for their children.
FIRST RESPONDERS AND VETERANS As your representative, I will vote to crack down on violent crime, fund police, and support our veterans.
Those who put their lives on the line to defend us are owed a debt of gratitude.
I will always stand with our military, veterans, first responders, and law enforcement.
CUTTING TAXES As an accountant, I have learned one very basic rule—DON’T SPEND MORE THAN YOU HAVE.
We need to prioritize cutting wasteful spending and continue lowering taxes.
Even though taxes have been cut in recent years, the state continues to see record surpluses which simply means we are collecting too much in taxes.
We need to make the state spend less instead of charging taxpayers more.
With record inflation and the drag of Biden’s economy, it’s more important than ever to ease the burden on taxpayers.
Putting more money in people’s pockets will help fuel Arkansas’s economy and grow jobs right here.
