Issues
A law-and-order record and a clear set of priorities for Assembly District 13.
Public Safety
Brian Hibbetts backs common sense public safety that keeps Nevada neighborhoods safe and gives police the resources to protect the people they serve.
Safe neighborhoods are the foundation of a strong economy and a good quality of life.
Brian Hibbetts rose through the ranks from a Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department Explorer to police sergeant, so he understands that crime drives poverty and instability and that keeping neighborhoods safe takes real support for the officers who answer the call.
In the Nevada Assembly he backs proposals that give the men and women who protect our communities the tools, training, and resources they need, and he supports stronger laws against violent criminals and those who prey on children and vulnerable Nevadans.
Public safety works best when local, state, and federal partners enforce the law together, and that is the standard Brian brings to Assembly District 13.
Education
Brian Hibbetts supports school choice and parental rights so a child's zip code never decides the quality of their education.
Every child deserves a strong education that prepares them for real life.
Brian Hibbetts believes Nevada families should have more say and more options, especially when a school is not meeting their child's needs.
He supports expanding school choice and protecting parental rights, and he backs reforms that let local communities take a more responsive role rather than leaving families stuck inside one of the largest school districts in the country.
His goal is simple and widely shared, that where a child grows up should never limit the quality of the school they can attend.
For families across Nevada Assembly District 13, Brian is focused on better options, higher standards, and real accountability.
Economic Prosperity
Brian Hibbetts is focused on lowering the cost of living in Nevada through fiscal responsibility, more good jobs, and a more diversified economy.
Nevada families are feeling the squeeze, and Brian Hibbetts is focused on making life more affordable.
He supports policies that grow good jobs, raise wages, and welcome new industries so the state is more resilient when tourism slows.
Brian believes we can strengthen the economy without piling on mandates, higher taxes, or heavy regulation, and as a careful steward of taxpayer dollars he fights wasteful government spending.
He also works across the aisle when it serves Nevada, including the bipartisan cigar tax reform he sponsored with a Democratic colleague to protect small businesses while preserving state revenue.
That practical, results first approach is what Brian brings to economic policy in Assembly District 13.
Constitutional Rights
Brian Hibbetts stands up for constitutional rights, individual liberty, and a government that stays accountable to the people it serves.
Brian Hibbetts spent his career defending the rights and safety of his fellow Nevadans, and he brings that same commitment to protecting individual freedom.
He believes every person is entitled to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness, and that government must respect constitutional limits and remain accountable to the people.
Brian supports reforms that keep decision making close to the people's house and preserve the balance of power, and he stands firm in defense of the individual liberties Nevadans hold dear.
In Nevada Assembly District 13, that means a representative who takes the Constitution seriously and works to ensure your freedoms are never infringed.
