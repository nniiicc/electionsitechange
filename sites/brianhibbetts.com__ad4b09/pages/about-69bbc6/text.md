Meet Brian Hibbetts
Brian Hibbetts was born and raised in Las Vegas.
He grew up in a working class family.
From a young age, Brian learned the value of hard work and serving your neighbor from his mother and father.
At the age of 15, Brian started volunteering with the Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department (LVMPD) Explorer program where he developed a passion for helping others.
Brian joined LVMPD full time in 1994 and quickly rose through the ranks and committed himself to protecting our communities and enforcing and implementing our laws.
Since retiring from the force in 2021, Brian has been a leader to the community that has given him so much.
Brian understands that our state needs dedicated and proven leaders if we are going to make Nevada the comeback state.
If re-elected, Brian is committed to keeping our communities safe; promoting school choice for students and parents; strengthening our economy through economic diversity and fighting against job killing legislation; and protecting our individual freedoms.
Community Involvement
Statewide Boards and Committees
Former member of several professional committees and associations, including the Nevada Sheriffs' and Chiefs' Association CCW subcommittee, the Nevada Organized Retail Crime Association board, and the Critical Incident Response Team peer program.
Nonprofit Leadership
Co-founder and Director of Ex-Con Inc. of Nevada, a 501(c)(3) dedicated to developing the next generation of law enforcement professionals through the Explorer Program.
Community Service
Active volunteer with Just One Project, Jobs for Nevada's Graduates, and Leaders in Training, supporting families and youth across Southern Nevada.
Lifetime Memberships
Life member of the Republican National Committee, the National Rifle Association, and the Las Vegas Emerald Society.
