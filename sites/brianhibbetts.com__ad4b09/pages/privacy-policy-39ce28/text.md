Friends of Brian Hibbetts ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide through this website.
Information we collect
We collect information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you sign up for updates, volunteer, or contact the campaign.
We may also collect basic, non identifying analytics about how visitors use the site.
How we use your information
We use your information to send campaign updates, share news and events, request volunteer support, solicit contributions, and respond to your questions.
We do not sell your personal information.
Text messaging program
By providing your phone number and checking the consent box on our signup form, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing and informational calls and text messages from Brian Hibbetts and Friends of Brian Hibbetts, including through artificial, pre-recorded, autodialed, and automated means.
Consent is not a condition of any purchase or contribution.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.
Mobile information sharing
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or message delivery, is permitted.
All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
How we protect your information
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you provide.
No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your choices
You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message, and from texts by replying STOP.
You may contact us to review, correct, or delete your information.
