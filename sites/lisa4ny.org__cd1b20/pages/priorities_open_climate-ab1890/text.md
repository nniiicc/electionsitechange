We all have a right to them.
But rising housing prices, taxes, and rents make it hard for young people to start their lives and for seniors to stay in their homes.
A broken healthcare system means that too many people cannot access the care they need, and one major incident can bankrupt a family.
Whether a kid has access to a good public education depends on the zip code their family lives in, and community colleges are too expensive for many.
As your state senator, I will be laser-focused on solving these problems, so the Hudson Valley remains a place where families grow and thrive together.
Support Working New Yorkers › Barriers like inadequate childcare and eldercare, transportation gaps, immigration status, disability barriers and an unfair justice system keep working people from flourishing.
I will fight for a New York where everyone has the chance to thrive.
This includes investing in worker power and the labor movement.
Strong unions built the middle class, and strong unions will rebuild it.
Working people deserve fair wages, safe working conditions, and the right to organize and bargain collectively.
I will always stand with organized labor because the fight for workers' rights is the fight for all of our rights.
Tackle the Climate Crisis › Climate change is slowly robbing our children of the beautiful world we grew up in.
Extreme weather damage and climate-driven inflation are already upending our lives and jobs, hitting working families hardest, and driving the affordability crisis in the Hudson Valley.
I will advance climate solutions that lower energy costs, create jobs, protect our communities from costly climate disasters and protect New Yorkers’ health.
Keep New Yorkers Safe › I will defend New Yorkers’ reproductive freedom and bodily autonomy, work to protect our clean air and water, and address the epidemic of gun violence.
Protect Our Democracy › New York must remain a bulwark for American democracy.
I will stand against ICE.
I will fight to protect the voting rights and freedoms of every New Yorker, and hold those who seek to violate them accountable.
