Skip to main content ← Back to News Democratic Legislative Campaign Committee Names Lisa Kaul's Campaign a National Target Race DLCC Designation Highlights the Critical Role of New York’s 39th District in Building a Democratic Supermajority July 31, 2026 | Contact: Sapna Moudgil-Shah · Sapna@lisa4ny.org POUGHKEEPSIE, NY — The Democratic Legislative Campaign Committee (DLCC) has named Lisa Kaul’s campaign for New York Senate District 39 a National Target Race, recognizing it as a must win race for Democrats in the New York Senate.
The designation highlights the importance of the race as Democrats work to gain one additional seat needed to build a supermajority in the New York Senate.
The DLCC’s National Target Race program identifies the nation’s top legislative battlegrounds and provides strategic investments, campaign infrastructure, and organizational support to help elect Democratic candidates in key races.
The designation highlights the importance of New York’s 39th Senate District in strengthening the Democratic Majority and advancing policies that improve the lives of working families across New York.
“I'm honored that the Democratic Legislative Campaign Committee has recognized the importance of this race and the momentum our campaign is building,” said Lisa Kaul.
“This designation reflects what we are hearing everyday from voters across Dutchess, Orange and Putnam Counties, that people are ready for leadership that listens, brings people together and focuses on making life more affordable, protecting our environment, and tackling the issues that keep our communities thriving.
I am grateful for the DLCC’s support and I am excited to continue building a winning coalition that will help flip this seat in November.” DLCC President Heather Williams said: “Democrats need to gain just one seat to build a supermajority in the New York State Senate.
We’re proud to uplift Lisa Kaul in this must-win race.
Lisa has already hit the ground running, and the DLCC will be there every step of the way to get her and New York Democrats across the finish line.” The DLCC is the official Democratic Party committee dedicated to building Democratic power in state legislatures nationwide.
Its National Target Race program highlights candidates in the most competitive legislative contests across the country and directs critical resources to candidates best positioned to expand Democratic majorities.
The DLCC is on offense this year and already working to deliver resources and infrastructure across the country after launching its most expansive Target Map to date across 42 chambers in 27 states.
The DLCC designation adds to the growing momentum behind Lisa Kaul’s campaign, which has earned the support of a broad coalition of organizations such as Eleanor's Legacy, Moms Demand Action, Planned Parenthood Empire State Votes PAC, New York State Nurses Association (NYSNA), 1199 SEIU, The Campaign for New York Health, Communications Workers of America (CWA), Indian American Impact and Patriot PAC.
