Current town service Serving Barrington, one decision at a time.
These roles involve the land use, budgets, and capital investments that shape the town’s future.
Planning Board · Member Land use and planning I serve on Barrington’s Planning Board, which reviews land-use proposals and works within the town’s planning and zoning framework.
Advisory Budget Committee · Member Municipal budgets and priorities As a member of Barrington’s Advisory Budget Committee, I review the town’s municipal budget—not the school district budget —and consider spending priorities, costs, and implications for taxpayers.
Capital Improvement Program Committee · Chair Municipal capital planning and oversight I chair Barrington’s newly created Capital Improvement Program Committee , helping strengthen oversight of municipal capital planning and spending.
We evaluate proposed projects, priorities, and costs across multiple years.
Community and regional involvement Volunteering beyond town hall.
