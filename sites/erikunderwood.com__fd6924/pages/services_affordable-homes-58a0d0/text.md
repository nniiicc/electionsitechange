I will create a special department within the Division of Housing to offer home loans to any Coloradan who wants to buy a home (minimum one year Colorado residency).

We’ll invest $500 million initially.
Each home buyer agrees to contribute 1.5% back to Colorado when they sell, rolling money back into the fund to help the next family.
Home sellers who sell to Colorado at market evaluation receive tax credits.
This isn’t charity – it’s smart investment in our people.
