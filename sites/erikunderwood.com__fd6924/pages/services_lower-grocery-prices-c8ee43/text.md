Donate Now STATE-RUN GROCERY STORES: COMPETING FOR COLORADO FAMILIES Growing up hungry in the projects, I know what it means when food costs spiral out of control.
Eggs and milk are almost at gold prices.
We’ll create state-run grocery stores to compete with big chains, but our prices will be lower and our food comes directly from Colorado farmers and local businesses who can’t get their products into King Soopers.
We’ll start in rural communities ( then expand to metro Denver ) where families drive over an hour just to reach a Walmart.
Soon-to-expire items get donated to food banks and homeless shelters – creating a complete ecosystem of food security.
