Get to Know Representative Kirby
- Integrity Leading with compassion, faith, and experience, Roger Kirby aims to provide every constituent of District 101 with the kind of representation he would want for his family.
That means honest, approachable, and hopeful leadership, for everyone.

He knows District 101 is an opportunity to come together as neighbors and friends, and prosper as a unified community.
