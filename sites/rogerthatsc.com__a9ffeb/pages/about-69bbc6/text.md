Meet Roger Kirby
Representing SC House 101
Welcome, Friends!
I am so happy you’re here!
I wanted to take a chance to introduce myself.
I am Roger Kirby, and I am running for re-election in 2022, this time to represent SC House 101.
I couldn’t be more honored for the chance to serve both new and familiar constituents.
I was born and raised in Lake City, South Carolina.
I am deeply connected to my hometown and the person it shaped me to be.
I hope you’ll get to know me and trust me to lead us forward, together, into a new and unified District 101.
Family Ties
Not only was I born and raised here, but I have been raising my own family here for almost 30 years.
In 2021, my wife Pam and I welcomed our first grandchild.
My daughters, my wife, my granddaughter, my parents, and the family and friends that surround me in Lake City are what keep me striving to help build a bright and prosperous future.
Not just for my family, but for every family.
Faith and Encouragement
I have served as music director at the Lake City Baptist church for over 27 years.
My faith has led me through the many ups and downs of life, and it is my honor to express that faith through song and praise.
I will always take the chance to spread some encouragement wherever I go, whether that be singing for Congress or the choir.
Leading with Love
Focusing on the love I have for my family and the faith I have in my community, I do my best to provide honest and efficient leadership to my great hometown of Lake City and beyond.
I hope I can show all constituents of District 101 what quality care can look like!
Get in touch.
I can’t wait to hear from you, or better yet meet you!
Please feel free to reach out with any comments or questions regarding myself or District 101.
We are in this together!
