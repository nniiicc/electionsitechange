VAN DREW CAMPAIGN DISMISSES LATEST DCCC WISH LIST
October 2, 2026 — United States Congressman Jeff Van Drew’s campaign released the following statement on the DCCC adding Congressman Van Drew’s district to their “Districts in Play” wish list.
“Every cycle the Democrats get together, commission a fake poll saying this seat is in play to use as a fundraising ploy, and every year Congressman Van Drew proves them wrong – this is nothing new,” said Van Drew’s Campaign Manager and Director of Political Affairs Ron Filan.
“We predicted back in June they would follow this same playbook and appreciate them proving us right.
The DCCC has about a third of House Republicans on one of their silly wishlists or another, but Congressman Van Drew doesn’t take anything for granted and will continue to campaign as hard as he always has.”
