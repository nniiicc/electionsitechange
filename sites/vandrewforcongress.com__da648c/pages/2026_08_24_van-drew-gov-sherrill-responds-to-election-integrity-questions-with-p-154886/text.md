“When we first raised this issue, we did so because we were genuinely concerned about what happened in New Jersey and wanted to understand exactly how it happened,” said Congressman Van Drew.
“For years, those of us who raised concerns about the integrity of our voter rolls were dismissed as conspiracy theorists.
Then New Jersey admitted that thousands of individuals who said they were not citizens were registered to vote, and many registered through the error went on to cast ballots.
“We did not blame Governor Sherrill for a failure that occurred under the previous Murphy administration.
We did not attack her personally or drag unrelated Democratic politicians into our letter.
With the help of my colleagues in the Republican Study Committee, we asked basic, fact-finding questions about how the system failed mechanically and electronically, why the safeguards did not catch it, and what is being done to make sure it never happens again.
