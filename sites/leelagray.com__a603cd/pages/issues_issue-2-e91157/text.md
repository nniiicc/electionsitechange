Growing Economic Opportunities
Supporting Small Businesses and Creating Jobs
When local shops struggle to survive, our entire community feels the impact.
We must end illegal tariffs, and cut red tape and regulatory burdens that drain capital from our entrepreneurs so they can grow and stay in Florida’s 13th District.
Investing in Our Workforce
Skilled labor is a critical resource in our community, and it’s time we invest in our people.
We must ensure the next generation has more than just a paycheck; we must build career paths to success, stability, and wealth for families here at home.
Strengthening the Middle Class
The middle class should be the engine of the American economy, but that motor is stalling under a Congress that defaults to chaos over responsible leadership.
We need to widen the path to the middle class and ensure workers and families have more money in their pockets.
We must focus on aggressively lowering prices and tackling the insurance crisis head-on.
We also must commit to making childcare affordable and homeownership attainable, ensuring the next generation has better opportunities.
Securing the Future for the Next Generation
Many young people today face an uphill battle against soaring housing costs, student debt, and a shifting job market.
The next generation needs a fierce advocate, and we need to provide them with the tools and resources to adapt and thrive in a modern economy.
