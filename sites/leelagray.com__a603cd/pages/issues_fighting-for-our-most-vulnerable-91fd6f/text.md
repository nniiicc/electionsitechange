Fighting for Our Most Vulnerable
Upholding the Promise to Our Veterans
Our veterans served on the front lines, yet return home to a web of bureaucratic red tape to access their earned benefits.
They have earned quality and timely healthcare.
We must ensure our veterans cannot only access these benefits, but also have a path to success and stability upon their transition from service.
Securing A Dignified Retirement for Our Seniors
Today, too many seniors face economic strain.
We have a duty to honor those who came before us.
We must protect and strengthen Social Security and Medicare to ensure every senior can age with dignity.
Protecting Our Children
From a rising mental health crisis to increasing online exploitation and soaring education costs, America’s children face a reality they didn’t create.
Our kids need a fierce champion in Congress for their mental health, safety, and success.
