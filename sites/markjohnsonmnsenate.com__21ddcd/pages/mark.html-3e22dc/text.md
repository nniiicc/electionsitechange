Why?
Because we need an advocate for SD1 who is able to fight for the unique interests of Northwestern Minnesotans.
We have an opportunity in the Minnesota Senate that hasn’t existed since 1982 – to be heard in St.
Paul!
Northwest Minnesota has many concerns that need immediate attention: -Our rights as property owners are being threatened by jurisdictional grabs by the federal and state governments.
This may have little impact in Minneapolis or St.
