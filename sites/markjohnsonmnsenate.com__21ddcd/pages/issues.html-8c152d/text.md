Building a Minnesota where families can thrive, businesses can grow, and our children and grandchildren have every reason to stay, build their futures, and raise families of their own.
Affordability & Responsible Spending.
Minnesota families and businesses have to make choices about what they can afford, and state government should too.
Mark supports responsible budgets, lower tax burdens, and a regulatory environment that allows Minnesota businesses to compete, invest, and grow.
Strong Rural Communities.
Roads, bridges, flood protection, water systems, housing, and broadband directly affect whether Northwest Minnesota communities can grow.
Mark's focus is making sure rural Minnesota receives the infrastructure investment it needs to support families, employers and the next generation. ﻿ Growing Northwest Minnesota's Economy.
Agriculture remains the foundation of Minnesota's economy, alongside manufacturing, health care, and small businesses.
Keeping those industries strong requires workers, housing, reliable and affordable energy, and a tax and regulatory environment that encourages businesses to expand rather than look elsewhere.
Fraud & Accountability Minnesotans should be able to trust that their tax dollars are going where lawmakers said they would go.
Mark has made stronger oversight, fraud prevention and accountability in state government a priority, including pushing for reforms designed to identify problems earlier and hold agencies accountable.
