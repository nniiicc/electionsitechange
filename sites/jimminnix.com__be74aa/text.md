Welcome to my campaign website!
Western Kansas has always been my home and I”m honored for the opportunity to represent my friends and neighbors in the Kansas House of Representatives.
The 118th House district has changed a bit and now includes Sheridan, Logan, Gove, Trego, Greeley, Wichita, Scott, Lane and Ness counties.
We are the largest House district in the state.
I have been and will continue to be a strong conservative voice for our rural communities.
I have fought passionately to lower taxes, fund our roads and schools, defend the second amendment, stop government overreach, support our agriculture community and protect life and the values that matter most to us.
I look forward to visiting with you in the months ahead about the issues most important to you and your family.
