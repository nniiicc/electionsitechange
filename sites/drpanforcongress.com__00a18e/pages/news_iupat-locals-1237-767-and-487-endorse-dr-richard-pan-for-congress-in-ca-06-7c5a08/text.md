Richard Pan for Congress in CA-06 May 27 Written By Daisy Stein Sacramento, CA — The International Union of Painters and Allied Trades (IUPAT) Locals 1237, 767, and 487 have endorsed Dr.
Richard Pan in his campaign for Congress in California’s 6th Congressional District, backing the only Democratic candidate in the race with a proven record of supporting workers, protecting public health, and lowering costs for working families.
IUPAT Locals 1237, 767, and 487 represent skilled trades workers across Northern California, including painters, drywall finishers, and glaziers who help build and maintain homes, schools, and critical community infrastructure.
Throughout his career as a physician and State Senator, Dr.
Pan has focused on lowering costs for families and expanding access to health care – issues that directly impact working people on and off the job.
He worked to reduce the uninsured rate by almost two-thirds and fought to make prescription drugs more affordable for families.
“I’m honored to earn the endorsement of IUPAT Locals in our district,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“Their members do essential work in our communities every day.
In Congress, I will fight to protect the right to organize and ensure workers have safe conditions on the job.” Dr.
Pan’s campaign has built the strongest labor coalition in the race, with support from organizations representing workers across health care, education, public service, public safety, and beyond.
## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
