National Union of Healthcare Workers Endorses Dr.
Richard Pan for Congress
Sacramento, CA — The National Union of Healthcare Workers has endorsed Dr.
Richard Pan in his campaign for Congress in California’s 6th Congressional District.
NUHW represents nearly 19,000 health care workers across California and Hawaii, including mental health clinicians, nurses, nursing assistants, medical technicians, clerks, and service workers.
NUHW cited Dr.
Pan’s record as a pediatrician, union member, and former chair of the Assembly and Senate Health Committees, where he helped implement the Affordable Care Act, strengthened Medi-Cal, expanded access to care, and fought for greater health plan accountability and mental health parity.
“Dr.
Richard Pan has spent his career fighting for patients, families, and access to quality care,” said Sophia Mendoza, NUHW President.
“As a pediatrician and legislator, he understands the challenges facing both patients and frontline health care workers.
NUHW is proud to endorse Dr.
Pan because he has the experience and record to stand up for caregivers, improve access to care, and deliver for working families.”
Dr.
Pan thanked NUHW for its endorsement.
“As a pediatrician and union member, I know that quality care depends on supporting the workers who deliver it,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“Health care workers are on the front lines for patients every day, and they deserve safe staffing, fair wages, and a real voice in the workplace.
I’m honored to have NUHW’s support, and in Congress I will fight to expand access to affordable care, protect collective bargaining rights, strengthen mental health care, and make sure patients and caregivers come before corporate profits.”
NUHW’s endorsement highlights Dr.
Pan’s support from frontline health care workers who know he has the experience to protect collective bargaining rights, strengthen safe staffing and workplace standards, expand mental health care, and hold health care corporations accountable.
##
Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
