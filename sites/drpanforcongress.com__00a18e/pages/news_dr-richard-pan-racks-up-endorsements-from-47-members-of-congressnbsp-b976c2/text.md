Richard Pan Earns Endorsement of 47 Members of Congress, 37 Members of the California Delegation SACRAMENTO CA – Today, Dr.
Richard Pan announced endorsements from 47 Members of Congress – including 37 Members of the California delegation – underscoring the broad support behind his campaign to defeat MAGA Republican Kevin Kiley in California’s 6th Congressional District, one of the nation’s most competitive races to determine the majority in Congress.
“I am honored to have the support of 47 Members of Congress committed to stopping the extreme Donald Trump and Kevin Kiley agenda, as we work together to flip CA-06 and build a House Majority that puts working families first,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“This campaign is about lowering costs, protecting access to health care, and making government work for the people it serves.
Kevin Kiley has been a loyal rubber stamp for the worst of the Trump agenda hurting California families, while I’ve spent my career caring for patients, expanding access to health care, and delivering results.

## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
