Richard Pan for Congress in CA-06 May 28 Written By Daisy Stein Sacramento, CA — Today, Dr.
Richard Pan announced the endorsement of Congressman Mark Takano in his campaign for Congress in California’s 6th Congressional District.
Congressman Takano cited Dr.
Pan’s record as a pediatrician, public servant, and longtime champion for children, families, and civil rights.
“Dr.
Richard Pan is passionate about caring for people, both as a pediatrician and an elected official,” said Congressman Mark Takano.
“He won his first campaign on protecting LGBTQ+ and reproductive rights in a Republican district.
Then, in the legislature, he faced vitriol and personal attacks to keep children protected.
We need his courage and compassion in Congress.
I’m proud to endorse him because we need his strength in Washington to help California families.” Takano’s endorsement adds to the strongest endorsement coalition in the race, with support from labor unions, health care professionals, educators, firefighters, community organizations, and state and local leaders across California.
“Congressman Takano has spent his career fighting for civil rights, veterans, and working families,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“I’m honored to have his support in this campaign.
I’ve never backed down from tough fights when children, families, and our rights are on the line, and I’ll bring that same courage and compassion to Congress.” Dr.
Pan is a pediatrician, former State Senator, and small business owner running for Congress to bring courage and compassion to Washington, protect children and families, defend fundamental rights, and make government work for people again.
## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
