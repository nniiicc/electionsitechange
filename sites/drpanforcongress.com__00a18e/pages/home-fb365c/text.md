Protecting Our Healthcare Dr.
Pan has dedicated his career to ensuring everyone has access to quality affordable healthcare.
As a state senator, Dr.
Pan passed laws that cut the price of insulin and secured healthcare for millions more Californians.
In Congress, Dr.
Pan will stop Trump from gutting the Affordable Care Act, protect Medicare, stop insurance companies from denying critical care prescribed by doctors, and fight to lower prescription drug costs.
Taking on Trump & RFK Jr.
Dr.

Helping Families Afford Necessities and Lower Costs Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter in bringing costs down for working people.
In the State Senate, Dr.
Pan worked to protect patients and consumers from greedy corporations and high prices.

In Washington, he will prioritize making necessities more affordable, loan relief for educational debt and fight to roll back Trump's tariff taxes that are raising prices on Californians.
Keeping Our Neighborhoods Safe Dr.

In Congress he’ll work to institute universal background checks and a ban on assault weapons.

Immigration As the son of immigrants from Taiwan, Dr.
Pan believes immigrants are vital to growing the American economy and contribute to America’s world leadership in innovation.

Dr.
Pan will stand with small businesses, not corporate monopolies, and fight to keep our local economy strong.
Ending Gun Violence Dr.
Pan understands that gun violence is the leading cause of death for children in America.
