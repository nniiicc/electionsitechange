Richard Pan Added to DCCC’s Nationally Targeted ‘Red to Blue’ Program In CA-06 Jun 10 Written By Daisy Stein Pediatrician, Health Advocate, Small Business Owner and Father, Dr.
Richard Pan Will Face MAGA Republican Congressman Kevin Kiley in November To Determine The Majority In Congress SACRAMENTO CA – Today, the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC) announced their addition of Dr.
Richard Pan to its highly competitive ‘Red to Blue’ program, following his primary victory in California’s 6th Congressional District.
Dr.
Pan will face off against MAGA Republican Kevin Kiley and is one of just 24 candidates included on the Red to Blue lineup, signaling this is a nationally targeted General Election that will determine the majority in Congress.
“I’m proud to have the support of the DCCC as we enter the next phase of this campaign," said Dr.
Richard Pan .
“This race is about the future of our communities and whether the federal government can deliver on helping families struggling with losing their healthcare and the rising costs of food, gas, and housing or continuing the current gridlock and extremism of the Republican Congress.
Together, we’re building the coalition needed to defeat Kevin Kiley, defend our healthcare, lower costs, and deliver the leadership Sacramento families deserve.” “From the pediatrician’s office to the State Capitol, Dr.
Richard Pan has spent his career defending public health, standing up to extremists, and delivering results for California families.
He is exactly the kind of battle-tested leader needed to take on ‘MAGA champion’ Kevin Kiley, who is desperately trying to rewrite his long record of fealty to Donald Trump.
CA-06 voters won’t be fooled by Kiley’s fake ‘independence,’ and will elect Dr.
Pan come November,” said DCCC Chair Suzan DelBene.
CA-06 is one of Democrats' strongest pickup opportunities in the country.
Following the overwhelming passage of Proposition 50, the new district now gives Democrats an 8 point advantage.
Dr.
Pan was previously elected to represent over 70% of CA-06 in the State Legislature, giving him strong name recognition and an established coalition of support for the general election.
Dr.
Pan is running to defeat 2020 election denier Kevin Kiley, who has built his career as a hardline MAGA extremist, earning Trump's personal endorsement as a “ MAGA champion ,” voting with Trump's agenda 98% of the time , backing extremist Republican leadership, and rubber-stamping policies that take away health care, freedoms, and economic security for California families.
## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
