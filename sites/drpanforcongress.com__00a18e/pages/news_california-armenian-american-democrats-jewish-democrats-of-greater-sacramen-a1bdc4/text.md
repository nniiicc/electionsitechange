Pan in CA-06 May 18 Written By Daisy Stein Sacramento, CA — Three additional Democratic clubs have endorsed Dr.
Richard Pan in his campaign for Congress in California’s 6th Congressional District: the Jewish Democrats of Greater Sacramento, California Armenian American Democrats, and the Town & Country Democratic Club.
The endorsements reflect Dr.
Pan’s strong support from Democratic activists and community organizations across the district and state who know his record of delivering results for families, protecting civil rights, and standing up for communities under attack.
The Jewish Democrats of Greater Sacramento represent Jewish Democrats and allies committed to civic engagement, democratic values, and community advocacy.
California Armenian American Democrats serve as a voice for Armenian Americans within the California Democratic Party, working to strengthen civic participation, protect truth and justice, support a free and democratic Armenia, and fight for social justice, civil rights, and economic fairness.
The Town & Country Democratic Club has been organizing Democrats in Arden-Arcade and Carmichael since the early 1950s. “ I’m honored to earn the support of the Jewish Democrats of Greater Sacramento, California Armenian American Democrats, and the Town & Country Democratic Club, ” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“These clubs represent important constituencies of the broad coalition that is the Democratic party who organize and advocate for our shared Democratic values.
In Congress, I will fight to protect democracy, defend civil rights, lower costs for families, and make sure every community has a voice.” Dr.
Pan represented over 60% of the district in the State Legislature, where he helped reduce the uninsured rate in California by almost two-thirds, lowered prescription drug costs, protected children from preventable disease, and delivered results for the Sacramento region.
The endorsements add to Dr.
Pan’s broad coalition of support from Democratic clubs, labor unions, health care professionals, educators, local elected officials, and community leaders across CA-06.
## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
