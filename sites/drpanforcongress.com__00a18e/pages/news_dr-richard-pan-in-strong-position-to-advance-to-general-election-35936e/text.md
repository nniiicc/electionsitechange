Richard Pan In Strong Position To Advance To General Election Jun 3 Written By Daisy Stein With Well Over 100,000 Ballots Yet To Be Counted In Sacramento County Alone, Dr.
Pan Is In A Strong Position To Be The Democratic Nominee In CA-06 Sacramento, CA — As the leading Democratic candidate, Dr.
Richard Pan, pediatrician, health advocate, and former State Senator, is in a strong position to advance to the General Election in California’s 6th Congressional District.
“I am grateful to the voters of the 6th Congressional District.
Once every vote is counted, we are confident I will advance to the General Election,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“People in the district are struggling as the cost of gas, food, and housing spike and health care is being taken away by the Trump Administration.
This district deserves a Congressmember who has worked to improve lives in our neighborhoods and will represent the people to make sure the government works for us.
I will be that Congressmember.” The General Election will give voters a clear choice between Dr.
Richard Pan, who obtained health care for millions of Californians and former Republican Kevin Kiley, who has a 98% voting record with Donald Trump, falling in line to cut health care and food for millions of Californians.
Donald Trump’s “MAGA Champion”, Kevin Kiley has been a reliable vote for an extreme agenda in Congress that is driving up prices for California families.
“Kevin Kiley has voted with Trump and the Republican Congressional leadership as families in the 6th district have struggled with the rising cost of gas, groceries, child care, and health care from Trump’s chaotic tariffs and wars,” said Dr.
Pan.
“People of the 6th District deserve a representative that has a proven record of addressing their needs first, whether it’s ensuring children and families have health care, distributing food when they are hungry, or helping seniors and children with caregivers.” Dr.
Pan thanked the voters across CA-06 for placing their trust in him and appreciation for his fellow candidates who put themselves forward in this primary.
Dr.
Pan is supported by a large coalition of working people and health professionals including nurses, teachers, firefighters, caregivers, public employees, and elected officials from across CA-06, California and the nation.
With the largest number of grassroots donors of the Democratic candidates, Dr.
Pan’s campaign is powered by volunteers and union members who walked neighborhoods and called voters across the district.
Dr.
Pan has devoted his career as a pediatrician, educator, small business owner, and public servant to building healthier, safer communities in the Sacramento region and California.
He has been a champion for science, facts, human rights, and democracy.
## Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
