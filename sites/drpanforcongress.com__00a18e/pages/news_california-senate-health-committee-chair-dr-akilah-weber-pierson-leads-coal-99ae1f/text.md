California Senate Health Committee Chair Dr.
Akilah Weber Pierson Leads Coalition of Legislative Endorsements For Dr.
Richard Pan
Assembly Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry, State Senators Tom Umberg, Josh Becker, and Dr.
Akilah Weber Pierson Join Former Assemblymembers Paul Fong and Warren Furutani and Senate Majority Leader Emeritus Bill Monning in Endorsing Dr.
Pan
Sacramento, CA — Today, Dr.
Richard Pan announced the endorsements of seven current and former California state legislators and legislative leaders in his campaign for Congress in California’s 6th Congressional District: Assembly Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry, State Senator Tom Umberg, State Senator Josh Becker, State Senator Dr.
Akilah Weber Pierson, former Assemblymember Paul Fong, former Assemblymember Warren Furutani, and State Senate Majority Leader Emeritus Bill Monning.
State Senator Dr.
Akilah Weber Pierson is the Chair of the California Legislative Black Caucus, Assemblymember Cecilia Aguiar-Curry is the Chair of the California Legislative Women’s Caucus and Former Assemblymembers Paul Fong and Warren Furutani both served as Chair of the California Asian and Pacific Islander Legislative Caucus.
The endorsements reflect the strong support Dr.
Pan has earned from leaders across California who know his record of delivering results for families, lowering health care costs, standing up for science, and taking on tough fights to protect children and communities.
“I am honored to earn the support of these current and former legislative leaders,” said Dr.
Richard Pan.
“I served in the Legislature because I believe the government should solve real problems for families, from lowering health care costs to strengthening schools and keeping our communities safe.
In Congress, I will bring that same focus to Washington and fight for the people of Placer, Sacramento, and Yolo counties.”
A pediatrician, former State Senator, and former Assemblymember, Dr.
Pan represented much of the new 6th Congressional District in the State Legislature.
He built a record of delivering results for families, including expanding access to health care, lowering prescription drug costs, protecting children’s health, and standing up to MAGA extremism and attacks on science.
Dr.
Pan has earned broad support from federal, state, and local leaders, labor unions, health care professionals, educators, first responders, and community advocates who trust him to deliver for CA-06 in Congress.
##
Dr.
Richard Pan is a pediatrician, educator, small business owner and Former State Senator dedicated to keeping California’s families healthy and safe.
In the State Senate, he championed landmark child and school safety laws and lowered the uninsured rate among children by two-thirds – taking on RFK Jr. twice after preventable disease outbreaks.
A Harvard-trained pediatrician and UC Davis professor, he has cared for underserved children, trained future doctors and co-founded Healthy Kids Healthy Future, providing health coverage to more than 65,000 kids.
Known for flipping his state legislative seat from Red to Blue, Dr.
Pan is a proven fighter who brings people together to get results — expanding access to health care, strengthening schools, and improving public safety.
A husband, father, and small business owner, he understands the challenges families face and is running for Congress to make government work for people again, so every child and family in California has the chance to succeed.
