MEET FRANK CARROLL
I was born and raised in Chicago and made the move to Arizona some thirty years ago.
My wife Joanne and I recently celebrated twenty-six years together.
Joanne retired from the airlines having worked most of her career in customer service.
My career has always centered around the transportation technology, from the muscle cars of the ’60 & ’70’s through the alternative vehicles of today.
I became involved in the Arizona Republican Party shortly after arriving in Arizona.
I always serve as a Precinct Committeeman representing our grassroots, and I have also served as Precinct Captain, Area Director, and State Delegate.
I also served as past President of the Sun City West Republican Club.
Before being elected to the State Senate in 2022, I served two terms in the Arizona House of Representatives.
I’m an Endowment Life Member of the National Rifle Association, member of the Arizona State Rifle and Pistol Association, member of the AZ Citizens Defense League, and an activist with Americans for Prosperity, pursuing smaller government and lower taxes.
Joanne and I are both Christian constitutional conservatives who stand for the Republican principles that make this country the great republic it is.
I’m honored to serve you and ask for your continued support.
Thank you!
"Arizona has been one of the best governed states in the country for years, and I'm proud to have played a part in that."
