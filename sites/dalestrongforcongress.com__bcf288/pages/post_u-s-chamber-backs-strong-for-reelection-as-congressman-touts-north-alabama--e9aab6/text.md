U.S.
Chamber Backs Strong for Reelection as Congressman Touts North Alabama Growth
Mecca Musick | Yellowhammer News
Standing in front of rows of tractors and heavy equipment at a longtime North Alabama family business, U.S.
Rep.
Dale Strong (R-Huntsville) picked up the endorsement of the U.S.
Chamber of Commerce on Monday in his bid for another term representing Alabama’s Fifth Congressional District.
