Access to reproductive health care is the issue that first drew me into this work.
As a parent and a person who has sought and advocated for reproductive healthcare for myself and people I care about, I believe health care decisions belong between a person and their doctor.
I’ll vote to protect access to that care in Montana and to let doctors provide it without fear.
A Montana value Montanans prize independence and privacy, and our state constitution protects the right to privacy in plain words.
In 2024 Montana voters wrote reproductive freedom into that constitution with CI-128.
That was Montanans deciding for themselves, and a legislator’s job is to respect it.
Common ground worth building There’s more common ground here than we’re usually offered.
The policies that help a family plan are the same ones that support a family welcoming a child: prenatal care close to home, childcare a working parent can find and afford, and a wage that can raise a kid.
I’ll fight for all of it, alongside neighbors who see this issue differently than I do.
On the record: In 2025 the current representative for House District 39 voted for HB 316, a proposed constitutional amendment to define a person.
Thank you, Planned Parenthood Advocates of Montana.
I’ll work to earn it.
