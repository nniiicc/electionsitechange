Skip to content Home About Issues Get Involved Home About Issues Get Involved Donate Donate Issues Categories Affordability AI and Data Centers Education Environment & Public Lands Healthcare Search Recent Posts Staying Home, Near Family The Decision Is Still Ahead Relief Should Follow the Home Relief Should Follow the Home About one in four households in the Heights rents.
Those neighbors pay property taxes too.
They pay them every month, folded into the rent.
Here is how this year’s property tax relief reached them.
Homeowners mostly got it automatically.
Anyone who had claimed the earlier rebate was enrolled in the lower homestead rate without lifting a finger.
(If you own your home and live in it, confirm your enrollment at homestead.mt.gov through March 1, 2027.
I walked through how in Go Check Your Homestead Enrollment .) Renters depended on someone else’s paperwork.
The lower rate for long-term rentals only applied if the landlord filed for it.
Many landlords did not.
A homeowner who misses the deadline can appeal.
A landlord cannot, so the renter is left with no remedy at all, and the higher tax shows up in next year’s rent.
The renter did everything right and still lost the relief.
That is the part I want to fix.
What I will push for in Helena Relief that follows the home.
Montana already knows which properties are long-term rentals from past filings.
I’ll push to make the reduced rate the default for those homes, with owners certifying rather than reapplying, so the relief reaches the family living there.
A fair appeal for renters’ homes.
When a filing gets missed, the rental should get the same chance to appeal that a homeowner gets.
A simple way for tenants to check.
Renters can already look up whether a landlord filed, using a tool on the Department of Revenue site.
I’ll push to make that easier to find and to understand.
The bigger picture on property taxes, including the lower class for the largest data centers and a stronger circuit breaker for fixed incomes, is in A Tax Class for Them, a Form for Us .
A family that rents in the Heights is as much a part of this neighborhood as a family that owns.
Relief should follow the home.
