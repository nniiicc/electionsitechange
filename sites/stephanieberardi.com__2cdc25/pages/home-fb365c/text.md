Why I’m Running I’m running for State Representative 154th Legislative District because Cheltenham, Jenkintown, Springfield, Pennsylvania deserves a future where the needs of our community come first — not one where they take a back seat to politics as usual.
I believe we can do better.
With leadership that listens and a real commitment to this community, we can build the kind of future that Cheltenham, Jenkintown, Springfield, Pennsylvania deserves.
Stephanie served in the Pennsylvania State government for eight years and brings a fresh new perspective on how to enhance the district with positive changes.
