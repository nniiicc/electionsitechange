Home Meet Stephanie Issues Events News Contribute Make Endorsement Yard Signs Volunteer Meet Stephanie Stephanie Berardi is an entrepreneur, mother, forward thinker, and experienced leader who understands the challenges facing small businesses and the communities they serve.
She has lived in the 154th district for 15 years with her family.
Stephanie began her career as a radio personality with Disney, where she spent more than a decade developing the communication and leadership skills that would ultimately help her launch her own business, Stylin Steph Productions, an event-planning company.
After earning a Master’s Degree in Sustainability from the University of Pennsylvania, Stephanie made it her mission to work in the environmental sector.
While living in Jenkintown, she became actively involved in her community, serving on the Environmental Advisory Committee, the Steering Committee for Jenkintown 2035, and the Recreation Board.
Her commitment to public service led to an opportunity with former State Representative Steve McCarter, who hired Stephanie to assist constituents as a dedicated environmental specialist.
During her time in his office, she played an important role in helping establish the Climate Caucus.
When the COVID-19 pandemic created unprecedented challenges for families across Pennsylvania, Stephanie stepped up to help hundreds of constituents navigate the unemployment system.
She also helped residents recover more than $380,000 in unclaimed funds.
McCarter’s office ultimately recorded 219 paid claims—the highest number among the entire legislative delegation.
Stephanie later joined the Pennsylvania Senate, where she worked with policy leaders to help shape environmental legislation, including GO100PA, an initiative focused on advancing Pennsylvania’s transition toward clean, healthy energy.
Her public-service career continued with the Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection (DEP), where she served as Regional Communications Manager.
In that role, Stephanie helped residents and communities navigate complex environmental crises, including the situation at Bartram’s Garden, the pipeline leak at Washington Crossing, and the SPS factory fire.
She was also appointed as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) for the Energy Programs Office, building extensive knowledge of DEP programs and environmental issues, including environmental justice.
Throughout her career, Stephanie has remained committed to helping people, solving problems, and making government work more effectively for the communities it serves.
After eight years in state government, Stephanie brings a unique combination of communications, business, environmental, and public-service experience.
She is ready to put that experience to work helping create thoughtful, practical, and smart legislation for Pennsylvania.
