Home Meet Stephanie Issues Events News Contribute Make Endorsement Yard Signs Volunteer Home ❭ Issues ❭ Environment Environment Recycling: One persons trash is another's treasure.
Commodities like cardboard, metals can help generate income for small towns and businesses when the infrastructure is there to support the collection of these materials.
We can innovate what we do with our trash to make it work for us.
Single stream recycling might be convenient, but we pay more to have it sorted.
Having dedicated collections for cardboard, glass or metals which can be sold back in bulk, can generate income and reduce hauling fee's.
Energy: Solar and wind are viable options to alternative energy, powered by the natural elements.
We need to break through the barriers to make these options more accessible to our communities.
Preserving Natural Lands: Pennsylvania is home to a vast landscape of natural beauty.
We need to protect our land from data centers, warehouses and legacy pollution.
We need laws that protect our pollinators and incentivize planting native species, wildflower pollinator gardens, and trees.
Foodwaste- is something we can mitigate to reduce the amount of methane that is trapped in our landfills.
Awarding contracts to composters, we can divert more than 28 percent of what we toss to creating beneficial soil.
This seems like such a simple solution.
Let's get the conversation going.
