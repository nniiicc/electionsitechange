Support for the Arts and Entertainment I fully support and stand with artists, musicians, performers, dancers and gig workers.

From health insurance to income instability its hard when you don't have job protections.
I will work towards finding solutions, so you can continue doing what you love to do.
Mental Health So many of us suffer from mental health issues.
Going to therapy should not have a stigma attached to it.
We need to ensure that everyone has access to getting help when they need it.

Veterans Support I will support our Veterans and ensure they have access to basic needs, mental health support, and career needs.
We need to do more to make sure they know they are appreciated for the service.
Protecting our LGBTQ community Love is love.
I fully support our LGBTQ families in your unique journey and will stand by and fight to make sure you always have equal rights.
Education Our schools need funding to make sure they have the resources to provide to students, faculty, and parents.
If a student is struggling, we need to make sure they can be tested to get to the root of what serves them the best.
We need to invest in the building structures to make sure they are more energy efficiant and provide a comfortable space for kids to thrive.
Fair Districts Gerrymandering is wrong plain and simple.
Redistricting boundaries to give one party or another an advantage over an election cycle should never be decided by a politician.
This hurts voters by making it harder to elect their preferred candidates.
Everyone's voices should be heard.
I support Fair Districts and having a nonpartisan commission in place to ensure that there are fair voting districts and boundaries drawn.
Legislative Rules New legislation often sits in a committee and often never makes it to the top of an agenda in order to get out of the committee for a vote.
Having smart legislative procedures in place to ensure that the majority leader doesn't have full control over what bills are considered and which ones never brought up to the committee are needed.
This is too much power for one legislator to have and creates an imbalance when it comes to getting smart legislation past.
I've seen this time and time again where bills have to be reintroduced every year because they sit in the committee and never make it to the floor for a vote.
Creating legislation takes an enormous amount of time and resources.
We need a better system in place to balance the power of these committees.
Emergency Responders Support The most important job in our communities, is the work that our first responders do.
THEY are always there when you need them the most.
THEY are the first line of defense to make sure we stay safe from harm.
Emergency responders are essential workers that dedicate their lives to helping others, no matter what time of day or night they are needed.
That's why I will fight to make sure they get the funding that they need to support the work that they do.
Firefighters should NEVER go out there and fight a fire without getting compensated for the work that they do.
Emergency Responders should never go without overtime pay when they are called into action.
That's why i will fight to make sure they have the resources and compensation that they deserve.
