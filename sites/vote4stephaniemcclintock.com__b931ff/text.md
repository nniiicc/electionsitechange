Meet Stephanie
For WA State Representative
Dist. 18 Pos. 1
Common Sense Conservative Values
I ask for your support as I launch my campaign for re-election to continue my position as your State Representative in the 18th Legislative District, position 1.
It’s an honor to serve you in Olympia, and I’m proud to have been able to accomplish results for us.
I had bipartisan bills signed into law that improve communications for children’s services, increase accountability within the Department of Licensing to better us and remove barriers for job creators.
I worked to advance policies that will keep our taxes from increasing, increase resources for special educators and paraeducators, and bring back safety to our communities, restoring police pursuit.
As the daughter of a police detective, I support law enforcement and will continue to support them in returning public safety to our communities.
I will continue to defend your hard-earned income from reckless spending and policies that negatively impact our families, businesses, and community.
Representation in Olympia should reflect our values in policy, spending and public education.
I would be honored to continue to be your voice for common sense in Olympia, and I humbly ask for your vote.
