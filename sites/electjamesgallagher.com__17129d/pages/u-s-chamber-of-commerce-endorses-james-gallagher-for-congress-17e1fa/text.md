Chamber of Commerce Endorses James Gallagher for Congress YUBA CITY, CA — The U.S.
Chamber of Commerce has endorsed Congressman James Gallagher for reelection in California’s 1st Congressional District, backing Gallagher’s work to strengthen Northern California’s economy and support the businesses, farms and ranches that create jobs across the North State.
Gallagher has made cutting costs and removing unnecessary barriers for local employers a priority in Congress, while fighting for the farmers, ranchers and small businesses that form the backbone of Northern California’s economy.
“Small business owners, farmers and ranchers don’t need Washington making it harder to earn a living,” said Gallagher.
“They need lower costs, less red tape and someone willing to fight for them.
I’m proud to have the U.S.
Chamber standing with us, and I’ll keep working to make the North State a place where businesses can grow, families can prosper and our kids can build a future.” The U.S.
Chamber of Commerce is the nation’s largest business organization, representing companies and local chambers across industries and regions throughout the country.
