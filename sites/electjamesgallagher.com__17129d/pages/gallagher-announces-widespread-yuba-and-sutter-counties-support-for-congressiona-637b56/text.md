Gallagher Announces Widespread Yuba and Sutter Counties Support for Congressional Campaign
Yuba City CA – Assemblyman James Gallagher today announced broad support from respected local leaders across Yuba and Sutter Counties, highlighting strong grassroots momentum in his campaign for Congress and reinforcing his deep roots in the North State.
Community leaders, elected officials, and longtime advocates from across the region have rallied behind Gallagher’s campaign, citing his record of standing up for taxpayers, fighting government overreach, and advocating for rural communities and agriculture.

Gallagher, a lifelong North State resident, has represented the region with a reputation for principled leadership and a commitment to protecting the values that define the Sacramento Valley.
“I’m incredibly grateful for the support from leaders throughout Yuba and Sutter Counties,” said Gallagher.
“These communities are where I was raised, where my family lives, and where I’ve spent my career fighting for common-sense policies that strengthen our local economy, protect our farmers and small businesses, and keep government accountable to the people it serves.”
Supporters in both counties emphasized Gallagher’s accessibility, work ethic, and consistent advocacy for public safety, water reliability, and responsible fiscal policy.
As the campaign continues to build momentum across Northern California, Gallagher’s strong backing from local leaders reflects the trust he has earned throughout the region and the growing coalition supporting his campaign for Congress.
