James Gallagher for Congress Announces Endorsement From California Farm Bureau
Yuba City CA – James Gallagher for Congress announced today the endorsement of the California Farm Bureau for his campaign in the Special Election for Congressional District 1.
The California Farm Bureau is a leading voice for farmers, ranchers, and rural communities across the state.
In announcing the endorsement, California Farm Bureau President Shannon Douglass highlighted Gallagher’s deep understanding of the agricultural community and the urgent need for strong rural representation in Congress.
“The passing of Congressman Doug LaMalfa left a huge void in Congress when it comes to understanding the challenges faced by farmers and ranchers.
James Gallagher has the experience, knowledge, and proven record to help fill that void and be a strong voice for farmers and rural communities,” said Shannon Douglass, President, California Farm Bureau.
Gallagher said he is honored to earn the support of the California Farm Bureau and remains committed to fighting for the families, businesses, and communities that keep California agriculture strong.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the California Farm Bureau,” said James Gallagher.
“Farmers and ranchers are the backbone of our rural economy and way of life, and they need leaders in Congress who understand the real challenges they face every day — from water and regulatory burdens to rising costs and threats to their livelihoods.
I will always fight to protect our agricultural heritage, strengthen rural communities, and ensure the voices of our farmers and ranchers are heard in Washington.”
As a longtime advocate for agriculture and rural California, Gallagher has built a record of standing up for farmers, ranchers, and the communities that depend on them.
His campaign for Congress continues to gain momentum with strong support from leaders and organizations who recognize the importance of having a proven conservative voice fighting for the North State in Washington.
