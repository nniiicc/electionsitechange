Paul, MN 55075 A Life Rooted in Family, Work, and Service Todd Podgorski has spent his life serving his community, both in public office and on the front lines of public safety.
With more than two decades of experience in law enforcement, Todd brings a real-world understanding of what it takes to keep communities safe and strong.
His commitment to service began early and has remained consistent throughout his life.
Todd served on the South St.
Paul School Board from 2003 to 2006, working to support students, families, and educators.
He later continued that service on the South St.
Paul City Council, where he has represented his community across multiple terms since 2011.
Todd’s approach to leadership is grounded in experience, accountability, and a deep understanding of the challenges families face.
Whether it’s public safety, education, or rising costs, he believes government should focus on practical solutions that make a real difference in people’s lives.
Todd is running for State Senate to bring that same commitment to service, experience, and common-sense leadership to St.
Paul.
He believes Minnesota deserves leaders who understand real life, listen to their communities, and are willing to do the work to get things done.
