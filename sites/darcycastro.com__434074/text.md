Meet Darcy
A community servant and leader at heart, Darcy brings a powerful professional background in public relations, journalism and nonprofit marketing to her campaign for Senate District 37.
She is deeply committed to creating community, building meaningful relationships, meeting the needs of others and leaving things better than she found them.
Above all, Darcy believes in collaboration for the common good.
A proud West Cobb County resident, Darcy knows District 37 — its families, its challenges, and its extraordinary potential.
She is an Elder at Kirkwood Presbyterian Church and a devoted advocate for the communities she calls home.
She represented our state as Mrs.
Georgia and was a tireless advocate alongside the International Rescue Committee for Afghan refugee families resettling in Georgia.
As a proud Air Force wife (retired!) and mother of four children who have attended Cobb County schools and KSU, she understands firsthand the issues that matter most to local families.
