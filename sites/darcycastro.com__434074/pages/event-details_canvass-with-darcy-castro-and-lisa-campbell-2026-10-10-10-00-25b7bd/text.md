Join Darcy Castro and Georgia House District 35 Representative Lisa Campbell for a canvass in Kennesaw!
We’ll meet voters, listen to the issues that matter to them, and talk about what’s at stake in this election.
These one-on-one conversations are how we build the support and momentum to flip SD 37 in November.
Whether you’ve knocked a hundred doors or this is your first time, we’ll make sure you feel prepared and supported.
We’ll begin with a quick training, pair you with another volunteer if needed, and provide everything you need to feel confident at the doors.
Every conversation makes a difference.
Come canvass with Darcy and volunteers from across the community as we work to elect Darcy Castro this November.
