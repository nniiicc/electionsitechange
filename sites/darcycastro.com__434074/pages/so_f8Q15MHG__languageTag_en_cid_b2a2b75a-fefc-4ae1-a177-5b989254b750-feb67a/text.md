Suicide Prevention I had the honor of attend the "You Are Loved" public art installation for Suicide Awareness Month, hosted by Cobb Parks at Lenore Price Park.

Most of us have been touched by this kind of grief.
Visit AFSP Georgia for meaningful ways to both give help and get help.
