Priorities
Affordability for All
- Address rising costs of living
- Stop passing the costs of data centers onto families through higher utility bills
- Keep private equity out of our housing market so Georgians can afford to own a home
Public Education
- Protect the HOPE scholarship so every Georgia student has a path to an affordable college education
- Keep schools safe
- Give kids the future they were promised by investing in their futures
Healthcare
- Keep the government out of private decisions between patients and their doctors
- Fight rising premiums and healthcare costs
- Ensure no one falls through the cracks by closing gaps in coverage
Voting Rights
- Protect our right to vote
- Prevent mid-decade redistricting and unfair manipulation of electoral maps
- Increase civic engagement, making sure every Georgian feels empowered to participate in their democracy
