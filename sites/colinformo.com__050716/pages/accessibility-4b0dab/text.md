Accessibility
This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.
In practice that means:
- Text and background colors meet AA contrast ratios.
- Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator.
- Headings are structured in order so screen readers can navigate the page.
- Images carry descriptive alternative text.
- Touch targets are at least 44×44 pixels.
- Animation is reduced automatically if your device requests it.
- Text can be resized to 200% without breaking the layout.
If any of this is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it.
Email info@colinformo.com and tell us the page and what went wrong.
We aim to respond within three business days.
