Privacy
What we collect
If you sign up for updates or volunteer, we collect what you give us: your name, email address, ZIP code, and optionally your phone number and any message you send.
We also collect standard, anonymized web analytics — pages viewed, general location, and how you arrived at the site.
What we do with it
We use it to contact you about the campaign, to organize volunteers, and to understand which parts of the site are useful.
That is all.
What we don’t do
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We do not share it with other campaigns or commercial third parties, except for the service providers who run our email and forms on our behalf and are bound to protect it.
Donations
Contributions are processed by Anedot, not by this website; we never see or store your card details.
Missouri law requires candidate committees to report certain contributor information to the Missouri Ethics Commission, and those reports are public records.
Your choices
Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.
You can also email info@colinformo.com at any time to be removed from our lists or to ask what information we hold about you.
Children
This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
Changes
If this policy changes, we’ll post the updated version here.
