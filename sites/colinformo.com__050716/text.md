Meet Colin A St.
Charles dad, and your state rep.
Colin and his wife are raising their two girls here.
He has represented District 105 in the Missouri House since 2025.
Before that, he spent his career bringing mayors, businesses, and communities together to solve shared problems along the Mississippi — work that comes down to keeping water where it belongs and towns like ours thriving.
First term What Colin got done 01 Cleaner air for St.
Charles Restored Missouri's Air Pollution Control Program.
Our river valley is hit hardest by what settles in it.
02 Standing up to the data centers Developed and led the Missouri House's data center bill to protect grid capacity and water — without passing the costs to Missourians.
03 Flood protection through parks Passed a bill in the House to store floodwater naturally by expanding and connecting our parks.
04 Disaster-ready neighborhoods Expanded the Neighborhood Improvement District program so neighborhoods can fund disaster resilience and mitigation work.
05 A long-term footing for the state Advanced a constitutional amendment to create a Missouri Sovereign Wealth Fund for lasting fiscal sustainability.
Next session What Colin will do next 01 Bring down insurance costs Reform how insurance is regulated at the state level to slow the increases hitting households hardest.
02 Guardrails on AI infrastructure Protect grid capacity, water resources, and neighborhoods from hyperscale AI facilities.
03 Keep coal ash out of the floodplain New authority for the Department of Natural Resources to prohibit coal ash dumping and contamination.
04 Fully fund what families count on First responders, disability services, youth day-hab programs, schools, women's health programs, and childcare.
05 Cut the cost of dealing with the state Revise Department of Revenue procedures that drive up costs for Missourians.
