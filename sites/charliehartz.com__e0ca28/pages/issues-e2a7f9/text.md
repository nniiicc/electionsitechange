KEY ISSUES
Charlie will work tirelessly with state leadership to address our most pressing issues:
Fix Crumbling Roads & Bridges
Ensure Aiken County’s fair share of state funding to fix and upgrade our infrastructure is returned home.
Back The Blue
Fully fund law enforcement and first responders, ensuring deadly drugs like fentanyl are off our streets and our schools and families are safe.
Safeguard Conservative Family Values
Always defend your personal freedoms, especially the right to life and the right to bear arms.
Say No To Wasteful Spending
Cut the state income taxes and make sure the state uses your tax dollars wisely and effectively.
Fight For Our Classrooms
Stand for school choice, increased teacher pay, parental rights, and all measures to make our schools safe (such as SROs in every school) and free from CRT and woke ideologies.
