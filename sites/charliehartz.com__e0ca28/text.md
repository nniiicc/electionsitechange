Charlie Hartz Means Business.
Charlie Hartz is widely known in the Aiken community.
Hartz started four successful Dunkin Donuts franchises in Aiken and North Augusta, with over sixty employees and generating $4.5 million in revenue for the local economy.
Elected in November 2024, he is now serving his first term in the South Carolina State House.
Hartz recently sold the franchises to focus on his latest venture, ScentHound Aiken, a dog grooming service.
