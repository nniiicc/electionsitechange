Policy Priorities
Committed to fiscal responsibility, public safety, and the long-term prosperity of our district.
Workers Advocacy
- Improving workers comp laws to actually help workers.
- increase or remove cap for workers compensation claims.
- support reversing right to work.
- invest in more infrastructure by using federal grants.
- modernizing roads ensure safe and efficient travel
Housing affordability
- Address housing affordability by limiting large-scale corporate ownership of single-family homes,
- discouraging bulk purchases by institutional investors, and looking at policies that help return more homes to individual Nevada buyers.
Costs & Fiscal Health
- Ensuring Nevada's budget is managed with transparency and accountability.
- Protecting essential public services while maintaining a balanced state budget.
- Advocating for responsible tax policies that support Nevada's working families.
Education & Healthcare
Education & Workforce
Education should prepare Nevadans for a real future, whether that means college, the skilled trades, military service, or going directly into the workforce.
I support expanding career and technical education, apprenticeships, community college partnerships, and industry-recognized training that connect people to good-paying careers.
Nevada should bring educators, employers, unions, and workers themselves to the table so our training programs reflect the jobs our communities actually need.
I will support strong wages, safe workplaces, fair treatment, and opportunities for advancement for both union and unrepresented workers.
A four-year degree is one path to success, not the only one.
Every Nevadan should have a fair shot at learning a skill, earning a good living, and building a secure future here at home.
Healthcare Affordability
Healthcare Affordability
Nevadans should be able to see a doctor, fill a prescription, and get needed care without putting their family finances at risk.
I support protecting and responsibly expanding Medicaid where it improves access, especially for working families, children, seniors, and rural communities, while making sure taxpayer dollars are spent carefully and programs are measured for results.
We should also focus on lowering prescription drug costs, increasing price transparency, strengthening competition among providers, and expanding access to primary and preventive care.
The goal is not simply to spend more on healthcare, but to spend smarter so Nevada gets better outcomes, lower costs, and a system people can actually afford.
