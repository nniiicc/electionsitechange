Skip to content Amy Lavallee For State Representative • 1st Franklin District Home Contact 💙 Venmo ✉ By Check About Amy Lavallee Meet Amy From Our Hills to Beacon Hill I’m a proud Franklin County resident, a mom of three, and a lifelong believer in the strength of local communities.
My work—both professionally and personally—has always been rooted in service, transparency, and a commitment to doing what’s right for the people around me.
Today, I serve as the Whately Town Clerk, where I’m responsible for maintaining accurate public records, supporting fair and accessible elections, and ensuring that local government remains transparent and accountable to the people it serves.
I also sit on the Franklin County Technical School Committee, where I advocate for strong vocational and technical education programs that prepare students for meaningful, real-world careers.
I believe deeply that not every path looks the same, and that hands-on, career-focused education is just as important as traditional academic routes.
At home, I’m a mom raising three children in our public school system.
That experience has shaped so much of how I see the world.
I’ve witnessed firsthand the impact that dedicated teachers, strong schools, and supportive programs can have on a child’s life.
It’s also shown me where we need to do better—where families need more support, where resources matter, and where thoughtful decisions can make a real difference.
“My educational background reflects my passion for helping others.” My educational background reflects my passion for helping others.
I hold a degree in Early Childhood Education, a degree as a Medical Assistant, and have also pursued nursing studies.
These experiences have given me a unique perspective on both education and healthcare—two systems that are deeply connected and essential to strong, healthy communities.
I was raised in a military family as an Air Force brat, with a broader family history of service.
Growing up that way meant learning adaptability, resilience, and the importance of stepping up when it matters.
Those values have stayed with me and continue to guide my approach to both leadership and community involvement.
I’m also proud of my Puerto Rican heritage, which is an important part of who I am and how I connect with others.
Giving back is something I believe in strongly, and I stay involved in charitable work throughout our community whenever I can.
Outside of my work, you’ll often find me outdoors.
I enjoy reading, hiking, fishing, and hunting—activities that keep me connected to the land and to the traditions that are such a meaningful part of life in our region.
I’m a strong supporter of conservation and believe in protecting our natural resources so future generations can enjoy them the same way we do.
I approach my work from a non-partisan perspective.
To me, it’s not about politics—it’s about people.
It’s about making practical, thoughtful decisions that support families, respect taxpayers, and strengthen the communities we call home.
Whether I’m working in town hall, supporting our schools, or raising my family, my focus is always the same: building a stronger, more connected community where everyone has the opportunity to succeed.
