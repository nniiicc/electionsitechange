Our voices deserve strong representation on Beacon Hill.
I will advocate for fair resources for our towns, support for working families, protection of our farms and environment, and policies that strengthen — not overlook — Western Massachusetts.
My Priorities
What I’ll Fight For
Supporting Local Farmers
Farming is the backbone of our community.
I want to make sure our farmers have all the resources they need to continue and thrive for generations to come.
Infrastructure
Our area has been overlooked when it comes to state improvements — aging roads, bridges in disrepair, and gaps in power and communications access.
That changes now.
Education
A good education is fundamental for our children and our future.
I’ll fight for appropriate rural school funding, including support for technical and vocational schools.

About Amy
Rooted in Franklin County
I currently serve as the Whately Town Clerk and on the Franklin County Technical School Committee.
My work in local government has given me a front-row view of how state decisions affect our towns every single day.
I am a wife, mother of three grown boys, homesteader, hiker, hunter, and proud conservationist.
I understand the values of this district because I live them.
I am running because effective government starts with listening — and I will always listen to you.
