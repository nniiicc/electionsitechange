Minnesotans are concerned about the issues we’re facing, and rightfully so.
The residents of District 20A deserve a State Representative who works in facts and truth.
You deserve someone who understands the role of government, its direct impact on taxpayers, and how it can support you and our communities.
That’s why I'm running.
These are the core issue areas I will focus on in St.
Paul.
Strengthen Our Communities We are strongest when we have affordable housing , good jobs , and well rounded communities with good public schools, strong infrastructure, and access to affordable healthcare and childcare.
Every aspect of our communities is dependent on our mutual success.
I will work with local governments, school districts, and community organizations to secure the support and resources needed to succeed.
I will defend your livable wages while protecting our small businesses because our rural communities and local economies depend on it.
Protection from Predators Whether it's predators like data centers and AI, corporate interests undermining affordable housing, theft of taxpayer money, or weak system easily taken advantage of - Minnesotans deserve strong protections.
I will work to strengthen laws protecting our communities, ensure powerful interests are held accountable, strengthen state and county programs against exploitation, support prosecution efforts, and where needed draft laws to hold predators accountable and close loopholes.
Make Every Dollar Count You work hard for you money, you deserve a legislature who respects that.
We know, our tax dollars provide necessary support to our local governments, our communities, and folks in need.
We also know there are many competing interests for how our tax dollars are spent, and how they are to be collected.
I will work tirelessly to ensure your tax dollars are maximized and spent in fiscally responsible ways.
I am opposed to any new taxes on the hard working middle and low income families and our small businesses.
Defend our Democracy, Constitution, and Civil Rights.
Our Democracy, our rights and freedoms are essential to our nation’s success and must be protected.
Like many of you, I am deeply concerned by ongoing attacks on our nation’s core democratic principles.
Our Constitutional and Civil Rights are non-negotiable.
To protect our Democracy I will support and introduce legislation which protects Minnesotans from federal overreach, safeguards civil rights, and ensures our state remains a place of freedom, fairness, and equal protection under the law.
Protect the Rights of All Government does not belong in middle of your private life and your personal decisions.
As Americans, we have the right to be who we are and love who we love.
It is not open to any level of government to decide that for you.
The healthcare decisions for you and your family are yours to make.
Your personal decisions should be based on consultation with your doctors those you trust.
