Ontario J.
Tillman is a proud native of Bessemer, Alabama, where he was born and raised.
A devoted family man, he is the father of three children and has been happily married to his wife, Yvette, for over ten years.
As a graduate of the Jess Lanier High School Class of 1996, Ontario has dedicated his life to service, leadership, and advocacy for his community.
Ontario’s career began in education, where he has served in the Bessemer City School District since 2008.
He taught at Westhills Elementary School and Bessemer City High School.
In addition to his teaching duties, he served as the Head Boys Varsity Basketball Coach at Bessemer City High School.
Under his leadership, the team earned several accomplishments:
- 2014 – Class 6A Final Four Appearance
- 2015 – Regional Appearance
- 2016 – Regional Final Appearance
In 2012, Ontario expanded his commitment to service by entering the field of law, focusing on criminal defense, juvenile law, and personal injury.
He is:
- Licensed to Practice in the State of Alabama
- Admitted to the U.S.
District Courts for:
- Northern District of Alabama
- Middle District of Alabama
- Southern District of Alabama
He is currently a Partner at Maxwell Tillman Law Firm, located in downtown Birmingham, Alabama.
Alabama A&M University
- Bachelor of Science in Special Education
- Master of Education in Collaborative Teaching
Miles Law School
- Juris Doctor (J.D.)
Throughout his career, Ontario has kept the community at the center of his work.
Whether in the classroom, the courtroom, or on the court, he remains committed to empowering others and creating opportunities.
He understands the unique challenges and strengths of the people of District 56 and continues to advocate for their needs with integrity and determination.
