At the Gold Dome
Bills Introduced
HB 1480: Georgia Medicare for All Act, a bill to make all forms of healthcare affordable and accessible for all Georgians.
HB 343: Georgia Living Wage Act, A bill to make the minimum wage $20/hour.
It also removes any exclusions like prison labor and those with disabilities.
HR 1681: A resolution that calls on Congress to convene a convention to amend the Constitution and end money in politics.
HB 679: End Rental Price Fixing Act, A bill to stop AI and algorithmic price fixing of rental properties.
HB 864: A bill to end the ownership of homes by major corporations.
HB 889: A bill to require ICE and CBP to be in uniform with publicly marked vehicles and visible badges when conducting business.
Bills Passed
HB 1587: A bill to abolish the office of elected county surveyor of Cobb County
HR 2057: A resolution recognizing March 31, 2026, as Underserved Communities Day
