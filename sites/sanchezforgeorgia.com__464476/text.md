Meet Gabriel
I was raised in Cobb County by an immigrant family who taught me to fight for myself and for my community.
It’s our responsibility to look out for each other.
I believe in what we can accomplish together, and I am honored to serve our community as your Representative in House District 42.
As the first Democratic Socialist member elected to the Georgia General Assembly, I see it as an honor to take the fight for working families in my district to the state capitol every single day.
