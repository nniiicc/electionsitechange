2020 Candidate Interview: Kelly Breen for State Rep 38th District
(Crystal A.
Proxmire, June 23, 2020)
Novi, Northville, South Lyon, Lyon Township, and Walled Lake, MI –In Michigan’s 38th District State Representative race, there is contention in both the Republican and the Democratic Primaries which are held Aug. 4, 2020.
The Democratic race is between Kelly Breen and Megan McAllister.
On the Republican side there are three candidates: Sreenivas Cherukuri, Krista Spencer, and Chase Turner.
The winner of each primary will appear on the Nov. 3, 2020 ballot.
State Representative is a two-year position.
