LOWER DRUG COSTS People are rationing insulin, and that’s not okay.
As your legislator, I will work to: Cap the cost of insulin, inhalers, and other drugs people can't live without, and take on Big Pharma to do it.
Stop companies from price-gouging seniors and vulnerable adults on prescription medications.
Care Close to Home Where you live shouldn't decide whether you can see a doctor.
As your legislator, I will work to: Push back on Republican efforts to cut Medicaid and Medicare.
Keep rural and local hospitals open — not shuttered because they're not "profitable enough." Fix the shortage of doctors and nurses that's leaving whole communities in Michigan without care.
Healthier Moms & Babies Michigan has a maternal health crisis, and it's costing lives.
As your legislator, I will work to: Grow programs like Rx Kids that put real money in the pockets of new and expecting parents for diapers, formula, and the basics.
Close the gap in birth outcomes for Black and Brown families and fix what's driving it.
