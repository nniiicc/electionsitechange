A MORE AFFORDABLE MICHIGAN
Working families deserve to keep more of what they earn.
That starts with lowering costs.
HELPING FAMILIES THRIVE
No family should be stretched to the breaking point just to raise their kids.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Bring down the cost of childcare.
- Guarantee paid family leave.
- Expand tax credits so moms and dads keep more cash in their pockets.
MAKING HOMEOWNERSHIP POSSIBLE
Owning a home shouldn't feel out of reach.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Build more homes and invest in affordable housing to bring rents down statewide.
- Stop billionaires and big investors from buying up houses so Michiganders can actually compete for a place to live.
MAKING ENDS MEET
Everyday costs keep climbing, and I’m doing something about it.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Keep utility bills — energy, water, the essentials — from spiraling out of control.
- Ban surge pricing and the junk fees that are designed to rip you off.
- Crack down on predatory practices and hold corporations accountable when they take advantage of Michiganders.
