A TIGHTER-KNIT MICHIGAN
People want to feel safe where they live and see a real path forward in life.
That's what I’m helping to build toward, with investments in growth that actually improve people's lives and not just the balance sheet.
Building Michigan the Right Way
When a big project lands in your town, you should have a say in it.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Secure union labor and real quality on every large-scale build.
- Grant a real seat at the table for local input — not a rubber stamp.
- Implement protections for the environment we all live in.
Keeping Our Neighborhoods Safe
You can't have a thriving community without a safe one.
To actually prevent violence and not just react to it, as your legislator, I will work to:
- Put real money behind community violence intervention programs.
- Make mental health and substance use treatment easier to access.
- Back responsible gun ownership.
- Pay first responders what they're worth and invest in better training.
Good Jobs and Smart Growth
Michigan's economy runs on good-paying jobs, small businesses, and innovation that actually works for people.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Stand up for union jobs and workers' rights, which means living wages and safe workplaces, no exceptions.
- Grow clean energy jobs that put people to work across the state.
- Have small businesses' backs, because they're what keep Michigan's economy running.
- Set real guardrails on AI so it protects jobs, privacy, and data instead of running over them.
