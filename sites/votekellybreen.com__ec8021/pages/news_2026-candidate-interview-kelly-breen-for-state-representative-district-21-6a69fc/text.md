(Crystal A.
Proxmire, July 12, 2026)
Farmington, Farmington Hills, Lyon Township, Northville, and Novi, MI – In the race for State Representative in the 21st District there is no competition in either the Republican or Democratic Primary on Aug. 4.
Candidates will presumably advance to the Nov. 3 election.
Incumbent Kelly Breen is the Democratic candidate.
Ross Barranco is the Republican candidate.
The position has a two-year term
The 21st District includes Farmington and Novi, plus parts of Farmington Hills, Lyon Township, and Northville.
