A BETTER EDUCATED MICHIGAN
Our public schools hold our communities together.
I’ll keep fighting so every kid gets a great education and is given a real opportunity at what comes next.
Kids Who Are Fed and Safe Learn Better
It's simple: kids can't learn if they're hungry or scared.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Continue defending universal school meals from Republican cuts.
- Put real money into school safety, mental health support, and student well-being.
- Get reading and literacy scores back on track.
Every School Deserves Real Funding
Your kid's education shouldn't depend on their ZIP code.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Make sure every public school in Michigan has what it actually needs to help kids succeed.
Good Jobs Don't Always Start with a Four-Year Degree
Whether you end up in a lecture hall or a union hall, you deserve a real chance at a good career and a comfortable life.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Expand Career and Technical Education and apprenticeships so people can train for good-paying, in-demand jobs without drowning in debt.
- Grow programs like MI Reconnect so more Michiganders can get a degree without crushing debt hanging over them.
Teachers Who Stay Because They're Valued
Great schools start with great teachers and staff.
As your legislator, I will work to:
- Raise pay, protect healthcare, and strengthen retirement so we can both keep and recruit good teachers and school staff.
