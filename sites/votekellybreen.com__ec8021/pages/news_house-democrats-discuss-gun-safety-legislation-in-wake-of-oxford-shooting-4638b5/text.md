January 11, 2022
Democrats in Michigan’s state legislature have introduced a package of bills designed to strengthen gun safety in the state following November’s shooting at Oxford High School — but some fear that the measures will face an uphill battle in the Republican-controlled legislature.
The four bills — H.B. 5627 and 5628, and S.B. 785 and 786 — were introduced about two weeks after the Oxford shooting which left four students dead
