Oakland voters historically have given the major parties alternating turns at the state's top elected office.
Republican candidates carried Oakland in three of Michigan's last six gubernatorial contests (1998, 2010 and 2014); Democrats came out on top in the other three (2002, 2006 and 2018).
In each instance, the candidate who prevailed in Oakland also won statewide.
