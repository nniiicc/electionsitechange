The Data Sledgehammer
The Trump administration has swung what many are calling a “data sledgehammer” at the gates of American universities.
Under a new memorandum called Ensuring Transparency in Higher Education Admissions, the Department of Education is demanding five years of detailed, student‑level data from colleges.
This means every application, every test score, every demographic detail will be handed over to the federal government.
One can call it “transparency”.
What’s really happening is the weaponization of data to intimidate universities and control who gets a fair shot at higher education.
