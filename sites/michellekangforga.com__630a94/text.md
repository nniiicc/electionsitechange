Meet Michelle
I’m Michelle Kang, a proud first-generation Korean American immigrant who’s called Georgia home since 1992.
I came here to build a better life for myself and my future family, and over the past 30 years, I’ve grown deeply rooted in this community.
I’m running for State House because I believe Georgia should be a place where working families can thrive, not just survive.
I’m running to represent all of us; every neighbor, every story, every voice, because Georgia’s future should belong to everyone who calls it home, no matter their income, background, or the language spoken at home.
