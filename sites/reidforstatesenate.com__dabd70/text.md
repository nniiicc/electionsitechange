Meet Carolann Reid
"I am running because I believe this moment calls for leadership that brings people together, not divides them further.
In a time when politics often feels divisive and disconnected from everyday life, I focus on what improves people’s lives: good jobs, strong schools, and a community where everyone has a fair shot.
After more than twenty years working alongside families, educators, and local businesses, I know that real progress happens when we listen to one another, work across differences, and prioritize solutions over slogans.
I am running to restore trust in local leadership and to ensure that Monroe, Seymour, Shelton, and Stratford move forward together."
