I am Tennessee Born, Tennessee Raised, and Tennessee Proud
That is why I serve
I believe the core values of Faith, Family, and Individual Liberty are more than slogans to print on a t-shirt.
They are the foundational cornerstones upon which this nation was built upon.
I believe our Faith in a divine Creator and a divine purpose has been at the epicenter of every great achievement this country has ever accomplished...including winning the fight for our independence from the British monarchy.
I believe the Family unit is the most sacred institution in modern society.
It should be defended, protected, promoted, and revered by our citizenry and our government.
I believe that the Individual Liberties protected by our Constitution and the rule of law are the only things that separate us from the other 194 recognized countries in the world.
The Constitution is our only defense from tyranny, and we have a duty to protect it from all attacks regardless of the source.
I believe Tennessee is one of the few beacons of hope remaining in America that upholds these values.
I have volunteered to serve my community, my district, and my state because I am committed to protecting the future of Tennessee from those that would seek to destroy the way of life that we enjoy and the values that we represent.
Jody Barrett - A Proven Track Record
Jody Barrett is a small business owner, community leader, a husband, and father who serves as State Representative for Tennessee’s 69th House District.
Known for his unique brand of aggressive, courageous, and outspoken conservative leadership, Jody is taking on the tough fights in Nashville and delivering concrete results for local families.
He has quickly established a reputation for standing his ground for what he believes to be right, regardless of the consequences.
When radical leftists stormed the Tennessee Capitol, Jody didn’t blink.
He stood up, voting to expel the instigators, and aggressively took the fight to the lying corporate media.
He has been relentless in his public criticism of the progressive socialists who have taken over the Democratic Party in Tennessee, and continues to challenge their extreme and dangerous ideology.
Jody has advanced legislation to protect our Second Amendment rights, support law enforcement, end illegal immigration, and rid our state of the filth of obscenity and child pornography.
He also works hard to lower taxes and support local community businesses, which will allow us to create good, stable careers that will provide all Tennesseans with opportunities to provide a better life for themselves and their families.
As a family-focused business and community leader, Jody Barrett embodies Tennessee values.
With a proven track record of service and a deep understanding of our state's challenges and opportunities, Jody is committed to protecting our values, promoting economic growth, and ensuring a bright future for all Tennesseans.
Jody and his wife Holly live on a farm in Dickson County.
They have three children and are active members of First Baptist Church of Dickson.
