My Bills SB24-161 Parks and Wildlife Licenses and Passes This bill amends certain requirements and procedures related to licenses and passes from the Colorado Parks and Wildlife Commission.
The bill lowers the age threshold for senior annual fishing licenses to 64 years of age and amends the definition of "low-income senior".
Status: Introduced and assigned to the Senate Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee.
SB24-121 Licensure of Critical Access Hospitals Senators Perry Will and Rod Pelton at the Rocky Mountain Farmers Union Luncheon.
Rural hospitals must be licensed as general hospitals under state law and under federal law are designated as critical access hospitals.
This bill allows the department of public health to license critical access hospitals separate from general hospitals.
