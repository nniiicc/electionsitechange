2023 Session Newsletter May 5th SENATOR ROD PELTON Today marks the 117th day of the 2023 Legislative Session.
We are down to the last three days of session, and as of yesterday, we still have about 167 bills on the calendar awaiting action in either a committee and/or on the floor.
Many of these bills are being held up on the House side, and I can honestly say that I do not miss the antics that happen over there.
The best way to sum up the two chambers is that the House is like a cage match, and Senate is like a chess game.
Bills are still being introduced during this last week of Session I do not understand why we are still introducing new bills, bills that are over 50 pages in length, during this last week of session.
If these bills are really of such high importance, why wouldn’t they have been introduced earlier so that way we can fully address them, get stakeholders involved, and have a more transparent process.
One of these bills was SB23303, which was just introduced this past Monday and has been forced full speed through the process – having already passed through the Senate, and it will be voted on by the House soon.
Summary on Bills I’ve Sponsored Here at the Colorado State Capitol, the prime sponsor in the first chamber (known as the chamber of origin) is responsible for asking a legislator in the second (opposite) chamber to carry the bill in that chamber.
