Eight claims from Adam Hamilton, and what the record actually shows.
01 Healthcare Medical debt Lie Adam Hamilton claims Senator Marshall charged exorbitant interest rates, sued patients and had them arrested over medical debt.
Fact The Kansas Bill of Rights states, “No person shall be imprisoned for debt,” proving this to be a blatant lie.
For 25 years in Great Bend, Dr.
Marshall treated every patient regardless of ability to pay.
In fact, 1 out of 5 patients at the hospital received free care; in the final 4 years of Dr.
Marshall’s medical career, their hospital provided over $15 million in uncompensated care, routinely writing off unpaid balances for families who couldn't pay.
It is ridiculous to suggest that Dr.
Marshall ever arrested patients. $15M uncompensated care provided by his hospital in the final four years of his medical career 02 Healthcare The $50 payment Lie Adam Hamilton claims a pregnant patient was arrested for missing a single $50 payment.
Fact This claim leaves out a very important fact: Kansans cannot be arrested for medical debt.
Court records show the woman in the ad had failed to appear in court 16 times in the previous 3 years, and a judge issued a warrant — not for missing payments.
16 missed court dates in 3 years, per court records.
A judge issued a warrant for that, not for missing payments.
03 Kansas roots Kansas is home Lie Adam Hamilton claims Senator Marshall lives in Florida, not Kansas.
Fact Roger Marshall is a fifth-generation Kansan and farm kid who was born in El Dorado and spent 25 years serving as a physician in rural Great Bend.
He is registered to vote at his home in Stafford County, land the Marshall family has held for decades.
Kansas is home and always has been.

08 Consumers Credit card rewards Lie Adam Hamilton claims Senator Marshall's credit card bill will take away your rewards points and miles.
Fact The Credit Card Competition Act will not remove rewards points or miles.
