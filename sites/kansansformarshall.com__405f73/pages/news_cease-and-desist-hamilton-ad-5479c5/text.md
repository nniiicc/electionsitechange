Hamilton knew her case details but instead chose to peddle a lie to Kansans to support his own interests.
“Democrat Adam Hamilton is relying on lies for his bid to get Schumer’s support for his race,” the Marshall campaign stated.
“The truth is, Kansans are protected from imprisonment over medical debt, which means this ad is a total falsehood.
