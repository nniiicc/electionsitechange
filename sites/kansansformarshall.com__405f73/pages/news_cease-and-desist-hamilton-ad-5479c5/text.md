WICHITA – Fact-checkers have exposed Democrat Adam Hamilton’s new campaign ad as a deliberate and outright lie, prompting the National Republican Senatorial Committee and the Marshall Campaign to issue cease and desist letters.
The Washington Reporter completely dismantled the false statements in Hamilton’s recent attack ad, exposing how Kansas legal records completely shatter Democrat Adam Hamilton’s narrative, showing that he lied about the patient’s debts and previous court records—like the fact that she had failed to appear in court 16 times in the previous 3 years.
Hamilton knew her case details but instead chose to peddle a lie to Kansans to support his own interests.
“Democrat Adam Hamilton is relying on lies for his bid to get Schumer’s support for his race,” the Marshall campaign stated.
“The truth is, Kansans are protected from imprisonment over medical debt, which means this ad is a total falsehood.
Adam Hamilton is a liar.”
