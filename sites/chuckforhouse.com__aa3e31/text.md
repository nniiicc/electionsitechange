About Chuck
A former assistant district attorney and chairman of his county Republican Party, Chuck Efstration made a career of putting dangerous criminals behind bars and helping elect conservatives.
Chuck was elected to the State House in 2013 and was elected Majority Leader by his Republican colleagues in November of 2022.

On the Issues
Chuck has been championing conservative policies for more than a decade.
As Majority Leader, he helped lead the fight to return billions of dollars in rebates to taxpayers, give our teachers a pay raise, increase safety funding for our public schools, defend victims of sex trafficking, and crack down on violent crime.
