ON THE ISSUES Affordability & Cost of Living Make housing more attainable for first‑time homebuyers, seniors looking to downsize, and working families who are being priced out of the communities they love.
Push for fairer, more predictable property tax and state aid policies so towns are not forced to choose between gutting services and overburdening taxpayers.
Support reforms that lower health care costs and protect access to quality care for South Coast residents.
Strong Public Schools Update state education and school funding formulas so communities like Somerset, Dighton, Swansea, and Taunton receive their fair share from Beacon Hill.
Use Jamison’s experience as a former School Committee member to fight for classroom resources, mental health supports, and safe, modern school facilities.
Advocate for career, technical, and early college pathways that connect students directly to good local jobs and regional industries.
Local Aid & Municipal Support Reform state funding formulas and unfunded mandates that shift costs onto municipalities without giving them the tools or flexibility to manage them.
Partner with local officials to secure infrastructure investments in roads, bridges, water and sewer systems, and public safety.
Ensure that the 5th Bristol’s town halls and city halls have a strong voice at the State House when decisions are made about local aid.
Economic Development & Small Business Champion smart, strategic economic development that grows jobs and the tax base without constantly reaching deeper into residents’ pockets.
Support small businesses with policies that simplify permitting, cut red tape, and encourage responsible development along the South Coast.
Work with regional partners to attract new industries while protecting the character and quality of life in local neighborhoods.
Fair Taxation & Corporate Responsibility Advocate for a tax system that asks large corporations to pay their fair share while protecting middle‑class families and seniors on fixed incomes.
Ensure that state economic incentives deliver real, measurable benefits—local jobs, local investment, and long‑term community gain, not just corporate profit.
Push for transparency and accountability whenever public dollars are used to support private development.
Social Services & Community Support Support social services as a hand up, not a hand out—focused on helping people get back on their feet and stay there.
Invest in mental health, addiction treatment, and family support services that address root causes and strengthen community safety.
Partner with nonprofits, faith communities, and local organizations that are on the front lines serving vulnerable residents.
A Unified South Coast Delegation Work to build a more unified South Coast legislative delegation that can advocate together for regional priorities on Beacon Hill.
Coordinate around shared needs such as transportation, economic development, environmental resilience, and education.
