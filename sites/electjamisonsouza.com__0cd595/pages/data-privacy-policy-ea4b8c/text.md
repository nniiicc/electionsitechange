Privacy & Data Policy
Information We Collect
We collect information that you provide directly to us—such as when you complete a volunteer form, make a donation, sign up for updates, or send us a message.
This may include your name, email, phone number, mailing address, and any other details you choose to share.
We also collect limited technical data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, and how you interact with our pages.
This data may be gathered through cookies or analytics tools.
How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to:
- Send campaign news, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities.
- Respond to questions or requests.
- Improve our website, outreach, and communications.
- Comply with election and contribution reporting requirements.
We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share it only with trusted vendors—such as email distribution or text‑messaging platforms—that assist campaign operations under strict confidentiality requirements.
Cookies and Tracking
Our Site uses cookies and similar technologies to understand visitor activity and improve the user experience.
You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
Disabling cookies may limit some features of the Site.
Data Security and Your Choices
We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.
You may:
- Opt out of campaign emails or texts using the unsubscribe links or by contacting us directly.
- Request that we update or delete your personal information where permitted by law.
We also do not consent to any automated scraping, data mining, or AI model training using content or data obtained from this Site.
Updates and Contact
This policy may be updated from time to time, with the latest version posted on this page.
For privacy questions or data requests, please contact the campaign through the methods listed on the Site.
