ABOUT THE CANDIDATE
I am Chief of Staff at a local civil engineering firm and a former Catoosa County elections worker.
For over a decade, I've helped teams take big ideas and turn them into real, finished projects: keeping budgets honest, timelines realistic, and decisions grounded in what's actually true, not what's convenient.
And as a poll worker, I've seen up close what it looks like when neighbors with different politics show up, do the work, and take care of each other anyway.
That's the spirit I want to bring to Atlanta.
District 3 doesn't need someone who votes the party line and avoids hard questions.
It needs a representative with real experience who will show up, put Catoosa County first, and answer to the people they serve.
