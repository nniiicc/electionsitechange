Education
Our kids are the future of Minnesota.
Dave believes that investing in our schools is essential, though financial resources alone cannot resolve all educational challenges.
Dave's Education Priorities
Address Achievement Gaps
Target solutions for the widening disparity between high-performing and struggling students.
Smart Resource Allocation
Intelligent deployment of existing funds to maximize effectiveness rather than simply increasing spending.
Support Our Teachers
Provide educators with necessary training, professional development, and improved compensation for early-career teachers.
Reduce Mandates
Cut back on school mandates to allow greater flexibility in how institutions operate.
Teacher Pipeline
Create incentive programs to encourage college students to pursue teaching careers.
School Choice
Committed to funding public, private, and charter schools equitably throughout Minnesota.
