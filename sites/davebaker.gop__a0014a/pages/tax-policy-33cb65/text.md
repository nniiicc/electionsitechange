Policy Position
Tax Policy
Minnesota is collecting far too many dollars from hardworking families.
Dave believes surplus funds should be returned to residents through tax cuts, not spent on new government programs.
Dave's Tax Policy Priorities
Eliminate Social Security Tax
Minnesota is among only 12 states still taxing Social Security benefits.
Dave will fight to eliminate this tax on social security income for our senior citizens.
Lower Tax Rates
A primary objective is lowering tax rates across all income levels so families can keep more of what they earn.
Return the Surplus
When we have billions in surplus, the state should return money to taxpayers rather than growing government spending.
Bipartisan Solutions
Be smart, use our resources wisely, and work together to pass meaningful tax relief legislation.
