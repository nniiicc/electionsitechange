Government Overreach
Government is there to protect and educate, not determine which businesses can operate or force us into making personal decisions.
Dave is committed to preventing governmental intrusion into our daily lives.
Dave's Commitment to Freedom

I will fight to ensure government respects the rights and freedoms of every Minnesotan."

Protect Personal Freedom
Fight against government mandates that force personal medical decisions or restrict how families live their lives.
Support Small Business
Businesses should be able to operate without excessive government interference or arbitrary closures.
As a small business advocate, Dave will also fight government mandates that would prevent Minnesotans from earning a living as independent contractors if they so choose.
Defend Religious Liberty
Churches and faith communities should be free to gather and worship without government restrictions.
Limited Government
Government should be limited to its core functions and respect individual liberty and local decision-making.
