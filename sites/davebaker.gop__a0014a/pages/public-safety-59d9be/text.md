Public Safety
Dave is grateful for the men and women in uniform who keep us safe.
Strong community trust in law enforcement is essential, enabling effective response during crises when residents can rely on officers to perform their duties.
Dave's Public Safety Priorities
Support Law Enforcement
Local law enforcement agencies excel at both protecting communities and building citizen trust.
Dave is committed to giving them the resources they need to keep our communities safe.
Rebuild the Metro Area
Residents should be able to enjoy a nice dinner or event in the Minneapolis-St.
Paul area without fear of being harmed.
Dave is committed to rebuilding safety in our urban centers.
State-Level Action
Dave will work with colleagues in the Minnesota legislature to hire additional police officers, improve officer training programs, and provide necessary resources for law enforcement agencies.
