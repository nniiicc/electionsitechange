A Leader Who Gets Things Done
Dave is a businessman, community leader, husband, and father.
He owns a number of businesses in the Kandiyohi County area and has led numerous community initiatives that have made a real difference in people's lives.
First elected to the legislature in 2014, Dave is now serving his fifth term.
He has established himself as an effective legislator with a proven record of delivering results for Kandiyohi County.
Dave has been an outspoken leader on the issue of opioid abuse, chief authoring major reforms to Minnesota's prescription monitoring program and label requirements for opioid prescriptions.
He is committed to continuing the fight for legislation that will reduce addiction and prevent opioid overdoses in Minnesota.
