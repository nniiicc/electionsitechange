Energy
The cost of energy here in Minnesota is rising at a pace faster than many other states, largely due to additional mandates and rules.
Dave is committed to policies that prioritize both affordability and reliability for Minnesota residents.
Dave's Energy Priorities
Affordable Energy First
Families and businesses need affordable, reliable energy.
Dave will fight against mandates that drive up costs for hardworking Minnesotans.
Oppose Costly Mandates
Push back against requirements that force utilities to purchase expensive energy sources, passing costs on to consumers.
Reject California Standards
Minnesota should set its own energy standards, not adopt policies designed for other states that don't fit our needs.
Reliable Power Grid
Ensure our energy infrastructure can handle Minnesota's demanding climate without forcing investment in unproven technologies.
