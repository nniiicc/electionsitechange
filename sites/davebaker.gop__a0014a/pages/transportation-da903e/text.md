Transportation
Dave advocates for dedicating more of our sales tax income to improving and maintaining our roads and bridges in rural Minnesota.
We have no need for new taxes at a time when we're already collecting more than we should.
Dave's Transportation Priorities
Rural Road Investment
Reallocate current sales tax dollars toward keeping the roads safe for all Minnesotans, with particular emphasis on rural areas.
Bridge Maintenance
Prioritize the maintenance and improvement of bridges throughout Minnesota to ensure safety and reliability.
No New Taxes
Utilize existing tax revenue rather than implementing new levies.
We already collect more than we should.
Fiscal Responsibility
Oppose costly transit projects with excessive cost overruns that burden taxpayers.
