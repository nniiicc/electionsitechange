Before taking office, I never would have guessed how much I would learn and the amount of work it takes to be an effective State Representative.
It is no doubt the toughest job I have ever had, but also one of the most rewarding.
My wife, Mary, still supports this life of mine, and my small hospitality company and staff work hard to cover my absence when I am out of town.
I am so grateful for their support.
I am running for re-election and am asking for your vote.
If you can donate to my campaign I would be grateful.
The prior legislative session showed the recklessness of Democrats' one-party control in St.
Paul.
They blew through a $# billion budget surplus, raised taxes by nearly $# billion, and still managed to plunge the state into a structural budget deficit by increasing spending by #%.
In addition to reckless spending, Democrats passed major policy bills largely to get the headlines with little regard to the real-world impacts these new policies will have on Minnesotans.
Changes in energy policy will result in higher electric bills and more pain at the gas pump.
Soft-on-crime policies will put more Minnesotans in harm's way.
Local property taxes will rise thanks to dozens of mandates placed on our public schools.
It is clearer than ever that we need balance in St.
Paul.
One-party control is causing significant damage to Minnesotans' pocketbooks, and reckless policies are making neighborhoods less safe.
I can't do this without your help.
Together, we can build our Republican bench and stop the runaway Democrat trifecta that only knows how to spend and regulate.
Job creators like me are finding it harder to do business in Minnesota with some of the largest employment mandates in the entire country.
We need balance!
My Work in the Legislature During my 10 years in office I have been a leader on issues like Workforce and Education .
As the ranking GOP member on the Workforce committee, I offered a reasonable alternative to the heavy-handed Earned Sick & Safe Time mandate: a private short-term insurance plan open to all businesses with Paid Family Medical Leave.
Rather than being forced on every employer, this plan would have been voluntary for businesses and would have encouraged them to offer employee benefits that fit their needs.
Government should not be writing our HR handbooks by continuing to issue damaging mandates.
I have also worked hard on mental health and substance use disorder issues .
I have dug in deep in my Human Service committee and worked to support nursing homes, streamline regulations, and support mental health reimbursements that help those fighting the disease of addiction.
Bringing Results Home My accomplishments while in office have included bringing important projects home like the final completion of the HWY 23 4-lane highway and the brand new Children and Adolescent Behavioral Health Services (CABHS) facility near the Willmar Civic Center that helps children all over the state through the most challenging mental health events kids can have.
The bottom line is the work never ends and the issues are endless.
Please share an email if you have thoughts on anything!
Thanks again for the privilege to be your voice in St.
Paul over the past 10 years.
I would appreciate your support with a donation and your vote.

She is the right leader for this time in MN when we need to build back trust!" Dave's 3-minute nomination speech Policy Priorities Where I Stand 01 Education Minnesota must invest smartly in schools while addressing the achievement gap.
Support teachers with better training and salaries, reduce mandates, and target resources to help struggling students succeed.
02 Public Safety Support law enforcement by hiring more officers, improving their training, and providing necessary resources.
Rebuild trust between police and communities while keeping Minnesota families safe.
03 Transportation Prioritize road and bridge maintenance in rural Minnesota through existing sales tax revenue.
Oppose additional light rail funding and redirect current revenue to road safety initiatives without new taxes.
04 Tax Policy Lower tax rates for all income levels and eliminate Minnesota's tax on social security income for seniors.
Return the state's budget surplus to taxpayers through meaningful tax relief.
05 Energy Oppose aggressive energy mandates that increase costs for Minnesota consumers.
Prioritize affordability and reliability over rapid regulatory requirements that lack reliable alternatives.
06 Government Overreach Oppose government intrusion into businesses, churches, and personal medical decisions.
Government's role should be protection and education, not dictating citizens' freedoms.
