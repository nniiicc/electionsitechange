Meet Kelabe Kelabe was raised in a working class union family by his single mother who came to this country as a refugee.
She had dreams that were put on hold because of the barriers that come with raising a child on your own.
They were priced out of neighborhood after neighborhood because no matter how hard she worked, rising costs always moved faster than her wages.
They dealt with housing insecurity, but they were supported by their tight knit community.
When they lost his grandma to lymphoma, Kelabe and his mother were not only grieving, they were buried under medical debt that drove his mother to bankruptcy.
Parents shouldn’t be worried about eviction or where their next meal is going to come from because our legislators are unwilling to create safety nets for those in need.
That’s why Kelabe is running to represent the 37th LD because our families deserve better.
Growing up, Kelabe’s education was made the number one priority by his mother.
At an early age Kelabe saw his mother advocate for his education at every turn.
She saw education as a way to keep her son safe.
She saw Kelabe’s older cousin become a victim of gun violence and made sure Kelabe was on a positive pathway, with education as her north star.
His mother’s values stuck with him as he went on to become an educator.
She enrolled him in Rainier Scholars, a decision that changed the trajectory of his life.
That program kept Kelabe engaged, challenged, and on a positive path.
He watched friends in the program who were recruited to join gangs in middle school stay the course.
They were too invested in the work to fall into it.
That experience showed Kelabe firsthand what happens when you invest in young people early.
It's where he learned that the best prevention isn't punishment, it's opportunity.
Over the past 8 years Kelabe has been an educator at Rainier Scholars, working with students routinely left behind by our school system as a teacher, an advocate, and a mentor.
He has spent his entire career working with kids all over this district.
In his time as an educator he has done what he can to support students as they face hardships and become leaders in their communities.
The issues he faced as a kid growing up in Seattle continue to impact the students he serves.
Families continue to be displaced from their long-time homes in the Central District.
Parents have to make difficult decisions between going into medical debt or keeping food on the table.
Students fear for their safety in their neighborhoods losing classmates to gun violence.
Kelabe has done everything he can inside the classroom.
Now it's time to lead outside of it.
The 37th deserves a representative that will do all he can to ease the burden on families from the Central District all the way to Skyway.
