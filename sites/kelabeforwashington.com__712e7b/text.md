Why I’m running
The safest and healthiest communities are those that are well-invested in and well-resourced.
For far too long South Seattle and Skyway have not been prioritized by our local leaders.
I support policies that will center community solutions that protect working class families, our children, and our most vulnerable populations.

These aren't policy talking points: they're the daily realities of the 37th.
