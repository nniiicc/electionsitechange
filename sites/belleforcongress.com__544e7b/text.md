Where He Stands Issues I’m running for Congress to make life more affordable, keep our communities safe, and give working families a fair shot.
In South Bay San Diego, that means tackling housing costs, fixing our broken immigration system, investing in public schools, and helping small businesses grow.
South Bay deserves a representative who focuses on results, not politics.
I will work every day to lower costs, strengthen families, support schools, and make government more responsive to the people who live and work here.
I will be honored to serve you and help make South Bay, San Diego a safe, secure and prosperous place for everyone who resides here and who works here.
Housing Housing costs are crushing too many families, seniors, and young people.
I will support policies that increase housing supply, speed up approvals, and make it easier to build starter homes, apartments near transit, and affordable units for working families.
I will also push federal housing programs to work better in high-cost regions like San Diego so first-time buyers and renters get real relief.
Immigration Our immigration system should be secure, fair, and functional.
I support stronger border security, tougher action against cartels and smugglers, and a lawful immigration, swift process that works for employers, families, and our economy.
In South Bay, that means paying attention to cross-border impacts, local service capacity, and the need for practical federal coordination.
Public Schools Every child deserves a strong public school, and federal funding should help support low-income students, special education, tutoring, and career training.
I will fight for fairer formulas and more stable funding so San Diego-area schools receive the support they need.
My goal is to make sure more dollars reach classrooms, not bureaucracy.
Small Businesses Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy.
I will work to reduce red tape, improve access to capital, and make federal programs easier to use for neighborhood shops, contractors, and family-owned companies.
I also support workforce and apprenticeship programs that help small businesses find good employees and grow.
