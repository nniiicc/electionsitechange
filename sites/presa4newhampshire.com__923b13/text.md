Meet Adam
I have lived in New Hampshire my entire life.
Growing up right here in Merrimack, I went through our K-12 public school district, learning early on what makes our community so special.
Driven to serve and lead, I earned my Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration from New England College in 2024, followed by my Master’s Degree in Strategic Leadership in 2025.
Today, I’m proud to put those leadership skills to work for you.
I currently serve as one of your State Representatives in Concord, while simultaneously working as a NH-based Realtor® and giving back to the next generation as an assistant college wrestling coach at New England College.
Merrimack is the town that raised me, and I want to ensure it remains a safe, prosperous, and great place to live for generations to come.
Since you first elected me in 2024, I have dedicated myself to making sure your voice is heard loud and clear in Concord—and with your support this November, I will continue doing exactly that.
Our Values
Tough On Crime
I proudly stand with the Merrimack Police Department to ensure our law enforcement officers have the full resources and support necessary to tackle the fentanyl crisis, secure our communities, and keep our neighborhoods safe.
Fiscal Responsibility
I am committed to keeping the Granite State broad-based tax-free and fighting to lower local property taxes so that Merrimack remains an affordable place to live, work, and raise a family.
Local Control
New Hampshire was built on the principle that the citizens of Merrimack know what’s best for our town, and I will always fight to protect our community from government overreach and preserve our local decision-making.
