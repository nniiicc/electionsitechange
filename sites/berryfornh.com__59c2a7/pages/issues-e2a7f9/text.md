Yard Signs Contact Donate Back to Home The Issues Where I stand on the issues that matter most to New Hampshire families.
Taxes & Cost of Living I have never voted for a new tax or a tax increase, and I never will.
Every dollar taken in taxes is a dollar that can't go toward a mortgage, groceries, or childcare.
I'll keep fighting to control spending, eliminate waste, and protect New Hampshire's taxpayer-first tradition.
Property Tax Relief Property taxes are the single largest burden facing many families, especially seniors on fixed incomes.
I've worked to put taxpayers back in control through transparency and accountability, and proposed measures to prevent people from being taxed out of their homes.
Education Students and parents should come first, not bureaucracy.
I support giving families a real voice in their children's education while protecting property taxpayers from runaway school spending.
Strong schools and responsible budgets must go hand in hand.
Ending Mandatory Vehicle Inspections I supported repealing mandatory vehicle inspections because they placed a disproportionate burden on working families, especially those in rural areas who rely on their vehicles daily.
Drivers are capable of maintaining their own vehicles without government mandates.
Public Safety & Bail Reform For five years I worked to repeal a broken bail system that put families at risk.
Today we have a stronger framework that keeps violent offenders off the streets while protecting the rights of the accused.
Community safety is government's most basic responsibility.
Second Amendment I am a strong and unwavering supporter of the Second Amendment.
Law-abiding citizens have the right to defend themselves and their families.
I oppose efforts to restrict responsible gun owners and will continue defending constitutional carry.
Election Integrity Confidence in elections is essential to a healthy democracy.
Requiring ID to vote is a common-sense safeguard that ensures every legal vote counts.
I'll continue supporting reasonable measures that protect election integrity while preserving access for eligible voters.
Live Free or Die I believe in the power of the individual over the power of government.
My goal is simple: protect freedom, defend taxpayers, keep communities safe, and ensure New Hampshire remains the best place in America to live, work, and raise a family.
