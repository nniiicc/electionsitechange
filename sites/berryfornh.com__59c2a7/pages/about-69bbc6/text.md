Economics & Political Science Occupation Business Owner (Two Childcare Centers) Party Republican My Story "Those who were born in New Hampshire were lucky, those who moved to New Hampshire are smart, those who stay are smart and lucky." I moved to New Hampshire over #ago with $400 in my pocket and a 1997 Honda Accord with 6 (probably more) oil leaks.
Since then I have started a family, started a business, and begun to focus more on giving back than moving up.
New Hampshire is a wonderful state and has afforded me and my family limitless opportunity.
These advancements did not come without hard work, determination, and a few random acts of kindness.
In January of 2023 my son was born and had emergency transportation to Children's Hospital at Dartmouth.
That was an eye opening experience on many levels and the impact that has had on my resolve cannot be understated.
I learned quickly that life is short and we must maximize the amount of good we can do in it.
My day-to-day is spent keeping our two childcare centers running, including one in Weare, and keeping up with our two children.
We cherish our home in Weare and while I am new to the rural lifestyle, my only regret is I did not get here sooner.
I sit on the board of the Elliot Perry Foundation which focuses on giving back to the community, especially around issues impacting children and young adults.
We fund handicap accessible playgrounds as well as support youth sports teams.
I previously had the honor of serving two terms in the State House where I focused on increasing the access and affordability of childcare and reforming New Hampshire's election system for the first time in 30 years.
I am running to continue the fight for New Hampshire's families so that every person has the same opportunities that I have had.
Sammi and I consider ourselves blessed and want to give back in every way we can.
What I'm Fighting For Affordability The way to make New Hampshire more affordable is to keep as much of your family's money with you.
I'm fighting to reduce the burden on working families so they can thrive.
Family Values Grandparents deserve to watch their grandkids grow up here.
Parents deserve to raise their families as they see fit.
Children deserve the best NH can offer.
Election Reform I helped reform New Hampshire's election system for the first time in 30 years, ensuring fair and accessible elections for all citizens.
Community Service Through the Elliot Perry Foundation, I support handicap-accessible playgrounds and youth sports teams, investing in our community's future.
