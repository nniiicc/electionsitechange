Meet Lee
A Cheyenne native, Lee spent more than 90% of his life right here in the heart of District 44.
He knows his neighbors and their needs.
A small business owner and entrepreneur deeply rooted in community and service.

Top Issues
- Lower Property Taxes The legislature made good progress on lowering property taxes, but there is more to do.
We must focus on providing property tax relief for those who need it, but also keep our local governments budgets whole.
- Mineral Extraction Wyoming’s legacy mineral extraction industry is the backbone of our state and provides over 1/3 of the operating revenue for our state budget.
Lee will work to support our existing industries and pave the way for emerging technologies and industries.
- Education Wyoming students are the next generation and a Wyoming education must meet students where they are at to prepare students with the skills to directly move into the workforce or prepare them to continue their education with our community colleges or at the University of Wyoming.
- 2nd Amendment Lee is a staunch advocate for your 2nd amendment rights.
As a former member of the National Guard, he understands what it means to serve to maintain our freedom.
- Economic Diversity Spurring on economic diversity largely means keeping government out of the way.
Government regulations should be limited and government systems should bridge the gap for businesses to thrive.
- Small Business Friendly Lee is a small business owner and knows how to create jobs.
He will work to keep Wyoming businesses in Wyoming and recruit new ones.
