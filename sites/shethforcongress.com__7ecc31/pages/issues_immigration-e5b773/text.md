November 2: Meet And Greet at Bistro Baffi - 1st Ave S, Suite 101, Normandy Park WA 98148
Guest Speaker: Doug Basler and others
I am running for the 7th Congressional District in Washington State and present a nuanced approach to immigration and border security.
As an immigrant myself, I deeply value the contributions of those who come to America seeking opportunities.
I will advocate for a balanced policy that upholds the nation’s sovereignty and ensures safety by securing the borders, while also recognizing the essential role of immigrants in the U.S. workforce.
I will propose reforms to streamline the immigration process, making it more accessible for individuals who wish to work and contribute positively to American society.
My stance is rooted in a love for my adopted country and a commitment to safeguarding its security, ensuring that immigration is managed in a way that benefits both the nation and those seeking a new life in the U.S
