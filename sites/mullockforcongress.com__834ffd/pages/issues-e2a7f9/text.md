Economy & Jobs Today we are living with more income and wealth inequality than we have ever had in the history of our country.
93% of our nation's wealth is owned by the top 1%.
To grow the middle class, we must have a fair tax system that holds corporations and the wealthiest accountable while removing corporate greed and corruption from our economy.

Our healthcare system, if run as a business, would be the worst-run business in American history.
It's time for serious change.

I Will Fight To: Overturn Citizens United and end unlimited dark money in elections Support public financing of elections Ban members of Congress from trading stocks Require full transparency in political spending A Tax Code That Works for the Many, Not the Few For too long, our tax system has been rigged to benefit billionaires, giant corporations, and Wall Street insiders while working families pay more and get less.
That is not an accident—it is the result of decades of political choices.
As a Democrat in Congress, I am fighting to make a different choice: a fair, progressive tax system that asks those who have gained the most from our economy to finally pay their fair share.
This plan is guided by a simple principle: no one who works for a living should pay a higher effective tax rate than a billionaire who lives off wealth.
1.
Tax Relief for Working and Middle-Class Families Expand and make permanent the Child Tax Credit.
Provide tax relief for caregivers, seniors, and people with disabilities.
Cut taxes for small businesses that reinvest in their workers and communities.
Bottom line: Working families deserve a tax code that helps them get ahead—not just scrape by.
2.
Invest the Revenue in What Matters Strengthen Social Security and Medicare & Medicaid Lower healthcare and prescription drug costs Make public college and job training more affordable Rebuild infrastructure and combat climate change Bottom line: A fair tax system is not about punishment—it is about priorities.
3.
A Wealth Tax on Extreme Fortunes Establish an annual tax on the net worth of the wealthiest households in America.
Protect small businesses, family farms, and retirement savings.
Use the revenue to invest directly in working families and communities.
Bottom line: Billionaires can afford to contribute a small percentage of their vast fortunes so every child in America can have a decent shot at success.
4.
End Special Treatment for Capital Gains and Wall Street Tax capital gains and dividends at the same rates as wages for high-income households.
Close loopholes that allow hedge fund managers and private equity executives to dodge taxes.
Crack down on stock buybacks that enrich executives while workers fall behind.
Bottom line: Work should be rewarded—not financial speculation.
5.
Corporate Taxes That Reward Jobs, Not Offshoring Raise the corporate tax rate so profitable corporations contribute to the country that made their success possible.
Eliminate tax breaks for companies that ship jobs overseas.
Provide targeted tax credits for companies that create good-paying jobs here at home.
Bottom line: If you do business in America, you should invest in America.
6.
Close Offshore Tax Havens and Enforce the Law End the use of offshore tax havens by the wealthy and large corporations.
Fully fund the IRS to enforce the law fairly, focusing on high-income tax evasion—not working people.
Increase transparency so everyone plays by the same rules.
Bottom line: Tax cheating by the rich is not clever—it is theft from the American people.
