Election Day Voting Vote in person at your assigned polling place on Election Day from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM .
Find your polling location at vote.nj.gov.
Early In-Person Voting Vote early at designated locations during the 6-day period before Election Day.
Check vote.nj.gov for locations and hours in your county.
Vote by Mail Request a mail-in ballot and vote from home.
Return by mail (postmarked by Election Day), drop box, or deliver to your County Board of Elections.
How to Vote by Mail Follow these steps to successfully cast your mail-in ballot in New Jersey.
1 Request Your Ballot Apply online at vote.nj.gov, download the application form, or contact your County Clerk.
You can request a ballot for a single election or all future elections.
2 Receive Your Ballot Your County Clerk will mail your ballot to the address you provided on your application.
Singed, singed envelopes are included with detailed instructions.
3 Mark Your Ballot Follow the instructions carefully.
Use a black or blue ink pen.
Fill in the ovals completely for your chosen candidates.
4 Seal & Sign Place your completed ballot in the inner certificate envelope and sign the back.
Then place that envelope in the outer return envelope.
5 Return Your Ballot Mail it back (postmarked by Election Day), drop it in a secure ballot drop box, or deliver it to your County Board of Elections.
Track your ballot status at vote.nj.gov.
Track Your Ballot You can track the status of your mail-in ballot online at voter.svrs.nj.gov .
You'll be able to see when your ballot was mailed, received, and counted.
