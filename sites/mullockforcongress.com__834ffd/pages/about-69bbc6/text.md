My Story From Cape May to Congress I call Cape May, New Jersey home.
Growing up in this beautiful coastal community taught me the value of hard work, the importance of looking out for your neighbors, and what it means to be part of something bigger than yourself.
After graduating from Mount Saint Mary's University and earning my MBA from Saint Joseph's University, I came back home to build my career and my family.
As a former PGA professional and business owner, I learned firsthand the challenges that working people and small business owners face every day.
I first ran for Mayor as a non-partisan candidate on the "Community First" slate because I've always believed in putting service to my community over allegiance to any one political party.
Now in my second term, I've never once raised taxes because I believe in fiscal responsibility and putting more money back in working families' pockets.
I've worked to protect affordable housing, support small businesses, and preserve the character of our town.
Serving as Construction Manager for the Harriet Tubman Museum was one of the proudest moments of my career.
My wife Justine and I are raising our three children—Bobby, Tallulah, and James—right here in Cape May.
Every decision I make, I think about what kind of community and country we're leaving for them.
