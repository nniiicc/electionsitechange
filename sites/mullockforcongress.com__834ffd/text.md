Zack Mullock was born and raised in South Jersey.
Cape May is not just where he lives — it is home.
It is where he grew up, where he built his business, where he and his wife are raising their three children, and where his roots run deep.
He is running for Congress because he loves this community and feels called to serve it — just as he always has.
Zack believes that South Jersey deserves real, homegrown representation.
Washington is too often driven by corruption, special interests, and profiteering that leave working families squeezed and unheard.
Zack is focused on the issues that matter most here at home: affordability, access to healthcare, responsible energy policy, and protecting our environment.
This district cannot afford another election lost to politics as usual.
And he can win here.
Zack has won multiple elections in Jeff Van Drew's backyard — earning support in a region that values independence, fiscal discipline, and accountability.
In proudly non-partisan Cape May, voters have elected and re-elected him because his name is tied to results, not rhetoric.
As Mayor he didn’t raise taxes for 6 years and has kept taxes affordable for working families.
All the while, under his leadership, Cape May built a new firehouse and library, strengthened public safety by hiring new police officers and firefighters, improved infrastructure and parks, supported small businesses, and delivered the pay increases municipal employees earned and deserved.
The construction of a new police station is also currently underway.
He has proven that strong fiscal discipline and meaningful investment can go hand in hand.
Zack served as Construction Manager for the Harriet Tubman Museum, sits on the Foundation Board of Atlantic Cape Community College, and has long been active in civic life across the region.
He is a graduate of Mount Saint Mary's University and holds an MBA from Saint Joseph's University.
He played college ice hockey and golf.
Zack Mullock has won here before — and he will do it again.
