SUPPORT STEVE'S CAMPAIGN DONATE SHOWING UP WHEN IT MATTERS Meet Steve Steve Sauter is a fifth-generation North Dakotan and Bismarck resident.
He is proud to call District 7 and North Dakota home and is excited the oportunity to help the community and state grow without losing what makes them so special.
A longtime leader in North Dakota’s energy, manufacturing, and industrial sectors, Steve has spent the past 19 years with Interstate Companies, starting in the warehouse and working his way through outside sales, branch management, regional leadership, and ultimately to Executive Vice President of the Interstate Bearing Systems division.
His career has taken him across North Dakota and the region, working directly with the people and industries that build, fix, transport, and produce the things our state depends on every day.
Steve and his wife, Crystal, are raising their three children—Ethan (11), Bentley (7), and Hailey (5)—in Bismarck.
Family is the foundation of everything Steve does, and it’s the reason he decided to step forward and serve.
Priorities for North Dakota Steve cares deeply about the kind of North Dakota our children will grow up in.
He believes in strong families, responsible energy development, and the common-sense leadership that keeps our state affordable, competitive, and moving in the right direction.
Z Strong Families & Safe Communities Steve believes North Dakota should remain a place where families can afford to live, work, and raise kids.
Rising costs—from housing and property taxes to childcare and everyday expenses—are putting pressure on working families.
Steve will support practical policies that keep neighborhoods safe, respect family values, and ensure North Dakota remains a place where families can thrive.
Z Affordable Living & Common Sense Government From taxes to everyday costs, North Dakotans deserve an affordable place to live.
Steve believes in practical, limited government that spends responsibly, respects taxpayers, and focuses on real results instead of politics.
Z Responsible Energy & Good Paying Jobs Energy, agriculture, and manufacturing are the backbone of North Dakota’s economy.
Steve supports responsible energy development that protects good-paying jobs, strengthens local communities, and provides the revenue our state relies on for schools, infrastructure, and services.
North Dakotans don’t just talk about work—we do the work—and Steve will push for the policies that keep our state one of the best places for business.
