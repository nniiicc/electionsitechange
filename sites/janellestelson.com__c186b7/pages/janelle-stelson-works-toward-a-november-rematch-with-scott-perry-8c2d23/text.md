Rep.
Scott Perry in 2024, Janelle Stelson is hoping that another run for PA-10 – and a favorable midterm election cycle – will yield different results this year.
Stelson is a familiar face in Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional District.
She was a longtime news anchor for the central Pennsylvania TV station WGAL before setting her sights on Congress two years ago.
Now, with an election cycle under her belt and support from the highest levels of the Democratic Party, Stelson is aiming to bring fresh energy to a seat she believes hasn’t been representative of the people in its district.
