Janelle Stelson is the Democratic candidate for Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional District, running against incumbent Republican Scott Perry.
For more than 30 years, Central Pennsylvanians have relied on Janelle to tell the truth, trusted her to shine a light on our problems, and counted on her to get answers and hold the powerful accountable.
Whether she was with small business owners, riding along with local law enforcement, or covering the rising costs of housing and health care, Janelle has been listening and telling the stories of countless families across the district.
What congressional district is Janelle Stelson running in?
Janelle Stelson is running to represent Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional District (PA-10), challenging incumbent Republican Congressman Scott Perry.
Why is Janelle Stelson running for Congress?
You learn a lot about a place when you tell its stories for more than 30 years.
Central Pennsylvanians are being squeezed by higher costs for housing, health care, utilities and groceries.
She has heard over and over how people in Central Pennsylvania are sick and tired of career politicians who have been ignoring them and their problems.
Janelle is not a politician — she’s someone who will listen, work for real solutions, to lower costs, fight for term limits and sit down with anyone who has a good idea about how to make life better for all of us in Central Pennsylvania.
What does Janelle Stelson support?
Janelle will work with Republicans and Democrats to lower the costs of gas, utilities, buying a home and health care.
She will fight to lower taxes for the middle class, make no tax on tips and overtime permanent and invest in our small businesses, family farms and rural hospitals.
She will work to protect Social Security, Medicare and Medicaid, and a woman’s right to choose.
She’s fed up with career politicians in Washington: she’ll cut government waste and address the national debt, supports term limits and promises to limit her own terms, supports banning members of Congress from trading stocks and vows to vote against pay raises for members of Congress.
Cost of Living & the Economy What is Janelle Stelson’s plan to bring down the cost of living?
Janelle supports tax breaks for the hard-working middle class, not billionaires, including no tax on tips or overtime, which she would vote to make permanent.
She believes we need bipartisan problem-solving to pass laws that lower the cost of necessities — including food, gas, housing, utility costs, prescription drugs and more.
Janelle will repeal the tariff taxes that are increasing costs and vote to end the war in Iran that has made our gas prices skyrocket.
She believes we must address the national deficit, which has reached $41 trillion under Perry’s leadership, and will prioritize cutting wasteful government spending and making sure taxpayer dollars are not abused.
Where does Janelle Stelson stand on tariffs?
Janelle Stelson supports repealing the tariff taxes that are raising costs for Central Pennsylvania families, and opposes the chaotic policy of ever-changing tariff-taxes that raise prices and kill jobs.
Every household has already been forced to pay over $2,500 due to tariffs , and small businesses are being forced to raise prices on their neighbors or close up shop.
This is a direct contrast with Scott Perry, who supports the tariff-taxes.
Does Janelle Stelson support tax cuts for working families?
Yes.
Janelle supports tax breaks for the hard-working middle class.
She supports no tax on tips or overtime, which she would vote to make permanent.
What does Janelle Stelson say about the national debt?
Janelle is a fiscal conservative and believes we must address the national deficit, which has reached $41 trillion under Perry’s leadership.
She will prioritize cutting wasteful government spending and make sure taxpayer dollars aren’t abused.
Scott Perry was the deciding vote on a budget that slashes food assistance, raises utility bills and adds $3.4 trillion dollars in new debt, mortgaging our children’s future — all to support continuing permanent tax breaks for the wealthy. [5] [6] Tax on Tips & Overtime Does Janelle Stelson support no tax on tips and overtime?
Yes.
Janelle supports no tax on tips and overtime and would vote to make that permanent — one of the tax provisions she’s said should stay in place for working families.
Did Janelle Stelson oppose a tax cut for Pennsylvania families?
No.
Janelle supports tax breaks for the hard-working middle class, including making no tax on tips or overtime permanent.
Housing What is Janelle Stelson’s plan to lower housing costs?
Janelle has laid out clear housing priorities and a plan: stop Wall Street from buying up homes, cut red tape to make it faster and easier to build homes, make homes cheaper to build, expand small-dollar home loans, reward towns that actually build more homes and rein in the national debt and return to fiscal sanity.
Does Janelle Stelson support stopping Wall Street from buying single-family homes?
Yes — stopping Wall Street from buying up homes is the first plank of Janelle’s housing plan.
Scott Perry voted against a bipartisan bill to lower housing costs and stop Wall Street from buying single family homes. [1] [2] Instead he sided with his Wall Street donors, who gave him more than $300,000 .
When it came time to actually vote on lowering housing costs, Congressman Scott Perry was the only member of Pennsylvania’s Congressional delegation — Republican or Democrat — to vote no. [1] Fixing Congress & Government Reform Does Janelle Stelson support term limits for Congress?
Yes.
Even if Congress does not act to require term limits, Janelle will limit her own terms and will not serve more than 12 years — she says we do not need more career politicians, we need fewer.
She also supports age limits for members of Congress.
Does Janelle Stelson support banning members of Congress from trading stocks?
Yes.
Janelle supports a ban on members of Congress and their immediate families trading individual stocks while in office so they cannot profit from insider information.
She sold her own individual stocks before running for office and will not own or trade individual stocks while in office.
What is Janelle Stelson’s plan to fix corruption in Washington?
This is Janelle’s plan to confront Washington corruption directly and restore accountability: limit terms to 12 years; age limits for members of Congress; ban members of Congress and their immediate families from trading individual stocks while in office; a lifetime ban on members becoming lobbyists after serving in the House — no cashing in on their service; and cut the perks of first-class flights — Congress members’ perks are out of control.
She also supports a constitutional amendment to end or significantly curb the President’s pardoning power; a constitutional amendment to reverse the Citizens United decision and stop dark money groups from spending unlimited and untraceable money in campaigns; a truly independent Congressional Ethics Agency with the power to investigate members for wrongdoing, so members of Congress no longer regulate themselves; a strengthened Federal Election Commission with more power to hold candidates and public officials who break campaign finance laws accountable; reconstituting the FBI’s public corruption unit and fully funding the Justice Department’s public integrity unit; and ending prediction market corruption.
What government waste does Janelle Stelson want to cut?
Janelle Stelson is a fiscal conservative who wants to tackle our national debt, which has risen to more than $40 trillion on Scott Perry’s watch.
Janelle Stelson wants to crack down on Washington corruption that raises your costs and cut out the wasteful spending of your tax dollars.
That includes ending the Iran War that is costing $1-2 billion per day, stopping the flood of taxpayer dollars to wasteful projects like the $1 billion ballroom and voting against bills like the One Big Beautiful Bill Act that added nearly $5 trillion to our national debt.
She would hire back the inspectors general who are in charge of rooting out waste in the government, unlike Scott Perry who supported firing these watchdogs.
Janelle believes taxpayer dollars should be used to improve the lives of Central Pennsylvanians, not just add to government bloat.
She would make sure unused office buildings are repurposed and even take federal pensions away from legislators who break the law.
Health Care Where does Janelle Stelson stand on health care costs?
Janelle supports high-quality, affordable health care and funding for rural hospitals.
She says we also need better access to urgent care and primary care, so it doesn’t take six months to get a physical or three days to get an appointment when you’re sick — the current system needs reform.
She supports lowering the price of prescription drugs by allowing the government to negotiate prices.
Does Janelle Stelson support lowering prescription drug prices?
Yes.
Janelle supports lowering the price of prescription drugs by allowing the government to negotiate prices directly.
How will Janelle Stelson Lower Healthcare Costs?
Janelle believes you should never have to decide between putting food on the table and paying for the medical care you need.
She believes we need to ban surprise billing, close the loophole for ambulance trips, and improve the dispute arbitration process to better protect patients.
Janelle would vote to restore the Affordable Care Act subsidies to help lower healthcare costs.
It’s unacceptable that people who have health insurance coverage still fall into medical debt due to sky-high medical costs — that’s why she believes medical debt should be taken off credit reports.
Rural Hospitals & Health Care Access What would Janelle Stelson do to protect rural hospitals in Central Pennsylvania?
Janelle has a rural priorities plan that includes reversing the cuts to rural hospitals that Scott Perry has voted for and fighting for increased funding and staff for our rural hospitals to ensure every Pennsylvanian has access to medical care, regardless of zip code.
Scott Perry cast the deciding vote on a bill that cuts health coverage for thousands of people in the district, which will worsen the local doctor shortage and threaten rural hospitals. [5] [6] Does Janelle Stelson support government-run health care?
No.
Janelle has described her health care approach as a public/private option — where people who want to keep private insurance can keep their insurance.
She does not support Medicare for All.
Protecting Social Security What is Janelle Stelson’s position on Social Security?
Janelle says people earned their Social Security and she will always fight to protect it.
She will never cut the Social Security benefits that seniors have earned.
Congressman Perry wrote on his own website that he wants to cut Social Security benefits, raise taxes, and even raise the retirement age to 69.
Unlike nearly every other Republican and Democratic Member of Congress from Pennsylvania, Perry voted to deny nurses, teachers, and firefighters their full Social Security benefits. [9] Energy, Utilities & Data Centers Where does Janelle Stelson stand on data centers in Central Pennsylvania?
Janelle believes local communities and their leadership should have the right to approve or reject a data center project without getting sued by big corporations.
She believes data centers should not simply be dropped into the middle of neighborhoods, driving up utility prices, polluting water, creating noise pollution and lowering property values for hardworking families.
If communities do not want them, they should not be built.
Congressman Perry took campaign cash from data center developers and voted for nearly $70 billion in tax breaks to data centers, giving them our money to build data centers in our communities.
Scott Perry even voted for these data center developers to override local control, and said “we simply cannot afford to slow down the build-out of data centers.” What would Janelle Stelson do about rising electric bills?
Janelle believes families in the area are already struggling with the rising cost of everything and are watching their electric and utility bills climb faster than their paychecks.
She will fight for accountability on utility rate increases and push for investments that bring costs down — because no family should have to choose between keeping the lights on and paying for their health care.
Perry took more than $150,000 from energy companies and voted for policies that caused our electric prices to skyrocket.
Janelle will also stand up for local communities to make sure large data center developers pay for their own power and do not drive up ratepayer costs.
Agriculture & Rural Communities What is Janelle Stelson’s plan for family farms and agriculture?
In Congress, Janelle will make fighting for a new, 5-year Farm Bill a top priority — one that lowers input costs, provides certainty to farmers, and helps get more young people into farming careers.
Four companies control over half of all chicken processing, raking in record profits for themselves while they squeeze family farmers out of business.
In Congress, she would support legislative efforts to break up consolidation in the marketplace, and she supports efforts to mitigate the impact of avian flu outbreaks through stronger disease-prevention support and investments to protect farmers.
She will also advocate to ensure that farmers in Central Pennsylvania have the resources to have the most up-to-date farming equipment, and for legislation to give farmers the right to repair their own equipment and ensure they have the manuals, diagnostics and parts to do so.
Jobs, Wages & Innovation What is Janelle Stelson’s position on the minimum wage?
The minimum wage of $7.25 in Pennsylvania is outrageous.
It’s lower than any surrounding state, including West Virginia.
At PA’s minimum wage, an hour’s worth of work barely buys a gallon of milk and a loaf of bread.
Central Pennsylvanians deserve a Representative who will fight for policies that create good-paying jobs, keep them here with our highly-skilled workforce, train workers for the jobs of the future and support our hard-working families.
Does Janelle Stelson support vocational and trade education?
Yes.
Janelle thinks a lot of those jobs should not require a college degree.
She believes we need to concentrate more on vocational and technical training in schools — more mechanics, plumbers, electricians, woodworkers, and tradespeople in general.
These jobs pay family-supporting wages and cannot be outsourced to China, and she will be a champion for these jobs in Congress.
Where does Janelle Stelson stand on advanced manufacturing and innovation?
Janelle says innovation is how we build the industries and jobs of the future right here in Pennsylvania — like advanced manufacturing.
She will fight to protect and grow the investments that make that possible, like the CHIPS and Science Act and stronger research funding, so American breakthroughs happen here at home.
China is racing to overtake us in the critical technologies that will define this century, and we cannot afford to fall behind.
Small Business What would Janelle Stelson do to help small businesses?
Janelle has sat down with business owners across Central Pennsylvania and heard over and over that they need help accessing capital and getting health insurance and benefits for their employees.
Small businesses are key to Central Pennsylvania’s economy, and in Congress she will ensure her office helps with SBA loans and will advocate to cut through red tape so small businesses can thrive.
Scott Perry’s tariff war has led to price spikes for Central Pennsylvanians on everything from goods to groceries, and made it harder for small businesses to compete in the global marketplace.
Scott Perry voted to increase insurance premiums that many small business owners rely on.
Janelle would do the opposite.
Honoring Veterans Where does Janelle Stelson stand on veterans’ benefits?
In Congress, Janelle will vote to ensure that those who serve in the Armed Forces receive the benefits they have earned, including expanded access to health benefits, continuing education, and job training to equip veterans for continued success back home in the community.
Congressman Perry voted against the largest expansion of veterans’ benefits and health care in VA history.

Guns & the Second Amendment Does Janelle Stelson support the Second Amendment?
What is her stance on guns?
Yes.
Janelle supports the Second Amendment.
Her father was in the first class of Alaska State troopers and he was vice president of his gun club.
Janelle also supports universal background checks to prevent guns from falling into the hands of criminals and the dangerously mentally ill.
She will pursue solutions to reduce gun violence that are supported by wide majorities of Americans on both sides of the aisle – including stronger enforcement of existing gun laws and implementing red flag laws.
What is Janelle Stelson’s plan for public safety?
Janelle believes in funding law enforcement and strengthening coordination with local communities to make our neighborhoods safer.
She believes in giving police departments the resources they need to hire, train and support officers – unlike Scott Perry, who voted to deny funding to police departments to support police officers and voted to cut the DOJ budget by nearly $1 billion, including a $654 million cut to the FBI.
Does Janelle Stelson support flock cameras?
As the daughter of a State Trooper, I stand with local law enforcement and will work with anyone in Congress to give police the tools they need to save lives and tackle crime.
But we need much stronger guardrails in place to ensure that the data is protected and only used for crime prevention.
Individual communities should be able to decide for themselves whether or not they want the cameras.
Trans Athletes in Girls’ & Women’s Sports Where does Janelle Stelson stand on transgender athletes in girls’ and women’s sports?
Janelle does not believe that transgender women should be allowed to play in women’s sports.
Protecting Abortion Access Where does Janelle Stelson stand on abortion?
