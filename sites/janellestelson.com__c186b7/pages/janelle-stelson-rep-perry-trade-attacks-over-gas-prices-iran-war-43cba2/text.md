Perry trade attacks over gas prices, Iran war HARRISBURG, Pa. — Rising gas prices tied to the ongoing war in Iran are becoming a central issue in the race for Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional district, as Democratic candidate Janelle Stelson criticized Republican incumbent Scott Perry for his support of the conflict.
Speaking in Harrisburg, Stelson argued that the war is directly contributing to higher costs for families across central Pennsylvania.
