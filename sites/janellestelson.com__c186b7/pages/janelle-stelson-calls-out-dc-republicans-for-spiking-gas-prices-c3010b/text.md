Standing just steps away from a Harrisburg gas station where the cost of fuel has risen to $4.24, Democratic congressional candidate Janelle Stelson on Monday blamed Republicans in Washington, D.C., for the rising cost of gas, groceries and other everyday goods.
Stelson, who is seeking the Democratic nomination in Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional District in hopes of a rematch with U.S.
Rep.
Scott Perry in the fall, cited tariffs and the ongoing war in Iran as two major contributors to rising costs – and looked to draw a contrast between herself and Perry as she seeks a rematch of their 2024 matchup.
