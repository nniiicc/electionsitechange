After losing a close race against a Rep.
Scott Perry in 2024, former TV news anchor and Democrat Janelle Stelson on Monday officially kicked off another bid for Pennsylvania’s 10th Congressional District next year.
And this time, Stelson said she’s addressed one of the major criticisms in her first bid—that she didn’t live in the district—by moving to Cumberland County.
“Honestly, for people to be talking about that instead of Scott Perry’s record, that’s a distraction,” she said, while acknowledging her voter registration had not yet been updated to reflect the move.
Stelson, a former anchor at WGAL, said in an interview with News 8 that she believes she can build off the momentum of last year’s race, in which she lost to Perry by a little more than 5,000 votes.
The 1oth congressional district in Pennsylvania is comprised of Dauphin County and parts of Cumberland and York counties.
The seat is expected to be among a number of races targeted by Democrats seeking to gain control of the U.S.
House in 2026.
