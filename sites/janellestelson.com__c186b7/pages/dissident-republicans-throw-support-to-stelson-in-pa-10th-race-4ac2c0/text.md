A group of Republicans dissatisfied with the direction of the party under President Donald J.
Trump called Wednesday for 10th Congressional District voters who feel likewise to vote for Democrat Janelle Stelson this fall.
The event at a farm near York Haven was headlined by former Illinois Congressman Adam Kinzinger, one of two Republicans who served on the House Select Committee investigating the circumstances surrounding the Jan. 6, 2021 attacks on the U.S.
Capitol.
