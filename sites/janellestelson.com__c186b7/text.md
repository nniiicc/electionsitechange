You learn a lot about a place when you tell its stories for decades.
For more than 30 years, Central Pennsylvanians have relied on Janelle to tell the truth, trusted her to shine a light on our problems and counted on her to get answers and hold the powerful accountable.
Whether she was with small business owners, riding along with local law enforcement, or covering the rising costs of housing and healthcare, Janelle has been listening and telling the stories of countless families across the district.
She has heard over and over how people in Central Pennsylvania are sick and tired of career politicians who have been ignoring them and their problems.
Janelle is fed up with career politicians in Washington.
She supports term limits and promises to limit her own terms.
Janelle also supports banning members of Congress from trading stocks and vows to vote against pay raises for Members of Congress.
Janelle will work with Republicans & Democrats to lower the costs of utilities, healthcare, buying a home, and invest in our small businesses and family farms.
She will fight for Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid and a woman’s right to choose.
Janelle is not a politician.
If you are looking for someone who will listen to you, who will work for real solutions, who will fight for term limits, and who will sit down with anyone who has a good idea about how to make life better for all of us in Central Pennsylvania, then support Janelle Stelson.
What We're Up Against After decades in politics, Congressman Scott Perry is part of the problem in a very broken Washington.
His focus has become helping himself, not the people of Central Pennsylvania.
Perry voted against a bipartisan bill to lower housing costs and stop Wall Street from buying single family homes.
Instead he sided with his Wall Street donors who gave him more than $300,000.
He supports the tariff-taxes and has voted for the war in Iran 5-times that are raising prices in Central Pennsylvania.
He voted to gut Medicaid and slash food assistance, all to pay for tax cuts for billionaires.
Perry supports a national abortion ban with no exceptions, touted a plan to cut Social Security benefits and raise Social Security taxes, and opposed the largest expansion of VA benefits in history.
He also played a central role in the plot to overturn the results of the 2020 election— including the deadly January 6th insurrection at the U.S.
Capitol, for which he sought a Presidential pardon.
As a non-partisan trusted voice, Janelle Stelson has spent her career listening and addressing the concerns of this district.
Janelle will stand up to business as usual in Washington and be the new voice and fresh leadership that we need.
