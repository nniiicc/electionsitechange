Catherine Cullen’s first term has included work on education, public safety, healthcare, workforce issues, and capital investments directed to communities across Rio Rancho and Sandoval County.
Work in the Legislature.
A look at legislation Catherine sponsored or co-sponsored during her first term.
Education Catherine’s first-term education work has focused on literacy, teacher requirements, retirement provisions, and student reporting.
2026 SB 29 Sponsor • Signed into law Math Requirements for Teaching License Updates requirements related to mathematics instruction and teacher licensure.
2026 SB 37 Sponsor • Signed into law High Quality Literacy Instruction Act Addresses literacy instruction and evidence-based approaches to teaching students to read.
2025 HB 251 Sponsor • Signed into law Ed.
Retirement Beneficiary Changes Updates provisions involving beneficiaries under the Educational Retirement system.
Public Safety & Communications Practical legislation modernizing first-responder communications and closing loopholes in organized crime statutes.
2025 HB 192 Sponsor • Signed into law Digital Trunked Radio System Subscriber Fees Addresses subscriber fees involving New Mexico’s statewide digital public-safety radio system.
2025 SB 70 Co-sponsor • Signed into law Add Racketeering Crimes Adds specified offenses to New Mexico’s racketeering laws.
Healthcare & Workforce Stabilizing regional healthcare capacity and making professional licenses portable between states.
2026 HB 99 Co-sponsor • Signed into law Medical Malpractice Changes Changes portions of New Mexico’s medical malpractice system, one of the major healthcare bills considered during the 2026 legislative session.
2026 HB 50 Co-sponsor • Signed into law Social Work Licensure Interstate Compact Allows New Mexico to participate in an interstate compact designed to make professional social-work licensing more portable between participating states.
