About Shad
Shad Pearce is a 27-year public servant and entrepreneur.
As a Law Enforcement Professional, with a degree in Criminology, he has dedicated his career to serving and protecting the public and currently holds the title of Captain, C-1 District, Arkansas Game & Fish Commission in which he supervises a team of law enforcement officers covering 6 counties in North Central AR.
As a self-employed entrepreneur, he has grown a successful rental, land, and light construction business.
He is also a cattle farmer.
Shad has always pursued personal growth and will continue to do so indefinitely.
As a person who values positivity and collaboration, he places emphasis on maintaining and developing
strong relationships both personally and professionally.
Shad cares about his community, state, and country.
He is a dedicated patriot.
He believes strongly in the priorities of God, Family, Work, and Community.
His interests include “anything outside” but most notably fishing, watching college football, boating, and working on heavy equipment.
His favorite past-time is cooking for his family & friends and hosting outdoor gatherings.
Shad has been a recovery diver, a youth baseball coach, a youth trap coach, and a youth group volunteer.
He is a life-long resident of Arkansas and wouldn’t want to be anywhere else.
