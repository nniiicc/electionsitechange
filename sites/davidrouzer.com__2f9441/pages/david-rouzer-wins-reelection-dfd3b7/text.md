(RALEIGH, N.C.) — David Rouzer released the statement below following his re-election to serve North Carolina’s 7th District:
“It’s an honor and privilege to serve the citizens of the 7th District, and I am humbled to be elected to represent them for another term.
I will continue to work on the issues important to the people of this district and remain a strong advocate for our national defense and a less intrusive and smaller federal government so that we can once again be prosperous at home and strong abroad.”
