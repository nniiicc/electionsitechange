Contribute Thank You Veterans November 11, 2014 To every veteran in Eastern North Carolina and around the country: thank you for your service.
I am proud to represent the more than 60,000 men and women in North Carolina’s 7th District who have served our country in the armed forces.
Our veterans and their families make great sacrifices to keep us safe and protect the freedoms we hold dear.
They deserve the very best that our country has to offer.
