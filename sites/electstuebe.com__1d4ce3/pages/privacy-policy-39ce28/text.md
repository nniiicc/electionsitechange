For stronger communities in Southwest Washington Donate Privacy Policy Last Updated: June 23, 2026 Welcome to the campaign website for David Stuebe For State House District 17 Position 2 (R) ("we," "our," or "us").
We are committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, https://electstuebe.com , use our contact forms, or opt-in to our mobile text messaging program.
1.
Information We Collect We may collect information about you in a variety of ways depending on how you interact with our site: Personal Data: This includes voluntarily provided information such as your name, email address, phone number, and comments when you fill out our contact forms, subscribe to campaign updates, or interact directly with the campaign.
In alignment with state and federal election transparency guidelines, this data helps us maintain accurate records of supporter engagement.
SMS Opt-In Data: If you choose to sign up for mobile text alerts, we collect your mobile phone number and your explicit opt-in consent.
Providing your phone number is entirely optional.
Technical and Derivative Data: Our website servers automatically collect standard web server logs to ensure site security and integrity.
This may include information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and access times, which may be processed by automated spam prevention tools and our hosting infrastructure.
Traffic Analytics and Optimization: To monitor website traffic and continually optimize your user experience, our site may utilize traffic measurement and analytics tools.
These services collect anonymized, aggregate data regarding how visitors navigate and interact with our web pages.
2.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to support the campaign and communicate with you, specifically to: Respond to your comments, questions, and inquiries submitted via our contact forms.
Send you campaign updates, newsletters, and important news.
Send mobile text alerts and political updates (only if you have explicitly opted in).
Solicit campaign donations to support the Stuebe for State Rep campaign.
Monitor and prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and security threats.
3.
Information Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
We may share information with trusted third-party service providers, vendors, or campaign consultants who assist us in operating our website, managing campaign operations, or distributing campaign communications, strictly under confidentiality agreements.
Important SMS Carrier Compliance Disclosure: Notwithstanding any other provision in this policy, SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared, rented, or sold with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Your mobile information is strictly used to communicate campaign information with you as authorized by your opt-in selection.
5.
Data Security and Retention We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill campaign communication goals or to comply with relevant legal obligations.
This includes retaining data to satisfy mandatory financial transparency and record-keeping requirements mandated by the Washington State Public Disclosure Commission (PDC).
6.
Your Rights and Choices You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, subject to state campaign record retention laws.
You can control how we communicate with you via the following methods: Email Communications: You can unsubscribe from our email lists at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any campaign email.
Text Messaging (SMS): In compliance with federal TCPA regulations, you can revoke your text messaging consent at any time.
To stop receiving text updates, reply STOP to any message received.
Text HELP for assistance.
Message and data rates may apply.
Once you opt out, your phone number will be moved to an internal do-not-contact registry to ensure your preferences are honored.
