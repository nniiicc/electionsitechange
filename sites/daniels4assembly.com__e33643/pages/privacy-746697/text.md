Privacy Policy
Effective date: April 27, 2026
Carolyn Daniels for Assembly 2026 ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our supporters, volunteers, and contributors.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit daniels4assembly.com (the "Site") or interact with the Campaign by phone, email, text message, mail, or in person.
Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us, including:
- Name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code when you sign up for updates, volunteer, or contact us.
- Billing and contribution information (name, address, employer, occupation, payment details) when you make a donation.
Federal and state law require us to collect employer and occupation for contributions over certain thresholds.
- Volunteer interests, availability, and skills you share when you sign up to help.
- Messages, comments, and other communications you send us.
We also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit the Site, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referral source.
We use this information for analytics and Site security.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Send you updates, news, and communications about the Campaign and election.
- Process contributions and comply with campaign-finance reporting requirements.
- Coordinate volunteer opportunities and respond to your questions.
- Send text-message alerts about events, voter information, and volunteer opportunities (only if you have opted in).
- Improve the Site, our outreach, and our security.
- Comply with applicable laws, including the California Political Reform Act and the Federal Election Campaign Act, and respond to lawful requests from regulators.
Text Message (SMS) Information
If you opt in to receive text messages from the Campaign, we collect and use your phone number to send recurring automated and informational text messages from Carolyn Daniels for Assembly 2026.
Message frequency varies; you can expect up to 10 messages per month, with potentially higher frequency in the days leading up to the election.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
For help, reply HELP or contact us at Daniels4Assembly@pm.me.
Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
All categories of personal information disclosed in this policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
How We Share Your Information
We share information only as described below:
- Service providers we use to operate the Campaign — for example, our website host, email platform, donation processor, texting platform, and analytics tools — only to the extent necessary for them to provide their services to us, and under agreements that limit their use of your information.
- Government agencies and the public when required by campaign-finance disclosure law.
Contributions of $100 or more must be reported on public campaign-finance reports filed with the California Secretary of State and/or the FPPC.
- Other parties when required by law, court order, subpoena, or legal process, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, property, or safety.
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
Cookies and Analytics
The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, measure performance, and improve user experience.
You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the Site may not function as expected if you do.
Data Retention
We retain your information for as long as necessary to operate the Campaign, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
After the conclusion of the 2026 election cycle and any legally required retention period, we will delete or de-identify information that is no longer needed.
Your Rights
California residents have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) to know what personal information we collect about them, to request deletion of their information (subject to legal exceptions), to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
We do not sell personal information.
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Daniels4Assembly@pm.me.
We will respond within 45 days as required by law.
Children's Privacy
The Site is not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.
Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.
No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The most current version will be posted on this page with the effective date at the top.
Material changes will be communicated to subscribers.
