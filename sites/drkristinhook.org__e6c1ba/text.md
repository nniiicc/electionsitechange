Putting My Policy Experience to Work

Senate, I fought corruption and helped write bipartisan laws that invest in safer communities, clean water, and good-paying union jobs.

And at the U.S.
Government Accountability Office, I helped Congress use facts to solve real-world problems, protected your hard-earned tax dollars from waste, and served as an elected union leader to stand up for hundreds of federal workers.
