Clermont County Law Enforcement Stands Firm with David J.
Taylor
Monday, February 12, 2024
AMELIA – America First conservative and outsider businessman David J.
Taylor announced Monday endorsements from the law enforcement community in Clermont County.
As Taylor’s campaign continues to gain momentum, he released the following endorsements of his campaign from the Clermont County law enforcement community:
Clermont County Sheriff Robert Leahy
“I have rarely endorsed candidates for public office, however, this year is different as I feel that our country is at a crossroads.
It is important to support candidates who will stand up for our conservative values.
I am proud to endorse David Taylor in the 2nd Congressional District because I know that we can trust him to enact policies that will make our communities safer.
David will always stand firm with the law enforcement community as we work to crack down on the human and drug trafficking that is coming from the southern border.”
Clermont County Prosecutor Mark Tekulve
“I have the privilege of serving the people of Clermont County as the elected prosecutor.
Many years ago, while serving as an assistant prosecutor, I met a young law student named David Taylor.
Here’s what I learned about David early on and since then.
He’s committed to his family and community, he’s a hard-worker, and his word is good as gold.
As an assistant prosecutor, David handled criminal matters from misdemeanors to felony violent crimes, including prosecuting gang members and drug dealers.
In Congress, David is the ONLY candidate in this race I know we can trust to get tough on fentanyl crossing our border and toughen penalties on gangs at the federal level.
Since the first day I met David, he’s grown from a young law student to a successful businessman, and most importantly, a husband to Charity, and a father to three incredible daughters.
I’m proud to call David a friend, and even prouder to give my full endorsement to David in his run for Congress.”
Former Clermont County Sheriff Tim Rodenberg
“Having known David for many years now, I know that he is the right person to represent the 2nd District in Congress.
I am proud to endorse David’s candidacy for Congress.
David is the only candidate in the race who I know we can trust to secure the southern border and help stop the flow of drugs into our community.”
“I am humbled by the amount of support my campaign has received from the law enforcement community in Clermont County and honored to announce these very important endorsements,” said David J.
Taylor.
“Robert, Mark, and Tim are on the frontlines keeping our families safe, and I am grateful to have their trust and support in my campaign for Congress.
In Congress, I will always stand up in support of law enforcement in the 2nd District, starting with securing the southern border and putting an end to the human, drug, and labor trafficking that is currently running rampant due to Biden’s failed policies.”
