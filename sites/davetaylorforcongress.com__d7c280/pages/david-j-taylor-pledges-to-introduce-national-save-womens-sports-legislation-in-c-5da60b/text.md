David J.
Taylor pledges to Introduce National Save Women’s Sports Legislation in Congress
Tuesday, January 23, 2024
AMELIA – America First conservative and outsider businessman David J.
Taylor pledges to introduce National Save Women’s Sports Legislation in Congress.
In December, David called on the Ohio House and Senate to override the Governor’s veto of House Bill 68.
He applauds the House for voting to override the veto and urges the Senate to do the same.
“Title IX is under attack by Joe Biden and the radical left.
When I am in Congress, one of the first pieces of legislation that I will introduce will focus on protecting Title IX and saving women’s sports,” said David J.
Taylor.
“We have an entire party that doesn’t know what bathroom to use and are confused about what the doctors put on their birth certificate when they were born.
For a party that claims to ‘trust the science’, they certainly aren’t following their own logic when it comes to gender,” continued David J.
Taylor.
“It is time that we take a stand against Joe Biden and the radical left to ensure that only biological women can participate in women’s sports.”
