America First Conservative and Outsider Businessman David J.
Taylor Declares Victory
Tuesday, March 19, 2024
AMELIA – America First Conservative and Outsider Businessman David J.
Taylor Declares Victory in the March 19th Primary for Ohio’s 2nd Congressional District.
“I am honored by the trust the voters have placed in me today,” said David J.
Taylor.
“I am exceptionally grateful to my family, friends, and our countless volunteers that made tonight’s victory possible.
I am looking forward to continuing to reach out to the voters in the 2nd District.
Onward to Victory in November!”
