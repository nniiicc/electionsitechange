America First Conservative and Outsider Businessman David J.
Taylor Releases First Ad on Broadcast Television
Wednesday, February 7, 2024
AMELIA – America First conservative and outsider businessman David J.
Taylor announced Wednesday he placed an ad on broadcast television, starting today in the 2nd District.
The ad will air in the Cincinnati and Charleston-Huntington markets.
Taylor’s ad, Agenda, speaks to his commitment to put America First and finish President Trump’s wall.
Since day one, David Taylor has been leading the way and today’s announcement further solidifies his frontrunner status in Ohio’s 2nd Congressional District.
“My announcement today symbolizes the strength of our America First campaign in Ohio’s 2nd Congressional District,” said David J.
Taylor.
“I am running for Congress to put America First, close the border, stop inflation, defend our Second Amendment, and protect girls’ sports.
In Congress, I will help President Trump crush the cartels and finish the wall, even if I have to take my trucks to the border and pour the concrete myself.”
