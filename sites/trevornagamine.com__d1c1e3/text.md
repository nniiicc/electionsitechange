I also believe the State should be investing in tranportation modes other than cars, and should be building infrastructure that makes it easier for people to walk, bike, or use public transit.
About Me I am a lifelong Mililani resident (MHS c/o 2007) who has also served on the Mililani Neighborhood Board since 2014.
I am also a working adult who lives with his parents because that is the only way I can afford to live in Hawaii.
I want to change things so that people of my generation are not forced to choose between living here and owning a home.
I have been a TheBus rider since I was in middle school, and I still regularly use TheBus and Skyline to travel into town.
