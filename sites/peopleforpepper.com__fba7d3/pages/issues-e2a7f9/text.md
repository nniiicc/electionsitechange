Issues
Pepper Snyder is running for State Representative to Make Indiana Affordable Again—because everyday life shouldn’t feel like a constant struggle for working Hoosiers.
From the moment families get up in the morning to the time they go to bed, costs keep rising while the people in charge keep looking the other way.
Pepper believes Hoosiers have been forgotten.
It’s not sweet to ignore working families, seniors, and young parents while giving break after break to the wealthiest Hoosiers and well-connected insiders.
Indiana should work for the people who live here, work here, and raise their families here.
PUT PUBLIC SCHOOLS FIRST
Protect public school funding instead of continuing to expand taxpayer-funded private-school vouchers for the wealthiest Hoosiers.
CUT YOUR UTILITY BILLS
Eliminate the 7% sales tax on residential utility bills and fight excessive rate hikes.
If billion-dollar data centers get the break, Hoosier families should too.
PROTECT RURAL HEALTH CARE
Fight to keep hospitals, maternity care, pediatric care and other essential health services available close to home.
CHILD CARE FOR WORKING FAMILIES
Support a child care tax credit for working Hoosier families and fully fund CCDF vouchers.
PROTECT OUR HOMES, FARMS & NATURAL RESOURCES
Make billion-dollar data centers pay their fair share, protect local control, and make sure massive developments don’t pollute our land, water, or air or leave Hoosier families paying the price.
FULL LEGALIZATION OF CANNABIS
Hoosiers regularly spend millions in tax revenue to other states.
A regulated, adult-only cannabis market can generate revenue, create businesses and jobs, and allow Indiana to decide where those tax dollars can do the most good, here in Indiana.
That tax revenue helps to fund schools, roads, mental health services, and even provide property tax relief.
It’s well past time to keep that tax revenue home instead of helping to fund things that Hoosiers need, in other states.
