Pepper Snyder gets Veterans for Indiana Endorsement
Huntington, IN - Pepper Snyder, candidate for Indiana State Representative, District 50, today announced the endorsement of Veterans for Indiana, a statewide organization dedicated to supporting veterans and advancing policies that honor their service.
As the wife of a veteran, Snyder brings a firsthand understanding of military life that few candidates can offer.
She understands what is required of families who serve alongside their loved ones in uniform.
"Military spouses and families carry a weight that often goes unseen," said Snyder.
"We hold down the home front, raise our children, and support our veterans.
When elected, I will fight not only for the veterans who served our country, but for the spouses and families who served right alongside them."
Snyder has pledged to prioritize state policies that support military families, including expanded access to mental health resources, employment support for veterans and military spouses, and stronger benefits for caregivers of veterans.
"This endorsement means so much to me," Snyder added.
"It's a recognition that the sacrifices of military families matter, and that their voices deserve a seat at the table."
