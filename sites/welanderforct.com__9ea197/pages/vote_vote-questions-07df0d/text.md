QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
Q: When do polls open?
A: Polls will be open from 6 AM to 8 PM on Tuesday, November 3, 2020.
Q: Why should I vote for Mary?
A: Mary Welander has seen what the people in our communities need and is ready to fight for us in Hartford.
Check out the campaign website for Mary's experience and platform!
Q: Will I need to present identification to vote?
A: If you have a government-issued photo ID, you will be asked to present it.
However, if you do not have ID, you can request to sign an affidavit to confirm your identity.
For more information, refer to the Secretary of the State's website↗.
Q: What do I do if I encounter trouble on Election Day?
A: First, you can refer to Connecticut's Voter's Bill of Rights↗.
Be sure to report problems at the polls to the campaign so we can help!
Q: Can I register to vote on Election Day?
A: Yes!
Each town has a designated location (usually Town Hall) for same-day registration.
Contact your town's Registrar of Voters to find out where to register.
Q: Can I help the campaign on Election Day?
A: Of course!
We continue to contact voters through Election Day and will need help making calls, holding signs, and driving voters to the polls.
Fill out this form to get in touch!
