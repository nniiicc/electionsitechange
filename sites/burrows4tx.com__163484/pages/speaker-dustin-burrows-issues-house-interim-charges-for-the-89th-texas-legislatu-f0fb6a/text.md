The charges build on recent legislative successes, enhance accountability, eliminate inefficiencies, ensure effective implementation of key priorities, and lay the groundwork for another productive session next year.
Speaker Burrows also announced the creation of three new select committees: the House Select Committee on Governmental Oversight , the House Select Committee on Health Care Affordability , and the House Select Committee on General Aviation .
“Following a legislative session defined by historic solutions, it is critical the Texas House remains engaged in thoughtful policymaking and oversight as our state continues to grow and advance,” said Speaker Burrows.
“These interim charges reflect a commitment to building on that progress while further empowering committee chairs to identify fraud, waste, and abuse and safeguard taxpayer dollars.
“House committees are charged with strengthening Texas’ economy and improving affordability through further tax relief, regulatory reform, and efforts to reduce insurance costs.
The House will continue prioritizing commonsense public safety measures, enhancing emergency response and coordination, and reinforcing protections against our foreign adversaries.
Members will also identify new opportunities to support Texas teachers and students, preparing Texans for success in high-demand careers.
As our state grows, we must safeguard critical resources while responsibly embracing innovation to ensure that growth benefits all Texans.
Additionally, three new select committees have been created to address issues central to government accountability, health care access, and rural economic development.
Above all, the House will remain focused on championing families, businesses, and the opportunity that drives Texas.
I am grateful to our committee chairs and all who contributed their insight to this process, and I encourage Texans to stay engaged by following the House’s work as we approach next session.” The 13-member House Select Committee on Governmental Oversight will have broad jurisdiction over matters related to the accountability and integrity of state and local governments.
Speaker Burrows appointed the following members to serve on this select committee: Representative Cody Vasut, Chair Representative Armando Walle, Vice-Chair Representative Richard Hayes Representative Brooks Landgraf Representative Mitch Little Representative AJ Louderback Representative Christian Manuel Representative Eddie Morales Representative Richard Raymond Representative Shelby Slawson Representative Carl Tepper Representative Ellen Troxclair Representative Erin Zwiener The 11-member House Select Committee on Health Care Affordability will provide a cross-jurisdictional forum to examine factors impacting health care costs and access for Texans.
Speaker Burrows appointed the following members to serve on this select committee: Representative James Frank, Chair Representative Toni Rose, Vice-Chair Representative Daniel Alders Representative Jay Dean Representative Mary E.
Gonzalez Representative Suleman Lalani Representative Shelley Luther Representative Tom Oliverson Representative Lauren Ashley Simmons Representative Denise Villalobos Representative Trey Wharton The nine-member House Select Committee on General Aviation will advance aviation infrastructure across Texas, supporting rural economic growth, improving transportation access and emergency services, and promoting aviation safety.
Speaker Burrows appointed the following members to serve on this select committee: Representative Keith Bell, Chair Representative Armando Martinez, Vice-Chair Representative Terry Canales Representative Philip Cortez Representative Pat Curry Representative Gary Gates Representative Cole Hefner Representative John McQueeney Representative Mary Ann Perez Read Speaker Burrows’ proclamations on the creation of the House Select Committee on Governmental Oversight , the House Select Committee on Health Care Affordability , and the House Select Committee on General Aviation .
