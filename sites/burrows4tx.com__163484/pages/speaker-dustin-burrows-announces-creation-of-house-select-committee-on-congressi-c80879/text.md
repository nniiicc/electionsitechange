The 21-member committee will have jurisdiction over all matters pertaining to congressional districts.
Speaker Burrows appointed the following members to serve on the House Select Committee on Congressional Redistricting: Representative Cody Vasut, Chair Representative Jon Rosenthal, Vice-Chair Representative Josey Garcia Representative Charlie Geren Representative Barbara Gervin-Hawkins Representative R.D.
