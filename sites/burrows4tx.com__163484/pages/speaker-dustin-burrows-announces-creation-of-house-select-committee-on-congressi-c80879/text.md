AUSTIN, Texas — Speaker of the Texas House Dustin Burrows today announced the creation of the House Select Committee on Congressional Redistricting.
The 21-member committee will have jurisdiction over all matters pertaining to congressional districts.
Speaker Burrows appointed the following members to serve on the House Select Committee on Congressional Redistricting:
- Representative Cody Vasut, Chair
- Representative Jon Rosenthal, Vice-Chair
- Representative Josey Garcia
- Representative Charlie Geren
