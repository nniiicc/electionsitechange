Serving the people of House District 83
From agriculture and energy to higher education, healthcare and national security innovation — HD 83 represents the independent spirit, ingenuity and values West Texas is known for.
Anchored by Lubbock, defined by its communities
For the Texans fortunate enough to call HD 83 home, Speaker Burrows and his office are committed to serving the people and communities that make this part of the state great.
Whether assisting with a state agency, helping constituents navigate government services, arranging a Capitol tour, or requesting a flag flown over the Texas Capitol — we are here to help.
