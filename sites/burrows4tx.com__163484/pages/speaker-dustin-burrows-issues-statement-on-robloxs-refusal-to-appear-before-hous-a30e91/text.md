Instead, Roblox refused to appear publicly before the committee and answer its questions.
That refusal speaks volumes.
A company that profits from the attention of millions of young users has a responsibility to confront serious questions about their safety, not evade them.
“Roblox may have chosen to hide from accountability today, but it cannot hide from the consequences of its continued negligence.
The Texas House will continue to demand the accountability and action necessary to protect our children.” ### ### JOIN OUR EMAIL LIST First Name Last Name Email Sign Up By providing my mobile number I consent to receive informational text messages from Dustin Burrows Campaign.
