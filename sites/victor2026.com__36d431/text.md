VICTOR MARX
Victor Marx is a proven leader, devoted husband, father, and community servant who has dedicated his life to protecting families and strengthening communities.
From overcoming personal adversity to founding organizations that bring hope and healing, Victor’s journey reflects the resilience and grit that define Colorado.
With decades of experience working on the front lines—supporting veterans, rescuing the vulnerable, and building programs that change lives—Victor understands the challenges hardworking Coloradans face.
His leadership has always been guided by integrity, accountability, and an unwavering belief that government should serve the people, not special interests.
As a candidate for Governor, Victor Marx is committed to restoring trust in public office, defending Colorado values, and building a future where families can thrive.
His vision is simple: a safer, stronger, and more united Colorado—where opportunity, freedom, and community come first.
