Updated: Oct 11, 2025

As a United States Marine Corps veteran, high-risk missionary, and founder of All Things Possible Ministries, Victor brings a lifetime of courageous leadership to this critical race.
His message is clear: It is time to restore faith, protect families, and reclaim freedom for every Coloradan.

A Leader Forged in Service and Sacrifice
Victor's journey is one of unwavering dedication.
A black belt martial artist and survivor of profound personal trials, he has spent over two decades rescuing orphans from war zones in Iraq, Syria, and North Africa.
