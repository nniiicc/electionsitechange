VICTOR MARX SHATTERS FUNDRAISING MILESTONE RAISING OVER $2 MILLION FROM ALL 64 COLORADO COUNTIES
Broad grassroots support reflects the coalition needed
to win in November
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
April 17, 2026
(COLORADO SPRINGS, CO) — The Victor Marx campaign today announced a significant milestone: more than $2 million raised in contributions since October 1, 2025, with donors from every single one of Colorado's 64 counties.
This milestone is not just a fundraising number.
It reflects where this campaign stands with everyday Coloradans.
With an average donation of $107.42 and a median gift of $52, the numbers tell the story of a movement powered by working Coloradans across the state.
This strong support, from ranchers on the Western Slope to families on the Front Range to communities across rural Colorado, points to something real: people from very different walks of life choosing to invest in this campaign.
"This is Colorado speaking," said Victor Marx.
"From Colorado Springs to Grand Junction, from Pueblo to Fort Collins, people are investing in a future built on secure communities, common sense leadership, government accountability, strong infrastructure, and a return to affordability.
I am humbled and energized by every single one of these contributions."
This fundraising milestone comes on the heels of meaningful campaign momentum, including more than 28,000 petition signatures gathered entirely by volunteers and a strong finish at the Colorado GOP Assembly.
Since October 1, 2025, the campaign has been powered by grassroots donors, not dependent on a handful of large contributors.
The data tells the story:
· Average donation: $107.42 with a median gift of just $52, showing participation at every income level
· $27,562 in recurring monthly donations from supporters committed for the long haul
· 7,883 in-state donors representing 75.3% of all contributions, a genuinely Colorado-funded campaign
· $103,000 in merchandise sales.
