A PERSONAL MESSAGE FROM VICTOR…
Well, here we are—just over the first 90 days of the campaign—and I want to give you a quick update on where things stand.
Honestly, we are far beyond what we ever thought we’d accomplish this early.
The response from the people of Colorado—and from those who love this state—has been motivating and deeply encouraging.
From day one, we committed to not running the same tired Republican or political playbook that has a long history of losing.
Instead, we set a disciplined focus: unite the party, energize people, reach independents, and raise the resources needed to win.
And it’s working.
I’ve said this from the beginning, and I’ll keep saying it: no matter how many events candidates hold,
matter how much talking or debating goes on, there’s one question that has to rise above everything else—can this person actually win the general election?
That’s what matters.
If someone can’t win the general, then why are they running?
What’s the benefit to Colorado?
We’ve already seen two candidates step out—one moving into a U.S.
Senate race, another leaving the Republican Party to run as an independent.
My hope is that others will take an honest look at the race and, if they truly love Colorado, rally behind the candidate with the strongest path to victory.
To all of you who’ve supported us—thank you.
God is in control.
Prayers are working.
We’re updating the website regularly, building momentum, and we’re just getting started.
— Victor

DEMOCRAT’S BURN THROUGH REFUNDS
Colorado's Democrat-led legislature has squandered a staggering $3.8 billion surplus in just three years, leaving hardworking families without their rightful TABOR refunds.
This isn’t just a fiscal miscalculation; it’s a betrayal of the trust placed in our government.
The Taxpayer Bill of Rights (TABOR) is a vital constitutional safeguard against excessive taxation, yet under Governor Polis, it has been ignored, resulting in less money for families and the promotion of policies that fail to serve the people.
As your future governor, I will prioritize families with common-sense fiscal responsibility.
Our state urgently needs a leader who respects taxpayer dollars.
Medicaid costs are consuming one-third of the General Fund and unchecked government growth continues to threaten our wallets.
I will work tirelessly to restore fiscal balance, deliver real relief, and protect our constitutional rights.
A government that grows unchecked only leads to shrinking bank accounts for Colorado's hardworking citizens.
