THOUGHTS FROM EILEEN MARX…
My first visit to the Colorado State Capitol impacted me more than I expected.
What moved me most was seeing the state seal throughout the building, even on the silver doorknobs, accompanied by the Latin words Nil Sine Numine: “Nothing without Providence.”
It served as a powerful reminder that those who came before us understood this place was meant to stand under God’s authority, not apart from it.
Standing there, I felt it clearly, what was once consecrated can be consecrated again.
We were also told that some members of the House had been smoking pot on the balcony during session with the smoke drifted into the chamber.
Hearing that was deeply sobering.
Such behavior shows a complete lack of respect for the position they hold and for the place itself.
This Capitol represents the people of Colorado and should be a sober space, marked by honor, humility, and accountability.
As I stood there, I could envision Victor in this place — leading with reverence, restoring dignity, and setting a tone from the top that honors God and respects the people.
I believe this with all my heart: God honors those who honor Him.
I’m hopeful because I believe restoration is coming to Colorado.
— Eileen

SUPPORTING COLORADO'S LAW ENFORCEMENT
In Colorado, our law enforcement officers, men and women dedicated to their mission, deserve our unwavering support.
They are entrusted with upholding the laws that we, as a society, have established through our democratic process.
This includes not only our local police but also the ICE agents who enforce our immigration laws.
These brave individuals are not simply performing a job; they are safeguarding our communities and ensuring justice.
We must recognize their sacrifices and the challenges they face daily, recently under intense scrutiny and opposition.
The tragic stories of Coloradans like Melissa Powell and her son, Karen Diamond, Jasmine Carpio, and Katelyn Weaver remind us of the high stakes involved.
Their lives were taken by individuals who should not have been in our country, and their memories must be honored.
It is our duty to make sure that we stand with those who uphold the laws of our nation and protect our way of life.
We must reject harmful rhetoric that vilifies our law enforcement and instead celebrate their courage.
God bless those who bravely keep us safe!
