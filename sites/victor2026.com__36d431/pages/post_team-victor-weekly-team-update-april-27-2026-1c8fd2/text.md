TEAM VICTOR WEEKLY TEAM UPDATE April 27, 2026
This week I had a great time at Sportsman's Day at the State Capitol, wild game cookout, great people, and a real conversation about what matters to hunters, anglers, and families across this state.
I was there because this fight is personal.
Colorado’s sportsmen aren’t weekend hobbyists; you’re the ones actually keeping wildlife healthy and on the landscape.
The licenses and tags you buy keep Colorado Parks and Wildlife running.
Take you out of the picture, and the conservation system collapses.
I met up with John Howard, former Chairman of the CPW Commission and we agreed, Colorado needs wildlife decisions driven by real science and a commission that answers to everyday Coloradans, not to urban special-interest groups.
Right now, not a single big game hunter sits on that commission.
That changes when I'm governor.
The governor's office is a CEO position.
Here's what a Victor Marx administration delivers for sportsmen:
· Reform the CPW Commission: appoint qualified people with science-based wildlife management backgrounds who will stand up and fight for hunters and ranchers
· Place an immediate moratorium on wolf imports: until a full, transparent impact assessment puts ranchers and hunters at the table not just activists
· Restore stream and land access: for every working Coloradan, not just wealthy private leaseholders
Colorado's wildlife belongs to all of us.
Together, we're going to get our state back.

COLORADO IS PRICING OUT THE PEOPLE WHO BUILT IT
Colorado is becoming unaffordable for the very people who make this state great.
Families can't keep up with rising housing costs.
Energy bills are crushing small businesses.
Seniors and farmers are being taxed off land they've owned for decades.
This didn't happen by accident, it happened because of bad policy, reckless spending, and a government that treats you like an ATM instead of the people it's supposed to serve.
I'm done watching that happen.
When I'm governor, I'm coming after this affordability crisis directly and aggressively.
· Housing: I'll cut the red tape and zoning restrictions that make it slow and expensive to build in Colorado.
When it's easier to build, it gets cheaper to buy and more Colorado families can actually afford a home.
· Energy: I'll end the ideologically driven mandates that have driven up your electricity and heating bills.
Colorado deserves a common-sense, “all-of-the-above” energy strategy.
One that keeps the lights on, the grid reliable, and your monthly bills manageable.
· Taxes and Spending: I'll fight for real property tax relief… for homeowners, seniors, farmers, and small business owners who are being taxed off land they've worked their whole lives to keep.
I will oppose any new taxes or fee hikes that haven't been approved by voters.
And I will demand a full audit of state spending to cut the waste and put your tax dollars to work for Coloradans… not for government bureaucracies.
· Pushing Back on Washington: I'll also stand up against the federal regulations making it harder and more expensive to do business here, protecting our ranchers, manufacturers, and small business owners from Washington overreach.
Prosperity isn't built in a statehouse.
It's built by you, the people of Colorado.
My job as governor is to make that possible, not harder.
That's a promise.
