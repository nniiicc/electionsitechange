CAPITOL VISIT AS A CANDIDATE Walking through the Colorado State Capitol this week felt like stepping into a living history book.
If you haven't been there, you really should check it out.
Get involved, testify on bills that matter to you, talk to your legislators, and come meet your governor.
Every corner of the Capitol is filled with Colorado stories.
I particularly enjoyed exploring the rows of portraits of our former governors from our states early mining and ranching days.
It was inspiring to see myself in those leaders who came before me, each bearing the responsibility of guiding our great state.
Being there fueled my passion for pushing for common-sense legislation that truly benefits all Coloradans.
Eileen and I took our time to appreciate everything, from the stunning architecture to the faces of those who paved the way.
I now feel an even stronger sense of urgency.
Colorado needs leaders who genuinely care about the people and are ready to stand up against the divisive politics creeping in.
With each step, my determination to win Colorado back for its residents grew stronger.
Our fight for a brighter future is just beginning!

THE POWER OF PRAYER As I journey across our beautiful state of Colorado, I am consistently reminded of the profound role that faith plays in our lives.
My family, campaign staff, and I draw strength from our deep faith, as do so many of you.
As your governor, I will never shy away from discussing faith and God.
Our nation was founded on the principles of belief and trust in a higher power, and I believe that recognizing this truth is essential for our future.
We need to embrace open conversations about faith, as it is the very foundation upon which our great country stands.
In times of uncertainty, I find comfort in the words of Matthew 18:20: “For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them.” As we campaign across Colorado, we are relying on prayer for strength and guidance, inviting God into our journey.
