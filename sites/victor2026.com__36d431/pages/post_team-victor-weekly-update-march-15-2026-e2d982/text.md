Almost every week now, someone approaches me claiming they can “help me win.” The conversations usually sound similar promises of money, influence, or connections but they come with expectations.
Sometimes it’s influence.
Sometimes it’s access.
Sometimes it’s positions of authority in a future administration.
That’s how the system often works.
Deals are made long before the voters ever cast a ballot.
Promises are traded for power, and once someone takes those deals, they become beholden to the individuals or special interests who funded or brokered their path.
I’ve been very clear with every one of them.
I don’t need your help to win.
The people of Colorado will decide this race, not political brokers, not special interest groups, and not individuals looking to secure power inside a future administration.
If the voters choose me, it will be because they believe in the mission: restoring integrity to government, protecting children, defending families, supporting our farmers and ranchers, and standing up for the values that made this state strong.
Running this way makes me an outsider in politics.
And to be honest, that’s exactly where I intend to stay.
I’m not running to build a political machine.
I’m running to serve the people of Colorado and to honor God in the process.

WHAT’S YOUR REAL NAME “Is Vaughn Kennedy your real name?” That was the first tough question Nick Rodgers asked me during an upcoming episode of the Big Timber Lodge podcast .
I’ve heard that question before, but I want to take a moment to answer it again and set the record straight.
For years, I lived with a last name that carried both darkness and evil — the name of my adopted stepfather, the man’s last name was Kennedy — a pedophile.
He did awful and unspeakable things.
I forgive.
I don’t forget.
But as terrible as the things he did to me were, I couldn’t let that legacy be attached to the woman I’d marry or the children I’d one day hold.
When Christ got ahold of my heart, everything changed.
I reunited with my real father, and through forgiveness and redemption, I took back the name I was born with — VICTOR MARX.
My birth name represents freedom, restoration, and the life God gave me — not the pain one twisted evil man inflicted.
My children will never bear the weight of someone else’s sin.
They bear a name redeemed by grace.
