We strive to provide an accessible online experience that enables everyone to engage with our campaign information, resources, and activities on Victor2026.com.
Compliance Standards This website is designed and maintained to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, as recommended by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).
These guidelines address a broad range of accessibility considerations, including but not limited to perceivable content, operable navigation, understandable information, and robust compatibility with assistive technologies.
In addition, our accessibility efforts are informed by: - **ADA Title III**: Ensuring that our digital services, as a place of public accommodation, are accessible to individuals with disabilities. - **Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act**: Incorporating federal standards for electronic and information technology to promote usability for people with disabilities. - **FEC Guidance**: Adhering to best practices for federal election-related communications, including the Federal Election Commission's commitment to accessible websites and data.
We draw from established practices observed in top-tier political campaigns and federal agencies, such as those emphasizing ongoing audits, user testing, and inclusive design to foster broader civic engagement.
Ongoing Accessibility Efforts Victor Marx for Governor recognizes that accessibility is an iterative process.
We conduct regular evaluations of Victor2026.com using automated tools, manual testing, and feedback from users with disabilities to identify and remediate potential barriers.
Our team works diligently to implement updates and enhancements as needed, including compatibility with screen readers, keyboard navigation, alternative text for images, and sufficient color contrast.
Despite these efforts, some content—such as third-party plugins, user-generated submissions, or legacy materials—may not fully meet our standards.
We are actively addressing these areas and appreciate your patience as we continue to improve.
Feedback and Assistance We welcome your input to help us enhance accessibility.
If you encounter any barriers or require accommodations to access our content, please contact us at: - Email: accessibility@victor2026.com - Phone: (303) 123-4567 (available during business hours, Mountain Time) - Mailing Address: Victor Marx for Governor Campaign, [Campaign Address], Denver, CO 80202 Please provide details about the issue, including the web page URL, your assistive technology (if applicable), and how we can assist you.
We aim to respond to accessibility-related inquiries within five business days and will work to provide alternative formats or resolutions where feasible.
Third-Party Content Links to external websites or resources are provided for informational purposes.
Victor Marx for Governor does not control the accessibility of third-party sites and encourages users to review their respective accessibility statements.
Effective Date and Updates This Accessibility Statement was last updated on November 22, 2025.
We review and revise it periodically to reflect changes in technology, standards, or our practices.
For questions about this statement or our accessibility initiatives, please use the contact information provided above.
Victor Marx for Governor is dedicated to making our campaign accessible to all Coloradans, ensuring that every voice can be heard in shaping the future of our state.
