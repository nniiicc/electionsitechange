VICTOR MARX LAUNCHES SIX CITY “COLORADO RESCUE TOUR”
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
February 6, 2026
(Colorado Springs, Colorado) – Victor Marx, the Republican candidate for Colorado governor, is excited to announce the kick-off of his Colorado “Rescue Tour,” set to begin on February 13th in Golden, Colorado.
This tour will span multiple cities, including Greeley, Westminster, Denver, Pueblo, and Thornton, with additional dates to be announced soon.
Since declaring his candidacy, Victor Marx has emerged as the clear front-runner in the race for the Republican nomination for governor.
He has significantly outpaced his competitors, raising over a million dollars through small donations averaging $150 from supporters in all 64 Colorado counties.
This remarkable grassroots support underscores the growing momentum behind Victor's campaign.
“I’m really excited to get out and meet all the people who have been writing our campaign, asking us to come to their communities,” said Victor Marx.
“You know, there are so many Coloradans who are hurting — feeling pushed out, shut out, or simply knocked out by a system that seems to no longer care about them or their families.
I’m telling you, the time for change is long overdue.”
Victor Marx has already hosted more than 75 rallies and events within just 150 days, often drawing standing room only crowds, which highlights his dedication to engaging with the community.
When asked about the name of his Colorado “Rescue Tour,” Victor passionately said, “Have you stepped into a grocery store recently?
Have you tried to feed a family on minimum wage?
Have you spoken to a senior struggling to make ends meet on a fixed budget while juggling prescription costs?” He emphasized, “The reality is, Colorado isn’t just in need of rescue; it’s crying out for restoration!
Our communities are hurting, and it’s time we take action to lift them up.”
Alongside Victor Marx, attendees can look forward to special guests, surprise speakers, exclusive merchandise, and a chance to sign a petition supporting Victor's candidacy for the ballot.
Join Victor Marx on his journey to restore Colorado’s common sense values and priorities.
For more information about the Colorado “Rescue Tour” and to stay updated on upcoming events, please visit Victor2026.com.
