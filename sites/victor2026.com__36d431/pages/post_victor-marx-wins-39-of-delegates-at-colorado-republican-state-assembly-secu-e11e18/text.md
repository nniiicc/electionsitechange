Colorado Republicans Unite Behind Marx to Take Back Colorado.
DENVER, CO — In a commanding display of Republican unity, Victor Marx won 39% of delegates at the Colorado Republican State Assembly, earning ballot position for the governor's race by a decisive margin.
Tonight’s result is one of the strongest showings in recent Colorado Republican primary history and sends an unmistakable signal: THE PARTY'S GRASSROOTS BASE HAS FOUND ITS CANDIDATE.
Having already demonstrated extraordinary grassroots strength, with a 100% volunteer effort that submitted 28,541 signatures to the Colorado Secretary of State, Marx chose the hybrid path, competing at the State Assembly while simultaneously qualifying through petition.
The dual approach reflects the depth and reach of a campaign that is firing on all cylinders from the ground up.
"What happened at that assembly wasn't politics as usual," Marx said.
"It was a room full of patriots saying enough is enough.
We are taking back our economy, taking back our schools, taking back our communities and we are taking back Colorado." Combined with nearly $# million raised since entering the race, Marx enters the next phase of the campaign with both the financial firepower and grassroots credibility to go the distance.
"I've never backed down from a hard mission in my life," Marx said.
"Colorado is the mission.
And we will not stop until it is won." This weekend’s delegate victory reflects months of intensive grassroots organizing, coalition building, and a campaign message "Take Back Colorado" that has resonated deeply with Republican voters frustrated by years of Democratic governance in Denver.
From rural ranchers on the Eastern Plains to small business owners on the Front Range, Marx has assembled a coalition united by a shared belief that Colorado has been taken in the wrong direction and that it's time to reclaim it.
Marx now turns his full attention to the June primary and ultimately to replacing the failed leadership in the Governor's mansion.
ABOUT VICTOR MARX Victor Marx is a U.S.
Marine Corps veteran, founder of All Things Possible Ministries, author, and candidate for Governor of Colorado.
His campaign is centered on restoring economic freedom, protecting constitutional rights, supporting law enforcement, and fighting for rural Colorado.
