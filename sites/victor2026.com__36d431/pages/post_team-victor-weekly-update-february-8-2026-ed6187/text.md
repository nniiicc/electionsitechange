LET’S TALK ABOUT INSANE PROPERTY TAXES Colorado homeowners are facing a potential 40% increase in property taxes, leaving many to wonder how it became so expensive.
So, what happened?
Back in 2022, lawmakers passed Senate Bill 22-238 , which reduced property tax assessment rates by $15,000 for 2023 and 2024.
The state covered most of the reduced payments for counties and special districts, including essential services and K-12 education.
In 2024, lawmakers aimed to tackle the “property tax wars” with Senate Bill 24-233 , increasing the valuation discount to $55,000.
However, Democrats, along with Sen.
Barb Kirkmeyer, introduced “short-term fix” bills that expired on January 1st for 2025 assessments, which have now caused a sharp rise in property tax bills.
This jump is forcing many Coloradans to reconsider if Colorado is just too expensive to live here any longer.
The reality is, government isn't the solution; it often becomes a burden.
The supposed relief vanished, leading to higher bills and eroded trust in government.
Colorado has stopped listening to its residents.
We need to limit government size and focus on what truly matters: community safety and allowing hardworking Coloradans to live without unnecessary oversight.
If we don’t act now, more families may leave for greener pastures.
Colorado should be a place where families thrive – not fight to survive!
