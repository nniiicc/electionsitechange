VICTOR MARX TO BE FEATURED SPEAKER AT INTERNATIONAL FAITH SUMMIT DURING CPAC USA 2026
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
March 14, 2026
(Colorado Springs, CO) — The Victor Marx for Governor campaign is honored to announce that Republican candidate Victor Marx has accepted an invitation to be a featured speaker at the International Faith Summit during CPAC USA 2026, taking place Wednesday, March 25th, at the Gaylord Texan Resort & Convention Center in Grapevine, Texas.
This prestigious invitation to one of the largest annual gatherings of conservative leaders, officials, and activists is more than an acknowledgment — it’s a testament to the growing excitement and confidence surrounding Victor Marx’s candidacy and his vision to lead Colorado forward.
“I’m deeply honored to be invited to speak on such a distinguished platform at CPAC,” said Marx.
“It reflects not only the growing recognition of our movement in Colorado but also the power of faith to shape the future of our nation.”
At the International Faith Summit, Victor Marx will deliver an inspiring address outlining his common-sense plan to restore fiscal responsibility, ensure safer communities, invest in strong infrastructure, and renew integrity and trust in public service.
His message will connect these priorities to a higher calling — one rooted in service, responsibility, and faith.
“The year 2026 stands as a pivotal moment for freedom-loving nations,” Marx said.
“The historic momentum of 2024 must not fade — our progress must be protected, our unity strengthened, and our resolve deepened.
In his speech, Marx will emphasize that the path forward requires unity and renewed faith, both in God and in one another.
“It is time to bring courage and compassion back into leadership,” Marx added.
“We will build a Colorado that stands tall as a beacon of opportunity, security, and moral strength.
With faith as our foundation, nothing is impossible.”
Marx expressed how encouraged and proud he is to represent Colorado on the CPAC stage, noting that this invitation underscores the confidence leaders across the nation have placed in his vision for the state’s future.
The Victor Marx for Governor campaign will be available for press interviews before and during the CPAC summit.
