STANDING WITH LAW ENFORCEMENT Denver’s recent move to impede federal immigration enforcement is a troubling step away from the rule of law.
This new directive requires Denver police officers to intervene with and even detain ICE agents if they PERCEIVE any "excessive force." This approach not only obstructs federal law enforcement but also undermines the safety of our communities.
This move is a classic case of misunderstanding both federal law and the Supremacy Clause.
It’s a slippery slope Denver is sliding down, one that continues to breed lawlessness and puts our citizens and businesses in harm's way.
It should be clear – no local official has the right to stop ICE from enforcing federal law on public property when they are protecting our communities.
I stand firmly with our local, state, and federal law enforcement officers, as do the vast majority of law-abiding Coloradans.
These dedicated individuals put their lives on the line every day to keep our neighborhoods safe, and we cannot allow political agendas to interfere with their crucial work.
Instead of hindering federal agents who are tasked with enforcing our laws, we should be supporting them in their efforts to maintain order and protect our families.
It's time for Colorado to reaffirm its commitment to the rule of law.
We must prioritize the safety and well-being of our citizens and ensure that our laws are respected and upheld.
The safety of our communities should always come first, and that means standing together with our law enforcement officers against any efforts that seek to undermine their authority.
Our citizens safety must always come before political posturing.

WE’RE GONNA DOGE MEDICAID The ongoing issues within Colorado’s Medicaid program are a glaring example of what happens when government spending spirals out of control.
This unchecked growth is not just a statistic; it has real consequences for the people of Colorado.
An ever-expanding Medicaid program is a primary driver of this unsustainable trajectory.
As your next governor, I will take decisive action to tackle these challenges head-on.
First, we must implement a rigorous audit process to eliminate wasteful spending.
Independent auditors will shine a light on every corner of our Medicaid program, ensuring that every dollar is accounted for and that no fraudulent activities slip through the cracks.
Next, I will establish a dedicated task force focused specifically on tackling Medicaid fraud.
This team will investigate anomalies, conduct regular reviews, and hold accountable those who misuse taxpayer dollars.
We need to send a clear message: waste will not be tolerated in our state.
Finally, transparency is essential.
We must foster a culture of openness in government, making budgetary processes accessible to all Coloradans.
When citizens can see where their money goes, they can help us ensure it’s spent wisely.
Together, we will restore integrity to Colorado’s Medicaid program, prioritize the needs of our citizens, and put an end to wasteful practices that jeopardize our state’s future.
