FROM EILEEN: THE HIDDEN COST OF REACTING WITHOUT THINKING
In today’s fast-moving world, especially on social media, it’s become normal to react before we take time to think.
Opinions form quickly, often from just one perspective, and get shared just as fast.
Before long, it turns into a kind of chain reaction.
One voice sparks another, and soon the noise is louder than the truth.
But real thinking requires something we don’t practice enough anymore.
It requires pause.
There’s a powerful reminder in Scripture: “The first to present his case seems right, until another comes forward and questions him.” (Proverbs 18:17)
It’s a simple truth.
The first thing we hear can sound convincing, but that doesn’t mean it’s complete.
When we only hear one side, we risk forming opinions that are shallow or even wrong.
Critical thinking isn’t about being negative or doubting everything.
It’s about being willing to slow down and ask honest questions.
Is this actually true?
What might be missing here?
Why am I reacting the way I am?
Getting back to this kind of thinking starts with each of us.
It means resisting the urge to immediately agree, repost, or react.
It means allowing space to listen, to question, and to understand before forming a conclusion.
We live in a culture that rewards quick responses, but there is real value in slowing down.
When we choose to think before we react, we bring clarity not just to ourselves, but to the conversations around us.
Otherwise, we’re just adding to the noise.
-- Eileen
