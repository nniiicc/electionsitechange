Seawright is a lifelong advocate for women’s rights and is a champion for education at all levels, senior citizen, human rights, and LGBTQ+ rights, affordable housing, environmental protection, public health, election reform, criminal justice reform, and government transparency.
Along with her colleagues, she achieved the strongest rent reforms in New York State’s history, making rent stabilization and rent control permanent.
She is a fighter against discrimination, intolerance and violence.
She obtained passage for a new law providing for insurance coverage for women and men who need 3-D mammograms for breast cancer detection.
She introduced and passed an Equal Rights Amendment to the New York State Constitution.
She has secured $12 million of funding for her district’s public schools, senior centers and parks.
