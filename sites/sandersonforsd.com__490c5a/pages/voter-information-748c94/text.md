Communities within District 25 include Baltic, Colman, Crooks, Dell Rapids, Egan, Flandreau, Garretson, Humboldt, and Trent.
For more details on voter registration, visit the SD Voter Information Portal.
SD Voter Information Portal The SD Secretary of State provides the SD Voter Information Portal, a valuable resource for residents to check their voter registration information and find their polling place location, including those in District 25 voting.
If you have any questions about voting in South Dakota, be sure to visit the SD Secretary of State website.
Voter Information Portal Voter Registration You can register to vote for District 25 voting by filling out a Voter Registration Form and submitting it through the SD Voter Information Portal or to your County Auditor.
