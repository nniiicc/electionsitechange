A longtime public servant, community leader, and advocate for public safety and fiscal responsibility, Helvy enters the race with over two decades of experience in criminal justice, investigations, compliance, education, and community engagement.
His campaign centers on restoring trust in government, strengthening economic opportunity, and ensuring that state leadership reflects the values of District 38.
“District 38 deserves leadership that is present, prepared, and principled,” Helvy said.
“Our families are facing rising costs, public safety concerns, education challenges, and growing infrastructure demands.
It is time for disciplined leadership that prioritizes taxpayers, protects our communities, and delivers measurable results.” Helvy emphasized that his campaign will focus on direct voter engagement, neighborhood forums, small business roundtables, and grassroots outreach across District 38.
“This campaign is about service, not slogans,” Helvy added.
“We will listen.
We will lead with integrity.
And we will put District 38 first.” Eric Helvy invites residents, volunteers, and supporters to join the movement for accountable leadership and common-sense solutions.
