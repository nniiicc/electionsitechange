Meet Eric
Eric J.
Helvy, MS, MPA, Ph.D.
(ABD) is a father, husband, an educator, public safety advocate, and community leader based in Atlanta, Georgia.
With more than two decades of experience in education, criminal justice, and community development, Eric has dedicated his career to strengthening communities through leadership, mentorship, and service.
His work focuses on expanding educational opportunities, promoting workforce readiness, and supporting initiatives that help individuals and families achieve long-term stability and success.
Eric holds advanced degrees in criminology, criminal justice, and public administration and is completing doctoral studies focused on homeland security and public safety systems.
His academic and professional experience has shaped his commitment to practical solutions that address community challenges while promoting accountability and responsible governance.
Eric is particularly focused on issues that impact everyday families, including educational advancement, election integrity, healthcare access, and public safety.
He believes that strong educational pathways prepare individuals for meaningful careers, while transparent and secure election systems help maintain public trust in democratic institutions.
He also supports policies that strengthen community-based healthcare solutions and emphasizes proactive approaches to crime prevention, emergency preparedness, and neighborhood safety.
Through civic engagement, mentorship, and public service, Eric continues to advocate for initiatives that promote opportunity, strengthen families, and create safer, healthier communities.
His leadership reflects a belief that informed citizens, responsible institutions, and collaborative community partnerships are essential to building a stronger future for Atlanta and the State of Georgia.
