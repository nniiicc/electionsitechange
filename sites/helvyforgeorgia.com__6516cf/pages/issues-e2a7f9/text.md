Eric Helvy’s campaign is built on three (3) core pillars:
Public Safety & Community Stability
- Strengthening partnerships between law enforcement and the community
- Supporting crime prevention initiatives and neighborhood stabilization
- Ensuring state policies empower — not undermine — local safety efforts
Tax Relief & Economic Growth
- Advocating for responsible property tax reform to protect homeowners and seniors
- Supporting long-term state income tax reduction strategies
- Promoting small business growth and entrepreneurship
School Choice & Educational Excellence
- Expanding educational options for families
- Supporting workforce development and career-readiness programs
- Ensuring literacy and foundational academic performance remain a top priority
