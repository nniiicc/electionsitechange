Contact your County Auditor or the South Dakota Secretary of State Clay County Auditor: 605-677-7120 Union County Auditor: 605-356-2101 South Dakota Secretary of State: 605-773-3537 Register to Vote To vote for me in the primary, you must be registered Republican at your current address in District 17. – This is particularly important if you’ve moved recently.
If you need to update your registration, you must have that done by Monday, October 19, 2026 to vote in the June 2 election.
The printable form to update your registration is available here: https://sdsos.gov/elections-voting/assets/VoterRegistrationFormFillable.pdf (You can also do this in person at the County Auditor’s Office) Early Voting In-Person At your County Auditor’s Office – During business hours on any weekday.
Clay County Auditor Clay County Courthouse 211 W Main St, Suite 200 Vermillion, SD 57069 Union County Auditor Union County Courthouse 209 E Main St #200, Elk Point, SD 57025 By-Mail Request a Ballot The printable form to request a ballot be mailed to your home is available here: https://sdsos.gov/elections-voting/assets/AbsenteeApplication_202012.PDF If you use this option, you can return the ballot in person to your County Auditor’s Office or return it by mail.
If you choose to mail it in, please allow enough time for it to be in the Auditor’s hands by the Election, November 3, 2026 .
Late ballots do not count!
