absentee ballots
The Town Clerk issues and processes Absentee Ballots for the City of Stamford.
If you cannot vote in person for the General Election on November 5, use the resources below to request an absentee ballot!
ABSENTEE BALLOT FAQs
How can I obtain an Absentee Ballot application?
- You can download the application below:
- You may write to townclerk@stamfordct.gov to request that an AB application be sent to your home.
- Call 203.977.5280 to request an AB application; please provide your name and address when you leave a message.
- Visit the Town Clerk's Office at 888 Washington Boulevard - Ground Floor, Stamford, CT 06901
Where can I return my Absentee Ballot Application and Ballot?
- You may place your documents into one of the secure drop boxes located at the following locations:
1.
Government Center Parking Garage
888 Washington Boulevard
2.
Harry Bennett Library
115 Vine Road
Mail to: Town Clerk – AB Application
P.O.
Box 891, Stamford, CT 06904-0891
What is the last date that the ballot needs to be received?
- Ballots need to be received in the Town Clerk's Office via US mail at the latest on Election Day to be counted.
- The final collection of ballots from the ballot box is at 8pm on November 5, 2024.
register to vote
Registering to vote is easy!
There are three ways to register to vote.
- Register online here.
Must have a Connecticut license to register online
- Print and fill out a completed Voter Registration Form here.
Cards are available in English and Spanish.
Once you've filled out the form, please mail it to:
Registrar of Voters
888 Washington Blvd
Stamford, CT 06901
- To have a registration card mailed to you, please call (203) 977-4011 or (203) 977-4012.
