If you cannot vote in person for the General Election on November 5 , use the resources below to request an absentee ballot! absentee ballot Resources ABSENTEE BALLOT FAQs ​ How can I obtain an Absentee Ballot application?
You can download the application below: For English applications click here ​ For Spanish applications click here You may write to townclerk@stamfordct.gov to request that an AB application be sent to your home.
Call 203.977.5280 to request an AB application; please provide your name and address when you leave a message.
Visit the Town Clerk's Office at 888 Washington Boulevard - Ground Floor, Stamford, CT 06901 Where can I return my Absentee Ballot Application and Ballot?
You may place your documents into one of the secure drop boxes located at the following locations: 1.
Government Center Parking Garage 888 Washington Boulevard 2.
Harry Bennett Library 115 Vine Road Mail to: Town Clerk – AB Application P.O.
Box 891, Stamford, CT 06904-0891 What is the last date that the ballot needs to be received?
Ballots need to be received in the Town Clerk's Office via US mail at the latest on Election Day to be counted.
The final collection of ballots from the ballot box is at 8pm on November 5, 2024 .
