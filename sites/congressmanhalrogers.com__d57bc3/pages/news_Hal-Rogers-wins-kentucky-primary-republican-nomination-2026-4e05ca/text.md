Kentucky’s 5th Congressional District Votes to Keep Hal Rogers as their Conservative Voice in Washington
“I’m honored to have the loyal confidence of Southern and Eastern Kentuckians to continue the fight for conservative values on Capitol Hill, securing their seat at the House Appropriations table, and executing President Trump’s America First agenda to create more jobs here at home and strengthen our energy independence,” said Rogers, Dean of the House.
“Our rural region is moving forward with advancements in technology, stronger infrastructure, expanded access to healthcare and education, and more collaborative investments in our tourism economy.
Together, we are building on another victory for a brighter future in Southern and Eastern Kentucky.”
