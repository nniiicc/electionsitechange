District 81 is in southeast Sedgwick County and includes parts of Derby; Oaklawn-Sunview; Wichita; and Gypsum, Riverside, and Rockford townships.
The Kansas Aviation Museum, Planeview Park, and companies such as Boeing and Spirit Defense are here, reflecting our deep aviation heritage and expertise.
Agriculture is also an essential part of our history and future, as demonstrated by the many farms and ranches in the district.
Small businesses find a welcome home in our growing community, and families continue to be attracted by our schools, amenities such as High Park, and quality healthcare facilities such as Rock Regional Hospital and Wesley Derby ER.
