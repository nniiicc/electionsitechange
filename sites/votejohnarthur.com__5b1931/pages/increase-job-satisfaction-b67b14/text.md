Protect the Rights of All Utahns
From attacking collective bargaining for public workers to fighting against fair maps and vote by mail, the Utah Legislature has continued to pass laws that disregard the rights of citizens in our state.
We need legislators who will strongly defend our constitutional rights, and I look forward my advocacy work from the floor of the House.
Under Attack:
- Access to the ballot for seniors, voters with disabilities, and working class Utahns
- Public workers and our right to collectively bargain for better wages and working conditions
- Independent Redistricting Commission and maps free from partisan gerrymandering
- The Judicial Branch and the separation of powers in our state
What We Need:
- Preservation of universal vote-by-mail
- Repeal of HB267 and greater protections in statute for public workers
- Full implementation of Proposition 4 and the reinstatement of the Independent Redistricting Commission
- Stop legislation aimed at weakening the judiciary and protect them against retaliatory threats of impeachment
