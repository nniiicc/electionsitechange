A Democratic Member of the United States House of Representatives
Zoe represents the vibrant and diverse 18th Congressional District in California.
This district includes Zoe’s home in San Jose and encompasses communities in Santa Clara, Monterey, Santa Cruz, and San Benito counties.
The cities and communities included in CA-18 include Salinas, downtown and east San Jose, Morgan Hill, Gilroy, Watsonville, San Juan Batista, Hollister, King City, Soledad, Castroville, Gonzales, Lockwood, Greenfield, and Prunedale.
