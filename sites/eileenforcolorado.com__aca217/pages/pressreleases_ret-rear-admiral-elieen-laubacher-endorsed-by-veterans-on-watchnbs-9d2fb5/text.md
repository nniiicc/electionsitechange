Ending the Second Quarter of 2026 with Over $3.5 Million Cash on Hand, the Laubacher Campaign Shows Increasing Momentum
With another dominating fundraising performance, Eileen Laubacher’s campaign demonstrates growing grassroots support
Highlands Ranch, CO – Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher’s campaign for Colorado’s 4th Congressional District ends the second quarter of 2026 showing increasing momentum.
This filing underscores the growing momentum the Laubacher campaign has garnered as voters increasingly look for new leadership focused on lowering costs, protecting healthcare, and delivering results for Colorado families.
“I'm incredibly grateful to the over one- hundred thousand supporters who have invested in this campaign,” said Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher.
“People are tired of rising costs, threats to their healthcare, and leaders who seem more interested in Washington than their own districts.
We need leaders who will lower costs, protect healthcare, strengthen our economy, and restore common-sense leadership.
I will be that leader, and I'm honored by the support I have received in pursuit of that goal.
Together, we're building the campaign that will win in November.”
The contrast in the race is clear.
Eileen Laubacher is entering the general election with $3.5 million cash on hand, while incumbent Lauren Boebert reported significantly less in her pre-primary report.
Eileen's comes from over 400,000 donations to date while Boebert continues to take money from corporations to fund her campaign.
As voters become increasingly frustrated with rising costs, attacks on healthcare, and partisan dysfunction in Washington, Eileen's message of practical leadership continues to resonate across the district.
The second-quarter report makes clear that this race is competitive, momentum is on Eileen's side, and she has the organization and support necessary to win this November.
