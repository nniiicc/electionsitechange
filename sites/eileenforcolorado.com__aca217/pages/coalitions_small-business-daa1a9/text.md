Drawing on their experience building businesses, creating jobs, and serving their communities, coalition members help ensure the voices of small business owners are heard throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow Small Business Owners through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting Small Business Owners and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
