(Ret.) Rear Admiral Elieen Laubacher Endorsed by Veterans On Watch
Highlands Ranch, CO - Today, retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher announced the endorsement of Veterans On Watch, a veteran-led organization dedicated to protecting constitutional governance, promoting civic engagement, and ensuring elected officials remain accountable to the people they serve.
“Eileen Laubacher stands for the Constitution we swore to defend and the veterans who swore to defend it.
That’s why we stand with her.” said Carla Felts, U.S.
Army Veteran, President and Co-Founder of Veterans On Watch.
The endorsement adds to growing support for Laubacher's campaign and reflects her lifelong commitment to service, leadership, and defending the Constitution.
Eileen has spent her life serving our nation, beginning her military career as a student in the U.S.
Naval Academy under President Reagan and rose to Rear Admiral, serving as an intelligence officer.
Carla Felts added: “When a platform speaks to the protection of constitutional rights, the integrity of our institutions, the dignity owed to those who served, and the kind of governance our Founders sacrificed to establish — Veterans On Watch (VOW) will say so.
Loudly.
And without apology.
That is why VOW will stand with RADM Eileen Laubacher, USN (Ret.), in her run for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Colorado's Fourth Congressional District (CO-04).
Her unwavering, principled leadership and commitment to our shared oath to protect the Constitution is exactly what Coloradans need from a representative."
"Veterans On Watch understands something that has guided my entire career: our Constitution only works when leaders have the courage to put their oath above politics and service above self.
I spent 34 years in uniform defending this country, and I'm running for Congress for the same reason I served—to fight for the people of Colorado and protect the values that make our nation strong.
I'm proud to have the support of fellow veterans who understand what's at stake and who know that public service is about responsibility, not personal ambition." said Eileen Laubacher.
Eileen Laubacher is a retired Rear Admiral, Naval Academy graduate, intelligence officer, and mother of five.
She served her country for more than three decades.
She’s running for Congress to defend the Constitution, protect veterans and working families, and ensure that rural Colorado’s land, economy, and way of life are respected—not run over—by Washington.
Titles, ranks, and military service are used for identification purposes only and do not imply endorsement by the Department of Defense.
