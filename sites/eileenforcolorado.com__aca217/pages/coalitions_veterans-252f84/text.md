Veterans for Eileen
The Veterans for Eileen coalition brings together veterans from communities across Colorado's 4th District who share a commitment to service, integrity, and putting country before politics.
Drawing on their military experience and continued dedication to their communities, coalition members help ensure veterans' voices are heard throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow veterans through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting veterans and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
HOW BOEBERT
HURTS VETERANS
- Boebert voted twice against the PACT Act to guarantee needed healthcare for veterans exposed to toxic burn pits during their service.
- Boebert voted for the bill to cut $1 trillion from Medicaid and $187 million from SNAP, threatening the healthcare and food security of over 24,000 veterans in Colorado.
- Boebert was one of only 17 members to vote against a measure protecting veterans from fraud
- Boebert voted to gut the VA, backing a bill that sought to cut $2 billion in funding for veterans that was forecast to result in 30 million fewer veteran outpatient visits and 81,000 jobs lost across the VA
- Boebert was one of only 12 members to vote against a bill to expand coverage for in-home care programs for veterans and require the VA to provide Veteran Directed Care at local VA medical centers.
