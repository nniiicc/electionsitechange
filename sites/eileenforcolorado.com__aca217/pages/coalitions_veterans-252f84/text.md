Drawing on their military experience and continued dedication to their communities, coalition members help ensure veterans' voices are heard throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow veterans through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting veterans and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
