Drawing on their diverse backgrounds and perspectives, coalition members help ensure the voices of independent voters are heard throughout the campaign while building a broad coalition focused on solving problems for Colorado families.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow Independents through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting Independents and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
