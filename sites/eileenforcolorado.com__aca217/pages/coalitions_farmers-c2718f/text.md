Farmers & Ranchers for Eileen
The Farmers for Eileen coalition brings together farmers, ranchers, and agricultural producers from communities across Colorado's 4th District who share a commitment to protecting Colorado's agricultural heritage and strengthening rural communities.
Drawing on generations of experience working the land and feeding our communities, coalition members help ensure the voices of agriculture are heard throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow Farmers and Ranchers through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting Farmers and Ranchers and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
