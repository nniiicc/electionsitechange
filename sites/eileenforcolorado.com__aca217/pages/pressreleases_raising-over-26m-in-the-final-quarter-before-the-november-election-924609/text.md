Highlands Ranch, CO – Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher’s campaign for Colorado’s 4th Congressional District has again proven its fundraising strength and seriousness, raising over $2.6 million this quarter.
This is nearly $1 million more than she raised in the second quarter, demonstrating the incredible grassroots backing and momentum that continue to grow across the district, state, and country.
Nearly 200,000 donors nationwide, including more than 31,000 Coloradans , have contributed to the campaign, with an average donation of just $21 .
Laubacher continues to build the momentum, field organization, and financial resources needed to deliver common-sense representation for Colorado families.
She stands alone as the candidate with the grassroots support, leadership experience, and proven credentials required to win this November.
“I am incredibly grateful to every person who has chosen to invest in this campaign.
Every donation, from $5 to $500, is a reminder that people across this district believe in what we are fighting for and want real, serious representation.
I will never take that support for granted.
Unlike Lauren Boebert, I believe in showing up for the people, focusing on the needs of all constituents and meeting them where they are to listen,” said Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher .
Eileen has built a campaign powered by grassroots support.
Throughout the campaign, Laubacher has made it a priority to listen directly to the people of Colorado’s 4th Congressional District, traveling across all 21 counties to meet with families, healthcare workers, farmers, small business owners, and veterans.
She has visited all 14 rural hospitals and driven more than 33,000+ miles across the district, an area as large as the state of South Carolina.
These conversations have shaped her commitment to lowering costs, protecting healthcare, supporting rural hospitals, and strengthening the local economy.
