Teachers for Eileen
The Teachers for Eileen coalition brings together educators from communities across Colorado's 4th District who share a commitment to strengthening public education and creating more opportunities for every student.
Drawing on their experience in classrooms and schools, coalition members help ensure the perspectives of educators, students, and families are represented throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow Teachers through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting Teachers and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
HOW BOEBERT HURTS Teachers
- Boebert has voted to gut funding for education, voting for an extreme measure that would have led to over 60,000 educators losing their jobs and gutting funding for science and math education
- Boebert voted for a national voucher program that would have funneled millions of taxpayer dollars from public schools to private schools
- Boebert has worked to eliminate the Department of Education, co-sponsoring a bill to terminate it
