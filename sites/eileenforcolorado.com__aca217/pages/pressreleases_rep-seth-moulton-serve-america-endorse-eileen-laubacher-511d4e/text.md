SETH MOULTON, SERVE AMERICA ENDORSE EILEEN LAUBACHER Apr 28 Written By Press - EL4CO WASHINGTON, D.C. — Today, Congressman Seth Moulton (MA-06) and his organization Serve America announced their endorsement of Rear Admiral (Ret.) Eileen Laubacher for the upcoming midterm election in November.
Laubacher joins this diverse group of decorated military veterans as a service leader who is running to bring integrity and mission-driven leadership to Coloradans. ​​“To take back the House and stop the chaos in Washington, we have to win in the toughest districts in America,” said Congressman Seth Moulton , founder of Serve America.
“Eileen Laubacher isn’t a career politician– she is a service-minded leader who has spent her life answering the call.
I am incredibly proud to endorse her.” Serve America’s mission is to elect leaders from diverse service backgrounds who pledge to put the interests of the American people before their own.
Notable elected leaders endorsed by Serve America include Rep.
Joe Neguse (CO-02) and Rep.
Jason Crow (CO-06).
Successful Serve America candidates were instrumental in taking back the House in 2018 and maintaining a majority in 2020.
“I decided to run for Congress because servant leadership is core to who I am”, said Rear Admiral (Ret.) Eileen Laubacher .
“I knew from the time I was a little girl that I wanted to attend the U.S.
Naval Academy and serve my country.
Colorado’s 4th Congressional District is long overdue for a representative who is ready to put their needs first, and I am the only candidate in this race with a lifetime record of doing just that.
I’m proud to be endorsed by an organization devoted to electing like-minded leaders to Washington across the county.” A retired Navy Rear Admiral with 34 years of service, Laubacher served as an intelligence officer and as a Senior Director on the National Security Council.
She is running to provide serious, honest representation for Eastern Colorado.
Moulton’s endorsement joins the growing list of local and national leaders backing Eileen in her fight to bring fair representation to Coloradans, including Jason Crow , Brittany Pettersen , Bob Marshall , and Angela Thomas .
To see the full slate of candidates endorsed by Serve America, visit: www.serveamericapac.com .
