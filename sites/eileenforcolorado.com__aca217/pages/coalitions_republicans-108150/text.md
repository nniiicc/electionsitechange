Republicans for Eileen
The Republicans for Eileen coalition brings together Republicans from communities across Colorado's 4th District who believe leadership should be guided by integrity, service, and a willingness to put people ahead of partisan politics.
Drawing on their commitment to responsible leadership and practical solutions, coalition members help demonstrate that support for Eileen extends across traditional party lines and help ensure those perspectives are represented throughout the campaign.
Once you sign up, a member of our team will reach out to welcome you to the coalition and connect you with fellow Republicans through our coalition Slack channels.
You'll have opportunities to participate in regular meetings, attend events and roundtables across the district, share your perspective on issues affecting Republicans and their families, and help build support for Eileen throughout Colorado's 4th District.
HOW BOEBERT HURTS Republicans
- Boebert has voted for record high government spending and to add nearly $5 trillion to the national debt
- Boebert voted to raise the debt ceiling to over $41 trillion – nearly double the level it was when she took office
- Boebert has repeatedly voted against priorities which even had near unanimity among Republicans:
- She was one of only 9 members to vote against increasing maternity care for veteran mothers
- She was one of only 2 members to oppose the National Marrow Donor Program for cancer patients
- She was one of only 13 members to oppose a bill covering lymphedema treatment for seniors on Medicare
- She was one of only 20 members to vote against an effort to protect religious freedom
- She was one of just 20 members to oppose a bill providing more than $1.1 billion in funding to anti-trafficking programs
- She was one of just 20 members to vote against a new law that allows family members of victims to request review and potential reopening of cold cases or murders of their loved ones
- She opposed the majority of her own party and voted against banning the sale of “suicide kits” that could be purchased online by children
