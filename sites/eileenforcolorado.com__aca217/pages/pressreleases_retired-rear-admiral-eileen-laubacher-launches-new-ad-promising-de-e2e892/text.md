Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher Launches New Ad, Promising Decency, Common Sense, and Service
Highlands Ranch, CO —Today, Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher launched the first ad of her campaign for Colorado’s Fourth Congressional District, introducing voters to a different kind of leadership: less noise, more listening, and a commitment to decency, common sense, and service.
“A dysfunctional government serves no one, and we should expect better from Washington,” said Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher.
“It’s time to send leaders to Congress who will treat each other with decency and respect, listen to each other and the people we represent, and work together to find common sense solutions to our shared problems.”
The ad draws a clear contrast between Ret.
Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher, who served in the United States Navy for 34 years, and Lauren Boebert, whose brand of politics has been defined by confrontation, attention-seeking, drama, and failing to deliver for Coloradans in both the 3rd and 4th district.
The ad closes with “Less Talking.
More Listening.” — a defining theme of Laubacher’s campaign and an approach she has put into practice across the Fourth District.
Laubacher has traveled to all 21 counties, visited all 14 rural hospitals, and held more than 75 meet-and-greets, along with meetings with farmers, veterans, small-business owners, local leaders, and community groups across the district.
“People are tired of politicians who are more interested in being heard than in listening,” Laubacher said.
“I’m running to listen, serve, and get things done.”
