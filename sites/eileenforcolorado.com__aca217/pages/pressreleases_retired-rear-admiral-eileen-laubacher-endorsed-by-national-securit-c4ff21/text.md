RETIRED REAR ADMIRAL EILEEN LAUBACHER Endorsed by national security leaders for america
National Security Leaders for America (NSL4A) – a non-partisan network of former military and civilian leaders from across the political spectrum – endorses Eileen Laubacher.
Highlands Ranch, CO – Today, retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher announced a key endorsement from National Security Leaders for America (NSL4A), a non-partisan coalition of over 1,400 former senior military, diplomatic, and national security officials committed to upholding democratic principles and protecting the integrity of U.S. national security.
“It’s a great honor to be endorsed by this esteemed group of national security experts who understand implicitly the challenges our democracy is facing, and the importance of working outside of party lines to deliver for the American people,” said Retired Rear Admiral Eileen Laubacher.
“Now more than ever, we need leaders who will put people over partisanship, which is exactly what I will do when I get to Congress.”
NSL4A recognizes that Eileen is a candidate uniquely qualified to represent this district, as someone who swore an oath to protect and uphold the Constitution and takes that oath seriously, who understands the importance of strong national security, and who will work with colleagues on both sides of the aisle to defend American democracy.
“NSL4A supports candidates who demonstrate sound judgement, commitment to public service, respect for the Constitution and the rule of law, and an understanding that elected office carries obligations greater than personal or partisan interests,” said NSL4A in their endorsement statement.
“Eileen Laubacher has earned our endorsement because we are confident she will bring those principles to the United States House of Representatives.
The challenges facing our country demand serious leadership.
America’s security depends on strength at home and abroad: a resilient economy, safe communities, a capable military, strong alliances, support for those who serve and their families, and institutions worthy of the public’s trust.
Laubacher is prepared to meet those responsibilities.”
Titles, ranks, and military service are used for identification purposes only and do not imply endorsement by the Department of Defense.
