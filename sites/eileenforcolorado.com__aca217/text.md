MEET Eileen LauBacher
All my life, I have felt called to serve my country.
That call to serve is why, at 17 years old, I raised my right hand at the United States Naval Academy and took an oath to support and defend the Constitution.
It’s why I spent the next 34 years serving in the Navy, going wherever my country asked me to go across America and around the world.
But Colorado has always been home.
It’s where my husband and I raised our five kids, where we built our lives, and where we chose to put down roots.
I know what it means to raise a family here: to balance work and kids, worry about the bills, and want to leave your children a community and a country even stronger than the one you inherited.
And it’s why I’m answering the call to serve one more time.
The people of Colorado’s 4th District deserve a representative who works as hard as they do - someone focused on lowering costs, solving problems, and making their lives better.
Lauren Boebert has had her chance.
She hasn’t delivered.
I will.
