LINDA’S PRIORITIES
Practical Solutions.
Thoughtful Leadership.
Putting People First.
Every issue and every priority begins with the people I will represent.
Throughout my career, I've worked with organizations, businesses, educators, & community leaders to solve problems by listening first, bringing people together, & focusing on practical solutions instead of politics.
That's the same approach I'll bring to every issue facing Senate District 10.
Safe Communities
Why This Matters to Me
I've spent my career helping organizations become stronger, more accountable, and more effective.
Every family deserves to feel safe in their neighborhood, at school, and in their local businesses.
My Commitment
I support strong partnerships between law enforcement and our communities, holding criminals accountable, addressing homelessness with compassion and responsibility, and ensuring our first responders have the resources they need to keep our neighborhoods safe.
Affordable Living
Why This Matters to Me
Like so many families, I've watched the cost of living continue to rise.
Housing, groceries, gasoline, insurance, and everyday expenses have become increasingly difficult for working families, seniors, and young people just starting their careers.
My Commitment
I'll work to reduce unnecessary regulations, encourage housing solutions that fit our communities, support responsible budgeting, and make California a place where families can afford to live, work, and retire.
Education
Why This Matters to Me
Teaching has been one of the greatest privileges of my life.
For more than thirty years I've taught leadership, management, communication, and organizational development at colleges throughout the Bay Area.
I've seen firsthand how education transforms lives and opens doors to opportunity.
My Commitment
Every student deserves access to a quality education, excellent teachers, safe schools, and opportunities to succeed whether they pursue college, technical education, or careers in the skilled trades.
Strong Local Economy
Why This Matters to Me
As a small business owner and organizational consultant, I've worked alongside employers trying to grow their businesses while creating opportunities for employees.
Healthy businesses create healthy communities.
My Commitment
I'll support policies that encourage innovation, reduce unnecessary bureaucracy, strengthen our workforce, and help small businesses succeed throughout Senate District 10.
Government Accountability
Why This Matters to Me
I've spent my career helping organizations improve communication, solve problems, and become more effective.
Government should be held to the same standard.
My Commitment
I believe government should be transparent, fiscally responsible, accountable to taxpayers, and focused on delivering results rather than creating unnecessary bureaucracy.
Transportation & Infrastructure
Why This Matters to Me
Anyone who lives or works in Senate District 10 understands the daily challenges of traffic congestion and aging infrastructure.
Reliable transportation affects our economy, our environment, and our quality of life.
My Commitment
I'll support practical investments that improve roads, public transit, and infrastructure while ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.
Housing
Why This Matters to Me
Young families struggle to buy their first home.
Seniors worry about remaining in their communities.
Workers face increasingly difficult commutes because housing has become less affordable.
These challenges affect all of us.
My Commitment
I'll work with local communities to expand housing opportunities while respecting neighborhood character, streamlining responsible development, and reducing unnecessary barriers that increase costs.
Environment
Why This Matters to Me
Protecting our environment and maintaining a strong economy should not be competing goals.
California can do both.
My Commitment
I support practical environmental stewardship, responsible resource management, clean air and water, wildfire preparedness, and policies that protect future generations while supporting jobs and economic growth.
Bringing People Together
Throughout my career, I've learned that lasting solutions rarely come from one political party or one point of view.
- They come from listening.
- They come from respect.
- They come from working together.
- That's the leadership I'll bring to Sacramento.
One District...One Conversation...Every Community Matters.
