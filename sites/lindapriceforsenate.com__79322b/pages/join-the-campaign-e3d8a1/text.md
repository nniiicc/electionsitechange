Why Volunteer?
People volunteer for many reasons.
- Some want to make a difference in their community.
- Some care about better government.
- Some simply believe it's time for respectful leadership that brings people together instead of pushing people apart.
Whatever brings you here, we're grateful you're considering becoming part of our campaign.
When you volunteer, you're helping neighbors learn about Linda's lifelong commitment to public service, community leadership, education, and practical problem solving.
- Every conversation matters.
- Every volunteer matters.
- Every community matters.
There’s a Place for Everyone
You don't need political experience.
You don't need to know everything about the issues.
You simply need a willingness to help your community.
Whether you're a student, working professional, retiree, veteran, parent, or first-time volunteer, we'd love to have you on the team.
Ways to Get Involved:
- Time Commitment: 2–3 hours Meet friendly volunteers and help introduce Linda to voters in neighborhoods throughout Senate District 10.
Training and campaign materials are provided.
Great for individuals, couples, families, and groups of friends.
- Time Commitment: About 90 minutes Invite a small group of neighbors, friends, or coworkers to meet Linda in an informal setting.
These conversations are one of the most effective ways to connect with voters and hear directly from the community.
We'll help with planning and provide campaign materials.
- Time Commitment: 10 minutes One yard sign helps hundreds—sometimes thousands—of people recognize Linda's name throughout the campaign.
It's one of the easiest ways to show your support.
We'll deliver the sign whenever possible.
- Time Commitment: 1–2 hours Help us reach voters by making friendly phone calls from campaign headquarters or from your home.
Scripts and training are provided.
No experience necessary.
- Time Commitment: Flexible Help represent the campaign at community festivals, farmers markets, neighborhood events, parades, and civic gatherings.
You'll greet residents, answer basic questions, and help create a welcoming presence.
- Time Commitment: Flexible Help organize campaign materials, prepare mailings, enter data, or assist with day-to-day campaign operations.
Every campaign depends on dedicated volunteers behind the scenes.
