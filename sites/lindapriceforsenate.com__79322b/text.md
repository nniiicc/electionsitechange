Why Linda Is Running
As I've traveled throughout Senate District 10, I've met people from every walk of life.
We share many of the same hopes: safe neighborhoods, quality schools, affordable housing, a healthy economy, opportunities for our children, & a government that is accountable to the people it serves.
That's why I'm running.
I want to bring integrity, professionalism, practical experience, & common sense to Sacramento while ensuring every community has a voice.
The issues we face & opportunities to embrace are not Democrat or Republican issues.
They are California issues & opportunities!
I am not a local politician.
I am not a career politician.
My commitment to you is simple.
- I'll listen before I speak.
- I'll treat every person with respect.
- I'll work hard.
- I'll tell you the truth, even when the answers aren't easy.
- And I'll never forget that public office is about serving people—not politics.
