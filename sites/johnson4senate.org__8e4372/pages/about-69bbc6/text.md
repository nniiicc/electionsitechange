Meet Nivek
Nivek M.
Johnson is an educator, community leader, and public servant from Chestertown, Maryland, dedicated to expanding opportunity, strengthening public education, and advocating for transparency in government.
Born and raised on Maryland’s Eastern Shore, Johnson has built a career centered on service to young people, families, and communities across the state.
Johnson attended public schools in Kent County and Queen Anne’s County before continuing his studies at Chesapeake College.
He earned a Bachelor of Arts in Communication from Salisbury University and later completed a Master of Education from Bowie State University.
Professionally, Johnson is an Information Technology and Computer Science educator with Montgomery County Public Schools, where he teaches students digital literacy, technology, and problem-solving skills needed for the modern workforce.
His work in education focuses on preparing students for the future while ensuring that every child—regardless of background—has access to high-quality learning opportunities.
Johnson’s commitment to public service began early.
As a young leader, he served as a Legislative Page in the Maryland General Assembly, gaining firsthand experience in the legislative process and the importance of civic engagement.
He later went on to serve as the youngest elected member of the Kent County Board of Education, where he also served as Vice Chair.
During his tenure, he worked to strengthen accountability, improve collaboration between educators and communities, and guide local schools through major statewide initiatives such as the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future.
Beyond local government, Johnson has also been appointed to several state and regional leadership roles.
He currently serves as a member of the Maryland State Public Information Act Compliance Board, an oversight body that helps ensure transparency, access to public records, and accountability in government across Maryland.
Johnson has also been appointed to the Judicial Nominating Commission for Cecil, Kent, and Queen Anne’s Counties, where he helps evaluate and recommend candidates for judicial appointments in Maryland’s courts.
Throughout his career, Johnson has remained deeply engaged in civic and nonprofit leadership.
His community service includes roles with organizations such as Sumner Hall, the Historical Society of Kent County, the Kent Attainable Housing Project, and other local boards dedicated to preserving history, expanding housing opportunities, and strengthening community life on the Eastern Shore.
With more than a decade of experience in education, nonprofit leadership, and public service, Johnson’s work has focused on empowering young people, preserving community heritage, and ensuring government works for everyday people.
Today, Johnson continues his commitment to service as a candidate for the Maryland State Senate, bringing a vision focused on strong schools, transparent government, economic opportunity, and community-driven leadership for Maryland’s Eastern Shore.
