How To Vote In The Election
Vote-By-Mail
Voting by mail is a safe, quick, convenient, and secure way to make your voice heard in the upcoming election.
Registered voters can apply for a vote-by-mail ballot.
3 ways to send your vote-by-mail ballot:
1.
Send by mail as soon as possible.
2.
Drop in a secure ballot box.
Place your vote-by-mail ballot in one of your county’s secure ballot drop boxes by 8:00 PM on election day.
- Camden County Drop Box Locations
- Gloucester County Drop Box Locations
- Burlington County Drop Box Locations
3.
Deliver in person to the County Clerk’s Office.
In-Person Early Voting
Daily beginning May 26, 2026, to May 31, 2026.

- Tuesday-Saturday: 10AM – 8 PM

In-Person Voting on Election Day
Election Day is Tuesday, June 2, 2026.
Vote in person at your polling place, from 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Election Day.
Still Need to Register to Vote?
Check on your voter registration status.
The deadline to register to vote, so you can vote by Tuesday June 2 is: May 12, 2026.
