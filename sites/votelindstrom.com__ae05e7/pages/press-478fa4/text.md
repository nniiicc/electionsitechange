Brett Lindstrom submits signatures for Nebraska governor bid Omaha World-Herald Mon, Aug 3 Op-ed by Brett Lindstrom: Nebraska's budget problems will turn into a crisis if we don't act Statement Brett Lindstrom Files for Governor of Nebraska July 13, 2026 · Omaha, Nebraska I'm running for Governor of Nebraska.
I was born here.
I built my career here.
I served this state in the Legislature.
Everything I care about is tied to Nebraska's future — so believe me, this wasn't a decision I made lightly.
Nebraska is at a turning point.
The choices we make right now — about our budget, about our economy — will decide whether our kids and grandkids get to build their lives here or watch the door close behind them.
Nebraskans work hard and play by the rules.
They deserve leaders who answer to them — not to two parties, and not to politics as usual.
That's why I'm running as an independent.
I'm not here for the fight between red and blue.
I'm here to get things right for Nebraska: a budget that respects the people who pay for it, an economy that keeps our families close to home, and a government that never forgets who it works for.
Today, I filed to make this run official.
First, we get on the ballot — that means collecting signatures from Nebraskans who are ready for a real choice.
Once we've done that, we get to work.
