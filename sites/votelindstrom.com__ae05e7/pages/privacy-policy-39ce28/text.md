This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect through votelindstrom.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the safeguards in place to protect it.
1.
Information Collection and Use — We may collect personal information you provide when you interact with the Site — such as when you sign up for updates, volunteer, contact us, or make a contribution.
This can include your name, email address, mailing address, ZIP code, phone number, employer and occupation (required by law for contributions), payment information processed by our payment provider, and any other information you choose to provide.
We use this information to communicate with you, organize volunteers and supporters, solicit and process contributions, provide supporter assistance, analyze Site usage, and comply with applicable law, including campaign-finance reporting requirements.
2.
Data Security — We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
However, no method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and while we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
4.
Third-Party Services — We do not share or sell any personal information collected on this website with any outside third parties.
Mobile phone numbers collected through the Site will not be shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes.
Third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf (such as email, texting, website hosting, analytics, and payment processing) may access information only as needed to perform those services and are obligated to keep it confidential.
Contributions are subject to public disclosure to the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission as required by law.
The Site may contain links to external websites; we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites and encourage you to review their policies.
5.
Text Messages and Email — If you provide your mobile number and consent, you may receive recurring automated text messages from us.
Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.
Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
Your consent to receive text messages is not shared with third parties.
You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email.
6.
Children’s Privacy — The Site is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.
7.
Your Rights — You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or object to processing by emailing info@votelindstrom.com .
We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
8.
Changes to This Privacy Policy — We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
We encourage you to review it periodically.
9.
Contact Us — Questions about this Privacy Policy?
Email info@votelindstrom.com .
