Steady Hand.
Brett Lindstrom was born in Lincoln and raised in Omaha.
He graduated from Millard West High School, walked on as a quarterback for the Nebraska Cornhuskers, and earned his degree from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln.
He and his wife Leigh are raising their three children as proud Nebraskans.
He built his career in wealth management — spending nearly two decades helping Nebraska families and businesses plan for the future.
That work taught him the discipline this moment demands: solve the problem in front of you, live within your means, and keep building for the next generation.
From 2015 to 2023, Brett served District 18 in the Nebraska Legislature, where he chaired the Banking, Commerce and Insurance Committee and served as Vice Chairman of both the Revenue Committee and the Retirement Systems Committee.
He earned a reputation for doing the work over chasing the spotlight — focused on Nebraska's economy, not national fights.
Brett sponsored and passed more than 60 bills during his time in the Legislature.
Among his most significant achievements was championing and passing the elimination of the state income tax on Social Security benefits — providing meaningful tax relief for Nebraska seniors.
Like a lot of Nebraskans, Brett grew tired of a political environment that rewards division over results.
So he stepped back from the party system, registered as an independent, and entered this race with one loyalty: Nebraska.
No party bosses, no litmus tests — just practical problem-solving, personal responsibility, and a belief that our best days are still ahead.
