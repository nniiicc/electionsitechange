I grew up in Tifton, Georgia swimming in the backyard pond and sword fighting with cattails.
I loved tap class, got bored in ballet, and could quote every word to the movie Grease.
My curfew was the streetlights flickering on as I raced my bike down the hill towards home.
I read every Nancy Drew book in my school library and earned my personal pan pizza every year.
I attended college at Valdosta State University earning my Bachelor’s of Business Administration with a focus in Accounting and a year later my Masters of Accountancy.
My junior year of college I attended VSU’s study abroad program in Germany and fell in love with traveling.
After college, I moved to DeKalb County in 2013 to work as a corporate accountant in the telecom and later IT/IaaS industries.
My entire career has been spent with two companies and I’ve been fortunate enough to love both jobs.
In 2020, I moved to Forsyth County because it reminds me of where I grew up.
Fun Facts: I have five rescue dogs: a husky, a black lab, and three pit mixes.
My favorite holiday is Christmas I’m a Ravenclaw Deciding to run a political campaign is scary - but scary things have to happen so we can make things better.
Join me in fighting to ensure that we can afford our healthcare and our groceries so that we can live our best lives.
