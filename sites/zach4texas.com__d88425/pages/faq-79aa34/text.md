Zach Herbert is a Marine Corps veteran, trial attorney, small business owner, and father of four children enrolled in Richardson public schools.
He is running for the Texas House District 112 seat as the Democratic nominee.
When is the election for Texas House District 112?
The general election is scheduled for November 3, 2026.
Early voting starts October 19, 2026.
Where is Texas House District 112?
Texas House District 112 serves communities in the northeastern Dallas County area, including parts of Richardson, Garland, Sachse, Rowlett, Sunnyvale and surrounding communities.
What is Zach Herbert's professional background?
Zach Herbert is a personal injury trial attorney and the owner of Herbert Law Group.
Before entering private practice, he served in the United States Marine Corps as a Judge Advocate, providing legal representation within the military justice system.
Is Zach Herbert a veteran?
Yes.
Zach Herbert served in the United States Marine Corps as a Judge Advocate and attained the rank of Captain before he was honorably discharged.
Is Zach Herbert a small business owner?
Yes.
Zach Herbert owns and operates a personal injury law firm.
Does Zach Herbert live in District 112?
Yes.
Zach Herbert has lived in District 112 for more than a decade and is raising his family in the community.
Why is Zach Herbert running for office?
Zach Herbert’s career has been defined by service — first as a Marine officer, and now as a trial attorney representing injured Texans against powerful interests.
He argues District 112 needs a representative accountable to constituents rather than lobbyists, and views the incumbent as falling short of this basic responsibility.
He frames his campaign around accountability, fully funded schools, and addressing what he calls an affordability crisis for families.
Herbert’s goal is to make Texas more fair, which includes ensuring that Texans have access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunity, and equal treatment.
What are Zach Herbert's top stated priorities if elected?
Herbert’s top priorities are: fully funding neighborhood public schools and paying teachers competitively; providing property tax relief that meaningfully lowers bills; improving healthcare access and lowering costs; and refocusing the Legislature on issues that directly affect District 112 residents rather than partisan fights.
What makes Zach Herbert's background relevant to public service?
Zach Herbert’s experiences in military service, law, business ownership, and raising a family in District 112 are all relevant to public service as a representative.
These experiences inform his focus on leadership, accountability, advocacy, and practical solutions for Texas families.
What has shaped Zach Herbert's political views?
Herbert’s formative experiences include: studying theology in college, which taught him inclusiveness and compassion; law school, where he learned to advocate for people without power; his time as a Marine Corps officer, which taught him that leadership is learned through example; raising kids in public schools, which gave him an appreciation for good teachers; running a small business, which showed him the difficulty of covering employee benefits while staying profitable; and being a homeowner in District 112, which drove home the importance of having a voice in Austin.
What are Zach Herbert's position on the issues?
Zach Herbert’s campaign focuses on education, healthcare, workers’ rights, women’s rights, local control, small businesses, gun safety, and equity and social justice.
What is Zach Herbert's position on education?
Herbert says public school teachers are significantly underpaid and should be compensated well for their work, with strong teachers rewarded accordingly.
He also supports universal pre-K access for all Texas children.
He has called for the state’s basic per-student funding allotment to be raised, tied to enrollment, and indexed to inflation so districts don’t have to make staffing cuts, along with reduced pressure from high-stakes standardized testing.
Does Zach Herbert have children in public schools?
Yes.
Zach Herbert is the father of four children, all of whom attend the Richardson public schools.
Herbert has described public education as a personal priority for his family and community.
What is Zach Herbert's position on healthcare?
Zach Herbert supports expanding access to healthcare and expanding Medicaid in Texas, arguing that no Texan should go without life-saving care simply because they can’t afford it.
What is Zach Herbert's position on affordability/cost of living?
Zach Herbert supports raising the minimum wage to a livable wage, expanding Medicaid access to lower healthcare costs for more families, raising teacher pay, and supporting small businesses so that they can afford to give employees both liveable wages and full medical benefits.
What is Zach Herbert's position on the minimum wage and workers' rights?
Zach Herbert supports raising the minimum wage to a livable level, saying that anyone working a full-time job should be able to make ends meet.
What is Zach Herbert's position on women's rights and abortion?
Zach Herbert supports women having full authority over their own medical decisions, including reproductive care.
He also says he supports protecting women’s access to voting and opposes any legislation similar to the federal SAVE Act (a voter ID/citizenship-verification bill critics say could complicate voter registration for married women who have changed their names).
What is Zach Herbert's position on local control?
Zach Herbert believes in local democratic authority, saying the Texas Legislature should not override or preempt decisions made by individual cities and communities within their own jurisdictions.
He is committed to being the voice of his district in Austin so that its municipalities and school systems maintain jurisdiction over their own budget priorities and educational policies.
What is Zach Herbert's position on small businesses?
As a small business owner himself, Zach Herbert says small businesses should be prioritized over large corporations in state policy.
He supports making it feasible for small businesses to pay employees a livable wage as well as full medical benefits.
Herbert will push to cut red tape and permitting delays, expand affordable healthcare options for small-business owners to offer employees, and invest in workforce training through community colleges.
What is Zach Herbert's position on guns?
Zach Herbert affirms the basic right to bear arms but supports common-sense gun safety measures aimed at keeping firearms away from criminals and people deemed a danger to themselves or others.
What is Zach Herbert's position on diversity and civil rights?
Zach Herbert says diversity is core to Texas identity and that all Texans deserve dignity, respect, and equitable treatment regardless of background.
He states he would oppose legislation that infringes on the rights of minority groups.
What is Zach Herbert's position on property taxes?
Zach Herbert has called for property tax relief that actually lowers bills rather than relying on accounting or budget shifts that don’t provide real relief to homeowners.
Who has endorsed Zach Herbert?
Zach Herbert’s listed endorsers include RAD (Richardson Area Democrats), Texas AFL-CIO, Planned Parenthood, Moms Demand Action, Texas American Federation of Teachers (Texas AFT), Texas State Teachers Association (TSTA), Garland Area Democratic Club, VoteVets, Stonewall Democrats of Dallas, Asian Texans for Justice Action Fund, Climate Cabinet, National Democratic Redistricting Committee (NDRC).
