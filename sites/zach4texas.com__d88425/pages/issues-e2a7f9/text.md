Texas.
My journey has shaped what I stand for today.
As a Theology major in college, I learned inclusiveness and compassion.
In law school, I learned how to stand up for the powerless.
In the Marines, I learned that true leadership is earned through example.
As a father raising children in public schools, I learned the importance of great teachers.
As a small business owner, I learned how tough it is to pay employees’ benefits and keep the company afloat.
As a homeowner in District 112, I learned how important it is to have a voice in Austin.
Education
I believe our Teachers are grossly underpaid and should be rewarded for their hard work.
Good Teachers should be paid a good salary, and great Teachers should be rewarded in kind.
I believe in Pre-K for all Texans.
Healthcare
I believe in increasing access to healthcare and expanding Medicaid, so that no Texan is left without lifesaving care merely because they cannot afford it.
Worker's Rights
I believe that the minimum wage should be increased to a livable wage, and that any Texan who works a full-time job should be able to make ends meet.
Women's Rights
I believe that women should have the unequivocal right to make medical decisions for their own bodies.
I believe in protecting women’s access to the ballot box and oppose any legislation akin to the SAVE Act.
Local Control
I believe in local Democracy, and the Texas Legislature should not preempt the decisions communities make for their respective jurisdictions.
Small Businesses
I believe small businesses should be prioritized over large corporations.
I believe every small business should be able to afford to pay a living wage and full medical benefits for all Texas employees.
Gun Safety
I believe in the basic right to bear arms.
I believe that guns are inherently dangerous, and common-sense gun safety measures should be implemented to keep guns out of the hands of criminals and others deemed dangerous to themselves.
Equity/Social Justice
I believe that diversity is what makes us Texans, and all Texans are equally deserving of dignity, respect, inclusion, and equitable treatment.
I will oppose any legislation that infringes on the rights of the few, and promote equity and ensure that Texans of all backgrounds are treated with dignity and respect.
