Cut Inflation = Long-Run Affordability
Real actions rather than rhetoric and hand-waving are critical now.
At its most fundamental level, most inflation is caused by federal deficit spending and the Federal Reserve (FED) “creating money” to pay for it.
Six steps are needed to cut inflation: 1) Stop being the puppet of Israel and end the war in Iran; 2) Eliminate trade tariffs (the 2018 trade war with China alone costs America $50 billion a year and 166,000 jobs); 3) Cut regulations.
Federal regulations are costing the US over $6.5 trillion per year.
One example is the 1920 Jones Act forcing the use of vessels that cost 5 times as much to purchase when shipping between US ports; 4) reduce the bureaucracy in granting green-cards and work visas; fill the job openings that will produce more goods and services at lower prices; 5) cut federal spending (see my tab on cutting federal agencies, https://www.steveparsonslibertarian.com/additional-page-2); 6) eliminate (or at least limit) the Federal Reserves’ ability to “create” money out of thin air to fund deficit spending; this money creation is a crucial cause of inflation.
Immigration Reform
The current situation is the worst of all possible worlds.
We need to 1) Eliminate federal subsidy/safety-net programs for non-citizens (build a wall - around federal welfare); 2) Cut the bureaucracy in granting green-cards and work visas. 3) Eliminate tariffs on Latin American goods (think keeping jobs south of the border to reduce migration flows north); 4) prosecute criminals (immigrants or not) and negotiate a deal to have Mexico incarcerate convicted immigrants; 5) Eliminate the 3/10 rule that incentivizes remaining as an illegal immigrant.
We need a clearer path to legal permanent residency for long-time immigrants who have made a life in the US.
The first five items above will reduce migration flows. 6) Create a fee (say $4,000) for expedited immigration review in Mexico.
This will help pay for more manpower to deal with immigration issues.

Knee-jerk tendencies to simply build more jails and prisons is not working.
