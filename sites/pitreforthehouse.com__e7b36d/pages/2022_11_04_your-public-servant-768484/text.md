This article is beautifully and simply written.
Government officers are trustees and servants to YOU their constituents .
This is because all power is originally inherent , (inseparably attached or connected), and derived from the people .
If elected I will be your public servant charged with defending you and protecting YOUR individual rights.
This is something I take very seriously.
I am not running for State Representative as a career move.
I believe in a citizen run legislature with term limits for all offices.
You can rest assured I will defend YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS to live however you choose.
If this resonates with you please join the many others in your district and vote for me on November 3rd.
