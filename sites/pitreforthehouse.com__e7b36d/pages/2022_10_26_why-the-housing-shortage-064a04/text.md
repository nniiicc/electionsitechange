Everyone would agree that Vermont is in a housing crisis.
Act 250, which is over 50 years old, and more recently Act 181 are a major part of this problem.
A community’s wellbeing depends on its workforce and that workforce needs a place to live.
Over regulation and the delays it causes greatly increases the price of a home.
Without new legislation that streamlines building regulations, affordable housing for the hard-working middle class will become, and is already mostly nonexistent.
These Vermonters will be forced to leave and take up residence in a state with far less financial pressures.
Simply stated by a local contractor, “If you want more housing, you have to allow it to be built.”
The housing crisis is a big problem.
But until we realize the simple truth that new construction is a necessary sign of flourishing we will never solve our shortage.
