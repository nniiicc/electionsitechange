For more than four decades, Jack Stockton has answered the call to serve.
As Roane County Sheriff for the past 20 years — and the only Roane County Sheriff in our county's history to run unopposed for two consecutive terms — Jack has earned the trust of citizens, colleagues, and community leaders alike.
He has balanced multi-million-dollar budgets, led through every kind of crisis, and built strong working relationships with officials and everyday Tennesseans on both sides of the aisle.
Now he's ready to take that same proven, principled leadership to Nashville.
