It's About You
This year's election is one of the most important our community has faced in years.
The decisions we make today will shape the future of our neighborhoods, our businesses, our schools, public safety, our local economy, and the trust we place in our government.
I believe voters deserve to know what guides the people asking to represent them.
For me, it's simple: my focus is you—your family, your neighborhood, your livelihood, and the future of Connecticut's 97th District.
As your State Representative, I will listen to every resident and represent our community with honesty, integrity, and accountability.
My commitment is to serve all constituents, regardless of political affiliation, because every voice deserves to be heard.
My priorities are clear:
**Strengthening our local economy and supporting small businesses.
**Addressing homelessness with practical, compassionate, and effective solutions.
**Supporting Second Chance initiatives that reduce recidivism while promoting personal responsibility.
**Working with law enforcement, first responders, and community leaders to improve public safety.
**Strengthening education and supporting the important role parents play in their children's education.
I believe leadership begins with listening.
I'll always be honest about where I stand, explain the reasons behind my decisions, and remain accessible to the people I represent.
Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more prosperous future for our community.
Now more than ever... it's about you.
~~ Anthony Acri
