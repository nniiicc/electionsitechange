Anchored by major cities such as Durham, Chapel Hill, Cary, and parts of Raleigh , the district is home to some of North Carolina’s most dynamic communities, leading universities, and innovation-driven industries.
The area includes the world-renowned Research Triangle Park (RTP) , a hub for technology, science, and education that attracts professionals from around the globe.
With a mix of historic neighborhoods, growing suburbs, and thriving downtown areas, residents enjoy access to exceptional schools, cultural landmarks, and green spaces.
Demographically, the 4th District is one of the most diverse in the state, bringing together people of many backgrounds, languages, and traditions.
Its population reflects a rich tapestry of college students, professionals, families, and retirees — all contributing to a distinctive sense of community and progress.
Together, these communities make the 4th District a microcosm of opportunity and innovation, where diversity fuels creativity and quality of life continues to attract people from across North Carolina and beyond.
