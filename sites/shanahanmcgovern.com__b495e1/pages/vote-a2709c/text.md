Before you can vote you must be registered.
Chicago residents of the 18th District need to register to vote with the Chicago Board of Elections and not the Cook County Clerk.
The rest of the 18th District will register at the Illinois State Board of Elections.
If you have any questions contact us.
Soon we will be providing polling locations or voting centers here so you know where to vote.
There is another need from our registered voters, and that is to consider becoming an election judge.
The priority to have fair and honest elections with all election judges (both Republican and Democrat) in the election process is crucial.
Election Integrity is VITAL!
If you can not commit to being an election judge, please consider signing up to be a pollwatcher.
We can connect you to trainers across the district.
It is our duty to to be involved in elections to prevent any election or voter fraud in the voting process.
Meanwhile, you can gain valuable experience in learning the political process!
If you would like to help with GOTV, please consider signing up to be a precinct captain .
Precinct captains can also be pollwatchers and even register people to vote in their precincts.
Please consider getting engaged in the voting process and elections!
We need "eyes on every ballot".
In addition, if you'd like to help assist voters to the polls let us know that as well.
