Priority Issues
JOHN IS READY TO GET THINGS DONE
PROTECT PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Strong public schools are essential to Wisconsin’s future.
By fully funding classrooms, supporting teachers, and expanding career and technical education, we can prepare every student—college-bound or trade-bound—for good jobs, strong communities, and a competitive state economy.
LOWER THE COST OF LIVING
Wisconsin families are being squeezed by rising childcare, housing, and utility costs.
We must lower everyday expenses, stand up to corporate price-gouging, invest in good-paying jobs, and make state government work for working families—not the super-rich.
AFFORDABLE AND ACCESSIBLE HEALTHCARE
Accessible health care means families can get care when they need it, without fear of crushing costs.
Wisconsin must expand access, lower premiums and prescription prices, and ensure working families aren’t forced to choose between their health and their household budget.
STAND UP AGAINST DATA CENTERS
End taxpayer handouts for Big Tech and hold them accountable by making them pay their fair share to stop rising utility bills.
PROTECT OUR DRINKING WATER AND NATURAL RESOURCES
Clean water is fundamental to Wisconsin’s way of life.
We must protect our wells, streams, and lakes by holding polluters accountable, investing in conservation, and ensuring families can safely drink their water and enjoy the outdoors for generations.
CREATE JOBS WITH GOOD PAY
Good-paying jobs are the foundation of strong communities.
By investing in workforce training, technical education, and local industries, Wisconsin can create careers that let families stay, succeed, and afford a good life without leaving the state.
