A Bit About Judson
Judson Sapp was raised on the banks of the St.
Johns River.
He caught his first fish at his family’s home in Putnam County and earned his Eagle Scout in Clay County.
Judson received his Bachelor of Science in Philosophy from Florida State University.
He is the CEO of W.J.
Sapp Railroad Contractor, which builds and maintains rail throughout the Southeastern United States.
He also helps many charitable causes.
Judson Sapp lives in Green Cove Springs, Florida with his wife Kelly and two sons, Willard and Ryder.
