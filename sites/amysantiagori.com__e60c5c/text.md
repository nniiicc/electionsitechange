About Amy
My name is Amy Santiago, and I am running for State Representative in District 7.
I wasn’t raised to chase titles or power.
I was raised to show up for people.
To listen.
To do the work.
And to leave things better than I found them.
I don’t believe a State Representative’s job is to tell people what’s best for them.
I believe it is to listen – and to fight for what their community is asking for.
