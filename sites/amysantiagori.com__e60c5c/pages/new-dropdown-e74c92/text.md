Who is Amy?
Amy is a current resident of the Mt.
Pleasant neighborhood.
She lives with her children and two cats.
She works in supporting our unhoused neighbors as the Regional Access Point Coordinator at The WARM Center in Westerly.
Her second job is as a chef for a catering company, as many of our neighbors she works two jobs to support her family.
Amy has worked as a Special Education teacher, volunteered in the Harm Reduction space in Boston, and is a dedicated member of her church.
Amy is a proud member of the Kanienke community, also known as the Mohawk, and has Latine heritage.
