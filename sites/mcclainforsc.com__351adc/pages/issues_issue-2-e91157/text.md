That’s not right.
Big insurance companies keep jacking up prices on basic healthcare while families across the Upstate are left to scrape by.
It’s time they’re held accountable.
That means making sure healthcare is a right, not a luxury.
Courtney has a real plan to: Fully fund the Affordable Care Act (ACA) and increase subsidies so families can afford lower monthly premiums.
Expand Medicare and support Medicare for All to replace private insurance companies and ensure all South Carolinians have guaranteed healthcare coverage.
Expand mobile health services to serve disabled, senior, veteran, and homeless populations with sliding-scale services.
Extend coverage to dental, vision, and reproductive healthcare Fiercely advocate to increase access to safe and affordable contraceptives and decrease the number of contraceptive deserts.
Oppose the dangerous restrictions placed on women's right to choose by ensuring equal and safe access to reproductive care within South Carolina and across the U.S.
