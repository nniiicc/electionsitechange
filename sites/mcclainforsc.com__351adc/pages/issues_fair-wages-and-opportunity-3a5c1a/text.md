Courtney's Bold vision for
Fair Wages and Opportunity
District Four residents have been forced to take up multiple jobs, and thousands have lost their jobs due to the Trump-DOGE mass layoffs.
The wealthy few have continued to get richer, while everyday Americans live paycheck to paycheck.
But Courtney has a real plan to:
- Protect and expand Social Security
- Enact fair scheduling laws that help create predictable schedules and incomes for hourly workers.
- Work to secure federal appropriations for workforce development and job training programs that bring high-paying jobs back to Greenville and Spartanburg
- End the tipped minimum wage and raise the federal minimum wage to a family-sustaining level that increases annually with the cost of living.
- Establish industry-wide standards for job quality, including a process for establishing a wage and benefit floor.
- Fully fund the IRS and empower staff to hold the rich who cut corners accountable.
