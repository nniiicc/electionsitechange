Courtney's bold vision for
Defending Those Who Defended Us
Courtney’s family has served this country since World War I.
She has seen the struggles that many of our veterans are facing today: layoffs, defunding of vital programs, and the prioritization of profit over people.
But Courtney has a real plan to:
- Support legislation reinstating veterans unjustly terminated from federal jobs under the Trump-era mass layoffs, ensuring they regain employment, benefits, and dignity.
- Fight back against efforts to privatize or cut Veterans Affairs services.
- Protect and expand HUD-VASH vouchers to combat veteran homelessness, and increase investment in VA healthcare.
- Expand and secure funding for the Veterans Crisis Line and integrate mental health services into primary VA care.
- Co-sponsor a War Powers Resolution to end Trump's abuse of the military for personal gain and restore Congress’s constitutional authority.
