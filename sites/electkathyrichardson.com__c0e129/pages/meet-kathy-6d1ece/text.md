I am an Army veteran, nurse practitioner, educator, healthcare leader, and small business owner with nearly 30 years of service to patients, students, military families, and communities across Washington State.
I served 20 years in the United States Army, including assignments at Joint Base Lewis‑McChord, healthcare recruiting, Human Resources Command, and deployment to Iraq with a Forward Surgical Team during the Surge.
After my military service, I continued serving through emergency medicine, healthcare leadership, and nursing education.
I held leadership roles at Pacific Lutheran University, including Graduate Program Director and Associate Dean for Academic Affairs.
I currently serve as owner, CEO, and medical director of Cossmedic Wellness & Aesthetics and Pacific Northwest Aesthetics Academy, organizations focused on patient‑centered care, healthcare workforce development, provider education, and safe, ethical healthcare practice.
I served as an elected board member for ARNPs United of Washington State before stepping down to serve as a pro‑tem member of the Washington State Board of Nursing.
I earned my Doctor of Nursing Practice degree from Marymount University, my Master of Science in Nursing – Family Nurse Practitioner degree from Pacific Lutheran University, my Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree from Eastern Washington University, and my Associate Degree from Spokane Falls Community College.
I am board‑certified as both a Family Nurse Practitioner and Emergency Nurse Practitioner and am a Fellow of the American Academy of Nursing, Academy of Emergency Nursing, and Academy of Wilderness Medicine.
Throughout my career, I have remained committed to serving veterans, military families, students, healthcare professionals, and working families throughout Washington State.
