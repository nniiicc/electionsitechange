Dr.
Kathy Richardson Stands with Our Educators and Students
As an educator, former Associate Dean for Academic Affairs, and longtime mentor to students and healthcare professionals, I understand the importance of strong schools, workforce development, and preparing the next generation for success.
I have worked directly with students from entry-level nursing through doctoral education, helped develop academic programs, secured workforce-focused grant funding, and supported pathways that connect education to real careers and community needs.
I stand with educators and students because education changes lives and strengthens communities.
Teachers, faculty, and students deserve leaders who understand the challenges facing education today — from workforce shortages and affordability to balancing educational quality with real-world preparation.
I believe we must support strong educational opportunities while ensuring students are prepared to succeed in the workforce, support their families, and contribute meaningfully to our communities.
