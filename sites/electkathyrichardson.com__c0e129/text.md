I’m running for State Representative because too many families feel stretched thin by rising costs, economic uncertainty, public safety concerns, and a government increasingly disconnected from everyday life.
After nearly 30 years of military, healthcare, education, and business leadership, I believe hard work should still create opportunity—not make it harder for families to get ahead.
We need to bring grounded, practical, common‑sense leadership back to Olympia.
