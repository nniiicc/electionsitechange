Montez’s Priorities
Quality of Life
Montez Aiken believes that real safety means more than just a badge; it means building a community where everyone has the chance to succeed.
As a retired police officer and former teacher, he will fight for better mental health training for officers and faster response times for all emergency services.
He knows that safety starts with strong foundations, so he will work to provide better schools, healthcare, and safe neighborhoods for every family.
By updating laws to protect our children and fixing the root causes of crime, Montez is committed to a plan that keeps our homes secure today and tomorrow.
He is ready to take this bold vision to the state capitol to ensure a brighter, more peaceful future for South Carolina!
Education
Montez Aiken is a former teacher and retired School Resource Officer who is ready to take his fight for our children and educators to the state capitol.
He believes that South Carolina’s 43rd-place ranking in education is not good enough and that our students deserve a fair shot at a successful life.
To advocate for this, he will fight to raise teacher pay and improve working conditions so our best educators can focus on the classroom instead of second jobs.
Montez is also committed to providing students with modern technology, better textbooks, and safer buildings to help them thrive.
With his heart and experience, Montez Aiken will collaborate with local and state leaders to build a school system that makes South Carolina a leader in the nation!
Individual Rights
Montez Aiken believes strongly in protecting individual rights and personal freedom.
He believes government should respect the decisions people make about their own lives and families, including a woman’s right to make her own reproductive healthcare decisions without interference from politicians.
Montez also believes South Carolina should take a commonsense approach to hemp products that have proven beneficial to many citizens.
Instead of shutting down a growing industry, he supports responsible regulation and taxation, with the revenue put to work improving our roads, bridges, and communities.
Above all, Montez believes the right to vote is fundamental to our democracy.
He will oppose efforts that make it harder for eligible citizens to cast their ballots and will work to make our elections secure, accessible, and fair.
Montez will always stand up for the basic freedoms and individual rights of the people he represents.
Infrastructure
Montez Aiken understands our rural communities will thrive on better infrastructure.
Through comprehensive planning and collaboration with state and local officials, he will secure our water systems and power lines to ensure they are safe and reliable for every family.
He is committed to using high-quality materials to fix broken roads and eliminate the potholes that damage our vehicles.
Montez is committed to decreasing traffic and getting you home to your family faster and safely.
He has the vision to lead a modern infrastructure plan that creates a stronger and more stable future for all.
