Childcare Tax Credits
I will sponsor tax credits worth up to $5,000 for people who live in rural Oregon and work in childcare.
Credits like these work.
When the Rural Practitioner Tax Credit was initiated, it ushered in a wave of talented medical workers in rural Oregon who seized upon the $5,000 tax credit.
We will use that same method to ensure that there is not a workforce shortage throughout the district.
Childcare is too hard to find.
And when an employee gets sick, a parent is forced to take a week of vacation to look after their child.
Tax credits will bolster the childcare job market, making it more affordable and accessible.
