What we need to do:
- Make Them Pay Their Way: End the tax break for massive data centers and stop them from draining our drinking water.
- Put Guardrails on Big Tech: Require real community benefits and require AI systems to loop in a real human when someone's in danger.
How we do it:
- Make Them Pay Their Way: Repeal the sales tax exemption for 100MW+ data centers; require reclaimed/non-potable cooling water with hard caps and public quarterly disclosure.
- Put Guardrails on Big Tech: Require binding community benefit agreements for any data center seeking special tax or zoning treatment and a full ban until this happens; require AI systems marketed to Florida consumers to mandatorily flag self-harm or violence content to a human reviewer or face serious consequences.
