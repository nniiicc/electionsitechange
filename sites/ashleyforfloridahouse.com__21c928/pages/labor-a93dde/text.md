So why do we have one of the worst gaps between wages and cost of living in America?
Why do we state preemptions on heat protection in a state known for dangerous temperatures?
The answer is simple - the rich and powerful gutted our ability to work together through unions, and that changes now.
What we need to do: Give Power Back to Workers: Repeal the state bans on water breaks, local living wages, and union dues deduction.
A Real Safety Net for Working Families: Win real paid family leave, a living wage floor for home care workers, and a wage theft unit with teeth.
Invest in Working People: Make community college actually free for the students who need it.
How we do it: Give Power Back to Workers: Repeal HB 433/SB 1492 (2024, heat-protection and living-wage preemption) and SB 256 (2023, public-sector payroll dues deduction ban, which exempted police/fire).
A Real Safety Net for Working Families: Replace Florida's voluntary-insurance-only 2023 paid leave law with a mandatory payroll-funded program; raise and index the $15/hr Medicaid direct care wage floor set in 2022; create a wage theft enforcement unit (Florida has had none since abolishing its Department of Labor in 2002).
Invest in Working People: Create a needs-based, last-dollar Florida Promise scholarship covering costs Bright Futures doesn't (housing, books, fees), modeled on Tennessee Promise.
