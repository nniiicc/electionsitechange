Pride For All
Y’all means all, simply put.
A handful of powerful people are actively robbing us of our future, stealing money from our schools, taking away our homes, and they want you to think trans kids are somehow the real problem.
It’s not just morally wrong, it’s ridiculous and wasteful.
Treat people how you’d like to be treated, and let’s make life affordable and safe for everyone.
What we need to do:
- Leave trans people alone: Repeal the care ban, the bathroom bill, and the classroom censorship law.
- Queer rights are non-negotiable: Pass a real statewide law banning discrimination against LGBTQ+ Floridians everywhere in the state.
How we do it:
- Leave trans people alone: Repeal SB 254 (2023, gender-affirming care ban), HB 1521 (2023, bathroom restriction), and the K-12 classroom restrictions on sexual orientation and gender identity.
- Queer rights are non-negotiable: Pass a statewide LGBTQ+ nondiscrimination act covering employment, housing, and public accommodations.
