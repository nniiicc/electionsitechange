A word about Town Meeting: Town government works best when people participate.
Don't leave budget decisions to someone else!
Only about 100-150 voters out of 7,000+ typically attend Town Meeting.
There are no absentee ballots or proxies allowed.
So, a relatively small group of voters can have a major impact on the outcome.
Please make a plan to be at the YPAC on June 2nd.
Doors open at 6 pm to check in and the meeting begins at 7 pm.
