Today we show our gratitude to state Rep.
Jim Arciero, and all of those who contributed to his annual drive-in and drop-off event , which raised $18,435 this year.
Arciero has been a friend of Sun Santa for 16 years, partnering with the Littleton Police Department for the last six.
We thank our returning friends for their years of steadfast dedication to our cause.
