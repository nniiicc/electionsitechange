Kirk believes every child deserves the opportunity to succeed.
Kirk believes strong schools begin with great teachers, engaged parents, and a commitment to helping every student reach their potential.
Since 2021, Utah has made major investments in public education while continuing to focus on results in the classroom.
Those investments support teachers, strengthen early literacy, expand opportunities for students, and help prepare Utah's kids for what comes next.
Kirk believes parents and educators should be partners in a child's success.
He has supported policies that give parents more information and a stronger voice in their children's education while helping teachers focus on what they do best: teaching.
That also means preparing students for a changing world.
Utah is investing in literacy, career and technical education, and profession-based learning that connects students with real opportunities beyond the classroom.
