For Kirk Cullimore, public service has never been about politics.
It's about making Utah the best place in America to raise a family.
Family Comes First On any given Saturday, you'll probably find Kirk Cullimore on the sidelines of a soccer game, in a school gym cheering on one of his six children, or volunteering in the community.
During the week, he can often be found at Brookwood Elementary, where he serves on the Community Council and accompanies the school choir.
Kirk and his wife, Heather, are raising six children in Sandy.
Their family shapes everything about how he approaches public service.
When Kirk goes to the Capitol, he is fighting for the same things he cares about at home: strong schools, safe neighborhoods, and opportunities for every Utah family.
Why I Serve Giving back to the state that has given so much to my family.
For me, public service has never been about titles.
It's about serving the community my family calls home.
My wife, Heather, our six children, and the people I meet throughout our community remind me every day why this work matters.
I'm grateful for the opportunity to help keep Utah the best place in America to live, work, and raise a family.
His Story My Path to the Senate Was Anything but Typical Before I ever served in the Utah Senate, I spent my career teaching music, running a small business, and practicing law.
Each experience taught me something different about leadership, responsibility, and serving others.
Those experiences continue to shape how I approach every challenge today.
I believe good policy starts with listening, solving problems, and understanding the real challenges Utah families and businesses face every day. 🎼 Music Teacher I began my career helping students discover confidence, discipline, and a love for learning through music. 💼 Small Business Owner Owning a business taught me firsthand what it takes to meet a payroll, manage budgets, and create opportunity. ⚖️ Attorney My legal career has focused on helping businesses, property owners, and communities solve complex problems. 🏛 Utah Senate Today, I have the privilege of serving as Utah Senate Majority Leader, working every day to strengthen Utah's future.
Serving Utah Working to Deliver Real Results Since first being elected in 2018, I've had the privilege of representing the people of Senate District 19 and serving as Utah Senate Majority Leader.
Every legislative session is an opportunity to strengthen Utah for the next generation.
Whether it's lowering taxes, investing in education, supporting public safety, or creating opportunity for Utah businesses, my focus has always been on practical solutions that improve people's lives.
Lower Taxes Utah has reduced the state income tax six years in a row while maintaining one of the strongest economies in the nation.
Invest in Education Public education funding has increased by more than $2.3 billion since 2021 while Utah continues to rank among the nation's best.
Keep Utah Strong Supporting safe communities, responsible budgets, and an economy that continues to create opportunity for Utah families.
