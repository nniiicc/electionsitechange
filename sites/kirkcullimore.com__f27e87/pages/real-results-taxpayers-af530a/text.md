6 Consecutive Years of Income Tax Cuts REAL RESULTS FOR TAXPAYERS Keeping More Money In Your Pocket.
I believe government should live within its means so Utah families can keep more of what they earn.
Families and businesses, not government, know best how to spend the dollars they've earned.
That's why I've consistently supported lower taxes, responsible budgeting, and policies that put more money back into the pockets of Utah taxpayers.
Since I joined the Utah Senate, we've reduced the state income tax six years in a row .
Today the rate stands at 4.45% , an 11% reduction that returned $101 million to Utah taxpayers this year alone.
I also supported reducing the gas tax by 15% , limiting unnecessary property tax increases, and increasing price transparency so Utah families know exactly what they'll pay at the register.
Fiscal responsibility isn't just about cutting taxes.
It's about making government work better.
That's why I've supported reducing unnecessary spending while helping Utah remain #1 in Fiscal Stability and maintaining the nation's top ranking for Economic Outlook for 18 consecutive years.
Government should never forget whose money it is.
