18 Consecutive Years as America's #1 Economic Outlook REAL RESULTS FOR UTAH BUSINESSES Creating Opportunity.
Kirk believes government should create the conditions for success and then let Utah businesses do what they do best.
Kirk knows firsthand what it takes to run a business.
Before serving in the Utah Senate, he was a small business owner, and that experience continues to shape how he approaches economic policy.
He believes Utah's entrepreneurs, employers, and workers, not government, are the real engines of economic growth.
That's why Kirk has consistently supported keeping taxes low, reducing unnecessary regulation, maintaining responsible budgets, and creating an environment where businesses can invest and grow.
The results are reflected in Utah's national standing.
Utah has held the nation's #1 ranking for Economic Outlook for 18 consecutive years , while continuing to attract investment, create opportunities, and maintain one of America's strongest business climates.
Kirk believes economic policy should ultimately be about people.
Strong businesses mean good jobs, stronger communities, greater opportunity, and more financial security for Utah families.
Government doesn't create prosperity.
Utahns do.
