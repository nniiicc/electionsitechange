Tim O’Brien is a lifelong Hoosier.
Tim moved to Southwest Indiana for college, earning a Bachelor of Science degree in economics from the University of Southern Indiana.
He chose to make Southwest Indiana home and established himself as a real estate broker.
He remains active in community leadership, serving on the Board of Directors for Heritage Federal Credit Union and previously on the Evansville Land Bank Corporation Board.
In 2025, Tim was named Easterseal’s Evansville Legislator of the Year and Boys & Girls Club of Indiana Legislative Champion.
