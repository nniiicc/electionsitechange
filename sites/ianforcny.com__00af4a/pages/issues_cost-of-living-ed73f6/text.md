COST OF LIVING
Rising costs are killing the American Dream.
Even dual-income households struggle to afford a home, pay off their student loans, hire childcare, or deal with unexpected healthcare costs.
And our seniors are forced to cut back on their groceries or prescriptions.
Ian understands these issues firsthand.
While he’s fortunate enough to have health insurance, he would be suffering from a mountain of medical debt if he did not, as he requires a medication that would cost him $11,000 a month without insurance.
He knows that’s outrageous, and that’s why he’ll fight to ensure that the state budget isn’t balanced on the backs of our seniors or hardworking middle-class Central New Yorkers.
As Assemblyman Ian will work to help first-time homebuyers, support universal healthcare for all New Yorkers, hold utility companies accountable, and bring down energy costs.
