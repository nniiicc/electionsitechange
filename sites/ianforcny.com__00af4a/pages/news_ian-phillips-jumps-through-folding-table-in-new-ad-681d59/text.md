Sep 25 2026 PRESS RELEASE Ian Phillips jumps through folding table in new ad Auburn, NY — Ian Phillips, Democratic candidate for NY Assembly in District 126, is releasing a new TV ad this weekend, which will debut during the September 27th Bills vs.
Chargers Fox Pregame Show and air on local stations in the Syracuse media market.
In the ad, Phillips calls out rising energy prices and do-nothing politicians like his opponent, Republican Assemblyman John Lemondes, who take donations from energy utility and AI data center developers (Source: NYS BOE candidate filings for Friends of John Lemondes).
Then, he jumps through a folding table to prove he’ll jump through anything for working families in Central New York.
Phillips says, “The issues are serious, but it's important not to take yourself too seriously.
I'm a lifelong Central New Yorker and a lifelong Bills fan.
And, like every Bills fan, I'm loyal, and I show up even when times are tough.
This year is our year.
It's time to take on the utility companies and data center developers and kick out the corrupt politicians on their team.” Watch the ad here: https://youtu.be/GYLcTCt_vFc About Ian Phillips Ian Phillips is a teacher, union organizer, and former Auburn school board president running for Assembly in AD126.
Phillips is running on a common sense platform: clean lakes, corporate money out of politics, and a lower cost of living.
The race is a rematch of 2024, with Phillips fundraising strongly and in a position to win.
Phillips is endorsed by major unions, including NYSUT, 1199 SEIU, IBEW Local 43, CWA Region 1, UAW Region 9, and more.
He has also received endorsements from Planned Parenthood, Cayuga County Democrats, and the Working Families Party.
