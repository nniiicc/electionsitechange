What Voters Need to Know
Voters of all parties need to know that Ian Phillips is a teacher, union organizer, and former Auburn School Board President who is running for Assembly to get money out of politics, lower the cost of living, and clean up our lakes.
Ian Phillips has a long track record of fighting to keep families in their homes.
Ian stood up to the big banks during the subprime loan crisis and developed a housing program that helped thousands of families from losing their homes.
If we’re going to make Central New York affordable for everyone, we need a fighter like Ian.
Voters should hear this message in their mailboxes, on the radio, and on social media.
All voters need to hear that John Lemondes has been one of the least effective members of the New York State Assembly and hasn’t passed a single sponsored bill into law in 3 years.
He even avoids his constituents by no-showing to two of his own town hall meetings in recent months.
Lazy Lemondes is hurting Central New Yorkers.
We deserve someone who will show up and work for us, and that’s Ian Phillips.
Voters need to hear this message on social media and traditional TV.
