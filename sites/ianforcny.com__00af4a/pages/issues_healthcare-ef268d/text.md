HEALTHCARE
Healthcare costs keep rising for Central New Yorkers, while quality of care degrades.
Healthcare access is not only an affordability issue, it’s a moral one.
In a wealthy state like New York, no one should go without medical insurance or be forced to pay sky-high out-of-pocket costs for life-saving medicine.
In the Assembly, Ian will co-sponsor the New York Health Act, a critical piece of legislation that would guarantee health coverage to all New Yorkers, regardless of income or pre-existing conditions.
He will also support lowering the costs of prescription drugs and increasing funding for rural community hospitals and health clinics.
