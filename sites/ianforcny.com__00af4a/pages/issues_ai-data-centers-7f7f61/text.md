COST OF LIVING MONEY OUT OF POLITICS CLEAN LAKES AI DATA CENTERS JOBS EDUCATION HEALTHCARE AI DATA CENTERS In Lysander, right here in the 126th district, a company called Ranalli Super DC LLC is proposing to build a 300-megawatt hyperscale AI data center over the objections of our local community.
AI data centers have been shown to increase utility costs , deplete our water supply, and pollute the air and water.
AI itself is an unproven technology, whose proponents claim will render many jobs obsolete.
We don’t need AI data centers in our district.
We’ve lost enough jobs, and the promises AI data center developers make can’t be backed up with facts.
Ian supports the Governor’s executive order placing a 1-year moratorium on data center development, but we need to go further.
In the Assembly, Ian will support legislation that stops data centers from being built until they are proven safe and beneficial to our communities.
