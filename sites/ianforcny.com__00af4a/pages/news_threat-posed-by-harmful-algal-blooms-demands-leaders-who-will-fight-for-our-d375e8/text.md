Threat Posed by Harmful Algal Blooms Demands Leaders Who Will Fight for Our Lakes
AUBURN, NY – Harmful algal blooms (HABs) have appeared on Owasco, Skaneateles, and Otisco Lakes.
HABs, which are caused by nutrient runoff, occur when cyanobacteria grow at a rapid rate, producing toxins and cutting off oxygen supply.
Due to increased nutrient loading and the impacts of climate change, HABs are occurring in the lakes earlier each year, endangering both people and wildlife and posing an existential threat to the water supply.
The Ian for Assembly team is making a significant ad buy to highlight Ian’s record as a champion for the Finger Lakes.
Phillips is a member of the Owasco Watershed Lake Association, Skaneateles Lake Association, Otisco Lake Preservation Association and Save Owasco Now.
