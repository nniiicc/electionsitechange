Sep 11 2026 PRESS RELEASE New Poll Shows Ian Phillips and John Lemondes in a Virtual Tie for AD 126 Auburn, NY — In a new poll conducted by Public Policy Polling, Democrat Ian Phillips and Republican incumbent John Lemondes are locked in a close race with less than two months to go until the November 3 election.
Ian Phillips polls at 38% among likely voters to 41% for John Lemondes, with 21% of voters undecided.
Phillips also polls higher among independent voters, 36% to 31% with 33% undecided.
The margin of error is +/- 3.8% The district has a Republican registration advantage, and while GOP voters break towards Lemondes, Phillips’ strength with independent voters gives him a clear path to victory in the race.
About Ian Phillips Ian Phillips is a former teacher, union organizer, and Auburn school board president running for Assembly in AD126.
Phillips is running on a common sense platform: clean lakes, corporate money out of politics, and a lower cost of living.
The race is a rematch of 2024, with Phillips fundraising strongly and in a position to win.
Phillips is endorsed by major unions, including the AFL-CIO, NYSUT, 1199 SEIU, IBEW Local 43, CWA Region 1, UAW Region 9, and more.
He has also received endorsements from Planned Parenthood, Sierra Club of Central-Northern New York, and the Working Families Party.
