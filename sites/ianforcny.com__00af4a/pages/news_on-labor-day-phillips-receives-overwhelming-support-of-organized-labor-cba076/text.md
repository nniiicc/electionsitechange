On Labor Day, Phillips Receives Overwhelming Support of Organized Labor
AUBURN, NY – Ian Phillips, Democratic candidate for State Assembly, District 126, has received overwhelming support from organized labor, representing tens of thousands of Central New York workers.
“I am proud to be supported by the hardworking people of Central New York and their unions.
Together we want to strengthen the middle class, raise wages, lower utility bills and stand up to mega-corporations robbing us of our future.”, said Ian Phillips.
He has received the endorsements of the IBEW Local 43, the NASRCC, NYSUT, the CSEA, the CWA, the UAW, the UUP, AFSCME, the 1199SEIU, and the NY AFL-CIO.
Phillips will march in the Labor Day parade at the NYS Fair and pictures and video are available upon request.
The general election will take place on November 3, 2026.
