- Who is Elliot Valdez?
Elliot Valdez is a dedicated community leader and candidate for the Georgia House of Representatives in District 139.
With a deep commitment to the values of the Democratic Party and a lifelong passion for serving others, Elliot is running to ensure that every resident of our district, regardless of their background, has a strong, authentic voice in the State Capitol.
- Elliot is proud to have received the highest number of Democratic primary votes ever cast for a Hispanic candidate in this geographic area, dating back to before 2000.
This milestone represents a historic shift toward inclusive representation and a mandate for a new era of leadership in our district.
- Our district is a vibrant tapestry of cultures and backgrounds.
For too long, the diversity of our community hasn't been fully reflected in the halls of power.
By electing a representative who understands the unique challenges and strengths of the Hispanic community, we ensure that policy decisions are made with a full understanding of the people they actually affect.
