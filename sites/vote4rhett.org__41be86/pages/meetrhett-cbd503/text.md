top of page Meet Rhett Priorities Election Information Contact & Get Involved Meet Rhett Rhett Rosenquest Smith is a U.S.
Navy veteran, longtime Texan, and Republican candidate for Texas House District 116.
Raised in Eastland, Texas, Rhett comes from a family shaped by military service and later served four years in the U.S.
Navy himself.
His professional background includes work in private security, along with years of involvement in veteran and community organizations. ​ Rhett's commitment to public service has led him to stay engaged in civic life and advocate on issues including veterans' services, individual liberty, education, and community concerns.
Today, he is seeking to bring that experience and commitment to Austin as the representative for Texas House District 116.
