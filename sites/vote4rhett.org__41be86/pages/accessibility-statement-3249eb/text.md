This statement was last updated on August 23, 2026
What web accessibility is
Rhett Rosenquest Smith for Texas House District 116 is committed to providing a website that is accessible to all visitors, including individuals with disabilities.
We strive to make our website easy to navigate and accessible using commonly available assistive technologies.
We will continue working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website as content is added and updated.
If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website or need information provided in an alternative format, please contact the campaign at rhettsmith@gmail.com.
We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility concerns.
