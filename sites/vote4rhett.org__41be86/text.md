Meet Rhett
Rhett Rosenquest Smith is a candidate for the Texas House of Representatives, District 116.
Rhett is committed to representing the people of his district with principled, conservative leadership.
A veteran and longtime Texan, Rhett believes public service begins with listening to the people you represent and standing firm on the principles that have made Texas strong.
