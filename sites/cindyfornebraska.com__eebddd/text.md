Meet Cindy
Cindy is a mom, small business owner, and community leader.
She moved to Omaha over 30 years ago for our great public schools, affordable lifestyle, and thriving business community.
She built a career in human resources and recruitment, and started a family with her husband, Fred.
Now, the two run a small insurance and financial services business and remain involved in the community.
Cindy is the president of Better Ballots Nebraska, on the board of Nonpartisan Nebraska, and volunteers with various organizations such as the Food Bank for the Heartland.

On the Issues
- Ending Our High Property Taxes Nebraskans continue to suffer from high property taxes.
Young families are struggling to be able to afford a home and seniors are being priced out of theirs.
I am committed to bringing bipartisan solutions to fix our broken property tax system so you can keep more of what you earn.
- Strengthening Public Education Every child deserves a high quality education that prepares them for a career.
I will work to ensure our schools have the funding, staff, and resources they need to to set up each Nebraskan for success and will stand up to political attacks on our schools.
- Supporting Workers and Small Businesses Nebraska has over 76,000 regulations on businesses, driving up costs for consumers.
We must regularly examine these regulations and remove outdated provisions which hurt small businesses.
I will also support workforce development programs to help workers obtain higher wages and businesses end staffing shortages.
