Why I’m Running
As residents of one of the most talked about cities in the nation, we often times feel as though the highlights and the accolades are not our reality.
While our legislators’ are playing politics with the lives and well being of our communities, people have approached me to know what I would do differently.
As State Representative, I will put District 19 first and be present in Harrisburg to fight for our needs.
We need a representative that will support policies that promote economic growth, entrepreneurship, enable our young people to stay in and succeed in school, and that allow our communities to grow without displacing longtime residents.
We need someone that will advocate for and support a formula for success that will change the realities for residents.
If you believe that we can have a brighter future, I want you to join my campaign today.
We deserve access to opportunities, accountable leadership, and a government that works for all of our communities.
When you vote, I humbly ask that you support me; a candidate that will tirelessly fight for you and put our communities needs above politics.
Yours in the movement,
Aerion Andrew Abney
