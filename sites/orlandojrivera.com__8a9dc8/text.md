Donate Now Volunteer Our Priorities 01 PUBLIC SAFETY Supporting law enforcement and keeping our communities safe 02 SCHOOL CHOICE Empowering families with educational freedom and opportunity 03 JOBS & GROWTH Creating economic opportunity and cutting red tape for businesses Community Leadership ▸ Chairman, Rochester Hispanic Business Association ▸ Commissioner, City of Rochester Civil Service Commission ▸ NAHREP Top 250 Award Recipient ▸ Multiple Sales Master Awards (GRAR) Meet Orlando A Voice for Our Community Orlando Rivera is a licensed real estate professional and longtime community leader with deep roots in Rochester.
He currently serves as Chairman of the Rochester Hispanic Business Association, where he works to support small businesses, promote job creation, and expand economic opportunity across the region.
Orlando also serves as a Commissioner on the City of Rochester Civil Service Commission, helping ensure fair hiring practices, transparency, and accountability in city government.
His record of service includes leadership and volunteer roles with the Knights of Columbus, the Cancer Support Community at Gilda's Club Rochester, and the American Red Cross of Western New York.
Recognized for both professional excellence and community impact, Orlando has earned multiple Sales Master Awards from the Greater Rochester Association of Realtors and has received national recognition from the National Association of Hispanic Real Estate Professionals as a recipient of its prestigious Top 250 award, honoring the top Hispanic real estate professionals in the United States.
Driven by service, integrity, and results, Orlando Rivera is committed to putting the community first and delivering responsible, transparent leadership for Rochester.
