As a result, our cities and towns are facing the burden of unplanned growth without the resources to support it.
If elected, I will fight for collaborative housing policy solutions that respect local voices and prioritize building infrastructure first.
Public Education: Investing in Our Future As an education professional, I see firsthand that investing in our schools and supporting our teachers is non-negotiable.
Utah’s demographics are changing rapidly, and our school funding needs to change with them.
I am committed to fighting for a responsive, fully funded public education system that protects our classrooms and gives every child the resources they need to succeed.
Childcare: Empowering the Workforce Access to affordable, reliable childcare powers our economy by allowing working families to enter and stay in the workforce.
Unfortunately, skyrocketing childcare costs have pushed quality care out of reach for too many Utah families, forcing parents to choose between a paycheck and staying home.
If elected, I will champion solutions that lower costs for parents, support our childcare providers, and ensure working families have the solid foundation they need to thrive.
