Bianca is a dedicated public education professional and civil rights advocate running to represent Utah House District 7.
With deep roots in Ogden, Bianca understands that the strength of our state relies on the strength of our neighborhoods.
From the classroom to the state capitol, Bianca is committed to fighting for strong public schools, sustainable resource management, and transparent, accountable representation.
Let’s build a brighter future for House District 7, together.
As a working mother and the proud wife of a veteran, Bianca believes this campaign is about protecting the fabric of our community.
For her, starts with investing in public education, supporting our workforce, and ensuring housing affordability.
Real leadership starts with listening, and effective policy begins with understanding the everyday lives of the people it impacts.
