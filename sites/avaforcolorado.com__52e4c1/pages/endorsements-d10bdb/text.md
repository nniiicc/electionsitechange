The Colorado Fraternal Order of Police (FOP) proudly endorse Representative Ava Flanell for election to Colorado House District 14.
As Colorado’s largest law enforcement organization, FOP represents more than 10,000 sworn law enforcement officers dedicated to protecting the health and safety of our communities.
Representative Flanell has been a critical partner in advancing effective, evidence-based public safety policies at the Colorado State Capitol that respect constitutional rights.
She understands the realities facing front-line officers, values their expertise, and consistently supports policies that strengthen public safety.
A business owner, Representative Flanell brings extensive experience in business, innovation, and fiscal responsibility to her legislative efforts.
She is a key member of the Judiciary and Education Committees while also championing issues critical to her constituents and all Coloradans, including affordability, responsible government, good business, public safety, and constitutional rights.
FOP is confident that Representative Flanell will continue to be accessible, thoughtful, and a fighter for her district as she works to advance smart, effective policy at the Capitol.
She is well-respected, principled, and has built productive relationships that position her as a leading, influential voice for the people of HD14 who works tirelessly to address the needs of her district and the state.
Electing the right person to serve as a State Representative is a serious responsibility – one that is immensely important to the people of Colorado.
Representative Flanell’s record of service, integrity, and commitment to public safety has earned our endorsement.
The Colorado Fraternal Order of Police respectfully asks you to join us in supporting Representative Ava Flanell for election to Colorado House District 14.
