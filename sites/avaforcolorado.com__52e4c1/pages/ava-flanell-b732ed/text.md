VOTE FOR AVA
Voting plays a critical role in shaping Colorado’s future, especially in highly competitive districts that can be decided by just a handful of votes.
One striking example is House District 16, where the 2024 election was decided by only three votes.
This reality underscores why every ballot matters and why your decision to vote for Ava Flanell can truly make a difference.
In 2024, Colorado Republicans gained three seats in the House, ending the Democratic supermajority.
This was a significant milestone after more than a decade without a single Republican House pickup.
Republicans also gained a seat in the Senate.
This progress shows that momentum is shifting.
We must continue building on this momentum to level the playing field so that unaffiliated voters, moderates, and Republicans have their voices represented in the legislature.
