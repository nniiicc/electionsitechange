My skills and experience position me as a leader who can put the needs of working families and my community first.
Now, more than ever, we need leaders in Madison willing to put good faith efforts into resolving our state’s problems.
Leaders that are willing to forward and advance workable solutions for the people of Wisconsin.
I am a leader who will put people over politics.
Kewaunee has always been my home; it is where I was born and raised.
I am a proud graduate of Kewaunee High School, after which, I attended the University of Wisconsin-Green Bay, where I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Human Development.
After college, I married my husband Brett.
We reside in Kewaunee County in the Town of Carlton, raising our young daughter, Norah.
