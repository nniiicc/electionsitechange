As a husband, father, educator, community leader, and conservative advocate, Mike has spent his life giving back to his community and fighting for the causes and values he believes in. ​ Mike graduated from St.
Xavier High School in 1981 before earning his degree in History from the University of Cincinnati in 1985.
He went on to graduate from the University of Cincinnati College of Law in 1988 and later completed a Master of Education from Mount St.
Joseph University in 1999. ​ A respected local educator with a strong background in law, Mike has built a career rooted in service, leadership, and advocacy.
Through his community involvement and volunteer work, he has developed strong relationships across Green Township, Hamilton County, the Ohio State Legislature, the Governor’s Office, the Ohio Department of Developmental Disabilities, and Ohio’s federal congressional delegation.
Mike has also served as a guardian ad litem in Hamilton County Juvenile and Dependency Courts.

Mike Odioso is delivering conservative results for Ohio's 30th District.
Mike is committed to fighting for the needs of all district residents and is working hard to ensure Ohio remains a strong, safe, and vibrant place to live, work, and raise a family.
Protecting Victims and Standing up Against Violent Criminals Closes crowdfunding loopholes for violent criminals Rep.
Mike Odioso's HB 505 prevents people accused or convicted of violent crimes from profiting through online fundraisers and requires crowdfunding platforms to provide greater transparency over how donated funds are used.
Tackling Affordability and Lowering Child Care Costs Expands child care access for working families Rep.
Mike Odioso's Workforce Investment Now (WIN) Child Care pilot program helps working families access child care and support workforce participation.
Leading the Way to Strengthen Courtroom Integrity Protects grand juror privacy and strengthens courtroom integrity Rep.
Mike Odioso's Grand Jury Privacy and Protection Act Protects grand jurors from intimidation or harassment by exempting their names, home addresses, and grand-jury foreperson signatures from public records disclosure.
Pushing for Student Privacy in our Schools Protects student privacy in school directory information Rep.
Mike Odioso's HB 127 Protects student privacy by allowing schools to withhold directory information from public records requests.
Standing Up for Women and Protecting the Unborn Strengthens informed-consent protections before elective abortions Rep.
Mike Odioso's SHE WINS Act Requires women seeking an elective abortion to receive informed-consent information and meet with a physician at least 24 hours before the procedure, while preserving exceptions for miscarriage, ectopic pregnancy, and medical emergencies.
Advocating for Disabled Ohioans Helps Ohioans with disabilities stay in the workforce Rep.
Mike Odioso's HB 581 helps Ohioans with disabilities keep working by allowing up to $20,000 in unearned income to be disregarded when determining eligibility for key Medicaid work-support programs.
