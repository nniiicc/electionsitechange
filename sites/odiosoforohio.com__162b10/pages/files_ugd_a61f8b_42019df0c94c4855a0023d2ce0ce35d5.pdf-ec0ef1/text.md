FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: info@odiosoforohio.com March 5, 2024 Sheriff Richard Jones Endorses Mike Odioso for State Representative Green Township, OH — On Tuesday morning, Mike Odioso announced that he has been endorsed by Butler County Sheriff Richard Jones in his campaign for the Ohio House of Representatives from Ohio's 30th District.
In his endorsement of Odioso, Sheriff Jones said "Mike is a strong conservative with a long track record of helping conservative candidates and causes.
I look forward to partnering with him to continue building a strong and vibrant region here in Southwest Ohio." In announcing the endorsement, Odioso said "I'm honored to have the endorsement of one of Ohio's leading voices in law enforcement.
Sheriff Jones has done great work in Butler County and I look forward to working with him to support all of our first responders here in Southwest Ohio and across our state." Sheriff Jones joins a long list of leaders backing Odioso in his run for the statehouse.
Other endorsements include Rep.
Bill Seitz, Hamilton County Prosecutor Melissa Powers, and Former Congressman Steve Chabot.
