FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: info@odiosoforohio.com November 6, 2024 Mike Odioso Thanks Supporters Following Election to the Ohio Legislature Green Township, OH — Mike Odioso issued the following statement following his election to the Ohio House of Representatives on Tuesday.
"I am humbled by the overwhelming support and confidence placed in me by the voters of Ohio’s 30th House District.
To those who supported our campaign: I am incredibly grateful for the time, energy, and passion each of you dedicated—whether you knocked on doors, worked a poll, shared our message, or offered words of encouragement.
I am deeply honored to represent the residents of our district and am eager to get to work for you at the Ohio Statehouse.
Together, we will build a brighter future for our families, our neighborhoods, and all who call this community home!" ### odiosoforohio.com | info@odiosoforohio.com PAID FOR BY MIKE ODIOSO FOR OHIO
