FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: info@odiosoforohio.com February 18, 2026 Mike Odioso Officially Qualifies for the 2026 Ballot Green Township, OH — State Representative Mike Odioso has officially qualified for the ballot in the 2026 election, marking an important milestone as he looks ahead to continuing his work in the Ohio Legislature.
Since taking office, Odioso has focused on advancing practical legislation that protects Ohio families.
During the current General Assembly, three bills he sponsored as primary author successfully passed the Ohio House: House Bill 386, which requires individuals to present a valid photo ID when applying for a marriage license, ensuring greater integrity and juvenile safety in the marriage licensing process.
House Bill 260, legislation that modernizes and streamlines existing law to eliminate inefficiencies, reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens, and improve how government serves Ohioans.
House Bill 127, which permits schools to withhold student directory information.
This strengthens student privacy protections and gives families greater control over their children’s personal information.
Odioso said qualifying for the ballot signals the beginning of the next phase of the campaign and an opportunity to build on the work already underway.
“I’m proud of the work we’ve accomplished and the results we’ve delivered for the Westside,” Odioso said.
“There’s more to be done and I’m excited to continue that work in Columbus on behalf of Ohio’s 30th District.” The campaign’s strong momentum began with a successful launch in August 2025 and has continued steadily since.
In the few short months following his August reelection kickoff, Odioso has received broad and sustained support from community members, supporters, and leaders.
The campaign continues to build on that support as efforts move forward toward the 2026 General Election.
Odioso also emphasized his appreciation for those who have supported him along the way.
“I’m incredibly thankful for everyone who has supported this effort—volunteers, supporters, and community members alike.
I don’t take that trust lightly, and I look forward to the campaign ahead.” With ballot access secured, the campaign will remain focused on engaging with constituents and continuing to build trust amongst the residents of the 30th District.
