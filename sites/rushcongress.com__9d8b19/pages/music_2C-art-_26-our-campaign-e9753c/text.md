Through my campaign and Gaia Knights, I am developing songs about a more just society, a healthier planet, and the lives we share.
I offer these songs freely to listeners and welcome collaboration with musicians and visual artists.
Why I Use AI I see AI as drawing from generations of human knowledge, creativity, and experience.
My purpose in using that shared inheritance is to contribute messages that help people and encourage us to protect humanity’s future.
I carry out much of my campaign and Gaia Knights work alone, with limited resources and time.
AI tools under my direction help me develop songs, graphics, and other creative materials I otherwise could not produce at this scale.
I shape the messages, revise the work, and take responsibility for what I choose to share.
The recorded musical performances are generated with AI, and I acknowledge that openly.
I respect human artists and their concerns about consent, credit, compensation, and livelihoods.
I want to build relationships with people who can bring their own talents to these projects.
An Invitation to Musicians and Artists I am looking for musicians and singers who would like to help form a live Gaia Knights band, performing these songs at community gatherings, campaign events, and other live settings.
I also welcome existing bands and independent performers who want to interpret the songs.
Bring your own genre and arrangement while keeping the lyrics intact.
Please collaborate with me on shorter versions or wording changes so the core meaning remains faithful.
Send me your recordings.
I would love to hear them and help promote your work, with credit to you as the performer and acknowledgment of Gaia Knights, my campaign, or both as the source.
I would also love to receive human-created artwork inspired by the songs: paintings, illustrations, photography, song covers, album artwork, and creative work for Gaia Knights or the campaign.
I’m especially interested in work created without generative AI.
We will agree on permission and credit before using submitted artwork.
I want people to discover the artists contributing to this project.
My Responsibility in Using AI My use of AI does not mean support for every direction the technology is taking.
I oppose the pursuit of artificial general intelligence and artificial superintelligence and support a moratorium on their advancement.
I also take environmental impacts seriously.
I live off-grid in a tiny home, produce my home electricity through solar panels, and power my computers and electric vehicles from that system.
I continue to reduce fossil-fuel use.
I recognize that remote AI services have their own resource footprint.
I intend to become more selective about generation and reduce usage as my core creative projects are completed.
Help Bring This Work to Life Much of this work has been a solo effort.
I want to build a community of people creating and working together.

PAY ATTENTION — Are We Failing Our Children?
A New Gaia Knights Song About Childhood, Technology, Education, and Our Responsibility to the Next Generation “Everybody wants their attention.
Who's paying attention to them?” What happens when we raise an entire generation in a world designed to compete for every second of their attention?
PAY ATTENTION , the latest song from Gaia Knights, asks a difficult but essential question: Are our children failing to meet society's expectations, or are we failing to provide the environment they need to thrive?
Through a powerful musical narrative, the song follows the everyday experiences of children, parents, and teachers struggling to connect in an increasingly distracted world.
A young child tries to get a parent's attention.
A tired teacher struggles to reach students.
An exhausted mother hands her child a tablet for a moment of peace.
A teenager scrolls late into the night, caught in an endless stream of digital stimulation.
These are not stories about bad children, bad parents, or bad teachers.
They are stories about a society that has allowed technology, economic pressures, and the demands of modern life to reshape childhood.
Childhood Is Practice for Being Human One of the song's central messages is that childhood is not simply preparation for tests, grades, or future employment.
Childhood is when we learn to cooperate, disagree, negotiate, imagine, create, fail, recover, and understand one another.
These essential human experiences cannot always be replaced by a screen.
Yet children are increasingly growing up surrounded by digital systems designed to capture their attention, while opportunities for unstructured play, meaningful relationships, and personal discovery compete for time.
A Call for Adult Responsibility PAY ATTENTION challenges the tendency to blame children for behaviors that may be influenced by the environment adults have created.
It asks parents, educators, technology companies, policymakers, and society as a whole to reconsider our priorities.
We need to support families, give parents more time with their children, strengthen education, and take seriously the effects of digital technology on childhood development.
The goal is not to reject technology.
It is to ensure that technology serves humanity—especially our children—rather than allowing human development to become secondary to commercial interests.
Music With a Message Created and produced by Rob Rush [Ruszkowski] , Independent write-in candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Georgia's 14th Congressional District, PAY ATTENTION is part of the Gaia Knights music project: Songs for a Brighter Tomorrow .
Gaia Knights uses music to explore the challenges facing humanity and encourage people to imagine a better future.
The message of PAY ATTENTION is simple but urgent: “The children aren't failing us.
We're failing the children.” Our children deserve more than criticism.
They deserve our time, attention, understanding, and commitment to building a better world.
