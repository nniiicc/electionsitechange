Born in Franklin, North Carolina and raised in a hardworking, blue-collar family, I learned early the values of faith, craftsmanship, and community.
I built my career in the construction trades—working my way from framing and roofing to leading timber framing crews that now build homes, pavilions, and chapels across the country.
Today, my wife, daughter, and I live near Clayton, where we are proud to call Rabun County home.
I’m running for Georgia House District 10 because families here deserve leaders who understand the real cost of groceries, housing, and healthcare—and who are ready to fight for affordable living, strong local businesses, and a future where every Georgian has a fair shot.
I believe in bringing people together, not dividing them, and in building a Georgia that works for everyone.
