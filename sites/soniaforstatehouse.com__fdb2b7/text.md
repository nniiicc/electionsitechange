Meet Sonia Forty-eight years ago, I arrived in this country from the West Indies with my parents and a heart full of hope.
I later developed a deep belief in the American Dream.
I didn’t come with a title or a political pedigree; I came with the values my parents instilled in me: a love for God, a commitment to hard work, a strong character, and the understanding that an education is the key that unlocks every door.
My journey as an immigrant has defined how I see our community.
I chose South Carolina, and specifically Richland County, because it is where I came to work and now call home.
Richland County is a place where families look out for one another and where faith is the cornerstone of our lives.
I’ve lived the beauty of the American Dream, working my way from entry-level roles into the financial halls of Merrill Lynch and top firms for legal consulting.
But I’ve also seen that for too many of our neighbors, that dream appears further out of reach.
I am running for the South Carolina State House for District 78 because I believe that listening to the people is the only way to protect the legacy, advance prosperity and peace in our neighborhoods, and to be a true representative.
For our Children: The Promise of Education As a high school teacher in Richland School District Two, I have seen the brilliance in our children every day.
But I also see a system that is struggling and yes, failing our kids.
I know what it’s like to work hard to give your children a better life.
I saw my parents do that.
It breaks my heart to see people who ensure their own children have every advantage of an elite private education while voting to keep their children in schools that are ranked near the bottom of the nation.
I believe quality education shouldn’t be a luxury for the few, it should be a reality for every child in District 78, and South Carolina.
For our Families: Keeping Life Affordable We have all felt the squeeze lately.
Whether it’s rising property taxes or the cost of insurance, it is getting harder for hardworking families to get ahead.
My background in finance isn’t just a line on a resume; it is a tool I will use to protect your family budget.
I’m reporting for duty to be a watchdog for your tax dollars, ensuring that the system works for you, not the other way around.
We deserve a representative who understands that a prosperous community starts with an affordable home.
For our Community: Safety and Peace Our neighborhoods should be sanctuaries of peace.
As a mother and a Christian, I believe we are called to be our brother’s keeper.
That means ensuring our streets are safe, our seniors are protected, and our local businesses can thrive.
I am committed to working with our community leaders to ensure that Richland County remains a place where families can grow and thrive for generations to come.
A Heart for Service My life was nearly taken from me years ago by a sudden brain hemorrhage.
I had to relearn how to walk and talk, read and write.
That experience didn't just teach me resilience; it taught me that every day is a gift from God intended for service.
I am not a career politician looking for a promotion.
I am a neighbor, a teacher, and a fellow disciple of Jesus Christ who is ready to roll up my sleeves and do the hard work of making our district the best place in South Carolina to call home.
I am Sonia Pollard, and I am asking for your trust and your vote.
Let’s build a future for Richland County that is safe, prosperous, and full of opportunity for everyone.
