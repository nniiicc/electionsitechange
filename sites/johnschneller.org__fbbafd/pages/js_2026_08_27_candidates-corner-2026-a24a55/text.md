John sat down with Ray Chadwick for a interview on Bedford’s community television station.
It’s my favorite time of Year!
Candidates Corner Interview time.
This is a “cherished” chance I had to sit down with former long-time Chairman of the Granite State Taxpayers Association, Ray Chadwick, to talk about how the prior two years were in Concord and what we can expect from me and from Concord in the coming two years and beyond, if Bedford voters choose to send me back for another term.
It has been a pleasure serving Bedford voters and I hope I have a chance to continue for another two years on the Science, Technology and Energy Committee where much is happening that will benefit New Hampshire residents.
Under Republican leadership we will continue to tamp down costly and burdensome regulation and taxes to support our existing businesses as well as attract new and vibrant businesses to our state.
The interview is airing on BCTV channels 16 and HD1072 between Friday, August 28 through Election Day.
