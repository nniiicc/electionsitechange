Key Issues
What Dave Stands for
Restoring Faith in the American Dream
Dave Beckwith believes in conservative leadership, focusing on economic vitality, safe and secure communities, and fiscal responsibility.
These core themes form a governing vision built on order, growth, accountability, and national strength.
Every policy pillar is an answer to the question:
“What's standing between the people of VA-10 and their American Dream right now, and how do we remove it?”
Learn more about each of his pillars below.
RESTORING ECONOMIC VITALITY
Dave will fight to keep taxes low, cut burdensome regulations, and pursue an all-of-the-above energy strategy that lowers costs for families and small businesses.
FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY
Dave believes Washington should live by the same rules Virginia families do: don't spend more than you take in.
He will be a steadfast voice for eliminating wasteful spending and combating the fraud that costs taxpayers up to $521 billion every year.
SAFETY AND SECURITY IN OUR COMMUNITIES
Dave is a staunch supporter of law enforcement and believes that well-resourced law enforcement agencies make safer communities.
UPHOLDING OUR CONSTITUTION
Dave will defend the constitutional rights of every law-abiding citizen.
The Constitution is not a suggestion, it is the foundation this country stands on, and every right it guarantees deserves a Congressman who will defend it without exception.
RELEASE AMERICAN ENERGY
Dave supports an all-of-the-above energy strategy that ends market-distorting subsidies and unleashes American innovation, including a bold reinvestment in nuclear power to lower costs and create good jobs.
EDUCATION FOR OUR MODERN ECONOMY
Dave will advocate for parental rights and work to expand access to vocational training and apprenticeships, ensuring every student has a path to a high-paying career.
SUPPORTING OUR VETERANS
Dave will fight for the veterans who defended this country, ensuring they get timely healthcare, real support for their mental health, and the benefits they've earned without the bureaucratic runaround.
FOSTERING HEALTHY COMMUNITIES
As a two-time cancer survivor, Dave knows firsthand what it means to depend on quality care when it matters most.
Dave will fight to ensure every VA-10 family has access to quality, affordable health care without Washington dictating your choices or driving up your costs.
