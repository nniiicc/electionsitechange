America is the world's leading energy producer.
We have the resources, the technology, and the workforce to power our country affordably and reliably for generations.
And yet Washington continues to pick winners and losers, manipulate markets with subsidies, and layer regulation upon regulation that drives up costs for families and businesses.
That stops when Dave Beckwith gets to Congress.
Dave believes in a true all-of-the-above energy strategy, one that lets the market, not the government, determine the best mix of sources.
That means ending the distorting subsidies that artificially inflate some energy sectors while strangling others, removing the bureaucratic obstacles that slow down domestic production, and giving American innovators the freedom to compete and win.
Lower energy costs are not just an economic issue.
They are a national security issue, a family budget issue, and a jobs issue all at once.
Central to Dave's energy vision is a serious, long-overdue reinvestment in nuclear power.
Nuclear is safe, clean, reliable, and American-made, and it has been held back for decades by regulatory inertia and political timidity.
Dave will fight to streamline nuclear permitting, support the development of next-generation reactor technology, and make sure Virginia and the rest of the country can access the abundant, affordable baseload power that nuclear provides.
The future of American energy is bright, if Washington gets out of the way.
