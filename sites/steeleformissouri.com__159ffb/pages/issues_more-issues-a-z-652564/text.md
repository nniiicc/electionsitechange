Data-centers have been around for years, but new expansions that incorporate AI have impacts to our people to consider.
Developers with data center projects need to be transparent with the public, no sweetheart deals.
The people that live and work in our community, that should staff these projects, should be included in these decisions.
I support projects that will be a guaranteed asset to our community.
S - Safety I support fully-funding public safety budgets so our first responders have the resources they need to keep us safe.
When have diverse and well-equipped professional public safety teams, we can better address the needs in our community.
T - Term Limits I support term limits at all levels of legislature to maintain a government of the people, for the people, and by the people.
