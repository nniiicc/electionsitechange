Steele for missouri believes in
Affordable Healthcare
All families deserve affordable and accessible healthcare.
No one should have to choose vital medications over other basic necessities, nor should anyone face bankruptcy due to a sudden medical emergency.
To achieve this I will advocate for policies to:
- Protect the Affordable Care Act
- Fight for paid sick leave and Prop A which Missourians overwhelmingly voted for in 2024
- Insurance reform and accountability
- Defend and expand access to abortion care and reproductive health providers.
- Missourians, including District 12, overwhelmingly voted for amendment 3 in 2024.
Republican politicians forced another ban on abortion onto the 2026 ballot, and named it amendment 3.
Additionally, I believe the only person who should make decisions on a woman's body is that woman in consultation with her health care provider.
Lawyers and politicians have no place in your doctor's office.
