Home Candidate Issues-Solutions For the people of Coös County & Northern Grafton County My Top 5 Priorities for the North Country Senate Dist 1 🏠🏫 1.
Keep the North Country Affordable Families, seniors, and young people should be able to afford to live, work, and raise their families here.
I will support policies that address the rising costs of housing, healthcare, energy, and everyday necessities so our communities can grow and thrive.
Our children should not have to leave the North Country just to find opportunity. 🌲💧 2.
Protect Our Land, Water, and Rural Communities The North Country's natural beauty is one of our greatest assets.
I will fight for responsible environmental policies that protect our drinking water, forests, lakes, and communities.
I oppose turning our region into a dumping ground for problems created elsewhere, and I believe environmental decisions must be based on sound science, transparency, and local input. 🩺➕ 3.
Improve Access to Quality Healthcare No one should have to travel hours to receive quality healthcare.
I will work to strengthen rural healthcare services, support our healthcare workforce, improve access to mental health care, and help ensure that seniors and working families can receive the care they need close to home. 🎓🔧 4.
Support Strong Schools and Workforce Opportunities Every student deserves the opportunity to succeed, whether they choose college, the trades, military service, or direct entry into the workforce.
I will advocate for strong public schools, career and technical education, apprenticeships, and workforce development programs that prepare people for good-paying jobs right here in New Hampshire. 💬🏛 5.
Bring North Country Voices to Concord Too often, decisions are made without listening to the people most affected by them.
My commitment is simple: I will listen, I will be accessible, and I will work across party lines to find practical solutions.
The people of Senate District 1 deserve a Senator who puts communities before politics and represents all residents with honesty, respect, and accountability.
