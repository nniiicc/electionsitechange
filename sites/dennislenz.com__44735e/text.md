When you vote this June, choose a State Senator who brings real-world experience, proven leadership, and an unwavering commitment to Montana values.
I deliver on all three, with a track record that puts conservative principles first and fights for a better future for our state.
As your Senator, I will continue working to grow Montana's economy by attracting new businesses and supporting the responsible development of our natural resources.
I will promote common-sense fiscal policies that reduce the burden on Montana families while providing the type of services we need most.
Most Importantly, I will continue to focus much of my energy on the issues that affect the children and elderly in our state.
I will continue to work for a more consistent child protection and foster care system for children that are removed from their parents.
Our elderly need to be helped with great dignity as they move into the last part of their life and need more state services.
I will also support a brighter future for all our children by supporting school choice and making our education system stronger, smarter, and more accountable.
