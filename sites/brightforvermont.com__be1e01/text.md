Meet Caroline
Hi!
I’m Caroline.
When I was born, my parents drove me straight home from the hospital to Georgia, Vermont- to the very house where I’m now raising my three kids as fifth-generation Vermonters.
I’m passionate about Vermont, Franklin County, and making this wonderful corner of the world a place where people can live the lives they want, raise the families they want, and pursue the American dream.
I’m a Unitarian Universalist minister, a longtime union flight attendant, and a small business owner, and I bring that mix of real life experience into everything I do.
I know how hard people here are working to make it all come together- because I’m in the middle of it myself.
I believe we can build a Vermont where more of us can truly thrive and where more people can afford to come home.
