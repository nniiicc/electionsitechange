SALEM, OR – In a major show of momentum for his campaign, State Senator David Brock Smith (R-Port Orford) , Republican candidate for U.S.
Senate, has earned the endorsements of three of Oregon’s leading Republican candidates for Governor: Marion County Commissioner Danielle Bethell, State Representative Ed Diehl, and State Senator Christine Drazan.
The endorsements mark a significant show of confidence in Brock Smith’s campaign and reinforce his standing as one of Oregon’s leading conservative voices fighting for affordability, accountability, and public safety.
“I am honored to have the support of respected leaders who know that Oregon cannot afford more of the same failed policies,” said Brock Smith .
“This campaign is bringing together Oregonians who are ready for accountability, affordability, and a fighter who will take their values to Washington.
