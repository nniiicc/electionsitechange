DAVID BROCK SMITH WINS THE GOP NOMINATION FOR U.S.
SENATE WILL TAKE ON JEFF MERKLEY IN THE GENERAL ELECTION
PORT ORFORD, OR – Today, Republican State Senator, David Brock Smith, secured the Republican nomination for the United States Senate following a hard-fought and competitive primary campaign, positioning himself to take on Senator Jeff Merkley this November.
Following the race being officially called three days after primary election day, David Brock Smith thanked voters and supporters across Oregon for embracing a campaign focused on affordability, public safety, economic opportunity, and restoring common sense to the federal government.
“Republicans across the Beaver State have sent a clear message: they’re tired of being ignored by political insiders and policies that make life harder for local businesses and working families,” said Smith.
“This campaign is about putting Oregon first.
Fighting for affordable living, safer communities, good-paying jobs, responsible government, and protecting the values that make our beloved state strong.
Oregon families deserve a Senator who understands what it means to balance a budget, run a business, and fight for local communities.
I’m running to give a voice to the people who feel forgotten; the workers, parents, farmers, fishermen, small business owners, and rural communities who keep Oregon moving.
This election is bigger than politics.
It’s about restoring hope, opportunity, and accountability for every Oregonian.
Together, we will build a better Oregon for today’s generation and so many generations to come,” Smith continued.
More About David Brock Smith: David is a third-generation resident and small businessman from Southwest Oregon who has spent his life serving his community and standing up for the people who live and work here.
His approach to public service is simple: working for you, not for political insiders.
David is running for U.S.
Senate to bring common sense, accountability, and a strong rural voice to the federal level, so that all the people of Oregon are heard, respected, and represented.
