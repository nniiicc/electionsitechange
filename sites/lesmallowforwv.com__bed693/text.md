West Virginia House of Delegates, District 67
I’m running for the West Virginia House of Delegates because I believe our state is strongest when leadership is grounded in real experience, shared values, and a genuine commitment to serve.
West Virginia has made meaningful progress, and I want to help build on that work with steady, practical leadership focused on results.I believe public service demands preparation, honesty, and accountability—not empty promises or political theater.
As a working attorney, I see firsthand how laws and policies affect families, small businesses, and local communities.
That experience reinforces the importance of preparation, accountability, and common sense in decision-making.
I was raised in an Appalachian, blue-collar culture that values hard work, personal responsibility, and earning your way, and those principles continue to guide how I serve.My faith and upbringing instilled in me a belief that leadership is about service, integrity, and doing what’s right—even when it’s not easy.
I believe in strong schools, safe communities, and an economy that rewards work and keeps families here at home.
Most importantly, I’m a husband and father.
My wife, Madison, and our children keep my priorities grounded and remind me every day why public service matters.
I’m running for office to help move our Mountain State forward—so our children, our grandchildren, and future generations can build their lives right here at home.
