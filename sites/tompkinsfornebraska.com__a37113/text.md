Meet Darin Tompkins Raised in rural northeast South Dakota, Darin grew up working alongside his father, an electrician and volunteer firefighter, and learned the value of hard work , accountability , and serving others .
After earning degrees in Microbiology and Biology, he spent over 30 years in science-based manufacturing—rising from quality control to lead an animal health vaccine plant for 12 years.
He didn’t get there by talking — but by solving problems, leading teams, and getting things done.
Darin stays active in the community through volunteering with Habitat for Humanity, food drives, and Eagle Scout projects—hands-on work that builds strong communities.
Why Now?
Nebraskans deserve leaders who listen, lead with common sense, and honor the will of the people.
Darin is running because working families are stretched thin, skilled trades and small businesses are overlooked, and politics often blocks practical solutions.
Darin brings fresh perspective, practical leadership, and Nebraska common sense to Lincoln.

Building a Strong Economy Darin has helped drive years of steady job growth in Nebraska.
With over 30 years of leadership experience, he played a key role in securing a $200 million investment in a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility — creating good-paying jobs right here at home.
He understands the regulations, permits, and tax policies that make businesses choose Nebraska — and he’ll use that expertise to strengthen communities, support hardworking families, and keep our economy growing for the long term.
Learn More Expanding Access to Affordable Healthcare Every Nebraskan deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare — no matter their zip code or income.
Rising medical costs are squeezing hardworking families, small business owners, seniors, and vulnerable populations, forcing too many to choose between paying bills and getting the care they need.
With over 30 years of experience solving problems at all levels of management, Darin knows how to bring people together, cut through red tape, and get results.
He’ll work to lower healthcare costs, strengthen rural healthcare access, and protect coverage for families, seniors, and those with preexisting conditions.
Darin will put his proven leadership to work to make Nebraska’s healthcare system more affordable, efficient, and focused on the people it serves.
Learn More Targeted Property Tax Relief for Nebraska Families With over 30 years of experience solving problems at all levels of management, Darin knows how to make systems work efficiently and deliver results.
He believes hardworking Nebraskans deserve targeted property tax relief that truly eases the burden on homeowners and working families — not broad breaks that benefit those who need it least.
Darin will use his proven leadership to ensure tax relief supports families, strengthens communities, and protects vulnerable populations while keeping Nebraska’s economy competitive for the long term.
