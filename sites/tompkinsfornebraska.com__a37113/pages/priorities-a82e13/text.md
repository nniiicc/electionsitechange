HOME About Darin Priorities VOLUNTEER CONTACT DONATE NOW Select Page Darin's Key Priorities Building a Strong Economy Darin knows how to bring jobs to Nebraska.
His leadership helped secure a $200 million investment in a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, creating good-paying jobs right here at home.
He understands the regulations, permits, and tax policies that make businesses choose Nebraska—and he’ll put that know-how to work for our communities.
Expanding Access to Affordable Healthcare Every Nebraskan deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare — no matter their zip code or income.
Rising medical costs are squeezing hardworking families, small business owners, seniors, and vulnerable populations, forcing too many to choose between paying bills and getting the care they need.
With over 30 years of experience solving problems at all levels of management, Darin knows how to bring people together, cut through red tape, and get results.
He’ll work to lower healthcare costs, strengthen rural healthcare access, and protect coverage for families, seniors, and those with preexisting conditions.
Darin will put his proven leadership to work to make Nebraska’s healthcare system more affordable, efficient, and focused on the people it serves.
Targeted Property Tax Relief for Nebraska Families With over 30 years of experience solving problems at all levels of management, Darin knows how to make systems work efficiently and deliver results.
He believes hardworking Nebraskans deserve targeted property tax relief that truly eases the burden on homeowners and working families — not broad breaks that benefit those who need it least.
Darin will use his proven leadership to ensure tax relief supports families, strengthens communities, and protects vulnerable populations while keeping Nebraska’s economy competitive for the long term.
Expanding Career and Technical Education Darin knows what it takes to keep Nebraska’s economy moving forward.
At the manufacturing facility he led, success depended on every kind of worker—scientists, engineers, diesel mechanics, welders, electricians, machinists, and technicians.
That’s why he partnered with public schools and community colleges to create awareness and strengthen career paths in technical and trade skills.
Darin believes every Nebraskan should have the chance to succeed and drive the innovation that defines the good life.
Making Government Work Like It Should For years, Darin led a manufacturing facility where he set goals to reduce costs and eliminate waste—producing real results through efficiency and smart systems.
He brings that same approach to Nebraska’s budget: ensuring every tax dollar is spent wisely to maximize results for families and communities.
Community Engagement Growing up in a rural community, Darin learned the true meaning of helping your neighbor.
When illness, job loss, or tragedy strikes, families rely on friends, neighbors, and local nonprofits for support.
Darin has lived these values—organizing food drives, building with Habitat for Humanity, and supporting Eagle Scout projects.
He also believes government has a role to play: offering a hand up, not a handout, so every Nebraskan has the chance to get back on their feet and thrive.
Protect Local Control and Rural Communities Darin believes the best decisions for rural Nebraska are made close to home.
Strong public schools, responsive city governments, and thriving family farms are the backbone of our communities.
Local voices—not distant bureaucrats—know what works best for our kids, our towns, and our land.
Darin will fight to keep control in the hands of Nebraskans, where it belongs.
