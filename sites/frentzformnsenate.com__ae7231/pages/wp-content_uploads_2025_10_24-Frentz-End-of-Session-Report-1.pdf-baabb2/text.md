Frentz Successes Senator Nick Frentz’s Office This past two-year biennium saw historic success at the legislature.
We passed bills supporting schools, reproductive freedoms, democracy, public safety, small businesses, children, working men and women, and a lot more.
The state’s economy is in solid shape with unemployment at a 50 year low, and business and personal incomes at all-time highs.
Minnesota now boasts a AAA credit rating, which saves us millions of dollars each year in borrowing costs.
However, not all of our work got done this past session – the two parties failed to find agreement on a bonding bill.

State Senator Nick Frentz 2024 CAPITOL REPORT 6/14/24 2:36 PM 2024 Session Highlights Tax Bill (HF5247) YES Vote I supported the bill for an important reason – the new MN Child Tax Credit.
The credit lifts as many as one-third of children out of poverty.
It provides up to $1,750 per child for eligible families.
Since February, this has already provided $321 million of benefits to these Minnesota families.
It is now also possible to receive benefits during the tax year, as opposed to having to wait until April of the following year.
Energy & Agriculture Omnibus (SF4942) YES Vote The Energy & Agriculture Omnibus bill advances our clean energy economy forward.
It also invests in meaningful agriculture policy, including research, soil health funding, and nitrate reduction in Southeastern Minnesota.
Transportation Bill (HF5242) YES Vote We are funding roads and bridges, an ongoing need every year.
We also struck a deal between Uber + Lyft drivers and their employers to give drivers a raise while keeping the companies in Minnesota.
Judiciary & Public Safety (HF5216) YES Vote The bill makes strong investments in our Department of Corrections and judicial system budgets.
It also contains provisions to prevent and reduce copper wire theft, a problem in many of our cities.
24 Frentz End of Session Report.indd 2 Local Impact Legislation Mankato Schools Absenteeism Program (HF5237) This pilot program provides $398,000 to Mankato area schools to study and improve student attendance.
The program will address which methods produce the most improvement in student attendance and will engage families, teachers, and communities.
Hotel Reclassification (SF4820) I chief authored this bill at the request of a local boutique hotel operator.
It allows a change in definition for “hotels” to allow a liquor license for smaller venues.
Environment Bill (SF3631) YES Vote The Environment Omnibus invests in our parks and various outdoor projects while protecting our natural resources.
It also sets up new waste management systems that will help reduce the volume of plastic packaging and decrease landfill use.
Human Services Bill (SF4399) YES Vote The bill provided targeted relief to communities, caregivers, and people who access disability services.
This includes rural families living with relatives who have complex mental health needs, people moving from congregate care facilities back to home, and more.

Bonding Bill DID NOT GET PASSED “Bonding” is state borrowing for larger projects and then paying off the loans with taxpayer money over time.
It requires both parties to provide “yes” votes.
The failure to reach a deal meant that important local projects did not receive funding.
We need to do better.
