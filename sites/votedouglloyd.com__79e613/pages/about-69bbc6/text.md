Cunningham and Jeffrey L.
Sauter.
That appointment began a decade long journey of service, accountability, and justice, one that I have proudly continued by winning four consecutive elections, including the county's first contested prosecutor's race since 1972.
Today, I am ready to take that experience to the next level, and that is why I am running to become Michigan's next Attorney General.
For the past 30 years, I have dedicated my career to the pursuit of justice, first as a front-line prosecutor, then as Eaton County's chief law enforcement officer, and always as a public servant committed to protecting both public safety and fairness under the law.
My record includes prosecuting and securing convictions in some of our community's most serious cases, including murder, rape, armed robbery, and large-scale drug trafficking, and even securing prosecutions of elected officials and individuals committing ballot theft.
At the same time, I have worked to strengthen our justice system by building a nationally recognized Economic Crimes Unit and expanding access to innovative specialty courts that give deserving offenders a second chance while still holding them accountable.
As Attorney General, I will bring that same no nonsense, tough but fair approach to serving the people of Michigan.
I believe our state deserves a top law enforcement officer who is experienced, steady handed, and focused on what truly matters.
Protecting victims, defending our constitutional rights, and upholding the rule of law without fear or favor must always come first.
This campaign will be a true statewide effort, and I know it will not be easy.
Reaching voters in every corner of Michigan will take hard work, energy, and the support of people who believe in restoring trust and integrity to our justice system.
Your help, whether through a contribution, putting up a yard sign, or simply spreading the word, is essential to building a system that works for everyone.
Michigan deserves an Attorney General who understands what it means to prosecute cases, lead with integrity, and make our communities safer.
I am ready to serve, and with your support, we will win this race together.
Thank you for standing with me, and together we can work together to take Michigan back.
Sincerely, Doug Lloyd Eaton County Prosecuting Attorney Biography The know-how Michigan needs.

Eaton County Prosecutor, Douglas R.
Lloyd, serves as the chief law enforcement officer for Eaton County.
In that capacity, Lloyd manages 10 attorneys and 15 administrative staff.
He is responsible for an approximate caseload averaging 6000 cases per year.
Lloyd is also responsible for the administration of the Eaton County Prosecutor's Office's domestic violence, drug, and economic crimes units.
Prior to being named Prosecutor in June 2013, Lloyd served as Assistant Prosecutor, Senior Assistant Prosecutor for the Economic Crimes Unit, and Chief Assistant Prosecutor.
The Eaton County Prosecutor's Office Economic Crimes Unit ("ECU") is a separate office located in Delta Township.
The ECU manages the bad check, welfare fraud, retail fraud, failure to return property/library property, social security fraud diversion programs as well consumer protection complaints for Eaton County.
The success of the ECU resulted in Lloyd being appointed as Special Prosecutor in Ingham and Clinton counties to handle their bad check and/or welfare fraud diversion cases.
In 2013, Lloyd was appointed by Governor Snyder to the Organized Retail Fraud Advisory Board.
He also served on the Insurance Fraud Task Force launched in September 2013 by Secretary of State Ruth Johnson.
Lloyd has also been confirmed by the Michigan Legislature to serve on the State Drug Treatment Court Advisory Committee.
Lloyd is a Past President for the Prosecuting Attorneys Association of Michigan.
Doug is the proud father of two sons, Matthew and Nicolas, both have which have graduated from MSU, and has been married to his wife Mina, a recently retired Assistant Prosecuting Attorney with the Ingham County Prosecutor's Office, for 30 years.
They reside in Delta Township.
