Aaron’s Vision
- Regional Equity Equitable treatment for Western Mass in funding and investment in our communities including regional employers and economic development engines like UMass.
- Protecting Open Spaces Robust investment in land conservation coupled with appropriate support for communities hosting recreational and protected spaces.
- Energy and Climate Change Urgent transition to a carbon neutral energy grid and the wholesale transition to renewable energy.
- Mental & Behavioral Health An all hands on deck approach to ensuring Western Mass has access to affordable mental and behavioral health care including wraparound addiction recovery.
- Reproductive Health Ensure women maintain their fundamental right to accurate, unbiased reproductive health care in Massachusetts.
- Education Provide adequate funding, resources, and support for our suburban and rural school districts
- Veterans Services Prioritize health care, job opportunities, and housing options for those who served and their families.
- Improving Transportation Meet our obligation to ensure that our roads, bridges, and sidewalks are in good repair and well maintained while moving forward with West/East Rail.
- Economic Development Fight for our fair share of economic development, funding, and resources supporting Western Massachusetts families and businesses.
