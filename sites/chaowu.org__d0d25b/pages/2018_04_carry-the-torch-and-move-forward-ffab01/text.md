I really like it and he motivated and changed this county.
If it were not for Dr.
King and the civil rights movement, I would not even be here, let alone running for BOE.
Civil rights movement never ends until everyone has the opportunity to succeed.
Education is one of the key parts of this movement and this dream.
We must provide equitable learning opportunities for each student.
We must also encourage our students to overcome all difficulties and pursue their dream.
