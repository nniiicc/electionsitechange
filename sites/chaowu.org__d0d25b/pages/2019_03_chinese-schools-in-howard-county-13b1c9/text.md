There are three Chinese schools in Howard County.
1: Howard County Chinese School.
They have Saturday’s class at Centennial High School and Sunday’s class at Howard Community College.
Their website is https://www.hccs-md.org/
2: Peiying Chinese School.
They have Saturday’s class at River Hill High School.
Their website is
http://www.peiying-md.org/contact-us/
3: Chinese Language School in Columbia.
They have their Sunday’s class at Howard High School.
Their website is http://www.clscweb.org/
Now it is the registration season if you or your child are interested in learning Chinese language or culture.
There are classes for students as young as four year old without any prior Chinese knowledge.
