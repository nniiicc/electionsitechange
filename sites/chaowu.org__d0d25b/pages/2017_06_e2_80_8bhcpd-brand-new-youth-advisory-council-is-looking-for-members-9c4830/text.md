26 Jun 2017 Chao Wu HCPD’s brand NEW Youth Advisory Council is looking for members!
This new council is designed to provide youth with a safe and engaging environment to interact with local law enforcement.
The group will meet monthly with members of the department.
Who can join?
Howard County residents in 8th-12th grade.
What are the benefits? • Earn student service hours • Advise the Chief of Police on youth concerns • Be a positive agent of change in your community, school or place of worship • Attend free educational and social field trips • Gain and strengthen life skills such as leadership, public speaking and social skills How do I apply?
Complete and return an application form: https://www.howardcountymd.gov/Departments/Police/Youth-Programs Questions?
Contact HCPD Youth Liaison PFC W.
