In South Carolina, legislators who are also lawyers get to handpick the judges they argue in front of.
Then they appear in court before those very same judges.
Everyone knows what that means — quiet pressure, whispered influence, and favorable rulings for those with power and access.
As Wes said on the show:
“The best justice money can buy in this state is hiring a lawyer-legislator.

It is a real conflict of interest, and we have to fix it.”

His new bill, backed by a coalition of reformers across the state, would eliminate legislative control over the selection of judges and restore checks and balances by handing appointment power to the governor — with legislative confirmation.
