Close Home About Pillars News Volunteer Contact Donate The 9 Pillars of Wes Climer’s Campaign 1.
Stop the Self-Dealing Politicians Wes filed a lawsuit to stop the unconstitutional $18,000 pay raise lawmakers gave themselves.
He believes public service should never be about personal profit.
In Washington, he’ll fight to expose corruption and force transparency on every dime spent.
2.
Stand Up for Trump, Stand Up for America Wes is proud to support President Donald Trump.
He’ll advance the America First agenda with no excuses—on immigration, energy, national security, and restoring the rule of law.
3.
Fix the Border or Shut It Down The open border is a national crisis.
Wes supports building the wall, reinstating Remain in Mexico, defunding sanctuary cities, and stopping fentanyl and human trafficking at the source.
4.
Protect Kids and Take Back the Classroom Wes will fight for parental rights, eliminate woke curriculum, and return control of education to parents and local communities.
He supports abolishing the federal Department of Education entirely.
5.
Cut Taxes, Shrink Government, and Return Power to the People Wes helped deliver $2 billion in tax cuts in South Carolina.
In Congress, he’ll vote to slash spending, block tax hikes, and restore federalism by returning power to states and citizens.
6.
End the Deep State Bureaucracy Wes supports eliminating bloated agencies, gutting executive overreach, and reinstating constitutional balance.
He backs reforms like DOGE to dismantle entrenched, unaccountable bureaucracies.
7.
Real Family Values — Not Just Talk As a father of five, Wes lives what he believes.
He’s pro-life, pro-parent, and pro-freedom.
He’ll defend faith, family, and the cultural foundations of American life.
8.
We Don’t Need More Republicans — We Need Better Ones Wes isn’t going to Washington to play along.
He’s going to fight weak Republicans and career politicians who sell out their voters.
No more talking points.
No more caving.
No more excuses.
9.
Fix Overdevelopment by Getting Government Working Together The 5th District is growing fast—but federal, state, and local governments aren’t keeping up.
Wes will fight to align all levels of government to ensure our infrastructure, schools, and services meet the needs of a booming population without destroying our quality of life.
