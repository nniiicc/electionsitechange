PRESS RELEASE FOR IMMEDIATE DISTRIBUTION OCT 2nd, 2026 JENN MACK TO ORDER FIRE SAFETY INSPECTIONS OF DATA CENTERS AND BATTERY STORAGE SITES IN TEXAS ON DAY ONE Campaign cites direct gubernatorial oversight of the Texas Department of Insurance and State Fire Marshal's Office as an immediate tool for landowner safety SPRING, TX — Jenn Mack said, “In accordance with Texas Government Code Sec.
417.008, I will, on my first day in office, direct the Texas Fire Marshal’s Office to conduct examinations of all data centers and battery storage facilities under construction or preparing for construction in the State of Texas.” I will ask for the Texas Department of Insurance to conduct an extensive audit of national reports relating to fires caused by or related to data centers and battery storage facilities across the US to determine our state’s preparedness and risk management protocols.
This audit must be provided before the end of the 2027 general session for our lawmakers to make appropriate decisions related to data center guardrails that they have promised their constituents.
This audit must be conducted using independent research, one without affiliation to the industry and another guided by the industry, for full transparency.
Any data centers or battery storage facilities found to be a significant threat of safety to their local community will be investigated, injunctions filed, and held to compliance by the proper authorities.
