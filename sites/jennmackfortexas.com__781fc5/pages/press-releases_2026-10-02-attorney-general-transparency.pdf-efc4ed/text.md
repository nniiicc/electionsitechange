PRESS RELEASE FOR IMMEDIATE DISTRIBUTION OCT 2nd, 2026 JENN MACK CALLS FOR DAY-ONE PARTNERSHIP WITH AG TO EXPAND TRANSPARENCY ENFORCEMENT BEYOND WATER REPORTING TO LOCAL GOVERNMENT CONDUCT SPRING, TX — The Texas Attorney General's office has opened a statewide investigation into hundreds of data center developments for failing to comply with mandatory state water-use reporting — with nearly 83% failing or refusing to respond to the state’s mandatory survey.
Across Texas, homeowners, legacy landowners, and multiple organizations are sounding the alarm on a wave of AI data centers, 765kV transmission lines, battery storage sites, and wind and solar farms moving forward with little public input and even less transparency.
Jenn Mack said “This process is broken, and it's moving too fast for anyone to catch the damage before it's done.” The Texas Office of the Attorney General (OAG) has responsibility regarding municipal noncompliance and public transparency.
Actions available to their office include, but are not limited to: Challenging local government actions taken in violation of the Open Meetings Act, filing mandamus actions under the Public Information Act to compel the release of records, or filing quo warranto petitions to challenge officials exceeding their authority.
There have been countless reports of overstepping by City Councils, Economic Development Corporations, and County Officials across our country.
I want Texas to lead the way in transparency and cooperation.
Texans deserve to know what their city councils, economic development corporations, and county governments are doing with public money and public authority.
If elected, one of my first actions as Governor will be to collaborate with the Office of the Attorney General to investigate the numerous complaints of TOMA violations occurring, lack of transparency, and violations of our citizens’ rights.
I will call for an immediate moratorium on 765kV transmission approvals, large-scale battery storage siting, and data centers, both new and in progress, until we can have a special session, putting real enforceable guardrails in place, while assessing the damage already caused to the citizens of Texas — and I will appoint PUC and TCEQ commissioners who will honor that pause.
Citizens are encouraged to contact the Attorney General’s Office and CC our office with their documented TOMA violations.
Our team will add them to an ongoing list held by our campaign office to be made public on our website October 7th.
