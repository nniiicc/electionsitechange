Home About Issues News Donate Shop Contact Us Toggle Menu About Jenn Mack Raphoon Independent Candidate for Texas Governor Jenn Mack isn't a career politician.
She spent years turning complicated operations into success stories, then left to commit to this campaign and preserve the culture and values of Texans.
Her career taught her that big problems get solved only when leadership listens, plans, and delivers measurable results — not when parties fight for credit.
Raised with a deep respect for faith, family, and hard work, Jenn learned early that freedom survives only when ordinary citizens take responsibility for protecting it.
Her decision to step away from the private sector and run for governor came from the same conviction that drives every true Texan: when something you love is being attacked, you don't complain — you take the reins.
Jenn believes Texans are ready to reclaim the spirit that built the Republic — self-reliance, transparency, and respect for every person's liberty under one law.
She's running as an independent because she believes both parties have become too invested in winning factions instead of solving problems, catering to special interests and unelected bureaucrats that grow the political machine.
