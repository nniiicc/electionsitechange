PRESS RELEASE FOR IMMEDIATE DISTRIBUTION OCT 2nd, 2026 JENN MACK CALLS FOR NEW ENFORCEMENT DIVISION TO PROTECT TEXAS AGRICULTURAL INFRASTRUCTURE, FARMERS, AND RANCHERS Campaign to push Legislature and work with Texas Department of Agriculture to safeguard the industry that keeps Texas the nation's agricultural leader SPRING, TX — Texas farmers, ranchers, and the rural communities that depend on them are facing growing pressure from encroaching development, water competition, and land-use conflicts that threaten the infrastructure holding up the state's agricultural economy.
Jenn Mack today called for a new, dedicated enforcement capability to protect that foundation.
"Texas leads the nation in agriculture because generations of farmers and ranchers built it that way,” Jenn said.
