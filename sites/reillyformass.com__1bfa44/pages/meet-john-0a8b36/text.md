John Reilly Jr. is an award-winning DJ, producer, computer/audio engineer, and entrepreneur with a career built on creativity, leadership, and real-world results.
Recognized as “Worcester’s Best DJ” seven times, he has spent years mastering the art of connecting with people—whether through music, business, or community engagement.
With a background in both computer and audio engineering, Reilly has worked with some of the most recognizable organizations in the country, including MTV in Santa Monica, as well as with Google and Android.
His professional experience spans complex licensing, developing a global brand, marketing strategies, budgeting, and large-scale event coordination—skills that require precision, adaptability, and a deep understanding of how to bring people together and drive success.
Beyond the tech and entertainment industries, Reilly has also worked in the legal field, serving as a legal assistant at several respected law firms in both Massachusetts and California.
This experience strengthened his attention to detail, understanding of regulatory systems, and ability to navigate complex challenges.
A committed member of his local community, Reilly served as a coach for Murdock High School softball, leading the team to a league championship in 2018.
His leadership on and off the field reflects a belief in discipline, teamwork, and mentorship.
At the center of everything he does is family.
Reilly is a proud, full-time father to his daughter, Kalea, who graduated high school in 2025 while simultaneously earning her associate degree through the Pathways Program at Mount Wachusett Community College—an achievement that reflects the values of hard work and perseverance he instills every day.
Reilly’s diverse career—spanning music, technology, law, and community leadership—has been defined by one constant: the ability to bring people together, solve problems, and deliver results.
