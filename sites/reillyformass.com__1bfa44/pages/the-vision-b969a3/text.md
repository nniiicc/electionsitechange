1.
Cost of Living & Local Economy Families are feeling squeezed from every direction—housing, groceries, energy, and taxes.
I will push for policies that bring real relief, support small businesses, and make it easier for people to stay and build their lives in this district.
2.
Public Safety & First Responders Every community deserves to feel safe.
I support giving law enforcement, firefighters, and correction officers the tools, protections, and respect that they need to do their jobs.
We can support public safety while still holding systems accountable—those things are not up for debate.
3.
Education & Opportunity Strong schools are the backbone of our district.
I will advocate for better funding, support for our schools, and expanded opportunities like vocational training and dual enrollment programs that give students a head start on their futures.
4.
Government Accountability People are tired of feeling like decisions are made behind closed doors.
I will prioritize transparency, accessibility, and responsiveness—showing up, listening, and making sure constituents always have a voice in the process.
I will also provide consistent content on my District 2 Platform, informing residents what is really going on in our district.
5.
Community Stability & Growth We need to protect what makes our area amazing by planning responsibly for the future.
That means smart development, protecting local character, and investing in infrastructure that actually meets our needs.
