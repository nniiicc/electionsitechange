Local Control
Towns need to be able to make decisions about their own future without being forced into arrangements by legislators in Concord. we've seen the state try to strip towns of the ability to regulate things like data center development, taking decisions away from the people who actually live with the consequences.
Residents know their towns better than politicians, and Carryl believes local boards, not statehouse committees, should have the final say over what happensin their own backyard.
