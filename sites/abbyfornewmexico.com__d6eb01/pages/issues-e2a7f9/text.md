The Issues
After years of knocking doors and talking with neighbors across the district, Abby has heard clearly what families need most.
Her priorities are shaped by listening to our community.
Affordable Housing
Housing costs are rising too fast for working families.
- Support rent control in mobile home parks
- Create incentives for multifamily housing
- Expand sustainable housing options for people of all ages
- Require new housing projects to remain affordable by law
Child Wellbeing, Schools, and CYFD
Children do better when they have safe schools, strong support, and a system that works with families.
- Support safe schools and equitable access to education.
- Reform CYFD so it works better for children and families
- Improve staff retention in schools and child-serving systems
- Build stronger pipelines from higher education into CYFD jobs and related fields
- Proper heating and cooling for public schools
Healthcare Access
We need affordable, reliable care close to home, without long waits or unfair insurance barriers.
- Address doctor shortages across the state
- Strengthen rural healthcare access and funding for rural clinics
- Push back on monopoly insurance practices
- Support Paid Family and Medical Leave
- Provide up to 12 weeks of care for a close family member, not only for pregnancy
Where Abby stands on other issues:
- Abby opposes the purchase of TXNM Energy (PNM) by Blackstone.
Abby opposes corporate monopolies and anticompetitive trade practices.
Utilities should be publicly regulated and held for the public benefit.
- Abby supports free universal childcare, expanding New Mexico Pre-K, and expanding access to paid family and medical leave.
- Abby supports growing clean energy jobs while responsibly managing New Mexico's natural resources.
- Abby supports lowering healthcare, housing, and everyday costs while holding corporations accountable.
- Abby supports fully funding public schools and expanding early childhood education and workforce pathways.
- Abby supports common-sense gun safety laws.
- Abby supports expanding access to healthcare and lowering prescription drug costs.
- Abby supports border security alongside fair, humane immigration reform and pathways to citizenship.
She strongly opposes the recent misuse of ICE.
- Abby supports creating good-paying jobs, strengthening small businesses, and investing in infrastructure.
- Abby supports expanding access to mental health care and substance use treatment in local communities.
- Abby supports our National Labs and the critical role they play in our national defense, scientific innovation, and the New Mexico economy.
- Abby supports giving law enforcement the tools they need while investing in prevention and survivor support.
- Abby supports protecting abortion access and believes healthcare decisions belong to individuals.
She opposes government restrictions on access and care.
- Abby supports protecting Social Security and Medicare so that New Mexicans can retire with the dignity and benefits they earned.
- Abby supports cutting taxes for middle and working-class families and small businesses while ensuring the wealthiest corporations pay their fair share.
- Abby supports workers’ rights.
- Abby supports ensuring veterans receive the healthcare and services they’ve earned.
- Abby supports protecting voting rights and ensuring every eligible voter has access to the ballot.
- Abby supports protecting New Mexico's water supply through smart conservation and investing in infrastructure to handle long-term drought.
