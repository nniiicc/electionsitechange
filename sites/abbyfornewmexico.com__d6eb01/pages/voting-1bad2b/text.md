Same-Day Registration You can also register OR update your registration on the same day you vote in person.
This is available at any voting location during early voting or on Election Day.
To use same-day registration, you will need to bring one of the following: A photo ID A utility bill or bank statement A government check or paycheck A student ID Any other government document with your name and address This document needs to show your name and your current address .
It needs to be dated within 90 days of you using same-day registration.
