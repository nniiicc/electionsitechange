His priorities focus on strengthening communities, supporting families, ensuring accountable government, and protecting the future of our state.
Strong Communities Kevin believes that strong communities are the foundation of a thriving West Virginia.
He advocates for: Local Infrastructure: Upgrading roads, bridges, and public facilities to ensure safety and accessibility for all.
Broadband Access: Expanding high-speed internet to connect rural areas, support small businesses, and provide better educational opportunities.
Public Services: Strengthening healthcare, emergency services, and community programs that keep neighborhoods safe and resilient.
Working Families Kevin is dedicated to creating an environment where every family can succeed.
His focus includes: Fair Wages: Supporting policies that ensure workers earn a living wage for hard work.
Affordable Healthcare: Making healthcare accessible and affordable for all West Virginia residents.
Economic Opportunity: Encouraging job growth, vocational training, and support for local businesses to create sustainable livelihoods.
Honest Government Kevin believes that government should serve the people, not political interests.
He is committed to: Ending Corruption: Holding leaders accountable and promoting ethical decision-making.
Transparency: Ensuring that government actions and spending are open and clear to the public.
Restoring Trust: Building confidence in leadership by listening to citizens and delivering real results.
Protecting Our Future Kevin is passionate about preserving West Virginia’s natural beauty and resources for generations to come.
His approach includes: Environmental Stewardship: Supporting responsible policies that protect forests, rivers, and wildlife.
Sustainable Growth: Balancing economic development with conservation to ensure long-term prosperity.
Future Generations: Advocating for education, clean energy, and community programs that safeguard opportunities for the youth.
