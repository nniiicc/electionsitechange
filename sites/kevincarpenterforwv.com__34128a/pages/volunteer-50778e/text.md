Volunteer
Kevin Carpenter’s campaign is powered by people who care about West Virginia.
Volunteers are the heart of our movement —
from connecting with neighbors to helping spread our message, every hand makes a difference.
We welcome anyone who wants to support honest leadership, strong communities, and real solutions.
Whether you have a few hours a week or can help organize events, your contribution matters.
Ways to Get Involved
- Community Outreach: Talk to neighbors, distribute flyers, and help raise awareness about Kevin’s priorities.
- Event Support: Assist at town halls, rallies, and community events.
- Digital Advocacy: Share updates on social media, write emails, or help with online campaigns.
- Fundraising Assistance: Help organize local fundraising events or support our online fundraising efforts.
Why Volunteer?
Volunteering is more than giving time — it’s being part of a movement that puts people first.
You’ll meet like-minded citizens, develop new skills, and directly impact the future of West Virginia.
