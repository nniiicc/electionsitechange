Leading Voices Endorse Judge Mike Diaz
"Diaz knows the law inside and out ... and now serves on the Court of Appeals where he authored opinions overwhelmingly supporting workers, challenging corporations, and protecting civil rights. ...
Vote Diaz."
"Diaz combines a keen knowledge of the law with [the right] kind of measured temperament. ...
Diaz, with a track record of more than 200 appellate opinions, is the clear choice for the high court."
“I twice appointed Judge Diaz to the bench, first to the Superior Court and then to the Court of Appeals.
I appointed him for his track record of proven reform work, dedication to public service, and understanding of complex state issues.
I have no doubt he will be the most qualified candidate to serve on the highest court in our state.
He possesses the judicial virtues of intelligence, courage, temperament, and fairness—qualities that every judicial officer should possess."
Jay Inslee
Former Governor of Washington
“Judge Diaz is exactly what our Supreme Court needs right now."
Christine Gregoire
Former Governor of Washington
Gary Locke
Former Governor of Washington
"I worked with Judge Diaz for half a decade at the United States Attorney’s office in Seattle.
Through his civil rights work and leadership in the office, he made that office better, our streets safer, and our community fairer.
When elected, our state should rest assured that it will have a justice who has the experience essential for the job, and who will listen to all sides, think critically, and decide our gravest disputes fairly, with a deep commitment to the rule of law.
We need his voice and expertise on the Washington Supreme Court."
Nick Brown
Washington Attorney General
"I know Judge Diaz from his tireless work in the civic community, his stellar reputation in the legal community, and his excellence and thoughtful written opinions as an appellate judge.
I am honored to endorse him as my successor for those reasons and also because he has a unique combination of life and legal experiences to bring to this court, all of which are informed by his relentless desire to serve his fellow Washingtonians.
When elected, he will be ready on day one and will serve this court and our beloved community faithfully and enthusiastically for years to come."
Raquel Montoya-Lewis
Washington Supreme Court Justice
Sheryl Gordon McCloud
Washington Supreme Court Justice
“For nearly four years, Judge Diaz and I worked collaboratively with our respective teams to improve policing in Seattle.
In close partnership, he convened officers at SPD, community groups, and various governmental entities locally and nationwide to do the hard work of reform.
He was always reasonable and zealously focused on advancing effective and constitutional policing in his hometown.
I’m proud to support his candidacy for the state’s highest court.”
Kathy O'Toole
Former SPD Chief of Police
"Mike Diaz worked intensely and wholeheartedly with the CPC on reform efforts.
His leadership at DOJ in seeking to transform the Seattle Police Department was integral in beginning the ongoing process of improving police accountability in an effort to better serve our communities and begin to build trust."
Fé LopezGaetke
Civil Rights Activist & founding Director of the Seattle Community Police Commission (CPC)
