November General Election Candidates Forum
*** Come out & support Rich ***
Candidate Forum organized by the Highway 14/11 (Dark Corner) Community Watch Group.
This Candidate Forum is open to the General Public
Come out and listen to candidates address questions that are relative to your vote!
Candidates for the November General Election for the following offices have been invited to participate:
U.S.
Senate
U.S.
Congress (South Carolina’s 4th Congressional District)
State Governor
State House of Representatives Districts 17 & 18
State Attorney General
Secretary of State
State Treasurer
State Comptroller General
State Superintendent of Education
State Commissioner of Agriculture
The candidates in each race will be provided with a set of five questions, pertinent to the particular office, the night of the event.
They will be allotted 2 minutes per question, for a total of 10 minutes.
No questions will be taken from the floor, but you will have an opportunity after the event ends to seek them out and ask them any individual questions you may have.
