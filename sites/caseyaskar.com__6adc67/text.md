Casey Askar
Casey Askar is a national business leader, U.S.
Marine, philanthropist, and family man, who will to work alongside President Donald Trump to fix the economy for our families in South Florida, and across America.
Casey comes from very humble beginnings.
His family legally immigrated to the U.S., escaping religious persecution, and is a product of public education.
Casey’s first jobs working at McDonald’s and mopping floors at CVS, taught him the value of hard work.
After graduating from high school, Casey enlisted in the U.S.
Marine Corps where he served honorably in defense of our great nation.Casey employs thousands of people in the district and across South Florida.
He is the Chairman and CEO of Askar Family Office, a company specializing in franchising, commercial real estate, energy, and construction.
He built his company from the ground up and operates businesses in two dozen states across the country.
Now, Casey is running in our new Congressional District to put his job-creating expertise and business leadership to work for all of us in Washington.
Casey is a married father of seven children, who practices his Judeo-Christian values with his family and in his work.
Casey is running to create an “opportunity society” so that we may all share in the blessings of prosperity.
