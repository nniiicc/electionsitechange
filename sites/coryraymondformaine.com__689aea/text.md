He's not a career politician.
He's a neighbor who got tired of watching Augusta drift further from the values and priorities of the people who actually live and work in District 41, and decided to do something about it.
Cory ran for this seat in 2024 because he believed the people of Rockport and the surrounding communities deserved a representative who would fight to keep taxes low, protect individual freedoms, and stop the regulatory overreach that squeezes small businesses and working families.
He came close, and the issues that drove him to run haven't gone away — they've gotten worse.
In 2026, he's back with more determination, deeper community roots, and a clear agenda for what Midcoast Maine actually needs.
What sets Cory apart is simple: he listens.
He's not reading from a party script — he's talking to his neighbors at the hardware store, at the boat launch, and at the kitchen table.
He understands that the challenges facing District 41 — rising property taxes, unaffordable energy costs, and an economy that's pricing out working families — require real solutions, not Augusta talking points.
