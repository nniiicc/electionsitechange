Cory Raymond Wins Republican Nomination for House District 41
By Campaign Team
Cory Raymond is the Republican nominee for Maine House District 41 after sweeping 100% of the Republican primary vote — and pulling 65 write-in votes on the Democrat ticket.
Cory Raymond is officially the Republican nominee to represent Maine House District 41 this November.
Cory captured 100% of the vote in the Republican primary, with no challenger on the ballot — a clear show of unity from District 41 Republicans heading into the general election.
Notably, Cory also pulled 65 write-in votes on the Democrat ticket, a sign of crossover support from voters who want practical, accountable representation in Augusta regardless of party.
