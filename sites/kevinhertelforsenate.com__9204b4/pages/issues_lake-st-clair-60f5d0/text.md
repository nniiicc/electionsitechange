Kevin Hertel is focused on putting aside political games, finding common ground, and delivering solutions for our lakeshore community.
From a young age, Kevin has been inspired by his family's legacy of public service.
His grandfather, Jack, was a World War II veteran and public school teacher, and his dad, Curtis, was a former Speaker of the Michigan House of Representatives during a time of divided government.
From them, Kevin learned the importance of community service and working together to get things done.
While in the Senate, Kevin has put these values and lessons into action, championing policies to lower costs for seniors and working families, keep Michigan communities safe, expand educational opportunities for students, and more.
