October 5, 2020 / in Uncategorized / by Dan Hudson As someone who has been dealing with the effects of COVID-19 personally, I implore you all to WEAR A MASK.
This is not a political issue, or a deprivation of constitutional rights.
This is a matter of common sense, common decency and scientific truth.
MASKS WORK TO PREVENT THE SPREAD OF COVID-19.
Feel sick?
Get tested.
