Lauren Cole Sep 12 1 min read Hi Friend, Want to show your support for Lauren Cole and help spread the word across House District 37?
Yard signs are a simple but powerful way to build visibility for our campaign.
Every sign helps more voters see Lauren’s name, starts conversations with neighbors, and shows that there is support for Lauren throughout our community.
The best part?
Yard signs are completely free!
We have two options available: • Regular yard signs — perfect for your front yard! • Large 4×4 signs — If you live along a main road, have a business, or have another highly visible location where a larger sign would make an impact, we would love to hear from you!
To request a sign, simply reply to this email with your address and let us know which size sign you would like.
If you're interested in a 4×4 sign, please also let us know a little about the location so we can coordinate the best placement.
Please reply by 9/19 to be included in our next round of sign requests.
Thank you for helping us spread the word and build visibility for Lauren across House District 37!
