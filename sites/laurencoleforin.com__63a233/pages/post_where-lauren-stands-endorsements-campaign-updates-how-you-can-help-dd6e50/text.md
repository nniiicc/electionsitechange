We want to make sure you have that information, too!
Lauren is a scientist, a neighbor, and an advocate for our community.
Her campaign is focused on putting people first, protecting access to healthcare, supporting public education, strengthening local businesses, and building a future where Hoosiers have the opportunity to thrive.
We’re proud to share the organizations that have recognized Lauren’s work and the priorities she brings to this race.
Here’s who is standing with Lauren: Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates endorsed Lauren for the 2026 election cycle in recognition of her commitment to advancing reproductive healthcare, protecting reproductive rights, and promoting reproductive justice.
Indiana Reproductive Freedom PAC (IRFPAC) IRFPAC endorsed Lauren for the 2026 election cycle and works to educate Hoosiers about reproductive freedom, the impact of abortion restrictions, and the importance of reproductive rights in Indiana.
Indiana Young Democrats — 2026 Priority Endorsement Indiana Young Democrats named Lauren a 2026 Priority Endorsement, committing additional resources to her campaign, including a $1,000 contribution, in recognition of her work to strengthen communities across Indiana.
3.14 Action As a scientist with experience in clinical trials, Lauren is backed by 3.14 Action, an organization that works to elect scientists who bring evidence-based thinking to public policy, healthcare, and climate issues.
Voters for a Green Indiana — 2026 Green Voters Guide Lauren was recognized as an environmentally friendly candidate in the 2026 Green Voters Guide, which examines candidates’ records and campaign priorities on utility affordability, data centers, renewable energy, and environmental protection, alongside their responses to an environmental questionnaire.
MADVoters — Verified Politician Lauren received the MADVoters-Verified Politician designation.
As a MADVoters-Verified Politician, she is dedicated to fighting for a better life for all Hoosiers through affordable and accessible healthcare, fully funded public education, making housing, utilities, childcare, and everyday essentials more affordable, s afeguarding voting access and protecting our freedoms, building safe and inclusive communities for all Hoosiers.
Indiana Coalition for Public Education (ICPE) — Candidate Pledge Lauren signed ICPE’s pledge addressing public education, including support for traditional public schools, local school board authority, and accountability for schools receiving public funds.
These recognitions reflect important issues in this race, but we know there is still more work to do to get Lauren’s name and priorities in front of voters.
We have come so far, and we’re not slowing down now.
Let’s keep building our future together!
