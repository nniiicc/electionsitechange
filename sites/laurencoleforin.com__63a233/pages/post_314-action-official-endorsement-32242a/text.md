314 Action Official Endorsement
I am proud to share that my campaign has been officially endorsed by 314 Action Fund, a national organization dedicated to electing leaders who believe in science, evidence-based policy, and stronger communities.
This endorsement means a lot.
Not just for our campaign, but for what it represents.
As someone with a background in biochemistry, this recognition is especially meaningful to me.
I've always believed that the best decisions, especially when it comes to healthcare, public policy, and our communities, should be grounded in evidence and a commitment to understanding real world impacts.
My experience in science has shaped how I approach problem solving.
I have learned to ask questions, listen first and focus on solutions that actually work.
Those are the same values I will bring to the Statehouse.
I'm grateful for this endorsement and excited to keep connecting with people across District 37 as we continue this campaign.
