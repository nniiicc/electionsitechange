Information We Collect We collect information you provide directly and certain information automatically: Information you give us Contact and supporter information — such as your email address and ZIP code when you sign up for updates, or your name and details when you volunteer.

Information collected automatically Usage and device data — such as your browser type, device, pages viewed, and general interactions, collected through analytics tools including Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) and Vercel Analytics.

How We Use Your Information To send you campaign updates, news, and other communications you request.
To process and acknowledge contributions and volunteer sign-ups.
To understand and improve how people use our website.
To protect the security and integrity of our website and forms.
To comply with applicable campaign-finance and other legal requirements.
How We Share Your Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share it with: Service providers that help us operate the Campaign — for example, our email and data platforms (such as Segment and Iterable), our hosting and analytics providers, and our donation processor (WinRed).
As required by law — including disclosures required under federal and state campaign-finance law, or in response to lawful requests by public authorities.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Contributions and Legal Disclosures Federal law requires political committees to collect and report certain information about individuals whose contributions exceed specified thresholds, including name, mailing address, occupation, and employer.
Contribution information may be disclosed to the Federal Election Commission and made publicly available as required by law.
Your Choices Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect.
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children’s Privacy This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
