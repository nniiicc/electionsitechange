Lower taxes.

She will work to make the Trump tax cuts permanent, root out waste and fraud in federal spending, and stop Washington from mortgaging our children's future.
For nearly 30 years Darline has helped South Carolinians find work and provide for their families, and she will fight for lower taxes and less red tape for small business, real investment in workforce training and the skilled trades, and work requirements for able-bodied adults.
Border Every state is a border state, and the crisis at the southern border reaches South Carolina families directly.
Secure the border.
Enforce the law.
Stop the fentanyl.
Darline supports finishing the wall, fully funding border enforcement, and ending catch-and-release.
She will fight the flow of fentanyl that is killing South Carolinians, back the removal of criminal illegal aliens, and oppose amnesty and taxpayer-funded benefits for those here illegally.
Life Darline is pro-life and believes every child is a gift from God.
Protecting the unborn.
Supporting mothers.
Defending families.
She supports mothers, adoption, and pregnancy resource centers, and opposes taxpayer funding of abortion.
She will defend religious liberty, stand up for parents' rights in their children's education, and protect girls' sports and locker rooms.
Election Integrity South Carolinians deserve elections they can trust.
Voter ID.
Clean rolls.
Transparent counting.
Darline supports voter ID, accurate and up-to-date voter rolls, and counting that is transparent and secure.
She opposes federal takeovers of state elections and any effort to let noncitizens vote.
Crime Darline will always stand with the men and women in law enforcement who run toward danger.
Back the blue.
Prosecute the criminals.
She opposes every effort to defund the police, supports the funding, training, and equipment our officers need, and backs tough penalties for fentanyl traffickers and violent repeat offenders.
Safe streets and safe schools are the foundation everything else is built on.
Guns The right to keep and bear arms is guaranteed by the Constitution, and Darline will defend it without apology.
No registries.
No confiscation.
No apologies.
She opposes gun registries, red-flag confiscation schemes, and bans on the firearms law-abiding South Carolinians own.
She supports concealed carry reciprocity — and going after criminals instead of law-abiding gun owners.
Fighting for South Carolina Darline has spent her career serving South Carolinians, and she knows this state's needs are not Washington's talking points.
Rural communities, farmers, seniors, and people with disabilities.
As agency head at the SC Commission for the Blind she led 130 employees across 10 offices delivering vocational rehabilitation, independent-living, and blindness-prevention services — and she will cut the waiting lists and red tape that keep people from working and living independently.
She will stand up for South Carolina farmers, expand rural broadband, fight to keep rural hospitals open, and protect Medicare and Social Security for the seniors who paid into them.
Strength President Trump asked Darline to run, and in the United States Senate she is a reliable vote for the America First agenda.
A strong military, secure borders, and American energy.
South Carolina is a military state — home to Fort Jackson, Parris Island, Shaw Air Force Base, and Joint Base Charleston.
Darline will fight for the resources our installations and service members need, hold the VA accountable so veterans get the care they earned, and back an all-of-the-above American energy strategy that lowers costs and ends our dependence on foreign adversaries.
