On The Issues
Darline’s Plan for
South Carolina
Darline is fiercely protective of taxpayer dollars.
She managed a $90 million budget and turned around a state agency known for inefficiency — and she brings that same standard to Washington.
Lower taxes.
Less waste.
More jobs.
She will work to make the Trump tax cuts permanent, root out waste and fraud in federal spending, and stop Washington from mortgaging our children's future.
For nearly 30 years Darline has helped South Carolinians find work and provide for their families, and she will fight for lower taxes and less red tape for small business, real investment in workforce training and the skilled trades, and work requirements for able-bodied adults.
Darline is bringing her record of service to the United States Senate.
