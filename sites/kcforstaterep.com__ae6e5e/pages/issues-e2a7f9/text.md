Making Life More Affordable
Prices haven’t come down fast enough and our families are feeling the pinch of everyday life.
Most of the problems come out of Washington, but KC is determined to find solutions here at home.
That’s why she’s fought to cut taxes for working families, small businesses, and our seniors.
Now she’s working with Governor Shapiro to lower energy prices, including repealing a family crushing energy tax.
Fighting Crime
While we share a border with Philadelphia, KC has been determined to make sure we don’t share the lawlessness the city has faced.
She’s been a partner with law enforcement, delivering more resources to our local forces and voting to put new cops on the streets.
She’s also authored legislation that has cracked down on criminals, including porch pirates and drug dealers.
Investing In Our Students
KC has worked across the aisle to deliver for our students, parents, and teachers.
She worked with Republicans and Democrats, including Governor Shapiro, to deliver record state funding for our local schools.
Those resources help local districts avoid property tax increases.
And KC has been a strong advocate for our teachers, making sure they have the support they need to teach our kids.
