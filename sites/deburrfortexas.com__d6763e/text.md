Hi, I'm Detrick DeBurr.
Thank you for visiting my website.
Here you can learn all about me, what I stand for, and more importantly why you should invest in me becoming your next Texas House Representative in District 65.
I'm a 54-year-old software engineer, father of 3, and grandfather of 3.
I have served North Texas over the past 23 years in various capacities.
I currently serve as a Planning and Zoning Commissioner in The Colony, Texas.
I previously Chaired the Planning and Zoning Commission in Cedar Hill, Texas.
I’ve served on the Board of St.
Philips School and Community Center and Chaired the Dallas Blueprint for Leadership Program, a program that prepared minorities to serve on non-profit boards and committees throughout the metroplex.
I’m also a proud and active member of The Omega Psi Phi Fraternity Incorporated.
In my spare time, I write.
I’m the author of Building Gamified Websites published in 2013 by Packt Publishing and Deal Us In!
How Black America Can Play and Win in the Digital Economy” published in 2001 by Anji Publishing.
Why am I running?
As a planning and zoning commissioner, I have been behind the scenes watching North Texas grow over the past 20 years.
It has given me a unique perspective on where we've been, where we are, and more importantly where we are going.
I'm running for District 65 because I believe the demographics of the community have shifted to the degree that we must have a representative in Austin who understands the priorities of the district.
That representative must understand the area's growth and can navigate that growth.
I’m running because I'm not beholden to any political agenda.
I am running as a progressive because my personal views align more with today’s Democratic Party than today’s Republican Party.
With that said, I consider myself very conservative, yet thoughtful and compassionate.
I'm running to bring back two-party politics in the State of Texas.
According to demographers by the bicentennial of the state (2036), it will be overwhelmingly “Minority Majority”.
Hence our priorities are shifting.
This doesn't mean that we abandon fiscal responsibility and common sense.
But these waters will require skillful navigation and I am a skillful navigator.
What are my legislative priorities?
My campaign priorities are funding public education, funding healthcare through the expansion of Medicaid in the state, and slowly and intentionally expanding the social net in Texas.
Texas is a US leader in job growth and people moving to the state.
However, our current education system, healthcare system, and income disparities are NOT sustainable over the coming decade.
The time to address these issues is now.
Texas will turn 200 years old in 3 election cycles (12 years).
We have to put the policies in place to ensure that we continue to be a viable state, rather than policies that work to keep it looking like it did #ago.
There's a way to lead Texas into the future.
It requires a long view and thoughtfulness.
My legislative perspective is the “compassionate longview”.
Why should you support me?
