Meet Allison
I’m Allison Russo, and I’m running for Ohio Secretary of State because every voice matters and every vote counts.
At a time when politicians in Columbus and Washington are working to hold onto power, it’s time to put power back where it belongs: with the people of Ohio.
The Secretary of State’s job is about protecting your voice, safeguarding your vote, and ensuring our elections remain fair, secure, and accessible.
Ohioans deserve a Chief Elections Officer who believes that voters should choose their politicians, not the other way around.
For years, I’ve fought against politicians who rig the system to protect their own power instead of addressing the challenges Ohio families face every day.
I’ve worked to ensure government is accountable to the people so voters can demand action on affordability, rising costs, and the issues that matter most.
I’m running to restore trust in our elections, protect your personal data, and make sure government is accountable to the people it serves.
I’ll be ready on DAY ONE to defend democracy and fight for every Ohio voter.
