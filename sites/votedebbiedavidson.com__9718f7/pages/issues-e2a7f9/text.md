Take Back Your Money Take Back Your Money Smart Growth Stop property tax and electricity rate hikes.
Stop property tax and electricity rate hikes.
Stop property tax and electricity rate hikes.
Secure State Funding for Schools You are paying more locally because the state is paying less.
As your State Representative, I will push the state to fully fund education so local communities are not forced to make up the difference through higher property taxes.
End Costly Giveaways to Developers When politicians hand out tax breaks for developers, you end up paying the difference through higher taxes and fewer services.
As your State Representative, I will stop unfair tax giveaways and require every project to deliver real value for the community.
Stop Rising Property Taxes Property taxes keep going up, and families are being priced out of their homes.
As your State Representative, I will focus on lowering property tax pressure and stopping the constant cycle of new levies that hit homeowners year after year.
Lower Utility Costs Families are seeing rising electricity and infrastructure costs tied to poorly planned development.
As your State Representative, I will stop projects that increase your cost of living, like data centers, and push for transparency in how these decisions are made.
Make Developers Pay Their Fair Share Too often, development drives up utility costs, damages roads, and strains emergency services while you are left with the bill.
As your State Representative, I will require developers to pay for the infrastructure and services their projects use so costs are not passed on to you.

Bring Back Local Jobs Ohio used to offer stable careers that allowed families to build a life.
As your State Representative, I will focus on attracting employers that create long-term, good-paying jobs so people can work and succeed right here at home.
Strengthen Local Businesses Small businesses are the backbone of our communities, but they are often overlooked in favor of large outside corporations.
As your State Representative, I will help local businesses grow so our economy stays strong and local.
Support Rural Communities Too many rural communities have been left behind.
As your State Representative, I will prioritize investment in Main Street, infrastructure, and local development so communities across District 63 can grow and thrive.
Prepare Students for Local Workforce Students need clear pathways into good jobs.
As your State Representative, I will strengthen connections between schools and employers so students graduate ready for real, local career opportunities that exist in today’s economy.

End Backroom Deals Decisions that affect your taxes and your community are being made behind closed doors.
As your State Representative, I will ban non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for public projects and ensure decisions are made openly and honestly.
Increase Transparency in Government Government works best when people can see what is happening.
As your State Representative, I will ensure decisions, proposals, and impacts are clearly communicated so you always know what is being done and why.
Stop Costly Development You Did Not Approve Too many projects move forward without community support and leave residents paying the price.
As your State Representative, I will stop developments that raise costs or damage your community.
Require Local Input Before Major Development Communities deserve a say before major projects move forward.
As your State Representative, I will make sure your voice is heard on decisions that impact your life.
Keep You Informed and Involved You deserve direct access to your representative.
As your State Representative, I will regularly share updates, meet with residents across the district, and make it easy for you to stay informed and involved in decisions that affect your life.
