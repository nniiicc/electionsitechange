Meet Debbie
I grew up in a working family in southwest Ohio, where strong schools, good jobs, and affordable living made it possible to build a life and raise a family.
I’m a mom, military spouse, and proud small-town Ohioan who made a career in healthcare, government, and helping non-profits.
After 9/11, my husband joined active duty while I supported our family.
We lived briefly in Washington, where I learned firsthand how D.C. politicians fail small town communities and economies.

Local Action
Local Action
When we moved back to Ohio, I took pride in fighting for my neighbors.
In 2025, I stopped a major development plan that was going to raise taxes and utility fees for our community.
Then, I helped change the zoning rules to prevent something like that from happening again.
But that's only the beginning.
Today, taxes are too high, jobs are leaving Ohio, and we’re paying for development we never approved.
I'll use my experience working with government systems to fix what's broken at the state level.

Why I'm Running
Why I'm Running
I'm running for State Representative to help families take back their money, bring back good jobs, and restore a real voice in their communities.
I believe:
- You should not be priced out of your home by rising taxes.
- You should be able to get a good-paying job close to home.
- You deserve a say in decisions that affect your community.
I'm not running to play politics.
I'm running to restore what made Ohio a place where working families could succeed.
