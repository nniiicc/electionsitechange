Monica Stonier has spent 25 years in Washington classrooms.
She works as an Access Coach for Evergreen Public Schools while serving as House Majority Floor Leader in Olympia.
She is the first person of color to represent the 49th Legislative District, and the 17th before that.
Her background is Mexican-American and Japanese-American.
Her uncles served in the 442nd "Go For Broke" Infantry in World War II.
That family story shapes the way she shows up for her neighbors.
Monica grew up in a military family, earned her teaching certificate at Western Washington University, and her master's at WSU Vancouver.
She and her husband Brandon have raised two kids in Clark County.
Both are now in college here in Washington.
In Olympia, she leads her caucus on public education, healthcare access, worker protections, and behavioral health.
She serves on the Appropriations, Rules, Education, and Health Care & Wellness committees.
She is known for working across the aisle and getting bills across the finish line.
With neighbors across the 49th District The Record What Monica has delivered for the 49th.
Public service is about showing up and getting things done.
Here is some of the work Monica has led in Olympia.
Books for every kid Brought Dolly Parton's Imagination Library to Washington.
Every child under five in the state can now receive a free book in the mail each month.
Financial education in schools Chairs the Financial Education Public-Private Partnership.
Expanded free financial education training for teachers so every Washington student learns how money works before graduation.
Breakfast After the Bell Wrote and passed HB 1508 with bipartisan support.
Kids who come to school hungry get fed, even if they miss the morning bell.
Learning works better when basic needs are met.
Ending child marriage Led the unanimous House passage of HB 1455 to set the minimum marriage age at 18 in Washington.
The law protects children from coercion and trafficking.
Dignity at work Passed the Domestic Workers Protection Act in 2020.
Pushed for sanitary workplace conditions for women in the construction trades and new protections for immigrant workers.
Affordable childcare in SW Washington Secured funding for a shared services model so childcare providers in our region can keep their doors open.
Working parents and small providers both benefit.
Dental care you can afford Co-chaired the Dental One Table and helped secure $1.95 million in capital funding to expand dental access for low-income families.
Freedom to read Sponsored HB 2331 to stop school boards from banning books through discrimination.
Students deserve to see themselves, and their neighbors, in what they read.
Washington's bottle bill Led the floor push to pass HB 1607, the Washington Recycling Reform Act — the state's first bottle deposit system.
A 10-cent refund on cans and bottles, cleaner neighborhoods, and a recycling rate that actually works.

Monica is already taking up new projects in the region.
If re-elected, here is where she goes next.
01 Simple Majority for School Bonds Every year since joining the legislature, Monica has pushed to return school bond approval to a simple majority.
Rural districts have voted for their schools.
The state should let them pass.
02 Pupil Transportation and Special Education Reform the funding formulas so schools can actually serve kids with special learning needs.
Monica's experience in Special Services in Evergreen Schools shapes this work.
03 Future Funds Partnering with State Treasurer Mike Pellicciotti and Senator Yasmin Trudeau on an initiative to break the cycle of generational poverty.
The next version of the bill is ready for the coming session.
04 Fertility Insurance Coverage Washington should be a great place to start a family.
HB 1730 would require insurance to cover infertility diagnosis and treatment.
No more second mortgages to try to have a baby.
05 Affordable Housing and Behavioral Health The housing crisis and the behavioral health shortage are the biggest pressures on families right now.
Monica is organizing regional responses to both.
