Republican Candidate, Texas House District 148 (2026)
Born and raised in District 148, Amanda Elizabeth LaBrie is running to restore Houston’s reputation as a global leader in medicine, space, and energy.
Houston is home to the Texas Medical Center, NASA’s Johnson Space Center, and the Energy Capital of the World—but today it is also marked by record crime, poverty, and human trafficking.
Amanda believes it’s time to turn that around.
- Graduate of Lamar and Waltrip High Schools; collegiate swimmer and 11-time All-American at University of Texas Permian Basin.
- Holds degrees in Political Science, Business Management, and Education, with an MBA in Energy Management.
- Career: Landman in the oil & gas industry (Energen, Callon, EOG Resources) and co-founder of PetroleumETC/The Energy Think Tank.
- Entrepreneur: Founder of The Social Tank, LLC, producing corporate events for Fortune 500s, nonprofits, and political organizations.
- Community leader: spearheaded a $1.2M park renovation, Precinct Chair 0446, and active member in veteran, Republican, business, and energy organizations.
- Advocate: involved in anti-trafficking initiatives and Houston Livestock Show & Rodeo International Committee.
