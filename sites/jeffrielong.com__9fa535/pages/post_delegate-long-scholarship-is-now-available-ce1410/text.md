Are you a District 27B resident who will be attending a higher learning institution in Maryland?
Are you looking for some extra money for your scholarship package?
If so, apply here.
Applications are due no later than May 13th!
All Maryland residents living in Legislative District 27B attending a college, university, trade school or equivalent in the State of Maryland are eligible for the House of Delegates Scholarship.
Current high school seniors and full-time or part-time, degree-seeking undergraduate, graduate students, and students attending a private career school may apply.
Please share widely with your network of students, family, and friends!
Please note, all applications MUST be completed in its entirety.
