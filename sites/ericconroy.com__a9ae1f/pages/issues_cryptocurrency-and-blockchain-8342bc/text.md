Eric Conroy believes blockchain technology and cryptocurrency represent a generational opportunity for innovation, economic growth, and individual financial success.
Eric wants the United States to lead the way in creating a clear regulatory framework that protects consumers while encouraging innovation.
Eric opposes efforts by Washington to overregulate or ban decentralized technologies, and he will work to ensure U.S. policies promote freedom, privacy, and responsible innovation.
In Congress, Eric will champion emerging technologies and the Americans who want to harness them to create a better future, starting right here in Ohio.
