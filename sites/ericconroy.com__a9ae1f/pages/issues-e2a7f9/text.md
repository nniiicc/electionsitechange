Eric Conroy is committed to tackling the issues that matter most to Ohioans, both at home and in Washington.
Jobs & the Economy
Eric Conroy will fight to lower inflation, cut red tape, and champion pro-growth policies that put Ohio workers and small businesses first.
Safe Neighborhoods
Eric Conroy will stand with law enforcement, oppose defunding the police, and fight for policies that keep violent criminals off our streets.
Border Security
Eric Conroy will secure the southern border with stronger enforcement, smarter technology, and policies that stop illegal immigration and protect American communities.
Transportation and Infrastructure
Eric Conroy will fight for smart infrastructure investments that reduce congestion, improve roads and bridges, and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.
Education
Eric Conroy will empower parents, support local control, and expand career-focused education to prepare students for real-world success.
Small Business
Eric Conroy will cut red tape, lower costs, and champion policies that empower small businesses over corporate special interests.
Veterans
Eric Conroy will fight to fix the VA, expand care and benefits, and ensure every veteran gets the respect and support they’ve earned.
Cryptocurrency and Blockchain
Eric Conroy will defend innovation and financial freedom by promoting clear, pro-growth policies for cryptocurrency and blockchain technologies.
Social Security and Medicare
Eric Conroy will protect Social Security and Medicare, oppose benefit cuts, and ensure these vital programs remain strong for generations to come.
National Defense
Eric Conroy will strengthen America’s military, invest in next-generation defense, and be a steadfast ally of Israel and our national security.
Protecting Women's Sports
Eric Conroy supports legislation to ensure women’s sports are reserved for biological females, protecting fairness and equal opportunity.
