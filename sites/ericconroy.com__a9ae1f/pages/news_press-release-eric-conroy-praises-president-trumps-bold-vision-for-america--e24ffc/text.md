Eric Conroy Praises President Trump’s Bold Vision for America, Slams Landsman for Putting Partisanship Before the Results
CINCINNATI, OH — Eric Conroy, Air Force, former CIA officer and Republican candidate for Ohio’s 1st Congressional District, praised President Donald Trump’s State of the Union address as a powerful and unapologetic vision for American renewal, while sharply criticizing Congressman Greg Landsman for his reflexive opposition to the President’s agenda.
“President Trump laid out exactly what the American people sent him back to Washington to do: secure our border, cut wasteful spending, rebuild our economy, and put America First,” said Conroy.
“It was a bold, optimistic speech, and Ohioans in the 1st District should be proud to have a President fighting every day to help us recover from the failed policies of the Biden years.”
Conroy contrasted the President’s vision with Landsman’s record of opposition.
“While President Trump is fighting for the working families of Southwest Ohio, Greg Landsman is fighting against them.
Time and again, Landsman has chosen to side with the radical left over his own constituents — opposing the President’s agenda at every turn, no matter the issue and no matter the cost to the people he’s supposed to represent.”
“Greg Landsman doesn’t work for Ohio.
He works for liberal billionaires and the D.C. liberal establishment,” Conroy added.
“I’m running to give the 1st District a representative who will actually stand with President Trump, deliver results, and put Ohio First.”
About Eric Conroy
Eric Conroy’s mission began on the West Side of Cincinnati, where four generations of blue-collar grit and a World War II veteran grandfather taught him that service is bigger than self.
After 9/11, Eric answered his generation’s call, graduating from the U.S.
Air Force Academy near the top of his class, rising to the rank of Captain in Special Operations units, and later serving with the CIA as a Case Officer where he conducted high-risk intelligence missions that kept Americans safe.
