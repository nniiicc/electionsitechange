734), which prevents biological males from participating in school sports programs under Title IX.
Eric believes that women’s sports should be reserved for biological females to ensure fair competition.
He supports legislation that clearly defines sex based on biological difference in federal law to protect the fairness of women’s athletic competitions from elementary school to college.
Eric sees this as a common-sense issue of fairness and safety, and he will fight in Congress to protect equal opportunities for girls and women in sports.
