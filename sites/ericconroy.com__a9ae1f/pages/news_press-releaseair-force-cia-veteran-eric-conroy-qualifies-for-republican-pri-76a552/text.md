Air Force, CIA Veteran Eric Conroy Qualifies for Republican Primary Ballot in OH-01
Cincinnati, OH – Yesterday Republican Eric Conroy’s nominating petitions were certified, qualifying him for the Republican Primary ballot for Congress in Ohio’s 1st Congressional District.
“I’m running for Congress because southwest Ohio deserves a representative who will stand up to liberal extremism, bring back common sense, and put our communities first,” Conroy said.
“This campaign is about restoring common sense, advancing America First policies, and fighting for middle-class families who are tired of being ignored by career politicians like Greg Landsman.”
Conroy’s successful petition filing marks another key milestone for his growing campaign, which has built strong grassroots support since launching earlier this year and dominated his primary opponents in fundraising.
With ballot access secured, Conroy will continue focusing on meeting voters across the district and laying out a clear contrast with liberal career politician Greg Landsman–who has failed Ohio on inflation, crime, and border security.
“Voters want leadership that’s serious, principled, and ready to fight for them on day one,” Conroy added.
“This campaign is about service.
I’m going to defeat Greg Landsman and then go to Congress to get the job done.”
