“Voters in OH-01 are ready for new leadership that will put working families, public safety, and America First policies ahead of Washington dysfunction.
This fundraising report shows that our campaign has the momentum and the resources to win the Republican nomination and defeat out-of-touch liberal Greg Landsman.” Despite redistricting and uncertainty about the congressional map dominating part of the fall, Conroy raised $195,000 in the fourth quarter, bringing his total fundraising since entering the race this summer to over $330,000.
In addition, Conroy has loaned his campaign $300,000, bringing his total fundraising to over $600,000 and further underscoring his commitment to building a serious, well-funded that can secure victory in November.
Eric outpaced his closest primary competitor in fundraising 4-to-1 in the last three months and 2-to-1 for the duration of the campaign.
Since launching his campaign, Conroy’s support has come from grassroots donors throughout the district, Ohio, and across the country.
With strong early fundraising and significant personal investment, the campaign is positioned to continue expanding its voter outreach and to contrast Conroy’s common-sense, conservative vision with the failed, extreme liberal policies of Greg Landsman and Washington Democrats.
