Meet Joe Kerwin
Joe Kerwin’s roots in the 125th District run deep.
He is a lifelong resident of Dauphin County.
Joe’s family has a rich history of community involvement and service to Dauphin County, Pennsylvania, and America.
Joe is a graduate of Upper Dauphin Area High School, Penn State University, Penn State Dickinson School of Law, and the University of Pennsylvania’s Fels Institute of Government.
While at Penn State, Joe received an Army ROTC Scholarship, and upon graduation, he received a commission as an Army officer.
He attended Penn State Dickinson School of Law and the University of Pennsylvania while serving in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard.
He credits his academic achievements to the educational incentives that the United States Army offered him as a Soldier.
He is the recipient of numerous military decorations for his leadership and exemplary service.
Joe Kerwin has served in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard for 12 years and is presently a Captain in the 56th Stryker Brigade Combat Team.
He is a graduate of the U.S.
Army Ranger School and the Army Airborne School at Fort Benning, Georgia.
He deployed to East Africa in 2023 to support the Global War on Terrorism and led an infantry company in a combat zone while protecting vital American assets.
Joe Kerwin is very active in his community.
He is a member of the Knights of Columbus, Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, the American Legion Post 239, the National Rifle Association, the Catholic War Veterans of America, the Pennsylvania House Second Amendment Caucus, the Lower Paxton Lions Club, the Linglestown Area Civic Association, and the Short Mountain Conservation Club.
Prior to being elected as State Representative, he was a prosecutor in the Dauphin County District Attorney’s Office, where he worked to keep our community safe from drugs and violence.
The 125th Legislative District is in Dauphin County and consists of the boroughs of Berrysburg, Elizabethville, Dauphin, Halifax, Gratz, Lykens, Millersburg, Pillow, and Williamstown, and the townships of Halifax, Jackson, Jefferson, Middle Paxton, Lower Paxton, Mifflin, Reed, Rush, Upper Paxton, Washington, Wayne, West Hanover, Wiconisco, and Williams.
