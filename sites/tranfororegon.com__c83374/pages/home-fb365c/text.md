Thuy Delivers for Our Community Fighting for Working Families: Passed legislation preventing employers from stealing wages and expanded opportunities for employee-owned companies and B-Corps, helping workers build wealth and share in business success.
Supporting Veterans: Championed bills to prevent veteran suicide and expanded tuition benefits for children of disabled veterans.
Combating Human Trafficking: Cracked down on illegal massage businesses linked to trafficking and secured a hearsay exception in court to help survivors of sex trafficking seek justice.
Prioritizing Emergency Preparedness: Secured risk insurance for fuel tank owners for disaster recovery, preserved the state’s volunteer emergency response database, and supported legislation to improve agency coordination during disasters.
Protecting Families’ Choices: Worked to safeguard access to reproductive healthcare and secured critical investments for child care.
Committed to Transparency and Accountability: Thuy will continue pushing for an open, accountable government and delivering practical solutions that strengthen families, protect rights, support veterans, and ensure our community is prepared, resilient, and able to thrive.
