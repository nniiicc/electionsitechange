It is with profound respect, admiration, and immense gratitude that I and my team recognize the vast contributions the Indigenous Peoples of Idaho have made to our State, and to the entire country.
Without their foresight, effort, labor, and passion to preserve, protect and restore our natural resources, the Idaho we know and love would be vastly different.
