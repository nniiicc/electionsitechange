Meet Miguel
Miguel was born and raised here in Miami-Dade County in a working class family.
He graduated from Florida International University and later St.
Thomas College of Law.
While working his way through school, he served as legislative assistant where he helped families cut through state bureaucracy to get their needs met.
Miguel knows what it is like to struggle.
He has had to overcome bad choices he made in his youth.
His passion for service called him to run so he can make positive change for South Dade families.
