From standing alongside fellow Tennesseans at town halls and cleanups to preparing for a section hike of the Appalachian Trail with the group from Waypoint Vets ( https://waypointvets.org/ ) from June 10-15th, the momentum is building.
Waypoint Vets is an incredible organization and I’m poud to hit the trail with fellow veterans who remind me what service, freedom, and camaraderie truly mean.
Unfortunately, this means I won’t be present for the No Kings Day rally in person, but please believe that I’ll be there in spirit.
My energy and motivation will be with every person standing up for a democracy that listens, serves, and protects all of us.
Every day on this path reminds me why I stepped into this race: not for attention, but for the people who’ve been left behind.
This month is packed with movement, literally and figuratively.
From the Juneteenth Pints with Progressives night in Gallatin to the cookout in White County, I’ll keep showing up where it matters.
I want to ensure that everyone’s voices are heard.
The road ahead isn’t easy, but I didn’t sign up for easy.
I signed up to stand up for what I believe in and protect those that need protecting.
Thank you for believing in this campaign.
If you’re ready to walk with me, visit MikeforTN6.com to learn more or lend your support.
We’re building this together.
Let’s keep believing.
