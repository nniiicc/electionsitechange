This latest act of aggression by the Trump administration follows a pattern of brutality that has been waged against the American people this past year, all under the guise of ‘immigration enforcement.’ From local protests in Minnesota to citizens coast-to-coast, we are united in condemning this killing and demanding immediate accountability for this unwarranted loss of life.
What we are witnessing are the heinous consequences of allowing a federal agency to operate like an unregulated private army, accountable to no one but the executive branch.
If our federal government remains complicit in the killing of citizens in broad daylight, then it is our duty to remove those responsible from power and restore the rule of law to prevent such tragedies from ever happening again.
We cannot stand by while our neighbors are treated as enemies of the state.
Please join me in offering our deepest condolences to the friends and family of the victim in Minneapolis.
We must work tirelessly to replace those in power who have fostered this culture of violence, ensuring that tragedies like this never happen again.
