I’m Mike Croley, a Navy veteran, former law enforcement officer, and lifelong public servant.
I’m running for Congress in Tennessee’s 6th District because when I asked for help, no one answered my call.
My path was crumbling under my feet.
This isn’t a career move.
This is a call to serve.
Our district deserves a representative who listens, who shows up, and who tells the truth; even when it’s hard.
That’s the promise I’m making.
This campaign is for the teachers, the truckers, the nurses, the farmers, the folks busting their ass every day and still falling behind.
I’m here for you and I’ll be here with you every step of the way.
Let’s build something better, together.
