Host a postcard writing party and bring people together to write to voters across TN-6.
Postcard parties are an easy, meaningful way for supporters to take action together.
The campaign will provide the postcards, voter addresses, message guidance, instructions, and next steps so hosts and volunteers know exactly what to do.
Hosts are responsible for purchasing postage for the postcards they mail.
Because postage is a volunteer-provided campaign expense, hosts will also need to complete an in-kind donation form so the campaign can properly track the contribution.
Whether you gather three friends or fill a room, every postcard helps us reach more voters, grow the Croley Crew, and build this campaign county by county. prosperity, freedom, equality!
