This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website, email, SMS program, and other campaign activities.
Information We Collect We may collect the following types of information: Personal information: Name, address, phone number, email, and payment details (if you make a donation).
Automatically collected information: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and device identifiers.
SMS and email engagement data: Message opens, clicks, and responses to help us improve outreach.
How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Communicate campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, events, and fundraising appeals.
Provide voter information, such as election dates and polling locations.
Process contributions in compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) regulations.
Improve the effectiveness of our outreach, website, and digital tools.
Text Messaging Program By opting in to our SMS program, you consent to receive recurring campaign messages as described in our Terms & Conditions.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
How We Share Your Information We will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
We may share limited information with trusted vendors, service providers, or partners who help us operate the campaign (e.g., texting platforms).
Your Choices Opting out of SMS or email: You can stop receiving texts by replying STOP or unsubscribe from emails by clicking the unsubscribe link.
Updating your information: Contact us at team@mikecroley.com to update or correct your information.
Children’s Privacy Our campaign does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
