Fundraising House Party Program
One of the best ways to grow a people-powered campaign is neighbor to neighbor.
Hosting a fundraising house party gives supporters a chance to introduce Mike to their friends, family, and community while helping raise the resources needed to compete across TN-6.
A house party can be simple.
It can be coffee and cookies, a backyard gathering, a dinner, or a casual meet-and-greet.
The goal is to bring people together, share why this race matters, and invite them to invest in a campaign built on service, honesty, and showing up.
The campaign can help hosts with planning guidance, donation links, invitation language, and next steps.
