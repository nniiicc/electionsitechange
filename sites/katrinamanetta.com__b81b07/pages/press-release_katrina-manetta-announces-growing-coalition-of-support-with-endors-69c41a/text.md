Manetta has earned the endorsements of State Representative Mike McFall (House District 14), Sterling Heights Councilman Mike Radtke, the Eastern Michigan University College Democrats , and United Steelworkers District 1 .
She has also been recognized as a Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate.
Together, the endorsements reflect support from leaders committed to strengthening Michigan’s communities, expanding opportunity for working families, empowering the next generation of civic leaders, and advocating for safe neighborhoods.
United Steelworkers District 1 represents thousands of workers across Michigan and the Midwest, advocating for good-paying union jobs, workplace safety, and the rights of working people.
The Eastern Michigan University College Democrats are a student-led organization focused on increasing civic engagement, developing future leaders, and encouraging young people to participate in the democratic process.
The Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate distinction recognizes candidates who have demonstrated a commitment to governing with gun safety in mind if elected.
“I’m incredibly honored to earn the trust and support of leaders and organizations that are deeply invested in building stronger communities,” Manetta said.
“Whether it’s standing up for working people, encouraging young people to get involved in public service, or working toward safer neighborhoods, these supporters share a belief that government should listen to people, solve problems, and deliver results.
That’s exactly the kind of representative I intend to be for the people of the 58th District.” Manetta, a lifelong Macomb County resident, service worker, and community organizer, has built her campaign around lowering costs for working families, protecting workers’ rights, strengthening public safety and education, and delivering practical solutions rooted in local experience.
Michigan’s 58th House District includes parts of Sterling Heights, Warren, Utica and Shelby Township and is widely considered one of the most competitive districts in the state.
About Katrina Manetta Katrina Manetta is a Democrat running for Michigan State House in the 58th District.
A lifelong Macomb County resident, Manetta is a community leader and service worker running to lower costs for families, strengthen public safety, protect workers’ rights, and deliver practical solutions rooted in our community.
For more information about the campaign, visit katrinamanetta.com .
