SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice.

TABLE OF CONTENTS 1.
WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
Personal Information You Disclose to Us In Short: We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us.
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you sign up for email updates, volunteer opportunities, or events; make a donation through linked third-party platforms; contact us; or otherwise interact with our Services.
Name Email address Phone number Mailing address Contact preferences Sensitive Information In Short: We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information beyond political engagement.
We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, or health information.
Engagement with a political campaign may be inferred from your interactions with the Services.
Payment Information Donations are processed by ActBlue.
We do not directly collect or store full credit card or bank account numbers.
Please review ActBlue’s privacy policy for more information.
If merchandise is offered in the future, payments may be processed by additional third-party providers, which will handle payment data according to their own privacy policies.
Information Automatically Collected In Short: Some information is collected automatically.
IP address Browser and device characteristics Operating system and language preferences Referring URLs Pages viewed and time spent on the site Location Information We may receive approximate location information (such as city or state) inferred from IP address.
We do not collect precise GPS location data.
Google APIs and Maps Our website may include embedded Google Maps.
We do not receive personal location data from Google Maps.
Google’s data collection is governed by its own privacy policies.
Information From Other Sources In Short: We may receive limited information from third-party platforms.
This may include information from social media platforms when you interact with our content, or from service providers that assist with campaign communications.
Lawful voter file use, if applicable in the future, is governed by election and privacy laws.

In Short: To operate the campaign and communicate with you.
Campaign communications and updates Volunteer coordination Donation processing and acknowledgements Responding to inquiries Website analytics and improvement Legal compliance 3.
WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?
In Short: With trusted service providers only.
Email and communications platforms Website hosting and analytics providers Payment processors (such as ActBlue) Event and volunteer platforms (such as Mobilize.us, when you choose to register) Consent to Receive Text Messages We may send you SMS text messages if you have opted in to receive them.
Mobile information, including your phone number and opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
All categories of personal information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.
This information will not be shared with any third parties.
We do not sell your personal information.
In Short: Yes.
We may use Google Analytics and similar tools, which may be configured now or in the future for aggregated reporting, remarketing, or demographic insights.

HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?
In Short: Only as long as necessary.
Personal information is retained only as long as needed for campaign purposes or as required by law, then securely deleted or anonymized.
6.
HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?
In Short: With reasonable safeguards.
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
7.
DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?
In Short: No.
Our Services are not intended for children under 18.
If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian contact us before providing personal information.

In Short: You may have rights depending on where you live.

You may unsubscribe from emails at any time.
