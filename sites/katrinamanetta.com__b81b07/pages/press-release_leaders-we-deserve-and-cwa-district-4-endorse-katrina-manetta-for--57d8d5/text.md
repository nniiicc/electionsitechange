14, 2026 — Katrina Manetta, Democratic candidate for Michigan’s 58th House District, today announced endorsements from Leaders We Deserve and Communications Workers of America (CWA) District 4 , adding additional national support for a new generation of political leadership and organized labor to her campaign.
Leaders We Deserve is a grassroots organization focused on helping elect young leaders to Congress and state legislatures across the country.
The organization works with endorsed candidates on areas including fundraising, messaging, scheduling, and campaign strategy as it seeks to build the next generation of elected leadership.
CWA District 4 covers Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, and Wisconsin and includes union members working across telecommunications, public services, and other industries.
The district’s political program works to build workers’ political power and support candidates and policies that defend workers’ rights.
“Working people deserve leaders who understand what their lives actually look like, and our communities deserve a new generation of people willing to step up and serve,” Manetta said.
“That’s what makes these endorsements so meaningful to me.
CWA members know the importance of having a voice on the job and leaders who will stand with working families.
Leaders We Deserve is investing in candidates who are ready to bring fresh energy and a different perspective to government.
I’m proud to have both organizations standing with our campaign as we work to bring that kind of representation to the 58th District.” The endorsements build on support Manetta has received from labor unions, community organizations, advocacy groups, and Democratic leaders at the local, state and national levels.
Manetta, a lifelong Macomb County resident, service worker, and community organizer, is running on a platform focused on lowering costs for working families, strengthening public safety and education, protecting workers’ rights, and delivering practical solutions rooted in local experience.
Michigan’s 58th House District includes parts of Sterling Heights, Warren, Utica and Shelby Township and is widely considered one of the most competitive districts in the state.
About Katrina Manetta Katrina Manetta is a Democrat running for Michigan State House in the 58th District.
A lifelong Macomb County resident, Manetta is a community leader and service worker running to lower costs for families, strengthen public safety, protect workers’ rights, and deliver practical solutions rooted in our community.
For more information about the campaign, visit katrinamanetta.com .
