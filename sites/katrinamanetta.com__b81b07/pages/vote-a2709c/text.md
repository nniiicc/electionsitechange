How to Vote in Michigan’s 58th House District
Voting should be simple and accessible.
This page explains how to vote in Michigan’s 58th House District, whether you plan to vote in person, early, or by mail.
Where You Live
Michigan’s 58th House District
Michigan House District 58 includes parts of Shelby Township, Utica, Sterling Heights, and Warren in Macomb County.
If you live in one of these communities, this page will help you get ready to vote.
2026 Elections
What Election Is This?
In August, voters choose which candidates advance to the general election in November.
Katrina Manetta is running as a Democrat in the August primary for State Representative in Michigan’s 58th House District.
Primary Election
August 4, 2026
General Election
November 3, 2026
Not Required
Party Registration
Michigan does not require party registration.
When you vote in the August primary, you choose one party’s ballot at the polling place.
That choice is private and does not register you with a party.
Your Options
How Can I Vote?
Election Day
Vote in Person
- Polls are open 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- You can vote if you are in line by 8:00 PM
- Bring photo ID or sign an affidavit
Before Election Day
Vote Early
Michigan offers early in-person voting before Election Day.
- Dates and locations vary by city and township
- Same voting options as Election Day
Absentee
Vote by Mail
Any registered Michigan voter can vote by mail.
Steps:
- Request a ballot
- Vote at home
- Return by mail or drop box
Ballots must be received by 8:00 PM on Election Day.
