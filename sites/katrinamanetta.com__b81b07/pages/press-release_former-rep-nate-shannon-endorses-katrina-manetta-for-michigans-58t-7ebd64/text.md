Nate Shannon endorses Katrina Manetta for Michigan’s 58th State House district September 4, 2026 STERLING HEIGHTS, Mich., Sept.
4, 2026 — Katrina Manetta, Democratic candidate for Michigan’s 58th State House District, today announced the endorsement of former state Rep.
Nate Shannon, who represented the district before incumbent Republican Ron Robinson won the seat in 2024.
Shannon, a former teacher who served in the State House from 2019 to 2025, said voters are looking for a new generation of leaders who understand their communities and are focused on the issues affecting people’s everyday lives.
“I’m proud to support Katrina Manetta because she represents the kind of strong, community-focused leadership we need in Lansing,” Shannon said.
“Having represented this district for six years, I know what it takes to fight for the people who live here and deliver results for our communities.
Katrina has the energy, determination, and understanding of the issues facing working families to be an effective voice from day one.
She’s already doing the work, building relationships, and showing that she’ll put people ahead of politics.
I know this district, I care deeply about its future, and I’m confident Katrina is the right person to represent the 58th District.” “Nate knows this district and what it takes to represent the people who live here,” Manetta said.
“His support says something important about this moment.
People are ready for leadership that is connected to the community, understands the pressures working people are under, and is willing to put problem-solving ahead of politics as usual.” “I’m a political outsider running for office because too many people feel like Lansing and politics at large have stopped working for them,” Manetta continued.
“We have an opportunity to turn the page and bring a fresh generation of leadership to Lansing that listens, works hard, and stays focused on delivering results for the people we represent.” Manetta, a lifelong Macomb County resident, service worker, and community organizer, is running on a platform focused on lowering costs for working families, strengthening public safety and education, protecting workers’ rights, and delivering practical solutions rooted in local experience.
Michigan’s 58th House District includes parts of Sterling Heights, Warren, Utica and Shelby Township and is widely considered one of the most competitive districts in the state.
About Katrina Manetta Katrina Manetta is a Democrat running for Michigan State House in the 58th District.
A lifelong Macomb County resident, Manetta is a community leader and service worker running to lower costs for families, strengthen public safety, protect workers’ rights, and deliver practical solutions rooted in our community.
For more information about the campaign, visit katrinamanetta.com .
