The Michigan Building and Construction Trades Council cited Manetta’s commitment to working families, responsible economic development, infrastructure investment, workforce development, public safety, and policies that support good-paying union jobs.
The council also praised her leadership, integrity, and willingness to listen to residents, calling her “the right choice to be the next State Representative for District 58.” In announcing its endorsement, the Arab American Caucus of the Michigan Democratic Party said Manetta understands the challenges working people face because she has lived them.
The caucus praised her integrity, accountability, and commitment to giving everyday people a stronger voice in Lansing.
“I’m incredibly grateful to have the support of both of these organizations,” Manetta said.
“The 58th District is strengthened by hardworking union families and by the incredible diversity of our communities.
I’m honored to earn the trust of organizations that are committed to expanding opportunity, strengthening neighborhoods, and ensuring every resident has a voice.
I’ll work every day in Lansing to fight for working people and deliver practical solutions that improve people’s lives.” The Michigan Building and Construction Trades Council represents skilled trades professionals across Michigan and advocates for policies that strengthen the state’s workforce, expand economic opportunity, invest in infrastructure, and uphold strong labor standards.
The Arab American Caucus of the Michigan Democratic Party works to ensure Arab American voices are represented throughout Michigan’s Democratic Party and in public life.
Together, the endorsements reflect the broad coalition behind Manetta’s campaign, which continues to gain support from labor organizations, community leaders, and advocates across Michigan.
Manetta, a lifelong Macomb County resident and longtime service industry worker, is running for Michigan House District 58 on a platform focused on lowering costs for working families, strengthening neighborhoods, protecting workers’ rights, and delivering practical solutions.
Her campaign emphasizes a new generation of leadership rooted in lived experience and a commitment to making government work for the people it serves.
Michigan’s 58th House District includes parts of Sterling Heights, Warren, Utica and Shelby Township and is widely considered one of the most competitive districts in the state.
About Katrina Manetta Katrina Manetta is a Democrat running for Michigan State House in the 58th District.
A lifelong Macomb County resident, Manetta is a community leader and service worker running to lower costs for families, strengthen public safety, protect workers’ rights, and deliver practical solutions rooted in our community.
For more information about the campaign, visit katrinamanetta.com .
