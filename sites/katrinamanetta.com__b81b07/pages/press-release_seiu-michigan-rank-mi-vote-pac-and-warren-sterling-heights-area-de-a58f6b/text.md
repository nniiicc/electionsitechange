STERLING HEIGHTS, Mich., August 27, 2026 — Democratic candidate Katrina Manetta today announced endorsements from SEIU Michigan, Rank MI Vote PAC, and the Warren Sterling Heights Area Democratic Club in her campaign for Michigan’s 58th House District, adding labor, democracy advocates, and local grassroots Democrats to her growing coalition of support.
SEIU Michigan represents nearly 35,000 public- and private-sector workers across the state.
Its members include health care workers, public employees, property service workers, and corrections officers who care for patients, maintain roads and public infrastructure, protect communities, and help deliver essential services to Michiganders.
Rank MI Vote PAC supports pro-voter, pro-democracy candidates across Michigan and advocates for expanding voter choice through ranked choice voting.
The organization works to give voters a greater voice in elections and encourage a political system that is more representative and responsive to the people it serves.
The Warren Sterling Heights Area Democratic Club brings an especially local voice to the latest round of endorsements.
The club organizes Democrats in Center Line, Sterling Heights, Utica, and Warren, including three communities represented in the 58th House District.
“These endorsements represent the kind of coalition we’re building to win this race: working people, local organizers, and Michiganders who believe their government should actually listen to them,” Manetta said.
“SEIU members keep our communities running every day.
Rank MI Vote is fighting to give voters a stronger voice in our elections.
And the Warren Sterling Heights Area Democratic Club is made up of people doing the grassroots work right here in the communities I’m running to represent.
I’m proud to have their support as we build a campaign focused on working families, accountable government, and real representation for our community.”
The endorsements continue to broaden the diverse coalition supporting Manetta’s campaign, which includes labor unions, community organizations, advocacy groups, and leaders from across Michigan.
Manetta, a lifelong Macomb County resident, service worker, and community organizer, is running on a platform focused on lowering costs for working families, strengthening public safety and education, protecting workers’ rights, and delivering practical solutions rooted in local experience.
Michigan’s 58th House District includes parts of Sterling Heights, Warren, Utica and Shelby Township and is widely considered one of the most competitive districts in the state.
About Katrina Manetta
Katrina Manetta is a Democrat running for Michigan State House in the 58th District.
A lifelong Macomb County resident, Manetta is a community leader and service worker running to lower costs for families, strengthen public safety, protect workers’ rights, and deliver practical solutions rooted in our community.
