| | A major topic of the legislative session is the Blueprint for Maryland's Future, also known as "Kirwan" or the Commission on Innovation & Excellence in Education.
The legislation is the culmination of years of work by a blue ribbon panel studying how Maryland funds education and what we are paying for.
Much of the focus has been on the total cost, how to allocate funds, and how to obtain the revenue necessary, but I also want to talk a bit about what the money will be used for. |
