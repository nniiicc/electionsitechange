How Did Colorado Go From A $1.3 Billion Surplus To A $1.5 Billion Deficit In Eight Years?
Here’s How:
In 2018, Colorado Governor John Hickenlooper signed a $28.9 Billion state budget into law, and we had a $1.3 Billion surplus.
In November of 2018, Jared Polis was elected Governor of Colorado, and the Democrats took control of the General Assembly.
Eight years later, the proposed budget is $50.5 Billion and has a $1.5 billion deficit.
Over the past eight years, no one wanted to deal with the structural deficit.
Colorado’s Structural Deficit Problem Explained.
The budget in the 2018-19 fiscal year had huge economic growth and a windfall from the federal tax reform.
In addition, the massive influx of one-time money during the COVID recovery masked the structural deficit.
Now the bill is due, and the budget is short of $1.5 Billion.
