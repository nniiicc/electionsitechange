Here’s what Scott has delivered and what he’s ready to tackle next.
01 · Affordability & Housing More homes.
Fewer people on the street.
San Diego is one of the most expensive places in America to buy a home, and too many families are getting priced out.
The other end of the same crisis is on our sidewalks.
This year, Scott co-led a bipartisan law that scraps a 50-year-old rule holding back affordable homebuilding.
His earlier law lets nonprofits put federal homelessness money to work directly, and he's fought to get homeless veterans off the street and into homes.
Next up Build more homes, get more people off the street and into housing, and cut the red tape that slows both down.
What he's already done Housing Supply Expansion Act Scraps a 1974 rule that forced manufactured homes onto a permanent steel chassis, so builders can make more affordable homes for less.
Co-led with a Republican and signed into law as part of the ROAD to Housing Act.
2026 Housing Assistance Efficiency Act Cut the red tape so local nonprofits can get federal homelessness aid straight to the people who need it.
2015 02 · Health Care Care you can afford.
Help when you need it.
Nobody should have to choose between a prescription and groceries.
Scott’s plan laid the groundwork for Medicare drug-price negotiation, $35 insulin and a cap on what seniors pay for medicine.
He’s also fought to get more San Diegans into addiction treatment and mental health care, close to home.
Next up Bring lower drug prices to more families, keep premiums in check, and make it easier to get mental health and addiction care in San Diego.
What he's already done Reduced Costs and Continued Cures Act Laid the groundwork for Medicare drug-price negotiation, $35-a-month insulin and a $2,000 yearly cap on seniors’ drug costs, all now law through the Inflation Reduction Act.
2022 Opioid Recovery Centers Renewal Keeps federal funding flowing through 2028 for recovery centers that offer addiction treatment, mental health care and support all in one place.
2025 Methamphetamine Response Act Co-led the bipartisan push to name meth an emerging national drug threat and require a federal plan to fight addiction and overdoses.
2022 03 · The Tijuana River Stop the sewage.
Open the beaches.
For years, sewage pouring across the border has shut down beaches in Imperial Beach and Coronado and made people sick, including Navy SEALs who train in that water.
Scott and San Diego’s delegation in Congress went after the money to fix it, and got it: $650 million, enough to fully repair and expand the South Bay plant that treats the sewage on our side of the border.
Work is underway on both sides, and every phase that wraps up means cleaner water.
Next up Finish the plant, keep the federal money coming, and get the state, our cities and local partners to help run it and keep it running for the long haul.
What he's already done $650 Million to Fix the Plant It started with $300 million in the 2020 trade deal.
By late 2024, Scott and the San Diego delegation had locked in enough to fully repair and expand the South Bay treatment plant.
2020–24 Brought the EPA to the River Got the EPA Administrator to the Tijuana River Valley to see the crisis firsthand, alongside Rep.
Juan Vargas.
2021 Pushed for an Emergency Declaration Pressed for a federal emergency declaration for the Tijuana River Valley through the annual defense bill.
2023 04 · Clean Energy & Climate Clean power.
A coast that's ready.
Climate change isn't abstract here.
It's wildfire smoke, crumbling bluffs and rising seas.
Scott works with both parties to cut pollution and build clean energy, and to get San Diego ready for what's already coming.
Next up Speed up clean energy projects, prevent the next big wildfire, and plan now for higher seas around San Diego Bay.
What he's already done Methane Pollution Resolution Restored limits on methane leaks from oil and gas operations.
The first climate law of that Congress.
2021 POWER ON Act Strengthens the power grid and builds transmission for clean energy, through the infrastructure law.
2021 Fix Our Forests Act Speeds up forest work that cuts wildfire risk.
Co-led with the Republican chair of the Natural Resources Committee and passed the House 279 to 141.
Passed the House, 2025 05 · Defense & Veterans This is a Navy town.
Act like it.
San Diego is home to one of the biggest military and veteran communities in the country, and the Navy, the shipyards and the defense industry put food on a lot of tables here.
Scott has fought to steer more Navy repair work to local shipyards, expanded GI Bill benefits for Purple Heart recipients and helped get homeless veterans into housing.
Keeping promises to people who served is the floor, not a talking point.
Next up Faster VA care, more veteran housing, and keeping San Diego's bases and shipyards strong.
What he's already done Veteran HOUSE Act Opens HUD-VA housing vouchers to veterans with other-than-honorable discharges, who face higher risk of homelessness.
2021 Sgt.
Daniel Somers Network of Support Acts Lets troops and veterans name family and friends who get mental-health resources to support them.
Brought to Scott by the Somers family of Coronado.
2019, 2020 Veteran PEER Act Expands veteran-to-veteran peer counseling at the VA.
2018 06 · Jobs & the San Diego Economy Good jobs, made in San Diego.
From the biotech labs in Torrey Pines to the working waterfront, San Diego's economy runs on smart people and hard work.
Scott fights for the research dollars, defense work and infrastructure money that keep those jobs here.
Next up More research funding, a stronger ship-repair industry and more federal dollars for local projects.
What he's already done Employer Participation in Repayment Act Lets employers help pay down workers' student loans tax-free.
2020, permanent 2025 Building Chips in America Act Cuts permitting delays so new American chip factories get built faster.
2024 Make Every Small Business Count Act Gives defense contractors credit for hiring small businesses at every level of a project, not just the first.
