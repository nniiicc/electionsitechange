Scott doesn't just vote on bills.
He writes them and gets them signed.
Roughly two dozen are now federal law, most with a Republican partner.
Six laws to know Health Care Reduced Costs and Continued Cures Act Scott’s plan laid the groundwork for letting Medicare negotiate drug prices, capping insulin at $35 a month and seniors’ out-of-pocket drug costs at $2,000 a year.
Those ideas became law in the Inflation Reduction Act.
2022 Housing Housing Supply Expansion Act Scraps a 1974 rule that forced manufactured homes onto a permanent steel chassis, so builders can make more affordable homes for less.
Co-led with a Republican and signed into law as part of the ROAD to Housing Act.
2026 Housing Housing Assistance Efficiency Act Cut the red tape so local nonprofits can get federal homelessness aid straight to the people who need it.
2015 Veterans & Military Veteran HOUSE Act Opens HUD-VA housing vouchers to veterans with other-than-honorable discharges, who face higher risk of homelessness.
2021 Health Care Opioid Recovery Centers Renewal Keeps federal funding flowing through 2028 for recovery centers that offer addiction treatment, mental health care and support services all in one place.
2025 Health Care Methamphetamine Response Act Scott co-led the bipartisan effort to name meth an emerging national drug threat, requiring a federal plan to fight addiction and overdoses.
San Diego was once called the meth capital of America.
2022 The Full Record Every Scott Peters bill that became law Health Care Preventing Drug Shortages Act Requires early warning when critical medicines are at risk of running short.
2020 Health Care LAB Act Protected seniors' access to lab tests by stopping Medicare cuts that threatened small labs.
2019 Health Care ORPHAN Cures Act Fixes a drug-pricing rule that punished treatments for more than one rare disease.
2025 Health Care Medicare and Medicaid Fraud Prevention Act Stops payments to providers who have died by checking Medicaid rolls against federal death records.
2025 Health Care STANDUP Act Teaches students and teachers to spot a classmate in crisis and get them help.
The idea came from students at Bernardo Heights Middle School.
2022 Veterans & Military Sgt.
Daniel Somers Network of Support Acts Lets troops and veterans name family and friends who get mental-health resources to support them.
Brought to Scott by the Somers family of Coronado.
2019, 2020 Veterans & Military Veteran PEER Act Expands veteran-to-veteran peer counseling at the VA.
2018 Veterans & Military VA Family Therapists Lets the VA hire more marriage and family therapists for veterans.
2016 Veterans & Military GI Bill Protection During Rehab Keeps veterans from losing GI Bill time while their vocational rehab is on hold.
2018 Veterans & Military Purple Heart GI Bill Fix Every Purple Heart recipient now gets full Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits, no matter how long they served.
2017 Veterans & Military Smart Ship Repair Act Steers more Navy repair work to private shipyards, backing San Diego's ship repair industry and the thousands of local jobs it supports.
2025 Jobs & Economy Employer Participation in Repayment Act Lets employers help pay down workers' student loans tax-free.
2020, permanent 2025 Jobs & Economy Building Chips in America Act Cuts permitting delays so new American chip factories get built faster.
2024 Jobs & Economy Make Every Small Business Count Act Gives defense contractors credit for hiring small businesses at every level of a project, not just the first.
2013 Jobs & Economy Government Printing Reform Stopped Congress from printing paper copies of documents already online.
2016 Energy & Climate Methane Pollution Resolution Restored limits on methane leaks from oil and gas operations.
The first climate law of that Congress.
2021 Energy & Climate POWER ON Act Strengthens the power grid and builds transmission for clean energy, through the infrastructure law.
2021 Energy & Climate USEIT Act Funds research to capture carbon pollution and turn it into useful products.
2020 Energy & Climate Advanced Reactor Fee Reduction Act Lowers licensing fees for next-generation nuclear reactors.
2024 Energy & Climate HYPE Act Gives hydropower projects more time to get permitted.
