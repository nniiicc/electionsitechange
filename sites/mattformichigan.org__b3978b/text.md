Protecting Access to Healthcare I was proud to spearhead efforts in Lansing to enshrine Affordable Care Act protections into state law.
This will ensure that those with pre-existing conditions can still have quality health insurance.
It also ensures essential benefits will be covered, and children can stay on their parents’ health insurance until age 26.

That’s why I created the Downtown Business Coalition to give small business owners a direct line to their representatives as well as the governor.
I was also proud to introduce HB 5176 which would suspend Michigan’s sales tax for one day in August for back to school shopping to help out parents and kids who are getting ready for the upcoming year.
