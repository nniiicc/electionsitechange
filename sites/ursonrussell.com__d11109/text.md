Once a beacon of opportunity and innovation, our great state of California has long been admired for its stunning landscapes, thriving agriculture, and dynamic economy.
Yet, in recent years, many have noticed challenges that seem to cast a shadow over its former glory and the question is can we get back there.
Government is not very complex.
It is either run as a public service/public benefit entity or it’s not.
When we understand that government is a tool for our benefit and protection, we also need to understand what the requirements of government are to ensure that.
The basic requirements of government are: Establishing Equal Blind Justice Ensuring Domestic Tranquility Providing for the Common Defense Promoting General Welfare Securing our Current and Future Generations Is it possible to get back to basics?
You’ll be encouraged to know that the answer is YES!
Then how do we do it?
