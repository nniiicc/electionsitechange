AARP’s Livability Index platform ranks America’s communities using indicators important to people of all ages.
Included are essentials, such as accessibility to quality medical care, parks, grocery stores and affordable housing.
There are also social and cultural considerations, such as lively, engaged neighborhoods; plus access to exercise, walkability and easy/affordable transportation options.
