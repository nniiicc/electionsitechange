About the Candidate
Rudolph Drake is running for Alabama State Senate to bring integrity, service, and real results to Alabama.
Born in Huntsville and raised in Harvest, growing up in North Alabama taught him the value of hard work, honesty, and taking care of your neighbors.
After high school, Rudolph served in the United States Navy in aviation quality assurance and later worked as a Department of Defense Police Officer and in aviation leadership supporting aircraft operations worldwide.
Married to his wife, Pamela, and a proud father and stepfather, Rudolph is a Democrat who puts people over politics and is committed to delivering results for North Alabama.
