Refund Policy
Herzig for Congress
All contributions made through WinRed to Herzig for Congress are considered final and non-refundable, except in cases required by law or clearly resulting from error.
However, we understand that mistakes can happen.
WinRed provides a process for refund requests under limited circumstances.
1.
General Refund Policy
In compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) regulations:
• All donations are final once processed.
• Refunds may only be issued for:
– Duplicate transactions
– Obvious processing errors
– Fraudulent or unauthorized charges
– Contributions that exceed federal limits
– Contributions prohibited under federal law (e.g., from corporations or foreign nationals)
Herzig for Congress reserves the right to review each refund request on a case-by-case basis.
2.
How to Request a Refund
Since all contributions made through our website are processed by WinRed, refund requests must be submitted directly to WinRed:
WinRed Support:
https://winred.com/support/
Or email: support@winred.com
When contacting WinRed, please include:
• Full name
• Email address used for donation
• Date and amount of the contribution
• Last four digits of the card used
• Screenshot or receipt of the charge (recommended)
3.
Recurring Donations (Subscription Refunds)
If you accidentally enrolled in a recurring donation:
• You may cancel the subscription at any time through your email receipt or WinRed account.
• Refunds for recurring charges follow the same rules as above (duplicate or erroneous charges may be refunded; general cancellations do not guarantee refunds).
4.
Campaign Rights Under Federal Law
The campaign may refuse, cancel, or refund any contribution that:
• Violates FEC regulations
• Exceeds lawful contribution limits
• Is made by a prohibited source
• Appears fraudulent or suspicious
If such a refund is required, it will be issued through WinRed.
5.
Questions About This Policy
For campaign-specific questions (not refund processing), contact:
