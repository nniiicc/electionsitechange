Accessibility Statement:
Herzig for Congress is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all individuals, including people with disabilities.
We strive to comply with the requirements of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the New Jersey Law Against Discrimination (N.J.S.A. 10:5-1 et seq.),
and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and working to ensure that all website features, content, and tools are accessible,
usable, and understandable.
Our Accessibility Efforts Include:
• Improving compatibility with screen readers
and assistive technologies
• Providing text alternatives for non-text content
• Ensuring clear navigation and readable design
• Maintaining color contrast
that supports visual accessibility
• Reviewing new and existing content
for accessibility compliance
Help Us Improve

Ongoing Commitment
Herzig for Congress will continue to evaluate and update this website to remain compliant with accessibility requirements under federal and New Jersey law, and to ensure equitable access for all users.
