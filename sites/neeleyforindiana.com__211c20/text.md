Meet Darrell Neeley Darrell Neeley is a 33-year Navy Veteran who was honorably discharged as a Commander.
He serves the community now as an associate pastor at Howard Baptist Church.
With a strong commitment to service, Darrell actively listens to the concerns of all of us and is dedicated to being our voice in the General Assembly.
His approach is rooted in integrity, empathy, and a deep understanding of the needs of all of us.
