I had the privilege of being with Judge Blair Edwards yesterday as she qualified to run for the Louisiana Supreme Court.
Blair has served Livingston Parish with integrity and a passion for people for more than 18 years in the 21st JDC and now on the 1st Circuit Court of Appeals.
I wholeheartedly support her candidacy.
She will serve us well as a Supreme Court Justice.
— Jeff Taylor, Livingston Parish Assessor
