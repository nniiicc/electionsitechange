Title IX: Women in Sports
Title IX(9) is the landmark federal civil rights law that was enacted as part of the Education Amendments of 1972.
Title IX was enacted to promote women’s athletics.
Since its introduction, female athletic participation in both high school and college has dramatically increased.
The Biden-Harris Administration and the radical Left are now directly attacking women and women’s sports by requiring schools to allow men who “self-identify” as female to compete against women.
