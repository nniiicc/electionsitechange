Following the conclusion of candidate qualification last week, no other Republicans qualified to run for District 56, which now includes portions of Cherokee, Cobb, and Fulton counties after 2020 Census-based redistricting.
“I am grateful and excited for the opportunity to again serve as our Republican nominee,” Senator Albers said.
“My record reflects that I have stood up for Republican values like limited government, lower taxes, and securing the integrity of our elections, and I am pleased that my fellow Republicans have recognized that.” Senator Albers now advances to the November general election.
Election Day is set for November 8.
