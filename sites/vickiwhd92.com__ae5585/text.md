With over 30 years as a public defender, Vicki knows what it takes to work hard to make a difference in someone else’s life.
When she moved back home to Oklahoma in 2022, Vicki saw a ton of change!
So much growth and promise, especially right here in House District 92.
In the midst of all the wonderful things happening around here, our state is also facing a crisis in education, great disparities in access to healthcare, and feeling the effects of a rocky economy.
By serving her community in the legislature, Vicki will fight to get back on track to being the best Oklahoma possible, for all of us.
