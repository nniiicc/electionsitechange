We are at an inflection point: we must come together and focus on the issues that unite us–instead of letting our differences and disagreements rule us.

“We are facing challenges in every corner of our state–from cost of living to getting to work and school safely.
I will not be intimidated and I will not back down when I believe policies will do more harm than good for my constituents and set us back as a state.
