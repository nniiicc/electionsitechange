Rep.
Daniel Nguyen Wins Re-Election in Landslide
Democratic incumbent fighting Trump and growing the economy approved by voters for 2 more years
SW Portland & Lake Oswego, Ore. – Rep.
Daniel Nguyen was easily re-elected to the State legislature yesterday by the voters of House District 38, spanning SW Portland and Lake Oswego and shares the following statement:
“Thank you for trusting me once again to represent you in Salem.
We are at an inflection point: we must come together and focus on the issues that unite us–instead of letting our differences and disagreements rule us.
I am committed to finding pragmatic solutions that create opportunities for all Oregonians.
“We are facing challenges in every corner of our state–from cost of living to getting to work and school safely.
I will not be intimidated and I will not back down when I believe policies will do more harm than good for my constituents and set us back as a state.
“I am so proud of the coalition that we have built around protecting Oregon values and finding creative ways to grow here at home in a time when the federal government is causing fear, chaos, and uncertainty.
Let’s get back to work.”
