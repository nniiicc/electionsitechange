House Resolution 66 , which directs state officials to study how to best manage the state’s native bees, butterflies and other insects native to Pennsylvania passed the Pennsylvania House by a bipartisan vote of 112-89 on Monday, May 4th 2026.
House resolutions do not need ratification by the state Senate or the governor’s signature, so this marks the legislation’s final step.
The Legislative Budget and Finance Committee (LBFC) will complete and release the report within the next six months." Proud of the work we are doing to help protect our Commonwealth's pollinators, and also as importantly, our State Insect: the Lightning Bug!
