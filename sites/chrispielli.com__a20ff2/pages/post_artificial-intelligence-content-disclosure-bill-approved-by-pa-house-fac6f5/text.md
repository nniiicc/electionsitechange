FriendsofChrisPielli@gmail.com All Posts Search Artificial intelligence content disclosure bill approved by PA House Chris Pielli Jun 24 1 min read The premise of this bill is simple.
If it’s AI, it has to say it’s AI.
People deserve to know if what they’re looking at is real or not when making purchases.
Disclosures should be clear and conspicuous in the same medium as the content generated to help consumers make better- informed and more secure purchases.
After passing the State House, the bill has now been sent to the State Senate for consideration.
