FriendsofChrisPielli@gmail.com All Posts Search Make Data Centers Pay Their Fair Share Chris Pielli Apr 6 1 min read Data centers represent rising electricity costs for entire communities in Pennsylvania.
This is unacceptable during a time of already rising costs for gas, groceries, housing, and utilities.
That's why I voted YES on the Data Center Act - House Bill 1834 - which passed the PA House yesterday.
House Bill 1834 would: Protect ratepayers by explicitly prohibiting large data centers from shifting costs to households and small businesses.
Require data centers to pay their fair share for costs and invests in energy affordability and low-income assistance to Pennsylvanians.
Protect the environment by requiring data centers to use cleaner energy, improve energy reliability, and reduce emissions.
Invest in new clean energy development.
