This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit votejeffclark.org and interact with our campaign.
By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined below.
01 Information We Collect We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you join our mailing list, submit the contact form, or donate to the campaign.
This may include: Identity Data: First name, last name, and mailing address.
Contact Data: Email address and phone number.
Financial Data: Donations are processed through WinRed, a secure third-party processor.
We do not store payment card details on our servers.
02 How We Use Your Information Your information is used strictly to further Jeff Clark's campaign for State Representative, including: Sending campaign updates, event invitations, and announcements.
Providing information on how to get involved and volunteer.
Processing donations and meeting Pennsylvania campaign finance disclosure requirements.
Improving website performance and user experience.
03 No-Sharing Policy We do not sell, rent, or lease our supporter lists to third parties.
Your personal information is never shared with outside organizations for their own marketing purposes.
We only disclose information when required by law — such as Pennsylvania campaign finance reporting — or to trusted service providers (such as email delivery services) who are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.
05 Google reCAPTCHA This site uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 to protect our forms from automated abuse. reCAPTCHA operates in the background and collects hardware and software information to distinguish humans from bots.
This information is sent to Google and subject to their Privacy Policy and Terms of Service .
06 Your Rights and Opt-Out Options Email: Every campaign email includes an unsubscribe link.
Text Messages: If you receive SMS updates, reply STOP at any time to opt out.
Data Access or Deletion: Contact us at any time to request a copy of your data or to have it removed from our records.
07 Security We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
API keys and configuration files are stored outside the publicly accessible web directory.
08 Pennsylvania Campaign Finance Compliance As a political committee, Friends for Jeff Clark complies with all Commonwealth of Pennsylvania campaign finance laws.
Certain donor information — including name and address — may be included in public disclosure reports filed with the Pennsylvania Department of State as required by law.
09 Changes to This Policy We reserve the right to update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
