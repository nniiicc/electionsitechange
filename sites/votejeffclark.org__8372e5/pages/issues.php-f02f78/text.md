JEFF CLARK 88TH DISTRICT ☰ Home About Issues Events Donate Join Contact ★ VOTE NOVEMBER 3 — General Election | Jeff Clark for State Representative · 88th District ★ Jeff Clark's Platform Jeff Clark Will Fight For You Common-sense conservative priorities for the 88th District 1 Controlling Spending State spending has increased almost 65% under two Democrat Governors — while household income has not.
Jeff Clark will go to Harrisburg and fight to rein in wasteful government spending, demand accountability for every dollar, and put the brakes on a spending trajectory that is unsustainable for our state's future.
2 Reducing Energy, Gasoline, and Grocery Prices Protecting family budgets and expanding opportunity for a brighter future.
Pennsylvania families are feeling the squeeze at the gas pump, at the grocery store, and on their energy bills every single month.
Jeff Clark will fight for policies that lower costs for hardworking families across the 88th District — cutting the red tape and burdensome regulations that drive up prices, and putting Pennsylvanians first instead of special interests.
3 Eliminating Property Taxes There has been too much talk and no action for years .
Seniors on fixed incomes should not be forced out of the homes they've owned for decades.
Jeff Clark is committed to real reform — not more promises, not more studies.
He will push hard for meaningful property tax elimination so that Pennsylvanians can stay in the homes and communities they love.
4 Ensuring Public Safety Jeff Clark knows that public safety is the foundation of everything else .
Jeff will be an unwavering advocate for our First Responders — our Police, Firefighters, and EMTs — fighting against defunding efforts, ensuring they have the resources and support they need, and working to make every neighborhood in the 88th District safer for every family.
5 Expanding Pennsylvania Energy Pennsylvania has the resources, the workforce, and the potential to be a world leader in energy development .
Jeff Clark will work to unleash that potential — supporting responsible development of Pennsylvania's natural gas, coal, and nuclear resources, and creating good-paying jobs in the 88th District and across the Commonwealth.
6 Term Limits Public service should be exactly that — service, not a career .
Jeff Clark believes that state representatives and senators should be term limited.
Career politicians who entrench themselves in Harrisburg year after year stop serving the people and start serving themselves.
Fresh voices, fresh ideas, and real accountability are what Pennsylvania families deserve.
Jeff will be a consistent and vocal advocate for term limits at the state level.
