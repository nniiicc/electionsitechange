Jeff has dedicated his career — and much of his life — to protecting and serving his neighbors, his community, and his country.
Military Service Jeff served his country with honor as a decorated veteran of the United States military.
His time in service instilled in him the values of discipline, sacrifice, and commitment to something greater than himself — values he carries with him every day.
Jeff understands the cost of freedom because he's paid part of that cost personally.
As your State Representative, he will always stand with veterans and ensure they receive the support and recognition they have earned.
Career in Law Enforcement After his military service, Jeff built a distinguished career in law enforcement.
Over the course of his career, he witnessed firsthand the challenges facing our communities — and the critical importance of public safety for families throughout the 88th District.
He developed deep respect for the men and women who put on a uniform every day to protect our neighborhoods.
As a retired officer, Jeff is uniquely qualified to advocate for strong, common-sense public safety policies in Harrisburg.
Why He's Running Jeff Clark is running for State Representative because he believes the 88th District deserves a voice in Harrisburg that actually fights for working families — not special interests, not career politicians, not wasteful government programs.
After watching state spending balloon by nearly 65% under two Democrat governors while household incomes have stagnated, Jeff knows that Pennsylvania families need a fighter who understands their struggles and will do something about it.
Jeff Clark has spent his life serving others.
Now he's asking for the honor of serving you in Harrisburg.
