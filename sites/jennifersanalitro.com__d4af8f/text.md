JOIN JENNIFER'S MISSION About Jennifer Meet Jennifer Sanalitro Jennifer Sanalitro is a sales executive, small business owner and current Illinois State Representative.
She serves the 48th District, covering Northern DuPage and Northwest Cook County.
Jennifer was elected to the Illinois House of Representatives in 2022 and brings a strong background of business management and community service to her role.
As a mother and active neighbor, she has been a leading voice for responsible leadership, economic opportunity, workforce development, public safety, and lower taxes.
Jennifer and her husband currently reside in their Hanover Park home where they raised two adult children.
Bobby owns and operates a small business in the automotive industry located in Roselle.
The 48th District includes the communities of Addison, Bensenville, Bloomingdale, Carol Stream, Elk Grove Village, Hanover Park, Itasca, Medinah, Roselle, Schaumburg, Wood Dale in suburban DuPage and Cook counties.
Jennifer is a proven leader, proud business owner, and two-term State Representative for Illinois’ 48th District.
With a passion for service and a deep commitment to her community, Jennifer has dedicated her career to making Illinois a better place to live, work, and raise a family.
For nearly three decades, Jennifer and her husband have called the 48th District home, raising two children and growing a successful small business.
Her firsthand experience as a parent, entrepreneur, and legislator gives her a unique and practical perspective on the challenges families and businesses face today.
In Springfield, Jennifer has fought for policies that strengthen our economy, support working families, and create safer, more vibrant communities.
Guided by common-sense leadership and real-world experience, she remains focused on delivering real results for the people of Illinois.
Jennifer believes in building a future where every family has the opportunity to thrive — together.

Jennifer on the Issues. . .
Putting Taxpayers First Jennifer is committed to protecting suburban families from reckless spending and unfair tax burdens.
She believes government must operate transparently and responsibly, ensuring every tax dollar is used wisely.
In Springfield, she has opposed bloated budgets and hidden deals that prioritize politics over people.
Whether standing against new transit taxes or billion-dollar spending increases, Rep.
Sanalitro continues to fight for fiscal responsibility and a fair deal for DuPage and Northwest Cook County residents.
Supporting Women and Strengthening Families Jennifer has made it a priority to support women’s health and empower families through thoughtful, compassionate policy.
She has sponsored legislation to expand access to fertility information and advocated for workplace protections for mothers.
Her work reflects a broader commitment to ensuring families have the knowledge, resources, and support they need to thrive.
From healthcare to family planning, Rep.
Sanalitro is focused on policies that strengthen the foundation of Illinois communities.
Fighting for Small Businesses, Job Creators, and Workers Jennifer is a strong advocate for small businesses and the hardworking men and women who power Illinois’ economy.
She believes government should not be a barrier to job creation and economic growth.
In Springfield, she has supported legislation that cuts red tape, promotes workforce development, and provides tax relief for local employers.
Whether it's helping small businesses recover, protecting workers from rising costs, or encouraging innovation and entrepreneurship, Rep.
Sanalitro is focused on building a stronger, more competitive Illinois where businesses can thrive and families can prosper.
Improving Access to Healthcare Improving healthcare access and affordability remains a cornerstone of Jennifer’s legislative agenda.
She has backed efforts to lower prescription drug costs, expand access to life-saving screenings, and promote children’s health and safety.
In addition to legislative action, she continues to connect constituents with vital health resources through local events and initiatives.
Rep.
Sanalitro’s goal is simple: make quality healthcare more accessible, affordable, and effective for every Illinois family.
