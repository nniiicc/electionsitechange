Living the American Dream Chris is passionate about protecting the American Dream because he has lived it.
Growing up in the small Texas towns of Throckmorton and Graham instilled in Chris a strong work ethic and sense of community.
Because of his upbringing, Chris also knows that rural Texas can be overlooked if we don’t fight for it.
That’s what he’ll do.
The Plan 01 Make Life Affordable Again • Cut the cost of gas, groceries, and healthcare by suspending the gas tax when prices surge, going after the middlemen who inflate grocery prices, and eliminating hidden PBM markups on prescription drugs. • Cut the cost of buying a first home with new tax deductions for first-time buyers and for down payments on a new primary residence.

02 Keep the Border Secure • Write the current border-enforcement policies into law so a future president can’t undo them with the stroke of a pen. • Treat the cartels as the terrorist organizations they are and impose tougher penalties on those trafficking fentanyl into our communities.
