He moved back to Texas and built the nation’s top conservative law practice, consistently winning for conservatives in the toughest legal and political battles of our time.
In 2024, Chris led Elon Musk’s America PAC as its director and chief lawyer, deploying nearly $300 million to help ensure President Trump defeated Kamala Harris and returned to the White House.

Time and time again, Chris has proven himself to be a fighter who wins and delivers results for President Trump.
Chris isn’t just a brawler in the courtroom—he’s a proven conservative businessman who built his companies from the ground up with $10,000 of savings.
What began as an ambitious idea to build the nation’s top conservative law practice has grown exponentially into a thriving enterprise that now employs more than 50 people across 17 states.
His businesses have been honored three times on the Aggie 100 list of the fastest-growing Aggie-owned companies, a testament to his commitment to hard work, entrepreneurship, and Texas values.
