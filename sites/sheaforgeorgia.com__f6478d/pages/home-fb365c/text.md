Proud to Serve
My name is Shea Roberts, and it is my honor to serve as State Representative for Georgia House District 52.
I narrowly won in 2020 by only 377 votes, turning the Fulton County district blue for the first time since 2004.
In 2021, the state legislature redrew the voting maps and now District 52 includes parts of Fulton and DeKalb counties, making the district a little more favorable to Democrats.
Winning in 2022 was rewarding as I have so much more I want to accomplish.
For the 2023-24 Legislative Term, I’m proud to be serving on the Leadership Team of the Georgia House Democratic Caucus as our Treasurer.
Our team is building the infrastructure necessary to secure more Democratic seats and a path to the House majority.
While I’m proud of some of the bipartisan successes we’ve accomplished in my first 3 years, there is still so much more we must do to move Georgia forward, together.
Our state majority leaders have spent far too much time in recent years steering our state towards an extremist and provincial vision of Georgia that, based on my 27 years in north Atlanta, is not what the majority of our community desires.
Here is what I believe: I believe all Georgians deserve access to quality health care, including women seeking reproductive care.
I believe our kids should be safe at school and have an education that opens doors for success in life.
I know that diversity and a strong economy go hand-in-hand.
And I believe that free and fair elections must be protected as the cornerstone of our democracy.
