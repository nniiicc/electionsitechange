Cart 0 Meet Shea Platform Vote Contribute Cart 0 Meet Shea Platform Vote Contribute Scroll MEET SHEA ROBERTS In My House, And In This Community, There Is a Place For Everyone I’m a mom, so I know being a parent is both the best and hardest job in the world.
My children and all of Georgia's children inspire and motivate me every day.
I was raised to leave this world better than I found it.
Our current leaders are failing.
I want to build a Georgia where everyone has an equal opportunity to thrive.
I’m an attorney and small business owner, focused primarily on land use and real estate.
That means I’m also a fighter.
I interpret and apply legislation every day, know how to advocate for issues I believe in, and have been successful at bringing opposing parties together on complex emotional issues that impact families, homes, and neighborhoods.
I put these skills to use in the Georgia State House.
I’m a daughter and a sister from a family with widely varying political views.
I understand that disagreement doesn’t have to mean disrespect; and I was taught to dislike ideas, rather than the people who hold them.
I’m a Georgian, who split my time as a child between Dalton and numerous farm towns in California.
I’m proud to have spent decades serving metro Atlanta families and business owners through the law firm I started in 2002.
This is my home, and I’m committed to making it a place where everyone can thrive.
I’m an active member of our community who has always been inspired by service.
I'm your neighbor and I want everyone in our community to feel safe, have food to eat, a quality public education, a place to live and an opportunity to work and thrive.
It's great that Georgia is the best place to do business, but I want us to also be the best place to live and work.
I'm a State Representative for House District 52 who has served for three terms.
I originally stepped up to run for office when I watched yet another community be devastated by gun violence.
I still fight every day for common sense gun saftey solutions to keep our communities and families safe.
But since being elected, I have seen firsthand how policy can impact Georgians' daily lives, including the accessibility and cost of healthcare, housing, education, childcare, and basic necessities.
Georgians are suffering because everything just costs too much.
And our current leaders are prioritizing special interest groups and corporations rather than hardworking Georgians.
Finally, Georgia Republicans have been dismantling our representative democracy and stripping our fundamental rights.
I have been and will continue to fight to change our state’s political climate and restore some decency, common sense, and progressive policy that prioritizes the people of Georgia.
Lastly, I’m incredibly grateful – to all the people who have supported me during my all my campaigns and continue to support me now as the representative for House District 52.
It is my honor to represent every citizen in our district and in the great State of Georgia.
I look forward to receiving your input as we work together to create a better Georgia for all.
