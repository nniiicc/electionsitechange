REPRESENTATION
No taxes without a voice!
Since January, our community hasn’t had anyone speaking up for us in the Missouri State Legislature.
That means no one from our area has helped make big decisions that affect our lives.
That’s not fair.
We deserve better!
Here’s what I plan to do:
- Take the place of Mike O’Donnell, who only worked one day and then quit.
- Stay in touch with people in our district and hold events so I can hear what you think.
- Always speak up for you—because that’s what a representative should do: work for the people not for themselves.
Let’s make sure our voices are heard!
