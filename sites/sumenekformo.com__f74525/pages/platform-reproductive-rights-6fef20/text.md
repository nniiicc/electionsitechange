Reproductive rights
People in Missouri voted yes on Amendment 3 to protect our right to reproductive health care.
That means we want to make our own choices about our bodies and health.
But now, some politicians are trying to take that vote away.
That’s not fair.
We deserve better!
Missouri voters already spoke up—we want doctors, not lawmakers, to help us make personal medical decisions.
Here’s what I plan to do:
- Teach people in my community about how some politicians are trying to ignore our vote
- Stand up for the choice Missouri voters already made on Amendment 3
- Support a new rule that would stop lawmakers from undoing our votes in the future
Let’s make sure our voices are heard—and respected!
