That means we want to make our own choices about our bodies and health.
But now, some politicians are trying to take that vote away.
They have put a new Amendment 3 on the ballot that, if passed, would overturn our vote from just two years ago.
That’s not fair.
We deserve better!
Missouri voters already spoke up—we want doctors, not lawmakers, to help us make personal medical decisions.
Here’s what I plan to do: Teach people in my community about how some politicians are trying to ignore our vote, and ask my neighbors to vote no on Amendment 3 on November 3rd 2026 Stand up for the choice Missouri voters already made on reproductive freedom Support a new rule that would stop lawmakers from undoing our votes in the future Let’s make sure our voices are heard—and respected!
