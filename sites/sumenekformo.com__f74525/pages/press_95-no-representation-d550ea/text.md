Missourians who live in the 95th State House District in South St.
Louis County have no representation right now in the Missouri State House!
The candidate who won the last election for this seat resigned before taking a single vote in the state legislature .
Meredith Sumenek is running to make sure her community will have a representative who is willing to work to make sure the voices of the people of the 95th District are heard.
This seat has been vacant for over six months.
The Governor of Missouri has yet to declare a special election to fill the spot.
If the Governor fails to call a special election, the first opportunity to elect a new representative will be in November of 2026.
Meredith Sumenek is ready to run, now or in November.
