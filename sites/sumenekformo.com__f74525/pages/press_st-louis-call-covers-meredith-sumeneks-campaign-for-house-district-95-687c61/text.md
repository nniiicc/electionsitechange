Louis Call covers Meredith Sumenek’s campaign for house district 95
The St.
Louis Call newspaper, which covers South St.
Louis County news featured Meredith Sumenek’s campaign for State Representative in a February 4th article about the ongoing vacancy in State House District 95.
A quote from the article: “A full year has gone by without a representative in Missouri’s 95th House District, after the seat was vacated by Michael O’Donnell in January 2025.
Now, Democrat Meredith Sumenek has announced her intention to run for state representative and bring representation back to Oakville residents.”
