Meredith Sumenek attends Camp Carnahan
Meredith Sumenek attended Camp Carnahan, a training camp for candidates, campaign staff, and community activists, in July of 2025.
Meredith took the candidate training track at the camp, which included several workshops on how to run a successful campaign.
While at Camp Carnahan, Meredith met several other new candidates for office from across the St.
Louis region.
Camp Carnahan is named after former Missouri Governor Mel Carnahan, who established the first Camp Carnahan training camp in 1997.
