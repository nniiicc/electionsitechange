CLARKS SUMMIT, PA – February 24, 2020 – On Thursday, February 27th, State Representative Bridget Malloy Kosierowski will be hosting a campaign kickoff party at Café Rinaldi in Old Forge.
The event will be held from 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM with light fare and refreshments provided.
Café Rinaldi is located at 426 S Main Street in Old Forge.
The event is free to attend but donations are welcomed and appreciated.
State Representative Bridget Malloy Kosierowski was elected in the 114th District’s special election on March 12, 2019.
She will be running for reelection in 2020.
Bridget filed over double the amount of required petition signatures for reelection to be on the ballot.
