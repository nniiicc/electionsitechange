FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE – MARCH 5, 2019
CONTACT: info@BridgetForPA.com
CLARKS SUMMIT – Today candidate for State Representative of Pennsylvania’s 114th District, Bridget Malloy Kosierowski, announces the support of the Pennsylvania State Education Association.
“The PSEA-PACE is proud to announce its recommendation of Bridget Malloy Kosierowski for State Representative in Pennsylvania’s 114th District,” said Joe Scheuermann, PSEA PACE Chairman.
“Bridget has demonstrated to our membership her commitment to a strong public education system in Pennsylvania.
We look forward to working together with Bridget when elected by the voters on March 12th.”
“It is an honor to have the support of the dedicated men and women who educate our young people in Pennsylvania,” said Malloy Kosierowski.
“Our leaders have a responsibility to fight for our children and for the teachers who shape our children’s future.
A world-class education begins right here at home.
When elected, I will fight to ensure that our teachers and our students have the tools they need to succeed in a very competitive world.”
An affiliate of the National Education Association, PSEA represents approximately 181,000+ future, active and retired teachers and school employees, and more than 1.5 million school children in Pennsylvania.
Today’s endorsement follows recent endorsements from U.S.
Senator Bob Casey, PA Senator John Blake, Representative Kyle Mullins (PA-112), Representative Marty Flynn (PA-113), the United Brotherhood of Carpenters and Joiners Local 445, IBEW Local Union #81, UFCW Local 1776 KS, and the PA Nurses Association.
