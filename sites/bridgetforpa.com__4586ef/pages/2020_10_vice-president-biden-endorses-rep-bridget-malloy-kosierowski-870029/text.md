Bridget Malloy Kosierowski FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE – October 12, 2020 CONTACT: info@BridgetForPA.com CLARKS SUMMIT – Today State Representative of Pennsylvania’s 114th District, Bridget Malloy Kosierowski, announces the endorsement of Vice President Joe Biden and Senator Kamala Harris.
“I am truly honored to have Joe Biden’s personal support in this campaign.
The stakes in this fall’s election are enormous, which is why he’s counting on us to win and deliver progress into Pennsylvania and beyond,” said Rep.
Kosierowski.
“As Vice President Biden has said over and over, winning back the White House is just the beginning.
If we want to reclaim the soul of America and secure a brighter future for all, we must win up and down the ballot.
Let’s do it together.” By BridgetForPA | 2020-10-12T14:18:13+00:00 October 12th, 2020 | press release | Comments Off on Vice President Biden Endorses Rep.
