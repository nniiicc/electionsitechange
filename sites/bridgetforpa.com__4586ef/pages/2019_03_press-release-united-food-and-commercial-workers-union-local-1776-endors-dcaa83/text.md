FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE – MARCH 6, 2019
CONTACT: info@BridgetForPA.com
CLARKS SUMMIT – Today candidate for State Representative of Pennsylvania’s 114th District, Bridget Malloy Kosierowski, announces the endorsement of the United Food and Commercial Workers Union (UFCW) Local 1776 KS.
“The United Food and Commercial Workers Union Local 1776 KS is glad to endorse the candidacy of Bridget Malloy Kosierowski for state representative to Pennsylvania’s 114th District,” said Wendell Young, President of UFCW Local 1776 KS.
“We are confident that Bridget will be a strong and dedicated supporter of our hardworking membership and our many families across the district.”
“Thank you to the men and women of the UFCW Local 1776 KS,” said Malloy Kosierowski.
“It is an honor to have your trust and your support.
UFCW is dedicated to building better lives for hardworking Pennsylvania families.
The organization’s workers roll up their sleeves and get the job done each and every day.
When elected, it is my pledge to mirror this ethic as your representative.”
Today’s endorsement follows recent endorsements from U.S.
Senator Bob Casey, PA Senator John Blake, Representative Kyle Mullins (PA-112), Representative Marty Flynn (PA-113), the United Brotherhood of Carpenters and Joiners Local 445, IBEW Local Union #81, and the PA Nurses Association.
