Skip to content Donate Volunteer About Facebook Instagram Tiktok Donate View this post on Instagram Your priorities are my priorities 🏫 EDUCATION: Fund Our Future, Close Loopholes Fully fund public education and special education.
No more waiting.
Close the 77 tax loopholes allowing corporations and the wealthy to benefit while our children are neglected.
Give our kids in D91 and Bonneville Joint District 93 the resources they deserve, not empty promises from Boise.
Support teachers through loan forgiveness programs and competitive salaries—the same things that attract teachers to neighboring states. 💰 FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY: Invest in Communities True fiscal responsibility means investing in working families, not continually providing tax cuts that disproportionately benefit the wealthy.
State revenue should better fund roads, public safety, and infrastructure without shifting additional burdens to homeowners and renters.
It’s time to stop letting the state legislature engage in fiscally irresponsible policies that only cut revenue without investing in the policies and people that pay dividends in economic prosperity. 🏥 HEALTHCARE: Protect Our Care Defend Medicaid expansion.
Idaho voters passed it, and we won’t let politicians take it away.
Here in Idaho Falls, that means protecting access at EIRMC, supporting working families, and keeping rural healthcare alive.
Medicare for All is a federal fight, but there’s plenty we can do locally.
I’ll fight to lower costs and protect the care we already have.
Women’s reproductive health matters.
We need to protect women’s bodily autonomy and return to a time when medical decisions were made between a woman and her care provider. 🏡 HOUSING: You Should Be Able to Afford a Roof Housing is a human right.
No one who works in Idaho Falls should be priced out of living here.
Expand first-time homebuyer programs and utilize the Affordable Housing Fund that the legislature instituted but has never properly implemented.
Encourage cities to focus on nonprofit efforts to provide affordable housing and limit the number of homes corporate landlords can own in our state.
