KJ for SC, including its authorized committee, campaign staff, volunteers, and representatives (collectively, the "Campaign," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit KJAtwood.com, communicate with the Campaign, sign up to receive campaign communications, make a contribution, attend an event, volunteer, complete a survey or form, or otherwise interact with us.
By using our website or providing information to the Campaign, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1.
Information We May Collect
Depending on how you interact with the Campaign, we may collect the following categories of information.
Information You Provide Directly
We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:
● Your name
● Email address
● Telephone or mobile phone number
● Mailing or residential address
● Employer and occupation information
● Contribution and transaction information
● Volunteer interests and availability
● Event registration information
● Survey, petition, or questionnaire responses
● Communications that you send to the Campaign
● Social media usernames or profile information

Contribution Information
When you make a contribution, the Campaign or its fundraising and payment processors, including ActBlue and Oath.Vote, may collect information required to process the transaction and comply with campaign-finance laws, including your name, address, occupation, employer, contribution amount, and payment information.
Payment-card information is generally processed directly by our third-party payment processors.
The Campaign may not receive or store your complete payment-card number.
If you contribute by check, we collect the information provided on and with the check as necessary to process and report the contribution.Campaign-finance laws may require the Campaign to collect, retain, and publicly disclose certain contributor information.
Information required to be reported may become publicly available through government campaign-finance databases, including the South Carolina State Ethics Commission.

● Internet Protocol address
● Browser type

3.
Text-Message Communications
When you provide your mobile number and affirmatively sign up to receive text messages, you authorize the Campaign and its service providers to send you campaign-related text messages at the number provided.
Messages may include campaign updates, event information, donation requests, volunteer opportunities, surveys, voting reminders, and other political communications.
Messages may be sent using an automated telephone dialing system or other automated technology when permitted by applicable law.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a contribution, purchasing anything, volunteering, or receiving campaign services.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.You may opt out of campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP.
You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section below.
We will use reasonable measures to honor text-message opt-out requests.
An opt-out from text messages may not automatically unsubscribe you from emails or other forms of communication.
No mobile opt-in information will be shared with third parties.
Mobile information, including mobile telephone numbers and text-message consent records, will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their independent marketing or promotional purposes.
We may provide mobile information to vendors and service providers solely as necessary to operate the Campaign's messaging program, deliver messages, maintain records, process opt-outs, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable law.
Those providers are permitted to use the information only to perform services for the Campaign or as legally required.
By opting in, you agree to receive campaign updates, donation asks, event information, volunteer opportunities, surveys, voting reminders, and other informational or political messages from KJ for SC.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
For help, reply HELP or email Hello@KJAtwood.com.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
