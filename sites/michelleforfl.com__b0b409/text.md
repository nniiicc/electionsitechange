A Note From Michelle Thank you, Seminole County.
I love our community and I love this state.
After a great deal of thought, I have withdrawn my candidacy for Florida House District 38.
This was not an easy decision, and it is not the end of my commitment to this community.
The work is not done.
I am passing the baton to Nik Dean , who has officially qualified as the Democratic candidate for House District 38.
I know he will carry this across the finish line and win, and I will be helping him in every way I can.
I am so grateful to the incredible supporters who powered this campaign.
To every neighbor who knocked a door, made a call, hosted a sign, shared a meal, or simply believed in what we were building: thank you, so very much.
