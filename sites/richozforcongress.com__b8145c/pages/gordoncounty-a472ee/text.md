This pause allows the county to draft strict ordinances protecting community interests and preserving the area's agricultural zoning.
KNOW THIS Leaking anaerobic bio-waste into a lake or a body of water that flow into a lake can act as a potent fertilizer, introducing high concentrations of nitrogen and phosphorus .
Combined with summer temperatures and sunlight, this triggers rapid cultural eutrophication , fueling massive and potentially toxic algal blooms (cyanobacteria) Cyanobacteria, commonly referred to as blue-green algae , is exactly what bloomed in Lake Allatoona. Citizens can attend Gordon County Planning & Zoning Commission meetings in person on the second Monday of each month at 6:00 p.m. .
The meetings are held in the Assembly Room of the Gordon County Judicial Building , located at 10 1 S.
