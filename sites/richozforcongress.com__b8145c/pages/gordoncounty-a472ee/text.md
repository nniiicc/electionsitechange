Gordon County
DATA CENTERS
The Board of Commissioners extended temporary moratoriums on data centers and anaerobic bio-waste facilities through January 29, 2027.
This pause allows the county to draft strict ordinances protecting community interests and preserving the area's agricultural zoning.
KNOW THIS
Leaking anaerobic bio-waste into a lake or a body of water that flow into a lake can act as a potent fertilizer, introducing high concentrations of nitrogen and phosphorus.
Combined with summer temperatures and sunlight, this triggers rapid cultural eutrophication, fueling massive and potentially toxic algal blooms (cyanobacteria) Cyanobacteria, commonly referred to as blue-green algae, is exactly what bloomed in Lake Allatoona.
(Not saying they are connected, but we NEED to be watching our environment.)
BOARD MEETINGS
Zoning & Development
Citizens and the Gordon County Board of Commissioners are navigating the pressures of rural growth, with frequent public hearings on industrial projects, such as poultry houses in the Ranger area, and large-scale data facilities.
Citizens can attend Gordon County Planning & Zoning Commission meetings in person on the second Monday of each month at 6:00 p.m..
The meetings are held in the Assembly Room of the Gordon County Judicial Building, located at 101 S.
Piedmont Street, Calhoun, GA 30701.
Public Participation
The public is encouraged to attend and provide input on zoning and development matters.
Confirm the Schedule
Meetings are held "as needed." You should verify that a meeting is scheduled for the current month by checking the official Gordon County Calendar or calling the Planning and Development office at (706) 879-2172.
Administration
The Planning & Zoning Department is located in the Government Plaza at 215 N.
Wall St., 2nd Floor, Calhoun, GA 30701.
CURRENT ISSUES FOR GORDON COUNTY
Economic Pressures
Working-class residents face tight credit and high prices for essentials, forcing many to prioritize automotive maintenance, home repairs, and budget tracking.
Housing Affordability
A lack of affordable housing has driven some residents to live in RVs and campers, prompting county code compliance reviews.
Property Taxes
The Gordon County Board of Assessors has issued 2026 property valuations, initiating a 45-day review and appeal window for citizens to dispute their property's fair market value. *DO THIS DISPUTE ASAP
Citizens can attend Gordon County Board of Education meetings in person at the Gordon County Schools Central Office, located at 7300 Fairmount Highway SE, Calhoun, GA 30701.
Regular meetings typically occur at 6:00 p.m..
To verify specific dates, view the Gordon County Schools Events Calendar.
Verify all dates before attending by calling their office.
Public Participation
Citizens wishing to speak must adhere to the Board Meeting Public Participation guidelines.
Speakers are generally limited to three minutes, and the board primarily listens rather than answering questions during this portion of the meeting.
Contact
You can reach the central office at (706) 629-7366 for confirmation of the meeting agenda or to address specific concerns.
