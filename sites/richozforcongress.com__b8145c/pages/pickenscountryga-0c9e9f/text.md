BOARD MEETINGS Zoning and development matters in Pickens County, Georgia are handled primarily by the Pickens County Planning Commission and the Board of Commissioners .
Planning Commission Meetings Schedule: Third Monday of each month at 5:30 PM (Note: schedules can occasionally shift due to holidays or alternate notices).
Location: Pickens Room, Pickens County Administration Building, 1266 East Church Street, Jasper, GA 30143.
Role: Reviews land use, rezoning requests, and development proposals, then makes official recommendations to the Board of Commissioners.
CITIZENS CAN STOP ALL NON-BENEFICIAL DECISIONS HERE THE MOST EFFECTIVELY.
Board of Commissioners Schedule: Typically meets monthly (work sessions and regular meetings usually held on Mondays).
Location: Pickens County Administration Building, 1266 East Church Street, Jasper, GA 30143.
Role: Takes the Planning Commission's recommendations and makes the final legal and voting decisions on zoning changes and county development.
BE PRESENT.

Citizens can participate with Pickens County BoE by attending open monthly meetings, submitting written requests to speak during open sessions, and reviewing proposed policy changes .
Attending and Speaking at Meetings Open attendance Meetings are open to the public, though they are structured business sessions rather than open forums.
Advance sign-up Legal county residents must submit a Public Participation Request Form or email the superintendent's administrative assistant by 6:00 p.m. the day before the meeting.
Speaking limits Speakers get up to five minutes at the discretion of the Board Chair to address their concerns.
Submission of comments Written concerns can also be handed directly to board members, though the board takes comments under advisement without immediate replies.
Policy Review and Further Community Involvement Public review periods The board regularly places policy adjustments (such as medication or facility rules) out for 30-day public review.
Partnerships Local groups and businesses can engage via coordinated community support and volunteer efforts.
