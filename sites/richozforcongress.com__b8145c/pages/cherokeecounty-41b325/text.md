While there are no active development applications, the Cherokee Office of Economic Developmen t has actively marketed properties like the Canton Corporate Park (a 50-acre site near I-575 in Canton) specifically to data center developers.
However, local leaders and residents have expressed concerns about the impact of such facilities, with the county emphasizing strict requirements for power, water, and non-intrusive operations if developers do apply.
BOARD MEETINGS Attend Cherokee County, GA board meetings in person at the Cherokee County Administration Complex at 1130 Bluffs Pkwy, Canton, GA.
The Board of Commissioners holds work sessions at 3:00 PM and regular meetings at 6:00 PM, typically on the first and third Tuesdays of each month.
For zoning matters, the Zoning Board of Appeals meets once per month.
To speak at a public hearing, you must sign up before the meeting starts.
If you cannot attend in person, you can stream the Cherokee County Board of Commissioners meetings live online at Cherokee County Government Live Stream . [ 1 ] BOARD OF EDUCATION For education-related matters, you can also attend the Cherokee County Board of Education meetings.
These sessions (work sessions at 5:30 PM, business meetings at 7:00 PM) are hosted at the district’s central office and are open to the public.
Before attending, always verify the schedule, as dates and locations are subject to change.
PUBLIC NOTICES Public notices for Cherokee County are published in the county's official legal organ , the Cherokee Tribune & Ledger-News .
You can also search for these notices online via the Georgia Public Notice website, which compiles legally required announcements such as foreclosures, government bids, and ordinances.
