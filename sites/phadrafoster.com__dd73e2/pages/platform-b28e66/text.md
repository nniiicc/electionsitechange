Phadra Foster is running for Alabama State Senate District 25 with a commitment to practical leadership, strong communities, and public service that puts people first.
This campaign is grounded in four core priorities: economic security and opportunity, healthy and safe communities, education that works, and accountability in public service.
These priorities reflect what families in Montgomery and Crenshaw Counties need most: stability, safety, opportunity, and leadership they can trust.
Families and seniors across District 25 are feeling the pressure of rising costs and economic uncertainty.
Too many people are working hard and still struggling to keep up.
Phadra Foster believes Alabama should be a place where hard work is respected, opportunity is real, and families have a fair chance to build a stable future.
She supports policies that strengthen economic security, encourage job growth, and invest in the long-term well-being of local communities.
Economic progress should not be something people hear about from a distance.
It should be something families can actually feel in their daily lives.
Families deserve to live in communities that are safe, healthy, and supported.
That means more than talking points.
It means taking seriously the need for safer neighborhoods, access to healthcare, support for children and seniors, and stronger community well-being.
Phadra Foster believes public safety and public health are both essential to strong communities.
She supports efforts to protect local hospitals, strengthen senior and maternity care, expand access to mental health services, and promote conditions that help families thrive.
Healthy, safe communities do not happen by accident.
They require leadership that is present, informed, and committed to the people it serves.
Strong schools are essential to strong communities.
Families deserve safe, supportive schools that prepare students for college, careers, military service, and life.
As an educator, Phadra Foster understands the importance of public education and the need to support students, families, and teachers in meaningful ways.
She believes education should open doors, protect student well-being, and equip young people with the tools they need to succeed.
Education policy should reflect both high expectations and real support.
Our children deserve nothing less.
The people of District 25 deserve leadership that is honest, accessible, and accountable.
Public service should be rooted in responsibility to the people, not politics as usual.
Phadra Foster believes leadership means showing up, listening carefully, telling the truth, and being willing to answer for the decisions that affect people’s lives.
She supports transparency, responsible stewardship of public resources, and a standard of service that puts the community first.
Trust matters.
Accountability matters.
The people deserve both.
This campaign is about more than promises.
It is about building a stronger future for Montgomery and Crenshaw Counties with leadership that listens, works hard, and stays connected to the people.
Phadra Foster is running to bring trusted, community-centered leadership to District 25—leadership rooted in service, advocacy, accountability, and a deep belief in the people of this district.
