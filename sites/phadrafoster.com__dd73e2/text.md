Leadership Families Can Trust!
Phadra Carson Foster is an educator, advocate, and candidate for Alabama State Senate District 25.
As a college instructor and experienced community leader, she has spent years working on behalf of families through education, family engagement, and public service at the local, state, and national levels.
She is running to bring trusted, community-centered leadership to Montgomery, Elmore, and Crenshaw Counties—leadership rooted in service, accountability, and a deep belief in the people of this district.
