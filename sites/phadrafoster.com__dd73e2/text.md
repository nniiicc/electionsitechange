Meet Phadra Carson Foster Phadra Carson Foster is an educator, advocate, and candidate for Alabama State Senate District 25.
As a college instructor and experienced community leader, she has spent years working on behalf of families through education, family engagement, and public service at the local, state, and national levels.
She is running to bring trusted, community-centered leadership to Montgomery, Elmore, and Crenshaw Counties —leadership rooted in service, accountability, and a deep belief in the people of this district.

The Platform : LEADERSHIP Families Can Trust Economic Security & Opportunity Families and seniors are feeling the pressure of rising costs and economic uncertainty.
Phadra will work to strengthen economic stability, support job growth, and expand opportunity here at home for the next generation.
Healthy, Safe Communities Families deserve safe neighborhoods and healthcare close to home.
Phadra will work to protect local hospitals, strengthen senior and maternity care, and expand access to mental health services.
Education That Works Families deserve safe, supportive schools that prepare students for college, careers, and military service.
Phadra will work to invest in education that safeguards students’ well-being and helps every child reach their full potential.
Accountability & Trust Public service begins with listening.
Phadra will show up, lead with integrity, and put people first.
