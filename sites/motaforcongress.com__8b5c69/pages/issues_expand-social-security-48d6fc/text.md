Priorities
Economic Security
Many of us are denied a basic standard of living.
We live paycheck to paycheck, can barely afford housing, food, and other necessities to thrive.
It's not just us as individuals, but us as communities and neighborhoods that aren't able to meet their basic and essential needs sustainably.
We have a right to a life that uplifts and empowers all.
There are programs and fund to ensure and promise the American people a guaranteed stable income.
As well as a future where our communities and neighborhoods can prosper.
That is why I will work to:
- Support Raise the Wage Act
- Champion the Social Security Expansion Bill
- Expand the Earned Income Tax Credit
- Raise Corporate Tax Rates
- Co-sponsor New Producer Economic Security Act
