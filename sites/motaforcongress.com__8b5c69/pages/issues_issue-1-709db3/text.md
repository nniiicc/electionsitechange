Priorities
Community-based Projects
Our communities and neighborhoods have remained underrepresented, underserved, and neglected by leaders who haven't advised for the enhancements of our neighborhoods: lack of quality infrastructure, transportation, and community focused programs that serve their constituents.
Our communities, neighborhoods, and we as a people deserve better.
As your congresswoman, I will be a voice for the needs and issues within communities to improve the well-being and quality of life for all.
More importantly, I'll advocate for direct federal funding for community-based projects, initiatives, and programs to enhance community development and resilience.
Some funds would be utilized for:
- Expand Community Project Funding and Congressionally Directed Spending
- Increase funding for Disaster Recovery and Special Initiatives
- Improve Investments in Local Economies
- Modernize Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) and Indian CDBG (ICDBG) programs
- Revamp the Community Reinvestment Act (CRA)
- Support the Sarah Debbink Langenkemp Active Transportation Safety Act
- Strengthen PROTECT Program
