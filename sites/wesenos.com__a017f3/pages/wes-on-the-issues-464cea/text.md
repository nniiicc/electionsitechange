I’m running for the Iowa State Senate to stand up for working families, protect our local communities, and deliver practical solutions that make life more affordable and secure for eastern Polk County.
No family should lose their land or home so a private developer can profit.
I oppose the use of eminent domain for private projects and will always defend the fundamental property rights of Iowa families.
Property taxes are rising faster than family incomes, forcing homeowners, seniors, and young families to shoulder an unfair burden.
I support real property tax reform that brings predictability, fairness, and relief — while ensuring essential local services remain strong.
I believe Iowans know how to spend their money better than government does.
I will fight to cut taxes, control spending, and demand accountability in state government — so families can keep more of what they earn.
Safe neighborhoods are the foundation of strong communities.
I proudly support our law enforcement officers, firefighters, and first responders and will work to ensure they have the resources, training, and respect they deserve to keep our families safe.
The right to keep and bear arms is fundamental.
I will always stand up for the Second Amendment and oppose efforts that punish law-abiding gun owners.
Parents should be the primary decision-makers in their children’s education.
I support policies that increase transparency, expand opportunity, and ensure parents have a real voice in their schools.
