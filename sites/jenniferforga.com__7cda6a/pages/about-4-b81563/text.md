Why Jennifer?
The Full Story Jennifer Ambler has lived in Forsyth County for close to 6 years and has fully adopted Georgia as her new home.
Originally from Florida, she graduated from the University of Florida with a BA in Political Science and Economics, and after college worked as a Mathematics Teacher at a high school in Philadelphia as part of Teach For America.
She then moved to NYC, starting her own tax preparation company and working in musical theater, but she missed warmth and backyards so she and her husband Adam moved to Forsyth in 2020.
Jennifer has always had a passion for politics and believes that government should be a tool to help people live their best lives.
Starting from being active in her high school's Youth in Government Club to volunteering for Warnock and Abrams as well as being on the Executive Board of the Forsyth Democrats, Jennifer has always been a strong liberal who stands up for the values she believes in - equality, freedom, and opportunity.
In addition to her day job, she is also a political comedian, writing parody songs about current affairs.
