Healthcare Access
The people of GA have the right to bodily autonomy, and to access necessary medical care without the law getting between them and their doctors.
And every Georgian should have access to high quality healthcare without fear of bankruptcy or being forced to choose between medicine or rent and food.
Not to mention it makes fiscal sense - Georgians pay federal income tax for Medicaid, but get no benefit, and our hospitals are closing at alarming rates due to the number of patients that can't pay for necessary care.
Smart Growth
Georgia's population is growing and we need to make this state a better place to live for both long-time and newer residents!
We need affordable housing, traffic and transportation planning, well-funded public schools, and a business climate that boosts our quality of life.
Incentivize building more starter homes and a suburban bus network.
Stop draining schools with voucher programs or giving money to businesses that don't deliver on jobs for Georgians.
No giveaways to Data Centers that suck up water and power resources without paying their fair share, and no more letting Wall Street buy up single family homes.
Good Government
Georgia should be doing everything possible to make it easier to vote while preserving security of elections, such as stopping purges of voters, making mail-in voting more accessible and using an independent redistricting committee to draw lines rather than legislators themselves.
Georgia should also consider implementing Ranked Choice Voting to eliminate costly runoffs while ensuring all voices are heard.
Finally, the people of GA deserve representatives who seek to make their lives better and not just enrich themselves through corruption.
