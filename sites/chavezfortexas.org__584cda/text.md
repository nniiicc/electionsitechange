Here is my plan to protect our water and my view on AI Data Centers:
Stop all AI Data Center progress.

Water:
Stop the Drain: I will fight the permits that let these big companies take our water for their profit.
Listen to Local Experts: I will bring together and listen to our local landowners, farmers, and independent water scientists who see their water levels every day to get the real facts.
End the Loophole: The rich use an outdated law called the Rule of Capture to justify draining our wells.
I will push for legislation to end this unfair rule and replace it with a law that protects local homeowners' wells first, making sure our water stays where it belongs: in East Texas.
Our water belongs to the people of East Texas.
We must protect it, not sell it.
Because, as the Indigenous Peoples' Water Declaration states, "We recognize and respect water as sacred and sustains all life."
