Michael Mosteller's Commitment to the 21st District Michael is focused on practical solutions for West Virginia families, including stronger schools, better healthcare access, addiction recovery, local jobs, reproductive freedom, and protecting the land that makes our state home.
Schools and Education West Virginia students and teachers deserve better support, better resources, and leadership that treats public education as a priority.
Expanding Healthcare Access Too many families in District 21 struggle to afford basic care.
Michael supports better access to affordable healthcare for working families, seniors, and rural communities.
Combating the Opioid Crisis The opioid crisis has devastated families across West Virginia.
Michael supports recovery services, prevention programs, and real support for those affected by addiction.
Women's Health Personal healthcare decisions should be made by women, families, and doctors, not politicians.
Michael supports protecting reproductive freedom and access to essential care.
Jobs and the Local Economy Michael supports local businesses, infrastructure investment, and economic development that creates real opportunities for West Virginia workers.
Protecting West Virginia's Land West Virginia’s parks, forests, trails, and public lands are worth protecting.
Michael supports preserving them for families, recreation, and future generations.
