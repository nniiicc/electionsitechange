Juneau, AK – Bernadette Wilson, a well-known leader in Alaska’s business and political spheres, announced today her candidacy for governor, pledging to bring bold leadership, common-sense policies, and a renewed focus on opportunity and prosperity for all Alaskans.
Wilson is the Founder and President of Denali Disposal Inc., a locally owned waste management company she launched in 2016.
Under her leadership, the company has become a trusted name in Anchorage, known for personal service and deep-rooted community values.
Her tireless entrepreneurial drive is inspired by her family’s legacy in Alaska’s waste management and trucking industries, and the business and political acumen of her great uncle, former two-term Gov.
Walter J.
“Wally” Hickel.
“From the towering trees of our Tongass National Forest to our fish thriving in the deep waters of the Valdez Harbor or our legendary sockeye runs in Bristol Bay; from our mineral-rich islands in Southeast Alaska to our Arctic Slopes; from our vast oil reserves of the NPRA and ANWR to the unmatched grit and determination of her people—there is no doubt, America’s strength starts with Alaska,” Wilson said.
“And yet, we haven’t seen a major highway project in 40 years.
Alaska simply can’t afford to wait another 40.
Partnering with President Trump’s administration, it’s time to kickstart our economy, invest in our future, and ensure that prosperity reaches every corner of our state.
If this is to be the golden age of America, then let’s make sure the history books are filled with Alaska’s stories.”
Wilson’s public service record is as robust as her business credentials.
She first gained statewide attention as the campaign director for the successful 2010 “Parental Notification” ballot initiative.
Since then, she has held key leadership roles including state director of Americans for Prosperity–Alaska and interim executive director of the Alaska Policy Forum.
She also served as a senior advisor to the 2024 Nick Begich congressional campaign and is
one of the sponsors of Repeal Now, the ballot initiative to repeal Alaska’s ranked choice voting system in 2026.
A powerful grassroots organizer, Wilson played a leading role in the 2020 People’s Memorial Day remembrance event in Anchorage and was on the front lines advocating for the rights of Alaskans during the Covid shutdowns.
Her influence has extended across media as well, where she has connected with Alaskans through her radio and television shows.
A proud Alaskan of Aleut heritage, Wilson’s campaign will focus on robust development, restoring accountability in government, strengthening local economies, and championing policies that reflect the values and needs of everyday Alaskans.
“I’ve talked to people all across the state—and they’re ready to roll up their sleeves and join this team for 2026,” she said.
“But this isn’t just my campaign—it’s Team Alaska.
I hope that you will join me in this campaign because we are about to show America what Alaska is made of!”
