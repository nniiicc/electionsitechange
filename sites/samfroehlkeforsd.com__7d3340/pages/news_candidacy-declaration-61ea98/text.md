My name is Sam Froehlke, and I am declaring my candidacy for District 4 in South Dakota’s House of Representatives.
I live in southern Deuel county, and have for most of my life.
I support life, property rights, and responsible local government.
I am running as an independent candidate because I don’t see that the two main parties are offering the true solutions that we need today.
The people of South Dakota need an answer to their problem of unaffordability.
The costs of health care, housing, transportation, and child care are what truly matter to us all.
But there will be no progress unless we ask hard questions about where we want to be, and take hard answers about what we need to do to get there.
It would be easy to simply declare that all of our problems are caused by too much government, or too little.
Too much financial freedom, or too much regulation.
But the reality is more complicated, and the solutions are painful.
We need to roll back property tax assessments to the last sale price adjusted for inflation, not the current market price.
But any tax cut has to mean cutting spending, even where it hurts.
Our needs must come before our wants, and we need to maintain local fire and ambulance services.
We need to support our townships, and their upkeep.
The taxes must be cut, and these needs take precedence over infinitely expanding school budgets.
