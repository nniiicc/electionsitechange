Encouraging independent health care
Allow independent unlicensed medical practitioners to perform medical services to a willing and informed patient, such as home births, emergency care, or unregulated clinics.
To keep these services at a cost which the people that need them can afford, there would need to be wide, if not blanket, protection from lawsuits.
By not requiring the massive cost of carrying malpractice insurance, state and unlicensed clinics could drastically reduce their cost of business, and provide a far more affordable service.
