About Sam Froehlke
I am running for the South Dakota House of Representatives as an independent candidate in 2026.
Who am I?
I grew up and have spent most of my life outside of Toronto, South Dakota.
While I am a fundamentalist Christian, I believe strongly in the separation of church and state, for the preservation of both.
I believe in a balanced government, limited in its authority, but still with the authority to curb the worst excesses of our human nature.
I believe in financial responsibility, cutting spending, and stopping corruption.
Why am I running as an Independent?
The Republican and Democratic parties have taken some position on either side of all current political talking points, and seem to think that the voters must submit themselves to one party or the other.
Left behind are the actual people of South Dakota, who may not always fully align with the policy or leadership of either.
What matters to us are the bread and butter issues of health care, housing, debt, and public safety.
I am not running to represent the needs of the parties in Washington, but the needs of the people of District 4.
Why am I running now?
We have reached a point where the current system is unacceptable.
It is taken for granted that we will spend infinite amounts of money on our school systems, while our life and death public services lie understaffed, and underfunded.
Mortgages are issued wherein homebuyers pay more than the cost of the entire house just in interest.
We pay more in health insurance premiums than it costs to actually run the hospitals, but still can't afford to use them, even with insurance.
We made a world that we can no longer live in, and we need a way out.
