Meet Sam Platform Yard Signs and Highway Signs News and Articles Property tax reform The tax on unrealized value needs to end.
The assessments should be mainly following inflation, and the tax levy the number raised or lowered.
The tax system has to be be simplified, allowing the counties to set their own tax rates; albeit with limitations on how much they can change the number.
If a larger change is truly needed, it can go to a vote in the general election.
Make taxation subject to initiative and referendum of some kind.
Should property assessments be rolled back, county revenue will drop, but the power would be in the hands of the people.
If the county needed more money to make its budget, they would have to run on raising taxes, and the local citizens would be able to vote whether they thought it was worth it.
And if the people do not vote for higher taxes, the local government and schools will just have to make do with less.
I could also support adjusting the tax levy after fixing the assessments, to ensure the budgets are not immediately affected.
To avoid a sudden shock raise to recent homebuyers, this would have to come with a phase in period, potentially of several years.
