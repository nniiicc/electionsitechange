Meet Sam Platform Yard Signs and Highway Signs News and Articles Fair play for business If a corporation wants to buy land, build on it, and pay their taxes, they have the freedom to do so.
But we do not need to make special concessions, tax breaks, and handouts for corporations to come here.
All must be treated equally, and if that's what it takes, we are fine without them.
Corporations cannot have it both ways.
If they need these tax breaks because they claim that they will be a benefit to the community, then the community has a say in what they do.
If they want to claim the right to do whatever they want on their own land, then they should be treated like anyone else, and taxed.
