Meet Sam Platform Yard Signs and Highway Signs News and Articles We need to take radical responsibility for the direction of our own government, and that means asking hard questions, and making hard choices.
I may not always be right, but I will always be completely honest.
South Dakota state health care cooperative We should look into the possibility of allowing willing South Dakota residents to pay a yearly membership into a state health care co-op, the proceeds of which would build hospitals and clinics across the state for those paying members to use at will and free of charge.
Read More Encouraging independent health care We need to allow an environment in which small scale local health services can thrive, and provide affordable, personal care.
Employers who offer health benefits should also be required to offer the cash equivalent to employees who opt out.
This would allow more open competition, and allow employees to choose their own level of health care.
Read More Layers of health care Right now, we have only two choices of care; the best and most expensive, or the worst.
The people of South Dakota need the option to receive the care that they can afford.
This is not a right.
But it is a want and need, and it is a want and need that we can fill.
Read More Property tax reform We need to roll back property tax assessments to the last sale price adjusted for inflation, not current market value.
I support a cap of 40% of property taxes going to the school district, and a floor of at least 3-5% going to local fire and EMS.
As much as we may want the greatest, largest educational system money can buy, we just don't always get what we want.
Our needs come first.
Read More Local control of schools Regardless of our own personal beliefs and preferences, the minutiae of school organization and operation should remain in the control of local voters.
But local control requires local responsibility, and our school districts need to pay their own bills.
Read More Supporting local fire and EMS As some of our most essential services, the counties should be required to fully maintain and equip their fire and ambulance services.
If they do not have the resources, the state will help support them.
Radical financial responsibility A massive amount of our state's budget and financial support is federal money.
Whether it takes cutting spending or raising taxes, we need to start paying our own bills.
Abortion Life begins at conception.
That is where this issue begins and ends.
2nd Amendment rights I support 2A rights, and will fight for the protection of our constitutional guarantees.
Fair play for business It is always said that South Dakota is open for business.
But that should not mean special treatment, or partiality toward large businesses.
Read More Usury laws Freedom to do business should not mean freedom to exploit the vulnerable.
Getting rid of payday loans was a good first step, but we need to continue by limiting the rate and term of auto loans, capping credit card interest, and limiting the total interest that can be charged over the course of a mortgage.
Read More Limit the power of zoning Zoning boards shouldn’t just be able to say no, they should have to prove why not.
Read More Death The state should allow private burial, or provide free cremation, for those families who cannot afford the expense of a funeral home.
Corporate and out-of-state land ownership No corporation, or person residing out of the state, should own single family dwellings or ag land.
Drunk/high driving One strike system.
If you drive impaired, you lose your car and your license.
Marijuana I may not support the use of cannabis, but you can make a better case against alcohol and tobacco.
If the state votes to legalize, I won't try to block them.
The lottery and gambling Gambling only exists because gamblers always lose.
Just like predatory lending, freedom of exploitation shouldn't be protected.
Ban sports betting and get rid of the lotteries.
Campaign finance reform We need to keep corporate money out of our elections.
I support reforming the campaign finance system to only allow individual contributions to a candidate or ballot measure.
No entity of any kind should be able to fund our representation.
