Why I am running
bay county - District 6
This is my home, and I love it.
The water, the land, and the people of Bay County are part of who we are and what makes this community so special.
Over the years, I’ve watched our area grow and change, but I’ve also seen families struggle with rising costs, increasing pressure on our natural resources, and politics that too often forget the people who actually live and work here.
I believe Bay County deserves leadership that truly listens to the community and stands up for what matters most.
We must protect the land and waters that define our way of life while also making sure families and small businesses can afford to live, work, and build a future here.
I’m running because I care deeply about this place and the people who call it home.
My goal is simple: to protect what makes Bay County special while helping create a stronger, more affordable, and more hopeful future for the next generation.
