Priorities for Bay County Strengthen Local Economy A strong economy creates opportunity for local business growth, supports workers, and attracts investment while protecting the quality of life that makes Bay County unique.
Affordability for Bay County Families Everyone is feeling the squeeze from rising costs for insurance, electricity, groceries, and rent.
I support practical solutions that ease these everyday expenses while keeping our economy strong and resilient.
Protecting our Water and Natural Resources Bay County's waters, wetlands, and coastlines are essential to our environment and contribute to our biggest economic driver - tourism.
Healthcare Access Access to healthcare should not depend on where you live.
I support improving healthcare access for families, seniors, and veterans and strengthening our healthcare workforce.
Responsible Growth Florida and Bay County is growing quickly.
I support smart planning that protects infrastructure, water systems, and the character of Bay County communities.
Respect for our Community Strong communities are built when neighbors respect one another and work together.
I believe leadership should bring people together, not divide them, and we should always treat each other with respect.
Complete Transparency and Accountability Public trust is essential to good government.
I believe Bay County deserves transparent decision-making, strong oversight, and leadership that puts the public interest ahead of politics or insiders!
Taxpayers should always be able to see how decisions are made and how their money is being used.
