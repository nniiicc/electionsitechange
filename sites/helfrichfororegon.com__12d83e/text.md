Dear Neighbor:
I’ve always been driven to serve.
It’s the path I chose.
It’s why I served our nation in the Air Force and protected our community for over 25 years as police officer.
Those experiences taught me that solving problems means figuring out where to go and doing it together.
Now, I want to continue that service as your State Senator.
As your State Representative I worked to improve schools, create jobs, and support our local economy.
But, the big spenders in Salem have different priorities.
One-party rule in Salem is giving us divisive government, higher taxes, and more wasteful spending.
As your State Senator, I'll work to put you and your priorities first.
Thank you for allowing me the opportunity to earn your vote.
- Jeff Helfrich
