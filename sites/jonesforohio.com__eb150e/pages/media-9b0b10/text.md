Full Biography
Aaron Jones is a husband, father, grandfather, U.S.
Army veteran, and factory supervisor who’s spent over 20 years on the line at Toledo Molding & Die (TMD) in Tiffin.
He’s running for State Representative in the 88th District — all of Seneca and Sandusky counties — because he knows what it’s like to work hard, make ends meet, and fight for your family.
Born in Tiffin and raised near Green Springs, Aaron graduated from Clyde High School in 1991 and proudly served four years in the U.S.
Army as an Airborne Infantryman with the 3rd U.S.
Infantry Regiment — The Old Guard — an elite unit responsible for national and presidential ceremonial duties.
In 2006, he returned to Tiffin after meeting his wife Tracy.
Together, they raised their blended family and now enjoy being grandparents to four grandchildren.
Aaron works as a supervisor at Toledo Molding & Die, where he’s built a career alongside the hardworking families who keep this region running.
Since 2024, he has served on Tiffin City Council representing the 1st Ward, focusing on supporting local jobs, keeping neighborhoods safe, and making sure families have someone listening to their concerns — not just at election time.
Aaron isn’t running to serve the wealthy and well-connected.
He’s running to be your voice in Columbus — to bring down costs, create good jobs, protect the rights of working people, and make sure families can afford healthcare, education, and a decent life.
Aaron believes in the dignity of work.
He believes government should serve the people who keep our communities running.
And unlike too many politicians, he won’t forget District 88 once he gets to the Statehouse.
