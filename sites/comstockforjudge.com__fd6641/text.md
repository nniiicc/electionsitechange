As Judge of the Ohio Seventh District Court of Appeals, David “Chip” Comstock envisions a judiciary that is faithful to the Constitution, grounded in common sense, and committed to equal justice under law.
His vision is shaped not only by decades of legal experience, but also by his unique perspective as a first responder—a fire chief, firefighter, and EMT who has seen firsthand how the law affects real people in moments of crisis.
Chip believes the role of a judge is to interpret the law—not to legislate from the bench.
He is committed to upholding the rule of law and applying the Ohio and U.S.
Constitutions as they were written and intended.
His judicial decisions will reflect a deep respect for legal precedent and the separation of powers.
Every case deserves to be heard with objectivity, impartiality, and integrity.
Comstock understands that appellate decisions can impact individuals, families, and communities.
He is committed to clear reasoning, accessible legal writing, and rulings that are firm but fair.
Having served on the front lines of emergencies as a fire chief and EMT, Chip brings a unique human-centered perspective to the appellate bench.
He understands the value of calm judgment under pressure, the weight of responsibility, and the importance of public trust.
His life of service ensures he sees not just legal arguments—but the people behind the cases.
Candidate Comstock is deeply rooted in the Seventh District.
He respects the values of Ohio’s families, workers, and communities, and understands the unique challenges facing rural and industrial areas.
He will serve as a judge who reflects the values, work ethic, and common sense of the people who live here
Public service is a public trust.
Chip Comstock believes judges must be accountable, transparent, and ethically grounded.
He will strive to restore confidence in the judiciary by adhering to the highest standards of conduct and public responsibility.
