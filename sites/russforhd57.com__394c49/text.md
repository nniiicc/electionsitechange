Russ Andrews and his wife, Lori, have called Colorado home since 1994.
With a background in engineering, finance, and public service, Russ brings a wealth of experience and a results-driven mindset to everything he does.
Russ earned his Bachelor of Engineering in Marine Engineering (B.E.M.E.) from SUNY Maritime College at Fort Schuyler.
After graduation, he sailed on a Texaco oil tanker, gaining hands-on experience in the energy sector.
He later transitioned into defense technology, working for a technology firm in Crystal City, VA.
There, he advised the U.S.
Navy on major programs, including the FFG-7 trials program, the Ticonderoga Class trials, and the DDG-51 design process.
His work contributed to advancing U.S. naval capabilities and national defense.
In 1985, Russ and Lori partnered with an investor to renovate homes in Wilmington, DE, demonstrating entrepreneurial vision and financial expertise.
By 1987, Russ joined Merrill Lynch in Philadelphia as a financial adviser, launching a 37-year career managing hundreds of millions of dollars in client assets.
Russ has always been deeply involved in his community.
He has served on the boards of:
Independence Pass Foundation
Aspen Camp School for the Deaf
Aspen Junior Golf Program (Volunteer)
From 2008-2015, Russ was the Chief Gate Judge for all major ski races in the Aspen-Snowmass region, including FIS, NORAM, and World Cup races.
He also served as an ambassador for the Snowmass Ski Area, welcoming and assisting visitors.
Russ is an avid golfer, skier, and big game hunter.
As a multi-engine, instrument-rated pilot, he understands the value of discipline, preparation, and precision—qualities he will bring to his role in the Colorado Legislature.
For the past 15 years, Russ has been a weekly guest on KNFO’s David Bach Show, discussing politics, government policy, and how they impact personal finances.
His ability to clearly communicate conservative principles and economic policies has made him a trusted voice in the community.
