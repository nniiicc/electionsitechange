Staff Picks -- Bills Causing A lot of Buzz in the Capitol Many others in the building are doing important work that deserves highlighting!
Here are a few bills of interest to many: HB25-1133 - Requirements for Sale of Firearms Ammunition This bill prohibits the sale of ammunition to individuals under 21 and requires ammunition to be accessible to buyers only with vendor assistance.
HB25-1149 - Comprehensive Black History & Culture Education in K-12 This bill is about ensuring that Black History and cultural studies are integrated into the curriculum for public schools in Colorado.
This bill aims to provide a more inclusive education that highlights the important role of Black Americans in history and culture, while also giving schools the support and resources they need to teach these topics effectively.
HB25-1169 - Housing Developments on Faith and Educational Land Our municipalities have been vocal about this bill, which aims to make it easier to build residential buildings on faith and educational land.
Currently residential projects can be disallowed / denied if those buildings are taller than 45 feet tall, or based on the number of dwelling units.
This bill would remove those obstacles to developers.
