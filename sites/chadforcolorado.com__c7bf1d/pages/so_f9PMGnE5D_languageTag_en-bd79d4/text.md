HD37 Newsletter March 28th, 2025 Previous Newsletters If you have any ideas you want me to hear, or need help navigating state resources, please contact my office: E: Chad.Clifford.House@coleg.gov P: 303-866-5510 Legislative Updates on the Bills I'm Sponsoring We're over halfway into this Session and my bills have been making their way through the legislative process.
HB25-1017 – Community Integration Plan for Individuals with Disabilities is still awaiting a date in the House Appropriations Committee.
This bill codifies into state law the existing obligation, upheld by the US Supreme Court, that our government must provide disability services in the most integrated setting possible.
This bill will likely start moving again when the budget is finalized in the coming weeks.
I am thrilled to see the support and excitement this bill continues to have, particularly being a focal point at the Disability Rights Advocacy Day and the Colorado Intellectual & Developmental Disabilities Awareness Day.
I am happy to announce that HB25-1181 – Concerning the Colorado Rangers Law Enforcement Shared Reserve has passed both chambers and has been signed into law by the Governor.
This bill clarifies statute regarding the Colorado Rangers Law Enforcement Shared Reserve, which is the state-wide reserve of police officers.
HB25-1214 -- Appropriate Use of Prison Beds provides courts with better sentencing information, transitions low and very-low risk inmates to parole responsibly, expands recovery support and reentry housing, and removes barriers to community corrections.
This bill passed its first committee this week and is now onto the Finance Committee.
Not only is this bill common sense from a process standpoint, but it also saves the State money.
And in a budget year like this one, savings more than ever.
HB25-1136 -- Peace Officer Conduct Database passed the House Judiciary Committee on Tuesday and is awaiting a date in the Committee of the Whole!
This bill improves and clarifies procedure related to the law enforcement database managed by the P.O.S.T.
Board.
It closes some gaps that have created confusion or allowed the database to be used in malice against line-level officers.
This is a part of our Law Enforcement Working Group, and is a result of an almost year long process.
HB25-1031 -- Enhancing Whistleblower Protections , also a part of the Law Enforcement Working Group, passed on second reading today.
This bill protects peace officers from retaliation after reporting misconduct in their agency.
State employees have advanced whistleblower protections, but there have been many whistleblowers in local agencies which have come to the legislature asking for more protection.
For more information about these bills, as well as others I'm sponsoring, see our prior newsletters here.

CEA Public Education Funding Rally Cherry Creek and Littleton School Districts are both VERY vocal and INCREDIBLY protective of their students, and are partnering with me and other members of the Legislature to combat any proposals that decrease the funding they need.
Last year we paid down the budget stabilization factor and got education funding to 1989 levels (before cell phones, the internet, AI, etc).
Today, some proposals, such as abolishing enrollment averaging, are threatening to take that away.
Thank you to all of the parents, teachers, and school board members voicing your strong opposition -- I will always fight to protect dollars for education.

Our Weekend Holi Celebration!
Holi, also called the Festival of Colors, is a Hindu celebration that marks the arrival of spring, love, and good trumping evil.
It's an occasion where people throw brightly colored powders at one another, dance, and share festive treats.
My office spent the day celebrating in Centennial at the Hindu Temple and Cultural Center of The Rockies!
