In next week's newsletter, we will spend some time and recap the entire session.
SB24-230 - Oil & Gas Fees To Support Transit This bill has passed through the legislature and will bolster accountability and sustainability within Colorado’s transit and energy sectors.
It will establish fees to be collected from oil and gas producers will be used to pay for clean transit and wildlife and land remediation.
The bill also mandates timely reporting and analysis of fee amounts, ensuring transparency and informed decision-making.
HB24-1132 - The bill introduces the "CARE for Living Organ Donors Act," aimed at providing benefits and recognition for living organ donors.
It includes provisions outlining benefits available to donors and requires transplant centers to inform potential donors about organ voucher programs.
Additionally, the bill prohibits employers from retaliating against employees who become living organ donors and designates April 11 as "Living Organ Donor Recognition Day." Technical amendments to current law are also included in sections 2 and 4 of the bill.
HB24-1373 - "Save Local Liquor Stores" Bill - The bill addresses regulations for alcohol beverage sales, including converting specific liquor-licensed drugstore licenses to fermented malt beverage and wine retailer licenses.
It updates requirements for wholesalers and removes the cap on retailers' alcohol beverage purchases from retail liquor stores.
Additionally, it prohibits retailers from displaying alcohol beverages in certain areas of the sales floor and expands delivery options between licensees.
The bill also prohibits the sale of alcohol beverages with over seventeen percent alcohol by volume and includes an appropriation.
Bills That I've Had Nonstop Attention On TRAPs ( HB24-1324 ) - This bill has Passed!
Going forward the Attorney General will have the authority to restrict training agreements their office deems necessary.
Colorado Disability Opportunity Office (CDOO) - ( HB24-1360 ) - CDOO has passed!
This bill will create an office to support the integration of Coloradans with disabilities into their communities.
This is one of my proudest accomplishments and I look forward to being an advocate for Coloradans with disabilities.
Repeal CBI Criminal Justice Record Sealing Fee - ( HB24-1432 ) - This bill has passed!
This will remove the fee associated with sealing criminal cases.
The majority of people who have gotten approved to seal their criminal history are just waiting on funds so we are hoping to clear up the hold by simply removing the fee.
Airports ( HB24-1235 ) - This bill has Passed!
The bill will reduce lead in our air from aviation by supporting airports in developing programs to transfer into unleaded fuels.
State Employee Deductions Bill ( HB24-1293 ) - The bill has passed completely through the House and the Senate side as well!
This bill allows State employees to negotiate for non-healthcare-related benefits.
