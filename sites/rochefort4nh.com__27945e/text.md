About David Rochefort A second-generation pharmacist, David was born and raised in Lancaster.
Rather than leave the area after college, he returned and settled in Littleton; valuing the quality of life over the offerings of a city lifestyle.
Soon after college, he began working in his family’s drugstore in Lancaster and in 2006 opened Eastern States Compounding Pharmacy in Littleton.
It was the only pharmacy of its kind in New Hampshire at the time.
David worked as a small business owner to aggressively grow his business over the years and recently sold his company in 2022.
In 2017, Governor Chris Sununu appointed David to the New Hampshire Board of Pharmacy and reappointed him for a second term in 2021.
He currently serves as Vice President of the Board of Pharmacy.
During his time on the board, David has been active in drafting and adopting rules on non-resident licensees, pharmacy technicians, risk-based inspections, and pharmacist-prescribed oral contraceptives.
David understands and cherishes the North Country and is fortunate to ‌raise his family here.
He believes ‌parents should be able to pursue the education that they deem best for their children, whether that is public school, private school, or homeschooling, as he has personal experience with all three.
An advocate for the community, David has volunteered his time coaching and promoting youth sports, serving on the Littleton Zoning Board of Adjustment, and helping at church.
David's understanding of our community, business, and pharmacy will give the residents of NH Senate District 1 an experienced voice to represent them well in Concord.
