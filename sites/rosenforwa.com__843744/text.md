The Platform 3 Priorities for a Stronger District Michael is running for State House to focus on three deeply connected priorities.
Every decision will be centered on Washington families and small businesses — with independence, discipline, and accountability at the core.
Accountability For decades, Washington has been governed by one party.
In any system where power is concentrated for too long, complacency can take root.
Oversight weakens.
Tough questions go unanswered.
Spending grows faster than results.
Washington families are expected to balance their budgets — state government should operate with the same discipline.
Accountability is not about party — it's about responsibility.
