Prince George's Plaza Transit Center District
/
CR-56-2016 is a resolution that creates the Prince George's Plaza Transit District Overlay Zone.
This bill approves the new zoning standards for Planning Area 68 for the area generally bounded by the eastern boundary of the M-NCPPC Northwest Branch Stream Valley Park; on the north by the University Hills Subdivision.
This bill increases the density of around the transit area to allow for smart growth development mixed-uses of residential, commercial, schools, and stormwater needs.
