Fighting for Education
Most decisions about what happens inside a South Carolina classroom — curriculum, local funding, hiring — rightly belong to our state, our counties and our districts.
But Congress controls something just as important: whether the federal government keeps its promises to students with disabilities, low-income schools, first-generation college students, and working families trying to afford a degree.
Here is what I will fight for in Congress:
- Create a national teacher pay incentive program to increase teacher salaries.
- Defend Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and services for students with disabilities.
- Protect Title I funding for low-income schools.
- Make college and trade school affordable, with emphasis on persons seeking certification in early childhood education.
- Fully Fund Head Start and child nutrition programs.
- Streamline processing of the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), with a focus on direct qualification for low-income students.
- Increase number of therapists in schools including speech therapy, psychologists and social workers.
- Restore the full function of the Department of Education.
