I will oppose any legislation that limits your access to the ballot box and will propose the following reforms: Fair representation including use of ranked choice voting or instant runoff voting.
South Carolinians in the military who are serving overseas already vote this way.
Reduce gerrymandering by Using non-partisan councils of citizens to choose voting districts so that voters choose their leaders instead of politicians choosing their voters Prohibiting mid-decade redistricting Get big money out of politics allowing voters, not rich donors to decide elections.
Overturn Citizens United which made it possible for the ultra-rich to give unlimited political donations starting in 2010 Ban corporate PACs Create a system of public campaign financing Make dark money illegal in all 50 states (Currently dark money funds political campaigns in South Carolina.
Click here to look up any politician's dark money contributions).
Allow absentee voting for everyone without the need for an excuse (as we did in 2020).
Add early voting days on at least one Saturday and one Sunday each election (as we had in 2020 - 2024).
Protect mail-in voting and ensure that absentee ballots are delivered and counted.
Enact automatic voter registration when you turn 18 (or if you’re eligible) End precinct closures without notice by requiring that voters be notified at least 72 hours in advance if their polling place is closed or changed.
Guarantee accessibility at every polling place with adequate parking, working equipment such as rolling poll booths for curbside voting, and no structural barriers such as steep steps or extended walks.
Use multi-member districts to choose Representatives.
Support bills that advance voting rights such as the John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act .
Create a national holiday for national elections allowing everyone to vote.
