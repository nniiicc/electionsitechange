FROM CHRIS
It has been an honor to serve Central Washington in Olympia since 2019.
I bring the shared values of Central Washington to policy making.
Ronald Reagan once said "The nine most terrifying words in the English language are 'I'm from the government, and I'm here to help.'"
I am not a government elitist.
I work tirelessly to protect the values of Central Washington.
Values like the common-sense conservative principles of upholding the Constitution, supporting lower taxes, and preserving personal accountability.
My priorities are straightforward.
I am:
- For families
- For businesses
- For Central Washington
I hope you will take the time to engage with me about your ideas and concerns.
I want to represent you.
Through common-sense conservative principles we can keep Central Washington strong for generations to come.
