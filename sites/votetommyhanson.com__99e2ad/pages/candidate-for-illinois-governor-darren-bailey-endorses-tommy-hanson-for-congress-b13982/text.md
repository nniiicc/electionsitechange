(Chicago, IL) – Tommy Hanson, the Congressional candidate for the 5th Congressional District in Illinois, is proud to announce that he has been endorsed by Darren Bailey, the Republican candidate for Illinois Governor.
Bailey is running for Governor because he believes in protecting the freedoms of all Illinois’ citizens.
“I am honored to be endorsed by Darren Bailey, a conservative grounded in the values of hard work, liberty, and social responsibility.
Darren Bailey is fighting for the families of Illinois, and we all must continue fighting to protect our home state,” said Hanson.
Tommy Hanson is running for US Congress for the Illinois 5th Congressional District because he loves his community and believes in protecting our freedoms.
He believes in using common-sense solutions to combat inflation and crime throughout Chicago and the northern suburbs.
Hanson was born and raised in the 5th Congressional District and has continued to be involved in our community—working with people and organizations from every political and social part of life.
Tommy Hanson added, “I love this community and believe in protecting its values.
However, we must fix the out-of-control issues that Chicago is facing.
Crime, inflation, and illegal immigration must be addressed.
I want to serve my community by representing them in Congress.”
