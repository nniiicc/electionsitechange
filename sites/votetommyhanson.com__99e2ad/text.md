Replace individual income taxes with corporations making up the difference for federal taxes.

Don’t be afraid of this; because it’s a mathematical formula which will benefit small businesses and you!
Fill ALL pot holes in our streets, with federal tax revenue from marijuana products tax.

Tackle poverty and homelessness.
No tax on food.
No tax on gasoline.
Lower real estate taxes.
Lower electrical bill costs.
Lower cell phone taxes.
Stop insurance costs, lower premiums and create more competition.
Bring high paying jobs to Illinois.
Demand residential landlords maintain their rental properties and cooperate with their tenants.
Help first time home purchasers buy their homes.
Interest on credit cards = tax deductible.
Education expenses, tuitions = tax deductible.

These are a few of my objectives; while I believe in being a common-sense problem solver.
