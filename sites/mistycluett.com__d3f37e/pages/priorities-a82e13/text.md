Transparent Government.
Stronger Families.
Kauaʻi families are being squeezed by rising costs, limited housing, aging infrastructure, and government that too often feels disconnected from the people it serves.
My priorities are rooted in service, stewardship, and common sense: protect families, respect taxpayers, support local businesses, and create a future where our children can afford to stay home.
1.
Accountability Audit spending and demand transparency.
Government should be accountable to the people who fund it.
I support transparent budgets, measurable outcomes, and regular audits so taxpayers can see where money is going, what is working, and what is not.
2.
Affordability Lower barriers so families can stay.
Too many local families are being priced out of the place they call home.
We need to reduce unnecessary costs, cut red tape, and make it easier for working families, small businesses, and young people to build a life on Kauaʻi.
3.
Education Restore critical thinking and trades.
Our schools should prepare students for real life, not just test-taking.
I support restoring critical thinking, expanding vocational and trades education, protecting parental rights, and helping the next generation build meaningful futures here at home.
4.
Families Strengthen homes and protect children.
Strong families are the foundation of a healthy community.
I will support policies that respect parents, protect children, defend life, and strengthen the home rather than replacing the role of families with government programs.
5.
Freedom Defend parental and individual rights.
Freedom is not given by government; it must be protected by government.
I will defend parental rights, religious freedom, medical freedom, property rights, and the individual liberties that allow families and communities to thrive.
6.
Housing Create attainable homes for local residents.
Housing policy should prioritize local families and practical solutions.
I support removing unnecessary barriers to building, expanding responsible options like ADUs and ʻohana units, and focusing on homes our residents can actually afford.
7.
Infrastructure Fix basics before expanding growth.
Before pushing more growth, we need to address the basics: roads, wastewater, water systems, drainage, and landfill capacity.
Responsible planning means making sure infrastructure can support the people already here.
8.
Small Business Cut red tape and support entrepreneurs.
Small businesses create jobs, serve our community, and keep money circulating locally.
As a small business owner, I understand how regulation, permitting delays, and rising costs affect real people.
We need a government that helps local businesses succeed, not one that stands in the way.
