A Proven Record on the Chicago City Council For the past seven weeks, we have shared the experiences that have prepared Bob Fioretti to serve as Illinois Attorney General.
Bob has built his career around independent leadership, professional integrity, and accountability to the public.
The Illinois Committee for Honest Government presented Bob with a Distinguished Service Award for his commitment to his ward and the people of Chicago.
Independent organizations also recognized Bob’s record of reform-minded public service.
He received the Leon Despres Award for independent reform and public-interest leadership.
Highly respected University of Illinois Chicago professor Dick Simpson also highlighted Bob’s consistent independence as a Chicago alderman, noting that in one year he voted “no” 38% of the time.
Bob has also served on the Illinois Supreme Court’s Character and Fitness Committee and contributed to the Chicago Bar Association’s Judicial Evaluation Committee.
These responsibilities require careful judgment, professional integrity, and a commitment to maintaining public confidence in the legal system.
