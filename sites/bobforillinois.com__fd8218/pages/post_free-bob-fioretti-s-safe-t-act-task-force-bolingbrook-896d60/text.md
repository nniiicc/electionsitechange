FREE Community Forum: Bob Fioretti's SAFE-T Act Task Force – Bolingbrook
Earlier this year Bob Fioretti announced the creation of a new Illinois Public Safety & Accountability Commission, led by retired Riverside Police Chief Thomas Weitzel.
One issue Bob Fioretti sees with current law is how it handles electronic monitored release of inmates, which has been widely expanded since the 2023 law eliminating cash bail statewide.
Fioretti believes that the SAFE-T Act is not working properly; designed to allow low-level offenders to remain at home, instead of using taxpayer funds to keep them incarcerated full time.
“Electronic monitoring was never intended to let the violent criminals go home, enjoy home cooking, roam the streets for two days a week looking for new victims and their victims and families suffer,” Fioretti said.
Please join us on Thursday, Oct. 1st. to learn more about the SAFE-T Act in Illinois.
