STANDING WITH FAMILIES Sep 30 2 min read October 2026 Edition Bio Series — Part 5 Bob’s Advocacy Helped Strengthen Protections for Illinois Families “True legal leadership means stepping into the courtroom when vulnerable lives hang in the balance- and ensuring the law changes for everyone thereafter.” Throughout his distinguished legal career in Chicago and across Illinois, Bob Fioretti has consistently stepped forward to confront institutional failures.
In one of his most consequential courtroom battles, the landmark Baby Tamia case, Bob’s decisive intervention exposed critical statutory loopholes in Illinois adoption and child welfare procedures, turning a heartbreaking case into lasting statewide protection for families.
Join the Campaign or Volunteer The Baby Tamia Case: Beyond the Immediate Trial When systemic gaps threatened the welfare of a young child, Bob recognized that winning in court was only the first step.
He challenged the status quo, advocating tirelessly before judges, state lawmakers, and child advocates to close dangerous adoption loopholes.
His efforts triggered crucial legislative and judicial reforms to adoption, creating stronger safeguards and establishing clear accountability across the state's foster and adoption systems.
Read the full Baby Tamia story » Honoring Community Impact Recognized by the Chicago Children’s Advocacy Center In recognition of his steadfast defense of young lives, Bob was honored by the Chicago Children’s Advocacy Center with the prestigious Tad Carr Memorial Award .
Among all his professional recognitions, none holds deeper personal meaning than the handmade award presented directly to Bob by the children served by the Center- a tangible reminder of why fighting for justice matters.
Bob’s Priorities as Illinois Attorney General Bob’s record in the courtroom reflects his core governing philosophy: the Attorney General’s office must be the state’s most proactive defender of children, seniors, and working families.
As we enter October 2026, Bob continues traveling across Illinois, meeting directly with parents, educators, and neighborhood leaders to craft real solutions for safer communities.
