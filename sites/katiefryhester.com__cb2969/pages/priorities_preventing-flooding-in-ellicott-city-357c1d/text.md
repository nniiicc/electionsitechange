Peter’s Church, and the County to clean up Ellicott City’s Main Street after the 2018 flood.
We know storms will continue to threaten Ellicott City, and we must continue investing in life-saving mitigation measures and infrastructure to protect our community.
In the years since the flooding, I have worked proactively with local, state, and federal partners to secure over $70 million of state funding for the Ellicott City Safe and Sound Plan , which will significantly reduce flood risk by strengthening infrastructure, managing stream debris, and expanding green space to better absorb stormwater.
The plan includes three now-completed dry flood mitigation sites – the H-7, H-4, and Quaker Mill detention ponds – as well as the game-changing Extended North Tunnel project, on track for completion in 2027.
