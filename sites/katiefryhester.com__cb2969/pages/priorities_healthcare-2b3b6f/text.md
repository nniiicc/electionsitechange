Howard and Montgomery Counties consistently rank in the top 100 healthiest communities in the country.
Still, 1-in-20 Marylanders are uninsured, and prescription drugs remain inaccessible to many seniors and low-income families.
I have fought to establish a Prescription Drug Affordability Board to prevent dramatic price increases, and voted to cap insulin copays at $30/month.
I have also consistently voted to protect our state's reinsurance program and expand healthcare coverage.
As a result, we have seen three consecutive years of lower insurance premiums in Maryland.
I sponsored legislation that required the Maryland Health Benefit Exchange to draft a strategy to scale our Small Business Health Options (SHOP) program to provide affordable insurance coverage for small employers and their employees.
In general, I support policies to: Reduce prescription drug prices Increase accessibility to quality health services, Promote transparency in medical care pricing, Preserve healthcare coverage for those with chronic illness, pre-existing conditions, and those qualifying for Medicaid, Ensure the availability and adequacy of mental health treatment in our communities, and Reduce the risk of addiction to drugs and provide adequate treatment for those who are addicted.
Legislation I have passed includes: ** SB357/HB424 – Lowering Prescription Drug Costs for All Marylanders Now Act (2025) Strengthens the authority of Maryland’s Prescription Drug Affordability Board and requires the Board to establish a process for setting upper payment limits on certain high-cost prescription drugs in special circumstances, like when a drug enters a shortage. ** SB165/HB204 – Student-Athlete Mental Health Training (2024) Requires coaches at Maryland public schools to complete mental health training in addition to existing physical health training to help coaches recognize signs of mental illness, behavioral distress, and substance abuse among student-athletes.
SB705/HB728 – Access to Care Act (2024) Establishes the Qualified Resident Enrollment Program to help eligible Maryland residents enroll in qualified health insurance plans through the Maryland Health Benefits Exchange, expanding access to affordable health coverage for residents who may otherwise face barriers to enrollment. ** SB0341/HB0477 – Reproductive Health Services Plans (2023) Requires health insurers to provide coverage for reproductive health services and establishes requirements designed to protect access to covered reproductive care. ** SB0632/HB0709 – Maryland Health Benefit Exchange - Small Business and Nonprofit Health Insurance Subsidies Program - Workgroup (2022) Required the Maryland Health Benefit Exchange to draft a strategy to scale our Small Business Health Options (SHOP) program to provide affordable insurance coverage for small employers and their employees.
SB802/HB814 – Protect Maryland Health Care Act of 2019 (2019) Makes it easier for uninsured Marylanders to access affordable health coverage by creating the Maryland Easy Enrollment Health Insurance Program, which allows residents to check a box when filing their taxes to see if they qualify for health insurance or financial assistance.
