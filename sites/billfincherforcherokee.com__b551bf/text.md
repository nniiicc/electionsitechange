Protect our Quality of Life Bill Fincher will work with the Cherokee County legislative delegation to protect the quality of life that makes this community one of the best places to live in Georgia.
Public Safety – With decades of experience as a career prosecutor and former law enforcement professional, Bill will strengthen public safety, support law enforcement, and help keep our neighborhoods safe.
Infrastructure – He will also partner with the Georgia Department of Transportation to reduce congestion and improve the roadways families rely on every day.
Education – Bill’s wife Deanie spent 36 years as a teacher, and Bill will be a champion for our excellent Cherokee County public schools.
He will support School Choice so parents can decide the best learning environment for their child and work to expand trade and technical education opportunities to prepare students for successful careers.
Cut Waste and Cap Property Taxes Bill believes no Georgian should be taxed out of the home they have already paid for.
He will fight to cap the constant rise in property taxes that results from inflated valuations, which often bear no connection to a homeowner’s actual income or realized value.
Bill spent decades applying Georgia laws in courtrooms.
He knows how to craft legislation that works, avoids costly legal challenges, and protects taxpayers from waste.
Georgia state capitol building in Atlanta Tackle Rising Costs Bill will lead efforts to lower insurance rates for homeowners, drivers, small businesses, and families facing high healthcare premiums.
He supports market-based reforms that increase transparency, expand competition, and curb frivolous lawsuits that drive up costs, restoring fairness while keeping Georgia the number one place to do business.
