ABOUT DANIEL COLTEN SCHMIDT I grew up in St.
Louis, Missouri, and have lived in both rural and urban communities all across the country.
I graduated from Hampshire College in 2022 with a BA in Economics and Natural Philosophy.
As part of my thesis, I studied both the economic history of the 20th century and the effect of environmental factors on water quality.
My studies led me to my first job in Minnesota as a canoe guide.
I then worked as an AmeriCorps member with the Minneapolis Health Department where I facilitated solar education programs.
I also served on the Minneapolis Green Zone Council where I advocated for economic growth and public health.
Over the last 3 years, I served as Finance & Development Director of the East Phillips Neighborhood Institute, an economic development nonprofit focused on community-led re-industrialization of a redlined community.
In my role, I raised over $13 million from sources including state appropriations and agencies.
Under my leadership we grew a team of eight full time employees with good benefits.
As your Representative, I will guide investments directly into our community to support exceptional public schools, affordable healthcare, and a strengthened local economy.
