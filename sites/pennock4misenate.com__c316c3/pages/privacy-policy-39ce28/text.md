Privacy Policy
We want to be honest with you on what information is collected and how it is stored, used ,and disclosed by the Committee to Elect Anthony Pennock State Senator (herein, “CTE Anthony Pennock State Senator”).
That is why this Privacy Policy exists and applies to information collected from the website, mobile site, and other online services that link to this Privacy Police (we call them all the “Site”).
We also may change this Privacy Policy every now and then, but we will notify you by revising the date on this page and, if it is a major policy change, we may add information to the main website Home Page or send you an email notification.
Please review the Privacy Policy when you visit just in case the policy has had some modifications to ensure your privacy and data is secure.
INFORMATION THAT IS GATHERED FROM YOU
Just like other websites, log files are stored on the web server which saves data such as IP addresses, browser types, how you were referred to us, and the time of your visit.
Cookies may also be used to remember your preferences when interacting with our website.
Where registration is required, your email and username will be stored on the server.
HOW THE INFORMATION IS USED
We use the information collected to update the website so it is easier for you to access and for advertising the campaign.
Email addresses will not be sold, rented, or leased to 32rd parties.
We may email you with news updates, campaign activities, resources, or offers by us or our affiliates.
VISITOR OPTIONS
If you subscribe for updates via email, text, or social media, you can unsubscribe by following the direction in the subscription email or by emailing info@pennock4misenate.com.
You may also block cookies from our website via your browser settings.
Blocking cookies from the website may limit some features of the website.
COOKIES
Cookies are like fingerprints from your computer and web browser.
They are small digital fingerprints that are stored by your web browser to allow your preferences to be remembered when you visit us again.
They also allow us to track return visitors to the website.
Some 3rd party advertising companies the campaign works with may also use cookies for tracking purposes.
TEXTING
When you provide your phone number and opt-in, you agree to be contacted by CTE Anthony Pennock State Senator at that number.
Contacts could include phone calls or text messages for informational, invitational, or service-related purposes.
Some of these instances may include, but are not limited to; donation requests, advocacy actions, to discuss concerns, input for the campaign, etc.
Message frequency may vary and standard messenger and data rates may apply.
Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
If you want to stop receiving text messages, simply reply STOP.
A confirmation for your opt-out may be sent by text message to be removed from text messages.
If you are subscribed to several forms of text messages from us, we may unsubscribe you from the most recent subscription or clarify your request on which services you wish to stop.
For support or assistance in understanding this Privacy Policy, please email us at info@pennock4misenate.com.
