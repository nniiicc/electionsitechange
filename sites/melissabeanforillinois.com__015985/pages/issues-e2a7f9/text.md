America is strongest when everyone is treated with dignity and respect.
For gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgender Americans, that means the freedom to live openly, love who they love, and be protected under the law—without fear of discrimination or political attacks.
Melissa Bean has backed that belief with action, voting to end “Don’t Ask, Don’t Tell” so service members could serve openly, voting against the so-called Defense of Marriage Act, which would have outlawed marriage equality.
Melissa Bean will:
• Fight efforts to reverse constitutional protections for marriage equality, because the government should never stand in the way of love and equality.
• Protect civil rights: Defending equal treatment in housing, employment, health care, and public life.
• Stand up to extremism: Pushing back against political attacks that target gay and transgender Americans, their families, and their schools.
• Ensure safety and dignity: Supporting strong action against hate crimes, harassment, and discrimination so everyone can live without fear
