Healthcare Families in Illinois’ 8th District deserve affordable, accessible, high-quality care—without fear of losing coverage, being denied care because of pre-existing conditions, or having politicians interfere in personal medical decisions.
As a member of Congress, Melissa Bean proudly voted for the Affordable Care Act (Obamacare).
She supported expanding access to affordable healthcare, strengthening employer-based coverage, and protecting Medicare.
She opposed efforts to roll back patient protections and worked to reduce costs while improving quality of care.
Melissa Bean will: • Protect and strengthen the Affordable Care Act, including ironclad protections for pre-existing conditions and extending tax credits that have lowered premiums for thousands of families in Illinois’ 8th District. • Lower overall health care costs by pursuing reforms throughout the system and continue to allow Medicare to negotiate directly to lower prescription drug prices. • Reverse the Republican Medicaid cuts that jeopardize our hospitals, reduce coverage, and increase overall costs for the system that families in Illinois’ 8th District will incur.
