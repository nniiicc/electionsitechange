State Representative
DAVID ROWE
A PROVEN LEADER WHO SHARES OUR VALUES.
State Representative David Rowe is a strong voice for central Pennsylvania values since joining the State House.
As a solid conservative, he has a strong record of advocating for lower taxes, smaller government, more jobs and economic growth, fiscal responsibility, the sanctity of Life, the 2nd Amendment, and religious liberty.
As a local small business owner and community leader, Rep.
Rowe understands the importance of a vibrant regional economy.
That’s why he fights for policies that encourage economic growth, supports our farmers, and brings family-sustaining jobs to our area.
Recently, Rep.
Rowe sponsored legislation to strengthen Pennsylvania’s Human Trafficking statute; to ensure the rights of Pennsylvanians receiving mental health care; to simplify the tax code; and to protect local farms and agribusinesses.
