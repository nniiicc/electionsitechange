Dear residents of District 65A, I am deeply honored to have earned your trust and support to continue representing you at the State Capitol for another term.
Minnesota voters have spoken, and I am ready to answer that call: to work collaboratively and deliver impactful results that make our district the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
As I prepare to return to office in January, my focus remains steadfast: • Ensuring public safety in our neighborhoods, • Strengthening our educational system, • Enhancing healthcare access for all, • Tackling housing affordability to create sustainable living options.
I am committed to protecting our rights, dignity, and opportunities and ensuring that Saint Paul—and all of Minnesota—continues to grow and thrive.
Together, we will make tangible progress and build a community where every voice is valued.
Thank you for your continued trust and unwavering support.
Let’s move forward together to create a stronger, united District 65A!
