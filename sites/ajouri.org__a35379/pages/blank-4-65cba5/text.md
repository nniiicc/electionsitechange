Our Commitment
We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and in doing so aim to adhere to generally accepted guidelines and standards for accessibility.
Our goal is to conform as closely as possible to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA standards.
These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.
Ongoing Efforts
Accessibility is an ongoing effort.
We regularly review our website to identify and fix potential accessibility barriers and to improve overall usability.
