Erika Creydt is different: she cares
What sets Erika Creydt apart is simple: she cares.
As a clinical psychologist and School Board Director, she listens to struggling families and young people, helping them find a path forward.
She sees those who feel invisible, left behind by broken systems, and she brings that experience to public service.
As our next Senator, Erika will work with everyone to deliver results for Whatcom families.
