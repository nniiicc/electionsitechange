top of page Home About Me Mission Get Involved Meet David Asing Of Chinese-Hawaiian heritage and raised in Kaimuki, I am deeply rooted in District 20.
After graduating from Kalani High and the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, I dedicated over ten years to public education as a math teacher.
I followed my mother’s legacy, striving to equip our youth with the tools of critical thinking, logic, and the resilience needed for a bright future.
My commitment to our community extends far beyond my time in the classroom.
In 2007, I launched 'Akahi Productions to champion our local entertainers and safeguard Hawaii’s precious cultural legacy.
Transitioning from the DOE in 2014, I, with a former business partner, successfully opened a preschool the following year and eventually expanded to three locations.
Today, I also serve as a neighborhood handyman through 'Akahi Handyman Services, where I provide dependable, high-quality help to local families.
Navigating the world as a small business owner has taught me the real-world obstacles our economy faces.
These experiences drive my dedication to bringing practical, common-sense leadership to our district and ensuring a sustainable future for us all.
From my time teaching at Kalani and Kaimuki High School to managing island-wide talent and venue entertainment, I have always been driven by a passion for service.
Now, I am ready to bring that same dedication to the State House, ensuring our District 20 families have a representative who honors their history and champions a sustainable, common-sense future for us all.
Vision for Honolulu Affordable Living Tackling the housing crisis by proposing ideas that make sense and identifying the issues that cause the high costs.
Community Safety Investing in localized first responders and community-led programs to ensure every neighborhood in District 20 feels secure.
Efficient and Accountable Government Representing our community's needs while prioritizing transparency, Integrity, and accountability.
