About Kristie Rooted in Chandler.
Committed to Arizona.
I’m a mother, an East valley native who grew up in Chandler, lawyer turned mediator, and now I’m running to represent Legislative District 13 in the Arizona State Senate.
I was raised in a family rooted in service, where I learned early the value of hard work, giving back, and showing up for our community.
Those values have guided me throughout my career and shaped the way I approach difficult problems.
I’m running for the Arizona State Senate because our community deserves leadership focused less on division and more on results.
I’m ready to bring that approach to the State Capitol and deliver common-sense solutions that make life better for Arizona families.
