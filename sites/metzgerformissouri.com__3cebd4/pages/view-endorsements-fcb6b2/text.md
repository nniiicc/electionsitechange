Teamsters Local 823 proudly endorses Aaron Metzger for Missouri State Representative, District 161.
Aaron Metzger understands that strong communities are built when working people have access to quality health care, a strong public education system, and the rights and protections they deserve on the job.
He is committed to standing up for the issues that matter to Missouri’s working families.
Teamsters Local 823 believes that every working family deserves affordable, quality health care, excellent public schools, and a fair opportunity to earn a good living with dignity and respect.
Aaron Metzger will bring that commitment to Jefferson City and fight for policies that strengthen working people and our communities.
We encourage the members of Teamsters Local 823 and the voters of Missouri’s 161st District to support Aaron Metzger for State Representative.
