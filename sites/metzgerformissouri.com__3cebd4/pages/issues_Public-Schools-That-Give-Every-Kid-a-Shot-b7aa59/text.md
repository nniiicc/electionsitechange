That means classrooms that aren’t falling apart, teachers who are paid like the professionals they are, and a state government that builds up public education instead of starving it.
What I’ll fight for: Fully fund the public school formula — not just on paper, in practice.
Keep public dollars in public schools.
Pay teachers competitively so we stop losing them to other states.
