Public Schools That Give Every Kid a Shot
Whether your kid goes to a Joplin public school, a rural school in Jasper or Newton County, or a vocational program, they deserve a fair shot.
That means classrooms that aren’t falling apart, teachers who are paid like the professionals they are, and a state government that builds up public education instead of starving it.
What I’ll fight for:
- Fully fund the public school formula — not just on paper, in practice.
- Keep public dollars in public schools.
- Pay teachers competitively so we stop losing them to other states.
- Strong career and technical education that opens up real paths for kids who aren’t headed to a four-year college.
