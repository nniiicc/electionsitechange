Key Deadlines
- Wednesday, October 7 - Voter registration deadline.
Register online or in person by this date; mailed forms must be postmarked by this date.
- Tuesday, October 20 - In-person absentee voting begins.
No excuse and no notary needed.
- Wednesday, October 21 at 5:00 p.m. - Absentee ballot requests must be received by your election authority.
- Monday, November 2 at 5:00 p.m. - Last day of in-person absentee voting.
- Tuesday, November 3 at 7:00 p.m. - Voted absentee ballots must be in the hands of your election authority.
Postmarks do not count, so mail yours back early.
Voting by Mail in Missouri
Missouri does not have no-excuse mail-in voting.
To get a ballot by mail you must request an absentee ballot and give one of six reasons: absence from your jurisdiction on Election Day; illness, disability, or caring for someone who is incapacitated; religious belief or practice; employment as an election worker, first responder, health care worker, or law enforcement officer; incarceration while still qualified to vote; or participation in the Safe at Home address confidentiality program.
Most absentee ballot envelopes must be signed in front of a notary.
You are exempt from the notary requirement if you are incapacitated or confined due to illness or disability (or caring for someone who is), incarcerated but still eligible to vote, enrolled in Safe at Home, or a military or overseas voter.
Want to skip the notary?
Vote absentee in person starting Tuesday, October 20 - no excuse and no notary required.
Bring a Photo ID
Missouri requires a valid government-issued photo ID to vote - a Missouri driver's license or nonduplicate ID, a U.S. passport, or a military ID.
If you do not have one, the state will provide a free ID.
