Meet Aaron Issues News Volunteer Yard Signs Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Healthcare Everybody Can Use Healthcare Everybody Can Use I’ve worked nights at a Joplin hospital for 13 years.
I have seen, up close, what happens when people put off care because they can’t afford it.
I’ve scanned uninsured neighbors, families who drove an hour because their local hospital cut services, and people who waited so long that by the time we saw them, there wasn’t much we could do.
What I’ll fight for in Jefferson City: Defend Missouri’s Medicaid expansion — voters approved it; the legislature should respect that.
Push back on policies that drive up prescription costs.
Keep regional and rural hospitals open and staffed.
Mental-health care that working families can actually afford and access.
