Yard Signs
The signs are in - get one for your yard
Nothing beats a neighbor seeing your yard sign.
Every sign in District 161 is a conversation starter, and it costs you nothing but a spot on your lawn.
Two ways to get one
- We deliver.
If you live in District 161, a volunteer will drop a sign off at your address and can help you set it up.
- You pick one up.
Signs are available at the Minnie Hackney Community Service Center, 110 S.
Main St., Joplin:
- Wednesdays - 3:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.
- Thursdays - 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
How to request one
Fill out the form below and we will get in touch to schedule your delivery - or just stop by the Minnie Hackney Center during pickup hours.
Signs are free.
If you would like to help cover the cost of more signs you can chip in here, but it is never required.
A few quick notes
- Signs go on private property only, with the property owner's permission.
Please do not place them on public rights-of-way, medians, or government property.
- Renting?
Check with your landlord or HOA first.
- If your sign goes missing or gets damaged, just let us know and we will replace it.
- After Election Day we will come back around to collect signs, or you can drop yours off with us.
