Fuel The Campaign Donate Contribution Limits & Reporting Federal law limits individual contributions to $3,500 per election for the 2025–2026 cycle.
Jackson For Congress 2026 may accept up to $3,500 for the primary-equivalent election and up to $3,500 for the general election, for a combined maximum of $7,000 from an individual.
Contributions made online, by check, at an event, or through any other method count toward these limits.
Federal law requires Jackson For Congress 2026 to use its best efforts to collect and report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose aggregate contributions exceed $200 during the election cycle.
The campaign may request additional information and may refund any contribution that is excessive, prohibited, improperly attributed, or otherwise impermissible.
