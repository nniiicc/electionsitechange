Jackson Fuel The Campaign ﻿ Donate ﻿ Join The Campaign Volunteer ﻿ Know The Campaign ﻿ Where's My District? ﻿ Jackson For Congress 2026 DeVelle Jackson is 35 and Black, a statesman from Minnesota, and an Independent running for U.S.
Congress in Minnesota’s Fifth District.
He graduated with distinction, earning a degree in Political Science from the University of Minnesota, and is currently a prospective J.D. candidate.
His professional background in building taught him how to coordinate multiple stakeholders, balance competing priorities, and make decisions that came with real consequences.
Civic service strengthened the discipline and persistence behind his public life.
Minnesota’s Fifth District deserves more than party maintenance.
It deserves a representative with the independence to question what others avoid, the discipline to study what others simplify, and the voice to say clearly what the people already know deserves to be heard.
A stronger voice is not volume.
It is command of the question.
It is the ability to speak plainly where politics hides behind scripts, to press for details when the establishment expects silence, and to give force and direction to concerns the people have been carrying without representation.
Jackson writes his own message, builds his own case, and enters this race with the independence to speak without being filtered through what a party wants to hear or softened into talking points.
His voice is direct, studied, independent, and built to press for answers.
The district needs someone who can carry the argument, speak without a borrowed script, ask the questions politics too often leaves untouched, and press institutions until the people get answers.
This campaign respects the wisdom of age, but it does not believe public life should be trapped under stale authority, inherited scripts, or a new generation of politicians trained only to repeat the old order.
A House seat is renewed every two years, and two years is enough time to ask better questions, expose neglected issues, sharpen the fight, and make the district harder to ignore.
