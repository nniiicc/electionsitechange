T – Transparency
Government should be honest, accountable, and open.
I will work to restore public trust by ensuring your tax dollars are spent responsibly and that decisions are made with integrity.
E – Economy
Hawaii families deserve relief from the high cost of living.
I will support policies that strengthen our economy, lower the tax burden, support small businesses, and create opportunities for local families to succeed.
S – Safety
Every family deserves to feel safe in their home and neighborhood.
I will advocate for safer communities by supporting law enforcement, addressing crime, protecting our kupuna and keiki, and promoting responsible public safety policies.
S – Service
Public office is about serving the people—not personal interests.
I am committed to listening, leading with integrity, and working every day to represent the voices of District 29.
