Meet Aaron Hi, I’m Aaron Schweiger, a proud 5th generation Northlander, business owner, husband, and father.
I live in Hermantown with my wife, two daughters, and our dogs.
I graduated from Duluth East High School in 1999, before attending the University of Minnesota for undergrad, and then on to Western Michigan University and the University of Notre Dame Law Schools.
Since 2014, I’ve worked as a real estate developer to build and support housing across Minnesota and Wisconsin.
My wife is a fifth grade teacher for the Duluth Public Schools.
After both living abroad and in other states, we are happy to be back in the Northland with our friends and relatives, raising our family.
Throughout my life I have witnessed the power of public service, hard work, and sacrifice.
I have had the honor and privilege of working alongside amazing leaders like Congressman James Oberstar and Senator Amy Klobuchar, who taught me that true leadership is about listening to the voices of those we serve with compassion and delivering results that make a meaningful impact in their lives.
I believe we can build a brighter future together—where our children can dream big and achieve even bigger.

I promise to fight tirelessly for your rights; reinvest in our aging infrastructure; strengthen our public schools; and foster economic growth that benefits all Northland families.

Together, we can create a Minnesota that reflects our values—a place where every person has a fair shot at success.
Our current political climate demands that we put aside distractions and focus on the needs of our local communities.
I am committed to fighting for the people of District 3B at the State Capitol.
Standing shoulder to shoulder, ready to turn the tide and build a Minnesota that continues to thrive on hope, inclusion, and opportunity for all.
A state where we work together to find common sense solutions to difficult and complex problems.

Key Issues Building a stronger Minnesota for everyone Cost Of Living Our community is facing a cost of living crisis.
More and more, the price of the basics we rely on, like food, energy, and housing, is rising ahead of wages and squeezing our pocketbooks.
I will be laser-focused every day on making sure our residents can afford their lives and build a future for their families.
Housing Whether you rent or own, folks in our area and across the state are dealing with a housing sector that is not working.
We need to commit to a broad agenda to address the lack of affordable housing, barriers to homeownership and building equity in your home, skyrocketing home insurance and property tax rates.
Building more units across the housing spectrum and ensuring folks at all ages and incomes are secure in their homes will be a top priority for me.
Healthcare Everyone needs affordable, accessible, and quality healthcare but right now, we’re moving in the wrong direction.
We know that when people delay or avoid care due to affordability, they end up needing more intensive and expensive interventions later on.
We all bear this cost.
I will work tirelessly to hold healthcare insurance companies and providers accountable to their duty to provide adequate care and ensure our residents’ health comes before corporate profits.
Education Providing our children with a strong public education is a cornerstone to building prosperity.
When we invest in our students, we’re not only providing youth with a solid foundation to pursue their own dreams and passions, we’re also empowering the next generation of Minnesota’s workforce to succeed in an everchanging world.
