0 PRIVACY POLICY Revised June 14, 2026 We believe in complete transparency.
This privacy policy explains exactly what data we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and how we protect it.
Information Collected on Our Site When you interact with our campaign online, you might choose to share your personal details with us.
This happens when you sign up for emails, fill out a volunteer form, or make a donation.
We may collect your details including your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
Federal Election Commission Compliance We are required by federal law to collect and report specific information for financial contributions.
If you donate to the campaign, we must collect your occupation and employer name.
The Federal Election Commission requires us to report this information for any contributor who gives more than $200 during an election cycle.
These donor reports become part of the public record.
How Your Information is Used We use your information to power this grassroots movement.
Your data helps us send campaign updates, organize local volunteer efforts, process financial contributions, and keep you informed about upcoming events.
If you choose to receive text messages from us, we will use your phone number to send campaign alerts.
You can opt out of these messages at any moment by replying STOP.
Data Sharing Practices We value your trust.
We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to outside groups.
We share your data only with trusted vendors who help us manage our website, process donations, or send communications.
These partners are required to keep your information secure.
Mobile information and text messaging data are handled with extra care.
Your text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Our website uses cookies to understand how visitors interact with our pages.
This helps us improve the site layout and make our online tools easier to use.
You can change your internet browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.
Keeping Your Data Secure We implement security measures to guard your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.
While no internet transmission is completely secure, we do everything we can to keep your data safe.
Updates to This Policy We may update this privacy policy as our campaign grows or as legal regulations change.
Any updates will be posted directly to this page.
How to Contact the Campaign If you have questions about how we handle your data, you can contact us directly through the campaign website email form or by sending an email to: info{at}votekristiburke.com.
