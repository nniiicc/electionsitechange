I will be vigilant in protecting access and integrity of those public lands.
Protect Idaho’s Water Water is essential for the agricultural and tourist economies of our District.
I will promote forward thinking policies in the Legislature to help preserve water rights and the health of our rivers and aquifers.
Care That Reaches Rural Idaho Health care should be accessible to all Idahoans.
Legislative cuts have increased the costs of medical visits and treatments, and they have also undermined the ability of rural providers to stay in business, creating an additional burden of longer drives and higher travel costs for those in rural areas.
Medical decisions must be left to patients and their providers.
I oppose state restrictions on any individual’s health care.
Idaho citizens voted to expand Medicaid coverage, but recent cuts threaten to raise costs for all of us.
I will seek ways to use existing resources and broad coverage to lower health care costs across the state.
Find Affordable Solutions People who work here should be able to live here .
Our communities depend on a local workforce, and I will support more starter homes and workforce housing while honoring local control.
Locals should not be forced out of the homes they own.
Homeowners exemptions have not kept up with rising values.
Everyday expenses are too burdensome .
Idaho has the highest grocery tax in the nation, and I support lowering that along with providing support to make childcare and healthcare more affordable.
Fill good local jobs with local workers.
Workforce development creates opportunities and helps raise wages for workers.
Invest in Idaho’s Future Educational opportunities lay the groundwork for future generations to prosper.
The Legislature needs to provide teachers with the resources and the trust to open doors to our children.
From pre-K to universities and vocational training, education provides a strong return on public investment.
Infrastructure , especially transportation in our large district, is vital for all aspects of our economic health.
My focus will be to ensure resources and enforce transparency from state agencies.
Real Solutions, Built Together I will be consistent in promoting collaboration and respecting the choices of local communities and schools.
The majority party in the Legislature today is practicing gross financial mismanagement, reducing individual freedoms, and undermining local control.
A functional legislature focuses on real problems , offering practical solutions.
Cost of living, abundant water, strong education, and functional infrastructure are the real issues that the Legislature needs to work together on.
