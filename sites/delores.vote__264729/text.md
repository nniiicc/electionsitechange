COMMON SENSE LEADERSHIP FOR THE ARIZONA STATE HOUSE
Meet Delores McLaughlin
Delores McLaughlin, a dedicated community leader and former educator, is running for the Arizona State House in LD24.
Having taught communication at Estrella Mountain Community College, Delores is a Grand Canyon University Doctorate alumna who equips students from all backgrounds with practical skills to succeed in healthcare, business, and beyond.
Married to her soulmate Marcus and continuing her family’s tradition of pastoral service, Delores is committed to common-sense solutions: strengthening education, supporting families, promoting economic growth, and ensuring hardworking, diverse neighborhoods have a strong voice in Phoenix—fighting for policies rooted in hard work, responsibility, and opportunity for all.
Why This Matters
The Arizona State House decides if taxes are affordable for working families, how we keep our communities safe, and whether we prioritize practical solutions for education, housing costs, and jobs—or let special interests and out-of-touch policies take over.
Delores McLaughlin is running as a reliable voice to fight for you: protecting hard-earned paychecks, supporting strong families, and delivering common-sense representation that puts Glendale’s hardworking neighborhoods first.
Delores's Platform
The Arizona State House affects every family, every taxpayer, and every organization in this state — but most people don’t know much about it.
Delores McLaughlin is running to represent the everyday Arizonan who wants to see our state succeed.
As a Clean Elections candidate and trusted community leader, Delores is taking a stand against corporate cronyism, extremist ideologies, and backroom deals that hurt everyday Arizonans.
