Meet Delores McLaughlin
I’m Delores McLaughlin, a former educator and Glendale resident who’s passionate about helping people communicate, connect, and thrive right here in our community.
Having taught communication at Estrella Mountain Community College gives me the chance every day to work with students from all walks of life—many balancing jobs, families, and dreams of a better future.
I earned my Doctorate and Master’s degrees from Grand Canyon University in Organizational Leadership with an emphasis on Organizational Development, and Alumna of Arizona State University West in Communication Studies my focus has always been on how clear, respectful conversation can open doors, whether in a nursing program, a job interview, or family discussions.
I know firsthand the challenges our lower-income and diverse neighborhoods face, and I’m committed to bringing that understanding to the State House.
My faith has guided me through every step, just as it did for my mother before me.
As a pastor and a woman raised in service to our local communities, I carry my faith’s values quietly but firmly: integrity, compassion, hard work, and looking out for one another.
I’m blessed to share this life with my husband and soulmate, Marcus, who stands by me in everything we do.
In LD24, I want to represent the hardworking families who make this district strong: advocating for better schools, safer streets, economic growth that reaches every corner, and policies that put people first without dividing us.
Together, we can build a brighter Arizona where opportunity is real for everyone who shows up and puts in the effort.
