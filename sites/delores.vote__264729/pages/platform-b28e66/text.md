Helping Senior Citizens Delores believes seniors on fixed incomes deserve to age in place without the constant threat of rising property taxes forcing them out of their homes.
She supports eliminating or significantly reducing property taxes on primary residences for qualifying seniors to provide financial relief and stability.
This approach honors the contributions of our elders while ensuring they remain active, valued members of our community.
Lower Taxes for Working Families In our hardworking, lower-income neighborhoods, families and small businesses need to keep more of what they earn to cover essentials like groceries, housing, and education.
Delores backs lower income taxes to ease everyday burdens and encourage local entrepreneurship.
By prioritizing tax relief, she aims to help residents build savings, invest in their futures, and raise families.
Great Education Options for All As a community college educator, Delores knows quality education is the key to opportunity for students of all backgrounds.
She champions educational choice while investing in strong public schools and holding all schools accountable for real results.
Her focus ensures every child in our district has access to the tools and pathways needed to succeed both academically and professionally.
Reliable, Efficient Government Arizonans deserve a government that works transparently and efficiently: solving real problems instead of creating bureaucracy or waste.
Delores calls for leadership that prioritizes taxpayer dollars, cuts unnecessary spending, and focuses on delivering services effectively.
This means putting everyday Arizonan families first, not special interests or outdated policies.
Safe Communities Safe neighborhoods are essential for families to thrive, and law enforcement plays a critical role in keeping our streets secure.
Delores strongly supports police with the resources and tools they need while emphasizing professionalism, accountability, and building genuine trust with residents.
By fostering strong community-police relationships, she works toward safer, more united neighborhoods.
Economic Opportunity and Affordability Rising costs for housing, childcare, and daily needs hit working families hardest in LD24.
Delores advocates for policies that create good-paying jobs, support small businesses, and address affordability challenges head-on.
Her goal is to promote economic growth that reaches every corner of Glendale, helping residents achieve financial stability and upward mobility.
Strong Families and Personal Responsibility Strong families form the foundation of our communities, and personal responsibility drives progress for everyone.
Delores upholds values that encourage family stability, hard work, and mutual support without dividing people.
She seeks policies that empower individuals and families to take charge of their futures while fostering compassion and opportunity in our state.
