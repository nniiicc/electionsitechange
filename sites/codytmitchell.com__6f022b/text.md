Meet Cody
Republican for SC House
A native of Bethune, Cody Mitchell is an attorney, businessman, former municipal judge, and military officer who has dedicated much of his life to serving his community, state, and country.
Today, Cody proudly represents House District 65 and serves on the House Judiciary and Rules Committees.
Drawing on his legal, military, and business experience, Cody works to protect conservative values, strengthen our communities, and make sure the needs of rural South Carolina are heard in Columbia.
My Platform
“I was raised to believe that public service is about putting others first.
It is an honor to represent the people of District 65 in the South Carolina House, and I remain committed to protecting our conservative values, standing up for our rural communities, and making sure your voice is heard in Columbia.
I will continue working every day to earn your trust and deliver results for the communities we call home.”
— Cody Mitchell
