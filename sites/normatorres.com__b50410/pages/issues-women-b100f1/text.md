Women’s Rights
Upholding women’s rights and reproductive freedom is vital to building a just and equitable future for all.
In the wake of the Dobbs decision, which rolled back decades of hard-fought protections for reproductive rights, it is more crucial than ever to ensure that women have access to the healthcare they need.
Congresswoman Torres, is a dedicated advocate for protecting a woman’s right to choose and expanding access to essential reproductive healthcare services.
As a Vice-Chair of the Reproductive Freedom Caucus, she stands firmly against any attempts to undermine these rights and is committed to fighting for healthcare equity, particularly for marginalized communities.
Beyond reproductive rights, Congresswoman Torres is focused on creating economic opportunities for women by working to lower the cost of childcare, advocating for paid family leave, and supporting initiatives that advance women in leadership positions.
By empowering women in the workplace and in government, she aims to break down barriers and create a society where every woman has the resources and support necessary to thrive.
