Housing
Housing is a critical issue that resonates across our nation, but it is especially pressing in our communities, where the demand for safe and affordable homes continues to outpace supply.
Congresswoman Torres is a recognized leader in Congress, tirelessly advocating for policies that promote the construction of new housing and the preservation of affordable options.
As Chair of the New Democrat Coalition Working Group on Housing, Infrastructure, and Transportation, she understands that stable, affordable housing is the foundation for thriving families and communities, and she is committed to ensuring that everyone has a place to call home.
And, as a senior Member of the Transportation, Housing and Urban Development Subcommittee of Appropriations, Congresswoman Torres has successfully fought against Trump Administration funding cuts to critical affordable housing and homelessness prevention programs.
In addition to advocating for increased funding for affordable housing initiatives, Congresswoman Torres works to remove barriers to homeownership and support innovative housing solutions.
These efforts are essential to tackling the housing crisis head-on, creating pathways for families to secure their futures and build strong, vibrant neighborhoods.
