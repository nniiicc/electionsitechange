These races shape your schools, roads, property taxes, public safety, and local courts.
School board: local public-school oversight.
Noblesville Schools referendum: a public question on funding for Noblesville Schools.
Learn more from Noblesville Schools.
Township trustee: emergency assistance, and in some places fire protection and cemetery upkeep.
Township board: approves the budget and oversees trustee spending.
County commissioner, District 1: county executive decision-maker.
County council: controls budgeting and appropriations.
County sheriff: runs the sheriff’s department and jail.
County coroner: investigates deaths requiring formal review.
County assessor: sets assessed property values for tax bills.
County recorder: keeps deeds, mortgages, and land records.
Circuit court clerk: manages court records, elections, and voter registration.
Prosecuting attorney: decides which criminal cases to bring.
Superior court judges: hear local civil and criminal cases.
Heads-up: Noblesville and Fishers city offices such as mayor and city council are not on the ballot this year.
Those come up in 2027.
