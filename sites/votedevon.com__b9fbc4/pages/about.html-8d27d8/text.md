Her holiday-greenery business grew 3.5× a year, until tariffs made the market too unpredictable to plan around.
She knows what it feels like when policy decisions pause hard-earned growth instead of driving it.
She’s spent her career making sure no kid gets left out.
That started with degrees in Deaf Studies and Developmental Psychology, led to work with the U.S.
Department of Education, that turned into years spent figuring out what actually helps in a classroom.
