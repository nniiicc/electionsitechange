Your Information
How the Committee to Elect Devon Wellington handles information submitted through this website.
Last updated: July 22, 2026.
Depending on the form you choose to submit, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, street address, city, ZIP code, yard-sign quantity, and the message you send us.
We also receive limited security information needed to prevent automated abuse, such as a reCAPTCHA assessment.
We use submitted information only for campaign purposes: responding to messages, coordinating requested yard-sign delivery, sending campaign email that you requested, protecting our systems, and complying with applicable law.
We do not sell submitted personal information.
Access is limited to authorized campaign staff and service providers that operate the website and its security, storage, and email systems for us.
Access is protected with authentication, least-privilege permissions, encryption, and audit logging.
We may disclose information when legally required or when necessary to protect the campaign, its users, or the public.
- Contact messages are ordinarily deleted within 90 days, and sooner when no longer needed.
- Yard-sign requests are ordinarily deleted within 90 days or 30 days after fulfillment or the election, whichever comes first.
- Email subscription information is retained until you unsubscribe or the campaign no longer needs the list.
After an unsubscribe, a minimal suppression record may be retained to honor your request.
- Encrypted backups are ordinarily retained for 90 days.
Deletions age out of backup copies on that schedule.
Legal obligations may require a different period.
The campaign will document any exception.
Production submissions are sent to a private server API rather than directly to a browser-accessible database.
Sensitive fields are encrypted before storage.
The public intake service cannot decrypt historical submissions.
Backups are encrypted and access-controlled, and the campaign periodically tests restoration.
No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.
You may choose not to submit optional fields.
Campaign email includes a way to unsubscribe.
To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, email devon@votedevon.com.
We may need to verify your request before acting on it.
If you use the district address lookup, the address you enter is sent directly from your browser to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim geocoding service to obtain map coordinates.
VoteDevon does not receive or store the address.
Loading the map also requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.
OpenStreetMap’s handling of that information is described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
This site uses reCAPTCHA Enterprise to reduce abuse.
Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
We may update this notice as campaign operations or legal requirements change.
