Paul Gatewood is a lifelong Missourian, Marine Corps veteran, and dedicated conservative running to represent St.
Joseph in Missouri House District 10.
A veteran of Operation Desert Storm, Paul carries a deep-seated commitment to serve his country.
Now, that commitment is leading him to run for public office to defend life and liberty. ​ With a professional background as the current Evidence Manager for the St.
Joseph Police Department, Paul is a staunch supporter of law enforcement and first responders.
His platform focuses on keeping St.
Joseph affordable, increasing economic opportunities, and protecting the rights of blue-collar families.​ Paul’s dedication to his community is evident through his extensive record of volunteerism, including serving as Chairman of the Joint Veterans Committee and as a board member for the St.
Joe Museums.
A proud father of eight and grandfather of nine, Paul is running to ensure a secure and prosperous future for the next generation of Missourians.
