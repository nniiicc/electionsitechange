During my first term as State Representative, I have:
- Voted for the largest tax cut in state history.
- Helped pass the largest investment in public schools, including an additional $3 million for Vernon.
- Brought a bill to the floor that prevents crumbling concrete from being recycled back into circulation.
- Fixed the pension "cliff" for retirees.
- Helped pass a bill to protect seniors in nursing homes.
- Co-sponsored a law that provides property tax exemption for veterans with a 100% disability rating.
- Passed a bill to streamline teacher certification in CT, which will help put highly qualified teachers in our classrooms.
- Created, and co-chaired, the Civic Education, Civic Engagement & Media Literacy Task Force.
