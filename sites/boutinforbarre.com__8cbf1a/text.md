Barre City Is Home
I've called Barre City home for over 42 years.
I've had the privilege of serving our community for 14 years on the City Council, three years on the School Board, including two as Chair, and now as your State Representative.
My focus has remained the same: put Barre first and make Vermont a more affordable place to live.
