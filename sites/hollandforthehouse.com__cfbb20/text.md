Rooted in the Soil, Driven by Progress.
I’m Sarah Holland, and I'd like to thank you for your interest in my campaign for State Representative in PA’s 80th District.
Originally from Martinsburg, I'm now living in Williamsburg and proud to be running to represent the people of this hard-working district.
With a background in environmental chemistry and research, I’m committed to protecting our natural resources, supporting our farmers, and working together to build a healthy and prosperous future for everyone in this community.
Why I'm Running
I had the privilege of addressing the crowd at the March 28 No Kings rally to share my reasons for entering this race and what I want to accomplish for the people of this district, state, and nation.
My Commitments to the 80th District
ENVIRONMENT
Protective Environmental Policies
Preserving our natural resources is not just an ideal, it's a necessity.
In Harrisburg I will advocate for air and water quality standards that ensure a healthy future for every community in our state.
AGRICULTURE
Sustainable Farming Ecosystems
Supporting our family farms means investing in regenerative practices.
We should focus on tools and resources necessary to implement soil health initiatives that boost yield and long-term viability.
COMMUNITY
Building Resilience Together
Our state's approach should integrate environmental protection with economic growth.
By connecting and properly representing our district we can build a united front for a sustainable and prosperous future.
