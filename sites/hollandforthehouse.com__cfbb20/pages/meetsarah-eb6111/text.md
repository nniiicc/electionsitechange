Mission for the District
Working directly with local leaders, farmers, educators, and healthcare providers I will bring our community’s needs to Harrisburg and fight for resources to help us thrive.
My Values
Preserving the unique character and close-knit spirit of this district is important.
I will work to maintain the integrity that makes our communities special.
Community
Agriculture

Environmental Stewardship
I am dedicated to protecting our land, water, and air through policies that promote clean, sustainable practices for the health and well-being of current and future generations.
I believe in representation that listens, keeps people informed, and acts with integrity.

Legislative Goals
Invest in Infrastructure
I will push for funding for rural broadband expansion, road maintenance, and essential services to improve quality of life and economic opportunities.
Protect the Environment
I will support and introduce legislation that strengthens clean air, water, and land protections while limiting pollution and preserving farmland.
Prevent Unchecked Expansion
I will work to pass legislation that prevents development of AI data centers in the district in order to protect farmland, conserve energy, and preserve rural character.
Advance Environmental Education
I will back programs that educate our communities on environmental health, sustainable practices, and conservation to empower residents with knowledge and tools for a better future.
Support Agriculture
I will advocate for bills that provide assistance, resources, and opportunities for farmers.
I will fight to protect the rights and freedom of all people as outlined by the Constitution and Bill of Rights.
