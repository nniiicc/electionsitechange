Young Conservative Voice Fighting for Common Sense Values
AGRICULTURE
Farming is the biggest industry in Illinois, yet it receives little attention from the urban legislators who control our state government.
As a farmer, I know the struggles that our agriculture industry is facing.
Let's bring farmers to the table and pass bipartisan, pro-agricultural legislation.
PUBLIC SAFETY
Rising crime is out of control in Illinois.
Disastrous, soft-on-crime policies like the SAFE-T Act have endangered innocent Illinoisans.
We need to prioritize the rights of law-abiding citizens and support our law enforcement officers as they protect our communities!
LOCAL CONTROL
Local communities know their needs better than politicians in Springfield.
We need to empower local leaders, protect local decision-making, and ensure communities have a voice in the decisions that affect them.
AFFORDABILITY
Illinois families are feeling the strain of rising costs and high taxes.
We need to make Illinois more affordable by cutting unnecessary spending, providing meaningful tax relief, and allowing hardworking families to keep more of what they earn.
Growing up in rural Illinois I always heard the same phrase repeated to me.
Adults telling me to “grow up, get an education, and get out of Illinois”.
Well, as a fourth-generation family farmer, throwing in the towel and walking away isn’t in my blood.
Instead of walking away, I chose to stay here, roll up my sleeves, and get to work.
I hope you will join me in the fight to make our district, and our state, a better place for everyone.
