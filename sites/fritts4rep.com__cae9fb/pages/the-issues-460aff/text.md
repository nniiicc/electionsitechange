The Issues
AGRICULTURE
Farming is the biggest industry in Illinois, yet it receives little attention from the urban legislators who control our state government.
As a farmer, I know the struggles that our agriculture industry is facing.
Let's bring farmers to the table and pass bipartisan, pro-agricultural legislation.
PUBLIC SAFETY
Rising crime is out of control in Illinois.
Disastrous, soft-on-crime policies like the SAFE-T Act have endangered innocent Illinoisans.
We need to prioritize the rights of law-abiding citizens and support our law enforcement officers as they protect our communities!
HEALTHCARE
Decreasing access to healthcare in rural areas is putting the health and safety of our communities at risk.
As your legislator, I will continue fighting for legislation that keeps our hospital doors open.
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
From international corporations to mom-and-pop shops, businesses are fleeing Illinois and taking thousands of jobs with them.
It’s time to promote pro-business policies that stimulate growth in our local economies.
