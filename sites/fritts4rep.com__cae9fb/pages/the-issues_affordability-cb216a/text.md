Affordability
Illinois families are feeling the strain of rising costs and high taxes.
We need to make Illinois more affordable by cutting unnecessary spending, providing meaningful tax relief, and allowing hardworking families to keep more of what they earn.
As your State Representative I will:
- Fight against tax increases and anti-growth policies that restrict our economy.
- Support legislation to help working families, such as repealing the sales tax on gas and ending state income tax on tips and overtime work.
- Advocate for economic development.
