Public Safety
Rising crime is out of control in Illinois.
Disastrous, soft-on-crime policies like the SAFE-T Act have endangered innocent Illinoisans.
We need to prioritize the rights of law-abiding citizens and support our law enforcement officers as they protect our communities!
As your State Representative I will:
- Support law enforcement officers by listening to what they need to remain safe and effective.
- Fight against poor legislation that puts criminals before the people of Illinois.
- Partner with other legislators on bipartisan solutions to address our rising crime crisis.
