Local Control
Local communities know their needs better than politicians in Springfield.
We need to empower local leaders, protect local decision-making, and ensure communities have a voice in the decisions that affect them.
As your State Representative, I will:
- Fight against legislation that overrides our local control.
- Advocate to fully fund the Local Government Distributive Fund, which will support our local government and provide property tax relief.
- Work alongside local leaders to be a voice for our community in Springfield.
