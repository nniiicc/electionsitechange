DIXON, IL – To best represent residents of the 74th District, State Representative Bradley Fritts (R-Dixon) completed his summer tour of 38 stops, speaking with hundreds of district residents, local officials, job creators, nonprofit leaders, and constituents in need of assistance.
“Advocating for residents of the 74th District is my top priority, and this summer, I focused my time and energy on meeting the people I am so proud to serve,” said Fritts.
“Decisions made in Springfield impact each of the families in my district, and I will fight for their interests every day.
Every constituent I met on this tour reinforced my role as a State Representative is to focus on my community in District 74.”
