Agriculture
Farming is the biggest industry in Illinois, yet it receives little attention from the urban legislators who control our state government.
As a farmer, I know the struggles that our agriculture industry is facing.
Let’s bring farmers to the table and pass bipartisan, pro-agricultural legislation.
As your State Representative I will:
- Advocate for more economic development in rural communities.
- Re-introduce a bill I filed during the last legislative session aimed at helping young farmers obtain their CDL.
- Fight for landowner rights, while protecting productive farmground.
