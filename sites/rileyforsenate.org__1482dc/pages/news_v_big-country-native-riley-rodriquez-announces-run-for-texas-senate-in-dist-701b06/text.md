Big Country native Riley Rodriquez announces run for Texas Senate in district 28
Riley Rodriquez said his decision to run came after seeing a lack of focus on key issues, particularly the rapid expansion of data centers into the region, stating that prices keep going up, wages stay the same, and corporations are moving into the state needing resources and energy with no type of regulation.
He also highlighted rural water and electricity concerns, noting reports from Dallas showing energy bill impacts after 15 years of data center presence.
