The Fighter We Need.
The Leader We Deserve.
AMY HUFFMAN OLIVER is a native Hoosier whose heart and career are in public service.
She has stood up to bullies as a Felony Domestic Violence Prosecutor, expanded young minds as a 7th-grade world history teacher at Brown County Schools, and navigates shrinking budgets as a 2-time elected School Board member with Brown County Schools.
In the State House, Amy will fight to:
Lower utility costs by cutting the state sales tax on home utility bills
Make data centers pay their own way and get community support
Make health care more affordable for hardworking Hoosiers
Fully fund our public schools by reversing damaging funding cuts
Lower the cost of gas, housing, and everyday essentials
