Republican Meredith Craig was born and raised right here in Wayne County, Craig grew up on a chicken farm in Creston, OH.
She was able to learn the value of hard work and discipline.
Craig was an active member of 4H and FFA.
Craig is a Smithville and Malone University graduate and daughter of Paul and Julie Alexander.
She’s rooted in agriculture.
Craig worked at a local grocery store in Smithville for seven years.
That experience gave her proof positive that we need to fight for lower taxes, smaller government, and reduced regulations to truly promote economic growth and prosperity.
She's an advocate for our small business. ​ As the wife of a Wayne County Deputy Sheriff and a staunch Second Amendment advocate, Craig always prioritizes giving our first responders the support they need while safeguarding our fundamental rights.
Meredith has earned the highest possible rating from the Buckeye Firearms Association and the NRA.
She’s a proud voice for our men and women in uniform. ​ Craig is a strong Christian conservative and an active member of her church in Rittman.
Her guiding principles allow her to champion pro-life initiatives and advocate for school choice.
She is making Ohio a state that prioritizes the sanctity of life and empowers parents to choose the best education for their children.
