Legislative Priorities
Champion for Small Businesses
- Advocating for legislation that expands access to affordable healthcare options for small businesses without adding costly mandates.
- Promoting transparency in third-party litigation funding and working to curb frivolous lawsuits.
- Supporting tax relief measures that allow small business owners to reinvest in their employees and local communities.
Supporting First Responders
- Advancing legislation to strengthen Ohio’s Move Over Law to better protect emergency responders, in honor of Lt.
Philip Wigal of the Town & Country Fire District.
- Developing legislation to allow for the transfer of benefits between agencies and departments for first responders.
- Co-sponsoring the HALO Act, which establishes a required safety perimeter around emergency scenes, allowing law enforcement and first responders to perform their duties safely and effectively.
Fighting for Lower Taxes and Greater Transparency
- Strong advocate for policies that reduce the tax burden on Ohio families.
- Supported efforts to simplify Ohio’s income tax system through a flat tax rate and remains committed to eliminating the state income tax altogether.
- Proud co-sponsor of legislation that makes property tax levies easier to understand, ensuring voters clearly see how proposals impact their bills.
- Committed to government accountability and ensuring tax dollars are spent wisely and responsibly.
Protecting Life and Strengthening Families
- Championing legislation to manage the instant mail-order access to mifepristone and require in-person physician visits for safety and accountability.
- Co-sponsor of the SHE WINS Act, requiring doctors to provide women with detailed information at least 24 hours before an abortion and establishing civil penalties for providers who fail to comply—promoting informed consent and transparency.
- Supported key budget initiatives, including:
- $68 million for the Adoption Grant Program
- $1 million for Maternal and Infant Housing Assistance
- $20 million for Parenting & Pregnancy Programs offered through Pregnancy Care Centers
- A $750 state tax credit for donations to pregnancy resource centers
- $20 million for the Responsible Fatherhood Initiative
