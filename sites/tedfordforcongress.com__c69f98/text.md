BUILT ON MAIN STREET
For more than 30 years, Mark has built and run businesses in Tulsa, in insurance and construction, creating jobs and meeting a payroll in good years and lean ones.

In Congress, he'll bring that same results-first approach to lowering costs for working families, cutting waste and fraud in Washington, and bringing good jobs back home.
