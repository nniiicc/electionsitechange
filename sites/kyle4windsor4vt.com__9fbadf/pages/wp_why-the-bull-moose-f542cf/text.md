Why the Bull Moose?
The bull moose was historically used as a symbol to represent Theodore Roosevelt’s Progressive Party; a reformist, workers’ rights focused, anti-corruption party.
The “Bull Moose” Party was the most successful third party in United States history; one which nearly broke the two-party duopoly more than one-hundred years ago.
As for the colors, green and yellow symbolize Vermont; representing the fields and forests which make up our working lands.
Moose are steadfast, resilient, independent, and are the largest mammals native to the green mountains.
The bull moose is an obvious choice to represent this campaign, and our vision for an affordable Vermont; one where all working people will have a seat at the table.
