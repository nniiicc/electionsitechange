Our Indiana House District 47
Indiana State House districts are redrawn every ten years following the United States census.
Our district is made up of portions of Shelby and Johnson Counties.
It includes the communities of Franklin, Fairland, Boggstown, Trafalgar, Fountaintown, Bargersville, Nineveh, and parts of Shelbyville and Edinburgh.
