This Privacy Policy explains how Bo Renshaw for State Representative (“the campaign,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit borenshaw.com (the “Site”).
Information We Collect Automatically
Like most websites, this Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors use the Site, measure the effectiveness of our outreach, and improve your experience.
This can include information such as your browser type, pages visited, and how you arrived at the Site.
Cookies
Cookies are small text files placed on your device.
We use tracking cookies only after you provide consent through the cookie banner on this Site.
If you decline, we will not load tracking cookies on your visit.
You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data and revisiting the Site.
How We Use Information
We use the information collected through cookies to understand how people find and use this Site so we can improve it and support the campaign's outreach efforts.
We do not sell this information.
Third-Party Services
This Site may link to or use third-party services, such as our donation processor (WinRed), for specific functions.
Those services have their own privacy policies governing the information you provide directly to them.
