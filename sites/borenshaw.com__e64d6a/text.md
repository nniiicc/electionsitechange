Where Bo Stands
House District 70.
★ ★ ★
- Affordability: Lower the Cost of Living Arkansas families are getting squeezed.
Bo is fighting to keep cutting the income tax so you keep more of what you earn, and he'll never stop pushing back on the fees and taxes that make everyday life harder, like the used car sales tax that penalizes working families just for buying a reliable vehicle.
- Support Small Business Small businesses are the backbone of North Little Rock and Sherwood.
Bo knows it firsthand.
As a small business owner of more than 25 years, he'll cut red tape, keep taxes low, and make sure local businesses can compete, hire, and grow right here in our community.
- Better Schools and Student Success Every child in North Little Rock and Sherwood deserves to read, learn, and succeed, so that they will be ready for the future.
Bo supports paying teachers what they deserve and holding schools accountable for real results: reading scores, graduation rates, and student success.
- Protect Healthcare Access Healthcare costs are crushing families and small businesses alike.
Bo believes Arkansans should be able to see a doctor without breaking the bank.
He'll fight to protect Medicare and Medicaid, push for more price transparency, and keep healthcare accessible in our community.
- Safe Neighborhoods Our families deserve to feel safe at home, at school, and on our streets.
Bo will work to make sure law enforcement has the tools and support they need, while investing in the community programs that prevent crime before it starts.

Meet Bo
Bo and his wife have raised their family in North Little Rock.
Over 25 years he has stood on the sidelines of North Little Rock and Sylvan Hills games, providing volunteer sports medicine services to the community.
He started his physical therapy practice with four employees and has spent his career caring for patients across central Arkansas.
He's running for State Representative because that's the kind of work, listening, showing up, getting results, the people of House District 70 deserve.
