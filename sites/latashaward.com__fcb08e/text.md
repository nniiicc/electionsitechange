Ward was born at Walter Reed Hospital in Washington, DC, and raised in Prince George's County, Maryland, where she has lived in the 24th Legislative District her entire life.
A graduate of Fairmont Heights High School (Class of 1986), LaTasha was an elite student-athlete who led her team to the school's first and only Maryland State Championship in 1984 and was named Prince George's County Player of the Year.
She earned a full Division I athletic scholarship to St.
Peter's College, earning a BS with a double major in Business Management and Marketing Management (1991).
LaTasha is a member of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.
(Delta Zeta Chapter, 1989) and became a charter member of the Eta Theta Chapter of Iota Phi Lambda Sorority, Inc. in 2025.
From 1993 to 2007, she worked with at-risk youth in Maryland and Virginia.
She is owner of Go Promote You, LLC and Founder and Executive Director of The Solid Foundation, Inc. — providing services for returning citizens and their children.
She's not running to become a politician — she's running to represent the people of District 24 who've been waiting for a champion like her.
