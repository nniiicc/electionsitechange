Get To Know Maurice
Maurice McCaney was born, raised, and has been a life-long resident of Lauderdale county, Alabama.
Before earning his Juris Doctorate at Cumberland School of Law, Maurice graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science & Sociology and a Bachelor of Arts degree with a focus in English from Samford University, Birmingham, Alabama.
Continuing forward, Maurice became licensed to practice law in Alabama in 2014 which provided the foundation for his current role as Attorney at Woodruff & McCaney, LLC.
Maurice continues to give back to his community by participating on a board level in organizations like the American Association of Christian Schools and Lauderdale County Republican Executive Committee.
