Our priorities
Fighting for Our Community
These are some of the top priorities our campaign is focused on.
Stronger Public Schools
Carroll will fight to invest Rainy Day Fund dollars into education, reduce class sizes, and implement forward-looking reforms.
Affordable & Accessible Healthcare
Carroll will advocate for increased funding to keep hospitals open and explore smarter use of federal partnerships through the Affordable Care Act marketplace.
Lower Costs
She will go after corruption and corporate greed to reduce costs in utilities and insurance, and to end predatory sales and fees.
Better Jobs & Higher Wages
She will work to attract new industries and diversify Oklahoma’s economy so families have access to better-paying jobs.
