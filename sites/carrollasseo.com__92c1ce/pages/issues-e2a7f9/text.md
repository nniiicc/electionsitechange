Stronger Public Schools Affordable & Accessible Healthcare Lower Costs & Higher Wages Standing up for Working Families Stronger Public Schools Oklahoma’s public education system has been underfunded for decades, leading to overcrowded classrooms, teacher shortages, and declining outcomes.
Carroll has seen this decline firsthand and knows that our current system is failing both students and educators.
She will fight to invest Rainy Day Fund dollars into education, reduce class sizes, and implement forward-looking reforms.
View more Affordable & Accessible Healthcare Hospitals across Oklahoma—especially in rural areas—are at risk of closing due to chronic underfunding, putting lives at risk.
Carroll believes every Oklahoman deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
She will advocate for increased funding to keep hospitals open and explore smarter use of federal partnerships through the Affordable Care Act marketplace.

She will work to attract new industries and diversify Oklahoma’s economy so families have access to better-paying jobs.
Carroll also supports workers’ rights to organize and bargain collectively, strengthening the middle class.
