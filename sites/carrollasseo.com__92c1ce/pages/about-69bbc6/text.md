MEET CARROLL ASSEO:
Carroll Asseo is a native Oklahoman who has lived in Moore for 40 years.
She is a graduate of Lawton High School and Cameron University, where she earned a B.S. in 1975.
She has spent her career as a public educator, from teacher to principal to superintendent, prior to her retirement in 2018.
During her tenure, she has called Oklahoma City, Moore, Schwartz, and Mid-Del Public Schools home.
She and her husband, Roger, taught together for many years, and both come from military families.
They are both gun owners who believe in safety and responsible ownership.
Carroll’s favorite activities include spending time with her grandson and volunteering in the community.
In the Community
A tireless advocate for change.
Carroll Asseo has spent her entire life advocating for the people of Oklahoma, long before she ever decided to run for office.
As a teacher, principal, and superintendent, she stood up every day for students, parents, and educators, working to ensure every child had access to a quality education and every school had the support it needed to succeed.
She didn’t just manage systems, she fought for the people inside them.
Throughout her career, Carroll has been a consistent voice for fairness, accountability, and opportunity.
She has advocated for stronger public schools, better resources for teachers, and policies that put students first.
In retirement, her advocacy didn’t stop; she continues to volunteer in her community and works to help elect leaders who will stand up for everyday Oklahomans.
Now, Carroll is stepping forward to take that same advocacy to the State Capitol.
She’s running to be a voice for working families, public schools, and local communities—making sure that government works for the people, not special interests.
"Moore is my home, and I want to provide a strong voice for everyone in my district.
Let's build a stronger education system, tackle the affordability crisis, and make it easier for everyday Oklahomans to thrive."
