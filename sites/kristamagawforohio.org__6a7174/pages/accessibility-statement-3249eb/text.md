As a political campaign website, we are committed to ensuring accessibility for all visitors, including those with disabilities.
We are continuously working to enhance the accessibility and usability of our website, and strive to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1.
If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our site, please let us know so we can address the issue.
Web Accessibility Standards
Our goal is to make the Political Campaign - Krista Magaw website accessible to everyone, regardless of disabilities.
We have implemented the necessary adjustments to meet WCAG 2.1 guidelines, ensuring a seamless browsing experience for all visitors.
Our site is compatible with assistive technologies such as screen readers and keyboard navigation.
Ensuring Inclusive Browsing
We have taken proactive measures to ensure that visitors with disabilities can navigate our site with ease and enjoy the same level of access as any other visitor.
By adhering to WCAG 2.1 guidelines, we aim to provide an inclusive and accessible online environment.
Accessibility Features
Our website has been tailored to meet the requirements of WCAG 2.1 at the AA level, ensuring compatibility with assistive technologies.
Here are some of the key features we have implemented to enhance accessibility:
- - Utilized semantic HTML for clear page structure
- Provided descriptive alt text for all images
- Ensured color contrast for readability
- Limited the use of animations and flashing content
- Included transcripts and captions for multimedia content
Ensuring Accessibility Compliance
In some cases, certain pages on our site may contain third-party content that could impact accessibility.
We are dedicated to addressing these issues and ensuring the accessibility of all content across our website.
Commitment to Accessibility
Our commitment to accessibility extends beyond our digital presence.
We have implemented accessibility arrangements in our campaign offices and event venues to ensure that all individuals, including those with disabilities, can fully engage with our activities and events.
