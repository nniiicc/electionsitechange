Main Menu Home About Lisa Blog FAQs Sign Up for Emails Donate Login Register Home About Me About Me My name is Lisa Emerson and I am running for walking.
That means that I would like to represent District 126 in Austin to advocate for a more walkable district.
I would not make this effort if I felt that the interests of myself and my neighbors were being addressed.
Our Texas leaders are serving national agendas. and I feel I must do something.
I am running to offer an alternative.
Instead of electing a democrat or a republican that will use the voice of District 126 to advocate for far flung benefit, support me, Lisa Emerson, and independent for House District 126.
Campaign Blog Campaign Focus: Walkable District Our district has transformed from rural to suburban.
Population density is increasing.
My goal is to lead cooperation among the many different commercial, public, and private entities in our district to promote walkable connections between where we live, where we study, where we shop, and where we recreate.
Walkable communities have higher property values and healthier citizens.
Lets focus on things we can agree on, and get them done.
