I am motivated by the culture wars.
I want the conversation to change so I am running to offer an alternative.
I find the current topics of legislation at the state level to be are focused on a national agenda.
The political parties are driving conversations that don’t life much better on Champion Forest or Cypresswood.
Our leaders act in ways that would be intolerable from a High School Principal.
It is time for something different.
If we want something different, we must do something differently.
The What Let us turn our focus to something different.
Texas House district 126 is going through rapid population growth and also faces aging neighborhoods.
I think it is time to focus on something we can agree on, and I propose that a walkable district is a goal that serves us today and in the future.
I am running for walking!
