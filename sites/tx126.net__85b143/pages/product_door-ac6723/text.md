The price here reflects the time and materials for delivering 100 door hangers.
In our suburban district it takes about 2 hours to put out 100 hangers.
The minimum wage the campaign is willing to pay is $30/hour.
So, your $75 donation will fund 100 fliers at $0.10/each and two hours of distribution time and $5 of overhead (this website).
