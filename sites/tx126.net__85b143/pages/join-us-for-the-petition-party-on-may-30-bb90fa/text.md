Main Menu Home About Lisa Blog FAQs Sign Up for Emails Donate Login Register Post detail Home Outreach Ideas Single By: Brendan Toch Join Us for the Petition Party on May 30 May 29, 2026 Outreach Ideas , Petition It is easy to look at the political gridlock in Austin and feel like our voices don’t carry past our own driveways.
But right here in District 126, we have a rare opportunity to change the conversation entirely.
To give our community a real choice on the ballot this November, we need to gather 500 signatures from registered voters who didn’t participate in the primary.
It’s a hurdle, absolutely—but it’s one we can clear easily if we work together.
That is why we are hosting a Petition Party , and we want you there.
The Details The Goal: Bring neighbors together, share our vision for public schools and community infrastructure, and get those critical petition sheets signed.
What to Bring: Just yourself, a neighbor or two, and a commitment to keeping our district moving forward.
We’ll handle the clipboards and pens.
Why your signature matters: Signing this petition isn’t a vote for a candidate—it is a vote for choice .
It simply says that the people of Cypresswood, Champion Forest, and every neighborhood across District 126 deserve an independent alternative on the ballot.
RSVP Today To help us plan for space and supplies, please take 30 seconds to let us know you’re coming.
Click the link below to head to our official sign-up form: 👉 RSVP for the Petition Party Here Let’s prove that real change doesn’t start with big political machines—it starts in our own backyards.
See you there!
