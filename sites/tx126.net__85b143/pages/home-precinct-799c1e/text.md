About the Election Process for Independents As you read along the blog you will note that the strategy is to have a representative for every precinct.
It is only natural that I am the representative for my home precent.
And I am.
This post will be my journey to find 20 people in the precinct to sign my petition.
So far I have put out a 100 door hangers .
Update that (300 as of Feb 23, and aiming to do 150 more today) Results from First 100 A person I knew emailed me and was encouraging.
This has followed up into me trying to describe how to be a volunteer .
My daughter heard from a friend at school that they had gotten a door hanger.
I am imagining a conversation with a third neighbor.
No Strangers.
So I need to keep trying to engage.
I have not yet figured out a process that will be reproducible across the other 40 precincts.
What will it take to compel a stranger to action?
