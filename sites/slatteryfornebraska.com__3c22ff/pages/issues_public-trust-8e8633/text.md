Between tossing out valid ballot initiative petition signatures and releasing our sensitive, private voter data to the federal government, the current office holder has broken faith with the people of this state.

The citizens of Nebraska deserve a whole lot more respect than they have been given lately.
What Erodes Trust?
Trust isn't lost overnight, it’s worn away by specific failures in leadership: Corruption, lack of transparency, and perceived unfairness in how everyday state business is handled.
Misleading information and a flat-out failure to be responsive to public needs.
A growing perception that officials are looking out for their own political agendas rather than acting in the public's best interest.
My opponent wants to respond to these failures by implementing a partisan playbook that treats our local institutions like a national political battlefield.
I want to protect our elections, and protect your vote.
When Nebraskans see a problem, we don't look to Washington for a script.
We build a practical, independent solution.
As your Secretary of State, I will work hard every single day to cut the political drama, focus on common-sense efficiency, and bring real trust and transparency back to the office.
