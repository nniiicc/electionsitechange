Nicole Hasso For Iowa House
FIGHTING FOR IOWA FAMILIES
Making life more affordable so Iowa families can grow.
Nicole Hasso is not a career politician.
She’s a hardworking Iowan and a trusted financial advisor who has dedicated her career to helping Iowa families, small businesses, and family farms secure their financial futures.
Growing up in poverty, Nicole experienced firsthand the challenges some Iowa families face.
For decades, she has sat at kitchen tables with working families, helping them make ends meet and plan for a more secure future.
Now, she’s running for Iowa House District 43 to put her financial experience to work for Iowa families and help create greater opportunities, lower costs, and a stronger economy.
