“I believe every Iowa family deserves the opportunity to succeed.
By lowering costs, creating economic opportunity, supporting strong schools, protecting our communities, and safeguarding our Iowa way of life, we can build an even stronger future for the next generation.” – Nicole Hasso LOWER TAXES & IMPROVE AFFORDABILITY Provide property tax relief and lower taxes so Iowa families can keep more of what they earn, save for the future, and invest in their communities.
BUILD THE BEST EDUCATION SYSTEM IN THE NATION Support strong schools, improve student achievement, and ensure accountability for results.
Make our public schools so exceptional that families choose them with confidence.
CHAMPION IOWA BUSINESSES Promote economic growth by supporting Iowa-based companies that strengthen our communities and create jobs.
GROW IOWA'S WORKFORCE Attract and retain top employers in technology, financial services, agriculture, and other growing industries.
KEEP YOUNG PEOPLE IN IOWA Expand opportunity, improve affordability, and maintain a high quality of life so the next generation chooses to build their future here.
SUPPORT LAW ENFORCEMENT & FIRST RESPONDERS Ensure our communities remain safe by backing those who protect and serve.
PROTECT IOWA'S WATER Work alongside farmers to promote proven farming practices that protect Iowa’s rivers, lakes, and drinking water.
FIGHT IOWA'S CANCER CRISIS Support research, early detection, treatment access, and environmental improvements that help save lives.
PROTECT THE MOST VULNERABLE We have a responsibility to protect those who cannot protect themselves, from the youngest to the oldest, and to uphold the dignity and value of every human life.
DEFEND PRIVATE PROPERTY RIGHTS Protect Iowans from having their land taken for the benefit of private corporations.
