Privacy Policy – Terms & Conditions faq Volunteer FAQs I’ve never volunteered on a campaign before.
Can I still help?
Yes.
Most of our volunteers are everyday Iowans who are new to campaigns.
We’ll give you simple training and make sure you feel comfortable before you get started.
How much time do I need to commit?
You can volunteer as much or as little as your schedule allows.
Some people help a couple of hours on weekends, while others serve regularly each week.
Just let us know what works for you.
What kind of volunteer work is most needed right now?
Our needs change throughout the campaign, but we always need help contacting voters, placing yard signs, and assisting at events.
When you sign up, we’ll match you with the areas of greatest need.
Can I volunteer from home?
Yes.
We have options like phone calling, texting voters, data entry, and sharing campaign messages online that can all be done from home.
Is there an age requirement to volunteer?
Volunteers of most ages are welcome.
Some activities—like door‑knocking—may require volunteers to be 18 or older, but younger students can often help at events with a parent or guardian.
