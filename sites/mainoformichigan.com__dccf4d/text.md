Meet Jan Maino
Jan has lived in Jackson for over 50 years.
For her entire career, Jan has served individuals and families in her community— both directly and through leadership, school board service, and volunteer work.
As a licensed social worker, nonprofit executive, and community volunteer, she has spent decades working alongside families, educators, healthcare providers, and service organizations to strengthen systems and expand opportunity.
Priorities
Jan is running to make life work better for families in Jackson.
While Republicans in Lansing focus on politics and distractions, Jan is focused on results: lowering everyday costs for individuals, families and seniors alike; strengthening public schools, access to mental health care; and fighting for good-paying jobs people can raise a family on.

House District 46
The 46th district is split between Jackson and Washtenaw Counties.
In Jackson County, it includes the city of Jackson, Blackman Township, Leoni Township, and parts of Grass Lake and Summit Township.
In Washtenaw County, it includes the city of Chelsea and Sylvan Township.
