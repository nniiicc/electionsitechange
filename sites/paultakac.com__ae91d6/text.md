In His First Two Terms
Paul has worked tirelessly to deliver real results for every community in the 82nd District, including:
Over $42 million in total grants for roads and bike paths, ambulances and firetrucks, sewers and water mains, affordable housing, stream restoration, and much more
Historic investments in K-12 public education
Helped make college and career development more accessible and affordable
Expanded tax credits for seniors and working families
Established a state child & dependent care tax credit
Championed renewal of the Rural Jobs Investment Tax Credit to encourage job development in rural areas
Spearheaded legislation making it easier for veterans to access resources
Created the Agricultural Innovation Fund to help farmers adopt new technologies to improve farming practices and profitability while protecting the environment
Appointed to the State Board of Higher Education
Elected Chair of the Rural Population Revitalization Commission
Served on more than half a dozen House Committees including Education; Communications & Technology; Environmental Resources & Protection; Agriculture and Rural Affairs; and Veterans Affairs & Emergency Preparedness
What's Next
Paul will continue to work every day to:
Fully and fairly fund public education at all levels
Invest in career and workforce development
Reduce taxes and the cost of living for working families and seniors
Expand access to affordable, high-quality healthcare
Ensure data centers pay their own way instead of forcing ratepayers to foot the bill
Protect Pennsylvanians from corporate greed
Protect our environment and transition to a domestic, renewable energy economy
Defend our fundamental rights and freedoms
Promote small businesses and economic development in every community
Paul believes in putting people above politics.
He has a proven track record of working across the aisle with anyone willing to put in the work and to help make life better for all his constituents.
