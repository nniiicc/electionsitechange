Your Vote Matters
The right to vote is the most fundamental element of democracy.
In Pennsylvania, you must register to vote at least two weeks *before* you can cast your ballot.
This year's Voter Registration deadline is October 19th.
Keep reading to learn how you can get ready to cast your ballot on November 3rd and make your voice heard.
Important Dates
October 19th: Last Day to Register to Vote
October 27th: Last Day to Request a Mail-in Ballot
November 3rd: Election Day
