Term Limits
I have no aspirations to start a new career.
My motivation for continuing to be your representative to the House of Delegates is to serve my community, something I have done for over half a century through volunteering such as in Scouting and something I did for our country for over 20 years in uniform.
As I have committed elsewhere, as your Delegate I will sponsor term limit legislation.
