Business-Friendly Policies
Harry Sawicki champions comprehensive efforts to reduce taxes and regulatory obstacles facing District 2 businesses.
By fostering a business-friendly climate, he promotes job creation, economic development, and sustainable growth that benefits families and communities across Michigan.
Improving Education
By advocating for increased education funding and progressive policies, Harry Sawicki aims to elevate student achievement and create equitable opportunities for every child in Michigan Senate District 2.
His vision includes innovative programs and community partnerships that strengthen education outcomes.
Healthcare Reform
Harry Sawicki is dedicated to advancing healthcare reforms that target fraudulent practices and reduce the financial burden on patients.
His approach combines regulatory oversight, transparency, and support for effective healthcare programs to ensure better health outcomes for Michigan residents.
