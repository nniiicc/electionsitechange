Hi, I'm Phil Rubin–

From representing North Carolina and the United States in our courtrooms to standing up for you on the House floor, I’ve spent my life fighting for people who can’t afford to lose.
I came to serve in the House after being appointed to represent District 40 following the passing of Representative Joe John, a dedicated public servant and lifelong champion for justice.
I carry his legacy with deep respect and as a constant reminder of what honorable public service should look like.
My Background
I’ve built a career standing up for people who can’t afford to lose.
- Took on gun violence, polluters, and child abuse as a federal and state prosecutor
- Protected voting rights on President Obama’s campaign
- Pressured Big Tech to keep kids safe online
- Helped enforce public health protections during the COVID pandemic
My Principles
This is how I approach public service:
- Know what you’re solving for.
Clear goals lead to better outcomes.
- Do the homework.
Listen to experts.
Be willing to change your mind.
- Separate the policy from the person—disagree with respect, then shake hands.
- Be so good they can’t stop you.
Skill and preparation are how we move North Carolina forward.
My Priorities
A better North Carolina is within reach—if we make government work for the people again.
- Fully fund public schools, raise teacher pay, and modernize our classrooms
- Pass commonsense gun safety laws so parents worry about homework, not hiding places
- Build an economy where families can thrive—with fair wages, affordable housing, and good jobs
- End gerrymandering and restore real accountability to voters
