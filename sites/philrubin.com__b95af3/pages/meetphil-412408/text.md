Meet Phil Rubin I’m Phil Rubin—North Carolina Representative, former federal prosecutor and online safety expert, and proud girl dad.
From representing North Carolina and the United States in our courtrooms to standing up for you on the House floor, I’ve spent my life fighting for people who can’t afford to lose. ​ I came to serve in the House after being appointed to represent District 40 following the passing of Representative Joe John, a dedicated public servant and lifelong champion for justice.
I carry his legacy with deep respect and as a constant reminder of what honorable public service should look like.
