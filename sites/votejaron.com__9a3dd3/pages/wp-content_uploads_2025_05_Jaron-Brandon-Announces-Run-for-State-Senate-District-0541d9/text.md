FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Tuolumne County, CA 3/28/2025 JARON BRANDON, TUOLUMNE COUNTY SUPERVISOR, ANNOUNCES STATE SENATE DISTRICT 4 CAMPAIGN Tuolumne County Supervisor Jaron Brandon has officially launched his campaign for California’s 4th State Senate District, pledging to bring bold, rural-focused leadership to Sacramento.
A lifelong Gold Country resident, Jaron is running to ensure that the voices of rural Californians are no longer left behind.
“The truth is, no one is coming to save us but ourselves,” said Mr.
Brandon.
“For too long, the Valley and the mountains have been overlooked, left out to dry, and forced to deal with politics that are divisive and out of touch with our way of life.
It’s time for leadership that listens, leads, and delivers real results on the issues that matter most—housing, infrastructure, healthcare, and jobs.” Brandon’s philosophy is simple: work hard, be honest, and get stuff done.
Over the past ﬁve years on the Board of Supervisors, he’s earned a reputation as a results-driven leader—ﬁxing potholes, managing forests, streamlining housing, soliciting business development, ﬁghting fentanyl, and leading the strongest public safety record in county history.
His forward-thinking approach combines boots-on-the-ground pragmatism with digital innovation and coalition-building.
“I’ve seen ﬁrsthand how bold leadership can make a real difference,” Jaron added.
“Our challenges may be daunting, but we’ve proven time and again that we can tackle them when we stand together.
I’m stepping up because it’s our responsibility to build a future where our kids can come home and thrive.” With over a decade of experience in public service and community organizing, Jaron is committed to real solutions—not slogans.
He pledges to bring common sense, collaboration, and local perspective back into state governance.
“The best government is local,” Jaron concluded.
“And with your support, I’ll make sure Sacramento hears us—and delivers for District 4.” The election for California State Senate District 4 will take place on June 02, 2026.
For more information about Jaron’s campaign, visit votejaron.com.
