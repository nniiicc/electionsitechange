Join the movement Economy & Affordability Too many politicians in Washington talk about “affordability” while continuing to pass big spending bills that make costs worse for everyday Americans.
I’m tired of that.
In Congress, I will oppose massive spending packages and bureaucratic expansions that fuel inflation and drive up the cost of living.
I support President Trump’s efforts to cut taxes for working families, eliminate burdensome taxes, and unleash American energy — because when energy is affordable, everything else becomes more affordable too: groceries, transportation, housing, and utilities.
I believe in an economy built on hard work, small businesses, and opportunity — not government micromanagement.
That means cutting red tape for job creators, expanding access to capital for entrepreneurs, standing up for workers, and restoring American energy independence — not outsourcing our energy needs to foreign countries while families here at home struggle to pay their bills.
My priority in Washington will be to support sound fiscal policy that frees working families from unnecessary taxes, promotes growth, and protects affordability and opportunity for all Americans.
Healthcare Healthcare should be affordable, accessible, and patient-centered — not controlled by Washington bureaucrats.
As a cancer survivor and a mom, I know how personal healthcare decisions are, and how stressful it is when costs are out of control.
I support lowering healthcare costs and expanding access by increasing competition, transparency, and choice.
Patients should know the price of care upfront, be able to shop for services, and keep more control over their healthcare decisions — not lose it to government mandates.
I support protecting people with pre-existing conditions, while fixing what’s broken in our system instead of doubling down on bureaucracy.
That means strengthening Health Savings Accounts, allowing insurance to be sold across state lines, and reducing regulations that drive up premiums for families and small businesses.
For federal healthcare programs like Medicare, we must keep our promises to seniors — period.
Any reforms should focus on eliminating waste, fraud, and abuse so these programs are strong and sustainable for the people who depend on them.
Public Safety & Crime As a local Councilwoman, I work closely with our police officers and first responders, and I’ve seen firsthand how essential they are to keeping neighborhoods safe and communities strong.
At the federal level, I support fully funding law enforcement, and I strongly oppose any effort to defund or weaken the police.
We should be giving officers the resources, training, and backing they need to do their jobs safely and effectively.

Immigration I believe in a secure border, legal immigration, and respect for the rule of law.
America is a nation of immigrants — but also a nation of laws, and both must be honored.
I support President Trump’s border security policies that put safety first.
I believe that Border Patrol and ICE should be equipped with the tools they need to arrest and deport violent criminals.
Anyone who commits serious crimes while here unlawfully should be removed.
Public safety must always come first.
The recent chaos at the border hurts everyone — especially working families and legal immigrants who follow the rules.
I come from an immigrant family, and I’ve seen the sacrifices people like my own parents made to do things the right way.
That’s why I believe our system must be fair to those who follow the law.
Education As a mom of 11, education isn’t abstract to me — it’s personal.
I want every parent to have the freedom to choose the best education for their child, not be forced into a one-size-fits-all system run by Washington.
At the federal level, I support school choice, including charter schools, education savings accounts, and policies that let funding follow the student — not the bureaucracy.
Parents know their children best, and they deserve real options.
I also believe the federal government’s role should be limited and focused.
Instead of micromanaging classrooms, Washington should support states and local communities through flexible funding, cut waste, and stop pushing political agendas that distract from reading, math, science, and critical thinking.
On workforce readiness, we need to do a much better job valuing career and technical education, apprenticeships, and skills-based training.
College isn’t the only path to success.
I support expanding access to job training, partnerships with local employers, and making sure students graduate with skills that actually lead to good-paying jobs.
Education should empower families, prepare students for real life, and strengthen our country — not grow federal control.
That’s the approach I’ll bring to Congress.
Abortion I am proudly pro-life and believe abortion should be rare and limited, with exceptions in cases of rape, incest, or when the life of the mother is at risk.
This issue is deeply personal to me.
I became a single mother at the age of 18, when I had my first daughter Kennedy, and today I am a wife and mom of eleven smart and beautiful children, including two adopted from the foster care system.
I will always stand up to the extreme abortion-on-demand culture pushed onto women by today’s Democrat Party.
My goal is to work toward a society where abortion isn’t even a thought — where women and families are supported, protected, and given real opportunities to thrive.
That means policies that help people feel emotionally and financially secure enough to start and raise a family.
