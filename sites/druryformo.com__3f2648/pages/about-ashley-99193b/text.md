Events Sample Ballot - Nov 3 DONATE Meet Ashley Drury As your Jefferson County neighbor and a working parent, I see the daily struggles our families face.
I’m running for District 113 to be your voice in Jefferson City—fighting for living wages, fully funded public schools, and a future where healthcare is a human right, so every local family truly thrives.
Core Values and Priorities for Our Community Workers' Rights & Living Wages: Protecting labor unions and ensuring every worker earns a wage that supports their family.
Fully Funded Public Schools: Advocating for every Missouri student to have access to high-quality, fully funded education.
Healthcare as a Human Right: Defending reproductive freedom, expanding Medicaid access, and lowering prescription costs.
Community Safety: Promoting sensible gun safety reforms and mental health support to keep our neighborhoods secure.
Transparency & Equal Rights: Ending partisan gerrymandering and protecting the equal rights of every resident, regardless of background.
Ashley’s commitment is simple: your voice in Jefferson City.
She believes in a government that works for everyone—one that is honest, ethical, and focused on the future of Jefferson County families.
Together, we can build a Missouri where fairness and respect are the foundations of our progress.
JOIN THE CAMPAIGN Experience & Background Ashley is a sixth-generation Missourian who was raised in North County and has called Jefferson County home since 2018.
Ashley graduated with her MS in HR Management from Lindenwood University in 2015.
She and her husband, a proud Navy veteran, have two teenage boys, ages 14 and 13.
Ashley works as a substitute teacher and mental health trainer, and has also worked in HR, retail, hospitality, nonprofit, and the automotive industry.
Ashley is passionate about mental health and human rights, and volunteers as much as possible. © # Ashley Drury for Missouri House Representative.
