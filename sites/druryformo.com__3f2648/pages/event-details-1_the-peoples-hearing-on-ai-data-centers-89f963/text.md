About the event
From Nodaway to Festus to Joplin, we all deserve the freedom to live in healthy communities, make a good living, and have a say-so in the decisions that affect our lives.
But certain state lawmakers are doing the bidding of tech billionaires and developers who want to rake in record profits by running roughshod over our communities, to build dirty data centers that make their CEOs richer at the expense of our jobs, our water and energy, and our way of life.
So, we're taking our message directly to the Capitol.
Join MRCC and the No MO Dirty Data Centers Coalition to pack the Capitol Rotunda in Jeff City and make our voices heard.
