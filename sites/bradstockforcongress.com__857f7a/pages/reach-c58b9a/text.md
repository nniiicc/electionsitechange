Nothing you do in Reach makes anyone's information more public than it already is.
Registration status, party, and district come straight from Pennsylvania's public voter file.
When you log a conversation, it lands in the campaign's voter database exactly as if a volunteer had knocked on that door.
In practice that mostly helps your friends: once we know someone is with Alan, we stop spending mailers, texts, and door knocks on them.
Reach's own policy is at reach.vote/privacy.
Reach hands you the content and the wording, then sends through whatever app you pick: Messages, email, Snapchat, Slack, anything on your phone.
The person on the other end just gets a normal message from you, with no app required on their side.
Reach matches against the voter file, so searching "Bob" may miss a Robert.
