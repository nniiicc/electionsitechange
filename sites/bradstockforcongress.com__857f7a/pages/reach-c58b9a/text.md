01 Is the campaign collecting private information about my friends?
No.
Reach only shows public voter file data, and your answers follow the same path as a door knock.
Nothing you do in Reach makes anyone's information more public than it already is.
Registration status, party, and district come straight from Pennsylvania's public voter file.
When you log a conversation, it lands in the campaign's voter database exactly as if a volunteer had knocked on that door.
In practice that mostly helps your friends: once we know someone is with Alan, we stop spending mailers, texts, and door knocks on them.
Reach's own policy is at reach.vote/privacy .
02 Do the people I message need Reach too?
No.
Only the sender uses Reach.
Everything goes out through your normal text, email, or social apps.
Reach hands you the content and the wording, then sends through whatever app you pick: Messages, email, Snapchat, Slack, anything on your phone.
The person on the other end just gets a normal message from you, with no app required on their side.
03 Reach cannot find someone I know is a voter Try the full legal name.
Nicknames block the match.
Reach matches against the voter file, so searching "Bob" may miss a Robert.
Use the full legal name, and add a middle name or part of the address if the list is long.
A first name plus part of a last name is usually enough.
04 Should I import my whole contact list?
Only if you want to send one message to many people at once.
For everything else, you can skip it.
Contact import exists for bulk sends.
For day-to-day use, it is easier to search a person by name, or share content from the Library and pick anyone in your phone individually.
If you do import, matching only works when the contact's name in your phone matches their real name, and you control which contacts get searched and whether their phone numbers and emails are shared with the campaign.
05 Can I use Reach on a computer instead of my phone?
Yes.
Sign in at app.reach.vote and everything syncs with the same account.
Use the browser version at app.reach.vote on a laptop, the app on your phone, or both.
As long as you sign in with the same account, your network and your logged conversations stay in sync across devices.
06 What if I lose service while I am out?
Reach works offline and syncs your work once you reconnect.
The phone app keeps working without a signal, which matters in parts of our district where coverage drops.
Log conversations as usual and they sync when you are back online.
07 I used Reach on another campaign already You can pull your existing relationships over instead of starting from scratch.
Reach can look through your networks from other campaigns and bring over the people who are registered in this district.
Choose the option to import from other campaigns on the Network screen, and it will tell you how many it found before you confirm.
08 I am stuck and need help Reach keeps a full knowledge base, and you can always email the campaign directly.
Reach documents every screen at reach.vote/knowledge-base .
