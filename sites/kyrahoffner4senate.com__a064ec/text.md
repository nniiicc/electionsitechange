Senator Kyra Hoffner believes in the people of Delaware’s 14th District—and the people believe in her.

Senate Bill 214
Senator Hoffner backed a law that ensures biological evidence is preserved in criminal cases, safeguarding access to DNA testing and helping prevent wrongful convictions while strengthening accountability in the justice system.
Senate Bill 211
This Act increases the size of the Governor’s Council on Agriculture from 7 to 9 members.
The Act also requires that 1 member on the Council must be a livestock producer, and 1 member must be a farmer that is 40 years old or younger.
