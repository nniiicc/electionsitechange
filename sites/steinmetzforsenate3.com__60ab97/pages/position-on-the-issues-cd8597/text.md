Position on the Issues:
Disciplined spending is our obligation to our future and that of our children and grandchildren.
I will work to restore funding control to local governments.

Responsible regulation places limits on government not citizens, businesses and private property.
Government must be simple, fair and responsive to citizens.
I will work with other legislators and agencies to encourage responsible spending.
We must ensure productive employees are fairly compensated and reward agencies that limit wasteful spending.
I will work to review and repeal existing laws that over-regulate citizens, burden employees and inflate bureaucracy.
I will work to restore local control of education to parents, teachers and school boards.
I will apply foundational law and principles in evaluating current law and pending legislation to ensure that individual freedom and free enterprises flourish.
