FOR RELEASE Immediately CONTACT Senator Cheri Steinmetz (307)534-5342 Representative Allen Slagle (307)306-7384 Representative Scott Smith (307)575-3742 LEGISLATURE CONTINUES WORK CHEYENNE – On Monday, Jan.
16, the State Senate adopted its permanent rules for the 67th Wyoming legislature and immediately began work with the bills coming out of committee.
The Senate Agriculture, State and Public Lands and Water Resources Committee has been busy.
“SF0028 Livestock Infectious Disease Control Tribal Inclusion provides a framework for the State of Wyoming to work with the tribal governments on diseases, specifically brucellosis, in livestock,” Senator Cheri Steinmetz said.
“This is an important bill for the retention of Wyoming brucellosis free status and producers across the state.” “Additionally, SF0068 Prescriptive Easement for Water Conveyances is another bill dealing with subdivisions for the most part, and ditches, pipelines, and those things coming across one property to get to another and making sure that water-right holder has the ability to get their water and maintain the conveyance that supplies it,” Senator Steinmetz said.
SF0096 Omnibus Water Bill Planning Administration is an annual bill appropriated from the state’s water development accounts.
“The most significant portion of this bill this year is taking the board of control off the water development budget and funding them out of the traditional source, which has been the state engineer’s office,” Senator Steinmetz said.
“This will free up another $7 million per year ($14 million biennially) to be used on projects for our municipalities and irrigation districts across the state.” SF0082 Eligible Water and Sewer Projects Funding is a continuation of the America Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) direct money with leftover funds being available.
This would appropriate another $39.3 million for local water and sewer projects available for local governments who already applied but were not awarded funds.
