STANDING UP
FOR MAINERS
MEET MATT
Matt Dunlap is running for Maine's 2nd Congressional District because we deserve a representative who will help lower prices, take on the billionaire class, and fight for working families.
Matt was born and raised on a farm near Bar Harbor, and worked as a dishwasher, bartender, and fur-trapper before his time in government service.
As State Auditor, he works to improve the efficiency of state government, eliminate waste, and prevent fraud.
Through his audits he has saved taxpayers real money, because your government should work for you.
In Congress, Matt will fight to make billionaires and big corporations pay their fair share, strengthen Social Security, and make health care more affordable.
He’ll be laser focused on ending the foreign wars and tariffs that have driven up the cost of gas and groceries.
He'll fight for universal childcare and affordable housing to help working families get by.
He wants to reverse the Medicaid cuts that are devastating our rural hospitals, and stand up to Big Pharma to make health care more affordable.
Matt is running to take on the powerful interests that have rigged our economy against working class Mainers — because Matt is one of us and it's time Washington stopped working for special interests.
Matt is a founding board member of the Maine Youth Fish and Game Association, the former executive director of the Sportsman's Alliance of Maine, and assistant to the treasurer of his local Episcopal Church.
He and his wife, Michelle Dunphy, live in Old Town and have one daughter.
He is a proud graduate of the University of Maine.
