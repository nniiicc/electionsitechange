ITHACA, N.Y. — Today, Wednesday, October 23 marks 13 days until Election Day.
It’s also the same number of times that Marc Molinaro has voted against abortion rights in just one term in Congress.
“Unlike Marc Molinaro, Josh Riley is crystal clear that women’s health care decisions are women’s healthcare decisions,” said Riley Campaign Manager Daniel Fleiss.
“13 days from today Upstate New Yorkers will send Marc Molinaro a message and vote Josh Riley into Congress.”
