August 27, 2024 RILEY RELEASES NEW TELEVISION AD AND WEBSITE HIGHLIGHTING MOLINARO’S ANTI-ABORTION RECORD “The fact is, Molinaro has voted against abortion rights 13 times.” ITHACA, N.Y. — Today, Democratic congressional candidate Josh Riley released a new ad highlighting Marc Molinaro’s record as an anti-abortion extremist. ‘Facts,’ which is now running on broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital district-wide, lists 13 votes Molinaro has taken to restrict reproductive freedom for women in New York, and it encourages voters to visit molinarofacts.com to find out the truth about Molinaro’s dangerous record.
“Molinaro can say whatever he wants in his campaign ads, but his record speaks for itself.
He’s a 30-year career politician who has always opposed abortion rights.
He voted 13 times in Congress to restrict abortion access, and he’s on tape supporting an abortion ban–even here in New York,” said Josh Riley.
“I’m pro-choice, he’s anti-choice, and it’s really that simple.” The ad includes unearthed audio of Molinaro admitting that he opposes the 50-year precedent set in Roe v.
Wade.
His blunt admission underscores a career championing causes and legislation that restrict reproductive health and freedom for New York women.
