Dear Neighbor,
It is an honor and a privilege to advocate for you as a member of the State House.
I am running for re-election in 2026 to continue strengthening our community, and I am humbly asking for your vote.
I have been a grassroots leader for decades, first as a local activist and now as your State Representative.
And throughout it all, I have never tired of working to make our community a better place.
During my time in office, I have:
- Brought back millions in funding for local infrastructure
- Secured record investments in our schools
- Fought for dignity for seniors and workers
- Stood up for our values of mutual respect and tolerance.
My team and I have helped hundreds of constituents, answered thousands of constituent emails and calls, and held dozens of public events where anyone can share their thoughts with me.
But there is much more work to be done.
My platform is designed to make Michigan a safer, more affordable place for all, now and for future generations.
If re-elected, my top priorities will be:
- Reducing household costs to help you and your family afford the American Dream
- Investing in high-quality education from pre-k to college
- Fixing what Trump broke in our economy and our culture
- Restoring trust in government through greater transparency and accountability
Let’s keep making our community better, together!
