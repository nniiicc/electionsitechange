GOEBEN FOR ASSEMBLY About Donate Volunteer District Key Issues Contact Gallery ​Election Reform ​Our elections need to be safe and secure so that you know your vote counts.
We must continue to shine the light on election fraud and provide safeguards to prevent it. ​ ​Personal Freedom and the Role of Government I believe that smaller government is better government.
I will work to limit the size and scope of government so that it does not infringe on our rights as citizens.
Government needs to get out of the way and let the American entrepreneur create jobs and grow the economy. ​Taxes As a lifelong Wisconsin resident it grieves me that Wisconsin is on the list of the top ten highest taxed states.
As a legislator I would work to reduce the tax burden on families and businesses.
I would like to see Wisconsinites keeping their hard-earned money and deciding for themselves what they do with it.
It is your money, not the government's. ​Spending - Budget ​I believe the government should keep a balanced budget as a top priority.
A family or business who spends beyond their means will struggle a lot; this is the same with government.
2nd Amendment Rights I am a proud member of the NRA and support measures to protect out 2nd amendment rights.
While the people of Wisconsin use guns for hunting and self-defense, the purpose of the 2nd Amendment is to defend against a tyrannical government. ​Support Law Enforcement I stand with law enforcement who work hard to keep our families and communities safe.
Wisconsin Fraternal Order of Police has given me their endorsement. ​Education Parents know what is best for their children.
Joy supports school choice, curriculum transparency, protecting parent's rights through a Parent's Bill of Rights, and banning critical race theory from our classrooms.
Educational Advocates for Reform has given their endorsement. ​Sanctity of Life All life is precious.
The unborn, who are the most vulnerable among us, must be protected.
I am committed to work to protect those who cannot protect themselves.
Pro-Life Wisconsin has given me their endorsement.
