ACCESSIBILITY INFORMATION
Beginning with this election, voters with a disability may request an accessible remote ballot, a ballot which allows those who use screen readers and assistive devices to submit a mail-in ballot privately and independently.
Registered voters who wish to obtain an accessible remote ballot must apply for an absentee or mail-in ballot as outlined above, and then submit a request for an accessible remote ballot.
This can be done by filling out the online request form at https://expressforms.pa.gov/apps/pa/DOS/Remote-Access-Ballot-Request or the PDF application (https://www.votespa.com/Voting-in-PA/Documents/Accessible-Remote-Ballot-Request.pdf) which can then be printed and mailed to the county board of elections (see above) or emailed to ra-awib@pa.gov
