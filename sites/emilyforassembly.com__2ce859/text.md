What We've Accomplished
Building a Green Future
Overcame massive oil and gas industry opposition to enact a first-in-the-nation law tackling the state’s largest source of emissions with the All-Electric Buildings Act, while holding accountable polluters who abuse North Brooklyn’s environment.
Making McGuinness Blvd Safe
Worked for years to redesign the dangerous roads of McGuinness, adding bike and pedestrian accessibility and limiting crashes, finally securing the funding to complete the redesign after years of stalling from corporate interests.
Serving the People
Helped thousands of constituents access resources and services, hosted tenant clinics and community town halls, and secured investments for safer streets, open space and mass transit.
