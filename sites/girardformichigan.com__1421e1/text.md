Join the campaign MEET CHRIS GIRARD Chris Girard has spent his whole life in Bay City, raising his family, building his career, and serving the community he calls home.
He and his wife, Molly, have been married for more than 26 years.
Together they have fostered more than 47 children and adopted five daughters.
Chris began his career as a member of Teamsters Local 486 before joining Do-All Inc., the family business his grandparents helped start.
He spent 20 years there, including 14 as CEO, helping people with disabilities live and work more independently.
Chris went on to serve as a Bay City Commissioner and now serves as mayor.
He's running for State Representative because Bay County needs someone who knows how to get things done.
