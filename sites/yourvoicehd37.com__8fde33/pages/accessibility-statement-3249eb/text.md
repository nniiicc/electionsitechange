We at Working For Montana HB37 are dedicated to making our site accessible to all, including individuals with disabilities.
What web accessibility is
Web accessibility ensures that people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with the web, and that they can contribute to the web.
This encompasses all disabilities that affect access to the web, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.
