MEET DUANE GURULE
Duane Gurule is running for Colorado State Senate to bring a new generation of leadership—and innovative solutions—to rural Colorado.
Raised in Southeast Colorado, Duane understands the challenges rural communities face because he’s lived them.
From experiencing housing instability as a child to serving today as Mayor of Rocky Ford, his life has been defined by resilience, hard work, and a commitment to fighting for others.
Duane has built a track record of delivering results.
As Mayor, he’s worked to strengthen the local economy and move his community forward.
As a technology professional and nonprofit founder, he’s created opportunities in places often overlooked—proving that even with limited resources, real solutions are possible.
He believes rural Colorado needs more than talk—it needs leadership willing to fight for smarter, forward-thinking solutions on affordability, water, healthcare, and economic growth.
Duane isn’t just talking about change—he’s been doing the work.
Now, he’s ready to take that fight to the State Capitol and deliver real results for communities across Senate District 35.
