Privacy Policy
Effective Date: July 2026
Friends of Christine Maltese ("we," "our," or "the Campaign") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when you visit this website.
Information We Collect
When you visit our website, you may choose to provide personal information such as:
- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address (if voluntarily provided)
- Any information you include when contacting the campaign or signing up for updates
If you make a contribution, your payment information is processed securely through our third-party donation platform.
The campaign does not store your credit card or payment information.
How We Use Your Information
The information you provide may be used to:
- Respond to your questions or comments
- Send campaign updates and newsletters
- Notify you about volunteer opportunities and events
- Share information about the campaign
- Comply with applicable campaign finance and election laws
We will only use your information for campaign-related purposes.
Information Sharing
We respect your privacy.
The Friends of Christine Maltese campaign does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
We may share information when required by law or when necessary to comply with campaign finance reporting requirements.
Cookies and Website Analytics
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use the site.
These technologies help us improve the website but do not personally identify you unless you voluntarily provide personal information.
Third-Party Services
Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including:
- Donation platforms
- Social media platforms
- Email subscription services
These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing personal information.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide.
However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing electronic data can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Children's Privacy
This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the campaign evolves.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
