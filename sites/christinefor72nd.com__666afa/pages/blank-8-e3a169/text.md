On the Campaign Trail
Campaigns aren't just about speeches - they're about showing up, listening, and learning.
Every parade, community event, town hall, farm visit, and local gathering is an opportunity to hear directly from the people of Wisconsin's 72nd Assembly District.
This gallery shares the journey, the conversations, and the communities that continue to shape my campaign.
Thank you to everyone who has welcomed me, shared your ideas, and taken the time to talk with me along the way.
July 2026
July marked the beginning of many conversations across Wisconsin's 72nd Assembly District.
From parades and community gatherings to meetings with local organizations, every stop offered an opportunity to listen, learn, and better understand the priorities of the people I hope to serve.
🚜 Wood County Farm Bureau Picnic
📍 Marshfield, Wisconsin
What Happened
I attended the Wood County Farm Bureau Picnic alongside Gillian Battino, candidate for Wisconsin State Senate, and Andy Wuethrich, candidate for Wisconsin's 86th Assembly District.
It was a great opportunity to meet members of Wisconsin's agricultural community and hear about the issues that matter most to them.
Why It Mattered
Every conversation helps me better understand the opportunities and challenges facing rural Wisconsin.
Listening is the first step toward developing practical solutions that reflect the experiences of the people living and working in our communities.
🎆 Wa Du Shuda Days Parade
📍 New Lisbon, Wisconsin
What Happened
Participating in the Wa Du Shuda Days Parade was a wonderful opportunity to celebrate one of Juneau County's long-standing traditions.
It was great to see so many families lining the streets, reconnect with familiar faces, and meet new people throughout the day.
Why It Mattered
Community events like Wa Du Shuda Days remind us that strong communities are built through connection.
Whether it was a wave from the parade route or a conversation after the parade, every interaction reinforced the importance of being present, listening, and taking the time to get to know the people I hope to represent.
June 2026
June continued my campaign journey with opportunities to meet residents, attend community events, and build relationships throughout the 72nd Assembly District.
Every event provided another chance to listen, answer questions, and better understand the priorities of the communities I hope to serve.
🌽 Juneau County Democrats Meet & Greet
📍 Juneau County, Wisconsin
What Happened
I appreciated the opportunity to attend the Juneau County Democrats Meet & Greet and introduce myself to residents from across the area.
It was a wonderful opportunity to share why I'm running for the 72nd Assembly District and, more importantly, to begin building relationships through thoughtful conversations with community members.
Why It Mattered
Campaigns are built one conversation at a time.
Events like this provide an opportunity to listen to people's concerns, answer questions honestly, and begin earning trust by showing up and being present in the communities I hope to serve.
