Listening First
Every Community Has a Voice
The people of District 72 deserve a representative who listens before making decisions.
Too often, politics has become about talking instead of listening.
I believe the best ideas come from honest conversations with the people who live, work, raise families, own businesses, teach our children, and serve our communities every day.
No one person has all the answers.
Every community has unique challenges, and every resident deserves to be heard with respect.
My Priorities
As your representative, I will:
• Hold regular listening sessions throughout District 72.
• Meet with local governments, educators, farmers, business owners, healthcare providers, first responders, and community organizations before supporting major legislation.
• Be accessible and responsive to constituents, even when we disagree.
• Base decisions on facts, thoughtful discussion, and the real experiences of the people I represent—not political pressure.
• Communicate openly about the decisions I make and the reasons behind them.
Building Trust
Good government starts with trust.
That trust is earned by showing up, asking questions, and treating every person with dignity and respect.
Whether someone agrees with me or not, they deserve to know their voice matters.
Listening is not a campaign slogan.
It is how I intend to serve.
My Commitment
If elected, I will work every day to represent all the people of District 72.
I will continue learning, asking questions, and listening with an open mind because the best public policy is built together—not dictated from Madison.
