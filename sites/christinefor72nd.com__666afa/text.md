❤️ Running for Wisconsin's 72nd Assembly District
Like many people, my career didn't follow a straight line.
I began with a dream of becoming a pastry chef.
I loved creating something that brought people together, and I believed that's where my future would be.
I was told "no".
Early in my career, I was told that moving from the kitchen into hotel operations wasn't realistic.
For many people, that might have been the end of the conversation.
For me.......
it was just the beginning.
Curiosity changed everything.
That determination led me to become the General Manager of a hotel.
Then another question kept following me everywhere I worked:
How can we make this work better?
Technology opened a new world.
I joined a hospitality technology company and spent years helping hotels open around the world.
Whether I was working in Chicago, Spain, Austria, or throughout the Caribbean, I learned something that has stayed with me throughout my career:
No matter where in the world I traveled, people want the same things.
To be heard.
To be respected.
To have the opportunity to succeed.
That's the lesson I want to bring to Madison.
Good leadership begins by listening.
I'm not running because I've spent my life in politics.
I'm running because I've spent my life solving problems.
Listening.
Learning.
Working with people.
Finding better answers.
That's exactly how I'll serve District 72.
