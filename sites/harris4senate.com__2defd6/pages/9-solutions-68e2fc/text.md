Fix What’s Broken With What We Already Have 9 PROGRAMS.
THOUSANDS OF JOBS.
ZERO NEW TAXES.
Ocean Cleanup Fleet Initiative Utilizing Portsmouth Naval Shipyard to deploy a fleet that restores ocean health, bolstering environmental and economic benefits.
Mall Housing and Manufacturing Revival Transforming vacant malls into vibrant housing and manufacturing centers to stimulate job growth and economic renewal.
Space Infrastructure Development Building aerospace infrastructure that empowers New Hampshire’s growing space industry and strengthens regional innovation.
Skilled Trades Restoration Dedicated to expanding skilled trades opportunities in New Hampshire by investing in training, apprenticeships, and local partnerships.
East-West Superhighway Creating an east-to-west superhighway to enhance infrastructure, improve connectivity, and drive economic growth across New Hampshire.
GI Bill Restoration Restoring the GI Bill to honor veterans by ensuring they receive the full benefits they deserve for their service and sacrifice.
This commitment supports their education, career development, and successful transition to civilian life.
Farm Subsidies Restoration Engaging farmers to support future generations through junior high school gardens and funding agricultural initiatives, fostering environmental stewardship and sustainable food systems.
Empty Federal Buildings Repurposing empty federal buildings to create affordable housing for New Hampshire residents, promoting community stability and economic development.
BioChar / Sacred Soil Transforming waste into nutrient-rich soil and carbon credits to generate funding for property-poor schools, creating 200-400 new jobs across the region and boosting the state’s economy.

My Philosophy Our society has thousands of problems.
Multiply that by the other 220 countries in the world and we’re not going to run out of things to do.
Some of the problems we have created, some of them we’ve participated in, and some of them have nothing to do with us whatsoever.
But we don’t need to destroy our basic society and the facilities of our government in order to start over — because the problems will still be here.
Here’s a page of stuff that people have wanted to get done — and we can do it with our whole heart, our whole mind, our whole body, without destroying everything first.
I am not trying to blame anyone or push anything off on anybody.
And if all I end up being known as is the janitor — fine.
I’ll do the cleanup.
Let’s work together on this instead of fighting like we’re some reality TV show.
