Description
This heavyweight crewneck sweatshirt is a soft, wearable statement piece for people who show up and stand apart.
The black 50/50 cotton–poly blend drapes cleanly in a classic fit, giving a comfortable frame for bold, political artwork across chest and back.
The front mixes hand-drawn party icons with a centered bison labeled “Independent,” asking the playful question “How do you VOTE?!” — “There are a lot of ways people carry themselves through life.
One marker I think matters is our voting record.
We can be associated with those we’ve voted for, and the pattern of our voting tells a story about who we are.
I’ve always found myself aligned with the independents.
Looking at the symbols that represent the parties I didn’t pick—the dove of peace, the donkey, the elephant, even the MAGA rooster—I’m comfortable with my decision.
I’m comfortable with the group I find myself in, and with our symbol: the buffalo.
There’s something honest about that image.
The buffalo doesn’t promise peace while bombing seven countries.
It doesn’t wear stars and smile while serving corporate interests.
It doesn’t wrap itself in slogans that divide.
The buffalo stands for what it is: independent, sturdy, self-sufficient.
It survived near-extinction and came back through sheer resilience.
When I see that buffalo with “INDEPENDENT” across its side, I see people who think for themselves.
People who won’t be herded by party loyalty or frightened by campaign rhetoric.
People who look at what’s broken and ask, “What do we already have that could fix this?”
That’s the company I keep.
That’s the pattern of my voting.
And I’m damned proud to stand with the buffalo.”
Sleeves and the back carry bright, rainbow-gradient campaign-style patches that read HARRIS, bringing color and movement to the silhouette.
Wear it on canvassing days, at watch parties, or during casual meetups to start honest conversations while staying warm and comfortable.
The crewneck, ribbed collar, and reinforced stitching hold their shape through repeat wears.
Details like a tear-away label and printed inner neck/wrist tags keep things comfortable and low-fuss.
Ethically sourced US cotton and OEKO-TEX dyes mean the garment looks good and meets safety and environmental standards.
