Description
This cuffed beanie brings bold color and handcrafted charm to everyday cold-weather wear.
The deep green knit frames a small embroidered patch—bright, textured stitching that catches the eye without shouting.
It slips on easily, with a stretchy 12″ cuff that hugs the head and keeps ears warm while letting you shape the fold.
Lightweight but insulating, the turbo acrylic knit feels soft against the skin and is hypoallergenic for sensitive heads.
Wear it while wandering city streets, cheering at outdoor events, or layering up for an early morning commute—the subtle pop of embroidery adds personality to simple winter outfits and takes well to repeated wear.
