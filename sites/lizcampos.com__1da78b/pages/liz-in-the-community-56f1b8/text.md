LIZ IN THE COMMUNITY
Part of the joy I am honored to experience is giving back to the community that gives so much to me.
I am proud for the past 5 years since getting elected by House District 119, I have participated in Back to School give-a-ways, turkey and ham give-a-ways, Fourth of July celebrations and so much more.
I have always drawn from my small business and legal administrative background to personally assist with food, housing, unemployment, and loan applications to remain connected with constituent services.
I have also participated in many drive alongs with CPS and APS to make sure those Departments and staff get the help they need to serve our community.
I have even had the privilege to help connect families with SAFFE officers when necessary.
Thank you for the opportunity to serve you and I hope to continue to serve our community.
