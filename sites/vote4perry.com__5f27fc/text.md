ABOUT ME
Putting My Experience to Work
In December 2025, after achieving an massive voter turnout in our district, I was invited to go to Washington, DC with candidate Matt Van Epps and witness his swearing in ceremony on the floor of the U.S.
House chamber.
After the ceremony, we met with Congressman Van Epps and Congressman John Rose as we toured the US Capitol.
I’ve been blessed, through my service as Mayor and also as Chair of the Cheatham County Republican Party, to build strong relationships and friendships with leaders at the federal, state, and local levels.
We will work together with these leaders to advance the interest our district and serve our citizens with integrity.
