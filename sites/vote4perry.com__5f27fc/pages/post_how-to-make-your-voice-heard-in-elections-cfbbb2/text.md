Campaign Announcement
Updated: Feb 20
It’s official: I’m running to represent Cheatham and Dickson Counties in the Tennessee State House!
As a parent of two, my wife, Misty, and I have seen the challenges our friends and neighbors face firsthand—from rising costs at the grocery store to the need for better roads and stronger schools.
I have always been a fighter for our community, and I believe District 78 deserves a voice in Nashville that will protect our values AND our wallets.
I am running to: Protect Your Property: I will vote to permanently ban any state property tax and keep more of your hard-earned money in your pocket.
Cut Red Tape: I’ll fight to get the government out of the way of our small business owners.
Defend Parental Rights: I believe parents should lead the way in education, while ensuring our local schools remain fully funded and focused on the basics.
Fix Our Infrastructure: Stop the delays and expedite the safety improvements for Hwy 49!
I will continue the conservative fight for Cheatham and Dickson counties and our 78th District.
I’m asking for your support and your vote on August 6th.
Let’s get to work!
