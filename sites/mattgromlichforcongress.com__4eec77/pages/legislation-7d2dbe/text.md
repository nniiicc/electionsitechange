Interviews Get Involved Donate As an educator, researcher and problem solver, I believe leadership means listening, studying the challenges facing our communities, and developing real, workable solutions.
I also understand how imperative it is to collaborate with other legislators.
The legislation featured on this page reflects my commitment to turning the needs of Louisiana families into meaningful action, as well as demonstrating how efficient Congress can be when we support each other to achieve the same goal.
My Day 1 Legislation Supporting Toddlers And Readiness for Tomorrow (START) Small Business Wage Bridge Act Legislation I Will Support Day 1 Artificial Intelligence Data Center Moratorium Act (H.R.9442) H.R.9442 imposes a moratorium on the construction of new data centers until legislation in enacted that safeguards the public from the dangers of artificial intelligence.
Billionaires Income Tax Act (H.R.5427) H.R.5427 amends the IRS code of 1986 to eliminate tax loopholes that allow billionaires to defer tax indefinitely.
It modifies over 30 tax provisions so that billionaires are required to pay taxes annually.
Citizens Over Corporations Amendment This constitutional amendment would overturn Citizens United v.
FEC and limit corporate influence and spending in American politics.
Equality Act (H.R.15) H.R.15 prohibits discrimination on the basis of sex, gender identity, and sexual orientation.
Epstein Files Transparency Act II (H.R.9694) H.R.
9694 allows state attorneys general, district attorneys, or other authorized state officers to enforce the first Epstein Files Transparency Act.
(Instead of just the FBI and the U.S.
Department of Justice).
John R.
Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act of 2025 (H.R.14) H.R.14 modernizes and revitalizes the Voting Rights Act of 1965.
It creates a new framework to determine preclearance, address vote dilution and denial, and updates legal guidelines to help voters challenge discriminatory voting rules in court.
Supporting Teachers Through Tax Fairness Act (H.R.7157) H.R.7157 amends the IRS Code of 1986 to exclude the first $50,000 in wages from gross income tax for K-12 public school teachers.
Washington, D.C.
Admission Act (H.R.51) H.R.51 provides for the admission of the State of Washington, D.C. into the Union.
