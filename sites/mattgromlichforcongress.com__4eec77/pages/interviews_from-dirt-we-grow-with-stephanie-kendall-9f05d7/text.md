From Dirt We Grow
Stephanie Kendall is the host of the “From Dirt We Grow” podcast.
In her podcast, she interviews grassroots candidates across the country and highlights the importance of community-building and community activism.
Description taken from YouTube:
“Everyone agrees the system is broken — especially our education system.
But how many of us are willing to step up to run against the political big dogs and do something about it?
In this episode, I sit down with a congressional candidate from Louisiana who has spent his career inside the classroom, witnessing firsthand how policy decisions made from afar impact students, teachers, and families, every single day.
Instead of just complaining, he chose action — running for office to be part of the solution.
His main focus for the Bayou State?
Improving education and the infrastructure within the 4th district, for starters.
Today, Matt Gromlich discusses why real change requires ordinary people to step up.
As well as why that requires everyday people to vote a bit different this time around.
This is about leadership, accountability, and choosing to act instead of staying silent.”
