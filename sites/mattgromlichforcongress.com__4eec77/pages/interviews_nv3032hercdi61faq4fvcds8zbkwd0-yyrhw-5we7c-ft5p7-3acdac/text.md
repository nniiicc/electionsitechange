Why It MATTers Episode 1: New Congressional Maps, Governor Recall, Data Centers, & Mike Johnson
Why It MATTers is a show that discusses topics relevant to Louisiana's 4th congressional district.
Matt Gromlich is a democratic congressional candidate running to unseat Speaker Mike Johnson.
He is a career educator, initially starting his career teaching 6-12 math and science.
Now, he teaches future educators at LSU-Shreveport.
Lynn Robinson is a current Louisiana District 4 resident, a former educator, and person who believes community voices matter.
She is not a politician or political insider.
She is someone who cares about the needs of her neighbors and believes that informed conversations help everyone.
