Angela Stiens is fighting to fix that system — and won’t stop until Kansas homeowners are protected once and for all.
The Angela Stiens Solution: Give You the Power!
Topeka doesn’t need another hearing.
Taxpayers simply need the tools to stop their property tax bills from going up every year.
We need to break the logjam on reform created by Democrats and insiders.
That’s why Angela is fighting to: Fix the Appraisal System: Let the people vote on ending the outrageous spikes that fuel higher property tax bills.
Elect County Appraisers: Missouri and many other states elect appraisers and carry a lighter tax load.
If sheriffs and district attorneys answer to voters, so should the people who set the taxable value of your home.
Impose Real Local Limits on Property Tax Increases: Angela backed real taxpayer protections — including protest petitions — empowering the people to fight back so local governments can’t ram through large increases.
Demand Transparency: Build on the bipartisan Truth in Taxation law, which sends a notice to taxpayers before local governments vote to raise property taxes.
Angela wants a second notice after they vote — informing taxpayers how their local officials voted on the tax increase.
Why This Election Matters Angela Stiens Believes taxpayers, not politicians and bureaucrats, should have the final say when property taxes rise.
She will continue fighting for greater transparency, meaningful limits on local tax increases, and a property-tax system that protects homeowners.
