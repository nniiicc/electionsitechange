Annie Menz wins Primary Election Annie Menz won the Democratic primary for House District 45 over Evan Shepherd, and will advance to the November general election to face Carolyn Womack-Bielich .
As of 8:06 p.m., with 13 of 15 precincts reporting, Menz earned 93.23% of votes, or 2,576 total votes, according to unofficial results from the Oklahoma State Election Board’s website.
Shepherd earned 6.77%, or 187 total votes.
Census and district mapping data show House District 45 includes a mix of OU students, longtime Norman residents and rural‑edge neighborhoods.
Candidate websites show affordability, education and access to state services are the top concerns raised by residents.
