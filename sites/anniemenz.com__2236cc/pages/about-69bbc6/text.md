Meet the Representative!
Representative Annie Menz represents House District 45, covering East Norman and Lake Thunderbird.
In 2022, Rep.
Menz became the first Latina elected to the Oklahoma House of Representatives, and the first Woman Veteran elected to the Oklahoma Legislature in state history.
“Watching my family serve our country, church, and community, I learned the value of hard work and public service at an early age.
They showed me how to love my neighbors without exception.
Since concluding my service with the Navy, I’ve continually sought opportunities to make a difference.
For about 7 years as a staffer at the Capitol, I specialized in legislative research, constituent communication, and casework, and developed relationships on both sides of the aisle.
Since I have been elected to represent East Norman, I have been putting my expertise, grit, and energy to good use, fighting to make your voice heard.”
“I’m fighting for the East Norman children, veterans, and working families who need fully-funded public schools, affordable housing, health care, and a living wage.
Too many of our neighbors are struggling through poverty, yet real support for us appears less of a priority to some state leaders than a turnpike our community doesn’t want.
As your State Representative, I listen, demand accountability, meet folks where they are, and put service into action.”

Menz’s platform includes:
- protecting our natural resources from government overreach,
- fighting corruption,
- investing in public education and transportation,
- and promoting solutions for the housing and affordability crises.
