Kay believes Utah's challenges are best met with conservative principles, responsible planning and practical solutions that protect our quality of life and keep our state strong.
Practical Solutions for Utah
From keeping costs down to protecting Utah's water and infrastructure, Kay is focused on the issues that directly affect Utah families and communities.
Keeping Utah Affordable
Utah families should be able to keep more of what they earn.
Kay supports conservative policies that lower taxes, reduce everyday costs and help families build a secure future.
Keeping Utah Moving
Strong communities depend on roads, transportation systems and infrastructure that work.
Kay supports responsible planning and long-term investment that keeps Utah connected and prepared.
Protecting Utah's Resources
Utah's future depends on responsible stewardship of water and other critical resources.
Kay supports long-term planning that protects Utah's water rights, infrastructure and natural resources.
Strong Families & Communities
Utah is strongest when families, schools and neighborhoods are strong.
Kay supports parents, quality education and policies that keep communities safe and provide opportunity for the next generation.
Limited, Accountable Government
Government should live within its means, respect taxpayers and remain accountable to the people it serves.
Kay supports responsible budgeting, transparency and limited government.
