Transportation Experience That Matters
Transportation is not just another issue on Kay's list.
His background and legislative work give him a practical understanding of how transportation decisions affect communities, taxpayers and families.
Chair, House Transportation Committee
Kay serves as chair of the Utah House Transportation Standing Committee, helping guide transportation policy through the Legislature.
H.B. 481 — Transportation Modifications
Kay was the chief sponsor of H.B. 481, addressing road funding, corridor preservation, transportation administration and other long-term transportation needs.
S.B. 242 — Transportation Amendments
Kay carried this major transportation bill in the House, supporting corridor preservation, rural transit, rail infrastructure and a more connected statewide transportation system.
Major Transportation Investment Close to Home
Utah has committed nearly $1.4 billion to transportation infrastructure in northwest Utah County, including the 2100 North freeway connection linking Mountain View Corridor to I-15.
The project is designed to improve east-west travel through Utah Valley and reduce pressure on heavily congested routes such as Redwood Road.
