Water Solutions A Comprehensive Approach The Legislature addressed water through transparency, water rights, storage, leasing and protection of the Great Salt Lake.
HB 76 Data Center Water Transparency Requires large data centers to disclose water usage, giving policymakers and communities better information about impacts on scarce water resources.
HB 187 Protecting Long-Term Water Rights Preserves important lower-basin water resources and limits certain changes that could reduce future water availability.
HB 348 Dedicated Water Amendments Establishes a voluntary process allowing water-right holders to dedicate water for environmental and system benefits while retaining underlying rights.
HB 410 Water Leasing Amendments Provides a structured water-leasing process designed to deliver more water to the Great Salt Lake while preserving oversight and enforcement.
HB 247 Great Salt Lake Amendments Expands annual funding supporting the Great Salt Lake and helps ensure stakeholders contribute to its long-term preservation.
