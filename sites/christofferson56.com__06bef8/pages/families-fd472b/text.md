Practical Support for Utah Families
The 2026 Legislature adopted several policies focused on reducing barriers for families and strengthening the role of parents.
Expanded the Child Tax Credit
Utah raised the income threshold so more families can qualify for the state's child tax credit.
Strengthened Parental Access
Parents gained stronger access to their children's medical records, helping families stay informed and involved in their children's care.
Improved Maternity Leave
Utah extended maternity leave provisions for state employees as part of broader efforts to support families.
Strengthened Family Support
Family-law changes improved child and spousal support enforcement, transparency and timely support for families.
Expanding Utah's Child Tax Credit
H.B. 290 increased the income threshold for Utah's child tax credit, allowing more families to qualify for tax relief.
It is part of a broader effort to reduce financial barriers for families and allow parents to keep more of the money they earn.
Parents Belong at the Center
Families work best when parents have access to information and can remain actively involved in decisions affecting their children.
Access to Children's Medical Records
H.B. 259 requires health care facilities and electronic medical-record vendors to provide parents access to their minor children's medical records.
Fair and Reliable Family Support
H.B. 248 strengthened child and spousal support enforcement while improving fairness, transparency and timely support.
Keeping Gambling Out of Utah
H.B. 243 closed a loophole involving online prop-bet platforms and reinforced Utah's constitutional prohibition on gambling.
