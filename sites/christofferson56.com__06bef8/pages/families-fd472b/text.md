Strong Utah.
Kay believes Utah is strongest when families are strong.
That means supporting parents, reducing unnecessary financial pressures and protecting the institutions families depend on.
Families First Making Utah a Better Place to Raise a Family Utah families deserve policies that respect parents, ease the pressures of raising children and strengthen the stability families need to thrive.
Support Parents Parents should have the information and authority they need to care for their children and make important family decisions.
Reduce Family Costs Tax relief and targeted policies can help families keep more of their income and reduce the cost of raising children.
Protect Families Utah should continue strengthening policies that protect children, support stable families and preserve family-centered communities.
Why It Matters Family Is Personal For Kay, strengthening families is more than a legislative priority.
It is the reason the work matters.
Kay and Suzanne have built their life and raised their family here in Utah.
They understand that the decisions made today will shape the opportunities, affordability and quality of life available to the next generation.
That is why Kay believes parents should remain at the center of decisions affecting their children, families should be able to keep more of what they earn, and Utah should remain a safe and welcoming place to raise a family.
Strong families are not just part of Utah's success.
They are the foundation of it.
2026 Family Priorities Practical Support for Utah Families The 2026 Legislature adopted several policies focused on reducing barriers for families and strengthening the role of parents.
Expanded the Child Tax Credit Utah raised the income threshold so more families can qualify for the state's child tax credit.
Strengthened Parental Access Parents gained stronger access to their children's medical records, helping families stay informed and involved in their children's care.
Improved Maternity Leave Utah extended maternity leave provisions for state employees as part of broader efforts to support families.
Strengthened Family Support Family-law changes improved child and spousal support enforcement, transparency and timely support for families.
More Families Qualify Reducing the Cost of Raising a Family Expanding Utah's Child Tax Credit H.B.
290 increased the income threshold for Utah's child tax credit, allowing more families to qualify for tax relief.
It is part of a broader effort to reduce financial barriers for families and allow parents to keep more of the money they earn.

Supporting Parents Parents Belong at the Center Families work best when parents have access to information and can remain actively involved in decisions affecting their children.
Access to Children's Medical Records H.B.
259 requires health care facilities and electronic medical-record vendors to provide parents access to their minor children's medical records.

Keeping Gambling Out of Utah H.B.
