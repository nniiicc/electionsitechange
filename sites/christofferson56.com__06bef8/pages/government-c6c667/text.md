Federal Funding Prepare Utah for Federal Uncertainty.
Federal funding can support important programs, but it can also create long-term obligations and expose state budgets to decisions made in Washington.
Utah should understand those risks and plan ahead.
Understand the True Cost Consider the obligations and future expenses attached to federal funding.
Prepare for Funding Changes Evaluate how changes in federal support could affect state programs and budgets.
Keep Utah Prepared Build plans that reduce the disruption caused by unexpected changes in federal policy or funding.
2026 Examples Putting Accountability Into Practice Several measures enacted during the 2026 General Session addressed government processes, federal funding and local accountability.
HB 147 Government Form Submission Amendments Requires electronic submission options for many state and local government forms, records and information, subject to specified exceptions.
HB 249 Federal Funds Modifications Addresses analysis and planning related to Utah's dependence on federal funds and potential federal funding changes.
HB 342 Federal Grant Process Amendments Updates processes involving legislative review and approval of federal grant funding.
HB 425 Local Government Fees Amendments Establishes requirements for certain local government fees, including procedures for transportation utility fees, annual review and appeals.
