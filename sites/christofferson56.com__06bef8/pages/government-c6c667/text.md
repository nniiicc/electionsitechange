Putting Accountability Into Practice
Several measures enacted during the 2026 General Session addressed government processes, federal funding and local accountability.
Government Form Submission Amendments
Requires electronic submission options for many state and local government forms, records and information, subject to specified exceptions.
Federal Funds Modifications
Addresses analysis and planning related to Utah's dependence on federal funds and potential federal funding changes.
Federal Grant Process Amendments
Updates processes involving legislative review and approval of federal grant funding.
Local Government Fees Amendments
Establishes requirements for certain local government fees, including procedures for transportation utility fees, annual review and appeals.
