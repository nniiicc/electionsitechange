Priorities
No one has worked harder than State Representative Dane Watro to make our communities better places to live, work, and raise a family.
Pushed to enact pro-growth policies that would enable the private sector to protect and create family-sustaining jobs
Fought to eliminate wasteful government spending so our hardworking families and seniors on fixed incomes can keep more of their own hard-earned money
Voted for legislation to promote transparency in state government
Secured additional resources for our police, fire and other first responders who are putting their lives on the line to protect us
Worked to empower parents – not Harrisburg bureaucrats – with more control over their children’s education
Pushed for legislation that would require identification to vote in person on election day
Authored legislation to make it easier for veterans to get jobs in the private sector
Secured nearly $10 million in state funding
