Standing Up for the Colorado Way of Life
Jena will safeguard Colorado's natural resources, climate, and fight for middle and working class people.
As Attorney General, she will:
- Protect Working People: Jena will hold big corporations accountable to protect Coloradans from unfair practices and exploitation, and stand up for working people and the middle class.
- Protect the Environment: Jena will defend Colorado’s air, water, land, and climate, and hold corporate polluters accountable.
- Protect communities: As Attorney General, Jena will work to ensure our communities are always protected.
