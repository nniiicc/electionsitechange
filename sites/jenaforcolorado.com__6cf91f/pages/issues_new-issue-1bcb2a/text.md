Fighting for Justice
Jena believes in fairness and justice.
As Attorney General, she will prioritize:
- Support for Victims: Jena will provide resources and support for victims, including taking action to reduce Colorado’s rape kit backlog and speeding up testing to bring justice to survivors of sexual assault.
- Gun Violence Prevention: Jena is an advocate for common sense gun safety reforms, including a strong ban on assault weapons.
- Crime Prevention: As Attorney General, Jena will invest in programs that prevent crime before they happen, improve mental health, and reduce recidivism.
- Safe Communities: Jena will work to improve community safety.
