PRESS RELEASE
Griswold campaign releases campaign ad
Date: Monday, June 8
Contact: Zach Stout, Zach@jenaforcolorado.com
Griswold campaign releases campaign ad
DENVER, CO – Today, Colorado Secretary of State Jena Griswold released her first major ad of the cycle, called “Never Back Down.” The ad will begin running today across the state on broadcast and cable, and was created by the Adwell Group.
Secretary Griswold said, “As Secretary of State, I have protected our democracy.
When Tina Peters compromised her county’s voting equipment to try to prove Trump’s election conspiracies, I held her accountable and removed her from direct election oversight.
I’ve stood up to Trump and never backed down, even in the face of threats.
As Attorney General, I will continue standing up to Trump and MAGA extremists to protect our democracy and fundamental rights.”
For the next three weeks, Griswold has reserved 1,650 broadcast points in Colorado Springs and Denver, and made a statewide cable reservation.
The buy cost over $900,000.
At this time, Griswold is the only candidate in the AG race with an ad running on broadcast TV in June.
