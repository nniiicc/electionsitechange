Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy applies to the website www.jenaforcolorado.com, owned and operated by Jena for Colorado, a committee for Jena Griswold’s campaign for Attorney General in Colorado.
Thank you for visiting our website.
Jena for Colorado is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.
By using this site, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
We prioritize security and provide an option to opt out of future campaign communications in any messages you receive from us.
To keep you informed and engaged, we may offer opportunities for you to voluntarily provide personal information, such as your name, postal address, phone number, and email address.
This information is used for campaign-related purposes and will never be sold or shared for commercial marketing.
If you make a financial contribution, state law requires us collect and report some donor details, such as your name, mailing address, occupation, and employer.
However, we do not store payment details such as credit card, debit card, or banking information.
Additionally, third-parties may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect or receive data from our website and across the internet to provide analytics and targeted advertising.
If you wish to opt out of this data collection, please visit the NAI Consumer Opt-Out.
This website complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and does not knowingly solicit or accept personally identifying information from individuals under 13 years of age.
If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at info@jenaforcolorado.com.
