Meet Tricia Cotham
Tricia Cotham is a mom, community leader, and a business owner.
Tricia’s career of serving her community began in the classroom, where as an educator she was recognized for her leadership.
Tricia was awarded the 2002 Charlotte Mecklenburg Schools Teacher of the Year and 2003 Mint Hill Teacher of the Year in recognition of her work with students.
Tricia is now serving us in the NC House, where she is using her experience every day to get results for us.
