Support Mark Legvold with your FREE PCR contribution
The MN Political Contribution Refund (PCR) program gives Minnesotans the opportunity to get a refund for supporting the candidate of their choice.
The maximum refund is $75 per person, or $150 per married couple, per year.
Individuals may file one refund application per year.
PCR refund application cannot be amended.
Only financial contributions qualify, not donations of goods or services.
