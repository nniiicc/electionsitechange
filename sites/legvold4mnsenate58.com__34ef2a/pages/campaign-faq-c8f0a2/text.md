Legvold for Senate District 58 FAQ
POLICY PRIORITIES
How will Mark Legvold combat fraud and corruption in Minnesota?
- Protect our public funds by pursuing, prosecuting, and punishing fraudsters
- Streamline reporting and recording systems for state agencies for greater transparency
- Ensure the highest standard of accountability across the government via robust oversight with the Office of Inspector General
- Protect our taxpayer dollars to make sure that the government will carry it’s weight and match the drive and hard work of every Minnesotan.
Mark Legvold on AI and Big Tech in Minnesota
- Protect our water from big industry, including data centers.
Minnesota families deserve clean and plentiful water from their wells and city systems and Data Centers can’t be first in line
- Zoning of data centers should be in the right place.
Data Centers should never be close to homes and neighborhoods, and if proposed on farmland, they should only be built on the least productive land
- Data centers and big tech shouldn’t get a better deal on energy compared to the local members of the community.
Members also shouldn’t have to pay for the upgrade to the power grid to accommodate a data center
- Proposed data centers should have a bond put in place to remove, or repurpose the facility when the technology is no longer needed.
Costs for removal or repurposing shouldn’t be borne by local municipalities or townships.
- AI and Big Tech should never be allowed to hide their development behind non-disclosure agreements.
Mark Legvold on the Economy and Affordability
- Build a strong economy that supports higher wages across industires.
- Create systems that lower the cost of living, so Minnesotans can afford to live where they work
- Support programs for unhoused Minnesotans
- Expand locally-owned affordable housing and childcare for growing families.
- Add a new tier of income taxes for millionaires
Mark Legvold is a Veteran and will Stand up for Veterans, Service members and their families
- Increase access to housing and food surety programs
- Protect veterans from predatory benefit scammers
- Ensure access to mental healthcare and substance abuse providers
- Refine the property tax exemption for disabled veterans
- Fund programs proven to reduce suicide rates among service members and veterans
