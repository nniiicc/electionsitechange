Get to Know Mark Legvold: Veteran, Farmer, Educator
I’m Mark Legvold and I’m running for MN Senate in District 58.
My story centers around the core values I grew up with on the farm and continued in my career in the military and in public education: Work hard, serve others, act with integrity, value learning, and treat others with care and respect.
I’ve served others my entire professional life.
Throughout my 33 year career in the military, I had jobs that served others - a firefighter, a cop, a first sergeant, and the senior enlisted leader for my organization.
I learned how to achieve a mission while taking care of those who do difficult things for our country and state during incredibly challenging times.
I served children and families as an educator and administrator in St.
Paul Public Schools for 10 years, teaching others to value learning and treat people with decency and kindness.
After I retired from the military, I started a small business to continue my passion for education, teaching adults leadership and the skills to build a better workplace.
I became a single parent during my kid’s adolescent years, balancing work on the farm, a full-time career in the military and all their activities; eventually blending my family with my wife, Lisa and her three sons.
Now our kids are all adults and they’ve brought wonderful new additions to our family.
We're so proud of the love they have for one another and our five grandkids.
I believe Minnesota can do a better job helping our young people build their lives.
I continue farming today, where we work hard to leave our land, water, and air better each year through thoughtful agricultural work and I still love to teach!
I teach farmers about our family’s model of raising crops and I continue running my business, training workplace leaders how to increase productivity and profits.
Farmers deserve a voice in the senate from a real dirt road dweller.
Someone who has faced the same challenges they have to try and keep their family farm whole and who believes those who feed the world should be heard.
We deserve stability in our economy and common sense in our agricultural policies.
I plan to bring a lifetime of experience to our Agricultural policy development.
Our kids and teachers deserve a voice in the senate from an educator who has faced the challenges of the classroom and school – someone who believes that our Minnesota kids deserve the best education in the nation, regardless of where their classroom is.
Our small business owners deserve a voice in the senate from someone who understands the need for a strong and stable economy paired with a growth mindset.
Families deserve a voice in the senate of someone who’s had to balance all the demands of raising kids and working full-time.
Someone who believes we deserve to have money at the end of the month, instead of month at the end of our money!
Veterans and our military families deserve a voice in the senate from someone who has laced up their boots each day and done the hard work to make the country and the state of Minnesota safe, secure, and free.
We all deserve a voice in the senate from someone who really believes that the best of us is found in good relationships, a common sense approach to policy issues, and someone who knows what public service is about because they’ve served their entire life.
