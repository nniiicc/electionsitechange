Sunday, August 30, 2026 1:00 PM 5:00 PM Google Calendar ICS Ready to Cross the Finish Line strong?
We are closing out July with our biggest push yet, and we need you on the team!
Join us for Cross the Finish Line, an all-day door-knocking marathon where we are hitting the pavement to have those critical, face-to-face conversations that win elections.
Every door we knock builds our momentum heading into August, and your energy is exactly what we need to meet our end of month goals.
On this special door knock we will be joined by Minnesota State Auditor Julie Blaha!
Whether you are a seasoned canvassing pro or stepping up for your very first time, we will make sure you are fully prepared to hit the doors.
We will kick off the day with a comprehensive training session, review our talking points, and happily pair you up with a partner if you would like one.
Event Details What to Bring: Please wear comfortable walking shoes to navigate those Minnesota summer sidewalks, and bring a fully charged cell phone.
We will be using a mobile app to track our progress!
What We Provide: Training, literature, plenty of snacks to keep you fueled all day, and massive amounts of team spirit.
Your voice is the most powerful tool we have in this movement.
Let's get out there, meet our neighbors, and share our vision for the future!
Use this Mobilize link to let us know you’re coming and to find the assembly point: https://www.mobilize.us/mobilize/event/1016519/ Questions?
Please contact Will Fehrman at 312-307-0710 or willf@senatedflcaucus.com for any questions or additional information.
Let’s get to work!
