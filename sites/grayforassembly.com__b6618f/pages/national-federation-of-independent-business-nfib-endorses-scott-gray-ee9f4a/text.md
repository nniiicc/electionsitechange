NFIB NY PAC announces their endorsement of Scott Gray for the 116th River District seat.
NFIB is the nations leading small business association with representation for small businesses in Washington and all 50 states for a voice in public policy.
Their mission is to promote and protect small businesses and the growth of those small businesses in the free enterprise system.
Scott is a small business owner whose family business is 110 years old, which he has operated for the last 40 years.
Scott knows and understands the struggles and successes of running a small business and is uniquely qualified to represent the interest of small businesses while in Albany.
Scott will work tirelessly on behalf of the residents in the 116th assembly district to grow Main Street.
Scott shares NFIB’s mission to reduce the cost of doing business and ease regulatory barriers to rebuild Main Street and make New York a viable place to do business.
NFIB joins a long list of organizations endorsing Scott Gray for Assembly during the general election cycle, including NYSUT, PEF, IBEW 910 & 2032, Central Trades AFL-CIO, Teamsters, NRA, and NYLCV.
Also, Assembly Minority Leader Will Barclay, Sheriff Colleen O’Neill, Senator Patty Ritchie, Senator James Wright (Ret), Assemblymen Ken Blankenbush and Robert Smullen, and Tom Homan.
