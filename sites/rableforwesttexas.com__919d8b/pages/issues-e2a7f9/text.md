An Economic and Sustainable Plan on Data Centers The enormous growth of data centers in Texas is an economic opportunity that must be regulated at both the state and federal levels.
With the ever increasing amount of builds planned, it's essential that West Texas leaders create a comprehensive plan.
As your congressman, I will push for federal regulation of A.I. and of data centers.
We cannot continue to let A.I. companies run unchecked, and we must protect our farmland from being exploited.
We must ensure that the projects underway and/or completed actually support the rural communities they are in and have as limited a footprint as possible.
I will ensure an end to all “sweetheart deals” for these projects.
All of these companies must pay their full and fair share of taxes on the land, electricity, and water that they use.
All revenue should be used to bolster the communities' economies.
I will never bow to corporate interests at the expense of West Texas families.

In Pope Leo XIV’s encyclical Magnifica Humanitas , we are challenged to examine the principle of the common good.
His holiness writes, “The Second Vatican Council affirmed that the common good consists in “the sum total of social conditions which allow people, either as groups or as individuals, to reach their fulfillment more fully and more easily.” [76] This definition provides us with a valuable initial reference point, because the common good cannot be reduced to a mere list of conditions or institutions.
It is not the sum total of individual benefits, nor the intersection of their particular interests; it is a greater good that belongs to everyone, and it can only be achieved, nurtured and protected by our collective efforts .
We can say that social action reaches its fullness when it is directed toward this shared good, just as a person’s moral action finds its fulfillment in the choice of the true good.” I take this to heart, meaning that we must work as a nation to ensure our families are supported.
As your Congressman, I will fight for federal paid and protected maternity leave for mothers.
Passed more than #ago, the Family and Medical Leave Act already provides 12 weeks of protected leave.
