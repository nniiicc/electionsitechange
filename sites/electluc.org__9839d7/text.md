Your Ballot Three minutes now.
Done for the year.
Washington votes by mail.
Postage is paid.
The only thing that stops a ballot is forgetting about it.
Step One Check that you're registered Takes about a minute.
If you've moved since the last election, your ballot is going to the wrong address.
Check at VoteWA.gov Step Two Fill it out and sign it The signature on the envelope has to match the one on your registration.
Unsigned ballots get held up — the county will call you, but don't make them.
Track your ballot Step Three Get it back Any Spokane County drop box, or drop it in the mail.
Boxes close at 8:00 p.m. on November 3.
Find a drop box — days left.
If you have a question about your ballot, email info@electluc.org and a real person will answer.
