BRAD MACRAE About Issues Endorsed Contact Donate Serving Canaan, Dorchester & Orange Brad Macrae For State Rep, Grafton District 9 Local business owner fighting for real property tax relief—without new statewide taxes Get Involved Learn More Meet Brad I'm a proud resident of Dorchester , a husband, expecting father, and small local business owner.
My family runs a working farm that supplies fresh, local products to our neighbors, and I've built and operated multiple businesses in farming, agriculture, and health products that employ local people and keep dollars circulating right here in our communities.
For two decades I've been self-employed as an entrepreneur—waking up every morning knowing that my success depends on delivering real value, controlling costs, and making smart decisions with every dollar.
That hands-on experience has given me a deep appreciation for what families and small businesses in Canaan, Dorchester, and Orange face every day: skyrocketing property taxes that threaten our ability to stay and thrive here.
This is my first foray into politics, though i did just start serving as a Selectman for Dorchester in June.
I have spent four years as a volunteer firefighter and first responder, so I know what it means to actually serve and look out for your community and neighbors.
What drives me is simple: I refuse to stand by while Concord experiments with new taxes that would punish working families, professionals, and small businesses in our district.
Local Roots, Real Work Grown right here in Grafton 9.
My family runs a working farm in Dorchester.
We grow things people in Canaan, Dorchester, and Orange actually use — food, flowers, and health products that come from this land and stay in this community.
That’s the New Hampshire I’m fighting for in Concord: where families can keep the homes they’ve built, where small farms and small businesses can stay open, and where Granite Staters get to keep what they earn.
Fighting For You I was compelled to run after being badly set back by the enormous property tax increases our district saw last year.
Many neighbors talked of having to forgo life necessities just to cover these taxes.
We need real solutions, not more tax experiments.
Tax Relief Without New Taxes Oppose any income, sales, or broad-based tax.
Support voter-approved school tax caps every 2 years.
Cut waste in Concord spending first—redirect efficiency savings to adequacy aid without raising local bills.
Education Excellence Through Choice Expand Educational Freedom Accounts to every NH family.
Support full open enrollment, evidence-based curriculum, and a Parental Bill of Rights.
Choice and competition improve outcomes for everyone while holding costs down.
Economic Growth for Grafton County Fewer regulations, support for energy and telecom infrastructure.
Attract and retain high-skill jobs near Dartmouth Medical without tax experiments.
Keep more of your paycheck here.
Freedom & Local Control Strong Second Amendment support.
Election integrity.
Local control over schools, budgets, and planning.
Live Free or Die means protecting your rights and keeping decisions in our towns, not Concord mandates.
A Neighbor Who Understands I'm an avid outdoorsman who loves hunting, fishing, and exploring the woods and waters that make this part of New Hampshire so special.
I'm passionate about supporting local producers, farmers, and businesses because I've seen firsthand how strong local economies create good jobs and keep our towns vibrant.
Many families commute to Dartmouth and Dartmouth Health while wanting to raise their kids and retire right here.
We need policies that make that dream affordable again.
Real Experience, Real Solutions I'm not a career politician, Concord insider, or DC creature.
I'm a neighbor who balances budgets at home and in my businesses, is building a family here, and wants the next generation to be able to afford to stay in Canaan, Dorchester, and Orange.
If you want a representative who understands the real cost of government and will always put our pocketbooks, our schools, and our local way of life first, I ask for your support in November.
