Meet Jeff
Jeff is a lifelong resident of Leavenworth and a graduate of Leavenworth High School, Class of 1989.
As the son of a combat veteran, his family was stationed at Fort Leavenworth several times before making Leavenworth their permanent home in 1984.
Jeff previously served Leavenworth in the Kansas House and Kansas Senate, where he worked across party lines on schools, tax-relief, veteran's issues, public safety, and practical solutions for Kansas families.
He is running for House District 41 to put that experience back to work for Leavenworth County.
He lives in Leavenworth with his wife, Holly, where they are raising their three children.
His oldest daughter was named Miss Kansas and placed in the top 20 at Miss USA.
Outside of public service, Jeff spends his time in the community playing pickleball, working around the yard, and walking their dogs, Kennedy and Pika.
