ISSUES
Delivering Tax Relief for Kansas Families
Jeff Pittman works across party lines to make Kansas more affordable for working families.
He helped pass record tax relief, including reducing income taxes, eliminating the state tax on Social Security income, doubling the childcare tax credit, and lowering property tax burdens.
He will continue fighting for responsible tax relief that puts Kansas families first.
Strengthening Kansas Public Schools
Jeff Pittman believes strong public schools are essential to strong communities.
He helped repeal the Brownback-era cuts and restore funding to Kansas schools.
Now, he is focused on fully funding special education and increasing teacher pay to support students and attract great educators.
Expanding Access to Healthcare
Jeff Pittman has long supported expanding access to affordable healthcare for Kansas families.
He has fought for Medicaid expansion since 2017, a policy that would expand coverage to more than 150,000 Kansans, support thousands of jobs, and help protect rural hospitals.
He will continue working to expand access to care across Kansas.
