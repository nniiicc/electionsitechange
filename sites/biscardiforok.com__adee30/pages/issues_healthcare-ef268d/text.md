She has seen firsthand how decisions made at the Capitol directly impact patients, healthcare workers, seniors, and families.
Budget cuts and harmful policies do not exist on paper alone — they affect whether someone can see a doctor, afford medication, receive mental health treatment, or recover safely at home.
Andrea will fight to lower prescription drug costs, reduce insurance premiums, and expand access to quality healthcare in rural and underserved communities where hospitals and providers are increasingly difficult to find.
She believes Oklahoma must invest in preventative care, mental health services, and programs that help people receive treatment before small problems become medical emergencies.
Every Oklahoman deserves the opportunity to live a healthy life, and Andrea is committed to fighting for a healthcare system that puts people before politics.
