Massachusetts deserves elections that are secure, government that is transparent, public records that are accessible, and a business environment where entrepreneurs can succeed.
As Secretary of the Commonwealth, Anne Brensley will work every day to strengthen public trust in our institutions.
Read Anne's Plan
After traveling across Massachusetts and meeting with voters, Republican activists, Democrats, independents, election officials, and non-partisan town clerks, Anne realized that one of the greatest ways she could serve Massachusetts was by strengthening the institutions that every resident depends upon.
Election integrity begins long before Election Day.
Protecting democracy requires strong institutions, transparency, and accountability at every level of government.
The Secretary of the Commonwealth plays a vital role in safeguarding our elections, supporting local officials, improving access to government, and fostering the business environment that allows our economy to thrive.
Protect Election Integrity
Secure elections are the foundation of democracy.
- Ensure secure ballot access and prevent manipulation before it happens.
- Modernize election safeguards while maintaining simplicity for voters.
- Support our town clerks with better tools and resources.
- Restore confidence in elections through transparency and accountability.
- Election integrity is nonpartisan—it protects everyone's vote.
Modernize Ballot Access
The ballot process should reward honest candidates, not bad actors.
- Implement better verification tools to prevent fraud.
- Strengthen safeguards against manipulation and errors.
- Review outdated procedures that create vulnerabilities.
- Make deceptive practices more difficult through modernization.
- Support local election officials with updated systems.
Government Transparency
Citizens deserve to know how government operates.
- Improve public records access and faster response times.
- Embrace digital-first government services.
- Create a culture of openness and accountability.
- Make it easier for residents to access government information.
- Build trust through transparency and honest communication.
Support Small Business
Massachusetts entrepreneurs drive our economy and create jobs.
- Modernize the Corporations Division with updated systems.
- Make LLC formation simpler and faster online.
- Reduce unnecessary bureaucracy and compliance costs.
- Improve digital filing and processing systems.
- Work with the Legislature and Governor to eliminate unnecessary barriers to entrepreneurship.
Modernize the Secretary of State's Office
Government should work efficiently for every resident.
- Implement better technology and customer service systems.
- Transition to digital-first government operations.
- Improve accessibility for all residents.
- Faster processing and response times.
- Efficient government that respects residents' time.
Restore Public Trust
Government should work for everyone regardless of party.
- Build strong institutions Massachusetts residents can count on.
- Promote honest government and ethical leadership.
- Maintain transparency and accountability at every level.
- Foster public confidence through consistent, principled action.
- Remember that politics is temporary, but institutions are permanent.
