Endorsements
I am deeply humbled and honored to announce the endorsement of the Rainbow Ridge LGBTA Democratic Caucus of Polk County in my campaign for the Florida House of Representatives, District 50.The core message of the Ricky Santiago campaign has always been clear: Equal rights are human rights.For far too long, members of the LGBTQ+ community—like many other vulnerable and marginalized groups—have been subjected to discrimination, bullying, hate crimes, and targeted government policies.
These attacks have affected nearly every part of life, including employment, business opportunities, housing, education, healthcare, public funding, and access to essential services.This discrimination must end.As your State Representative, I will fight to ensure that every person is treated with dignity, protected under the law, and given an equal opportunity to live, work, raise a family, and succeed without fear of harassment or discrimination.On November 3, we will send a clear message: the abuse, discrimination, and political attacks must come to an end.
The LGBTQ+ community will have a State Representative who will proudly stand beside them, defend their rights, and make sure their voices are heard in Tallahassee.Thank you to the Rainbow Ridge LGBTA Democratic Caucus of Polk County for your trust, your support, and your commitment to equality.
I will work every day to honor this endorsement.Together, we will build a Florida where everyone is represented, respected, and protected.Help is on the way!Rainbow Ridge Polk County LGBTA Democratic Caucus
I am deeply honored to announce that State Representative and U.S.
Senate Candidate Angie Nixon has officially endorsed Ricky Santiago for Florida House of Representatives, District 50.Representative Nixon is a proven fighter, a fearless leader, and a tireless advocate for Florida families, working people, our public schools, and our most vulnerable communities.
She has shown time and again what it means to stand on the frontlines for justice, equality, affordability, and dignity for every Floridian.Receiving her endorsement is an honor I will not take lightly.
It is more than a show of support — it is a commitment to continue the fight for the people who need help the most.
I am committed to fighting for equal rights, protecting working families, defending our vulnerable communities, and making sure our public schools have the resources, teachers, support staff, and funding needed to help every student succeed.Florida families are facing a real affordability crisis created by the failed priorities of the current administration.
Families are struggling with rising rent, higher insurance costs, expensive groceries, and an economy that too often leaves working people behind.
Angie Nixon and I understand that this fight is about putting people first — not special interests, not political games, but the families who work hard every day and deserve a fair chance.Together, we will fight for Florida families.
Together, we will stand up for our children, our seniors, our teachers, our workers, people with disabilities, immigrants, and every vulnerable community that deserves to be seen, heard, respected, and protected.Thank you, Representative Angie Nixon, for your leadership, your courage, your support, and your trust in this campaign.
I am proud to stand with you in the fight for a better, stronger, and more just Florida.District 50, Help Is On The Way!Angie Nixon
