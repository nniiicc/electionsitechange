Join the movement Fuel the vision DEEP ROOTS IN GWINNETT My story in Gwinnett started like so many others—with a family looking for a place where their hard work could take root.
My parents moved us here because they believed in the promise of this county.
They saw what I see every day: a community that is a vibrant tapestry of cultures, languages, and dreams , all interwoven into a beautiful pattern.
Growing up in the hardworking streets of Norcross, I learned that our diversity isn't just a number—it's our strength.
It’s the intricate fabric of Gwinnett, a complex and colorful design where every stitch matters.
I was fortunate to attend our world-class public schools, an experience that opened doors for me and showed me what we can achieve when we invest in one another.
But I also know that for a tapestry to stay strong, every thread must be secure.
I’ve seen the gaps where families fall through—where healthcare is a luxury and housing costs threaten the very roots people have worked so hard to plant.
Our collective mission is to protect the excellence we’ve built while ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to bloom.
The Tapestry of GCo Our Time is Now.
I’m running for State House because the people of District 97 are ready to shine.
We are a district of high-tech innovation in Peachtree Corners , small-business immigrant hustle in Duluth, and deep working-class roots in Norcross.
We don't need leadership that just "holds the line" in government.
We need leadership that understands GCo—the unique spirit of resilience and community that defines Gwinnett.
I am running to be a bridge between the grassroots and government, ensuring that as Gwinnett grows, it grows for all of us.
