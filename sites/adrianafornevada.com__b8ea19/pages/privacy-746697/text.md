This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our text and email programs, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information we collect Information you give us: when you sign up, contact us, volunteer, or RSVP, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, ZIP code, and anything you include in a message.
Information collected automatically: like most websites, our site and hosting providers may log basic technical data such as your browser type, device, IP address, and the pages you visit.
This helps us keep the site secure and working well.
How we use your information We use your information to send campaign updates, respond to your messages, organize volunteers and events, and invite you to support the Campaign.
We may also use it to comply with the law, including campaign finance reporting requirements.
Text messages If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you agree to receive recurring calls and text messages from Adriana for Nevada, which may be sent using automated technology.
Messages may include campaign updates, event and election reminders, polling, and requests for donations.
Consent is not a condition of any purchase or donation.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.
We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or text-message opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.
Donations Donations are processed by WinRed, a third-party payment platform, and are subject to WinRed’s own privacy policy.
Federal and state law require campaigns to collect and report the name, address, occupation, and employer of certain contributors.
This information may become part of the public record.
How we share information We may share your information with vendors who help us run the Campaign, such as email, text, hosting, and form providers, only as needed to perform those services.
We may also share information with like-minded candidates, parties, or organizations, and when required by law.
We do not sell your mobile number or text-message consent.
Your choices You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email, and stop texts by replying STOP.
To ask us to update or remove your information, contact us using the details below.
Children This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
Security We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect.
No method of transmitting or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time.
When we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
