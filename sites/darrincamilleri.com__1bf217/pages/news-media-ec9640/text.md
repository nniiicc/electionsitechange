Typography
Museo Sans Rounded is an energetic, optimistic font used for all campaign headlines.
We use the boldest weight of Museo Sans with italics and all caps.
Our body copy font is Zeitung Pro, most often in regular weight and sentence case.
Museo Sans was designed by exljbris Font Foundry and Zeitung Pro was designed by Underware.
Purchasing a license is required to use these typefaces.
If a free substitute is needed, we like Asap on Google Fonts.
