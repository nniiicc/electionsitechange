On Monday, April 20, we’re gathering for something bigger than a fundraiser.
We’re building a Washington County that works for all of us—not just the wealthy, not just the well-connected, but the working families who keep this society running while remaining invisible and under-supported.
Meet the candidates MotherPAC and the Oregon Working Families Party are betting on: Nafisa Fai – Washington County Chair Blayne Soleymani-Pearson – Washington County Commission, District 2 Dr.
Tammy Carpenter – State Representative, House District 27 These leaders were chosen by our endorsement committee of parents and care providers because they understand: without the workforce behind the workforce, our society would come to a screeching halt.
Yet caregivers—disproportionately women of color, especially Black and Indigenous women—receive little recognition and even less compensation.
This isn’t an accident.
It’s a system rooted in racism and sexism that keeps our work invisible.
At The Mother PAC, we know having a PAC isn’t enough.
If the voices of mothers and caregivers aren’t centered and leading, we’re just replicating the same broken structures.
We believe all women deserve fewer barriers and more opportunities.
And we’re organizing to make it happen.
