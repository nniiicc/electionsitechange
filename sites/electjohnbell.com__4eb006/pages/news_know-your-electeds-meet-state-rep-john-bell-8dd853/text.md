North Carolina House Majority Leader John Bell (R-10) pulls no punches when it comes to his district: "I have the best district in the state." He elaborated about the singularity of agriculture in District 10,saying, "Whether it be row crops, whether it be livestock, our agriculture production is second to none and we're the home of some of the best sweet potato growers in the state and also the world."
When asked to describe his district in three words, Bell replied, "I'll describe it in one: awesome." He also credited his former assistant principal, Barbara Wilkins, with for "helping me grow and giving me a good foundation for where I am today."
