A Different Kind of Representative
I grew up in a working-class family where hard work wasn't optional.
Throughout my consulting career, I've helped organizations solve problems by figuring out what's working, what's not, and how to make it better.
That's the same approach I'll bring to the Minnesota House—listening first, solving problems together, and always putting people over politics.
The toughest problems can't be solved with slogans.
Simple questions often require complex solutions.
My job is to listen, gather the facts, bring people together, and find solutions that actually work.
Every decision begins with 5 simple steps.
Listen.
Hear from the people affected.
Learn.
Understand the issue, the history, and the different perspectives.
Evaluate.
Review the evidence, data, costs, benefits, and long-term impacts.
Act.
Make a decision and take responsibility for it.
Improve.
Measure results, learn from mistakes, and keep improving.
