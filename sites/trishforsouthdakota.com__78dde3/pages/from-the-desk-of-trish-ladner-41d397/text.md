From the Desk of Trish Ladner
First of all, thank you for your time ... and I'll be quick!!
Recently, I’ve become aware of negative campaigning in the District 30 race.
That’s disappointing, because I believe our communities deserve better.
My campaign will not take part in personal attacks or negativity.
That’s simply not who I am, and it’s not the kind of leadership I believe you deserve.
This past legislative session, we passed the largest property tax cut in South Dakota history.
We also passed the Data Center Bill of Rights legislation to help protect South Dakotans from rising electricity costs and preserve our valuable water resources.
These are just a couple of the important accomplishments we achieved together.
I believe elections should be about ideas, results, and the future of our communities.
It has truly been an honor to represent you and District 30 in Pierre, and I would be grateful for your support and your vote on November 3, 2026.
Thank you,
Representative Trish Ladner
District 30
