Effective Leadership with Positive Results for Our Community
Elaine Davis is honored to serve all residents of Tennessee’s House District 18.
With a proven track record of effective leadership and a commitment to our community’s values, Elaine has been a steadfast advocate for infrastructure, public safety, education, healthcare, keeping taxes low, election integrity, job creation, and lowering the cost to do business.
Her passion for public service and her unwavering dedication to her constituents has brought positive results for Knoxville and Knox County.
