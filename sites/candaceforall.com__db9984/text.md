Hi, I’m Candace Schutt, and I’m running my campaign to represent SC State House District 2 because too many of our neighbors are barely surviving when we should all be thriving.
Raised by a single mom and supported by my grandparents, I’ve dedicated my life as a healthcare provider and community organizer, listening to those who feel unheard.
I see families priced out of housing, rural communities without healthcare, crumbling roads, and people struggling without support.
I’m not running to play politics.
My goal is to engage with the community, to listen, learn, and fight for real solutions.
Together, we can build a community where everyone has the chance to thrive.
I'm not running for the SC State House District 2 to play political games or follow a party script.
I'm tired of leaders who don’t listen, don’t learn, and don’t show up.
I don't claim to have all the answers.
What I do have is heart, humility, and a deep commitment to community engagement by learning from our community.
We all want to feel valued, heard, and empowered, and I am ready to be a representative who honors that.
