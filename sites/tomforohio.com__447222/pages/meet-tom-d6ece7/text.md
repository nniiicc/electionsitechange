Why I'm Running
I'm running to flip our District 37 Ohio House of Representative seat because all Ohioans deserve leaders who will actually represent them.
Leaders who will be transparent in their work and who will be accountable to their constituents, not special interest groups or billionaires.
Ohio deserves leaders who aren't afraid to call out and combat public corruption, who aren't afraid to go to the statehouse and fight for them.
For too long we in Ohio have been abandoned by our legislators and government officials.
While we work hard to provide for our families and build our communities, our elected officials have ignored us.

Since then bad faith legislators have done everything in their power to fight the will of the people.
From attempting to pass unconstitutional anti-reproductive healthcare laws to repealing common-sense aspects of the marijuana legalization laws in 2025, these legislators have intentionally undercut our will as Ohio voters.

Yet, our state legislature has done nothing but make these issues worse.
Our legislature has repeatedly caused local taxes to rise by reducing state funding for schools, libraries, and other local services, all while asking us for more.
