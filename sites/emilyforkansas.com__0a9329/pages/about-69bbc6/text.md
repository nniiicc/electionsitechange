In 2020, Bonner Springs welcomed me from rural Leavenworth County, where I had grown up since 1991.
Bonner Springs immediately felt like home.
That sense of organic belonging inspired intentions to get to know and invest in our communities; I never looked back and have always worked to serve.
Earning a seat in the Kansas House of Representatives is my commitment to stepping up to serve our communities in a broader capacity.
The time is now, and I’m ready.
With my passion, consistency, and authenticity, I will represent District 33 with pride, respect, and a deep commitment to the people.
Our district is at a pivotal moment.
We need strong leadership that is ready to advocate and work together in Topeka.
We have been left out of the conversation far too many times.
Strong Community Benefits Agreements, infrastructure investment, and property tax relief must be top priorities for Economic Development.
You can count on me to continue leading District 33 into a bright future.
The Journey I began volunteering with Vaughn-Trent Community Services and, within a year, joined its Board of Directors.
Six months later, I accepted a seat on the Bonner Springs-Edwardsville Area Chamber’s Board of Directors.
Shortly after, the Executive Director position became available.
At the time, I had been in a role for six years, but something told me something greater was waiting, so I took a leap of faith.
The Chamber contract was originally for three months.
Fast forward, and I am now four years in as Executive Director—a decision that has changed my life in more ways than I could have imagined.
The gratitude I have is immeasurable.
The Chamber and Vaughn-Trent serve both Bonner Springs and Edwardsville.
These experiences showed me we are one big community and the importance of equal representation in neighboring communities.
It led me to serve on the Edwardsville Elementary Site Council and build more meaningful connections.
By then, it was an easy decision to run for City Council in 2023.
Every step helped lay the foundation for positive impact, leading to my election to the Council, Director of the Tiblow Days Festival, an appointment to the Bonner Springs Tourism Board, and, proudly, a member of the Bonner Springs Rotary Club and Bonner Springs NAACP.
