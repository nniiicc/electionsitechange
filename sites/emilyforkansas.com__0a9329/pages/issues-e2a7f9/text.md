Property Tax Relief With my experience on Bonner Springs City Council, we have lowered our City Mill Levy Rate 5 years in a row.
In 2026, we are proposing to lower it again.
We proudly have kept all of our City departments fully funded!
I would like to champion expanded property tax relief for seniors, 65+.
The Kansas Department of Revenue has a program for low-income seniors and veterans.
I would advocate expanding the limits by a reasonable amount to account for inflation and property tax valuations.
Let’s keep seniors and veterans in their homes!
Infrastructure Infrastructure in Wyandotte County is past due on investments.
With the continued growth of the Western County, long-term planning is a must.
Recently, many neglected bridge projects have negatively impacted communities and small businesses.
Currently, the State Avenue corridor & K7 should be at the top of the priority list at the State level, with KDOT.
Planning and subsequent funding are crucial to managing the economic investments planned.
As a State Representative, I will work to ensure our communities are at the table and a part of the conversation.
With major proposed development, we must hold developers to paying their fair share.
Our roads, bridges and utility infrastructure isn’t solely our residents responsibility.
They must build with us, not around us.
Education Our education is the foundation of Kansans quality of life, our future workforce and its leadership.
Ensuring our state continues to fully fund public education is important to students post-graduation.
Are we creating environments for our students to thrive?
The tools we give them determine their projected success, community impact, and positive environments.
Special Education must be fully funded.
Some of our most vulnerable community members have special needs and deserve advocates in legislation.
Their access is important to growth and quality of life.
Fully funding education at the State level, will in turn lower the burden on local taxing entity set by your school board.
Sales Tax Exemptions As someone who works multiple jobs, I understand that every dollar counts!
Prescription drugs and groceries aren’t taxed at the State level.
Let’s take it a step further by eliminating State taxes on over-the-counter medicine, prepared foods, feminine products, baby products, and hygiene products.
It’s simple, necessities shouldn’t be taxed.
It may seem minuscule, but it takes away from hard-earned dollars away from Kansans.
Economic Development For the foreseeable future, growth and development will impact our district.
Its local and state leaders’ responsibility to ensure it positively impacts the community, existing businesses and the tax base.
Strong development agreements and community benefit agreements need to be a non-negotiable.
Benefits such as public infrastructure investments, memberships with Chambers, scholarship programs, commitments to non-profits, and public art.
Economic development can leverage relief on the local tax levy.
We must commit to projects that benefit us not and in the future.
It’s important to ask, “Does this do harm or good to our communities?” Small Businesses Small businesses are the heartbeat of our communities.
They support the economy and provide necessary employment for our friends and family.
I fully support opportunities to grow our local workforce.
Encouraging Kansans to build their careers and work in the small business community is important.
As a Chamber of Commerce Executive Director, I see the challenges business owners face in retaining labor, navigating inflation on commodities and products, and I look for ways to support new business owners.
I will champion and defend our entrepreneurs in the Legislature.
We must expand and reclassify our small biz programs to be more inclusive of who qualifies.
Social Issues I fully support the rights of ALL Kansans.
Equity, equality and justice for all, not some, is a foundation of my core beliefs.
Healthcare expansion & affordability Housing initiatives Legalizing marijuana Women’s access to healthcare + the right to choose LGBTQ+ protections Accessible paths to citizenship Agricultural Wyandotte County is not a heavy agricultural producer, but we still have Ag roots as the, “Bread Basket” state.
I support Kansas farms, ranchers, and public access to locally produced food sources.
It’s critical that the USDA fund our State Department of Agriculture for state and local farmers.
Programs that support local efforts have been massively negatively impacted.
I would support our neighbors and work with State and Federal legislators to ensure proper funding.
