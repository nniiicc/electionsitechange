About Tobias Howard Your Neighbor Tobias Howard has been a Lincoln resident for nearly two decades, living on the Northeast side of the city for the vast majority of that time.
He arrived in Lincoln in 2008 to attend Southeast Community College.
Tobias has a strong focus on doing what is right and stepping up to get things done.
He believes the best way to ensure something is done correctly is to do it yourself.
That is why Tobias is stepping up and running for the Legislature in District 26.
Tobias Howard has worked as a night clerk at a convenience store, a delivery driver, a programmer, an IT systems analyst, a department manager, a small business owner, and a custodian.
He is not a person of wealth.
He is you, he is your neighbor, and he is here to serve you.
