Register to Vote!
Registering to vote is simply and only takes a couple minutes.
You can use Maryland’s Online Voter Registration System (OLVR) or submit a voter registration application to your local board of elections or the State Board of Elections at any time.
Financial Aid for Education
By completing the Free Application for Federal Student Aid, also known as FAFSA, you will be considered for:
- State of Maryland financial aid
- Federal financial aid
- Institutional financial aid
