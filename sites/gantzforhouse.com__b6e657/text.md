MEET JEREMIAH
Jeremiah Gantz was born and raised in Oklahoma, splitting his childhood between rural Kellyville with his father and in Tulsa with his mother.
That upbringing gave him a unique perspective on the strengths and struggles of both rural and urban life.
Working on tractor job sites with his dad, Jeremiah learned early the values of hard work, empathy, and adaptability.
At 18, Jeremiah enlisted in the Oklahoma Army National Guard.
Trained as a medical lab specialist, he proudly served from 2008 to 2019, including a deployment to Afghanistan from 2011 to 2012.
His service taught him duty, structure, and responsibility, reinforcing the lessons instilled by his parents: put others first, do the right thing, and never quit.
After returning home, Jeremiah built his career in both retail and the automotive industry.
At QuikTrip, he served as a clerk and assistant manager across Northeast Oklahoma.
Today, he works as an automotive claims adjuster, applying the critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills that have defined his professional life.
Jeremiah is running for State Representative because he believes Oklahoma needs leaders who listen, who bring people together, and who put neighbors before politics.
“In the Army, we said ‘Always forward.’ That’s my vision for Oklahoma: we don’t go back to 'again.' Victory lies in what’s ahead of us: stronger schools, healthier families, and a government that actually serves the people.”
