State Representative Eric Roberts went to work at the State Capitol with one job in mind - to work for you.
He has focused on job growth, keeping our state financially stable, and improving opportunities for our district and families!
Message from Eric Roberts
Dear Neighbors:
Serving, Growing, Thriving Together
It is a great honor to serve you, our district, and our great state as your State Representative.
In the past three years, my focus has been on issues that are important to our community.
I’ve focused on the priorities shared by thousands across the district as I visited with you on your doorstep: ensuring that our economy continues to grow, pursuing greater accountability and transparency in government, and working to keep businesses and families stable and our community safe.
It has been my honor to stand with other legislators to defend your freedom against overreaching politicians in Washington, DC.
As many of you know, my wife Leigh and I are committed to meeting the needs of neighbors.
We continue to work in several community groups, with Ozark Teen Challenge, and assisting women in prison to be equipped with life skills and abilities to find jobs and success in the future.
We continue to believe that challenges faced in life are chapters, not conclusions.
In Service,
State Representative Eric Roberts
