ENDORSED BY STATE SENATOR LYNDA SCHLEGEL CULVER “ Mike has a history of being involved in his home community as a volunteer firefighter and as a Shikellamy School Board member showing his commitment to be a true public servant. ” — State Senator Lynda Schlegel Culver Michael is a conservative Republican and a dedicated public servant who has spent his career protecting families and serving his community.
As a firefighter and former emergency medical technician with the Americus Hose Company, Michael knows firsthand the importance of keeping our communities and families safe.
As our State Representative, no one will fight harder than Michael to improve public safety for the residents of Northumberland and Montour counties.
A lifelong resident of Sunbury, husband and father of three daughters, Michael shares our strong family values and will defend our pro-life and Second Amendment values, and he will defend our constitutional rights.
He is committed to protecting the values that make our country great and will work tirelessly to represent the hard-working men and women of the Susquehanna Valley here at home and in Harrisburg.
Michael's community involvement and leadership experience extend beyond his work as a first responder.
He is a member of the Shikellamy School Board and an Eagle Scout who served as a committee member for the Boy Scouts of America Susquehanna Council Troop 331.
As a former EMS System Coordinator for the Susquehanna EHS Council, Michael has demonstrated his ability to navigate complex issues and provide effective leadership and commonsense solutions.
These are the same skills he will bring to Harrisburg while he supports American energy independence, backs our workers and small businesses and works to bring fiscal responsibility to state government.
A graduate of Shikellamy High School and Bloomsburg University, Michael embodies the qualities of integrity, leadership, and service.
He is proud to have earned the Republican nomination from the Montour and Northumberland County Republican Committees for the Special Election in the 108th PA House District.
He is excited to have been endorsed by State Senator Lynda Schlegel Culver and looks forward to working with her to ensure the voices of their constituents are heard in Harrisburg.
Michael is a dedicated public servant who is committed to working hard for his community and representing the values of hard-working men and women he seeks to serve.
