Women Empowered.
Join Dawn Richardson & Amelia Rabroker, two educators and candidates for the Texas House, for a meet & greet and candid conversation about women’s rights, the freedom to read, public education, and the decisions being made in the Texas Legislature.
We’ll look at Texas legislation impacting women and families—including SB 8 and HB 1280—along with laws affecting books and materials in Texas schools, such as HB 900 and SB 13.
Come learn what these laws do, ask questions, hear where the candidates stand, and understand why the people we elect to the Texas Legislature matter.
