Hosted in support of Amelia's campaign, join us for “Painting a Brighter Future,” a creative and relaxed evening supporting Amelia’s campaign for House District 55.
On Friday, January 23, 2026, from 6–8 PM at 103 Mountain Lion Rd., Harker Heights, we’ll gather to paint rocks, enjoy wine, and connect with neighbors who care about our community.
Your $# donation includes all painting supplies and refreshments.
Sign up to attend: https://goodchange.app/donate/paint-x9 No art experience needed—just bring yourself and your belief in strong public schools, working families, and honest leadership.
Come unwind, create something meaningful, and help power the voter outreach and organizing that will shape HD 55’s future.
