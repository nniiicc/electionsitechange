Thank you to everyone in Brookline, Mason, and Greenville who participated in the September 8 Primary Election and supported my 2026 campaign for re-election.
I am grateful for the 563 votes I received across our three towns.
I deeply appreciate the confidence you have placed in me over the past six years, and I would be honored to continue serving the people of Brookline, Mason, and Greenville by representing you in Concord.
As we move toward the November 3, General Election , I will be using this blog to share information about the issues that matter to our communities, provide campaign updates, and explain legislation and decisions made in Concord—and what they mean for us here at home.
My goal is simple: to keep you informed and make it easier to understand what is happening in Concord and how it affects our communities.
There is still much work to do, and I look forward to continuing that work on your behalf.
Thank you for your support, your questions, and your participation in our local government.
