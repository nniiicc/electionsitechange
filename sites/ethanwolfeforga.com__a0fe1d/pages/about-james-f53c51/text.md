top of page Home About Ethan Issues Blog Endorsements Election Info Donate Meet Ethan A veteran of the United States Air Force and a former Lieutenant Colonel, Ethan Wolfe is a native of Sumter County dedicated to representing Georgia’s 151st House District.
He is challenging the status quo to ensure that rural voices are no longer ignored in the halls of power in Atlanta.
After three decades of protecting our nation, Ethan returned home to Americus, fueled by a lifelong connection to the people and landscapes of rural Georgia, ready to serve his neighbors in a profound new capacity.
Graduating from Americus High School in 1988, Ethan’s early life was defined by the challenges and triumphs unique to our region.
He relies on a foundation of faith and resilience that guided him through thirty years in the military and led him back to the community that raised him. ​ ​ Fighting the Good Fight Ethan’s military journey began as an enlisted airman before he transitioned to an officer in 1999.
As a Master Air Battle Manager, he logged over 3,800 total flight hours, including 2,200 hours in high-stakes combat situations aboard the E-8C Joint Surveillance Target Attack Radar System.
His service spanned critical global operations from Iraqi Freedom to Resolute Support.
He was honored with the Defense Meritorious Service Medal and the Air Medal with eight oak leaf clusters, among many other accolades reflecting his tireless commitment to duty.
While serving, Ethan prioritized his personal development, completing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Systems and an MBA from American Intercontinental University.
Since retiring in 2020, he has become a pillar of local civic life, leading the American Legion Post 2, serving as Chaplain for the VFW Post 4784, and contributing to the Friends of Andersonville board.
Rooted in the Community Ethan is a fixture in the Kiwanis and Civitan clubs and a dedicated advocate for voting rights and democratic participation.
His candidacy is rooted in the belief that the 151st District needs a representative who is visible, accountable, and focused on people over partisanship across Georgia.
