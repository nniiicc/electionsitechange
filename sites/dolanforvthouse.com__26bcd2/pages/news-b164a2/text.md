July 20, 2020
A Voice for Resilience and Leadership
These are challenging times as we navigate our way through racial injustice, COVID-19, and economic uncertainty.
It has been heartbreaking to hear stories from friends and neighbors struggling to put food on the table, patiently waiting for unemployment checks to arrive, or finding out that their childcare has shut down permanently.
I have also heard community members courageously voice their personal experiences of harm from racism or other hate.
And others have expressed the general fear and anxiety they face as they try to do what they can to protect themselves and their families against COVID-19.
