Buller, a CNY native, graduated from the University of Buffalo in 2011, and moved to Washington D.C. shortly after starting at a nonprofit.
Most recently she served as a former senior official with the United States Department of Agriculture under two presidential administrations.
Buller describes herself as a moderate republican and fiscal conservative, saying her primary focus is remedying some top concerns she’s heard from voters, including high utility costs, food prices, and housing availability.
Buller also says that while she has voted for President Trump in the past, she doesn’t always agree with all of his policies.
During an interview with CNYCentral, Kailee Buller said this when asked about immigration: “This is a challenging topic and this is another one of those topics I hear from a lot from central New Yorkers.
We don’t have enough workforce here that are American.
We need to make sure that the labor that we bring in is legitimate but also that those who are criminals are out of our country.
The border is now secure.
I think that’s been very clear.
What’s been going on with ICE, there’s been mistakes made absolutely no doubt.
But I think the biggest thing here that is needed is we need a comprehensive immigration reform package.
And I’ve been working on H2A labor for over a decade with the farm community.
There’s a lot of people who don’t want to pick blueberries and they don’t want to pick strawberries and they don’t want to help milk cows and make sure that whole milk is getting where it needs to go.
We do need labor here that’s not American here.
We almost acknowledge that.” Buller is born and raised from Auburn, New York.
She is a mother of three children with her husband, Eric.
Buller first announced her campaign for the congressional seat earlier this month on Thursday, March 5.
The New York State Primary Election is on June 23.
The General Election is on November 3.
