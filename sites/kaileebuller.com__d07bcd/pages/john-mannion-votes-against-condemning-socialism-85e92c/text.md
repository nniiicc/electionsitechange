Auburn, NY — Socialism makes life more expensive for working families and Congressman John Mannion just voted NO on legislation condemning it.
H.Res. 1490 condemns socialism and the Democratic Socialists of America.
The legislation passed 220-192, with eight Democrats joining Republicans in voting yes.
Mannion voted no.
“This shouldn’t be a hard vote,” said Kailee Buller.
“Socialism means higher taxes, more government spending, and less money in your pocket.
Eight Democrats understood that and supported it.
Socialism means higher taxes, more government spending, and less money in your pocket.
John Mannion is on board with making your life more expensive.
And since he won’t share his voting record – I will.”
Background:
Kailee Buller is an Auburn native, working mother of three, and former Chief of Staff at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, where she helped lead a department of more than 100,000 employees and a budget exceeding $200 billion.
She is running for Congress to lower costs, support working families and farmers, and bring accountability and common sense back to Washington.
