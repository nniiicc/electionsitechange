Buller: Debate will go forward with questions from voters and moderators
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Date: September 1, 2026
Contact: [email protected]
Auburn, NY — Congressman John Mannion will no-show the first debate —Kailee Buller will move forward with the event, meet with voters, and answer questions without him.
Both candidates were invited to participate.
The event is open to the public.
The Alliance to Preserve the Finger Lakes is a nonpartisan, citizen-based environmental advocacy organization focused on protecting the region’s landscapes, wildlife, farmland, waterways and community character.
The group advocates for conservation, responsible development and greater local input in decisions affecting Finger Lakes communities.
“John Mannion can’t avoid answering for his record forever.
Eventually he’ll have to talk about raising taxes and utility bills,” Buller said.
“I’ll be there, taking questions and talking directly with the people we’re asking to represent.
Voters deserve to hear from both of us.”
