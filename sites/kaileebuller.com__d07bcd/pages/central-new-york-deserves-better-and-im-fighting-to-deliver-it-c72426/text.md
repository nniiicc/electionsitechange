By Kailee Buller, Candidate for Congress (NY-22)
I grew up in Auburn, New York, the daughter of a corrections officer and a nurse.
Like so many young people from Central New York, I had to move away after college because there weren’t many good-paying job opportunities in my field.
And I needed to pay off my student debt.
I landed in Washington, worked hard, paid off every dollar I owed, and built a career I’m proud of — becoming the first in my family to earn a four-year degree and rising to serve as Chief of Staff to U.S.
Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins.
In that role, I helped deliver results that mattered to families, farmers, and taxpayers.
Things like reducing egg prices, advancing better nutrition standards, bringing whole milk back to schools, protecting U.S. farmland from foreign adversaries, expanding nuclear energy across the U.S., and exposing billions in waste and fraud that Washington politicians insisted did not exist.
I am proud of that work because it was never about headlines.
It was about results, and it gave me the opportunity to work with people from different walks of life, across the country, and across the political spectrum.
But no matter where my career took me, Central New York was always home.
And everywhere I went, I kept thinking about families like mine: people who worked hard, followed the rules, and still fell further behind.
I have been all over this country and around the world.
The problems we face here in Central New York are serious, but they are not impossible to solve.
If Albany or Washington tells you otherwise, they either are not working hard enough or are not being honest with you.
That is what brought me home to Auburn and why I’m running to be Central New York’s next member of Congress.
Today, too many Central New York families are feeling the same pressures we felt growing up — and in many ways, those pressures have gotten worse.
Utility bills should not be this high.
Groceries should not cost this much.
Young people should be able to afford a home where they grew up.
Seniors should not be worried about keeping what they earned.
Farmers and small businesses should not be buried under costs, mandates, and red tape.
And we deserve better than politicians like my opponent John Mannion, who campaigned on affordability and bipartisanship, then went to Washington and voted the party line because Washington Democrats told him to — not because Central New York wanted him to.
It has only taken him a short time in Washington to forget where he came from.
This is a guy who had the opportunity to support lowering costs.
Instead, he voted with his party against eliminating taxes on tips, against eliminating taxes on overtime, against Social Security tax relief, and against the Farm Bill.
A voting record that lines up with Washington Democrats nearly every single time is not representation — it is abdication.
That is not the independent representation he promised.
That is letting Washington and party bosses call the shots.
I am running to put Central New York first.
That means fighting to lower costs, make energy more affordable, back law enforcement, protect Social Security and Medicare, and stop reckless spending from making life more expensive.
It means supporting nuclear, natural gas, and American energy production instead of expensive mandates like Albany’s Climate Law that have driven up utility bills and made New York less affordable.
It also means taking a serious look at New York’s fracking ban while Pennsylvania — with a Democratic governor — produces 20% of America’s natural gas and New York imports energy from next door.
And it means supporting good ideas no matter who puts them forward — because I answer to Central New York families, not party bosses or special interests.
That is what this campaign is about.
Affordability, real results, and making sure the people who work hard, grow our food, protect our streets, and build our communities have someone fighting for them — not just on the good-news days, but on the hard days too.
I did not get this far by accepting the status quo.
I have been relentless my entire career, and I will bring that same work ethic to Congress.
I will work to earn every single vote in this district, regardless of party affiliation.
Central New York deserves better, and I look forward to earning your vote on November 3.
Kailee Buller is a candidate for Congress in NY-22.
