John Mannion’s record on climate and energy makes him especially vulnerable as utility costs continue to rise , utility shut-offs surge for families unable to pay their bills , and growing strain on the energy grid puts Central New York at greater risk of blackouts.
Mannion voted for mandates targeting gas-powered vehicles, natural gas service, and the construction of industrial wind and solar projects on viable farmland — even as the state now acknowledges the transition could cost some households thousands of dollars more each year.
BACKGROUND Mannion voted for the RAPID Act , which makes it easier for the state to approve industrial solar and wind projects over the objections of local communities.
He voted for legislation directing New York to phase out gas-powered vehicles by 2035.
Mannion voted for the NY HEAT Act, which would curtail cheap & reliable natural gas service and infrastructure.
He supported the All-Electric Buildings Act , which is increasing construction costs by banning gas hookups and gas appliances like stoves & ovens in new buildings.
Mannion declared he is “100% supportive” of Albany’s energy transition.
Following Mannion’s votes, New York’s own energy officials have acknowledged that full implementation of the mandates could add more than $4,000 per year to households’ energy costs.
Despite rising costs and Albany’s retreat on many of his mandates, Mannion has doubled down saying he is still supportive of the expensive “lofty energy goals.” John Mannion owns these energy bills.
