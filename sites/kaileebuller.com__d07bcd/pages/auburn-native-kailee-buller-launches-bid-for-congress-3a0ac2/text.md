An Auburn native who served in President Donald Trump’s administration has been endorsed by the Republican chairs in the 22nd Congressional District to challenge Democratic U.S.
Rep.
John Mannion.
The Republican leaders in Cayuga, Cortland, Madison, Oneida and Onondaga counties are supporting Kailee Buller, who was chief of staff to U.S.
Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins since Trump began his second term in 2025.
Buller, who was born and raised in Auburn, also served in the first Trump administration.
She was the chief of staff to the deputy secretary of agriculture, a senior adviser on biofuels, chief of staff for the research, education and economics mission area and a policy adviser in the department’s Office of Congressional Relations.
Her duties included helping to negotiate the 2018 farm bill, federal legislation that guides agricultural policy.
Before serving in government roles, she was the president and CEO of the National Oilseed Processors Association she was the first woman to lead the organization and president and CEO of the Edible Oil Producers Association.
Buller also worked in legislative roles for other organizations, such as the Corn Refiners Association, National Grocers Association and SNAC International.
In a news release announcing the endorsements, the Republican chairs including Cayuga County GOP Chairman Nate VeVone noted that Buller has “spent her career fighting to lower costs, expand access to healthy food and create better opportunities for farmers, ranchers and working families.”
Joining VeVone in endorsing Buller are Onondaga County Republican Chairman Joseph Bick, Oneida County Republican Chairman Ken Roser, Cortland County Republican Chairman Daniel Quail and Madison County Republican Chairman Todd Rouse.
Buller will seek the Republican nomination to challenge Mannion, D-Geddes, who is in his first term representing the 22nd district.
Republicans faced a dilemma in the 22nd district because Assemblyman John Lemondes, who entered the congressional last year, recently dropped out to run for another Assembly term.
David Hollenbeck, a veteran from Oneida County, had entered the congressional race.
Hollenbeck’s status is unknown.
The 22nd district is rated in Mannion’s favor.
Democrats outnumber Republicans in the district, and Mannion won it in 2024 by 10 points.
