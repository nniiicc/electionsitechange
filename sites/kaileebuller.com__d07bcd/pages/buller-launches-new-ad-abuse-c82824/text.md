“I’ll cut your taxes and lower your energy bills.” WATCH THE AD HERE Mannion’s Record In 2024, three former staffers published a letter accusing Mannion of creating a hostile work environment in his State Senate office.
The investigator’s report has never been made public .
In Albany, Mannion voted to fast-track industrial wind and solar projects , phase out gas-powered cars , curtail natural gas service , and ban gas hookups in new buildings .
New York’s own energy officials now say those mandates could add more than $4,000 a year to household energy bills, yet Mannion still backs the “lofty energy goals” behind them.
POLITICO has highlighted NY-22 as a race where energy costs have become the defining issue.
In Washington, Mannion voted against the 2025 tax law that stopped a scheduled tax increase on middle-class families and cut taxes on tips, overtime, and Social Security.
Kailee’s Plan Kailee Buller has signed a no new taxes pledge and released a five-point energy plan to repeal Albany’s mandates, expand reliable American energy, and bring utility bills down.
