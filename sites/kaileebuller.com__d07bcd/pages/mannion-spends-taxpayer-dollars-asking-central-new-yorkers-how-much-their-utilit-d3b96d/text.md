I have a plan to actually lower your costs.
August 27, 2026 Screenshot Auburn, NY — Congressman John Mannion spent at least $10,000 in taxpayer dollars on ads between February and April of 2026 asking Central New Yorkers how much their utility bills have increased — after he spent years in Albany supporting the very energy agenda that has made utilities less affordable.
The taxpayer-funded ad directs constituents to a survey on Mannion’s official congressional website asking about increases in their utility bills.
He has yet to release the survey results nor has he shared his plans to reduce energy prices for Central New York families.
“John Mannion is spending your tax dollars asking if your utility bill went up.
You don’t need a survey to know the answer,” said Kailee Buller.
“I talk to Central New Yorkers daily who are getting crushed.
Instead of spending taxpayer money studying the problem, we should be fixing it.” While Mannion has stood by Kathy Hochul defending New York’s disastrous climate law and its “lofty goals,” Buller has called for repealing Albany’s costly mandates and replacing them with a true all of the above energy approach focused on affordability, reliability and consumer choice.
In April, Buller released a five-point plan to lower energy costs that includes: 1.) Calling for the repeal the broken Albany climate law which includes taxpayer funded mandates for foreign wind and solar projects.
Buller has already worked at the federal level to make solar and wind optional, not mandatory in her role as the Chief of Staff of the U.S.
Department of Agriculture and is committed to supporting federal legislation to override New York’s climate law should lawmakers in Albany fail to do so.
2.) Revisiting New York’s reckless ban on fracking and natural gas which are working in other Democrat states such as Pennsylvania If the state fails to lift these bans, Congress should pass legislation that Buller will support to stop Albany from stripping energy options away from consumers.
More energy options will result in lower consumer costs.
3.) Fast tracking nuclear expansion at the local, state, and federal levels to the extent that is safe and practical.
4.) Reforming permitting at the local, state, and federal levels to bring on new energy infrastructure like natural gas pipelines as quickly as possible while protecting people and the environment.
Buller supports projects such as the Constitution pipeline.
5.) Requiring full transparency into our current energy suppliers, many of whom have foreign ownership, coupled with a long-term plan to invest in key American and New York energy sources through tax incentives because American energy should be produced in America.
“Mannion is asking how much he raised your utility bill.
I’m telling you how we’re going to bring it down,” Buller said.
Background: Kailee Buller is an Auburn native, working mother of three, and former Chief of Staff at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, where she helped lead a department of more than 100,000 employees and a budget exceeding $200 billion.
She is running for Congress to lower costs, support working families and farmers, and bring accountability and common sense back to Washington.
