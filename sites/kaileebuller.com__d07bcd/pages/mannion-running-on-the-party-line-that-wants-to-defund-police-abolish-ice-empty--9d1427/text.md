September 8, 2026
Syracuse, NY — Kailee Buller today released the following statement denouncing Congressman John Mannion for taking the New York Working Families Party ballot line, the third party whose endorsement he won and whose ballot line he ran on in 2024:
“John Mannion wants Central New York to believe he’s a moderate.
Then he took the line of the Working Families Party and said he was grateful for it.
This is the party that posted ‘Defund the Police.’ Whose national director told supporters to call the mayor of Minneapolis and ‘tell him to cut the police budget.’ Whose New York chapter went to bat for cashless bail, ran an agenda to close our prisons and jails and is currently circulating a petition calling for ICE to be eliminated.
Give me a break.”
“This is the party that spent seven figures electing Democratic Socialist Zohran Mamdani as Mayor of New York City, instructed Democrats to kill the bipartisan border deal, and weeks after October 7th demanded Congress vote to deny $14 billion in aid to Israel.
These people are radical left and out of step with Central New York values.”
“Central New York families are getting crushed by utility bills and taxes.
They don’t need a congressman who runs on a New York City party’s ballot line and calls himself a moderate back home.
And they definitely don’t need that party’s six new state taxes.
They need someone who actually understands tax and fiscal policy and is ready to cut costs so you keep more of your hard-earned money, not less.
That’s why I’m running.”
Congressman John Mannion’s extremism is further shown by his recent vote against condemning socialism.
Eight moderate Democrats voted in support.
He has also failed to sign the Promise to America, which condemns socialism and aligns moderate Democrats around a set of six principles: capitalism, safety, fiscal responsibility, effective government, common purpose, and patriotism.
Nineteen moderate Democrats across the country have signed, including New York state moderates Tom Suozzi (D-NY-03) and Laura Gillen (D-NY-04).
While John Mannion claims he is a moderate, his record tells us who he really is.
Background:
Kailee Buller is an Auburn native, working mother of three, a former Chief Executive Officer, and most recently served as Chief of Staff at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, where she helped lead a department of more than 100,000 employees and a budget exceeding $200 billion.
She is running for Congress to lower costs, support working families and farmers, and bring accountability and common sense back to Washington.
