The organizations have proposed Monday, October 19th at 6:00 pm for the debate.
“Voters keep asking whether John Mannion will actually debate me.
He should.
After years of backing policies that have driven up energy bills and taxes on New Yorkers, he has an atrocious record to defend,” said Buller.
“I’m ready to stand on a stage, answer tough questions and defend exactly where I stand.
John Mannion should have to do the same.” Organizations and media outlets interested in hosting a debate should contact [email protected] .
Background: Kailee Buller is an Auburn native, working mother of three, and former Chief of Staff at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, where she helped lead a department of more than 100,000 employees and a budget exceeding $200 billion.
She is running for Congress to lower costs, support working families and farmers, and bring accountability and common sense back to Washington.
