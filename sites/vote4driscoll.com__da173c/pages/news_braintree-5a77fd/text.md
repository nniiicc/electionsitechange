Dear Friend,
Since 2025, I have had the honor of serving full-time as a State Senator for Braintree and six other towns.
Before that from 2017 - 2024 I was a State Representative for Milton and Randolph.
I’ve been working with elected colleagues in the legislature, town council and Mayor’s office on issues
important to you and your neighbors.
Together, we have been able to accomplish a great deal for our community.
