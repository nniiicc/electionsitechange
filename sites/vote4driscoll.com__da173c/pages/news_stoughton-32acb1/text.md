Details about some of the results for Stoughton during first term as the State Senator for Stoughton
Dear Friend,
Since 2025, I have had the honor of serving full-time as Senator for Stoughton and six other towns.
Before that from 2017- 24 I was a State Rep. for Milton and Randolph.
I’ve been working with colleagues in the Legislature, Selectboard and Town Manager’s office on issues important to you and your neighbors.
Together, we have been able to accomplish a great deal for our community.
I have never missed a vote at the State House and I go to work everyday to deliver for Stoughton.
Below contains details regarding what we’ve secured during the past year in partnership with Representatives Ted Philips and Bill Galvin.
SCHOOLS
- $180,000 toward the new Stoughton school building project
- $350,000 in additional educational funding for Stoughton Public Schools in FY27
- $20,000 for fencing, shading and capital improvements at the athletic fields at Wilkins elementary school
- Since 2021 has voted to continue funding universal, free school meals for all kids in public schools.
COMMUNITY
- $2M+ toward the Stoughton Community Center / Train Depot Restoration project completed in June 2026.
- $250,000 for Stoughton 300th events and activities celebrating Stoughton
- $10, 000 toward Stoughton AmVets Post 1977 Improvements
MUNICIPAL SUPPORT VIA EARMARKS
- $297,031 secured to offset snow removal costs incurred during our difficult winter
- $50,000 toward Stoughton Fire Dept.
Equipment Upgrades & Continuation of Essential Services
- $25,000 for new curbing abutting the MBTA parking on Wyman Street
