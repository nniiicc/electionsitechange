Details about some of the results delivered for Easton during first term as State Senator for Easton
Dear Friend,
Since 2025, I have had the honor of serving full-time as Senator for Easton and six other towns.
Before that from 2017- 24 I was a State Rep. for Milton and Randolph.
I’ve been working with colleagues in the Legislature, Select Board and Town Manager’s office on issues important to you and your neighbors.
Together, we have been able to accomplish a great deal for our community.
I have never missed a vote at the State House and I go to work everyday to deliver for Easton.
Below contains details regarding what we’ve secured during the past year in partnership with Representatives Bridget Plouffe and Lisa Field.
RESULTS FOR EASTON
SCHOOLS
- $20,000 toward capital improvement projects in Easton Public Schools
MUNICIPAL EARMARKS
- $25,000 toward the Easton Industrial Park Sewer Project
- $309,647 for snow removal after our difficult winter
- $95,000 Transportation infrastructure projects, improvements and planning
- $70,000 for renovations and rededication of Easton’s Swift Park
- $25,000 for the Easton Fire Dept
COMMUNITY
- $35,000 secured for Easton’s Tricentennial Celebration
- $51,000 for the Raising Multicultural Kids organization for programming in public schools
- $10,000 for the Easton Wings of Hope organization
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
- $3M in bonding authorizations for expansion of Easton’s - Industrial Park Sewer Project (still pending in conference committees)
