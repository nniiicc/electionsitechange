I am glad to help my constituents solve problems and cut through the red tape when necessary.
While the government was never intended to address or solve all problems, it does have a limited and important role to play.
I will collaborate and seek common-sense solutions that don't increase the size and scope of government.
MORE LISTENING As your representative, it is my duty to listen.
I strive to learn all sides of an issue as I try to make informed decisions.
The vitality of our republican model of government rests on your participation.
Each election year, the people of Kansas consent to what kind of government they want.
I am committed to being a servant leader who listens, encourages civility, and promotes a stronger Salina and Saline County.
REAL RESULTS For three years, it was an honor to serve as the Chairman of the Higher Education Budget Committee.
As committee leader, I was tasked with setting an agenda, conducting bill hearings, and making budget recommendations for our higher education institutions.
I also represented Kansas on the National Conference of State Legislatures’ Task Force on Higher Education, which worked on state and federal strategies to make college more affordable for students and taxpayers, improve completion rates, and reduce the incidence of unrepayable student debt.
