My Goals
Supporting Law Enforcement
I learned while living in Orange County, Ca for 38 yrs, that having a police department the community can trust requires support and engagement from the community.
I plan to support adequate funding for law enforcement while expanding community engagement with law enforcement
Expanding read by 3rd Grade
Keeping kids in school reduces crime and improves our communities while maintaining property values and improving businesses.
I will expand ‘The Read by 3rd Grade’ program into libraries, parks, 1st responders and informing parents of remedial resources
Prosecuting Crime
Judges and D.A.s in Nevada complain that laws are too lax to prosecute hardened criminals when the time comes.
I will support legislation to prosecute hardened criminals when it is appropriate
Reducing Homelessness
Homelessness is the biggest problem in East Las Vegas and is becoming unmanageable due to fentanyl, property and shopping center owners not able enforce their trespassing rights and inadequate channeling of homeless resources.
Homelessness is obstructing our prosperity.
I plan to focus on reducing drugs on our streets, working with property owners and businesses to enforce their trespassing rights, transparency of outreach centers , working with homeless outreach centers to get homeless to the services they need, and getting homeless people more engaged with homeless outreach/care centers.
Improving small business
Safe communities, low taxes and low licensing fees improve business.
I plan to improve safety in business areas, reduce business taxes and fees for new and existing businesses
Promote fiscal Responsibility
I will be a fiscal watchdog to reduce over spending .
I will vote against and call out policy which leads to irresponsible spending and over spending.
The last thing Nevada needs is a budget deficit like some Democratic run states.
Diversifying our Economy
Safe communities and education diversify the economy, I learned this while being an Electronics Engineer in Irvine, Ca (Hi Tech center) area for 38 yrs.
I plan to encourage our safest communities like Henderson to attract hi-tech businesses first, then expanding that growth to our inner city communities
