Stephanie M.
Vargas
for Congress

PLATFORM
I am stepping up into this fight because we are at a crossroads, and I refuse to stand by as our leaders steer us toward a future without promise.
My resolve is built on a simple principle: we must leave the next generation a nation that is stronger, safer, and more prosperous than the one we inherited.
I am fighting to ensure that when we pass the torch of leadership, it is burning with the light of freedom and opportunity - not flickering at the brink of disaster.
It's time for common sense economic and energy policies that make the dream of owning a home a reality again for the next generation.
LIST OF ISSUES
Public Safety
I am committed to supporting legislation that enables our Law Enforcement and Fire Service Officials to do their jobs efficiently and effectively.
Law Enforcement
Public safety is a non-negotiable, and I stand firmly behind the enforcement of our laws and the removal of dangerous criminals from our communities.
If we want our children to be able to grow up in safe neighborhoods we must be willing to deal with lawlessness and hold criminals accountable for their poor choices.
Fire Service
We need to take the common-sense approach to forest management and allow our fire service members the ability to address the root causes of wildfires.
While environmental values are important, I will advocate for policies that grant fire service officials the necessary authority to use every available prevention strategy in order to safeguard our homes and lives first and foremost.
Economy
I am committed to championing a common-sense legislative agenda that restores fiscal sanity and drives down the cost of living and energy costs for every American family.
My focus is on enacting policies that foster growth, remove bureaucratic barriers, and ensure absolute accountability for anyone - from government agencies to private entities- who engages in fraudulent activity at the taxpayers' expense.
Housing Crisis
I am committed to solving the housing crisis by championing legislation that incentivizes the construction of affordable, single-family homes, through voluntary partnerships rather than punishing local governments with the unfunded, 'one-size-fits-all' mandates that often burden our municipalities.
My plan focuses on unleashing the potential of federal surplus land to build new, vibrant communities that are affordable, providing the next generation with tangible options for homeownership and the space to raise a family, which have been out of reach for far too long.
Lowering Cost of Living
The cost of living isn't just "high" - it's being driven up by government spending and invisible rules that act like a "hidden tax" on everything we buy.
Here is exactly what I will do to fix it:
- Slash Energy Costs: I will vote to stop bans on American energy.
By producing more oil and gas here at home, we lower the cost to fill your tank and heat your home.
- Stop the "Hidden Tax": I will support legislation that stops unelected bureaucrats from passing expensive rules behind closed doors.
No more "hidden taxes" on your groceries or gas without your elected representative being held accountable.
- Protect Your Paycheck: I will vote against "blank check " spending bills.
When the government spends money, it doesn't have, your dollar buys less.
I will fight to keep more money in your pocket, not the government's.
- Making Housing Affordable: I will fight to use empty federal land to build new neighborhoods.
This creates more options for young families to own a home- the same way our parents and grandparents did.
Strengthening the Local Economy: For too long our local economy has been focused on logistics- simply moving goods from one place to another.
While these jobs are important, a "pass-through" economy doesn't build long-term wealth for our families.
I am committed to make a "Made in America" agenda that transforms our cities into manufacturing powerhouses.
- Incentivize Production, Not Just Storage: I will champion tax credits for companies that build factories and production lines in our cities.
We want to reward businesses that plant roots and create skilled, high-paying careers, not just those that build more warehouses.
- Train for Careers, Not Just Tasks: I will advocate for federal funding to go directly to our local trade schools and community colleges.
We will focus on "earn-while-you-learn" apprenticeships so our workers are ready for advanced manufacturing jobs in fields like aerospace, machinery, and technology.
- Fix the Supply Chain Locally: I will support legislation that works to bring the production of essential goods back to our soil.
This doesn't just create jobs; it protects our local economy from global disruptions and lowers costs for everyone.
Protecting Children and Family Values
Nothing is more important to me than the safety and well-being of our children.
I am 100% committed to advocating for family values that create an environment where every child can thrive.
Right to Life
I believe that true advocacy means a complete and continuous dedication to the next generation; it is a promise of protection that begins at the earliest horizon of life and never wavers as our children grow towards their full potential.
I stand -with conviction and total resolve- to fight to protect our unborn children because a person is a person no matter how small.
Child Exploitation
I am determined to combat child exploitation at every level - from strengthening federal law enforcement and holding digital platforms accountable to ensuring our local communities have the resources needed to rescue and protect the most vulnerable among us.
Online Safety
I am fully committed to championing federal safeguards that prioritize children’s mental health over digital platform profits and ensure a secure digital future for our families.
I will advocate for legislation like the Kids Online Safety Act that holds Big Tech accountable by establishing a ‘Duty of Care’ to protect our children from addictive algorithms and harmful content.
Family and Parental Rights
I stand firmly behind the principle that the right of parents to direct the upbringing, education, and healthcare of their children is a fundamental liberty that must be protected from government overreach by the highest legal standards.
Life Altering
My resolve is to ensure that kids can simply be kids, free from the pressure of making permanent choices before they reach maturity.
I stand firm against any ideology or medical intervention that risks a child's future well-being, advocating instead for a foundation of protection that allows them to develop fully and naturally.
Protecting Girls Sports
I am a steadfast advocate for protecting the integrity of female athletics, believing that common-sense rules must be maintained to protect girls' sports for biological females.
We must ensure that our daughters have a fair and level playing field where their hard work and dedication are rewarded in competitions designed specifically for them.
