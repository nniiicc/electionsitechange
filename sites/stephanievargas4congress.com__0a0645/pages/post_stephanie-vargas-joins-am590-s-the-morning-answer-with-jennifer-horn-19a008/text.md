Stephanie Vargas Joins AM590’s “The Morning Answer” with Jennifer Horn
This week, congressional candidate Stephanie Vargas joined Jennifer Horn on The Morning Answer radio broadcast to share her heart, her story, and her vision for California’s 33rd District.
The conversation gave listeners a deeper look into why Stephanie stepped forward to run for the U.S.
House of Representatives — and why she believes the district is ready for bold, authentic leadership.
Why Stephanie Chose to Run
During the interview, Stephanie opened up about the moment she realized she could no longer sit on the sidelines.
As a professional woman serving as Chief Deputy City Clerk, and as a wife, mother, and lifelong community member, she had grown tired of watching politicians act like politicians while families struggled with real‑world challenges.
Stephanie explained that her journey didn’t begin with politics — it began in prayer.
She prayed for California, for the nation, and for direction.
In that quiet place, she said the Lord gave her vision, inspiration, and the courage to run.
Even though she faced fears and apprehensions, she stepped forward in faith.
Finding Courage — and Inspiring Others
Stephanie shared that as she continued speaking up, her courage grew.
She realized she wasn’t alone — countless people in the community feel the same frustrations but are afraid to speak out.
Her message on the broadcast was clear: it’s time for everyday people to find their voice again.
Fighting for What Truly Matters
On the show, Stephanie outlined the issues she believes are critical to the future of District 33:
- Lowering the cost of living
- Reducing gas and energy prices
- Cutting the bureaucracy to make building more affordable
- Prioritzing Public Safety and Addressing the root causes of Homelessness
Stephanie emphasized that the district deserves long‑term, meaningful change — not band‑aids or political talking points.
She is committed to fighting for solutions that will actually shape a better future for families, children, and the entire community.
A Campaign Rooted in Faith, Courage, and Community
Stephanie’s appearance on The Morning Answer highlighted what sets her apart: her authenticity, her faith, and her deep love for the district she calls home.
Her story resonated with listeners because it reflects the experiences of so many — people who want safer streets, affordable living, and leaders who will stand up for what truly matters.
Stephanie Vargas is stepping forward with boldness, conviction, and a heart for service — ready to fight for the future of California’s 33rd District.
