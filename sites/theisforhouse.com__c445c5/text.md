About Geri
Ready for St.
Paul.
Geri Theis is a Redwood Falls resident, real estate broker, mom, and grandmother who has spent her life working alongside the families and small businesses that make our corner of Minnesota strong.
As a local real estate professional, Geri understands the economic challenges facing rural communities — rising costs, shrinking opportunity, and a St.
Paul that too often ignores life outside the metro.
Together with her husband Corey Theis, a Redwood County Commissioner, their family has remained actively engaged in serving and strengthening local communities for years.
Geri earned the Republican endorsement on the first ballot — a clear sign that grassroots Republicans across District 15B trust her to carry their values forward.
