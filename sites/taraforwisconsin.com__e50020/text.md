ABOUT
Tara Johnson represents Wisconsin’s 96th Assembly District, bringing decades of experience in public service and community leadership to Madison.
She approaches the job the same way she always has: by listening, showing up, and staying focused on the issues that make a real difference in people’s everyday lives.
In her first term, Tara has worked to make life more affordable, strengthen public education, expand access to healthcare, support working families, and make sure western Wisconsin gets the attention and investment it deserves.
She knows there is still plenty more to do—and she is running for reelection to keep that work going.

PRIORITIES
Tara is focused on lowering everyday costs, strengthening public schools, expanding access to healthcare, affordable childcare, standing with working people, and investing in western Wisconsin communities.
She will continue fighting to protect reproductive freedom and our democracy, support rural communities and family farms, strengthen infrastructure, and preserve Wisconsin’s land and water for future generations.
In the State Assembly, Tara will keep showing up, listening, and fighting for the people of western Wisconsin.
