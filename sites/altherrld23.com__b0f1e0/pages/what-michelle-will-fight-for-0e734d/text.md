Border
Wall
The primary use for walls in the Bible was for the protection from those who lived outside the city.
They kept unauthorized people from entering as well as protected the people from attack.
With everything else in today’s society, the meaning and purpose of a border wall has been changed to represent the opposite of what the Bible says.
Some no longer see it as a means of protection, but as a barrier to inclusivity.
In actuality, it is an effective deterrent to illegal entry into our country.

The same means that was initially used to make this country prosperous.
The American people should not be forced to carry the financial burden of this country due to corruption at all levels of government.
