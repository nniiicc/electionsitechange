I was an adjudicator for the 2020 election and participated in the canvas to help clean up the voter rolls.
I was involved with the Senate Audit from day one, initially as an observer and then became a table manager.
I continued to stay involved and actively supported Conservative candidates in 2022 and served as the Marshal for the 2022 Primary and General elections.
I was a delegate at the Republican Convention in 2024, and I currently serve as the Secretary for the Maricopa County Republican Committee as well as an AZGOP Member-at-Large.
The issues that I witnessed regarding our election process in 2020 drove me to become even more involved which is why I ran for office in 2024.
I am stepping forward once again to run for the Arizona State Senate, not out of ambition but out of conviction.
I am a grassroots patriot who stands with and for ‘We the people’ and will strive to bring servant leadership back to this elected office.
I will be guided by strong Biblical principles and the Constitution and will listen to and represent the constituents of Legislative District 23.
I served in the Air Force for 15 years and was always recognized for my ability to work with people to get the job done, without compromising my integrity.
One of the positions I held was as liaison between the Command Staff and the enlisted personnel.
I am not a stranger to standing up and being the voice for those I serve.
My vocation is in fashion design, but I have chosen to lay it down for now because nothing matters if we do not right this ship.
I support every Conservative cause such as Protecting the Children, Secure Borders, Legal Immigration, and especially Election Integrity which is the foundation upon which all else is built.
I am married to my husband Frank of 36 years.
I have been blessed with an amazing son, an awesome daughter-in-law and two beautiful grandsons.
I raised my son in a time when society was not trying to dictate who he was to become.
I am now in this fight to protect my grandsons and all children from the snares of what society has become.
