17 I am proud to endorse and support Mary Insprucker for North Carolina House District 36!
House District 36 is located within my Senate District, and I know how important it is to elect strong leaders who understand the financial pressures facing our families, seniors, and working North Carolinians.
Mary is the Affordability Candidate.
Her passion for lowering taxes, responsibly managing state programs, eliminating wasteful spending, and helping keep everyday life affordable is exactly why I support her.
At a time when families are watching the cost of housing, groceries, utilities, insurance, and nearly everything else rise, we need legislators who understand that government must respect every taxpayer dollar.
Mary will work hard for you.
She will listen.
She will advocate for responsible government.
And she will fight to make sure North Carolina remains a place where families can live, work, raise their children, retire, and prosper without being taxed out of the communities they call home House District 36—Mary Insprucker needs your vote!
