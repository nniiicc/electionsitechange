Join Our Team Meet Greg Greg Vital is a proven businessman and job creator, a nationally recognized land and wildlife conservationist, and a philanthropist committed to the education of young people in Southeast Tennessee.
As a conservative Republican seeking Tennessee's House District 29 seat following the passing of Rep.
Mike Carter, Greg is prepared to take his experience and vision to Nashville to serve the people of District 29.
Greg co-founded Morning Pointe Senior Living #ago.
The company operates more than 35 senior care and Alzheimer's care centers in five Mid-South states, employs more than 1,600 people - 400 in Hamilton County - and its corporate headquarters is located in Ooltewah – the heart of District 29.
As the recipient of the Hamilton County Republican Party’s “Chairman’s Award” and the Tennessee Republican Party’s “Statesman of the Year” for Tennessee’s Third District, Greg is committed to the conservative principles that make Tennessee a national leader in fiscal stewardship.
