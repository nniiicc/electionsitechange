General Michael Flynn Endorses Shamp for State Senate
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE:
Retired General Michael Flynn has endorsed Janae Shamp for State Senate in Arizona’s 29th Legislative District.
“Janae Shamp is exactly the kind of person who will help save our country from do-nothing RINOs and anti-American socialists.
She is a registered nurse, and her life story is one of strength and resolve because she relies on faith to guide her.
Janae Shamp is passionate, patriotic, and she won't be corrupted by the political elite who want to run our lives.
I give Janae Shamp my full endorsement!”
Michael T Flynn
LTG (R) USA
ABOUT GEN FLYNN:
With more than 33 years of service in the United States military, General Flynn’s military career culminated as the Director of the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) and as the nation’s highest serving military intelligence officer.
His public service included selection and service as a foreign policy advisor to several Republican U.S. presidential
candidates (2016) and subsequent appointment and service as the National Security Advisor to the 45th President of the United States of America.
After retiring from the Army in 2014 and as a private citizen, General Flynn went on to serve in a variety of business, educational, and non-profit roles, to include supporting veterans’ organizations around the country, something he continues to do today.
He is currently the Chairman of America’s Future.
General Flynn is a national bestselling author, holds three master’s degrees, and is recipient of numerous military, intelligence and law enforcement awards.
He is married with two sons and has several grandchildren.
