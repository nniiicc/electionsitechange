Haaland to Kick Off Statewide Tour Thursday
Haaland will travel the state to meet & hear from New Mexicans.
Albuquerque, N.M. – On Thursday, February 13, 2025, Deb Haaland, candidate for New Mexico governor, will kick off a statewide campaign launch tour.
She will travel the state to listen to New Mexicans and learn about their struggles and ideas for solutions that fit their communities.
The tour includes 19 stops in small towns and cities across New Mexico throughout February and into March.
The kick-off will take place in Albuquerque at the National Hispanic Cultural Center.
Doors will open at 5:30 p.m.
MT.
Haaland launched her campaign with a video highlighting her commitment to lowering costs, addressing crime and promoting healthy communities.
Press interested in covering the Albuquerque event should RSVP by Thursday, February 13, 2025 at 10 a.m.
MT.
