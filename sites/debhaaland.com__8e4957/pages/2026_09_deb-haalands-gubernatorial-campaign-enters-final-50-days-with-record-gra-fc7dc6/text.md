Deb Haaland’s Gubernatorial Campaign Enters Final 50 Days with Record Grassroots Support
Albuquerque, N.M. – Deb Haaland’s campaign for governor just posted a massive $3.4 million haul this reporting period — powered by tens of thousands of grassroots, small-dollar donors chipping in again and again.
This puts her total fundraising at $17.3 million, built on more than 446,600 total contributions and counting.
Deb Haaland’s momentum by the numbers for this reporting period:
✅ $21.78 average online donation
✅ Over 13,300 contributions from New Mexicans
✅ Over 96% of online donations less than $100
✅ Over $3.4 million cash on hand
Since the general election, Haaland has continued to travel the state on her Roadrunnin’ Tour to visit all 33 counties.
Deb Haaland released the following statement:
“I know firsthand how hard people work to earn every paycheck, and I am deeply grateful for everyone who shares my vision for New Mexico.
I am committed to standing up for New Mexicans, pushing back against Donald Trump, and bringing down the cost of living so families don’t have to choose between paying the rent or buying groceries in the same month.
From now until November, I will continue to travel across the state meeting directly with New Mexicans, because this campaign is about building a New Mexico that works for everyone.”
