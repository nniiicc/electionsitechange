“New Mexicans care about animal protection issues and seek out policymakers who value the role animals play in our lives.
We are delighted to endorse Deb Haaland for Governor of New Mexico based on her experience and proven track record on pro-animal policies.
Haaland is uniquely qualified and committed to improving outcomes for animals, families, and communities in our state,” said Stacy Sutton Kerby, Chief Government Affairs Officer for APV.
“The balance of nature and protection of our traditions in New Mexico, relies on protecting our animal relatives.
Whether it’s protecting endangered species or keeping habitats whole, I carry that value and strengthen the rights of animals in every position I serve in,” said Haaland.
“I am honored to have the support of Animal Protection Voters New Mexico, and will continue to protect the rights of animals by promoting the humane treatment of all animals.” Animal Protection Voters New Mexico works to reduce animal suffering and pass laws that protect animals.
Throughout her career, Haaland has sponsored and voted for legislation aimed at protecting wildlife.
In Congress, Haaland co-sponsored multiple bills to end horse slaughtering, address private possession with big cats, and strengthen federal prohibitions on extreme animal cruelty.
She earned a perfect score from Animal Protection Voters for her work fighting for animal welfare.
As Secretary of the Interior, Haaland worked to support wild horse management and conservation efforts to protect lands for our animals and removed several species from the endangered list.
Throughout her campaign, Haaland has traveled to all 33 counties in New Mexico where she met with New Mexicans in their community.
Her plans for affordability, education, public safety, and healthcare can be found at debhaaland.com/debsplan.
