Public service: a family value
I’m Veronica Klinefelt, a proud Democrat, Army brat and 30-year resident of Macomb County.
As a mother of 4 who sent her kids to East Detroit Public Schools, I’ve found no community I’d rather serve than this one.
I am serving my first term in the State Senate, and now running for re-election.

A passion for good government
As a mom, I decided to run for local school board in 1998 when I saw our children’s education being shortchanged.
I discovered a talent for scouring budgets and bills and used this talent to expose fraud of over $3 million.
The subsequent FBI investigation resulted in nearly 20 indictments.
Local experience
The underfunding of our schools was only the tip of the iceberg.
This motivated me to run for Eastpointe City Council.
I pushed to rebuild infrastructure that was neglected for years.
We updated facilities, improved local roads, and replaced crumbling pipes.
I also helped the City weather the Great Recession by renegotiating contracts.

National leadership
After serving eight years on city council, I ran for a seat on the Macomb County Board of Commissioners to take on the complex issues Macomb was facing.
In my ten years as a Commissioner, I’ve fought for a strong community mental health system, a veterans’ services department that rivals any in the nation, and a 911 system with updated technology capabilities.
Serving on the 8 ½ Mile Drain Board, I’ve approved major projects to help clean our waterways.
As the President of the Michigan Association of Counties, I advocated at the state and national level to bring more dollars back to local governments.
As one of two Michigan representatives on the National Association of Counties Board of Directors, I have traveled to the Capitol and White House to fight for issues unique to Michigan and the Metro Detroit area in particular.
Fighting for you in Lansing
After serving 24 years at the local level, I brought my experience and knowledge to Lansing.
I promise to keep working to invest in local infrastructure and listening to our local communities.
My passion isn’t government, it’s good government: government that keeps sewage out of our lakes, helps us retain great teachers, supports our firefighters and police officers, and funds our parks and community centers.
I will work with anyone who wants to build vibrant communities.
