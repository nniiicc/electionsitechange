Department Of Unemployment Assistance
The DUA oversees the provision of unemployment income assistance to eligible Massachusetts Residents.
Visit this website to see if you are eligible for unemployment income or unemployment insurance.
Finding A Job
The Massachusetts Website provides a list of resources to aid in finding a job.
You can visit this website to find information on a variety of jobs to apply for and methods to apply for these occupations.
Additionally, if you need help accessing or utilizing any of these resources or have any other questions in general, please email me at Jay.Livingstone@mahouse.gov and my staff and I will be happy to assist.
