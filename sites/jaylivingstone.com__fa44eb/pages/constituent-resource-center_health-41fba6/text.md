MassHealth
MassHealth is Massachusetts combined health assistance program that includes both Medicaid and the Children’s Health Insurance Program.
To find applications to become a MassHealth member, visit this link, and to find more information about MassHealth or find answers to your questions, you may visit this site.
COVID-19 Vaccinations and Testing
To get vaccinated for COVID-19, visit the VaxFinder on the mass.gov website, which will show locations nearby to you where the vaccine is available.
Additionally, for free COVID-19 testing, you can visit this website that provides nearby sites to get tested.
Additionally, if you need help accessing or utilizing any of these resources or have any other questions in general, please email me at Jay.Livingstone@mahouse.gov and my staff and I will be happy to assist.
