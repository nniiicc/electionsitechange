Parks
The City of Boston website has a list of parks and recreational facilities in Boston that you can visit and spend time at.
They also provide a map of all of the parks in the Greater Boston Area.
Exercise
The Boston Parks Department offers free fitness programming every day from June 5th through October 1st.
Visit this site to find a list of the available le exercise programming and classes.
For additional activities, the parks department also has an activity finder for the Boston area linked here.
