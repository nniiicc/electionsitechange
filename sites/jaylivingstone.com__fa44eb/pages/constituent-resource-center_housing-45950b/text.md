Homeless Services
The regularly updated Boston Government Website has a list of homeless services including everything from overnight and day shelters in the Boston area to meal assistance, outreach services, and help with finding permanent housing.
Housing Assistance Programs
For Emergency Housing Assistance, you can visit this site which provides information and help on how to apply to Emergency Housing Programs and find service providers near you, and you can fill out this page to find an Emergency Housing Provider,
Additionally, if you need help accessing or utilizing any of these resources or have any other questions in general, please email me at Jay.Livingstone@mahouse.gov and my staff and I will be happy to assist.
