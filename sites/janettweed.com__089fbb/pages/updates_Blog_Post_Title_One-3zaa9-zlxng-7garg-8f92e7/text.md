A gigantic perk of living in NYS's 102nd district is our access to year-round farmer's markets
The opportunity to say hello to our neighbors and connect directly with the local people who grow our veg, tend chickens, harvest honey and provide us with sustainably produced meat and cheese — connecting with community — not only helps to manage stress, but has been proven to reduce the risk of heart disease, stroke and dementia.
(Source: CDC.gov)
