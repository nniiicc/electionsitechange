Becky Cash, her husband Chris, and their six kids have called Boone County home for over 20 years.
Becky and Chris are the proud owners of a nutrition business and, for 17 years, have been dedicated to helping parents navigate services, educational options, and resources for children with special needs.
Rooted in faith, Becky’s family are active members at St.
Alphonsus Liguori Catholic Church.
Becky Listens, Rolls Up Her Sleeves and Delivers Results.
Protecting Taxpayers, Pushing for Reforms Becky Cash delivered $1.3 billion in real property tax relief starting this year.
As a result, 80% of homeowners in Hendricks and Boone counties will pay less in 2026 than 2025.
Most importantly, Becky supports long-term reform for a system that simply isn’t working anymore for Hoosier homeowners.
That means reining in local government debt, stopping dramatic increases, and fixing our assessment process.
Supporting Schools, Empowering Parents Becky voted to increase K-12 funding by $640 million in 2026 and 2027 — a 5% increase at a time when most state agencies were asked to cut spending by 5%.
At the same time, Becky empowered parents to choose the right education fit for their kids and expanded access to on-the-job training, internships and apprenticeships for Hoosier students.
Because all children should have a pathway to career success, even those not bound for a four-year college.
Keeping Our Communities Safe Becky will always stand with our first responders.
That’s why she supported legislation to keep dangerous offenders behind bars while awaiting trial and voted to increase oversight for Marion County courts.
Becky is the only candidate in this race endorsed by Boone County Sheriff Tony Harris, Hendricks County Sheriff Jack Sadler, the Indiana State Police Alliance and the Indiana Fraternal Order of Police.
