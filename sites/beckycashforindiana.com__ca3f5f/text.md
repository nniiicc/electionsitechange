Representative Becky Cash is Dedicated to Serving House District 25
Becky Cash has a proven record of advocating for Lower Taxes, Access to Affordable Childcare, Local Control, Clean Water, Public Safety, Parental Rights, Health Freedom, and Lowering Utility Costs.
Becky has a proven record of ....
- Protecting Children in Schools and Institutions (HEA 1412, HB 1413, HB 1073, HEA 1285)
- Passing Legislation and Funding to test Indiana's Rape Kits (HEA 1001, HB 1413)
- Working to protect our water from PFAS chemicals (HB 1110, HB 1286)
- Fiscally responsible childcare tax credit expansion (HEA 1177)
- Stopping efforts to eliminate long-standing protections for children with diabetes in schools.
(Hunter's Law, HB 1004)
- Voting for Lower Taxes and voting against Wheel Taxes and Toll Roads (HEA 1461)
She has also worked to amend legislation to protect the rights of students going into health careers, protect our water quality, and protect compounding pharmacists, just to name a few.
Becky is keeping her promises and working for ALL Hoosiers in District 25.
