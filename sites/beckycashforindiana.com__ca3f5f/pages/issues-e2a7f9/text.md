Becky Cash has a Record of Compassionate Conservative Leadership
Becky Cash believes government has a responsibility to protect those who cannot always protect themselves while ensuring that we empower those who can and preserve the liberties of all.
Her legislative work reflects a consistent commitment to defending vulnerable Hoosiers while promoting personal responsibility, strengthening families, and ensuring taxpayer dollars are used effectively.
Whether advocating for survivors of sexual assault, protecting children with disabilities, supporting families, or improving care for seniors, Becky has built a legislative record focused on delivering meaningful results for those who need it most.
Data Centers
Becky does not believe Hoosier taxpayers should be asked to subsidize massive data center developments through special tax abatements and incentives while families and small businesses continue to shoulder their own tax burden.
Indiana should welcome innovation and economic development, but those projects should provide a clear benefit to the communities where they locate and should not shift their costs onto taxpayers or existing utility customers.
New data center development must:
- Protects taxpayers by ensuring that data centers pay their share of taxes.
- Protects utility customers from having to pay for infrastructure and energy demands created by massive data centers.
- Protects Indiana's water supply by ensuring there is adequate water supply in the region and by ensuring that adequate, clean water returns to the watershed.
- Preserves local control so communities have a meaningful voice in where data centers are located and how they are developed.
- Requires transparency regarding water use, electrical demand, infrastructure costs, and the impact on surrounding communities prior to starting development.
- Protects farmland, neighborhoods, and quality of life as Indiana continues to grow.
Becky believes Indiana can be a leader in technology and innovation without giving large corporations a blank check or asking Hoosier families to pick up the tab.
The purpose of growth must always be benefiting the community and keeping Indiana a place where people want to raise families and grow old.

Protection of the Vulnerable
One of Becky Cash's highest priorities has been standing up for Hoosiers who need someone to stand with them.
Throughout her time in the Indiana House of Representatives, Becky has authored and supported legislation focused on protecting children, supporting survivors of sexual assault, defending individuals with disabilities, strengthening families, and improving care for seniors.
Standing with Survivors of Sexual Assault
Becky has been a leading advocate for improving Indiana's response to sexual assault.
- Authored legislation to eliminate Indiana's rape kit backlog by providing funding for testing, increasing transparency, and helping survivors receive justice more quickly.
- Secured funding for rape kit processing so evidence can be tested promptly and offenders can be held accountable.
As a State Representative, Becky has worked closely with the Indiana State Police to carryout this effort.
- Successfully secured an interim legislative study to develop a statewide Sexual Assault Response Plan that will help ensure survivors receive consistent, compassionate, and professional care regardless of where they live.
Protecting Children with Disabilities
Every child deserves to be safe at school.
Becky authored legislation to:
- Require cameras in special education classrooms, sensory rooms, and seclusion areas.
- Give parents access to video when concerns arise.
- Strengthen accountability and improve protections for students with disabilities.
Supporting Children with Diabetes
Becky worked to preserve important protections for students living with diabetes by supporting legislation that ensures children can safely manage their medical needs while attending school.
She has been applauded by diabetes organizations and parent groups on a state and national level for ensuring that Hunter's Law was secured.
Strengthening Families Through Child Care
Recognizing that affordable child care is essential for working families, Becky authored legislation expanding Indiana's employer child care tax credit, encouraging businesses to invest in child care solutions that help parents remain in the workforce.
Improving Mental Health Services
Becky has supported expanding access to community mental health resources, helping connect more Hoosiers with the care and services they need.

Caring for Premature Infants

Lowering Taxes to Support Hoosier Families
While Indiana boasts some of the lowest taxes in the country, we must strive to further reduce the tax burden on our residents.

I am committed to finding solutions to fund essential services like law enforcement, firefighters, local governments, and schools while easing the financial strain on House District 25 residents.

Parental Rights
I believe that parents have the rights and responsibilities to make decisions for their children.
No entity, including and especially the Government should infringe on those rights unless it is proven that a child is being abused.
As a mother of 6, ranging in age from 12 years old to 24 years old, I have both experienced and seen what happens when the rights of parents are infringed upon.
Parents have the right to make educational, medical and moral decisions for their minor children.
Parents should always have the right to change their child's educational environment, get a second opinion or forego medical treatment, make end of life decisions for their child and to choose what and when their children learn about moral decisions.
The religious rights and beliefs of a family should never be imposed upon by the government.
