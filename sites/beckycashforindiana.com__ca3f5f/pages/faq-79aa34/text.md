Does Becky Cash want to make birth control and contraceptives illegal?
No, Becky Cash does not want to make birth control and contraceptives illegal.
She has never stated or published that she wishes to make birth control and other contraceptives illegal.
Becky is heartbroken to see this misinformation being spread to women who genuinely may be fearful, and she asks that you help clarify this position so that others are not fearful.
Becky Cash’s Record on Birth Control Claims that State Representative Becky Cash has voted to restrict or ban birth control do not reflect her voting record.
Her record includes: HB 1568 (2023) — NO: This bill allowed pharmacists to prescribe hormonal birth control without an individual prescription from a physician or other traditional prescriber.
Cash’s concern was with removing physician/prescriber involvement—not with banning or opposing contraception.
SB 252 (2023) — YES: Cash supported legislation improving access to long-acting reversible contraceptives (LARCs) for women enrolled in Medicaid.
HB 1426 (2024) — YES: Cash supported legislation ensuring eligible Medicaid patients giving birth could be offered long-acting contraception before leaving the hospital.
Bottom line: Becky Cash has not voted to ban birth control .
Her record shows votes supporting access to contraception, including for women on Medicaid.
Her vote against HB 1568 concerned who should be permitted to prescribe hormonal contraception , not whether women should have access to birth control.
Is Becky working to ban fertility treatments such as IVF?
NO.
Banning fertility treatments is not part of Becky Cash's platform and never has been.
Again, this is disinformation.
(IVF is protected under Indiana law).
Does Becky support life-saving medical treatment for pregnant women and girls, even if that means that the baby will not survive?
Yes.
Becky understands that conditions such as ectopic pregnancy, diagnosis of a life-threatening illness such as cancer or a variety of other medical emergencies or conditions may require that a woman not continue the pregnancy.
While this is always a tragic situation for the mother, this may result in premature delivery of a baby who is not viable or can not survive.
Becky values the life of every pregnant woman and girl.
