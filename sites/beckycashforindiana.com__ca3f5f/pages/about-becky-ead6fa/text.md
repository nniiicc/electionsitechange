Dedicated to Serving House District 25 State Rep.
Becky Cash of Zionsville was first elected in 2022 to serve House District 25, which includes portions of Boone and Hendricks counties.
Her commitment to our community remains steadfast.
About Becky Cash Community Leader & Advocate Becky and her husband, Chris, run a successful nutrition business.
As a naturopathic practitioner, Becky is passionate about health and wellness.
With over 17 years of experience, Becky has dedicated herself to helping parents navigate services for children with special needs, explore educational options, and understand their rights.
In addition, as the mother of children with special and medical needs herself, Becky understands the importance of individuals and families having the freedom to explore the medical options that enable their loved ones to thrive.
Becky is passionate about meeting the educational needs of every child.
In her time in the legislature, she has both fought to improve our public schools while also supporting school choice.
Involvement & Service Becky has served on the board of advisors for St.
Ambrose Academy and the H2 Autism Foundation.
She has been actively involved with Purpose 4 My Pain, A Kid Again, Juvenile Diabetes Research Foundation, Make-a-Wish, Muscular Dystrophy Association, United Mitochondrial Disease Foundation, The Lords Pantry, St.
Vincent de Paul, and other charitable organizations.
Education & Affiliations Becky earned a bachelor's degree from Bowling Green State University, and she has a certification in Youth Ministry from Steubenville University.
She also has a certification in holistic healthcare.
She is a proud member of the Boone County Republican Women, Indiana Federation of Republican Women, and Stand for Health Freedom.
Personal Life Becky has lived in Zionsville (on the border of Whitestown) with her husband and their six children, for 20 years.
Their family is deeply rooted in faith and active in ministry at St.
Alphonsus Liguori Catholic Church.
When Becky Cash is not in legislative session or advocating for her constituents, she spends her time caring for her children, taking long walks with her husband, helping others navigate their health journey, and enjoying the many events in the area.
Becky and her daughters especially enjoy visiting the coffee shops around town.
