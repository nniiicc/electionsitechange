Pamela Gordon is a neighbor rather than a career politician.
She is running for District 18 to ensure our rural perspective is never ignored at the Capitol.
As a lifelong Oklahoman, Pamela understands that the true heartbeat of this state is found in our small towns and rural communities.
She is stepping up because she has seen firsthand how distant bureaucracies and heavy-handed regulations put a squeeze on our families.
Pamela believes that District 18 deserves a representative who is rooted in the same values of hard work and faith that define our way of life here.
