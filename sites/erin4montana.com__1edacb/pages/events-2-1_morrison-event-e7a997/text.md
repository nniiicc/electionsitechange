Join us for a gathering in support of Erin Farris‑Olsen, a leader committed to thoughtful, practical progress for Senate District 41.
On Tuesday, February 3, from 5:30–7:00 pm, we’ll gather at the home of our generous hosts John Morrison and Cathy Wright for an evening of conversation, connection, and reflection on what we want to see change and what we don’t want to keep repeating.
Erin will share what’s motivating her run, the priorities guiding her campaign, and how she plans to represent Helena's SD41 and Montana values in the 2027 State Legislature.
There will be plenty of time for informal conversation and questions.
We’re grateful to be joined by an incredible group of co-hosts, including Wilmot Collins, Ann Waickman, Cole Mannix, John and Stefanie Ambrose Tubbs, Kief and Alisa Strorrar, and Katherine Hausrath.
This will be a relaxed, welcoming evening with good people and meaningful dialogue.
Guests are warmly encouraged to bring a friend who may be interested.
