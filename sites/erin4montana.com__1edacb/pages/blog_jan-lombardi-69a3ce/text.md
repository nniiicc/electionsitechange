Jan Lombardi
Erin Farris-Olsen understands the challenges working families face.
Although I no longer live in Helena, I keep close track of what's happening in my hometown.
Something that excites me is Erin Farris-Olsen, who is running for the Montana Legislature and would have been my representative.
And a darn good one.
Erin is a problem solver.
She understands the challenges working families face today, with kiddos in school, aging parents and a tough economy.
Erin’s legal background and diverse work experience sets her up to be a good representative for her district.
I believe it’s time for old timers, like me, to open the door and welcome the next generation of leaders. like Erin.
I encourage Helenans in Senate District 41 to vote for Erin Farris-Olsen in this primary election.
