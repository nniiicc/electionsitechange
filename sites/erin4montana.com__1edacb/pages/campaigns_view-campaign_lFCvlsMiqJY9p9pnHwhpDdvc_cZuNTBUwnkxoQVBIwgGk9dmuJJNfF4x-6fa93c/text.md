As summer winds down, we’re ramping up for the general election.
While I am eager to begin drafting policy and building relationships with fellow legislators, I still have another election to win.
We are going to do that the same way we have built this campaign from the beginning: by talking directly with people.
We are hosting town halls, knocking doors, and expandinf our peer-to-peer outreach across the district.
Most importantly, we will stay focused on what I hear again and again from the people I meet: we want Montana to be a place where our families can thrive.
That means making it easier to afford a home, supporting strong public schools, protecting access to healthcare and clean water, defending our freedoms, and building an economy that works for people who live and work here.
I’m grateful to have the support of my primary opponent, Dana Toole.
Dana has spent decades serving Montana and knows as well as anyone that if we want to make progress, we have to work together.
I’m proud that after a competitive primary, we are coming together around the work ahead.
Support MaryAnn Dunwell for Senate District 42!
We must hold the line.
In addition to keeping SD 41 blue, there are three very important races to support this cycle so that we can keep and possibly grow our voting block in the Montana Senate.
Remember, it was 18 Democrats in the Montana Senate that renewed Medicaid, protected our impartial courts, and more.
MaryAnn Dunwell cast a critical vote on all of our accomplishments.
We need her to return and she needs our help!
As a result of redistricting, SD 42 has a very close margin.
We need MaryAnn and she needs our support.
I hope you will donate , volunteer, and reach out to her campaign to offer your support in whatever way that you can.
Rob and I are personally pitching in monthly so MaryAnn can count on our support as she makes critical campaign decisions.
Two other close races to track are Scott Rosenzweig , (SD 29- Park & Gallatin Counties) and Margaret Mitchell (SD 10- Great Falls).
We have the chance to turn 18 votes into 20 and keep bringing balance and compromise to the Montana State Senate.
What We're Doing… Defining the Campaign Platform This June the Montana Democratic Party met to discuss their platform priorities for 2026.
You can find the updated platform HERE .
Building on the work of the Montana Democratic Party, this campaign is putting forward the first draft of the THRIVE Platform, which is a focused list of priorities shaped by what I am hearing from constituents, the issues I anticipate will come before the 2027 Legislature, and initiatves that I can confidently champion as a Senator for SD 41.
This is a starting point and I look forward to hearing your feedback and continuing to refine a vision for a Montana where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
