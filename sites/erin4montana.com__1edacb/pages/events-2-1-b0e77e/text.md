Thank you for your vote.
Thank you for hosting a yard sign, donating, endorsing, co hosting events, knocking doors, making phone calls, writing postcards, and showing up in all the ways that keep a campaign moving.
Every single contribution has made a difference and we couldn't have built this campaign without it.
Final ways to take action: -> Sunday, 12:00p Group Canvass- Let me know if you can join us (406) 461-8530)! -> Monday & Tuesday Text & Phone Bank for Ballot Return- Let me know if you want to come by and make some calls or I can get you a list to call/text on your own! -> Let your people know that it is too late to mail ballots in.
Encourage people to track their ballot HERE .
We started out with a vision to be the change so many of us have been waiting for.
If we want to protect our democracy, we need to do more than react.
We need to show up earlier, tell our stories better, reach beyond our usual base, and make people feel like politics belongs to them again.
We did.
Through 3,500 doors , 13 events , 2,000 text messages , 22,000 mailers , 700 hand written peer to peer postcards , and many, many, conversations we are approaching election day with inspiration, support, and more than a darn good chance at victory.
I hope you will join us after polls close at 8:00p on Election Day.
If I don't see you between now and Tuesday, please know that I appreciate your support from the bottom of my heart and promise to continue to build on what we've accomplished one way or another.
