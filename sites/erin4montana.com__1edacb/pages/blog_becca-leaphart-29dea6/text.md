Becca Leaphart
Please join me in supporting Erin Farris-Olsen for Senate District 41.
I know Erin as a neighbor and fellow public school parent.
Most weekday mornings, we greet each other on the trail as we walk our kids to school.
In those conversations, Erin shows up as curious, energetic, and deeply committed to making Montana a place where all people can thrive.
Those qualities, paired with her experience, are exactly what we need in our state legislature.
Erin understands what it’s like to raise a family in this polarized and unpredictable political moment.
She knows firsthand the strain of expensive healthcare and childcare, underfunded public schools, and the growing challenge of finding affordable housing.
As a first-generation college graduate, she knows how transformative access to quality education can be, and she’s dedicated to making sure every Montana kid has the same opportunity.
Through her work as an attorney and public policy advocate, Erin has demonstrated a commitment to the principles Montanans value: fair and nonpartisan courts, strong public institutions, and our unique constitutional right to privacy.
I trust Erin to stand up for my family’s future, and I hope you’ll join me in supporting her.
