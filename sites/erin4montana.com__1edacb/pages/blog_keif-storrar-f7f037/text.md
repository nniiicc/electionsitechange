As Helena residents, we see firsthand how decisions made at the state level shape our daily lives.
That's why we need someone who understands how our courts and public institutions function in the real world.
I'm supporting Erin Farris-Olsen because she recognizes the same Montana we do: people working hard, raising families, planning for retirement, and trying to make a future here.
She understands that government should be accountable to the people it serves, not just for the powerful few.
Erin has built her career around that understanding.
During her time as a clerk for the Montana Supreme Court, she saw how legal decisions impact communities across the state.
She has also worked to help ordinary Montanans better navigate the legal system, practical work that makes a real difference when people need it most.
What stands out most is her sense of fairness.
She respects the role of our courts and believes they should apply the law consistently, without political pressure.
At a time when confidence in public institutions is under strain, that kind of approach matters.
She isn't chasing headlines or offering easy answers.
She represents the kind of steady, level-headed leadership Helena needs.
Erin understands that the decisions being made at the Capitol impact our community, and she will bring that understanding to every vote she casts.
Erin Farris-Olsen has already done the work.
Now Helena needs her at the Capitol.
