I support Erin Farris-Olsen for Senate District 41.
Over the course of this spring, Erin has knocked on countless doors to better understand the concerns and priorities of her neighbors throughout the district.
She is a thoughtful listener and an effective communicator, and she has made herself accessible to voters through informal public visiting sessions.

I am confident she will work tirelessly to represent Helena residents and thoughtfully address our community's needs.
