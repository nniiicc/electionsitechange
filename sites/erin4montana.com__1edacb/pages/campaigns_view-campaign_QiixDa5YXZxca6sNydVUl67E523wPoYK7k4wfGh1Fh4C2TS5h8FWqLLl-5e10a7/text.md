Campaigns of particular note are New York mayoral race, Virginia’s gubernatorial race, and Kalispell.
People are excited to see a new generation of leaders stepping up and finding success.
There are many people that have been struggling lately.
There are aging parents, struggling businesses, COVID, and sick kiddos.
Politics is not always top of mind, even for those of us who are actively involved.
Finding ways for people to give what they can, when they can, to defend our democracy will be key to our success.
Affordability is top of minds for many.
For that reason, this article caught my eye and provides an important directive for candidates like myself.
People want more than promises.
People want policies that work.
When asked about what I will do about affordability, I have to be honest with people that Montana is headed into another deficit in 2027.
What I will do is make sure that we don’t let just one factor (achem, property taxes) have all the limelight.
We need to address fairness in property, income, and other potential tax proposals.
It’s no secret that there are people that would like Montana to have a sales tax.
I disagree.
A sales tax is a regressive tax and we must do whatever we can to hold the line against putting Montana on that downhill trajectory. | | | What we’re doing… | Celebrating Veteran’s Day with the Magpie Drummers!

What an opportunity).
We have so much talent in this community and I look forward to plugging people into the campaign and during eight years in the Montana Senate. | | | | | | What we’re cooking… | Soup season is here and as I ramp up campaign activities for the end of the year, making a Sunday batch of soup for my family and our friends can help keep us healthy and going all week long.
Consider making homemade veggie stock for this.
It’s SOOOO worth it.
After learning about the added depth of flavor from homemade stock, I started a practice of saving veggie scraps (garlic and onion ends, carrots, herbs, cauliflower stems, etc.) in a zip lock bag throughout the week and using my instant pot for a 30 min quick broth.
It’s easier than you think with less food and packaging waste than prepared stock options.
