Proven Results.
Delivering Results for Colorado Since taking office in 2023, I have passed more than 100 bills to lower costs, strengthen communities, and prepare Colorado for the future.
As the Chair of the House Appropriations Committee and member of the Joint Budget Committee, I am committed to making government work for the people it serves while investing in Colorado’s long-term success.
Affordable Health Care I believe health care is a right, not a privilege.
Throughout my career, I have consistently fought to lower costs, expand access to care, and protect patients.
In the legislature, I have worked to strengthen Colorado’s Public Option, reduce medical debt, expand affordable coverage, protect reproductive health care, and improve access to behavioral health services.
As Vice Chair of the Medicaid Oversight and Advisory Commission, I help oversee one of Colorado’s largest public programs while working to protect health coverage for hundreds of thousands of Coloradans threatened by recent federal Medicaid cuts and administrative barriers.
Strong Communities: Housing, Wildfire Recovery, and Public Safety I am committed to making Colorado communities safer, more affordable, and more resilient.
Following the Marshall Fire, I helped secure millions of dollars in recovery funding, remove barriers for families rebuilding their homes, strengthen protections for renters, and improve accountability for insurance companies and other entities involved in disaster recovery.
I still continue to champion policies that improve wildfire resilience and help communities prepare for future disasters.
I also believe everyone deserves an affordable place to live.
I have supported investments in affordable housing, strengthened tools to increase housing supply, and backed legislation to help communities address Colorado’s housing shortage while keeping families in the neighborhoods they love.
I have supported commonsense gun violence prevention measures that keep schools, public spaces, and neighborhoods safe while respecting responsible firearm ownership.
Climate Leadership As a scientist, I understand that climate change is already impacting Colorado through more destructive wildfires, extreme heat, drought, and severe weather.
I have championed legislation to expand clean energy, modernize Colorado’s electric grid, regulate data centers, improve climate resilience, and protect Colorado’s air, water, and public lands.
I believe Colorado must continue leading with science-based policies that both reduce emissions and prepare communities for the challenges ahead.
Protecting Colorado Values I believe every Coloradan deserves to be treated with dignity, fairness, and respect.
I have fought to protect reproductive freedom, defend LGBTQIA+ Coloradans, expand access to gender-affirming care, advance racial equity, and ensure that everyone has the opportunity to succeed regardless of who they are or where they come from.
I believe Colorado is strongest when every person can fully participate in their community without fear of discrimination.
Investing in Colorado’s Future Colorado’s economy depends on strong schools, safe roads, affordable health care, and reliable public services.
I believe the state must have the ability to make the investments necessary to meet today’s challenges and tomorrow’s opportunities.
My service on the Joint Budget Committee showed firsthand how TABOR continues to restrict Colorado’s ability to invest in its future.
I support ending TABOR so Colorado can fully fund public education, improve infrastructure, expand housing opportunities, strengthen health care, and build an economy that works for everyone.
A Lifetime of Public Service Before serving in the legislature, I served on Louisville City Council and held senior positions in the administrations of two Colorado governors and as an advisor to two United States senators.
I earned my bachelor’s degree from Georgetown University and my doctorate in genetics from Harvard University.
I bring a scientist’s approach to policymaking — grounding decisions in evidence, data, and the needs of the people I represent.
I am running for re-election because I believe Colorado’s best days are still ahead.
I will continue working every day to lower costs, protect our environment, strengthen our communities, and ensure every Coloradan has the opportunity to thrive.
