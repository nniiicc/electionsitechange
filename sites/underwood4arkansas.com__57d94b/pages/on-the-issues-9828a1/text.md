ON THE ISSUES
PRO LIFE
I am 100% pro-life.
It is our obligation to defend the rights of the unborn and, without hesitation, I will always vote to do so.
JOBS
It is essential that Arkansas is competitive with surrounding states for increased job growth.
We need less government regulation with a focus on free market solutions so small businesses can thrive.
EDUCATION
A quality education begins with more educational freedom for parents.
I believe parents know what is best for their children rather than distant government bureaucrats.
I’ll fight for local control of our community schools.
LIMITED GOVERNMENT AND TAXES
As the founding fathers believed, government is best which governs least.
The result of big government is irresponsible spending, oppressive regulations, and disasters like Obamacare.
I will work tirelessly for lower taxes and limited government.
2ND AMENDMENT AND CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS
The Constitution is clear that the right to own guns shall not be infringed.
As a gun owner, concealed carry holder, and NRA member, I am a strong supporter of the 2nd Amendment and the Constitution.
I will defend your 2nd Amendment rights.
