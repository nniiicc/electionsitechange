ABOUT KENDON
Kendon Underwood was born and raised in Arkansas.
Growing up in a small Arkansas community, he was taught conservative values.
He has always advocated for the rights of the unborn, lower taxes, limited government, and protecting constitutional rights.
Kendon is a strong supporter of our veterans, having many close friends and family who have dedicated their lives to defending our country.
He graduated with honors from the University of Arkansas School of Law and is a local attorney and small business owner.
Kendon and his wife, Whitley, recently celebrated their 19th year together, are members of Trinity Fellowship Assembly of God, and are dedicated to their Christian faith.
