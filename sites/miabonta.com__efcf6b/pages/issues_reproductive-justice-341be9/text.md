Since the Supreme Court overturned Roe v.
Wade, California has become a refuge for people from across the country seeking reproductive healthcare.
Mia is committed to keeping it that way — and to strengthening protections here at home.
She supports the right to choose and is advancing legislation to ensure affordable, accessible family planning services, maternal health support, and protections for healthcare providers who serve patients from other states.
Reproductive justice means more than abortion access.
It means guaranteed paid family leave, affordable childcare, protections from pregnancy discrimination, and the right to raise a family with dignity and support.
As Chair of the Health Committee, Mia has direct influence over California's reproductive healthcare policy — and she's using it.
