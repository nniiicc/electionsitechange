Mia Bonta believes in
Immigration & Community Protection
Mia believes every person in our community deserves to live without fear — and she's fighting to protect East Bay families from federal overreach and unjust deportation.
Oakland's immigrant communities are a cornerstone of our city's culture, economy, and strength.
Right now they are under direct attack.
The Trump administration is deploying federal agents into California communities, separating families, and targeting the organizations that serve immigrants.
Mia has been unequivocal in her response: California will not comply, and she will not back down.
She also understands that immigrant communities' safety is everyone's safety.
When people fear calling the police or accessing services because of their immigration status, whole neighborhoods become less safe.
Mia supports policies that build trust between law enforcement and communities — so that every resident can seek help, report crime, and participate in civic life without fear.
Recent Legislation
- AB 1261 — Access to Counsel for Unaccompanied Minors & Immigrant Youth: Ensures unaccompanied minors and immigrant youth have legal representation when they need it most
- AB 2600 — Right to Counsel for All: Expands the right to legal counsel for immigrants facing detention and deportation proceedings
- AB 2624 — Privacy for Immigrant Service Providers: Protects the privacy of organizations serving immigrant communities from federal surveillance and political targeting
- AB 1318 — Protecting Nonprofits from Federal Political Retaliation: Ensures California nonprofits — including those serving immigrant communities — can continue operating without fear of federal targeting
Recent Wins in Sacramento
- $10M — Legal Services One California: Legal aid for survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, elder abuse, transnational abandonment, and human trafficking — serving immigrant communities across the state
Mia's Immigration Priorities
- Defending California's sanctuary policies at every level of government
- Guaranteeing legal representation for unaccompanied minors and immigrants facing detention
- Protecting the privacy of organizations that serve immigrant communities
- Shielding nonprofits from federal political retaliation
- Keeping immigration enforcement out of schools, hospitals, and courthouses
- Supporting pathways to stability for undocumented Californians in essential work
