- Improve accountability and reduce bureaucracy at the VA.
- Ensure veterans have timely access to quality care.
- Protect earned benefits.
- Expand veteran homeownership opportunities.
- Support veteran-owned small businesses
- End veteran homelessness.
- Protect the GI Bill.
- Ensure Congress takes its constitutional responsibility seriously before committing American troops to prolonged conflicts.
