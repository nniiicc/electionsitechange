ABOUT CRISTIAN MORALES Cristian Morales is running as a Republican for California’s 43rd Congressional District, which includes Compton, Watts, Hawthorne, Inglewood, Gardena, Lawndale, and Torrance.
Cristian brings valuable business experience and a unique perspective shaped by his personal and professional background.
Born in Guatemala and raised by highly educated Guatemalan parents, his family immigrated to the United States during the Cold War of the 1970s.
Inspired by his family, Cristian went on to earn a Master’s in Industrial and Labor Relations and a Bachelor of Science in Management from Cornell and Purdue.
His 20+ year career in manufacturing including Automotive, Aerospace, and Defense leading multicultural teams across every demographic and age group, combined with his early upbringing in Guatemala, has shaped Cristian into an insightful and broad-minded leader.
His political beliefs stem from his personal background and management approach where he genuinely connects with people and is known in the workplace as an advocate For All Voices.
A fair-minded and accomplished businessman, Cristian wants to bring that same approach to politics, focusing on California’s labor movement, immigration, economic opportunity, and the issues affecting working families.
Cristian’s main concern is that our differences have prevented us from discussing and resolving complex problems together.
