What personal information is collected and how it is stored
Information you provide to us directly when choosing to enter your name, email, telephone and message information under ‘contact us to learn more’.
We store this information via WordPress.
Types of donor information: contact information: name, organization/company, complete address, phone number, email address.
Donation information: donation date(s), donation amount(s), donation method(s), donation designation(s).
Credit card numbers are NOT retained by Cristian Morales For Congress 2026 as the donations go through our technology partner Donorbox, visit them for further information: https://donorbox.org/privacy
Cookies
We currently do measure or track with cookies, but our hosting partner, donation-partner, google analytics, advertising networks, and other technology partners may be setting cookies.
Embedded content from other websites
Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.).
Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the other website.
These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, including tracking your interaction with the embedded content if you have an account and are logged in to that website.
How the information is used
Cristian Morales for Congress 2026 uses your data to maintain donation records, send tax acknowledgements, mail year-end tax statements, share newsletters, and send direct mail pieces.
How the information is shared
Cristian Morales For Congress 2026 will not sell, share, rent, trade or transfer your personal information to any other organizations.
How long we retain your data
Cristian Morales For Congress 2026 stores the information as long as it’s needed for business purposes.
Donor Privacy Policy
Cristian Morales For Congress 2026 is committed to respecting the privacy of our generous donors, whether the donation is made online, by mail, or any other way.
We understand confidentiality is important, and we protect all our donor information using industry standard safeguards.
