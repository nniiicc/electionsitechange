May 21, 2025 1 min read Marc McMain is a dedicated conservative leader and small business owner running for Georgia State Senate District 46, which encompasses parts of Barrow, Clarke, Oconee, Walton, and Gwinnett counties.
As Chairman of the Walton County Republican Party, McMain has been instrumental in strengthening grassroots engagement and expanding the GOP's local presence through strategic outreach and principled leadership.
A lifelong Georgia resident, McMain brings over 30 years of entrepreneurial experience, having founded a karate school and the MTM Shopper magazine, which now reaches nine locations.
His campaign focuses on advocating for limited government, fiscal responsibility, parental rights, and the rule of law.
McMain is also a strong proponent of term limits, believing that career politicians are detrimental to the nation's founding principles.
With a commitment to faith, family, and freedom, he aims to represent the values and needs of District 46 residents at the State Capitol.
