ICYMI: Former Congressman Jody Hice Enthusiastically Endorses Marc McMain for State Senate!
Dec 5, 2025
“I am proud to enthusiastically endorse Marc McMain for State Senate.
As Chairman of the Walton County GOP, I watched Marc do an incredible job, not only supporting President Trump, but helping Republicans at every level of government get elected.
He knows how to build and lead a strong grassroots movement, and he stands firmly for our conservative values.
Marc has the rare ability to bring people together around what matters most and move the needle for the principles we believe in.
I am completely confident that, as State Senator, he will fight every single day for us.
I encourage everyone to join me in supporting Marc McMain for State Senate!" - Former 10th District Congressman Jody Hice
