NEWSLETTER - Agricultural Edition February 28, 2023 Colorado Ag Forum Ag Spotlight Last Thursday I had the privilege to attend the Colorado Agricultural Forum.
As the second-largest economic driver in CO, the agriculture industry must continue to innovate.
This forum brought together a wide range of stakeholders from the Ag sector to discuss the future of the industry in CO.
It is reassuring to know that our future is in bright hands thanks to organizations like the Future Farmers of America (FFA), 4-H, and the Colorado Agriculture Leadership Program (CALP).
These groups foster and grow the leaders that we need in our agriculture community.
Well Earned Recognition [Pictured are members of the Haxtun FFA] On February 23, Logan County Commissioner (former State Senator) Jerry Sonnenberg, was inducted into the Colorado Agriculture Hall of Fame.
I have been honored to have Jerry as my mentor.
This award could not have gone to a more deserving recipient.
