Energy
When it comes to energy, I am an "all of the above" kind of guy.
Within Senate District 1, we already have multiple forms of energy including, Wind, Solar, and our powerhouse - Oil and Gas..
I support any form of energy that partners with agriculture and helps us reduce our dependence on foreign oil.
We need to repeal SB 19-181 and roll back regulations, so that we can take advantage of the robust oil and gas industry that already exists in Colorado.
