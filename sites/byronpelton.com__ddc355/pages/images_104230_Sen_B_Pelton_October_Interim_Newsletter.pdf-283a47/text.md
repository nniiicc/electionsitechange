2023 Interim Newsletter October 27th Proposition HH is nothing more than a shell game, and the consequences of that shell game is taking away your TABOR refund.
With the repeal of Gallagher and the increase in property values across the State, we are seeing the largest property tax increase in Colorado history.
Colorado’s county assessors have been reporting a higher than usual number of people who are appealing their residential property values; this unprecedented amount of appeals shows that the people of Colorado are speaking up and saying that they cannot afford this huge property tax increase.
We, the elected leaders of the state, need to listen to our constituents’ protests and find a real solution that will lower rates without raiding Coloradans’ TABOR refunds.
Proposition HH is not the answer, but rather it’s an ill-conceived, bandaide cover-up that the Democrats rushed through the legislative process by making it look appealing with misleading promises that will only offer small, short term relief.
As a result of SB23-303, Proposition HH will offer short term relief to the taxpayers, but the long term Bills cost will far out way the short term gain.
Coloradoans will forever lose TABOR refunds, which is estimated to cost taxpayers up to $425 billion over the next three decades.
If Proposition HH is passed next month, we will just end up in a worse situation than we are currently, and Coloradoans cannot afford that.
We ALL need to come to the table and find a real, long term solution to these property increases that will not bankrupt Coloradoans.
Now is the time for the Colorado General Assembly and the Colorado Governor to listen and work to create practical solutions to better our state.
Proposition HH is not a viable solution to the taxpayers of this State.
Taxpayers deserve their TABOR refunds and taxpayers deserve to be told that Proposition HH is nothing more than a shell game, and the consequences of that shell game is taking away your TABOR refund.
This is why you need to vote NO on Proposition HH, and to tell your family and friends to do the same.
