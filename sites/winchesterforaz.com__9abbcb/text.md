Donate Candidate for Arizona House, District 17 Meet John Winchester Born and raised in Southern Arizona, John Winchester is a husband, father of four, and community leader.
He’s spent his life serving others — leading the Northwest YMCA, working in higher education, and giving back through community development.
John is a problem solver, not a career politician, and he’s running for Arizona House to bring results-driven leadership to Legislative District 17.
