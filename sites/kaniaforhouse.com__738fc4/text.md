More Info > Focused on practical outcomes Priorities These priorities come directly from conversations I’ve had with people across District 3A, and they’ll guide the work I focus on first.
LAND & RESOURCES Protect & Utilize Our Land The foundation of our economy is our land.
I will support forestry, tourism, and the responsible use of minerals while ensuring these industries do not threaten our woods, waters, or wilderness.
Transparency is the key to success when priorities come into conflict.
COSTS & SERVICES Make Life More Affordable The skyrocketing cost of living is hurting all of us.
We should keep more of our tax revenue in the Northland, fully fund our schools, and support rural healthcare while keeping the cap on prescription drug prices.
UNITY & RESULTS Leadership for the Northland We need our voices heard in St.
Paul.
District 3A needs a bipartisan representative at the Capitol.
I have decades of experience bringing people together across parties and communities to fight natural disasters, keep us safe, and deliver real results.
