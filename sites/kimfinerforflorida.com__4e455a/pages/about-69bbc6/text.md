Kim Finer has dedicated her life to community service and results-driven advocacy.
As a former educator and local leader, she brings a practical approach to the Florida State House, believing that common-sense solutions drive more progress than political rhetoric.
She is running to ensure every Floridian has a voice that is defined by better and brighter outcomes, not party loyalty. ​ Kim supports area organizations such as the Conservancy of Southwest Florida, Naples Botanical Garden, the Florida Native Plant Society (Coccoloba/Naples), and serves as President of the Friends of Delnor-Wiggins Pass State Park.
She is also a long-standing member of the American Society for Microbiology, a professional organization in which she held a variety of leadership positions.
Additional national memberships include the LWV, ACLU and AARP.
