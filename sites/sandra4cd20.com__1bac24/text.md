WHY SANDRA?
This isn't a career move.
It's a calling.
For 20 years as a Respiratory Therapist, Sandra was the person called in when everything was going wrong — calm, decisive, and focused when others couldn't be.
Today she works directly with intellectually and developmentally disabled adults.
Her son receives services from California's Regional Center system.
She has managed $500,000 in combined budgets for the people she serves.
She was appointed by the State Council on Developmental Disabilities to advise Kern Regional Center — which serves 19,000 people across Kern, Inyo, and Mono Counties.
She is not learning about these issues.
She lives them.
