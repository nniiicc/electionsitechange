Privacy Policy Effective date: October 2, 2026 ​ This Privacy Policy is published by Sandra Van Scotter for Congress (the "Campaign"), a federal political committee registered with the Federal Election Commission (FEC ID C00922666).
It explains what information we collect from people who visit electsandravanscotter.com, sign up, volunteer, or donate, how we use it, and the choices you have.
This policy covers information collected through this website and through the sign-up forms, email, and text message programs that this website connects you to.
Our other campaign domains, sandra4cd20.com and sandravanscotterforcongress.com, redirect here and are covered by this same policy.
It does not cover information you give directly to third-party platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, or X; those platforms have their own privacy policies. ​ By using this website, signing up, volunteering, or donating, you agree to the practices described here.
If you do not agree, please do not use the site or submit information to us. ​ ​ Information We Collect ​ Information you give us.
When you sign up for updates, volunteer, RSVP to an event, sign a petition, send us a message, or donate, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and anything else you choose to tell us.
If you donate, federal law also requires us to ask for your occupation and employer. ​ ​ Payment information.
Donations are processed by ActBlue, not by us.
Your credit card number goes to ActBlue and is governed by ActBlue's privacy policy .
We receive your name, contact information, occupation, employer, and the amount and date of your contribution so that we can thank you and meet our reporting obligations.
We do not store full card numbers. ​ Information collected automatically.
Like most websites, ours records technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view, the time you visited, and the site that referred you.
This is collected through cookies and similar tools described in the Cookies section below.
Information from other sources.
We may combine what you give us with publicly available voter registration data, which California makes available to candidates for campaign purposes.
We may also receive information from people who refer you to us, such as a friend who signs you up for an event. ​ How We Use Information ​ We use the information we collect to: ​ Send you campaign news, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, voting reminders, and fundraising requests Respond to your messages and requests Process donations, send receipts, and keep the records federal law requires Contact you if election law requires us to request additional information, such as a missing occupation or employer for a contribution Help you register to vote, find your polling place, and learn about campaign events near you Understand how people use our website so we can improve it Protect the Campaign, our volunteers, and the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats ​ ​ We do not sell your personal information.
We do not rent, trade, or exchange our supporter list with other candidates, committees, or organizations.
Public Disclosure of Contributions Required by Federal Law ​ Federal law requires the Campaign to use its best efforts to collect and publicly report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of every individual whose contributions total more than $200 in an election cycle.
These reports are filed with the Federal Election Commission and are available to anyone at fec.gov .
Contributions of $200 or less are reported in aggregate without your name, but we still keep a record of them as the law requires.
Federal law prohibits anyone from using information copied from FEC reports to solicit contributions or for commercial purposes.
The Campaign follows this rule and does not use other committees' reported contributor data for fundraising. ​ Text Messages ​ We send text messages only to people who have opted in.
By providing your mobile number and checking the opt-in box, you agree to receive recurring text messages from Sandra Van Scotter for Congress about campaign information, voting reminders, volunteer communications, and fundraising requests.
Message frequency is up to one message per week.
Message and data rates may apply.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of donating or volunteering. ​ You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
Reply HELP for help, or contact us at teamsandra@electsandravanscotter.com .
We send texts through Twilio and through ActBlue; your mobile number is shared with those providers only to deliver the messages you have agreed to receive.
We do not share your mobile number or your text-message consent with any other party for their own marketing purposes. ​ Email ​ If you give us your email address, we will send you campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer requests, and fundraising appeals.
Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and we honor opt-out requests promptly as required by the federal CAN-SPAM Act.
We send email through Mailchimp. ​ How We Share Information ​ We share personal information only in the following situations. ​ Service providers who work on our behalf.

Federal law also prohibits contributions from foreign nationals, and minors may contribute only under the conditions set by federal law.
If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. ​ Security ​ We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.
Donation pages use encrypted connections, and card numbers are handled by our payment processor rather than stored by us.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. ​ How Long We Keep Information ​ We keep supporter information for as long as the Campaign is active and for as long as federal law requires us to keep records.

After the election, supporter information stays with the Campaign.
It will not be sold, shared, or transferred to any other candidate, committee, party, or organization. ​ Links to Other Sites ​ This website links to other sites, including ActBlue, social media platforms, and government resources.
