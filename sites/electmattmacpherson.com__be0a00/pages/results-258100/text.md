Keeping More Money in Your Pocket.
With groceries, housing, gas, and everyday expenses costing more, Matt has supported tax relief aimed at letting Utah families keep more of what they earn.
- Reduced Utah's income tax rate from 4.5% to 4.45%.
- Supported another year of income tax relief for Utah families and businesses.
- Expanded eligibility for Utah's Child Tax Credit.
- Expanded tax credits to help businesses provide childcare.
- Reduced the state gas tax by six cents per gallon for six months.
Giving Law Enforcement Tools to Do the Job.
Matt's work on public safety includes statewide investments and legislation he sponsored during the 2026 General Session.
- Supported major state investments in public safety and law enforcement.
- Sponsored HB136, Unlicensed Driver Amendments.
- Sponsored HB539, Public Safety Modifications.
- Serves on the Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice Interim Committee.
More Resources for Utah Students.
As Vice Chair of the Public Education Appropriations Subcommittee, Matt works directly on the state budget that funds Utah's public schools.
- Increased the value of Utah's Weighted Pupil Unit by 4.2%.
- Invested additional resources in early literacy and at-risk students.
- Sponsored HB148, creating a way for Utah taxpayers to voluntarily help pay outstanding school meal debt.
Protecting the Place We Call Home.
Utah's water supply, the Great Salt Lake, and the quality of the air our kids breathe affect families, businesses, and communities across the state.
Matt has supported efforts to plan for Utah's long-term water future and protect these resources.
