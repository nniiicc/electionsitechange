School Meals Debt Relief
Creates a School Meals Debt Relief Fund so Utahns can voluntarily donate through their state income tax return to help pay outstanding student meal debt in Utah schools.

Unlicensed Driver Amendments
Updates Utah law governing unlicensed drivers, including vehicle impound provisions and enforcement tools available to law enforcement.
Read HB136 →
Public Safety Modifications
Updates Utah law involving public safety and prosecution, including domestic violence protections and pretrial release standards.
Read HB539 →
Judgment Information Amendments
Reduces the amount of sensitive personal information required on certain judgment liens, replacing full Social Security numbers with the last four digits.
Read HB82 →
District Court Amendments
Establishes procedures for certain constitutional challenges in Utah district courts, including a three-judge panel process.
