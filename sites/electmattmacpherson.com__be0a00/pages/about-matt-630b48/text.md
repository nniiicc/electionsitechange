This Community Is Home.
Matt MacPherson has called West Valley City and Magna home for over 20 years.
He is a small business owner, a dad whose kids went to school here, and a neighbor who has spent years supporting west side programs, businesses, and charities.
Before he ever ran for office, Matt was already showing up.
He serves on West Valley City's Police Oversight Committee and has volunteered across the community for years.
Today Matt represents House District 26 in the Utah Legislature.
He still approaches the job the same way: listen first, then get something done.
