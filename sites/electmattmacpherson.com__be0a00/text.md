A Message From Matt:
Friends and neighbors,
West Valley City and Magna have been my home for two decades, and representing our community at the Capitol is a responsibility I take seriously.
For me, public service starts with listening.
I want to know what is working for your family, what is not, and what you think state government should be doing differently.
During my time in the Legislature, I have worked on issues that affect our everyday lives, including taxes, public safety, education, and the challenges facing our community.
I will continue to approach this job the same way: listen first, work with people, and focus on practical solutions.
Whether we agree on every issue or not, I want you to know that I am always willing to listen.
Matt MacPherson
