Donate Proven Leadership Josh has dedicated himself to serving our community through public service.
As a member of the New York State Assembly, Josh has a record as a common-sense legislator, willing to work with all his colleagues to ensure our friends and neighbors have an affordable and safe environment to live, work and raise a family.
Restoring Economic Competitiveness Josh understands that higher taxes hurt businesses, which in turn cause our community to lose jobs.
That is why he supports job growth by focusing on long-term property tax relief, standing up against escalating state mandates and pushing back against policies that force New York families and businesses out of state.
Preserving Our Environment Josh believes it is critically important to protect our environment for future generations.
However, not by risking New York’s economic competitiveness to do so.
He believes that forced state mandates to move to green energy sources before the market is ready will hurt families, businesses and local governments.
Working Towards a Healthier State Josh knows that access to healthcare is one of the most important parts of New Yorker’s lives.
As one of the state’s leading health policy voices, Josh has continually spoken about the need to make our health systems operate more efficiently and effectively, increase access to doctors and providers and pursue reforms within the existing healthcare structure.
Standing Up for Families Josh knows that to ensure New York remains a place families want to call home, we must prioritize the things families care about most.
He has worked in a bipartisan manner to support our public education system, proposed innovative solutions to our state's childcare crisis and continues to be a leading voice for investing in community institutions, such as libraries and parks.
Protecting Our Community Josh believes in the importance of protecting our families and neighborhoods.
In the Assembly, he has fought against policies that have made our citizens feel less safe, spoken out against putting dangerous criminals before law-abiding citizens and supported the men and women in law enforcement.
