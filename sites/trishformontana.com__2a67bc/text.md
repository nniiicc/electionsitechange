About Trish We Chose Montana to be Home Hi, I’m Trish, and I’m running as a Republican to serve the people of House District 76.
My husband, Matt, and I made our home in Helmville, with our four dogs, nearly a decade ago and have come to deeply appreciate the values, work ethic, and spirit of our rural communities.
We chose to make Montana home intentionally.
We sought a rural location with ready access to the outdoors and made up of families who understand patience, hard-work, self-sufficiency, the preciousness of life, and the importance of caring for those around you—family, neighbors, and friends.
These principles are lost in urban settings, and it was our desire to live amongst people who strive to live virtuously and who are guided by a traditional morality.
Specifically, watching Montana’s rural teachers go above and beyond for their students during COVID was inspiring.
They showed up, adapted, and made sure kids kept learning no matter the challenge.
These teachers became my heroes as their courage and commitment reminded me how strong our communities are when we put people first.
Serving Others For over 25 years, I worked in educational therapy, helping children and adults overcome learning challenges in reading and writing.
My work has always been about helping people gain confidence, independence, and success through education.
This is the heart of who I am.
I grew up in a broken family.
My single mother of three girls often struggled to afford rent, we constantly moved around, and there were plenty of times when we had neither a phone, nor electricity, nor a functioning car.
Though my mother only had a high school degree herself, she is an avid reader, and she understood the opportunity education could offer her girls.
While I never excelled in academics in high school, I kept pushing myself to enroll in community college, and I eventually earned my master’s degree in Special Education so I could dedicate my life to helping others learn.
Why I’m Running It is my intention to serve the good people of HD76 by listening to the unique perspectives of parents, ranchers, teachers, business owners, and community leaders so that I can bring your voices to Helena.
In order for representative government to work, we all must participate in the communication.
I’m here to listen and serve.
For the last four years, I’ve volunteered as a citizen advocate in Helena, working on education policy because I believe Montana’s kids deserve every opportunity to succeed and educators should have every tool available to offer a true quality education.
I believe in an “all of the above” approach to education, whether it’s public, private, charter, or a customized home-based learning.
Every child, every parent, and every teacher deserves the freedom to choose what works best for them.
Yes, I’m passionate about education, but my experience in Helena has shown me how important it is to have leaders in office who truly listen to their constituents, who take the time to learn about the issues impacting the communities and who respectfully engage and respond with the voters back home.
There is so much work to do–from strengthening education, balancing our budget, returning surplus revenue to the taxpayers, lowering taxes, eliminating burdensome regulations, and providing true property tax relief.
But, that’s the Montana way: we roll up our sleeves, work hard, and take care of our neighbors.
I’d be honored to earn your support and represent the people of House District 76.
Please reach out to me, so we can talk about what issues are most important to you.

Lower Income Taxes, True Property Tax Relief, No Sales Tax Ever!
Montanans work hard, and they deserve a government that respects that.
We need to lower income taxes and return surplus revenue to the people who earned it.
We should lower property taxes for all Montanans to protect families, ranchers, and businesses without putting our schools or communities at risk.
Responsible Budgeting Montanans live within their means, and our state government should do the same.
We must balance the budget, end wasteful spending, and stop reckless growth in government.
Fiscal discipline ensures stability today and a stronger Montana for the next generation.
RESTORING THE AMERICAN DREAM Montana should always be a place where people can build a life, raise a family, and enjoy the land we love.
But rising costs and limited opportunities threaten that promise.
We need policies that make it possible for the kids of Montana to build their futures right here at home.
EDUCATION THAT WORKS FOR MONTANA Strong schools start with strong local control.
So, we need to return education to the districts.
Parents, teachers, and communities—not Helena—should decide what works best for their students.
We need to offer robust educational opportunities for everyone.
AGRICULTURAL GROWTH AND SUPPORT Agriculture is the backbone of House District 76’s economy and Montana’s way of life.
We must support our farmers and ranchers by reducing unnecessary regulations and empowering local innovation.
When agriculture thrives, our communities thrive.
PROTECTING SECOND AMENDMENT RIGHTS The right to keep and bear arms is fundamental to our freedom and our way of life.
I will always defend the Second Amendment and stand against any effort to weaken it.
The Constitution could not be clearer.
