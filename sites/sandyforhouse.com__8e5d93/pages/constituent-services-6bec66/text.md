Resources and Legislative Help I am here to help the people of House District 105, so please contact my office at sandy.donatucci@house.ga.gov .
Please include your name, address, and number if you contact our office via email so that we can get in touch with you quickly and efficiently.
Letters of Recognition I enjoy hearing about special events and accomplishments in my constituents’ lives, and I am happy to honor them by writing letters or resolutions to recognize those special events.
If you have a special event or accomplishment, such as, student achievement awards, sports awards, 50 th wedding anniversaries, or other special achievements, please reach out.
School Visits and Student Engagement Our students are our leaders of tomorrow, so it is very important to me that we educate them on the legislative process.
I am more than happy to visit local school and talk with students about what I do and how the legislative process works.
If you would like to schedule a visit, please contact me.
House Page Program If your student would like to get a first-hand view of the legislative process by serving as a page in the Georgia State House at the Capitol, please contact my office or see the resources here: https://www.legis.ga.gov/house/page-program Flag Flown at the Capitol Flags flown over our State Capitol make a great gift to recognize an individual or organization.
If you would like to receive a flag flown at the Capitol, please reach out for additional information.
Resolutions from the GA State House We can also write a State House Resolution to honor, recognize, and commend individuals, groups, schools, sports teams, etc.
If you know of any individuals or groups in District 105 that need to be recognized, please contact my office.
