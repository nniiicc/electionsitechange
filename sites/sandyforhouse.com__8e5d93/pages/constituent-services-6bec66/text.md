Please include your name, address, and number if you contact our office via email so that we can get in touch with you quickly and efficiently.
I enjoy hearing about special events and accomplishments in my constituents’ lives, and I am happy to honor them by writing letters or resolutions to recognize those special events.
If you have a special event or accomplishment, such as, student achievement awards, sports awards, 50th wedding anniversaries, or other special achievements, please reach out.
Our students are our leaders of tomorrow, so it is very important to me that we educate them on the legislative process.
I am more than happy to visit local school and talk with students about what I do and how the legislative process works.
If you would like to schedule a visit, please contact me.
If your student would like to get a first-hand view of the legislative process by serving as a page in the Georgia State House at the Capitol, please contact my office or see the resources here: https://www.legis.ga.gov/house/page-program
