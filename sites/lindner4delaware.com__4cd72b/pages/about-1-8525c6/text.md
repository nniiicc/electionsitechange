Endorsements, Distinctions, and Support Honored by the trust and support of our community.
Gregg is grateful to have the support of the following committees, organizations, and all Delawareans who believe in his commitment to honest leadership, practical solutions, and putting people first.
Congresswoman Sarah McBride “Gregg understands that strong leadership means putting people first and working to move our communities forward.
With a commitment to protecting our rights, investing in people and infrastructure, and ensuring fairness for every Delawarean, I know Gregg is ready to deliver for Sussex County and communities across our state.
That’s why I’m proud to support Gregg Lindner for State Representative in RD 4.” Jane Gruenebaum Sussex County Council District 3 "I am pleased to endorse Gregg Lindner for State Representative from District 4.
Gregg has a combination of business and community-service background that uniquely qualifies him for this position.
Gregg brings an expertise and commitment to quality education that is critical to Delaware’s future; as an excellent education system attracts businesses and much needed professionals.
He understands the necessity for helping Sussex County with our infrastructure needs - adequate roads and transportation systems; excellent schools and a thriving environment.
He believes in creating strong, positive State-County relations to advance the well-being of Sussex County.
I can’t imagine a better person to partner with me in the work to create the roadmap for smarter growth and healthy communities" Senator Russ Huxtable "I commend Gregg for his dedication to his community and his drive for meaningful change.
I know that he will advocate for his constituents with persistence, grit, and pragmatism.
He understands the unique needs of District 4 and will champion logical and effective solutions.
His background in leading corporations and being a former elected school board member gives him unique insights, and a substantial and timely base of knowledge to help address the needs of the 4th Representative District.
Please join me in supporting Gregg."
