Dominique Delivers for Norwalk and Westport.
Dominique learned the value of hard work and giving back to our community from her family while growing up in the Midwest.
Your Voice in Hartford as a State Representative since 2022, she proudly represents each resident in every neighborhood of the 143rd District.
As Deputy Majority Leader, Dominique works to improve the quality of life for all of our District’s residents, delivering results with compassion and respect.
Dominique answered the call to work hard and make things happen: Championing historic investments in our children for the best start in life; Fighting for more affordability for our families to help with increased costs and protect from Federal funding cuts; And, protecting our elders and Veterans so they can live with dignity and the quality of life they deserve.
A fierce advocate, leader, and listener for the 143rd District, Dominique works hard to be a voice for all.
A person of character who understands the issues that matter to you, Dominique Johnson is proud to be Your Voice in Hartford!
