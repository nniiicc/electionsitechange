Meet Mike Priorities Donate Join the Team Meet Mike Deering Mike Deering is a Missouri farmer, small business owner, and agricultural leader running for State Senate to bring commonsense conservative leadership back to Jefferson City.
Raised on a diversified family farm in northwest Missouri, Mike learned early that hard work and responsibility aren't theories, they're a way of life.
He was active in 4-H, FFA, and Boy Scouts, and he believes strong communities are built when people show up for one another.
Mike's life was shaped by hardship at a young age.
At 11, he lost his father in a farm accident.
A few years later, shortly after his 16th birthday, he lost his mother to a rare disease.
In that difficult season, Mike's faith and his community carried him.
Friends and neighbors stepped in to help keep the farm going, teaching Mike lessons about perseverance, service, and the strength of Missouri communities that he still carries today.
Mike pursued higher education through scholarships and hard work, studying agriculture and communications and building a career that took him from the farm to leadership roles across the country, including work in Washington, D.C. serving agricultural producers and rural communities.
In 2012, Mike returned home to Missouri to serve as Executive Vice President of the Missouri Cattlemen's Association.
Today, Mike and his wife Julie live in Montgomery City, where they raise cattle and sheep and are raising three children, Charley, Henry, and Margaret.
In 2024, they opened a local storefront selling farm-fresh meats, produce, and Missouri-made products.
Mike is running for State Senate because he believes Missouri can do better: less government, more freedom, and leadership that puts the good of the people ahead of politics.
