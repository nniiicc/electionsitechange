We’re In This Together I spent ten years on the Smithfield Town Council, four years as President, and I got there the same way I always have: by knocking on doors and being accessible to the people who voted for me and didn't vote for me, regardless of party, regardless of politics.
Now I'm running for State Representative in District 53.
My focus hasn't changed.
I want cost of living to be more affordable, stronger schools for our kids, and a state government that represents the people who elected it.

Three Concerns I Hear Every Day I have a son in fourth grade and aging parents, so these issues aren't abstract to me.
They're what my own family talks about at dinner.
Here's where I'll focus my energy in the State House.
Strengthening Our Schools I spent over a decade working in higher education, and I own a business that serves small children so I know why it’s important to get every child the resources they need, no matter their zip code.
Tackling Cost of Living My family feels it too.
I'll fight the rising costs on healthcare, housing, and groceries that make it hard for young families to plant roots here.
Supporting Our Seniors I have aging parents.
I'll protect seniors on fixed incomes so that they can stay in their homes and the towns they helped build.
