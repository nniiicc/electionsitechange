Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 9/21/23
1.
Information Collection and Use
As part of our political campaign, we may collect phone numbers and opt-in information (such as names, email addresses, and ZIP codes) from individuals who voluntarily provide this information through various means, including but not limited to:
-
Online forms on our website or campaign platforms.
-
In-person events, rallies, or gatherings.
-
Phone calls or text message communications.
-
Social media interactions.
We collect this information to communicate with our supporters, inform them about our campaign updates, events, and relevant political issues.
By providing your phone number and opt-in information, you consent to our use of this information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
1.2 Use of Phone Numbers and Opt-In Information
-
Campaign-related communication: We may use phone numbers to send important campaign updates, event reminders, or other relevant information related to our political campaign.
-
Volunteer outreach: We may reach out to individuals who have provided their phone numbers to engage them in volunteering opportunities, canvassing, or other campaign-related activities.
-
Fundraising: We may use phone numbers to contact individuals who have expressed an interest in supporting our campaign financially.
However, we will not engage in telemarketing activities or solicit contributions from individuals who have registered their phone numbers on a “Do Not Call” list.
We value the privacy of our supporters and pledge not to sell, share, convey, or otherwise disclose your phone numbers and opt-in information to third parties or outside sources for marketing or solicitation purposes.
We take the security of your personal information seriously and employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or destruction.
We will retain your phone numbers and opt-in information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.
