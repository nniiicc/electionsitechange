Cait Conley, Democratic Candidate for US House in New York’s 17th District, says data centers can be a net positive for communities.
Conley notes AI poses serious risks and that companies need to do the right thing as the technology advances.
Conley also discusses her race against Rep.
Mike Lawler (R-NY) and the controversy surrounding Rep.
Max Miller’s (R-OH) donation to Lawler’s race.
She speaks with Joe Mathieu and Kailey Leinz on the late edition of Bloomberg’s “Balance of Power.”
