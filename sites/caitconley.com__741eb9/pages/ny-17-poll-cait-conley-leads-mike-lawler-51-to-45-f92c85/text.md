OSSINING, NY – A new poll released today by House Majority PAC shows Army Combat Veteran and Democratic Nominee for NY-17 Cait Conley leading Mike Lawler 51% to 45% .
The poll also shows Mike Lawler and Donald Trump desperately underwater, with Lawler’s favorability rating at 39% favorable to 55% unfavorable.
Not far behind is Donald Trump, at 38% favorable to 60% unfavorable.
This poll comes just as nearly 500,000 New Yorkers have been kicked off their healthcare because of Mike Lawler’s deciding vote on the One Big Beautiful Bill.
Mike Lawler has voted 100% of the time with Donald Trump, and will continue to be a rubber stamp to President Trump.
“The voters of NY-17 are paying the price for Mike Lawler’s failure and putting his own political ambition ahead of Hudson Valley families,” said Cait Conley.
“Whether it’s him stripping healthcare coverage away from thousands of people to give tax breaks to the wealthy or supporting a reckless war in Iran that is jacking up gas prices here at home, people in the Hudson Valley are ready for a new kind of leadership.
This campaign is not about left versus right, but right versus wrong.
Voters do not want more talking points,photo-ops, or excuses.
They deserve results.
They want leadership that tells the truth, lowers costs, protects their freedoms, and remembers who Congress is supposed to work for.
Mike Lawler has had every chance to put this district ahead of his loyalty to Trump and his own ambitions.
Every time, he’s chosen wrong.
In November, voters are going to choose something better.” About Cait Conley: Cait Conley is a fourth-generation Hudson Valley native and the daughter of a postal worker and a construction worker.
She is a decorated combat veteran and national security expert who has spent her life answering the call to serve.
That call began on 9/11, when, as a junior in high school, she watched the Twin Towers fall.
The next day, she started the process for applying to West Point — earning acceptance and becoming the first in her family to graduate from college and then going on later to attend graduate programs at Harvard and MIT.
Cait served 16 years as an active duty Army officer, deploying six times to take down the world’s most dangerous terrorists, and later led counterterrorism efforts on the National Security Council and at the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency.
Throughout her career, Cait has been called upon to solve the toughest problems and fix things that are broken.
That’s why Cait is running for Congress — to fix a broken system and fight for a better future for New Yorkers.
