Cait’s mom, born in Peekskill, was a postal worker for 48 years, serving communities throughout the Hudson Valley.
Her dad, born and raised in Hopewell Junction, was a construction worker, and her grandfather and great-grandfather worked in the Montrose brickyard.
Cait was a junior at Pine Bush High School on 9/11 when she watched the Twin Towers fall.
In that moment, she felt her calling to serve and protect.
The next day, she started her application to the United States Military Academy at West Point and earned a congressional nomination from the late Congressman Maurice Hinchey.
Cait was the first in her family to graduate from college.
Decorated Combat Veteran and National Security Expert Cait has answered the call to serve our country time and again.
She served for 16 years as an active-duty Army officer, starting her career as a military police officer and then serving in special ops leadership, deploying overseas six times to combat zones including Iraq and Afghanistan.
She was awarded three Bronze Stars for displaying “the highest degree of professionalism, sound judgment, and technical and tactical competence while conducting combat operations.” Cait’s career as a public servant continued here at home, protecting our security and democracy while serving as the Director for Counterterrorism on the National Security Council at the White House (which led to her being banned from Russia).
She later helped safeguard our critical infrastructure and election systems at the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), defending our democracy by protecting the security and integrity of our elections and by standing up directly to Trump’s Big Lie about the 2020 election.
During her service at the White House and CISA, Cait worked directly with Congress on some of the most complex threats facing our country, including drafting and reviewing federal legislation to address critical national security issues and keep American families safe.
She led efforts to strengthen the security and resilience of our nation’s critical infrastructure—from the water we drink to the energy that powers our homes.
A Fighter For Us, Not Special Interests Cait has put her life on the line to protect Americans.
The oaths she has taken – from West Point to the Army and Special Operations – have been to this country: not to any person or political party, and certainly not to any special interests or corporate donors, like so many DC politicians.
Today, she looks around and sees a corrupt and dysfunctional system that is failing to deliver for working families in the Hudson Valley.
She watches party-first politicians doing what’s best for their political ambition and stock portfolio instead of doing what’s best for the people.
The same politicians who created this mess won’t be the ones who fix it.
She has the courage to do what’s right and the leadership to deliver results on Day One.
Here in NY-17, families are increasingly getting priced out, crushed by costs, shut out of opportunities, and feeling left behind.
Our infrastructure is crumbling.
Housing is unaffordable.
And quality healthcare is out of reach or being ripped away for far too many.
Cait is not a lifelong politician or political operative.
For the last 20 years, as a former utility lobbyist, political consultant, and professional politician, Mike Lawler has been profiting off of democracy while Cait has been out there leading America’s sons and daughters defending it.
It’s time NY-17 had a representative that knows what public service really is.
Cait has spent her life serving this country and fighting for the safety and security of American families.
Throughout her career, Cait has been called upon to solve the toughest problems and deliver.
That’s why Cait is running for Congress – to continue the fight for a better future for New Yorkers.
