About Denise Denise Daniels Fanning is a dedicated conservative, community advocate, and long-time Henderson resident with a strong background in service and leadership.
Raised in an Air Force family and educated at the University of Texas at Austin, she built a 20-year career as a television commercial producer before settling in Southern Nevada in 2008.
Learn More Endorsements Denise has earned the support of Governor Joe Lombardo, the Assembly Republican Caucus, the Vegas Chamber of Commerce, the Clark County Education Association, and more than two dozen law enforcement and trade organizations across Nevada.
