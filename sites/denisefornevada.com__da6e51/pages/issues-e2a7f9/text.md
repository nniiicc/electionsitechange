01
I will fight to clean up our elections in Nevada.
That is why I would support The Save Nevada Act to:
Count All Ballots By Election Day
End Automatic Voter Registration
Stop Universal Mail-In Ballots
We can make voting simple, easy, and safe -- and ensure every legal vote counts and every result can be trusted.
02
We must find ways to lower everyday costs for Nevadans.
Did you know that Nevada drivers pay among the highest auto insurance premiums in the nation?
Costs are soaring because of runaway litigation, non-driving rating factors, and auto thefts.
I believe we can lower costs through The Car Insurance and Gas Prices Reduction Act:
Rein In Frivolous Lawsuits
Rates Based On How You Drive, Not Credit Score
Crack Down On Auto Theft
End California's Energy Mandates Driving Gas Prices
03
Every Nevada child deserves a quality education -- regardless of their zip code, family situation, or household income.
Expand School Choice For Every Family
Hold Schools Accountable For Results
Reward Great Teachers
Solving Nevada's education crisis requires everyone at the table -- parents, teachers, administrators, and community leaders -- working together to put students first.
