Donate Issues The top priorities of our campaign Our Mission Kitchen Table Issues From food costs to healthcare, education to housing, these are the things discussed at the "kitchen table." We need to ensure these are accessible, affordable, and high quality.
Supported Communities Our diversity and varied perspectives makes us stronger.
We need to connect our kitchen tables and also make sure we're helping those who help us - including farmers, small business owners, and teachers.
Our Mission Responsible Budgets Minnesota's state programs lead the way in efficiency but we need to ensure oversight and accountability.
We need to reinforce our communities with a commitment to safety, infrastructure, and planning for continued growth.
Protecting Our Resources We need to continue our land and water-saving conservation programs and stop outside interests from taking Minnesota's precious resources to ensure they're available to the next generation of Minnesotans.
Policy Interest Areas 1 Lower Cost of Living Affordability starts at home by addressing healthcare and childcare costs.
Federal policy changes and global conflict have led to our families struggling to make house payments, have money for gas, and pay for childcare.
I'm committed to lowering childcare costs so no parent has to choose between a paycheck and their kids.
Workers across the board have seen healthcare cost increases.
Federal funding cuts paired with administration requirements will mean that plans will cost more to provide less.
No one should have to put off care when they’re sick.
I’ll fight for lower insurance costs and to keep care close to home.
2 Quality Education Right now our schools are facing rising healthcare costs, an increase in transportation costs, and funding gaps for special education.
Part of this is due to federal funding deficits, but we need to do what we can to find other ways to support our schools so they don’t need to turn to local taxpayers.
I'll fight for schools that are fully funded and focused on preparing students for real careers here in Minnesota.
3 Responsible Technology & Energy Technology should power Minnesota’s future without draining our water, worsening climate change, or compromising our data and privacy.
Projects coming into our communities and greenspaces should show concrete evidence that they’ll run on clean energy and protect our water and natural areas.
We should also reinvest in natural areas that need support, because Minnesota’s resilience against climate chaos depends on both a thriving environment and how thoughtfully we live within it.
