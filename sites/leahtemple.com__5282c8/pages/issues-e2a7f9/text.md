Issues
The top priorities of our campaign
Kitchen Table Issues
From food costs to healthcare, education to housing, these are the things discussed at the "kitchen table." We need to ensure these are accessible, affordable, and high quality.
Supported Communities
Our diversity and varied perspectives makes us stronger.
We need to connect our kitchen tables and also make sure we're helping those who help us - including farmers, small business owners, and teachers.
Responsible Budgets
Minnesota's state programs lead the way in efficiency but we need to ensure oversight and accountability.
We need to reinforce our communities with a commitment to safety, infrastructure, and planning for continued growth.
Protecting Our Resources
We need to continue our land and water-saving conservation programs and stop outside interests from taking Minnesota's precious resources to ensure they're available to the next generation of Minnesotans.
