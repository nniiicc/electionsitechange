Scott Burns has a proven track record of service to Franklin
To The Daily Sun,
I am writing to support the candidacy of Scott Burns, who is running for state representative for Franklin and Hill.
I have known Scott for a number of years and have served on a board with him, and I can attest for his commitment to the Franklin area.
He certainly has demonstrated his leadership qualities in a number of ways over the years while serving on several boards.
Scott is a graduate of Franklin High School and holds degrees from both Plymouth State University and New England College, where he earned his M.B.A.
Scott has certainly used that education to help his community and beyond.
I am confident that Scott would be an asset not only to the communities of Franklin and Hill, but to the state of New Hampshire.
He certainly has earned my endorsement as a state representative and I hope that you will consider voting for him to represent the communities with the dignity and respect that is required by the people that he will represent!
Thank you in advance for your vote on Nov. 3rd.
Charles North Franklin.
