Home Meet Bob Issues News Volunteer Contribute Contact Yard Signs I’m Bob Mason.
My wife and I live in New Mexico because we love this state: its people, its culture, and the beautiful outdoors.
But I see this great state deteriorating.
Because I’m concerned, and because I believe in our representative form of government, I’ve tried communicating with our representative, but to no effect.
I don’t feel I or my neighbors have a voice in Santa Fe.
So, I’ve decided to run as candidate for New Mexico District 21 to give you the voice in Santa Fe you haven’t had.
I have many concerns about what's happening in our state, but I want to know what concerns you.

If you live in District 21 I'd appreciate your support.
