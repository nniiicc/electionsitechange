Home Meet Bob Issues News Volunteer Contribute Contact Yard Signs Meet Bob I'm Bob Mason.
I live in Albuquerque with my wife Wanda and I'm asking for your support to be elected to represent you in New Mexico House District 21.
After a 30 year career in the U.S.
Air Force, I started a company providing leadership development for companies that wanted their supervisors and managers to understand the skills and techniques of better leadership and build more effective teams.
I have had the privilege of working with leaders in many companies from the U.S., Canada, Europe, and the Middle East.
The leadership skills I teach primarily come from my Air Force career.
Starting as a Basic Airman (the lowest rank) I rose to Lieutenant Colonel and had the honor of commanding four different squadrons, both in garrison and deployed, including the Air Force's largest munitions squadron.
We believe New Mexico is a great state with terrific potential, but is squandering that opportunity.
As the state representative for District 21, I will fight for this state to realize that potential.
Wanda and I enjoy hiking and exploring the rich history of New Mexico and the southwest.
