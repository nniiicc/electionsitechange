Children, Youth, and the CYFD
The state’s Children, Youth, and Families Department (CYFD) is a New Mexico embarrassment.
But more than that it is a danger to the very people it’s supposed to help – our youth.
We hear of children living under desks in the CYFD offices and even a 16-year-old U.S. citizen who was taken to the border and directed to go into Mexico to reunite with his abusive mother.
There have been many similar reports in the news about how badly children are treated in the system.
That seems to largely stem from a trend to prioritize returning a child to their family, even if that family has been detrimental to the child.
That has to stop.
The safety of the child must come first!
The department is directly under the governor and has had 5 directors in the last 8 years.
None of them seemed to be able to bring it under control.
This is not an easy issue and the solution will not be easy.
In fact, I believe the situation is so bad that the only solution will be a completely new start.
That means a new director who is not a political appointee but an actual expert in this difficult field.
It also means a complete overhaul of the department and removal from direct control of the governor.
As your representative I will sponsor and support legislation that
- Ensures the primary focus is on the safety of the child
- Realigns the department
- Provides necessary funding to ensure children in the CYFD system have proper living arrangements
- Provides a support system for children who age out (18 years) so they can thrive as adults
