Taxing and Spending
This year the legislature passed a budget that was a 10.8% increase over last year.
Do you feel you are receiving 10% better results?
Since 2017 state government spending has increased approximately 60%.
Are you 60% better off than you were in 2017?
In part this wild spending spree has been made possible by annual budget surpluses in the billions of dollars.
New Mexico has the highest personal income tax in region and more than $75 billion dollars in permanent funds and yet many in the legislature still want to raise your taxes.
The personal income tax you pay provides approximately 17% of the state’s revenue.
State spending has increased by 60% since 2017 and almost 11% in the last year.
Cutting spending would easily make up for any reduction in revenue.
But it’s been proven that reducing taxes results in increased revenue because consumers have more money to spend.
The New Mexico Gross Receipts Tax (GRT) system needs to be reformed.
The Gross Receipts Tax is a regressive tax and results in you, the consumer, paying higher prices because the tax is levied at each step of the supply chain.
The Gross Receipts Tax should be eliminated in favor of a smaller straight consumption tax that is only charged on the final product.
New Mexico should join the 9 other states that don’t have any income tax.
Remember it’s your money and your state government is responsible for carefully managing that money.
It’s been proven time and again that a reduction in income tax results in more revenue for the government.
It’s your money.
You earned it and I will fight to for you to keep more of it!
As your representative I will sponsor and support legislation that
- Eliminates the Personal Income Tax
- Reforms the Gross Receipts Tax
- Reduces state government spending
- Provides a tax deduction for mortgage payments
- Eliminate tax on overtime, tips, and social security
