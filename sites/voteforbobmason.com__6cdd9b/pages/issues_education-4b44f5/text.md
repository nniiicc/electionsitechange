Home Meet Bob Issues News Volunteer Contribute Contact Yard Signs Home ❭ Issues ❭ Education Education The state of education in New Mexico is dismal.
It’s common knowledge that we are ranked 50 th among the states for education.
Over half our students cannot read or do math at grade level.
Our governor and the majority of legislators refuse to take the actions necessary to get us out of this dire situation.
They are more interested in the desires of the teachers unions which provide much of their campaign funding.
When our young people do not acquire the basic skills in the school system, they find it difficult to secure good employment meaning they cannot contribute to society.
That also means that companies that want to expand here and create jobs will go somewhere else where there are more potential employees with the necessary skills.
One of the reasons that other states are surpassing us in education is that we don’t put an emphasis on reading ability.
Our state leadership has embraced what’s known as “social promotion,” meaning that children are promoted each year even if they haven’t mastered the basic skills necessary to move on.
Other states have had success by setting a bar at the 3 rd grade.
A student who can’t read at the 3 rd grade level will not advance to the 4 th grade.
When a student is found to be deficient, the school system takes positive action to help that student become proficient.
That’s in stark contrast to our current policy of just moving them on and letting them continue to struggle until they just give up.
It isn’t about money.
Even though student enrollment has declined steadily, and all projections are that enrollment will continue to decline, money allocated to our public schools continue to increase.
And yet, there is no improvement in results.
We do have charter and private schools that produce better results; but, private schools are expensive and charter schools have a limited capacity.
The state also prevents a successful charter school from expanding to more than one campus.
Last year the U.S.
Congress enacted the Education Freedom Tax Credit which allows you to take a $1700 tax credit for education choice.
Unfortunately, individual states must opt in to the program and our governor is more interested in poking President Trump in the eye than helping your kids.
You can still take advantage of the program, just not for anyone in New Mexico.
