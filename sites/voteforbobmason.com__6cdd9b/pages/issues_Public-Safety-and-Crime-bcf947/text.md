Home Meet Bob Issues News Volunteer Contribute Contact Yard Signs Home ❭ Issues ❭ Public Safety and Crime Public Safety and Crime Our state is often listed as one of the most dangerous in the country.
Even though there is some indication that there may be a slight decrease in crime, it is still a serious problem that affects all of us.
The legislature has failed us by not passing more stringent laws that give judges the ability to hold violent offenders and pass meaningful sentences.
That has made our state an attractive place for criminals.
New Mexico must stop “immigrant friendly” or “sanctuary” policies.
Those policies help bring violent crime and illegal drugs like fentanyl into our state.
A particular problem is the rise of juvenile crime.
Older criminals use young kids to commit crimes knowing those kids will likely not face consequences.
It is difficult, or even impossible, to prosecute minors under current law and that encourages young people to become lifetime criminals.
Our state has become a criminal training school.
We need to fix our criminal statutes to stop this cycle of crime.
Adding to the crime problem are our economic issues.
We need to lower taxes and reduce regulations to allow New Mexico business to grow, providing more jobs.
The legislature’s attempt to take guns away from law abiding people like you, instead of concentrating on criminals who threaten you, is misguided and doesn’t reduce crime.
I will work to strengthen our laws to make New Mexico a safer place to live while preserving your rights.
As your representative I will sponsor and support legislation that Provides clear guidelines to prevent judges from using the 2016 change to the constitution to continue our catch-and-release policies.
Prevents cities and counties from implementing or continuing “sanctuary city” policies that allow them to harbor dangerous criminal illegal aliens.
Toughens laws relating to juvenile offenders.
Provides more opportunities for behavioral health intervention, especially in drug and alcohol cases.
