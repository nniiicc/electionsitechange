Medical Care
It’s getting increasingly difficult to find a doctor in New Mexico.
That’s largely because of the extremely high malpractice awards the state allows.
The democrat controlled legislature is beholden to the state’s trial lawyers who don’t care about your ability to receive quality medical care as long as they reap big rewards.
A bill passed in January did put some limits on medical malpractice awards but did not cap punitive damages which are not covered by malpractice insurance.
Although medical professionals should be held accountable for their actions, and patients should be compensated for mistakes or malpractice, there must be reasonable limits.
Doctors will not practice in New Mexico if they know their entire financial life can be destroyed by a single disgruntled patient.
New Mexico must take action to bring malpractice awards in line with the rest of the country.
Our medical professionals are forced to pay Gross Receipts Tax on their fees, an expense that is not covered by insurance but comes directly from their pockets, again making it more expensive to practice here then in other states.
As your representative I will sponsor and support legislation that
- Puts reasonable caps on malpractice awards
- Removes medical Gross Receipts Tax
- Provides incentives to encourage medical professionals to locate in New Mexico
I will not support any effort to create a “Single Payer” system that puts the government in charge of your health care.
