Learn about Ranked Choice Voting About Absentee Voting: You can request an absentee ballot at https://absenteeballotapplication.alaska.gov/ Absentee Voting begins on October 3, 2026.
The deadline to request an absentee ballot is October 24, 2026.
If you received an absentee ballot, it will cost $1.46 or two Forever stamps to mail your ballot.
However, The USPS has stated they will deliver ballots with insufficient postage.
You can track your mailed ballot at https://myvoterinformation.alaska.gov/ .
If you have requested an absentee ballot but decide to vote in person instead, you can still vote in person.
