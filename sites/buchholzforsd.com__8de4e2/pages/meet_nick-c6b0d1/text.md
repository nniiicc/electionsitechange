Meet Nick News Volunteer Contribute Meet Nick I was born and raised in Yankton, South Dakota, where I graduated from high school.
Afterward, I attended university and flight school in Iowa, beginning a career that would take me across the country and around the world.
While I’ve lived in many places along the way, South Dakota has always been—and will always be—home.
After completing flight school, I married my wife, Kimberly, whose kindness and strength have been a constant foundation for our family.
As I built my career as a professional pilot, we moved several times before I joined the Department of Homeland Security as a federal agent.
In that role, I flew national security missions across the United States while we lived in North Dakota and Arizona.
During our time in North Dakota, Kimberly and I became foster parents to several sibling groups.
That experience had a profound impact on our lives.
We were blessed with the opportunity to adopt our two oldest daughters, and later welcomed two more children—a daughter and a son—into our family.
In early 2022, we made the decision to move back to South Dakota to be closer to our extended family.
It was one of the best decisions we’ve ever made.
We settled in Dell Rapids, where we’ve found an incredible community.
The schools, the people, and our church family have been truly exceptional.
Living here has reinforced the values we share with our neighbors—faith, hard work, and a commitment to one another.
It’s this strong sense of community and trust that inspired me to run for the District 25 State House of Representatives.
I believe in giving back to a place that has given so much to my family, and in serving when others may not have the opportunity to do so.
If elected, I will bring honest, conservative leadership to Pierre and work every day to represent the people of District 25 with integrity.
