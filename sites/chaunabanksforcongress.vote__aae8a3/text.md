Chauna Banks is running for U.S.
Congress in Louisiana’s 6th District to put people and communities first.
With proven leadership, a commitment to service, and a focus on real results, she’s ready to work for a stronger future for Louisiana.
Leadership starts with listening.
Chauna will keep the voices, needs, and concerns of District 6 communities at the center of her work.
Strong communities need real opportunity.
Chauna will champion good jobs, quality education, accessible healthcare, and investments that help families thrive.
Service should produce results.
Chauna will bring experienced, focused leadership to Congress and work across communities to deliver for District 6.
Support small business, expand workforce training, and attract good-paying jobs.
Invest in our schools, support teachers, and create pathways for student success.
Protect and expand access to affordable, quality healthcare for all families.
