Tiffany Brault: Local Leader with Local Roots
Tiffany Brault was born and raised in Fond du Lac.
She attended area schools and graduated with a bachelor’s degree from Marian University.
Following her college graduation, Tiffany worked in public and school libraries in Dodge County, but eventually left the workforce to become a stay-at-home parent due to high child care costs.
Tiffany currently works as a substitute teacher in the Fond du Lac School District.
Tiffany was first elected to local office in 2021 when she took a stand for residents who had been shut out of discussions on proposed developments in Lakeside Park.
She was elected to the Fond du Lac City Council, where she and fellow council members worked to restore order to the council and make Fond du Lac City Government stable and functional again.
Tiffany has supported investments in public safety, infrastructure, and local parks and community development.
She served as City Council President for two years and is currently Council Vice President.
In 2022, Tiffany was elected to represent District 20 on the Fond du Lac County Board.
She has served on both City Council and County Board since then.
She is the City Council representative on the Fond du Lac Public Library Board.
At the County level, she serves on the UW-Extension, Education, Agriculture and Recreation Committee, the Land Conservation Committee, and the Ripon Public Library Board.
She has previously served on the City-County Building Board, the Fond du Lac Metropolitan Planning Organization Policy Board, and the County Human Services Committee.
Tiffany is also a graduate of the Local Government Leadership Academy.
Tiffany is known for her willingness to dive into each issue and learn as much background information as she can before making a decision.
She works hard and values feedback from residents, because her decisions impact you!
Tiffany is not afraid to stand up for her constituents and has a record for challenging proposals that are not the best choice for Fond du Lac County residents.
Tiffany and her husband Andy have three children, a senior cat named Jasmine, and a golden retriever named Duke.
Tiffany enjoys reading, singing, and playing board games.
The most likely places you’ll find her outside of work and meetings is at a local thrift store, or at the library.
Tiffany Brault is an active member of our community.
Leader
Tiffany is a Fond du Lac native who has stepped up to serve her community in local government.
She is a member of both the Fond du Lac City Council and the Fond du Lac County Board.
She is currently City Council Vice President, having served as President for the past two years.
Volunteer
Tiffany is an active volunteer.
She is a member of the Water Monitors, a citizen group that collects data on Fond du Lac County waterways.
She has volunteered for Challenge Day, an anti-bullying workshop for high school students.
She regularly helps out at area events.
And she has sung over ten semesters with the South Shore Chorale.
Mom
Tiffany is the mom of three amazing children who are regularly recognized for being leaders in their schools, at Boys and Girls Club, and in extra-curricular activities.
Tiffany gave up a career she loved in libraries to become a stay-at-home mom, and she is forever grateful that she’s been able to dedicate so much of her life to raising some really great kids.
