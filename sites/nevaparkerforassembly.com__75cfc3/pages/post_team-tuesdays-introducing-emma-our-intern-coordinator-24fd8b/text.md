Introducing Team Tuesdays!
We’re kicking off a new weekly installment highlighting the team and the work they’re doing to build this campaign from the ground up!
First up: Emma, our Intern Coordinator!
Emma has put in an incredible amount of work to get Youth for Neva off the ground.
Drawing on her experience working on previous local congressional campaigns, she took an idea and built this program from scratch, helping create a space for young people to get involved, gain campaign experience, and make their voices heard.
We’re lucky to have Emma on Team Neva, and we have a feeling you’ll be seeing a lot more of her in the future of local politics!
Stay tuned every Tuesday as we introduce more of the team powering Team Neva.
