Doodling with Derwinne
Updated: Sep 19
Earlier this month I sat down with Westpark High School student, Derwinne, for her Doodling with Derwinne series.
Derwinne interviews candidates and community leaders from across the region all while doodling.
I had the privilege of being her very first interview.
It is not easy talking and trying to conjure up my dream house, but I did it!
It meant a lot that Derwinne asked me to be her first guest.
She wants to be a photojournalist, covering sports which sounds super cool to me!
And it was just a joy to watch her approach the whole process with a determined effort that blew me away.
She is a shinning example of what kids can do when education focuses its energy and resources on them.
Be sure to check it out.
I know for a fact she already has a few great interviews coming up.
Plus, when Derwinne's journalism career takes-off (and it will because she's amazing), you can say you knew her when.
