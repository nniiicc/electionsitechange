Neva Receives Endorsement from the California Women's List
Mar 30
During Women’s History Month, I’ve been reflecting on the generations of women who have paved the way for leadership, advocacy, and progress in our communities.
I’m honored to have the support of the California Women’s List, an organization committed to electing women who are ready to lead and fight for equity across our state.
Their mission reflects why I’m running: to bring honest, accountable, and forward-thinking leadership to our district, and to ensure more voices are heard at the decision-making table.
Grateful to be part of this movement, and ready to keep pushing forward.
