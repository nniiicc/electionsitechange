YOUTH FOR NEVA
This is a 6-week program specifically designed for young people in high school, college, or recent college graduates interested in politics, current events, and community organizing.
You will have the opportunity to choose a specific internship track based on your interests and learn about the inner workings of a political campaign.
The Youth for Neva internship is now over, but if you're still interested in interning or volunteering with the campaign, please email: info@nevaparkerforassembly.com
