Neva Parker Receives Endorsement from the California Democratic Party
Mar 2
I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the California Democratic Party.
It’s a recognition of a campaign grounded in people, standing up for working families, protecting our rural communities, and making sure government works for everyone.
Our district deserves to be the priority, not a political launching pad.
I’m running to bring resources home, listen to what our communities need, and do the work to deliver real results.
And while this endorsement comes from Democrats, my responsibility as your Assembly member will be to everyone.
No matter your party or your vote, you deserve to be heard, respected, and represented with integrity.
That’s the standard I’ll hold myself to every single day.
