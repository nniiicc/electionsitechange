Josie Tomkow is a 7th-generation Floridian, 3rd-generation cattle rancher, graduate of the University of Florida and former state representative from the Tampa Bay area.
During her time in the Florida House of Representatives, Josie served as Chair of the Health & Human Services Committee, the Pre-K–12 Appropriations Subcommittee and the Agriculture & Natural Resources Subcommittee, where she championed policies supporting Florida families, students, and the state’s agricultural legacy.
Prior to her public service, she gained extensive experience in public policy and agricultural advocacy, working in Washington, D.C. for the Florida Farm Bureau; in Tallahassee for the Florida Cattlemen’s Association; and in Texas for Superior Livestock.
Josie’s been a champion of protecting Florida’s natural beauty, combating flooding, and supporting our men and women in law enforcement.
Josie’s mission is to get results for our community.
Florida faces very exciting opportunities and challenges and needs leaders who will roll up their sleeves and get to work solving problems and not worrying about who gets the credit or whose idea it was.
Josie wants to solve the problems and focus on results.
Josie lives in South Tampa, with her husband, Thomas.
Florida is ranked #1 in the nation for fiscal health.
As a State Representative, Josie Tomkow has voted on billions in tax cuts aimed to help working Floridians put more money back in their pockets – including permanent sales tax holiday’s for back-to-school items, disaster preparedness items, state park admissions, life jackets, outdoor equipment and more.
In addition, Josie voted to remove sales tax on diapers and baby clothes and $450 million In toll relief as well as $502 million in tax, fee and assessment exemptions for homeowner’s insurance policies.

33 property insurance companies have asked for rate decreases and the top five auto insurers are averaging over a 6% rate reduction.
Additionally, we have to ensure that policyholders get the benefits they paid for and don’t have to fight for what’s rightfullly theirs and keep fraud and abuse out of the system that ends up getting passed down to consumers.
All the while, continuing to do more to bolster the reinsurance market to drive rates lower more quickly.
