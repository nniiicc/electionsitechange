Homeless Veterans
In 2017, I sold everything, packed my car, and traveled to 33 states in this beautiful nation with my Chihuahua, Boomer.
For two years, I passed out survival gear to 312 homeless veterans along with other people in need I met along the way.
I decided to run for office serving NC District 5 after neither Democrats nor Republicans in DC had time to talk to me about my proposed Character by Integrity Act which offers sustainable, permanent solutions to many of the recurring housing issues we face.
Now, I will make sure it gets done.
