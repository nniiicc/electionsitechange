Social Security, Medicaid, & Medicare must be expanded, not cut.

Conversely, passing audits opens budget doors.
Social Security, Medicaid, and Medicare regularly pass audits; the Pentagon never has.
If the Pentagon deserves a new $1 trillion defense budget without passing an audit, we can certainly justify expanding what every American has already paid for and deserves.

View more The Patient Priority Act The Patient Priority Act provides legal protections to healthcare providers for administering life-saving medical procedures.
In many states, healthcare providers' efforts to care for patients are hampered due to intrusive and oppressive laws.
Decisions between providers and patients should be private.
Providers should never be ordered to compromise their patients' health and safety to avoid criminal charges.
First, do no harm.

View more The Policing Wellness Act This national mandate requires that law enforcement officers receive randomized, functional psychological evaluations.
Law enforcement takes a mental and emotional toll and, systemically, the message stands that addressing these tolls is 'weak'.
This proposal ensures LEOs receive the emotional help they need which will simultaneously lower police brutality cases and civil suits that cost cities and taxpayers billions of dollars.
Good cops make good communities; bad cops abuse all communities.
View more The Due Process Act The Due Process Act ensures that federal agencies cannot detain and/or imprison anyone in any facility without due process.
State oversight is mandated for custody of individuals in federal custody.
Should individuals be apprehended without a judicial warrant stating the name of those apprehended, due process has not been met and the actions are unconstitutional.
Due process for all.
