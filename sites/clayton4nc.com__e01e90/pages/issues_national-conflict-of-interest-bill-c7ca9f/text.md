Key provisions include prohibiting elected officials from participating in legislation that financially benefits them, enforcing maximum salary increases aligned with minimum wage changes, and instituting regular performance reviews by an oversight committee.
The bill also includes measures to ensure transparency in donations and restricts the Supreme Court judges' terms, emphasizing the importance of integrity in government positions.
