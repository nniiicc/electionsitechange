Our commitment
Friends of Saira Draper, Inc. wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.
Our standard
We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.
This site has not had a formal third-party audit, so we describe this as our goal rather than a claim of full conformance.
What we do
- We test pages with automated accessibility checks (axe-core) on desktop and mobile layouts.
- We are reviewing key pages and forms by hand with a keyboard, zoom, and a screen reader.
- We offer the site in English, Spanish, and Vietnamese.
- We provide a text-size control in the site header.
- We respect your device’s reduced-motion setting and let you pause moving content.
Known limitations
- Interactive maps (provided by Mapbox) can be difficult to use with a keyboard or screen reader.
Most of the places and addresses they show are also listed as text on the same page.
- Donations are processed on a form hosted by ActBlue.
We do not control the accessibility of that form.
- Some articles imported from our newsletter platform (Beehiiv) may include images without text descriptions.
Voting information for people with disabilities
Information about the upcoming Elections
The November 3, 2026 elections will be critical for Georgia’s future.
Review the Democrats running for statewide office, and remember to vote all the way down your ballot.
Supporting the statewide candidates AND your local representatives is crucial.
Flipping a chamber would allow us to pass meaningful legislation that helps hardworking Georgians.
It would also allow us to push back against likely efforts to redraw maps this December.
What you can do now
- Confirm your voter registration and make any necessary updates by October 5.
- Make a plan to vote—and encourage your friends and family to do the same.
I strongly recommend voting early in person if possible.
Early voting offers flexibility (you may vote at any early voting site in your county), helps avoid long lines, provides time to resolve issues, and reduces the risk of encountering disruptions at your polling place.
- There is good additional voting guidance at the Democratic Party of Georgia website.
- Voters with disabilities can find excellent information on voting rights, accessibility tools, and available resources at REV UP Georgia.
If you can do more
- Volunteers are urgently needed.
You can serve as a poll worker, poll watcher, or support the Voter Protection Hotline.
Learn more about voter protection volunteering.
- If you have concerns or questions, call the Voter Protection Hotline at 888-730-5816.
