Let’s Get Going on Voter Outreach
The DPG coordinated campaign office for our region is at 4746 Flat Shoals Parkway.
You can sign up to canvass from this office on weekdays here, and weekends here.
And…packets will again be available from my porch starting this weekend.
Sign up here.
We’ll have shifts at Noon on Saturday Sept 19 and 1 on Sunday Sept 20 (and we’ll amp up from there…if you need us to get you a turf for another time, send an email to info@votesaira.com and we’ll get you set up).

Keep an eye out for more information on those activities.
Looking forward to seeing you all again and doing our part to ensure November brings a big blue wave!
Help Ensure Fair Elections
There are loads of volunteer opportunities to help voters and protect election integrity.
Phone bank to help Georgians update their voter registration!
Volunteer to be a poll watcher!
To find these and many more volunteer opportunities go to: https://gadems.info/voprovol
Finally–if you have any questions or concerns about voting…the Democratic Party of Georgia’s Voter Protection Hotline is live 1-888-730-5816.
I recommend saving the number in your phone and sharing it widely.
