We’re waiting.
House Speaker Jon Burns promised hearings ahead of any redistricting process to ensure transparency and public input.
Three months later, the hearings haven’t happened. ajc.com ↗ The AJC reports that promised statewide public hearings on Georgia redistricting remain unscheduled, raising transparency concerns ahead of future map redrawing.
Following calls to redraw Georgia’s political maps, House Speaker Jon Burns pledged in June that the state would conduct transparent, statewide hearings to allow voters to weigh in before any new districts were considered.
However, an analysis by The Atlanta Journal-Constitution reveals that three months later, no public hearings have taken place or been scheduled.
“If Keisha Lance Bottoms wins, they’re going to push this through, and I think any promises of halting for pending litigation or transparency will be out the window,” said state Rep.
Saira Draper, D-Atlanta, who is also an election lawyer.

Review the Democrats running for statewide office , and remember to vote all the way down your ballot.
Supporting the statewide candidates AND your local representatives is crucial.

It would also allow us to push back against likely efforts to redraw maps this December.
What you can do now Confirm your voter registration and make any necessary updates by October 5.
Make a plan to vote—and encourage your friends and family to do the same.
I strongly recommend voting early in person if possible.
Early voting offers flexibility (you may vote at any early voting site in your county), helps avoid long lines, provides time to resolve issues, and reduces the risk of encountering disruptions at your polling place.
There is good additional voting guidance at the Democratic Party of Georgia website .
Voters with disabilities can find excellent information on voting rights, accessibility tools, and available resources at REV UP Georgia .
