Why I Recommend Voting Early For this election, I am strongly recommending early, in‑person voting.
It offers several advantages: You can vote at any early‑voting site in the county where you’re registered.
It’s the best protection against registration or Election Day issues — bad weather, power outages, or shenanigans at the polls.
If something comes up, you still have time to try again.
Lines are short, especially after the first day.
Once you vote, you’ll come off many lists for mailings, door knocks, and calls/texts.
It helps the campaign target their efforts on voters that need info and reduces lines for last minute voters!
Early voting runs October 13–October 30 To find locations and times near you for Clayton, DeKalb and major metro counties use our voting tool at: www.votesaira.com/vote .
Or you can check mvp.sos.ga.gov for all other counties.

What you can do now Confirm your voter registration and make any necessary updates by October 5.
Make a plan to vote—and encourage your friends and family to do the same.
I strongly recommend voting early in person if possible.
Early voting offers flexibility (you may vote at any early voting site in your county), helps avoid long lines, provides time to resolve issues, and reduces the risk of encountering disruptions at your polling place.
There is good additional voting guidance at the Democratic Party of Georgia website .
Voters with disabilities can find excellent information on voting rights, accessibility tools, and available resources at REV UP Georgia .
