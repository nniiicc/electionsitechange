Deadline Alert: October 5 Is the Last Day to Register to Vote in Georgia
Georgia’s voter registration deadline is October 5.
Rep.
Saira Draper urges Georgians to verify their status and register before the cutoff.
The deadline to register to vote in Georgia’s upcoming election is Monday, October 5.
If you're a new voter, or if you've moved since your last registration, make sure you update and verify your registration BY TODAY to ensure your registration to vote in upcoming Midterm elections!
How to Register or Check Your Status
Registering to vote or verifying your current status is straightforward:
- Check Your Status: Visit the Georgia My Voter Page to confirm your registration address and precinct information.
- Register Online: Eligible citizens with a valid Georgia driver's license or state ID can submit or update their registration directly online.
- If You Need Assistance: Call the Voter Protection Hotline: 888-730-5816.
