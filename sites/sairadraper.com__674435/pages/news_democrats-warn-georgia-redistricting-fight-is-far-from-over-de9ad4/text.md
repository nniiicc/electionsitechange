AJC: Democrats Say Georgia Redistricting Fight Isn’t Over
Democrats warn Georgia redistricting fight is far from over
Republican state legislators announced they would not redraw Georgia’s political maps, but Democrats are wary.
Rep.
Draper urged transparency as Democrats warned Georgia’s redistricting fight may return after the November elections.
Rep.
Saira Draper is featured in The Atlanta Journal-Constitution as Georgia Democrats respond to Republican leaders’ decision not to redraw political maps during the special session:
Democratic state Rep.
Saira Draper of Atlanta said Republicans must be clear about their long-term plan.
“They need to break it down with voters about what their intentions are, because if their intentions are just to bring this back after November … before we go into a new administration, that is cowardly,” she said.
“Voters will see right through it.”
