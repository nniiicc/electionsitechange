Why I Recommend Voting Early
For this election, I am strongly recommending early, in‑person voting.
It offers several advantages:
- You can vote at any early‑voting site in the county where you’re registered.
- It’s the best protection against registration or Election Day issues — bad weather, power outages, or shenanigans at the polls.
If something comes up, you still have time to try again.
- Lines are short, especially after the first day.
- Once you vote, you’ll come off many lists for mailings, door knocks, and calls/texts.
- It helps the campaign target their efforts on voters that need info and reduces lines for last minute voters!
Early voting runs October 13–October 30
- To find locations and times near you for Clayton, DeKalb and major metro counties use our voting tool at: www.votesaira.com/vote.
- Or you can check mvp.sos.ga.gov for all other counties.
