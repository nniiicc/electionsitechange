CONTACT INFORMATION ​ Contact: Amy Hawkins​ ​ Email: info@anthonyforlini.com ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ Release Date September 21, 2026 ​ Democrat Leaders Cross Party ​ Lines to Support Forlini Leaders who know and have worked with ​ Anthony Forlini put People over Party ​ Macomb, Mich: Today, four well known Michigan Democrats set partisan politics aside and announced their endorsement and support of Macomb County Clerk Anthony Forlini, a Republican running for Secretary of State.
Anthony Forlini released the following statement, “I am humbled by these officials stepping across party lines to support me.
I have worked with each of them in some manner, and believe they understand I always do what’s best for the citizens I represent.
I do not play political games when in office.” Democrats endorsing and supporting Forlini include: ​ State Senator Paul Wojno Macomb County Commissioner Harold Haugh Former State Representative Richard LeBlanc Former State Representative Marilyn Lane Senator Paul Wojno noted, “Anthony is as independent as they come.
He doesn’t report to anyone other than the people he represents, and I admire that.
Anthony stands for people over politics.” Former State Representative Marilyn Lane commented, “Both as the Mayor of Fraser and as a State Representative I can attest firsthand his leadership qualities, his ability to bring people together, no matter what your opposing views are he always found a way to bridge it…I know he will make an excellent Secretary of State because those qualities are what we need more of.” Anthony Forlini has stressed throughout his campaign that he views the office as needing good customer service and transparency.
He believes strongly that these needs are not partisan.
