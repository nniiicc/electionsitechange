CONTACT INFORMATION ​ Contact: Amy Hawkins​ ​ Email: info@anthonyforlini.com ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ Release Date September 24, 2026 ​ Another Prominent Michigan Democrat supports Forlini for Michigan Secretary of State The List of Democrat Leaders Supporting Forlini Grows Macomb, Mich: Anthony Forlini, Republican candidate for Michigan Secretary of State is proud to announce the endorsement of the elected Democrat Macomb County Sheriff Tony Wickersham.
Sheriff Wickersham noted, “I am supporting Tony Forlini for Secretary of State this November.
Tony is an honest man with integrity.
He does the right thing and he puts people first.
So this November, vote for Tony Forlini for Michigan Secretary of State.” Anthony Forlini commented, “I have known Sheriff Wickersham for years and party politics has never mattered to either of us.
We always saw each other in our elected roles for who we were good, hard working Americans.
This endorsement means the world to me.
I believe strongly that processes and good services should always come above politics.
It’s true of a Clerk and true of a Sheriff.
We must be about putting people first in Michigan once again and Sheriff Wickersham does that well.” Sheriff Wickersham joins a growing list of Michigan Democrats who have endorsed Anthony Forlini for Secretary of State.
Earlier this week it was announced he had the support of current State Senator Paul Wojno, Macomb County Commissioner Harold Haugh, Former State Representative Richard LeBlanc, Former State Representative Marilyn Lane.
Forlini welcomes support from everyone in Michigan regardless of political party, and will continue traveling to every corner of our state to earn voter’s support leading into November.
