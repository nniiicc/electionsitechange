Dear neighbors,
My name is Mary Frances Clardy.
For 28 years, I have served as an educator, local leader, and disability advocate committed to building a Minnesota where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
In 2022, I had the great honor of being elected to serve in the Minnesota House of Representatives on behalf of the recently redrawn District 53A — which includes Inver Grove Heights, Sunfish Lake, Lilydale and portions of West Saint Paul and Mendota Heights.
In 2026, my re-election campaign will remain rooted in the hopes and concerns of our community.
I hope to see you again as I travel around the district to hear about your values, concerns, and priorities.
See you soon!
