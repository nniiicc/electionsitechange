Constitution The U.S.
Constitution has been under attack for several years now.
These attacks come in various forms, but they all seem to have one objective: to weaken the powers of the Courts and the Legislature, and to increase the power of the Executive. ​ In Federalist No.
47, James Madison defined "tyranny" as the excessive accumulation of power in one set of hands.
In order to combat against tyranny, the Founders divided power into three branches: the Executive, Legislative, and Judicial.
Each has its own sphere of influence and ways of interacting with the others. ​ The Executive has the power of the sword and the power to act.
The Legislative has the power of the purse and is closest to the People.
The Judicial has only the power of judgment over the operations of the other two and itself. ​ For this system to work as intended, all three branches must respect the other.
Each of the branches must "stay in their lane".
This requires respect, civility, and tolerance.
This is the way to civil peace. ​ I fully oppose the efforts of the current Administration to enlarge the power of the Executive beyond its proper bounds.
If elected to Congress, I will do all I can to reestablish the authority and power of the Legislative branch in our Federal affairs. ​ For a deeper dive on my thoughts on this issue, check out this article I posted back on May 10, 2025, going into the Constitutional Issues we are facing at a deeper level.
