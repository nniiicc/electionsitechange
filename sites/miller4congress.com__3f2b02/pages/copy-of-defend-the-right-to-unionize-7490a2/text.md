That means simply that the weather patterns we have been relying on for the past several hundred years are now changing.
This means that certain areas of the planet that have been habitable for a long time, may not be so in the future.
It also means that certain zones of agriculture that we have been relying on for quite sometime may no longer be fruitful in the future.
