BIO The American way of life is under threat like never before Like many of you, I have watched in disbelief as our Nation has deteriorated since January of 2025.

Beyond that, we are experiencing an unprecedented attack on the U.S.
Constitution itself from what James Madison referred to as an "Overgrown Executive". ​ We have serious problems, and we need serious people who are focused on solving them.

Both sides are to blame. ​ It needs to stop for the good of the Nation.

I believe our best days lie before us, but only if we get serious and come together as a Nation.
