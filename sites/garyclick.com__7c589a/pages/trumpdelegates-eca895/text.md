Gary Click selected as 2020 Delegate by the Trump Campaign
Gary is the only candidate for the 88th district to be invited by the Trump campaign to serve as one of six delegates for the president representing Ohio's fourth congressional district.
Gary signed the paperwork to serve as an alternate delegate on December 5.
Ohio Republican Party Chairman, Jane Timken, filed the paperwork with the Ohio Secretary of State's office on December 16.
The 2020 convention will be held in Charlotte, NC.
