Brian's Top Issues For Assembly District 34
Tax the Rich
Make the rich pay their fair share by increasing the corporate tax, increasing taxes on the top earners, and passing a stock transfer tax.
Housing
Build social and affordable housing and protect our tenants from displacement.
Public Transit
Universal Child Care
Fund free child care for children and pay child care workers what they deserve
Immigration
Protect families from ICE by passing the New York for All Act to prohibit law enforcement from collaborating with ICE, the MELT Act to ensure immigration enforcement cannot hide behind masks, and the Access to Representation Act to ensure we can support our neighbors with legal services.
Safety
As a survivor of domestic violence to me all safety is interconnected.
Community safety means ensuring that all of us are safe from systemic and interpersonal violence..
I will fund community violence interruptors, afterschool programs for youth, and mental health response teams, and work with Mayor Mamdani to stop overpolicing and surveillance in our neighborhoods.
Healthcare
Pass the New York Health Act to provide universal healthcare to all New Yorkers regardless of their immigration status, income, or employment.
LGBT Rights
As the only LGBT candidate in this race, I have fought and will fight for our queer and trans communities.
I will advocate for access to and expansion of gender affirming care, to support anti-bullying efforts in schools by updating law and fully funding its implementation, and to improve employment support for our queer and trans workers.
Clean Energy
As someone who grew up in Astoria the strains of asthma alley are personal.
I will push the New York Power Authority to build 15 GW of renewable energy, push for the passage of the New York Heat Act to ensure we protect our pockets and our planet, and push back on rate hikes from Con Edison.
Workers' Rights
I will stand up for all workers and their ability to organize and join a union.
It is time to fight for a living wage indexed to inflation and the end of all sub minimum wages.
I will fight to protect all of our excluded workers from wage theft, harassment, and abuse.
Gender Justice
I believe in advancing equity for all women, queer and gender-expansive people.
I will fight to expand reproductive healthcare and gender affirming care, and to protect our communities from gender-based violence, hate violence, and discrimination.
Regulating Artificial Intelligence
I support AI innovation with strict guardrails.
The environmental and economic costs are already too high to ignore.
I'm joining the call for a data center moratorium and demanding stronger private-sector regulation.
As a former therapist, I beleive we must ban AI "psychotherapy".
Therapy is a service that should only be provided by licensed professionals.
Small Businesses
Small businesses are the heart of Queens.
I will fight for commercial rent stabilization and a Small Business Inflation Rebate Program to help neighborhood businesses stay open and withstand rising costs.
I will advocate for the inclusion of Middle Eastern and North African (MENA) communities in the MWBE program to ensure more entrepreneurs can access opportunities and support.
Fully fund the MTA and Make Buses Fast and Free
