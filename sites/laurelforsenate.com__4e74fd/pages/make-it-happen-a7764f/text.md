Making It Happen
Communities work best when people have a fair chance to succeed and government delivers results that matter in their daily lives.
As your senator, I will commit to showing up, listening deeply, and bringing resources back to our district.
Too often, communities are forced to compete for limited resources.
I will work to ensure the 31st District has a strong voice at the table and receives its fair share of state resources.
Building relationships:
- I listen before I act.
- I bring people together.
- I work across differences.
- I show up in every community in the district.
- I believe the people closest to challenges are often closest to the solutions.
Creating effective solutions:
- I will work to lower the everyday costs that families face, from healthcare and housing to energy and property taxes.
- I will actively pursue state grants, infrastructure investments, economic development opportunities, and other resources so that Bristol, Plainville, Plymouth, Thomaston, and Harwinton receive their fair share of state support.
- I will focus on results, turning community priorities into action.
Budgeting for our priorities:
- I will work to ensure we spend tax payer money wisely and budget responsibly.
- I will support measuring the results of taxpayer spending, holding programs accountable, and reducing waste.
- I will work toward a tax system that supports economic opportunity while ensuring that working families and seniors are not carrying an unfair share of the burden.
