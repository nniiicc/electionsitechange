Why I’m Running
The people of Bristol, Plainville, Plymouth, Thomaston, and Harwinton deserve a senator who listens, shows up, and gets things done.
Throughout my career as a program developer, evaluator, community organizer, and advocate, I have brought people together to solve complex problems.
I've learned that the best solutions come from listening first, understanding people's experiences, and working across differences to create meaningful change.
That's exactly how I intend to serve the 31st District.
As your senator, I will work to bring resources back to our district, lower the everyday costs families face, and ensure that our communities have a strong voice at the table in Hartford.
Thank you for your support, encouragement, and friendship.
I'm excited about the opportunity to serve our communities, and I hope you'll join me in this effort.
