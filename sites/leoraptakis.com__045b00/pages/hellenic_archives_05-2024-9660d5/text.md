| Crete (01/20/24): "The Board of Directors of the World Interparliamentary Union of Hellenism led by the president Leonidas Raptakis, a senator from the USA, visited today the regional governor of Crete Stavros Arnautakis.
The members of the Board of Directors of PADEE participated in a working meeting in the Region of Crete in which the mayor of Heraklion Alexis Kalokairinos, the president of the Chamber of Heraklion Manolis Alifierakis, elected representatives of the Region of Crete, the Municipality of Heraklion and officials also took part."
