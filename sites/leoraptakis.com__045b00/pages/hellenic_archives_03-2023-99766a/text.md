Home About Agenda Hellenic Donate Newsroom Social Contact HELLENIC ISSUES Celebrating March 25th, as the 202nd Anniversary of Greek Independence Day and Democracy in Greece and the USA 3/22/2023 0 Comments Democracy in the USA and Greece RI STATE HOUSE – "State Senator Leonidas P.
Raptakis (D-Dist.
33, Coventry, West Greenwich) and Rhode Island's Hellenic communities hosted a celebration of Greek Independence Day at the Rhode Island State House on Tuesday, bringing together state leaders and members of all three Hellenic communities from Cranston, Newport and Pawtucket to recognize the 202nd anniversary of Greece’s independence." Leaders of Rhode Island’s Greek community, Consul General of Greece to New England Symeon Tegos and Nicholas Larigakis, the President and CEO of the American Hellenic Institute, meeting with Senate President Dominick J.
Ruggerio, Senate Majority Leader Ryan W.
Person, Sen.
Dawn Euer and Senator Leonidas Raptakis in the Senate President’s office before the celebration.
"His Eminence Metropolitan Methodios, Sen.
Raptakis and RI’s Hellenic community celebrate the 202nd anniversary of Greek Independence Day and the Feast of the Annunciation at the State House." Participants in the 202nd anniversary of Greek Independence celebration held in the State Room on March 21,2023 "Greek Consul General Symeon Tegos and Rhode Island General Officers join local Greek leaders for the 30th consecutive year of celebrating Greek Independence Day at the State House."
