Learn More Main Content Erika Hancock provides the steady and accomplished leadership we need in the General Assembly!
Erika Hancock is a married mother of two.
Her daughter, Paulie, is 20 years old, and her son, Houston, is 16.
Erika has lived in Frankfort and worked as a successful businesswoman for over 20 years.
She has been happily married to her husband, Jeff, for 23 years.
Whether it was working her way through school at the University of Kentucky, balancing a busy family life, growing a business, or being active in various volunteer boards and organizations in her community, Erika has consistently demonstrated a willingness to listen and be reasonable while standing up for what she knows is right.
As State Representative, Erika wants to follow in the footsteps of Derrick Graham and Jullian Carrol and fight for state workers to retirees.
For too long, legislators have used state workers as punching bags.
It's past time they get respect they have earned. erika hancock Through her years in business, Erika has learned that the best way to deal with a difficult person isn't to talk at them but to talk to them.
She plans to bring some much-needed civility to the Kentucky General Assembly.
Responsive to her constituent needs, Erika prioritizes making a real difference in people's lives.
She is available and accessible to the people she serves.
In the General Assembly Erika serves on the following committees: Elections, Constitutional Amendments & Intergovernmental Affairs, Natural Resources & Energy, State Government, Sub-committee on Personnel, Public Retirement, & Finance, Banking & Insurance and Tobacco Settlement Agreement Fund Oversight Committee.
Erika's office in the Legislative Annex is Room 467-B/ You can reach her legislative office by phone at: 502-561-8100 or email at: erika.hancock@kylegislature.gov.
