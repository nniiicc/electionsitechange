HB 500
Like you, I want affordable, stable health insurance for Kentucky’s state employees and teachers who serve our communities every day.
That’s why I support a responsible approach to dealing with rising health-care costs instead of capping the state’s contribution.
A cap may sound fiscally cautious, but it shifts costs onto workers and their families through higher premiums and reduced benefits.
There is a better path.
Kentucky has built a strong Budget Reserve Trust Fund (rainy-day fund) to help manage economic pressures and unexpected cost increases.
Using a portion of those reserves, alongside ongoing revenues, allows us to cover rising insurance costs without placing the burden on state employees.
State employees shouldn’t be balancing the budget with their health coverage.
We can be fiscally responsible, protect workers, and use our reserves for exactly what they’re meant for: stabilizing costs when Kentuckians need them most.
