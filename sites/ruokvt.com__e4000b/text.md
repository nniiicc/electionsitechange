Where Suz Stands
Oversight
Protecting Your Tax Dollars
Vermonters are feeling the pressure of rising taxes, education costs, and everyday expenses.
I’ll work to eliminate fraud, waste, and abuse in Washington, protect Social Security, and ensure your tax dollars are spent responsibly.
Accountability
Putting People Before Special Interests
Government should answer to the people—not powerful donors or political insiders.
I’ll fight for election integrity, transparent government, and a justice system that keeps our communities safe and fair.
Healthcare
Healthier People, Stronger Communities
Healthcare should focus on preventing illness, promoting wellness, and giving people real choices.
I support affordable healthcare, better mental health resources, and empowering Vermonters to live healthier, happier lives.
Investment
Building Vermont’s Future
Creating good jobs and affordable communities starts with smart investment.
I’ll work to bring federal resources to Vermont that strengthen our infrastructure, support local businesses, expand opportunities, and help our economy grow.
