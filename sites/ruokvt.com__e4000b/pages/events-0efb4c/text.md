Politics UNusual Bike Tour Across Vermont with SUZ SEYMOUR Mon, Sep 7 • 10:00am – Tue, Oct 27, 7:00pm EDT all across this Green Mountain State WATCH for SUZ, an Independent Candidate for U.S.
Congress, as she makes her way around Vermont this Fall on her bright yellow electric Tricycle.
Suz was in a head-on collision with a construction vehicle in 2022, breaking 12 bones including 9 ribs, her right arm and a compound fracture to her femur, resulting in two plates.
Since then, having spent 4 months in a wheelchair, and over 2 years in physical therapy, she learned how to walk again and reclaim her body.
An avid biker before the accident, Suz hopes to transform her body into even better, healthier version of her old self.
Through her campaign, she will continue talking about her healing journey, what it was like to live the disabled life and what can be done to help people in the healthcare/disease-care system we have inherited.
The creator of PTK (Physical Therapy Karaoke), Suz learned thru experience a new way to heal and wants to share that message to the masses.
Want Suz to travel to and speak in your town?
Reminiscent of Fred Tuttle, (Man With a Plan), Suz will transverse this great state and bring the messages of hope and inspiration through Independent thinking, to the 2026 Election cycle.
