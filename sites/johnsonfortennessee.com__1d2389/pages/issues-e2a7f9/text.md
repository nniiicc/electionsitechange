Protecting Tennessee's Elections
Jack is proud that Tennessee leads the nation in election integrity.
Under conservative Republican leadership, Tennessee ranks #1 in the nation for election security.
Jack has sponsored and passed multiple laws to strengthen election integrity, secure our voting process, and ensure that every legal vote counts while keeping illegal votes out.
Trust in our elections is essential to our democracy, and he will always fight to protect the integrity of our electoral process.
Jack sponsored and passed legislation requiring the Secretary of State to conduct random post-election audits to verify the accuracy of election results.
He also passed a law requiring hand-counted public audits of paper ballots to further enhance transparency.
In addition, he passed a law requiring all voting machines in Tennessee to produce a voter-verified paper audit trail, adding an extra layer of security to the voting process.
To prevent non-citizens from voting in Tennessee elections, he passed a law ensuring that legal, non-citizen residents receive a distinct driver’s license that clearly indicates their ineligibility to vote.
This law guarantees that only U.S. citizens can register and cast a ballot in our elections.
Additionally, Jack fought to pass and protect Tennessee’s strong Voter ID law, which requires voters to present valid identification before casting a ballot.
Voter ID laws stop fraud, prevent impersonation, and ensure confidence in our elections.
Jack will always stand up to protect Tennessee’s elections from fraud, manipulation, and outside interference.
While other states weaken their election laws, Tennessee will continue to lead the nation in security, fairness, and trust in the democratic process.
Protecting the Integrity of Party Primaries
Republican voters deserve the opportunity to select their party’s nominees in a free and fair election—without interference from Democrats or party insiders.
That’s why Jack sponsored and passed Senate Bill 799, which ensures that candidates for partisan office are nominated through a transparent process that gives every Republican a voice—not through closed-door conventions or insider-controlled caucuses that disenfranchise Republican voters, like our brave men and women in uniform and first responders.
This law strengthens voter confidence and protects the integrity of our primary elections.
Jack is also co-sponsoring legislation to bring party registration to Tennessee.
While current law makes it a crime to vote in a party’s primary without either being a bona fide member of that party, or without declaring affiliation with that party in good faith on election day, there is currently no formal process for voters to make that commitment in advance.
The Republican Party has always been a big tent—welcoming Americans from all walks of life who believe in freedom, opportunity, and limited government.
As President Reagan once said, “I didn’t leave the Democratic Party.
The Democratic Party left me.” That spirit still defines our movement today.
But if you want to help choose our nominees, you should make that decision before election day and declare your party affiliation.
Jack’s bill gives voters the opportunity to do just that—so that Republican primaries are decided by Republicans.
