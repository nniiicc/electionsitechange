More on Gise's priorities Conservative Values, Freedom and Faith Emily is dedicated to upholding conservative values like freedom and faith, working to strengthen our communities, schools, and defending conservative principles.
She is committed to protecting our values and rights, reducing the impact of inflation on hard working Oklahomans, protecting our borders, lowering taxes, supporting the Second Amendment, and Backing the Blue.
Government Accountability The government has no money; it's the people's money!
Gise is committed to ensuring our government remains accountable and transparent to its citizens.
She will advocate for policies and measures that promote open and honest governance, making it easier for citizens to understand how decisions are made and where taxpayer dollars are spent.
Gise believes overtaxation is nothing short of legalized robbery , stripping hard-working Oklahomans of their rightfully earned income.
She is dedicated to fighting against government overreach, reducing waste, and advocating for lower taxes to keep more of Oklahomans' hard-earned money in their pockets .
Thriving Communities Gise is committed to creating a community where families thrive, children grow, and everyone reaches their full potential.
Beyond prioritizing border protection, affordable housing, enhanced education funding, and teacher support, she extends her commitment to tackling the opioid epidemic and providing robust support for women and children who are victims of domestic violence.
Equipped with nearly a decade of state experience and a lifetime of nonprofit work, she's experienced and ready to tackle these complex issues within our community.
Safe and Stellar Schools for All Emily understands firsthand the challenges faced by her sisters, who are dedicated teachers striving to provide the support their students deserve.
With a deep commitment to ensuring every child has access to a top-notch education within a safe and nurturing setting, Emily prioritizes initiatives that empower parents to make decisions about their children's education, rather than rely on government bureaucrats.
From advocating for better teacher compensation to implementing robust school safety measures and addressing achievement gaps, Emily champions policies that foster equitable access to educational opportunities, uplift teachers and provide high support for students.
People-First Policies Every policy we enact should enhance the lives of our constituents, and Emily will work tirelessly to ensure that it does.
Emily is committed to listening to the concerns and needs of our community members, ensuring that your voices shape the policies we pursue.
Emily will be an advocate for fair and responsible funding of public services; always ensuring the impact puts people first.
Economic Prosperity Emily’s priority is to strengthen local businesses by reducing red tape and rewarding entrepreneurship.
