My name is Bentley Hudgins.
I am a human rights leader and voting rights expert who has spent the last decade organizing to build real progressive power.
I am also a union member with the NewsGuild-CWA Local 32035, running to be the next representative of Georgia’s 90 th State House District.
I have been on the frontlines of democracy, protecting our neighbors' rights and meeting their needs in real time.
I’ve led efforts to register hundreds of thousands of voters, install ballot drop boxes statewide during the pandemic, helped over 20,000 Georgians access COVID vaccines and secure $300,000 to curb gun violence and redevelop Longdale Park, where I live with my fiancé.
This fight is personal to me, my growing family and our lives.
I have organized, I have won, and I am ready to fight like hell with our incoming majority for the Georgia we deserve – a Georgia for us .
We are committed to being for and by our neighbors.
