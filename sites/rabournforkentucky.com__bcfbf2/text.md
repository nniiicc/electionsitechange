SUBMIT Meet Lifelong Republican Felicia Rebourn Pro Life I am unapologetically pro life and have consistently advocated for the sanctity of human life throughout my time in office.
I believe every human life is valuable and deserving of legal protection from conception until natural death.
As a legislator, I have remained committed to defending the unborn and supporting families.
I am proud to be endorsed by Kentucky Right to Life and Northern Kentucky Right to Life, organizations that have long fought to protect the unborn and defend pro life values across the Commonwealth.
Pro Second Amendment I am a strong supporter of the Second Amendment and firmly believe law abiding citizens have the constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
I will always stand against efforts to infringe on those rights and remain committed to protecting the freedoms guaranteed by both the United States and Kentucky Constitutions.
During my time in office, I have consistently supported and co sponsored pro Second Amendment legislation, including campus carry legislation that protects the rights of responsible firearm owners.
I believe Kentuckians should never have to choose between their safety and their constitutional freedoms.
I am proud to be endorsed by the National Rifle Association and the National Association for Gun Rights, organizations that continue to fight to protect the constitutional rights of Americans across the country.
Cutting Taxes I believe hardworking Kentuckians deserve to keep more of the money they earn.
That’s why I have consistently supported lowering Kentucky’s income tax and have voted twice to reduce the state income tax burden on families and small businesses.
I also voted in favor of a procedural measure to bring an amendment to the House floor that would have reduced Kentucky’s income tax rate to zero.
While the measure unfortunately failed, I was proud to support the effort because I believe Kentuckians, not government, know best how to spend their hard earned money.
I am proud to be one of the most conservative members of the Kentucky House, and my solid voting record reflects that commitment.
I believe in less government, lower taxes, fiscal responsibility, and policies that put Kentucky families, workers, and small businesses first.
During my time in office, I have never voted in favor of raising taxes.
I remain committed to responsible budgeting, conservative fiscal policies, and continuing the effort to reduce the income tax even further.
Lower taxes strengthen our economy, support job growth, and give families greater financial freedom.
I will continue fighting for policies that put taxpayers first and keep Kentucky moving in the right direction.
