I promised to you that I would lead with good customer service and focus on the areas of Property Tax Reform, Energy, and bringing dollars back.
We've done just this!
I led the Property Tax Reform efforts this General Assembly, doing more for property owners than the last 50 years with a rework of the local government funding system, savings of $3.8 Billion to taxpayers just in 3 years and more growing, and a security to know the last 6 years of spikes and huge tax increases will not happen again.
Serving as Ways and Means Vice Chair, I had stood in the way of more taxes on you, more spending, and constantly combat government growth.

Advocated for over $4 Million to come back to us in direct state budget allocations to projects in the 65th District, a record.
