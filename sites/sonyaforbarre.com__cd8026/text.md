Her grandparents lived down the road, and her Gram took care of her after school while her parents were working.
Her family heated their home with firewood, and she spent many a fall day splitting and stacking it.
Her dad was hired by John Miles as a delivery driver fresh out of Spaulding High School.
Her mom was the first college graduate in her family, earning a degree in Dental Hygiene from UVM.
Together, her parents worked hard and had the opportunity to buy John R.
Miles Supply, the company her dad worked for his whole career.
Sonya found out as an adult that the time she spent helping in the lunchroom at Orange Center School was because she was on free and reduced lunch.
Her family was lucky to have this safety net, which made sure their kids had food in their bellies and could learn.
Soon after Sonya graduated from Orange Center School, she moved to Barre and graduated from Spaulding High School, like her mom and dad.
Sonya met her husband, Kevin, at the Hedding Methodist Church youth group, then ran into him again after college, and they started dating.
They got married and moved for the first few years for Kevin’s job but ultimately decided to move back to Barre.
Sonya and Kevin have two children, Parker and EmilyGrace.
Both attended Barre City Elementary and Middle School and Spaulding High School.
Sonya, her brother, and another partner bought Miles Supply from her parents in 2010 and still run it today.
She has served 13 years on the school board and was just elected to her second term on the Barre City Council representing Ward 1.
Through hard work and determination, both her parents and now her own family have taken advantage of many opportunities and have been lucky enough to have support and safety nets along the way.
Sonya is running to be your Representative for Barre City because she believes that our government should work for everyone.
She wants to make sure that new families have the opportunity to buy an affordable house, to make sure students get a great education without bankrupting property owners, to make sure we are finding a way to lower the cost of health insurance for everyone, and to provide a safety net for kids, for people struggling with addiction or mental health issues, for people who are hungry, and for our unhoused neighbors.
Sonya knows that these are complex problems that will require hard work to solve.
Sonya is the same person who enjoyed splitting wood as a kid, who worked hard to understand the complex budget and funding needs of public education while serving on the school board, who has jumped into understanding the equally complex issues facing our local government on the city council, and who loves the challenge of a growing family business.
Sonya will not shy away from hard work because she has never known another way.
Sonya wants to represent Barre City because she believes in this community and she believes in Vermont.
Sonya wants to keep working hard to care for people, make sure they can take advantage of every opportunity, and help us all grow and flourish.
