Ginger Cloud I endorse Sonya Spaulding for State Representative (Barre City).
I do so with both real enthusiasm and the sure conviction that her considerable talents will indelibly benefit Barre City and Vermont.
At a time when our state government confronts myriad, complex challenges not soluble by quick fixes, simplistic sloganeering, or shopworn dogma, Sonya will bring to the underlying issues sophisticated understanding, deep learning, and creative ideas to help bring Vermont forward on behalf of all her citizens.
My belief in Sonya is the product of long association through several lenses.
As a teen at Spaulding High School, I had the true satisfaction of teaching her in two courses, Modern World History and A.P.
European History.
Over the decades we kept in close contact as she went on to undergraduate and graduate schools, assumed a leading role in her family business, and to the great pleasure of my wife and I moved into the house across the street from our home.
Spouse, parent, business owner, successful school board and city council elections are all roles we have observed Sonya embrace, develop, and ultimately master.
Sonya brings to the difficult work under the state house dome highly refined intellectual skills relevant to producing results in the gristmill of the legislative process.
She can absorb, synthesize, and apply diverse information from multiple perspectives; and she can critically think through this information to sort weak from strong arguments, valid from invalid fallacies of reasoning, and biased from corroborated evidence.
Sonya balances a keen mind with limitless compassion, rock-solid integrity, and good old common sense.
In sum, she packs the toolkit necessary to do the job.
I urge you to join me in voting for Sonya Spaulding on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Vermont is at one of those critical junctures in her history: she needs the electorate to send to Montpelier legislators with the ability, humanity, and tireless work ethic to yield not talk—but results the plurality of us can support.
