Maramec, OK — Dillon Travis, a 6th-generation Oklahoman, lifelong farmer, and rural business owner, officially announced his campaign today for the Oklahoma House of Representatives in the upcoming special election for House District 35.
The Republican primary is scheduled for December 9, 2025.
“Rural Oklahoma doesn’t need another politician — it needs a fighter who lives the same challenges we do,” said Travis.
“I’m running to protect our values, fix our roads, defend our schools, and make sure our district is never forgotten in Oklahoma City.”
Born and raised in Maramec, Travis operates Southern Plains AG-CO, a regional ag supply company, and manages a large cattle and hay operation serving farmers across the state.
He’s built his life, family, and business on Oklahoma values — faith, family, and hard work.
Travis’ Campaign Priorities
- Fixing Rural Roads: Deliver state resources to repair crumbling infrastructure in Pawnee, Noble, and surrounding counties
- Protecting Rural Schools: Defend local control, stop woke ideology in the classroom, and restore real-world education
- Combating the Drug Epidemic: Crack down on fentanyl and meth in small towns and support law enforcement
- Supporting Agriculture: Lower input costs, protect private property rights, and fight for family farms
- Defending Freedom: Uphold the Constitution and protect the rights of Oklahoma families
District 35 communities depend heavily on agriculture and face real threats — from rising input costs to broken roads and bureaucratic overreach.
For Travis, these aren’t abstract issues — they’re personal.
“I’ve worked this land, I’ve hauled feed down these roads, and I’ve built a business here.
I know what it takes to keep rural Oklahoma strong — and I’ll take that fight to the Capitol.”
Travis is a Christian, a proud NRA member, a pro-life conservative, and a strong supporter of President Trump and the America First agenda.
He lives in Maramec with his wife Kaylee, a lecturer in agricultural communications at Oklahoma State University, and their young daughter, Raylee.
