Watertown’s Advocate.
Longtime small-business owner and city council member Glen Vilhauer is asking the voters of Watertown to re-elect him to serve District 5 in the South Dakota State Senate.
Active in the community for more than 40 years, Glen offers proven, common sense experience and conservative values to his approach to public service.
He will be an advocate for the agriculture sector, working families, small businesses, economic development, and local control.
As a retired successful CPA, Glen understands the importance of a fiscal conservative approach to government and balancing South Dakota’s budget.
In 2026, cast your vote to re-elect Senator Glen Vilhauer!
