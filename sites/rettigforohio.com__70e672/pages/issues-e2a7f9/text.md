Issues
Ohio is at a critical tipping point, and the time for a decisive change in the Statehouse is now.
For too long, out-of-touch politicians have manipulated voting districts through gerrymandering, diverted your hard-earned public tax dollars into private ventures in business and education, and stripped away your fundamental right to autonomy.
My campaign is a direct response to those failures—a commitment to restore transparency, fiscal accountability, and true representation to our state government.
Together, we can confront these systemic abuses, safeguard our personal freedoms, and build an Ohio where the Statehouse actually listens to the people it serves.
Fixing and Fully-Funding Public Education
As a public school teacherOhio's public schools are the foundation of our communities and the engine of our economy.
But decades of underfunding, political meddling, and a growing private voucher program are leaving our kids, and our teachers, behind.
Rick is committed to fixing Ohio's broken school funding system and making sure every child in Wood County gets a world-class education.
Environment & Ohio's Agricultural Future
Wood County is farm country.
Our agricultural heritage is part of who we are, and the men and women who farm our land deserve a state government that has their back: on trade, on technology, and on protecting the soil, water, and air they depend on.
I'm also committed to responsible environmental stewardship and a transition to clean energy that protects Ohio families and natural resources.
Utility Accountability & Ending Corruption
In 2020, Ohio became the center of the largest energy bribery scandal in American history.
The HB 6 scandal - a $1.3 billion ratepayer-funded bailout of two failing nuclear and coal plants - exposed how deep the rot of special interest corruption runs in Columbus.
Our families are still paying the price.
I will fight to restore integrity and accountability to Ohio's energy policy.
Supporting Ohio's Families
Building a strong Ohio starts with strong families.
I believe every family in Wood County deserves the tools they need to thrive: from quality early education to safe housing to a strong social safety net.
A community that invests in its families invests in its future.
