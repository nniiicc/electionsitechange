The program Alan Jimenez for Utah House District 61 sends text messages about the campaign: updates, event invitations, volunteer requests, voting reminders, and donation asks.
Volunteers may also send one-to-one, person-to-person texts to voters in the district.
Reply STOP to any of them and they'll stop.
How often Message frequency varies.
Expect up to about 2 messages a week, and more often in the final weeks before Election Day, up to daily in the last few days.
Cost Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
How to stop Reply STOP to any message to opt out.
STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT also work.
You'll get one text confirming you're unsubscribed, and then no more messages.
To rejoin, reply START .
Help Reply HELP to any message, or email alanforutah@outlook.com .
Consent You only get our recurring program texts if you agree to receive them.
By checking the text box on our signup form and submitting it, you agree to receive recurring campaign texts, including donation asks, at the number you provide, including messages sent by autodialer.
Agreeing to texts is never required to volunteer, attend an event, or donate.
You must be the mobile account holder or have the account holder's permission to sign up.
Privacy We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent with anyone else for their marketing.
Read our full Privacy Policy .
