Who we are
This website is run by Alan Jimenez for Utah House District 61.
Questions about this policy can go to alanforutah@outlook.com.
Forms
Our volunteer, event, and updates forms are provided by Microsoft Forms.
Information you submit is received by the campaign and used to respond to your request, coordinate campaign activity, or send updates you asked for.
Microsoft processes form data under its privacy terms.
Please don't submit sensitive personal information.
Text messaging
If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we use it only to send the campaign messages described in our Text Message Terms.
We do not sell or rent mobile numbers.
Text-message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
This does not apply to sharing (1) with vendors, consultants, and other service providers who need access to carry out work on our behalf, such as our text messaging vendor, and who will not use it for their own purposes, or (2) when we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation, or to comply with law enforcement or legal process.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
Donations
Donations are processed by ActBlue.
Donor information may be shared with the campaign and disclosed in campaign-finance reports as required by law.
Review ActBlue's Privacy Policy.
Website analytics
This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and page performance.
Cloudflare states that this service does not collect or use visitors' personal data.
We don't use advertising cookies.
Sharing
We don't sell your personal information.
We share it only with the service providers named above so they can do their jobs, or when the law requires it.
Your choices
Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
To have us update or delete information you've given the campaign, email alanforutah@outlook.com.
Information required in campaign-finance reports can't be removed from public filings.
