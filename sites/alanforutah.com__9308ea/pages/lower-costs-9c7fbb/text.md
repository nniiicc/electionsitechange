How the two sports projects are financed
The two Salt Lake-area projects have different financing arrangements.
For the Fairpark stadium, the 2024 legislation limited the district’s contribution to half the stadium’s development and construction cost, with a maximum of $900 million.
Bonds issued by the district are not general obligations of the state.
For the downtown arena district, Salt Lake City approved a 0.5% citywide sales-tax increase in October 2024.
The city identified a maximum of $900 million for Delta Center renovations and improvements to the surrounding entertainment district.
These figures describe separate funding limits.
They do not mean the Legislature wrote a $1.8 billion check or that the full amount has been borrowed or spent.
