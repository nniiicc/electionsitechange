01 Household affordability Lower Costs & Responsible Growth Welcome new homes, jobs, and investment without making families absorb the hidden costs.
The local problem Housing, child care, property taxes, tuition, utilities, and transportation all come out of the same family budget.
Big developments and public subsidies can add costs without making it clear who will pay.
What I will fight for Make sure commercial property is valued accurately, so homeowners don’t get stuck with the bill.
Support homes people can afford where roads, water, utilities, and schools can support them.
Make large power and water users show how they will affect capacity, rates, and infrastructure.
Before handing out big taxpayer deals, show what they cost, put the promises in writing, and get an outside review.
Oppose state decisions that push costs down to cities, counties, schools, and families.
My standard Good growth creates opportunity and plans for the services it needs.
It should not leave families with the bill.
Explore the affordability and growth plan → 02 Opportunity close to home Strong Schools & Local Opportunity A strong start, strong classrooms, and an affordable UVU keep education, training, and good careers within reach.
The local problem Families need child care they can count on, strong neighborhood schools, and affordable ways to earn a degree, learn a trade, or start a career close to home.
Utah’s child care assistance runs entirely on federal money, and when that money fell, 900 working families started losing help.
Changes at UVU should be judged by what students can actually access and finish.
What I will fight for Fill the child care gap so parents can keep the jobs that support their families.
Fair starting pay and practical support that helps good teachers stay.
Classroom resources that improve reading, learning, and student progress.
Protect affordable UVU programs that lead to in-demand careers.
Clear reporting on class access, graduation, staffing, and tuition.
Steady support for school suicide prevention and Hope Squads.
My standard Education funding should reach classrooms, help good teachers stay, and give students more choices.
