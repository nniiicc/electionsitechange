01 Household affordability Lower Costs & More Homes Housing, groceries, health care, and taxes keep going up.
Families shouldn’t have to leave Utah to afford a life.
The local problem Housing, groceries, insurance, child care, property taxes, tuition, and transportation all come out of the same family budget.
Big developments and public subsidies can add costs without making it clear who will pay.
What I will fight for More homes, not more red tape: make starter homes easier to build.
Cut the grocery sales tax, then work toward ending it.
Require state approval for big insurance rate hikes and rein in the middlemen driving up drug prices.
Make sure commercial property is valued accurately, so homeowners don’t get stuck with the bill.
Before handing out big taxpayer deals, show what they cost, put the promises in writing, and get an outside review.
Oppose state decisions that push costs down to cities, counties, schools, and families.
Why this is personal All three of my siblings left Utah because they couldn’t afford housing.
I don’t want more families to go.
Explore the affordability plan → 02 Water, power & growth Water & Responsible Growth New jobs and investment are welcome.
They just shouldn’t cost us our lake or raise your utility bill.
The local problem Data centers and other big projects can use a huge amount of power and water.
When nobody has to say how much, families can end up paying for it on their utility bills.
And sometimes the community doesn’t get a say at all.
What I will fight for Require data centers to disclose their water and energy use and pay a premium on their utilities.
Help farmers switch to more efficient irrigation to protect the Great Salt Lake.
Make large power and water users show how they will affect capacity, rates, and infrastructure.
Protect residents’ right to a referendum on major decisions like the Stratos data center.
Plan housing and business growth alongside roads, transit, utilities, and schools.
My standard Good growth pays its own way and plans for the services it needs.
Our lake should be here for generations to enjoy.
Explore the water and growth plan → 03 Opportunity close to home Strong Schools & Local Opportunity A strong start, strong classrooms, and an affordable UVU keep education, training, and good careers within reach.
The local problem Families need child care they can count on, strong neighborhood schools, and affordable ways to earn a degree, learn a trade, or start a career close to home.
Utah’s child care assistance runs entirely on federal money, and when that money fell, 900 working families started losing help.
Changes at UVU should be judged by what students can actually access and finish.
What I will fight for Fill the child care gap so parents can keep the jobs that support their families.
Fair starting pay and practical support that helps good teachers stay.
Classroom resources that improve reading, learning, and student progress.
Protect affordable UVU programs that lead to in-demand careers.
Clear reporting on class access, graduation, staffing, and tuition.
Steady support for school suicide prevention and Hope Squads.
My standard Education funding should reach classrooms, help good teachers stay, and give students more choices.
