THE PLATFORM
Lower Costs
Rent is too high.
Insurance is too high.
Groceries, utilities, childcare, and healthcare keep going up while wages have not kept pace.
I’ll fight to lower everyday costs, raise wages, expand affordable housing, hold insurance companies accountable, and stop corporations from making life harder for working families.
Universal Healthcare
Healthcare is a human right.
Nobody should have to skip medicine, stay in a bad job, beg an insurance company, or go broke just to get care.
I’ll fight for universal healthcare and use every tool we have now to expand Medicaid, open the state health plan, expand KidCare, lower premiums, fund mobile clinics, and address the Black maternal health crisis.
Housing and Insurance Relief
Rent is too high.
Buying a home feels out of reach.
Big investors are buying up houses, and property insurance is making it harder for people to stay in the homes they already have.
I’ll fight to build more affordable housing, protect renters and homeowners, limit corporate ownership of homes, protect housing trust funds, and hold insurance companies accountable.
Protect Water and Farmland
Our land and water are not for sale.
We need clean water, protected springs, preserved farmland, safer streets, better transit, and growth that does not leave local people behind.
I’ll fight to protect Marion County’s Farmland Preservation Area, defend our aquifer and springs, stop reckless sprawl, support high-speed rail and better buses, and put a moratorium on AI data centers until Florida has real protections for water, workers, privacy, and local communities.
