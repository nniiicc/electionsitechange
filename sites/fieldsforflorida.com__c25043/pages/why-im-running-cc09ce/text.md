For a long time now, working families have been asked to carry more and more — higher rent, higher insurance, higher healthcare costs — while the people at the top keep taking more for themselves.
If you work hard, play by the rules, and still feel like you’re falling behind, you’re not imagining it.
That’s the system doing exactly what it was designed to do.
And the people in power know it.
Instead of fixing what’s broken, they’ve chosen a different strategy: divide us, distract us, and keep us fighting each other while corporate donors and political insiders keep calling the shots.
I believe most people in this district want the same basic things: a good-paying job, quality healthcare, strong public schools, clean water, and a home they can actually afford.
But instead of delivering that, too many politicians keep siding with the corporations and special interests driving up costs while working families are left to pick up the tab.
Rent is too high.
Property insurance is too high.
Healthcare is too expensive.
Wages have not kept up.
And too many people are wondering if they can afford a future in the place they call home.
That’s why I’m refusing corporate PAC and lobbyist money.
Because I don’t believe you can fix a broken system if you’re funded by the same people who broke it.
No matter your party, people know something is broken.
Tallahassee works too well for insurance companies, utility monopolies, corporate landlords, developers, lobbyists, and political insiders, and too poorly for everyone else.
I’m not running to protect that system.
I’m running to change it.
If we’re going to move Florida forward, it won’t be by playing it safe or promising the bare minimum.
It will be by giving people something real to believe in.
That means affordable housing.
Property insurance relief.
Universal healthcare.
A living wage.
Paid family leave.
Strong public schools.
Clean water and protected farmland.
Better transit, safer roads, and infrastructure that keeps up with growth.
And a government that works for people, not corporations.
I believe all of this is possible.
But only if we are willing to fight for it and build real grassroots power.
I’m running because working people deserve more than excuses.
I’m running because this seat should belong to the people of this district, not corporations, lobbyists, or political insiders.
I’m running because I’m ready to fight.
