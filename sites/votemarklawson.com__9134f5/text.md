Before Mark Lawson ever served in elected office, his work was already centered on Oklahoma children and families.
He worked with Youth Services of Tulsa’s Safe Place program, Children’s Hospital at Saint Francis and Court Appointed Special Advocates (CASA), serving children and families during some of their most difficult moments.
He also served as chairman of the board for Youth Services of Creek County.
That experience shaped the approach Mark brought with him to the State Capitol.
First elected to represent House District 30 in 2016, Mark has spent the last decade serving the communities he calls home and working to make a difference for Oklahoma families.
Today, Mark serves as House Majority Leader, helping lead the Oklahoma House while continuing to represent the people of House District 30.
From his work with children and families before taking office to his leadership at the Capitol today, Mark has built his career around service, responsibility and delivering results.
- Serving House District 30 since 2016 and now as House Majority Leader.
- Delivering results to lower costs, strengthen schools and demand accountability.
- A career dedicated to serving Oklahoma children and families.
- A Sapulpa resident committed to the communities he represents.
Mark Lawson is focused on practical results that make a difference for families across House District 30.
Mark helped eliminate Oklahoma’s state grocery tax and cut the state income tax, helping Oklahoma families keep more of what they earn.
Mark helped make sure massive data centers pay their own way—not pass their costs onto Oklahoma families.
Mark supported investments in teachers, reading and math—and helped get cell phones out of classrooms.
Mark led the House investigation into how millions in mental-health funding were spent, working to bring greater accountability to state government.
