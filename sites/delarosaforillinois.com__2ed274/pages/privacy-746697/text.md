This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you interact with our campaign website and sign up for updates.
Information We Collect When you sign up for campaign updates, we collect: First and last name Email address Phone number (optional) How We Use Your Information Your information is used exclusively for campaign-related purposes: Sending campaign updates and news Informing you about volunteer opportunities Sharing information about campaign events Mobilizing supporters for election-related activities We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.
Your data is used solely to support Committee to Elect Margaret DeLaRosa's campaign for Illinois House District 42.
SMS/Text Messaging By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages (e.g. campaign info and event reminders) from Committee to Elect Margaret DeLaRosa at the number provided, including autodialed messages.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
Donations will be solicited.
Opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us, or reply HELP for help.
Email Communications When you sign up for our mailing list, your information is managed through Mailchimp.
You can unsubscribe from our email list at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email you receive from us, or by visiting our unsubscribe page .
Website Analytics This website uses Plausible Analytics , a privacy-friendly web analytics service.
Plausible is designed with privacy in mind and: Does not use cookies Does not collect personal data Does not track users across websites Provides only anonymized, aggregated statistics Is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR Plausible helps us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it, but it does so in a way that respects your privacy.
No personal information is collected or stored through our analytics.
Data Security We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
Data Retention We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, or as required by law.
Campaign-related data may be retained through the duration of the election cycle.
Your Rights You have the right to: Access the personal information we have about you Request correction of inaccurate information Request deletion of your information Opt out of email or text communications at any time Third-Party Services We use the following third-party services to operate our campaign: Mailchimp: For email list management and communications Plausible Analytics: For privacy-friendly website analytics ActBlue: For processing campaign contributions (if applicable) Each of these services has their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.
Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
Contact Us If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact the campaign through our official channels.
This privacy policy applies to the Margaret DeLaRosa for Illinois House District 42 campaign.
By signing up for updates or using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy policy.
