Meet Margaret
Margaret DeLaRosa is a proven public servant with deep roots in the district.
She received the most votes in each of her four elections to the Glenbard District 87 School Board and served as President from 2022–2025.
Margaret was appointed in 2025 to serve as State Representative for Illinois’s 42nd District, bringing her experience in education, fiscal oversight, and community leadership to Springfield.
