Sign up for updates Get Involved Become a volunteer Contribute Today Support our candidate Meet Jeff Find events near you Register to Vote Submit your application About Jeff McDonald Jeffry McDonald was born and raised in Baltimore County, living in Timonium, Glen Arm, and Cockeysville throughout his life.
He was educated in Baltimore County Schools and later graduated from Essex Community College and Towson University.
Jeffry is a father of three and raised his family in Baltimore County.
Today, the McDonald family continues to grow, and he is the grandfather of three.
His professional life has also been rooted in and around Baltimore County, with diverse experience across a broad array of businesses including the driving range business, precision machining, film, video and commercial production, commercial insurance, supply chain and logistics, and talk radio broadcasting.
One of Jeffry's passions has always been community involvement.
His faith has been put into action through service to his church as a Sunday School teacher and volunteer.
His concern for children and desire to be involved in their lives also led him to serve as a youth sports coach in the Lutherville-Timonium Recreational Council and to volunteer for various causes.
Never one to sit on the sidelines, Jeff has long been active in public policy debates and the political process.
He has served as President of the Greater Towson Republican Club, the Reagan Republican Club, and currently serves as President of the Patriot Club of America.
Throughout his political involvement, Jeffry has been a champion for taxpayers, parental rights, constitutional rights, and public safety.
He is passionate about God, Country, and Family, and believes every citizen has the right to Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness.
