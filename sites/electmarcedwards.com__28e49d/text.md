It’s Time to Refresh Your Voice in Augusta
For the past 15 years, our representatives in Augusta have done little to support our rural communities, schools, small businesses, and hard working families.
I look around and see hard working families in my community and communities throughout our district struggling to afford healthcare, housing, and basic needs.
No one should have to put a donation bucket at the local General Store because they got sick.
I also see hope and opportunity for a prosperous future in our district.
This is why I am running.
About Marc
Marc and his wife, Michaela, have lived on Maine Street in Strong for nearly 20 years.
Marc is currently semi-retired, working part-time as an assistant carpenter.
He is also working to become a Registered Maine Guide and starting his recreational guiding business in western Maine.
Marc spent much of his career working in the parks and recreation and natural resources fields.
He has been a park ranger at Acadia National Park, served as the naturalist at Baxter State Park, and as a Park Manager for Vermont State Parks.
While at the University of Maine Cooperative Extension, working in the area of Tourism Economic Development in Franklin County, Marc received IF&W’s Commissioner’s Print award for his research on brook trout anglers in Maine.
