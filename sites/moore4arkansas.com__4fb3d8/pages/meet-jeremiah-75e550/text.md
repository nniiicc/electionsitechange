MEET JEREMIAH
Jeremiah Moore is a sixth-generation Arkansan and resident of Clarendon.
He’s a conservative and a life-long Republican, dedicated to the ideals of individual freedom and personal responsibility.
Having built a business across the Arkansas Delta, he works with farmers, land-owners and investors, helping them buy, sell and develop agricultural and recreational real estate as a broker with Wellons Real Estate.
Jeremiah believes in a strong community, and is heavily involved in giving back to the region he calls home.
He’s a member of the Stuttgart Rotary Club, an active leader in St.
Alban’s Episcopal Church of Stuttgart, and currently serves as a commissioner for the Arkansas Natural Heritage Commission.
He’s running for state representative because he deeply cares about his home, his neighbors, and the values of good government - a government that works for the people, not the other way around.
