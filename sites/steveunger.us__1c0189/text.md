Meet
Steve
Steve Unger is a 4th generation Arkansan whose family has called Northwest Arkansas home since the 1870s.
Steve spent 31 years of active-duty service to the United States
Navy, serving in various capacities including medic, surface warfare officer, and Chaplain.
Steve retired in 2015 at the rank of Captain.
Steve and Lynette have been married for 31 years.
