ROCK ISLAND, IL — Today, JB For Governor released a new ad showcasing Governor JB Pritzker’s support from Illinoisans in the Quad Cities and Rockford areas and emphasizing his record of delivering for the regions.
The 30-second ad features supporters thanking Gov.
Pritzker for all the work he’s done to stand up for Illinois working families and fight for progress across the state.
Gov.
Pritzker has delivered for Illinoisans in the Quad Cities and Rockford areas by:
- Securing a $22 million investment by Parr Instrument in Moline.
- Investing in the construction of the I-74 bridge.
- Dedicating funding to the revitalization of 15th Avenue in East Moline.
- Expanding clean energy transit by adding electric buses and charging stations to the Rockford Mass Transit District.
- Making Rockford International Airport an economic powerhouse through major infrastructure investments.
- Revitalizing the BMO Harris Bank Center, the Rockford’s largest entertainment venue, to bring in business and grow the local economy.
The ad will air on broadcast and digital platforms in the Quad Cities and Rockford media markets.
