In 2019, Gov.
Pritzker launched the Rebuild Illinois capital program, the state’s largest infrastructure plan to invest a record $45 billion in roads, bridges, transit, rail, airports, ports, broadband, and other critical infrastructure across all of Illinois’ 102 counties.
Since the program started, Gov.
Pritzker has helped improve over 21,000 lane miles of highway, 815 bridges, and more than 1,100 safety projects.
He also secured $1 billion in federal funding for high-speed internet in Illinois.
Through Rebuild Illinois and the Climate and Equitable Jobs Act , Gov.
Pritzker has created and supported hundreds of thousands of new jobs, achieved Illinois’ highest-ever payroll numbers, and brought new future-forward industries to the state.
Thanks to his investments, CNBC’s 2026 Top States for Business rankings named Illinois a top-three state for infrastructure.

Pritzker’s Rebuild Illinois capital program, proving yet again that he would reverse all the progress the state has made.
