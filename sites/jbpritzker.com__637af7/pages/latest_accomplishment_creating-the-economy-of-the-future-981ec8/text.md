Creating The Economy of The Future
From creating clean energy jobs to attracting quantum technology companies, JB is helping grow the economy while being a nationwide leader in the fight against climate change.
Quantum
Charted a course to make Illinois the national leader in quantum technology, the next-wave industry of the future that has already attracted $1 billion in private investment to the state
Clean Energy
Enacted a nation-leading climate action that puts Illinois on the path to 100% clean energy by 2050 and creates thousands of new jobs
Electric Vehicles
Attracted electric vehicle manufacturers and suppliers to Illinois and helped existing companies expand, bringing new jobs and investments from across the world
Advanced Manufacturing
Established Manufacturing Training Academies to train downstate workers for well paying jobs of the future
Tech Companies
Attracted industry leading tech companies to invest in the state of the art Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park
Startups
Funded Tech Incubator Grants, to support entrepreneurship and innovation
Secured a $1.5 billion investment from Rivian in Central Illinois, which is expected to create over 550 more electric vehicle jobs
Launched Connect Illinois to bring broadband internet access to the entire state
