Frequently Asked Questions When does early voting start in Illinois?
Early voting in Illinois officially began on September 24, allowing residents to cast their ballots ahead of Election Day.
Expanded early voting locations and sites open across counties in the weeks leading up to the election.
How can I register to vote or check my registration status in Illinois?
You can check your current registration status, update your address, or look into registration options online through the Illinois State Board of Elections or by visiting IWillVote.com .
Eligible citizens can also utilize grace-period registration and same-day registration options at designated early voting sites and polling places.
What are the key voting options and deadlines in Illinois?
Illinois voters can choose from multiple convenient methods to cast their ballots: Early In-Person Voting available starting September 24 at designated regional sites; Voting by Mail (any registered voter can apply); and Election Day Voting where polling places are open from 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
Where can I find my local polling place or early voting location?
Voters can locate their assigned Election Day polling place, view sample ballots, and find nearby early voting sites by checking their local county clerk’s office or election authority portal, or by using tools provided on IWillVote.com.
