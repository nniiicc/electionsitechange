They were essentially on the run in hopes of stopping Donald Trump from thwarting democracy.
Texas Governor Greg Abbott and his MAGA Republican zealots in the Texas legislature were told by Donald Trump to redraw their congressional maps to remove Democratic members of Congress and give those seats to Republicans.
They had the votes to do Trump’s bidding, but they needed Texas House Democrats to show up for the vote, even if the Democrats wouldn’t vote for it.
Democrats wouldn’t do it, so the Republicans threatened to arrest them.
As the country watched, Democrats fled the state to stop the vote and showed the nation what it means to stand up and fight against Trump’s authoritarianism.
I invited them to Illinois because I wanted to support them in their fight, and when they came, I was proud to host them.
While Texas House Democrats were here in Illinois, I got to know State Representatives Gina Hinojosa and James Talarico.
This was before either of them decided to run for Texas governor and U.S. senator.
Anyone could immediately see that these two are fighters.
Eventually, the Republicans forced their maps through the legislature, but these Texas Democratic heroes didn’t stop fighting.
Now, both of them are on the verge of making history.
To win, they need a groundswell of support from across the country to get their message out to every single Texan before Election Day.
That is why I am doubling down on my commitment to help Texas Democrats, especially Gina Hinojosa.
When Gina declared her campaign for governor last year, it looked like an uphill battle.
She knew she was facing a three-term incumbent with deep pockets who had won his previous race by ten points.
She also understood that Texas had not elected a Democratic governor since Ann Richards in 1990.
It would have been easy enough for Gina to coast through the year, but that’s not the kind of person she is.
She has been outspent by her opponent, but she has also out-worked him.
She treated every day of the campaign like the race was even.
Now, with just three weeks to go until Election Day, the most recent Marist University , Siena University , and YouGov polls show Gina Hinojosa in the lead.
She has built a new Texas coalition that goes from the cities to the suburbs to every corner of the state.
She is inspiring rank-and-file Democrats.
She is breaking through to Latinos who feel betrayed by Trump as grocery and gas prices soar and as federal agents torment their communities.
Gina has won over some Republicans who are sick of watching Greg Abbott hand over their state to Trump and a bunch of wealthy tech bros like Elon Musk.
She just rolled out an ad featuring a Texas high school football coach and three-time Abbott voter who decided to support her campaign.
Gina built this coalition on sweat alone.
Now victory is within reach, but we can’t let the Republicans overwhelm her with their big TV spending.
I am supporting Gina’s campaign because she’s a real fighter, and this is too great an opportunity to let slip away.
The working families of Texas deserve a leader who will stand up for them by lowering costs and improving public education.
Greg Abbott has sold out to Donald Trump and a bunch of tech CEOs.
Too often, Democrats want to play defense.
It’s time to go on offense.
Ten years ago, nobody could have imagined a Georgia with two Democratic senators but now they have them.
Ten months ago, nobody imagined Democrats would be in such a strong position in Texas.
Yet here we are.
So I’ve decided to invest in turning Texas blue.
I saw Gina’s and James’s grit during the quorum break.
I saw the movements they’ve built when I was in Texas last month and decided to support them both.
Something is happening in the Lone Star State.
We can’t let this moment slip through our fingers.
It’s time to rally around Texas.
In addition to supporting Gina and James, I’ve been proud to support the Texas Democratic Party in electing Democrats up and down the ballot.
In addition, Ready for the Fight PAC , an organization of which I am proud to be Honorary Chair, is backing Katy Padilla Stout in her congressional race against MAGA lunatic Brendan Herrera.
These races are winnable, but these Texas candidates need all the support we can muster.
Texas Democrats are putting it all out on the field in this election.
They’ve made this a real fight.
I’ve got their back.
I hope everyone will join me.
