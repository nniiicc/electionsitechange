Pritzker, Mitchell at Veterans for JB Event CHICAGO, IL — Today, Governor JB Pritzker and lieutenant gubernatorial candidate Christian Mitchell received endorsements from VoteVets, Common Defense, Illinois Veterans for Change, and National Security Leaders for America (NSL4A).
These endorsements reflect Gov.
Pritzker and Christian Mitchell’s commitment to uplifting Illinois veterans and providing support to active-duty service members across the state.
VoteVets, Common Defense, Illinois Veterans for Change, and NSL4A are four major organizations representing veterans, national security leaders, military families, and their supporters.
They work every day to advocate for policies that empower members of our military and veterans and elect leaders that uphold the very liberties they fought to protect.
“Here in Illinois, we look out for our veterans.
We believe in standing up for the people who have sacrificed so much for us,” said Governor JB Pritzker .
“As the proud grandson, son, and brother-in-law of naval officers, I grew up with deep admiration for the service members who protect our nation.
While Donald Trump rips services from veterans and puts service members in harm’s way, I am working every day to make sure that the Illinoisans who risked everything for our nation get treated with dignity when they return home.
I am honored to be endorsed by VoteVets, Common Defense, Illinois Veterans for Change, National Security Leaders for America, and the dedicated veterans these organizations represent.” “Today’s endorsement from VoteVets, Common Defense, Illinois Veterans for Change, and National Security Leaders for America is truly an honor that I don’t take for granted,” said lieutenant gubernatorial candidate Christian Mitchell .
“I’m the grandson of an Army veteran and a proud member of the Illinois National Guard.
I believe in always doing right by our nation’s heroes and honoring their sacrifices every day.
That means funding healthcare, housing support, and social services so that our veterans are taken care of, not left behind.
I look forward to working alongside Governor Pritzker to continue his work investing in and supporting our veteran communities.” “At a time when Donald Trump is leading the most anti-Veteran Administration in history, it’s as important as ever that we elect leaders who put our heroes first,” said U.S.
Senator Tammy Duckworth .
“Coming from a military family, JB’s respect for those brave enough to serve runs deep and shines through his work as Governor.
I couldn’t be prouder to support JB Pritzker as he seeks re-election so we can continue fighting for our Veterans.” “Donald Trump has failed veterans in almost every way imaginable,” said Major General Paul Eaton (Ret.), VoteVets Senior Advisor .
“He started a deadly war with no reason and no plan to end it, and at least 22 service members have paid with their lives.
He’s gutted funding for Veterans Affairs offices across the country, making it harder for veterans to get lifesaving care, safe housing, and good-paying jobs, and then he calls us ‘losers’ on top of it.
Governor JB Pritzker, and his running mate Christian Mitchell, have been true champions for veterans, and VoteVets is proud to endorse them both.” “Common Defense is proud to endorse Governor JB Pritzker for reelection as Governor of Illinois,” said Naveed Shah, U.S.
Army veteran and Political Director at Common Defense .
“Governor Pritzker understands that honoring military service means making sure veterans and their families have the support and opportunities they’ve earned long after their service ends.
Under his leadership, Illinois has invested in modernized veterans’ housing, expanded educational opportunities, and made it easier for service members to put their military experience into civilian careers.
These investments reflect a simple principle: when someone raises their hand to serve our country, we have a responsibility to stand with them when they come home.
Governor Pritzker has shown he takes that responsibility extremely seriously, which is why Common Defense is proud to stand behind him.” “Illinois Veterans for Change endorsed Governor JB Pritzker and Christian Mitchell, a proud member of the Illinois Air National Guard, because they have shown up for Illinois veterans and their families each and every day,” said Jesse Rojo, Chair of Illinois Veterans for Change .
“They are champions for policies that have helped make Illinois one of the best states in the nation for veterans.
We are so proud to endorse Gov.
Pritzker and Christian Mitchell in this election as they continue to safeguard democracy and ensure our nation’s heroes always have a seat at the table.” “As retired senior military leaders, diplomats, and national security professionals, we understand that leadership is ultimately a matter of character, judgment, service, and responsibility,” said NSL4A member, former Congressman, and Major General William L.
Enyart .
“We’re endorsing Governor Pritzker because he brings those principles to life every day.
At a time of extraordinary challenges at home and abroad, we need leaders who understand both the privilege and the responsibility that come with public office.
JB Pritzker is prepared to meet those responsibilities.” Gov.
Pritzker has championed policies that empower service members, veterans, and their families who have dedicated their lives to protecting our state and nation.
