Governor JB Pritzker has done everything in his power to soften the blow and support farmers.
Bailey said he believes in Trump’s tariffs and repeatedly described them as “short-term pain for long-term gain,” even as Illinois farmers struggle to make ends meet.
He supports Trump’s war of choice in Iran, which drove up the cost of essentials like fuel and fertilizer .
In contrast, Gov.
Pritzker is fighting to protect Illinois farmers from Trump’s failing economic agenda.
Just this month, he issued an executive order to provide farmers needed relief from rising diesel prices.
He called on Trump to suspend federal import taxes on fertilizers farmers rely upon.
While Trump’s trade war caused export contracts to disintegrate overnight, Gov.
Pritzker secured more than $4 billion in commitments from overseas purchasers of Illinois corn and soybeans and promoted domestic markets for agriculture commodities.
Over the last eight years, Gov.
Pritzker has made major investments in agriculture education, combating food deserts , and providing sales tax exemptions for farm equipment.
He also affirmed his support for the Family Farm Preservation Act to sustain Illinois family-run farms and agriculture for generations to come.
“Donald Trump’s tariffs amounted to a war on farmers — but Darren Bailey called it ‘short-term pain for long-term gain,’” said JB for Governor campaign spokesperson Leah Hurwitz .
“Governor JB Pritzker has worked to bring back the export business Donald Trump ripped away from them.
