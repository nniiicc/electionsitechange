Lowering Costs for Working Families
JB has made it his mission to make life more affordable for Illinoisans.
From cutting taxes and raising wages to reducing grocery bills and making prescription drugs cheaper, he is getting big things done for people across the state.
Groceries
Eliminated the state sales tax on groceries.
Child Tax Credit
Created and recently doubled a state Child Tax Credit, saving working families up to $600 per year.
Child Care
Lowered the cost of child care for up to 80% of Illinois families.
Prescriptions
Reduced the cost of prescription drugs, saving families thousands of dollars a year
Property Taxes
Saved taxpayers tens of millions by reducing the cost of local police and fire pension plans
Small Business
Cut taxes for 400,000 small businesses
Raised Wages
Veterans
Protected military veterans by cutting property taxes and building a new Veterans’ retirement home.
