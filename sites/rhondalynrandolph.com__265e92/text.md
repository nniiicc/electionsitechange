Rev.
Rhondalyn Randolph is a community leader, pastor, and advocate with deep roots in Western Kentucky.
A native of Madisonville and Owensboro resident for decades, she is running for KY State Representative, District 13 because she wants more for you.
Working for you and this community is Rhondalyn's purpose.
In 2025, Rev.
Rhondalyn was selected by the City of Owensboro to lead the Human Relations Commission as the Executive Director and continue their mission to advance the community and support citizens.
For decades, Rhondalyn has served this beautiful and diverse city always working for families and everyday people.
Rhondalyn is running to bring community-grounded leadership to Frankfort — leadership rooted in service, fairness, and real results for everyday people.
Her focus is simple and steady: expanding opportunity, strengthening education, supporting working families and people, improving safety, and building long-term economic stability across Owensboro and District 13.
She believes our community deserves more than standing still or slipping backward.
In a region built on faith, hard work, integrity, and the richness of the land, families should be moving forward — not settling for less.
Rhondalyn is stepping forward because she knows progress is possible when leadership listens and serves.
This campaign is not about politics first — it’s about people first, Owensboro first.
Rhondalyn has spent her life showing up for neighbors, families, and those who need a voice.
She will bring that same approach to public office: listening carefully, being accessible, and working every day to help solve real problems.
She knows many Kentuckians feel unheard and disconnected from decisions made in the State Legislature.
Change is needed — but it should be thoughtful, practical, and grounded in community values and standards.
With the right leadership, a brighter future for our youth, families, working people, and everyone is within reach.
Rhondalyn is running to help build that future — together.
