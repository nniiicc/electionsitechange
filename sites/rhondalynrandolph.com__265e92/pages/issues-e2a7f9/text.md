Rhondalyn is running to deliver practical, people-first leadership focused on the everyday challenges families face — rising costs, job opportunity, education access, healthcare affordability, housing stability, and safe communities.

Issues & Priorities
💼 Good Jobs & Economic Opportunity
Every hardworking Kentuckian deserves access to good jobs and fair wages, and Rhondalyn will support workforce training, skilled trades, small business growth, and economic invest for Owensboro and Western Kentucky.
It takes vision, work, and perseverance.
💰 Cost of Living & Inflation
With everyday expenses rising, Rhondalyn will push for responsible policies that protect household budgets and reduce financial pressure on working families and people try to have a quality life.
🎓 Affordable Education & Career Training
College, technical training, and career pathways should be within reach, and Rhondalyn supports affordable education options that prepare students for real jobs.
The KY State Legislature controls the costs, accepting federal funds, and investment.
🏠 Safe & Affordable Housing
Families should be able to live in safe neighborhoods and stable housing in their own communities, and Rhondalyn supports practical solutions to bring back the American Dream.
❤️ Affordable Healthcare
No one should delay care because of cost or access to providers and Rhondalyn supports efforts that expand healthcare options in our community and across Kentucky.
🛡️ Safe Communities
Strong communities depend on safety and prevention, and Rhondalyn supports balanced public safety approaches that protect businesses, neighborhoods, and children.
She will work harder for you, for families, and for everyone.
🤝 People Over Politics
Rhondalyn believes the Kentucky Legislature must spend less time on division and party conflict and more time delivering results for taxpayers and citizens.
Years of public service and community develop make her a collaborator who wants more for Owensboro.
