Register to Vote — It’s Your Right & Responsibility
Voting is one of the most powerful ways we participate in our democracy.
Every voice matters — especially in local and state elections that affect everyday life: schools, jobs, healthcare, safety, and our communities’ future.
If you are a U.S. citizen living in Kentucky, at least 18 by the next general election, and meet residency requirements, you can register to vote today.
Learn more or register online here: Register or update your voter registration in Kentucky: https://vrsws.sos.ky.gov/ovrweb
Kentucky also offers options to register by mail or in person through your County Clerk’s office.
Once your registration is approved, your County Clerk will send a confirmation card with your polling location.

How Mail-in (Absentee) Voting Works in Kentucky
In Kentucky, voters may vote by mail if they meet certain eligibility requirements, such as: being ill, disabled, or unable to appear at the polls, being temporarily outside the county or state, military or overseas assignments, or other state-defined criteria.
You can request a mail-in absentee ballot through the Kentucky Absentee Portal once it opens (typically 45 days before an election).
Requests must be made no later than 14 days before Election Day — for the May primary that’s May 5, 2026.
Once you receive your ballot:
✔ Read the instructions carefully.
✔ Sign and date your ballot envelope.
✔ Return it so it is received by Election Day.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Who can register to vote in Kentucky?
You must:
✔ Be a U.S. citizen
✔ Be a Kentucky resident for at least 28 days before an election
✔ Be 18 years old on or before the next general election
✔ Not have voting rights removed by a felony conviction or court order
Can I check if I’m already registered?
Yes — once you’re registered, your County Clerk will send a confirmation card.
You can also check your status at the online portal above.
What types of IDs do I need at the polls?
Kentucky requires voters to present an acceptable form of ID at the polls (e.g., driver’s license, state ID).
Check the state board’s latest rules for current ID requirements.
Can I vote early?
Yes.
Kentucky offers in-person early voting (absentee in person) prior to Election Day at designated locations and dates.
What if I move or change my name?
Update your registration online or with your County Clerk as soon as possible so your ballot reflects your current address and information.
