Endorsements
On behalf of Rhondalyn Randolph and our entire campaign team, we extend our sincere gratitude to the Kentucky State AFL-CIO’s Committee on Political Education (COPE) for your endorsement of Rhondalyn’s candidacy for Kentucky State Representative, District 13.
We are honored to receive the support of an organization representing more than 60 union affiliates and over 131,000 union members across the Commonwealth.
This endorsement is deeply meaningful to our campaign and reflects a shared commitment to ensuring that the voices of Kentucky’s working families are heard, respected, and represented.
