Currently a member of the House Judiciary Committee.
AN ACCOMPLISHMENT

I was Prime Sponsor and the LSR became HB 1205, relative to women's school sports.
This bill became law in New Hampshire on August 18, 2024.
This law does not allow males in female sports, grades 5 through 12.

When she decided to take

For these reasons, and many more, I endorse Representative Louise Andrus.
Now, more than ever, we need to keep a conservative in charge of the purse strings in this beautiful granite state.
