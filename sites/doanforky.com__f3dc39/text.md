A lifelong resident of Erlanger, Kentucky, he resides there with his devoted wife and two young children. ​ His journey began at Lloyd High School in 2005, paving the way for his academic pursuits at Northern Kentucky University.
There, he excelled, earning degrees in economics and business administration.
His passion for law led him back to his alma mater, where he earned his Juris Doctor from Northern Kentucky University's esteemed Salmon P.
Chase College of Law in 2012. ​ Steven's commitment to public service is undeniable.
He has gallantly served Kentucky across all three branches of government, showcasing his dedication to the state.
From a pivotal role in the executive branch under Governor Matt Bevin to a proficient private attorney litigating in the judicial branch, his expertise spans the legal spectrum.
Additionally, his tenure as an Erlanger City Council Member and legislator in the legislative branch underscores his multifaceted commitment to his constituents. ​ Beyond his official duties, Steven is deeply engaged in the local community, contributing his time and expertise to various Northern Kentucky non-profit associations as a board member.
His proactive involvement underscores his unwavering dedication to uplifting and strengthening the community he calls home.
