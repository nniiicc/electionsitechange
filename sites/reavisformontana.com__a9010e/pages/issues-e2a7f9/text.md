House District 47 Map PROTECTING YOUR RIGHTS I support protecting all your fundamental rights under the U.S. and Montana Constitutions.
You have the right to live your life the way you choose as long as you do not harm others.
You have the right to choose when to start a family and how many kids you have.
I support the LGBT+ community.
PROTECTING YOUR FAMILY I support paid family leave and affordable, quality child care.
People must be able to care for a sick child without losing their job.
I support public schools and a quality education for all.
School buildings must have reliable funding for repair and maintenance.
I support public safety policies that prevent and solve crimes.
This includes major reforms to our mental health and juvenile institutions.
I support a healthy environment, clean water, and clean air.
PROTECTING YOUR wallet I support raising the minimum wage to a livable wage.
I support protecting seniors on fixed incomes from unfair price hikes.
I support affordable health care.
No one should ever have to file for bankruptcy just because they’re sick.
