Siobhan McIntyre is a dedicated community member with a passion for helping others.
With a BA in World Religions, Siobhan has always been driven by a deep curiosity about diverse cultures and beliefs, fostering a strong sense of empathy and understanding.
After working as a 911 dispatcher in Brookline, MA, where Siobhan received commendations from both the town and State 911 department for meritorious service, Siobhan developed a commitment to serving others in moments of need.
This experience reinforced Siobhan’s belief in the importance of community and the power of collective support.
After moving to Kansas to be closer to family, who have deep roots in the state, Siobhan has worked at the customer service desk of a local grocery store, where she continues to engage with and support the community daily.
In addition to her professional work, Siobhan actively volunteers as a 4-H leader for the dog project, where she shares her love of animals and helps guide young people in developing responsibility and leadership skills.
Guided by the core values of community and helping others, Siobhan is running for the Kansas State House to ensure that everyone has a voice and access to the resources they need to thrive.
When not working, volunteering, or campaigning, Siobhan enjoys hiking the beautiful Kansas trails with her beloved dog, Dumplin.
