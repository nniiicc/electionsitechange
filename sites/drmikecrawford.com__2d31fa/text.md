I’m Dr.
Mike Crawford, your State Representative for the 31st District.
In my first term, I've worked hard to pass meaningful legislation that uplifts students with disabilities, protects youth in care, invests in education at all levels, creates good jobs, and shields our seniors from scams; but we've got more work to do, and I look forward to doing it with you.
That's why I am looking forward to your support in my re-election.
Together, we’re building stronger communities from the South Side and Southwest suburbs to across the State of Illinois.

What are Dr.
Crawford's main priorities for the district?
Dr.
Crawford's main priorities include increasing education funding and opportunities, supporting equal rights for all, equitable healthcare access, supporting workers and their families, economic development, and ensuring that the 31st District is a safe place to live, work, and play.
