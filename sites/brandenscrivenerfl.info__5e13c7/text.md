About Contact Federal Congressional District 12, No Party Affiliation Candidate Branden Scrivener Send a Message My Bio My name is Branden Scrivener, I’m a 27-year-old working class father of two.
I have a bachelor’s in human services, along with internship experiences at Catholic Charities, Tampa Crossroads, Department of Child Services, and Flagler Education Foundation.
I’ve also worked in healthcare for over three years.
One of the main duties in my role is to secure insurance authorizations for patients.
My experience has led me to work with a diverse group of people which has shown me firsthand our broken insurance system.
I have experience advocating for my community and seeing our vulnerable populations difficulties directly.
All this experience has led me to the same conclusion; our government policies are the source of many of our issues.
For a while, I waited it out, thinking surely when “this” party won things would be different.
I’ve watched the administrations and party affiliations change, and yet the systems which created our issues seem to rarely change.
I’ve decided to stop wishing for someone to change things and become the change I want to see.

That means there’s a significant portion of eligible voters unable to participate in FL primaries.
Invest in local public education We have shifted our focus to develop Charter and other 3rd party school systems over public education.
I believe in parent choice, the problem stems from where that choice funding comes from.
Funding for public education should not be diverted to these programs.
Teachers need to be supported and valued.
Students and staff should feel safe in the public school system.
We must take steps to ensure our children are not harmed while attending school.
Quality of Life Cost of living has skyrocketed and continues to rise.
We need to change fundamentals rather than only addressing the symptoms.
The interest of working class Americans must be prioritized over those of foreign nations or corporate capital.
Data Privacy & Development Your data should be protected and required to be accessed by the government with the use of a warrant.
