Sara’s Platform
Key Issues
- Raise Minimum Wage Sara Gullion will fight to: Raise the minimum wage so full-time work actually pays the bills, index wages to inflation so workers don’t lose ground every year and raise the subminimum wages that leave tipped workers vulnerable.
- Affordable and Safe Housing Sara Gullion will fight to: Increase state investment in affordable housing, strengthen renter protections and eviction prevention, align state policy with proven housing solutions, and treat housing as essential infrastructure for healthy communities.
- Strengthen Public Education Sara Gullion will fight to: Provide Pre-K for all, ensure Hoosiers’ taxes go toward public schools, and that a college education in Indiana is as strong as anywhere.
- Affordable Childcare Sara sees childcare as infrastructure — just like roads and broadband.
District 34 families deserve solutions that treat it that way.
- Data Centers Sara believes lawmakers should put a moratorium in place for any more data centers.
Before another permit is approved, communities deserve the right to weigh in through public hearings, honest environmental impact assessments, and limits tied to local capacity.
- Restore Reproductive Freedom Sara supports restoring reproductive freedom!
Sara believes personal medical decisions are exactly that — personal.
She’d work to restore trust in Indiana health care by putting patients ahead of politics.
- LGBTQ+ Non-Descrimination Protections Sara believes freedom applies to everyone in District 34 — full stop.
- Protections for Immigrant Families & Workers Sara will fight to make sure immigrant neighbors can live and work here with the security they’ve earned.
- Access to Mental Healthcare Sara believes mental health IS health — and would fight for the access and investment District 34 needs to actually support it.
