LGBTQ+ Non-Desrimination Protections
LGBTQ+ residents in District 34 can still be fired from their job, denied housing, or turned away from health care because they have no statewide protection.
Meanwhile, political attacks treat their lives as wedge issues rather than what they are: real lives, real families, real neighbors.
Sara supports comprehensive non-discrimination laws covering employment, housing, public accommodations, and health care.
She opposes anti-trans bills banning gender-affirming care and supports safe, inclusive schools with real anti-bullying training.
She’d also push to allow ID updates without unnecessary humiliation.
When LGBTQ+ teachers, nurses, business owners, and parents can live openly without fear, everyone benefits.
Communities retain talent.
Schools become safer.
Families stay instead of leaving for somewhere more welcoming.
Sara believes freedom applies to everyone in District 34 — full stop.
