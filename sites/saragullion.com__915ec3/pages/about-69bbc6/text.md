Over the past two years on the Muncie City Council, I have championed accountability, transparency and fairness.
I view every vote through the lens of Muncie’s citizens’ best interests and I will continue to serve the residents of Muncie faithfully throughout this campaign.
Integrity matters to me, and that will not change.
My experience in local government provides a unique perspective on how state-level decisions impact our everyday lives.
I’m committed to advocating for the issues that hit closest to home here in District 34: housing affordability, fair wages, accessible healthcare, and strong public schools.
I believe District 34 requires a representative who understands both the challenges facing us as well as our immense potential.
I will continue to advocate for and respond to my constituents’ needs.
And, I will go to the Statehouse and remind the state’s decision-makers that their votes affect real people with real needs, who deserve real affordability.
