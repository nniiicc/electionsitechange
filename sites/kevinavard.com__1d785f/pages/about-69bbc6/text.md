Family man, small business owner, and a steady voice of reason in Concord.
Senator Kevin Avard works to cut through the gridlock by focusing on the basics: getting the drug crisis under control, fixing our roads and bridges, supporting our veterans, funding mental health, passing legislation to assist the free-market in lowering healthcare costs, and making sure our students have first-class schools.
Kevin is a small business owner who will protect our rights, hold the line on taxes and spending, and create new jobs.
While others want to grow government and increase burdensome regulations, Kevin has worked to lessen the weight on New Hampshire's small businesses.
Outside of politics, Kevin is heavily involved in fundraising to support music programs at Nashua Public Schools — helping underprivileged students afford musical instruments.
Kevin and his wife Tracy have five children and live in Nashua, NH.
