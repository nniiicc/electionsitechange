Dan Cox: A Husband, Father, Grandfather, Small Businessman, and Problem Solver.
About Dan Cox – Dan Cox for Governor.
Dan Cox is a lifelong Marylander, husband, father, grandfather, and small‑business owner who has dedicated his career to serving the people of our state.
Raised with a deep respect for faith, family, and hard work, Dan understands the challenges Maryland families face — from rising costs to concerns about safety, education, and opportunity.
His commitment to public service is rooted in a simple belief: government should be accountable, communities should be empowered, and every Marylander deserves the freedom to build a better future.
As an attorney and advocate, Dan has spent years standing up for individuals, families, and small businesses.
His work has focused on protecting constitutional rights, improving government transparency, and ensuring that everyday Marylanders have a voice in the decisions that affect their lives.
Dan’s approach to leadership is grounded in listening, problem‑solving, and bringing people together to create real, lasting solutions.
As a former elected Town Council Member and State Delegate, Dan’s experience in public service has given him a clear understanding of how state government works — and where it needs to work better.
He believes Maryland can be a place where families feel safe, businesses can grow, and students receive an education that prepares them for success.
His vision for Maryland is built on restoring trust, strengthening local communities, and ensuring that state government serves the people, not the other way around.
Above all, Dan is driven by his love for Maryland and his desire to see it thrive.
He and his wife, Valerie, are raising their family here, and they want every Marylander to have the same opportunities for stability, prosperity, and freedom.
Dan’s leadership is guided by integrity, responsibility, and a commitment to protecting the rights and well-being of every citizen.
Dan Cox is running for Governor to bring Maryland a future defined by keeping more of our hard-earned money, affordable living, safety, opportunity, and accountable leadership — a future where families can flourish, communities can grow stronger, and the promise of Maryland is renewed for generations to come.
