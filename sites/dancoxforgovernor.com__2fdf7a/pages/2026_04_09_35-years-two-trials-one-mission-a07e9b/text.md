April 9, 2026 Elections , Political Dan Cox Most Marylanders already know it: your government isn’t working for you.
It’s working against you.
Three years ago, I lived it.
The DOJ and politically motivated US Attorneys of Maryland raided my businesses.
Terrorized my family.
For over two years, they drained our bank accounts, tried to wear us down, and bury us — not in the name of justice, but to make a name for themselves.
A compliant judicial system bent the rules for them.
They wanted me behind bars for 35 years for a crime I did not commit.
We fought back.
With Dan Cox, we won twice.
The last time, in front of a jury of my peers, who saw exactly what I saw: government lies.
Government deceit.
Government abuse of power.
That’s why I’m running.
Not for a title.
Not for ambition.
Because I’ve been where you are — and I refused to back down.
I won’t start now.
Dan Cox has spent his career defending Marylanders’ rights and freedoms.
Together, we will stop a government that has lost sight of the people it was elected to serve — one more focused on pet projects, political futures outside this state, and personal gain than on you.
We will keep our streets safe.
Secure our kids’ futures.
And put YOU first.
No more corruption.
No more weaponized government.
It’s time for new leadership in Annapolis.
Every dollar you contribute is a vote against the kind of government abuse I faced.
Help us fight back.
A vote for Dan Cox is a vote for strong families, strong homes, and a strong Maryland.
