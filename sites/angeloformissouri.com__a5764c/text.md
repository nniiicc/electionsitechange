Angelo For Missouri Meet Jerry Putting Families First Jerry has been working to help strengthen Springfield families and families all across Missouri for over 20 years.
Before he founded a family non-profit with his wife Kate back in 2018, Jerry was a senior-level network & security engineer for nearly 3 decades.
All that experience has taught him how to successfully manage complex, multi-faceted, multi-million dollar projects.
His work with family was no less-complicated, as he and his wife work with with real lives in real families to help them achieve success in the relationships with the ones that they love the most.
His background has uniquely equipped Jerry to serve our district by putting our families first, and also handle the complex issues that we face in our community.
