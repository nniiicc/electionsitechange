01 🚑 A Healthcare System That Works for Ohioans: U niversal, S imple, A ffordable + Healthy communities are strong communities, yet healthcare in the United States is too political, too expensive, and too unreliable, and our local families are paying the price.
No one should ever have to choose between getting medical care and putting food on the table or clothes on our back.
It’s time to reduce the bureaucracy and take concrete steps to ensure a universal, streamlined system that puts people first.
We must cut red tape, lower costs, and guarantee that every Ohioan can see a doctor when they need to.
That’s why I will champion legislation like HB289 and SB78 to establish and operate the Ohio Health Care Plan, ensuring comprehensive, reliable care for every Ohioan.
This is how we build a stronger, fairer future for our families and our state.
02 ⚡ Lowering Utility and Essential Services Costs + Electricity, water, internet, and other essential services are eating up more and more of household budgets.
Wages have not kept up.
Housing and food insecurity across our communities have also skyrocketed.
These things aren’t luxuries.
We know they’re necessities.
As data centers threaten to increase prices even further, I would stand for a ban, or a moratorium in the very least on these centers, along with pushing for protective regulation and fair pricing that puts people first , and forces transparency and accountability.
Ohio families have been squeezed enough by rising utility costs.
We deserve reliable, affordable services without surprise fees or corporate price hikes.
Communities deserve a voice in what happens in their backyard, and they should trust that their state representative isn’t taking bribes from energy companies, like they did with HB6 (largest bribery scandal in Ohio's history.) Instead of giving billions to highly profitable corporations, we should be investing directly in our residents in the form of subsidies for essential services like childcare and housing , both of which have been shown to strengthen and grow the economy.
03 💰 Fixing Our Broken Tax System + Working families are paying more than their fair share while the wealthiest individuals and corporations benefit from loopholes and special treatment.
Our current representative has chosen to give tax breaks to the wealthiest Ohioans, which in turn has driven our property taxes through the roof.
A tax system that works for EVERYONE, not just the well-connected, will strengthen our schools, infrastructure, and local services WITHOUT burdening everyday Ohioans.
The economic benefit of the constant tax cuts for the wealthiest Ohioans and corporations has not materialized.
These amounts total $12 billion EACH year, taken from our common wealth.
No more welfare handouts to the rich!
I will support legislation that brings back a corporate income tax and higher tax brackets on personal income that will restore balance and fairness to our overall tax system.
