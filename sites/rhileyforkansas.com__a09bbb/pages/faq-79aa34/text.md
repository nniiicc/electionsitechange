Do you support corporate welfare programs in Kansas?
For the purposes of this question, corporate welfare is defined as a practice in which specific businesses receive taxpayer-funded cash incentives, favorable tax treatment, government-backed loans, or other exemptions, refunds, or abatements not offered to other businesses in the state.
Examples of such programs in Kansas include PEAK (Promoting Employment Across Kansas), HPIP (High Performance Incentive Program) and STAR Bonds.* I do not support corporate welfare programs in Kansas.
