Wendy Rogers for Re-election June 4, 2026 Gun Owners of America (GOA) has endorsed Senator Wendy Rogers for re-election in Arizona Legislative District 7, citing her proven record of defending the Second Amendment and standing up for Arizona’s law-abiding gun owners.
“GOA is proud to once again endorse Senator Wendy Rogers for Arizona Legislative District 7,” the organization said.
“Senator Rogers has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to defending the Second Amendment and protecting the rights of Arizona’s law-abiding gun owners.” Throughout her time in the Arizona Legislature, Rogers has consistently championed pro-Second Amendment legislation.
Her efforts have included protecting firearm owners from retroactive gun registration and confiscation schemes, expanding campus carry rights, defending the ability of tenants to possess and store firearms in their homes, eliminating concealed carry permit fees for veterans and retired law enforcement officers, repealing restrictions on suppressors, protecting firearms businesses from discrimination by banks and government entities, and fighting for Constitutional Carry in Arizona.
“This is a significant honor to me as I hold the 2nd Amendment as a precious cause upon which all of our freedoms are based,” Rogers said.
“Thank you so much.” The endorsement highlights Rogers’ long-standing commitment to protecting the right to keep and bear arms and her continued leadership on behalf of Arizona’s gun owners.
