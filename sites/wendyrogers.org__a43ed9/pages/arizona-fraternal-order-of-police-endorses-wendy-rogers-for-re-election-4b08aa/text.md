Senator Wendy Rogers has earned the endorsement of the Arizona Fraternal Order of Police (F.O.P.), one of the state’s largest law enforcement organizations, in her campaign for re-election to Arizona Legislative District 7.
Representing more than 10,000 members across Arizona, the Arizona F.O.P. cited Rogers’ consistent support for public safety and law enforcement as the basis for its endorsement.
“Your endorsement by the F.O.P., one of Arizona’s largest police organizations, is based on your demonstrated support for significant public safety issues and reflects our belief that you best understand the difficult work faced by members of the law enforcement community,” the organization wrote.
“Your support for those efforts has not gone unnoticed.”
The Fraternal Order of Police advocates for professional law enforcement officers serving in agencies throughout Arizona and works to improve officer safety, training, working conditions, legislation, and community engagement.
Nationally, the Fraternal Order of Police is the world’s largest organization of law enforcement officers, representing more than 387,000 members.
Throughout her time in the Arizona Senate, Rogers has consistently supported legislation to strengthen public safety, secure the border, combat violent crime, improve cooperation between state and federal law enforcement agencies, and provide officers with the tools they need to protect Arizona communities.
As Chair of the Senate Judiciary and Elections Committee, she has also worked to advance legislation supporting victims’ rights, election integrity, and the rule of law.
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the Arizona Fraternal Order of Police,” Rogers said.
“The brave men and women of law enforcement put their lives on the line every day to protect our families and communities.
I will always stand with those who wear the badge and fight to ensure they have the support, resources, and respect they deserve.”
The endorsement adds to Rogers’ growing coalition of support from Arizona’s law enforcement community and recognizes her long-standing commitment to public safety and backing those who serve on the front lines.
