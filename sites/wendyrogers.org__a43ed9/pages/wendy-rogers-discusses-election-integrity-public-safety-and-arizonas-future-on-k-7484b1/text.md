During a wide-ranging interview on KMOG Radio, Senator Wendy Rogers discussed her campaign for re-election, reflected on her legislative accomplishments, and shared her priorities for Arizona’s future.
She highlighted her work on public safety, election integrity, and border security, addressed recent legal victories protecting free political speech, and emphasized her commitment to serving the people of Legislative District 7.
Wendy also answered listener questions and offered her perspective on the state’s political landscape and the importance of strong conservative leadership.
