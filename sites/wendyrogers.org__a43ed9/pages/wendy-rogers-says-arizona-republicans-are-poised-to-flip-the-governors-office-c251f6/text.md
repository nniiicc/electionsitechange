In an interview with RSBN’s Matthew Alvarez, Senator Wendy Rogers discussed the results of Arizona’s Republican primary, highlighting President Trump’s endorsed candidates and expressing confidence that Andy Biggs can defeat Governor Katie Hobbs in November.
She contrasted the Republican-controlled Legislature’s work on election integrity, border security, and fiscal policy with what she described as repeated obstruction from the governor, while emphasizing the need to replace Arizona’s top statewide officials with conservative leadership.
Rogers also outlined her legislative efforts on illegal immigration, election reforms, and government accountability, and encouraged supporters to help build momentum for the Republican ticket ahead of the general election.
