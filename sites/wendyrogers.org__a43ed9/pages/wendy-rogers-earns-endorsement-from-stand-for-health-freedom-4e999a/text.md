Stand for Health Freedom endorsed Senator Rogers after reviewing her responses to its candidate survey, recognizing her long-standing commitment to defending health freedom and advocating for policies that put patients and families—not government bureaucrats—in control of their own healthcare decisions.
As part of its endorsement process, Stand for Health Freedom supports only one candidate per race on its voter guides in order to unite health freedom voters behind a single candidate.
Senator Rogers will be featured in the organization’s Arizona Vote for Health Freedom Voter Guide , helping voters identify candidates who share their commitment to medical freedom and constitutional liberties.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of Stand for Health Freedom,” said Senator Wendy Rogers.
“Arizonans deserve the freedom to make their own healthcare decisions without government coercion or overreach.
I will continue fighting to protect individual liberty, informed consent, and the rights of every Arizona family.” Stand for Health Freedom represents a nationwide network of more than one million advocates and works year-round to educate voters, encourage civic engagement, and support candidates who champion health freedom.
