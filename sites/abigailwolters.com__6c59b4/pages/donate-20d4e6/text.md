Your Donation May Come Back to You The Minnesota Political Contribution Refund Program Abby’s campaign is eligible for the Minnesota Political Contribution Refund (PCR) program.
The PCR program allows eligible Minnesota voters to be reimbursed by the State of Minnesota for up to $75 of what they donate to qualifying local campaigns like Abby’s.
How PCR works: Make a donation through Anedot or by check: Make checks payable to: Campaign Fund of Abigail Wolters Campaign Fund of Abigail Wolters PO Box 13102 Minneapolis, MN 55414 Abby’s campaign will mail you a receipt and a PCR form.
Fill out the form and mail it with the receipt to the address on the form before Tax Day 2027.
Receive your reimbursement from the State of Minnesota within a few weeks.

I am making this contribution with my own personal credit card and not with a corporate or business credit card or a card issued to another person.
Contributions or gifts are not tax deductible.
The maximum amount an individual may contribute to a candidate for Minnesota State Senate is $1,000 per election segment of the election cycle, and $1,000 per nonelection segment.
