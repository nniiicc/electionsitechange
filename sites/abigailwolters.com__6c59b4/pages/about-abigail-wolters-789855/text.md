About Abby Issues Get Involved Contact Donate Menu Menu Building Bridges for a Stronger Minneapolis Abigail Wolters for Minnesota SD 60 Scroll to next section Meet Abby Wolters Abby Wolters is a lifelong Minnesotan who believes our state is worth fighting for.
Raised in Minnesota and educated by our public schools, Abby went on to graduate from the University of Minnesota.
Today, she works as a software engineer.
Abby is a renter in the Marcy Holmes neighborhood.
Abby is not a politician, she is a volunteer who decided to step up because she believes NE, SE, campus, and Cedar-Riverside should not have to accept the direction Minnesota is headed.
We have all seen firsthand how decisions made in St.
Paul have impacted our ability to afford a home, pay for school, find a job, feel safe, and build a life here.
She is endorsed by Minneapolis Republicans.
