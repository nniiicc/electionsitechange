Privacy Policy
Abigail Wolters for Minnesota Senate District 60 | Last updated: July 2026
1.
Information We Collect
We collect information in the following ways:
- Donations: When you donate through our Anedot donation page, Anedot collects your name, mailing address, email address, phone number, employer, occupation, and payment information.
This information is required by federal and Minnesota campaign finance law.
Contributions of $200 or more are reported to the Minnesota Campaign Finance Board and become part of the public record.
- Volunteer signup: When you complete our volunteer signup form (hosted by Google Forms), we collect your name, email address, phone number, zip code, and your availability preferences.
- Contact form: When you submit our contact form (hosted by Google Forms), we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
- Website usage: Our website may collect basic technical information such as your browser type, IP address, and pages visited through standard server logs and cookies.
We do not use third-party analytics tracking such as Google Analytics.
2.
How We Use Your Information
Information collected through this website is used solely for campaign purposes, including:
- Processing donations and complying with campaign finance reporting requirements
- Contacting volunteers and coordinating campaign activities
- Responding to inquiries submitted through the contact form
- Sending campaign updates and information (you may opt out at any time)
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.
3.
Third-Party Services
This website uses the following third-party services, each of which operates under its own privacy policy:
- Anedot (donation processing) — Anedot processes all online donations made through this campaign.
Your payment and personal information is subject to Anedot’s Privacy Policy.
Anedot uses industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption to protect your payment information.
- Google Forms / Google LLC (volunteer signup and contact forms) — Our volunteer and contact forms are hosted by Google.
Information you submit is stored in Google’s infrastructure and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
Only authorized campaign staff have access to form responses.
4.
Campaign Finance Disclosure
As a registered Minnesota political campaign committee, the Campaign Fund of Abigail Wolters is required by law to report contributions to the Minnesota Campaign Finance Board (CFB).
Contributions of $200 or more, including the contributor’s name, address, employer, and occupation, are reported publicly.
For more information about campaign finance reporting requirements, visit the Minnesota Campaign Finance Board.
5.
Cookies
We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.
You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some site features.
6.
Data Retention
Donor information is retained as required by Minnesota campaign finance law.
Volunteer and contact form information is retained for the duration of the 2026 campaign and disposed of securely thereafter.
You may request deletion of your contact information at any time by emailing us (see below), subject to any legal retention requirements.
7.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not directed at children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
8.
