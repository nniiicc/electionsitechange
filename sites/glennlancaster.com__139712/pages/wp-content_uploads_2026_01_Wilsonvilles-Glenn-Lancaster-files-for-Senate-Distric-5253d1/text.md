Glenn Lancaster, a resident of Wilsonville and 2024 mayoral candidate, has filed to run for Oregon Senate District 13, according to documents from the Oregon Secretary of State’s office.
If he wins his primary, Lancaster, the Republican Party chair for Clackamas County precinct 203, will likely face incumbent state Sen.
Courtney Neron Misslin, D-Wilsonville.
Neron Misslin defeated Lancaster in the 2022 House District 26 election and Lancaster lost to Shawn O’Neil in the 2024 Wilsonville mayoral election.
He is the only Republican who has filed to run in the May Senate District 13 primary.
