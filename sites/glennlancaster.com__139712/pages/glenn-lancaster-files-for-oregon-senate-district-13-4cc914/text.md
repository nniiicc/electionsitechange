Glenn Lancaster Files for Oregon Senate District 13
Published on WilsonvilleSpokesman.com
Glenn Lancaster has officially filed to run for Oregon Senate District 13 and is currently the only Republican candidate in the primary.
A longtime Wilsonville resident and retired electric utility professional, Lancaster brings decades of leadership experience and a continued commitment to local representation.
