Fix What’s Broken Oregon is an incredible place to live.
People love it here — and they want it to succeed.
But for too many families, life is getting harder every year.
That didn’t happen by accident.
For decades, the same political leadership in Salem has made the same decisions — and Oregon is paying the price.
Costs are rising.
Services are failing.
Safety and schools are moving in the wrong direction.
Glenn Lancaster is running for Oregon Senate to bring common sense back, hold leadership accountable, and fix what’s broken. → Stop the Cost-of-Living Crisis Oregonians are working hard — but it’s getting harder to keep up.
Gas prices are higher because taxes keep going up.
Grocery bills take a bigger bite out of every paycheck.
Rent and housing costs are out of reach for too many families.
This isn’t unavoidable.
It’s the result of policy choices.
What’s gone wrong • Oregon passed the largest tax increase in state history, including a major gas tax hike • Higher fuel taxes raise the price of everything — from food to commuting • Restrictive housing and land-use policies limit supply and drive up rent and home prices Glenn’s approach • Stop the gas tax and stop tax increases • Fix housing policies that drive up rent and home prices • Make affordability a priority so families can live, work, and stay in Oregon Government shouldn’t be making everyday life more expensive. → End Failed Oregon Leadership Oregon should be leading.
Instead, we’re falling behind.
For forty years, one-party control in Salem has produced the same results: higher taxes, lower performance, and no accountability.
We are last in too many things we should be first in — and first in too many things we should be last in.
What Oregonians see • High taxes paired with poor outcomes • Programs that grow even when they fail • Ideology prioritized over practical results Glenn’s approach • Demand accountability for what actually works • Challenge policies that protect the system instead of the people • Replace excuses with results Managing decline is not leadership. → Restore Safety, Order, and Common Sense People deserve safe communities and a government that enforces the law.
Today, crime, homelessness, and addiction are no longer isolated problems — they’re visible across Oregon.
At the same time, schools are spending more than ever while outcomes continue to fall.
What’s not working • Laws that aren’t enforced • Addiction and homelessness treated as permanent • Schools distracted from education Glenn’s approach • Restore public order and enforce the law • Demand real solutions to addiction and homelessness • Refocus schools on education, not politics Compassion works best when there is accountability and results.
Why Glenn Lancaster Glenn Lancaster is a proud Oregonian who loves this state and knows how special it is — and how much better it can be.
He believes: • Oregon’s decline is a choice, not destiny • Leadership decisions matter • Common sense still works Glenn isn’t running to manage decline.
He’s running to fix what’s broken.
Join the Effort Oregon doesn’t have to keep getting worse.
If it’s not working, change it.
