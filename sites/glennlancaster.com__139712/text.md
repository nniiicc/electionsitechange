Oregon Senate, District 13 Oregonian Business Leader Community Advocate Fix What’s Broken.
I’m Glenn Lancaster, and I’m running for Oregon Senate because I love this state — and I don’t believe we have to accept what it’s becoming.
Oregon is one of the best places in the country to live.
But for too many families, life is getting harder.
Gas, groceries, and housing cost more every year.
Communities feel less safe.
Schools keep spending more while delivering less.
That didn’t happen by accident.
It’s the result of bad decisions and failed leadership in Salem.
I’m not running to manage decline.
I’m running to bring common sense back and fix what’s broken.
Why I’m running I’ve spent my life working in the private sector, fixing systems that weren’t working and holding people accountable for results.
I know this: when something is broken, pretending otherwise doesn’t help.
You fix it.
Oregon can do better — and it should.
