How to help!
May 25
Here are just a few ways you can help Clint Collamore get elected.
- Share this website with your friends
- Like, comment, and share his content on Facebook and Instagram
- Write a letter to the editor to the Lincoln County News, Midcoast Villager, or PenBay Pilot.
- Host a sign in a high-visibility location
- Sign up on Unison to drive Clint Collamore as he goes door-knocking (2-3 hour shifts)
- Donate some funds to help offset costs
- Remember to vote.
Encourage your friends and family to register and vote.
