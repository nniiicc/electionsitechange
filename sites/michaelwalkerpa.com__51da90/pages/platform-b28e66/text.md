No Reality TV Politics: He is not looking to win a popularity contest.
He wants to bring state resources to our communities.
Continued investment in infrastructure: Bridges are rusting and crumbling, water and power systems are outdated and failing.
We need to invest more resources into fixing these issues, creating more local jobs and a more viable Commonwealth for citizens and businesses to grow.
Pushing for stronger adherence to ADA compliance: Too much is slipping through the cracks in this area, and we need to be stricter to make it more viable for individuals to live life on their own terms.
Tax cuts and grants for PA based businesses and agriculture: Freeing revenue for PA businesses and agriculture will promote growth for our communities.
Healthcare and prescription drugs: We need to lower prescription drug costs and enhance transparency in pricing.
We also need to protect access to community health centers and rural hospitals.
Strengthen education: We need to shift away from standardized testing and go back to teaching critical thinking, and make sure taxes are appropriately funding public education.
