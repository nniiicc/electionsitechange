CHARLESTON, WV (LOOTPRESS) – Senate President-elect Randy Smith, R-Preston, has announced key leadership roles for the Senate Finance Committee in the upcoming 87th Legislature, which convenes on January 8, 2025.
Senator Jason Barrett, R-Berkeley, will serve as Chairman of the influential committee.
Senator Rollan Roberts, R-Raleigh, has been named Vice Chairman.
