ISSUES
Election Integrity
To ensure faith in our electoral process, the State of Michigan must make sure voter rolls are clean, enforce our laws already on the books, and build a stricter audit system to keep bad actors from penetrating our election process.
We must know that only legal votes are cast and that every legal vote is counted.
It is the only way forward!
Protect Life
I'm proud to be
pro-life and will stand for pro-life principles in Lansing.
I have volunteered for years with organizations such as the Cheboygan Crisis Pregnancy Center and Michigan Right to Life.
I'm proud to stand as the proven voice of those who have no voice.
100% Pro-2nd Amendment
The Michigan Constitution is clear - "Every person has the right to keep and bear arms for the defense of himself and the state." As your next Representative, I will work tirelessly to defend our rights and ensure bureaucrats and politicians do not infringe on our sacred right to bear arms.
Limited Government & Local Control
Over the past few years we've seen that our government and Governor have been given far too much power over our daily lives.
In Lansing, I will fight to shrink the size of our government and return power to "We the people."
CRT has been creeping into our schools and parents are being phased out from important local decision-making.
Our parents must be empowered in the education of their children.
We must truly fund our students and stand-up for our future.
No more mandates.
No more overreach.
Pro-Environment & Pro-Energy
We don't have to sacrifice our energy and economic needs to protect the environment, and vice-versa.
It's imperative we protect our shoreline, our natural resources, our ecosystem, and our energy needs.
I'm proud to support the Great Lakes tunnel project, and once elected, I will work to ensure this project moves forward and our Great Lakes are protected.
