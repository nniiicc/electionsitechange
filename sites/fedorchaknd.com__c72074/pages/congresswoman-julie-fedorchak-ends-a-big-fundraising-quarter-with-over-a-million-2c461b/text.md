Bismarck, ND – Julie Fedorchak for Congress today announced a strong first quarter fundraising report, reflecting broad support for her reelection campaign and positioning the campaign well for the 2026 election.
The strong quarter comes just weeks after hundreds of supporters gathered in Minot to support Julie and the 2026 North Dakota Republican ticket, as well as previously in Fargo, Bismarck and Williston.
President Donald Trump formally endorsed Julie Fedorchak’s reelection campaign last summer.
“I’m deeply grateful for the strong support my campaign has received from North Dakotans across our state,” Fedorchak said.
“Representing and serving the great people of North Dakota in Congress is the honor of a lifetime.
We’ve secured meaningful wins for our state and our country—strengthening border security, unleashing American energy, and delivering historic tax relief for hardworking families.
I’m proud to work with President Trump to keep delivering results for North Dakota.
Our agenda for the remaining months of this Congress and the next is aggressive and vital to our state and nation.”
