CAMPAIGN FUNDRAISING ASSISTANT Fedorchak for North Dakota – Based in Bismarck Please send resumes and a brief statement of interest to John Odermann – John.Odermann@gmail.com SUMMARY: Fedorchak for North Dakota is seeking a friendly, detail-oriented, and proactive Campaign Fundraising Assistant based in Bismarck.
This role supports the fundraising and donor engagement efforts for Congresswoman Julie Fedorchak’s campaign and plays a key role in building lasting relationships with supporters across the state and country.
The ideal candidate is outgoing, highly organized, and thrives in a fast-paced, people-focused environment.
You’ll assist with planning and executing fundraising events, managing donor data and correspondence, and ensuring every supporter feels valued and appreciated.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: Event Planning & Support Assist with planning and executing donor events, from small gatherings to large receptions.
Work with hosts and vendors to coordinate event logistics.
Prepare and organize event materials, guest lists, and follow-up plans.
Donor Relations & Stewardship Assist Julie in making phone calls to hosts and donors.
Help build and maintain strong relationships with donors and supporters.
Make follow-up calls and check-ins to thank donors and provide updates.
Assist Julie in writing personalized thank-you cards and donor appreciation notes.
Assist event hosts, ensuring they have the information and materials they need.
Database Management & Reporting Maintain accurate and up-to-date donor information in campaign database.
Track pledges, contributions, and event attendance.
Generate call lists, donor profiles, and reports for finance and candidate use.
Communications & Design Create visually appealing invitations, social media graphics, and thank-you notes.
Contribute to supporter communications and donor materials with a polished, professional tone.
Political Engagement Attend local Republican Party meetings, community gatherings, and other events as a campaign representative.
Build positive relationships with political activists, elected officials, and community leaders.
QUALIFICATIONS: Friendly, outgoing, and extroverted with excellent interpersonal skills.
Highly organized with strong attention to detail and follow-through.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines independently.
Strong writing and verbal communication skills.
Familiarity with Canva or similar graphic design tools.
Comfortable making phone calls, attending events, and engaging with supporters.
Experience with fundraising, event planning, or political campaigns is helpful but not required.
Passion for electing conservative leaders and advancing shared values.
POSITION DETAILS: This is a part-time campaign role based in Bismarck.
Some evening and weekend availability will be required for events.
