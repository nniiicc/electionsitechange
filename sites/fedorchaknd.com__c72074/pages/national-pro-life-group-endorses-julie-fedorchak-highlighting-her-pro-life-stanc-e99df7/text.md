Fedorchak Receives Endorsement from Leading National Pro-Life Organization
Bismarck, ND – Julie Fedorchak, candidate for North Dakota’s at-large congressional seat, announced today she has been endorsed by the Susan B.
Anthony Pro-Life America, the nation’s leading pro-life organization.
“Julie Fedorchak is exactly the kind of pro-life leader we need in Congress to take on Joe Biden and Kamala Harris’s assault on unborn babies and women,” said Hon.
Marilyn Musgrave, SBA Pro-Life America’s Vice President of Government Affairs.
“Julie will always stand strong for North Dakota values and fight for national protections for the unborn.
We are proud to endorse her candidacy for Congress and urge pro-life North Dakotans to cast their votes for her.”
Fedorchak said she is deeply honored by the endorsement.
“I am very grateful to SBA for this support and look forward to working with this great organization on pro-life policies,” Fedorchak said.
“As a woman of faith and a proud and grateful mother of three, I am committed to building a culture of life here in North Dakota and across our nation that gives women true choice by providing the support they need to choose life.
This is how we transform America into a pro-life nation.”
With the support of Susan B.
Anthony Pro-Life America, Julie Fedorchak is poised to bring strong pro-life advocacy to Congress.
This endorsement underscores Julie’s long commitment to the pro-life values shared by millions of Americans.
Fedorchak has called for a federal ban on abortion of 16 weeks, allowing for Pro-Life states like North Dakota to enact more Pro-Life legislation if they so choose and preventing more pro-abortion states from expanding late-term abortion.
About Julie Fedorchak:
Julie Fedorchak is the Trump Endorsed conservative candidate for North Dakota’s lone seat in the House of Representatives.
A fourth-generation North Dakotan, Fedorchak has deep roots in the state.
The youngest of eight kids, she was born in Williston, raised in Fargo, graduated from high school in Bismarck, and completed college at UND in Grand Forks.
She has 32 years of experience in business and public service.
During her time on the Public Service Commission, she has lowered utility rates, fought back against the Obama and Biden Administration’s attacks on coal, and cut red tape at the commission to support North Dakota energy production.
She is ready to bring Fresh Energy to Washington and work with President Trump to secure our southern border, support our agriculture industry, and unleash our energy industry to grow jobs, reduce inflation and to make America energy independent again.
She and her husband Mike have been married for 25 years, have three children, and live in Mandan.
