BISMARCK — 54 North Dakota Republican legislators have endorsed Julie Fedorchak’s campaign for Congress.
“I am thankful for this support from my friends and colleagues in the Legislature,” Fedorchak said.
“We have worked tirelessly together for years to advance our conservative priorities, and this strong and consistent leadership has made North Dakota the best place in the country to live, work, and raise a family.”
Fedorchak has worked with the State Legislature during her time on the Public Service Commission on a variety of initiatives focusing on keeping utility costs low, growing our energy industry, expanding gas into rural communities, and cutting red tape.
“In North Dakota, we tackle our challenges head-on.
I have worked closely with these Republican leaders over the years, adopting a results-driven, conservative approach that is desperately needed in Washington, D.C.,” Fedorchak said.
“I’m excited to bring this fresh energy to Congress.”
