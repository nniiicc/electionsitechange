Trump endorses Congresswoman Julie Fedorchak for re-election Aug 19, 2025 BISMARCK, ND – President Donald J.
Trump has announced his official endorsement of Congresswoman Julie Fedorchak in her 2026 re-election campaign, calling her a champion for North Dakota and a relentless fighter for American energy, agriculture, and freedom.
“Congresswoman Julie Fedorchak is doing a fantastic job representing the Great People of North Dakota, a State I love, and WON BIG THREE TIMES, in 2016, 2020, and 2024!” President Trump stated.
“Julie has an incredible Record of Success, and strong support from her Community.
In Congress, she is working tirelessly to Grow our Economy, Cut Taxes, Promote MADE IN THE U.S.A., Support our Amazing Farmers and Ranchers, Ensure American Energy DOMINANCE by helping to keep the price of Gasoline, Oil, and all forms of Energy VERY LOW, Continue to Secure our now Secure Border, Champion our Military/Veterans, and Defend our always under siege Second Amendment.
Julie Fedorchak has my Complete and Total Endorsement for Re-Election — SHE WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!” Congresswoman Fedorchak, who was first elected to Congress in 2024, has quickly emerged as one of the most effective conservative voices in the House, advancing common-sense solutions to protect American energy independence, push back against federal overreach, and restore strength at home and abroad.
Her work on behalf of North Dakota families, farmers, veterans, and small businesses has earned her broad support across the state.
“I’m incredibly honored to have President Trump’s endorsement,” Fedorchak said.
“President Trump’s America First agenda is unleashing American Energy Dominance, and has already strengthened our national security, secured our border, and gave voice to millions of Americans who had been forgotten by Washington.
