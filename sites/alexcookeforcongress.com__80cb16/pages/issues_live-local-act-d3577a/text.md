The Problem
Florida's Live Local Act has stripped municipalities of their zoning authority over multifamily and mixed-use projects, clearing the way for developer-driven density changes that bypass local residents entirely.
Juno Beach, Jupiter, Tequesta, and Stuart are watching projects move forward they had no role in approving.
At the federal level, both parties have flirted with preempting local zoning to force higher density.
Whether the goal is right (more housing supply) or wrong (developer giveaways), the question of who decides — Washington, Tallahassee, or Town Hall — is settled in the Constitution: it's local.
