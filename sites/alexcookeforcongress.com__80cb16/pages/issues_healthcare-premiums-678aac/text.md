The Problem The enhanced ACA subsidies enacted in the American Rescue Plan and extended in the Inflation Reduction Act are set to expire — threatening to roughly double premiums for over 85,000 District 21 residents who depend on those plans, particularly those over 60.
Florida has the highest ACA marketplace enrollment in the country and the largest population of pre-Medicare retirees buying coverage on the exchange.
The cliff hits us hardest.
