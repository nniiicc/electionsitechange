Your Voice in Dover.
I'm the Republican candidate for the 8th Senate District, which includes Pike Creek, Newark, and surrounding areas.
I am a lifelong resident of the district, a small business owner, husband, and father who is deeply invested in the future of our community.
I attended the Christina School District from kindergarten through graduation, and today my family and I are raising our children in the same community that helped shape me.
Like many families in our district, our lives revolve around local schools, youth activities, community events, and the everyday challenge of balancing work, family, and planning for the future.
As a small business owner, I understand firsthand the challenges facing families, workers, and employers.
I've had to meet payroll, manage rising costs, navigate regulations, and make difficult decisions to keep a business moving forward.
Those experiences have taught me the importance of accountability, fiscal responsibility, and practical problem-solving.
I'm running for State Senate because I believe government works best when it focuses less on political division and more on practical solutions.
Families want strong schools, affordable energy, safe neighborhoods, thriving local businesses, and opportunities for their children to succeed.
Those are the issues that matter most to our community, and they deserve thoughtful, results-oriented leadership.
My approach is simple: listen first, treat people with respect, and work with anyone who has a good idea.
I believe public service is about representing people, solving problems, and earning trust through honesty, transparency, and hard work.
I look forward to earning your trust and working together to build an even stronger future for our district and the generations that will call it home.
See His Platform Join the Campaign Rooted in the Community Key Issues Key Issues These are the issues affecting District 8 the most.
They aren't talking points — they come from living and working right here alongside you.
Top Priority Schools I attended K–12 in the Christina School District and I see firsthand how lower enrollment hasn't reduced costs.
I want a better balance: less over-reliance on electronics and more hands-on learning that prepares students for real life, while upholding strong family values.
Top Priority Energy Delaware families shouldn't be at the mercy of big electric companies that dump every extra cost onto consumers.
I support emerging Small Modular Reactor (SMR) technology to build local energy independence and prevent rolling power outages from high demand.
Top Priority Jobs & Small Business Delaware's 111,000 small businesses produce more than half the state's jobs — yet they're treated like a nuisance.
I'll fight unfair fees and red tape, demand transparency in the unemployment fund, and protect the family livelihoods and community bonds that depend on a real voice in Dover.
Top Priority Safety First responders deserve our full support, and law-abiding citizens should never be treated as second-class to criminals.
I'll back the police and firefighters who protect us and work with neighbors to keep District 8 safe and family-friendly.
