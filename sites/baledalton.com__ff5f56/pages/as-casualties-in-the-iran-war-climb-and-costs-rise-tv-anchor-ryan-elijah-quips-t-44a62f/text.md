Elijah has expressed his support for the Iran war previously, saying “ We’ve got to finish the job” in Iran.
He doubled down on his support for the war, saying Floridians “understand there is a cost of freedom.” Bale Dalton, a Navy combat veteran of Iraq, Afghanistan, and the Horn of Africa, and the Democratic nominee for Congress in Florida’s Seventh District, issued the following statement: “I’ve spent 24 years and counting in the Navy and Navy Reserves, fighting the terrorists who said “Death to America” after 9/11.
I know there’s a cost to freedom because I have lived it – flying injured troops to safety as a medevac pilot, leading combat operations against Al-Qaeda, ISIS and the Taliban, and helping the families of fallen service members when the worst happens.
Only someone who’s never seen war could be so clueless and callous about the cost of freedom.
Unlike Mr.
Elijah, I know what I’m talking about – which is why I’m willing to ask the hard questions about the new needless war he is all too willing to plunge your sons and daughters into.
Because its cost is lives lost with no strategic goal, our military readiness eviscerated, and our economy in tatters.
All for an end-state (if we ever get there) that leaves us worse off in the Middle East, and more vulnerable to the terror elements I spent years fighting.
Although he’s never served, I’d advise Mr.
Elijah to speak with some actual families of those in harm’s way before he talks about costs he’s never paid.” ABOUT BALE DALTON: Bale Dalton is a decorated Navy Reserve Captain and former NASA Chief of Staff who has always answered the call to serve America.
One of four raised in a hardworking Central Florida family, Bale graduated from the U.S.
Naval Academy and has served in the U.S.
Navy and Navy Reserve for 24 years.
He deployed to Iraq, Afghanistan, the Horn of Africa, and the Pacific, flying Navy helicopters and directing air combat support with Navy SEALs in Afghanistan.
Following active duty, he worked for Senator Bill Nelson, helping Florida veterans access the care and benefits they had earned.
After an additional stint on active duty, Bale returned to government service as NASA Chief of Staff.
At NASA, he managed 16,500 employees and helped run one of the federal government’s most effective agencies.
After a lifetime of service, Bale is now running to represent his home, Florida’s 7th Congressional District, in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
