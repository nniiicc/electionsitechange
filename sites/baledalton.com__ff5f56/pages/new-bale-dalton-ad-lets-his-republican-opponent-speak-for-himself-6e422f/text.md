SANFORD, FL – Today, Democratic candidate for Florida’s 7th Congressional District Bale Dalton released his second ad of the general election, featuring Republican candidate Ryan Elijah’s own words on the economy, Trump’s tariffs, gas prices driven up by the Iran war, and his intention to trade stocks if elected.
“According to Ryan Elijah, our economy is in a ‘great spot,’ the tariffs are ‘working,’ and gas prices ‘could be worse,’” said Dalton.
“Nothing will change if we elect someone who doesn’t feel our pain at the pump, in the checkout line, or at the doctor’s office to represent us in Washington.
Ryan Elijah is running to pad his stock portfolio, not protect our pocketbooks.” A Navy combat veteran and lifelong public servant, Dalton has made lowering costs, cracking down on corruption, and putting Central Floridians first his priorities.
Dalton pledged to not trade stocks if elected and has already released an anti-corruption plan to restore public trust in public service.
ABOUT BALE DALTON: Bale Dalton is a decorated Navy Reserve Captain and former NASA Chief of Staff who has always answered the call to serve America.
One of four raised in a hardworking Central Florida family, Bale graduated from the U.S.
Naval Academy and has served in the U.S.
Navy and Navy Reserve for 24 years.
He deployed to Iraq, Afghanistan, the Horn of Africa, and the Pacific, flying Navy helicopters and directing air combat support with Navy SEALs in Afghanistan.
Following active duty, he worked for Senator Bill Nelson, helping Florida veterans access the care and benefits they had earned.
After an additional stint on active duty, Bale returned to government service as NASA Chief of Staff.
At NASA, he managed 16,500 employees and helped run one of the federal government’s most effective agencies.
After a lifetime of service, Bale is now running to represent his home, Florida’s 7th Congressional District, in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
