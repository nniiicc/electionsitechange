Sanford, FL – Navy Reserve Captain Bale Dalton has released his first TV ad, in the race to unseat Rep.
Cory Mills in Florida’s Seventh District.
The ad emphasizes Dalton’s service and leadership credentials, highlighting his experience fighting terrorists in Iraq and Afghanistan, and serving as NASA’s Chief of Staff.
The ad also underscores Dalton’s focus on cracking down on corruption and price gouging.
Watch Bale’s first TV ad here.
Dalton commented on this moment in his campaign, saying:
“This campaign is laser-focused on making Florida affordable again and tackling D.C. corruption – and that all starts with sending Congressman Mills packing.
I’ve spent my life serving this country, and Central Florida is my home.
We’ll keep working hard every single day to give this community a better option in November.”
Dalton has earned the support of prominent Florida leaders, including former Reps.
Val Demings and Stephanie Murphy, former U.S.
Senator Bill Nelson, Rep.
Kathy Castor, Orlando Mayor Buddy Dyer, State Sen.
LaVon Bracy Davis, State Reps.
RaShon Young and Rita Harris, Maitland Mayor John Lowndes, and Commissioner for the City of Winter Springs, Sarah Baker.
Dalton is the clear fundraising leader in the race, having out-raised Congressman Cory Mills by over a 4:1 margin since launching in November.
ABOUT BALE DALTON:
Bale Dalton is a decorated Navy Reserve Captain and former NASA Chief of Staff who has always answered the call to serve America.
One of four raised in a hardworking Central Florida family, Bale graduated from the U.S.
Naval Academy and has served in the U.S.
Navy and Navy Reserve for 24 years.
He deployed to Iraq, Afghanistan, the Horn of Africa, and the Pacific, flying Navy helicopters and directing air combat support with Navy SEALs in Afghanistan.
Following active duty, he worked for Senator Bill Nelson, helping Florida veterans access the care and benefits they had earned.
After an additional stint on active duty, Bale returned to government service as NASA Chief of Staff.
At NASA, he managed 16,500 employees and helped run one of the federal government’s most effective agencies.
After a lifetime of service, Bale is now running against Congressman Cory Mills in Florida’s 7th Congressional District.
Mills is under investigation by the House Ethics Committee for sexual misconduct, dating violence, and for improperly profiting from arms sales in government contracts.
In October, a Florida judge issued a restraining order against Cory Mills, to protect his ex-girlfriend from blackmail and intimidation.
