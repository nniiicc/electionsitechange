Chance Requa is a sixth generation farmer, coach, educator, and service-oriented leader running for Idaho House District 24B.
Meet Chance Requa: Farmer and Community Man Chance Requa is a sixth-generation Idahoan, farmer, educator, and coach who understands the values that make the Magic Valley strong.
Raised in a family rooted in agriculture, Chance has spent his life working the land and learning firsthand what it takes to support Idaho families.
For more than a decade, Chance has served in Idaho schools as a guest teacher and coach, working with students from all backgrounds.
Through that experience, he has seen the importance of strong schools, involved communities, and the role leadership plays in shaping the next generation.
Living and working in both agriculture and education, Chance has witnessed the real challenges facing Idaho families: rising costs, mental and behavioral health struggles, pressure on family farms, and concerns about water and resources.
Chance represents a new generation of leadership, focused on the future of Idaho and the opportunities ahead for our families and communities.
He brings a conservative vision rooted in limited government, personal responsibility, and strong family and community values, ensuring Idaho’s future is both prosperous and principled. ​ That’s why Chance is running. ​ He believes Idaho needs strong, practical leadership that will: Protect our agricultural heritage Strengthen local schools Support mental health resources Restore affordability for working families Keep our communities safe ​ Chance Requa is committed to standing up for Idaho values and ensuring future generations can live, work, and thrive right here at home.
