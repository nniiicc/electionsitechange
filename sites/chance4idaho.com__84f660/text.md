ABOUT CHANCE
Rooted in Idaho.
Committed to Our Future.
Chance Requa is a sixth-generation Idahoan, farmer, educator, and coach rooted in the values that make the Magic Valley strong.
With over a decade serving Idaho schools and a lifetime in agriculture, Chance understands the challenges facing working families, rural communities, and local farms.
He’s running to protect Idaho’s agricultural legacy, strengthen communities, and help build a stronger future for the next generation.
