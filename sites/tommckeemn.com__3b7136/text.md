Practical Solutions.
Public service is not a part-time hobby for Tom.
It is something he pours himself into every day.
He is constantly meeting with residents, taking phone calls, attending community events, building relationships with leaders across the region, and staying engaged with issues that affect our cities.
Corcoran City Council Member Jeremy Nichols Why I'm Running I'm Tom McKee - mayor, husband, father, and your neighbor.
For the past nearly six years I've worked to guide responsible growth, protect taxpayers and keep our community safe.
Now I'm running for State Representative to bring that same practical, results-driven leadership to St.
Paul.
Families across our district are feeling the pressure of rising costs, increasing uncertainty, and a growing, yet drastically underperforming, State government that has let fraud run rampant while wasting billions of taxpayer dollars.
We need leaders who focus on real solutions for our real issues - making life more affordable, ensuring our communities are safe, our schools are strong, and holding government accountable.
That's the approach I've taken as mayor, and it's the approach I'll bring to the Minnesota House of Representatives.
Together, we can keep our communities strong, affordable, and a great place to raise a family.
