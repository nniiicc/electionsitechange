Day 1 Agenda
On Day 1, I will sign onto the:
- Homes Act to build deeply affordable public housing, cooperatives, and community land trusts with rent caps and other protections for tenants
- Tenants Right to Organize Act so renters receiving housing vouchers are protected from landlord retaliation
- Protecting the Right to Organize (PRO) Act to build union density and give everyone who wants to form a union the fair chance to exercise that right
- Raise the Wage Act to raise the federal minimum wage from $7.25 to $15 an hour
- Green New Deal to transition to a 100% clean energy economy with high-wage union jobs and a livable world for everyone
- American Dream & Promise Act to provide a path to citizenship for millions of DREAMers and immigrants with Temporary Protective Status (TPS)
- Child Care for Every Community Act to create universal child care nationwide
- Social Security Expansion Act to increase benefits so our seniors live in dignity
- Prescription Drug Price Relief Act so corporations can’t charge Americans more for a drug in the U.S. than they would overseas
- Ultra-Millionaire Tax Act and Equal Tax Act so millionaires and billionaires pay their far share
- College for All Act to guarantee tuition-free community college for working class students
- Block the Bombs Act to ban the U.S. from sending weapons to Israel that are used to bomb Gaza
- Medicare for All Act so everyone has health insurance including dental, vision, and hearing aids
