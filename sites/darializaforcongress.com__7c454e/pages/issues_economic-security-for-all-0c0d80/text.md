Economic Security for All
As the cost of rent, groceries and utilities go up and up and up, the amount on our paychecks stay the same.
That’s because our leaders in Washington are bankrolled by the same billionaires and corporations raising our prices every day.
We need leaders who reject corporate greed and their lobbyists, and fight for working people across our district.
As your Congresswoman, Darializa will fight for workers to have power over their bosses.
Darializa will fight to:
BUILD UNION DENSITY
- Pass the PRO Act, so that everyone who wants a union can form one
- Make it easier for workers to secure a first collective bargaining agreement after forming their union
- End attacks on federal workers and their unions
- Fully fund the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) to hold union-busting bosses accountable
- Ensure that federal funding isn't going to union busting companies
KEEP MONEY IN YOUR POCKET
- Raise the minimum wage to $15 an hour
- Enshrine cost of living adjustments, paid sick and paid family leave in federal law
- Establish a universal basic income
- Fund social security and secure pensions for all
- Create a four-day 32-hour work week with no loss in pay
CREATE JOBS FOR ALL
- Establish federal jobs guarantee to ensure those able to work can find employment
- Issue a federal moratorium on AI data centers to ensure the technology benefits workers, not just tech oligarchs
