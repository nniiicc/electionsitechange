Babies, Not Bombs
For decades, both establishment Democrats and Republicans alike have used billions of our taxes to fund senseless wars abroad.
The U.S. sends more than $3 billion to Israel in military aid each year.
We spend $1 billion per day in the U.S. war in Iran.
Meanwhile, more than 100,000 children in New York City’s public schools are homeless.We must stop funding the bombing of children and families overseas, and start funding the programs here at home that can support our children reaching their full potential instead.
Darializa will fight to support our families with:
- Child tax credits ($800 per child)
- Baby boxes with a mattress, clothing, and other basics for newborns
- A federally-funded savings account for each child known as baby bonds
- Free breakfast and afterschool programs
- Paid family leave
