Medicare for All
Healthcare is a human right: access to care should not depend on one’s employment, age, or income.
The United States is the only country in the world that does not guarantee health care to everyone.
We spend more on healthcare than any other country.
As your Congresswoman, Darializa will fight for everyone – no matter their race, gender or sexuality – to receive high quality physical and mental healthcare without discrimination.
Darializa will fight for Medicare for All: free, universal healthcare system with primary care, mental health services, substance abuse treatment, dental, vision, and hearing aids.
Medicare for All also includes:
- Lower prescription costs
- Reproductive care, including abortion
- Long-term care for our seniors and people with disabilities
- Gender affirming care for everyone, especially LGBTQ+ communities
