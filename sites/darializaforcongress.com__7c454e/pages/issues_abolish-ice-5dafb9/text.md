Abolish ICE As the daughter of Dominican immigrants, Darializa has seen firsthand how unnecessarily chaotic and complicated our immigration system can be.
As a PhD student, she studies how this system criminalizes Afro-Latino and Black New Yorkers.
Congress has given ICE an unprecedented budget of $27.8 billion annually — larger than the entire national budget of many entire countries.
This agency exists to control our communities, not to keep us safe.
It was created in 2002: we can return to a world where it does not exist.
