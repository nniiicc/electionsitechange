Get in Touch About the Candidate 59th Legislative District State Representative Candidate Jordan R.
Omlor was born in Latrobe and graduated from Greater Latrobe Senior High School.
He immediately went on to attend Basic Training for the US Army and served 16 years in the Reserve and PA National Guard including deployment to Afghanistan and achieving the rank of Staff Sergeant.
In 2018 he started his own handyman business to help people repair their homes at affordable rates.
In 2020, he purchased an abandoned house in 1st Ward Latrobe to renovate.
It now serves as a home for a family which was being crushed by their previous rent payments.
At the height of the COVID outbreak, he volunteered for National Guard missions which supported nursing homes in Westmoreland and Allegheny counties.
In 2024-2025 Jordan served a year in the Ukrainian Armed Forces to defend the Western ideals of Freedom and to support Liberty and Justice FOR ALL.
The Platform Expand Property Tax Relief and Affordable Housing • Reduce taxes for fixed income individuals and families living below the poverty line, which will significantly improve the lives of our seniors. • Fight to eliminate unfunded mandates from the State which unfairly increase the tax burden of our homeowners. • Apply State funds to the construction of apartment buildings.
Enact a Plan to Reduce Healthcare Costs Jordan will work with representatives and experts to develop a proposal to the State to revise healthcare options, with an aim to provide universal healthcare for all.
Keep the Government Out of Your Private Life - and Pockets • Review the State budget and find inefficiencies. • Refuse lavish perks and benefits that waste taxpayer money. • Argue in favor of term limits for elected officials. • Protect your Constitutional rights regardless of race, gender, or belief, particularly women's rights.
Preserve Clean Air & Water and Promote Recycling and Sustainability • Work with the Pennsylvania Dept. of Environmental Protection to ensure that proper protocols are followed. • Support community cleanup programs. • Stop the unhindered expansion of AI data centers which do not provide the long-term jobs that were proposed and drain the communities' resources.
Support and Protect Small Business • Review Business Privilege Tax necessity. • Refute the new PA Dept of State Annual Report filing which began in 2025. • Work with farmers who grow and distribute locally to get them what they need to continue producing. • Propose tax credits up to $500,000 for small businesses so they can remain competitive against large corporations.
Sponsor Driver's License Availability for All Taxpayers and expand Public Transportation Where Needed. • Work with MILPA to sponsor House Bill 1518. • Bring public transportation to underserved communities.
The Promise I hereby promise that I will donate at least 50% of my salary as House Representative if elected, and that the monies will go to projects within the 59th Legislative District of Pennsylvania.
