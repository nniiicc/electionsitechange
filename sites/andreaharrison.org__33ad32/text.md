ABOUT
A trusted leader with deep roots in Prince George’s County, Delegate Andrea Harrison has delivered meaningful results for District 24—expanding economic opportunities, advocating for working families, and investing in safer, healthier communities.
As a member of the House Appropriations Committee and Vice-Chair of the Bi-County Committee, she has championed legislation to strengthen small businesses, protect workers, and support infrastructure development across the region.
With a proven track record on the Prince George’s County Council—including service as Chair and Vice-Chair—Delegate Harrison brings decades of experience, community commitment, and policy expertise to every issue she tackles in Annapolis.
