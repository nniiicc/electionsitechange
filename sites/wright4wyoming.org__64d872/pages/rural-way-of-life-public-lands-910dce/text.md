| This is Cam Wright.
One issue that I've heard brought up again and again is our public lands.
It's a key part of our traditions and the outdoor lifestyle that makes our state unique.
It also plays a key role in our economy.
Protecting this land is important for the well-being of all our families, and our future generations. |
| |
| |
| Public Lands |
| |
| As your Representative, I will support legislation that protects our public lands and ensures Wyomingites can continue to access the lands we have raised our families on.
I think it's crucial that decisions about these spaces are made close to home, not by politicians in D.C.
Those of us who live and work near these lands understand best how to be responsible stewards and conserve them. |
| |
| |
| Rural Way of Life |
| |
| Wyoming residents enjoy a lifestyle built on personal freedoms as guaranteed by the Constitution, and I am committed to keeping it that way.
I will protect Second Amendment rights, defend the privacy of individuals and families, and ensure that the government does not infringe upon the freedoms that define our state.
Wyoming has always been a place where we respect self-reliance and value individual liberty, and I will work to ensure that this remains true for Albany County. |

I am committed to working hard, listening carefully, and doing the right thing for District 46.
Thank you again for your consideration.
Cam Wright Candidate for Wyoming House District 46 |
