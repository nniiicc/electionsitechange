Cameron Wright Announces Endorsement from the Petroleum Association of Wyoming
Laramie, Wyoming — Cameron Wright, candidate for Wyoming House District 46, today announced the endorsement of the Petroleum Association of Wyoming (PAW), the statewide organization representing Wyoming’s oil and natural gas industry and the thousands of workers whose livelihoods depend on it.
Petroleum Association of Wyoming stated:
“On behalf of Wyoming’s 19,000 oil and gas workers, PAW is proud to endorse Cameron Wright’s campaign to represent the people of Albany County.
Cameron understands the importance of Wyoming’s energy industry to our economy, our communities, and our way of life.
He recognizes that responsible development of Wyoming’s natural resources supports jobs, schools, local governments, and families across our state.”
PAW’s endorsement reflects confidence in Wright’s commitment to practical, solutions-oriented leadership and his support for policies that strengthen Wyoming’s economy while preserving the state’s values and traditions.
Cameron Wright said:
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Petroleum Association of Wyoming.
Wyoming’s energy workers have helped build and sustain our communities for generations.
As a lifelong Republican, retired military officer, educator, and engineer, I understand the importance of balancing economic opportunity with responsible stewardship.
I will continue listening to the people of House District 46 and working to ensure Wyoming remains a place where families, businesses, and core industries can thrive.”
Wright emphasized that Wyoming’s future depends on thoughtful leadership that respects the contributions of the state’s energy workers while preparing for emerging opportunities.
“My commitment is simple: be available, accessible, and accountable to the people I serve.
I am grateful for PAW’s support and look forward to earning the trust and votes of the people of House District 46.”
About Cameron Wright
Cameron Wright is a retired U.S.
Air Force Lieutenant Colonel, retired University of Wyoming professor of electrical engineering, former Dean of the College of Engineering and Physical Sciences, and candidate for Wyoming House District 46.
His campaign is focused on service, constitutional principles, strong communities, quality education, and support for Wyoming’s core industries.
