“A new election season is front and center here in Wyoming, and frankly, this may be one of the most important elections that I have witnessed in my decades as a Wyoming voter.
When I think about the right stuff it takes to serve the people of Wyoming at all levels of government, I give weight to the character, the contributions to community, and the life experiences of a candidate.
Cam Wright has a history of character-centered leadership, service in military uniform, service to others in his community, and as a teacher of students and a leader of faculty and staff in the UW College of Engineering.
Cam has fully demonstrated that he has the right stuff to serve not only constituents in Wyoming HD 46 but all of Wyoming.
I am both honored and proud to endorse Cam Wright for the Wyoming House of Representatives.”
Former Wyoming State Auditor | Retired military officer | Retired utility executive | Chief Executive Officer of Wyoming Energy Futures, LLC
