Cam supports strong K–12 schools and school choice, career and technical education programs, and a strong University of Wyoming and community colleges that prepare students for the careers and industries that will shape Wyoming’s future.
Building an Economy Where Wyoming Kids Can Thrive Too many young people feel they must leave Wyoming to find opportunity.
Cam supports policies that attract new industries, support entrepreneurs, and create high-quality jobs so Wyoming families and graduates can build their futures here at home.
Protecting Wyoming's Public Lands Wyoming’s public lands are central to our heritage, recreation, and economy.
Cam believes these lands must remain accessible for responsible use while being carefully stewarded for future generations.
Strengthening Wyoming's Core Industries Wyoming’s economy is built on hard work and innovation.
Cam supports advancing the industries that power our state — including energy, agriculture, mining, tourism, and small businesses — while encouraging new opportunities that diversify and strengthen Wyoming’s economy.
Defending Constitutional Freedoms Cam is a lifelong supporter of the Second Amendment and private property rights and believes Wyoming citizens should always retain the freedoms that define our state.
