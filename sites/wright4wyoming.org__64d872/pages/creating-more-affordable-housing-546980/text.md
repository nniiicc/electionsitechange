Housing Affordability Finding an affordable home has become more difficult for many Wyoming families, young professionals, and first-time homebuyers.
We need practical solutions that make it easier for people to live, work, and build their future here without sacrificing the character of our communities.
Addressing housing affordability starts with creating an economy where Wyoming families can thrive.
Too many young people feel they must leave the state to find opportunity.
By supporting policies that attract new industries, encourage entrepreneurship, and create high-quality jobs, we can help more Wyoming graduates and families put down roots here at home.
We also need to remove unnecessary barriers that make it harder to build housing while respecting local communities and private property rights.
Expanding housing options through responsible growth will help keep Wyoming an affordable place to live for current residents and future generations.
Affordable housing is about ensuring Wyoming remains a place where families can succeed, businesses can grow, and young people can see a future for themselves.
I will work to support practical housing solutions, strengthen Wyoming's economy, and help create more opportunities for families across House District 46.
I would be honored to earn your vote on August 18th.
