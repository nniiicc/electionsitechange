Happy Independence Day!
The United States of America has provided opportunities for countless individuals, and celebrating our nation means celebrating the freedoms and values that make America a global leader.
None of the freedoms we enjoy would be available without the brave men and women who sacrifice so much for our country.
Having spent my career serving in the armed forces, celebrating American independence means a lot to me.
Young men and women continually protect and defend this nation because they see it as something worth defending.
No other country in the world provides its citizens with the freedoms that we enjoy here in America.
We are a nation that values individuals and believes that everyone can work for and achieve their dreams.
Citizens have access to an education, the ability to speak their minds, and the freedom to worship how they choose.
People are free to pursue their passions and collaborate freely.
Today, and every day, I am grateful to have served this nation and raise my family in the United States.
To celebrate 250 years of American independence and Wyoming's statehood, the city of Laramie is hosting the following events: Freedom Has A Birthday Laramie Jubilee Days Come gather with us as we honor what makes this nation great!
I would be honored to earn your vote and support.
I am committed to working hard, listening carefully, and doing the right thing for District 46.
Thank you again for your consideration.
