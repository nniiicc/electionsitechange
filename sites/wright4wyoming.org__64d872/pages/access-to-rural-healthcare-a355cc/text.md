Rural Healthcare
| |
| Access to quality, affordable healthcare is one of the biggest challenges facing many Wyoming families.
Whether it's finding a primary care provider close to home, accessing specialized care, or paying for medical expenses, too many people face unnecessary obstacles to getting the care they need.
In rural Wyoming, protecting access to healthcare means supporting the hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals who serve our communities.
We need practical policies that help recruit and retain doctors, nurses, and other providers so families can receive quality care without traveling long distances.
We also need to address the rising cost of healthcare.
Families should not have to choose between paying for medical care and meeting other household expenses.
By encouraging competition, reducing unnecessary regulations, and supporting policies that improve affordability, we can help make healthcare more accessible without sacrificing quality. |
