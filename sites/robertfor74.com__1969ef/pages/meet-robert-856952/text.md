DONATE VOLUNTEER Log In ABOUT ME Hi, I'm Robert WHERE I COME FROM I am a proud native of Georgia, being born in Atlanta.
I was raised in Decatur, GA and I attended school in both Dekalb county and Atlanta Public Schools.
I graduated high school from Walter F.
George and I received my degree in Business from Georgia State University.
A THRIVING CAREER I have been fortunate to have been a part of outstanding organizations and companies throughout my career.
As a student in college, I interned with the Inroads Atlanta, gaining valuable experience at Publix supermarkets.
Upon graduating from college, I received full time employment from Publix where I obtained the position of Asst.
Common Area Manager.
Following my time at Publix, I transitioned into the legal/real estate industry as a title examiner.
I began at RTCS Title services, and later spent several years at Smith, Welch, Webb and White Attorneys at Law as well as other companies.
I later joined Fulton County as a property appraiser, further expanding my expertise, where I worked until 2025.
In addition, I have also been a small business owner of Flournoy Consulting Partners, LLC, providing consulting services and title services for numerous clients. ​ ​ A PASSION TO SERVE As a passionate individual who values serving others, I have had the privilege of holding various leadership positions throughout my college years.
One of my most notable roles was serving as the Chair of the Student Advisory Council to the GA Board of Regents.
Additionally, I have also served as the Assistant Pastor of United In Faith Christian Ministries in Jackson, GA.
These experiences have allowed me to develop my leadership skills and have instilled in me a deep sense of commitment to serving others.
FAMILY FOCUSED I have been married to my wife Jamilia since 2023.
I am a proud father of a daughter and 3 bonus children.
I have one grandchild and am happy to call many others family and friends.
I value the support of the 74th district and I am happy to refer each of you as a part of my family!
MISSION STATEMENT Let's Make Change Together I am honored and excited to have the opportunity to serve as a GA State Representative for my district!
My ultimate goal is to bring about positive change and make a meaningful impact on the lives of the people I represent.
To achieve this, I am fully committed to championing policies that promote educational reform and support our public schools, protect the rights of women and reproductive rights, social justice, economic opportunity, and environmental sustainability.
I believe that transparency, accountability, effective communication, visibility and hard work are the keys to success, and I am confident that by working together, we can make our community an even better place to, work, and thrive! ​Support State Rep.Robert Flournoy Jr.
