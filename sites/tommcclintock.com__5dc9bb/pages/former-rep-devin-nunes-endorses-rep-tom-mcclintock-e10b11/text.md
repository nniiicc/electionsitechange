El Dorado Hills, CA – In an interview with Washington Examiner, retired Rep.
Devin Nunes, 22nd Congressional District, endorsed Rep.
Tom McClintock for the 5th Congressional District.
Rep.
Nunes said, “He has my complete support.”
“Tom has been a champion for the conservative movement for many years,” said Rep.
Nunes.
The newly drawn 5th Congressional District encompasses El Dorado, Amador, Calaveras, Tuolumne, Mariposa, Stanislaus, Madera, and Fresno counties.
Rep.
McClintock has represented all the counties except Stanislaus County in the current 4th Congressional District.
Rep.
Tom McClintock said, “I am honored to have the endorsement from my friend Devin Nunes.
His support for my re-election campaign underscores my commitment to our founding Constitutional principles.”
Rep.
Devin Nunes joins Fresno City Councilmember Garry Bredefeld, former Sen.
Andy Vidak, the Fresno County Republican Party, Madera County Republican Party, Calaveras County Republican Party, Tuolumne County Republican Party, Stanislaus County Republican Party, and the Republican County Chairs in the 5th Congressional District in endorsing Rep.
Tom McClintock.
