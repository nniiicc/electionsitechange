El Dorado Hills, CA – Former Rep.
Jeff Denham endorsed Rep.
Tom McClintock for the 5th Congressional District.
Rep.
Denham said, “Tom has my enthusiastic support for re-election.
I know from experience that there are few folks like him in Washington who stand up for their districts and for principle—even if their own party doesn’t like it.
Tom is one of them, and he always joins me in the fight for guaranteed water deliveries to our farms and communities throughout the Central Valley.
We need Tom back—not only to fight for more water—but for the soul of this country, which is rapidly vanishing under Biden’s leftist regime.”
Rep.
Jeff Denham represented Stanislaus County in Congress from 2010-2019 and in the State Senate from 2002-2010.
The newly drawn 5th Congressional District encompasses El Dorado, Amador, Calaveras, Tuolumne, Mariposa, Stanislaus, Madera, and Fresno counties.
Rep.
McClintock has represented all the counties except Stanislaus County in the current 4th Congressional District.
