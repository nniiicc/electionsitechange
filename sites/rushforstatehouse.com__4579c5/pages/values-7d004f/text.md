Values and goals
I have dedicated my life to improving our community through my work, volunteering, and political service.
I am committed to continue to represent our district's conservative values and to make our community the absolutely best it can be for all of us.
Some ways I plan on accomplishing these values are to:
- Keep taxes low and government small
- Vote pro-life
- Support our Second Amendment rights
- Back a climate friendly to small business and high-paying local jobs
- Advocate for great local schools and career training
- Support free market solutions in health care that increase competition and lower costs
- Protect our robust and healthy system of parks, trails and recreation areas
- Seek increased funding from the state for local road and bridges
If these are issues and values that are important to you and your family, I would appreciate your support, your donation, and your vote on November 8th!
