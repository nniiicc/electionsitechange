Misti Cordell isn’t a career politician - she’s a wife, mother, business owner, and proven leader who brings real-world experience to the table.
From running a successful healthcare consulting firm to advocating for rural communities and independent physicians, Misti understands the challenges families face every day - from rising costs to access to quality education and care.
With a strong background in public service, including leadership as Chair of the Louisiana Board of Regents and roles within Governor Landry’s administration, Misti has been at the forefront of driving meaningful change.
Rooted in faith, family, and a legacy of service, she is committed to fighting for hardworking families, strengthening communities, and delivering results that matter.
