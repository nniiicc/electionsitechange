Key Issues
Fighting for District 92.
I’m focused on keeping costs low, strengthening our infrastructure and public safety, and protecting the Lowcountry.
Lowering Costs for Families
KEEPING MORE OF YOUR MONEY IN YOUR POCKET.
South Carolina should remain a place where families can afford to live, work, and build a future.
- Continue cutting taxes and help families keep more of what they earn.
- Keep the tax burden low as families face rising costs.
- Put taxpayers first with responsible state spending.
Infrastructure That Works
KEEPING PACE WITH A GROWING LOWCOUNTRY.
As Berkeley County grows, our roads and infrastructure need to keep pace.
- Invest in roads and bridges our families rely on every day.
- Keep infrastructure ahead of growth in our communities.
- Prioritize transportation improvements that make a real difference for residents.
Keeping Our Communities Safe
BACKING THOSE WHO PROTECT US.
Safe neighborhoods and schools start with supporting those who protect them.
- Back law enforcement and first responders with the resources they need.
- Support SROs and strong school safety measures.
- Address violent youth offenses and strengthen public safety.
Standing With Our Veterans
KEEPING OUR PROMISE TO THOSE WHO SERVED.
As a 16-year Air Force veteran, I understand the commitment and sacrifice of those who serve.
- Protect veterans’ benefits and the promises made to those who served.
- Support military families across South Carolina.
- Stand up for veterans at the State House.
Preserving the Lowcountry
PROTECTING THE PLACE WE CALL HOME.
Growth should not come at the expense of the character and natural beauty that make the Lowcountry special.
- Protect local decision-making so communities have a voice in their future.
- Preserve green space and natural resources as our area grows.
- Support responsible growth while protecting the character of the Lowcountry.
