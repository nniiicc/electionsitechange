We all have personal beliefs and values that shape our perspectives, and I am no exception.
I am firmly pro-life, support school choice, and believe in protecting our Second Amendment rights.
But having principles does not mean being unwilling to consider other perspectives.
I believe it is my responsibility to examine the facts, listen to all sides, challenge my own assumptions, and understand the potential consequences of every decision.
My goal is not to approach every issue without convictions, but to approach every issue with an open mind, informed judgment, and a commitment to serving the people I represent.
