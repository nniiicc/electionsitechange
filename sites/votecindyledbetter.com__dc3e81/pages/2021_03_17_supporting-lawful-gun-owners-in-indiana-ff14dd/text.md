The Second Amendment declares Americans have the right to keep and bear arms, which many Hoosiers exercise daily.
To strengthen this right, I am supporting legislation, House Bill 1369, this session that would allow lawful Hoosiers to carry a handgun without having to get a government-issued permit.
This legislation would not change the requirements to purchase a handgun.
Hoosier firearm buyers would still have to complete the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives form, and be approved by the National Instant Criminal Background Check System.
