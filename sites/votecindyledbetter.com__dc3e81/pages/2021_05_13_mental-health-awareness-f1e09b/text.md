We also are investing an additional $100 million over the biennium for other mental health initiatives at local and state levels.
New laws open up communication channels between first responders and mental health professionals and hospitals, and establish community-based partnerships.
Public safety personnel will be able to access patient care information, like an individual’s mental health safety plan.
This will not only help them better serve our communities, but also get more Hoosiers the care they need.
