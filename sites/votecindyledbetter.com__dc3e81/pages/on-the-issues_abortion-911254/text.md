Abortion is a human rights issue of our time.
Abortion is a moral issue that has been made political.
The politics of abortion has taken away the dignity of a woman by making a statement that the capacity for motherhood is a liability.
Abortion, in political terms, is not healthcare.
Contraception (prevention of pregnancy) is healthcare.
There are situations in pregnancy that may require the direction of a medical professional, but in most instances women facing unwanted pregnancy have the option of adoption, and how selfless and empowering is it to provide an opportunity for another women who may not have the ability to conceive to be a mother.
