Indiana’s 2024–2025 budget funded public safety operations while making targeted investments in state police compensation, firefighter training, emergency equipment, forensic investigations, and correctional services.
Major public safety investments $# million — Indiana State Police: Funded operations, equipment, personnel, and law enforcement services over two years.
Over 30% Average Trooper Pay Increase: Increased compensation for sworn Indiana State Police officers, with starting salaries reaching $70,000.
State Police Retention: Shortened the salary progression schedule from 20 years to 15 years, allowing troopers to reach full compensation sooner. $7.7 million — Firefighter Training Facilities: Supported construction of statewide fire training facilities.
Volunteer Firefighter Equipment: Increased funding for the Regional Public Safety Training Fund to purchase critical firefighting equipment. $# million — Internet Crimes Against Children: Funded investigations and efforts to combat online child exploitation over the biennium.
Approximately $29.8 million — Forensic Laboratories: Supported state forensic and health sciences laboratories over two years. $# billion — Corrections: Funded correctional operations in FY 2024–2025.
School Safety: Continued funding for school safety programs, including grants supporting security improvements.
