FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Friday, December 13, 2019 Contact: Jon Conradi Phone: 513-202-4023 Congressman Dave Joyce Files for Reelection in 2020 Cites Leadership & Record of Results on Great Lakes, Opioid Crisis, Economy BAINBRIDGE TOWNSHIP – Representative Dave Joyce (R-OH-14) filed for reelection in 2020 with the Lake County Board of Elections on Friday, pledging to continue to focus on delivering results for hardworking Ohio families.
“Whether leading to preserve and protect the Great Lakes, fighting to eliminate the scourge of the opioid epidemic, or working to create jobs, lift wages and lower taxes for middle-class families, I’m proud of my record of results for northeast Ohio,” Joyce said.
“It is an incredible honor to represent Ohio’s 14th district and I humbly ask the voters for the opportunity to continue delivering results for hardworking Ohio families in Congress.” Joyce, who served for 25 years as the prosecuting attorney for Geauga County, is a proven leader who delivers result for Ohio families.
Joyce has been a leader in the preservation and protection of the Great Lakes and authored landmark bipartisan legislation to restore the ecosystem and economic health of the Great Lakes area.
In 2016, Congress passed into law the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative Act of 2016 sponsored by Joyce.
Joyce has since led the fight to keep the GLRI funded and also worked to combat threats to the health of the lakes, including from Asian carp and harmful algal blooms.
Joyce has worked tirelessly to combat the opioid epidemic and helped pass more than 70 bills to support treatment, recovery, education and law enforcement.
Joyce also supported and helped pass comprehensive tax reform that delivered savings for middle-class families, helped create Ohio jobs and lift wages.
Joyce’s campaign will enter 2020 in a strong position to win reelection with over one million dollars cash-on-hand.
Joyce was reelected in 2018 with more than 55 percent of the vote.
