FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Tuesday, January 14, 2020 Contact: Jon Conradi Phone: 513-202-4023 Congressman Dave Joyce Starts 2020 with $1.3 Million War Chest Campaign Raises More than $400,000 in Fourth Quarter BAINBRIDGE TOWNSHIP – Friends of Dave Joyce announced Tuesday it raised nearly $410,000 in the fourth quarter of 2019 and closed the fundraising period with a war chest of nearly $1.3 million cash on hand.
“I am overwhelmed and incredibly grateful for the outpouring of support I’ve received as I seek reelection to continue delivering results for Ohio families in Congress,” Rep.
Dave Joyce (R-OH-14) said.
“Our campaign starts 2020 well prepared to execute a robust and successful effort that puts focus on the issues that matter to middle-class families in northeast Ohio.” More information on Joyce and his campaign can be found HERE.
