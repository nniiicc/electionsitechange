FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Date: September 17, 2020 Contact: Press@joyceforcongress.com Dave Joyce for Congress Releases First TV Ad Joyce Highlights Endorsement from Lake County Sheriff Amid National Calls to Defund Police PAINESVILLE – Today Dave Joyce for Congress released the campaign’s first TV ad, highlighting the endorsement of Lake County Sheriff Frank Leonbruno and Joyce’s strong record of supporting law enforcement.
“Now more than ever we need leaders we can depend on to have law enforcements’ back,” ​said Sheriff Leonbruno​.
“Dave Joyce is a former prosecutor who understands what it takes to keep our communities safe.
I’m proud to support Dave’s reelection to Congress as he rejects radical demands to defund the police and continues his work with our law enforcement community to protect Northeast Ohio families.” Watch “Protect” ​Here​: The ad will appear on Cleveland broadcast and cable, reaching voters across Ohio’s 14​th​ Congressional district.
A transcript from the ad can be found below.
Dave Joyce spent more than 25 years as the prosecuting attorney of Geauga County helping to make the county one of the best places to raise a family.
As a representative for Ohio’s 14​th​ Congressional district, Dave has voted to increase resources to combat the opioid epidemic and recently introduced ​legislation​ signed into law by President Trump that provides support for first responders and public safety officers exposed to COVID-19.
“Protect” I’ve been in law enforcement protecting our communities for more than thirty years.
That’s why I support Congressman Dave Joyce.
Dave Joyce is a former prosecutor who understands what it takes to keep our communities safe.
When radical voices try to defund the police and turn to looting and chaos, we can count on Dave Joyce to defend law enforcement and keep our communities safe.
