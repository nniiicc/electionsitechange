For Immediate Release Date: Friday, July 10th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Poll Shows Christine Drazan Leads Tina Kotek by 4 Points WILSONVILLE, Ore. – A new poll conducted by Public Opinion Strategies shows gubernatorial candidate Christine Drazan leading incumbent Governor Tina Kotek by four points, reflecting an electorate increasingly dissatisfied with the direction of Oregon after four years under the Kotek administration.
“People are frustrated by the rising cost of living, the lack of affordable housing, worsening homelessness and a governor who keeps making life more expensive instead of fixing problems.
Every day, more Oregonians are looking to Christine Drazan as the leader who can turn things around,” said Trey Rosser, campaign strategist for Drazan.
The poll found Drazan leading Kotek 48% to 44%, with 6% of voters undecided.
It also found: ●​ 66% of respondents want to see a great deal of change in the direction Oregon's next governor takes the state. ●​ 57% disapprove of the job Tina Kotek is doing as governor. ●​ 59% believe Oregon is on the wrong track.
