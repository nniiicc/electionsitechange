Media Advisory Date: Friday, July 3rd, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Pear Growers Offered "$0 Per Bin" to Speak Out Alongside Senator Christine Drazan on Catastrophic Industry Crisis PARKDALE, Ore. – On Monday, July 6th, Oregon pear growers will join Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) for a press conference to discuss the unprecedented financial crisis threatening one of Oregon's signature agricultural industries.
Following a devastating 2025 crop year, some growers have been told they will receive as little as $0 per bin for certain pear varieties.
Growers will share how the crisis is affecting their farms, their families and the future of Oregon agriculture, while Senator Drazan will discuss her recent call for immediate state action and next steps to help the industry survive.
Event Details: What: Press conference Who: Sen.
Christine Drazan (R-Canby) and three Columbia Gorge pear growers When: Monday, July 6th, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.
Where: A pear orchard in Parkdale, OR (approximately 20 minutes south of Hood River).
