For Immediate Release Date: Thursday, August 20th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Launches Second TV Ad of the General Election WILSONVILLE, Ore. – Christine Drazan's campaign for governor today announced the launch of its second television advertisement of the general election, which is now airing statewide on broadcast television and digital platforms.
“I grew up watching my parents struggle to find stability as politicians stood by and watched mills close and good jobs disappear.
Today, Tina Kotek is putting more Oregon families through that same experience with policies that are killing jobs and leaving people behind,” said Drazan.
“I’m running for Governor with a plan to turn that around, fight for Oregonians and deliver the results they deserve.” The ad draws a comparison between the politicians who stood by as mills shut down and jobs went away when Drazan was growing up and Tina Kotek’s leadership today, as businesses leave the state and Oregonians struggle to find the stability and opportunity they deserve.
The new advertisement can be viewed here: https://youtu.be/GGeK5UD5Fuc The transcript: “When I was young, we moved more than a dozen times just trying to get our feet underneath us.
The politicians sat by and watched while the mills closed and jobs disappeared.
Today, it’s Tina Kotek’s policies that are killing jobs and leaving kids and families like yours and mine behind.
My plan changes all that.
I’m Christine Drazan.
I’m running for Governor to fight for you and your family because you deserve results.” ###
