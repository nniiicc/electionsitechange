For Immediate Release Date: Wednesday, July 29th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Accepts Two More Debates; Tina Kotek Has Accepted None CANBY, Ore. – Gubernatorial candidate Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) has accepted invitations to participate in two additional debates in Eugene and Medford, while Tina Kotek has yet to publicly accept a single debate invitation.
The newly accepted debates include: ●​ Oregon Journalism Project, University of Oregon School of Journalism and Communications, KEZI (Eugene, October) ●​ KOBI (Medford, October) “I believe Oregon can be the best place in the nation to raise a family, start a business, buy a home and receive a world-class education.
Oregonians deserve better than what they're getting today, and these debates are an opportunity to show them they don't have to settle,” said Drazan.
To date, Drazan has accepted invitations to five debates and one candidate forum: ●​ Oregon Newspaper Publishers Association (Salem, July (Drazan attended after Kotek declined the invitation)) ●​ Oregon Business & Industry Candidate Forum (Portland, September) ●​ KGW/The Oregonian (Portland, October) ●​ KPTV/ODAA/OSSA/OACP (Portland, October) ###
