For Immediate Release Date: Tuesday, August 25th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Lays Out Her Vision for Oregon in Downtown Portland PORTLAND, Ore. – In the heart of Portland, gubernatorial candidate Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) today announced The Drazan Plan, her commitment to an Oregon that is more affordable, more accountable and filled with ambition, energy and opportunity again.
“It’s a plan to make Oregon the best place to raise a family, start and grow a business, buy a home, learn a trade and receive a world-class education.
And most importantly, it is a plan built on a simple belief that Oregon's best days are still ahead,” said Drazan.
View The Drazan Plan here: https://thedrazanplan.com.
