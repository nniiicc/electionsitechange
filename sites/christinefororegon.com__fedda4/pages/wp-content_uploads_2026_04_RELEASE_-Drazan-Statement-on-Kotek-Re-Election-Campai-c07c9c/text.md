For Immediate Release Date: Thursday, December 4th, 2025 Contact: Sam@christinefororegon.com DRAZAN STATEMENT ON KOTEK’S RE-ELECTION CAMPAIGN ANNOUNCEMENT “ALL TALK AND NO RESULTS.
THIS IS OREGON’S TIME FOR CHOOSING.” CANBY, Ore. – Today, Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) released the following statement in response to Governor Tina Kotek’s re-election campaign announcement: “Tina is all talk and no results.
She wants Oregonians to believe this race is about Washington, D.C., when it’s really about our home.
This is Oregon’s time of choosing.
This is our chance to choose a brighter future for our families — not the broken promises and stalled progress of her time in office.
We can’t keep doing the same thing and expect different results.
Our state has everything it needs to be the best place in America to raise a family, build a home, start a business and receive a world-class education.
But we won’t get there with Tina Kotek — we need new leadership." Drazan is campaigning on cleaning up Kotek’s messes here at home including lowering costs and unleashing prosperity, repealing tent cities and reuniting families, making homeownership affordable, restoring academic excellence and high standards, and holding criminals accountable.
Christine Drazan and her husband, Dan, live in Canby where they raised their three children.
Christine formerly served as Oregon House Republican Leader and now represents the people of Senate District 26 as their State Senator.
