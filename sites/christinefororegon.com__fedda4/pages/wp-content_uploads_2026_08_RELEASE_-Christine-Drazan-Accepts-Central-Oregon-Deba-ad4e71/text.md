For Immediate Release Date: Wednesday, August 12th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Accepts Central Oregon Debate Invitation CANBY, Ore. – Today, Gubernatorial candidate Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) accepted KTVZ's invitation to participate in a gubernatorial debate in Bend on October 13th.
“I appreciate KTVZ for hosting this debate and providing another opportunity to discuss the future of our state with Oregonians.
This campaign is about bringing together people from every community and every political background who want change,” said Drazan.
“I’m excited to share my vision for Oregon where families can afford to live, students are prepared for success, businesses have the confidence to invest and create jobs and government is accountable to the people it serves.” To date, Drazan has accepted invitations to six debates and one candidate forum: ●​ Oregon Newspaper Publishers Association (Salem, July (Drazan attended after Kotek declined the invitation)) ●​ Oregon Business & Industry Candidate Forum (Portland, September) ●​ KPTV/ODAA/OSSA/OACP (Portland, September) ●​ Oregon Journalism Project, University of Oregon School of Journalism and Communications, KVAL (Eugene, October) ●​ KOBI (Medford, October) ●​ KGW/The Oregonian (Portland, October) ###
