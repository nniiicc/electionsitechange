Christine Drazan is running for Governor because she wants Oregon to be a place where families and working people can afford to put down roots, find a good-paying job, get a high-quality education, and know they are safe in their neighborhood.
Growing up in Southern Oregon, Christine learned the value of a hard day’s work and of family and community.
When the government and extremists crippled the timber industry, her family moved often in search of work and stability.
Before graduating from high school and heading off to college, Christine had lived in 15 different homes.
Those experiences taught her how decisions made in Salem can hurt people who just want to build a better life.
Christine believes government should focus on clearing the path to opportunity and bettering people’s lives – not making things harder.
After graduating from college, Christine began her career in the Oregon Legislature, working on policies to lift up everyday Oregonians.
She quickly rose up, becoming Chief of Staff to the Speaker of the House.
She later worked alongside Oregon's small businesses, advocating for the local employers who support families and communities across the state.
As a mom of three, wife, and active community volunteer, Christine has always believed public service extends beyond elected office.
Christine volunteered in her local schools, served on the PTA, and participated on the local planning commission and budget committee because she believes strong communities are built by neighbors who step forward when they’re needed.
She has spent years supporting parents, advocating for students, and investing in opportunities that help young people succeed.
When Christine was elected to the Oregon House of Representatives, she fought to cut red tape, lower taxes, bring more accountability to state government, strengthen classrooms, and improve public safety.
As House Republican Leader, Christine earned a reputation for focusing on commonsense solutions rather than partisan politics.
As State Senator, Christine has worked with Republicans, Democrats, and Independents to get results for the people of Oregon.
Today, Christine is running for Governor because she sees too many Oregonians facing the challenges her family faced growing up: Rising costs, economic uncertainty, and a sense that our leaders have lost sight of the people they were elected to serve.
Christine will never forget that she is here to improve the lives of Oregonians and be accountable to you.
Christine and her husband Dan live in rural Clackamas County with their sheep, chickens, rescue dog, and ancient cat.
Their three children are now young adults chasing their own dreams in Oregon, which drives Christine’s relentless commitment to a future where every individual and family has the opportunity to succeed.
