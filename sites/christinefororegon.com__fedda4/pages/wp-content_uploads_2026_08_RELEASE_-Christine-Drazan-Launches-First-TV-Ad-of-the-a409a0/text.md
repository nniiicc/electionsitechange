For Immediate Release Date: Thursday, August 6th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Launches First TV Ad of the General Election WILSONVILLE, Ore. – Christine Drazan's campaign for governor today announced the launch of its first television advertisement of the general election, which will begin airing statewide on TV and digital platforms.
“Oregon has everything it takes to be the best place in America to live, work and raise a family, but we need leaders who are focused on solving problems for Oregonians,” said Drazan.
“I’m running for Governor because it’s time to hold government accountable to the people of Oregon and deliver the results they deserve.” The ad points to the state’s worsening homelessness crisis, struggling schools, a lack of accountability in state government and businesses leaving the state as evidence that Oregon's challenges are the result of failed leadership from the Governor's Office.
The new advertisement can be viewed here: https://youtu.be/VcqI0mH-SqE The transcript: “In Oregon we pay more taxes than every other state but one – but where’s the money gone?
Zero accountability.
Zero results.
The Governor loves to talk about all the money she’s spending – but what do we have to show for it?
Businesses are leaving, homelessness has gotten worse and education is absolutely failing our kids.
I’m Christine Drazan.
Our problems are not partisan problems – they’re leadership problems.
I’m running for Governor to hold government accountable to you.” ###
