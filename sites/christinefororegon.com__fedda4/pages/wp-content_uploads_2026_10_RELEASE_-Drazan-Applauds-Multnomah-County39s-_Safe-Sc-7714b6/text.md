For Immediate Release Date: Thursday, June 25th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com DRAZAN APPLAUDS MULTNOMAH COUNTY’S “SAFE SCHOOL ZONE” ORDINANCE Portland, Ore. – Today, the Multnomah County Board of Commissioners voted unanimously to pass an ordinance prohibiting needle exchange programs from distributing free needles and other drug paraphernalia within 1,000 feet of a school.
Sen.
Christine Drazan (R-Canby), candidate for governor, applauded the ordinance as a first step to keep kids safe: “We shouldn’t place the burdens of addiction on kids trying to walk to school.
This ordinance shows what clear persistent community engagement can produce.
Congratulations to Stadiumhood and Friends of Couch Park for their determination.
“There is more work to do to protect students and communities across our state.
The legislature must take necessary steps to increase accountability and end public funding of programs that enable addiction and harm our communities.” During a May 7, 2026 Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Public Hearing, Senator Drazan testified in support of the ordinance creating the 1,000 foot buffer zone, applauding it as a first step and noting the lack of enforcement.
During the 2025 and 2026 Legislative Sessions, Drazan worked with the Stadiumhood neighborhood and introduced HB 3956 (2025) and Senate Bill 1573 (2026) which would prohibit the distribution of needles and other drug paraphernalia within 1,000 feet (HB 3956) and 2,000 feet (SB 1573) of a school or child care facility, statewide.
