For Immediate Release Date: Friday, July 24th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Responds to IP 28 Failing to Qualify for the Ballot CANBY, Ore. – After the Oregon Secretary of State announced today that Initiative Petition 28 (IP 28) failed to gather the number of certified signatures needed to qualify for the November 2026 ballot, gubernatorial candidate Senator Christine Drazan (R-Canby) released the following statement: “The people behind IP 28 will be back, and so will the next extremists with another extreme idea.
Oregon has paid the price for bad ballot measures before, and we can't afford to make that mistake again.
Rather than waiting for them to come after Oregon families, communities and businesses again, it’s time we fight back.” ###
