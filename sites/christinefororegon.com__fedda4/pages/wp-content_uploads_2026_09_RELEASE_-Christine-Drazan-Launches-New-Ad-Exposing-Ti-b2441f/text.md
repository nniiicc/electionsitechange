For Immediate Release Date: Friday, September 4th, 2026 Contact: Press@christinefororegon.com Christine Drazan Launches New Ad Exposing Tina Kotek’s Record of Tax Breaks for Data Centers and Tax Hikes for Everyone Else WILSONVILLE, Ore. – Christine Drazan’s campaign for governor yesterday announced the launch of a new television ad highlighting Tina Kotek’s yearslong history of giving special tax breaks to data centers while repeatedly raising taxes on Oregonians.
“For more than a decade, Tina Kotek has supported tax giveaways that helped fuel Oregon’s massive growth in data centers while continuously raising taxes on everyday Oregonians.
Oregon has done more than its fair share, and we cannot continue to be the Wild West for data center development,” said Drazan.
Kotek’s Record Giving Tax Breaks to Data Centers: ●​ 2012: Kotek voted for legislation which created tax breaks for data centers. ●​ 2015: Kotek again voted for legislation extending tax breaks to data centers leading to more data centers in Oregon. ●​ 2023-2026: Policies Kotek previously supported fueled a massive surge in data center development during her tenure as governor.
Some of the world’s largest technology companies expanded their footprint in Oregon and secured hundreds of millions of dollars in tax breaks. ●​ Oregon now has 142 data centers built or planned. ●​ Oregon’s data centers now occupy 2,900 acres of land and use 11% of the state's electricity. ●​ Oregon’s data center industry now ranks third largest in the nation.
Meanwhile, Kotek imposed billions of dollars in higher taxes that has made Oregon more expensive for everyone else.
