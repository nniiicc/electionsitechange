Oregon’s Best Days Are Ahead
Oregon is an incredible state full of hardworking people, strong communities, and limitless potential.
We should be the best place in the nation to raise a family, start a business, buy a home, and receive a world-class education.
But for too many Oregonians, that promise feels out of reach.
The cost of living keeps climbing.
Families are struggling to afford a home.
Employers are choosing to invest and create jobs elsewhere.
Far too many people remain trapped in cycles of addiction, untreated mental illness, and chronic homelessness.
Our schools are failing to prepare our kids for success.
And despite record government spending and high taxes, Oregonians are still asking a simple question: Where has the money gone?
These are not partisan problems.
They are leadership problems.
Oregon can do better.
As Governor, Christine will work to lower the cost of living, make Oregon open for business again, build more homes people can afford, break the cycle of homelessness, strengthen our schools, and bring accountability back to state government.
Christine Drazan Will Deliver.
