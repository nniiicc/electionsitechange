Introducing Rep Carris Duncan
I’m honored that you have chosen to have me represent you in Montpelier.
During the course of this election I have had the pleasure of learning that our concerns about housing, education, taxation, and general cost of living go far beyond party.
I believe that this offers us the opportunity to heal our divisions.
I look forward to working with and for everyone in the community, including those who voted for my opponent, as we chart the best path forward.
I am grateful that you have welcomed me into your homes and businesses to share your worries, hopes, and visions for this vibrant community.
Thank you for putting your trust in me to help bring this vision for a resilient, flourishing region to life.
We are in a pivotal time and it means using our best creative ideas to build for a resilient future.
In Montpelier I will work hard to find ways to fund more small safe housing units and increase our weatherization efforts to bring down the overall energy costs of our housing and to create education solutions that use our tax dollars to fully serve our community’s health and education needs.
I certainly couldn’t have done this alone.
I had fantastic support behind me.
