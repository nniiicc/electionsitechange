Art in the Valley is a distinctive three-day, juried, indoor fine art and craft exhibition in southern Vermont.
All booth fees will benefit the Old School Community Center in downtown Wilmington, Vermont, an important community and economic hub for the entire Deerfield Valley.
The indoor show features the works of over 40 artists and craftspeople, taking place at a perfect time to visit the southern Green Mountains as there is sure to be beautiful fall foliage that weekend.
Dates and Times
Friday, September 27: 10 am – 5 pm
Saturday, September 28: 10 am – 5 pm
Sunday, September 29: 10 am – 4 pm
