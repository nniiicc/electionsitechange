The Vermont Commissioner of Health will receive recommendations from the Vermont Immunization Advisory Council.
Immunizations are a personal and family matter, and this bill does nothing to alter that.
It doesn’t require anyone to be vaccinated—or not vaccinated.
It does allow the state of Vermont to create those schedules on its own, rather than relying solely on the federal government.
H545 now moves to the Senate.
We continue to monitor and respond carefully to changes in federal policy, always keeping in mind the health and safety of Vermonters.
