I grew up in West Virginia.
Like so many people my age, I had to leave to find opportunity.
Not because I wanted to, but because the jobs I trained for didn't exist here.
Years later, life brought my fiancé, Nick, and me home to care for family.
Coming back made something clear: Decisions made at the State Capitol don't stay in Charleston.
They show up at our kitchen tables, shaping whether families can afford childcare, pay their utility bills, care for loved ones, and stay rooted in the communities they love.
I chose to come home for good.
To put down roots, show up, and do my part.
I'm running for the West Virginia House of Delegates because too many decisions are made without listening to the people who live with the consequences.
District 47 deserves a representative who understands that policy isn't abstract.
It's personal.
And I will fight for working families with honesty, transparency and respect.
