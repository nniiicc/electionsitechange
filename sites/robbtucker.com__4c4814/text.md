I am a business owner, a public servant, and a champion for our community.
I was born and raised in Nevada County and have seen a lot of changes over the years, some good and some bad.
I have experienced firsthand what a special place the Sierra Foothills are and my wife and I have chosen to raise our own children right here in Nevada County.
My family has a long history of service to this community and our country.
After a successful career running our family business, I turned my attention to public service and ran and won a seat on the Nevada County Board of Supervisors.
As a Supervisor, I have stood up for meaningful solutions to protect public safety, support small businesses and create jobs, and protect our quality of life through a responsible balance of growth, development, and agriculture.
Now, I am running for Congress to take that solutions-oriented leadership to Washington, D.C.
With a lifetime commitment to our communities, experienced executive leadership as a local business owner, and a heart for service, I am ready to be your next Congressman.
I would be honored to have your vote.
Robb Tucker
