CAMPAIGN NEWS PRESS RELEASE
Robb Tucker Secures Key Endorsements from Sacramento-Area Leaders and Organizations
April 7, 2026
Sacramento, CA — Nevada County Supervisor Robb Tucker, a lifelong resident of California’s newly drawn Congressional District 3, has secured a growing list of endorsements from prominent Sacramento-area leaders and organizations.
Supporters include State Senator Roger Niello, former Sacramento County Supervisor Sue Frost, Folsom City Vice Mayor Anna Rohrbough, the Capitol Lincoln Club, and Congressman Kevin Kiley.
Tucker emphasized the importance of strong, representative leadership following recent changes to the district.
“When this new district was drawn under Proposition 50, it diluted the voices of many voters by combining Sacramento with more rural areas,” Tucker said.
“To win this seat, we need a representative who understands both urban and rural challenges and is committed to improving quality of life for all residents.
I’m ready to take on that responsibility and deliver results for the people of District 3.”
Former Sacramento County Supervisor Sue Frost praised Tucker’s commitment to public service, stating Robb is “a proven community leader and experienced business owner” who is “deeply committed to enhancing safety and quality of life for all California families.
I urge voters to support Robb Tucker for Congress in 2026.”
State Senator Roger Niello also highlighted Tucker’s leadership and record: “Robb has earned my endorsement because of his thoughtful plans, strong values, and proven willingness to make tough decisions—even in the face of significant opposition.
I encourage conservatives to unite behind his campaign.”
Congressional District 3, currently represented by Congressman Kevin Kiley, has undergone significant changes following the passage of Proposition 50.
Now encompassing Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, and Nevada counties, the district requires a unified coalition that reflects its diverse communities.
In addition to individual endorsements, Tucker has received backing from the California Republican Party (CAGOP) and Republican Party organizations in all four counties within the district.
Additional endorsements and campaign updates will be announced in the coming weeks.
