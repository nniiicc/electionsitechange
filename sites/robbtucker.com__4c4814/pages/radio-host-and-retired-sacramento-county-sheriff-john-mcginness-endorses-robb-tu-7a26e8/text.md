CAMPAIGN NEWS PRESS RELEASE
Radio Host and Retired Sacramento County Sheriff John McGinness Endorses Robb Tucker for California’s 3rd Congressional District
May 7, 2026
Sacramento, CA — Nevada County Supervisor Robb Tucker has received the endorsement of retired Sacramento County Sheriff and radio host John McGinness in his campaign for California’s newly drawn 3rd Congressional District.
A longtime advocate for law enforcement and public safety, McGinness cited Tucker’s consistent support for law and order as a key reason for his endorsement.
“Law and order are fundamental to maintaining a strong quality of life in our communities,” said McGinness.
“In this race, one candidate stands head and shoulders above the rest—Robb Tucker.
Throughout his career, Robb has consistently demonstrated a strong commitment to supporting law enforcement and prioritizing public safety.
He is the leader we need in Congress to stand up for law and order and ensure our communities remain safe.”
A lifelong resident of the district, Tucker expressed gratitude for the endorsement and emphasized the urgent need to strengthen public safety across the region.
“I recently read about an incident in the Arden-Arcade area involving an individual seen on doorbell cameras carrying a knife and approaching homes,” Tucker said.
“It’s easy to imagine the fear those families experienced.
We must be stronger advocates for public safety and ensure we take a firm stance against crime to prevent dangerous situations like this.
When public safety is compromised, families suffer, and too many people feel forced to leave our state in search of safer communities.”
California’s 3rd Congressional District includes portions of Sacramento, Placer, and El Dorado counties, as well as all of Nevada County.
Tucker’s campaign continues to gain momentum, with growing support from community leaders, elected officials, and local organizations, while maintaining a focus on the issues that matter most to residents.
