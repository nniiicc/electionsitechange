CAMPAIGN NEWS PRESS RELEASE
Assemblyman James Gallagher Endorses Robb Tucker for California’s 3rd Congressional District
May 13, 2026
Northern California — Nevada County Supervisor Robb Tucker has earned the endorsement of Assemblyman James Gallagher in his campaign for California’s newly drawn 3rd Congressional District.
A lifelong resident of the district, Tucker has built his campaign around public safety, economic opportunity, and defending the interests of local communities across Northern California.
Gallagher, a respected conservative leader and longtime advocate for rural communities, praised Tucker’s leadership and commitment to standing up for the people he represents.
“When I look at Robb Tucker and what he has accomplished throughout his career, I see someone who will fight tirelessly for his constituents and stand firm in his principles,” Gallagher said.
“We need leaders like Robb Tucker in Congress fighting for our communities and our values.
I am proud to fully endorse Robb Tucker for Congress.”
Tucker expressed gratitude for Gallagher’s support and highlighted the Assemblyman’s leadership during difficult times for California families and businesses.
“I have long admired the work James Gallagher has done for our state and local communities,” Tucker said.
“During COVID, he stood up for small businesses, families, and students when many others would not.
Receiving the endorsement of such a principled and effective leader is truly an honor.”
California’s 3rd Congressional District includes portions of Sacramento, Placer, and El Dorado counties, as well as all of Nevada County.
Tucker’s campaign continues to gain momentum with growing support from community leaders, elected officials, and grassroots activists across the region.
