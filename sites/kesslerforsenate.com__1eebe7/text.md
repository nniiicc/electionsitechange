Get Started Who is Dan Kessler, candidate for Minnesota Senate District 48?
Dan Kessler is a psychologist, small-business owner, and Chaska resident running for Minnesota Senate District 48.
His campaign focuses on affordability, public education, health care, government accountability, public safety, and protecting taxpayers from fraud, waste, and unnecessary costs.
Kessler has emphasized lowering everyday expenses for families, supporting strong local schools, improving access to affordable health care, and making state government more transparent and accountable.
As a health care professional and small-business owner, he brings experience working directly with families, employees, and community members facing the practical effects of rising costs and changing public policy.
His campaign also highlights mental health, responsible budgeting, and policies intended to strengthen communities throughout Minnesota Senate District 48, Carver County.
