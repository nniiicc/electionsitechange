Upcoming Events with Dan Kessler
How can I meet Dan Kessler or get involved with the Minnesota Senate District 48 campaign?
Our campaign hosts community events, volunteer opportunities, neighborhood conversations, canvassing events, fundraisers, and opportunities for residents across Minnesota Senate District 48 and Carver County to get involved.
Events are a chance to meet Dan, talk with neighbors, learn more about the campaign, volunteer with the team, and help build a stronger future for our communities.
Upcoming Dan Kessler Campaign Events Include:
- Community conversations with voters across Senate District 48.
- Door-knocking and neighborhood canvassing opportunities.
- Volunteer gatherings and campaign trainings.
- Community festivals and local events.
- Fundraising events supporting the campaign.
- Opportunities to meet Dan Kessler and ask questions directly.
- Learn more about Dan Kessler here.
How can I volunteer for Dan Kessler?
Volunteers can support the campaign by:
- Talking with neighbors.
- Knocking doors.
- Hosting campaign conversations.
- Delivering yard signs.
- Helping at community events.
- Sharing campaign information with friends and family.
- More information on our Volunteer Page.
Every conversation helps build a stronger voice for Chaska, Chanhassen, Victoria, Waconia, and surrounding Carver County communities.
