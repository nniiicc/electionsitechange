Dan Kessler: Healthcare Professional, Community Leader, and Candidate for MN Senate District 48
What experience does Dan Kessler bring to the Minnesota Senate?
Professional Background
Dan Kessler brings more than two decades of experience as a psychologist, healthcare provider, small business owner, patient advocate, and community leader.
His professional career has centered on helping people navigate challenges, improve their health and well-being, and access the support they need to thrive.
As a psychologist, Dan has worked directly with individuals, couples, and families, giving him a firsthand understanding of the challenges Minnesotans face when it comes to mental healthcare, affordability, and access to quality services.
His experience as a healthcare provider has shaped his belief that patients and their healthcare providers, not politicians or insurance companies, should be at the center of healthcare decisions.
As a small business owner, Dan understands the challenges entrepreneurs face, from managing costs to serving their communities and building sustainable organizations.
He brings that perspective to conversations about supporting local businesses, strengthening Minnesota's economy, and making communities more affordable.
Beyond his professional work, Dan has been actively involved in community service and nonprofit leadership, reflecting his commitment to giving back and improving the communities where Minnesotans live, work, and raise their families.
Dan believes his experience caring for people, running a small business, and serving his community provides a practical perspective that Minnesota needs at the Capitol..
Dan Kessler is the DFL-endorsed candidate for Minnesota State Senate District 48, representing Chaska, Chanhassen, Victoria, Waconia, and surrounding communities in Carver County.
Dan Kessler's Background and Community Roots
Dan has made Chaska, Minnesota his home for over 20 years, where he and his wife Michelle raised their family.
Their children attended Eastern Carver County Schools, giving Dan firsthand experience with the importance of strong public education and thriving local communities.
Dan is a member of the Chaska Curling Center, where he enjoys the community, friendship, and camaraderie that come from being part of a local organization.
Dan's family story reflects the opportunities that define Minnesota.
His grandparents immigrated to the United States in search of the American Dream.
As first-generation Americans, Dan's parents were the first in their families to attend college.
Their commitment to hard work, education, and community shaped Dan's values.
Dan earned his doctorate from Florida Institute of Technology and has served in leadership and volunteer roles with nonprofit organizations and community groups.

Dan Kessler's Healthcare Experience
As a cancer survivor and healthcare professional, Dan understands healthcare from both sides: as a patient facing a serious diagnosis and as a psychologist helping people navigate difficult challenges.
Dan believes every Minnesotan deserves access to affordable, high-quality healthcare and that medical decisions should be made by patients and their healthcare providers.
His healthcare priorities include:
- Expanding access to affordable healthcare.
- Supporting mental healthcare access.
- Reducing barriers that prevent people from getting the care they need.
- Ensuring patients—not insurance companies or politicians—are at the center of healthcare decisions.
Dan Kessler is a Small Business Owner
Dan is a small business owner who built a successful psychology practice serving patients across the country through telehealth.
As a small business owner, Dan understands the challenges entrepreneurs face, including managing costs, serving customers, supporting employees, and adapting to a changing economy.
Dan believes Minnesota should support innovation, entrepreneurship, and economic opportunities that allow communities and families to thrive.
Dan Kessler's Approach to Public Service
As a psychologist, Dan spends his career listening to people, understanding challenges, and helping individuals find solutions.
He believes those same skills are needed in government: listening first, finding common ground, and developing practical solutions that improve people's lives.
Dan's priorities include:
- Making Minnesota more affordable.
- Strengthening public schools.
- Expanding access to healthcare.
- Supporting working families.
- Protecting Minnesota's environment and clean water.
- Creating economic opportunities for future generations.
Why Dan Kessler Is Running for Minnesota Senate District 48
Dan Kessler is running for Minnesota Senate because he believes communities are strongest when people work together toward common goals.
He will bring his experience as a psychologist, healthcare advocate, small business owner, parent, and community member to the State Capitol to represent the voices of families across Senate District 48 and Minnesota.
Dan believes leadership means listening, solving problems, and building a future where every generation has the opportunity to succeed.
