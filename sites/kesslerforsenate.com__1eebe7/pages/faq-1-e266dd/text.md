Volunteer Donate Frequently Asked Questions What is Dan Kessler's plan to make Minnesota more affordable?
Dan Kessler believes affordability is about more than one issue, it is about ensuring families have access to the basic building blocks of a stable life: a home they can afford, quality healthcare, excellent schools, reliable childcare, and good-paying jobs.
Dan supports: Expanding affordable housing and workforce housing opportunities.
Helping more Minnesotans achieve homeownership.
Making quality childcare more affordable and accessible.
Improving access to affordable healthcare and mental healthcare.
Supporting workforce training and career opportunities.
Strengthening public schools so every child has the opportunity to succeed.
Investing in policies that help working families live, work, and thrive in Minnesota.
Why is housing affordability such an important issue in Senate District 48?
Housing affordability is one of the biggest challenges facing families, seniors, and young adults across Carver County.
Rising home prices, limited housing options, and increasing costs make it harder for many people to stay in the communities they love.
Dan Kessler believes Minnesota needs more housing opportunities so teachers, healthcare workers, first responders, young families, and seniors can afford to live in the communities they serve.
Dan supports: Increasing housing supply.
Expanding affordable and workforce housing.
Protecting residents and homeowners.
Supporting policies that make communities more affordable while preserving what makes them special.
What does Dan Kessler believe about healthcare affordability?
Dan Kessler believes access to affordable, quality healthcare is essential for Minnesota families.
Healthcare should be accessible when people need it, and medical decisions should be made by patients and their healthcare providers, not politicians or insurance companies.
Dan supports: Improving healthcare affordability.
Expanding access to mental healthcare.
Protecting access to reproductive healthcare.
Reducing unnecessary barriers to care.
Ensuring Minnesota families can access the care they need without overwhelming costs.
What does Dan Kessler believe about public education?
Dan Kessler believes strong public schools are the foundation of strong communities.
Excellent schools prepare students for success, strengthen local economies, attract businesses, and create opportunities for the next generation.
Dan supports: Fully funding public education.
Supporting teachers and school staff.
Expanding career and technical education.
Preparing students for good-paying jobs.
Ensuring schools are safe, welcoming places where every student can succeed.
Why are public schools connected to affordability?
Quality schools attract families and businesses, strengthen neighborhoods, and provide students with the skills they need for future careers.
Dan Kessler believes investing in public education is an investment in the community and in Minnesota's future.
Why is childcare part of the affordability conversation?
For many Minnesota families, childcare has become one of the largest household expenses.
When childcare is unaffordable, parents are forced to make difficult decisions about work, finances, and family.
Dan Kessler believes affordable childcare is both a family issue and an economic issue.
Affordable childcare helps: Parents remain in the workforce.
Employers find and retain workers.
Children receive a strong start.
Minnesota's economy remain competitive.
How does Dan Kessler approach economic growth?
Dan Kessler believes a strong economy means more than business growth, it means families can afford homes, workers earn good wages, and communities have opportunities to thrive.
Dan supports: Workforce development.
Career and technical education.
Good-paying jobs.
Housing affordability.
Affordable healthcare.
Innovation and clean energy investments that create economic opportunity.
What makes Dan Kessler's approach to affordability different?
Dan Kessler believes affordability should be the lens through which every major policy decision is evaluated.
Families should not have to choose between paying for healthcare, raising children, buying a home, or staying in the communities they love.
His approach focuses on strengthening the foundations that help families succeed: affordable housing, quality healthcare, excellent public schools, accessible childcare, and good-paying jobs.
Why is Dan Kessler running for Minnesota Senate?
Families are struggling with affordability.
Healthcare costs are rising.
Public schools need support.
Communities need leaders who listen and solve problems.

What is Dan's position on the Fraud in Minnesota?
We Deserve a Government We Can Trust Fraud is a serious problem because every dollar stolen from a public program is a dollar that cannot serve the Minnesotans those programs were designed to help.
It is important to be clear about the timeline: Dan Kessler was not serving in elected office when the fraud occurred or when the underlying state oversight failures took place.
He was a private citizen and psychologist at the time.
He is now running for the Minnesota Senate.
Going forward, Dan believes Minnesota needs stronger oversight, better fraud detection, meaningful audits, recovery of stolen public funds, and accountability when wrongdoing occurs.
Minnesota has already begun implementing additional anti-fraud measures, including stronger screening, payment withholds, data sharing, and increased oversight of high-risk providers.
Dan is committed to protecting taxpayer dollars and ensuring public funds reach the people and programs they are intended to serve.
He believes accountability should apply regardless of political party or who is responsible.
