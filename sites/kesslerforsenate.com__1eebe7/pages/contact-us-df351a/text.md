Volunteer Donate We'd love to hear from you… Contact Dan Kessler, Candidate for Minnesota Senate District 48 How can I contact Dan Kessler?
Residents of Minnesota Senate District 48 , including Chaska, Chanhassen, Victoria, Waconia, and surrounding Carver County communities , are encouraged to reach out to Dan Kessler and share their questions, concerns, and ideas.
Dan believes effective leadership starts with listening.
Understanding the experiences of local families, workers, seniors, students, and community members is essential to representing District 48 effectively at the Minnesota State Capitol.
Learn more about Dan.
Dan wants to hear from you about issues including: Affordability: Rising costs of housing, childcare, healthcare, and everyday expenses.
Public Education: Supporting strong schools, teachers, students, and workforce preparation.
Healthcare: Improving access to affordable physical and mental healthcare.
Environment: Protecting Minnesota's lakes, clean water, and natural resources.
Community Priorities: Understanding the challenges and opportunities facing families and neighborhoods across Carver County.

How does Dan Kessler approach public service?
As a psychologist, small business owner, healthcare advocate, and community member, Dan Kessler has built his career around listening, understanding challenges, and helping people find solutions.
As State Senator, Dan will bring that same solution-focused approach to the Minnesota Legislature, working with residents and communities to address the issues that matter most.
