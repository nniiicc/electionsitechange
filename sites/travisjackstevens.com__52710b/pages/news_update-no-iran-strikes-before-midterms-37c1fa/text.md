By midday, the president answered.
On Truth Social, President Trump wrote that the United States "will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections to be held in the United States on November 3rd." He said his team is "having productive discussions" with Iran, and that "the Blockade will remain in full force and effect." Read that sentence again.
It isn't a promise of peace.
It's a promise with an expiration date, and the date is Election Day.
Look at what doesn't change.
The blockade stays.
The Hill reports more than 4,000 Marines and sailors aboard three warships, led by the USS Makin Island, are heading to the region.
Axios reports that on Tuesday, U.S. officials briefed Israel's army chief on preparations to possibly resume major combat "within the next three weeks." An Israeli official told Axios that after the midterms, the chances "increase significantly." The president says oil is flowing in record numbers.
That's his claim.
Reuters and the shipping group BIMCO say last week saw the most tanker attacks of the war.
Brent crude briefly topped $105 a barrel Thursday morning, for several reasons traders cite, not just the war.
A war that pauses for the vote and resumes after it is still a forever war.
It's just a forever war with better timing.
