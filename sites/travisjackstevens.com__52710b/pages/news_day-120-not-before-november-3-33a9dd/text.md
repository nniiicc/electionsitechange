Then What?
T Travis Jack Stevens • Saturday, October 10, 2026 • 2 min read Dear Delaware, Regular gas in Delaware averages $4.24 this morning, AAA says.
#ago it was $2.96.
Diesel is $6.32.
We're paying for this war at the pump, so we deserve a straight answer about it.
On Thursday, President Trump wrote on Truth Social that the United States "will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections to be held in the United States on November 3rd." On Friday, asked outside the White House why that decision was tied to the election, he told reporters, "We may.
I mean, we'll see," according to The Jerusalem Post.
Friday night, at a rally in New York, he said of Iran, "Either they give us everything, or they don't exist any longer," The Jerusalem Post reported.
Those statements don't match, and I can't tell you which one is the plan.
That's the problem.
A decision about war that changes in a day is not a plan, and it hasn't been put in front of the people paying for it.
The Congressional Budget Office estimates this war has cost more than $38 billion so far.
On September 24, the Senate voted 50 to 49 against a war powers resolution that would have required Congress to authorize these hostilities.
Thirty-eight billion dollars has been spent, and Congress never voted to authorize this war.
I spent years as a travel ICU nurse.
When a family asked what was coming, we told them the truth, even when the truth was "we don't know yet." What we never did was tell them one thing on Thursday and something different on Friday.
A decision this big belongs to the people and the representatives they elect.
You should hear the plan before you vote, not after.
