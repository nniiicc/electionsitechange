How to Vote → A War on an Election Clock Back to News & Updates Letter A War on an Election Clock T Travis Jack Stevens • Thursday, October 8, 2026 • 3 min read Dear Delaware, Regular gas in Delaware averages $4.19 today, up about seven cents since yesterday.
#ago it was $2.90, AAA says.
Diesel is $6.33.
Now we're learning what Washington may be weighing to fix that.
The Atlantic reported Wednesday, citing two administration officials, that the White House asked the Pentagon for options to strike Iran that could be carried out before the November 3 midterms.
Central Command is developing them.
The targets, the scope, and whether the president authorizes anything at all are still undecided.
Officials told The Atlantic the planning is tied to showing progress in the war and bringing down energy prices before the vote.
Axios reported that the Pentagon told Central Command several days ago to finish preparing to resume major combat operations in Iran.
No date has been set and no final decision made, and a White House official said the president has all options available at any time.
An Israeli official told Axios, "The chances of it happening before the elections is not high but we don't rule it out.
After the midterms the chances of it happening increase significantly." The Atlantic also reported that some of the president's advisers would rather wait until after the vote, worried that renewed fighting, especially if American troops are killed, could hurt his party's candidates.
Even supporters of limited strikes concede, according to The Atlantic, that the strikes alone would not bring Iran to the table, reopen the Strait of Hormuz, or lower gas prices before Election Day.
That's the part I can't get past.
Bombs don't fix a gas bill.
Yesterday another tanker was struck by multiple projectiles north of Qatar, with casualties reported.
This war is already spreading, and it has no exit plan.
Before the vote or after it, the calendar they're arguing over is an election calendar.
Nobody should time a war to a campaign calendar.
I spent years as a travel ICU nurse, and we never scheduled care around how it would look.
We did it when the patient needed it, and only then.
I will not take Super PAC money.
I will not vote to fund wars without an honest accounting or a plan to end them.
I am a Qualified write-in candidate.
My name is not printed on the ballot.
