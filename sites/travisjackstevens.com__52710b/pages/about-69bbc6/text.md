A Place for Umul, Part 2 Hi, I'm Travis I'm Travis Jack Stevens, and I'm running for U.S.
Senate in Delaware as a write-in.
My name won't be printed on your ballot.
On November 3, I'm asking you to write it in yourself.
I've been a registered nurse since 2012.
I spent years as a travel ICU nurse, and when COVID hit, I felt called to serve on the front lines.
I was there when families said goodbye over phones held up at the bedside.
At the bedside I watched people skip medications, put off a diagnosis, and go into debt over a single ER visit.
You don't forget that, and it's a big part of why I'm running.
I live paycheck to paycheck in a one-bedroom with my dog and two kittens.
I'm not a career politician, and I don't have a Super PAC.
This campaign is people-funded, so the only people I'll owe are you.
Here at home I founded the CAMP Rehoboth Cares Committee and served as Secretary of the Eastern Sussex Democrats.
I learned that politics works best neighbor to neighbor.
My values are simple.
Every person has dignity.
We should live with moral wholeness, so that what we say matches what we do.
And we have to see the world as it is and as it could be at the same time.
I can't close my eyes to the hard parts, and I won't give up on the better version.
Right now too much of our system is built to keep people out.
Exclusionary systems lock in fees, paperwork and red tape.
Crony capitalism lets the people with money write the rules for everyone else.
I want an economy that works for the people doing the work.
That's why I'm fighting for: Justice and accountability.
No one is above the law, including a president.
I want full, independent investigations, with the facts and the courts deciding.
An end to forever wars.
Congress, not the president, decides when we go to war.
I'll vote to end U.S. involvement in the Iran war and to stop sending weapons that kill civilians in Gaza.
Every dollar spent on endless war is a dollar taken from Delaware families.
Student debt forgiveness for all, and free education going forward, with fully funded public schools.
Stop trapping people in old debt, and stop manufacturing new debt.
Health care and housing for everyone, with the richest Americans and the biggest corporations paying their fair share.
Real rules for AI.
That means independent safety testing before release, liability when products cause harm, protections for workers, and data centers paying their own power bills.
I won't take a dime from AI or tech companies or their executives.
A safe, clean environment.
I stand with Beyond Pesticides.
In the Senate I'll cosponsor the pesticide warning-label bill and the Opportunities in Organic Act.
I'll fight for polluter-pays PFAS cleanup and a PFAS ban under the international definition.
The companies that poisoned our water should pay to clean it.
My training: a BSN from Frostburg State University; associate degrees in Arts and in Science from Hagerstown Community College; and Occupational Studies in Massage Therapy from the Heritage Institute.
