What Is a Declared Write-In Candidate?
In Delaware, a declared write-in candidate has formally filed with the state to run for office.
This is different from an unofficial write-in — declared write-in votes are counted and reported separately, so your vote is tracked and tallied.
Travis has filed as a declared write-in for the 2026 U.S.
Senate general election.
Your vote for him is a real, counted vote.
How to Cast a Write-In Vote in Delaware
Step 1: Go to your polling place on Election Day
Delaware's general election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Find your polling place at ivote.de.gov.
Step 2: Locate the write-in line on your ballot
On your U.S.
Senate ballot, there will be a designated write-in space below the printed candidates.
It may be a blank line or a write-in oval with a text field, depending on your county's voting equipment.
Step 3: Write in the name
Write clearly: Travis Jack Stevens
Make sure to spell the name correctly and write it in the designated write-in area for the U.S.
Senate race.
Step 4: Fill in the oval (if required)
On many Delaware ballots, you must also fill in or select the oval next to the write-in line for your vote to be counted.
Check your ballot instructions carefully.
Step 5: Submit your ballot as normal
That's it.
Your write-in vote will be counted along with all other ballots.
Can I Vote Absentee?
Yes.
Delaware allows no-excuse absentee voting.
If you plan to vote absentee, request your ballot early at elections.delaware.gov.
The write-in process on an absentee ballot is the same — write the name clearly in the designated space and fill in any required oval.
Frequently Asked Questions
Will my write-in vote actually be counted?
Yes.
Because Travis has filed as a declared write-in candidate with the Delaware Department of Elections, votes cast for him are counted and reported in the official results.
What if I misspell the name?
Election officials use reasonable judgment when counting write-in votes.
Write as clearly as possible: Travis Jack Stevens.
Minor spelling variations are generally accepted if the intent is clear.
Can I write in Travis on any race?
Only for the U.S.
Senate race.
Write-in votes must be cast in the correct race to be counted.
Do I need to register as a write-in voter?
No.
Any registered Delaware voter can cast a write-in vote.
You just need to be registered to vote before the registration deadline.
