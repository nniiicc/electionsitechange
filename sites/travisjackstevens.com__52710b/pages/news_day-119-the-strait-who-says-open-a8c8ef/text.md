Back to News & Updates Letter "The Strait Is Open," Says Who?
T Travis Jack Stevens • Friday, October 9, 2026 • 2 min read Dear Delaware, Regular gas in Delaware averages $4.23 this morning, AAA says.
#ago it was $2.93.
Diesel is $6.32.
That's the one war number I can verify for you today, because it comes out of your wallet.
Everything else about the Strait of Hormuz depends on who you ask.
President Trump, the same day he promised no attack on Iran before November 3, wrote that oil is flowing through the strait in "Record Numbers," with "22 Million Barrels, last night alone." That's his claim.
The deputy for political affairs of Iran's IRGC Navy, Ali Mohammadi, told the IRGC-affiliated Fars news agency that its forces carry out operations "every night" against ships it says break Iran's rules.
He called reports of oil passing through "a media maneuver." That's his claim.
Then there are the people who actually move the ships.
The shipping-security firm Marisks has advised owners to suspend Hormuz crossings, the Gulf business outlet AGBI reports.
Marisks counts at least 14 attacks on tankers and gas carriers since September 28, the highest frequency since the war began.
Data firm Kpler says only seven commodity vessels crossed on Tuesday, the fewest in a day since July 23, and that crude flows fell 27 percent to about 10.1 million barrels a day.
I can't make those numbers agree.
I can't tell you which one is right.
That is exactly the problem.
I spent years as a travel nurse.
You chart what you measure, not what you hope.
When two numbers don't match, you don't pick the one you like.
You find out why.
A government that sends Americans into a war owes us that same honesty about what the war is costing.
I will not vote to fund wars without an honest accounting, and the first accounting is the truth about what's happening in that strait.
I will not take Super PAC money.
This campaign is people-funded.

My name is not printed on the ballot.
