TOPEKA, KS – Today, Democratic nominee for governor Cindy Holscher launched her newest television ad, “Sunflowers,” highlighting the need for firm guardrails around new data center development in Kansas.
The ad will air statewide on broadcast, cable, and digital platforms starting today.
“Cindy Holscher is bringing common sense and lower costs back to politics,” said campaign spokesperson Hans Torgerson.
“Cindy believes Kansas should hit pause on data centers until we have guaranteed protections for our communities, farms, land, and wallets.
She will never allow powerful corporations to write the rules while ordinary Kansans are left footing the bill.
On the other hand, Ty Masterson is beholden to Big Tech donors and will let them put a data center in every cornfield.”
Holscher has called for a pause on new data-center development until Kansas establishes protections for water and energy consumption, ratepayer protections, local input, and job creation.
Meanwhile, an AI Super PAC spent at least $800,000 for Republican Ty Masterson because of his support for expanding data centers and giving special tax breaks to build them.
Transcript of “Sunflowers”:
This field of our iconic Kansas sunflowers is under threat because Big Tech wants to bring in a giant data center.
Without protections, that could raise our electricity costs, put our farmland and water supplies at risk, all while taxpayers foot the bill.
As governor, I’ll hit pause on data centers until we can set up firm guardrails to protect our communities, our farms, our jobs, and our budgets.
Let’s do this the right way, Kansas, so our state and our sunflowers can grow stronger for generations to come.
