Kansas farmers and ranchers are the backbone of our state, and right now they're getting squeezed from every side.
Fuel and fertilizer costs keep climbing.
Tariffs are closing off international markets, even as the Trump administration brings in cheap foreign beef to compete with Kansas producers.
Cindy will fight to bring costs down for farmers and ranchers and protect their access to fair markets.
