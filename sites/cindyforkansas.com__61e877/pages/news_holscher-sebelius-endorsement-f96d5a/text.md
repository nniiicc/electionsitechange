Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release Former Kansas Governor Kathleen Sebelius Endorses Cindy Holscher For Immediate Release · September 16, 2026 TOPEKA, KS — Former Kansas Governor and U.S.
Secretary of Health and Human Services Kathleen Sebelius today endorsed Democratic nominee Cindy Holscher for governor.
Sebelius’ endorsement adds one of Kansas’ most prominent former governors to the growing leadership coalition standing behind Holscher in the general election, including current Governor Laura Kelly and former Governor John Carlin.
Sebelius, who served as Kansas’ 44th governor from 2003 to 2009 before joining President Barack Obama’s cabinet, cited Holscher’s commitment to supporting public education, increasing access to healthcare, and making life more affordable for Kansas families.
“I know firsthand what it takes to serve as Governor of Kansas, and Cindy Holscher is ready to lead our state forward,” said former Kansas Governor Kathleen Sebelius.
“Cindy will protect our public schools, fight to finally expand Medicaid and lower prescription costs, and focus on making Kansas a more affordable place to live and raise a family.
There is too much at stake to go backward, and I’m proud to endorse Cindy and do everything I can to help elect her as our next governor.” “Governor Sebelius showed Kansas what strong, pragmatic leadership looks like, and I am deeply honored to have her support in this race,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“She has spent her own career fighting for Kansas families, and she understands both the enormous responsibility and incredible opportunity that come with the job.
I will follow in her footsteps as we fight for a state that puts ordinary Kansans first.” Global Strategy Group recently released a new poll of likely Kansas voters showing that Democrat Cindy Holscher is leading Republican Ty Masterson in the gubernatorial election.
The poll is the first to be publicly released since the primary election.
Holscher starts the race with 47% support , one point ahead of her Republican competitor, Ty Masterson, with 46% support.
7 percent are undecided.
### Cindy Holscher grew up on a farm with common sense, Midwest values, and a love of her country.
Cindy and her husband Greg have lived in Kansas for over 25 years, and their three kids graduated from public schools.
She first ran for office to end the disastrous Brownback experiment and restore school funding.
As a legislator, Cindy co-founded the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus, and she focused on lowering costs for working-class and middle-class Kansans.
Now, she’s running for governor because Kansas deserves a leader who has a proven track record of fighting for all Kansans.
