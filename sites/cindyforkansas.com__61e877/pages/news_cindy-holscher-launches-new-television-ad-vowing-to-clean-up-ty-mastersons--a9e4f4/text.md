Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release Cindy Holscher Launches New Television Ad Vowing to Clean Up Ty Masterson’s Messes For Immediate Release · October 14, 2026 TOPEKA, KS — Democratic nominee for governor Cindy Holscher launched a new ad today, “Muck,” highlighting the toughness she learned cleaning up after farm animals and her readiness to clean up the even messier, costlier plans from Ty Masterson.
The ad airs statewide on broadcast, streaming, and digital platforms starting today.
WATCH “Muck” contrasts Holscher’s record with Ty Masterson, one of the chief architects and authors of the Brownback tax experiment.
Holscher helped build the bipartisan coalition that cleaned up the Brownback and Masterson mess and restored stability to Kansas.
Now, Masterson wants to drag the state back into that pile of manure.
Since the end of the Brownback era, Masterson’s wealthy backers have benefitted from enormous tax breaks while working families face rising property taxes, higher utility bills and the costs of unchecked data center development.
“Cindy Holscher is well-versed in cleaning up messes from her time on the farm, and Ty Masterson’s may be nastier than those, but Cindy’s ready to keep shoveling and clean up after him too,” said campaign spokesperson Hans Torgerson .
“Masterson is sinking because Kansans can smell the shams and scams from a mile away.
Families remember his contributions to the disastrous Brownback plan that gave every break possible to the highest bidder while ordinary Kansans paid the price.
Cindy grew up on a farm, and she’s cleaned up messes like this before.
She beat the Brownback agenda before, and in three weeks she’s going to beat it again.” As governor, Holscher will stay focused on lowering costs, delivering meaningful property-tax relief, stopping giveaways to greedy corporations, and pausing data centers.
Transcript of “Muck”: [CINDY HOLSCHER]: I’m Cindy Holscher, and I grew up on a farm cleaning up messes from these guys.
Then I ran for office to clean up the mess from these guys.
Now, I’m running for governor to clean up the corruption driving up your costs.
From the Brownback plan, to property taxes, to data centers, Ty Masterson’s rich backers get huge tax cuts.
Ty gets a million-dollar no-show job, and we pay the price.
Come on, Kansas.
Let’s cut the crap.
# # # State Senator Cindy Holscher understands the struggles everyday Kansans face.
Holscher was raised on a farm by parents who worked hard for a living in construction and public schools.
She worked her way through college and is one of the first in her family with a college degree.
She is a mom who sent her three kids to public schools.
Holscher will make sure government serves everyday Kansans, not the wealthy and powerful.
She will fight to fully fund our public schools, lower property taxes, and make things more affordable in Kansas.
