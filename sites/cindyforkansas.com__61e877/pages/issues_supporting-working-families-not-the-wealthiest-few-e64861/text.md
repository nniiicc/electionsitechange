Cindy will fight to increase the minimum wage to $16 statewide, and higher in high-cost cities and counties.
She'll fight to make sure that when people need to miss work because they’re sick, they don’t have to go without pay.
And she'll work to lower the cost of everything from groceries to housing to healthcare.
