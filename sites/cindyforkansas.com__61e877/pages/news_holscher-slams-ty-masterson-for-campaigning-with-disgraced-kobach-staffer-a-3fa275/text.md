TOPEKA, KS – At Saturday’s Kansas State Fair gubernatorial debate, William Hendrix, the disgraced former vice chair of the Kansas Young Republicans, spent the debate in the second row of the audience, leading chants for Ty Masterson and interacting with members of Masterson’s campaign team.
Hendrix was fired from Attorney General Kris Kobach’s office last October after Politico revealed that he and other Young Republican leaders traded racist and antisemitic messages in a leaked group chat, in which Hendrix repeatedly used racial slurs including the N-Word to refer to Black people.
As Politico reported:
They referred to Black people as monkeys and ‘the watermelon people’ and mused about putting their political opponents in gas chambers.
They talked about raping their enemies and driving them to suicide and lauded Republicans who they believed support slavery. [...]
The group chat members spoke freely about the pressure to cow to Trump to avoid being called a RINO, the love of Nazis within their party’s right wing and the president’s alleged work to suppress documents related to wealthy financier Jeffrey Epstein’s child sex crimes.
Saturday was not Hendrix’s first appearance for Masterson: he was photographed holding a Masterson campaign sign at the launch of Masterson’s campaign for governor.
“Extremism and hate have no place at our State Fair or anywhere in Kansas,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“Why was this disgraced former staffer who got caught spreading extreme racist, violent and pro-Nazi messages welcomed into the Masterson campaign on Saturday?
I call on Ty Masterson to disavow extreme hate and violence in all its forms.”
Senator Holscher calls on Ty Masterson to answer the following questions:
- Is Hendrix being paid by the Masterson campaign?
- Is the campaign comfortable with Hendrix’s public support?
- Did anyone on the campaign invite Hendrix to the debate?
- Why did nobody on the campaign ask Hendrix to leave?
