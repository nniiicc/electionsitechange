Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release Holscher launches new webisode on data centers with lifelong Republicans For Immediate Release · September 29, 2026 TOPEKA, KS — In a new video today, Democratic nominee for governor Cindy Holscher is highlighting the Marple family, who have raised serious concerns about a proposed 10-building data center complex threatening their family farm.
The Marples are lifelong Republicans, but they are supporting Cindy Holscher because she’s the only candidate for governor taking data center concerns seriously.
WATCH: “Protecting Our Farmers From Data Centers” Hedge Tree Farm in Wakarusa has been in Ben Marple’s family since 1857.
Ben, 77, and Carol, 72, raised their family on the farm and still rely on it for their income.
The Marple family praised Holscher for listening and supporting a commonsense pause on new data centers until Kansas adopts strong protections: “Cindy’s going to hit pause on data centers, and that’s really a relief to farmers like us,” said Carol Marple.
“Cindy’s going to do the right thing about data centers,” said Ben Marple.
“Her heart’s in the right place, and the more I talk to her, the more I’m convinced that she’s the person for the job…I will vote for Cindy, and I would encourage all of you to listen to what she’s saying.” “As a registered Republican, this is uncharted territory, but I’m ultimately going to vote for what impacts my family and what I feel is right,” said Molly Marple.
“We’ve got to get the guardrails in place to protect our environment, protect our people, protect our water supply, protect us from utility rates going up, and to ensure that there are jobs that come along,” said Cindy Holscher.
“What you all are asking for is reasonable.” Holscher has called for Kansas to hit pause on new data-center development until there are enforceable guardrails to protect families from higher electric bills, preserve farmland and water supplies, guarantee meaningful local input, and ensure taxpayers are not left subsidizing giveaways to Big Tech.
# # # State Senator Cindy Holscher understands the struggles everyday Kansans face.
Holscher was raised on a farm by parents who worked hard for a living in construction and public schools.
She worked her way through college and is one of the first in her family with a college degree.
She is a mom who sent her three kids to public schools.
Holscher will make sure government serves everyday Kansans, not the wealthy and powerful.
She will fight to fully fund our public schools, lower property taxes, and make things more affordable in Kansas.
