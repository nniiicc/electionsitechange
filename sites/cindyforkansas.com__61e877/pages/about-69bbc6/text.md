Meet Cindy Meet KC For Governor Meet Cindy Raised on a farm, Cindy was brought up with Midwest values, common sense, and a love of her country.
Her parents taught her the meaning of caring for her community and investing in the future of our children, and their values guide her service to the people of Kansas.
Her grandparents were tenant farmers.
Her father, a veteran, was a construction worker and a proud union member.
Her mom worked at her elementary school.
They worked hard to provide her with a better life, and her father's union job and strong public schools enabled her to graduate from college.
Cindy’s commitment to our schools is what led her to public service.
During the disastrous Brownback experiment, which drained billions from Kansas schools and services in order to provide corporate tax breaks, she served as a substitute teacher in her local school to help out with the teacher shortage.
Then she ran for office to help restore common sense to state finances.
In the Kansas House, Cindy co-founded the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus, and worked across the aisle to reverse budget cuts and put the Kansas economy back on track.
In 2020, she was elected to the State Senate, where she has worked to solve problems for Kansas families: to cut the cost of living, make sure Kansans have healthcare they can afford, fully fund our public schools, and more.
Cindy and her husband Greg have lived in Kansas for over 25 years and their three kids are all graduates of the state’s outstanding public schools.
Their youngest is now a sophomore at the University of Kansas.
Cindy will be a governor for all Kansans, who will always put working people ahead of billionaires and greedy corporations.
Learn more about what she'll do at cindyforkansas.com/issues .
For Lieutenant Governor Meet KC Born in Wichita and a product of Kansas public schools, Ohaebosim (Oh-HOB-oh-sum) currently serves in the Kansas House of Representatives, where he has built a reputation as a pragmatic leader focused on good-paying jobs, public schools, and worker protections for Kansas families.
Ohaebosim has earned national recognition for his legislative leadership, including being named Regional Legislator of the Year by the National Black Caucus of State Legislators in both 2022 and 2025.
As a state representative, Ohaebosim voted to end the disastrous Brownback tax experiment, strengthen civil rights protections, and support policies aimed at lowering costs for working families.
He was first elected in 2017 to the Kansas House.
Now, he’s running for Lieutenant Governor to fight alongside Cindy Holscher for a better future for every Kansan.
