Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release United Food and Commercial Workers District Union Local Two Endorses Cindy Holscher for Governor For Immediate Release · September 10, 2026 TOPEKA, KS — Democratic nominee for Governor Cindy Holscher today announced the endorsement of United Food and Commercial Workers District Union Local 2 (UFCW Local 2), adding the support of thousands of Kansas workers to her growing coalition.
UFCW Local 2 represents over 15,000 workers across Kansas, Oklahoma and Missouri.
Its members include the workers who keep Kansas' meatpacking and food-processing plants running, as well as retail and food-safety sanitation workers.
The endorsement recognizes Holscher's record in the Kansas Legislature standing with organized labor and fighting for working families — including her 100% score on the AFL-CIO Legislative Scorecard.
Throughout her time in public office, Holscher has stood against efforts that undermine workers and has fought for policies that put the needs of Kansas families ahead of wealthy corporations and powerful special interests.
“UFCW Local 2 is proud to stand with Cindy Holscher in this race because she has always had our backs,” said UFCW Local 2 President Martin Rosas .
“Our members work some of the toughest and most essential jobs in our state, and they deserve a governor who respects their work and will stand up for their right to good wages, safe workplaces and a dignified retirement.
We are confident that Cindy will be that governor.” Holscher has made supporting Kansas workers a cornerstone of her public service.
Her father was a union member, and she has frequently credited the stability his union job provided with helping her family get by and for helping put her through college.
“This endorsement is important to me because UFCW Local 2 members represent the very best of Kansas — people who work hard, take care of their families and keep our communities and our economy moving,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“I grew up in a union household, so I know firsthand what a good union job can mean for a family.
As governor, I will keep fighting for better wages, safer workplaces and an economy where the people who do the work actually have a chance to get ahead.” Holscher is committed to lowering costs and making Kansas a more affordable place to raise a family.
She has pledged to fight back against corporate giveaways, lower property taxes for everyday Kansans, protect public education, and ensure state government works for working people rather than the wealthy and well-connected.
Holscher has also been endorsed by AFGE, AFT-Kansas, and the Kansas AFL-CIO.
State leaders have also lined up in support of Holscher, including Governor Laura Kelly and former Governor John Carlin.
### Cindy Holscher grew up on a farm with common sense, Midwest values, and a love of her country.
Cindy and her husband Greg have lived in Kansas for over 25 years, and their three kids graduated from public schools.
She first ran for office to end the disastrous Brownback experiment and restore school funding.
As a legislator, Cindy co-founded the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus, and she focused on lowering costs for working-class and middle-class Kansans.
Now, she’s running for governor because Kansas deserves a leader who has a proven track record of fighting for all Kansans.
