Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release NEW: Holscher calls out Masterson for no-show job in Wichita For Immediate Release · October 16, 2026 WICHITA, KS — Democratic nominee for governor Cindy Holscher held a press conference today outside GoCreate at Wichita State University, calling out Ty Masterson’s million-dollar “no-show job” and the corrupt system that has allowed wealthy special interests to bankroll his career while influencing his work in the Legislature.
Masterson has collected more than $1.1 million over the past few years as director of the Koch-backed program.
Yet when reporters visited the facility, a front desk worker did not recognize him, and students said they had never seen him there.
New records raise even more questions.
During a four-month period when the Legislature was not in session, Masterson sent just 14 work emails—compared with 532 sent by GoCreate’s assistant director.
Many of the emails were fully redacted.
“This is pay-to-play politics at its worst,” said State Senator Cindy Holscher .
“Ty Masterson collected more than a million dollars from a donor-backed organization for a job where students did not know who he was, staff could not explain how often he worked, and his records show he sent just 14 emails over four months.
Wealthy special interests gave Ty a growing paycheck as a reward for letting them influence the laws Kansans live under.
Ty Masterson works for the billionaires and corporate insiders who fund him.
I work for Kansas families.” The press conference comes the same day a new Kansas Reflector investigation exposed Masterson’s extensive ties to the American Legislative Exchange Council, where corporate interests and politicians work together to advance extremist legislation across the country.
# # # State Senator Cindy Holscher understands the struggles everyday Kansans face.
Holscher was raised on a farm by parents who worked hard for a living in construction and public schools.
She worked her way through college and is one of the first in her family with a college degree.
She is a mom who sent her three kids to public schools.
Holscher will make sure government serves everyday Kansans, not the wealthy and powerful.
She will fight to fully fund our public schools, lower property taxes, and make things more affordable in Kansas.
