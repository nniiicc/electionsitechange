Legislative achievements
- Champion for Public Schools Cindy helped secure $500 million in new public school funding and protect teachers' jobs, reversing years of Brownback-era policies.
During the Brownback emergency, she served as a substitute teacher in her own community to help out with the teacher shortage.
And she has consistently blocked extremist efforts to cut public school funding and expand private school vouchers.
- Ended the Brownback Budget Disaster Cindy helped design and pass the 2017 legislation that cleaned up the Brownback mess, restoring funding to schools and public services while protecting middle-class Kansans from unfair tax increases.
She's fought to eliminate the sales tax on food and the income tax on Social Security benefits, too.
- Fought to Expand Healthcare Access Cindy has voted over and over to expand Medicaid, protect our rural hospitals, and legalize medicinal cannabis, proposals that were blocked by Ty Masterson in the Senate.
She supported expanding telemedicine, expanding mental health care, and legalizing fentanyl test strips to help prevent overdoses.
