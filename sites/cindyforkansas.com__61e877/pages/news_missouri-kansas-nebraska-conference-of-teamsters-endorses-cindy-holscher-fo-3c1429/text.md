Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release Missouri-Kansas-Nebraska Conference of Teamsters Endorses Cindy Holscher for Governor For Immediate Release · October 9, 2026 TOPEKA, KS — Today, the Missouri-Kansas-Nebraska Conference of Teamsters endorsed Cindy Holscher for governor, adding to the growing coalition behind her campaign.
The Teamsters represent more than 6,000 working men and women across Kansas, in areas as diverse as freight distribution, car-haul, heavy highway construction, wholesale and retail food distribution, small parcel and package delivery, brewery and soda manufacturing, railroad and maintenance of way workers, school bus drivers, and many of Kansas’ transportation industries.
“Cindy Holscher understands that working people are the backbone of Kansas, and she has the record to prove it,” said Mike Scribner, President of the Missouri-Kansas-Nebraska Conference of Teamsters .
“In the legislature, she has always sided with workers and our families.
As governor, she will work to create good-paying jobs and to bring down the cost of living.
The Missouri-Kansas-Nebraska Conference of Teamsters is proud to endorse Cindy Holscher for governor of Kansas.” “I am deeply honored to have the support of the Missouri-Kansas-Nebraska Conference of Teamsters,” said State Senator Cindy Holscher .
“Teamsters drive our economy, deliver the goods our families depend on, and keep Kansas moving every day.
My dad was a union worker, so I know firsthand how a good union job can give a family stability and a path to a better future.
As governor, I will always stand with working people and make sure they have a seat at the table.” Holscher has made strengthening Kansas’ workforce and lowering costs for working families a central part of her campaign.
As governor, she will protect the right to organize, support strong labor standards and apprenticeship programs, invest in infrastructure, and oppose corporate giveaways that leave ordinary Kansans holding the bag.
The Teamsters’ endorsement joins a list of labor organizations supporting Holscher, including: the Kansas AFL-CIO, AFT-Kansas, KNEA/KPAC, SEIU Local 513, and UFCW Local 2.
# # # State Senator Cindy Holscher understands the struggles everyday Kansans face.
Holscher was raised on a farm by parents who worked hard for a living in construction and public schools.
She worked her way through college and is one of the first in her family with a college degree.
She is a mom who sent her three kids to public schools.
Holscher will make sure government serves everyday Kansans, not the wealthy and powerful.
She will fight to fully fund our public schools, lower property taxes, and make things more affordable in Kansas.
