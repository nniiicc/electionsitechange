TOPEKA, KS — The Kansas National Education Association through its Kansas Political Action Committee announced the recommendation of Sen.
Cindy Holscher for governor of Kansas.
Holscher, a longtime champion for Kansas public schools in the state legislature, has made strengthening public education a central part of her campaign for governor.
Holscher first ran for office to end the disastrous Brownback experiment that decimated Kansas public schools.
Once elected, Holscher helped form the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus which successfully ended the failed Brownback policies and restored funding for public schools across the state.
During the state’s teacher shortages, Holscher stepped up again and got her emergency substitute teacher license.
The support underscores the stakes of November’s election for Kansas public education.
Holscher has pledged to build on the progress Kansas has made in restoring its schools while rejecting a return to policies that put tax breaks and special interests ahead of classrooms and families.
“The Kansas Political Action Committee recommended Cindy Holscher for governor because, throughout her service in the Kansas Senate, she has consistently advocated for public education,” said Sherri Schwanz, KPAC Chair and KNEA President.
“This is not the first time KPAC has recommended Cindy.
She was also recommended as a Friendly Incumbent in the 2024 Kansas Senate race.
Cindy understands that public schools are the foundation of thriving communities.”
“I will always stand with our Kansas educators and with working families,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“Our teachers show up every single day for our kids, our families, and our communities.
They deserve a governor who will show up for them, too.
I know what happens when politicians treat our schools like a line item they can cut to pay for giveaways to the wealthy and well-connected.
Kansas families paid the price for that kind of experiment once.
We cannot afford to go back.”
Global Strategy Group recently released a new poll of likely Kansas voters showing that Democrat Cindy Holscher is leading Republican Ty Masterson in the gubernatorial election.
The poll is the first to be publicly released since the primary election.
Holscher starts the race with 47% support, one point ahead of her Republican competitor, Ty Masterson, with 46% support. 7 percent are undecided.
Holscher has also been endorsed by AFGE, AFT-Kansas, UFCW Local 2, and the Kansas AFL-CIO.
State leaders have also lined up in support of Holscher, including Governor Laura Kelly and former Governor John Carlin.
###
Cindy Holscher grew up on a farm with common sense, Midwest values, and a love of her country.
Cindy and her husband Greg have lived in Kansas for over 25 years, and their three kids graduated from public schools.
She first ran for office to end the disastrous Brownback experiment and restore school funding.
As a legislator, Cindy co-founded the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus, and she focused on lowering costs for working-class and middle-class Kansans.
Now, she’s running for governor because Kansas deserves a leader who has a proven track record of fighting for all Kansans.
