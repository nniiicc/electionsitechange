TOPEKA, KS – Today, Senator Cindy Holscher announced Republicans for Cindy, a coalition of twenty-one current and former Republican elected officials supporting her campaign for governor.
Many served alongside both Holscher and Masterson and saw firsthand how she worked across party lines to deliver for Kansas families.
Throughout her time in the legislature, Holscher has brought Republicans, Independents, and Democrats together around practical and pragmatic solutions.
That record began in her first term in the Kansas House, when she co-founded a bipartisan coalition that ended the Brownback tax experiment and restored stability to the state budget.
“Kansans have never cared much about party labels when it comes to fixing real problems,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“When the Brownback tax experiment was bankrupting our schools and roads, Republicans and Democrats came together to clean up the mess that Ty Masterson helped create, and I’m grateful to have these leaders in my corner, because Kansas works best when we put our state ahead of our party.”
“As a life-long Republican and 12-term member of the Kansas House of Representatives, I value common sense, consensus-building elected officials who have the long-term interests of Kansans in mind,” said Rep.
Tom Sloan, a Republican endorsing Holscher. ”Short-term and divisive policies are a disservice to all Kansans.
I worked with Cindy in the Legislature and know that as Governor she will work with members of both political parties to strengthen our public and higher education schools, keep rural hospitals open, and grow the Kansas economy."
