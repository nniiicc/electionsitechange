Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release Ks-Gov: No-Show Masterson Says No To More Debates After Losing The First One For Immediate Release · September 17, 2026 TOPEKA, Kan – Days after a disastrous appearance at the State Fair debate hosted by WIBW Radio, Republican nominee for governor Ty Masterson has rejected future debates against Senator Cindy Holscher.
The Holscher campaign had previously accepted debate invitations by Gray Media and Nexstar for September 24 and October 1 debates, respectively.
Gray Media stations include KCTV (Kansas City), KWCH (Wichita), and WIBW (Topeka).
The Nexstar debate would run on six television stations covering 95% of the state.
Masterson turned down both invitations.
Masterson’s rejection of future debates come just days after Holscher dominated him at the Kansas State Fair debate in Hutchinson, where she pressed him on public school funding, billionaire data center deals, his history of personal bankruptcies, his role in creating and defending the Brownback disaster, and his $217k-per-year no-show job at GoCreate.
Everyday Kansans in the crowd booed him repeatedly and called him a “liar” to his face as he tried to explain away his record.
“Kansans deserve to hear directly from the people asking to lead their state, and that’s why I’m not afraid to debate anywhere, anytime,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“Ty Masterson is running away from voters and hiding from debates because he can’t defend his record of shortchanging everyday Kansas families while doing the bidding of his corporate donors and championing Sam Brownback’s disastrous schemes.
Kansans deserve a governor who always shows up, and Ty Masterson won’t even do that.” ### State Senator Cindy Holscher understands the struggles everyday Kansans face.
Holscher was raised on a farm by parents who worked hard for a living in construction and public schools.
She worked her way through college and is one of the first in her family with a college degree.
She is a mom who sent her three kids to public schools.
Holscher will make sure government serves everyday Kansans, not the wealthy and powerful.
She will fight to fully fund our public schools, lower property taxes, and make things more affordable in Kansas.
