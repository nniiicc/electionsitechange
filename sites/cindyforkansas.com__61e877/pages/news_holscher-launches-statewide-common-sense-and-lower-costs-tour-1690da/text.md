Cindy Holscher Launches Statewide “Common Sense and Lower Costs Tour”
Kansas Gubernatorial Candidate Will Visit Communities Across the State With 16 Stops Planned Over the Next Two Weeks
TOPEKA, KS – Today, Senator Cindy Holscher announced the launch of her statewide Common Sense and Lower Costs Tour, kicking off Saturday, September 26, in Marysville.
Over the next two weeks, Senator Cindy Holscher will travel the state, with at least 16 stops planned, to hear directly from Kansans and share her plan to lower costs for working families.
The tour spends its first week in eastern Kansas before heading west for week two.
“I’m hitting the road to hear from everyone, because the best ideas for Kansas don’t come from insiders in Topeka; they come from the teachers, nurses, and farmers who keep this state running,” said Senator Cindy Holscher.
“Over the next two weeks, I’m going to talk with voters in every corner of this state about the costs squeezing their families, and I’m going to share my vision for a Kansas where common sense leads, not extremism.
This tour is about showing up, listening, and putting Kansans first, and that is exactly how I’ll lead as governor.”
The tour opens with stops in Marysville, Manhattan, Emporia, and the Kickapoo Tribe – with more dates and locations coming soon.
