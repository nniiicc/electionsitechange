Most Kansas voters think marijuana should be legal in our state, but because it isn’t, Kansans buy it out of state.
Legalizing it here at home would raise money for schools and public services, build an industry in Kansas of farmers and small businesses.
And it would bring Kansas in line with 22 other states across the country, including our neighbors to the east and west.
