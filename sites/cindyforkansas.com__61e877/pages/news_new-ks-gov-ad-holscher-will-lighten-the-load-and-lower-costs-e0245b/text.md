Skip to main content About Issues Supporters News Store Get Involved Donate Donate Press Release New KS-Gov Ad: Holscher will lighten the load and lower costs For Immediate Release · September 9, 2026 TOPEKA, KS —Today, Democratic nominee for governor Cindy Holscher launched her first statewide television ad of the general election, highlighting her record of standing up for everyday Kansans and against corrupt politicians who increase our costs by serving their donors instead of us.
The 30-second ad running on Broadcast TV and YouTube statewide emphasizes that Holscher is the only candidate committed to bringing down costs for families and making life a little easier for hard-working Kansans.
"Cindy is focused on making life easier for our families because she gets it," said campaign spokesperson Riley Osen.
"Cindy is a working mom and has spent her time in the legislature fighting to lower costs on everyday essentials.
At a time when many families are struggling to make ends meet because of skyrocketing costs, she remains the best candidate to fight back against the corruption that is costing our families every day.
This ad is a reminder that Cindy is the only candidate in this race offering a path focused on working families and making life more affordable for everyday Kansans." Global Strategy Group recently released a new poll of likely Kansas voters showing that Democrat Cindy Holscher is leading Republican Ty Masterson in the gubernatorial election.
The poll is the first to be publicly released since the primary election.
Holscher starts the race with 47% support , one point ahead of her Republican competitor, Ty Masterson, with 46% support.
7 percent are undecided.
Transcript of “Lighten the Load”: My name is Cindy Holscher, and I know what it means to work hard and stretch a dollar.
I grew up on a farm.
Dad worked construction, and Mom worked at the grade school.
I was raised to believe that if you work hard, you won’t have to struggle to get by, and I raised my kids to believe the same.
But everything costs too much these days.
And instead of looking out for us, politicians look out for themselves and their donors, and leave everyday Kansans stuck with the bill.
That’s why I’m running for governor, to lighten the load for working families like mine.
Cindy Holscher for Governor.
# # # Cindy Holscher grew up on a farm with common sense, Midwest values, and a love of her country.
Cindy and her husband Greg have lived in Kansas for over 25 years, and their three kids graduated from public schools.
She first ran for office to end the disastrous Brownback experiment and restore school funding.
As a legislator, Cindy co-founded the Women’s Bipartisan Caucus, and she focused on lowering costs for working-class and middle-class Kansans.
Now, she’s running for governor because Kansas deserves a leader who has a proven track record of fighting for all Kansans.
