My Priorities
Introduction
Massachusetts is a wonderful place to live, but years of rising taxes, increasing regulations and mandates, and a startling lack of transparency in our legislature have made life harder for families, seniors, schools, and businesses of every size in Massachusetts.
Every budget season is a civil war, pitting seniors against students.
Seniors are getting taxed out of their homes and schools are struggling to offer the basics.
It's not fair or sustainable.
How did we get here?
The Democrat supermajority in our state legislature has been leading us down this trajectory for decades.
They talk a good game, but we have to evaluate them on their results.
It's time to bring more balance into the state legislature.
We need representatives who are truly committed to affordability, reducing taxes, transparency, and who will respect the decisions of local communities and the rights of individuals.
In Massachusetts, it's the Republicans who support those values.
To restore balance and ensure a democratic process, please restore balance by voting for me and other Republicans in the upcoming election.
You can read more about my priorities below.
Restoring Trust in Government
Government must be accountable to the people it serves.
I will fight for greater transparency, fiscal responsibility, and common-sense solutions—including supporting the independent audit of the state legislature approved by 72% of Massachusetts voters in 2024.
Our tax dollars should be spent responsibly, transparently, and directed toward programs that truly serve citizens.
Making Massachusetts More Affordable
Massachusetts has become increasingly unaffordable for working families, young adults, seniors, and small businesses.
Too many young families cannot afford to buy homes.
Seniors face growing property tax burdens.
Families are struggling with high costs of housing, energy, and everyday necessities.
We need to:
• Prioritize responsible state spending
• Reduce unnecessary taxes and regulations
• Increase unrestricted government aid to cities and towns
• Give municipalities more control over local decisions
• Create an environment where families, seniors, young people, and businesses alike can afford to stay and thrive
Restoring Local Control
Decisions affecting our communities are best made by the people who live there.
Over time, state government has taken greater control over local resources while placing more mandates and restrictions on cities and towns.
I support restoring the balance between state and local government and ensuring communities have the flexibility to make decisions that best fit their residents’ needs.
Strengthening Education
As a school committee member, I work to ensure that every child deserves an excellent education that prepares them for the future.
Our schools should emphasize:
• Reading, writing, math, STEM, civics, financial literacy, and the arts
• Career and vocational opportunities
• A healthy learning environment
• Strong partnerships between parents and schools
Parents are essential partners in their children’s education, health, and wellbeing.
Schools should respect parental involvement and communicate openly with families.
Prioritizing Taxpayer Resources and Securing our Communities
Massachusetts taxpayers are facing rising costs, strained public resources, and challenges funding essential services.
Government has a responsibility to prioritize the needs of citizens, taxpayers, seniors, veterans, and families who have built their lives here.
As a first generation American, I absolutely support legal immigration and the contributions that immigrants make to our communities.
However, a state already struggling with affordability, housing, and service demands must carefully consider the financial impact of providing extensive taxpayer-funded benefits and services to people who are here illegally.
We need a balanced approach that:
• Secures our borders and upholds immigration laws
• Prioritizes limited taxpayer resources for residents and those legally entitled to services
• Supports law enforcement and public safety
• Ensures Massachusetts can meet its obligations to students, seniors, veterans, and vulnerable citizens
Responsible government means making difficult choices and ensuring that taxpayer dollars are used wisely.
Supporting Small Businesses
Small businesses are the backbone of our economy.
As a small business owner, I understand the challenges of starting, operating, and growing a business in Massachusetts.
I will work to:
• Reduce unnecessary regulations
• Lower costs for employers
• Expand opportunities for small business growth
• Support policies that allow entrepreneurs to create jobs and succeed
A thriving small business community benefits everyone.
Protecting Our Environment
A healthy environment is the foundation of a healthy community.
I support practical solutions that protect our air, water, soil, and food supply while encouraging innovation and economic growth.
My priorities include:
• Addressing harmful environmental exposures
• Protecting clean drinking water
• Supporting responsible renewable energy projects
• Encouraging regenerative farming practices that improve soil health, optimize nutrients, and reduce chemical dependence
I support smart energy solutions, such as solar canopies over parking lots, that create energy without unnecessarily sacrificing forests and open space.
Improving Public Health
As a health professional with a strong background in nutrition and functional medicine, I believe public health should focus on helping people achieve better health and address the root cause of physical and mental health issues—not simply managing a growing disease burden and forcing simplistic one-size fits all approaches.
We need a more effective approach:
• Prioritizing prevention of chronic disease that detracts from physical and mental resilience
• Addressing environmental factors that negatively affect health (for example; water fluoridation, endocrine disrupting chemicals like atrazine and PFAS, and EMFs)
• Ensuring access to alternative healing modalities through supporting Safe Harbor bills
Public health policies should be built on trust, transparency, respect for individuals and families, and informed consent.
Supporting Law Enforcement and First Responders
Our communities are safer because of the dedication and sacrifice of our first responders.
Police officers and other public safety professionals deserve respect, resources, training, and support.
Public safety challenges should be addressed with thoughtful solutions that help officers do their jobs effectively.
Standing with Veterans
Those who served our country made tremendous sacrifices to protect our freedoms.
We have a responsibility to ensure veterans receive the care, support, and respect they have earned.
Our priorities should include improving access to quality healthcare, removing barriers to services, and ensuring veterans are never forgotten.
Upholding Election Integrity and Civic Engagement
A healthy democracy requires trust in our elections and confidence in our institutions.
I support efforts to ensure accurate voter rolls, secure elections, and transparency in the electoral process while encouraging more citizens to participate in civic life.
I also believe we must restore respectful political dialogue.
As a leader with Braver Angels, a national organization dedicated to improving political discourse, I work to bring people together, listen to different perspectives, and find common ground.
