For more than 25 years, I have built my life, my business, and my family right here in Legislative District 26.
Now I am running for the Arizona House because the place I have called home for a quarter-century deserves a representative who actually knows it, listens to it, and is willing to fight for it.
Building businesses, creating jobs.
I am a small-business owner.
My Phoenix-based company produces private-label, all-natural progesterone and hormone-balance creams sold to retailers across the country.
I studied Business Administration in college, and running a real business has taught me the lesson Arizona needs at the Capitol: when you spend your own money, you sweat every dollar.
Government should treat your tax dollars the same way.
Tackling the housing crisis.
I am also a real estate investor, and I partner with the City of Phoenix to provide rental housing for low-income families.
I have seen up close how broken our housing market has become, and I know the answer is not more bureaucracy or more empty promises.
It is more good actors getting more good homes built, and a Legislature that finally takes affordability seriously.
A lifetime of service.
I am an Eagle Scout, the highest rank in the Boy Scouts of America, and I still try to live by what it taught me: duty, honor, and putting others first.
I am also an elected State Precinct Committeeman, which means I have been doing the unglamorous, on-the-ground work of strengthening our community long before my name was on any ballot.
Drive, discipline, and a big dream.
When I am not running my business or knocking doors, I am usually in the air or at the track.
I am a licensed aircraft pilot and a certified flight instructor.
Flying teaches you that careful preparation and clear thinking can take you anywhere.
I also own thoroughbred racehorses that I race at Turf Paradise and across the country, with one big dream: to one day win the Kentucky Derby.
Why I am running.
I am running for the Arizona House to lower the cost of living, defend our neighborhoods, support our schools and small businesses, and make sure LD26 finally has a representative who works as hard for you as you work for your family.
I am asking for your vote, and I would be honored to earn it. - Frank Roberts Frank Roberts for Arizona State House LD26 Paid for by Frank Roberts for Arizona.
