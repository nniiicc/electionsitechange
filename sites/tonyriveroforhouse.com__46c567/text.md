Tony Rivero is a Conservative Republican seeking to represent one of the two seats in the Arizona House of Representatives in District 27.
After winning election to Peoria's Acacia District on the City Council by a landslide in 2010, he quickly earned a reputation as an advocate for Fiscal Responsibility and Open Government.
He led the crusade against wasteful spending, and tax increases.
He carried his conversative credentials to the Arizona Legislature and was elected by the voters 3 times.
At the legislature Tony voted for balanced budgets, less government, and was a leader in cutting taxes.
Fiscal responsibility will continue to be his top priority as a conservative at the legislature.
