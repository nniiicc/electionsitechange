GABE FIRMENT FOR CONGRESS
PROVEN CONSERVATIVE LEADER
Gabe Firment was elected to the Louisiana House of Representatives in 2019 and currently serves as Chairman of the House Insurance Committee.
He and his wife, Erica, a public school educator, are the proud parents of three children and grandparents of three grandchildren.
Gabe’s roots run deep throughout Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District.
He grew up in Rapides Parish, earned degrees from Louisiana Christian University and Louisiana Tech University, and raised his family in Grant Parish.
As a small business owner, legislator, and community leader, Gabe understands the values, challenges, and opportunities that define rural Louisiana.
He has built a reputation as a principled conservative who fights for lower taxes, stronger families, public safety, and common-sense solutions that put Louisiana families first.
