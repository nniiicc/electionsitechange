As a renter in Bozeman, Brady Bremer sees firsthand just how expensive living in Montana has become.
He has always been a vocal advocate for a better Montana, spending time as an organizer on campus at Montana State University and as a Precinct Representative in House District 64.
During the 2024 election cycle, he spent nearly 200 hours on campus registering students to vote.
As an organizer, he heard from people who are anxious about Bozeman’s rising cost of living and frustrated with leadership in Helena that does too little to stand up for hard working Montanans.
The passion and enthusiasm that voters share is what drives Brady to fight for a more affordable state that creates opportunities and security for every Montanan.
MEET BRADY Brady has spent hundreds of hours organizing throughout Bozeman.
On-campus, off-campus, at homecoming parades or tailgates, he’s heard Montanans raise the same concern again and again: Montana has become far too expensive.
It should be a place where growing families can thrive and every worker can get ahead, not just get by.
Brady is committed to building a more affordable Bozeman by supporting responsible, robust development to lower housing costs, supporting universal childcare so that every family has the opportunity for a head start, and by passing simple tax reforms that make corporations pay their fair share, relieving the burden on hard working Montanans.
