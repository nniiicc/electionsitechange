Heather on the Issues
Fights to Reduce Connecticut’s Tax Burden & Support Small Business
- Lowered the income tax and taxes for small businesses and seniors.
- Reduced taxes on energy, like diesel fuel.
- Fights new tax hikes and burdensome regulations from Hartford.
- Supports the growth and competitiveness of small businesses.
- Advocates for solutions to address punitive property taxes.
Supports Access to Affordable, High-Quality Health Care
- Introduced comprehensive plan to address the mental health crisis, with a focus on children.
- Passed legislation to ensure access to preventative screenings for patients.
- Prioritizes access to health care for women.
- Passed landmark legislation to combat the opioid epidemic.
- Established working groups to address gaps in the health care system.
Fights for Safe Communities & Stands With Law Enforcement
- Supports law enforcement and first responders.
- Passed bill for local departments to receive restitution when a police K9 is injured or killed on duty.
- Ensured law enforcement and first responders have access to PTSD treatment.
- Exposed the state Parole Board for increasing commutations while disregarding the concerns of victims, families, and the interests of public safety — and led the fight for reform.
- Passed solutions to protect women from sexual harassment and domestic violence.
Holds Hartford Accountable to Connecticut Taxpayers
- Exposed mismanagement at the Connecticut Port Authority — and introduced solutions to crack down on corruption.
- Uncovered abuse and mismanagement at Whiting Forensic Hospital and advanced reforms.
- Held a quasi-public energy co-op (CMEEC) accountable for corruption and misuse of ratepayer resources, leading to arrests and jail time for perpetrators.
Fights for Eastern CT’s Unique Assets and Strong Schools
- Fights for Connecticut’s commercial fishing fleet, and recently passed solutions to unlock access to new revenue for our fishermen.
- Secured support for local employers and assets, like the Mystic Aquarium.
- Fights to strengthen support for our military presence, including the Sub Base and Electric Boat.
- Supports eastern Connecticut’s agriculture producers and passed legislation to protect farms from roaming livestock.
- Passed solutions to boost eastern Connecticut tourism, protect Pachaug State Forest and safeguard our coastal environment.
- Secured funding for our schools and champions schools that prepare children for success in the 21st century economy.
- Works with school and community leaders to protect the rights of parents.
Leads the Fight to Tackle High Energy Costs
- Called for Ratepayers to stop being forced to pay for hidden Hartford mandates, fees, and taxes.
- Advocated for unspent state pandemic funds to go toward relief for consumers.
- Supported call for a special session for lawmakers to return to Hartford to act on increased energy costs.
- Proposed a cap on future contracts with utility companies to prevent overcharging.
- Supports greater utilization of Connecticut’s nuclear and hydropower assets to lower the cost of these energy sources.
- Supported plan to explore options to bring more natural gas to the state to reduce electricity costs.
