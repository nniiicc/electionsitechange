CORE COMMITMENTS
01
ENFORCE THE AUDIT
The Attorney General must uphold and defend the laws already on the books including the legislative audit.
No selective enforcement.
No political shielding.
If the law requires transparency and accountability, it will be enforced.
02
PROTECT LOCAL CONTROL
Local zoning decisions belong to cities and towns not unelected state bureaucrats.
Mike opposes state imposed zoning mandates that override Home Rule and coerce municipalities into compliance under threat of funding loss.
03
SERVE THE PEOPLE
The Attorney General is a constitutional officer elected by the people of Massachusetts not an extension of the Legislature and not a political arm of the Governor's Office.
The role demands independence, not loyalty to Beacon Hill insiders.
04
LOWER UTILITY BILLS
Families shouldn't have to choose between heating their homes and paying the bills.
As Attorney General, I'll fight rate hikes and hold utility companies accountable so working families keep more of what they earn.
05
VETTING BALLOT QUESTIONS
Voters deserve ballot questions that are clear, lawful, and properly vetted, but that's not what they've gotten.As Attorney General, I'll bring competence and rigor to this process so every question put before voters is honest, lawful, and truly in their best interests.
06
FRAUD, WASTE AND ABUSE
Taxpayer dollars deserve a watchdog, not a rubber stamp.
I'll aggressively investigate fraud, waste, and abuse wherever it hides to make sure your money is spent honestly and effectively.
MEET MIKE WALSH
Michael Walsh was born and raised in Lynnfield and has practiced law in Massachusetts for 15 years.
He is the youngest graduate in Suffolk University history, earning his bachelor’s degree at 17 and his Juris Doctor at 21.
His legal career spans nearly every forum in the Commonwealth, including Land Court, criminal courts, military courts-martial, the Civil Service Commission, the Labor Relations Commission, and multiple administrative agencies.
Walsh is a general-practice litigator with experience in public employment and labor law, criminal defense, military law, administrative procedure, land use, zoning, commercial paper, and appellate practice.
He has represented workers, small businesses, homeowners, municipalities, and individuals facing government overreach.
His work has been especially focused on defending the rights of military service members, the disabled, and underrepresented parties navigating complex regulatory systems.
Walsh is currently litigating multiple active cases directly opposing the Massachusetts Attorney General, including constitutional and administrative challenges involving legislative audits, public records access, electronic signatures, MBTA zoning mandates, and the state’s implementation of the MBTA Communities Act (Chapter 3A).
Several of these matters are pending before the Superior Court and the Supreme Judicial Court.
He is running to insist that constitutional disputes be decided in court, not by executive fiat.
Walsh believes no constitutional officer should be silenced, no legal question should go unanswered, and no case should be “won” by keeping the opposing side off the field.
That is not leadership.
That is cheating.
