About the event

It became a contest of teams, red versus blue, where the labels matter more than the people wearing them.
This event is a chance to set that aside for an evening.
We're starting the night together in front of the big screen for the first gubernatorial debate.
Watch the candidates make their case, side by side with your neighbors, and then stay for the part that really matters: an honest conversation about what we do with it.
Because once the debate ends, the labels come off.
Join your neighbors for a non-partisan gathering open to every voter, no matter how you're registered or how you vote.
