House of Representatives.
WILLMAR — The Minnesota 7th Congressional District Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party endorsed Erik Osberg for the U.S.
House of Representatives.
The district convention was conducted on Saturday in Granite Falls.
The endorsement was announced in a social media post by DFL Party chair Richard Carlbom.
A post on the Erik Osberg for Congress page on Facebook said Osberg was “humbled and honored by the support.
Our task now becomes uniting western Minnesota in the common cause of restoring our representation at the federal level.” Read Original Article 0 Likes 0 Shares 0 0 0 0 About Erik Osberg Erik Osberg is a husband, father, small business owner, and working-class Minnesotan running to represent Western Minnesota in Congress.
Erik believes public service starts with listening.
Across thousands of conversations with people throughout the district, he has heard the same concerns again and again: families are working hard and still falling behind, family farms are under pressure, rural healthcare is harder to access, public schools need support, and Washington is not focused enough on the people it is supposed to serve.
Erik is running because we can do better.
