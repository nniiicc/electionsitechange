What’s happening to senior care in Western Minnesota… I met with leadership at a senior living facility in Little Falls, MN recently.
I was truly impressed.
The architecture gave the place a community feel.
The tour was like a stroll through town.
Each door front was painted a different color and it looks like a neighborhood.
Appeared to me to be a provider that is doing it right.
I was told funding delays are costing them dearly.
They get 90% of their revenue from Medicaid/MediCare/VA and they have a thin profit margin.
The Regional Director of Operations said immigration is a big issue for them.
They need workforce and new Americans make up a large portion of their talent pool.
I’ve often said I believe the immigration issue is a workforce issue.
She also said as an out-state provider, there are times where they feel forgotten.
She talked about rewards for good providers rather than punishment for all.
Her co-worker referred to it as beyond a “carrot vs. stick” situation.
He used the word sledgehammer.
Our rural communities are aging.
Our elder care facilities are critical for not only quality of life, but for our rural economies as well.
As your Congressperson I pledge to fight for both.
The elderly in rural places will not be forgotten on my watch.
I was raised to respect my elders, not treat them like numbers on a spreadsheet.
Medicare Advantage… We’ve heard a lot from seniors who have been affected by Medicare Advantage cancellations across Western Minnesota.
So we decided to take action.
Erik addressed the issue on social media this week via a video on the topic.
We also launched a webpage with information for seniors affected. erikosberg.com/medicare Please share it with a neighbor who needs it.
Nothing can change the 2027 plan options now, but the next Congress has to hold these private plans accountable, especially in rural Minnesota.
We can do better.
