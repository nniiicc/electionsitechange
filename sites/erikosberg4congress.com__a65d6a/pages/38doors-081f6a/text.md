38 doors. 38 counties.
One big push.
We’re asking 1,000 volunteers to each knock 38 doors — one for every county in Minnesota’s 7th District.
That’s 38,000 conversations, on 38,000 porches, powered by neighbors instead of corporate money.
Campaigns aren’t won in Washington.
They’re won on porches.
Erik believes we can do better — and doing better starts with showing up.
Minnesota’s 7th is 70% rural, 38 counties from Morrison County to Moorhead, and the only way to earn every one of them is the old-fashioned way: face to face, door to door.
The 38 Door Club is our answer to a campaign finance system built for corporations, not people.
Elected officials should be beholden to the people, not profit — and nothing proves that faster than 1,000 neighbors knocking on 1,000 more.
Accessibility & responsiveness create accountability, and it starts with a knock and a conversation, not a check from a PAC.
