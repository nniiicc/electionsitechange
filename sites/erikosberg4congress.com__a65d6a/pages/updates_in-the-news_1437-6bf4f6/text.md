Voting Events Updates Get Involved Donate Erik Osberg for Congress > Blog > In The News > September 23, 2026 Erik Osberg By Erik Osberg September 23, 2026 0 0 Shares Congressional candidate holds fish fry town hall in Breckenridge BRECKENRIDGE — Minnesota’s 7th Congressional District candidate Erik Osberg held a Fish Fry Town Hall at the Breckenridge Community Center Tuesday as part of a campaign pledge to hold a town hall in every county in the district before the Nov.
3 election.
The free event, open to the public regardless of political affiliation, combined a community fish fry with an opportunity for residents to ask questions directly of the candidate.
The Sept.
22 stop marked the 306th event since Osberg’s campaign launched in March 2025.

Erik believes public service starts with listening.
Across thousands of conversations with people throughout the district, he has heard the same concerns again and again: families are working hard and still falling behind, family farms are under pressure, rural healthcare is harder to access, public schools need support, and Washington is not focused enough on the people it is supposed to serve.
Erik is running because we can do better.
