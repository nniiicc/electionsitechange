Congressional candidate holds fish fry town hall in Breckenridge
BRECKENRIDGE — Minnesota’s 7th Congressional District candidate Erik Osberg held a Fish Fry Town Hall at the Breckenridge Community Center Tuesday as part of a campaign pledge to hold a town hall in every county in the district before the Nov. 3 election.
The free event, open to the public regardless of political affiliation, combined a community fish fry with an opportunity for residents to ask questions directly of the candidate.
The Sept. 22 stop marked the 306th event since Osberg’s campaign launched in March 2025.
