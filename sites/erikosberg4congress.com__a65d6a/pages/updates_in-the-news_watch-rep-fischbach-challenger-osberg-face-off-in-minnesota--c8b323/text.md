Fischbach, challenger Osberg face off in Minnesota U.S.
House debate October 15, 2026 Erik Osberg Republican Rep.
Michelle Fischbach will face Democratic challenger and small business owner Erik Osberg on the debate stage Thursday.
Fischbach is seeking her fourth term in the U.S.
House.
The Cook Political Report with Amy Walter rates the race as a Solid R.
Fischbach’s district – Minnesota’s 7th Congressional District – is the most conservative in the state.
The district is also the largest geographically in Minnesota, spanning most of the state’s western border with the Dakota states and reaching northward to the Canadian border.
