Every person.
Every community.
No exceptions.
When this campaign launched, the first thing we did was recite the Pledge of Allegiance.
And when we finished, Erik talked about the final six words:
“With liberty and justice for all.”
Not for some.
Not just for the people we agree with.
Not just for the people with power, money, or the right connections.
For all.
That includes; indigenous communities, immigrant neighbors, the LGBTQ+ community, women, men, children, the elderly & beyond.
That is not a partisan statement.
It is the standard this country set for itself — in the words we ask schoolchildren to memorize and new citizens to swear by.
Right now, we are not living up to it.
Too much of our politics is built on fear, anger, cruelty, and division.
Too many leaders use people as targets instead of treating them as neighbors.
And too often, the people hurt most are the ones with the least power to protect themselves.
Erik believes that is wrong.
Western Minnesota is full of people who disagree on plenty of things and still manage to be decent to each other.
People show up for neighbors.
They give folks the benefit of the doubt.
They judge people by what they do, not by the label someone else puts on them.
That is the standard Erik will bring to Congress.
We can do better.
Erik’s Priorities
Erik will fight to:
- Defend equal protection under the law for every person.
Regardless of age, race, color, gender or creed
- Represent every constituent, whether they voted for him or not
- Reject politics that use fear, cruelty, or division as a weapon
- Restore decency, honesty, and empathy to public service
- Protect access to the ballot, courts, housing, employment, education, and public life
- Treat disagreement with seriousness instead of contempt
- Put people and communities ahead of party labels and political theater
- Hold himself to the Golden Rule: treat people the way you want to be treated
…by sending a representative who treats every constituent with dignity, refuses to use division as a political weapon, and holds to a simple standard: liberty and justice — real liberty, real justice — for all.
Reject Politics That Divide Us
Division does not happen by accident.
When political leaders use fear, name-calling, and us-versus-them language, it is often because keeping people angry at each other is easier than solving real problems.
The outrage, the personal attacks, the constant attempt to turn neighbors into enemies — none of that helps working families, farmers, seniors, students, small businesses, or rural communities.
Erik believes Western Minnesota needs something better.
He will not treat people as props in someone else’s culture war.
He will not demean constituents who disagree with him.
And he will not use cruelty as a campaign strategy.
The people of this district are not talking points.
They are neighbors.
Defend Equal Protection Under the Law
The Fourteenth Amendment guarantees equal protection under the law.
That promise should mean what it says.
Erik believes every person should be treated equally by their government regardless of race, ethnicity, age, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, income, or background.
Equal protection is not a special privilege.
It is a constitutional guarantee.
In Congress, Erik will oppose efforts to single out groups for unequal treatment and support strong civil rights enforcement that protects people’s access to housing, employment, education, healthcare, the ballot, and public life.
The law should protect people fairly.
No exceptions.
Restore Decency to Public Service
Civility is not weakness.
Treating people with respect, listening before judging, and disagreeing without demeaning are signs of strength.
Too much of politics has become name-calling, performance, and bad-faith arguments designed to humiliate instead of solve problems.
Erik believes better leadership starts with a better standard.
That does not mean avoiding hard conversations.
It means having them honestly.
It means telling the truth, listening to people who disagree, and remembering that public service is supposed to be about service.
Erik will work with people in good faith when there is a chance to help Western Minnesota.
And when he disagrees, he will do it honestly.
Represent Every Constituent
Real representation means everyone in the district has a voice.
Not just the loudest voices.
Not just the biggest donors.
Not just the people who voted for you.
Not just the people who belong to your party.
Everyone.
Erik has spent this campaign traveling across Western Minnesota, listening to people who agree with him and people who do not.
That is not just a campaign tactic.
It is how he believes representation should work.
In Congress, Erik will govern the same way: listening first, taking every constituent seriously, and remembering that the job is to represent all of Western Minnesota.
Practice the Golden Rule
The Golden Rule is not complicated:
Treat people the way you want to be treated.
That standard does not change based on party, background, faith, identity, income, ZIP code, or whether someone agrees with you.
Erik believes public officials should hold themselves to that standard every day.
That means showing up.
Telling the truth.
Listening with respect.
Taking responsibility.
And remembering that every person has dignity.
This is not about being soft.
It is about being decent.
And decency matters.
Put People Before Politics
Washington spends too much time rewarding outrage and too little time solving problems.
Erik believes the people of Western Minnesota need leadership that is grounded, honest, and focused on real life: healthcare, schools, farms, jobs, housing, public safety, constitutional rights, and the future of our communities.
That starts with remembering who the job is for.
Not party leaders.
Not special interests.
Not cable news.
Not social media.
The people.
