“My biggest concern is corporations.
We have to close loopholes so that corporations that make money in America pay taxes in America.
There’s mega corporations, they’re making billions and billions of dollars and they’re not paying their fair share,” Erik Osberg explained.
Osberg said he believes our country’s $40 billion debt is a collections problem.
