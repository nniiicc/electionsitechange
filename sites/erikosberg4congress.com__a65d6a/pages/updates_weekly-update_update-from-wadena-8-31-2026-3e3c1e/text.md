Education quality shouldn’t be driven by your zip code… “The quality of your education shouldn’t depend on your zip code.” I visited with two Superintendents this week to listen to their needs and talk about solutions.
One of my takeaways was the importance of having someone fighting for rural education.
We need to fully fund special education.
We need to protect funding for Head Start.
We need to protect Title I Funding.
And we still need local solutions for local problems.
I want to take a moment to thank all the educators out there who are returning to the classrooms.
I hope you can hold onto your WHY.
Bless your hearts for doing what you do.
A Look Back More miles, more conversations, great connections… Two Fish Fry Town Halls, two full crowds.
130 people outdoors at Maple Hills Orchard near Frazee, then standing room only in Elbow Lake with well over 100.
Huge thanks to our hosts Gary & Jonna & to the Becker Co. & Grant Co.
DFL.
Questions ranged from the Constitution to healthcare, agriculture & the national debt, but corruption kept floating to the top.
My answer hasn’t changed in 18 months: get corporate $ out of politics.
