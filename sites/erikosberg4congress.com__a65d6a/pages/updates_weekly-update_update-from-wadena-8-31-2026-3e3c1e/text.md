A Look Back
More miles, more conversations, great connections…
Two Fish Fry Town Halls, two full crowds. 130 people outdoors at Maple Hills Orchard near Frazee, then standing room only in Elbow Lake with well over 100.
Huge thanks to our hosts Gary & Jonna & to the Becker Co. & Grant Co.
DFL.

My answer hasn’t changed in 18 months: get corporate $ out of politics.
From there it was meetings in Chippewa & Yellow Medicine Counties, the Wood Lake Fair Parade, a visit to the new Minnesota Veterans Home in Montevideo, & a stage shared with our current Congressperson at the Todd-Wadena Electric Cooperative forum.
We capped it in Clay County at the TAK. 14 Fish Fry Town Halls down, 24 to go.
