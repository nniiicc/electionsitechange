Privacy Policy for Erik Osberg for Congress
Last Updated: March 28, 2025
This Privacy Policy outlines how Erik Osberg for Congress (the “Campaign,” “we,”, “E4C”, or “us”) collects, uses, and shares information about you when you interact with our website, mobile sites, and services that link to this policy (collectively, the “Site”).
By using the Site, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of information as described in this Privacy Policy.
We may update this policy from time to time, and any changes will be communicated by updating the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.
Please review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about our practices.
Collection of Information
We collect information that you provide to us directly, such as when you sign up for updates, request information, volunteer, donate, or contact us via email or social media.
The information we collect includes:
- Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, address, occupation, employer, payment information (for donations), etc.
- Donor Information: For donations exceeding $200, we are required by federal law to collect and disclose your name, address, occupation, and employer to the Federal Election Commission (FEC).
We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit the Site, such as:
- Log and Usage Information: Information on how you access and use the Site, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, etc.
- Device Information: Information about your computer or mobile device, including device identifiers and operating system.
- Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and web beacons to enhance the user experience and gather data about Site usage.
- Use of Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and improve our Site and services.
- Respond to your inquiries, process donations, and fulfill requests.
- Send you updates and information about the Campaign, events, and volunteer opportunities.
- Communicate with you regarding your participation, donations, and other Campaign-related matters.
- Monitor and analyze Site usage and trends.
- Personalize your experience and deliver targeted content.
- Comply with legal requirements and FEC regulations.
Sharing of Information
We may share your information as follows:
- With service providers and vendors that assist us in managing the Site and processing transactions.
- With other campaigns, candidates, and organizations that share similar political goals.
- As required by law, such as in response to legal processes or FEC reporting requirements.
- With your consent or as directed by you.
We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot be used to identify you.
Text Messaging Privacy
No Third-Party Sharing of Text Message Data
Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
Text Message Opt-Out Instructions
If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with either “STOP” or “UNSUBSCRIBE” to the number from which you received the message.
You will not receive further text messages unless you opt back in.
Links to Other Websites
The Site may contain links to third-party websites.
These external sites have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing personal information.
We are not responsible for the practices of these third-party sites.
Social Media and Sharing Features
The Site may include social sharing tools (e.g., Facebook “Like” button) that allow you to share content with others.
Please note that your use of these features is subject to the privacy policies of the respective social media platforms.
Advertising and Analytics
We may allow third-party vendors, including Google, to use cookies and tracking technologies to deliver advertisements and analyze Site usage.
These third parties may track your activities across different websites for targeted advertising.
For more information on how to opt out, visit aboutads.info/choices.
Transfer of Information
The Campaign is based in the United States, and your information may be processed and stored in the U.S.
If you are located outside of the U.S., your data may be transferred to and stored in jurisdictions with different data protection standards.
Your Choices
Cookies: You can adjust your browser settings to remove or reject cookies, though this may impact the functionality of the Site.
Promotional Communications: You can opt out of receiving promotional emails or text messages by following the instructions provided in those communications.
Text Message Opt-Out: If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with either “STOP” or “UNSUBSCRIBE” to the number from which you received the message.
You will not receive further text messages unless you opt back in.
California Privacy Rights: California residents can request details on how their information is shared for direct marketing purposes by contacting us.
Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@erikosberg4congress.com
