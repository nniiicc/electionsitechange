Erik’s Bio
Erik Osberg is a lifelong Minnesotan shaped by the values of hard work and community.
His career has spanned sports journalism, financial services, marketing, business ownership, and public sector work—always with a focus on serving others.
Erik is a leader & a problem solver.
Erik understands the challenges facing western Minnesota, from struggling family farms to underfunded schools and a strained healthcare system.
He’s running for Congress to fight for working families, bring honesty and accountability to politics, and stand up for rural Minnesota.
Erik & his wife live in Wadena, MN where they raised 3 kids since moving there 20 years ago.
Erik has served on numerous non-profit boards and has coached.
A graduate of Upsala Area Schools, he worked his way through college, spending time in construction before earning his degree from Hamline University.
He is an avid angler & if given a choice, he’d rather be fishing.
