Make sure you are ready.
Minnesota voters can register, check their registration, vote early, vote by mail, or vote in person on Election Day.
The easiest way to make a plan is to use the official Minnesota Secretary of State voting tools.
Check Your Registration Register to Vote Find Your Polling Place Request an Absentee Ballot Important 2026 General Election Dates Ballots available for in-person voting: Friday, September 18 Start of Early Voting*: Friday, October 16 Last day to vote early in person: Monday, November 2 *Early Voting is the process that allows a voter to cast their ballot directly into a ballot counter before Election Day.
Ways to Vote Vote Early Minnesota voters can vote early before Election Day.
Early voting is a good option if you want flexibility, will be away from home, or simply want to make sure your vote is cast.
Learn About Early Voting Vote by Mail You can request an absentee ballot and vote by mail.
Be sure to request and return your ballot with enough time for it to be received by the deadline.
Request Your Ballot Vote on Election Day You can vote in person at your assigned polling place on Election Day.
Polling places can change, so check your location before you go.
