Make your plan to vote
Every vote matters.
Make sure you are ready.
Minnesota voters can register, check their registration, vote early, vote by mail, or vote in person on Election Day.
The easiest way to make a plan is to use the official Minnesota Secretary of State voting tools.

Important 2026 General Election Dates
Ballots available for in-person voting: Friday, September 18
Start of Early Voting*: Friday, October 16
Last day to vote early in person: Monday, November 2
*Early Voting is the process that allows a voter to cast their ballot directly into a ballot counter before Election Day.
