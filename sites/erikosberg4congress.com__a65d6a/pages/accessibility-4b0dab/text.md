Voting Events Updates Get Involved Donate Accessibility Erik Osberg for Congress > Accessibility Accessibility Statement Updated: February 2025​ General ErikOsberg4Congress.com is committed to ensuring accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities.
We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information, services, and opportunities, and we strive to make our website easy to use and fully accessible.
Accessibility Features ErikOsberg4Congress.com utilizes accessibility tools, including the UserWay Website Accessibility Widget, powered by a dedicated accessibility server.
This software helps improve compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1), ensuring a more inclusive online experience.
Enabling the Accessibility Menu You can enable the accessibility menu by clicking the icon that appears in the corner of the page.
Once activated, please allow a moment for the menu to load fully.
This feature provides enhanced usability options for users with disabilities.
Disclaimer We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of ErikOsberg.com and ensure that all users can navigate our site with ease.
While we strive to meet the highest accessibility standards, some content may not yet be fully adapted.
We appreciate your patience and feedback as we work toward full compliance.
Need Assistance?
If you experience any difficulty accessing content on ErikOsberg.com or require assistance, please contact us during normal business hours.
We are happy to help ensure your experience is smooth and accessible.
