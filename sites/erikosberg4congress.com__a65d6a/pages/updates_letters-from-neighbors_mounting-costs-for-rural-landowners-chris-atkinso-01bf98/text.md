The cost to mow grass and maintain a farm continues to rise.
Our place in central Minnesota is about seven to eight acres, with approximately four acres of mowed land.
Based on current diesel prices, it becomes increasingly difficult to maintain acreage.
The current cost of diesel fuel in Minnesota is 6.22 (AAA Minnesota), October 1, 2026.
Using the Farm Calculator Hub, it costs approximately 16.50 to mow our four acres based on current fuel costs.
This estimate does not include other expenses like oil and grease.
Imagine a farmer with 100 acres pulling an implement, the cost in fuel alone sits at 165.00.
Most farms today exceed 100 acres.
