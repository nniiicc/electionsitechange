38 calls. 38 counties.
One big push.
We’re asking 1,000 volunteers to each call 38 phones — one for every county in Minnesota’s 7th District.
That’s 38,000 conversations, on 38,000 phones, powered by neighbors instead of corporate money.
Campaigns aren’t won in Washington.
They’re won on the phones.
Erik believes we can do better — and doing better starts with showing up.
Minnesota’s 7th is 70% rural, 38 counties from Morrison County to Moorhead, and the only way to earn every one of them is the old-fashioned way: person to person, call to call.
The 38 Call Club is our answer to a campaign finance system built for corporations, not people.
Elected officials should be beholden to the people, not profit — and nothing proves that faster than 1,000 neighbors calling 1,000 more.
Accessibility & responsiveness create accountability, and it starts with a knock and a conversation, not a check from a PAC.
