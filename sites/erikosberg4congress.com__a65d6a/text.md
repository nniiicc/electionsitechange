This campaign believes in fiscal responsibility, personal freedoms & local solutions to local problems.

Erik is running because we can do better.
#1 Defend the Constitution Defend our constitutional rights, protect fair elections, and stand up for the institutions that keep power in the hands of the people.
#2 Get Corporate Money Out of Politics We believe in a constitutional amendment ending Citizens United.
We believe in banning individual stock trading for congress.
We want dark money out of politics.
#3 Healthcare for All Make healthcare more affordable and accessible, especially for rural families, seniors, and communities that rely on local clinics and hospitals.
#4 Federal Funding for Public Education Protect & expand strong federal support for public schools, students, teachers, and the future of our communities.
#5 Strengthen Rural Economies Support farms, small businesses, local innovation, and rural economies.
We need federal support for local solutions to local problems like housing, childcare & public safety.
Meet Erik Grounded.
Principled.
Neighbor.
Erik Osberg is a husband, father, former small business owner, current county employee, and working-class Minnesotan.
He is a rural candidate for a rural district.
Across his life and career, Erik has worked in the financial sector, public sector, media, trades, and was a small business owner.
He knows what it means to work hard, solve problems, and show up for your neighbors.
Erik is running for Congress because Western Minnesota needs leadership that listens, tells the truth, and gets to work.
No more blame games.
No more excuses.
Just real representation for the people who live here.
