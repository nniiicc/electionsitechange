District 97 Link to the map South Carolina House District 97 is a large, mostly rural district in the Lowcountry that stretches across parts of Dorchester, Colleton, and surrounding counties.
The “yellow” area on the map includes communities such as St.
George, Harleyville, Ridgeville, Cottageville, Bowman, and parts of Walterboro and Holly Hill, along with smaller towns and rural areas like Reevesville, Ruffin, and Canadys.
Overall, the district covers a wide mix of small towns, agricultural areas, and growing communities along key corridors like I-26 and Highway 78.
