Uncommon Sense
Following strong social media censorship, I took a page out of Thomas Paine's playbook and wrote a pamphlet.
"Uncommon Sense: The Forgotten Beliefs of the American Revolution and Natural Rights" is 36 pages overviewing the discovery of our rights, their importance, and how this affects us today politically and sociologically.
In a way I consider it my manifesto.
