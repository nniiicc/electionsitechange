ABOUT ME
Hi, I'm Alec Pavlik
WHERE I COME FROM
I was born and raised in Pinellas County, Florida.
There I developed a relationship with God, and saw a growing divide in America's culture.
This inspired me to learn more about what gave me hope, and how to bring hope to others.
In my own time I studied mental health, history, theology, and Constitutional Law; while in school I studied Worldview, Apologetics, Ethics, Film Production, Social Media Marketing, and Local Government.
During the Summer I would work for my family's Construction Company; which over the last decade I've served as a Laborer, Marketing Directer, Office Clerk, and Drafter for.
It's in this industry that I've learned the value of hard work, the expense of red tape, and the damage of shakedown lawsuits.
During all of this time, instead of buying a car or pursuing a degree, I began renovating a house in Putnam County (an area my family has visited my whole life).
This began in 2017 when I was 19 years old.
Instead of flipping the property, I moved in shortly after.
This is where I still live today with my wife.
WHO I AM
I'm a follower of Jesus, whom I trust to be the Messiah.
Like anyone's foundational beliefs, this influences how I view reality and try to live my life.
In my opinion the Holy Bible seems to promote the Law of Noncontradiction, the Golden Rule, and Natural Rights; which greatly influences my politics and how I treat others.
Most people who know me wouldn't think I'm a very political person, because inherently, I am not.
I love animation, music, video games, and deep conversations.
I hate confrontation and want to bring people closer together; I wouldn't have gotten into politics if I didn't feel our country hangs in the balance.
With how difficult life in America has become, I believe many once apolitical people are feeling the same way.
