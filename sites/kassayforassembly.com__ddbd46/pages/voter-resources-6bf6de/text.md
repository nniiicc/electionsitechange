Make Your Plan
Vote By Mail
Any New York voter can vote by mail.
The Suffolk County BOE must receive your request more than 10 days before the Nov 3rd election.
If you receive a mail-in ballot, be sure to follow instructions fully and mail it postmarked on or before November 3rd.
