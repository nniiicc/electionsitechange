She has worked to deeply understand the unique needs and goals of each community in her district so that she can deliver funding and legislation that’ll help us all thrive.
Beyond these community-specific results, she has furthered our shared goal of making our communities safer, healthier, and more affordable.
Rebecca helped to: lower middle-class income tax rates to their lowest since the 1950s; expand access to child care, pre-K, and free school meals; provide utility relief for working families; and address public safety concerns from e-bikes and illegal cannabis sales to hate crimes and auto insurance fraud.
A small business owner, environmental educator, and experienced leader, Rebecca’s knowledge of local issues and solutions-oriented leadership have proven effective in delivering meaningful support for our communities.
