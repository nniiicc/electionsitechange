Every family in Georgia carries a quiet concern for the people they love, whether it is ensuring an aging parent is safe in senior care, making sure a child in foster care gets a real chance at a future, or protecting a loved one with special needs from being taken advantage of by predators.
As Senate Judiciary Chairman, Brian turned those personal concerns into law, carrying landmark reforms for senior care, foster care college opportunities, and caregiver background checks.
As your Attorney General, Brian will use the full force of state law enforcement to ensure predators, scammers, and abusers who target vulnerable Georgians are brought to justice.
Vote for Brian Strickland for your next Georgia Attorney General this November 2026.
Core Pillars: Connecting Senate Action to the Attorney General’s Office Protecting Georgia Seniors from Abuse, Scams, and Neglect As Attorney General The seniors who built our state should never live in fear of exploitation.
As Attorney General, Brian will deploy fraud units to hunt down scammers who target retirement savings and aggressively prosecute bad actors who abuse or neglect elderly Georgians in care facilities.
Senate Record Carried Senior Care Home Reform (HB 987) through the Senate and supported the Opioid Settlement Fund distribution (SB 500).
Standing Up for Foster Youth & Vulnerable Children As Attorney General No child in state care should ever be left without a safe bed or a real future.
As Attorney General, Brian will hold state agencies accountable, enforce child safety standards, and ensure every child in foster care is protected by the full weight of Georgia law.
Senate Record Authored foster custody reform (SB 133) to keep foster children out of hotel rooms and state offices, and authored the postsecondary tuition waiver (SB 107) for foster youth.
A Fierce Advocate for Special Needs Families As Attorney General Raising a child or family member with special needs requires relentless dedication.
Brian’s core commitment as Attorney General is to make Georgia the number one state in the nation for special needs families and mental health services, ensuring state government shows up to support families when they need it most.
Senate Record Supported comprehensive mental health access and family care measures.
Ensuring Peace of Mind in Caregiver Trust As Attorney General When you invite a caregiver into your home to look after an ill spouse, an aging parent, or a child, you deserve complete transparency.
As Attorney General, Brian will enforce background check laws to guarantee bad actors are kept away from vulnerable loved ones.
Senate Record Authored Georgia’s Long-Term Care Background Check Program (SB 406) and the Grandparents Visitation Rights Act (SB 576).
