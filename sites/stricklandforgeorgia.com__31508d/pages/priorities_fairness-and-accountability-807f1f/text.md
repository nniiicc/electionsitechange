Core Pillars: Connecting Senate Action to the Attorney General’s Office Georgia’s Lawyer, Not Washington’s: Equal Justice Under Law As Attorney General The oath of the Attorney General is to the Constitution, not a political party or Washington agenda.
As Attorney General, Brian will enforce Georgia’s laws firmly and impartially, protecting personal privacy and individual liberties from federal overreach.
Senate Record Authored fair court discovery reform (HB 352) and sponsored measure HR 251 for nonpartisan probate judge elections.
Elections You Can Trust: Restoring Voter Confidence As Attorney General Secure, transparent elections are the absolute bedrock of a free republic.
As Attorney General, Brian will investigate voting irregularities, enforce election security standards, and ensure every legal vote is protected so all Georgians have total confidence in election outcomes.
Senate Record Supported election integrity measures as Senate Judiciary Chairman.
A Level Playing Field for Family Businesses As Attorney General Behind every small business is a local family that took a major financial risk.
Brian will use the Attorney General’s office to enforce market fairness so large corporations don’t manipulate regulations to crush local shops or exploit consumers.
Senate Record Authored occupational licensing reform (SB 207) to help Georgians earn an honest living, and shut down exploitative booking photo mills (SB 482).
Cracking Down on Medicaid Fraud & Tax Dollar Theft As Attorney General Every single dollar stolen from Medicaid is stolen from vulnerable patients who genuinely need care.
As Attorney General, Brian will aggressively deploy the Medicaid Fraud Control Division to hunt down criminal rings stealing state tax dollars and hold them accountable.
Senate Record Advanced accountability standards for state programs as Judiciary Chairman.
