When you tuck your children into bed at night or walk through your local town square, you deserve the peace of mind that comes from knowing violent criminals, street gangs, and drug cartels are being held accountable.
As Senate Judiciary Chairman, Brian didn’t just talk about tough choices.
He authored the laws that back our police officers, choke off the deadly narcotics supply, and put human traffickers behind bars.
As your next Attorney General, he will bring that same relentless drive to state law enforcement to ensure every Georgia community remains a safe place to live, work, and raise a family.
Vote for Brian Strickland for your next Georgia Attorney General this November 2026.
Core Pillars Standing Shoulder-to-Shoulder with Law Enforcement As Attorney General As Georgia’s chief legal officer, Brian will use the full authority of the Attorney General’s office to stand firm with local sheriffs, police departments, and prosecutors, ensuring our first responders have the backing and legal tools they need to protect our streets.
Senate Record Authored the Daniel D.
Podsiadly Jr.
Act (SB 371 / HB 105) so officers injured protecting us never lose their paycheck.
Eradicating Deadly Street Narcotics & Defending Our Children As Attorney General Deadly street narcotics are a threat destroying Georgia families.
As Attorney General, Brian will target the transnational cartels and local dealers pushing poison into our neighborhoods, using state-wide investigative power to protect our kids.
Senate Record Cosponsored Senate Bill 79 to crack down on poison entering our communities.
Relentless Prosecution of Human Traffickers As Attorney General Human trafficking is modern-day slavery that preys on our most vulnerable.
Brian will establish a dedicated Special Victims task force within the Attorney General’s Office to hunt down trafficking rings and give survivors a voice and a pathway to healing.
Senate Record Authored the Anti-Human Trafficking Protective Response Act (SB 158) and the Survivors First Act (SB 435).
Dismantling Street Gangs & Violent Networks As Attorney General Gangs that recruit children and terrorize neighborhoods will find no quarter in Georgia.
Brian will aggressively enforce state gang statutes to dismantle criminal networks across city and county lines.
Senate Record Spearheaded the passage of tougher gang laws (SB 44) as Senate Judiciary Chairman.
Fixing Broken Systems & Restoring Swift Justice As Attorney General A justice system that stalls is a system that fails victims and puts communities at risk.
As Attorney General, Brian will ensure our court systems run efficiently so dangerous offenders are taken off our streets without delay.
Senate Record Authored SB 533 to clear the jail competency backlog and reform court procedures.
