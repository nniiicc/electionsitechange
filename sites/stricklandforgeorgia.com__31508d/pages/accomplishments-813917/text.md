Brian's Record
Brian is not asking Georgians to judge him only on what he promises.
He has a record, and it shows where he stands.
Keeping Georgia Safe
Worked on laws to confront gangs and organized crime, including efforts to keep children from being recruited into them, and supported stronger laws against deadly street narcotics and human trafficking.
Helped protect the pay of law enforcement officers injured in the line of duty, so an officer hurt while protecting the public does not face a financial crisis at home.
Protecting the Vulnerable
Wrote Senate Bill 133 to end the housing of foster children in state offices and hotel rooms.
It passed the Senate 53 to 1 and opened the door to college for young people aging out of foster care.
Worked on laws to protect seniors from abuse, improve care in nursing homes, and let families run background checks on the people they hire to work inside their homes.
Fairness and Accountability
Guaranteed a fair discovery process in misdemeanor cases and fixed the backlog in competency hearings that left people waiting in jail for months.
Established nonpartisan elections for probate court judges so those races stay focused on qualifications and the work of the court.
Land, Water, and Farms
Helped create and fund the Georgia Farmland Conservation Fund to protect the working farms that feed the state and carry its heritage forward.
Drafted legislation to address the release of hazardous substances like ethylene oxide, because communities deserve a serious response when dangerous chemicals threaten public health.
