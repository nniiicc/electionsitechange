He has a record, and it shows where he stands.
Keeping Georgia Safe Gangs and Organized Crime Worked on laws to confront gangs and organized crime, including efforts to keep children from being recruited into them, and supported stronger laws against deadly street narcotics and human trafficking.
Supporting Officers Helped protect the pay of law enforcement officers injured in the line of duty, so an officer hurt while protecting the public does not face a financial crisis at home.
Protecting the Vulnerable Foster Care Reform Wrote Senate Bill 133 to end the housing of foster children in state offices and hotel rooms.
It passed the Senate 53 to 1 and opened the door to college for young people aging out of foster care.
Protecting Seniors Worked on laws to protect seniors from abuse, improve care in nursing homes, and let families run background checks on the people they hire to work inside their homes.
Fairness and Accountability A Fair Process Guaranteed a fair discovery process in misdemeanor cases and fixed the backlog in competency hearings that left people waiting in jail for months.
Nonpartisan Courts Established nonpartisan elections for probate court judges so those races stay focused on qualifications and the work of the court.
Land, Water, and Farms Farmland Conservation Helped create and fund the Georgia Farmland Conservation Fund to protect the working farms that feed the state and carry its heritage forward.
Hazardous Releases Drafted legislation to address the release of hazardous substances like ethylene oxide, because communities deserve a serious response when dangerous chemicals threaten public health.
