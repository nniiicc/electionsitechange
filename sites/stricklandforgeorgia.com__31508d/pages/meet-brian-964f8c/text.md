Why I Serve
“Behind every case are real people whose lives can be changed forever.
The Attorney General should never forget that.”
Brian Strickland has spent his whole life in Henry County.
He was born and raised there, still lives in McDonough, and his family's roots in the county go back generations.
He graduated from Henry County High School and earned an economics degree from Valdosta State University before going on to law school and coming home to practice law, where he still practices in McDonough today.
He and his wife, Lindsay, are raising their two young sons in the same community where Brian grew up.
That is not a talking point for him.
It is the reason he does the work he does.
