Having grown up on a family farm and built his legal practice as a small-town lawyer, Brian knows firsthand the deep pride and hard work it takes to steward the land.
Families across Georgia deserve clean drinking water, safe communities, and protection from out-of-state polluters and heavy-handed federal bureaucrats.
As Senate Judiciary Chairman, Brian authored strict environmental reporting laws to protect local water supplies and stood up for local communities in court.
As your Attorney General, Brian will defend property owners, support responsible economic growth, and protect Georgia’s natural resources for generations to come.
Vote for Brian Strickland for your next Georgia Attorney General this November 2026.
Core Pillars: Connecting Senate Action to the Attorney General’s Office Standing Up for Georgia's Family Farms & Agriculture As Attorney General Agriculture is Georgia’s #1 industry.
As Attorney General, Brian will stand shoulder-to-shoulder with farm families, ensuring Washington bureaucrats do not push burdensome federal mandates that threaten family-owned operations.
Senate Record Raised on a family farm; consistently voted to protect property rights and reduce regulatory burdens on Georgia farmers.
Protecting Clean Water & Rapid Disclosure of Chemical Spills As Attorney General Clean water is vital for healthy families and thriving farms.
As Attorney General, Brian will aggressively enforce state environmental disclosure laws, holding chemical operators accountable if they pollute our water systems or endanger local communities.
Senate Record Authored SB 426 requiring mandatory, rapid public disclosure of ethylene oxide releases and hazardous chemical spills.
In private legal practice, Brian stood with local residents against chemical plant misconduct (BioLab).
Pushing Back Against Federal Bureaucracy & Overreach As Attorney General The federal government should not be dictating how Georgians manage their private land or water.
Brian will use the legal power of the Attorney General’s office to challenge federal overreach in court whenever Washington tries to bypass state authority.
Senate Record Led legal and legislative efforts to safeguard state sovereignty over land and natural resources.
Supporting Responsible Growth While Preserving Our Heritage As Attorney General Georgia can remain America’s top state for business while preserving our parks, waterways, and rural communities.
As Attorney General, Brian will maintain a fair legal framework that supports economic expansion without sacrificing our natural heritage.
Senate Record Supported state conservation initiatives while fostering a booming business environment in Georgia.
