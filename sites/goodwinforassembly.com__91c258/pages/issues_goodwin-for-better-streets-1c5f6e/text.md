GOODWIN FOR BETTER STREETS
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is committed to aggressively addressing the issue of potholes in Milwaukee to improve road safety and infrastructure quality for all residents.
With a background in public works as a former Milwaukee County Supervisor and Chairman of the Transportation Committee, Russell has the knowledge and experience to tackle this pressing issue.
In response to the 14,981 potholes reported in the city in 2023, Russell will prioritize action to repair and prevent potholes through proactive measures and strategic planning.
Russell will work towards enhancing road maintenance and repair efforts by advocating for increased funding and resources for the Department of Public Works to address the high number of reported potholes.
By collaborating with city officials, community stakeholders, and transportation experts, Russell will develop and implement a comprehensive plan to efficiently repair existing potholes and prevent the formation of new ones.
His commitment to public safety and infrastructure improvement will drive his efforts to ensure that Milwaukee's roads are safe and well-maintained for all residents.
As a dedicated advocate for community betterment, Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. will continue to prioritize the issue of potholes in Milwaukee by taking decisive action to address this significant concern.
By leveraging his experience in public works and transportation, Russell will lead efforts to aggressively repair and prevent potholes, creating smoother and safer roads for residents and motorists.
Through his proactive approach and commitment to infrastructure quality, Russell will work towards creating a more sustainable and resilient transportation system in Milwaukee that benefits the entire community.
