GOODWIN FOR HOMEOWNERSHIP
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is running for re-election with a clear commitment to expanding homeownership and addressing long-standing disparities in Milwaukee’s housing market.
For too many families, especially in Black and Hispanic communities—the dream of homeownership has remained out of reach.
Russell believes that must change.
He is committed to advancing practical, results-driven solutions that help more families move from renting to owning, building generational wealth, stabilizing neighborhoods, and strengthening the overall tax base.
Increased homeownership not only creates opportunity for individual families, but also helps ease the burden on taxpayers and supports long-term community growth.
Russell will advocate for policies that expand access to homebuyer counseling, increase down payment assistance, and provide support for existing homeowners to remain in their homes.
He also supports strategic, community-informed efforts like the Collective Affordable Housing Strategic Plan to ensure that solutions are targeted, effective, and equitable.
His vision is simple: stronger neighborhoods, more homeowners, and a Milwaukee where opportunity is truly accessible to all.
