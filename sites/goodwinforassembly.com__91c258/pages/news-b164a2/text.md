Russell Goodwin Earns Key Endorsements for Re-Election Campaign
State Representative Russell Goodwin continues to earn the confidence of organizations representing educators, public safety professionals, social workers, and skilled trades across Wisconsin.
His re-election campaign has been endorsed by the Milwaukee Police Association (MPA), the Wisconsin Education Association Council (WEAC), the National Association of Social Workers–Wisconsin Chapter PAC (NASW-WI PAC), and the Wisconsin State Conference of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW).
These endorsements reflect broad support for Representative Goodwin’s proven leadership, bipartisan approach, and commitment to strengthening public safety, improving education, supporting working families, and delivering real results for Wisconsin’s 12th Assembly District.

Russell Goodwin Introduced Innovative Technology to Combat Reckless Driving
Reckless driving continues to put families, neighborhoods, and innocent lives at risk.
That’s why State Representative Russell Goodwin has introduced innovative technology designed to help stop repeat reckless drivers before another tragedy occurs.
The proposal would require certain repeat offenders to install GPS-based speed assistance technology that prevents excessive speeding, similar to the ignition interlock devices used for drunk driving offenses.
The goal isn’t just enforcement; it’s changing dangerous driving behavior and making Wisconsin’s roads safer for everyone.
This is the future of public safety.
