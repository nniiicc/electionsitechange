GOODWIN FOR NORTHRIDGE
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is running for re-election in Assembly District 12 with a bold vision to revitalize the long-abandoned Northridge Mall area and transform it into a thriving hub of economic activity, family engagement, and community pride.
Once a cornerstone of Milwaukee’s north side, the Northridge Mall, originally opened in 1972 with major anchors like J.C.
Penney, Sears, Boston Store, and Gimbels, has suffered decades of decline due to changing economic conditions, limited infrastructure access, and public safety challenges.
Today, the site stands vacant and is slated for demolition after years of neglect.
Representative Goodwin sees opportunity where others see abandonment.
His plan is to reimagine the site as a modern, outdoor destination featuring retail stores, local vendors, food trucks, restaurants, entertainment venues, and family-friendly gathering spaces.
This redevelopment would create jobs, stimulate small business growth, and provide a safe, welcoming environment for residents and visitors alike.
With support from Tony Evers, the City of Milwaukee has already secured $15 million to begin demolition of the former Boston Store building, marking a critical first step toward redevelopment and renewal.
Goodwin’s vision for Northridge reflects his broader commitment to Assembly District 12, building safer neighborhoods, strengthening local schools, improving infrastructure, creating economic opportunities, and ensuring fair property taxes for working families.
By transforming a long-neglected space into a vibrant community asset, Russell Goodwin is focused on delivering real results and building a stronger, more prosperous future for District 12.
