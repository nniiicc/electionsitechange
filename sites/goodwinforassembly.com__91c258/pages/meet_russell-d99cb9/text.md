Meet Russell
Rev.
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is a former Milwaukee County Supervisor and former Chair of the Transportation, Public Works, and Transit Committee, known for his unwavering commitment to community service.
He has also served on the Urban Development, Jobs, and the Economy Committee, where he worked to advance growth, opportunity, and investment in local communities.
He is a proud graduate of the Bowhay Institute for Legislative Leadership Development (BILLD), a prestigious program for Midwestern legislators, and is widely recognized as a powerful negotiator and bridge builder, bringing people together to deliver real solutions.
From his early days in youth ministry to his impactful work combating teen pregnancy and providing essential resources for schools, Russell has dedicated his life to uplifting others.
Currently, Russell is a local small business owner.
He also serves as a volunteer pastor at The Movement Center Church, where he works to feed the homeless and spread a message of hope through the broadcast “Get On the Move Today.” A multifaceted leader, he is also a published author, actor, singer, and musician, using his talents to inspire positive change.
A devoted husband, Russell is married to his lovely wife, Misty, and together they are committed to faith, family, and service.
He resides in Assembly District 12 and continues to inspire and uplift others through his work.
