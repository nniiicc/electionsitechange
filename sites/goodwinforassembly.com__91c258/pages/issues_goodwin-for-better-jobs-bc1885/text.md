GOODWIN FOR BETTER JOBS
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is running for re-election with a clear focus on bringing family-sustaining, good-paying jobs to Assembly District 12.
At a time when rising costs are putting pressure on working families, Russell understands that real economic stability starts with strong wages and meaningful employment opportunities.
That’s why he is committed to attracting and supporting jobs that allow individuals and families not just to get by, but to thrive.
During his time in the State Assembly, Russell has served on key committees focused on economic growth and community investment, including the Jobs and Economy Committee and the Urban Development Committee.
Through this work, he has helped advance conversations and policies aimed at strengthening Wisconsin’s workforce and revitalizing local communities.
Russell believes that investing in education, workforce training, and career pathways is essential to preparing the next generation while retaining talent right here in Milwaukee, Brown Deer, and Wauwatosa.
By connecting residents to stable, well-paying careers, his plan helps families better navigate rising costs and build long-term financial security.
His mission is simple: create opportunity, strengthen the workforce, and ensure that economic growth reaches every corner of District 12.
