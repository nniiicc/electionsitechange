Russell Goodwin Endorsements Yard Signs Contact Home ❭ Issues ❭ GOODWIN FOR PROTECTING YOUR PAYCHECK GOODWIN FOR PROTECTING YOUR PAYCHECK As he runs for re-election in Wisconsin’s 12th Assembly District, Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. understands the real financial pressures families are facing every day.
From rising grocery bills to higher housing costs, inflation has stretched household budgets thin, leaving many families struggling to keep up, cover unexpected expenses, or build savings for the future.
For too many, financial stability feels just out of reach.
Russell believes government should be focused on easing that burden, not adding to it.
That’s why he is committed to advancing practical solutions that put more money back in people’s pockets, strengthen economic opportunity, and support working families across Milwaukee, Brown Deer, and Wauwatosa.
His focus includes lowering everyday costs, supporting job creation, investing in local economic development, and ensuring fair and responsible tax policies.
Russell’s campaign is rooted in one clear mission: fighting for the financial well-being of the people he serves.
