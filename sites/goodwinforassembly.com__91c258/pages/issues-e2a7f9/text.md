GOODWIN FOR PUBLIC SCHOOLS
If re-elected to the Wisconsin State Assembly, Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. will continue working to address the critical challenges facing Milwaukee Public Schools (MPS) by advocating for increased and more equitable state funding.
He is committed to reforming the public school funding formula so schools are less reliant on local referendums and property taxes, and more supported through fair state investment, relieving the burden on homeowners while strengthening educational outcomes.
Goodwin will also work to ensure that educators are paid fairly, supported, and protected—recognizing that strong schools begin with strong teachers.
He is committed to making sure we are not cutting teachers out of classrooms when students need them most, and that public school students have access to the qualified educators they deserve.
By improving the overall funding structure, he aims to create stability that allows schools to retain talent and invest in student success.
In addition, he will prioritize modernizing school infrastructure by investing in updated technology and ensuring that school facilities are safe, functional, and equipped to meet the demands of a 21st-century education.
With his personal connection to MPS as a former student and proven leadership, Russell Goodwin remains committed to building a stronger, more effective public education system that provides every student with the opportunity to succeed.

GOODWIN FOR NORTHRIDGE
Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. is running for re-election in Assembly District 12 with a clear vision: to transform the long-neglected Northridge Mall site into a vibrant, family-friendly destination that serves the entire community.
