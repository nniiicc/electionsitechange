GOODWIN FOR A SAFER MILWAUKEE
As a former Milwaukee County Supervisor and champion of public safety, Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. will address the issue of crime in Milwaukee by prioritizing community collaboration and transparency in policing.
With a background in youth ministry, mediation, and peer education, Russell understands the importance of fostering positive relationships between law enforcement, communities, and businesses to create safer neighborhoods.
Additionally, Russell will tackle the problem of reckless driving, which is a significant concern in many Milwaukee neighborhoods, by advocating for targeted initiatives to address this issue.
Russell will work towards building a positive bridge between law enforcement and the Milwaukee community by promoting open communication, accountability, and transparency in policing practices.
By encouraging mutual respect and understanding between law enforcement agencies and residents, Russell aims to strengthen trust within the community and enhance public safety measures.
His dedication to inspiring and uplifting others, demonstrated through his work as a Pastor and community leader, will guide his efforts to address crime and reckless driving in a manner that prioritizes the well-being of all individuals.
In his mission to create a safer and more inclusive Milwaukee, Russell Antonio Goodwin, Sr. will strive to address the root causes of crime and reckless driving by advocating for comprehensive and community-centered solutions.
By engaging with stakeholders and residents, Russell will seek to implement proactive strategies that prevent reckless driving and promote responsible behavior on the roads.
Through his commitment to uniting the community for positive change, Russell will lead efforts to address crime and reckless driving in a way that reflects his values of compassion, service, and empowerment, ultimately creating a safer and more resilient Milwaukee for generations to come.
