Nick DiCeglie has called Pinellas County home since 1996.
He and his wife Erica, an accomplished musician and composer, are raising their two children, Livia and Carlo, here — and it is why Nick fights for this community like it is his own.
Because it is.
A small-business owner turned lawmaker, Nick was elected to the Florida House in 2018 and to the Florida Senate in 2022.
He has delivered toll rebates for commuters, made it harder for local government to raise taxes, and led the rebuild after Hurricanes Helene and Milton.
Today he serves as Chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee on Transportation, Tourism, and Economic Development, and as President & CEO of Hope Villages of America — fighting hunger, homelessness, and domestic abuse right here at home.
