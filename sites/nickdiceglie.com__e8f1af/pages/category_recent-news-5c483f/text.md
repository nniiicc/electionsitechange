Farm Bureau Names 2025 Legislators of the Year
October 20, 2025
Florida Farm Bureau Federation
GAINESVILLE, Fla. — Sen.
Nick DiCeglie (Senate District 18) and Rep.
J.J.
Grow (House District 23) have been named the 2025 Florida Farm Bureau Legislators of the Year.
This award is given to Florida lawmakers for their support of agriculture and rural Florida.
