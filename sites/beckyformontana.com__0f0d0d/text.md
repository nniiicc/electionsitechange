ABOUT ME Putting My Experience to Work Hi neighbors.
I’m Becky Edwards, a farm kid turned 28-year resident of Montana.
I’m also a sitting state legislator, a working mom of three amazing daughters, a local small-business owner, and an executive director of a national conservation organization.
I’m excited to run for state house district 57, to continue fighting for and serving my neighbors in Gallatin and Park Counties.
I have a proven track record in the state legislature, I authored and passed HB932, a conservation financing bill that directs $37 million of recreational cannabis revenue to parks, trails, working lands, and wildlife habitat every other year.
I’m fighting to lower taxes and improve affordability, protect and improve support for our public schools, and ensure every Montanan has access to quality healthcare.
I’m one of you.
Our family struggles with affordability issues and I stay awake at night worried that my teenage daughters won’t be able to live, work, and raise their families here as my husband and I have been able to.
And I’ve made a career of protecting our cherished, shared, public lands and climate.
Whether at your front door, at a community event, or in the grocery store check-out line, I look forward to connecting with you.
I’d love to know what keeps YOU up at night.
What makes living in Montana so special?
What issues are important to you?
What does your family struggle with?
This hard-working, tenacious, and courageous mama would be incredibly honored to represent the thoughtful citizens of HD57 in the state legislature.
