Prior to Serving as State Representative,
Glassburn served as the Councilman for Ward 2 in North Olmsted.
He previously served on the North Olmsted School Board.
Chris is a member of the League of Women Voters and in his spare time volunteers with the Maple Elementary PTA Produce Market distributing food to those in need.
Chris is a lifelong Northeast Ohioan.
Born in Painesville, Chris grew up and graduated high school in North Olmsted.
He has a bachelor's degree in History from Baldwin Wallace College.
Glassburn has been active in working for his community.
He was named the 2020 Civilian Of The Year by the Western Cuyahoga FOP for his work with the Makers for Medics campaign producing over 10,000 face shields for healthcare and first responders.

On City Council, Chris led as the Chairman of the Finance Committee, balancing the city budget each year.
Some highlights of his work on Council as include:
• Lowered city property taxes three times for a total of 1.5 mills to help residents afford school levies.
• Doubled the city's road repair programs by securing numerous county, state, and federal grants.
• New city park features such as walking paths, bathrooms and baby changing stations.
• Small business grants to help local companies survive the pandemic.
• Creating North Olmsted's programs for helping seniors and low income families with home repairs.
