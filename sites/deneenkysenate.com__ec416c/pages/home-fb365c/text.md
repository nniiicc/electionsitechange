Matt Deneen is a highly regarded individual who has dedicated himself to improving the community and uplifting those around him.
With a passion for making a difference, Matt has become a prominent community advocate, leader, and philanthropist.
Throughout his life, Matt has been actively involved in various initiatives aimed at enhancing the well-being of others.
As a community advocate, he has worked tirelessly to address pressing issues and create positive change.
Through these positions, he has demonstrated his ability to inspire others and rally support for important causes.
Matt is known for his unwavering commitment to philanthropy, community service, and leadership.
As an educator, school administrator, city council member, Heritage Council board member, Airport board member, Greenspace board member, Community Parks advocate, ADA advocate, President of the Elizabethtown Paddlers Association Kayaking and Canoeing Club, and Chairman of the Elizabethtown Trail Town Advisory Board overseeing Kentucky's First Urban Trail Town, Matt's commitment to improving his community and active leadership follows in his family tradition of "service to others." His compassionate nature and dedication to service have made him a valued member of the community and our Commonwealth.
During State Senator Deneen's first legislative session in 2023, he continued his relentless work ethic by passing three Bills into law unanimously through both the State Senate and the House of Representatives.
His common sense legislation for the Commonwealth approach has gained tremendous support, benefiting both his District and the Commonwealth.
Senate Bill 49, related to workforce shortages in Education, saved thousands of teaching jobs and expanded teacher employment opportunities.
Senate Bill 119 a Sexual Assault Bill
clearly defined the law and put in place a clear path to justice for victims and punishment for their perpetrators.
SB 60, a Motorcycle License Bill was passed to assist our active duty service members in obtaining their Kentucky Motorcycle license and safety training more efficiently by removing red tape, regulations, and fees.
His leadership is evident in his role as Co-chair of the State Senate Veterans, Military Affairs and Public Protection Committee, Co-chair of the Lottery Task Force Interim Committee, Co-Chair of the BR sub-committee on Education Assessment and Accountability Review Subcommittee, and membership on the Senate Appropriations and Revenue Committee, Judiciary, Agriculture, Tobacco Settlement Fund Committee, and the SLC Committee on Agriculture and Rural Development.
When not in Frankfort, Senator Deneen is an active member of his District, closely working with civic organizations, city and county governments, local education providers, and small and large business owners.
Senator Deneen is also the CEO of Deneen Properties LLC.
