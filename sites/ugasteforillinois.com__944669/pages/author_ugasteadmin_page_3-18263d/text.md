2 David Quintavalle Dan Ugaste Earns Small Business Association Endorsement September 12, 2024 by Ugaste Admin SPRINGFIELD – State Representative Dan Ugaste has been endorsed by the National Federation of Independent Business for his performance as a small business owner and for his advocacy of a low taxes and restricted regulations in Illinois state government.
“I am honored to be recognized by my fellow small business owners, who employ more Americans than every Fortune 500 company combined.
People may not realize it, but it is the smaller businesses throughout our state and country that provide the opportunities for working class to support their families,” said Ugaste.
“Whether small businesses are sole proprietorships, close partnerships or small corporations, such as my former firm, we are familiar with our employees and are engaged in their lives beyond just supervising their work.
We have a responsibility to run our businesses efficiently and correctly so that we can continue to provide income for ourselves and our employees.” The National Federation of Independent Business, Illinois’s leading small business association, recognized Dan Ugaste for his views on key economic development and tax limitation efforts crucial to the survival of small businesses.
“Small businesses face a challenging economic and regulatory environment,” said Noah Finley, Illinois State Director for NFIB.
“Inflation, workforce shortages, and governmental mandates continue to hamper local job creators, discouraging investment in local communities across the state.
These lawmakers have demonstrated that they understand the challenges that small-business owners face.” “Illinois has become increasingly unfriendly to businesses both large and small, and that needs to be reversed before we force our economic base to abandon Illinois,” said Ugaste.
“Once they go, they will not be coming back.
It is simple economics.
It is very expensive and risky to start a new business, restart an old business or relocate an existing business.
