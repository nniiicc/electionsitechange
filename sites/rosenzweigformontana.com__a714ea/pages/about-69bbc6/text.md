MEET SCOTT
I’m Scott Rosenzweig, and I’m running for our State Senate to continue the fight for Park and Gallatin Counties.
In my term as a State House Representative representing our counties I’ve fought to fix taxation, defend our rights, and make sure we get our fair share from the state’s budget.
That fight is not over, and I want to take my experience, dedication and skills to our Senate to more effectively fight for what our counties deserve.
The Montana we all love, the Montana that invests in the future and carries a tow rope to help a stranger out a ditch, is under attack from partisan politics in Helena.
I am running to work with both sides to continue the fight to fix property taxes, defend our right to privacy, and stand up for public education and public lands.
We all deserve better from our government — and I’m running to help us get a better government.
Thank you,
Representative Scott Rosenzweig
