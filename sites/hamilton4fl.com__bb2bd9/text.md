Michaelangelo Hamilton is a qualified write-in candidate for U.S.
House in Florida’s 25th Congressional District .
Florida’s 25th Congressional District runs along South Florida’s East Coast from the Delray Beach/Boca Raton area through parts of Broward County and down toward Miami Beach, including communities across Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties.
He is running to lower costs, grow small businesses and good jobs, secure our communities, protect working families, and restore accountability in Washington.
In 2022, Michaelangelo made history as one of the youngest people in modern American history to run for Congress.
Now, he is running again to give South Florida a new voice and a new choice.
This campaign is about faith, freedom, opportunity, strong families, and a stronger future for Florida.
This November, write in Michaelangelo Hamilton for U.S.
House, Florida District 25.
