Representative in Florida’s 25th Congressional District TALLAHASSEE, FL — Michaelangelo Hamilton has filed as a write-in candidate for United States Representative in Florida’s 25th Congressional District for the 2026 general election.
Hamilton said the campaign will focus on persistence, voter education, affordability, opportunity, public safety, small business, working families, and restoring the American Dream for regular people.
“This campaign is about giving voters another choice,” Hamilton said.
“The path is not easy, but I believe Florida’s 25th District deserves a voice that is independent-minded, practical, and focused on the future.” Hamilton’s campaign, Hamilton for Florida 2026, will now focus on building a grassroots write-in operation across the district.
Because write-in campaigns require voters to physically write the candidate’s name on the ballot, the campaign will place heavy emphasis on voter education, digital outreach, direct contact, local visibility, and clear instructions on how to write in Michaelangelo Hamilton for U.S.
Representative, Florida District 25.
“This is not just about filing paperwork,” Hamilton said.
“This is about building a campaign from the ground up and proving that regular people still have a path to be heard.” Hamilton said the campaign will continue updating its website, organizing supporters, launching outreach efforts, and developing a broader voter-contact strategy for the general election.
“This is the beginning of the next phase,” Hamilton said.
“Now the work is making sure voters know there is another option.” Michaelangelo Hamilton Write-In Candidate for U.S.
