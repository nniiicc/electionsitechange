Not sure if you live in Florida’s 25th Congressional District?
Congressional district lines can be confusing, especially in South Florida, where boundaries can change from one neighborhood to the next.
The newly drawn Florida 25th Congressional District includes communities in Palm Beach County, Broward County, and Miami-Dade County, stretching along South Florida’s east coast from the Delray Beach and Boca Raton area south toward Miami Beach.
Communities Across FL-25.
The district includes all or parts of communities such as Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Highland Beach, Deerfield Beach, Hillsboro Beach, Lighthouse Point, Pompano Beach, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea, Davie, Fort Lauderdale, Dania Beach, Hollywood, Hallandale Beach, Aventura, Golden Beach, Sunny Isles Beach, North Miami Beach, North Miami, Bal Harbour, Bay Harbor Islands, Surfside, North Bay Village, and Miami Beach, along with surrounding neighborhoods and unincorporated areas.
Because some cities are divided between congressional districts, living in one of these communities does not automatically mean your address is located in Florida’s 25th District.
Before Election Day, voters should confirm their congressional district using their current home address.
Voters should also check their registration status, polling location, vote-by-mail status, and mailing address with their county Supervisor of Elections.
Important 2026 Election Dates.
For the 2026 General Election, the deadline to register to vote in Florida is October 5, 2026.
Early voting is scheduled for October 24–31, 2026, and Election Day is November 3, 2026.
Early voting gives voters more flexibility.
If you are registered in Palm Beach, Broward, or Miami-Dade County, you may vote at any designated early voting location within your county during the early voting period.
On Election Day, voters must vote at their assigned precinct.
