The ad is part of an ongoing six-figure paid media investment districtwide, spanning across broadcast television, cable, streaming, radio, and digital platforms in the final weeks before the June 9 Democratic Primary.
Titled “The Lowcountry,” the spot draws a connection between the values Lacore learned over her 35 years in the Navy and the values she sees represented across the Lowcountry: service, sacrifice, resilience, and love of country.
It highlights her experience leading 60,000 sailors through times of crisis, framing her campaign for Congress as a continuation of a lifetime of service.
The ad also speaks directly to economic frustrations facing families across the district, including rising costs, veterans struggling to access healthcare, and distrust in national political leadership.
Lacore launched her campaign earlier this year, and raised over $1 million within her first ten weeks on the trail.
Lacore has been actively campaigning since initially announcing her candidacy, participating in forums , county conventions , and hosting events - meeting voters where they are throughout the Lowcountry.
Additionally, Lacore has earned national endorsements from The Bench , EMILYs List , VoteVets , and New Politics .
The ad will air districtwide on broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital platforms today through the June 9 Primary Election.
“The Lowcountry” Script: There’s no place like the Lowcountry.
Reminds me a lot of the military.
Hard-working people who love their country and put service first.
I’m Democrat Nancy Lacore.
I served in the U.S.
Navy for 35 years and led 60,000 sailors through times of crisis.
The crisis today is people work hard and can’t get ahead, veterans can’t get healthcare, and politicians are loyal to a broken system.
I’m running for Congress to put service first – like the Lowcountry does.
