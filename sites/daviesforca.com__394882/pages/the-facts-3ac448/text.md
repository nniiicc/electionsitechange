I take great pride in being your voice in Sacramento and as a voter, I believe you deserve the facts.
Claim: “My opponent has missed over 1,000 votes in the Assembly.” Facts: I have an A rating in both attendance and voting in the State Assembly.
I received a call in the final week of our legislative session that my mother had suffered a stroke and was sent to hospice.
I was forced with the decision to stay in Sacramento or fly out to visit my mom one last time before she passed away.
I was given an “excused absence” by the California State Assembly for these unfortunate circumstances .
There were approximately 600 votes in those final days that were missed, but I can ensure our district that their voices were heard on these bills throughout the process.
The additional “missed votes” were votes that I had abstained on.
Even though I was present for the votes, I chose to abstain on these particular bills because I often feel like legislation is “half baked” and I have always been willing to work with the author on amendments to ensure the bill is as strong as it can be before I vote on it.
