Proven Leadership That Delivers Results Sam Givhan understands that public service is about solving problems.
Throughout his time in the State Senate, he has built a reputation for working through difficult issues, building consensus, and passing legislation that makes a positive difference for Madison County families and businesses.
Growing Madison County's Economy Sam knows that a strong economy creates opportunity for everyone.
He has been a consistent advocate for attracting new investment, supporting job creation, strengthening infrastructure, and ensuring Madison County remains one of the best places in America to live, work, and start a business.

He has consistently supported responsible budgets, lower taxes, and policies that allow families, retirees, and small businesses to keep more of their hard-earned money while maintaining Alabama's strong financial footing.
