Sam Givhan understands that public service is about solving problems.
Throughout his time in the State Senate, he has built a reputation for working through difficult issues, building consensus, and passing legislation that makes a positive difference for Madison County families and businesses.
Sam knows that a strong economy creates opportunity for everyone.
He has been a consistent advocate for attracting new investment, supporting job creation, strengthening infrastructure, and ensuring Madison County remains one of the best places in America to live, work, and start a business.
Sam believes government should live within its means.
He has consistently supported responsible budgets, lower taxes, and policies that allow families, retirees, and small businesses to keep more of their hard-earned money while maintaining Alabama's strong financial footing.
From improving transportation and infrastructure to supporting quality education, public safety, and economic development, Sam has focused on the issues that matter most to the families and communities of North Alabama.
His goal is simple: leave the district stronger than he found it.
Experience matters.
Sam has earned the respect of his colleagues and constituents by being accessible, thoughtful, and prepared.
He understands how to navigate the legislative process, build relationships, and deliver meaningful results without losing sight of the people he represents.
