Lower Taxes and Affordability Liz believes government should live within its means.

Healthcare Liz serves on the Senate Health Committee and has a strong understanding of the issues impacting patients, doctors and hospitals.

Education Liz served on several parochial school boards, was a substitute teacher and an adjunct professor; she understands students learn best with parents and teachers engaged in their learning.

Second Amendment Liz is a member of the NRA and has voted to make gun ownership and the exercise of our Second Amendment rights accessible for all law-abiding Hoosiers.
Digital Safeguards Liz believes in consumer rights, so she introduced the Data Privacy Act that became law in 2026, allowing Hoosiers to control their online data.
