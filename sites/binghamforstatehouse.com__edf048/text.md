Chad Bingham is the Neighbor that Fights For You Chad Bingham isn't your typical politician—he's your neighbor.
Born and raised in the shadow of Cape Kennedy Space Center, Chad has called Jackson County home for years, building both his family and his business right here in our community.
After graduating from the University of West Alabama, he spent over a decade in sales before founding Bingham Insurance Group in 2013, growing it from a single office to six locations across the Southeast.
But Chad's real passion has always been serving others.
Married to his wife Sarah for 20 years with four kids ranging from 16 to 6, he understands the daily challenges facing working families—from sitting in our traffic to navigating our growing infrastructure needs.
Chad's commitment to Jackson County runs deep through his hands-on leadership in our community.
As a Braselton Rotary Club member, he leads the charge to raise over $100,000 annually for underprivileged children and helped secure funding for two life-saving NICU beds at Northeast Georgia Medical Center Braselton.
His work co-founding the Empower College and Career Center has literally changed lives—creating pathways for our young people to earn good-paying jobs without crushing debt.
Whether serving as County Commissioner in 2023-2024 or leading safety teams at Free Chapel Church, Chad tackles hard problems with common-sense solutions because he lives with the consequences of those decisions every day.
Now Chad is ready to take that same neighbor-first approach to the State House.
While other candidates talk about Jackson County from a distance, Chad experiences our rapid growth firsthand—the traffic, the strain on our schools, the need for better infrastructure.
He's knocked on thousands of doors not to campaign, but to listen.
Because Chad knows that real change starts with real conversations, and a better House District 31 starts with you.
When he gets to Atlanta, he won't just represent Jackson County—he'll be Jackson County's voice, fighting for the resources and respect our community deserves.
