Governor Josh Green has spent his life caring for Hawaii families — first as a local physician and ER doctor, then as a legislator, lieutenant governor, and governor of Hawaii.
From 2018 to 2022, Josh served as lieutenant governor and helped lead Hawaii’s COVID pandemic response — vaccinating over a million people and saving thousands of lives.
Since 2022, Josh has served as the ninth governor of Hawaii — working to lower the cost of living, build affordable housing, and reduce homelessness statewide.
In 2005, Josh met Jaime Ushiroda, a local girl from Kaneohe, and they were married in 2006.
Together they are the proud parents of daughter Maia and son Sam.
