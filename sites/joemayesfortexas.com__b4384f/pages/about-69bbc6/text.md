Get to know Joe
"I am Joe Mayes and I intend to be your representative from Texas House District 106."
Joe grew up in a military family and spent twenty-years in the US Air Force, rising to the rank of Master Sergeant.
After serving our country, he completed a bachelor's degree in Legal Administration and spent several years managing non-profit organizations throughout the South before finding a second calling in the cybersecurity field.
Joe married Diane, his high school sweetheart and retired Texas public school educator, joining her in Texas.
He took a role in Cybersecurity leadership, rising to Director of Cybersecurity Strategy at a global tech firm.
Joe and Diane fervently believe in the power of reading and the criticality of a foundational education for all.
Their belief led them to open and operate an independent bookstore in Denton in 2021.

Cybersecurity Executive
