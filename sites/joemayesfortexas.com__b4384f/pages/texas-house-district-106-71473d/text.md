Texas House District 106 is one of 150 districts in the Texas House of Representatives.
The district is located in Denton County and includes all or portions of several communities in North Texas.
District 106 includes all or portions of communities such as Aubrey, Celina, Cross Roads, Denton, Frisco, Hackberry, Krugerville, Lewisville, Little Elm, Pilot Point, Prosper, Providence Village, Sanger, and The Colony.
Members of the Texas House represent their district in the Texas Legislature.
Along with the Texas Senate, the House considers legislation and resolutions, considers proposed constitutional amendments, and appropriates funds for state government.
Texas has 150 House members, each elected to a two-year term.
During which they are responsible for the following:
Legislation
Introduces, evaluates, amends and votes on state legislation.
State Budget
Participates in decisions about appropriations and the operation of Texas government.
Constituent Representation
Represents district residents and communicates district concerns within state government.
