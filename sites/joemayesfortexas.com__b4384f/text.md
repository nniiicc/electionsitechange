Meet Joe
Joe is a 20-year US Air Force veteran, a small business owner, a retired Cybersecurity executive, and a staunch believer in, and defender of, the freedoms assured to all of us.
As the Democratic candidate for Texas House District 106, Joe is committed to fighting for:
- The right to a world-class public education for all Texas students;
- Economic opportunities for all Texans to build the life they choose; and
- The freedom to enjoy our fundamental rights, unencumbered by government overreach.
No public dollars to private schools
World Class Public Education for all Texas students
Every Texas student deserves a world class public education, and Texas is distinctly positioned to provide it.
Our Texas public school educators are doing incredible work, educating 5.5 million students with some 400,000 students graduating high school every year.
All while being hamstrung by some of the lowest funding among the United States.
A public education endowment would create a permanent source of supplemental educating funding, without raising taxes!
It's time to stand with the professional educators who are carrying the burden of insufficient public school funding.
Make Texas Affordable for Texans
Living and Doing Business in Texas should not be a luxury
Living and doing business in Texas should be within reach for all Texans.
This includes housing, food, healthcare, education, transportation, and child care.
Small businesses are the engine of the Texas economy, representing 99.8% of all businesses and employing 44.4% of the state’s workforce.*
For those Texans who want to start and operate a small business, Texas should invest in these ventures, including grants, loans, and education programs designed to optimize the likelihood of success.
* Texas Small Business Report 2025
Let Freedom Read
Defend Freedoms for All Texans
Parents and individuals — not the state — should determine the books they read, with whom they assemble, and how they express their beliefs.
I stand in defense of these and other freedoms from state or local communities who seek to impose their beliefs on all members of a community.
Leave No Veteran Behind
Support our Texas Veterans
The United States – including Texas – is free due in large measure to the sacrifices made by our US Military Veterans.
As such, I will ensure Texas reaches every veteran in need of support or services.
I will work with the Texas Veterans Commission to ensure every Veteran in Texas receives the support available to them — and that they deserve.
