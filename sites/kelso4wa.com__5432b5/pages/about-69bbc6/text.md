Introduction Early life and background Michaela Kelso grew up in Germany and immigrated to the US in 1993 as the spouse of a US Soldier.
Growing up she was surrounded by people who fought for the environment and against war mongering.
The nuclear meltdown in Chernobyl brought home the futility of thinking "What I do only effects Me".
We are living in an interconnected world!
Now, more than ever, we all have a shared responsibility to preserve and protect this world for our children.
A life of patriotic service Michaela Kelso enlisted in the United States Army in 1995 and (together with her husband) retired at the rank of Sergeant First Class from Active Duty in 2018.
During her service she deployed to Bosnia Herzegovina, Iraq, and Afghanistan, and was stationed in Germany, Hawaii, California, and Texas.
She honors her oath to the Constitution to this day!
Michaela enlisted as a Food Service Specialist (Cook), working long hours to feed her fellow Soldiers.
While deployed for a year to Bosnia in 1996, she helped serve over 1100 Soldiers per meal.
After 3 1/2 years she re-enlisted to work as a Personnel Administration specialist, which brought her to Hawaii, where she became a naturalized citizen.
In 2003 Michaela chose to continue her military career by switching into the Military Intelligence Branch, becoming a Cryptologist Linguist (French) and later a Human Intelligence Specialist (Interrogator and Source Operator).
She graduated at the top of her class at the Defense Language Institute and was recognized for her strong writing competence during Interrogator Training.
Throughout her military career Michaela Kelso was recognized for her hard work, dedication to duty, superior work performance, and high levels of integrity.
One of her favorite anecdotes is of one of her Commanders calling her “his own personal pit bull” and selecting her for a special assignment due to her ability to “herd cats”.
There's no place like Home After Michaela Kelso and her husband retired from Active Duty in 2018, they settled their family in Chattaroy, WA, an area they chose after coming here on a vacation in 2009, between schools and deployments.
These days they spend their time enjoying the outdoors with their dogs, gathering eggs from their chickens, working the garden, listening to music, serving their feline overlords, and defending Democracy.
