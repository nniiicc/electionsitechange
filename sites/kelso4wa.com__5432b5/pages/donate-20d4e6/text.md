Michaela Kelso is a retired US Army Veteran running for Washington's 6th Legislative District State Representative.

When representation is ineffective, it has to be replaced.
Now we have a host of individuals running for this office who are just going to continue in the same direction.
Sponsoring bills designed to create outrage and further divide us, or voting down bills because they protect the rights and welfare of everyone.
I don’t want that for our district, and I hope you don’t either.
Please help me in my continued efforts to protect our Freedom, our Dignity, and the Truth.
Thank You, from the Bottom of My Heart!
