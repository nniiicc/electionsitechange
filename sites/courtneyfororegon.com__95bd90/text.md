A Voice For Our Communities.
Donate Volunteer Get in Contact As an educator and parent, I’m focused on our future.
I’ve served our communities as your State Representative and State Senator, consistently listening to your voices and working diligently to invest in quality education , make childcare and housing more affordable, expand mental health care and addiction treatment , improve community safety , strengthen consumer protections, and protect access to reproductive healthcare .
I ask for your support to continue serving as your State Senator on behalf of our shared Oregon values.
COURTNEY IS COMMITED TO: Fighting For Oregon Families Responding to Homelessness and Housing Affordability Championing Affordable Healthcare Addressing Community Safety Supporting Workers and Small Businesses Protecting Our Environment Confronting Discrimination Investing Responsibly In Education Building Community, Investing Locally State Senator Courtney Neron Misslin Senate District 13 What we now call Oregon Senate District 13 is the traditional lands and waterways of the Atfalati , the Kalapuya and the Ahantchuyuk people, now members of the Confederated Tribes of Grand Ronde and Confederated Tribes of Siletz Indians .
I honor the Indigenous people of this land as a vibrant, foundational and integral part of our community.
I commit myself to ongoing practices of listening, learning, reconciliation, growth, and connection.
