Voting Information
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026
Polls are open 7am-7pm
Tuesday, October 6: Absentee, Vote by Mail and Early In-Person Voting Begins
- You can request a ballot anytime between today and October 20.
- Click here to track the status of your absentee ballot.
- You can also walk into your County Clerk’s office and cast a ballot.
Saturday, October 17 — Expanded Early Voting Begins
- All early vote locations can be found HERE.
Tuesday, October 20 — Deadline to Request a Mail Ballot
- October 20 is the deadline for the county clerk to receive an absentee-ballot application.
- Returning your absentee ballot is also easy.
You can:
- Return your ballot by mail, using the pre-paid return envelope.
- Hand deliver your absentee ballot to any Early or Election Day polling site beginning October 17 through Election Day on November 3.
Saturday, October 31 — Expanded Early Voting Ends
- This is the last day to vote before Election Day.
Tuesday, November 3 — Election Day
- Mail Ballots must be returned by 7PM
For more information on Same Day Voter Registration and other Election info visit NMVOTE.ORG
