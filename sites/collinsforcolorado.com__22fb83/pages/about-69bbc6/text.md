Teddy Collins is a Colorado business owner, entrepreneur, and civic leader committed to individual liberty, responsible governance, and restoring common sense in Colorado .
As the founder of one of the state’s largest family owned and operated firearms stores, he’s created jobs, supported local economies, and championed constitutional rights and personal freedom.
His background in multiple business endeavors gives him firsthand insight into the real challenges facing small businesses, working families, and local economies.
Teddy has served in leadership roles with the Colorado Federal Firearms Licensee Association and the Colorado State Shooting Association , advocating for responsible firearm ownership, public safety, and the Second Amendment.
Through this work, he’s built meaningful relationships across the state—bridging divides, uniting conservatives, and standing up for Colorado values.
Teddy’s leadership is rooted in personal experience.
He met his wife during a student government race—running against each other—and that shared commitment to service became the foundation of their family .
Today, they are raising two daughters with a third child on the way, and that responsibility drives his focus on parental rights, community safety, and protecting the next generation.
He also hosts a widely followed podcast focused on Second Amendment rights, conservative principles, and civic engagement—giving a voice to Coloradans who feel ignored by Denver’s political establishment.
Teddy is not a career politician.

His priorities are simple: defend individual liberty, support working families and small businesses, and ensure Colorado remains a place where freedom and opportunity can thrive.
With the perspective of an entrepreneur, the conviction of a constitutional advocate, and the heart of a father, Teddy is running to help Colorado become safe, strong, and free again.
