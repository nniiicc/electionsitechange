Landsman: “The Court’s ruling makes clear how truly horrifying a second Trump presidency would be.”
July 1, 2024
CINCINNATI — Today, Rep.
Greg Landsman released the following statement following the Supreme Court’s decision to grant legal immunity to President Trump for crimes committed using official power:
Landsman: The Court’s ruling makes clear how truly horrifying a second Trump presidency would be.
He was unhinged with rules in place.
Now, there would be no boundaries if he wins, no safeguards.
Our democracy would be upended.
Our lives upended.
