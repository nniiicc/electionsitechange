In Final Report Before Election, Landsman’s Opponent Raises Little, Spends Big on Yard Signs and Billboards
October 16, 2024
CINCINNATI, OH – On Tuesday, Rep.
Greg Landsman (OH-01) announced raising over twice that of his opponent in the third quarter, ending with over 5 times as much cash on hand as Orlando Sonza.
In a statistically competitive district, the mismanagement of Sonza’s campaign contributed to yet another race-rating shift toward Landsman; Inside Elections reported that the race is “off the board”, citing Sonza’s “lack of funding, candidate quality” and the “strength of the incumbent”.
Unlike his opponent, Landsman does not accept corporate PAC money, relying instead on his broad grassroots support.
Landsman’s strength heading into the final 3 weeks is evident; the campaign has been unanswered on television for the past 5 weeks with ads about freedom, safety, and making it easier to raise a family in Southwest Ohio.
