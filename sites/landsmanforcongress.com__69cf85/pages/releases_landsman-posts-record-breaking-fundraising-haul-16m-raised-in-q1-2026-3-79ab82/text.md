April 3, 2026
CINCINNATI, OH – On Friday, Greg Landsman (OH-01) underscored the strength and momentum of his campaign with a record-breaking fundraising haul, raising over $1.6M in Q1 of 2026, the highest quarterly total raised by any congressional candidate in Ohio.
With over $2.8M cash on hand, Landsman enters Q2 in his strongest position ever, all without a single dollar in corporate PAC money.
Over 9,000 individuals have contributed to Landsman’s campaign this cycle, with an average donation of $250.
Notably, 80% of contributions were under $100.
These fundraising numbers are a testament to the grassroots support energized to send Landsman back to Congress.
Voters are enraged with the constant chaos and corruption from Trump and Congressional Republicans.
Ohio’s 1st congressional district is standing behind Greg because they trust him to fight against the chaos and corruption and for working families by lower costs, continue the fight for accessible and affordable healthcare, and make our communities safer.
