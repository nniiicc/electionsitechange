LANDSMAN LEADS REPUBLICAN IN INITIAL POLLING, UP 20 PERCENT WITH INDEPENDENT VOTERS
February 5, 2026
CINCINNATI, OH – An internal poll finds Greg Landsman (OH-01) holding a 6-point districtwide advantage against the most active Republican candidate, Eric Conroy.
Greg is earning an impressive 57% among Independent voters (57-37).
A 500-sample survey from highly-reputable Impact Research conducted November 18 - 23 found Greg is well-liked by voters, and his popularity translates to a significant, early advantage.
Greg holds a higher net-popularity rating than several national and statewide figures tested on both sides of the aisle – including both well-known Democrats and Republican leaders like Trump and JD Vance.
Voters see Greg as honest, hard-working and relatable, delivering an initial 6-point lead against GOP opponent Eric Conroy (49% Landsman / 43% Conroy) despite the R+5 nature of the district.
Trump’s popularity rating is 8 points in the red (45% Fav / 53% Unfav), including 63% of Independents who give Trump a negative job rating.
Further, Democrats lead a generic ballot for the House by 4 points (44% Dem / 40% GOP) – demonstrating an improved climate for Democrats compared to 2024.
Even as voters are provided balanced statements on both candidates, Landsman
maintains a 6-point margin and leads Independents by 20 points (57% to 37%).
Greg’s lead expands to 15 points (54% to 39%) and a 34-point margin with
Independents after communicating his reform credentials of refusing corporate
PAC dollars, backing a strong stock ban for members of Congress, and refusing his pay during the shutdown.
Greg's strength is further demonstrated by the lack of donor excitement for any would-be opponent.
The frontrunners for the GOP nomination have been forced to keep their campaigns afloat with their own money, each having self-funded more than they've raised.
