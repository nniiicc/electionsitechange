Don Roberts is a veteran, attorney, and lifelong public servant running for Ohio’s 25th District in the State Senate.
With over 20 years of legal experience, he has built a career focused on serving families, protecting communities, and delivering practical results.
As a former Legal Aid supervising attorney, Air Force veteran, and police officer, Don brings real-world experience in public safety, the courtroom, and community service.
He is committed to strong, safe communities, responsible government, and leadership grounded in service and accountability.
