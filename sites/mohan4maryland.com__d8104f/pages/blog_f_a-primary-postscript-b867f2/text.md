All Posts A Primary Postscript June 25, 2026 A busy mom life with high energy, special needs kids… A busy professional life with hours of research, writing, sending manuscripts for publications, presenting at professional conferences… Busy semesters which are a sprint of lesson plans, constantly adjusting and tweaking lectures, on-the-fly changes to keep student interest, endless grading… Volunteering with State Commissions and REALLY doing the work to connect communities with information and resources… Volunteering with County government to help with citizen-driven government initiatives… Leading a volunteer-driven non-profit to connect vulnerable communities to food, health, financial, civic, legal and other resources… Sitting member of the Baltimore County Democratic State Central Committee and active member of several local Democratic clubs with campaign cycles of canvassing, phone banking, text banking, GOTV, sign waving, volunteering at polls… Political representation at State and National levels as a South Asian American representing both community values as well as American ideals… Constant medical vigilance with multiple autoimmune disorders, a complex medication regimen and a stubborn refusal to let these conditions take over my life… I’ve been told many times that I take on too much, do too much…but if this Primary Campaign has shown me anything, these are the lesson learned: 1.
The same family, kids, pets that keep me harassed and busy also give me joy on my worst day.
2.
I do my professional work- teaching, research, writing- because it energizes me and helps me constantly keep the evolving global issue of equity in perspective.
3.
My volunteer work provides me with the deep, DEEP satisfaction that only eating an entire bar of dark chocolate would!
It sates me, makes me grateful for what I have and deeply thankful for the capability and capacity to help those who need more than I do.
4.
The importance of the first person in improving our lives: instead of deflecting blame onto the vague third person ‘they are responsible’, I take ownership and push ahead to see what I can do to change systems, laws and social mores that are less than equitable or just.
It gives me a sense of purpose, empathy for people from all walks of life and has made me the most AMAZING friends who are similarly moved.
At the end of the day, I do all of these things because I want to!!
And because I want to, I manage to get them all done (although my life can be a hot mess and terribly chaotic often!!
Plus I’m perpetually running late- but that is more to do with the deep seated Indian Standard Time embedded within me than with improper time management!!).
So thank you- family, friends, volunteers, students, colleagues, pets and all those who have been subjected to one of my frequent and random opinions about the world and its contents!!
You have sustained me for 6 months now- now I’m asking you to stay in this mad race with me for four more months!!
