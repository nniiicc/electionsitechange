Why I'm Running Our current elected leaders in Annapolis, including Maryland Delegate candidates, have completely tuned out our local community.
What we truly need are community-driven solutions and more conversations that address the issues our residents care about.
Our community deserves a champion like Jyoti Mohan, who embodies fearless advocacy and prioritizes families over politics.
"As a community leader, parent, and educator, I've witnessed firsthand how smart investments and effective constituent service can harness existing resources in our local community, creating opportunities for everyone to thrive.
I'm running for State Delegate to continue that work in District 42A—with accountability, transparency, and results you can count on.
Together, we can ensure every public dollar works harder for our families, small businesses, farmers, and students." LEARN MORE My vision for District 42A is a community where everyday families thrive in a strong local economy built on small business and agriculture, our open spaces and farmland are preserved, and every child has access to quality education right here in Baltimore County.
