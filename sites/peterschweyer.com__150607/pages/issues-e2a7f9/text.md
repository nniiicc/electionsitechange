For years, it has been my mission to fight for economic, racial, social, and environmental justice for all.
The Issues Education Education is the foundation for our children’s success.
I have brought millions of dollars back to the Allentown School District, but we must continue working toward education funding equity.
As the Education Chair, I continue to lead the fight for fully and fairly funded public schools in Pennsylvania.
Minimum Wage We must raise the minimum wage to $15 an hour.
The benefits of a $15 minimum wage would be far-reaching, lifting pay for so many Pennsylvanian workers and helping reverse decades of growing pay inequality.
I promise that I'll continue fighting to increase the minimum wage.
Economic Development We have seen tremendous growth in our local communities and neighborhoods throughout Lehigh County because of strategic investments.
I will continue supporting responsible economic development projects that help all of our communities.
Public Safety It is always a goal to make sure our law enforcement and first responders have the tools they need to keep our families and children safe.
We helped them through the COVID-19 pandemic, and we’ll continue to ensure they have what they need for our neighborhoods.
