Committee to Elect Randy Novak
Effective Date: January 5th, 2025
The Committee to Elect Randy Novak is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all people, regardless of ability.
We are actively working to improve the user experience for everyone and to apply relevant accessibility standards to our website and campaign communications.
If you have any questions about this statement, please contact the campaign directly
Our Commitment
We want every supporter, volunteer, and visitor to be able to engage with our campaign, both online and offline.
We aim to make this website accessible and inclusive by following the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and by using best practices for usability and responsive design.
This means we strive to ensure:
- Clear and consistent navigation
- Alt-text for meaningful images
- Keyboard navigability
- Proper use of headings and structure
- High color contrast and readable fonts
- Compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies
Ongoing Efforts
Our team is continuously reviewing website content and features to improve accessibility.
As part of this effort, we work with developers and partners who understand and implement accessibility standards during website updates.
Feedback and Assistance
If you encounter any difficulty accessing content on this site, or if you have specific questions or concerns about the accessibility of any part of our site, we welcome your feedback.
We will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information or support you need in an accessible format.
Third-party Tools and Platforms
Some campaign content may link to or be hosted on third-party platforms (e.g., social media, ActBlue).
We cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party services but encourage those platforms to prioritize accessibility as well.
Policy Updates
This Accessibility Statement is effective as of November 25, 2025.
As digital accessibility standards evolve, we may update this statement to reflect our ongoing efforts and improvements.
We encourage you to review this page periodically for updates.
