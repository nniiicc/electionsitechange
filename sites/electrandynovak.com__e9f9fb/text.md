Meet Randy Randy’s Priorities for District 9 Public Safety You Can Count On Supporting first responders, strengthening emergency services, and keeping our neighborhoods safe for the families who call District 9 home.
Affordable Living for Hoosier Families Fighting rising costs by expanding access to affordable housing, protecting working families, and making sure Indiana remains a place where people can afford to live, work, and raise a family.
Good Jobs & Local Economic Growth Growing living-wage jobs, investing in workforce training, and supporting local businesses so our economy works for the people who power it.
