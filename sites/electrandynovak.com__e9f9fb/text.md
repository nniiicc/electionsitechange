Randy’s Story
At a Glance
Randy Novak has spent his entire adult life serving the people of LaPorte and Porter Counties; as a firefighter, Fire Chief, county leader, and now as our State Representative.
He’s the kind of leader who shows up, puts in the work, and never forgets where he comes from.
Today, Randy is fighting for the same things he’s always fought for: safe neighborhoods, responsive emergency services, strong local jobs, responsible budgets, and a community where every family can build a good life.
Randy’s Priorities for District 9
Public Safety You Can Count On
Supporting first responders, strengthening emergency services, and keeping our neighborhoods safe for the families who call District 9 home.
Affordable Living for Hoosier Families
Fighting rising costs by expanding access to affordable housing, protecting working families, and making sure Indiana remains a place where people can afford to live, work, and raise a family.
Good Jobs & Local Economic Growth
Growing living-wage jobs, investing in workforce training, and supporting local businesses so our economy works for the people who power it.
Responsible Budgets & Honest Government
Balancing budgets, respecting taxpayers, and delivering transparent government that treats people with fairness and accountability.
