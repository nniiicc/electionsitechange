Privacy Policy
Your privacy is a priority for us.
This statement outlines our privacy practices.
Collection and Use of Information:
Tanner for Kansas is solely responsible for the information collected from you.
We collect data you provide directly via email, text, forms, or other direct interactions.
This information helps us respond to your inquiries and engage with you effectively.
We may share your data with third parties such as service providers, volunteers, and campaign staff who require access to perform tasks for us.
This may include organizations with aligned political views or goals and those that help facilitate communication among like-minded entities.
Aggregated or de-identified data, which cannot be used to identify you, may also be shared.
Exceptions include:
- Sharing with trusted vendors and consultants fulfilling services on our behalf.
- Complying with legal obligations or law enforcement requests.
- Sharing with your consent.
When you register, contribute, volunteer, or engage in other activities, we may request your contact details, such as your name, address, phone number, and email.
We might also gather additional information from external sources to enhance our records.
We use your email to send updates and your phone number for communication purposes.
Your Rights and Choices:
You can opt out of future communications at any time by reaching out to us via the contact page on our website.
You're entitled to review, request deletion, or express any concerns regarding your data.
Unsubscribe or Opt Out:
To stop receiving promotional emails or texts, follow the instructions provided in those messages.
You may still receive non-promotional communications related to your account or the campaign.
With your permission, we may send push notifications to your mobile device, which you can deactivate through your device settings.
Security Measures:
We employ industry-standard security protocols to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, or alteration.
Access is restricted to employees or contractors who need it to offer services to you.
Despite our efforts, complete security cannot be guaranteed.
Policy Updates:
Any changes to our privacy practices will be reflected in updates to this policy.
We’ll notify registered users via email and post updates on this site.
This policy does not affect text messaging opt-in data and consent, which will not be shared with third parties.
Text Message Consent:
By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive texts from or on behalf of Tanner for Kansas.
Messages may include donation requests, event information, and advocacy actions.
Standard messaging rates may apply.
To stop messages, reply with "STOP." You can contact us for assistance at info@tannerforkansas.com or through our website contact form.
