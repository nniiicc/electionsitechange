Meeting with county party chairs
Over the past few days, I’ve had productive conversations with county party chairs to discuss my intention to run and the issues facing our district.
These meetings are an important part of listening and learning before moving forward.
House District 54 includes a mix of urban and rural communities, and it’s important to understand the priorities of people across the district.
I appreciate the time and openness of those I’ve met with and look forward to continuing these conversations.
