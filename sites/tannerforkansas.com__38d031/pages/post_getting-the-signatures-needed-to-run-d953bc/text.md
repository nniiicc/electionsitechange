Getting the signatures needed to run
Over the past several weeks, I’ve been out in the community talking with neighbors and gathering the signatures required to file by petition.
These conversations have been the most meaningful part of this process so far—listening to people talk about rising costs, health care, and what they want to see from state leadership.
This step isn’t just paperwork.
It’s a reminder that this campaign starts with real people and real conversations, and I’m grateful to everyone who has taken a moment to share their thoughts and support.
