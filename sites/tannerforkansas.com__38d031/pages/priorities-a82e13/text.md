Issues that matter
Chris Tanner’s decision to run for office comes from a lifetime of service and listening.
After years of working with neighbors across Topeka and rural Osage County, he saw families struggling with rising costs, small businesses squeezed, and health care becoming harder to afford—all while politics grew more divided and less responsive to everyday life.
Chris is running because he believes leadership should be about fairness, accountability, and practical solutions.
His priorities are shaped by real conversations with working families, seniors, and rural communities, and grounded in a simple belief: Kansas works best when leaders put people before politics.
A Practical Plan for Kansas
Affordable Health Care That Works for Kansans
Kansas families are paying too much for health care, and the state consistently ranks near the bottom nationally for affordability.
Chris believes this is unacceptable and preventable.
He supports expanding access to affordable care, including Medicaid expansion, lowering out-of-pocket costs, and protecting rural health care access.
Chris’s goal is to move Kansas from #49 to #1 in health care affordability by putting patients and families first.
Strong Public Schools and Quality Education
A strong education system is the foundation of a strong community.
Chris Tanner believes every child in District 54 deserves access to quality public schools, well-supported teachers, and the tools they need to succeed — whether that path leads to college, a trade, or starting a business right here in Kansas.
Chris supports fully funding public education, investing in workforce and technical training programs, and keeping curriculum decisions where they belong — with local educators, parents, and communities.
His focus is on preparing Kansas kids for the opportunities ahead while keeping schools accountable to the families they serve.
Freedom, Fairness and Protecting Our Constitutional Rights
Chris is committed to protecting constitutional rights and ensuring every Kansan is treated with dignity and fairness.
He believes freedom and fairness go hand in hand.
Chris supports equal treatment under the law, religious freedom, and personal liberty—without favoritism or discrimination.
Government should serve everyone fairly, regardless of background, belief, or zip code.
Lower Property Taxes, Stronger Small Businesses, Better Jobs
Rising property taxes are putting pressure on families, seniors, and small business owners across Kansas.
Chris Tanner believes people who work hard should be able to stay in their homes and invest in their communities without being priced out.
Chris supports lowering the property tax burden and strengthening locally owned businesses that create stable, good-paying jobs.
His focus is on building an economy that rewards work, keeps dollars local, and supports long-term community growth.
Support for Workers, Small Business, and Rural Communities
Rural Kansas is the backbone of this state, and Chris Tanner believes working families — whether they run a small business on Main Street, farm the land, or punch a clock every day — deserve a representative who fights for them, not political connections or corporate interests.
Chris supports fair wages, workers' rights, and policies that level the playing field for small businesses and independent farmers.
He believes rural communities deserve real investment, fair markets, and a voice that actually gets heard in Topeka — not empty promises.
His vision is a Kansas where hard work is rewarded and rural towns can thrive without losing their identity or values.
Leadership That Shows Up
Chris Tanner’s priorities reflect a simple promise: listen first, show up, and do what’s right for Kansas families and communities.
He believes leadership isn’t about ideology; it’s about responsibility, fairness, and results.
Chris invites you to learn more, share your perspective, and be part of this campaign.
Together, we can build a Kansas that works for everyone.
