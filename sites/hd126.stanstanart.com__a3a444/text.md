Conservative Priorities Stan Will FIGHT For
Supporting Law Enforcement & Protecting Our Community
Stan will back the blue.
He will work with Constable Mark Herman, the Harris County Sheriff, Texas DPS, Texas Rangers, and law enforcement at all levels to ensure our citizens are safe.
Lower Property Taxes and Reduce the High Cost of Insurance
Stan will work to deliver permanent property tax relief for Texas families and small businesses.
In addition, the cost of insurance is exploding.
We must stop frivolous lawsuits and implement reasonable limits so lawyers don't win the lottery in excessive judgments.
Partner with Local Leaders to identify Infrastructure solutions for Flood Mitigation
Stan, like many others in the district, helped his mother-in-law when her assisted living apartment was flooded.
Stan will work with Commissioner Tom Ramsey, Flood Control, and the other state and local stakeholders for lasting solutions.
Uphold our God-given and Constitutional Freedoms
Stan will work with President Trump, Governor Abbott, and fellow Republican Legislators to prevent Democrats from destroying our freedoms.
Stan will fight attempts to weaken free speech, strongly defend the Second Amendment and protect our religious liberty.
Grow the Economy & Cut Government Waste
Too often government and special interests make it difficult to obtain the American Dream.
We must ban lobbying, cut waste, and reduce burdensome regulations.
Let's release the power of our Texas workers and businesses to provide jobs and prosperity.
Stop the Woke and Defend the Truth in Biology
The left wants to destroy our country and replace it with a radical ideology.
The woke mindset wants to force our girls to not only compete with boys but accept them in their showers and bathrooms.
We must stand for Truth in biology.
Stand with Stan for Integrity
in our Elections
Stan is uniquely qualified to enhance the Integrity of our Texas Elections.
As the Harris County Clerk, he ran our Elections for 8 years and for the last 2 1/2 years has served as the Harris County Republican Party Vice Chair for Election Integrity where he has worked with Texas Legislators and national election integrity leaders.
Protect Life and our Texas Values of Faith, Family, & Freedom
Charlie Kirk's mission - and the impact of his death - is waking up and changing America.
Faith, Family, and Freedom are critical for a stable country.
Stan is fully pro-life and will stand for the conservative values that made Texas and this nation great.
Protect Parents' Rights & Support Excellence in Education
We have many good schools in our area.
Texas must continue on the path of ensuring that parents are in control of their education choices.
Competition improves the quality of education for all.
If a school is failing a student, parents need the option of providing the best education for their child.
