PRO-INNOVATION.
PRO–SOUTH TEXAS.
South Texas has the potential to become a major hub for innovation and job creation.
Laredo is one of the top three inland ports in America; a critical gateway for trade and commerce.
With the right leadership, we can build on that strength and turn South Texas into a center for technology, logistics, and economic growth.
But innovation should never come at the cost of our water, our land, or our rural way of life.
As the technology boom brings a wave of data centers across Texas, Tano knows these projects can drain scarce water, consume massive amounts of power, and swallow up the land our rural communities depend on.
Real leadership means getting all the facts first and putting South Texas families ahead of outside interests.
As Webb County Judge, Tano has stood up to protect our land and water, insisting that any project prove it will strengthen our communities, not strain them.
He will bring that same fight to Congress: we can lead in technology and still refuse to trade away the water, land, and affordable power our children will inherit.
Tano will fight to: Attract new industries and high-paying jobs that put South Texans first Invest in infrastructure and workforce development so our people fill these jobs Position South Texas as a leader in AI, tech, and logistics Protect our water, land, and rural communities from data centers and developments that take more than they give Stand with ratepayers so families and small businesses aren't stuck paying for big tech's power demands Demand transparency and the full facts before any project moves forward The future is being built now and South Texas should lead it, on our terms.
Foreign Policy: America First, Peace Through Strength Tano believes in an America First foreign policy rooted in strength, security, and common sense.
That means: Standing with our allies, including Israel Confronting hostile regimes that threaten American security Securing our borders and protecting critical infrastructure, including our ports Peace is achieved through strength — and weakness invites danger.
Tano will support policies that keep America safe while protecting our interests at home and abroad.
Washington has lost accountability — and the American people are paying the price.
Tano believes no one is above the law not politicians, not bureaucrats, not anyone.
As County Judge, Tano balanced budgets, lowered taxes, and made tough decisions to serve his community.
He’ll bring that same approach to Congress — putting people over politics and results over rhetoric.
