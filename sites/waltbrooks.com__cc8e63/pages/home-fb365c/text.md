Over the past five terms, I'm proud that we've been able to pass legislation protecting the unborn, reducing regulations, securing 2nd Amendment rights, increasing transparency on state properties, and lowering taxes - especially on Social Security.
I've learned a great deal and believe I've stood firm on the conservative principles that reflect our Southern Utah values.
Of course, there's always room for improvement, and I'm committed to continuing to learn and grow in order to best represent the people.
I take this responsibility very seriously and will work my hardest to uphold the Constitution and fight for the issues that matter most to our community.
I welcome any feedback or questions you may have.
Please let me know how I can better serve you going forward.
