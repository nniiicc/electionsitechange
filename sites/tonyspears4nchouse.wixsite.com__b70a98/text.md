TONY SPEARS FOR NC HOUSE DISTRICT 6
WELCOME
Tony Spears is seeking the office of NC House District 6, representing Harnett County.
As former president of the Harnett County NAACP, Spears has demonstrated leadership and a commitment to community service.
He as also served multiple terms as Harnett County Board of Elections Chair.
His candidacy is focused on addressing key issues such as quality education, teacher pay, and community development.
Spears' involvement in local events, like the NAACP Freedom Fund Banquet and the annual MLK Breakfast, showcases his dedication to uplifting the community.
Platform
- Quality Education:
Support our schools with adequate funding, advocate for teacher pay increases, and promote programs that benefit students and families in Harnett County.
- Community Development:
Foster economic growth, support local businesses, and create opportunities for residents to thrive.
- Voting Rights & Election Integrity:
Protect access to the ballot, ensure fair district maps, and maintain transparent elections.
- Affordable Housing:
Increase access to safe, affordable housing options and support initiatives that address housing needs in Harnett County.
