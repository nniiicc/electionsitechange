Check voter registration Register to vote Early in-person voting locations Election Day polling locations Request a mail / absentee ballot Find your local election commission Register or check your status by October 5 You must be registered to vote in the November 3 general election.
Check your status now, and if you have moved or never signed up, you can register in just a few minutes.
Voting by mail?
Read this first Your ballot must be mailed in time to arrive by the close of polls on November 3.
Hand delivery, drop boxes, or handing it to a poll worker is not permitted.
02 Three Ways to Cast Your Ballot Pick the option that works best for you.
Each path has its own deadlines – note them now so you do not miss your chance.
Option 1 Early In-Person Voting October 14 - October 29 Vote at any early voting location in the county where you are registered.
Hours vary by site.
Find Early Voting Locations Option 2 Vote on Election Day Tuesday, November 3 Polls are open across the county on Election Day.
Hours vary by location.
Find My Polling Place Option 3 Vote By Mail Request by October 24 Confirm you are eligible to vote absentee, then request your ballot by October 24, 2026 .
It must reach your county election commission by the close of polls on November 3.
