Taranto Former School Committee Chairman, North Chelmsford I am pleased to offer my strong support for Lorena Betts as Representative of the 16th Middlesex District.
Lorena brings a wealth of experience, dedication, and a genuine commitment to public service that will strengthen and uplift our community.
Lorena’s background as a legislative aide has equipped her with valuable insight into the needs of constituents, and her ability to provide bilingual services demonstrates her commitment to ensuring all voices are heard and represented.
She understands the importance of accessibility in government and has consistently worked to bridge gaps within the community.
As a civic advocate and community organizer, Lorena founded the Peacemaker Network to empower and inform residents.
This initiative reflects her passion for fostering engagement and building stronger, more informed communities.
She has made a sincere and ongoing effort to connect with constituents, taking the time to listen to their concerns, understand their priorities, and advocate for their hopes for the future.
Lorena’s depth of knowledge and hands-on experience provide her with meaningful insight into the needs of the 16th Middlesex District.
Her commitment to political advocacy ensures that she will represent all citizens fairly and thoughtfully.
I am confident that Lorena will serve with honesty, pride, and dedication.
She has the integrity, work ethic, and vision necessary to be an effective and compassionate representative for our district.
