Updated 28 Sep 2026 Our Campaign’s Top Issues The spiraling cost of living is neither an accident nor inevitable.
It is the product of laws and policies created by those with power and wealth, including within our own Great State.
Affordability and Economic Opportunity A Diversified Energy System Public Education Health Care for Every Alaskan Moratorium on AI Data Centers The Permanent Fund and PFD Affordability and Economic Opportunity Together, we can develop a vibrant, strong economy that works for the people.
A strong Alaska economy should work for the people by keeping our natural wealth and investment local, supporting small businesses, and respecting the value of working Alaskans.
I support a forward-looking energy strategy alongside investments in housing, food production, and transportation that strengthen communities across the state.
View more A Diversified Energy System The cost of energy is out of control and drives the cost of everything else up with it.
We need more energy, cheaper energy, and reliable energy not only in 10 years, but now.
Wind, solar, hydro, tidal, and geothermal are all rapidly reaching cost parity with fossil fuels, can be brought online much more quickly and are under-represented in our state’s search for energy.
Those solutions should be as much a part of the discussion as future pipelines.
For your lights to turn on when you flip the switch, power transmission is as important as generation.
We need to fund projects like the Cook Inlet PowerLink Project .
View more Public Education Public education provides opportunity and a better society.
Revitalization of Alaska’s public education system is foundational to a brighter future for our state.
A quality education costs money; not educating our kids well will cost a lot more in the long run.
View more Health Care for Every Alaskan Health Care for every Alaskan.
I support guaranteed access to quality health care for everyone in Alaska regardless of where they live or their ability to pay.
View more Moratorium on AI Data Centers Alaskans are rightfully alarmed about the sudden push for artificial intelligence (AI) and the data centers that drive it.
I advocate for an Alaska-wide moratorium on new data centers designed or intended for Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems or capable of consuming more than 50 megawatts until both federal and state legislation adequately protect our own enduring interests.
View more The Permanent Fund and PFD So many Alaskans depend on their PFD just to get by that it is a moral imperative.
However, if we follow the old formula, the Permanent Fund will be temporary and gone within a lifetime.
It is time to end the annual ritual of spending most of the legislative session each spring haggling over PFD amounts that don't comply with existing law anyway.
It time to update the law to codify the PFD at about current levels each year, adjusting for inflation as we go.
