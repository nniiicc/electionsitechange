Today is International Overdose Awareness Day, and just like the majority of Windsor County residents I too have felt the impact of overdoses.
My generation has lost classmates, friends, and young people who had so much more life left to live.
In my own journey with alcohol recovery I have seen the remarkable power that community and peer support can have on preventable death from substance abuse disorder.
I was heartbroken, but inspired by the stories shared on the common in Windsor tonight and I will work as your next State Senator to make sure those stories continue to be heard in Montpelier.
