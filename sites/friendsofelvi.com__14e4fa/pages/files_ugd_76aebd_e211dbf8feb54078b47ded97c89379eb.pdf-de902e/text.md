Hi Neighbor… It has been an honor to work for you during my first four years serving as Senator in Midtown, U-Med, and Spenard.
Together, our accomplishments have brought forward change for the betterment of public safety, cultural recognition, education, equal rights, personal caregivers, infrastructure and more.

Always representing your interests, Senator Elvi Gray-Jackson SENATE DISTRICT G FRIENDSOFELVI.COM ACCOMPLISHMENTS SB7 - Enacted law requiring Department of Public Safety to publish its policies & procedures on the department website.
SB47 – Enacted law which allows speech-language pathologists, physical therapists, and occupational therapists to provide proof of disability to clients for vehicle disability placards.
SB80 – Introduced legislation to require mental health education in schools.
SB183 – Introduced legislation to address issues with home and community-based care givers.
Co-sponsored various bills to include HB209 which authorizes the Commissioner of the Department of Natural Resources to hire emergency firefighters using general fund appropriations, HB55 relating to retirement benefits for peace officers & firefighters, and HB123 requiring State recognition of tribes.
SB216 – Introduced legislation establishing a Climate Change Commission.
Supported funding for education including an increase in the Base Student Allocation and forward funding school district appropriations.
Proposed funding for community projects to include completion of the Northern Lights Sound Barrier Fence, Campbell Area Traffic Study, and supported funding for the Long Trail and other projects that enhance our community and provide jobs for our hard-working Alaskans.
Supported School Bond Debt Reimbursement which provides property tax relief to citizens within the Municipality of Anchorage.
