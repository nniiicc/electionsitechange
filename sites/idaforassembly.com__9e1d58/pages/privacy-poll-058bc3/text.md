Campaign Contributions When you make a contribution, we or our payment-processing and fundraising service providers may collect information necessary to process your contribution.
Federal, state, and local campaign-finance laws may require the Campaign to collect, maintain, and publicly disclose certain information about contributors, including a contributor’s name, address, occupation, employer, contribution amount, and other information required by law.
The Campaign does not directly store complete credit or debit card information when transactions are processed by a third-party payment processor.
How We Use Information We may use information we collect to: Communicate with you about the Campaign; Send campaign news, election information, event invitations, and other updates; Respond to questions or requests; Coordinate volunteers and campaign activities; Process contributions; Maintain campaign records; Comply with campaign-finance, election, tax, and other legal requirements; Improve our website and communications; Measure the effectiveness of campaign outreach and advertising; Protect the security and integrity of our website and campaign operations; and Carry out other activities related to the Campaign.
Email and Text Messages If you provide your email address or mobile telephone number and consent to receive communications, the Campaign may send you campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event information, fundraising messages, and other campaign communications.
Message and data rates may apply to text messages.
Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP .
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails by following the unsubscribe instructions included in the email.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a contribution or otherwise supporting the Campaign.
Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe; you'll receive one confirmation message and no further texts.
Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at [email].
Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in information will not be shared, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers who help us operate the text program.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
How We Share Information We may share information with vendors and service providers that assist the Campaign with services such as website hosting, email and text communications, fundraising, payment processing, voter outreach, analytics, advertising, event management, and campaign operations.
We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, campaign-finance reporting requirements, or other governmental process.
The Campaign may share information with affiliated political organizations, campaign partners, or other organizations when appropriate for campaign-related activities and permitted by law.
We do not sell personal information for monetary compensation.
Public Campaign-Finance Records Political contributions are subject to campaign-finance disclosure laws.
Information required to be reported by the Campaign may become part of publicly available campaign-finance records maintained by government agencies.
The Campaign cannot delete or modify information that it is legally required to maintain, report, or disclose.
Data Security We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information under our control.
However, no website, electronic communication, or information-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Data Retention We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, legal compliance, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, security, or other legitimate purposes.
Certain campaign records must be retained for periods established by applicable law.
Children’s Privacy Our website and campaign services are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13.
Your Choices and Privacy Rights You may unsubscribe from campaign emails using the unsubscribe link included in our messages and may opt out of campaign text messages by replying STOP.
Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights regarding certain personal information we maintain about you.
Requests may be subject to verification and to exceptions required or permitted by law, including campaign-finance and recordkeeping requirements.
To submit a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information below.
Third-Party Websites Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.
The Campaign is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites.

Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or campaign operations.
