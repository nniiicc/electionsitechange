How New York’s Public Matching System Works
NYS Assembly Campaigns
New York’s Public Campaign Finance Program is designed to amplify small, local donations.
The Matching Formula (Assembly Races)
For eligible donations between $5 and $250:
- First $50 of a contribution is matched at 12 to 1
- Next $100 is matched at 9 to 1
- Next $100 is matched at 8 to 1
Only the first $250 of a contribution is matchable.

Why This Matters
- Small donors have real power
- Local residents carry more weight than large outside donors
- Grassroots campaigns can compete

Important Notes
- Donor must live in the district
- Donation must be within the eligible match range
- Candidate must qualify and comply with all program rules
- Matching funds are capped per election cycle
