Meet Raúl Torrez
Attorney General of New Mexico
Raúl Torrez is New Mexico’s 32nd Attorney General.
A former federal prosecutor and senior advisor in President Obama’s Department of Justice, and former elected District Attorney for the Albuquerque metro area, he has worked at every level of the criminal justice system to protect New Mexico communities and hold powerful interests accountable when they put families at risk.
Standing up for new mexico families
As Attorney General, Raúl Torrez has made a habit of taking on people bigger and richer than him — and winning.
He forced Big Pharma to cap insulin at $35.
He blocked a grocery merger that would have raised prices on New Mexico families.
He beat Mark Zuckerberg and Meta in court over child safety.
He’s held price-gougers accountable after wildfires.
Now he’s running for re-election to keep that record going.
Torrez is the first state attorney general in the country to take Meta to trial — and win.
A jury found that Meta’s product design intentionally harmed children and that the company lied to parents about it, and the court has now ordered sweeping changes to make Meta’s platforms safer for kids.
Torrez has emerged as a leading voice among Democratic attorneys general challenging federal overreach — from funding freezes to immigration enforcement policies — to protect New Mexicans’ rights and resources.
From capping insulin at $35, to blocking a grocery merger that would have hiked prices, to winning $115 million in utility rate credits for working families, Torrez holds the powerful accountable when they try to take advantage of New Mexico families.
When wealthy landowners fenced off the Pecos River to keep the public out, Torrez took them to court — and won.
New Mexico’s rivers belong to the people, not private interests.
He’s also suing chemical manufacturers over PFAS “forever chemical” contamination in New Mexico’s water.
