Contribution rules, plainly stated.
Contributions to Friends of Michael Riley are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes.
The campaign does not accept contributions from business entities.
Under R.I.
Gen.
Laws § 17-25-10.1(a)(1) , no one person may contribute more than $2,000 to a candidate within a calendar year, and a campaign may not accept more than that from any one person.
The cap is on the aggregate, so every contribution you have made to this campaign during 2026 counts toward it.
Business entity contributions are prohibited outright by § 17-25-10.1(h)(1) , which is why we do not accept them.
Contributors at or above the reporting threshold must provide occupation and employer.
