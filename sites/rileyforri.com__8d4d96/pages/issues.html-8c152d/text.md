Everything here is grounded in his day-to-day work as a real estate attorney, not national talking points.
Plank One Don’t Providence My Scituate Local Control · Zoning & Land Use Zoning, land use, and development decisions belong to the people who live here.
Scituate is the only municipality in Rhode Island without a home rule charter, which makes the General Assembly effectively our governing document — and makes this seat unusually consequential.
Lou will oppose one-size-fits-all state housing mandates that treat a rural town like a city block.
Read the full position → Plank Two Our Schools, Our Call Education · Local Boards & Parents Elected local school committees and the parents who show up to their meetings should be making the calls about our kids.
Lou’s job at the State House is to defend that authority, and to fight for a state funding formula that doesn’t quietly penalize a small rural district.
Read the full position → Plank Three Cut the Tax Burden Affordability · What It Costs to Stay The real estate conveyance tax, a non-portable estate tax, and a new surcharge on non-owner-occupied property all landed on Rhode Island families in the last two years.
Lou sees each of them on settlement statements every week, and will work to roll them back.
Read the full position → Plank Four Keep Our Doctors Access to Care Rhode Island can’t keep the physicians it has or attract the ones it needs.
Lou will work on reimbursement, licensing, and the cost of practicing here.
In a district where the nearest hospital is a drive, physician supply is a practical problem, not a policy abstraction.
Read the full position → Also on the record Where Lou stands on the rest These aren’t afterthoughts.
They’re positions Lou holds and will defend — they just aren’t what this campaign is being run on.
2nd Amendment Second Amendment Rights Rhode Islanders have a constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
Lou opposes the statewide ban on the sale, manufacture, and transfer of certain semi-automatic firearms, and supports policy that targets actual criminals instead of law-abiding sportsmen, hunters, and homeowners.
He is a member of the Cranston Fish & Game Association.
Read the full position → Constituent Service Veterans & Constituent Service Every constituent deserves a State Representative who answers the phone and follows through.
Lou is committed to responsive, hands-on constituent service — including for District 41’s veterans, who have earned better than a voicemail box.
