Lou is committed to responsive, hands-on constituent service for every resident of District 41 — including the veterans who have served the country and now call Scituate and Cranston home.
For veterans specifically, most of what a State Representative can actually do is casework: helping a constituent get a straight answer on a state benefit, chasing down an application that has stalled, or sorting out a municipal veterans' property tax exemption that was applied incorrectly.
It isn't glamorous work and it doesn't make headlines, but it is the part of the job that changes someone's month.
Whatever the issue — a state agency that isn't returning calls, a benefits question, or a local problem that needs a State House ally — Lou's office will be a resource for every constituent in District 41, not just those who voted for him.
