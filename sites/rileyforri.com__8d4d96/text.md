I’ll fight state mandates that treat a rural town like a city block.

Three ways to vote
Every voter in District 41 has three options.
Pick whichever one you’ll actually follow through on — they all count exactly the same.
Vote by mail
Request a mail ballot, fill it out at your kitchen table, and send it back.
You have to apply first — the application deadline is well before Election Day, and it is the one that catches people out.
Vote early, in person
Rhode Island holds an early in-person voting period at your local board of canvassers before Election Day.
No excuse or reason required.
Shorter lines, and you get it done.
Vote on Election Day
Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at your regular polling place.
Bring photo ID.
