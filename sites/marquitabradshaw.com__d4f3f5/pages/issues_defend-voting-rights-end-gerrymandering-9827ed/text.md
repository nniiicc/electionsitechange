Marquita believes every eligible Tennessean should have equal access to the ballot and a meaningful voice in government.
Her priorities include protecting voting access, improving election transparency, and ensuring voting systems are secure, accessible, and fairly administered.
She also supports an open, impartial redistricting process that creates representative districts based on clear standards and public input—not partisan advantage.
Fair maps and accessible elections help ensure voters can choose their representatives and hold government accountable.
