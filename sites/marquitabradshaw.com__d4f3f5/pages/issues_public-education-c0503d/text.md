Support Students
and Teachers
Marquita believes strong public schools are the foundation of thriving communities.
She will work to ensure every Tennessee student—regardless of ZIP code—has safe classrooms, modern learning resources, and opportunities to succeed.
She will also support competitive teacher pay, manageable workloads, professional development, and the respect educators deserve.
By investing in early and special education, career and technical programs, counselors, and updated facilities, Marquita will help students reach their full potential while strengthening families, communities, and Tennessee’s future.
