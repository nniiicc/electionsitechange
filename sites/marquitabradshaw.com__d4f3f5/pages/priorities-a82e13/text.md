Universal Affordable Broadband Environmental and Climate Justice Support Students and Teachers Every student deserves a well-funded public school, and every teacher deserves competitive pay, strong resources, and respect.
Investing in classrooms, educators, and student support services builds brighter futures and stronger communities.
View more Build a Fair and Equitable Economy Everyone deserves a fair opportunity to prosper.
By expanding access to good-paying jobs, supporting small businesses, ensuring fair wages, and removing barriers to economic opportunity, we can build an economy that works for every family and community.
View more Invest In Affordable Housing Marquita believes every Tennessean deserves a safe, stable, and affordable place to call home.
She will support policies that expand affordable housing, strengthen assistance for renters and first-time homebuyers, address homelessness, and protect families from unfair housing practices.
View more Healthcare Is a Right Every Tennessean deserves access to affordable, high-quality healthcare—regardless of income, ZIP code, employment status, or preexisting conditions.
That means lowering prescription-drug costs, protecting rural hospitals, expanding mental-health services, supporting maternal care, and ensuring families never have to choose between medical treatment and paying their bills.
Healthcare should serve people, not profits.
View more Defend Voting Rights.
End Gerrymandering.
Marquita believes every eligible Tennessean should have equal access to the ballot and a meaningful voice in government.
She supports fair voting laws, transparent elections, and independent redistricting that puts voters—not politicians—first.
View more Universal Affordable Broadband Marquita prioritizes affordable, reliable high-speed internet for every Tennessean, especially in rural and underserved communities.
Her approach supports broadband infrastructure, greater affordability, and expanded access to education, healthcare, jobs, and essential services.
View more Environmental and Climate Justice Every community deserves clean air, safe water, and protection from climate threats.
Bold action means advancing clean energy, reducing pollution, creating sustainable jobs, and ensuring vulnerable communities are not left behind.
View more Tennesseans deserve a senator who answers to them -- not to corporations and billionaires who have spent decades calling the shots.
