Universal Affordable Broadband
Marquita believes every Tennessean deserves reliable, affordable high-speed internet, regardless of income, location, or ZIP code.
She supports investing in modern broadband infrastructure, closing connectivity gaps in rural and underserved communities, and making internet service more affordable for working families.
Expanding broadband access will help students complete schoolwork, connect patients with telehealth services, support remote employment, and give small businesses new opportunities to grow.
It will also improve access to public resources and essential services.
By treating broadband as critical infrastructure, Marquita will work to ensure every community can participate fully in Tennessee’s digital economy.
