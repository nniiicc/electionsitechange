Build a Fair and Equitable Economy
Marquita believes every Tennessean deserves access to stable, well-paying jobs and meaningful opportunities to succeed.
She will pursue policies that ensure fair wages, strengthen workforce development, support small businesses, and protect workers from discrimination.
By expanding access to affordable childcare, housing, healthcare, education, and reliable transportation—and investing in historically underserved communities—Marquita will work to build shared prosperity and lasting financial security for families across Tennessee.
