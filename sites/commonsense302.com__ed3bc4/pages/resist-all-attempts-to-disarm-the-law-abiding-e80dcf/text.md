The U.S.
Supreme Court case District of Columbia v.
Heller (2008) established that the Second Amendment protects the right of INDIVIDUALS to keep and bear arms.
The Court ruled that the Second Amendment says the Right of individual PEOPLE – NOT the Individual State governments and/or the National Guard units ("well-regulated militia") under their command – but the Right of individual PEOPLE to keep and bear Arms, shall not be infringed .
