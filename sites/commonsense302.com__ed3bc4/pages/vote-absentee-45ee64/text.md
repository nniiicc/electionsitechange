Vote Absentee In Delaware!
It's Easy!
Just Follow This Step‑By‑Step Guide.
You don't have to figure this out alone!
If you are eligible to vote absentee, here are the basic steps from requesting your ballot to getting it safely back to the Department of Elections.
Make Sure You Qualify
Delaware absentee voting is for registered voters who cannot go to their assigned polling place on Election Day for a reason allowed by Delaware law.
Examples include being sick or physically disabled, being away from your district, certain work or public-service duties, being a student, providing constant care for someone who is ill or disabled, or certain religious reasons.
Request Your Ballot
The easiest way is online.
Go to Delaware's official Voter Portal, look up your voter record, and choose the option to request an absentee ballot.
You can also submit a paper application or contact your county Department of Elections office.
Vote Your Ballot
When your ballot arrives, open it and read the instructions that came with it.
Mark your choices carefully, then review your ballot before putting it into the ballot envelope.

Sign and Seal the Envelope
Put your voted ballot in the BALLOT ENVELOPE provided by the Department of Elections.
Return Your Ballot
You may return your completed absentee ballot by mail or hand-deliver it to the Department of Elections office for the county where you live.
If you are mailing it, mail it early.
The U.S.
Postal Service recommends mailing absentee ballots back at least 7 days before the election to help ensure timely delivery.
