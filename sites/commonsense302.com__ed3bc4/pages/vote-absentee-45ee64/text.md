It's Easy!
Just Follow This Step‑By‑Step Guide.
You don't have to figure this out alone!
If you are eligible to vote absentee, here are the basic steps from requesting your ballot to getting it safely back to the Department of Elections.
1 REQUEST IT 2 GET IT 3 VOTE 4 SIGN IT 5 RETURN IT 1 Make Sure You Qualify ✓ Delaware absentee voting is for registered voters who cannot go to their assigned polling place on Election Day for a reason allowed by Delaware law.
Examples include being sick or physically disabled , being away from your district , certain work or public-service duties , being a student , providing constant care for someone who is ill or disabled, or certain religious reasons.
Not sure whether you qualify?
Call your county Department of Elections office.
The phone numbers are below.
2 Request Your Ballot 💻 The easiest way is online.
Go to Delaware's official Voter Portal, look up your voter record, and choose the option to request an absentee ballot.
GO TO DELAWARE VOTER PORTAL You can also submit a paper application or contact your county Department of Elections office.
DON'T WAIT UNTIL THE LAST MINUTE.
For the November 3, 2026 General Election, October 30 is the last day the Department may mail absentee ballots.
The Department's deadline to issue absentee ballots is noon on November 2.
3 Vote Your Ballot When your ballot arrives, open it and read the instructions that came with it.
Mark your choices carefully, then review your ballot before putting it into the ballot envelope. ✓ Mark it ✓ Check it ✓ Put it in the ballot envelope 4 Sign and Seal the Envelope ✍️ Put your voted ballot in the BALLOT ENVELOPE provided by the Department of Elections.
VERY IMPORTANT: Check the information on the envelope, SIGN AND DATE THE OATH , and seal the envelope.
5 Return Your Ballot ✉️ You may return your completed absentee ballot by mail or hand-deliver it to the Department of Elections office for the county where you live.
DO NOT take your absentee ballot to an Early Voting site or your Election Day polling place.
If you are mailing it, mail it early.
