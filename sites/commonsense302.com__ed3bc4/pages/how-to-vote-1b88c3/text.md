Find My Polling Place ✉ Absentee Voting If you are unable to vote in person for a reason allowed under Delaware law, you may be eligible to vote by absentee ballot.
Important Your completed absentee ballot must be received by the Department of Elections no later than 8:00 p.m. on Election Day.
