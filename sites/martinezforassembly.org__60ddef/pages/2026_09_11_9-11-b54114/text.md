We pause to honor the nearly 3,000 lives lost on September 11, 2001, and to remember the courage shown by Flight 93 passengers, first responders, service members, and everyday people who stepped forward in the face of that unimaginable tragedy.
We should reflect on the resilience of our nation, the unity that followed, and the enduring spirit that continues to define America.
May we never forget.
May we always remember.
May we continue to stand together against adversaries.
And, may God bless the United States of America, now and forever.
