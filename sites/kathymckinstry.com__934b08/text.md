Opponent Profiles A deeper look at the records of the establishment candidates in this race.
MK Marcy Kaptur Incumbent Democrat Time in Office: Serving since 1983 (over 40 years).
While experienced, her long tenure represents the entrenched political establishment that often prioritizes party loyalty over bold, independent action for the district.
Key Criticisms: Accepts significant corporate PAC money.
Has been slow to adopt progressive stances on universal healthcare and aggressive climate action, often favoring incremental approaches that fail to meet the urgency of the moment.
Voting Record Highlights: Voted for numerous military budget increases while domestic programs remain underfunded.
Mixed record on trade agreements that have impacted Ohio manufacturing.
GovTrack Record ProPublica Profile DM Derek Merrin Republican Challenger Background: State Representative in the Ohio House.
Represents a rigid partisan approach that prioritizes culture wars and corporate interests over the daily needs of working families.
Key Criticisms: Strong advocate for policies that weaken public education through aggressive voucher expansion.
Opposes reproductive freedom and has consistently voted against minimum wage increases and union protections.
Voting Record Highlights: Supported legislation in the State House that restricted voting access.
Backed measures to roll back environmental protections in favor of corporate deregulation.
