As your representative, I will fight to bring higher-paying careers and new business opportunities to District 9, support small businesses, and advocate for fair wages.
I will strengthen Ohio’s core industries, including manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, and skilled trades, while expanding growth in technology and clean energy careers, through strong workforce and apprenticeship programs.
It is time to invest in our infrastructure, strengthen our workforce, and create quality careers and opportunities right here at home.
Education & Youth Our children are our future.
As an educator, I have seen firsthand the challenges facing our public schools.
I will fight for increased and equitable federal investment in public education, advocate for competitive pay and stronger support for teachers, and expand access to vocational training and affordable higher education.
Every child deserves a high-quality education and a real pathway to success, regardless of their zip code.
Housing & Property Taxes Homeownership remains out of reach for many Americans.
Only 23% own their home.
As your representative, I will fight to launch an Affordable Homeownership Initiative that makes ownership accessible to working families, first-time buyers, and underserved communities by reducing regulatory and financial barriers to entry.
I will work toward eliminating burdensome property taxes on homeowners, ensuring that once you own your home, you can truly afford to keep it.
Homeownership should be a path to stability and generational wealth—not a lifelong financial strain.
Immigration The Constitution is the law of the land.
The 14th Amendment, Section 1 states that no State shall deprive any person of life, liberty, or property without due process of law, nor deny any person within its jurisdiction equal protection of the laws.
This guarantees due process and equal protection for all.
I will push for the consistent and constitutional enforcement of immigration laws, and I will fight for legislation and oversight that protects due process, reinforces a lawful and structured immigration system, and improves administrative efficiency and border management.
Universal Healthcare More than 100 countries around the world provide universal or near-universal healthcare coverage, and nearly every developed nation guarantees care for its people.
The United States stands alone among its peers without guaranteed healthcare.
Today, approximately 26–27 million Americans—about 8% of the population—remain uninsured.
Healthcare should not be a privilege—it is a fundamental right.
I will fight for a universal healthcare system that ensures every American has access to affordable, high-quality care, reduces costs, and puts people over profits.
Abortion & Medical Freedom Medical decisions are a matter of individual rights and personal privacy.
As a member of Congress, I will fight for legislation and oversight that protect the integrity of the doctor-patient relationship, safeguard medical privacy rights, and limit unnecessary government interference in personal healthcare decisions.
Veterans Affairs As a Marine Veteran, I understand the sacrifices our service members and their families make.
We must honor our commitments to our veterans by fighting for full and responsible funding of the VA, reducing wait times for care, and expanding access to mental health services and successful transition programs.
Our heroes deserve not only gratitude but the highest standard of care, support, and opportunity when they return home.
Police Brutality & Public Safety Public safety must be rooted in both protection and accountability.
No unarmed American should die at the hands of law enforcement.
As your representative, I will fight for policies that strengthen accountability, improve training, and prioritize de-escalation in policing.
Too many incidents involve situations that could have been resolved without the use of force, particularly those involving mental health crises and welfare checks.
I will advocate for expanded mental health response programs and support legislation that ensures law enforcement is properly trained and equipped to handle these situations safely and effectively.
Federal Spending & National Priorities Federal resources must be aligned with the needs of the American people.
As your representative, I will advocate for a thorough reevaluation of foreign aid to ensure it reflects U.S. interests and accountability, while prioritizing strong domestic investment in infrastructure, housing, healthcare, and education.
I will also fight to increase transparency and oversight in federal spending so that taxpayer dollars are used effectively, responsibly, and in direct service to our communities.
Data Centers & Community Protection No new data centers should be approved without strict standards that protect the public.
As your representative, I will advocate for policies that require full transparency before approval, ensure clear and measurable community benefits, promote responsible use of energy and water resources, and establish strong local oversight and accountability.
Communities must have a real voice in what is built around them.
