Home How to Vote Issues Events News Volunteer Contact Donate Select Language ​ ▼ A Life of Service The Name for Change Kathy McKinstry's story is not one of privilege, it is one of perseverance, discipline, and unwavering commitment to family and country.
Raised in the Midwest by a strong, determined single mother who migrated from Tennessee in search of opportunity, Kathy grew up in a large family where resources were limited, but love, resilience, and responsibility were abundant.
From an early age, she understood what it meant to sacrifice, to share, and to step up—not because it was easy, but because it was necessary.
There were no shortcuts.
No safety nets.
Only faith, hard work, and the belief that a better future had to be built, not given.
With a clear vision to change the trajectory of her life, Kathy made a courageous decision: she enlisted in the United States Marine Corps.
The Marines became more than a career, it became a proving ground.
It was where discipline was sharpened, leadership was forged, and purpose was solidified.
Kathy served honorably, committed to protecting the very freedoms she had grown up striving to access.
But service comes with a cost.
During her time in the military, Kathy became disabled—an experience that would reshape her path, but never break her spirit.
Instead, it deepened her resolve.
She understood firsthand the realities that many veterans face: navigating healthcare systems, rebuilding identity, and continuing forward despite physical limitations.
Amid her service journey, Kathy met the love of her life, Marcus.
Their connection was rooted in shared values, commitment, family, and faith.
Together, they built a life grounded in love and partnership, raising two daughters who would become the center of their world.
Kathy embraced motherhood with the same intensity she brought to her service—present, protective, and purposeful.
But life would test her again.
In 2016, Kathy lost her husband Marcus, becoming a widow and the sole pillar for her family.
In the face of unimaginable loss, she did what she has always done—she rose.
She made the intentional decision to relocate from Murrieta, California to Fremont, Ohio, bringing her daughters closer to family, community, and support.
It was not just a move, it was a rebuilding of foundation, rooted in stability and connection.
Kathy returned to school and stepped into education, becoming a teacher and mentor while continuing to care for her family and support those around her.
Then, in a short span of time this year, Kathy experienced profound loss once again, losing both her mother in March and her brother in April.
These moments did not weaken her—they clarified her.
After a lifetime of service, sacrifice, and standing strong for others, Kathy knows one thing with certainty: The people of this country deserve better.
She is not stepping forward because it is easy.
She is stepping forward because it is necessary.
Kathy McKinstry is running for Congress to bring accountability, restore stability, and fight for the people of Ohio's 9th District—families, veterans, and communities who have been overlooked for far too long.
This is not the beginning of her service.
It is the continuation of it.
