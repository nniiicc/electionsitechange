Her platform focuses on common-sense policies that put citizens first and secure a better future for every member in our community.
Make Houston Affordable Again Keeping Life Affordable for Families Rising costs are affecting families across our communities.
By prioritizing responsible spending and policies that reduce inflationary pressures, we can help lower the cost of essentials like groceries, housing, and everyday household needs.
Families deserve economic stability so they can plan for the future and focus on what matters most — their homes, children, and communities.
Public Safety Safe Communities & Strong Families Every family deserves to feel safe in their neighborhoods and communities.
Supporting local safety initiatives and community programs helps protect children, families, and seniors.
Encouraging engagement with local law enforcement, neighborhood programs, and public safety measures ensures that everyone can thrive in secure, stable communities.
We need leaders who can voice support for our law enforcement and be a bridge to the community.
Secure Our Borders Support our law enforcement and Border Patrol in stopping illegal crossings and protecting our most vulnerable from human trafficking.
Houston is one of the nation’s largest hubs for trafficking — with major transportation routes, international ports, and a high volume of reported cases — and local authorities have recovered dozens of victims, including juveniles, in recent years.
Raising awareness, strengthening enforcement, and expanding community resources are essential to prevent traffickers from exploiting children and families.
Make Healthcare More Accessible Accessible and Affordable Healthcare Healthcare should be reliable, affordable, and accessible to all families.
By promoting transparency, competition, and options that put patients first, communities can ensure that everyone has access to quality care.
Families should have confidence that their healthcare needs — from preventative care to urgent services — can be met without undue burden or complexity.
Expand Educational Freedom & Strengthen Family Values Education and Family Engagement Strong communities start with strong families and strong education.
Empowering parents to participate in their children’s learning, providing safe and supportive school environments, and fostering programs that strengthen family involvement helps children succeed academically and socially.
