Our district
I love our district for its natural beauty, people, and sense community in all our neighborhoods.
District 61a is in the heart of Minneapolis, and includes Bryn Mawr, Cedar-Isles-Dean, Downtown, East Isles, Elliot Park, Harrison, Kenwood, Loring Park, Lowry Hill, Lowry Hill East and a portion of Uptown.

How to vote in elections in 2026
Election Days:
- The 2026 primary is August 11th.
Katie does not have a primary opponent, but many other important races are at stake.
- The 2026 general election is November 3rd.
On this date, you can vote for Katie and all other races in your district.

New to the district?
Minnesota has same-day voter registration, but you can also pre-register to vote from now until July 21.
Doing this helps you avoid longer lines or delays on Election Day.
