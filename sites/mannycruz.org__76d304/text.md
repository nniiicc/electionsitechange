"Ready on Day One!" Meet Manny Dad.
Public Servant.
E ntrepreneur.
Non-profit leader.
Salem has always been home.
I was raised by a single mother, and we survived domestic violence and abuse.
When I almost gave up on school, it was my mom and mentors like Linda Saris and Marshall Bradstreet who wouldn’t give up on me.
They saved my life and taught me the importance of giving back to my community. ​ Public service is my way of giving back, whether as a coach and mentor at LEAP4Ed and the Boys and Girls Club, a non-profit leader, or Vice Chair of the Salem School Committee.
As District Director for State Representative Paul Tucker, I’ve secured millions of dollars for Salem schools, tourism jobs, and community projects like the Council on Aging. ​ I’m running for State Representative to create good-paying green jobs, recover from COVID-19, and solve the housing crisis.
I’m asking for your vote on September 6th because I’m ready to fight for our community on day one.
Read More Priorities New Category Housing & Seniors We are facing a housing crisis in Salem.
I’ll fight for more affordable homeownership and rental assistance programs.
I’ll secure funds to stop senior property tax increases and provide quality affordable long-term and in-home care.
Education & Young People Kids in our public schools are struggling.
Access to youth programming and summer jobs literally saved my life.
As your next State Rep I’ll be an effective champion for our kids and fight for our share of education dollars from Boston.
Economic Development & Tourism Jobs As a former restaurant entrepreneur and park ranger, I know that small businesses, green jobs, and tourism are the backbone of Salem’s economy.
We need good-paying local jobs and I’ll secure state funding to expand and protect them.
Climate & Environmental Justice I’ll prioritize expanding bus, ferry, and commuter rail services and ensure that they are safe and reliable.
We need to build infrastructure right now to prevent flooding, invest in off-shore wind power, and expand tax incentives for home solar panels.
Public Safety & COVID-19 To improve public safety, we must provide pathways out of poverty and build trust between the community and law enforcement.
I'm ready on day one to secure state and federal funding to combat the short-term and long-term effects of COVID-19.
Mental Health & Opioids I’ll fight for additional funding for on the ground addiction recovery and mental health services.
I’ll work with community health centers, small businesses, and organizations to create a long-term policy solution to address substance use disorder.
