Meet Mike Jacobs
Mike Jacobs is a lifelong Anne Arundel County resident, small-business owner, youth coach, and community volunteer.
He serves as President of the Lake Shore Athletic Association and has dedicated over 25 years to coaching youth sports.
With experience in business and public service, Mike is committed to responsible growth, environmental stewardship, and family values in District 31.

Priorities
- We believe too many Maryland families are squeezed by high taxes and rising utility bills.
We’ll fight for honest budgets, stop tax hikes, and roll back unfair energy mandates so your paycheck goes further and your electric & gas bills don’t keep climbing.
- Public safety comes first.
When criminals act, they must face serious consequences.
We’ll back law enforcement, strengthen penalties for repeat offenders, and ensure that our streets, schools and homes are places citizens feel safe and proud to live.
- Promote clean water initiatives, responsible shoreline management, and environmental programs that protect our natural resources for future generations.
- Ensure law enforcement, firefighters, and emergency personnel have the resources, respect, and training they need to protect our families and neighborhoods.
- Invest in education while empowering parents to have a voice in their children’s classrooms and curricula, ensuring accountability and excellence.
- Streamline local and state regulations to help small businesses grow, create jobs, and contribute to a stronger Maryland economy.
- Encourage open dialogue between government and citizens, ensuring every voice is heard and every decision is made in the public’s best interest.
- Prioritize balanced budgets, efficient spending, and transparency to ensure taxpayer dollars are used wisely and effectively.
