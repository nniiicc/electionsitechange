Meet Mike Donate Now Meet Mike Jacobs Mike Jacobs is a lifelong Anne Arundel County resident, small-business owner , youth coach, and community volunteer.
He serves as President of the Lake Shore Athletic Association and has dedicated over 25 years to coaching youth sports .
With experience in business and public service, Mike is committed to responsible growth , environmental stewardship , and family values in District 31 .
Mike Jacobs, Pasadena MD At a glance: Board of Appeals - 8 years President, Lake Shore Athletic Association 25+ years girls softball coach; 12 years youth football Founder/co-founder of local community events VP, JB Machine Parts & Supply Priorities Lowering the cost of living We believe too many Maryland families are squeezed by high taxes and rising utility bills.
We’ll fight for honest budgets, stop tax hikes, and roll back unfair energy mandates so your paycheck goes further and your electric & gas bills don’t keep climbing.
Tough on crime — protecting our neighborhoods Public safety comes first.
When criminals act, they must face serious consequences.
We’ll back law enforcement, strengthen penalties for repeat offenders, and ensure that our streets, schools and homes are places citizens feel safe and proud to live.
Protect the Chesapeake Bay Promote clean water initiatives, responsible shoreline management, and environmental programs that protect our natural resources for future generations.
Public safety and support for first responders Ensure law enforcement, firefighters, and emergency personnel have the resources, respect, and training they need to protect our families and neighborhoods.
Strong schools and parental involvement Invest in education while empowering parents to have a voice in their children’s classrooms and curricula, ensuring accountability and excellence.
Cut red tape for small businesses Streamline local and state regulations to help small businesses grow, create jobs, and contribute to a stronger Maryland economy.
Community engagement and transparency Encourage open dialogue between government and citizens, ensuring every voice is heard and every decision is made in the public’s best interest.
Fiscal Responsibility Prioritize balanced budgets, efficient spending, and transparency to ensure taxpayer dollars are used wisely and effectively.
