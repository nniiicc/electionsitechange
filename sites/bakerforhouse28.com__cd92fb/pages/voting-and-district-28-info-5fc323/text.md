Before, the boundary of the district extended as far south as West Point, but didn’t run directly against the banks of the Ohio as it moved north, and encompassed a large section of the Parkwood and Medora neighborhoods to the east.
Now, District 28 stretches to the banks of the Ohio from Meadow Lawn to Valley Station, ends it’s southern expansion at Meadow Lawn and no longer includes Medora, stops in the east at Waverly Hills, but does expand north to include Pleasure Ridge Park and parts of the Sylvania neighborhoods.
