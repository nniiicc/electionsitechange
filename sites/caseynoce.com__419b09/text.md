Families First
DIAPERS ARE HEALTHCARE
The average family with little ones spends about $100 a month on diapers or pullups.
These are essential items for a baby or toddler.
They are not a luxury, they are a necessity.
Casey will create a State Child Tax Credit and exempt certain baby products from sales tax.
RAISE THE CHILD TAX CREDIT
The current State Child Tax Credit is $500 per child.
Casey plans to raise that number to $2000 per child up to $6000 of State Income Tax Credit.
Families need someone to put them first.
