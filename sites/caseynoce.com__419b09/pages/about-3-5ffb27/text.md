top of page MENU Close Home Events Donate Get Involved Education About Small Government Fiscal Policy Endorsements Endorsement Form Campaign Internship Blog About Casey Noce Casey Noce is a husband and a father.
He is a Middle School History Teacher, a Pastor, and a Realtor.
As a teacher, Casey became active in the legislative process when he endorsed and testified in favor of HB4141, a bill signed into law this February by Governor Whitmer, creating a statewide mandate for schools to prohibit cellphones in class.
Casey provided pivotal testimony in committee.
You can view the video here .
After this experience, he began to feel a calling to serve as a legislator.
Casey has served on the Downtown Development Authority for the City of Adrian.
He was the Chairman of the Branding and Beautification Committee.
He was a Board Member of the Lenawee County GOP Executive Board.
He also served as the Campaign Manager for former State Rep Bronna Khale.
Community Involvment Casey loves this community.
He moved to Northville in 2013 to join the pastoral staff at Northville Christian Assembly.
He was the Varsity Boys Tennis Coach at Stevenson High School in 2025 and Varsity Football Coach at Westland Lutheran and Plymouth Christian Academy 2022-24.
He recently stepped back into the Theater as the Director of Plymouth Christian’s production of Newsies this spring.
A Career of Serving Casey began his career as a pastor and teacher at Northville Christian Church and School, where he currently teaches 7/8 History.
Casey is a dedicated public servant with a deep desire to encourage the next generation.
Casey is also a successful author and speaker.
He and his wife are licensed Realtors with National Realty Centers based in Northville.
They love to assist people through the complex and often tense experience of home purchasing and selling.
