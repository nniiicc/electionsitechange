Don't Tax Teachers
Michigan's education system needs reform.
The main issue I will address is taxation.
DON'T TAX TEACHERS.
Teachers and all school employees should be exempt from state income tax.
This would effectively give the average teacher a nearly $6,000 raise.
We need to prioritize teachers when it comes to school spending.
Lansing spent 1.3 billion dollars of School Aid money intended for K-12 schools on colleges and universities.
Spending School Aid money on... schools... shouldn't have to be something we debate.
Casey Noce will make sure schools have the money they need to pay teachers and educate students.
Z's TO A’s CAMPAIGN
The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends that middle and high schools start no earlier than 8:30 a.m., recognizing that adolescent circadian rhythms naturally shift later during puberty.
Aligning school schedules with biology is not indulgence – it is evidence-based prevention.
See recommendation here.
Casey Noce's plan would push the start time of schools back till no earlier than 8:30.
Local high schools begin begin at 7:25!
The average student spends over 30 minutes to get ready for school plus an additional average 28 minutes of travel time.
These kids need to wake up at 6am just to make it to school on time.
They are falling asleep in the classroom and their physical and mental health are suffering.
Adolescent brains and bodies need sleep.
