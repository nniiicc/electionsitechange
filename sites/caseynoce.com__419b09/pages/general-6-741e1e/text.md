Fiscal Responsibility
Accountability
Michigan lawmakers often put pork and benefits to special interests and lobbyists in the bills they draft.
Legislators should work for the taxpayer, not lobbyists.
No State Property Tax
I would cut the state property tax and transfer tax.
The State shouldn't hold most people's biggest asset hostage.
Michiganders should not need to pay an annual tax to the State to live on their own property.
LOW SMALL BUSINESS TAX
I will propose an expansion of the small business tax credit so more businesses receive much needed tax relief.
I will also develop a plan to implement a scaffolded business income tax rate, so startups can get on their feet.
