District 4
District 4 encompasses over 169,000 people spanning the distance of 2,848 square miles.
We are a diverse district with historical sites, national parks, tourist destinations, and nationally recognized festivals.
Our communities have much to offer our current residents and to those who will potentially move in.
There are strengths and weaknesses to each of our counties, but together we are better than when we act alone.
Sharing our knowledge, understanding areas of growth as well as our limitations can help us all make District 4 even better tomorrow than it is today.
I want to work with you to see our district become a powerhouse of the state so others are looking to us to see how they could be improving their communities.
Let’s work together to make this possible.
