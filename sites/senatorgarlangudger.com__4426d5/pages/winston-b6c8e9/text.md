Winston County
Winston County is known as the “free state” county in Alabama.
It is the smallest of the four counties in both population and geography.
There is a very active Arts Council and it is the home of Looney's Tavern.
The Winston County School District consist of four high schools, 1 middle school, 4 elementary schools, and a Career Academy.
The Haleyville School District which consists of a high school, middle school, elementary, and a technical school.
Senator Gudger's district includes Addison and Haleyville schools.
