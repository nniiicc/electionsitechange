Randy Meier for Iowa House District 69
Randy grew up on a family Century Farm in Jackson County and built a lifetime of public service through military, law enforcement, and community work.
A U.S.
Army veteran and retired police detective commander, he continues serving as an advocate for seniors and fraud victims.
Grounded in integrity, collaboration, and fairness, Randy is running to put people—not politics—first.
For a Better Clinton County
Fair Taxes and Wages for Working Families
Fair taxes and wages matter in Clinton County because working families deserve to keep up with rising costs, support local businesses, and invest in their futures without shouldering an unfair burden while the wealthiest pay less.
Water Quality and Cancer Rates in Iowa
Poor water quality threatens Iowa families’ health, drives up medical costs, and harms agriculture and local economies.
Rising cancer rates raise serious concerns about environmental exposure, making clean water essential to protecting lives, communities, and future generations.
Strengthen Public Education in Iowa
Public education is vital to Iowa’s future because it prepares students for good-paying jobs, strengthens local communities, and keeps the state competitive.
Strong schools support families, attract employers, and ensure every child has a fair chance to succeed.
