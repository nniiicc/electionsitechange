What We’re Looking For
We are looking for an experienced Field Director who shares our values.
This is a people-powered, volunteer-driven grassroots campaign.
Many of our staff and volunteers already have organizing experience; we're looking for someone who can build on that foundation with strategic thinking, strong management, and disciplined execution.
The ideal candidate enjoys creating infrastructure and opportunities for volunteers to plug-in and in the process cultivate leadership within the coalition.
To Apply
To apply, please email a resume in a PDF format to nairka@maiforus.com with a list of three references with the subject line “Field Director - [First, Last NAME].”
Please respond to the following questions in the body of the email.
As a note, candidates that don’t respond to these questions won’t be considered for the position.
- 1) Are you willing to relocate to Sacramento, California for the position? 2) When is your earliest start date? 3) Do you have a driver’s license and reliable access to a vehicle?
References will not be contacted until the final round of interview.

- Work with the Campaign Manager to develop and execute a campaign field strategy and goals for the race.
- Develop and execute a comprehensive field plan, including volunteer deployment, precinct strategy, field budget management, phone banking, canvassing, and voter contact goals.
- Develop a robust volunteer recruitment program infrastructure.
And Recruit, train, and manage field staff and volunteers.
- Plan and execute field events, including launches, town halls, canvass kickoffs, volunteer training, and community events.

- Build coalitions with partner organizations and expand the campaign's grassroots network, including managing endorsement processes and questionnaires in partnership with the Campaign Manager.
- Manage overall field operations, tracking performance goals, and establish accountability systems to ensure campaign goals are met.
- Duties as assigned.

About the Campaign
Mai is the daughter of Hmong refugees, born and raised in Sacramento.
Mai’s parents fled war in Laos and arrived in California to find a new life.
As the oldest of 16 children, she helped raise her siblings while working hard to build a career dedicated to helping her community thrive.
Mai is a proud product of public schools and was the first in her family to attend college.
As a young organizer, Mai returned home to serve the community that raised and supported her.
Mai Vang is running in California's 7th Congressional District.
