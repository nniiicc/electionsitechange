Enhancing Public Safety in California's District 39
As a representative of the people, I believe that public safety is a top priority.
Our law enforcement professionals put their lives on the line every day to protect our communities, and they deserve our full support.
It's essential that Sacramento provides our law enforcement agencies with the resources they need to effectively serve and protect our communities.
Law Enforcement Funding
Our law enforcement agencies need adequate funding to support their operations, including personnel, equipment, and training.
For their safety and ours!
Community Policing
Building trust between law enforcement and the communities they serve is crucial.
We need to support initiatives that foster positive relationships and improve communication.
Crime Prevention
We need to support evidence-based programs that address the root causes of crime and work to prevent it from happening in the first place.
