Army Veteran | High Desert Resident | Community Advocate Paul Marsh is a proud U.S.
Army Veteran and a High Desert resident for over 40 years.
With a long career in the mortgage industry, Paul has helped countless families achieve the American Dream of homeownership—giving him a deep understanding of the financial challenges facing working families and small business owners today.
Dedicated to serving his community, Paul currently serves as a Victorville Planning Commissioner and has been an active member of the City of Victorville’s Homelessness Solutions Task Force .
Through these roles, he has worked to bring practical, results-driven solutions to local issues—always putting people before politics.
Paul’s passion for reform deepened as he witnessed firsthand the impact of government waste and inefficiency while his wife courageously battled cancer.
That experience strengthened his resolve to fight for accountability , transparency , and responsible spending in Sacramento.
Paul believes it’s time for local voices to be heard in state government.
He’s not a career politician— he’s one of us, standing up for all of us .
