Home About Where I stand Book a Call Volunteer Donate Home About Where I stand Book a Call Volunteer Donate I believe government works best when it stays focused on the real needs of the community.
My priorities are simple: safer neighborhoods, stronger families, responsible spending, and protecting the rights of Pennsylvania residents.
Protecting Constitutional Rights The rights guaranteed by the Constitution and the Pennsylvania Constitution must always be protected.
I support policies that defend free speech, religious freedom, and the rights of citizens to participate openly in their government.
Protecting the Second Amendment Law-abiding Pennsylvanians have the right to responsibly own firearms.
I support protecting the Second Amendment while ensuring laws focus on criminals, not responsible gun owners.
Protecting Life I believe every life has value.
Pennsylvania should support policies that protect life while also strengthening support systems for mothers, families, and children.
Supporting Families & Parental Rights Parents should have a strong voice in their children’s education and well-being.
I support transparency in schools and policies that keep families involved in decisions affecting their children.
Education & School Choice Every child deserves access to a quality education.
I support strong public schools while also giving families options such as charter schools, career training programs, and other pathways that best serve their children. ⚠ Domestic Violence Education & Awareness Domestic violence often begins in unhealthy teenage relationships.
I support incorporating education in Pennsylvania high schools that teaches students how to recognize the warning signs of abuse, understand the different forms domestic violence can take, and know how to report it and seek help.
Prevention and awareness can save lives.
Supporting Volunteer Firefighters & First Responders Volunteer firefighters are the backbone of many Pennsylvania communities, yet departments across the state struggle with recruitment and retention.
I support providing tax relief and vehicle registration or fee waivers for volunteer firefighters and first responders who dedicate their time and risk their safety to protect our communities.
Supporting them is a practical way to strengthen local emergency services.
Support for Special Needs Children & Families Families raising children with special needs deserve strong community support.
Pennsylvania must ensure these families have access to services, education resources, and programs that help individuals with disabilities succeed.
Special attention should be given to helping young adults with disabilities transition from school into adulthood, employment, and independent living.
Responsible Spending & Lower Taxes Taxpayers deserve responsible government.
I support transparent budgeting, reducing wasteful spending, and making sure tax dollars are used efficiently to benefit our communities.
Public Safety & Support for Law Enforcement Safe communities are the foundation of a strong society.
I support law enforcement officers who work every day to keep our neighborhoods safe, while also ensuring accountability and community trust.
Strong Local Communities Local governments should focus on practical solutions that improve everyday life—safe streets, reliable services, responsible development, and support for local businesses and jobs.
My Commitment My commitment is simple: listen to the people, focus on practical solutions, and represent our community with integrity and accountability.
I am Here for you!
