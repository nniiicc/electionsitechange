Environmental Protections
West Boylston is a unique and beautiful community with distinctive land characteristics; nearly 70% of the town’s land is conserved by the Department of Conservation & Recreation (DCR) to protect the Wachusett Reservoir.
We must treat our planet with care so that generations to come have a healthy, substantiable, and affordable way of living.

Public Safety & Criminal Justice Reform
Public safety is the cornerstone of my work, and for me, it is deeply personal.
Growing up, my father was a police officer in Worcester.
Although he was a member of law enforcement committed to upholding the law, he was also fair – a philosophy that has shaped my advocacy for a justice system that actually works.
To me, public safety means prioritizing evidence-based strategies that help reduce crime.

Recovery Community Support
As a member of the recovery community, I work every day to ensure our state is expanding upon the resources necessary for those in recovery and those in the process of choosing recovery.
By embracing harm reduction strategies, patient education, and compassionate care, we are advancing public health and offering renewed hope to those affected by substance use disorders.
Together, we are forging a path towards a healthier, more supportive future for all.
