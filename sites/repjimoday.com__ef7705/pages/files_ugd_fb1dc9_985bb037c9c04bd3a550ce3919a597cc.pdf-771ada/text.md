FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: Anna Duffy, 617-722-2090 February 3, 2021 CENTRAL MASSACHUSETTS CAUCUS AND METROWEST CAUCUS REQUEST ANSWERS FROM BAKER ADMINISTRATION ABOUT VACCINE DISTRIBUTION Legislators share concerns and questions about the COVID-19 vaccine in Massachusetts BOSTON– Representative James O’Day (D-West Boylston), Representative Carolyn Dykema (D-Holliston), and Senator Michael Moore (D-Millbury) along with their colleagues in the MetroWest and Central Massachusetts regions sent a letter on Wednesday to Governor Charles Baker, Lieutenant Governor Karyn Polito, and Health and Human Services Secretary Marylou Sudders to express their concerns and questions about the COVID-19 vaccination program.
While the group of legislators recognizes the extraordinary task of community-wide vaccination, they urge the Baker-Polito Administration to take additional steps to prepare for the future and alleviate the concerns of local residents.
“We hear daily from our constituents about the troubles they have encountered when trying to schedule a vaccination appointment and find information about the rollout”, said Representative James O’Day (D-West Boylston).
“We have the same exact questions and concerns- without any clear, concise answers to provide.
We want to help our constituents and every Massachusetts resident, but can only do that with more guidance and support from the Administration.” “Our pandemic recovery and safety of our residents will depend on providing an organized process for vaccine delivery and clear communication with the public,” said Representative Carolyn Dykema (D-Holliston).
“Massachusetts has a wealth of talent and expertise and we should be leading the country in demonstrating what an efficient and effective COVID-19 vaccine program looks like.
As legislators, we're committed to working with the administration to get this right and to making sure that all of our constituents have access to these life-saving vaccines.” "It has been disheartening to hear from so many constituents on the difficulties that are being faced in this rollout.
The current system is not good enough, and there is a need for clearer communication and better coordination with senior centers and towns to expedite the process,” said Senator Michael Moore (D-Millbury).
“I thank the Lieutenant Governor for taking the time to speak to me and explain the improvements that are going to be made, but given the months the administration had to prepare for this rollout, we should not be in a situation of needing to make significant changes to the process." Moderna vaccine Website upgrades o Centralized website o Pre-registration tool or similar feature The delegation eagerly looks forward to working with Governor Baker and will follow up with the Administration in a timely manner given the urgency of this matter.
