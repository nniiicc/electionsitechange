I love our great state
I seek to continue the legacy of stalwart conservative champions who have occupied the People's seat here in Gilbert.
I believe strongly in American exceptionalism and that the principles of rugged individualism are what built this nation and propelled Arizona to greatness over the past 100+ years.
This spirit of The West, its freedom and opportunity, is what continues drawing people to our state.
Arizona is worth protecting.
My focus will be on ensuring appropriate checks on the growth of government, protecting the taxpayer, defending the family & parental rights, restoring election integrity, protecting school choice, and ensuring stewardship of our water.
I love the principles of the small town of Gilbert I grew up in and still call home.
My focus will be on protecting freedom for the next generation of patriots in this great State 48.
I pledge to be a common sense representative and an iron-clad fighter who will help Keep Arizona Free.
I humbly ask for your support and your vote.
Onward.
-Tyler Farnsworth
