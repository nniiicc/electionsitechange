PRIORITIES
PUBLIC EDUCATION
Students must be at the center of Arizona’s education policy.
That includes modernizing the education funding system to support teacher and staff pay, advocating for student-centered wellness, and reversing the failed voucher expansion experiment.
WORKING FAMILIES & AFFORDABILITY
Working families deserve leaders who prioritize them over corporate profits.
It’s time to invest in childcare, affordable housing, affordable healthcare, and crucial services while tackling utility price hikes and corporate greed that harm consumers.
CLIMATE & ENVIRONMENT
Our state is at a climate crossroads.
We must address extreme heat, mega drought, wildfires, flooding, and air pollution, water conservation, land protection, and tribal sovereignty.
REPRODUCTIVE FREEDOM
Protect abortion and advance laws keeping personal healthcare decisions between a patient and their healthcare provider.
VOTING RIGHTS & DEMOCRACY
Support pro-voter issues like vote centers, expanding voter registration opportunities, and vote-by-mail.
IMMIGRATION
We must keep families together, treat people with dignity, and provide an opportunity for citizenship.
