Leadership grounded in real life, real values, and real courage for House District 43A
I’m Kyrstin Schuette, an organizer, movement builder, and proud Crystal neighbor running to represent District 43A in the Minnesota State House.
I’ve spent more than 15 years helping people harness their power, pass life-changing legislation, and build movements that put Minnesotans first.
My own story, from standing up against discrimination as a teenager to navigating housing insecurity while finding my footing, shaped my belief that politics should meet people where they are.
I know what it means to fight for stability and to find your voice when the world feels stacked against you.
Those experiences led me to a life of organizing, lifting up leaders, and building communities rooted in care and possibility.
In this moment, with families stretched thin, our democracy under attack, and our neighbors being targeted and harmed, I’m stepping forward to serve my community in a new way.
District 43A deserves leadership grounded in real life, real values, and real courage — and with your support I’m ready to show up and fight alongside you.
