Registering to vote is one of the most powerful ways to make your voice heard in our democracy.
By registering, you not only exercise your fundamental right as a citizen, but you also play a crucial role in shaping the future of your community, state, and country.
Your vote has the power to influence decisions on important issues such as education, healthcare, the economy, and social justice.
Deadline Alert: 04/08/2024 is the last day to register before the 04/23/2024 election.
If you are not a U.S. citizen and a resident of Pennsylvania at least 30 days before the next election, you CANNOT register to vote.
If you have any questions, please call 1.877.VOTESPA (1.877.868.3772)
