Elect Diana Perez for 17th Legislative District, Position 2 (D)
Building a Brighter Future Together
Meet Diana Perez
Having had the honor of serving on the Vancouver City Council, I am ready to take that experience to Olympia as your next State Representative for Legislative District 17, continuing to lead with purpose and intention, and fighting to ensure every resident has the opportunity to succeed and reach their fullest potential.
Together, we can build on our shared vision that is both powerful and inclusive, to create a brighter future for all.
My Priorities
Creating Affordability for Families
Too many Southwest Washington families and seniors are being priced out of the communities they love.
We need leaders who understand that struggle.
I want my children and all of our children to be able to live where they grew up, get a living wage job, own their own home, and build a stable future for themselves and their families.
Keeping People Safe
Public safety is the government’s most fundamental responsibility.
All people deserve safe neighborhoods, and first responders deserve the resources they need to do their jobs effectively and safely.
Reliable, Affordable Energy for All
We cannot afford energy policies that look good on paper but fail in practice.
Families need energy that is affordable, reliable, and available when they flip the switch.
