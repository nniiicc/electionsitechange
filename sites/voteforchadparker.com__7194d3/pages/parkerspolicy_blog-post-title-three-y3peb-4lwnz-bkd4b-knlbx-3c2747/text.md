We deserve an economy where everyone can thrive, from entrepreneurs launching their dreams to families building secure futures, to those who dream of buying their first home.
But right now, challenges like the Corporate Activity Tax (CAT) on growing small businesses, overlapping regulations that hinder growth, and threats to repeal tax cuts for overtime wages and tips are holding us back from our full economic powerhouse status.
This election, we have an amazing opportunity to unleash that potential.
We can do this together!
We’ve got the vision, the talent, and the community to make Oregon the ultimate destination for jobs, innovation, and prosperity.
No more settling, no more watching businesses leave our state for Arizona or Texas.
We're ready to lead the nation with an economy that works for everyone and attracts investment.
Imagine the vibrant future we can create together: Small and growing businesses flourishing without punitive taxes like the CAT, freeing them to hire more locals, innovate boldly, and drive real growth.
Streamlined, supportive regulations that empower our small and medium enterprises to expand right here at home, creating abundant jobs and boosting state revenue for better schools, roads, and services.
Protecting hard-earned tax relief for working families relying on overtime and tips, ensuring fairness and fueling consumer spending that lifts us all.
We don't need to wait for change, we have the ability to elect the leaders who can make it happen.
With real economic champions in Salem, people who've actually built businesses in Oregon, navigated the challenges, and emerged with unstoppable optimism we'll prioritize smart policies that cut red tape, reward innovation, and celebrate our entrepreneurs.
Your ideas, your energy and your vote will power this transformation.
Join our campaign: Oregon's economic boom starts with us stepping up today and putting some real change in Salem.
Let's make our state the envy of the nation, one thriving business at a time.
