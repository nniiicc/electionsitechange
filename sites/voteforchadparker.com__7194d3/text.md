A Message From Chad Parker
Oregon is absolutely wonderful.
Yet too many of our friends and neighbors are packing up and leaving.
Families we’ve known for years are heading to Arizona, to Texas, to anywhere they believe opportunity knocks a little louder.
Oregon grown businesses, companies built with sweat and dreams right here are relocating, taking jobs and futures with them.
Why?
Because deep down, we all know the truth: Oregon’s beauty is undeniable, but the policies coming out of Salem are disconnected from the realities we live every day.
High taxes, burdensome regulations, and a government that seems to forget the people it serves are driving many people and businesses away.
This is our moment to step up, to get involved and build the Oregon we want.
Let’s start by Electing Chad Parker for State Representative.
