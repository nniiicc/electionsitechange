Elect Chad Parker for State Representative and let’s rebuild that Oregon…together A Message From Chad Parker Oregon is absolutely wonderful.
Yet too many of our friends and neighbors are packing up and leaving.
Families we’ve known for years are heading to Arizona, to Texas, to anywhere they believe opportunity knocks a little louder.
Oregon grown businesses, companies built with sweat and dreams right here are relocating, taking jobs and futures with them.
Why?
Because deep down, we all know the truth: Oregon’s beauty is undeniable, but the policies coming out of Salem are disconnected from the realities we live every day.
High taxes, burdensome regulations, and a government that seems to forget the people it serves are driving many people and businesses away.
This is our moment to step up, to get involved and build the Oregon we want.
Let’s start by Electing Chad Parker for State Representative.

Unlocking Oregons Potential Education Making Oregon THE Best Place to Get an Education Oregon has incredible potential and our kids deserve world-class opportunities right here at home.
The latest National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP, the Nation's Report Card) shows us the reality: In 2024, Oregon's 4th-grade reading proficiency ranks near the bottom nationally, with our students facing real challenges in building strong foundational skills.
This isn't the Oregon we know or want for our future.
Lets Put Optimism Into Action for Our Kids Economy Making Oregon THE Best Place to Work and Grow a Business Oregon is brimming with innovation, hardworking people, and untapped potential especially right here in the 37th.
We deserve an economy where everyone can thrive, from entrepreneurs launching their dreams to families building secure futures, to those who dream of buying their first home.
But right now, challenges like the Corporate Activity Tax (CAT) on growing small businesses, overlapping regulations that hinder growth, and threats to repeal tax cuts for overtime wages and tips are holding us back from our full economic powerhouse status.
Lets Put Optimism Into Action for Our Economy Transportation Making Oregon House District 37 THE Best Place for Safe, Efficient Roads and Transportation The 37th is full of energy, wonderful families, and opportunity we deserve roads and transportation systems that match that spirit!
Right here in the 37th District, we navigate an impressive (and sometimes overwhelming) mix of transportation agencies overseeing our streets, highways, and intersections.
A quick drive from spots like Lark Coffee to Bridgeport Village can cross five or even six different jurisdictions in just seven miles.
And thanks to the ongoing conversations around road funding in Salem including with those (thankfully) stalled toll loving people we know firsthand how critical smart, accountable transportation leadership is to keeping our communities moving safely and smoothly.
Salem Created This Problem, We'll Fix It, Together!
