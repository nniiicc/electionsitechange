John E.
Norris began his journey of service two weeks after the terrorist attacks of September 11th, 2001, enlisting in the United States Army where he served for over a decade.
During his military service, John demonstrated exceptional dedication by completing his bachelor's degree in biochemistry from Bridgewater State University.
After leaving the Army, John worked as a protein chemist for Biolegend and later for Pharmaron, applying his scientific expertise to advance medical research.
Not content to rest on his achievements, John pursued his law degree at night while working full-time in the lab, attending the Massachusetts School of Law.
After graduating and passing the bar exam, John opened Norris Law P.L.L.C., a veteran-owned law firm dedicated to serving the legal needs of our community.
John has also served the Town of Bridgewater as a Town Councilor, bringing his dedication to public service directly to local government.
John brings a keen mind for detail, a love of challenges, and a genuine passion for helping others to everything he does.
Now, John is ready to bring that same dedication and work ethic to the State House, fighting for the people of Bridgewater and Raynham.
