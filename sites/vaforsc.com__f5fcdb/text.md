A Better Choice Serving Communities in Lyman, Wellford, Startex, Duncan, Reidville, and Greer Donate Advocating for Our Future I believe that every child in South Carolina deserves a world-class education, and every teacher deserves the resources and respect necessary to provide it.
I am running for the House because I am tired of seeing our public schools and educators sidelined; it’s time for a representative who treats our children’s future—from those first preschool milestones to high school graduation—as their top priority.
Values & Vision Investing in Our Growth Spartanburg County is no longer the best-kept secret in the South—we are growing at a rapid pace, and while that brings incredible opportunity, it also brings a significant strain on our daily lives.
As I talk to neighbors across the district, the message is clear: our infrastructure is struggling to keep up.
We cannot continue to add new homes and businesses without a serious, long-term plan for our roads and schools.
I am committed to advocating for smart, sustainable growth that ensures our community remains livable and safe.
This means fighting for our fair share of state funding to help maintain our infrastructure as we grow.
A South Carolina for Everyone At the heart of my faith and my service is a simple, unwavering belief: every person deserves to be treated with dignity, respect, and fairness under the law.
True equality means building a state where everyone can live authentically, work without fear of discrimination, and feel a genuine sense of belonging.
Our district is a tapestry of different stories and identities.
I will make sure that liberty and justice truly applies to all South Carolinians.
