“I’m proud to have the support of the police association in my hometown of Indio,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“Our communities deserve leaders who have the backs of law enforcement.
The men and women who put their lives on the line to protect us deserve our support, and I’ll keep fighting to make sure they have the resources they need to do their jobs safely and effectively.” The IPOA endorsement adds to the growing list of law enforcement supporters backing Gonzalez’s re-election campaign.
For Gonzalez, public safety is not a talking point.
It is a commitment rooted in his years of military service and his belief that every family deserves to feel safe in their community.
