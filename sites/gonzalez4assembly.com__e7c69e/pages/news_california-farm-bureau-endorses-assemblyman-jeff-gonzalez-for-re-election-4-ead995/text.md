IBEW Local 47 Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today that his re-election campaign has been endorsed by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 47.
IBEW Local 47 represents thousands of skilled electrical workers and utility professionals across California and is recognized as a leading voice for working families and infrastructure jobs.
“I am honored to earn the endorsement of IBEW Local 47,” said Gonzalez.
“The hardworking men and women of organized labor are the backbone of our communities.
I will continue fighting for good-paying jobs, economic opportunity, and policies that strengthen working families across the 36th District.”
“Jeff Gonzalez understands the importance of supporting workers, investing in infrastructure, and standing up for the people he represents,” said IBEW Local 47.
“Local 47 leadership appreciates his commitment to working families within his district, along with the gratitude of our members working and living in Assembly District 36.
We are proud to endorse his re-election campaign.”
For more information on Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, visit: www.Gonzalez4Assembly.com
