California Farm Bureau Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today that the California Farm Bureau has endorsed Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez in his re-election campaign for Assembly District 36.
The endorsement reflects growing support for Gonzalez’s work on behalf of agricultural communities across the region and his efforts to ensure their voices are represented in Sacramento.
The California Farm Bureau released the following statement:
“California agriculture is much stronger with a champion like Jeff Gonzalez in the Assembly.
From ensuring Colorado River discussions are fair, to improving air quality, Jeff has made sure that farmers and ranchers are represented and Sacramento works for them.”
Assemblyman Gonzalez released the following statement:
“I’m honored to earn the support of the California Farm Bureau.
Agriculture is the backbone of so many communities across our district and our state.
I will continue working to make sure our farmers and ranchers are heard and that Sacramento understands the importance of protecting and supporting our agricultural economy.”
For additional information about Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, please visit www.Gonzalez4Assembly.com
