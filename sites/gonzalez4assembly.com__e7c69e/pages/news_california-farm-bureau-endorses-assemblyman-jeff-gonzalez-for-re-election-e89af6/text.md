The California Farm Bureau released the following statement: “California agriculture is much stronger with a champion like Jeff Gonzalez in the Assembly.
From ensuring Colorado River discussions are fair, to improving air quality, Jeff has made sure that farmers and ranchers are represented and Sacramento works for them.” Assemblyman Gonzalez released the following statement: “I’m honored to earn the support of the California Farm Bureau.
Agriculture is the backbone of so many communities across our district and our state.
