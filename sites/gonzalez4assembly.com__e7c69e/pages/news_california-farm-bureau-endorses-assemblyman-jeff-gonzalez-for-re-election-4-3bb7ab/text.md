California Medical Association Political Action Committee Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Reelection
INDIO, CA — The California Medical Association Political Action Committee (CALPAC) has endorsed Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for reelection to represent California’s 36th Assembly District, adding a major voice from the healthcare community to the growing coalition supporting his campaign.
The endorsement comes as Gonzalez continues to establish himself as an advocate for patients, healthcare providers, and rural communities throughout the district.
In June, Gonzalez was appointed to the Assembly Health Committee, giving AD-36 a seat at the table on some of California’s most important healthcare issues.
As a member of the Legislature, Gonzalez has advocated for protecting critical healthcare services, supporting individuals with disabilities and their families, and strengthening access to care in rural and underserved communities.
His legislative work has included efforts to support rural hospitals and address healthcare challenges facing farmworker communities.
“I’m honored to have the support of CALPAC and the physicians who care for families across our state,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“Healthcare is personal to me, and I will continue working to ensure our doctors, medical professionals, and patients have a strong voice in Sacramento.”
The endorsement adds to a growing coalition of organizations and community leaders supporting Gonzalez’s reelection.
CALPAC joins organizations including the California Farm Bureau, Riverside County Farm Bureau, Imperial County Farm Bureau, IBEW Local 47, CAL FIRE Local 2881, and others in backing Gonzalez’s campaign.
For additional information about Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, please visit www.Gonzalez Assembly.com.
