ISSUES
Cost of Living:
California’s economy is on the wrong track.
Gas prices, grocery prices, utilities prices– the costs are out of control.
Families are being crushed by inflation.
Jeff is working to make California more affordable by eliminating taxes on gas and groceries.
He also supports passing the largest middle-class tax cut in California history to help working people get by.
Fixing our Schools:
A high-quality education prepares students for life.
But, as a parent, Jeff is concerned with falling test scores and graduation rates.
Jeff is fighting to hire more teachers and ensure students can read, write, and do math before graduation.
Jeff also advocates to hire school security to create a safe learning environment for all students.
Bipartisanship:
Jeff is a first-generation American who served in the US Marine Corps for over 20 years.
As a combat veteran, he has learned that there is no such thing as a Republican or Democrat in a foxhole.
Jeff believes people are more important than politics, and to get things done, we need to stop the political party fighting.
Jeff votes for good ideas no matter who has them: Democrats, Independents, or Republicans.
