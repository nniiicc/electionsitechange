Imperial Councilman and Former Mayor James Tucker Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today the endorsement of Imperial Councilman and former Mayor James Tucker, highlighting Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez’s growing support across Imperial County.
“I’m proud to support Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for re-election,” said Councilman James Tucker.
“Jeff has made a real effort to build relationships throughout Imperial County and consistently shows up for our communities.
He understands the needs of the Imperial Valley and has been a strong voice for us in Sacramento.
We need someone who will continue to fight for our region, and Jeff is that leader.”
Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez welcomed the endorsement and emphasized his commitment to showing up for Imperial Valley communities.
“I’m honored to have the support of Councilman James Tucker,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“James is a respected leader who has spent years serving the people of Imperial, and I’m grateful for his partnership.
From day one, I’ve made it a priority to build strong relationships throughout Imperial County, to show up, listen, and be a voice for the Imperial Valley in Sacramento.
I look forward to continuing that work together to deliver real results for our communities.”
