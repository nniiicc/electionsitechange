San Bernardino County Sheriff Shannon Dicus Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today that Shannon Dicus, Sheriff of San Bernardino County, has endorsed Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez in his re-election campaign for Assembly District 36.
The endorsement continues to reflect strong support from one of the region’s most respected law enforcement leaders and underscores a shared commitment to public safety and community trust across the Inland Empire.
Assemblyman Gonzalez released the following statement:
“I’m honored to have the endorsement of Sheriff Shannon Dicus.
He is a respected leader in our region who has dedicated his career to protecting our communities.
I appreciate his support and look forward to continuing to work together to support law enforcement and keep our communities safe.”
For additional information about Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, please visit www.Gonzalez4Assembly.com
