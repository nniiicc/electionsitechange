Hemet Firefighters Association Local 2342 Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Reelection
INDIO, CA — The Hemet Firefighters Association Local 2342 has endorsed Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for reelection to represent California’s 36th Assembly District, adding another leading public safety organization to the growing coalition supporting his campaign.
The Hemet Firefighters Association Local 2342 cited Gonzalez’s unwavering support for California firefighters and public safety as a key reason for its endorsement, recognizing his commitment to standing with the men and women who protect California communities.
“I’m honored to have the endorsement of the Hemet Firefighters Association Local 2342 and grateful for the trust they have placed in me,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“Our firefighters put their lives on the line every day to protect our communities, and I will continue standing with them and fighting for the resources, support, and safe working conditions they need to do their jobs.”
The endorsement adds to a growing coalition of public safety organizations, community leaders, businesses, and local stakeholders supporting Gonzalez’s reelection.
As a 21-year Marine Corps combat veteran, Gonzalez has made supporting those who serve and protect California communities a central part of his work in Sacramento.
Gonzalez has consistently made public safety a priority during his first term, working to ensure law enforcement, firefighters, veterans, and first responders have the support they need to protect the communities they serve.
“I’m proud of the growing coalition behind this campaign,” Gonzalez said.
“From firefighters and law enforcement to healthcare professionals, farmers, small businesses, and families, I will continue bringing people together and putting the needs of our communities first.”
For additional information about Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, please visit www.Gonzalez4Assembly.com.
