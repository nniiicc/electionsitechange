Blythe Councilwoman Summer Spraggins Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today the endorsement of Blythe Councilwoman Summer Spraggins.
“I am proud to support Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for re-election,” said Councilwoman Summer Spraggins.
“Jeff has been a true fighter for the Palo Verde Valley and has always made our community a priority.
He takes the time to show up, listen, and work alongside local leaders to get things done.
I’ve appreciated his support and value him as a strong and effective leader for our region.”
Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez welcomed the endorsement and highlighted the importance of working closely with local leaders in communities like Blythe.
“I’m honored to have the support of Councilwoman Summer Spraggins,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“Summer is a dedicated leader who cares deeply about the Palo Verde Valley and works every day to improve the lives of her constituents.
I’m grateful for her partnership and proud to stand alongside her as we continue fighting for cities like Blythe and across our region.”
