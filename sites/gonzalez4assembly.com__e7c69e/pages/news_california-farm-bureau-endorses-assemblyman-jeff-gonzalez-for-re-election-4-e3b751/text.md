Imperial Valley College Trustee Peter Martinez Endorses Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez for Re-Election
INDIO, CA — The Gonzalez for Assembly campaign announced today that Peter Martinez, Imperial Valley College Trustee for Area 3, has endorsed Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez in his re-election campaign for Assembly District 36.
Martinez’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition of local leaders supporting Gonzalez’s campaign across Imperial, Riverside, and San Bernardino Counties.
Assemblyman Gonzalez released the following statement:
“I’m grateful to have Peter’s support.
He understands the needs of Imperial Valley students, families, and workers, and his leadership reflects a strong commitment to the community.
I look forward to continuing to work together to make sure our region has a strong voice.”
Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez represents Assembly District 36 and is running for re-election to continue focusing on public safety, affordability, and delivering results for local communities.
For additional information about Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez and his re-election campaign, please visit www.Gonzalez4Assembly.com
