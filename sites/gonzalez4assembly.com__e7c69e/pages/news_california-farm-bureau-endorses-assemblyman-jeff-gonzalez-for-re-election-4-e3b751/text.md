Martinez’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition of local leaders supporting Gonzalez’s campaign across Imperial, Riverside, and San Bernardino Counties.
Assemblyman Gonzalez released the following statement: “I’m grateful to have Peter’s support.
He understands the needs of Imperial Valley students, families, and workers, and his leadership reflects a strong commitment to the community.
I look forward to continuing to work together to make sure our region has a strong voice.” Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez represents Assembly District 36 and is running for re-election to continue focusing on public safety, affordability, and delivering results for local communities.
