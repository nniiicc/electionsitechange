The endorsement reflects the Chamber’s support for Gonzalez’s continued advocacy on behalf of Hispanic and Latino families, small businesses, and communities throughout the Coachella Valley and across the district.
“I am honored to have the support of the Hispanic American Chamber of Commerce of the Greater Coachella Valley,” said Assemblyman Jeff Gonzalez.
“Hispanic and Latino families are an essential part of the fabric of our communities, and they deserve to have a strong voice representing them at the State Capitol.
I’m grateful to the executive council of the Hispanic American Chamber of Commerce for their partnership and for their continued advocacy on behalf of our community.” Gonzalez has made representing the diverse communities of the 36th Assembly District a central priority of his first term, working across party lines to address affordability, healthcare access, public safety, support for small businesses, and economic opportunity.
The Hispanic American Chamber of Commerce of the Greater Coachella Valley joins a growing coalition of community leaders, businesses, agricultural organizations, public safety groups, labor organizations, and residents supporting Gonzalez’s reelection.
“I’m proud to have earned the trust of people throughout this district, and I will continue fighting every day to deliver results for the families and communities I represent,” said Gonzalez.
