I believe every woman has the absolute right to decide what happens to her own body, her health, and her future—free from political or governmental interference.
Reproductive rights are not privileges; they are fundamental human rights.
Without control over one’s own reproductive choices, there can be no true equality, no real personal freedom, and no dignity.
The decision must always rest with the person who is pregnant, because only they live with the physical, emotional, and economic consequences.
No lawmaker, no court, and no political agenda should ever take that choice away.
