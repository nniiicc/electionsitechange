PRESIDENT FRANKLIN D.
ROOSEVELET'S
SECOND BILL OF RIGHTS / ECONOMIC BILL OF RIGHTS
I.
The every person on United States soil has the right to a real job — one that’s useful, dignified, and pays a living wage.
II.
The right to earn enough to afford food, clothing, and a life beyond just surviving.
III.
The right of farmers to earn a fair return so their families can live decently
IV.
The right of small and large businesses to compete without being crushed by monopolies
V.
The right of every family to a safe, decent home
VI.
The right to medical care — not bankruptcy disguised as healthcare
VII.
The right to protection from the economic fear of old age, sickness, disability, or unemployment
VIII.
The right to a good education.
