TriCare Prime for All is a single-payer, universal healthcare system modeled on TRICARE Prime — the system that already provides comprehensive care to active-duty service members and their families.
If this model is strong enough to protect those who defend the United States, it should be the baseline for every person who calls this country home.
No co-pays.
No deductibles.
No medical bills.
Just healthcare — because the strength of the United States comes from its people.
How TriCare Prime for All Works
TriCare Prime for All would operate as a wholly single-payer system.
Every American would be automatically enrolled, with care delivered through a nationwide network of public and private providers.
Under this plan:
- There are zero co-pays, zero deductibles, and zero surprise bills
- Every person has access to a primary care provider
- Specialty care is available through coordinated referrals
- Preventive care is emphasized to keep people healthy, not just treated when they are sick
Healthcare becomes a public service — not a financial gamble.
Federal Funding Requirement
Any healthcare facility that receives federal funding would be required to participate in the $0 deductible TriCare Prime for All option.
This ensures that public dollars are used to deliver public benefit, not to subsidize cost-shifting, billing complexity, or profit-driven denial of care.
Hospitals, clinics, and providers that accept federal funds must serve the public under the same zero-cost rules — because taxpayer money should guarantee access, not paperwork and debt.
Why TriCare Prime for All Is Better Than Medicare for All
Medicare was designed decades ago for seniors and later expanded in fragmented ways.
It still relies on:
- Deductibles and co-insurance
- Multiple “Parts” and private intermediaries
- Supplemental insurance to fill coverage gaps
TriCare Prime for All is different.
TRICARE Prime is already a fully integrated, single-payer system designed to serve families, working adults, and children — with minimal administrative overhead and no financial barriers at the point of care.
Rather than adapting a senior-focused program, TriCare Prime for All scales a proven national readiness model to the entire population.
Healthcare as National Security
Healthcare is not only a human right — it is a national security concern.
A nation cannot be strong if its people are:
- Avoiding doctors because of cost
- Bankrupted by medical bills
- Living with untreated chronic or mental health conditions
- Losing housing, employment, or stability due to illness
A healthy population strengthens:
- The workforce
- Military readiness
- Economic stability
- Disaster response and resilience
TriCare Prime for All treats healthcare as essential infrastructure — as fundamental to national strength as roads, clean water, or electricity.
The Bottom Line
TriCare Prime for All delivers what Americans deserve:
- Universal coverage
- Zero out-of-pocket costs
- Simpler administration
- Stronger families
- A stronger nation
