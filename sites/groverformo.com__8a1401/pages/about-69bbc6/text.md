Kevin has spent his career working with families, students, and communities - listening first and leading with purpose.
As an educator, he understands how strong public schools shape opportunity and strengthen neighborhoods.
As a minister, he has walked alongside people during life’s most difficult moments, reinforcing his belief that leadership requires empathy, accountability, and service.
Kevin is running because he believes Missouri works best when leaders put people over politics.
He is committed to protecting the will of the voters, strengthening public education, standing with working families, and supporting safe communities.
He believes in livable wages, affordable healthcare, including protecting reproductive healthcare, and a strong local economy.
