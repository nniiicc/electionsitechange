Andy Warren — A Path Forward Home News Feed Contact Issues Meet Volunteer Spotlight on Community Leaders ☰ Health Neighbor • Problem‑solver • Champion for NC’s future Health Compassion, care, and community—because every North Carolinian matters.
Good health is the foundation of a strong, thriving community.
Here in North Carolina, too many of our neighbors struggle to access reliable, affordable healthcare.
Families worry about the cost of basic care, seniors are stretched thin, and rural communities often travel miles for essential services.
I believe we are strongest when we look out for one another .
Compassion isn’t weakness—it’s the beginning of real progress.
That’s why I support strengthening healthcare access, investing in mental health resources, and ensuring that no family has to choose between a doctor’s visit and paying their bills.
And when it comes to personal medical decisions, especially private decisions involving pregnancy and reproductive health, I trust women, families, and doctors —not politicians.
These deeply personal choices belong at home, not in Raleigh.
Every person deserves dignity, privacy, and the freedom to make medical decisions that are right for them.
My promise is simple: lead with compassion, protect medical freedom, and fight for a healthier future for all of us.
