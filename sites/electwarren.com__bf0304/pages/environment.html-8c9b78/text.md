Environment
Protecting the place we love—for today and tomorrow.
North Carolina’s natural beauty is a gift—and a responsibility.
Protecting our environment supports everything we care about: clean water for our families, safe air for our kids, strong tourism, vibrant agriculture, and resilient communities.
I believe in responsible stewardship, smart conservation, and sustainable growth.
That means:
- Keeping our drinking water clean and holding polluters accountable.
- Protecting farmland and supporting family agriculture.
- Preparing for storms and flooding with resilient infrastructure.
- Supporting clean energy innovation and good local jobs.
- Preserving the natural beauty that defines North Carolina.
Caring for the environment means caring for one another—and honoring future generations by leaving them a North Carolina that is safer, healthier, and full of possibility.
