Meet Andy Warren
Neighbor.
Problem-solver.
Committed to a North Carolina where everyone has a fair shot.
Andy Warren has spent his life lifting up neighbors and solving problems—not scoring political points.
From organizing local volunteer efforts to helping families navigate tough times, Andy believes public service should be practical, compassionate, and focused on results that improve people’s lives.
Andy’s approach is simple: listen first, lead with respect, and deliver for the people who call this community home.
He knows that when leaders bring folks together around real solutions, we strengthen our schools, support small businesses, protect our natural resources, and keep families healthy and safe.
Rooted in Community
From church food drives and youth programs to supporting veterans and seniors, Andy’s work has always centered on dignity, fairness, and opportunity.
He believes that every person deserves respect and a voice in the decisions that shape their future.
Highlights
- Led local volunteer initiatives supporting families in need and community nonprofits.
- Advocated for strong public schools, fair pay for educators, and safe classrooms.
- Champion for clean water, smart growth, and responsible stewardship of our environment.
- Standing up for medical freedom, equality under the law, and basic human dignity.
