Education
Investing in our children, supporting our teachers, strengthening our future.
Every child in North Carolina deserves a world‑class education, no matter their ZIP code or family income.

We can do better, together.

- Support teachers with fair pay, respect, and classroom resources.
- Ensure safe, well‑maintained learning environments.
- Strengthen early childhood education and literacy.
- Expand after‑school and family support programs.
- Keep politics out of the classroom and trust our educators.
Our kids deserve the best.
Our teachers deserve respect.
Our future deserves commitment—and I’m ready to fight for all three.
