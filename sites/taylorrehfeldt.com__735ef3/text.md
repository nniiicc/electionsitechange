Raised in West River and now raising her family in East River, she understands the values and work ethic that define South Dakota.
A graduate of the South Dakota School of Mines & Technology, South Dakota State University, and Mount Marty University, Taylor earned her Doctor of Nurse Anesthesia Practice and works as a Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist.
Her clinical experience has shaped her commitment to safe, accessible healthcare for South Dakotans.
Taylor has served in the South Dakota House of Representatives as House Assistant Majority Leader and continues to serve District 14, focused on healthcare policy, child protection, and combating the opioid crisis.
She and her husband are raising their three children, Flynn, Judy, and Tallie.
As a mother and healthcare provider, Taylor remains committed to protecting families and strengthening communities across South Dakota.
