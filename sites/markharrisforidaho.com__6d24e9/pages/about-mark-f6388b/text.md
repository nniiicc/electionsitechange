MEET MARK
I am proud to be a native Idahoan, and to have raised my family here, because it means I understand the challenges, struggles, and opportunities that face families across the state every day.
My wife, Cheryl, and I raised our four boys just outside Soda Springs, teaching them the values of hard work and personal responsibility.
I have been humbled to serve in the Idaho legislature, protecting the same values that we taught our sons for all Idahoans.
It is an honor and a privilege to have the trust of my constituents and to serve the people of Idaho’s District 35.
