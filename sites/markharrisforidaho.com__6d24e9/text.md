My record clearly reflects my dedication to securing our borders, staunchly supporting law enforcement and the Second Amendment, advancing educational opportunities for our kids, and promoting a strong Idaho economy.
Cheryl and I have been blessed to raise our boys here.
To build a life here, to get up each morning, work hard in one of the most incredible places and get to call it home.
My focus has always been doing my very best for the hardworking people of Southeast Idaho.
That's why I've tirelessly fought against outside groups like the Idaho Conservation League, who’ve spent years trying to stifle and shut down our local industries.
My opponent appears to align with their extremist environmental agenda and paints one of our biggest local employers as “big business” just for a political win.
In defense of our local industries, particularly in Soda Springs, I sponsored legislation aimed at curtailing frivolous lawsuits that not only threaten our businesses but also our community's livelihood.
Critics dub these efforts as favoring big business, but let's be clear: these laws are about protecting common sense and ensuring that if someone disregards clear instructions or warnings, trial lawyers shouldn’t be able to exploit the legal system for easy financial gain.
This isn't just about protecting companies; it's about protecting jobs.
In Soda Springs alone, the phosphorus mining operation—vital for producing crop inputs, products essential to agriculture nationwide—is a cornerstone of our local economy.
The 800 employees at Bayer are not just workers; they are our neighbors, friends, and integral community members who contribute significantly to our local economy and social fabric.
Let's also consider the broader implications: our community's products are crucial to farmers across Idaho and the entire country.
From the sugar beets in our fields to the crops across the nation, our work in District 35 plays a pivotal role in America's food security.
Shifting this production to countries like China not only jeopardizes our local jobs but also our national security.
I've always believed in practical, tangible solutions that benefit our people, not in pandering to groups whose policies would hinder our progress and prosperity.
As we move forward, I pledge to continue advocating for policies that bolster our local economy, support our community's way of life, and ensure that Idaho remains a place of opportunity for all.
Your support is crucial as we work together to maintain the strength and security of District 35.
Let's keep Idaho strong and independent, focused on real issues and real solutions.
