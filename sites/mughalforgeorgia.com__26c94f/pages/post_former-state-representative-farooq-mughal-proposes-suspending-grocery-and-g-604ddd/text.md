Former State Representative Farooq Mughal Proposes Suspending Grocery and Gas Taxes Until Georgia’s Economy Recovers
Dacula, GA — September 7, 2026 — Former State Representative Farooq Mughal is proposing a temporary suspension of state taxes on groceries and gasoline to provide immediate financial relief to Georgia families struggling with the rising cost of living.
Mughal said the proposal would remain in effect until Georgia’s economy reaches a stronger level of stability and families are no longer facing the same affordability pressures.
“Georgia families should not have to choose between putting food on the table, filling up their tank, and paying their bills,” said former State Representative Farooq Mughal.
“When families are hurting, government needs to step up with solutions—not excuses.
Suspending these taxes temporarily would put money back into the pockets of hardworking Georgians when they need it most.”
Mughal emphasized that the proposal is intended as a temporary economic-relief measure, not a permanent change to Georgia’s tax structure.
The suspension would be reviewed periodically based on economic conditions, state revenues, and the financial impact on Georgia families.
“Georgia has the resources to provide meaningful relief,” Mughal said.
“We should be focused on making life more affordable for working families, small businesses, seniors, and everyone who is feeling the pressure at the grocery store and the gas pump.”
Mughal said he plans to work with lawmakers, economic leaders, and community stakeholders to develop a fiscally responsible framework for implementing the temporary tax suspension while protecting essential state services.
“Our economy will recover, but families cannot wait for relief,” Mughal said.
“Let’s give Georgia families a break now.”
