About the event
Former State Representative Farooq Mughal Opposes DHS Plans to Convert a Warehouse in Oakwood, Georgia and Near the Mall of Georgia (HD‑105) Into a Federal Detention Facility.
Buford, GA — Former Georgia State Representative Farooq Mughal plans to address the community and media to express his strong opposition to any current or future Department of Homeland Security (DHS) plans to transform a warehouse near the Mall of Georgia in House District 105 into a federal facility.
Mughal will highlight the major community, infrastructure, and public safety concerns linked to such a proposal, stressing that District 105 is already facing heavy congestion, overdevelopment, and limited local resources.
During his tenure in the Georgia House, Mughal supported homeowners, families, and local leaders from all political backgrounds to preserve the district's character and quality of life.
He will reaffirm this commitment and discuss future steps for community involvement and advocacy.
