CONTRIBUTE SUBSCRIBE All Articles Search Republican Veteran and Former HD 105 Candidate Arefeen Chowdhury Endorses Farooq Mughal for Georgia House District 105 #ago 2 min read GWINNETT COUNTY, GA — Arefeen Chowdhury, a Republican, military veteran, and former candidate for Georgia House District 105, has endorsed Farooq Mughal in his campaign for the Georgia House of Representatives in District 105.
Chowdhury’s endorsement highlights the importance of addressing issues that directly affect Georgia families and communities, including adequate funding for veterans’ services, strong public schools, and the rising cost of living.
As a veteran and former candidate for the same legislative district, Chowdhury brings a perspective shaped by military service and civic engagement.
His endorsement underscores the importance of leadership that recognizes the needs of veterans, supports educational opportunities for students, and works toward making life more affordable for working families.
Veterans’ Funding and Support Supporting veterans and ensuring they have access to the resources and services they have earned through their service remain important priorities for Chowdhury.
Adequate funding for veterans’ programs is essential to helping former service members and their families access the support they need.
Investing in Georgia’s Public Schools Strong public schools are fundamental to Georgia’s future.
Ensuring schools have the resources necessary to support students, teachers, and local communities is a key component of building a stronger education system and expanding opportunities for the next generation.
Making Georgia More Affordable As families face rising costs for housing, groceries, utilities, and other essential expenses, affordability remains a central concern.
Policies that address the cost of living and help families manage everyday expenses are critical to maintaining economic stability and opportunity.
Mughal’s campaign for House District 105 provides an opportunity to engage voters on these priorities and discuss how state government can respond to the needs of residents throughout the district.
The endorsement also highlights the importance of civic engagement and constructive dialogue among Georgians with different political backgrounds who share concerns about veterans’ services, education, and economic pressures on families.
About Arefeen Chowdhury Arefeen Chowdhury is a military veteran, Republican, and former candidate for Georgia House District 105.
About Farooq Mughal Farooq Mughal is a candidate for the Georgia House of Representatives in District 105.
