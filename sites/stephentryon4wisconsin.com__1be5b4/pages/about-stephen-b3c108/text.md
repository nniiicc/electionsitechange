Home About Stephen Share Your Priorities Home About Stephen Share Your Priorities About Stephen A Lifetime of Service and Achievement Over 21 years as a Soldier, Stephen earned the Ranger Tab, Master Parachutist Badge, and a Bronze Star for heroism.
He completed a Fellowship in the United States Senate and served as a liaison between senior Department of Defense leaders and Congress.
He holds Masters Degrees from Stanford University and the University of Colorado.
Internet Executive Stephen spent nearly ten years as a Vice President and Senior Vice President with Overstock.com.
In this capacity, he managed the company logistics function as well as its human resources and international business units.
Author/Individual Contributor Since resigning from his executive responsibilities, Stephen has published four books addressing the challenges and opportunities that information technology presents for the American Republic.
During this period, Stephen has gained experience of different facets of the economy while working as a Linux engineer, a secondary school teacher, and a courier.
He currently works as a dangerous goods inspector for a major parcel delivery company.
