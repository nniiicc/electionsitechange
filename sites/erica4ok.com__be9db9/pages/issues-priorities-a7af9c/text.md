What We're Fighting For
People
Put workers, families, veterans, children, the disabled, and vulnerable communities ahead of corporations and billionaires.
Protect the right to unionize and earn a fair, livable wage.
People over profit – not just on election day.
Affordability
Bring down healthcare, housing, and childcare, end costly tariffs, end student loan debt, and fight rising costs due to inflation.
Make the wealthy and big corporations pay their fair share, thereby lowering costs and investing in people.
Freedom
Protect the freedom to live your life and the economic freedom to afford it.
Protect abortion access, medical privacy, equal rights, and civil liberties.
Fight policies that keep everyday people struggling, poor, hungry, and sick while the rich get richer.
Accountability
Ban self-dealing and close loopholes that let insiders profit.
Hold hearings, demand audits, and force public reporting to end backroom deals.
Demand consequences for abuse of power and discriminatory practices in law enforcement, especially ICE.
