Not More Bureaucracy.
A Common-Sense Platform for Bennington County Montpelier has a spending and red-tape problem, not a revenue problem.
As a business operator, I don’t manage with empty speeches—I manage with budgets, clear timelines, and measurable results.
It’s time to bring that same discipline to state government.
1.
Restore Local Control: Reform Act 250 & Repeal Act 181 State mandates are choking growth and making housing unaffordable.
Towns know what is best for their own communities, not unelected state boards. ​ Empower Local Towns: Municipalities with active planning and zoning boards should have the final say on local land use without redundant state interference.
Repeal Act 181 & Modernize Act 250: Eliminate duplicative bureaucracy that inflates building costs and stalls starter-home construction.
Sensible Growth: Let our communities build the workforce housing and commercial spaces needed to keep young families in Bennington County.
2.
Affordability & Fiscal Discipline Vermonters are being taxed out of their homes.
State spending cannot continue to outpace the family budget. ​ Hold the Line on Taxes: Prioritize tax relief and rein in unchecked state spending before asking working families to pay more.
Run Government Like a Business: Require clear cost-benefit analyses, strict timelines, and performance metrics for every state-funded program.
Stop the Excuses: Identify underperforming programs and redirect funds where they actually produce results for taxpayers.
3.
Support Small Business & Job Creation Local businesses are the backbone of rural Vermont, yet state policy treats them like an endless revenue source. ​ Cut Bureaucratic Red Tape: Simplify permitting and licensing to encourage entrepreneurship and job growth.
Keep Our Kids in Vermont: Create an economic environment where young people can find good-paying jobs, afford a home, and build a future here.
Practical Infrastructure: Invest in reliable roads, dependable utilities, and broadband that support everyday commerce.
4.
Real Accountability & Direct Leadership I am not a career politician.
I answer directly to the people of the Bennington District. ​ Open-Door Representation: Available to listen, answer questions, and discuss issues directly with voters—no scripts, no political spin.
Results Over Party Rhetoric: Focused on practical, collaborative problem-solving in Montpelier to deliver real outcomes for our towns.
