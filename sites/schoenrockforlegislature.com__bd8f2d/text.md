Meet Mark Schoenrock
Deep Nebraska Roots
Born and raised in Jefferson County and graduate of Fairbury High School and the University of Nebraska College of Agriculture.
Putting Family First
Proud husband to wife, Denise.
Proud father, & grandfather.
Serving Our Country
Former officer of the United States Army on active duty and as an Army civilian as a logistician serving the combat forces of our country.
Making our Communities and State Better
Jefferson County Commissioner of nearly 11 years actively working to make our communities and state better by serving in leadership capacities for Nebraska Association of County officials, Blue Rivers Area Agency on Aging, Public Health Solutions district health board, Blue Valley Community Action and his church board.
Mark was selected as the Nebraska County Official of the year for 2023.
Mark’s Priorities as our District 32 Senator
Fight for Tax Relief & Affordability
Deliver real property tax relief, combat the rising cost of living, and help Nebraska families keep more of what they earn.
A Strong Voice for Veterans and Law Enforcement
Honoring those who serve by supporting our veterans, standing with law enforcement, and promoting safe communities.
Defend Our Rights & the Unborn
Protect our constitutional freedoms, defend the Second Amendment, and stand for the unborn.
Continue Investing in Great Education
Ensuring all children receive a high-quality education is critical to the future of Nebraska.
I strongly support any efforts to improve educational outcomes for our kids in private and public K-12 schools.
Defend Our Constitutional Rights
I’ll always protect our Constitutional Rights— including our right to Bear Arms.
The founding fathers knew the importance of the 2nd Amendment, and I will never allow the government to infringe upon your right to bear arms.
Support Nebraska's Farmers & Ag Producers
Champion Nebraska's farmers and ranchers by protecting their livelihoods, defending private property rights, and ensuring agriculture remains strong for future generations.
