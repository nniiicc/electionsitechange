About Lindsey
A Proven Leader
for Kentucky
Lindsey Tichenor is a mother of four, wife of 28 years to Bowin, and in 2022 made history as the first woman from Oldham County ever elected to the Kentucky Senate.
A Christian, Realtor, former missionary, sales executive, and teacher, Lindsey brings her faith, real-world experience, proven leadership, and an unwavering commitment to serving others.
From the mission field to the classroom, the boardroom, and now in the Kentucky Senate, Lindsey has dedicated her life to serving others and standing up for families, strengthening communities, and fighting for the future of the Commonwealth.
Lindsey was drawn to serve in Senate District 6 after witnessing government overreach during the COVID years and uncovering troubling issues within the broader education system.
Those experiences convinced her that Kentucky needed someone willing to fight and serve as a representative of the people's voice in Frankfort.
At the heart of her work is her dedication to ensure that our government — established to work of, by and for the people — continues to uphold that promise.
As Vice Chair of the Senate Education Committee, Lindsey has led the fight for meaningful education reform.
She has championed legislation restoring communication safeguards between students and educators, returning cursive writing to Kentucky's academic standards, and advancing a renewed emphasis on phonics instruction to improve literacy across the Commonwealth.
Also a vocal advocate for school choice, Lindsey believes parents should be respected as the primary guide in their children's education and has worked tirelessly to protect their rights.
Lindsey also helped pass one of the nation's strongest laws pushing back against the trans agenda — ending the pronouns nonsense, keeping boys out of girls' bathrooms, and protecting minors from the dangers of irreversible gender transition procedures.
She has consistently stood with families and supported policies that protect Kentucky's children.
Lindsey has earned a reputation as a principled conservative fighter who doesn't back down, and she leads with conviction, courage, and common sense.
Every day, she remains focused on delivering results for the people of Senate District 6 and building a stronger future for Kentucky.
