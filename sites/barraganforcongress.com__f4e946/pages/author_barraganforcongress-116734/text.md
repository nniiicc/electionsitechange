President of the Progressive Democratic Club Endorses Nanette Barragán in CA-44 Primary
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE July 21, 2015 SAN PEDRO, CA - Today, Julian Burger, the President of the Progressive Democratic Club, endorsed Nanette Barragán in her bid for Congress in California’s 44th District.
“CA-44 is one of the most Democratic districts not only in the state, but in the country,” said Burger.
