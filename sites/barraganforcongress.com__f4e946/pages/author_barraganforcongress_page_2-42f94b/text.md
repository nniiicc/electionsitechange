Building Our Leadership Diversity (BOLD) PAC Unanimously Endorses Nanette Barragán in CA-44 Primary
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE June 17, 2015 SAN PEDRO, CA - Today, BOLD PAC, the fundraising arm of the Congressional Hispanic Caucus, unanimously endorsed Nanette Barragán for Congress in California’s 44th District.
BOLD PAC supports Democratic Hispanic candidates in races around the country.
