Budd previously served as the Congressman for much of the new 6th district before winning one the nation’s most competitive senate races in 2022.
McDowell is also endorsed by President Donald Trump and the North Carolina Police Benevolent Association and other community leaders.
“Addison McDowell was raised in Davidson County, next door to my home in Davie County, said Budd.
“Addison McDowell is an authentic America First Conservative fighter who has already worked hard for the people of the 6th district and will be a great advocate for our working families.
President Trump and I need a partner in the US House who is going to fight to secure our Southern Border.
I know Addison.
I know Addison’s family.
And I know the heartache they suffered due to illegal fentanyl.
I know securing the Southern border is personal for Addison and he's going to fight to do the right thing.
President Donald Trump and I know Addison McDowell is the right Conservative for NC-06 and hope you do too.” “I was proud to work for our constituents when I worked for Congressman Ted Budd,” said McDowell.
“Except I didn’t call them constituents, I called them neighbors and old friends.
Ted Budd is a servant leader and I hope to follow his model in Congress, and I’m honored to have his endorsement in this campaign.
