Trump Endorses Addison McDowell in North Carolina’s 6th Congressional District Jan 18 Written By Kate Karnes McDowell Pledges to Pursue an America First Agenda and Help Secure the Southern Border December 12, 2023 Addison McDowell today announced his candidacy for US Congress, seeking the Republican nomination in North Carolina’s 6th Congressional District.
President Donald J.
Trump endorsed McDowell tonight at an event in Mar-A-Lago noting the McDowell family’s long-time roots in Davidson County and how important it would be to have a strong voice in Congress to support efforts to stop the violent crime and drugs coming across our Southern border due to Joe Biden’s failures to do so.
“Today I am announcing my candidacy for Congress for the Sixth Congressional District, my home,” said McDowell.
“President Trump needs a fighter who is going to stand with him in his efforts to secure our Southern border and stop the flow of illegal drugs and I’m the right man for the job.
We lost our brother seven years ago to fentanyl overdose and it still hurts like it was yesterday.
I don’t want anyone to have to endure that heartache and I can’t wait to get to Congress to help Ted Budd and President Trump make sure our border patrol and law enforcement officers get the support they need and deserve.” “I am honored to have earned the endorsement of President Donald Trump,” McDowell continued.
“A lot of the jobs previous generations in this region enjoyed no longer exist because previous leaders allowed those jobs to be shipped overseas.
We’ve seen firsthand in NC what happens when we don’t protect and promote American jobs as priority one and that’s why I’m proud to support the America First Agenda that President Trump champions.
I was born and raised in Davidson County and one of the best jobs I’ve ever had working with Ted Budd’s constituents because I wasn’t helping constituents, I was helping my neighbors.
The good people of North Carolina’s 6th Congressional District have been there for my family and me throughout my life.
