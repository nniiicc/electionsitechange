President Donald Trump Tells NC-06 Voters Why They Should Vote for Addison McDowell
McDowell’s New Ad Let’s Republican Primary Voters Hear Directly from President Donald Trump
February 15, 2024
(Arcadia) – Addison McDowell’s campaign for Congress is up on air with a new ad with President Donald Trump making it pretty clear to voters why Addison McDowell is the only candidate President Trump has endorsed in this campaign.
President Trump focuses on how McDowell is the candidate he trusts to help him secure the border and create jobs.
“Addison McDowell is such an incredible guy, he’s going to be strong with the border and with our military, go with Addison.
He has my complete and total endorsement,” says President Trump.
“In Congress, Addison will work to secure our border, defend the second amendment, lower our taxes, grow our economy, and strengthen our military,” added President Trump on Truth Social.
“I am honored to be the only candidate President Trump has endorsed in this race and really appreciate President Trump making it clear I am the only Trump-Endorsed-America First candidate in this race,” said McDowell.
“I look forward to standing shoulder to shoulder with President Trump and fighting to secure our Southern border and the America First Agenda.”
