Why Volunteer?
Volunteering for this race is not only about supporting Rory Taylor.
It’s also about actively contributing to the betterment of our community and state.
By volunteering, you can:
- Influence Change: Your efforts will directly impact the issues you care about, helping to shape policies and decisions that affect our everyday lives.
- Empower Democracy: Every volunteer plays a crucial role in strengthening the democratic process, ensuring that the voice of the people is heard loud and clear.
- Build Connections: Meet like-minded individuals who share your passion for making a positive difference, creating lasting friendships and networking opportunities.
- Gain Experience: Whether you are a seasoned activist or new to politics, volunteering offers valuable experience and insight into the workings of a political campaign.
- Create History: Be part of something bigger than yourself and contribute to a campaign that could make history in our state.
How Can You Help?
We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds and skill sets!
There are various ways you can get involved, including:
- Canvassing: Hit the streets and connect with voters directly.
Engage in meaningful conversations, listen to their concerns, and share Rory’s vision for a better future.
- Phone Banking: Make a difference from the comfort of your home by reaching out to potential voters via phone.
Your persuasive and friendly voice can help sway opinions and encourage voter participation.
- Social Media and Digital Outreach: Utilize your social media prowess to spread Rory’s message far and wide.
Engage with online communities, share content, and help us create a buzz around the campaign.
- Fundraising Support: Assist in organizing fundraisers, reaching out to potential donors, and helping to secure the necessary financial resources for the campaign.
- Event Coordination: Help plan and execute campaign events, rallies, and town halls to create a strong presence in the community.
- Data Entry and Administration: Contribute to the smooth functioning of the campaign by providing administrative support and handling crucial data entry tasks.
