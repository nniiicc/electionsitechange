I had a great time speaking at the Warren County Democrats Fall Dinner last Thursday, October 23rd!
I had the opportunity to keep the conversation going on some big issues — like how tariffs are hurting our small farmers, the true backbone of our state.
We also talked about the need to bring healthcare access back to our rural communities, because every Iowan deserves quality care close to home.
And we touched on the importance of freedom and our democracy — and why we can never take them for granted.
The incredible turnout at No Kings Rallies across the country shows just how much that matters to so many people.
