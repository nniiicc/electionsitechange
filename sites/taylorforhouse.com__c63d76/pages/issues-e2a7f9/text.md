The main issues that I will be fighting for:
1) Public Schools:
Rural Schools are losing out to the school voucher program.
I will fight for public school funding and stand up for our teachers, students, and parents
2) Support for Small Farmers and Agriculture:
I will support programs that strengthen small farmers and establish policies that assure strong markets.
Along with that, I support new farmers with educational programs.
These programs support young farming families to help acquire land and equipment.
3) Access to Healthcare:
I will fight against cuts that reduce access to quality medical services to all Iowans, especially in rural communities.
4) Smart Economic Growth:
I support policies that improve our state’s economy.
These policies also protect our natural resources.
This makes Iowa a beautiful place to live.
