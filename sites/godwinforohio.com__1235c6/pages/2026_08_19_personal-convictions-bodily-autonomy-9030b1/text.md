Skip to content Godwin for Ohio People over Party Home About Home About Personal Convictions & Bodily Autonomy As a Libertarian running to represent Ohioans, my campaign is built on a simple foundation: the government should not use its power to micromanage your private life, your family, or your body.
Personally, I do not favor abortion.
It goes against my personal beliefs.
But being a principled leader means recognizing that my personal convictions should not be enforced on my neighbors through the law.
The people of Ohio spoke clearly when they voted to keep healthcare decisions strictly between a woman, her family, and her doctor.
I respect that decision.
True bodily autonomy means that healthcare choices belong to individuals—not to politicians or state bureaus in Columbus.
My focus in the General Assembly will be keeping government out of your personal life, defending your civil liberties, and ensuring the state sticks to its core responsibility: protecting your fundamental rights.
