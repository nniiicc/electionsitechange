LAWYER.
YOUR SENATOR.
Pennsylvania State Senator Maria Collett is a lifelong advocate for working families.
A former registered nurse and Deputy Attorney General, she brought her commitment to children, patients, and public service to Harrisburg when she was elected in 2018.
Now in her second term, she's delivered real results: expanding affordable healthcare, fighting for frontline healthcare workers, championing paid family & medical leave, and securing major state investments in public safety and community infrastructure.
Elected by her colleagues as Senate Democratic Caucus Chair, she is also the Minority Chair of the Aging & Youth Committee and serves on the Health & Human Services, Institutional Sustainability & Innovation, Judiciary, and Rules & Executive Nominations Committees.
