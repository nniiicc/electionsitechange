No one should have to choose between caring for a new baby or sick parent and paying rent. ​ The Family Care Act would let workers take paid time off when they need it most.
It covers care for a new child, a seriously ill family member, your own health emergency, or a military family member.
Research shows paid leave reduces infant mortality, improves maternal mental health, and helps workers recover without falling into debt.
Studies also show that strong paid leave policies improve employee retention, reduce turnover costs, and boost productivity, making them good for business as well.

Corporate Tax Fairness CLOSE THE DELAWARE LOOPHOLE Right now, 71% of corporations subject to Pennsylvania's corporate income tax pay nothing.
Zero. ​ They shelter profits in Delaware shell companies—post office boxes with no employees—and avoid hundreds of millions in taxes while small businesses and working families carry the load. ​ Maria's fighting for combined reporting legislation to close this loophole.
When corporations pay their fair share, we can invest in schools, fix roads, and fund healthcare without squeezing working people.
Healthcare You Can Afford SAFE STAFFING SAVES LIVES AND MONEY When a nurse is responsible for too many patients at once, people can get hurt and the costs follow.
Medication errors, longer hospital stays, and avoidable complications drive up bills for both patients and insurers. ​ Maria's Patient Safety Act, legislation she has championed since her first day in office, sets safe, unit-specific staffing minimums in Pennsylvania hospitals.
A trauma unit looks different from a neonatal ICU, and her law treats them that way.
Safe patient limits reduce malpractice claims and eliminate avoidable, uncompensated care, saving money for hospitals, patients, and taxpayers.
The companion bill passed the House with bipartisan support, and Maria continues fighting to get it across the finish line in the Senate.
Safer care is more affordable care.
Pennsylvanians deserve both. ​ BAN COPAY ACCUMULATOR SCHEMES Insurance companies use "copay accumulators" to pocket manufacturer assistance dollars without counting it toward your deductible—forcing chronically ill patients to pay thousands more from their own pockets for life-saving medications. ​ Maria's legislation (SB 268) stops this scam.
When people can afford their prescriptions, they stay healthier and avoid costly ER visits. ​ PROTECT BIRTH CONTROL ACCESS Contraceptive access is basic preventive healthcare.
Maria's legislation requires insurance to cover birth control without co-pays—no barriers between you and your doctor. ​ STOP PRIVATE EQUITY GREED IN NURSING HOMES Maria's legislation requires 75% of taxpayer funds go to actual bedside care, not executive bonuses.
It blocks transfers to bad actors with a history of closures.
Our seniors deserve dignity, not profit-driven neglect. ​ TRAIN MORE NURSES As a registered nurse who worked trauma units and long-term care, Maria knows: we can't care for patients if we can't staff hospitals. ​ Her bipartisan legislation expands nursing school capacity so qualified students aren't turned away for lack of faculty.
She created the Nursing Shortage Assistance Program providing up to $30,000 in student loan relief for nurses.
She championed the original $55 million Student Loan Relief for Nurses program in 2021. ​ More nurses = shorter ER waits, better patient outcomes, healthier communities.
