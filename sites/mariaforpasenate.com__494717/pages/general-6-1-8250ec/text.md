Economic Security for Working Families
THE FAMILY CARE ACT
Maria has been fighting for paid family leave since 2019.
No one should have to choose between caring for a new baby or sick parent and paying rent.
The Family Care Act would let workers take paid time off when they need it most.
It covers care for a new child, a seriously ill family member, your own health emergency, or a military family member.
Research shows paid leave reduces infant mortality, improves maternal mental health, and helps workers recover without falling into debt.
Studies also show that strong paid leave policies improve employee retention, reduce turnover costs, and boost productivity, making them good for business as well.
