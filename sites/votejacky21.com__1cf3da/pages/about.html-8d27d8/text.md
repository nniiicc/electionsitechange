Her Story Hi, I'm Jacky.
Hi, I’m Jacqueline “Jacky” Hernández.
But feel free to call me Jacky.
I’m a lifelong Southeast Texan Latina, and pretty much everything I do comes back to one thing: my community.
That’s why I’m running to be your State Representative.
I’m the oldest daughter in a working-class immigrant family, so I learned pretty early that you show up, you work hard, and you take care of your people.
Life threw some pretty big challenges at my family early on, but I kept pushing forward.
During my senior year of high school, my mom battled cancer.
I still earned a full scholarship to Lamar University, worked full time, and went to school full time.
While at Lamar, I got involved in my community.
I organized bilingual college forums, helped register voters, and connected people with free legal resources.
Service & Experience From Southeast Texas to the State Capitol That work eventually took me to the Texas State Capitol, where I saw firsthand how laws are actually made.
I even worked on legislation that became Texas law.→ But Austin was never really home.
Southeast Texas is.
So I came back to keep showing up for my community.
I worked at a law firm, moved into the corporate world, and built a career.
Then my dad’s health took a turn, and I made a pretty big change.
I left the office and stepped into the family business.
The Work Steel-Toe Boots and All.
Now I’m a bricklayer, steel-toe boots and all.
I’m not just pointing fingers and telling people what to do.
I’m hands-on.
I lay the brick.
I put up the scaffolding.
I make the mortar mix by hand.
I do the work.
Why I’m Running Service Means Showing Up.
And honestly, that changed the way I look at a lot of things.
There’s a difference between talking about working families and actually knowing what it means to get up every morning and do the work.
For me, service has never been about having the right title.
It’s about showing up.
It’s about doing the grunt work when nobody is watching.
It’s about being the person people call when they don’t know where else to turn.
I’ve spent years working behind the scenes as a mentor, community advocate, resource, and someone willing to roll up her sleeves and help.
And Southeast Texas saw me.
So when it was time to step up, I answered the call.
This Is My Story I Run on Actions, Not Words.
I’m Jacqueline “Jacky” Hernández.
I’m a daughter of immigrants, a former State Capitol staffer, a bricklayer, and a community advocate.
But more than anything, I’m somebody who believes you don’t just talk about what needs to be done.
You get to work.
Southeast Texas is my home.
And this is my story.
