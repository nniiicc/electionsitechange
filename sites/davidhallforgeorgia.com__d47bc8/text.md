That oath didn't end when my active duty service ended.
Read More Top priorities (I'll be honest, we're lacking in everything but I'll state my priorities) Healthcare— we’ve seen hospitals close in our region, and people shouldn’t have to drive an hour just to get basic care.
I want to protect and expand access so families aren’t left behind with illnesses, debt, or coffins.
I'm not for universal healthcare, it isn't practical right now.
I would support what's known as the "Public Option" (choice between federal, private, or no healthcare for all Americans) Responsible Gun Ownership— I support the right to own firearms, but I also believe in basic safety training.
We already require a hunter safety course to hunt, and I think a similar approach makes sense for firearm ownership.
From my military training, I’ve seen how someone without proper training—even with good intentions—can become a danger in a split second.
This is about responsibility and keeping people safe.
Cost of Living— power bills, insurance, groceries, and everyday expenses keep going up.
I’ll push for more oversight and accountability so working families aren’t getting squeezed more than we already are.
Jobs that pay a Living Wage— not just any jobs, but ones that actually let people support a family.
And I want to make sure we’re bringing in jobs that don’t damage our land, water, or communities, so we’re building a future our children and grandchildren will want to live in.
Accountability— people deserve to know their representative is working for them, not special interests.
I believe in transparency and making sure your voice actually matters.
If you pay attention to the representatives that are being silent, look where donations come from, where they're spent, and what policies they're voting for silently, you'll see exactly what I mean.
Brantley County is still struggling with the aftermath of the wildfires.
Many people have donated campers and RVs for the families to live in who lost their homes from the fire.
Currently, the county is allowing them to live in these as our primary residence for up to a year; afterwards I worry what will happen.
It's no secret that this was the poorest part of the county, and we have no real help coming outside of regular Americans.
I'm pressuring our county officials, as are others, to change this.
