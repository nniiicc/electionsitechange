Skip to content Home About Issues Contact Home About Issues Contact Contribute About Emma Emma Kerr-Carpenter lives in the heart of Billings.
During the past four legislative sessions she fought to fund public safety in Billings, defend healthcare for all Montanans, protect the environment, and strengthen public education.
She has spent her career working with youth and their families to overcome poverty, trauma, and crisis.
Lately, she spends her days trying to keep up with her two young sons.
Emma and her husband, Dan, just welcomed their second son and are raising both of their children in Billings.
She wants the Montana he grows up in to reflect the values that make this place home— community, joy, and neighbors helping neighbors.
When she is not working, Emma can be found hiking, knitting, or in her kitchen crafting (and eating) delicious food.
