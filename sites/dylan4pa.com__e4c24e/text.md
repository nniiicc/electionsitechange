Fighting Corruption in Harrisburg
Dylan is proud to support MarchOnHarrisburg’s fight for a government that works for people, not politicians, lobbyists, or wealthy special interests.
As your state representative, Dylan will fight to:
- Ban gifts from lobbyists and special interests valued over $50.
- Prohibit legislators from holding outside jobs that create conflicts of interest.
- End taxpayer-funded junkets and special-interest travel.
- Strengthen campaign-finance disclosure and government transparency.
- Establish tougher ethics rules with meaningful enforcement and accountability.
Public office is a public trust.
Dylan will provide honest, full-time representation and work to ensure that every elected official answers to the people, not their private employers or political donors.
