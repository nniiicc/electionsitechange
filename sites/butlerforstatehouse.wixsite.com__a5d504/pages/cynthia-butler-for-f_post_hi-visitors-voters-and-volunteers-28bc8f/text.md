DONATE VOLUNTEER News & Updates Search Hi Visitors, Voters, and Volunteers! cynthia butler Aug 30 1 min read Updated: Sep 2 It’s the final stretch to November, and I want to express my sincere gratitude to the Charlotte County Dems, North Port Dems, Venice Dems, and Sarasota Dems for giving me the opportunity to meet voters, share my platform, and hear your input, ideas, and strategies for getting it done.
A lot of focus has been on the primary races, but now that those races have been decided, it’s time to put all our energy into winning in November — up and down the ticket!
I hope you’ll help me get there by contributing, volunteering, and connecting me with as many voters as possible.
Every conversation, every dollar, every volunteer hour, and every introduction makes a difference.
I also want to thank the Progressive and Environmental Caucuses for their endorsements and for providing forums and events where we’ve been able to share our ideas, priorities, and policies.
Much thanks!
And a big shout-out to my Charlotte County Dems for hosting The Big Blue Roundup — an incredible opportunity to showcase our local candidates, bring volunteers together, and build momentum heading into November.
Now, I’d love to see even more events, house parties, rallies, and opportunities to connect with voters.
We also need volunteers to knock on doors, make phone calls, send texts, spread the word, and do whatever else you can to help us win.
We are in it to win it!
Let’s get to work and finish strong in November!
