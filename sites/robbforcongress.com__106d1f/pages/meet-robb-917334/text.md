Robb Ryerse
Faith.
Family.
Freedom.
These words have been used to divide us.
But Robb Ryerse (RYE-er-see) believes they can bring us together.
They are the values to which he has devoted his life.
Robb is a pastor and nonprofit leader running for Congress in Arkansas’ 3rd District.
He’s not a career politician.
He’s a neighbor who believes we all deserve leaders who care more about people than politics.
Robb first ran for Congress in 2018 and was known for telling the truth and standing up to the system.
Since then, he’s helped start national and local groups that fight extremism and work for a better future.
He’s worked with Vote Common Good and co-founded with Dr.
Chris Jones Vortex PAC to bring people together and support good leaders.
Robb and his wife Vanessa have lived in Springdale for 20 years.
They raised four kids and started a church called Vintage Fellowship.
Robb knows what it’s like to worry about health care bills, student loans, and an economy that leaves too many people behind.
That’s why he’s fighting for working families—because he’s one of them.
