South Whitehall, Parkland, Upper Macungie, and Allentown don't all face the same challenges.
Here's where Caren stands — on affordability, schools, student safety, and accountability — alongside her fight against the Atlas data center.
See her fight against the Atlas data center → 01 · Affordability Lower costs for families and local businesses.
Practical, common-sense solutions that help families keep more of what they earn.
Energy Reduce energy costs that hit family budgets and local businesses alike.
Taxes Lessen the tax burden on working families and small businesses.
Local economy Support local economic development that keeps dollars and jobs in the community.
Transparency Improve price transparency so families aren't blindsided by unexpected costs.
Why this connects to the Atlas data center: the same push for cheap, fast data-center power is a driver of rising electric costs across the grid — Caren's affordability agenda and her opposition to the Atlas data center come from the same place: keeping costs and decisions close to home.
Caren in one of the schools she works in as a drug prevention and education specialist.
02 · Education Stronger schools, built with the people who show up every day.
Drawing on her own background as an educator and a parent of three kids in the district.
Teachers Support teachers with the resources they need to do the job well.
Parents Strengthen parent communication and involvement in their kids' education.
Pathways Build real career pathways for students, not just a single track.
Partnerships Grow community-school partnerships that extend resources beyond the classroom.
03 · Student Safety Safety and prevention, from someone who does this work.
This isn't a talking point for Caren — it's her career.
She works as a drug educator and prevention specialist across Lehigh Valley schools, serves on the Lehigh County Drug and Alcohol Advisory Board, and is a certified recovery specialist in long-term recovery herself.
Prevention Expand substance-use and mental-health prevention programs that meet students where they are.
Early support Prioritize student mental health and preventative support services before a crisis, not after.
Recovery resources Fund the prevention and recovery resources she's seen, firsthand, save lives and strengthen families.
School partnership Bring her working relationships with Lehigh Valley schools directly into policy.
Caren speaking to students about her family's history — part of the school outreach work she does across the Lehigh Valley.
04 · Accountability Transparency, accountability, and real results.
Government and schools alike owe residents a straight answer and a real outcome — not just a process.
Transparency Increase governmental transparency so residents can see how decisions are actually made.
Spending Push for responsible use of taxpayer dollars, with real oversight behind it.
Oversight Strengthen oversight systems in both government and education.
Voice Build more community voice into decisions that affect residents directly — the same principle at the center of the fight against the Atlas data center.
Verification Check that policies actually deliver the outcomes they promised, not just the ones they were sold on.
This is the same fight, in every direction.
Keeping decisions close to the people they affect — whether that's a data center, a school budget, or a tax bill.
