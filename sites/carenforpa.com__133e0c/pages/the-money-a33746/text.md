What his campaign finance report actually shows
This isn't speculation — it's Schlossberg's own campaign finance report, filed with the PA Department of State.
We're linking the primary source below so you can check it yourself.
To be precise about what we do and don't know: we have not found a direct contribution to Schlossberg's campaign from a named data-center developer or AI company.
What we found, and what's shown above, is campaign cash from the electric utilities that would profit from the power demand a project like the Atlas data center creates — alongside his co-sponsorship of a bill that would make projects like it easier to build over local objections.
HB 502 has not yet come to a floor vote.
We think the co-sponsorship is worth knowing regardless.
