The House passes a real, bipartisan pause — without him
House Bill 2496 — which lets municipalities pause new data-center applications for up to 180 days to update local zoning — passes the PA House 201–1, with 42 co-sponsors from both parties.
Schlossberg is not one of them.
Co-sponsor, House Bill 502
Schlossberg is a co-sponsor of HB 502, which would create a state board empowered to approve data-center energy facilities over local zoning objections statewide.
The bill has not yet had a floor vote.

The bigger fight
This isn't just about one data center.
It's about who decides.
House Bill 502 would create a new state board — the Reliable Energy Siting and Electric Transition (RESET) Board — with the power to approve energy-generation facilities for data centers and bypass local zoning entirely.
It has not yet come to a floor vote.
If it becomes law, decisions South Whitehall residents are fighting to have a voice in today could be made in Harrisburg instead, with no township review at all.
