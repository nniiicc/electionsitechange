The bigger fight This isn't just about one data center.
It's about who decides.
House Bill 502 would create a new state board — the Reliable Energy Siting and Electric Transition (RESET) Board — with the power to approve energy-generation facilities for data centers and bypass local zoning entirely.
It has not yet come to a floor vote.
If it becomes law, decisions South Whitehall residents are fighting to have a voice in today could be made in Harrisburg instead, with no township review at all.
The bill HB 502 would create a RESET Board empowered to approve siting for major energy projects, including on-site generation built for data centers — overriding local land-use authority.
It is still in committee; no floor vote has been held.
Her opponent Rep.
Mike Schlossberg is a listed co-sponsor of HB 502, alongside a growing list of legislators pushing data-center-friendly siting rules through Harrisburg.
Caren's position Zoning decisions that affect a specific township — its roads, its schools, its water and power infrastructure — belong with the people who live there, not a board in the Capitol.
