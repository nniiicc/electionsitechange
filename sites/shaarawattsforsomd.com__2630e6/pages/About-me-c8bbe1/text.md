She’s running for State Delegate to deliver practical, people‑first solutions for District 29C.
Shaara shows up, listens, and brings people together to get things done.
Experience that Matters Shaara has more than 20 years' experience in the legal field, particularly environmental law, as a paralegal and law firm administrator and Government systems consultant.
She currently works for her family's business, Heron Point, LLC, as a property manager and is a long-time volunteer at the Calvert Marine Museum and other community organizations.
Record of Service Shaara’s record includes assisting seniors with healthcare and transportation, and supporting local businesses to grow and serve the community.
Her experience with Heron Point has enabled her to understand the property rental business from both sides (landlord and tenant).
She has been active in environmental and other issues in Southern Maryland for years.
Our Values Guided by honesty, hard work, and fairness, Shaara will fight for sustainable growth options for the region as well as stronger schools, lower utility rates and affordable housing.
The beauty and fragility of Southern Maryland is why she lives here.
Now she wants to help protect it -- our people, our water, our wetlands, our forests and our farms.
