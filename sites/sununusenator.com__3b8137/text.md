ABOUT
JOHN SUNUNU
John Sununu is a lifelong Granite Stater who strives to reflect the character of New Hampshire: independent, practical, and results-oriented.
Trained as an engineer and with significant private sector experience, John always brings a common-sense approach and a desire to fix things to public office.
During his time representing New Hampshire in Washington, John took on government waste, defended the rights and privacy of ordinary Americans, opposed tax hikes, and protected our seniors.
He'll bring the New Hampshire way to Washington.
Never afraid to do what’s right, John Sununu always puts New Hampshire first.
