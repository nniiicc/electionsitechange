Senator Sununu Commends Supreme Court Ruling Protecting Girls’ Sports
Jun 30
Chris Pappas Would Let Men in Women’s Sports
John Sununu issued the following statement today following the Supreme Court ruling that protects Girls’ Sports.
The Supreme Court ruled today that states can ban transgender athletes from participating in girls' and women's sports.
“Title IX was designed to ensure opportunity for women and girls to compete in all levels of sports.
Protecting those opportunities is common sense, and today’s Court ruling did just that.
Sadly, Chris Pappas is out of step with New Hampshire and has opposed efforts to keep boys out of girls' sports.
Instead of standing up for the safety and integrity of women’s athletics, he chose yet again to vote in lockstep with the extreme left wing."
