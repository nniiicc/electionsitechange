MEET MARIO
A WORKING PARENT FOR WORKING FAMILIES
I am the proud son of immigrants.
My mother came from Guyana and my father from Italy.
They worked hard, played by the rules, and became proud American citizens.
I grew up in Queens learning the value of community and hard work.
After college I moved to Clifton Park and built a career as an engineer supporting the world’s greatest Navy.
Today I serve on the Clifton Park Town Board.
I’m not just a public servant.
I’m a working parent.
My wife and I have two young children, one in elementary school and one in preschool.
I know the real struggle of balancing career and family, because I live it every day.
Affordability isn’t a talking point for me.
It’s my kitchen table.
