Elect Kelly Hale State Senate District 28
First Responder • Horseman • Advocate for Veterans
Through Kelly's experience as a First Responder in Red Lodge and as a whistleblower at the Montana VA, he has seen Montanans struggling firsthand.
Join Kelly as he stands up for Montana to help people live better lives.
Together, we'll address affordability, rural healthcare, water rights and public schools.
As an EMT, I am dedicated to ensuring quality of life and equality of opportunity for all individuals and families, regardless of socio-economic status.
Fundamental to such quality and equality are the basic necessities of life, including – at a minimum – healthcare, education, shelter, adequate nutrition, a livable environment and climate, human dignity, and lives free of violence.
Public education not only acts as the primary catalyst for a strong economy, upward mobility, and the quality of life in Montana, but also fosters the lasting bonds of understanding, acceptance, and community that make Montana such a desirable place to live.
We must be committed to a continuation of high-quality, equitable public education opportunities with adequate state funding to develop the potential of every Montanan.
Our state’s bounty of natural resources and wild country have always been at the heart of our prosperity, our heritage, and our lifestyle.
With our planet in the state of a climate emergency, our 21stcentury challenge is to ensure that these resources not only contribute to the strong economy needed to sustain families and communities, but also provide the clean and healthful environment that is our constitutional right.
I worked at the VA for years and became a whistleblower because I witnessed firsthand the unacceptable treatment of our veterans.
We ask Americans in the military to risk their lives and disrupt their families’ lives to serve our country.
In gratitude for their sacrifice, I am in lockstep with Montana Democrats who regard as sacred our obligation to care for American’s military – during active duty and for the rest of their lives.
Strong family farms and ranches are essential to our Montana heritage, the life-blood of our rural towns and an important part of our economy.
We must create incentives and inspiration for younger generations to return to the family ranch or farm, seize new opportunities in agriculture, or successfully hold the remote jobs in rural Montana made possible by reliable infrastructure.
