This extends well beyond reproductive rights and includes closing the gender pay gap, providing affordable childcare for working moms, supporting funding for research to improve women’s physical and mental health, addressing maternal health deserts and disparities, and breaking down the systemic barriers women all too often still face in the workplace.
The Ohio GOP supermajority has relentlessly attacked reproductive rights since the fall of Roe V.
Wade.
In 2023, the people of Ohio sent a clear message: We care about women’s rights.
From a strong defeat of a special election aimed at blocking the reproductive rights amendment to an overwhelming YES for reproductive freedom, Ohio voters showed the legislature that they care about women being treated as equal citizens.
Women are the backbone of our society.
Rose is committed to making sure they receive the support and freedoms they deserve, freedoms that Ohio voters overwhelmingly support protecting.
