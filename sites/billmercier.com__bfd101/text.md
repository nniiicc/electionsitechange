MEET BILL MERCIER
Hello Friends and Neighbors!
Thank you for visiting this online home to get to know me better.
Columbus has always been my “home”.
My roots in the community were shaped attending Windermere Elementary and enjoying our family home in Upper Arlington.
My dedication to my community started delivering suburban newspapers to my neighbors and mowing yards and shoveling snow.
These childhood experiences in Upper Arlington truly showed me the value of community.
My Eagle Scout project (which still stands today) at Fancyburg Park deepened my appreciation for our local communities and it cemented my belief that thriving cities are built through personal care and active participation.
Attending The Ohio State University, I majored in Aviation and began working in the Ohio House of Representatives.
I was honored to have supported Ohio’s first female Speaker of the House, Jo Ann Davidson, and built my experience of how government worked via my tenure at the House and through the guidance of Mrs.
Davidson.
After college, I began my working career in the maintenance department at Don Scott Field.
My early experiences tinkering around with airplanes and operations led me to my current role of Senior Consultant for Technical Services and Aviation Operations internationally.
Currently, I consult with the Pentagon, and all major airlines on safety, maintenance and fleet planning.
Through my career, I have honed my skills in government regulation, financial management and public safety.
The greatest moments of my life were marrying my wife, Leslie, at St.
Agatha Church and welcoming our son, Jack, into the world.
We have chosen to raise Jack on the same streets where I once biked, delivered newspapers, and spent long summer days playing baseball and football with my friends.
This community is our home and I am committed to preserving it’s exceptionalism, for Jack’s sake and all the families that call District 7 home as well.

BILL’S PRIORITIES
To help give perspective as your new State Representative, I have had the unique opportunity of living and working in cities that have fallen to misguided mismanagement and as a result have seen lack of safety, affordability and economic opportunities.
My experiences in Chicago and NYC have shown me how delicate a community like Columbus is and how quickly extreme policies can deteriorate the very fabric of our communities.
That’s why I am passionate about giving back and making a meaningful impact in our communities that have shaped me as a person.
Through a vision of common sense ideas and smart innovation, I hope to preserve our dynamic community for both little Jack and the next generations of Central Ohio kids and families.
