Wyoming's budget should protect the services our communities depend on — roads, schools, and libraries — without wasteful spending.
"Fiscal responsibility means smart priorities, not gutting the services that keep our communities running.
We can be tight with a dollar and still take care of our own." 🏫 Education Priority Allen will fight to protect K-12 and University of Wyoming funding.
Extreme budget proposals threatened deep cuts to education — Allen believes investing in our schools is investing in Wyoming's future.
"Our kids deserve the best education we can give them, right here in Wyoming.
Gutting school funding isn't fiscal responsibility — it's abandoning our communities." 🏠 Property Tax Balance Allen supports reasonable property tax relief for families and landowners — but not at the cost of starving local schools and services of revenue.
The balance matters.
"Property taxes are a real burden for working families.
But we can provide relief without defunding the very schools and services that make our communities worth living in." 📍 Local Control Allen believes local boards — not state-level mandates — should maintain control over our schools and libraries.
The people of House District 28 know their communities better than anyone in Cheyenne.
"Our roads, our schools, our local boards — these things matter.
My neighbors matter.
That's not politics.
That's just how I was raised." 🤝 Practical Conservative Leadership Allen prioritizes results over political theater.
He's not interested in ideological distractions — he's focused on the practical work of governing: protecting local services, supporting ranchers, and keeping Wyoming communities strong.
"I'm not a politician, but I am someone who understands how government decisions impact people on the ground.
I'm running to make sure our communities are protected by practical leadership, not sidelined by ideological distractions." 🏔️ Public Lands & Ranching Allen will protect the longstanding partnerships between ranchers and public lands.
These partnerships have sustained Wyoming's agricultural economy and open spaces for generations — they deserve a representative who will defend them.
"Ranchers and public lands have worked together in Wyoming for over a century.
That partnership isn't broken — and I won't let anyone in Cheyenne try to fix what isn't broken." 🛣️ Roads & Infrastructure Rural Wyoming depends on reliable infrastructure.
Allen will fight for the state funding our district needs to maintain roads, bridges, and the basic infrastructure that connects our communities.
"Out here, a road isn't a luxury — it's how you get your kids to school, your cattle to market, and your neighbor to the hospital.
I'll make sure Cheyenne remembers that." 🦅 Conservation & Wildlife As a rancher whose family helped save the black-footed ferret from extinction, Allen has worked alongside researchers to ensure that wildlife management decisions are shaped by both sound science and the firsthand experience of the landowners who steward the land every day.
He supports responsible stewardship that protects both livestock and wildlife habitat.
"My family has ranched this land for three generations.
We know that taking care of the land means it takes care of you.
That's conservation the Wyoming way — bringing together research and real-world experience." Stand With Allen If these positions reflect your values, join the campaign and help bring common-sense leadership to Cheyenne.
