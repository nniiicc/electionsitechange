Allen Hogg is a third-generation Meeteetse rancher whose family has worked the Upper Greybull River country since 1918.
He and his wife Kristine have been married 45 years and raised three sons on the land where the black-footed ferret — once thought extinct — was famously rediscovered.
With more than two decades of public service on county boards, Allen is running for House District 28 to bring practical, common-sense leadership to Cheyenne — protecting our roads, schools, and local way of life.
