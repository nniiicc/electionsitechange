That means lowering the cost of living, investing in our schools, and protecting our natural resources.
Access to Affordable Housing The cost of housing is too high.
We need to do a better job of ensuring that people can call Colorado home.
Families looking to grow, seniors trying to downsize, renters struggling with higher costs, and first-time homebuyers all need relief.
William has worked tirelessly to lower the cost of housing by: Sponsoring legislation to streamline the development and approval of needed housing Passing the nation’s first middle-income housing tax credit Brought Democrats and Republicans together to expand the use of community land trusts to lower the cost of land for new affordable housing units Investing In Kids & Classrooms William helped deliver a fully funded education budget for the first time in a decade.
This helped avoid crowded classrooms and retain quality teachers while also investing in more mental health support for kids.
As a proud graduate of Broomfield public schools and local higher education, William knows that making education affordable is essential.
That’s why he has: Helped high school students reduce college costs and get a jump start on their futures with college-level classes to earn college credit available to them in high school.
Helped expand opportunities for high school students to earn industry-based certificates recognized by local and national workforces, preparing them to succeed after graduation.
Building a Greener Future Moving towards a more sustainable economy is about more than just climate change.
It’s also a huge opportunity to build an economy that works better for everyone .
That means good jobs installing solar panels, wind turbines, sustainable HVAC systems in homes and commercial buildings, and so much more.
William has a proven track record when it comes to the environment: He supports policies to meet Colorado’s climate goals.
William has voted to make electrification more affordable for Colorado families, including for solar panels and electric vehicles.
William sponsored legislation to improve Colorado’s air quality and reduce pollution.
Protecting Our Reproductive Rights William is the pro-choice candidate.
He will always protect the fundamental right to safe and legal abortion.
Each pregnancy and circumstance is different, and when patients make personal medical decisions, a patient’s health, not politics, should drive those decisions.
William supports legislation that enables every person to have the right to control their body, life, and future.
He will always oppose efforts to limit reproductive health care.
William supports protecting funding for health centers and stands with healthcare providers to protect access to family planning services.
Healthcare That Works for Everyone William believes everyone should have access to affordable, quality care.
He has fought to expand access to behavioral health services and for more transparency in health insurance plans.
He is committed to protecting access to care throughout Colorado, including in our rural communities.
He’s voted to cap the price of epi-pens because no family should struggle to afford necessary medication.
He will protect the Affordable Care Act from Trump’s efforts to undermine it.
Sustainable Infrastructure & Transportation Colorado needs to make quality investments to fix our transportation challenges.
We need to think not only for today but also for decades in the future.
That means making investments in infrastructure now, increasing accountability and transparency regarding transportation dollars and how they are spent, and focusing on reducing congestion across Colorado.
William has brought Democrats and Republicans together while sponsoring legislation to: Repair bridges and tunnels more quickly and efficiently Protect vulnerable road users, including construction workers and bicyclists, so everyone returns home to their families Make mass transit more safe and reliable A Safer Community For All Of Us William is leading the way on tougher laws to curb gun violence, increasing penalties for auto theft, and helped expand support for victims and survivors of crime, including stronger protections for victims of domestic violence.
