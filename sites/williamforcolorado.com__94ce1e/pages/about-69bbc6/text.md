He is running for reelection to State Senate District 25 to continue getting results for Colorado families.
In the House, William has passed dozens of bills to lower the cost of living, fix our infrastructure, improve our public schools, protect the environment, and make communities safer .
In the short time William has been in the Senate, he has advocated for housing affordability, clean energy and consumer initiatives while introducing and sponsoring bills ensuring quicker access to mental health treatments for Colorado patients as well as improving transportation safety for Colorado drivers.
William was the 2023 Habitat for Humanity Legislator of the Year and has been recognized for his legislation to protect consumers from predatory practices.
He served as the Chairman of the House Finance Committee, Chairman of the Legislative Audit Committee, and currently serves on three committees in the legislature.
William is the youngest member of the Colorado State Senate.
William is a former assistant bench jeweler, public transit outreach advocate, community organizer, and former member of the Broomfield City Council.
During his time on the Broomfield City Council, he helped create Broomfield’s Housing Authority , authored municipal campaign finance reform to limit the influence of big money in local politics, and made it easier for residents to vote by adding additional ballot drop boxes across town.
William also served as Chairman of the Broomfield Transportation Forum and a Board Director of the Denver Regional Council of Governments.
William grew up in a broken home in Broomfield.
His father left when he was a child, and he and his mother struggled.
He put himself through college, sometimes working two or three different jobs at a time.
He holds a bachelor’s in political science and a Master of Public Administration degree from the University of Colorado Denver.
He lives in Broomfield with his wife Bria.
