ON THE ISSUES
"I believe in -- and have spent my life -- building communities.
Before I got to Congress, I built rural health clinics, helped start businesses, defended voting rights so everyone’s vote counted, helped families afford a home of their own and worked with Pueblos and Tribal communities across New Mexico.
My work in Congress expands upon these experiences, because, when you think about this nation’s aspirational foundation -- the pursuit of happiness and a more perfect union -- what it really comes down to is building thriving, healthy communities."
