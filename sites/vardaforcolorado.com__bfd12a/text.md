Danielle’s Priorities for Colorado
- Defending Democracy, Protecting Rights Defending democracy and protecting rights means standing up to abuses of power, including actions that undermine elections, weaken democratic institutions, and threaten the civil and human rights.
- Make Life More Affordable for Families Rising housing costs are making it harder for families to live and work in their communities.
Housing for all is non-negotiable.
- Strengthen Our Systems of Care for Coloradans Danielle’s experience in public health and healthcare innovation makes her qualified to tackle Colorado’s healthcare challenges.
- Support Small Business for a Strong Economy As a small business owner and entrepreneur, Danielle understands the importance of economic policies that create opportunity
- Ensure Safe and Supportive Community Danielle believes that safe communities require smart investments in prevention, public safety, and mental health.
- Sustainable Policies for a Green Colorado Danielle knows that Colorado’s natural beauty is one of our greatest assets—and that we must take action to protect it.
