Varda for Colorado is partnering with the Jeffco Young Dems and Jeffco Dems to host a community campaign festival on Jan 25, 2026.
Camp Campaign is a welcoming, drop-in festival for anyone curious about getting involved in local campaigns.
Whether you want to help from home, hit the field, or learn how representation works, Camp Campaign offers bite-sized workshops, community connections, and a low-pressure way to jump in.
- Learn how to join the caucus and vote for your candidate as a delegate
- Meet candidates and elected officials
- Visit community organization booths
Who should attend: Community members who want to learn about how to get involved, become a delegate, or work in the field.
For more information, contact: caitlin@vardaforcolorado.com
