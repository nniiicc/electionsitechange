“I am proud to endorse Dr.
Danielle Varda to become the next Colorado Representative for House District 27.
She is a strong community leader committed to expanding a better Colorado for all.
Her experience working across sectors makes her uniquely qualified to tackle our toughest challenges.
I have seen her lead with compassion, courage, and strength, always grounded in collaboration and respect.
She shows up for marginalized communities, fights for those who are too often unheard, and builds bridges across our diverse communities to create real, lasting change.
We need a leader like Danielle at the State Capitol.”
- House Majority Leader, Representative Monica Duran
“Danielle brings a wide spectrum of meaningful experience as a professor, entrepreneur, small business owner, public servant and a mom.
She will bring her skills, knowledge and dedication to work to address the struggles so many families are facing and will mitigate the detrimental impacts of harmful federal policies.
I hope you will join me in endorsing Dr.
Danielle Varda to represent our community in the state house.”
- Congresswoman Brittany Pettersen.
