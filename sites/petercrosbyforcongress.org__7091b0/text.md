Where Peter stands
People-First Policy for Northern Utah
Peter’s priorities come straight from the people he meets across Northern Utah.
They come down to three things:
- Affordability Confronting the housing crisis and the rising cost of living, including our healthcare costs, and standing up for the local jobs and small businesses that hold our towns together.
- Accountability Banning congressional stock trades, opening a real conversation on term limits, and getting corporate dark money out of our politics.
- Stewardship Protecting our public lands, restoring the Great Salt Lake, and standing up for communities against corporate developments like the Stratos data center project in Box Elder.
