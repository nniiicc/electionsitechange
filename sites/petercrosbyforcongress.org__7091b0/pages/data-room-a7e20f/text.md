Independent poll · full ballot The full five-way ballot “If the election for the US House of Representatives were held today, which of the following candidates would you vote for in 2nd congressional district?” Blake Moore 39.0%, Peter Crosby 34.0%.
The 5.0-point gap does not clear the margin of error, so this survey also leaves the race too close to call.
10.7% are undecided and 13.9% name one of the three minor-party candidates — together, about a quarter of respondents are not with either major candidate.
