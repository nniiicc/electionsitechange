This page reports only real, attributable figures — the campaign’s own polling and published public polls — never modeled or estimated ones.
The following are not represented:
- Ballot-test figures for Democrats and other-party voters.
Neither September survey breaks the Crosby-versus-Moore question out for those groups, so only Republicans and unaffiliated voters appear in the by-party chart.
- Forecast or win probability.
No predictive model is applied.
A forecast would require repeated independent polling of the district.
- County-level breakouts.
The sample spans five counties but was not published disaggregated; n = 657 is too small for reliable subgroup estimates.
