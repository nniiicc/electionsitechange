During her current term she has used her accounting background to address concerns regarding government spending and used her experience as a lifelong family farmer to advocate and protect local agriculture interests.
Deb Manjarrez is a fourth-generation farmer and graduate of Central Washington University.
She is a certified public accountant, apple orchardist who has lived all her life near Wapato.
She believes public safety and education are the states paramount duty.
She and her husband, Mark, have been married for 40 years.
They have two daughters and four grandchildren.
