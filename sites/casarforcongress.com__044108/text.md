MEET GREG CASAR Greg Casar is the progressive Congressman for families from the heart of Texas.
He serves as the Chair of the Progressive Caucus in the United States Congress.
A former labor organizer, Greg fights for an economy that works for working families, not big corporations.
The proud son of Mexican immigrants, he has passed policies to protect families from being separated, and is a champion for civil rights and voting rights for all people.
