Assemble with Community for JeffCo Caucus and Assembly
It is Caucus and Assembly time, where Jefferson County Democrats decide who is on the primary ballot!
Jefferson County Democrats run a virtual caucus and assembly that is held over zoom.
Make sure you are registered for caucus and assembly via the Jefferson County Democrats website.
We know folks are looking for connection so we found a space where people can bring their device, log into zoom and be together while participating in the Jefferson County Caucus and Assembly progress.
This community event is open to anyone in the Jefferson County community who is looking to be in-person as we participate in the Jeffco Dems caucus and assembly process via zoom.
Anyone who is supporting any candidate is welcome.
