top of page About Join the Campaign News DONATE TODAY All Posts Strengthening District 33 with Practical Leadership Max Burchett Mar 23 2 min read Updated: Mar 25 District 33 deserves leadership that is practical, accountable, and focused on results.
Families across our communities are facing real challenges, from rising costs and healthcare access to school quality and economic opportunity.
Meeting those challenges requires steady leadership and a clear plan.
Understanding the Vision My campaign is built around four priorities: improving healthcare access, strengthening public education, supporting local economic growth, and promoting accountable government.
These priorities reflect a simple belief: government should work for the people it serves and should help create stronger communities, not more obstacles.
Key Components of the Vision Improving Access to Healthcare Every family should have access to quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
In too many parts of Oklahoma, patients still face barriers related to distance, provider shortages, and cost.
That is why I support practical steps to strengthen care across District 33.
This includes supporting rural and community healthcare infrastructure, expanding telehealth where it makes sense, and encouraging solutions that improve preventative care and long-term health outcomes.
A stronger healthcare system helps families, supports providers, and strengthens the overall stability of our communities.
Strengthening Public Education Strong schools are essential to a strong future.
Our students need safe learning environments, well-supported teachers, and opportunities that prepare them for success after graduation.
I support reliable school funding, stronger support for educators, and expanded career and technical education pathways that connect students to real opportunities in Oklahoma.
We should invest in public education in a way that is responsible, future-focused, and centered on student success.
Supporting Local Economic Growth A healthy local economy creates jobs, strengthens families, and keeps communities moving forward.
District 33 needs policies that support small businesses, encourage innovation, and prepare workers for the jobs of today and tomorrow.
That means reducing unnecessary barriers to growth, investing in the infrastructure businesses rely on, and supporting workforce development aligned with real community needs.
Economic growth should be broad-based, sustainable, and rooted in local opportunity.
Promoting Accountable Government Oklahomans expect government to be transparent, responsible, and effective.
I agree.
Public trust is built when leaders are honest about priorities, careful with taxpayer dollars, and focused on delivering measurable results.
I support responsible budgeting, greater transparency, and efforts to reduce waste and inefficiency.
Government should be accountable to the people and committed to serving them well.
A Campaign Focused on Results This campaign is not about empty promises.
It is about practical solutions, collaboration, and steady progress.
I believe District 33 can move forward by focusing on what matters most to families and by working together to address the challenges in front of us.
With your support, we can build stronger communities, create more opportunity, and deliver the kind of leadership District 33 deserves.
Join the Campaign This campaign is about the future of District 33 and the people who call it home.
I invite you to join us, stay connected, and be part of the effort to move our district forward.
Together, we can build a stronger future for District 33.
