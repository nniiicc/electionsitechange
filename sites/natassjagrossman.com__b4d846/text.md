Learn My Story Mission Statement My mission is simple: to build a Utah where every person has the chance to live with safety, dignity, and opportunity.
That means protecting the programs that help families get back on their feet, ensuring schools and healthcare are strong and accessible, and fighting for the rights of every community — women, immigrants, LGBTQ+ people, working families, and future generations.
I believe government should work for people, not against them.
And I believe that together, we can create a Utah that reflects our values of fairness, compassion, and resilience.
Join the Movement Together, we can build a Utah where every voice matters and every family thrives I'm not a career politician.
I'm a mom who's tired of seeing families like ours ignored.
I believe it's time for a new generation to stand up and make our voices heard.
You don't have to be a political insider to make change.
You just have to care—and I care deeply about our community, our kids, and our future.
Join me, and together we can turn Utah County into a place where people feel seen, valued, and represented.
