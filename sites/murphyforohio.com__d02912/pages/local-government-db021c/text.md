Local leaders understand the challenges faced by Ashtabula and Geauga counties far better than the vast consensus of the state legislature.
Sadly, our current leadership has consistently undermined local authority, which strips away essential funding and forces out-of-touch mandates onto our communities.
I promise I will fight to return governance back to our district.
PROBLEMS TO BE SOLVED The state has drastically slashed the Local Government Fund (LGF) which keeps tax dollars in state coffers that should be returning to our communities to pay for essential services like police and fire protection.
State lawmakers are increasingly using "preemption" laws to block communities from making their own decisions, forcing a one-size-fits-all approach on our districts.
Legislators frequently pass new requirements for local governments without providing the necessary funding, placing a burden on local taxpayers and cities' budgets.
Goals Fight to return the LGF to its historical funding levels so that our smaller communities have the resources they need to provide vital services without constantly relying on local tax levies.
Vigorously defend Home Rule and oppose state-level preemptions, ensuring that local leaders have the authority to manage their own land use, economic development, and community standards.
Demand that any new state requirement placed on our municipalities or townships comes with the full state funding necessary to implement it.
We need a representative who views our local governments as partners.
By restoring local funding and protecting home rule, we can empower the communities of Ashtabula and Geauga counties to chart their own path forward and keep our neighborhoods safe, strong, and self-reliant.
