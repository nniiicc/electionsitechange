What Drives This Campaign?
Louis Murphy is a lifelong Ashtabula resident, Lakeside High School teacher and Edgewood High School coach who is running for State Representative to deliver real, active representation to Ohio’s 99th District.
Frustrated by lack of representation an elected officials who focus more on party politics than the issues that affect us, Louis is stepping up to ensure our neighbors have a dedicated voice in Columbus.
Deep Community Roots
Raised by a public school teacher who served at Jefferson for nearly 40 years, Louis understands the profound impact of public service.
Aproduct of Geneva School District, he continues his family’s legacy today by teaching at Lakeside and coaching at Edgewood.
Working in our schools and with parents gives Louis a firsthand understanding of the everyday challenges facing our neighbors.
Proven Policy Experience
Louis’s dedication is backed by experience.
With a Master’s degree in Environmental Policy and Global Sustainable Development from Lehigh University, he's already put his knowledge to work locally.
Louis secured critical funding and grants to combat shoreline as an environmental policy analyst for Geneva-on-the-Lake.
