The current practice of over-levying stems from a decades-old funding trap.
When Ohio lottery earnings were introduced to help fund schools in 1987², our state legislature offset this new money by cutting the general education budget.
To bridge the gap, municipalities had no choice but to pass property tax levies.
The state continues this cycle today, using levy passage as an excuse to further lower state funding.
The result is a continually increasing property tax burden on homeowners and consistently underfunded classrooms for our students.
Simply put, we're not getting back what we're paying to the state.
