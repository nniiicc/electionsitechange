Issues District Map News Volunteer Photos Contribute MEET SALLY Sally Hebert has spent her career using creativity and critical thinking to solve problems.
She improved government by identifying outdated systems and replacing them with efficient and current programs.
And her life experiences have informed what her community needs.
As an award-winning investigative reporter, Sally held government and business accountable, covering healthcare, economic development, and local government.
She knows good policy is built on facts, transparency, and asking the right questions before decisions are made.
While working for Dorchester County Council, Sally helped move council operations from paper to digital, making government more efficient while saving taxpayer dollars.
She also helped residents cut through government bureaucracy to build a launch pad to success rather than a tangled forest of bureaucracy.
When both of her parents were diagnosed with dementia, Sally became their caregiver.
She understands the financial and emotional strain families face and believes South Carolina must do more to expand access to affordable, reliable options for seniors and the families who care for them.
As a substitute teacher, Sally sees educators making the most of limited resources every day.
She believes our schools need practical support that helps teachers succeed and prepares students for today's workforce.
Sally is running for House District 94 because South Carolina deserves practical leadership that improves government, strengthens public education, supports caregivers, and spends taxpayer dollars wisely.
