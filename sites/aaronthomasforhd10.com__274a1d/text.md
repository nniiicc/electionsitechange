Meet Aaron Dear Friends and Neighbors, ​ From the bottom of my heart, thank you! ​ To everyone who knocked doors with me, contributed what you could, offered a prayer, or simply took the time to share your concerns—thank you.
You have reminded me why this race matters: because the hardworking families of Madison County deserve a representative who listens, understands, and fights for them.
Your encouragement and belief in this campaign have strengthened me, and I will carry that responsibility with me every day I have the privilege to serve. ​ To those who are still undecided as we head toward November, I want you to know this: there is real hope ahead.
I'm not a career politician.
I'm a husband, a father, and a small business owner who understands the responsibility of providing for a family.
I think about that every time payroll comes due, and every day I go to work at Redstone serving those who serve our nation.
I know what it's like to worry about rising costs, to cherish every hour spent with family, and to wonder whether the people making decisions in Montgomery truly understand that every dollar government spends comes from hardworking families like ours. ​ That's why I'm running. ​ I'm running to bring common-sense leadership to Montgomery—leadership that puts affordability, strong infrastructure, support for small businesses, and the priorities of our local communities first.
We don't need more politicians caught up in national political noise while ignoring the challenges facing our neighborhoods.
We need someone who will fight every single day for the families, veterans, workers, and small business owners who call House District 10 home. ​ I believe in Alabama.
I believe in Madison County.
And I believe that together we can build a stronger future — one where hard work is rewarded, families can thrive, opportunities are abundant, and every voice is heard. ​ If you're still deciding who deserves your vote, I simply ask for the opportunity to earn your trust.
Stop and visit with me at your door, send me a message, or introduce yourself at a community event.
I won't promise to have every answer, but I can promise this: I will always show up, I will always listen, and I will always put our community first. ​ Thank you for your support, your prayers, and your faith in the future of District 10.
I would be honored to earn your vote this November.
