DONATE Log In Meet Aaron Thomas Aaron's Why Together, Aaron and Amy have built a life grounded in faith, family, and hard work.
Aaron is deeply grateful for the family and life they are creating in Rocket City.
His gratitude extends to the opportunity he has to be a husband, father, business owner, neighbor, and friend. ​ Living as an expat for almost a decade, Aaron has gained a deep appreciation for what it means to be a citizen of the United States of America.
America is a country that affords its citizens the freedoms, opportunities, and blessings of prosperity.
He has also seen firsthand what happens when governments forget who they serve: suffering. ​ This gratitude and appreciation have led him to seriously reflect on what type of world he is leaving for his daughters and future generations.
This pondering has emblazoned the flame of duty in Aaron.
A duty to serve, to protect, and to build a brighter tomorrow for all Alabamians. ​ Aaron's Career At 16 years old, Aaron landed his first job at a local pizza restaurant — the same one he now owns with his family.
Aaron, an Eagle Scout, was lucky to be mentored by a business owner who believed in running his business and life with principles.
Upon his boss's retirement, Aaron was honored to carry that torch forward.
Today, Aaron and his family operate four restaurant locations across North Alabama.
During the COVID-19 crisis, the principles on which Aaron founded his business were tested.
Despite shutdowns and government regulations, Aaron stood by his team and didn’t lay off a single employee.
That’s the kind of leadership he believes in: steady, service-minded, and unafraid to make tough calls.
Aaron's small business has had the privilege of hiring and training minors, individuals with special needs, and others looking for a second chance.
It’s been a blessing to build something that gives back.
Aaron's Beginnings Aaron Thomas was born in St.
Louis, Missouri, to two working-class parents: His father, a civil servant, and his mother, a homemaker.
At the age of two, Aaron and his family moved to the Republic of Panama.
While living in Panama, Aaron had the opportunity to grow up immersed in other cultures, developing a love for people and cultures.
In addition to traveling throughout Central America, Aaron has visited all 50 States and over 30 countries, including Saudi Arabia.
When his family moved to the Rocket City area in 1999, he was amazed by the industry and the stark contrast between the developing world and the opportunities offered in the United States.
It was his love for Northern Alabama and the vast opportunities in the area that led him to plant roots and raise his family in Madison.
He has been happily married for ten years and has two daughters, ages seven and four.
He is currently a small business owner and is proud to provide jobs and economic opportunities in Madison County.
