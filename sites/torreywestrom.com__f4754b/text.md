A Unique Perspective Torrey Westrom is a lifelong, proven conservative with a record of creating rural jobs, while ensuring government operates within its means.
Westrom has fought and defended conservative principles in the state legislature.
Today, Westrom is a state senator representing a large portion of the counties that make up the 7th Congressional District.
Westrom lost his vision at age 14 in a farm related accident.
Westrom did not give up on his High School athletic career, rather he persevered by moving from the basketball team to becoming an accomplished wrestler.
In 2012 he was inducted into the National Wrestling Hall of Fame and received the Medal of Courage Award.
An Advocate For People With Disabilities Westrom continues to serve as a passionate advocate of independent living and employment opportunities for those with disabilities.
Most notably, Westrom is known for authoring marquee legislation to make Minnesota government websites accessible and for passing new driver's license options for families having someone with a disability, known as the “Disabled Relative License,” modeled after the decades-old “Farm Permit.” Westrom worked his way through law school and received his law degree from William Mitchell Law School in St.
Paul.
Torrey and his wife own and operate a family business and reside in Alexandria, MN.
The Westroms have 3 children, and attend New Life Church in Alexandria.
The Issues Where Torrey Stands A Vision For Our Future The past two years in Minnesota we have witnessed the heavy-handed, unconstitutional overreach of DFL Governor Tim Walz—now more than ever is the time to limit the size and reach of government!
With a record surplus, it is time for a major tax cut for all Minnesotans.
We MUST give hardworking taxpayers their money back permanently through a tax cut, including exempting Social Security income for our Seniors, like so many other states already do.
Let us improve the job creation environment that small and large business creators need to expand in rural Minnesota by more favorable tax and regulation policies.
Torrey will keep fighting for more rural broadband until the job is finished, so that rural Minnesota can benefit from the internet economy by highspeed internet in your home or business!
Fighting For Agriculture Torrey grew up working on his family dairy farm.
He brings farm logic and rural common sense to advocate on behalf of agriculture in Minnesota.
He will fight to expand our value-added agriculture market opportunities.
Senator Westrom served as the Chairman of the Senate Agriculture and Rural Development Committee from 2017 to 2023 and now serves as the Minority Leader on the Senate Agriculture, Veterans, Broadband, and Rural Development Committee.
In 2022 he chief authored the Senate Agriculture and Rural Broadband bill, that targeted $210 million dollars to be dedicated to the rural broadband program, the largest investment in State history, to finish providing the last 8% of residents with good internet service.
This is a huge win for rural Minnesota!
Defending Our 2nd Amendment Rights Senator Westrom has a strong record of fighting to protect the 2nd Amendment rights of every Minnesotan.
We must ensure that Minnesota's proud sporting and hunting traditions are preserved for the next generation to enjoy, and everyone's constitutional right of self-defense is preserved.
Pursuing Energy Independence Senator Westrom supports lifting the nuclear energy moratorium, advocating for more hydroelectric power, promoting home-grown biofuels, and opening more domestic drilling.
Promoting Infrastructure And Affordability We should dedicate the sales tax on auto parts to ongoing highway funding – rather than another 20-cent gas tax hike that the Democrats propose over and over, and we must repeal the huge car tab fee hikes that the Democrats passed in 2023.
