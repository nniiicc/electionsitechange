A Native Texan, Leilani Barnett makes her home in Wichita Falls, and her roots in this district go back to her great-great grandparents.
“My mother and her siblings grew up in Clay County,” she says proudly, “as did her parents and their parents.” Her father grew up in Nocona where Leilani's grandfather, silversmith W.D.
Barnett, designed and made cowboy spurs under the McChesney mark for the Nocona Boot Company.
Pictured: Leilani with her mother and late father, Joan and Clarence Barnett, both long-time residents of Wichita Falls
After retiring from Lone Star Gas Company in Petrolia, Leilani's great-grandfather, W.A.
Mayo, a Democrat, put on a suit and went to work as the town's mayor in the little brick office that still stands today.
"But, this election," says Leilani, "isn't about political parties.
It is about choosing the person who will fight for the things that are important to working families--things like affordable housing, clean and safe water, public school funding, and safe communities."

A Focus on Education
A political writer and teacher with more than 31 years of experience, Leilani Barnett holds a Bachelor’s degree with a double major in Journalism and English; an MA in Educational Leadership; and an MA in Literature.
She's now in the last phase of completing a Ph.D. in Education.
In fact, it is her commitment to education that first motivated Barnett to run for the Texas House of Representatives.
“Without fully-funded public schools, we turn off the lights on the bright future of our children," she explains.
Pictured: Leilani with grandaughter at graduation and "Great-Mommy" (Leilani's mother)
A Teacher's Heart
In addition to her professional experiences in education and leadership at local, state, and national levels, Leilani Barnett previously worked with the National Children’s Advocacy Center, training police officers, attorneys, social workers, mental health and medical professionals in more than 22 cities across the United States.
Leilani wrote national curricula on a multidisciplinary team approach that reduces trauma for victims of child abuse.
She cares deeply about the issues facing the children and the hard-working families who love and care for them.

Faith and Freedom
Leilani is a member of Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Wichita Falls and a friend of the Wichita Falls Unitarian Universalist Fellowship.
Her vision for Texas is guided, though, by what she first learned as a child in a Baptist Sunday school: Love your neighbor and Do unto others as you would want them to do unto you.
She is a staunch believer in separation of church and state, which allows every Texan to worship in the way they wish.
