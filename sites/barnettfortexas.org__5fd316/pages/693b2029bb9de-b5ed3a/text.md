Faith and Freedom
Leilani is a member of Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Wichita Falls and a friend of the Wichita Falls Unitarian Universalist Fellowship.
Her vision for Texas is guided, though, by what she first learned as a child in a Baptist Sunday school: Love your neighbor and Do unto others as you would want them to do unto you.
She is a staunch believer in separation of church and state, which allows every Texan to worship in the way they wish.
