District 54 is located entirely in Fulton County, and includes parts of the Buckhead neighborhood in the City of Atlanta, and the southern part of the City of Sandy Springs.
It is south of I-285, east of Lake Forrest/Roswell Rd, and west of the Dekalb county line.
District 54 also includes parts of the following zip codes: 30305, 30342, 30319, 30326, 30324.
It includes the Congressional district of GA-5, and the Georgia Senate districts of SD 6, SD 14, and SD 39.
