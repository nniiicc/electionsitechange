It's Campaign Season!
Last updated : Sara Fuchs While the weather may still be telling us it's summer, we're already gearing up for this year's Election Day, which is only 67 days away!
In Georgia, early voting begins on October 13, so we only have about six weeks to reach voters.
This will be a game-changing election year - critical races are on the ballot, including Senator Jon Ossoff, open seats for most statewide offices, and the ever-critical state legislators at the bottom of your ballot.
As families face rising prices, limited healthcare options, and underfunded public schools, it's critical to tell voters we need change at the state level.
Interested in supporting the movement to elect more Democrats to office?
We've got many ways to become involved below...plus an exciting announcement about our longtime Betsy for Georgia team member Sara Fuchs.
Read on for details!
Please join me at a fundraiser in Alpharetta to support the campaigns of Adam Cleveland (candidate for HD 48) and Beth Fuller (candidate for HD 53).
We are ten seats shy of flipping the State House from red to blue, and these seats are critical.
Special guest Shanette Williams will join us to share her story.
Shanette lost her daughter, Amber, after she did not receive timely reproductive care.
No woman should have her care delayed like Amber’s was, and no mother should have to endure the pain Shanette experienced.
When elected, Beth and Adam will join me to repeal Georgia’s six-week abortion ban and protect reproductive freedom.
Come out, get involved, and help flip these two critical seats.
RSVP here .
Join me, Beth Fuller (candidate for HD 53), Josh McLaurin (candidate for Lieutenant Governor), and Shelia Edwards (candidate for Public Service Commission, District 5) at a canvassing event held in North Buckhead!
In partnership with Necessary Trouble , Mid Fulton Democrats will knock doors in the North Buckhead are and share important election information with liberal and progressive voters.
Register to join us here .
