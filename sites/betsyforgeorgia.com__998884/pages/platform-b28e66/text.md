At a time when too many Georgians feel squeezed by rising costs and left out of decisions that shape their lives, I believe government should be a force for fairness, dignity, and opportunity.
It has been my honor to represent Georgia's 54th House District since 2018.
During that time, I've worked to lower costs for working families, defend reproductive freedom, expand access to healthcare, strengthen public education, reduce gun violence, protect voting rights, and stand up for every member of our community.
I'll continue fighting for practical solutions that improve people's lives—not partisan politics.
Lower Costs & Build a Strong Economy
Families deserve an economy that works for them.
I've supported targeted tax relief, including gas tax cuts, while protecting funding for schools and essential services.
I also champion policies that support small businesses, create good-paying jobs, protect workers, and help families keep more of what they earn.
Keep Homeownership Affordable
I've worked with local city and county leaders to reduce residential property taxes while ensuring commercial property owners pay their fair share.
I also support additional property tax relief for seniors and long-time homeowners who deserve to remain in the communities they've helped build.
I'll continue working to address housing affordability so families can continue to call Sandy Springs and Buckhead home.
Strong Public Schools, Strong Future
As a public school parent, I understand the challenges facing our classrooms.
I've secured critical funding for public schools and higher education, fought to protect neighborhood schools, and support giving teachers the resources, flexibility, and respect they deserve.
Investing in education means investing in Georgia's future.
Affordable, Accessible Healthcare
Every Georgian deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare.
I support expanding Medicaid, lowering prescription drug costs, strengthening our healthcare workforce, protecting patients from surprise medical bills, and ensuring families can get the care they need when they need it.
Protecting Reproductive Freedom
I believe personal healthcare decisions belong to patients and their doctors—not politicians.
I will continue fighting to restore reproductive freedom and protect access to contraception, fertility treatments, maternal healthcare, and postpartum care.
Safer Communities
Keeping our communities safe requires common-sense solutions.
I've co-sponsored nine bills to reduce gun violence, including legislation creating a Red Flag law and preventing domestic violence offenders from acquiring firearms.
I also support investments in mental health, first responders, and proven public safety strategies that reduce violence while protecting constitutional rights.
Protecting Rights, Democracy & Local Communities
I will continue standing up against policies that target immigrants, LGBTQ+ Georgians, and other vulnerable communities.
I believe every eligible Georgian deserves the freedom to vote, every voice deserves to be heard, and local communities—not politicians at the Capitol—should have the authority to make decisions that best serve their residents.
Protecting Georgia's Future
Georgia's future depends on smart, sustainable investments today.
I support expanding clean energy, protecting our air and water, improving transportation options, preserving our natural resources, and making thoughtful investments that leave our children a stronger, healthier state.
