Candidate Meets with Blue Ohio to Discuss Grassroots Momentum and 2026 Vision
March 27, 2026
Easton Retzke participated in a statewide Blue Ohio virtual meeting this afternoon, part of the Every State Blue program, where he introduced his campaign and discussed the grassroots momentum building across Ohio ahead of the 2026 election cycle.
Retzke, a lifelong Ohioan and recent college graduate, spoke about his background in organizing and community engagement, emphasizing that his campaign is being built through a growing base of small-dollar donors, volunteers, and local supporters across the district.
The conversation centered on the campaign’s core priorities: strengthening Ohio’s economy and infrastructure, improving K–12 education, expanding access to affordable housing, and increasing transparency in government spending and campaign finance.
Retzke also highlighted the need to restore public trust by ensuring working families have a stronger voice in state government.
Participants also discussed the broader role of grassroots organizing in Ohio’s political landscape heading into 2026, particularly the impact of local and emerging candidates in building long-term civic infrastructure.
Retzke noted that early campaign organizing efforts are continuing to expand through community outreach and volunteer engagement, with increasing interest from residents across the district.
He concluded by emphasizing that the campaign remains focused on building durable grassroots support and deepening local involvement in the months ahead.
