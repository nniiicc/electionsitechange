Home Meet the Candidate My Platform News Events Donate Follow us Donate Follow us Menu Retzke Thanks Wakeman Residents for Community Conversation and Engagement April 8, 2026 WAKEMAN, OH — Candidate Easton Retzke today expressed gratitude to residents of Wakeman following a community conversation focused on local concerns and priorities.
“I want to sincerely thank everyone in Wakeman who took the time today to meet, talk, and share your thoughts.
I truly value the opportunity to hear directly from residents about the issues that matter most to you and your families,” Retzke said.
Retzke also thanked Firelands Indivisible and the Talk Teams for helping organize and facilitate the event.
“Community-led spaces for real dialogue like this are important and deeply appreciated,” he added.
“These conversations are a key part of building a campaign rooted in real community input, and I am committed to continuing this work across House District 89 so every part of this district is heard and represented.
