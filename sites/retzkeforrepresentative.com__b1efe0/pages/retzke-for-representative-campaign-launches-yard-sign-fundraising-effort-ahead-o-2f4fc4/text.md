Retzke for Representative Campaign Launches Yard Sign Fundraising Effort Ahead of Primary
March 31, 2026
Sandusky, Ohio — The Committee to Elect Easton Retzke today announced the launch of a grassroots fundraising effort to support the production and distribution of campaign yard signs across Ohio House District 89.
With the primary election approaching, the campaign is working to build early visibility and connect with voters throughout the district.
Yard signs remain one of the most effective ways for supporters to show their enthusiasm and help spread the campaign’s message in neighborhoods and at voting precincts across the region.
“We want to make sure our campaign has a strong presence in every community we serve,” said Easton Retzke.
“These signs are more than just visibility, they represent a growing movement of working people who want to see real investment in our communities, better schools, and a more transparent government.”
Supporters are encouraged to request a yard sign and contribute to help cover initial printing and distribution costs.
Early fundraising will allow the campaign to get signs up well before the primary, building momentum and name recognition across the district.
Donations can be made through the campaign’s ActBlue link, which can be accessed by clicking “Donate” on the campaign’s website or by searching for the campaign in the ActBlue directory.
Residents of Ohio House District 89 who are interested in displaying a yard sign can sign up through the Yard Sign Interest Form below:
