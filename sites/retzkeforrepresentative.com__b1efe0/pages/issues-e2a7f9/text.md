Tax Reform: A System That Works Ohio deserves a tax system that is fair, sustainable, and built around the people who live and work here.
Tax reform should not simply mean cutting taxes and hoping for the best; it should mean examining who pays, who benefits, and whether our system is actually producing the communities we want to build.
That means protecting seniors and working families from unfair property tax increases, ensuring our highest earners and profitable corporations contribute their fair share, protecting Ohio farmers, and giving local communities the resources they need to provide essential services.
Ohio can provide meaningful tax relief while still investing in schools, infrastructure, public safety, and the services that make our communities strong.
Building a stronger future across Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties requires a tax system that recognizes the unique needs of our communities.
Homeowners should not be forced to shoulder an ever-growing share of the cost of public education and local services simply because the state has failed to adequately fund its responsibilities.
At the same time, communities that welcome millions of visitors, farmers protecting productive land, and municipalities managing major new developments all face unique financial pressures that state tax policy should recognize.
True tax reform means creating a more balanced system that protects the people who can least afford additional costs while ensuring corporations, high-income earners, and major developments contribute appropriately to the communities they depend upon.
Our Action Plan: Creating a fairer Ohio tax system requires targeted reforms that prioritize property tax relief, local services, economic fairness, and responsible state investment:
- Protecting Seniors and Homeowners From Property Tax Increases
Strengthening sustainable property tax relief for qualifying senior homeowners and working families facing significant increases in their tax bills.
Ohio should ensure that rising property values do not force longtime residents out of homes they can no longer afford to keep, particularly when household incomes have not increased at the same pace.
- Creating an Immediate Property Tax Spike Rebate Program
Establishing a targeted rebate mechanism for homeowners experiencing extraordinary or sudden property tax increases.
Relief should reach homeowners when they need it rather than requiring families to wait years for meaningful reform while their tax bills continue to rise.
- Fully Funding Public Education to Reduce Property Tax Pressure
Strengthening Ohio’s existing Fair School Funding Plan and ensuring the state adequately meets its responsibility to fund public education.
Local homeowners should not be expected to continuously make up for inadequate state funding through emergency levies and rising property taxes.
A stronger state commitment to public schools can provide better educational opportunities while reducing pressure on local taxpayers.
- Ending Unfair and Predatory Property Tax Collection Practices
Protecting Ohio homeowners from aggressive collection practices that can turn temporary financial hardship into the loss of a home.
State policy should provide clear notice, reasonable opportunities to resolve delinquent balances, and stronger protections for homeowners facing financial hardship.
- Giving High-Traffic Communities a Fair Share of Tourism Revenue
Strengthening Ohio’s existing lodging-tax framework so communities experiencing exceptionally high tourism demand have appropriate tools to help fund the public services and infrastructure required to support that tourism.
Erie and Ottawa County communities provide police, fire, EMS, roads, sanitation, parks, and other services to millions of visitors.
Visitors should help contribute to the communities and public resources they use.
- Ending Unnecessary Data Center Tax Exemptions
Reforming Ohio’s tax treatment of qualifying data centers so highly profitable corporations receiving substantial public incentives also contribute appropriately toward the infrastructure and resources their operations require.
Economic development should produce a meaningful public benefit rather than shifting costs onto local taxpayers while corporations receive special treatment.
- Ensuring Ohio’s Highest Earners Pay Their Fair Share
Examining Ohio’s income-tax structure and closing unnecessary loopholes so those with the greatest ability to contribute are not receiving disproportionate advantages.
Ohio should protect working- and middle-class families from bearing an unfair share of the state’s tax burden while ensuring that the highest income levels contribute adequately to the services and infrastructure from which they benefit.
- Protecting Ohio’s Family Farms and Agricultural Land
Strengthening tax policies that recognize the unique financial realities facing Ohio farmers and protecting productive agricultural land from unnecessary tax pressures caused by development.
Farmers should not be forced to sell land that has supported their families for generations simply because surrounding development dramatically increases its speculative value.
- Supporting Local Police, Fire, and EMS
Ensuring state tax policy provides local governments with sustainable resources to maintain police, fire, EMS, roads, and other essential services.
Communities should not be forced to choose between maintaining basic services and placing additional property tax burdens on their residents.
- Rejecting Unfunded Mandates on Local Governments
Opposing state policies that require local governments to provide new services or meet new financial obligations without providing the resources necessary to do so.
Columbus should not balance its budget by pushing costs down to counties, cities, villages, townships, and ultimately local taxpayers.
- Making Economic Development Work for Ohio Communities
Ensuring that state tax incentives are transparent, accountable, and tied to measurable public benefits.
Businesses that receive significant tax incentives should demonstrate that those incentives create meaningful jobs, investment, infrastructure, or other long-term benefits for the communities supporting them.
- Creating a Fairer Future for Ohio Taxpayers
Building a tax system that protects homeownership, supports public education, strengthens local services, protects Ohio agriculture, and asks those with the greatest ability to contribute their fair share.
Ohio should not have to choose between responsible taxation and strong communities.
With smarter priorities and a more balanced approach, we can provide meaningful relief to working Ohioans while making the investments necessary to build a stronger future for Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties.

Capping corporate exemptions ensures big organizations pay their fair share so working families and seniors on fixed incomes do not absorb skyrocketing tax bills.

Expanding access to revolving loan funds, capital grant opportunities, and streamlined permitting processes for local small businesses in District 89.

Partnering with regional vocational centers (such as EHOVE Career Center serving Erie and Huron counties, and career pathways in Ottawa County), trade unions, and community colleges to fund hands-on apprenticeship programs and technical certifications.
Equipping workers with high-demand skills bridges the local income gap and connects residents directly to high-paying careers in advanced trades, healthcare, and technology.
- Bringing Manufacturing Jobs Back to Local Communities
Capitalizing on state industrial programs and targeted capital grants to re-shore critical supply chains, modernize production facilities, and bring advanced manufacturing operations back to Northern Ohio.
Manufacturing accounts for over 20% of private sector employment in our area, and expanding state incentives will protect and grow these vital production jobs.
- Protecting Local Consumers and Small Business Purchasing Power
Holding market manipulators accountable and monitoring localized price gouging, including unfair fuel markups that burden daily commuters, farmers, and small transport fleets across Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties.
Economic fairness means protecting working families from artificially inflated daily living costs.
- Ensuring Growth Benefits Residents over Corporations
Conditioning corporate tax incentives on real community commitments, including local hiring preferences, infrastructure protections, and competitive starting wages.
Public policies must prioritize raising the average household income for working families across our region rather than boosting corporate profits.
- Ensuring Measurable Results for Tax Dollars and Incentives
Restructuring state economic incentives so that public tax credits and direct development grants are tied to strict clawback provisions, verified wage floors, and enforceable local job creation targets.
Tax dollars should only support economic development initiatives that deliver a clear, measurable return on investment for taxpayers rather than unearned corporate handouts.
- Increasing JobsOhio Transparency and Ending Insider Deals
Advocating for legislative reforms, such as the JobsOhio Transparency Act, to require direct biennial audits by the Auditor of State, Controlling Board oversight, and full public disclosure of state-supported economic deals.
As the natural home for JobsOhio development projects, our region must eliminate no-bid arrangements, prohibit political nepotism in economic agencies, and ensure every deal is conducted with complete open-book accountability.

Public development must directly benefit local residents, ensure public resources support the families who need them most, and prevent housing policies from draining our communities of the people who make them strong.
Everyone across Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties deserves a safe, predictable place to live with dignity.
Building a prosperous district requires protecting long-time homeowners, supporting workforce housing, keeping neighborhood decisions under local control, and capping unnecessary middleman costs.
Our region faces unique housing pressure, from rising residential property valuations to corporate out-of-state land speculation and seasonal tourism demand along Lake Erie.
When private equity funds and corporate investors over-involve themselves in development projects, excessive markups and fees artificially inflate final purchase prices and rents for working families.
Rebalancing state priorities, curbing speculative investor inflation, and re-investing public tax dollars into local community needs ensures that the people who work and live here can afford to stay here.
Our Action Plan: To make housing more affordable, protect long-time residents, and ensure community stability, direct policy solutions must focus on key reforms:
- Preventing Investor Markups and Excessive Fee Structures on Affordable Housing
Establishing strict statutory limits and transparency guidelines that stop institutional investors, private equity firms, and corporate middlemen from over-involving themselves in state-funded affordable housing projects.
Capping administrative fees, transaction markups, and speculative buyouts ensures public housing dollars flow directly into construction and homebuyer relief rather than inflating final sales prices for buyers and renters.
- Expanding Local and State Investment in Affordable and Workforce Housing
Expanding local and state investment in affordable and workforce housing development through existing housing and community development programs.
Utilizing tools like Welcome Home Ohio provides direct capital grants to build and rehabilitate affordable single-family homes, ensuring local healthcare workers, tradespeople, teachers, and public safety personnel can afford to buy homes near their jobs.
- Reforming Property Tax Structures for Longtime Homeowners
Reforming property tax structures to provide targeted relief for longtime and middle-income homeowners, particularly seniors and families on fixed incomes across Sandusky, Port Clinton, Bellevue and rural townships.
Statutory caps on annual property valuation spikes prevent sudden, drastic tax increases driven by temporary market spikes.
- Strengthening Oversight and Regulation of Short-Term Rentals
Strengthening oversight and regulation of short-term and transient rental properties to protect the long-term housing supply for local residents.
Empowering municipal governments and township trustees to set balanced local zoning guidelines ensures seasonal vacation lodging does not strip away year-round rental stock for working families.
- Prioritizing Residents Over Outside Speculative Investors
Prioritizing public investment in housing initiatives that directly benefit local residents rather than outside investors or speculative development firms.
Establishing state guardrails and local purchasing preferences protects local homebuyers from competing against institutional investment funds buying up single-family neighborhoods.
- Reevaluating Voucher Spending to Support Housing and Local Services
Reevaluating and restructuring publicly funded private school voucher programs to ensure they do not divert state resources away from community priorities, including local housing stability, public services, and emergency property tax relief.
Reclaiming over a billion dollars in state voucher spending allows Ohio to reinvest directly in local infrastructure, school funding, and homeowner protection measures.
- Supporting Responsible Zoning and Planning Reforms
Supporting zoning and planning reforms that encourage responsible development while preserving neighborhood stability.
Facilitating brownfield remediation, infill housing on vacant urban lots, and downtown historic preservation preserves community character while expanding modern housing choices.

Restoring integrity to Ohio government begins by holding decision-makers accountable at every level.

When political cliques and quasi-public agencies operate without meaningful public oversight, tax dollars get funneled away from local priorities into dark-money funds and unvetted corporate giveaways.
Bringing true accountability to state and local government means ending insider political loops, preserving independent board oversight, enforcing total transparency in state spending, and keeping governing power where it belongs: in the hands of local citizens.

Challenging political gatekeeping and insider deal-making that exclude everyday citizens from local governance.

Expanding public records laws to eliminate broad statutory exemptions that allow state agencies and legislative committees to hide internal communications, budget negotiations, and draft bills from public view.
Broadening public access to state records ensures complete visibility into how laws are drafted and how public funds are spent.

I am committed to ensuring that families have access to safe drinking water, that our air quality meets strong health standards, and that environmental protections are enforced in a way that prioritizes public health and long-term sustainability.

Strong environmental standards should not be viewed as obstacles to development, but as essential safeguards that ensure our communities across Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties remain safe, vibrant, and livable for generations to come.

- Cleaning Up Brownfields and Remediating Contaminated Sites
Directing Ohio Brownfield Remediation Program grants to restore contaminated industrial properties, abandoned commercial land, and hazardous sites across District 89.

- Targeting Industrial Emissions While Safeguarding Economic Stability
Promoting practical emissions reduction strategies that target major sources of air pollution near industrial corridors and high-traffic freight routes.
Enforcing clean air safeguards reduces localized respiratory health risks without penalizing compliant local employers or disrupting regional supply chains.
- Expanding Transparency in Environmental Reporting
Mandating accessible, open-data reporting platforms where residents can access clear, real-time information regarding local air quality indices, municipal drinking water tests, and watershed health.
Public transparency empowers communities and ensures state agencies remain accountable to the citizens they serve.
- Protecting Coastal Ecosystems and Combating Toxic Algae
Expanding H2Ohio program grants for coastal wetland restoration, natural filter beds, and shoreline stabilization along Lake Erie and Sandusky Bay.
Rebuilding natural wetland buffers traps excess nutrients before they fuel toxic algae blooms, safeguarding our multi-billion-dollar coastal boating, fishing, and tourism economy.

Enforcing strict statutory oversight of medical billing practices to eliminate surprise out-of-network bills, predatory collection tactics, and unvetted administrative fees.

Standing With Our Veterans.
While talking to voters across District 89, I have heard from many veterans who feel forgotten by the very decisions that are supposed to protect and support them.
Too often, the benefits they rely on do not cover the injuries or challenges caused by their service, leaving them to navigate a complex system that falls short.
I am committed to listening, advocating, and fighting for policies that ensure veterans receive the care, resources, and respect they have earned.
Our community thrives when those who served are supported, valued, and never left behind.
That means strengthening state-level support systems that complement federal veterans programs and ensure veterans in Ohio can access the services they are entitled to without unnecessary barriers.
It also means improving coordination between state agencies, local healthcare providers, and veterans service organizations to ensure benefits are delivered efficiently and effectively across Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties.
Standing with our military families requires making sure our local county veterans service offices have the resources needed to deliver direct relief and quality care close to home.
Our Action Plan: To better support veterans at the state level across District 89, I will advocate for practical, veteran-centered policies:
- Expanding Funding for Ohio Veterans Services and Benefits Navigation
Increasing state investment in programs administered through the Ohio Department of Veterans Services and local County Veterans Service Commissions.
Directing state resources toward benefit application assistance, direct emergency grants, and housing support ensures veterans receive full access to their earned state and federal benefits without delays.
- Strengthening Partnerships with County Veterans Service Offices
Formalizing coordination between state agencies and county veterans service offices in Erie, Ottawa, and Huron counties.
Streamlining communication channels and updating digital case-management tools cuts red tape, reduces claim processing times, and ensures local officers can quickly resolve benefit disputes.
- Increasing Access to Tailored Mental Health and Substance Use Treatment
Expanding state behavioral health grants for specialized, veteran-centric mental health care, Post-Traumatic Stress (PTS) treatment, and substance use recovery programs.
Partnering with regional healthcare networks and local veterans posts improves access to peer-to-peer support networks and rural crisis intervention teams.
- Supporting Veteran Job Training and Workforce Transition Programs
Expanding state workforce initiatives that translate military occupational training into civilian licenses, trade certifications, and academic credits.
Partnering with local building trades, manufacturers, and OhioMeansJobs centers connects transitioning service members directly to well-paying careers in our regional economy.
- Improving Housing Security and Eliminating Veteran Homelessness
Strengthening coordination between the Ohio Housing Finance Agency, local public housing authorities, and veteran support organizations.
Expanding emergency rental assistance, transitional housing grants, and supportive services ensures homeless or at-risk veterans secure safe, permanent housing.
- Ensuring Timely Processing and Systemic Accountability
Mandating performance tracking and clear communication standards within state-level veterans support systems.
Requiring routine state audits of benefit response times guarantees that inquiry calls, emergency assistance requests, and healthcare referrals are addressed promptly.
- Supporting Long-Term Care Infrastructure at the Ohio Veterans Home
Securing state capital funding to support and modernize long-term care facilities, including the historic Ohio Veterans Home in Sandusky.
Investing in nursing care, memory support units, and facility upgrades ensures aging and disabled veterans receive high-quality residential care in our district.
