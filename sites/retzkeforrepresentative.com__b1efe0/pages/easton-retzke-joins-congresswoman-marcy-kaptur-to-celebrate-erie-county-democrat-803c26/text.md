Home Meet the Candidate My Platform News Events Donate Follow us Donate Follow us Menu Easton Retzke Joins Congresswoman Marcy Kaptur to Celebrate Erie County Democratic Headquarters Grand Opening July 18, 2026 SANDUSKY, Ohio — A large crowd of local voters, party organizers, and community members gathered at the new Erie County Democratic Party headquarters in Sandusky for a standing-room-only grand opening event alongside U.S.
Representative Marcy Kaptur.
The event drew strong attendance from across Ohio House District 89, filling the headquarters as neighbors met to discuss key regional priorities.
Attendees participated in discussions focused on supporting working families, strengthening public services, and advancing economic development throughout Erie and Ottawa counties.
“Welcoming Congresswoman Kaptur to Sandusky alongside such a massive turnout demonstrates the real momentum building across our district,” said Easton Retzke, candidate for Ohio House District 89.
“Our campaign is grounded in showing up, listening, and bringing people together around shared solutions.
Seeing so many dedicated neighbors fill the room today shows what we can accomplish when our community unites behind a vision for a stronger Ohio.” The campaign emphasized that the high attendance reflects growing grassroots involvement leading into the fall election.
Following the grand opening program, many attendees transitioned directly into field action, joining organized door-to-door canvassing efforts across local neighborhoods.
