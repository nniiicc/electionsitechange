Erica embraced the challenges, working tirelessly in various roles to provide for her small family.
Graduating from South Central College in 2019 with an Associate degree, Erica served as a student recruiter, advocating for education across Southern Minnesota.
Transitioning to Minnesota State University – Mankato for Political Science with a History minor, she momentarily paused studies during the pandemic to focus on family and collaborate with Habitat for Humanity.
In the vibrant community of Nicollet, Erica found love again with Kevin Schwartz.
Blending their families seamlessly, they look forward to their upcoming wedding, symbolizing a new chapter of happiness.
Erica currently works for her husband’s family business, the Nicollet Mart, which serves as a pillar of the community.
