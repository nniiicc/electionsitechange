Yuba City, CA – Today Congressman Mike Thompson announced endorsements from elected officials in Colusa, Placer, Sutter and Yuba Counties.
The elected officials that endorsed are:
- Sutter County Supervisor Karm Bains, Republican
- Sutter County Supervisor Dan Flores, Republican
- Placer County Supervisor Shanti Landon, Republican
- Yuba County Supervisor Jon Messick, Republican
- Yuba County Supervisor Gary Bradford, No Party Preference
- Colusa County Supervisor Kent Boes, No Party Preference
- Mayor of Yuba City Marc Boomgaarden, Republican
- Mayor of Lincoln Richard Pearl, Republican
- Mayor of Marysville Chris Branscum, Republican
- Mayor of Colusa Denise Conrado, Democrat
- Mayor of Williams Kate Dunlap, Republican
- Mayor Pro Temp of Lincoln Whitney Eklund, Republican
- Williams Councilman Don Parsons, Republican
“I am truly honored to have earned the endorsement of these respected North State community leaders.” said Congressman Mike Thompson “Together, we worked to deliver much-needed relief for our peach farmers and I will continue to partner with them to lower the cost of living, ensure access to affordable health care, and strengthen our water infrastructure and flood protection.”
I endorse Congressman Mike Thompson.
Mike has been active in Yuba County for a full year now ahead of us being in his district.” said Yuba County Supervisor Jon Messick “Mike has taken the lead on many projects I have been working on that have needed not only federal approval but also state approvals.
From the Loma Rica Post office, Army Corps of Engineers permitting, BLM property to the Museum of Forgotten Warriors, whatever I have asked Mike for help on he has jumped in with both feet.
I trust Mike to keep up the hard work to get these projects over the finish line for Yuba County.
“Congressman Thompson and I share a passion for agriculture, supporting our veterans, and creating an environment where small businesses can thrive.” said Placer County Supervisor Shanti Landon “Just as importantly, I believe our residents are best served when their representatives are willing to work across the aisle, find common ground, and focus on getting results.
I believe Congressman Thompson is the best candidate in this race, and I look forward to working with him for our community!”
“As a Republican, I am endorsing Congressman Mike Thompson based not on party, but on who can best represent Yuba City.
Since redistricting brought our community into his district, Mike has maintained a strong local presence” said Yuba City Mayor Marc Boomgaarden My conversations with people in the smaller communities he represents—as well as my personal interactions with him in Washington, D.C., and during his visits to Yuba City—have demonstrated his experience, effectiveness, and commitment to his constituents.
He is by far the most qualified and capable candidate to represent Yuba City in Congress, and I am proud to support his re-election.”
Congressman Thompson is running for reelection in the Fourth Congressional District which contains parts or all of Colusa, Lake, Napa, Placer, Sacramento, Sonoma, Sutter, Yolo, and Yuba Counties.
Since January 2025, he has hosted 45 town halls, community coffees, and Meet and Greets in all 9 counties of California’’s 4th Congressional District.
He has been endorsed by the California Farm Bureau, local small business groups, nurses, firefighters, teachers, veterans, and more.
