Chris Brown is committed to restoring safety and stability so every family in our community can
live, work, and raise their children with peace of mind.
Public safety starts with strong, accountable leadership that prioritizes results over politics.
Chris
understands that safe neighborhoods are the foundation of a thriving community, and he will
work to ensure law enforcement has the resources and support they need to do their jobs
effectively.
At the same time, he recognizes that lasting safety comes from strong families, good
schools, and real economic opportunity.
By addressing the root causes of crime—failing schools, lack of opportunity, and disconnected
communities—Chris will take a balanced, common-sense approach to public safety.
His focus is
simple: safer streets, stronger neighborhoods, and a renewed sense of security for every
resident in the 55th District.
