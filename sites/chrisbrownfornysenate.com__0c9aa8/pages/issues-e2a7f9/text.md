Working to bring clean power plants back to New York Winter IS Coming & the sky’s the limit for your energy bills New Yorker’s energy bills rose 14.6% since last March – 4 times higher than the inflation rate.
Albany’s response?
Banning gas stoves & decommissioning power plants.
It’s about energy costs, your kids’ future, your neighborhoods, what new projects will harm the environment and public safety.
Chris Brown is fighting to lower energy costs so families and small businesses can keep more of what they earn and afford to stay in New York.
Rising utility bills are putting real pressure on households across Rochester and the surrounding communities.
At a time when families are already struggling with high housing costs and taxes, Albany’s policies have only made energy more expensive and less reliable.
Chris believes it doesn’t have to be this way.
He will push for smarter energy policies that prioritize affordability, reliability, and a balanced approach to meeting our state’s needs.
Lower energy costs aren’t just about saving money—they’re about protecting jobs, supporting local businesses, and ensuring our region remains competitive.
Chris will fight for practical solutions that ease the burden on working families while keeping our energy grid strong and dependable for the long term.

Our kids deserve results and that starts with listening.
I’m hearing about this frustration and more across the 55th District.
It’s about our schools and how we can help them achieve success.
Chris Brown is fighting to build a better tomorrow by putting families, education, and opportunity back at the center of New York’s future.
For too long, families in the 55th District have been asked to pay more while getting less—especially when it comes to education.
Despite record-high spending, too many students are being left behind.
Chris believes that every child deserves access to a high-quality education that fits their needs, not a one-size-fits-all system dictated by Albany.
That means expanding school choice, supporting charter schools, and investing in vocational and technical training that prepares students for real careers in trades, manufacturing, and healthcare.
A better tomorrow also means restoring trust between parents and schools.
Chris will fight for full curriculum transparency and accountability so families know exactly what their children are being taught.
By empowering parents, rewarding great teachers, and focusing on results—not bureaucracy—we can ensure the next generation has the tools to succeed and thrive right here in our community.

SAFER COMMUNITIES People want to feel safe in their neighborhoods and in the places they raise their families.
At local events, Bad Apples keep spoiling our evenings… Monroe County is blessed with dozens of unique events and beautiful public spaces.
However, from the rise in targeted car break-ins to disorder at our festivals, families are kept from experiencing all Rochester has to offer.
Chris Brown is committed to restoring safety and stability so every family in our community can live, work, and raise their children with peace of mind.
Public safety starts with strong, accountable leadership that prioritizes results over politics.
Chris understands that safe neighborhoods are the foundation of a thriving community, and he will work to ensure law enforcement has the resources and support they need to do their jobs effectively.
At the same time, he recognizes that lasting safety comes from strong families, good schools, and real economic opportunity.
By addressing the root causes of crime—failing schools, lack of opportunity, and disconnected communities—Chris will take a balanced, common-sense approach to public safety.
His focus is simple: safer streets, stronger neighborhoods, and a renewed sense of security for every resident in the 55th District.
