For too long, families in the 55th District have been asked to pay more while getting less—especially when it comes to education.
Despite record-high spending, too many students are being left behind.
Chris believes that every child deserves access to a high-quality education that fits their needs, not a one-size-fits-all system dictated by Albany.
That means expanding school choice, supporting charter schools, and investing in vocational and technical training that prepares students for real careers in trades, manufacturing, and healthcare.
A better tomorrow also means restoring trust between parents and schools.
Chris will fight for full curriculum transparency and accountability so families know exactly what their children are being taught.
By empowering parents, rewarding great teachers, and focusing on results—not bureaucracy—we can ensure the next generation has the tools to succeed and thrive right here in our community.
