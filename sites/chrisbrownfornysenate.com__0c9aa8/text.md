MEET CHRIS BROWN
Christopher Richard Brown Jr. is a long-time Rochester-area resident, technology leader, and dedicated community volunteer.
He serves as a Software Engineer Project Leader at L3Harris Technologies, where he manages teams and oversees complex projects with budgets ranging from $20,000 to $15 million.
Chris previously supported U.S.
Army Special Operations as a Site Team Leader with CACI International, managing personnel and contributing to long-term Department of Defense planning.
He currently serves on the Webster Conservation Board, helping guide responsible land use and environmental stewardship in the community.
A longtime volunteer, Chris is a member of the Knights of Columbus and the Webster Republican Committee, and has coached and led numerous local youth sports programs.
As the Republican nominee for New York State Senate in the 55th District, Chris is focused on empowering parents, improving education, and restoring fiscal discipline.
He is committed to cutting wasteful spending, lowering taxes, and delivering results for Monroe County families.
