Welcome to my website.
I am running for election to the Montana House of Representatives because I want to make Montana the best place to live, start a business, and raise a family.
As your Representative, I will be guided by these principles:
Common Sense Conservative: As a member of the Montana Legislature, I have worked hard to find common sense solutions to the problems we face.
I believe in the importance of hard work, personal responsibility, and living within our means.
Putting Montanans First: We spend too much time asking whether a political party or special interest wins or loses.
We need to stop that.
I believe we should ask whether the people of Montana win or lose.
Finding Solutions: My main priority is doing what is best for the people of Montana.
The best way to ensure we implement the right policies is to listen to everyone and to take the time to find solutions we can agree on.
