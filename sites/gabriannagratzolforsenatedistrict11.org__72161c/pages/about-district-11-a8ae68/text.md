Where is District 11?
District 11 includes Clay, Harris, Penn, Cleveland, Osolo, Washington, and York townships.
It includes portions of both Elkhart and St Joseph County.
What district do I live in?

Here you can check your voter registration status and see what township and precinct you live in as well as which Congressional, State House, State Senate, and County Commissioner districts you’re in!
While you’re there, be sure to make sure all of your information is up to date before registration ends!
Here you search your address and see your Congressperson, State Senator, State House Representative, and the current Indiana Senators!
