District 11 includes Clay, Harris, Penn, Cleveland, Osolo, Washington, and York townships.
It includes portions of both Elkhart and St Joseph County.
What district do I live in?
There are a few ways to learn about what district you live in!
Voter Registration Portal Here you can check your voter registration status and see what township and precinct you live in as well as which Congressional, State House, State Senate, and County Commissioner districts you’re in!
While you’re there, be sure to make sure all of your information is up to date before registration ends!
Find Your Legislator Here you search your address and see your Congressperson, State Senator, State House Representative, and the current Indiana Senators!
You can click on each one for more info on their authored bills and voting history to see if the Representatives you currently have are truly representing you and your neighbors.
Ballotpedia Here you can search for all of the election information you might need, such as detailed maps of the current district, past redistricting history, information on previous races, campaign spending by candidates on races, and so much more!
