Lisa Kinney was instrumental in getting a bond issue passed to pay for a new library in Laramie and the community came together to move the books from the old location to the brand new Albany County Public Library!
A clip of the Albany County Public Library’s move from the historic Carnegie Library to its new home on July 4, 1981.
Residents of Laramie, Wyoming would line up and pass the entire collection over, one book at a time, to be shelved in the new building.
