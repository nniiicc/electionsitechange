Campaign Team Sunday, October 4, 2026 Dear friends, In the 2024 Connecticut elections, over 110,000 more votes were cast by Republicans than in the 2022 midterm elections.
For years, we have talked about “drop-off Republicans” and the need to turn them out in midterm and local elections.
Candidates and consultants have tried countless strategies and spent millions of dollars searching for the secret sauce, often with little success.
We believe the reason is multi-pronged, but it essentially comes down to: WHO is talking to the individual voter WHAT they are being targeted with Our new app was created to address this problem by making GOTV efforts personal.
Whether you put 5, 50, or 500 contacts into your personal contact list, each one represents another opportunity to increase our candidate’s vote total on Election Day.
HOW The ideal use, and more importantly the success, of this system will be through Connecticut’s Early Voting process.
We have found that a “drop-off voter” is more likely to be convinced to vote if they are contacted by a family member, coworker, or friend rather than by a stranger knocking on their door or calling them on the phone.
We also know that Republicans tend not to favor early voting and often choose to vote on Election Day.
While that is understandable, Early Voting is here to stay, and Republicans need to use it to our advantage.
WHY In Connecticut, Republicans are clearly at a disadvantage in both raw registration numbers and ground-game resources.
There are only so many calls, door knocks, TV ads, texts, and mail pieces that can be utilized compared to the Democratic special interests supporting campaigns across the state.
We need to think outside the box and find a way to leverage what we do have.
Once our party better utilizes Early Voting by “banking” our reliable voters as early as possible, we can remove those voters from our final GOTV efforts.
Every voter who votes early on our side can be filtered out of subsequent mail pieces, texts, phone banking, and door knocking, leaving more time and resources to focus on drop-off or persuadable voters.
RESULT Our app makes the process as easy as possible for every user.
Each afternoon after Early Voting begins in Connecticut, our team will receive the updated voter list.
Our app will automatically determine who on your personal list has not yet voted and send that list directly to your email.
This process will happen each day, allowing you to talk to friends and family who have not yet voted and encourage them to vote as early as possible.
MAKING IT PERSONAL For us to begin turning out these “drop-off” voters early, we need personal conversations with friends, family members, coworkers, and neighbors.
Whether your personal list has one name or one hundred names on it, every vote matters.
ONE LAST NOTE The success of this app will ultimately depend on the number of people who use it, so we strongly encourage you to share this email with like-minded Republicans.
