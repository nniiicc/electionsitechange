Early Voting Starts Tomorrow!
The moment we've been working toward is here.
Early voting in Connecticut starts tomorrow, Monday, October 19!
Campaign Team Sunday, October 18, 2026 Dear friends, The moment we've been working toward is here.
Early voting in Connecticut starts tomorrow, Monday, October 19 !
You don't have to wait until Election Day to make your voice heard.
Starting tomorrow, every registered voter can vote in person at their town's early voting location.
Early voting runs every day, including weekends, through Sunday, November 1.
Early Voting Hours : Most days : 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM Tuesday, October 27 and Thursday, October 29 : Extended hours, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM If you aren't registered yet, you can still register and vote at the same time through Same-Day Registration at your early voting location.
To find your town's early voting location, visit portal.ct.gov/sots.
To find your town’s sample ballot, visit portal.ct.gov/sots/election-services/town-ballots/2026-november-town-election-ballots.
Voting early is the best way to make sure your ballot is counted, no matter what comes up between now and November 3.
It also frees our team to focus on reaching the voters who haven't voted yet.
Make a plan, pick a day, and bring a friend, family member, neighbor, or coworker with you.
Did you know over 110,000 Republicans in Connecticut stayed home in the last midterm election?
We cannot have that happen again and we need your help to make sure it doesn’t!
If you’d like to get a list of voters who we need to show up, specific to your town, email bille@peterlumaj.com.
Every vote counts, and the direction of Connecticut is on the ballot.
Let's get it done!
Together, we can win this!
