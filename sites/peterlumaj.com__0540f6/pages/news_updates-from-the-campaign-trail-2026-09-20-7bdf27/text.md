Campaign Team Sunday, September 20, 2026 Dear friends, Last week, our nation paused to remember the bravery of all who responded #ago, and the lives of those who were tragically lost on September 11th.
We must never forget their sacrifice.
As we reflect on the values that make our country strong, Team Lumaj remains hard at work on the campaign trail.
It was great to spend time Friday at the North Haven Fair and then Saturday at the Bethlehem Fair.
Despite being in two different parts of our great state, we heard the exact same concerns from voters who desperately want change.
The news out of Hartford continues to prove exactly why that change is needed.
Because of errors in my opponent's office, taxpayers are out $40,000 while faith in our election process is shaken, and the blame is being placed on an unnamed staffer.
Perhaps our current Secretary of the State should spend less time delivering political speeches and calling press conferences about nothing, and spend more time doing the job she was elected to do.
I encourage you to read about her record of failures here , and make a plan to vote for accountability and action this November.
Connecticut deserves an election system with integrity and a Secretary of the State who takes accountability seriously.
November 3 is approaching fast.
Please reach out at Info@PeterLumaj.com to get involved in our campaign, volunteer, and help spread our message.
Together, we can win this!
