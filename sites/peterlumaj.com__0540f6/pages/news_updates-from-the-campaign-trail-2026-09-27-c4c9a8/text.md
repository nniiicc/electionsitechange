Campaign Team Sunday, September 27, 2026 Dear friends, Team Lumaj remains hard at work on the campaign trail!
Last weekend, we joined fellow Republicans for the annual Guilford Fair parade.
We also recently joined the entire statewide ticket in Salisbury to share our visions on how to not only win in November, but also on what each of us would do to make CT more affordable, safer, and fairer for each and every resident of our great state.
With less than 50 days left until Election Day, I am working seven days a week to put Connecticut on a better path.
As part of that effort, we just released a brand new campaign video that I want to share with you.
The video highlights a simple truth: Connecticut families are already being asked to do more with less, and when you are forced to watch every dollar, your government should be doing the exact same thing.
Unfortunately, mistakes made by Secretary of the State Stephanie Thomas's office recently forced Enfield to hold another primary, costing taxpayers roughly $40,000.
You work far too hard to have your money wasted on government errors.
It's time to bring real accountability and fiscal responsibility back to Hartford.
Election Day is approaching fast, and we need all hands on deck.
Please reach out to Info@PeterLumaj.com to volunteer, grab a yard sign, and help us spread our message.
Together, we can win this!
