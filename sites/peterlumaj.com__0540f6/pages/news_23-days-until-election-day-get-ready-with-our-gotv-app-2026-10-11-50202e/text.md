# Days Until Election Day: Get Ready with Our GOTV App!
With just # days until Election Day, November 3rd is quickly approaching!
Campaign Team Sunday, October 11, 2026 Dear friends, With just # days until Election Day, November 3rd is quickly approaching!
As we enter the final weeks of this election, I want to revisit something we introduced in last week's newsletter: our new Get Out the Vote (GOTV) app.
For years, we have discussed the challenge of turning out voters who participate in presidential elections but often sit out midterm and local elections.
Our new app was developed to help address that challenge by making it easier to track voting participation among people you know.
HOW IT WORKS The process is simple.
By creating a personal contact list within the app, users can receive updated information about which contacts have not yet voted once Connecticut's Early Voting period begins.
Each afternoon, as updated voter participation records become available, the system will identify which individuals on your list have not yet cast their ballots and send an updated list directly to your email.

With Election Day approaching, we want to make sure everyone has the information and resources they need to participate in this year's election.
