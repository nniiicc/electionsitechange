About Cole Cuzick
Generations of Service to Northern Kentucky
Cole Cuzick is a husband, father of three, small business owner, and lifelong advocate for conservative values.
A proud resident of Erlanger, he has a proven record of fighting for limited government, constitutional rights, and the sanctity of life.
Cole is committed to building a healthier, freer, and more prosperous future for House District 63.
A 7th Generation Kentuckian, Cole has served alongside his grandparents, parents, and brothers in business and ministry.
Furthermore, he has worked as a public policy advocate for two conservative statewide organizations.
Now running a small business in real estate, Cole possesses the political know-how and determination to advance the conservative values that make Kentucky great.
Cole's Priority Issues
Cole is a Christian and constitutional conservative who will eliminate taxes and regulation, protect children, and defend your God-given constitutional liberties.
Eliminate Taxes
and Regulation
Kentuckians are ready to eliminate the state income tax and reform our out-of-control property taxes.
I'll push to reform the tax code and eliminate regulations so you can keep more of your hard-earned money and get government out of the way.
Protect Children
I've advocated for the unborn for over a decade and actively worked to make Kentucky an abortion free state.
Furthermore, I fought to protect fairness in girls’ sports and to shield children from the radical transgender policies that threaten their innocence and safety.
I'll oppose any push to expand access to abortion or irreversible transgender surgeries for minors.
Back the Blue
Strong communities begin with strong, respected law enforcement, and I will always have their backs.
Northern Kentucky is great place to live because the safety of our communities versus Democrat controlled cities.
As your State Representative, I will ensure our police are fully funded, properly equipped, well-trained, and supported with common-sense policies that allow them to do their jobs effectively while holding them to the highest
standards of accountability.
Make Education Great Again
Every child in Northern Kentucky deserves hands-on CTE starting in middle and high school, opening doors to high-paying careers in healthcare, manufacturing, IT, and the trades—jobs that give our young men and women real purpose and dignity.
As your State Representative, I’ll fight for stronger employer partnerships, cut outdated seat-time rules, and expand programs so students graduate with skills, certifications, and good jobs or college options.
Prioritizing career-technical training builds stronger families and a stronger Northern Kentucky economy—without leaving any student behind.
Defend Constitutional Liberties
I'll oppose any threat to our constitutional rights of free speech, religious liberty, and self-defense.
These rights come from God and it will be my job to protect your rights.
