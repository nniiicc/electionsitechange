As the daughter of working-class mother and immigrant father, Becca is deeply committed to defending American democracy and protecting vulnerable communities.
Now in Congress, Becca is a member of the House Judiciary Committee and a leader in the Progressive Caucus, where she is an outspoken defender of democracy, and civil rights.
Becca believes that even in the face of immense challenges, we cannot back away from fighting for each other and building connections across differences.
