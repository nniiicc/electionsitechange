STOP THE FRAUD
Restore integrity to the government.
My 7-point solution for stopping the widespread swindle of taxpayer money.
1.
EXTEND THE CLOCK:
Support extending statutes of limitations for prosecution of fraud.
2.
VERIFY BEFORE YOU PAY:
Require real-time eligibility verification before any payment is made.
3.
STRENGTHEN INSPECTORS GENERAL:
Protect and empower independent watchdogs.
4.
REWARD WHISTLEBLOWERS:
Incentivize fraud reporting with meaningful rewards.
5.
USE TECHNOLOGY SMARTLY:
Deploy AI and data analytics to catch fraud early.
6.
MAKE REFORMS PERMANENT LAW:
Codify protections for future administrations.
7.
HOLD STATES ACCOUNTABLE:
End sanctuary for fraud in state-administered programs.
