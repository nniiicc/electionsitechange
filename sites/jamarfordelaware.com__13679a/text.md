I am running because the 9th District deserves a representative who will listen to its residents and advocate for their needs.
Our educational system has shown only marginal improvement, leaving too many students without the opportunities they deserve.
At the same time, the cost of living continues to rise, driven by increasing property taxes and escalating energy costs.
At the core of these challenges is a lack of balance in our state government.
When one group holds unchecked control, policies are shaped without the broad debate, scrutiny, and compromise that best serve the public.
These combined issues are placing significant strain on families and diminishing our overall quality of life.
I am running to strengthen our educational system, reduce the size of government, lower the cost of living, and restore the balance necessary for effective, accountable governance.
— Jamar Money
