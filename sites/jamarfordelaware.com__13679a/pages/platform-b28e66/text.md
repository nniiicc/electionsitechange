Three priorities for the 9th District , in Jamar ’s own words.
01 · Education 02 · Cost of Living 03 · Balance Why am I running The 9th deserves a representative who listens.
I am running because the 9th District deserves a representative who will listen to its residents and advocate for their needs.
Our educational system has shown only marginal improvement, leaving too many students without the opportunities they deserve.
At the same time, the cost of living continues to rise, driven by increasing property taxes and escalating energy costs.
At the core of these challenges is a lack of balance in our state government.
When one group holds unchecked control, policies are shaped without the broad debate, scrutiny, and compromise that best serve the public.
These combined issues are placing significant strain on families and diminishing our overall quality of life.
I am running to strengthen our educational system, reduce the size of government, lower the cost of living, and restore the balance necessary for effective, accountable governance.
01 Education High-Quality Education Systems A strong and effective education system is essential to the long-term success of our state.
I am committed to ensuring that every school provides students with the resources they need to learn, grow, and thrive.
This includes maintaining supportive and safe learning environments, fostering active engagement from parents and the broader community, and sustaining a stable, dedicated faculty and staff.
02 Cost of Living Affordability The rising cost of energy, housing, and property taxes continue to place significant strain on household budgets across our district and the state.
To support long-term resilience and reduce this burden, we must invest in reliable, forward-looking energy solutions that meet our needs today while strengthening our communities for the future.
03 Balance Balanced Government A well-functioning government depends on balance.
When a single supermajority controls the full scope of lawmaking and policy decisions, it can limit the diversity of perspectives needed to serve the best interests of our state’s citizens.
Meaningful debate, thorough scrutiny, thoughtful questioning, and genuine compromise are essential to creating policies that reflect the needs and values of “the people.” When differing viewpoints are part of the process, our communities benefit from more accountable, transparent, and representative governance.
