Privacy Policy
Effective date: June 14, 2026 · Jamar Money for Delaware
1.
Who we are
This Privacy Policy applies to the official campaign website of Jamar Money for Delaware (“the Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), located at jamarforde.com.
The Campaign is a political committee organized under Delaware law for the purpose of electing Jamar Money to the Delaware House of Representatives, 9th District.
2.
Information we collect
We collect information you provide directly, including:
- Your name
- Email address
- ZIP code and phone number (optional)
- How you’d like to help (volunteer interest)
- Any message you include in a form submission
When you submit a form we also record the time of submission and the IP address and browser the submission came from, which we use only to identify automated abuse.
We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit the site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URLs, via Google Analytics (see Section 5).
3.
How we use your information
We use the information we collect to:
- Send campaign updates, event information, and volunteer opportunities
- Respond to your inquiries or requests
- Coordinate volunteer activities and yard sign deliveries
- Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
- Comply with applicable campaign finance reporting requirements
4.
How we share your information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:
- Service providers: Form submissions are stored in a database hosted by Cloudflare, and the notification email to the Campaign is delivered by MailChannels.
Both act only on our instructions.
- Legal requirements: If required by law, including campaign finance disclosure rules under Delaware law.
- With your consent: For any other purpose with your explicit permission.
5.
Google Analytics
This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our pages.
Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect anonymized data about site usage.
This data is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.
You may opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
6.
Cookies
This site uses only essential and analytics cookies.
We do not use advertising or tracking cookies beyond Google Analytics.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some site features may not function as expected.
7.
Email communications
By submitting your email address, you agree to receive campaign communications from Jamar Money for Delaware.
You may unsubscribe at any time by replying “UNSUBSCRIBE” to any campaign email or by contacting us directly at jamarmoneyde@gmail.com.
8.
Data retention
We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, and support campaign operations through the November 3, 2026 election and any required post-election reporting period.
9.
Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure.
10.
Children’s privacy

If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.
