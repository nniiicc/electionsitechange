Rooted in Delaware Jamar Money’s journey reflects what is possible when a deep belief in education is combined with a commitment to community.
A proud lifelong Delawarean, Jamar has built his life around service as a husband, father, educator, mentor, and leader.
He is grounded in the conviction that strong communities are built on the foundation of strong families and opportunities for all.
Born and raised in Wilmington and educated in the Colonial School District, Jamar graduated from William Penn High School.
He experienced firsthand how dedicated educators and supportive community members positively impact a young person's life trajectory.
His own educational journey, from Delaware Technical Community College to the University of Delaware and Wilmington University, and now as a doctoral candidate at Delaware State University, reflects his commitment to lifelong learning.
Seventeen years in the classroom For 17 years, Jamar was a steady presence in Delaware's public schools, teaching at the elementary, middle, and high school levels.
He consistently stepped forward wherever students needed support.
In addition to teaching, he coached multiple sports, supervised intramural programs, and supported academic initiatives such as Freshmen Focus and AVID (Advancement Via Individual Determination), helping students build confidence, resilience, and academic success.
Beyond the classroom Jamar's commitment to serving the community extends beyond the classroom.
He volunteered as a coach with I-9 Sports programs in the MOT area, where he listened to families and recognized a growing desire among young athletes to improve their skills and make their scholastic teams.
In response, he founded JM Sports LLC, a small business dedicated to helping children develop fundamental athletic skills, build confidence, and achieve their goals in athletics.
As a man of strong faith, Jamar also served as Youth Director at his church, mentoring children, adolescents, and young adults.
Today, he continues to expand his impact as a college instructor, adjunct faculty mentor, and dual-enrollment mentor, helping students recognize their potential and pursue meaningful futures.
Investing in people At the core of Jamar's work is a simple belief: investing in people strengthens families, and strong families build strong communities.
His life and service reflect that belief, and his dedication to Delaware's students and families continues to help shape the next generation of successful, productive citizens.
As a candidate for the 9th Representative District, Jamar is committed to bringing that same principle of investing in people to this public service.
He seeks to strengthen the district's neighborhoods, support its families, and create opportunities that empower every resident to thrive.
