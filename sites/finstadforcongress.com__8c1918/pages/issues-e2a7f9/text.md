Where Brad Stands
- What We’re Up Against: Washington bureaucrats and unscientific, politically motivated policies—such as California’s Proposition 12—that threaten the livelihoods of family farms and agricultural producers.
- The Mission: Ensure that federal farm policy avoids creating burdensome obstacles and is written by farmers, for farmers.
- The Benefit: Lower input costs, stronger supply chains, and a secure, high-quality food supply for the nation.
- The Proof (Results): Brad recently introduced the USDA Express Loan Act and supports the PRECISE Act to expand farmer access to advanced agricultural technology and capital.
He also championed the FARMER Act to expand crop insurance access and reduce costs for local producers.
