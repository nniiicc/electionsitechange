He will always stand strong for the family farmers who feed our nation and fuel our economy.
PROUD FAMILY MAN A husband and father of seven, Brad Finstad is fighting to leave our country better than he found it for his children and yours.
Committed To Service Brad Finstad has dedicated his career to fighting for the people of Minnesota and their families.
MY PLEDGE TO YOU I am running for re-election to Congress because Washington, D.C., sorely needs some Midwestern common sense, and southern Minnesotans deserve a representative who will work hard to advance our priorities.
That’s what I will bring to our nation’s capital every single day.
I grew up in our rural communities, and I still help to operate my family farm.
I will always apply that experience in D.C. for the benefit of the other great families who live here.
Washington has plenty of folks focused on “going viral” or getting the next 30-second TV soundbite.
I’m there to make a positive impact for Minnesotans by helping make our neighborhoods safer, our jobs more secure, our schools better, our government programs more effective, and our tax burdens lighter.
Above all, I will do everything in my power to leave things better than we found them for the next generation.
Common Sense Solutions Building a brighter future for southern Minnesota through proven leadership and common sense.
SUPPORTING OUR FARMING FAMILIES Ensure that federal farm policy avoids creating burdensome obstacles and is written by farmers, for farmers.
FIGHTING FOR FISCAL SANITY Keep more money in your paycheck and give small businesses the certainty they need to operate and grow.
SECURING THE BORDER & RULE OF LAW Continue to keep our border secure, restore the rule of law, resume construction of the southern border wall, and alleviate staffing shortages for Customs and Border Patrol.
PROTECTING ELECTIONS & CONSTITUTIONAL FREEDOMS Implement common-sense safeguards to ensure that only U.S. citizens can vote in our federal elections and to protect individual liberties from left-wing efforts.
