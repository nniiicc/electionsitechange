Georgia State Income Tax: Sales Tax Tradeoff Georgia is on a phased path to reduce and potentially eliminate the state income tax.
Because the income tax is Georgia's single largest revenue source (roughly $14 billion per year), eliminating it would require replacing that revenue — most likely through higher sales taxes.
Sales taxes apply to most purchases regardless of income.
