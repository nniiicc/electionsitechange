I'm running for Georgia State House because public safety and constitutional limits have to work together.
When any agency acts without warrants, oversight, or due process, it puts every family's rights at risk.
Fourth Amendment The Fourth Amendment protects people in their homes from unreasonable searches and seizures.
A judicial warrant is not paperwork; it is the safeguard that keeps government power accountable.
Due Process Lawsuits and reporting allege warrantless arrests and suspicionless stops, including cases involving U.S. citizens.
Every agency should be able to explain its authority and follow due process.
