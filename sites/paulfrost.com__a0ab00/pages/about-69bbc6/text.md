Paul Frost 7th Worcester District Rep.
Paul K.
Frost has proudly served the 7th Worcester District in the Massachusetts House of Representatives since January 1997, never losing sight of his commitment to the communities he serves.
Throughout his tenure, Rep.
Frost has maintained an impressive 100% Voting Attendance Record — a testament to his dedication to being present and voting on every issue that matters to his constituents.
Rep.
Frost has worked on both sides of the aisle to support the district and constituents, while never being afraid to stand up against legislation that would hurt the economy and drive away jobs.
He is a strong advocate for small businesses, lower taxes, and creating a business-friendly environment in Massachusetts.
A vocal supporter of law enforcement, Rep.
Frost stands firmly with the men and women in Blue who protect our communities every day.
He continues to fight for increased funding and support for public safety personnel across the district.
Rep.
Frost is equally committed to supporting Senior Citizens and Veterans, ensuring they receive the services and recognition they deserve.
He has consistently fought for state local aid to fund schools, roads, local services, and economic development in Auburn, Charlton, Leicester, Millbury, and Oxford.
Contact Information State House, Room 124 Boston, Massachusetts 02133 Phone: (617) 722-2100 Paul.Frost@mahouse.gov 7th Worcester District The 7th Worcester District consists of the town of Auburn, Precinct 4 of the town of Charlton, Precinct 3 of the town of Leicester, the town of Millbury, and Precincts 2, 3, and 4A of the town of Oxford — all in Worcester County, Massachusetts.
