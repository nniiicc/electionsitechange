By Dean Hesse, Decaturish
DeKalb County, GA — The DeKalb County Branch of the NAACP hosted their annual parade honoring Dr.
Martin Luther King, Jr. on Monday, Jan. 15.
The parade included performances from high school marching bands and appearances from elected officials and DeKalb-based chapters of fraternal and sororal organizations.
