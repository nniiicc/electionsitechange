Issues
Public Safety
- Fully fund local law enforcement and oppose “defund the police” efforts.
- Restore accountability in the justice system and end early-release policies for violent offenders.
- Prioritize victims’ rights and safer communities.
Affordability and Cost of Living
- Reduce the high cost of housing, utilities, and everyday living expenses.
- Support policies that make it easier for families to stay in California.
- Encourage business growth and job creation by cutting excessive regulations.
Local Control and Community Priorities
- Give local communities more say over housing, zoning, and safety policies.
- Push back against state mandates that overlook the needs of Contra Costa residents.
- Promote practical, community-based decision-making.
Responsibility
- Cut wasteful government spending and balance the state budget.
- Oppose tax hikes that hurt working families and small businesses.
- Ensure taxpayer money is used efficiently and transparently.
Education and Parental Rights
- Increase parental involvement and transparency in education.
- Focus schools on core academics — reading, math, science, and civics.
- Oppose politically divisive or ideological content in classrooms.
Government Reform
- Streamline state agencies and reduce bureaucratic red tape.
- Make Sacramento more accountable and responsive to local needs.
- Promote honesty, transparency, and integrity in public office.
