Wearing a farmer’s hat with the stars and stripes on the underside does not make us a patriot.
Going to a ribbon cutting event and welcoming a new business into our city makes us a patriot.
Chanting USA shows patriotism however buying local products made in the USA is being a patriot.
Saying you love this country is only words and it makes us patriotic, not a patriot.
Learning a new language and speaking it with those from other countries who will eventually become Americans themselves is being a patriot.
Patriotism is showing you love this great nation, its values, freedoms and liberties; its culture and traditions brought here from other countries; the differences in each one of us who make up America even if we look differently, worship differently, love differently, and vote differently.
The difference of showing and being come down to one thing – action.
Flying an American flag in our front yard displays patriotism, yet it does not mean we are a patriot.
Exercising our rights is being a patriot.
Standing up for other’s rights, running for office, speaking out against injustice, writing letters to our State Representative, working at the polls are all actions and therefore make us patriots.
It is the doing that makes us a patriot, not the showing.
Active engagement in making our nation a better place to live is being a patriot.
Showing and being are two different things.
Showing up and speaking at your local town council meeting is an act of patriotism which by merely doing makes us a patriot.
Today in our country of indifferent voters, the highest form of being a patriot is serving – through military, police/fire, and political representation.
Yet as citizens, there is one action each and every one of us can do to make us all patriots.
When we do this one action, we take responsibility and ownership.
When we exercise this right, we are actively displaying that America is worth it.
This act is asked upon us every two years, sometimes one.
It begins by being a citizen, then registering, then researching and then getting out there and performing this right.
It is VOTING.
When we vote, we are saying this country is our country.
This land is our land.
We are saying I love this country.
When we vote, we are taking ownership of the direction of our country, state, county, city, town, community and even street.
When we vote, we make this county a better county.
In Australia, after they vote, it is tradition to buy a democracy sausage in celebration of participating.
In America, we get an “I Voted!” sticker.
So, make our country proud and this November display “I Voted!” America is asking us to care about her, to show up for her, to give our time and energy to her.
If we stop voting, we will lose her.
So be a patriot and VOTE.
