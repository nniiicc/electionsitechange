Small farmer, performance artist, filmmaker, and children’s entertainer, Banks believes civility is the New Path Ahead.
“The current political vitriol of pitting ‘us’ against ‘them’ is unsustainable and obstructing our prosperity as a civilization.
We need to start talking to each other and above all listening.” As a performance artist, Banks brings people together of different beliefs, moderates a discussion on topics of our times and when it becomes tense, he turns on music, and everyone dances.
