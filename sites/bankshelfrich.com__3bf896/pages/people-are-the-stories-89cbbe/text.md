It’s about the stories
On the campaign trail this week, I met Treadwell CDB hemp farmers; citizens with erosion issues; a tax economist; a former political candidate; produce farmers from Sunsational Farms; numerous campaign strategists; friends who politically challenge me and people I asked to watch my computer as I go check if a charging space is available for my electric car which when I got back turned into a conversation on the impact of growth in Lake County.
As a candidate, I feel I have a legitimate purpose to meet people which parallels with my human nature of connecting with others.
So, why meet people?
Why reach out to them?
Why begin a conversation with a stranger?
Why spend time listening to their concerns?
I think simply – because people are the stories.
Banks Helfrich
Candidate for Florida House,
District 25
As a native Floridian, I love this state.
As a resident of South Lake County, I love farming and teaching sustainability to this community.
As a Candidate for State House, I love finding solutions to the issues of our time.
