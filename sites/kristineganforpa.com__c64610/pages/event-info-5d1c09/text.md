From the Classroom to the Capitol:
An Evening with Kristin Egan
Kristin Egan has dedicated her life to serving Bucks County as a mother and advocate for working families.
Throughout her career, she has served as a teacher, school board director, and union leader, bringing her experience to the fight for our community.
Kristin knows the challenges facing our community daily.
In Harrisburg, she will fight for strong public schools, lower costs for families, safe communities, and an accountable government.
Join us as we help send a proven leader to the State House.
