For more than 20 years, I’ve worked as a public school English teacher helping students build confidence in their reading and writing, supporting families through challenges, and seeing firsthand where our system works well and where it needs improvement.
At home, my husband Jim and I are raising three daughters; like many families in our community, our days are filled with early mornings, practices, school events, and the ongoing balance between work and family life.
My husband Jim owns an HVAC company that serves local families and businesses, and is affiliated with the Sheet Metal Workers Union where employees earn fair wages and have access to quality healthcare.
As a union leader and former chief negotiator in my school district, I have advocated for students, staff, and the community to ensure students have access to academic success while remaining mindful of the responsibility to taxpayers.
I am also a director of the Neshaminy School Board and serve on the Business Operations and Policy Committees, where I collaborate with fellow directors, administrators, staff, and residents to protect, preserve, and strengthen our district while helping move it forward.
