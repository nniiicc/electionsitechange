The greatest asset of this campaign is that multi-party democracy is simply a good idea. ​ The best way to help is to inform voters that we can choose it anytime.
We can persuade people that it's possible to fix politics.
Here are some ways to do that: Join the weekly campaign meeting Wear a campaign button Display a campaign yard sign Follow the campaign Instagram Volunteer to distribute leaflets Attend a campaign event Host a campaign event Donate money to the campaign It is often said that fundraising is the lifeblood of political campaigns.
While I can't dispute the power of purchased advertising, this campaign has one unassailable advantage over traditional campaigns in the two-party system: We are persuading people to join our cause.
Most campaigns focus on turnout: convincing people who already agree with them to choose to vote.
They have abandoned the vanishingly small persuadable middle, as their propaganda machines have scorched the middle ground.
By contrast, this campaign can reach anyone.
You may have a grandmother who would never vote Democratic, or a brother who would never vote Republican.
But, the dysfunction of our present system is so clear that individuals of any political stripe can be convinced to vote for a reform-centered independent.
So, if you'd like to support the campaign with a financial donation, I would be grateful.
But, please consider talking to the people you know about this effort first.
Ultimately, we cannot out-fundraise the incumbent.
But, we are uniquely positioned to out-persuade.
