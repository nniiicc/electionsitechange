Public goods/Research ownership While these are not easy problems, they have the advantage of broad agreement on what improvement would look like: less federal debt, more clean and cheap energy, less corruption, less crime, better and cheaper healthcare.
James Madison's system of checks, balances, and collaborative policy-making is well designed tackle issues like this.
The problem is that we're stuck in the mud of partisan bickering and ideological purity, which means we're failing to do anything.
Multiparty democracy is our best chance to cut through the dysfunction and make progress on these problems.
Not every solution we try will work, but we'll be able to have good-faith discussion on what is working and what needs a different approach.
