Who I am.
I'm a listener and a worker.
I grew up in rural Idaho in a conservative, faith-centered home.
I served a two-year mission in Spain, and for years I was a proud Republican.
Those roots taught me hard work, personal responsibility, and service to others.
I came to Utah for a better opportunity, and this community gave me that.
Now I want to give back as your neighbor.
If you're stressed, if you're tired, if you're spread thin, if you feel ignored — me too!
