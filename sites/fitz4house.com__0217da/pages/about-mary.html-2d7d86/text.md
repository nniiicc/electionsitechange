More News Re-Elected Chair of the Lawrence County Republican Central Committee 2023-2024 ​​ ​ ​I grew-up on a farm and developed a positive work ethic early, so I truly understand the people of South Dakota.
I’m a college graduate (USD & BHSU) with a BS in Accounting and Business Administration, this is beneficial as a member of the legislature and former member of Joint Appropriations Committee. ​I’ve learned how to work with others and develop relationships… both needed for success in Pierre.
I’m a good listener and my business experience has taught me how to prioritize issues.
I have zero ties to special interests, I have no political agenda, nor any financial interest to promote.
I’ve got the grit and determination to complete what I start.
I was an accountant for a Spearfish-based corporation with 30 retail locations in three different states.
I’m very familiar with what it takes to run a business -- much of it deals with local, state, and federal government regulations, licensing, taxes, and payroll. ​ I assist my husband with a family business that provides affordable housing for families in the Northern Black Hills and we also have a few calves and even some buffalo.
