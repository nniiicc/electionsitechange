They are the beliefs of this community — beliefs I share because I grew up here, worship here, and raise my family here.
My Christian faith and the values of Greenwood County are the foundation of everything I fight for in Columbia.
I believe that every life is precious and that the right to life is the foundation of all other rights.
Since my first day in the State House, I have fought to protect the unborn and to make South Carolina a state that values every human life from conception to natural death.
I authored the South Carolina Fetal Heartbeat Protection from Abortion Act , which prohibits abortions after a fetal heartbeat can be detected.
This landmark legislation was signed into law by Governor McMaster and upheld by the South Carolina Supreme Court.
It is one of the strongest pro-life laws in the nation.
I also authored Dylan's Law , which requires that an ultrasound be performed and offered for viewing before an abortion can take place, ensuring that mothers have the fullest information available to them.
In addition, I have worked to defund Planned Parenthood in South Carolina and to redirect those taxpayer dollars to community health centers that provide comprehensive care without performing abortions.
I am a chief sponsor of the Fetal Personhood Act , which would recognize the unborn as persons under South Carolina law from the moment of fertilization, affording them full legal protections.
I am also a chief sponsor of the Human Life Protection Act of 2025 , which would strengthen South Carolina's protections for the unborn and close remaining loopholes in our existing laws.

I have led the fight in the State House to shield children from dangerous ideologies and irreversible medical procedures.
I am the chief sponsor of the Help Not Harm bill , which bans gender transition surgeries, hormone treatments, and puberty blockers for minors in South Carolina.
Children deserve to be protected from life-altering procedures they cannot fully consent to or understand.
I am also the chief sponsor of the Save Women's Sports Act , which ensures that biological males cannot compete in women's and girls' athletic competitions in South Carolina.
This legislation protects the hard-won opportunities for female athletes and ensures fairness in competition.
