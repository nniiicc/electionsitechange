South Carolina Contribution Limits
South Carolina law limits contributions to state legislative candidates to $1,000 per person, per election.
Couples may contribute up to $2,000 per election ($1,000 from each spouse).
The primary and general elections are considered separate, so the maximum combined giving is $2,000 per individual / $4,000 per couple across both elections.
Contributions to McCravy for House are not tax deductible.
