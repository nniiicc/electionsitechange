About John McCravy
SC State House of Representatives, District 13
Proudly Serving Greenwood & Laurens Counties
Rep.
John McCravy has represented House District 13 in the South Carolina State House since 2016, serving the communities of Greenwood and Laurens Counties.
A Greenwood native, John graduated from Greenwood High School, Clemson University, and the USC School of Law.
John is the managing attorney at McCravy, Newlon, Sturkie & Clardy and teaches Commercial Law at Lander University.
He is married to his wife Dana of 45 years, and they have two children — JR (Amy) and Kelley Robertson (Dillon) — and five grandchildren.
A lifelong conservative, John founded the SC Family Caucus and has championed pro-life legislation, religious liberty, Second Amendment rights, and government transparency throughout his tenure in the State House.

Background & Family
John McCravy is a Greenwood native who has called the Lakelands home his entire life.
He graduated from Greenwood High School, earned his undergraduate degree from Clemson University, and received his law degree from the University of South Carolina School of Law.
John is married to his wife Dana of 45 years.
Together they have two children, JR (married to Amy) and Kelley Robertson (married to Dillon), and five grandchildren.
Faith, family, and service to community have always been at the center of John's life.
Professional Career
John is the managing attorney at McCravy, Newlon, Sturkie & Clardy, a Greenwood law firm where he has practiced for decades.
He also serves as an adjunct professor at Lander University, where he teaches Commercial Law.
His legal career has given him a deep understanding of the laws that affect our businesses, families, and communities, and that knowledge has served him well in the State House.
Legislative Service
John has represented House District 13 in the South Carolina State House since 2016, now serving his fifth term.
He represents the communities of Greenwood and Laurens Counties.
When John arrived in Columbia, there was no organized voice for pro-family, pro-life values in the State House.
So he built one.
He founded the SC Family Caucus — a coalition of conservative legislators dedicated to protecting life, defending religious liberty, and promoting family values.
The caucus has become one of the most influential voting blocs in the legislature, and its work has produced landmark legislation including the Heartbeat Bill and the Help Not Harm Act.
John also serves as Chairman of the National Association of Christian Lawmakers for South Carolina, working with like-minded legislators across the country to advance faith-based public policy.
