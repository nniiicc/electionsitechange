An Advocate for Public Schools, Environmental Conservation, and Working Families.
Mike Zimmer is a proven leader who stands with Iowa’s working families, fighting for stronger public schools, better wages, and opportunities that build real financial stability.
He knows the backbone of Iowa’s future is a thriving middle class—not just getting by, but truly getting ahead.
With decades of experience in leadership and innovation, Mike is ready to bring bold, people-first solutions to the State Senate, ensuring Eastern Iowa’s communities have the tools to thrive in today’s economy.
Help Working Iowans Get Ahead
- Raising the minimum wage
- Supporting paid family leave for Iowa’s working families
- Reinstate collective bargaining
- Restore unemployment benefits for workers
Support Our Public Schools and Teachers
- Ensure public dollars go to public schools
- Fund public schools at a sustainable rate
- Vote against unfunded mandates
- Expand Work-based learning programs
Help Lower the Cost of Living
- Make childcare affordable and accessible by expanding the Employer Child Care Tax, increasing the Child Tax Credit limit, and raise the Child Care Assistance income eligibility threshold
- Create affordable housing investments through private, government, and community grants and tax credits
- Expand post-secondary educational opportunities
Environmental Conservation
- Vote to fund Iowa's 2010 Water and Land Legacy amendment (IWiLL)
- Fund Iowa Water Quality Information System (IWQIS) real-time monitoring system
- Vote to fund the Iowa DNR AQulA database, which offers historical, physical, and chemical data points gathered from rivers, lakes, wetlands, and groundwater
