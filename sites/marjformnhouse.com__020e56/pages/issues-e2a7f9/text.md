SCHOOL CHOICE Parents should feel confident in the education their taxes pay for.

I support Constitutional Carry, Castle Doctrine, Stand your ground laws.
HEALTH FREEDOM Health freedom is not a right bestowed by government.
Minnesotans must make their own decisions about their healthcare without state interference.
I will fight for your health freedom, and against abusive mandates, and Emergency Powers.
LAW & ORDER We must support those who protect and serve with honor and courage.

I support voter ID, removing unmanned ballot drop boxes that allow for ballot harvesting, and keeping elections under the constitutional authority of state legislatures.
