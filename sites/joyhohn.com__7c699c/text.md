Hello!
I’m Joy Hohn , State Senator for District 9, proudly representing residents of northwestern Sioux Falls, Hartford, and Wall Lake.
It has been a true honor to serve District 9 and to meet so many of you across our communities—an experience that has deepened my understanding of both the challenges and opportunities facing South Dakota.
People are the priority driving my service and are central to my values as a conservative Republican rooted in faith, family, and freedom.
The people of District 9 deserve an open ear, a transparent process, and a strong voice in Pierre.
As a mom, wife, sister, neighbor, family farmer, and pilot, I am driven by a servant’s heart and a deep sense of responsibility.
After much prayer and reflection, I am seeking re-election because there is still important work ahead—experience, integrity, and faith-based leadership matter now more than ever.
During my first term, I took that responsibility seriously, knowing the votes I cast affect today and shape our future.
Alongside a strong grassroots movement, we delivered real results: Protected property rights by stopping eminent domain abuse for CO₂ pipelines Defended taxpayers by opposing corporate tax giveaways Stood for family values , earning recognition from Family Voice Action I also sponsored bipartisan SB 115 , closing a loophole in South Dakota’s sexual-contact laws.
The bill passed unanimously in both chambers—because protecting people should never be political.
As we look ahead, property tax reform, prison policy, public safety, and preservation of our energy and natural resources will be front and center.
I’ve worked across party lines, asked hard questions, and held leadership accountable—because real leadership means listening, learning, and serving the people.
My goal has always been to represent the voices and values of our district while keeping the broader good of our state in view.
I’m once again relying on the trust, prayers, and support of those who believe in servant leadership, accountability, and doing what’s right—even when it’s hard.
I am asking for your continued trust to keep advocating for our families, protecting our freedoms, and securing our future as a voice for the people.
