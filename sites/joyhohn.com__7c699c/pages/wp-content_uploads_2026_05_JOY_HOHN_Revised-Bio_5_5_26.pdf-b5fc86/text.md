Hohn State Senator – District 9 Senator Joy Hohn represents South Dakota’s District 9 in the State Legislature, where she serves on the Commerce & Energy, Military & Veterans’ Affairs, and Transportation Committees, and is Vice Chair of the Taxation Committee.
Hohn, a pilot, small business owner, and family farmer, was first elected in 2024 as a Conservative Republican.
She won the primary with 74% of the vote—the largest margin among legislative races—and was uncontested in the general election, reflecting strong grassroots support and her advocacy for property rights and limited government.
During her first term, Hohn has focused on protecting constitutional rights, defending taxpayers, promoting fiscal responsibility, supporting veterans and atrisk youth, upholding family values, and preserving South Dakota’s natural resources.
She sponsored HB 1052, prohibiting the use of eminent domain for carbon dioxide pipelines to protect private property rights, and helped defeat SB 177, which proposed sales tax rebates for data centers, in defense of taxpayers.
She has also been recognized by Family Voice Action and Protecting South Dakota Kids for her support of family-focused legislation.
Following the 2025 legislative session, Hohn was appointed by Governor Larry Rhoden to serve on the Project Prison Reset Task Force, where she was one of seven senators charged with evaluating and recommending plans for a new state correctional facility.
In that role, she became an active voice for transparency, advocating for bipartisan, well-informed, community-based solutions.
She was also appointed to the Correctional Rehabilitation Task Force, focusing on early intervention, reducing recidivism, and expanding rehabilitation programs.
A lifelong resident of Minnehaha County and graduate of West Central High School, Hohn grew up on a family farm near Hartford, where her family remains today.
Inspired by her father, a World War II veteran who taught her to fly, she became South Dakota’s first female airline pilot, logging nearly 13,000 hours of accident- and incident-free flight time over a 40-year career as a Captain and First Officer.
Today, she works as a charter pilot, check airman, and instructor for Legacy Aviation and operates her private contract service, Adele Aire, LLC.
She earned her B.A. in Aviation and Business Administration from Augustana College and enjoys spending time with family, gardening, traveling, and hunting.
Hohn’s entry into public life began with her advocacy for constitutional rights and her opposition to the use of eminent domain for CO₂ pipelines, standing alongside South Dakota landowners to defend private property.
That experience deepened her connection to the people of Minnehaha County and led to her active involvement in local and state government, working with citizens, municipalities, county commissioners, legislators, and others across the state to preserve constitutional rights.
