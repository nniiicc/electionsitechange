Voting Process and Requirements
Find answers to common questions about voting in Idaho District 27.
What identification is required to vote?
Voters must present a valid Idaho driver's license, state ID card, or other approved photo ID at the polls.
Where can I find my polling place?
You can locate your polling place by visiting VoteIdaho using the polling place locator tool.
Can I vote by mail?
Yes, absentee ballots are available.
Request one through the Idaho Secretary of State's website before the deadline.
What are the voting hours on Election Day?
Polling stations are open from 8 AM to 8 PM on Election Day.
How do I update my voter registration?
You can update your registration online or at your local election office until the registration deadline.
What should I do if I encounter problems at the polls?
Report any issues to the poll workers immediately or contact the local election office for assistance.
