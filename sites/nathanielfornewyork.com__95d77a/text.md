ABOUT ​ Nathaniel Hezekiah III ​Born and raised in South Jamaica, Nathaniel has spent more than twenty years serving Southeast Queens.
From working with Amalgamated Transit Union Local 1056 to becoming Deputy Chief of Staff to Congressman Gregory Meeks, he has built a career helping families navigate government and delivering results when this community needed it most. ​ ​ Now, he’s running for State Assembly to continue serving the community that helped raised him.
This community helped raise Nathaniel when his family needed it most.
He has spent his life returning that favor — by showing up when it mattered.
