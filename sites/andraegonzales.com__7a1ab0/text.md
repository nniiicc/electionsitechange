As our Assemblymember, Andrae will bring people together and work to:
Improve Local Schools
and expand access to early education.
Stop The Rising Costs
of gas, groceries, prescription drugs, and other necessities.
Expand Healthcare
and supports efforts to bring a Medical School to Kern County.
Prioritize Public Safety
and ensure fast police, fire, and ambulance response times.
Ensure Clean Water
in every community through investments in wells and storage.
Help Working Parents
by lowering childcare costs and expanding family leave.
Fix Our Roads
by delivering Kern County’s fair share of funding for infrastructure.
Build New Housing
and expand programs to help first-time homebuyers.
Help Businesses
create jobs that pay good wages and offer benefits for workers.
