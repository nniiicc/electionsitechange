I know firsthand what makes Kern County such a special place to live.
We’re home to farmers who put food on our tables, to artists who inspire us, and to the small businesses, educators, and community leaders who help build our future.
That’s why I’m incredibly excited to share that we are launching the first ever Made in Kern County: Concert Series!

Please join us, bring your friends and family, and celebrate Kern County!
