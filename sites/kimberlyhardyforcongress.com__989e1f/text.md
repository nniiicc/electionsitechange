Meet Kimberly
Kimberly Hardy is a mom, social worker, professor, and candidate for Congress in North Carolina’s 7th District.
Raised by a Marine and federal agent, service has shaped Kimberly’s life.
As a social worker, Kimberly has spent her career building bridges to help people obtain the tools and opportunities they need to be successful.
As your representative in Congress, Kimberly will continue the work of helping people by advocating for the things that make our lives better: better jobs, better healthcare, better schools.
