Go to our Volunteer Form Why Your Time Matters Winning this race won’t happen because of big money or political insiders.
It will happen because ordinary people step up and say, “I can help.” Volunteers help us: Reach voters who never hear from campaigns Share the message that leadership should be local and community-rooted Build real momentum, one conversation at a time Your time is just as valuable as any donation.
Ways You Can Get Involved You don’t need political experience — just a willingness to show up.
Door Knocking & Literature Drops Help us share information with voters in neighborhoods across the district.
Phone Banking & Texting Reach voters from home with simple, friendly outreach.
Yard Signs & Visibility Deliver signs, help with placement, and boost name recognition.
Events & Meet-and-Greets Help set up, greet guests, or host a small gathering in your community.
Data, Tech & Behind-the-Scenes Support Perfect for detail-oriented folks who like organizing, mapping, or admin work.
Community Connectors Introduce us to your networks — faith groups, service clubs, schools, and local organizations.
Topic Experts Help develop a deeper understanding of policy topics and positions What to Expect as a Volunteer We will: Respect your time and availability Provide training and clear instructions Never pressure you to do something you’re uncomfortable with Keep you informed without overwhelming your inbox You choose how involved you want to be.
Host a Community Conversation One of the most powerful ways to help is to invite friends, neighbors, or colleagues to a small gathering in your home, at your business, or at a local café.
We’ll help with: Planning Invitations Materials And making it easy and welcoming Let’s Build This Together Big change doesn’t come from one person.
It comes from a community choosing to act together.
