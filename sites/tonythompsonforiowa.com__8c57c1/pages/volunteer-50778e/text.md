This campaign is powered by neighbors helping neighbors.
Whether you have:
- 30 minutes a week
- A few hours a month
- Or you want to dive in and lead
There is a place for you here.
Every conversation, every sign, and every helping hand makes a difference.
Winning this race won’t happen because of big money or political insiders.
It will happen because ordinary people step up and say, “I can help.”
Volunteers help us:
- Reach voters who never hear from campaigns
- Share the message that leadership should be local and community-rooted
- Build real momentum, one conversation at a time
Your time is just as valuable as any donation.
You don’t need political experience — just a willingness to show up.
- Door Knocking & Literature Drops
Help us share information with voters in neighborhoods across the district.
- Phone Banking & Texting
Reach voters from home with simple, friendly outreach.
- Yard Signs & Visibility
Deliver signs, help with placement, and boost name recognition.
- Events & Meet-and-Greets
Help set up, greet guests, or host a small gathering in your community.
- Data, Tech & Behind-the-Scenes Support
Perfect for detail-oriented folks who like organizing, mapping, or admin work.
- Community Connectors
Introduce us to your networks — faith groups, service clubs, schools, and local organizations.
- Topic Experts
Help develop a deeper understanding of policy topics and positions
We will:
- Respect your time and availability
- Provide training and clear instructions
- Never pressure you to do something you’re uncomfortable with
- Keep you informed without overwhelming your inbox
You choose how involved you want to be.
One of the most powerful ways to help is to invite friends, neighbors, or colleagues to a small gathering in your home, at your business, or at a local café.
We’ll help with:
- Planning
- Invitations
- Materials
- And making it easy and welcoming
