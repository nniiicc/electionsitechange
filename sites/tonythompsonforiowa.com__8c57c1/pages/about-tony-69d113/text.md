He's running for the Iowa State Senate because he believes deeply in the power of strong families and strong communities to build a strong future.
This district isn’t just where Tony campaigns.
It’s where his family lives.
It’s where he works.
It’s where their story is rooted.
Tony graduated from North Polk High School, and then from Cornell College in Mt Vernon, IA with a degree in computer science.
When technology jobs were hard to come by in 2001 due to the dot com bubble, he went to volunteer with Habitat for Humanity in Americus, GA.
He stayed on with Habitat for Humanity for four years doing grant management, organizational capacity building, and leadership development, and also leading building teams to places around the globe.
Then he came back to Iowa in 2005 to be Des Moines Habitat for Humanity's first full time ReStore Manager and also joined the board of the Iowa Environmental Council.
From his work with Habitat, Tony caught the travel bug and also realized he would benefit from an advanced degree.
He began looking for MBA programs, and was invited to participate in a master's program Strategic Leadership toward Sustainability at Blekinge Institute of Technology in Karlskrona, Sweden.
Upon completion of that degree program, Tony was invited to pursue a Ph.D. program in the School of Engineering, completing that degree in 2012 while also helping to establish a new program in Sustainable Product Innovation.
After several years of researching, teaching, and travel, Tony was ready to return home and settle down near family.
In 2013 he began growing vegetables through his New Family Farm, operating as a Community Supported Agriculture (CSA).
He joined the boards of the Iowa Food Coop and the Iowa Farmers Union.
Tony and Amanda were married in 2015.
Shortly thereafter, Tony went back to his computer science roots and worked at Marsh McLennan as a data analyst, then Growers Edge as a data scientist.
Along the way Tony, along with family, bought Prudent Produce - a local produce delivery business - and transformed it into one of Iowa's leading food hubs , helping to connect Iowa's smaller farms and producers with consumers who want their products.
Roots & Family Amanda and Tony are raising their family right here in the heart of Senate District 23.
Like many families in the district, their lives are a mix of work, school events, community commitments, and doing their best to juggle the rising costs of everyday life.
They chose to build their lives here because they believe in: The strength of small towns The value of hard work The importance of neighbors looking out for one another Work, the Farm, & Community Tony grows food here.
Tony works here.
Tony builds community here.
His professional life has taken him through agriculture, education, finance, non-profits, and technology — always with a focus on how policy decisions ripple through real families, farms, and small businesses.
Tony has worked alongside: Manufacturers looking for ways to innovate for sustainability Farmers trying to stay profitable in a volatile economy Small business owners navigating rising costs and changing customers Nonprofits working to strengthen food systems, housing, and conservation Tony has seen firsthand what right-sized, thoughtful policy can unlock — and what careless policy can quietly take away.
Why Tony's Running Tony is running because too many families are doing everything right — working hard, caring for their neighbors, contributing to their communities — and still feeling like they’re falling behind.
We see it in: Property taxes rising faster than incomes The cost of healthcare and childcare Infrastructure that can’t keep up with growing communities Schools being asked to do more with less Too often, decisions at the Capitol feel disconnected from daily life here at home.
Tony believes our district deserves leadership that is rooted in local experience, not political calculation.
Tony is running to be a steady, practical, community-grounded voice for the people who actually live here.
How Tony Leads Tony believes leadership starts by listening — not talking.
As your State Senator, Tony will: Put working families before equity investors Focus on long-term solutions, not short-term politics Work across party lines when it benefits Iowa families Stand up when policies shift costs onto those least able to bear them Stay accessible and accountable to ALL the people he represents Tony doesn’t believe in governing from a distance.
He believes in showing up, hearing concerns directly, and doing the hard work of finding common ground.
A Little More About Tony When Tony is not working or out in the community, you’ll probably find him: Tinkering around the farm Grabbing coffee with neighbors Cheering at a high school sporting event or attending a community event Reading a book Or spending time with my family Tony believes the best leadership is grounded in everyday life — not in the political bubble.
