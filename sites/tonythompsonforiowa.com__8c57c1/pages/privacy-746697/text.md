This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when visiting tonythompsonforiowa.com or interacting with the campaign.
Information We Collect We collect information that supporters voluntarily provide when they: Sign up for campaign updates Register for events Volunteer with the campaign Make a donation Contact the campaign This information may include: Name Email address Phone number Mailing address or ZIP code Volunteer interests Other information submitted through campaign forms When you make a donation, payment information is processed by a third-party payment processor and is not stored on this website.
How We Use Information We use supporter information to: Send campaign updates and newsletters Coordinate volunteers Invite supporters to events Process and acknowledge donations Communicate about campaign activities Improve our website and outreach We aim to keep communications relevant and respectful.
We do not share mobile contact information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text Messages If you provide your phone number, you may receive periodic text messages from Tony Thompson for Iowa regarding campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, or events.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text message.
Third-Party Services The campaign uses trusted third-party tools to operate our website and campaign activities.
These may include: Action Network for email communications and event registration ActBlue for processing online donations Text messaging platforms used for supporter outreach These services maintain their own privacy and security policies.
Data Sharing The campaign does not sell or rent supporter information.
Information may be shared with authorized campaign staff, volunteers, and vendors solely for purposes related to campaign operations.
Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect supporter information and limit access to those involved in campaign operations.
However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices You may unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in messages.
If you receive text messages from the campaign, you may opt out by replying STOP.
