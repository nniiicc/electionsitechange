Priorities
The Platform
Seven priorities for a Michigan that works for its families, its taxpayers, and its law-abiding citizens.
- Reduce the Bureaucracy
- Reduce government spending to create a smaller, more efficient State Government
- Defend family values
- Protect Parental Rights
- Keep men out of women's sports, locker rooms, and restrooms
- Get the State Government's hands out of the taxpayers' pockets
- Smaller government equals smaller budget
- Create a more vibrant economy through deregulation and reducing commercial taxes on business entities
- Get rid of harmful "Restorative Justice" measures
- Abolish 911 Good Samaritan Law — Use/Possession Immunity (MCL 333.7403 and 333.7404)
- "Mercy to the guilty is cruelty to the innocent"
- Mandatory Prison Sentences for individuals convicted of Manufacturing/Delivery of Controlled Substances
- Get rid of Felony Sentencing Guidelines that provide cover for activist judges
- Create statutory factors that judges must consider and weigh on the record to show how they came up with the sentence/punishment for the convicted felon.
The factors would be:
- I.
Character of the Defendant
- II.
Facts & Circumstances surrounding the case
- III.
Deterrence — both general and specific
- Firmly against Red Flag Laws
- Advocate for Constitutional Carry
- Protect our communities, infrastructure, and resources
- Put a moratorium on data centers now
