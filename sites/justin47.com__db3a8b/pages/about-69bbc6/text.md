About Justin
Meet Justin Griffis
Attorney.
Former prosecutor.
Husband.
Common-sense Republican.
Justin Griffis and his wife are residents of Dexter, Michigan.
Justin played college football for the University of Dayton (Ohio) as an offensive lineman for 5 seasons.
He graduated from the University of Dayton with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science in 2011.
Justin continued his education at the University of Dayton School of Law and graduated with a Juris Doctor degree in 2015.
He spent the next 10 years practicing criminal law.
Justin started his law career as a criminal defense attorney in Ohio.
He became a partner at a law firm in his hometown of Sidney, Ohio.
In 2019, Justin moved to Wisconsin after his girlfriend (now wife) accepted a position as an Assistant Women's Basketball Coach at Marquette University in Milwaukee.
He continued to practice law as a criminal defense attorney in Wisconsin.
Justin moved to Dexter after his wife accepted a position as an Assistant Women's Basketball Coach at the University of Michigan in 2024.
After obtaining reciprocity, Justin began working as an Assistant Prosecutor for Jackson County, Michigan in January 2025.
He resigned from his position at the Prosecutor's Office in August 2025 due to frustration with Michigan's Criminal Justice System.
He felt it was too lenient on convicted criminals — which says a lot given that he spent the vast majority of his legal career defending accused individuals and convicted criminals as a criminal defense attorney.
I have had the privilege to practice law for 10 years.
The Criminal Justice Systems in Ohio and Wisconsin made sense to me.
The guilty were punished and their punishment fit their crime.
That is not the case here in Michigan.
"Restorative Justice" measures have made Michigan less safe.
Skewed felony sentencing guidelines have resulted in convicted felons receiving slaps on their wrists and have allowed activist judges to fly under the radar from the public.
I believe that "mercy to the guilty is cruelty to the innocent." I resigned from the Prosecutor's Office after 6 months because it felt like the job was pointless.
It was basically impossible to see actual justice be done in Michigan's Criminal "Justice" System.
On August 26, 2025, I was scrolling through X (formerly Twitter), and saw that a video made by my State Representative — Carrie Rheingans — was being shared by various X users due to its absurdity.
In the video, Carrie Rheingans was urging trans minors to contact her office directly so she could help them get puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, and what she called "safe and effective care." I was shocked that she was telling minors — not their parents — to contact her office.
She was completely circumventing parents and ignoring parental rights.
I thought to myself "someone needs to run against her." I did more research on Carrie Rheingans and found that she had voted to give illegal immigrants drivers licenses and to allow boys in girls' sports.
Upon learning that, I decided that I would run against her because if you believe that boys can become girls, and vice versa, you do not operate within objective reality and should not be deciding how our tax dollars are spent.
As an attorney, I am a problem solver.
When elected, I would work with other legislators to provide practical solutions to the problems affecting the district and Michigan as a whole.
One of the first problems I would tackle is the Criminal Justice System.
I will spearhead legislation to: (1) replace the Felony Sentencing Guidelines with statutory factors that judges must consider on the record when determining a defendant's sentence; (2) require mandatory prison sentences for individuals convicted of Manufacturing/Delivery of Controlled Substances; and (3) abolish the 911 Good Samaritan Fatal Overdose Prevention Law, which provides drug addicts with immunity from criminal charges.
I am a common-sense Republican.
I believe in less government, lower taxes, family values, and personal responsibility.
As a former offensive lineman, I spent my athletic career in the trenches doing a thankless job and enjoyed every minute of it.
That is the type of attitude that I would bring to Lansing.
I would roll up my sleeves, get in the trenches, and do the work that needs to be done to make the 47th District and the State of Michigan a better place to live.
It is time that we get rid of Carrie Rheingans and her far-left nonsense and get back to common sense.
