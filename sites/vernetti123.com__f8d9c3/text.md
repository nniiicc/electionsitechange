Jeff Vernetti, State Representative 123rd District
My Vision for a Stronger 123rd District
Eliminate the Personal Property Tax and Limit State Spending
Jeff Vernetti believes in fiscal responsibility and reigning in spending.
He is against citizens continuing to be taxed on personal property they already own.
Investing in Infrastructure
Jeff Vernetti believes that too often our tax dollars make their way to Jefferson City but they never make their way back.
He will ensure our community is getting our fair share of investments from the state for quality roads, bridges, and infrastructure.
Supporting Tourism
Jeff Vernetti knows that tourism is a major driver of the local economy and will work with the community to ensure smart, proactive investments that support business owners and residents.
Protect and Promote Agriculture in the 123rd District
Jeff Vernetti will work to ban foreign ownership of land, to prevent America’s enemies, like China, from buying our farmland.
He knows protecting domestic production of food must be a top priority, especially the rights of ranchers who are under siege from “fake meat” producers and land owners against unjust eminent domain issues.
Supporting our farmers will be one of his top priorities.
Lake Safety
Jeff Vernetti is for common-sense initiatives to increase safety on Lake of the Ozarks.
He will work with organizations and law enforcement to promote safe boating awareness and education.
He will work to ensure fire districts and law enforcement have the tools they need to protect residents and visitors.
Defending Our Values
Jeff Vernetti will ensure that Missouri is the most pro-life state in the nation, while stopping abortion providers, like Planned Parenthood, from getting their hands on your tax dollars.
He is a proud Constitutional Conservative who will protect our Second Amendment rights, crack down on illegal immigration and ensure only legal Missouri citizens can vote.
