Jeff completed a candidate values survey aligning with the Republican Party Platform and the United States Constitution.
The Republican Vetting Committee also reviewed his social media content, performed a case net search for criminal records, and a search with the Missouri Ethics Commission for any severe ethics violations.
Jeff passed all phases of this voluntary vetting session.
Jeff is a candidate for election on the Republican Party Ballot for State Representative District 123.
We ask all Republican voters to consider this information when deciding who to elect to represent our party in the August 2024 primary.
Jeff Vernetti has earned our stamp of approval.
