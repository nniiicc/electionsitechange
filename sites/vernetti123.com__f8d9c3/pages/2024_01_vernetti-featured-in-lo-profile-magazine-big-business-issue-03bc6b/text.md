In the January/February LO Profile Magazine, Candidate for the 123rd House District Jeff Vernetti was featured in a two-page “Big Business” feature story.
The publication, which is distributed all over the region and sold at stores across the state, selected Vernetti as its “Behind the Desk” feature and highlighted both his personal and professional accomplishments.
“It’s a real honor to be featured in LO Profile magazine,” said Vernetti.
“I’m very proud of the work that my fine staff of employees and I have done so far and I’m excited about all the things that are in store for us in the future.”
The feature discussed not only Vernetti’s vast communications and business background but also the efforts of all the entities he is involved with in the 123rd District.
Ballparks National has been a huge economic success for the District, bringing millions of dollars annually to the area.
The piece also discusses the success they have had at LOZ Sports Training and Vernetti’s Italian Grocer, both of which are overseen by Vernetti.
You can read the article by grabbing a copy of the January/February issue of LO Profile at participating locations or purchasing the recent issue online at loprofile.com.
