FAQs
How do I volunteer?
You can volunteer by door knocking or phone banking in your area.
You can also volunteer as a parade walker or event help.
Can I host events?
Yes!
Hosting neighborhood conversations is a great way to get involved!
It can be a simple one-hour long conversation, a fundraiser, or a meet and greet.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit cards and PayPal for financial donations.
You can also send a check to: Bjorn for Minnesota PO Box 147 Halstad, MN 56548.
Are there age restrictions?
Volunteers must be at least 16 years old to participate--but all ages are more than welcome for parades!
How do I contact support?
Reach out to us at Hello@BjornforMinnesota.com directly.
