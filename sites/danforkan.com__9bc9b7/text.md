My wife and I moved to our house almost nine years ago and could not be happier.
I’m the President of my HOA.
Our two girls attend Blue Valley’s public schools.
My wife and I both work in Overland Park.
Public service has always been a part of my life.
Since graduating from the University of Kansas’s School of Law, I’ve dedicated my career to helping the community.
I run a small business, working with developers and ensuring adequate representation for women and minority subcontractors and workers on construction project across the metro.
I was also elected twice to the Hickman Mills school board, serving for 6 years.
In my time in the legislature, I have: Fully funded our public schools and special education Cut taxes by billions including Reducing your income tax Reducing your property taxes Eliminating Social Security Taxes Almost completely removing any state grocery sales taxes Reformed civil asset forfeiture, so you have protections over your own property being unjustly taken Hosted dozens of town halls to listen to constituent concerns Sent out weekly legislative updates to keep you up to date with what’s going on in Topeka and to document every vote I’ve taken on the House floor.
I’m here to make our leaders in Kansas more accountable to voters, to invest in education for our future generations, and to restore responsible budgeting to Topeka.
