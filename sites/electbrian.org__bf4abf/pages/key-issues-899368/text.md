Champion for Public Education Brian is a steadfast supporter of public schools and believes that a strong education system is the cornerstone of a thriving community.
He will advocate for policies that ensure our schools are well-funded and equipped to provide every child with a quality education.
E.
Free and Fair Elections Brian is committed to ensuring that our elections remain free, fair, and accessible to all.
He will champion the use of secure electronic voting processes that are transparent and trustworthy, making it easier for everyone to participate in the democratic process.
R.
Respect and Inclusivity Brian believes in respecting all individuals, regardless of their political affiliation or position.
He is dedicated to fostering a community where every voice is heard, and every person is treated with dignity.
V.
Veterans and Senior Citizens Understanding the unique challenges faced by veterans and senior citizens, Brian is committed to advocating for services and programs that meet their needs.
He believes in honoring those who have served our country and ensuring that our seniors have the support they deserve.
E.
Equality for All Brian is dedicated to ensuring that every person, regardless of race, gender, age, or background, is treated with respect and given equal opportunities.
He believes in the power of inclusivity to strengthen the community and is committed to removing barriers that hinder individuals from reaching their full potential.
As a member of the Senate, Brian will continue to fight against discrimination and advocate for policies that promote fairness and equality for all.
