Endorsements Integrity Contact Donate Terms & Conditions Why Choose Brian for State House?
1.
Proven Leadership in Crisis Brian has over 40 years of experience in the fire service, including many years as a Fire Chief.
Throughout his career, he demonstrated exceptional leadership in times of crisis, whether managing natural disasters, emergencies, or other challenging situations.
Brian has consistently put the safety and well-being of the community first, providing calm and decisive leadership that is exactly what’s needed to navigate the challenges facing our county.
2.
Expertise in Government and Budgeting With 15 years of experience as an elected official across three different agencies in Washington State, Brian brings extensive expertise in government operations and budgeting.
He has successfully managed large budgets, ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently while maintaining essential services.
Brian has a proven track record of lowering taxes and supports the principle that those who use a service should pay for the service, helping to maintain a balanced and sustainable budget.
3.
Nonprofit Leadership and management Brian has 25 years of experience in nonprofit management, where he honed his skills in resource allocation, community engagement, and strategic planning.
He has successfully managed millions of dollars in grants, ensuring resources were used efficiently to achieve meaningful outcomes.
His work in the nonprofit sector taught him the importance of using resources wisely and building strong, inclusive communities, shaping his approach to leadership and making him an advocate for the people.
4.
Commitment to Transparency Brian believes in open and transparent government.
His track record as an elected official speaks volumes about his dedication to ensuring that the public is always informed and that decisions are made in the best interest of the community.
He will bring this same commitment to the State Senate.
5.
Compassion, Inclusion, and Community Engagement Brian is deeply compassionate and believes in the power of inclusion.
He respects all individuals, regardless of their background or political affiliation, and is committed to creating an environment where every voice is heard and valued.
Brian actively works with local businesses to foster economic growth and involves youth in the process of shaping the future, ensuring that the next generation is engaged and empowered to contribute to the community’s success.
6.
Results-Driven Brian is focused on achieving tangible results for the community.
Whether it’s improving public services, advocating for veterans and seniors, or ensuring fair and accessible elections, He is committed to delivering outcomes that make a real difference in people’s lives.
