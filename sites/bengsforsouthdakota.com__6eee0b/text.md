Brian Bengs has spent his life serving his country as a Navy enlistee, Air Force attorney, educator, and national park ranger.
Raised in a blue-collar family, he earned his college degree through the GI Bill and retired from the military as a lieutenant colonel.
His guiding principle is simple: tell the truth and take care of your neighbor.
Brian and his family made South Dakota home in 2016, first in Aberdeen and now in Hot Springs.
