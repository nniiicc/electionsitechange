Greg VanWoerkom
Greg VanWoerkom is currently serving his fourth term in the Michigan Legislature and is Chair of the House Appropriations Subcommittee on Medicaid and Behavioral Health.
He is also a member of the Appropriations Subcommittees on Labor and Economic Development, Licensing and Financial Services and General Government.
As your State Representative, Greg has worked on major projects in West Michigan including environmental cleanups to delist Muskegon Lake as an Area of Concern, securing funds for the cleanup of Grand Haven’s Harbor Island, and creating the Tri-Share program to help provide more affordable child care.
Greg formerly served on the board of the United Way of the Lakeshore and is Past-Board President of Western Michigan Christian High and Middle School.
Greg is raising his family in Norton Shores and is a member of the Muskegon Rotary Club and Covenant Life Church in Grand Haven.
He has a Bachelor’s Degree from Calvin College and a Masters’ Degree in Political Leadership from George Washington University.
Why I'm Running
My career has been about public service to improve the quality of life for the people of West Michigan.
I work everyday understanding and solving the problems facing West Michigan whether it is in economic development, reducing poverty, restoring our environment, improving education or helping a constituent who is having trouble with their government.
We all recognize that we are at a critical point in the next chapter for the state of Michigan.
In the age of term limits, we need to elect representatives that can have an immediate impact for our area.
I have lead public policy efforts at the highest level in Washington, D.C.
I have sat with employers who were on the verge of cutting jobs unless action was taken and delivered.
I know what being your representative takes and have a record of delivering results for our communities.
