PRINCIPLED, INDEPENDENT LEADERSHIP
“I will work with anyone when it helps Utah, challenge either party when it does not, and judge every proposal by whether it protects liberty, strengthens security, and produces real results.”
MEET BOBBY
Babak “Bobby” Darvish is an Iranian-American technology and cybersecurity professional with decades of experience across information technology, security, risk management, and responsible AI.
His career has included work in financial services, technology, and government-related environments where resilience, sound judgment, and accountability are essential.
Bobby came to the United States in 1974, at about one year old, and built his life in America.
His perspective is shaped by gratitude for this country, personal awareness of authoritarian ideology, his Christian faith, and a conviction that citizenship carries both rights and responsibilities.
He is running for Congress to defend constitutional liberty, strengthen national security, demand fiscal accountability, support Utah families and businesses, and ensure emerging technologies serve the public rather than undermine individual freedom.
His campaign emphasizes practical problem-solving, transparent leadership, and measurable outcomes.
THE MISSION
Confront hostile regimes, cyber threats, terrorism, espionage, and foreign influence with clear strategy and credible deterrence.
Protect critical infrastructure, strengthen public-private cyber defense, and establish responsible rules for powerful AI systems without crushing innovation.
Defend free speech, religious liberty, due process, privacy, lawful self-defense, and limits on government power.
Challenge wasteful spending, demand measurable outcomes, simplify government, and put taxpayers ahead of bureaucracy.
Support entrepreneurship, energy reliability, affordable living, safe communities, and policies that let families build their futures.
Stand with democratic allies, maintain military strength, protect American sovereignty, and negotiate from a position of strength.
Support an Iranian-led transition that removes the Islamic Republic from power and gives the Iranian people the right to choose a free, secular, representative government.
U.S. policy should stand firmly against the regime’s repression, terrorism, hostage-taking, nuclear threats, and regional aggression while supporting human rights, democratic opposition, secure communications, and accountability for regime officials.
Protect the supremacy of the U.S.
Constitution and American civil and criminal law.
No religious legal code, including Sharia, should be enforced by government or recognized in a way that overrides constitutional rights, due process, equal protection, or individual liberty.
I oppose hudud punishments such as flogging, amputation, stoning, or execution as criminal penalties and support clear legal safeguards against any attempt to impose them in the United States, while preserving every American’s constitutional freedom to worship and practice religion peacefully.
Protect gasoline-powered motorcycles and oppose government mandates that eliminate internal-combustion motorcycles.
Protect motorcyclists from profiling based solely on their motorcycle, clothing, patches, or lawful associations.
Preserve the right to repair and modify motorcycles, including access to parts, diagnostic information, and independent repair.
Ensure fair access to roads and highways without unnecessary motorcycle-specific restrictions, and allow states to adopt safe lane-filtering or lane-splitting rules.
Promote motorcycle-aware road design and maintenance, motorist-awareness and rider-safety education, and reasonable equipment, emissions, and noise standards that do not effectively prohibit otherwise lawful motorcycles.
Protect the ownership and operation of classic, custom, and modified motorcycles; lawful motorcycle clubs, associations, events, rallies, and group rides; fair treatment in insurance, parking, tolling, registration, licensing, and transportation policy; and consumer choice among gasoline, electric, hybrid, and future motorcycle technologies.
Oppose blanket bans on dogs in public recreational spaces.
For many Utahns, dogs are family members and should be welcome in the public spaces where families recreate whenever access can be managed responsibly.
Support expanded dog access to Utah’s canyons, trails, parks, and public lands, including a review of existing restrictions with the goal of allowing dogs wherever practical and lawful.
Protect watersheds, wildlife, other visitors, and public health through reasonable leash, waste-control, and responsible-owner rules rather than unnecessary blanket exclusions.
Protect due process, the presumption of innocence, and equal treatment under the law.
Allegations of domestic violence and sexual assault must be taken seriously and investigated professionally, while guilt must be determined by evidence and fair procedures rather than ideology, social pressure, or assumption.
Protect Christians and members of every faith from discrimination, threats, violence, vandalism, and unequal treatment.
Government must uphold the First Amendment consistently, protecting both the free exercise of religion and the right of every American to speak, believe, worship, or decline to worship freely.
Support Israel’s security and its right to defend its citizens while advancing American national interests.
Strengthen intelligence, cybersecurity, missile-defense, and counterterrorism cooperation; confront threats from hostile regimes and terrorist organizations; and encourage diplomacy, normalization, trade, and regional stability.
U.S. policy should remain grounded in the Constitution, congressional oversight, and the security of the American people.
Defend every woman’s freedom to choose how she dresses without government or religious coercion.
No woman in America should be compelled to follow Islamic modesty rules or punished for rejecting them, just as no woman should be prohibited from voluntarily wearing religious clothing.
I also stand with women and girls abroad, including in Iran, who seek freedom from compulsory veiling and morality-police enforcement.
Expand housing supply by reducing unnecessary barriers, encouraging responsible local zoning reform, speeding permitting, and using federal incentives carefully without overriding local control.
Pair housing reform with pro-growth tax and energy policies that reduce the everyday cost burden on Utah families.
Make health care more affordable through price transparency, competition, portable coverage, expanded health savings options, stronger rural access, and reforms that reduce prescription-drug costs without putting Washington in charge of every medical decision.
Support family formation with targeted tax incentives for employers that provide paid maternity leave and flexible work options.
Encourage businesses to help parents without creating a one-size-fits-all federal entitlement that burdens small employers.
Require stronger safeguards for minors on social-media platforms, meaningful parental controls, age-appropriate protections, clear data-privacy rules, and accountability when platforms knowingly expose children to harmful or exploitative content.
Ban members of Congress and senior staff from trading individual stocks while in office, strengthen financial disclosure and conflict-of-interest rules, and support congressional term limits through the constitutional process.
Public office should be public service, not a path to personal enrichment.
Secure the border, enforce immigration law, target cartels and human trafficking, improve visa and asylum processing, and preserve lawful immigration for people who follow the rules.
Immigration policy should protect national security while supporting the workforce needs of American communities.
Bring Utah stakeholders, local governments, water users, conservation groups, and federal agencies together to protect the Great Salt Lake while defending Utah's ability to manage its own lands and resources.
Conservation should be practical, locally informed, and respectful of property rights and multiple use.
Build stronger pipelines from Utah high schools, colleges, technical programs, and apprenticeships into advanced manufacturing, cybersecurity, aerospace, defense, energy, and skilled trades.
Federal workforce policy should help Utahns train for high-value jobs without forcing everyone into a four-year degree.
Protect benefits Americans have earned and paid for, oppose using Social Security as a general spending account, and pursue long-term solvency through growth, anti-fraud measures, administrative modernization, and bipartisan reforms that protect current retirees and near-retirees.
Improve Utah air quality and protect water, wildlife, and public lands while maintaining reliable and affordable energy.
Support an all-of-the-above energy strategy, cleaner technology, efficiency, nuclear power, responsible domestic production, and innovation instead of blanket bans and mandates.
Modernize outdated federal technology, use secure AI and automation where appropriate, eliminate duplicative programs, reduce fraud and improper payments, and require agencies to measure outcomes.
Government should deliver faster service at lower cost while protecting privacy and constitutional rights.
HOW I WILL SERVE
Support good legislation regardless of who introduces it, and oppose bad legislation regardless of party.
Publish clear positions, explain major votes, and tell constituents what was promised, what was delivered, and what still needs work.
Maintain regular constituent outreach, local events, prompt communication, and a serious casework operation for Utah residents.
Prioritize measurable improvements in security, affordability, government efficiency, technology policy, and economic opportunity.
Apply the same constitutional standards consistently, including free speech, due process, privacy, religious liberty, and equal protection.
Use real-world cybersecurity, technology, risk, and AI experience to scrutinize federal programs and emerging threats.
WHY I’M RUNNING
I have spent my career identifying risk before it becomes a crisis.
Washington too often reacts late, spends without discipline, and measures activity instead of results.
Congress needs more leaders who ask hard questions early and demand accountability for outcomes.
I’m running because the next decade will test America in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, national security, energy, economic competitiveness, affordability, and the basic question of whether government remains accountable to the people.
No party has a monopoly on good ideas, and party loyalty should never substitute for judgment.
Utah should have a representative who is prepared to listen, explain decisions clearly, work across ideological lines when it benefits the state, and stand firm when constitutional principles are at stake.
