MEDIA • PODCASTS • INTERVIEWS
Book Bobby Darvish
Bobby Darvish, a write-in candidate for Utah’s 1st Congressional District, is available for podcast appearances, television and radio interviews, news commentary, livestreams, debates, speaking engagements, and other media opportunities related to his campaign and the issues facing Utah and America.
INTERVIEW TOPICS
Topics for conversation.
Bobby is available to discuss Utah and national politics; his campaign for Utah’s 1st Congressional District; cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and national security; Iran and the Middle East; U.S.–Israel relations; religious freedom and Christianity; immigration and the Iranian-American experience; government accountability and public safety; technology policy and emerging threats; motorcycle and individual-liberty issues; and his personal journey from Iran to America and from Muslim leadership to Christianity.
INDEPENDENT MEDIA WELCOME
From quick comments to long-form conversations.
Bobby welcomes invitations from established news organizations as well as independent journalists, podcasters, YouTubers, livestream hosts, and other serious content creators.
Whether you are requesting a five-minute comment, a long-form podcast conversation, an on-camera interview, or an appearance at an event, please reach out.
