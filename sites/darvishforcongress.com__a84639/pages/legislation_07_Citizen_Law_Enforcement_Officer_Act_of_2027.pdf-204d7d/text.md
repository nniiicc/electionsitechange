A BILL To condition specified Federal law-enforcement assistance on U.S.-citizenship requirements for newly appointed sworn State and local officers and to restrict Federal deputation of noncitizens.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled: SEC.
1.
SHORT TITLE This Act may be cited as the 'Citizen Law Enforcement Officer Act of 2027'.
SEC.
2.
FINDINGS AND PURPOSE The purpose of this Act is to establish a uniform Federal citizenship rule for Federal lawenforcement authority and for newly appointed sworn officers in State and local agencies receiving designated Federal public-safety grants, while providing a transition period for officers lawfully serving before enactment.
SEC.
3.
DEFINITIONS “Sworn law-enforcement officer” means a person authorized by law to carry a firearm in official capacity and exercise general arrest powers.
“Covered agency” means a State, local, tribal, or territorial law-enforcement agency receiving Byrne Justice Assistance Grant or COPS program funding, subject to implementing regulations.
SEC.
4.
CITIZENSHIP REQUIREMENT FOR FEDERALLY SUPPORTED LAW ENFORCEMENT Beginning 1 year after enactment, a covered agency may not newly appoint a person as a sworn law-enforcement officer unless the person is a citizen of the United States.
Each covered agency shall certify compliance annually.
The Attorney General may withhold up to 10 percent of designated grant funds for noncompliance and shall restore withheld amounts upon timely cure as provided by regulation.
SEC.
5.
FEDERAL EMPLOYMENT-AUTHORIZATION LIMITATION Nothing in an employment-authorization document, permanent-resident status, or other noncitizen immigration status shall by itself confer eligibility for Federal sworn law-enforcement employment where United States citizenship is otherwise required by law or regulation.

6.
FEDERAL TASK FORCES AND CROSS-DESIGNATION A noncitizen may not receive Federal deputation, Federal arrest authority, cross-designation as a Federal officer, or a federally funded sworn position on a Federal task force, except for a narrowly defined foreign liaison or intelligence-support role that carries no domestic arrest authority.
SEC.
7.
EXISTING OFFICERS A noncitizen law-enforcement officer lawfully appointed before enactment may continue serving for up to 24 months after enactment, subject to State law and agency policy.
Federal agencies shall provide information regarding lawful naturalization pathways and may request expedited adjudication without waiving any requirement of the Immigration and Nationality Act.
SEC.
8.
STATE AUTHORITY A State may impose additional qualifications for law-enforcement officers and may apply a citizenship requirement more broadly than this Act.
SEC.
9.
EFFECT ON INCONSISTENT STATE LAW To the extent a State law authorizes newly appointed noncitizen sworn officers in an agency that elects to receive covered Federal grant funds, the agency shall choose between compliance with this Act and the affected discretionary grant funding.
This Act does not directly command a State to alter its law.
SEC.
10.
VALIDITY OF PRIOR LAW-ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS An arrest, citation, investigation, testimony, or other official act is not invalid solely because the officer performing the act was a noncitizen who was otherwise lawfully appointed at the time.
SEC.
11.
EFFECTIVE DATE Except as otherwise provided, this Act takes effect 1 year after enactment.
