A BILL To support peaceful Iranian democratic resistance, communications, civil society, sanctions enforcement, evidence preservation, defectors, and transition planning without recognizing an exclusive future ruler or authorizing lethal aid.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled: SEC.
1.
SHORT TITLE This Act may be cited as the 'Iranian Freedom and Democratic Resistance Support Act of 2027' and may also be referred to as the 'Iranian Rebel Alliance Support Act'.
SEC.
2.
FINDINGS Congress finds that the people of Iran have repeatedly demonstrated demands for representative government, civil and political rights, economic opportunity, and freedom from political repression; that censorship and communications shutdowns impede peaceful organization; and that democratic transition planning can reduce humanitarian and security risks.
SEC.
3.
STATEMENT OF POLICY It is the policy of the United States to support the ability of the Iranian people to determine their own government through peaceful, representative, and rights-respecting processes, without designating an exclusive future ruler, dynasty, party, or constitutional system.
SEC.
4.
DEFINITIONS “Iranian Democratic Resistance Alliance” means a pluralistic network or eligible set of organizations meeting the criteria in section 5.
“Freedom Fund” means the fund established by section 6.
“Civil society” includes independent media, labor, women’s rights, student, minority, professional, human-rights, and democracy organizations.
SEC.
5.
ELIGIBILITY FOR ASSISTANCE An organization receiving assistance under this Act shall support representative government and free elections; internationally recognized human rights; equality before the law; the right of Iranians to determine their form of government; financial transparency; and nonviolent democratic political activity.
An organization is ineligible if it is controlled by a foreign government or is responsible for a systematic pattern of terrorism, torture, war crimes, crimes against humanity, or targeted violence against civilians.

6.
IRANIAN FREEDOM FUND The Secretary of State, in coordination with the Administrator of the United States Agency for International Development, shall establish an Iranian Freedom Fund to carry out this Act.
SEC.
7.
SECURE COMMUNICATIONS AND INDEPENDENT MEDIA Not less than $75,000,000 annually, subject to appropriations, may support censorship circumvention, secure communications, satellite and internet access, independent Persian-language media, digital security, and documentation tools.
SEC.
8.
CIVIL SOCIETY AND PEACEFUL DEMOCRATIC ORGANIZATION Not less than $50,000,000 annually may support eligible civil-society organizations, labor and professional networks, women’s rights, student groups, minority communities, rule-of-law initiatives, and peaceful democratic organizing.
SEC.
9.
HUMANITARIAN ASSISTANCE Not less than $25,000,000 annually may support humanitarian relief for persons injured, displaced, detained, or economically harmed by political repression, with safeguards against diversion to sanctioned or terrorist entities.
SEC.
10.
EVIDENCE PRESERVATION AND ACCOUNTABILITY Not less than $25,000,000 annually may support secure preservation of evidence concerning gross human-rights abuses, corruption, torture, extrajudicial killings, hostage-taking, and other serious violations for use by lawful future accountability mechanisms.
SEC.
11.
DEFECTORS AND NONPARTICIPATION IN REPRESSION The Secretary of State shall develop lawful mechanisms to encourage officials and security personnel to refuse unlawful orders, protect civilians, preserve records, and cooperate with legitimate accountability processes.
Nothing in this Act grants automatic immigration status or immunity from prosecution.
SEC.
12.
SANCTIONS AND ILLICIT-FINANCE ENFORCEMENT The President shall prioritize enforcement against entities that finance repression, terrorism, hostage-taking, proliferation, censorship, or corruption, while using available humanitarian exemptions to reduce harm to the Iranian people.
SEC.
13.
ASSET IDENTIFICATION AND PRESERVATION The Secretary of the Treasury shall coordinate efforts to identify assets traceable to sanctioned Iranian officials or entities and preserve such assets as authorized by law.
This Act does not authorize confiscation without due process.

14.
DEMOCRATIC TRANSITION PLANNING Up to $25,000,000 annually may support nonexclusive planning for continuity of essential services, constitutional processes, independent courts, policing under civilian law, economic stabilization, refugee return, and protection of minorities during a potential transition.
Not later than 180 days after enactment, the Secretary of State shall submit a classified and unclassified transition-planning report to the appropriate congressional committees.
SEC.
15.
RESTRICTIONS No funds under this Act may be used to provide lethal assistance, conduct assassination, support a Foreign Terrorist Organization, finance terrorism, or designate an exclusive future ruler of Iran.
SEC.
16.
PROTECTION OF ACTIVISTS AND SENSITIVE INFORMATION Agencies shall minimize collection and public disclosure of personally identifying information that could expose Iranian activists or their families to retaliation.
SEC.
17.
OVERSIGHT AND REPORTING The Secretary of State shall report to the appropriate congressional committees at least every 180 days on recipients, expenditures, security controls, measurable outcomes, diversion risks, and steps taken to preserve pluralism and prevent domination by a single faction.
SEC.
18.
AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS There are authorized to be appropriated $250,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2027 through 2031 to carry out this Act.
Amounts shall remain available as provided in appropriations Acts.
SEC.
19.
CONSTRUCTION AND SENSE OF CONGRESS Nothing in this Act authorizes the use of United States Armed Forces.
Congress supports the right of the Iranian people to choose their political future through free and fair processes and rejects collective punishment based on ethnicity, religion, or political background.
