CONGRESS • UTAH 1ST DISTRICT PRIVACY Privacy Policy Last updated: August 30, 2026.
Bobby Darvish for Congress respects the privacy of visitors to this website.
This policy explains, in general terms, what information may be provided through the site, how it may be used, and the choices available to visitors.
Information you choose to provide If you contact the campaign, request information, volunteer, submit a media inquiry, or otherwise communicate with us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, ZIP code, interests, message, or other information you choose to include.
Website forms and email The current volunteer and media-contact features are designed to open a pre-addressed email message in the visitor's email application.
The campaign website itself does not currently store those form entries in a campaign-operated server-side database.
Information you send by email is transmitted through your email provider and the campaign's email provider and may be retained as part of campaign correspondence.
The campaign does not currently intentionally deploy advertising cookies or behavioral advertising trackers on this site.
Like most websites, the hosting, security, domain, or network providers that deliver the site may automatically process limited technical information needed to provide and protect the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, request logs, timestamps, and security-related data.
How information may be used Information provided to the campaign may be used to respond to inquiries, coordinate volunteers, communicate campaign information, process media requests, improve website operations, maintain security, comply with applicable law, and support legitimate campaign activities.
Third-party services and links This website may link to third-party websites or services.
If fundraising, mailing-list, analytics, embedded-media, event, or other outside services are added, those services may have their own privacy policies and practices.
The campaign is not responsible for the privacy practices of unaffiliated third parties.
Campaign-finance disclosure Federal campaign-finance law may require political committees to collect, maintain, and publicly report certain contributor information when contributions are accepted.
Any future contribution page or fundraising processor should provide the notices and data-collection disclosures applicable to that activity.
Data security and retention The campaign uses reasonable measures appropriate to the information it handles, but no internet or email transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, recordkeeping, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.
Children This campaign website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.
Changes to this policy This policy may be updated as the campaign adds services or as legal and operational requirements change.
The date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are posted.
