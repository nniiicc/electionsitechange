A BILL To create voluntary Federal incentives for States and localities that substantially reduce property taxes on owner-occupied primary residences and adopt higher tax treatment for certain second homes while protecting long-term rental supply.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled: SEC.
1.
SHORT TITLE This Act may be cited as the 'Utah Homeowner First and Second-Home Accountability Act'.
SEC.
2.
PURPOSE The purpose of this Act is to encourage participating States and localities to place primaryresidence affordability ahead of discretionary second-home ownership while preserving State sovereignty over property taxation.
SEC.
3.
DEFINITIONS “Primary residence” means an owner-occupied dwelling used as the owner’s principal home for at least 183 days in a taxable year, subject to reasonable temporary-absence rules.
“Second home” means a personal, seasonal, recreational, or other residential property that is not the owner’s primary residence.
“Covered second home” includes a Utah residential property owned by a person whose primary residence is in another State.
SEC.
4.
PRIMARY-RESIDENCE PROPERTY-TAX RELIEF INCENTIVE The Secretary of the Treasury may award formula or competitive grants to a State or political subdivision that adopts policies reducing the effective property-tax liability of qualified owneroccupied primary residences by at least 50 percent from the otherwise applicable amount, subject to maintenance-of-effort and anti-displacement safeguards.
SEC.
5.
SECOND-HOME PROPERTY-TAX SURCHARGE A participating jurisdiction may qualify for enhanced Federal incentive funding by imposing a surcharge equal to 100 percent of the ordinary property-tax liability on a covered second home, resulting in total liability equal to 200 percent of the ordinary amount.

6.
PROTECTION OF LONG-TERM RENTAL HOUSING A residential property leased to a tenant under a bona fide long-term lease shall not be treated as a covered second home solely because the owner resides elsewhere.
Regulations shall deter sham leases designed to evade the surcharge.
SEC.
7.
EXEMPTIONS AND HARDSHIP RULES A participating jurisdiction shall provide reasonable exemptions or transition rules for military housing needs, inherited property during a reasonable estate or transfer period, temporary vacancy during sale or repair, qualifying agricultural property, and extraordinary hardship.
SEC.
8.
USE OF SECOND-HOME REVENUE A participating jurisdiction is encouraged to dedicate incremental second-home revenue to primary-residence tax relief, affordable housing, infrastructure serving year-round residents, emergency services, or property-tax circuit breakers for low- and moderate-income homeowners.
SEC.
9.
HOMEOWNER-FIRST PRINCIPLE Federal implementing rules shall favor programs that measurably reduce the tax burden on yearround owner-occupants and avoid shifting the relieved burden onto long-term renters.
SEC.
10.
STATE SOVEREIGNTY Participation is voluntary.
Nothing in this Act directly imposes a property tax, surcharge, exemption, assessment method, or rate on any State or local government.
SEC.
11.
REPORTING AND EFFECTIVE DATE Treasury shall report annually on participating jurisdictions, household relief, second-home treatment, rental-market effects, and grant expenditures.
The program applies beginning with the first taxable year after enactment for which a jurisdiction elects participation.
