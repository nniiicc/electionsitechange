Voting Information for Teton County
General - Tuesday, November 8, 2022
Effective July 1, 2021, Wyoming voters will be required to show an acceptable form of identification when voting in person.
There are many ID options for you to use to prove your identity.
Click here for a list of acceptable IDs.
From Teton County, “The easiest way to register to vote before election day is to stop by the Teton County Clerk’s office at 200 S Willow Street, Jackson, WY 83001.
We are located in the basement of the County Administration Building.” For more information regarding voter registration please visit the Wyoming Secretary of State’s website.
On Election Day, you may vote at one of the following Vote Centers from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. regardless of where you live in Teton County:
TETON COUNTY/JACKSON RECREATION CENTER - 155 E.
Gill Ave, Jackson, WY 83001
TETON COUNTY LIBRARY - 125 Virginian Ln, Jackson, WY, 83001
OLD WILSON SCHOOLHOUSE/COMMUNITY CENTER - 5655 Main St, Wilson, WY, 83014
TETON COUNTY WEED AND PEST SHOP 7575 US-89, Jackson, WY 83001
ALTA BRANCH LIBRARY 50 Alta School Rd., Alta, WY 83414
If these locations are not convenient for you, you can also vote by Absentee prior to Election Day.
Wyoming is a "no excuse absentee voting" state which allows the voter to cast an absentee ballot for any reason whatsoever.
Where:
Teton County Administration Building
200 S Willow Street, Jackson, WY 83001
Hours:
Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
2022 General Election
September 23, 2022 to November 7, 2022.
The absentee polling site is not open on Election Day.
You must vote at a designated Vote Center on Election Day.
For more information about Absentee Voting please visit the Wyoming Secretary of State’s website.
