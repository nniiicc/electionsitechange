Julia’s Priorities
- Arizona families are working harder than ever, but rising costs continue to strain Arizonans.
Julia will fight to lower everyday expenses like rising utility bills and support policies that help working families keep more of what they earn.
- Water is Arizona's most precious resource and essential to our future.
Julia will support responsible water management, hold large corporate water users accountable, and protect our long-term water supply so our communities, businesses, and farmers can thrive.
- Every Arizonan deserves a safe, affordable place to call home.
Julia will work to increase the supply of affordable housing, reduce barriers to homeownership, and support smart growth that keeps communities strong without pricing families out.
- As an Air Force veteran, Julia understands the sacrifices made by those who serve.
She'll fight to ensure veterans receive the benefits they've earned.
