Beth Llewellyn McLaughlin
Beth is a career educator who taught French for 29 years at Southwest High School in the Fort Worth ISD.
She has lived and worked in HD97 for 44 years.
She also ran in 2018 for this seat, as a champion of public education, access to affordable healthcare, and social justice.
She has served as a volunteer docent at the Kimbell Art Museum since 2012, and on the boards of voter activation groups.
Beth cares deeply about her community.
She will listen to her constituents.
She will advocate for them, not special interests, in Austin.
She believes political campaigns should be funded by people, not dark money PACS.
- For fully-funded public education
- For freedom in decisions about your health and how to live your life
- For responsible investment of tax dollars to benefit ALL Texans
- For dark money out of politics
- For integrity and transparency
