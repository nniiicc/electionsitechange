Open and Accessible Leadership: As your senator, I have always made it a top priority to be open, accessible, and accountable.
My only special interests are the people I represent.
I work every day to earn your trust through hard work, honesty, and transparency.
I believe respectful dialogue and civil discourse are essential to solving problems.
Utahns deserve leaders who listen, collaborate, and serve with integrity and diplomacy.
Local Control and States’ Rights: Throughout my service in the Utah Legislature, I have consistently defended local control and pushed back against federal overreach in education, healthcare, and public lands.
Decisions are best made close to the people they affect.
I believe Utah leaders (and not federal agencies) best understand our lands, communities, and families.
I will continue to protect Utah’s constitutional right to manage itself responsibly and efficiently.
Proven Leadership: During my time in the Legislature, I have sponsored and passed the vast majority of the bills I have introduced on critical issues such as public lands, water, wildland fire prevention, small business support, healthcare reform, and tax policy.
Real leadership is not about how many bills you file but about the impact you create and the relationships you build.
Effective legislators work collaboratively, guided by strong values, deep preparation, and respect for others.
That is how I represent you at the Utah Capitol.
Economic Growth and Fiscal Responsibility: Utah’s long-term success depends on a strong, growing economy, and not higher taxes.
I have consistently supported policies that expand opportunity and reward hard work while keeping government spending in check.
I earned one of the top ratings in the Utah Senate from the Utah Taxpayers Association for responsible tax policy.
As a small business owner, I know firsthand how growth happens: when regulation is smart, taxes are fair, and innovation is encouraged.
Strong small businesses mean strong communities.
Energy and Natural Resources: Nearly 70 percent of Utah is federally controlled land, and that presents challenges for our economy and infrastructure.
I believe those who live and work on these lands, like our ranchers, farmers, sportsmen, and energy producers, are their best stewards.
I support responsible energy development that balances preservation with progress and keeps rural Utah strong.
I continue to advocate for policies that return more management authority to the state and ensure our rural roads and infrastructure receive the resources they need.
Responsible Government Spending: Government should focus on core responsibilities, including public safety, education, infrastructure, and health, and spend taxpayer dollars wisely.
I oppose creating new, unnecessary programs and believe every budget item should be evaluated by asking, “Is this needed?” and “Does it deliver value?” I stay closely connected with local officials to understand community priorities and advocate for those needs at the Legislature.
Healthcare: As a pharmacist and healthcare professional, I bring firsthand experience to healthcare policy.
Utah provides high-quality care at a lower cost than most states, but challenges remain.
I support efforts that increase access, reduce costs, and improve outcomes, while maintaining choice and competition.
We must move toward value-based care that rewards results, not just volume.
I have sponsored and supported legislation that strengthens our healthcare system and protects rural access to essential services.
Education: Education is central to Utah’s future.
As the first college graduate in my family, I know the power of opportunity.
I currently serve on the committees overseeing higher education budgets and policy, ensuring our colleges and universities have the resources they need to help students succeed.
I regularly meet with educators and administrators across my district to listen, learn, and advocate for their needs.
Southern Utah is fortunate to have exceptional institutions like Southern Utah University, Southwest Tech, and Utah Tech University that serve as engines for both education and economic growth.
I support continued investment in public education and responsible funding for our schools, while recognizing that federal land restrictions limit Utah’s revenue base.
I will keep fighting to ensure our classrooms receive every available dollar and that local educators have the flexibility and respect they deserve.
Immigration: Immigration remains one of the most complex and emotional issues facing our nation.
As the federal government previously failed to secure the border and enforce existing laws, it left states to deal with the consequences.
I support policies that uphold the law, protect our communities, and remove the criminal element of illegal immigration.
Enforcement must be fair, constitutional, and focused on safety and accountability.
A Record of Results Utah succeeds when we focus on strong families, sound policy, and civil leadership.
My approach has always been grounded in listening first, leading with integrity, and finding practical solutions that work for Southern Utah.
I believe collaboration, not conflict, is the key to lasting progress, and I will continue to represent you with respect, transparency, and commitment to our shared values.
