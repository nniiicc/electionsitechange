My Core Values
I believe Utah’s greatest strength begins at home.
Strong families and tight-knit communities are the foundation of everything good in our state.
I will always fight to protect the family, defend our freedoms, and keep government accountable to the people it serves.
Fiscal responsibility is essential to preserving the Utah way of life, and we must live within our means and let a healthy economy, not higher taxes, fund the services we truly need.
I believe in honesty, transparency, and ethics in every decision I make, and I value civility and diplomacy in public service.
Utahns deserve leaders who can disagree without dividing, who listen before they speak, and who work together to find common ground.
The Constitution is not just a document to me; it is the promise that every Utahn deserves opportunity, not guarantees.
Through integrity, respect, and collaboration, we can build a stronger Utah for generations to come.
