Meet Larry Larry Quintal proudly served two terms on the Taunton City Council.
As a City Councilor, he was deeply dedicated to helping our veterans, caring for our furry friends, and keeping Taunton affordable.
Larry has a long history of being involved in community activities, including serving as the past President of the Prince Henry Society of Taunton, a member of the Polish American Citizens Club of Taunton and the Auxiliary of Taunton Area Vietnam Veterans, and a communicant of Saint Anthony Church.
In 2020, Quintal was awarded the Portuguese-American of the Year.
Previously, he served on the Taunton Area Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors and as a member of the Taunton Eagles.
Larry Quintal is a small business owner in Taunton.
He and his wife, Christine, co-own Silva Funeral Home, and they are proud parents of 6 children.

As the next State Representative, Larry will fight for our shared priorities of affordability , accountability , and accessibility .
Here are his goals: Lower Cost of Living —It is time to restore affordability to Massachusetts.
As a business owner, Larry is the only candidate with the experience to fight high utility bills and other government controlled agencies in their fees to lower our cost of living.
He will work with Senator Dooner to reduce our utility costs along with reducing other fees.
Less Taxes —Larry is the only candidate who will hold the line on taxes.
Larry is the only candidate to sign the No New Taxes Pledge.
Larry supports reducing the income tax to 4%.
Larry supports rolling back the sales tax to 5%.
Larry is opposed to tripling the auto excise tax.
More State Aid for Communities —As a former City Councilor, Larry understands the importance of state aid to our communities.
He will work with Senator Kelly Dooner to increase local aid, which is vital to keeping property taxes low while providing quality services.
Accountability — As our next State Representative, Larry will support the audit.
Moreover, he will work to end the legislature’s exemption from the Open Meetings and Open Records laws.
It is time for transparency.
Accessibility — One of the most important jobs as a State Representative is helping constituents.
Unlike Lisa Field, Larry will hold weekly office hours.
He will be there, not an aide.
His door will always be open.

“It is time to lower the tax burden in Taxachusetts to make our lives more affordable.
As the next State Representative, I will not support raising taxes.
I am opposed to Governor Healey’s new taxes on prescription drugs, a candy tax, additional fees on cable tv, and increases in the auto excise taxes.

Quintal also wants to see the sales tax reduced from 6.25% to 5%.

As a City Councilor, he supported property tax breaks for seniors.
“The people of Easton and Taunton need a State Representative who will protect their wallets.
I am the only candidate with a proven track record,” added Quintal.
